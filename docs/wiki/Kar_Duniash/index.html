    Segmentum Fortress of the Ultima Segmentum

    Kar Duniash is the Segmentum Fortress of the Ultima Segmentum, the largest of the five segmenta of the Imperium of Mankind, which covers the eastern half of the galaxy. It is the headquarters of Battlefleet Ultima of the Imperial Navy, the seat of an academy that trains the Navy's officers, and the principal yard for the construction of Dominator-class cruisers.[1][2]

    Since the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, Kar Duniash has lain on the far side of the Rift from Terra, in the Imperium Nihilus, and close to the expanding frontier of the T'au Empire. Its defence and the protection of the warp routes leading to it have shaped Imperial strategy in the eastern galaxy during the Era Indomitus.[3]

    History

    [edit]

    Foundation

    [edit]

    The records of Kar Duniash's settlement have not been opened to general study. The world first appears in Navy archives as a fleet anchorage of the eastern Great Crusade in the late 30th millennium.[a] When the Imperium was divided into segmenta after the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31), probably about 020.M31 as part of Roboute Guilliman's reforms, it became the fortress of the Ultima Segmentum. Imperial historians have noted that this placed the command of the eastern fleets well away from Ultramar, whose primarch had briefly ruled the Imperium Secundus, so that no single power in the east would hold both the Legion and the fleet.[4][5]

    The eastern wars

    [edit]
    See also: Eastern Fringe

    From Kar Duniash Battlefleet Ultima fought the wars of the Eastern Fringe: against the Orks of Charadon and Octarius, the pirates of the Maelstrom, and in the 41st millennium the Tyranids. Its squadrons supported the defence of Macragge against Hive Fleet Behemoth in 745.M41 alongside the ships of Bakka, and the Damocles Gulf Crusade of 742.M41 against the T'au.[2][b]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    The Great Rift cut Kar Duniash off from the Astronomican in 999.M41. In the first decades of the new millennium the T'au launched their Fifth Sphere Expansion toward the region, and part of the purpose of the Chalnath Expanse campaign, fought by the Imperium against the T'au around 111.M42, was to protect the warp routes to Kar Duniash.[3][c]

    Geography

    [edit]

    The exact position of Kar Duniash is a naval secret. Published charts place it in the eastern Ultima Segmentum, nearer the Eastern Fringe than Ultramar, and within reach of the Damocles Gulf and the T'au border. The fortress consists of the planet and an orbital shipyard of great size, surrounded by defence stations.[2]

    Society

    [edit]

    Kar Duniash is a naval world. Its academy trains officers for Battlefleet Ultima and for other fleets, and many of its graduates come from naval dynasties of the eastern sectors. The yard is the primary builder of the Dominator-class cruiser, a design armed with a nova cannon that serves throughout the segmentum.[2]

    As at the other Segmentum Fortresses, authority is divided between the Lord High Admiral of the segmentum and the Administratum, whose Master of the Fortress keeps the records and administers the tithes that pay for the fleet. The Adeptus Mechanicus supervises the yards under treaty.[1]

    Battlefleet Ultima

    [edit]

    Battlefleet Ultima is responsible for the largest area of any segmentum fleet, and its squadrons are spread across hundreds of sector commands, from the borders of Ultramar to the Eastern Fringe. Kar Duniash is its headquarters and its chief source of new ships, crews and officers. Sector fleets draw replacements from its yards and its academy, and damaged capital ships are sent there for the repairs that lesser docks cannot perform.[2]

    The fleet's records note that its distances are its main weakness. Before the Great Rift, a summons from Kar Duniash could take years to reach the more distant sector fleets, and in practice the sector admirals of the east acted with great independence. Since 999.M41, with the Astronomican's light lost to much of the segmentum, the problem has become far worse, and the fortress has had to rely on local navigators and on the reinforcement of a few secure warp routes, which the Chalnath campaign was fought in part to keep open.[3][2]
