    39th millennium
    000.M39 – 999.M39
    AgeThe Waning
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra
    Key eventsTenth Black Crusade (001.M39); Redemption Crusades (from 131.M39); Eleventh Black Crusade (301.M39); Angevin Crusade (322–384.M39)
    Preceded by38th millennium
    Followed by40th millennium

    The 39th millennium (M39) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M39 to 999.M39. It falls within The Waning, but it was not only an age of loss. The Redemption Crusades of the 130s pushed the borders outward again, and the Angevin Crusade of 322–384.M39 conquered the Calyx Expanse and created the Calixis Sector, whose first Lord Sector, Drusus, was made a saint in 502.M39.[1]

    The Tenth Black Crusade opened the millennium with the Iron Warriors of Perturabo striking the Iron Hands at Medusa, and the Eleventh, the Doom of Relorria, followed in 301.M39. Beyond the Eastern Fringe the T'au Empire began its Second Sphere Expansion and took the Vespid into its service.[1]
