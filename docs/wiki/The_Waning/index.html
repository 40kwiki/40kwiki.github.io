    The Waning
    Era of the Age of the Imperium
    Imperial and traitor fleets in the Gothic War (139–160.M41), the greatest war of the late Waning
    Datec. 034.M38 (or 112.M38) – c. 744.M41
    Preceded byAge of Redemption
    Followed byTime of Ending
    Major warsNinth to Twelfth Black Crusades; the Redemption Crusades; the Angevin Crusade; the Gothic War; the Macharian Crusade; the First War for Armageddon
    OutcomeImperial power exhausted on every frontier; more worlds placed under Chapter rule

    The Waning is the name Imperial historians give to the long decline that followed the Age of Redemption, from the 38th millennium to about 744.M41. In those three thousand years the Imperium's armies, fleets and Chapters were worn down by the crusades of the previous age and by endless new wars, and star system after star system fell to Ork invasion, Chaos uprisings, xenos infestation or rebellion. The Waning ended with the prophecy that opened the Time of Ending.[1][2]

    The age is not defined by a single event. It saw four Black Crusades, the Redemption Crusades of the 39th millennium, the founding of the Calixis Sector, the Gothic War (139–160.M41) and the conquests of Lord Solar Macharius (392–399.M41), as well as the first meetings between the Imperium and the T'au Empire. Its last centuries are the best recorded; much of the 40th millennium survives only in the archives of frontier sectors.[1]

    Origins

    [edit]

    The Age of Redemption had been an age of crusading zeal, in which the Imperium sent its warships, regiments and Chapters against heretics and aliens across the galaxy. By the early 38th millennium those crusades had drawn so many forces from their posts that thousands of worlds were left undefended. Historians differ on where the Waning begins: the Annals of the Imperium close the Age of Redemption around 034.M38, others in 112.M38, the year the survivors of the Abyssal Crusade returned from the Eye of Terror.[2]

    Character of the age

    [edit]

    Through the Waning the Adeptus Terra answered disorder with harsher law and heavier punishment. In many lawless regions the Administratum handed whole systems to the direct rule of Space Marine Chapters, since only the Astartes had the strength to hold them. Seers and astropaths spoke of great disturbances in the Warp, like a swell before an unseen storm. Imperial historians agree that the Imperium entered the 41st millennium weaker than at any time since the Age of Apostasy, with much of the Segmentum Pacificus and the Ultima Segmentum thinly held.[1][3]

    Principal events

    [edit]

    38th millennium

    [edit]
    Main article: 38th millennium

    In 537.M38 Abaddon the Despoiler launched the Ninth Black Crusade against the naval fortress Cancephalus and the neighbouring world of Antecanis. The seventeen-year war that followed stripped both of their manpower, and Abaddon's sorcerers worked unopposed to weaken reality in the region. In 666.M38, in the event called the Grim Harvest, a fleet of space hulks came out of the Warp near Terra: some could be identified as ships of the Lost Crusade of 754.M37, the pilgrims of the Children's Crusade, now carrying mutants and daemons. The Inquisition hid the event.[1]

    39th millennium

    [edit]
    Main article: 39th millennium

    In 001.M39 the Tenth Black Crusade struck the Helica Sector, while Perturabo's Iron Warriors attacked the Iron Hands' world of Medusa. The Mausolean Cataclysm followed in 103.M39. In 131.M39 the Redemption Crusades began: a great hero arose in each segmentum, and their combined crusades pushed the Imperium's borders further than at any time in five hundred years. Some fifty years later all five vanished without trace, and the Ecclesiarch Inovian III declared them saints. In 301.M39 the Eleventh Black Crusade, the Doom of Relorria, ended in a war between the Black Legion and an Ork Waaagh!. The Angevin Crusade (322–384.M39) conquered the Calyx Expanse and founded the Calixis Sector in 384.M39.[1]

    40th millennium

    [edit]
    Further information: 40th millennium

    The record of the 40th millennium is scattered. It includes the waking of Necrons of the Oltep Dynasty at Cocholos in 052.M40, the Long Night of Verronus in 249.M40, the rebellion of Krieg in 433.M40 and its Atonement, and the first battle between Imperial ships and Necron warships, in the Yuctan System in 666.M40. The future Chapter Master of the Blood Angels, Dante, was born in 447.M40.[1]

    41st millennium

    [edit]
    Further information: 41st millennium

    The Waning's last centuries saw some of the Imperium's largest wars. The Twelfth Black Crusade, the Gothic War (139–160.M41), cost the Imperium four of the six Blackstone Fortresses. The Macharian Crusade (392–399.M41) took a thousand worlds, which collapsed into the Macharian Heresy after the death of Macharius. In 444.M41 the daemon primarch Angron led the First War for Armageddon. In 500.M41 visions of the Emperor weeping, the Tears of the Emperor, were reported on worlds across the Imperium. The Damocles Gulf Crusade against the T'au began in 742.M41.[1][3]

    End

    [edit]

    Imperial historians close the Waning in about 744.M41, when the seer Taggarath of Corrinto prophesied the coming of the end times. The arrival of Hive Fleet Behemoth in 745.M41 and the waking of the Necrons soon after seemed to confirm the prophecy, and the age that followed is called the Time of Ending.[1]
