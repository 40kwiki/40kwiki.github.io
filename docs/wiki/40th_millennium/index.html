    40th millennium
    000.M40 – 999.M40
    AgeThe Waning
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra
    Key eventsAtonement of Krieg (from 433.M40); Hadex Anomaly (656.M40); Bastion Founding (late M40); discovery of the Maw (997.M40)
    Preceded by39th millennium
    Followed by41st millennium

    The 40th millennium (M40) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M40 to 999.M40, the last full millennium of The Waning. No Black Crusade is recorded in it, and its history survives mostly as scattered entries from the frontier sectors: the Calixis Sector and its Koronus Expanse, and the Jericho Reach, where the Hadex Anomaly swallowed the old sector capital of Verronus in 656.M40.[1]

    The best-known event of the age is the rebellion of Krieg in 433.M40 and the five centuries of atomic war by which its loyal regiments retook their world. The Necrons began to wake on scattered tomb worlds, the T'au Empire grew by diplomacy under the Golden Ambassador, and the future Commander Dante was born on Baal Secundus in 447.M40. In 997.M40 the Rogue Trader Purity Lathimon found the safe passage through the Maw into the Koronus Expanse.[1]

    140.M40The battle of Vern IV, fought by the Death Spectres.
    228.M40The Castigations of Golgotha begin, with the Fire Hawks.Cousteau XI made uninhabitable before this date.
    236.M40The battle of Endikon IX, fought by the Red Scorpions.
    249.M40The Long Night of Verronus, last Imperial bastion of the old Jericho Sector.
    325.M40An Explorator fleet reaching the forge world of Samech is lured to its ruin by hereteks.
    360.M40A Kroot war sphere appears in the Jericho Reach.
    433.M40The governor of Krieg declares independence; Colonel Jurten begins the Atonement of Krieg with atomic war.
    447.M40Luis Dante born on Baal Secundus.
    550.M40Grymm's Landing, a recruiting world of the Dark Angels, destroyed in an uprising led by the Alpha Legion.
    552–570.M40The White Sorrows: Drukhari corsairs raid the Periphery.
    561.M40The Nexxas Exculpation: the Emperor's Children fight the Imperial Guard on Nexxas.
    600.M40Vior'la defeats a great Ork invasion of the T'au Empire.
    656.M40The Hadex Anomaly forms and swallows the hive world of Verronus.
    666.M40An Imperial patrol first fights Necron warships in the Yuctan System.
    673.M40The space hulk Cauldron of Savagery returns to the Drusus Marches.
    675.M40The hive world of Vidium lost to the Drukhari.
    709.M40The Tanis Incident in the Hazeroth sub-sector.
    738–741.M40The War of Brass in the Gelmiro Cluster against the self-proclaimed "Emperor of Brass".
    896.M40The Golden Ambassador leads the T'au drive to recruit alien worlds.
    c. 899.M40Dante becomes Chapter Master of the Blood Angels after the Kallius Insurrection.
    917–924.M40Port Wander built by the Imperial Navy in the Rubycon II System.
    924.M40A Vindicare Assassin kills the Ork warlord Urglak the Unstoppable on Roxanzand.
