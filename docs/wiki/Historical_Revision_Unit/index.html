    Historical Revision Unit
    Department of the Adeptus Administratum
    Seal of the Administratum, the parent body
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    Parent bodyAdministratum
    SeatImperial Palace, Terra
    HeadThe "historicus"
    StaffIndentured Human adepts ("historitors") and servitors
    Specialist teamsDeletion Teams
    Principal recordsThe Librarium Terra

    The Historical Revision Unit is one of the many bureaucratic departments of the Administratum. Its adepts are indentured Human servants and servitors, known as "historitors", who work within the Imperial Palace. Their task is to revise records of Imperial history that are judged subversive. The revised versions are meant to be more reverent towards the Emperor and to glorify His name. The chief adept of the unit is called the "historicus".[1]

    Revision and deletion

    [edit]

    A key duty of the unit is to find historical records and, when ordered, to destroy them. Specially designated groups, the Deletion Teams, carry out this work. Their most common task is an Edict of Obliteration. This is the deliberate destruction of every record concerning a person or organisation declared ''Excommunicate Traitoris'' by the Emperor of Mankind, the High Lords of Terra or the Inquisition.[1]

    Most deletion missions take place in the catacombs of the Librarium Terra, an endless repository of Imperial records. It lies deep beneath the crust of Terra. The sound of its bureaucracy hums through its corridors, as it does through the rest of the Hive World of Terra.[1]

    The Deletion Teams

    [edit]

    A Deletion Team has ten adepts under the command of an officer of Captain rank. The adepts wear black bodysuits with hoods that leave only the eyes visible, and rebreathers filter out the dust that gathers in the Librarium. Each carries a narrow-nozzled flamer fed from promethium canisters on the belt. The captain usually carries a silenced autogun with a flaring flash suppressor. Imperial scholars who meet a team on a deletion mission are silenced at once, because no witness may survive a historical deletion.[1]

    The members of these teams are drawn from the Terran masses and are often put to the task almost from birth. After decades in the Librarium, they hold an instinctive sense that its records contain a dangerous and especially pure history. No one knows the full size of the holdings. Estimates vary, because no one has reached its furthest reaches and returned.[1]

    When a team finds the records it was sent to destroy, the captain signals and the flamers burn the shelves. The protective clothing turns aside the worst of the heat, but the section of the Librarium under attack becomes a furnace. The team moves through the whole section without a word and does not leave until every book and data-stack that might hold the information is ash. Centuries of history can be lost in an instant, whole planets, races and campaigns vanishing from Imperial memory.[1]
