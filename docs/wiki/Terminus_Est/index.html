    Terminus Est
    The Terminus Est in its present form, a Daemonship of Nurgle
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    TypeCapital ship, now a Daemonship
    Builtc. M30, shipyards of Luna
    Original ownerDeath Guard Legion (then the Dusk Raiders)
    AllegianceDeath Guard, Chaos (Nurgle)
    CommanderTyphus (formerly Calas Typhon)
    AgeMore than ten thousand years
    Descendant classDespoiler-class Battleship
    Known forCarrying the Destroyer Plague; the bombardment that opened the Siege of Terra

    The Terminus Est is a Daemonship of the Death Guard and the flagship of Typhus, Herald of Nurgle. It was one of the first capital ships given to the Legion and has now sailed for more than ten thousand years. Its very name is a curse on the lips of Imperial Navy captains.[1]

    Wherever it has appeared, Plague Marines and the Destroyer Plague have followed. Its design, copied in M36 from stolen plans, became the Despoiler-class Battleship, and the flaw it passed on has cost the Navy dozens of ships.[1][2]

    History

    [edit]

    Construction and the Great Crusade

    [edit]

    Imperial tradition holds that the ship was built before the Great Crusade in the yards of Luna, to a pattern larger and more cumbersome than any earlier. Its forked prow served as both a launch bay and a gun deck, so it could land hundreds of Stormbird dropships while bombarding a planet. The Emperor of Mankind gave it to the Legion later named the Death Guard.[1][a]

    Its captain was Calas Typhon. He fell to Chaos before his Legion and before his primarch Mortarion, and his corruption passed into the ship.[1]

    Horus Heresy

    [edit]

    The ship took part in the Isstvan III atrocity and pursued the frigate Eisenstein as it fled to warn Terra, c. 005.M31. At the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V it destroyed the Raven Guard flagship Shadow of the Emperor, deprived the Loyalists of air support, and is recorded as an early battle between ships built to carry attack craft.[3][1][b]

    Bound for Terra, the ship was becalmed in the Warp and Typhon killed its Navigator. There Nurgle visited the Destroyer Plague on every soul aboard but Typhon, who became Typhus. When the ship came to Terra, its hull already showed the mark of Chaos, and legend says it was first to bombard the Imperial Palace. When Horus fell, it covered the Legion's retreat to the Eye of Terror.[1]

    After the Heresy

    [edit]

    It was thought destroyed until 545.M35, when it fell on the Imperium again, with plagues spreading wherever a battle was lost. In 216.M36 the Adeptus Mechanicus found its plans on Barbarus, and the Despoiler class followed; its Gellar fields proved to fail after long exposure to the Warp.[1]

    In 497.M36 it was at Yermoth VIII, in 299.M37 it destroyed the Dominus Aquila star fort, and in 834.M37 it sealed the fate of the asteroid cities of the Emperor's Halo. In 620.M39 it fought the Orks of Octarius in the Dust War.[1] In 139.M41 it was seen near Anvil 206 during the Gothic War, and in the 13th Black Crusade it destroyed the cruiser Depth of Fury above Kathur, taking over thirty per cent structural damage that it regenerated.[1][4]

    Form and crew

    [edit]

    Its bulkheads are held by rust, its decks are lined with living flesh, and its gun decks have been replaced by sphincter-like growths that spit caustic filth. Clouds of Warp-flies hide the hull from gunners and swarm boarding parties.[1]

    Only Plague Marines walk its corridors unharmed. The ship needs no crew, having a dark sentience of its own, and Typhus sits on a throne of bone at its helm. It often leads a Plague Fleet and is said to shadow plague-struck ships until the survivors pray to Nurgle.[2]
