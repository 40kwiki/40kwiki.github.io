    The Black Dragons are a loyal Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes raised in the Twenty-first Founding, the "Cursed Founding". It is long rumoured that they were made from Salamanders gene-seed, and the Magos Biologis of the Adeptus Mechanicus believes it. The Inquisition censures them for the mutation of their gene-seed, which makes bone grow in blades from the head and forearms and, it is reported, long poisonous fangs from the mouth.[1]

    Some afflicted warriors sharpen and sheath their growths in adamantium for close combat. These are grouped into specialist squads of assault and close-support marines called Dragon Claws. The Chapter pushes the Inquisition to the limits of its tolerance, and only the disorder after the opening of the Great Rift has halted its latest investigation.[1][2]

    History

    [edit]

    The Cursed Founding

    [edit]

    Little is known of the Black Dragons in the open records. They were raised just before the Age of Apostasy, the era of the usurper Goge Vandire, in the Founding in which the Adeptus Mechanicus tried to improve gene-seed by removing its known flaws. The result was a series of Chapters plagued by mutation and the taint of Chaos. All records of the Founding's Chapters have been struck from the archives, so nobody can say how many there are. The flaws in the Black Dragons' seed soon appeared.[3]

    Their mutations make long cooperation with other Imperial bodies impossible, and they seldom stay part of a standing force for long. Even so, they have fought with Imperial forces as late as the Third War for Armageddon.[1]

    The Bone Curse and other wars

    [edit]

    In 232.M40 the Drukhari haemonculi of the Hex Coven learned of the Chapter and, in a bargain with the pirate Duke Sliscus, attacked it while it purged the Donorian System of monsters. A running war followed through the space lanes and aboard ships. The Black Dragons under Captain Zartath purged the minor world of Cable of the Reincarnated Supplicants, herding them along a fiery crevasse called the Leap of Destiny. They answered the plea of the Inquisitor Werner Lettinger on Antagonis, where the Curse of Unbelief threatened an outbreak of undeath, though the planet in the end had to be destroyed even with their help and that of an order of the Adepta Sororitas.[1]

    In 998.M41 the Black Dragons committed nine of their ten companies to the Third War for Armageddon against Ghazghkull Thraka.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    See also: Indomitus Crusade

    The Fourth Company under Captain Jorn Tanna joined Battlegroup Haephestus of Fleet Tertius in the Indomitus Crusade. A Torchbearer fleet under the Custodian Alezandrios Menza brought the technology and Mechanicus staff to make Primaris Space Marines and a squad of Black Dragon Greyshields, made from the Chapter's own tithed gene-seed, to the Fifth Company. The Chaplain Darrig accepted them and advised the High Dragon to take them as full initiates. In M42 the Fifth Company under Darrig and three Cadian regiments defended Rendolia against the World Eaters. It was the first battle of the Greyshields drawn from the Chapter's seed.[2]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Chapter Master is called the High Dragon. The colour of the left knee plate gives a Black Dragon's company. The Chapter's heretical practices and its genetic tithe, which it keeps close, are matters the Inquisition has never been allowed to examine fully.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Volos: Captain of the Second Company, who purged a Drukhari kabal from a mass conveyor with twenty warriors.
    • Zartath: former captain, taken by the Drukhari to the Volgorrah Reef, six years a slave in their hunts.
    • Gherryn: captain of the Fifth Company, killed at Rendolia.
    • Darrig: Chaplain who commands the Fifth Company.
