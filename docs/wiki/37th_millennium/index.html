    37th millennium
    000.M37 – 999.M37
    AgeAge of Redemption (from c. 010.M37)
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra; a reformed Adeptus Ministorum
    Key eventsGreat Cull (020.M37); Abyssal Crusade (321.M37); Lost Crusade (754.M37); Seventh and Eighth Black Crusades (811 and 999.M37)
    Preceded by36th millennium
    Followed by38th millennium

    The 37th millennium (M37) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M37 to 999.M37. It is the core of the Age of Redemption, which began in about 010.M37. Shamed by the Age of Apostasy, the Imperium launched crusade after crusade to prove its faith. The Great Cull purged the Adeptus Terra, thirty Chapters judged wanting were sent into the Eye of Terror in the Abyssal Crusade, and pilgrim fleets left the Imperium on crusades from which many never returned.[1]

    The crusading drew fleets and Chapters far from home and left many worlds bare. The Seventh Black Crusade, the Ghost War of 811.M37, fell on the Imperium with little warning, and the millennium ended with the Eighth. Beyond the Eastern Fringe the T'au, united by the Ethereals at the end of M36, built their first colonies and septs and took in their first client race.[2]

    M37The Sons of Medusa ratified as a Chapter; the last Moirate holdouts purged.
    M37–M39The Age of Plunder: Rogue Traders and Free Captains pour into the Calyx Expanse.See Calixis Sector.
    133.M37The world of Sinophia granted to the Rogue Trader Teresa Sinos.
    321.M37The Abyssal Crusade: thirty Chapters, the Judged, enter the Eye of Terror.Survivors return in 112.M38.
    361.M37Alpha Legion forces under Orik the Vile fight the Cadian Shock Troops on Mihok Secundus.
    380–405.M37An Ork Freeboota fleet of a hundred ships ravages the northern Jericho Reach.
    502.M37The Great Expansion of the T'au Empire begins.Lasts to 956.M38.
    533.M37The T'au colonise Lu'val, a moon of their homeworld, their first offworld holding.
    551.M37The battle for Kurin's Acropolis on Persembe II against the Chaos magister Potchek.
    c. 550–560.M37The Occlusiad War against the Apostles of the Blind King.The Administratum table gives 555.M37.
    563.M37A Venenum Temple Assassin sent against Governor Yawell is caught in a Warp rift.She arrives in 261.M38.
    651.M37A Black Shield calling himself Hail arrives at Watch Fortress Erioch.See Deathwatch.
    754.M37The Lost Crusade: millions of young pilgrims set out and vanish into the Warp.Their ships return in 666.M38.
    756.M37The colonies around Tau'n become the first sept outside the T'au home system.
    772.M37The industrial world of Phalan 10 bombarded for a month by the daemon-possessed Admiral Koth.See Imperial Navy.
    811.M37The Seventh Black Crusade, the Ghost War; Abaddon fights the Blood Angels at Midian.

    987.M37The whole Ravenwing hunts the Fallen Angel Brother Solas on Picus.
