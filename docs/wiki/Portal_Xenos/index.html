    From 40,000 Wiki, the Imperial encyclopedia

    Xenos are the non-human intelligent species that the Imperium meets, fights and sometimes trades with. Imperial doctrine classes them as enemies of Mankind, though a few have been studied as curiosities or tools. This portal gathers the articles on the alien species of the galaxy and the Imperial response to them.

    Selected article

    [edit]

    Xenos is the Imperial term for non-human intelligent species, and the doctrine that governs how they are fought, studied or destroyed. Imperial scholars divide the xenos into those that are known enemies, those that are dormant and those whose status is disputed. The Inquisition keeps the most complete records of the xenos threat.
