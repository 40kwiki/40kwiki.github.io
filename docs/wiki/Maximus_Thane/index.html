    Maximus Thane was a Space Marine of the Imperial Fists lineage who became the last Chapter Master of the Fists Exemplar, a successor Chapter of the Second Founding, and in the War of the Beast (544–546.M32) the closest ally of Koorland. After Koorland's death at Ullanor he rebuilt the Imperial Fists from the other scions of Rogal Dorn, led the third assault on Ullanor that killed the Beast, and was named Lord Commander of the Imperium.[1][2]

    As Lord Commander he ordered the Fourth Founding, the largest creation of Space Marine Chapters since the First Founding, and decreed that the Imperial Fists would crusade in a fleet rather than garrison Terra. He appointed the Grand Master of Assassins Drakan Vangorich Lord Protector to rule in his absence, and Vangorich used that power to murder the High Lords of Terra in the Beheading. Thane did not intervene.[3]

    About a century later, learning that the Lord Protector had become a paranoid tyrant, Thane returned to Terra with four hundred Space Marines and shot Vangorich in the Eversor temple at the north pole. His Chapter had by then ceased to be the garrison of Terra and the Fists Exemplar had been struck from the Imperium's records at his order. The manner and date of his own death are not recorded.[3][1]

    Early career

    [edit]
    Further information: Horus Heresy

    Thane was a veteran of the Imperial Fists, whose account of his early years differs among Imperial records. One chronicle places him as a captain of the Legion's 22nd Company during the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, fighting at the Helios Gate under the Blood Angels' Raldoron, defending the Saturnine Gate with Rogal Dorn and Sigismund against Fulgrim, and leading the survivors in the counter-attack that drove the traitors from Terra. Records of the Fists Exemplar make no mention of this service and know him only as a company captain of that Chapter.[4][1][a]

    Thane joined the Fists Exemplar at the Second Founding, in about 021.M31. The Chapter was created from the more progressive Imperial Fists who, like its first master Oriax Dantalion, accepted Roboute Guilliman's division of the Legions. Dorn named them the Fists Exemplar, and gave them the star fort Alcazar Astra. When the fort was beached on the world of Eidolica after a warp storm, Dantalion was killed, and the Chapter made Eidolica its home. Thane rose to be a company captain, and by 544.M32 he was Second Captain.[1]

    The War of the Beast

    [edit]
    Main article: War of the Beast

    The fall of Eidolica

    [edit]

    One of the Beast's attack moons appeared over Eidolica without warning in 544.M32. Chapter Master Alameda was killed within hours when a collapse of desert rock crushed his Land Raider, and First Captain Garthas fell soon after. Thane took command of the remnant inside the fortress-monastery. When the Black Templars' High Marshal Bohemond came to evacuate the Chapter, Thane at first resisted, but he saw that Eidolica was lost and ordered the survivors onto the Templar fleet.[1][5]

    Phall and the Last Wall

    [edit]

    Thane attended the muster of the Dorn successors at Phall and was the first Chapter Master to accept Koorland's command under the Last Wall Protocol. He took part in the failed assault on the attack moon over Terra, and afterwards urged Koorland to defy the council. Koorland sent him to find the Soul Drinkers, while another part of his Chapter under Zerberyn made a dark alliance with an Iron Warriors warband under the Warsmith Kalkator against the Orks.[1][6]

    Mars, Caldera and Ullanor

    [edit]

    Under the new Lord Commander, Thane led the Fists Exemplar to Mars to take the tech-priest Eldon Urquidex into custody. At Pavonis Mons he refused to be turned back by the Skitarii, but took care not to provoke them, until an errant shot from a Dunecrawler struck a drop pod and a brief firefight began. The Fabricator-General Kubik ended it. Thane accompanied Koorland to Caldera to find Vulkan, and when the first assault on Ullanor began the Fists Exemplar, in the Battle Barge Alcazar Remembered, formed Vulkan's flagship. His squad helped Koorland lift Vulkan's hammer Doomtremor, the test of command at Ullanor.[7][5]

    By the second assault the Chapter was reduced to seventy-three Space Marines from the three companies that had escaped Eidolica. Thane co-commanded the invasion with Wienand while Koorland led the assassination team into Gorkogrod. When Koorland was killed, Thane felt that he should have died in his place.[1][2]

    Lord Commander

    [edit]
    Main article: Lord Commander of the Imperium

    Rebuilding the Imperial Fists

    [edit]

    At Koorland's funeral on Inwit, at the Feast of Blades, Thane resolved to act without the High Lords. Allying himself with Vangorich and the Fabricator-General Kubik, he began to plan a third assault on Ullanor. The first step was to refound the Imperial Fists. Each Chapter of the Last Wall gave a share of its strength, and a thousand Space Marines paraded through the Imperial Palace in freshly painted yellow, with Thane at their head as Chapter Master. The truth of Ardamantua was kept from the people of Terra, who rejoiced to see their old defenders at full strength.[2][3]

    The third Ullanor

    [edit]

    Thane chose unconventional methods. Using Mechanicus technology he diverted asteroids onto Ullanor's capital, then brought a modified asteroid, 44 Thoosa, down on it in a controlled landing that wiped out much of Gorkogrod. The assault came in five attack groups: the new Imperial Fists with the Sisters of Silence under Thane, the Mechanicus under Gerg Zhokuv, the Last Wall Chapters under Bohemond, the Deathwatch under Asger Warfist and Wienand, and the Imperial Fists' fortress-monastery Phalanx in orbit. In the throne room Thane's group met one of the Prime-Orks, reckoned the Beast of Beasts, and the Sisters of Silence under Knight-Abyssal Kavalanera Brassanas performed their ritual with the last captive psyker, causing its head to burst just before it could slay him.[2][5]

    The Fourth Founding

    [edit]

    The council named Thane Lord Commander, and he issued three decrees. Every store of gene-seed in the vaults of Terra was to be used in a Fourth Founding, the greatest since the First, and every Chapter was to give some of its veterans for it. The gene-seed tithe was doubled for a century. The Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra but would become a crusading, fleet-based Chapter, and he would carry his authority to the worlds of the Imperium. Finally he named Vangorich Lord Protector, to speak with his voice, reminding the High Lords that the threat of his return would keep Vangorich honest. He left aboard the Phalanx.[3]

    The Beheading

    [edit]
    Main article: Beheading

    Vangorich, angered by the Fabricator-General's concealment of the Ork capital on the Ark Majesty instead of its destruction, and contemptuous of the High Lords, had the High Twelve killed in a single day in 546.M32. Only Wienand was spared. Thane received the reports in the middle of his crusade and did not move against the Lord Protector, judging that a ruthless ruler might spare the Imperium the indecision that had nearly lost the war. Vangorich governed for almost a century, rebuilt Terra and the Imperium's forces, and oversaw the Fourth Founding, but after some eighty years he showed paranoia and ordered purges.[3]

    Thane returned to Terra in about 626.M32. Four hundred Space Marines, a company each from the Halo Brethren and Sable Swords and two hundred Imperial Fists of the 1st, 4th and 5th Companies, made planetfall under the Sable Swords' Chapter Master Qublicus Amar, who was shot by a Vindicare assassin soon afterwards. Hundreds of assassins cost the Space Marines half their number in securing the Palace. Wienand gave Thane the intelligence to find Vangorich in the Eversor temple at the north pole, where a hundred Eversor assassins fell on the force. Only Thane survived to reach the Grand Master and shoot him with his bolt pistol. The Imperium slid into political anarchy for almost two decades, until the Ultramarines' Chapter Master Agnathio brought fifty Chapters to Terra and forced the factions to agree to a new council.[3]

    Later life

    [edit]

    Thane remained Chapter Master of the Imperial Fists after the Beheading and put down many of the revolts that followed the Lord Protector's death. He rebuked the former Black Templar Helbrant Alderic for bringing his old Chapter's ways into the Imperial Fists' 8th Company. His name is carved on the first bone of Dorn's hand in the Chapter's reliquary. The date and manner of his death are not recorded.[8]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    Thane is remembered in two ways. To the Imperial Fists he is the master who refounded the Chapter after Ardamantua and who gave it its crusading character, a Chapter without a fortress on Terra. To the Administratum he is the Lord Commander who allowed the Beheading and then undid it, and the Fourth Founding he ordered filled the Imperium's frontiers with new Chapters, many of them from the stock of the Fists Exemplar, whose surviving Space Marines were sent to found new Chapters of Dorn's lineage. A new Chapter inherited Eidolica and the Alcazar Remembered, and the Inquisition classified the existence of the Fists Exemplar. His war with the Beast showed, in the Imperium's own records, that no single Chapter could withstand a great enemy and that the old Legion's bonds had to be joined again.[1][3][6]
