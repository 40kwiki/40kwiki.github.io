    The Mournival was the advisory war council of four Space Marine Captains of the Luna Wolves Legion. They served their Primarch and Warmaster Horus as advisers and companions throughout the Great Crusade, until the Horus Heresy began. The Mournival was a cultural tradition of the Luna Wolves with no official standing in the Legion's command. No other Legiones Astartes used it.[1]

    Membership

    [edit]

    In the last days of the Great Crusade, the Mournival was made up of four of the most celebrated officers of the Luna Wolves. They were the First Captain Ezekyle Abaddon of the 1st Company, Captain Horus Aximand, known as "Little Horus", Captain Tarik Torgaddon of the 2nd Company, and Captain Garviel Loken of the 10th Company.[1]

    Before Loken joined, his place was held by Captain Hastur Sejanus of the 4th Company, a close friend of Horus. Sejanus was murdered during negotiations to win Imperial Compliance for the world of Sixty-Three-Nineteen. The First Chaplain Erebus of the Word Bearers later took the appearance of Sejanus through sorcery while Horus was healing in the Temple of the Serpent Lodge on Davin's moon. Erebus used this disguise to lead Horus towards the Chaos Gods.[1]

    Purpose

    [edit]

    Those chosen for the Mournival were regarded as the best officers of the Legion. Each member had a very different temperament, so that together they gave the Warmaster a range of viewpoints. The four temperaments follow the medical theory of the Four Humours, which holds that the balance of blood, yellow bile, black bile and phlegm shapes a person's character. A sanguine man is courageous and hopeful, a choleric man is angry and quick to temper, a melancholic man is prone to despondency, and a phlegmatic man is calm and unemotional. Abaddon was the choleric member of the Mournival, Aximand the melancholic, Torgaddon the sanguine and Loken the phlegmatic. Hastur Sejanus, whom Loken replaced, was said to have held all four humours in perfect balance.[2]

    Each member wore a phase of the moon on his helmet above the right eye. When Loken joined, Abaddon wore the Full Moon, Aximand the Half Moon, Torgaddon the Gibbous Moon and Loken the New Moon, which Sejanus had worn before him.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    According to the Iterator Memed, the word came from an old language of Terra and meant a hand of cards holding one of each of the four court cards, usually a winning hand. Its origin is uncertain. It either began soon after Horus took command of the Legion, when the Great Crusade reached his homeworld Cthonia, or grew from the informal warrior lodges that began after the Legion's conquest of Davin, about sixty years before the Heresy.[1]

    The Mournival advised Horus on strategy, tactics and diplomacy, and could serve his politics in dealings with other primarchs, the Imperial Army, planetary governors, the Council of Terra and the Mechanicum. By the time the Heresy began, thirteen Luna Wolves had sat in it, including its final four. After the Battle of Dwell it was brought back to strength with two new members, so that fifteen warriors of the Legion served in it before its end with the death of Horus at the Siege of Terra.[1]

    After Horus was corrupted on Davin's moon, the Mournival divided. Abaddon and Aximand followed him into the worship of the Chaos Gods, while Torgaddon and Loken stayed loyal to the Emperor of Mankind. The Mournival broke during the Isstvan III atrocity, when the Traitors turned on the loyalists in their own ranks. Loken and Torgaddon faced Abaddon and Aximand before Horus's fleet began its final orbital bombardment. Both were believed to have died, though Loken survived and served the Emperor as one of the Space Marine agents of Malcador the Sigilite known as the Knights-Errant. Torgaddon was killed by Aximand.[1]

    Abaddon and Aximand chose replacements before the attack on Dwell: Sergeant Grael Noctua of the 25th Warlocked Tactical Squad and Captain Falkus "Widowmaker" Kibre of the Justaerin Terminators.[1]
