    Valhallan Ice Warriors
    Imperial Guard regiments of Valhalla
    HomeworldValhalla
    TypeInfantry regiments
    Known forAttrition warfare on frozen worlds

    The Valhallan Ice Warriors are Imperial Guard regiments raised on the frozen world of Valhalla. They are veterans of cold worlds, and they are resilient in extreme cold.[1]

    The Ice Warriors fight by attrition on frozen worlds. Their doctrine combines massed artillery bombardment with infantry assault waves, and it teaches that a regiment must be prepared to hold its ground until it is destroyed.
