    A Librarian is a psyker of the Adeptus Astartes: a Space Marine whose psychic gift has been trained and turned to war. Librarians serve in the Librarius of their Chapter, which keeps its records and lore as well as its psychic strength. The Codex grades of the Librarius are, from lowest to highest, Lexicanium, Codicier, Epistolary and Librarian, and the order is led by the Chief Librarian.[1][2]

    The Librarius was first raised in the Space Marine Legions during the Great Crusade. It was disbanded by the Emperor of Mankind at the Council of Nikaea in 001.M31, after a dispute over the use of sorcery, and restored after the Horus Heresy in the Codex Astartes, when the Imperium recognised that it could not face the powers of the Warp without psykers of its own. Every Chapter save those, like the Black Templars, that refuse psykers on principle now keeps a Librarius.[3][1]

    Librarians fight with psychic powers and force weapons that channel the psyker's will, and are protected by a psychic hood that shields them from enemy witches and from the Warp. They also serve their Chapters as astropaths, seers and judges of the psychic purity of recruits.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    The Legion Librarii

    [edit]

    Many aspirants of the Legions developed psychic gifts, either latent in the gene-seed of their primarch or from their own blood. The Thousand Sons under Magnus the Red were the first to train them systematically, and their example spread. The psykers were called Librarians because they soon became the keepers of their Legion's records and history. By the end of the 30th millennium most Legions kept a Librarius, among them the Ultramarines, the Blood Angels and the Dark Angels, each using it according to its primarch's temper. Leman Russ of the Space Wolves disdained them and kept instead his Rune Priests, who claimed their powers came from Fenris and not from the Warp.[4][3]

    The Council of Nikaea

    [edit]
    Main article: Council of Nikaea

    The growing use of psychic powers by the Thousand Sons, whom many accused of sorcery, led the Emperor to call the Council of Nikaea in 001.M31. He ruled against Magnus. The Council forbade the study of sorcery, disbanded every Librarius, and ordered its members to return to the ranks and never use their powers again. Most obeyed; some Librarians, such as those of the Thousand Sons, continued their studies in secret. Ahzek Ahriman, Chief Librarian of the Thousand Sons, was among them.[3]

    The Horus Heresy and restoration

    [edit]
    See also: Horus Heresy

    In the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) the traitor Legions used sorcery freely, and the loyalist commanders found themselves without defence against it. Several loyal Legions, notably the Ultramarines at Calth in 006.M31 and in the Imperium Secundus, restored their Librarians in defiance of the edict. Malcador the Sigillite also gathered Librarians for his own purposes, among them the warriors who became the first Grey Knights.[5]

    Guilliman's Codex Astartes of c. 019.M31 restored the Librarius as a permanent order, with its grades and duties, and placed its members under the testing of the Chapter and the oversight of the Inquisition. The edict of Nikaea has never formally been repealed, and Imperial jurists still debate whether the Codex overrode it or merely ignored it.[1]

    The Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    The first Primaris Space Marines of the Ultima Founding in 999.M41 included no psykers. Belisarius Cawl later produced Primaris Librarians, and in the first years of the 42nd millennium they joined the Chapters' Librarii. The opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41 made the Librarians' work more dangerous and more necessary: in the Imperium Nihilus they are often the only means of communication between Chapter forces.[6][7]

    Grades

    [edit]

    The Codex Astartes divides the Librarius into four grades. A Librarian advances by the growth of his power and the approval of his seniors:

    GradeRole
    LexicaniumThe novice grade. A Lexicanium learns to master his powers and serves as an assistant in the archives and on the battlefield.
    CodicierA trained warrior-psyker who has proved his control. Codiciers often serve as the Chapter's astropaths.
    EpistolaryA Librarian of great power and experience, attached to a company or a detachment, able to use more than one power at a time.
    Chief LibrarianThe master of the Librarius and a member of the Chapter Command, beside the Master of Sanctity, the Chief Apothecary and the Master of the Forge.

    Powers and wargear

    [edit]

    A Librarian's powers depend on his gifts and his Chapter's teaching. The Librarius classifies them by discipline. Common disciplines include divination, the reading of the future; telepathy, the reading and control of minds; telekinesis; pyromancy; and the manipulation of the Warp's energy in bolts and shields. Some Chapters keep disciplines of their own, drawn from the powers of their primarch's gene-seed: the Blood Angels' Sanguinary discipline and the Space Wolves' tempest magic among them.[2]

    The psychic hood is a crystalline matrix worn about the head, which focuses the Librarian's power and lets him block the powers of hostile psykers. The force weapon, a sword, staff, axe or mace with a psychically conductive core, kills by channelling the Librarian's will into the foe. Codex Librarians wear blue armour, the colour of the Librarius, with their Chapter's insignia on one shoulder; many Chapters keep their own colours.[1]

    Chapter variants

    [edit]
    • The Space Wolves keep Rune Priests, whose masters claim to draw on the spirits of Fenris. The greatest of them in the 41st and 42nd millennia is Njal Stormcaller.
    • The White Scars call their Librarians Stormseers, after the shamans of Chogoris.
    • The Dark Angels are led in psychic matters by Ezekiel, Grand Master of Librarians, keeper of the Book of Salvation.
    • The Black Templars have no Librarius, because they hold psykers to be unclean.
    • The Grey Knights are a Chapter in which every warrior is a psyker.
