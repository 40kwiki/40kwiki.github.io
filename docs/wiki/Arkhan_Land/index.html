    TitlesMagos; Machine-Archaeologist; Technoarchaeologist
    Allegiance Adeptus Mechanicus (then the Mechanicum)
    BornUnknown; probably on Mars
    ActiveLate M30 to M31, in the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy
    DiedLost on a second expedition into the Librarius Omnis, M31 (estimated)
    CompanionA psyber-monkey
    Known forRecovering the STC patterns of the Land Raider, the Land Speeder and the Land Crawler

    Arkhan Land was a magos and technoarchaeologist of the Mechanicum of Mars in the Great Crusade, remembered as the man who led the greatest expedition into the ruins of the Librarius Omnis. From it he brought back the Standard Template Construct patterns of the Land Raider, the anti-gravitic plating that became the Land Speeder, and the Land Crawler, a farm and haulage vehicle used on countless agri-worlds. All three carry his name. He did not live to see the last of them in service, and he vanished on a second expedition into the archive.[1]

    Life

    [edit]

    Little is known of his early life. His birthplace is not recorded, though he is thought to have been raised on Mars. Surviving accounts say more of his habits than of his learning. A marble etching on Mars shows him walking the sky-vaults of the Sierra Planum with his psyber-monkey, and Geronimus's Citations mentions his fondness for extravagant wigs, worn to hide a baldness that came early.[1] He had a name for perception beyond that of his peers, and the Mechanicum gave him the charge of the archive's search.

    The Librarius Omnis

    [edit]

    The Librarius Omnis is a continent-spanning archive beneath the surface of Mars, long abandoned, whose catacombs the Mechanicum had never fully searched. In the first decades of the Great Crusade Land led an expedition into it. He spent three standard years in its tunnels looking for working STC databases, and did not find any. He found three other things.[1][a]

    The first was a dataslab imprint of an armoured tank, almost complete: the Land Raider, at first called "Land's Raider". The Proteus pattern built from it served the Legions as a main battle tank, but its storage and rear hatch limited it, and it was succeeded by the Spartan and the Phobos. The second was data on rare anti-gravitic plates, from which the first "Land's Speeder" was built. The third, a farm vehicle, became the Land Crawler, which the Imperium's agri-worlds use by the billion. Some historians hold that this humble machine is his greatest gift, since even Space Marines need to eat.[1]

    A fourth machine is credited to him in some Mechanicus accounts: the Mars Universal Land Engine, or M.U.L.E., a walker he designed to escort tech-priests across the Martian wastes. The Mechanicus later armed and shielded it and made it the basis of the Onager Dunecrawler.[1]

    Service to the Emperor

    [edit]

    When the Emperor recovered the primarch Angron on Nuceria in 899.M30, he called on Land to help in examining the cortical implants called the Butcher's Nails that the gladiator-lords of that world had put in his mind. Records of the interview say little of the Nails, but they record that the Emperor told Land that the primarchs were tools and weapons to him and that he felt no kinship for them. He explained that he allowed the title of father only because every creation names its maker so.[1][2]

    In the Heresy Land was kept busy. He is credited with the gravitic suspensors in the Adeptus Custodes's Caladius grav-tanks and with the Paragon-pattern jetbikes, which he thought rather beautiful.[1] His own transport, Land's Raider, a grav-raider larger than a Spartan, was armoured in Martian alloys too rare for the Legions and carried a quad turret of volkite culverins.[1]

    Disappearance

    [edit]

    Land led a second expedition into the Librarius Omnis and did not return. Two Terran centuries later a rescue team recovered his vox diary, which seemed to show that he and his party were picked off one by one by something. Opinion is divided on what it was: a living beast, a psychic entity, or a sentient virus. The Mechanicus treats the matter as proof that some secrets of the Dark Age of Technology are better left in the dark.[1][b]

    The Landists

    [edit]

    Land's followers formed their own cult within the Adeptus Mechanicus, the Landists, who study the many possible variants of his STC finds. Their research has produced several patterns, among them the Land Raider Prometheus. The inner circle holds that a tank between the Predator and the Land Raider in weight existed in the Great Crusade and can be rebuilt from the compatibilities in Land's imprints. Other magi reject this. In the 39th millennium several Landists were tried as hereteks and sentenced to arco-flagellation, and the sect has worked in secret since. No record names a rival of Land's own day, and the hostility is chiefly between his disciples and the older Martian priesthood.[1]
