    Yasan Sector
    Sector of the Imperium of Mankind
    SegmentumUltima Segmentum
    Notable worldChogoris, the White Scars homeworld
    NearThe Maelstrom, a Warp rift

    The Yasan Sector is a sector of the Imperium of Mankind in the Ultima Segmentum, near the Warp rift known as the Maelstrom. It holds Chogoris, the Chapter homeworld of the White Scars.[1]

    Horus Heresy

    [edit]

    During the Horus Heresy in the early 31st millennium, the sector was ravaged by the Traitor forces of Horus. Later, Drukhari slave raiders attacked it.[1]

    The Great Scouring

    [edit]

    In the campaigns of the Great Scouring, the White Scars returned to their home sector. They cleared out the remaining Traitors and drove the Drukhari back into the Webway. In one battle, on the world of Corusil V in 084.M31, the White Scars' primarch, Jaghatai Khan, disappeared while pursuing Drukhari raiders into a Webway portal.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    After the Great Rift opened, the sector came under constant attack from the Heretic Astartes of the Red Corsairs. During the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41, the Red Corsairs invaded out of the Maelstrom and almost completely took the Gartuli System and the Thaxis System. The White Scars threw back the assault on Chogoris, but at a steep cost, and Chaos outposts in the sector keep the threat alive.[1]
