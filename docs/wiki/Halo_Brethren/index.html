    The Halo Brethren are a loyal Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes who follow the Codex Astartes. Their genetic lineage is unknown. They were raised in the Fourth Founding after the War of the Beast, in or soon after 546.M32, and their one recorded act is the part they played in the fall of the Lord Protector Drakan Vangorich.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The Fourth Founding

    [edit]

    The War of the Beast, 544–546.M32, left the Imperium shattered a thousand years after the Horus Heresy. The lord commander Maximus Thane, formerly Master of the Fists Exemplar and now Master of the restored Imperial Fists, told the High Lords of Terra that a Fourth Founding must be raised, as many Chapters as the gene-seed of Terra could sustain. Every Chapter released veterans to start the new ones, and the gene-seed tithe was doubled for a century. When it was done, hundreds of Chapters ringed the Imperium instead of dozens.[1]

    Thane also ordered the Imperial Fists to leave Terra and become a crusading Chapter, speeding the recovery of the stricken worlds. He left Drakan Vangorich as Lord Protector, his personal voice. Soon after, Vangorich had the Officio Assassinorum murder eleven of the twelve High Lords in the Beheading and installed puppets in the Senatorum Imperialis. For some eighty years he ruled with an iron hand while the Founding went on and the Halo Brethren were made.[1]

    Retribution on Terra

    [edit]

    By the time Thane received the reports of Vangorich's purges and massacres, the Lord Protector was unstable. Thane returned to Terra c. 626.M32 with the Imperial Fists.[a] A strike force of four hundred went to the surface: a company of the Halo Brethren, a company of the Sable Swords, and two hundred Imperial Fists from the First, Fourth and Fifth Companies. The Sable Swords' Master, Qublicus Amar, was shot dead soon after landing, and the force pressed on. The Assassinorum fell on it in hundreds and it lost half its strength securing the palace. An aged Inquisitorial representative named Wienand convinced Thane of her loyalty and told him that Vangorich had taken refuge in the Eversor temple at the north pole. A hundred Eversor assassins met the force there. Only Thane survived to reach the Grand Master and shoot him.[1]

    Imperial records say nothing more about the Halo Brethren.

    Legacy of the Fourth Founding

    [edit]

    The Fourth Founding was the greatest raising of Chapters since the First, and gave the Imperium the numbers it needed to recover after the War of the Beast. Thane's decree that the Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra but go crusading was the other half of the policy, and it left the Throneworld in the Lord Protector's hands for eighty years. Vangorich's fall ended his purges, but Imperial scholars record that a period of anarchy followed, and nothing in the record shows what part the Halo Brethren played in it.[1]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Chapter is described only as Codex-compliant. Its colours, badge, Master, homeworld and fortress are not recorded.[1]

    Notable campaigns

    [edit]
    • Retribution on Terra (c. 626.M32): a company of the Chapter joined the strike that removed Vangorich from power.

    Notable members

    [edit]

    None of the Chapter's members is named in Imperial records.
