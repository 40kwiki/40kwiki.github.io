    The Red Scorpions are a fleet-based Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes, of unknown Founding and descent. They are known for the purity of their gene-seed, which shows almost no deterioration or corruption across millennia, and for a rigidly purist creed that holds the Emperor divine and distrusts anyone, Astartes or human, whose genetic line is less pure. They led the Loyalist Space Marines in the Badab War of 906–911.M41 under their Lord High Commander, Carab Culln.[1][2]

    In the late 39th millennium the Chapter crusaded into the Ordon Rift in the south-west of the Segmentum Tempestus, and it has since kept its base on the battle station Vigil above the feral world of Zaebus Minoris. In the Era Indomitus Culln was believed slain on Sarum and later returned, entombed in a Leviathan Dreadnought; the Chapter is governed in practice by two regents.[3]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    No Imperial source records the Red Scorpions' Founding or their progenitor. Their gene-seed bears no hallmark that would trace it to one of the First Founding Legions, a fact that has left some in the Adeptus Terra suspicious of them despite a long and spotless record. Between 498 and 601.M34, in the Berillia Massacres, the renegade Shining Blades, once famous with the Red Scorpions and the Ultramarines for the purity of their gene-seed, captured the Chapter's strike cruiser Crux Puritatis. Chapter Master Thay Kraun recalled all of his warriors, hunted the renegades down, and beheaded their captain in single combat with the Blade of the Scorpion.[1]

    Crusades

    [edit]

    In 857.M38 the Chapter purged the traitors of Helios, and its Master of the Forge devised a solution to the campaign that did not depend on the Astra Militarum, whom the Chapter Master distrusted. In 633.M39 it fought in the Second Aegisine Crusade to retake the Mandragora Sub-sector from beneath its Warp storms. In the late 39th millennium the whole Chapter entered the Ordon Rift. What it did there is unknown, but when it reappeared, at the Battle of the Grey Sisters against the Night Lords on Oriflame, it had built the station Vigil above Zaebus Minoris. In 236.M40 it freed the hive world of Endikon IX from Aeldari corsairs.[1][a]

    The Badab War

    [edit]
    Main article: Badab War

    The Red Scorpions arrived in the Maelstrom in 906.M41 and were given command of the Loyalist Space Marine forces with the blessing of the Imperial legates, despite misgivings about the Chapter's independence. The war turned when Lord High Commander Verant Ortys and his honour guard were killed in mysterious and probably treacherous circumstances while parleying with Lufgt Huron of the Astral Claws. Carab Culln, commander of the First Company and the Chapter's heir apparent, took command at once and led the Loyalists for the rest of the war. After the fall of Badab the High Lords of Terra awarded the Chapter a Vexilla Imperialis, one of the rarest honours in the Imperium.[2]

    Last years of M41

    [edit]

    In 992.M41 the Red Scorpions fought Hive Fleet Kraken in the Second Tyrannic War, and in 998.M41 a single company arrived on Armageddon at the end of the first year of the Third War and waited out the Season of Fire before attacking the Orks of Ghazghkull Thraka.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    In the campaigns that followed the Indomitus Crusade, Carab Culln was believed killed on Sarum. Commanders Casan Sabius and Sirae Karagon, styled the Regents of the Ordon Rift, were chosen to lead the Chapter. Culln was later recovered, his broken body entombed in a Relic Leviathan Dreadnought, and he now fights as Carab Culln the Risen; the regents have kept their authority despite his return. A former captain, Oneius Prayd, renounced his vows after the Chapter refused to choose him as its master and became a renegade.[3][b]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Red Scorpions follow the Codex Astartes closely, but their captains are called commanders and their Chapter Master the Lord High Commander, whose second-in-command is by tradition the Master of the Apothecarion. The Apothecarion tests every battle-brother and every recruit relentlessly, and any found tainted are cast out. The Chapter believes that its pure gene-seed makes it one of the truest links to the Emperor, and it treats others, even other Chapters, with a disdain that has strained its alliances.[1]
