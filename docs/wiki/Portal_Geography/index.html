    From 40,000 Wiki, the Imperial encyclopedia

    The Imperium of Mankind spans the Milky Way, a galaxy of billions of stars divided into segmenta, sectors and subsectors. Its geography is charted by Imperial navigators and the Adeptus Mechanicus, and it changes with every war and crusade. This portal gathers the articles on its regions, its worlds and its great divisions.

    Selected article

    [edit]

    Milky Way is the galaxy in which the Imperium of Mankind lies, as charted by Imperial science. Its stars are grouped into the great divisions of the Imperium, from the Segmentum Solar around Terra to the frontier of the Eastern Fringe. Much of it remains uncharted, and the Great Rift has cut many regions off from the rest.
