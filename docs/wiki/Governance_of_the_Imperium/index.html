    Governance of the Imperium is the chain of rule by which the Imperium of Mankind is administered in the name of the Emperor of Mankind. Authority runs from the High Lords of Terra, through the segmenta and sectors, to the planetary governors who rule each world. The chain is the same in every region of Imperial space, although each level has different powers in different places.[1]

    The system is not a single hierarchy. It combines civil, naval, military, ecclesiastical and Inquisitorial lines of authority, and most of them meet at Terra. The Administratum keeps the records and collects the tithe. The Navy and the Astra Militarum defend the space that the Administratum governs. The Ecclesiarchy and the Inquisition act as checks on the civil line, and the Adeptus Arbites enforce the law at every level.[2]

    The chain of rule

    The table below sets out each level of the chain, from the top down. Each office is named with the division it governs. Where a level has more than one head, both are given.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The Great Crusade

    [edit]
    Further information: Great Crusade

    During the Great Crusade (798.M30 – 000.M31) the Imperium had no fixed chain of rule. Conquered worlds were governed by the expedition fleets that took them, and the Council of Terra met on Terra to direct the crusade from the Imperial Palace.[3]

    The reforms

    [edit]

    The Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) ended the Council of Terra's authority. The reforms of Roboute Guilliman, in roughly 015–021.M31, fixed the five segmenta, the sectors and sub-sectors, and the offices that govern each. Civil and military authority were separated, so that no governor commanded both a fleet and an army.[3][a]

    The High Lords were formally established before the middle of M32, as recorded on their own page. Their number and their seats have changed since, and some seats have stood empty for long periods.[1]

    The Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    In the Era Indomitus the chain of rule has been broken in many places. The Great Rift cut off whole segmenta from Terra, and the Indomitus Crusade has restored some levels in regions that were lost. Many sectors now answer to a governor who has never been seen by the Administratum, and the Imperial registers record them as held in trust.[1]

    Principles

    [edit]

    Tithe and returns

    [edit]

    Every world pays the Imperial tithe to its sub-sector, which passes it to the sector and the segmentum and on to Terra. Each level keeps part of the return for its own use. The Administratum records every payment, and the chain is judged by the completeness of its returns.[2]

    Oversight

    [edit]

    The Adeptus Arbites and the Inquisition hold powers that cut across the chain of rule. The Arbites answer to their own command on Terra, and the Inquisition may act in any level without the consent of the local governor. A governor who resists these powers is liable to be removed.[1]

    Religious authority

    [edit]

    The Ecclesiarchy has its own hierarchy, with a cardinal for each sector or group of sectors. Cardinals are not subordinate to the civil governors, although they often advise them and in some sectors hold the office of governor themselves.[1]
