    Kairos Fateweaver, called the Oracle of Tzeentch, is a two-headed Lord of Change, the mightiest of the Greater Daemons of Tzeentch. Once Tzeentch's vizier, he was thrown by his god into the Well of Eternity to learn the future, and came out of it changed. One of his heads sees all of the past and the other all the futures, but he is blind to the present. When asked a question, one head always tells the truth and the other a lie, and the two are equally convincing.[1]

    Tzeentch rarely sends his Oracle into realspace. Imperial records place him with Lorgar in the Eye of Terror before the Horus Heresy, aboard the Lion's flagship during the Heresy, and with the Thousand Sons in its aftermath. In 999.M41 he captured Roboute Guilliman in the Maelstrom during the Terran Crusade. In the Era Indomitus he has fought in the invasion of the Stygius Sector and was banished in the battle for the craftworld Ulthwé.[1][2]

    The Well of Eternity

    [edit]

    According to the daemon lore of the Malleus, Tzeentch knows all that has been and all that is, but the future is broken into infinite threads that even he cannot hold. To master it he went to the Well of Eternity, where space and time begin and end, but would not enter it himself. He sent his greatest Lords of Change into it, and none returned. At last he seized his vizier, Kairos, and threw him in.[1]

    Kairos survived, barely. His wings withered, his body was wasted, and his head split in two. Both heads came back mad with what they had seen. Kairos now sits at Tzeentch's right hand, muttering prophecies, and nine times nine Lords of Change record every word. Those who perform great services for Tzeentch are sometimes allowed to question him, though the answers of his two heads are as dangerous as they are true.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The counsel to Lorgar

    [edit]

    In the last century of the 30th millennium, some four decades before the Horus Heresy, Lorgar of the Word Bearers travelled into the Eye of Terror in search of the gods. Kairos appeared to him there. He offered Lorgar a choice: to take revenge on Roboute Guilliman for the humiliation of the Word Bearers at Monarchia, which would ruin Chaos for ten thousand years, or to set his grievance aside and serve the larger cause of Chaos against the Emperor of Mankind. The Malleus records this as the only known occasion on which both heads of Kairos told the same truth.[1][3][b]

    Horus Heresy

    [edit]

    During the Heresy (005–014.M31), the Dark Angels flagship Invincible Reason was thrown into the Warp without its Gellar field after a battle with a Night Lords cruiser, and daemons of Tzeentch boarded it. Kairos was among them. In a psychic exchange with Lion El'Jonson he let slip that the Emperor was still alive. The Lion stabbed one of his heads with his sword Despair and banished him, asking as he faded whether he had seen that coming.[3][c]

    After the Burning of Prospero in c. 006.M31, Kairos was found bound inside a device called the Iron Oculus. Ahzek Ahriman recovered it for Magnus the Red from the mechanical race called the Yokai, and the daemon was placed in a Yokai body named Aforgomon, which guided Ahriman's cabal in their search for the shards of Magnus's soul. On Nikaea Kairos took his true form through the sorcerer Hathor Maat, gave Ahriman the power to face a shard of Magnus, and then carried the Thousand Sons back to the Planet of the Sorcerers.[3][d]

    The Terran Crusade

    [edit]

    The return of Roboute Guilliman on Macragge in 999.M41 sent a wave through the Warp that the servants of Tzeentch could not miss. When Magnus the Red drew Guilliman's fleet into the Maelstrom during the Terran Crusade, Kairos began to torment the primarch in his mind, feeding his guilt over the fate of the Emperor. When the fleet found a way out through a graveyard of starships, Kairos sprang a trap there with the Red Corsairs, whose ships had been disguised as wrecks. The Corsairs crippled the fleet and boarded its ships.[2]

    On the bridge of Macragge's Honour, Guilliman, Captain Cato Sicarius, Saint Celestine and Inquisitor Katarinya Greyfax fought the boarders. Kairos came last. Nine Heralds of Tzeentch, hidden by illusions, drew out the guilt and anger he had planted in the primarch's mind and bound him with them. Kairos ordered the loyalists to surrender or see their primarch killed, and they did. Guilliman and his warriors were taken to a Blackstone Fortress held by the Red Corsairs.[2]

    Kairos meant to keep Guilliman alive and hidden until certain futures had come. He did not foresee the attack of Skarbrand, whose Khornate host fell on the fortress to take the primarch's skull. In the battle that followed, Guilliman was freed by the Fallen Angel Cypher and the Harlequins of Sylandri Veilwalker. Kairos did not face the primarch again, but fought from a distance through his servants, and Guilliman escaped through a Webway gate after defeating Skarbrand.[2]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    Since the opening of the Great Rift, Kairos has been among the servants of Tzeentch who entered realspace to seize the Stygius Sector as a domain for their god, an invasion said to have been driven by Tzeentch's jealousy of the realm Nurgle had made in the Scourge Stars during the Plague Wars. Later in the early Era Indomitus, while every warhost of Craftworld Ulthwé was away, daemons broke into the craftworld's Dome of Crystal Portents. Kairos and six Keepers of Secrets led them. The exiled farseer Eldrad Ulthran, with warriors of the Ynnari and the Harlequins, drove them back and banished them.[2][e]

    Powers and wargear

    [edit]

    Kairos is a psyker of great power who can turn whole battlefields into landscapes of Warp fire. Because he knows where every shot will fall, his bodyguard of daemons can step out of harm's way before an enemy fires. If he is wounded by something he did not foresee, he often flees to the Warp, fearing that Tzeentch has hidden the future from him. His staff, the Staff of Tomorrow, can turn even a great hero into a Chaos Spawn with a single blow.[1]
