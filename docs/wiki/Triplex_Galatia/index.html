    Triplex Galatia is a Dead world in the Triplex System of the Triplex Sector, Ultima Segmentum. It was once a major Forge world of the ancient Mechanicum and the home of the elder portion of the Legio Victorum, the "Foe Slayers". In the Horus Heresy its Tech-priests sided with the Traitors, and the Dark Angels reduced it to a lifeless waste during the Thramas Crusade.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    Galatia was the first of the three Forge Worlds of the Triplex System to be founded. Its birth belongs to the Age of Strife, when Explorator fleets of the Mechanicum travelled blind among the stars in search of new land. For centuries it ruled its region of space, secured by the strength of the Legio Victorum I and the output of its tech-priests.[1]

    The arrival of the Emperor of Mankind in the Great Crusade checked its rise. Mars moved to curb a rival and asked the Emperor to take only the Legio Victorum II to the war, leaving the other two portions of the Legio to guard the Triplex System. Galatia was therefore cut off from the spoils of the Crusade, which went to its sister world Triplex Phall. Its tech-adepts turned inward and began to explore paths forbidden by Mars and by Terra.[1]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]

    When agents of the Warmaster Horus and of Triplex Phall approached the tech-priests of Galatia, they found a willing ally. In return for the Warmaster's rewards Galatia stockpiled weapons built to new and dangerous specifications, and sent the more conventional output to the Imperium to maintain the appearance of loyalty.[1]

    Imperial historians suggest that Galatia took no direct part in the destruction of Triplex Thule, and that its warriors and Titans stayed out of the fighting. It is nonetheless guilty of standing aside while the Legio Victorum III and the defenders of Thule died, and of stripping the ruined world afterwards. It then unleashed its Heretek hosts to confirm its pact with the Traitors. Material from Thule fed the building of new armies and warships meant for the conquest of the Eastern Fringe.[1]

    The arrival of Lion El'Jonson and the Dark Angels ended that plan. The Legion's Dreadwing destroyed the forge utterly in the course of the Thramas Crusade, which is dated to 007–009.M31.[1]

    Aftermath

    [edit]

    By the end of the Heresy Galatia was still largely unvisited. Few were willing to risk the censure of the First Legion by disturbing the world, despite the technology said to lie in its ruins. The shattered surface remains a warning to traitors and a reminder to Triplex Phall of the Legion's wrath.[1]
