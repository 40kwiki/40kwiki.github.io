    Tsagualsa
    World of the Horus Heresy
    Notable eventThe Tsagualsa incident (009.M31)
    ParticipantsLion El'Jonson, Konrad Curze
    Later eventSite of Curze's death during the Great Scouring

    Tsagualsa is a world that is recorded chiefly for the meeting of Lion El'Jonson and Konrad Curze in 009.M31. The encounter, known as the Tsagualsa incident, was the moment when the two primarchs came to blows. It came during the Dark Angels' campaign to delay the Night Lords, which lasted almost three years, from 007 to 009.M31.[1]

    Imperial records give little detail about the world itself. Its importance comes from the primarchs' fight there.

    The Tsagualsa incident

    [edit]

    In 009.M31 Curze lured the Lion to a parley on Tsagualsa, and the two primarchs came to blows. The Lion was nearly strangled before a guardsman of the Dark Angels drove a sword through the Night Haunter's back. Imperial accounts record the fight as one of the most violent meetings between two primarchs of the Heresy.[1]

    Great Scouring

    [edit]
    See also: Great Scouring

    The Great Scouring was the Imperial counter-offensive that followed the death of Horus at the Siege of Terra. Its records list Curze's execution on Tsagualsa among its events. The date of that execution is not recorded.[a]
