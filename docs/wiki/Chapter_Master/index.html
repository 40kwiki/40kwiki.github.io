    A Chapter Master is the commander of a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. Under the Codex Astartes he is the highest officer of his Chapter and answers to no one within it. He commands its ten companies, its specialist orders, its fleet and its fortress-monastery, and very often rules the Chapter's homeworld or a realm of several worlds as their Imperial Commander.[1][2]

    The rank existed inside the Space Marine Legions of the Great Crusade, where a Chapter Master commanded one of the many chapters of a Legion. After the Horus Heresy the Second Founding of c. 021.M31 broke the Legions into Chapters of about a thousand warriors, and the Chapter Master became the senior rank of the Astartes, beneath only the primarchs who still lived.[1]

    Many Chapters use their own title for the office. The masters of the Dark Angels and the Grey Knights are styled Supreme Grand Master, the master of the Space Wolves the Great Wolf, and the master of the Black Templars the High Marshal. In the Era Indomitus several Chapter Masters hold commands far beyond their Chapters: Commander Dante of the Blood Angels is Lord Regent of the Imperium Nihilus, and Marneus Calgar of the Ultramarines commands Ultramar in the name of Roboute Guilliman.[3]

    History

    [edit]

    In the Legions

    [edit]
    See also: Space Marine Legions

    Each of the twenty Space Marine Legions was organised by its primarch in its own fashion. Most divided their tens of thousands of warriors into chapters, battalions or great companies of between one thousand and several thousand legionaries, and the officer who led such a body was in several Legions called its Chapter Master. A Legion's Chapter Masters ranked below the Legion's lord commanders and praetors and served as the generals of the Great Crusade's expeditionary fleets. Their importance varied greatly. In the Ultramarines, where Roboute Guilliman kept hundreds of chapters, the rank was a regimental command; in Legions of more personal rule it might not exist at all.[4]

    During the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) the Chapter Masters of the traitor Legions were among the first officers to learn of Horus's intent, and several of them led the attacks on loyalist forces at Isstvan III and Calth in 005–006.M31.[5]

    The Codex Astartes

    [edit]
    Main article: Codex Astartes

    Guilliman's Codex Astartes, completed in c. 019.M31, fixed the Chapter at ten companies of one hundred battle-brothers, with a Chapter Master at its head and a small headquarters staff, the Chapter Command, around him. The Codex makes the Chapter Master responsible for every part of his Chapter: its recruitment and training, its wars, its relations with the Adeptus Terra and other Imperial authorities, and the guarding of its gene-seed. He reports to no superior officer. The Codex expected that Chapters of the same founding would assist one another, but did not set any Chapter Master above another.[1]

    With the Second Founding in c. 021.M31, most of the loyalist Legions' surviving Chapter Masters became the first masters of the new Chapters. The Legion name and its fortress usually passed to the Chapter led by the former Legion's senior officer; the others took new names and new worlds.[1]

    Later millennia

    [edit]

    Because Chapter Masters are subject to no single Imperial office, their independence has repeatedly worried the High Lords of Terra. The Inquisition watches them closely. The worst case of a Chapter Master's rebellion was that of Lufgt Huron of the Astral Claws, the self-proclaimed Tyrant of Badab, whose secession caused the Badab War of 901–913.M41 and was put down by an alliance of loyal Chapters.[6]

    In 999.M41 the revived Roboute Guilliman, as Lord Commander of the Imperium, ordered that all Chapters receive Primaris Space Marines in the Ultima Founding. Chapter Masters who had served for centuries were invited to cross the Rubicon Primaris, the surgery that makes a veteran a Primaris Marine. Marneus Calgar of the Ultramarines did so in the early 42nd millennium, and many other Chapter Masters have since followed him. The Ultima Founding also restored the rank of Lieutenant, a deputy to each captain, which shortened the chain between the Chapter Master and his warriors.[3][7]

    Duties and powers

    [edit]

    A Chapter Master is first a general. He chooses his Chapter's wars, often in answer to petitions from the Astra Militarum, the Imperial Navy, the Inquisition or planetary rulers, and he leads the Chapter in its greatest battles. Below him the Chapter Command includes the masters of the Chapter's specialist orders: the Chief Librarian, the Master of Sanctity, the Chief Apothecary and the Master of the Forge. The Chapter's Ancient carries its banner, and its Champion fights in his name.[1]

    Many Chapter Masters are also civil rulers. Where a Chapter holds a homeworld, its master is usually recognised by the Administratum as that world's Imperial Commander and pays its tithes. Some rule whole realms: the Chapter Master of the Ultramarines is also Lord Macragge and master of the five hundred worlds of Ultramar, and the Great Wolf of the Space Wolves is High King of Fenris.[2]

    The Chapter Master may command a Chapter fleet of battle-barges and strike cruisers, may grant honours and expel warriors, and in some Chapters names his own successor. Others choose by vote of the captains, by ordeal or by the Chapter's priests. Most masters serve until their death.[1]

    Insignia and wargear

    [edit]

    The Codex does not prescribe a single badge of rank, and Chapters vary. The most common marks are:

    • the Iron Halo, a field generator worn above the backpack in the shape of a crown or halo, awarded for great service and carried by most Chapter Masters;
    • the laurels of victory, a wreath worn on the helm, pauldron or crest, often in gold;
    • artificer armour or relic Terminator armour, often of great age and handed down from master to master;
    • a relic blade, power fist or ancient bolter of the Chapter's armoury, together with the Chapter's standard and honour badges.

    Since the Ultima Founding some Chapter Masters wear Gravis armour in place of Terminator plate.[7]

    Variant titles

    [edit]

    Supreme Grand Master

    [edit]

    The title Supreme Grand Master is borne by two Chapter Masters. In the Dark Angels it marks the lord of the Chapter and of the Unforgiven, master of the fortress-monastery called the Rock, and Keeper of the Truth: the only member of the Chapter who knows every secret of the Fallen. The Dark Angels name the masters of their 1st and 2nd companies Grand Masters of the Deathwing and Ravenwing, and the Supreme Grand Master stands above them. The current holder is Azrael, who succeeded Naberius in 939.M41.[8]

    In the Grey Knights, the daemon-hunting Chapter of the Ordo Malleus, the Supreme Grand Master is first among the eight Grand Masters of the Chapter's brotherhoods and leads the Chapter from the fortress of Titan. Kaldor Draigo has held the title since 901.M41, when his predecessor was killed by Mortarion at the Battle of Kornovin; Draigo himself has since been cast into the Warp and wanders the Realm of Chaos, while the Grand Masters on Titan govern in his absence.[9]

    Other titles

    [edit]

    The master of the Space Wolves is the Great Wolf, chosen by the Wolf Lords. The master of the Black Templars, a crusading Chapter that keeps no Codex structure, is the High Marshal. The master of the Blood Angels is called Commander, and the master of the Red Scorpions Lord High Commander. The Iron Hands do not have one master at all, but a council of clan leaders called the Iron Council.[2]
