    Olympia was a mountainous world of the Segmentum Ultima, divided among hundreds of warring city-states, and the homeworld of the primarch Perturabo and the IV Legion, the Iron Warriors. Its culture prized fortification and siegecraft above every other art. Perturabo grew up at the court of Dammekos, Tyrant of the city-state of Lochos, and was found there by the Emperor in 849.M30.[1][2]

    In 004.M31, after Dammekos died, Olympia rose in revolt against the Imperium. Perturabo returned and crushed it with a slaughter that killed some five million people, an event that helped carry him and his Legion into the Horus Heresy. After the Siege of Terra the Iron Warriors held Olympia against the Loyalists for two years before destroying it themselves. It has been listed as Perdita ever since.[3][4]

    Geography and society

    [edit]

    Olympia was settled late in the Dark Age of Technology and survived the Age of Strife largely intact, at a pre-atomic industrial level. Its fissile and conductive metals had been mined out long before, which kept it from advancing further, and its surface was almost entirely mountains. Large cities and broad farmland were impossible, and the world divided into hundreds of city-states and client satrapies that fought endlessly over its valleys and plateaus.[2]

    Power on Olympia meant the ability to hold what one had taken. The city-states became masters of masonry, fortress-building and siege warfare. The twelve strongest rulers were called the Tyrants of Olympia. Their wars were fought by mercenary grenadiers and storm-gunners, armoured creeper-tanks, steam mortars and gas-borne scout airships, and decided as often by bribery and assassination as by battle.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    Perturabo and the Tyrant of Lochos

    [edit]

    Perturabo's capsule reached Olympia in about 792.M30. As a boy he wandered the outlying settlements as a fighter for hire and an artisan of startling skill, until the guards of Dammekos found him climbing the cliffs below Lochos. The Tyrant tested him against grown warriors and against his scholars, and offered him a bargain: service and loyalty, in return for protection and the best training and learning Lochos could give. Perturabo accepted but never returned the affection Dammekos offered. He later said that on reaching the top of that cliff he saw a storm in the heavens that no one else could see, and that it watched him for the rest of his life.[1]

    Perturabo became the general and engineer of Lochos and made it the strongest of the city-states, conquering and fortifying much of Olympia in the Tyrant's name.[2][a]

    The Great Crusade

    [edit]
    Further information: Great Crusade

    The Emperor came to Olympia in 849.M30. Perturabo accepted him quickly, having long reasoned that he was a made being, and Dammekos willingly brought the world into the Imperium as its satrap in return for releasing his foster son. Perturabo took command of the IV Legion, named it the Iron Warriors and ordered a decimation of its ranks for their failures. Olympia became its recruiting world, and generations of its young men were taken from it and never came back.[1][2]

    The rebellion of 004.M31

    [edit]

    When Dammekos died, Olympia's old rivalries burst into open revolt against Imperial rule, fed by resentment of the Legion's tithe. The news reached Perturabo during the costly Sak'trada Deeps campaign against the xenos Hrud. He returned in fury and gave the rebels a choice: execute one in every ten of their own, or face destruction. They refused. The Iron Warriors stormed the city-states one after another, many of them fortresses Perturabo had built, and killed every defender. Iron Warriors who refused to take part were executed by their brothers. About five million Olympians died; the survivors were enslaved.[3]

    Imperial historians regard the massacre as a turning point. Perturabo, standing over the pyres, understood that the Iron Warriors could not be forgiven for it, and soon afterward he took his Legion to the Isstvan System and joined Horus in 005.M31.[3][1]

    The Great Scouring and after

    [edit]

    After the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, the Iron Warriors fell back on a small realm of worlds around Olympia. In the Great Scouring the Imperial Fists supported the Ultramarines in a campaign of about ten years to retake the region. The Olympia garrison held for two years and, when defeat could no longer be avoided, detonated its own munitions stores. The world was left a burned waste and declared Perdita by the Inquisition. Perturabo and the surviving Iron Warriors withdrew into the Eye of Terror and took the Daemon World of Medrengard for themselves.[4][b]

    No resettlement of Olympia is recorded in the ten thousand years since. Iron Warriors Warsmiths are said still to name their fortresses after Olympian keeps, and the Legion's siege doctrine descends directly from the wars of the Tyrants.[5]
