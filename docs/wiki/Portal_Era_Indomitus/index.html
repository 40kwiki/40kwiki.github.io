    From 40,000 Wiki, the Imperial encyclopedia

    The Era Indomitus is the present age of the Imperium, which began when the Great Rift opened in 999.M41. It is an age of war on every front, from the Imperium Nihilus to the eastern fringe. This portal gathers the articles on its wars, its leaders and its changed galaxy.

    Selected article

    [edit]

    Era Indomitus is the present age of the Imperium, from the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41 to the wars of the 42nd millennium. The galaxy was divided by the Rift into the Imperium Sanctus and the Imperium Nihilus. Imperial scholars regard the age as the most dangerous since the Horus Heresy.
