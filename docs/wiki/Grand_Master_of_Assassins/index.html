    The Grand Master of Assassins, formally the Grand Master of the Officio Assassinorum, is the head of the Officio Assassinorum, the Imperium's order of assassins, and one of the High Lords of Terra. The holder directs the temples of the Officio and the deployment of their operatives against the enemies of the Imperium of Mankind, including, when the High Lords so decide by a two-thirds vote, its own governors, commanders and nobles.[1][2]

    The office was created during the Great Crusade by Malcador the Sigillite, who founded the Officio on the Emperor's order and was its first Grand Master. He was succeeded at his death in 014.M31 by Khalid Hassan, the first of his Chosen. Drakan Vangorich, ordered the Beheading of 546.M32, in which the High Lords were murdered in a single day, and ruled the Imperium as Lord Protector for nearly eighty years.[3]

    The incumbent, Fadix, sat on the council that Roboute Guilliman restored in the Indomitus Crusade. When the Hexarchy, a conspiracy of current and deposed High Lords, attempted a coup against the Lord Commander of the Imperium, it was foiled by Fadix.[4]

    Powers and duties

    [edit]

    The Grand Master leads the temples of the Officio, each of them led in the early days by a director primus. Assassins are deployed alone wherever possible, and the authority to kill a target must come from the highest echelons of Imperial command. Because the Officio's operatives have been used against rival High Lords in the past, they are watched closely. After the Wars of Vindication the Officio was limited, so that an assassination could be carried out only after a two-thirds vote of the High Lords of Terra, and the secret Ordo Sicarius of the Inquisition was formed to watch it, with all Assassins subject to inspection and to regular psycho-indoctrination.[1][2]

    The Grand Master's seat on the council has not always been secure. At the start of the War of the Beast Vangorich had formally lost his seat to the Inquisitorial Representative, but he continued to attend nearly every session of the council.[3]

    History

    [edit]

    Malcador and the clades (M30 – 014.M31)

    [edit]
    See also: Malcador the Sigillite

    Malcador, Regent of Terra, created the Officio during the Great Crusade from six clades, each led by a director primus, and was its first Grand Master. He held the office alongside his regency, and in secret. After the Warmaster Horus rebelled in 005.M31, and the atrocity at Isstvan III, Malcador ordered the first Execution Force sent against him: four assassins, one from each of the major clades. It failed, and all but one of the assassins were slain by the Traitors.[5][2]

    Khalid Hassan (014.M31 – unrecorded)

    [edit]

    Khalid Hassan, a former officer of the Imperial Army's Clandestine Orta, had been recruited by Malcador into his Chosen and by the last stage of the Siege of Terra was the first among them. Before Malcador took the Emperor's place on the Golden Throne, he passed his unfinished projects to Hassan and two others. After Malcador's death Hassan became the de facto master of the Chosen of Malcador, master of the Sigillites and the new Grand Master of Assassins. He sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31.[5][6]

    Drakan Vangorich and the Beheading (from 544.M32)

    [edit]

    Drakan Vangorich held the office at the outbreak of the War of the Beast in 544.M32. He had lost his formal seat to the Inquisitorial Representative but attended the council regardless, and he opposed the Proletarian Crusade proposed by the Ecclesiarch Mesring. He later joined Koorland's coup, with Wienand, Veritus and the Grand Provost Marshal Vernor Zeck, which removed the Lord Commander Udin Macht Udo.[3]

    After the war the Lord Commander Maximus Thane named Vangorich Lord Protector of the Imperium and left Terra on crusade. When Vangorich learned that the Fabricator-General Kubik had defied Thane's order to destroy Ullanor, he concluded that the council could not be reformed, and in 546.M32 he had the High Lords killed in one day, sparing only Wienand. He ruled through a council of his appointees for nearly eighty years and carried out the Fourth Founding, but grew paranoid and murderous. Thane returned with a Space Marine force and killed him in the Eversor Temple at Terra's north pole.[3]

    The Age of Apostasy (M36)

    [edit]

    In the Wars of Vindication, shortly after the death of Goge Vandire, a renegade Callidus assassin, Tziz Jarek, tried to kill the Grand Master of the time, and a series of atrocities followed within the Imperial Palace. In response the temples, until then all on Terra, were dispersed across the galaxy, and the Ordo Sicarius was formed to watch them.[2]

    Fadix (Era Indomitus)

    [edit]

    Fadix sat on the council that Guilliman restored during the Indomitus Crusade, after the earlier council was dissolved on his return. When the former Master of the Administratum Irthu Haemotalio formed the Hexarchy against Guilliman, its coup failed, foiled by the actions of Fadix. Several of the High Lords, among them the Lord Commander Militant and the Lord High Admiral, were slain in the attempt.[4]
