    From 40,000 Wiki, the Imperial encyclopedia

    The Horus Heresy was the galaxy-wide civil war of 005–014.M31 that ended the Great Crusade. Led by the Warmaster Horus, a host of Space Marine Legions turned against the Emperor and attacked Terra. This portal gathers the articles on its battles, its traitors and its loyalists.

    Selected article

    [edit]

    Horus Heresy was the civil war that split the Space Marine Legions and the Imperium in the early 31st millennium. It began with the atrocity at Isstvan III and ended at the Siege of Terra. Imperial accounts still dispute the exact causes, but all agree that the war cost the Emperor his greatest servants.
