    Great Wolf
    Chapter Master of the Space Wolves
    Chapter Master of the Space Wolves
    Current holderLogan Grimnar
    Selected byCasting of name-runes into the Dragon's Skull by the Chapter's Wolf Lords
    AllegianceThe Space Wolves and the Emperor of Mankind
    HomeworldFenris

    The Great Wolf is the title of the Chapter Master of the Space Wolves Space Marine Chapter. Although he is one of the most powerful figures in the Imperium of Mankind, the Great Wolf regards himself as a steward of the Chapter and not its lord. The Chapter holds that its primarch, Leman Russ, will return to lead it in its final battle.[1]

    Selection

    [edit]

    The Great Wolf is chosen from among the Chapter's Wolf Lords when his predecessor dies, and he holds the title until his own death. The Chapter votes by casting name-runes into the Dragon's Skull in the heart of The Aett, the Chapter's fortress-monastery. The Wolf Lord with the most runes at midnight is named Great Wolf. A tie is settled by a duel on the feasting table of the Great Hall, and the victor is hailed as the new Great Wolf.[1]

    Role

    [edit]

    The Great Wolf leads the Chapter in war and commands its eleven Great Companies, together with the Company of the Great Wolf. That company holds the Chapter's Wolf Priests, Iron Priests and Rune Priests, and keeps its Dreadnoughts in stasis. The Great Wolf's emblem is the Wolf That Stalks Between the Stars, the badge of Leman Russ.[1]

    The disappearance of Russ

    [edit]

    The tradition of the Great Wolf began after the Horus Heresy. At a feast on Fenris to mark the Emperor's victory over Horus, Leman Russ fell into a vision and left the hall with his closest retainers. He has not been seen since. Imperial accounts record this event, while Space Wolves tradition holds that the primarch remains in a place beyond the sight of the Chapter.[1][a]
