    Equerry
    Adeptus Astartes
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    TypeTitle of an aide and confidant

    Equerry was the title of the aide of a high-ranking Space Marine officer, a custom of the Great Crusade in the late 30th and early 31st millennia. An officer chose a Space Marine to serve as his trusted comrade, aide and confidant. The highest honour was to serve as equerry to a Legion's primarch.[1]

    Officers of the Imperial Army kept a similar custom of choosing a junior officer as an aide-de-camp. By the 41st millennium the practice had faded, though some Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes still assign each battle-brother a personal Chapter serf in a related role.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The tradition of a senior officer taking an aide predates the Imperium and goes back on Terra to the earliest armies. The first Space Marines, the core of the twenty First Founding Legions, carried it into the Great Crusade, launched in 798.M30, and created the title of equerry. Being chosen was a mark of honour and a chance to learn command from a renowned officer.[1]

    When the primarchs were found, most took an equerry of their own, either a trusted companion from their youth on their homeworld or a notable warrior of their Legion. The equerry helped a primarch learn the customs of the Legion while the primarch gave it his own character. More importantly, an equerry could speak against a primarch's decision. Few Astartes would dare show doubt before one of the Emperor's sons, and primarchs saw the use of keeping at hand a genetic kinsman whose caution was worth more than a thousand warriors.[1]

    After the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) the formal title lapsed in the Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes. In the 41st millennium the role is often a second duty of a Chapter or Company Champion, who defends a captain or Chapter Master and fights beside him through many battles. Officers often trust their champion's word as much as his sword. Some Chapters, often those most concerned with civilian casualties, give each battle-brother a serf as a personal equerry, to assist him and to keep for him a link to the Humanity he protects.[1]

    Notable equerries

    [edit]

    In the Great Crusade

    [edit]

    Malcador the Sigillite never held the title, but effectively acted as equerry to the Emperor of Mankind. Amon of the Thousand Sons tutored Magnus the Red in sorcery before the Legion came to Prospero, rose to captain of the 9th Fellowship, founded the Corvidae Cult and served Magnus as equerry, guarding him and officiating at rituals.[1][2]

    Artellus Numeon, First Captain of the Salamanders' 1st Company, the Firedrakes, served as equerry to Vulkan and commanded the primarch's honour guard, the Pyre Guard. Gabriel Santor of the Iron Hands was equerry to Ferrus Manus and First Captain of the Morlocks, the Legion's 1st Company. Khârn of the World Eaters was chosen by Angron as equerry after nearly being beaten to death by him.[1][3]
