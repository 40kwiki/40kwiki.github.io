    The Destroyers are a loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and a rare successor of the White Scars, raised in the Second Founding. They have won great victories for the Imperium. Their gene-seed has also drawn doubts, and so has the savage conduct of their battle-brothers.[1]

    The Destroyers share their genefather's relish for battle and his fast, aggressive way of war. After the Daemon rampage in the Lhorgus Sub-sector, in which the Chapter showed self-sacrificing heroism, the concern over its gene-seed grew rather than eased. For their part, the Destroyers are concerned with the next mission.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Corusil V

    [edit]

    The Destroyers were raised in the early 31st millennium, in the aftermath of the Horus Heresy. In 084.M31 they went with a White Scars force under Jaghatai Khan to Corusil V, to take vengeance on the Drukhari who had ravaged Chogoris and the Yasan Sector while the Legion was away. The White Scars and their successors beat the Drukhari, but the raiders' Archon fled through a nearby portal into the Webway. The Khan and much of the 1st Brotherhood followed, and the portal closed behind them for good. The Khan's fate is unknown.[1]

    The 13th Black Crusade

    [edit]

    The Destroyers were among the Chapters sent to defend Cadia against the 13th Black Crusade of Abaddon the Despoiler in c. 999.M41.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    One company of the Destroyers went to Vigilus in the War of Beasts (c. 001–025.M42) with the White Scars strike force, to defend the link between the Imperium Sanctus and the Imperium Nihilus. The Chapter also fought in the Nachmund Rift War, in the defence of the Nachmund Gauntlet from the Chaos forces seeking to seal the passage through the Great Rift. In that war the strike cruiser Glorious Hunt was destroyed at the Grakiliod Narrow, in the Imperial fleet that tried to take the fight to the Chaos fleet of Haarken Worldclaimer. In the Blackheart War the Destroyers reinforced the White Scars in the Yasan Sector against the Red Corsairs.[1][2][a]

    Organisation and heraldry

    [edit]

    Unlike the White Scars, who mark squads with tribal signs of Chogoris, the Destroyers use Codex Astartes squad numbers in yellow on the right-shoulder specialty symbol, and company is shown by the colour of the left knee plate: white for the 1st Company, yellow for the 2nd, red for the 3rd, green for the 4th and so on. Their armour is black with sunburst yellow shoulder guards, helmet and chest Aquila. The badge is a black skull ringed by four lightning bolts on sunburst yellow.[1]
