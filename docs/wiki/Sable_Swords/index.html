    The Sable Swords are a loyal Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes who follow the Codex Astartes. Their genetic origin is unknown. Imperial records give two accounts of their birth, and no scribe has reconciled them.[1]

    The older account places them in the Fourth Founding, raised after the War of the Beast in 546.M32, and has them fight in the removal of the Lord Protector Drakan Vangorich a century later. The later account says they were raised in 926.M41, after the Astral Knights were destroyed at the Danorra System, and took over the Astral Knights' fortress-monastery on Obsidia. They may be one Chapter, or two Chapters of the same name and heraldry created at different times.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    The Fourth Founding account

    [edit]

    The War of the Beast left the Imperium ruined, and the lord commander Maximus Thane ordered a Fourth Founding to raise as many Chapters as the gene-seed vaults of Terra could feed. The Sable Swords are said to have been among them. When Thane returned to Terra c. 626.M32 to depose Vangorich, who had ruled by terror since the Beheading of 546.M32, four hundred Space Marines were sent to the surface: a company each of the Sable Swords and the Halo Brethren, and two hundred Imperial Fists. The Sable Swords' Chapter Master, Qublicus Amar, commanded the strike. A Vindicare assassin's bullet killed him soon after landing. Captain Ethratan of the Second Company was also present. The force lost half its number in the Imperial Palace before it reached the Eversor temple at the north pole, where Thane alone survived to reach Vangorich.[1]

    The Danorra account

    [edit]

    Later records say that in 926.M41 the entire Astral Knights Chapter died to stop a Necron World Engine on the edge of the Danorra System. The few Astral Knights who had been elsewhere were too few to rebuild and were absorbed as a veteran training cadre into the new Sable Swords. The new Chapter took the empty fortress-monastery on Obsidia in the Ultima Segmentum.[1]

    Later wars

    [edit]

    While most of the Chapter fought the Aeldari of the Swordwind at Larical's Folly, a mutant warband of the Twisted Convocation fell on Obsidia. The few Sable Swords at home held until the Novamarines relieved them. The Chapter swore an oath of eternal debt to repay them. In 954.M41 a fighting retreat on Rogue's Spire saved Mechanicus explorators from WAAAGH! Dethrekka. In 998.M41 three companies carried out an Exterminatus on Eldritch, which proved a Necron Tomb World. The same year Codicier Sundic was still searching the Augran Nebula for Captain Noiran, missing after a skirmish with Aeldari pirates. In 999.M41 Tactical squads joined the Ultramarines in freeing Augura from the T'au in the Zeist Campaign.[1]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Sable Swords wear black armour with white trim, a white Aquila and white knee guards. A white squad role badge marks the right shoulder.[1] Both of the Chapter's origin stories describe it as faithful to the Codex.

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Qublicus Amar: Chapter Master, killed on Terra c. 626.M32.
    • Daegan: captain of the First Company.
    • Noiran: captain, missing since 998.M41.
    • Sundic: Codicier searching for Noiran.
