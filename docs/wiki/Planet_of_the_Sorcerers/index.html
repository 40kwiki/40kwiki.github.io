    The Planet of the Sorcerers, also called Sortiarius, is a daemon world ruled by Magnus the Red, Daemon Primarch of Tzeentch, and the home of his Traitor Legion, the Thousand Sons. Magnus and the survivors of his Legion were carried there through the warp at the end of the Burning of Prospero in 005.M31, and for ten thousand years it lay deep in the Eye of Terror. In 999.M41, after his attack on Fenris, Magnus brought the planet out of the Eye and into the material universe in orbit over the ruins of Prospero.[1][2]

    Sortiarius is a volcanic world of sorcerous towers, where the Thousand Sons keep their libraries and their slaves. It was the scene of Ahzek Ahriman's Rubric, which turned most of the Legion to dust in armour, and of his exile. In the Era Indomitus it has been the base of Magnus's great rituals of the Psychic Awakening (c. 000–020.M42, estimated).[3][4]

    History

    [edit]

    Arrival

    [edit]

    When the Space Wolves destroyed Prospero in 005.M31, Magnus, broken by Leman Russ, used his last power to save what remained of his Legion. Thousand Sons accounts say that Tzeentch stayed the end and moved the survivors, with a fragment of the city of Tizca, through the warp to a world in the Eye of Terror that the god had promised them. The planet was empty when they arrived. It became their home, and Magnus raised the Tower of the Cyclops at its heart.[1][5]

    The Rubric

    [edit]
    Main article: Ahzek Ahriman

    After the Heresy, the flesh-change that had afflicted the Thousand Sons before the Crusade returned on the Planet of the Sorcerers. To end it, Ahriman and a cabal of sorcerers cast the Rubric. It reduced almost every Legionary without strong psychic gifts to dust sealed in his armour, creating the Rubric Marines, and left the most powerful sorcerers changed but whole. Magnus judged the spell a crime and exiled Ahriman and his cabal from the planet. Thousand Sons accounts say that Tzeentch stayed Magnus's hand. The date of the Rubric is not recorded; it is placed after the Siege of Terra and before the end of M31 (c. 015–100.M31, estimated).[3][a]

    Ten thousand years in the Eye

    [edit]

    For the next ten millennia the Thousand Sons raided outward from Sortiarius in search of slaves, knowledge and psykers. A large underclass of servants grew on the planet, and many of them escaped into its wastes to form nomad warbands, whose champions were often recruited back into the Legion. Psykers were taken as thrall-wizards, and a population of beastmen, the Tzaangors, grew up in the wilds. Warbands of other Traitors, and sometimes warriors from other ages, appeared on the planet at intervals, a sign of its place in the shifting time of the Eye.[3]

    Before the Thirteenth Black Crusade (c. 998.M41, estimated), Magnus summoned Ahriman back to the planet for the first time since his exile, to join the attack on Fenris.[3]

    The Wrath of Magnus

    [edit]

    In 999.M41 Magnus led the Thousand Sons against the Space Wolves in the Fenris System. The attack was the culmination of a ritual: when it ended, Magnus drew the Planet of the Sorcerers out of the Eye of Terror and set it in orbit above Prospero, bringing a warp rift with it. Prospero was partly restored by his sorcery. The Inquisition declared the system a place of the first danger and forbade approach.[2][6]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    After the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, Magnus gathered psykers from across the galaxy to the Prosperan Rift around his world. In the Psychic Awakening (c. 000–020.M42, estimated) he began a great ritual from Sortiarius, which a force of Dark Angels and Grey Knights stopped before it was complete. Ahriman, who had learned from the Aeldari prophet Yvraine that the Rubric might be reversed, has continued his own search outside the Legion.[4][7]

    Geography

    [edit]

    Sortiarius is dark, rocky and volcanic, with skies of warp-storm and low clouds of many colours. Rain of raw energy fills its dark rivers and seas, and its continents shift. The towers of the sorcerers rise from rocks above plains of lava. While the planet was in the Eye it orbited a star that passed through nine phases. Wards surround the world: munitions fired at it are drawn into the warp, lasers are scattered, orbital bombardments are returned upon their firers, and enemy ships are thrown into the warp by their own drives.[3]

    • Tizca of Sortiarius, a twisted copy of Prospero's capital, with obelisks, crystal mounds and changed pyramids that house the Legion's libraries.[3]
    • The Tower of the Cyclops, or Obsidian Tower, the black monolith of Magnus, which dwarfs every other tower on the planet.[3]
