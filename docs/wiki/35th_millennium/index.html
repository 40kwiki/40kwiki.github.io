    35th millennium
    000.M35 – 999.M35
    AgeNova Terra Interregnum
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra in the east; the Ur-Council of Nova Terra in the Segmentum Pacificus
    Key eventsMoirae Schism (M35); Saint Sabbat's crusade (500–605.M35); Cataclysm of Souls (975.M35); Cursed Founding (991.M35)
    Preceded by34th millennium
    Followed by36th millennium

    The 35th millennium (M35) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M35 to 999.M35. For most of it the Imperium was divided by the Nova Terra Interregnum, the "Time of Twin Empires", in which the Ur-Council of Nova Terra ruled the Segmentum Pacificus apart from Terra. In the same years the Moirae Schism split the Adeptus Mechanicus and exiled a third of the Iron Hands. The Ecclesiarchy itself left Terra for a time and moved its Holy Synod to Ophelia VII.[1]

    The Interregnum ended in 975.M35 with the Cataclysm of Souls, the religious wars in which the Adeptus Ministorum reunited the Imperium and emerged as its strongest institution. The millennium closed with the Hrud Rising and the Cursed Founding of 991.M35, the largest Founding since the Second. It also saw the first Imperial contact with the tombs of the Necrons and the discovery of the T'au.[1]

    Chronology

    [edit]
    DateEventNotes
    Early M35Warp storm activity rises across the Imperium, an "echo of Old Night".See Age of Strife.
    Early M35The Ur-Council of Nova Terra repudiates the High Lords of Terra; the Nova Terra Interregnum begins.Some sector records give 401.M34.
    c. 000.M35The political power of the Adeptus Ministorum begins to wane.
    M35The Moirae Schism in the Adeptus Mechanicus; the forge world of Moirae destroyed.Last holdouts purged in M37.
    M35The Grimoire Hereticus, a register of the Traitor Legions, is completed.
    M35The Warlord Arrgard the Defiler scours the forge world of Tigrus and destroys the Vanquisher STC.
    200.M35Ecclesiarch Benedin IV moves the Holy Synod from Terra to Ophelia VII.
    257.M35The Dark Angels raid Nova Terra in pursuit of a suspected Fallen on the Ur-Council.
    267.M35First surviving record of Watch Fortress Erioch of the Deathwatch in the Jericho Sector.
    344.M35Imperial survey of tomb complexes on Angelis.Later the Ork world Gorkamorka.
    359.M35Explorators enter the tombs of Angelis: the first recorded contact with the Necrons.
    437.M35The Red Dawn of Iriad IV: the Ebon Knights meet a World Eaters raid.
    495.M35The Hrakon Campaign of the Dark Angels in the Segmentum Pacificus.
    500.M35Ecclesiarch Greigor XI declares that the Holy Synod will return to Terra.The cardinals resist.
    500–605.M35Saint Sabbat leads the crusade that brings the Sabbat Worlds into the Imperium.See Sabbat Worlds Crusade.
    566.M35A Chapter whose name is expunged is declared Excommunicate Traitoris.[data expunged]
    598.M35One summary table places the Eighth Founding, of the Angels Revenant and the Mantis Warriors, in this year.Most records give the mid-34th millennium; see 34th millennium.
    700.M35The Cleaving of the Black Wings: five companies renounce their vows.
    789.M35The Explorator ship Land's Vision finds the primitive T'au on their homeworld.See T'au Empire.
    831.M35The Barbarus Incursion of the Black Templars; its records sealed.
    867.M35Daemons enter the world spirit of the Exodite world Menimshemash.
    973.M35The Grief of Herodin: first record of the Decimator daemon engine.
    975.M35The Cataclysm of Souls ends the Interregnum; the Ecclesiarchy becomes the most powerful body in the Imperium.The Dark Angels fight in it.
    977.M35The Ultramarines battle barge Excelsior suffers a Geller field failure.
    980.M35The Hrud Rising; infestations purged across the Imperium.
    990.M35The Burning of the White Palace: Deathwatch kill-teams strike the governor of Vanir.
    991.M35The Cursed Founding, the twenty-first, the largest since the Second.Some records place it in early M36.
