    Cato Sicarius is a hero of the Ultramarines, for many years captain of the Chapter's Second Company, Master of the Watch and Knight Champion of Macragge, and since 849.M41 Grand Duke of his homeworld of Talassar. Brilliant, proud and fearless, he is counted one of the finest swordsmen and commanders of his Chapter, though critics within it have long argued that his ambition costs the lives of his warriors.[1][2]

    Sicarius fought at Black Reach, against the daemon prince M'kar the Reborn, and twice on the Necron tomb world of Damnos. In 999.M41 he went with the resurrected Roboute Guilliman to Terra in the Terran Crusade, and was then sent back to Ultramar with the first Primaris Space Marines of Guilliman's line, only to be lost in the Warp storms of the Great Rift. He returned, crossed the Rubicon Primaris, and in the Era Indomitus commands the Victrix Honour Guard.[3]

    Early life

    [edit]

    Sicarius was born into the ducal household of Talassar, one of the leading noble dynasties of Ultramar, and was trained in arms from infancy: by his fourth birthday he could wrestle, fence and shoot to his masters' satisfaction. The training masters of the Talassari agoge pressed him hard, seeing the glory he could bring their house, and he was accepted by the Ultramarines. As a captain he rose to Master of the Watch, and in 849.M41 he was named Grand Duke of Talassar and Suzerain of Ultramar.[2]

    As Knight Champion of Macragge he fought duels on the Chapter's behalf; in one, against the heretic Orchaedes, he struck off the heretic's hand as it emerged from his robes holding a hidden pistol.[2]

    Black Reach and M'kar

    [edit]

    In about 858.M41 Sicarius led the Second Company in the Assault on Black Reach, which saved the world from the Orks. A Terminator and twelve battle-brothers were killed, a toll that some in the Chapter council thought too high. In 878.M41 he first fought the daemon prince M'kar, called the Reborn or Thrice-born, who had masterminded a Chaos raid; the enmity lasted more than a century. When Sicarius returned to Macragge in 999.M41 he found Ultramar under M'kar's attack, and with Marneus Calgar he defended Talassar until M'kar was slain and banished.[2][a]

    Damnos

    [edit]

    In 974.M41 Sicarius led an assault on the mining world of Damnos, which had been overrun by Necrons awakening from its tombs. He was to relieve Kellenport, the planetary capital and its last surviving city, while the Chief Librarian Varro Tigurius destroyed a Gauss pylon battery. The Ultramarines could not hold the world. They evacuated what citizens they could and withdrew, and Damnos was declared a forbidden world. Sicarius was gravely wounded. The defeat changed him, and his courage afterwards often became recklessness.[2]

    In 999.M41, twenty-five years later, a Deathwatch team brought the decree of the High Lords of Terra and the Fabricator-General that the Ultramarines must return to Damnos and destroy the Necrons. Calgar led five companies and elements of the Tenth, over five hundred Space Marines, to the world, and Sicarius trained for it so hard that he slept in the battle-cage. The Return to Damnos was a long and bitter campaign against an enemy that seemed to anticipate every move. In the same year Sicarius commanded the Imperial forces in the Fall of Medusa V, which ended in Exterminatus, and led some thirty Chapters and Astra Militarum regiments in the Zeist Campaign against the T'au Empire.[2]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    After Guilliman was restored at Macragge in 999.M41, Sicarius went with him to Terra in the Terran Crusade. Guilliman then sent him back to Macragge with a force of Primaris Space Marines of his own line, but the small fleet was caught in the Warp disturbances that marked the birth of the Great Rift and the Noctis Aeterna, and Sicarius's ship was lost. He eventually returned to Macragge and, at some time after, crossed the Rubicon Primaris. He now commands the Victrix Honour Guard, the elite guard of the Chapter Master, while the title of Master of the Watch has passed to Demetrian Titus.[3][b]

    Character

    [edit]

    Sicarius is courageous, arrogant and certain of his destiny, and many believe that he intends one day to be Chapter Master. His warriors are fiercely loyal to him. His armour, the Mantle of the Suzerain, carries the honours of his career, among them the Victorex Alpha and the Eternium Ultra of the High Suzerain of Ultramar.[1]
