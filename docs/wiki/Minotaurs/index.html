    The Minotaurs are a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes, known for their bronze and red armour, their savagery in battle and the mystery of their origins. Imperial records list a Chapter of the name among the "Cursed" Twenty-First Founding of the 36th millennium, but much evidence contradicts this, and the Chapter's own gene-seed has been described by the Adeptus Mechanicus as "chimeric", with no clear primarch lineage. The Chapter is fleet-based, and its fortress-monastery is the battle barge Daedelos Krata.[1][2]

    The Minotaurs are widely believed to act as the private enforcers of the High Lords of Terra, sent against Chapters that have strayed from the Imperium's will. They took a central part in the Badab War of 907–913.M41, where they all but destroyed the Lamenters, and in the Era Indomitus fought on Terra in the Hexarchy Rebellion on behalf of a cabal of reactionary High Lords who opposed the reforms of Roboute Guilliman.[2][3]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    That a Chapter called the Minotaurs was created in the Twenty-First Founding is not contested. Those Minotaurs gained a reputation for unmatched ferocity, but their refusal to accept the command of others and their erratic gene-seed tithes, together with the curses that afflicted many Chapters of that Founding, raised dark suspicions. They vanished from the record in the late 36th millennium. The Chapter that reappeared under the same name some four thousand years later was markedly different: better equipped than almost any other Chapter, served by rare patterns of armour and war machine, and answering, it is believed, to the highest authority in the Imperium. Imperial scholars dispute whether the two bodies are the same Chapter.[1][a]

    8th Black Crusade

    [edit]

    During the eighth Black Crusade of Abaddon the Despoiler in 999.M37, a force of Night Lords and Iron Warriors seized the Andromax System and threatened the approaches to Terra. Imperial records credit the Minotaurs with a part in the counter-attack, though, like much of their early history, the account is fragmentary.[1]

    Wars of the 41st millennium

    [edit]

    The Minotaurs reappeared in full in the suppression of the Macharian Heresy between 400 and 425.M41, the wars of secession that followed the death of Lord Solar Macharius. Their most infamous act of that period was the Euxine Incident, in which they intervened in a feud between the Doom Warriors and the Inceptors Chapters and left both nearly destroyed.[1]

    In 760.M41 they relieved the hive world of Hermetica, capital of the Chonma Sector in the Segmentum Solar, which had been besieged since 659.M41 by a mutant horde under the Word Bearers Dark Apostle Leyak the Devourer. The Minotaurs were the first outside force to answer the planet's calls, and Chapter Master Asterion Moloc led the Terminators and assault squads that destroyed the Chaos foothold at Hive Casaubon.[1]

    The Badab War

    [edit]
    Main article: Badab War

    The Minotaurs reached the Maelstrom in 907.M41 at the call of the Legate Inquisitor, and were among the most effective of the Loyalist Chapters in the Badab War. Their best-remembered action was the destruction of the Lamenters' fleet in a close-quarters void battle in the Optera System in 908.M41, which broke the Lamenters as a fighting force. The Chapter withdrew in 913.M41, after the fall of Badab Primaris, and took several captured Lamenters warships with it to replace its losses.[2]

    Last years of M41

    [edit]

    In 989.M41 the Minotaurs fought on Rynn's World in the campaign to retake the Crimson Fists' homeworld from the Orks. Between 989 and 992.M41 they took part in the hunt for the Night Reapers, a Chapter declared Excommunicate Traitoris in the Laanah Rifts; the incident cost the Grey Knights and the Ordo Malleus heavily, and Moloc, at first reported slain, was seen leading his Chapter again some months later. From 992 to 995.M41 the Minotaurs fought in the failed defence of the Orpheus Sector against the Necrons of the Maynarkh Dynasty, with Moloc in command of the Space Marine forces at the Battle of Amarah.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Soon after the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, a sepsis cohort of the Death Guard's 7th Plague Company attacked the Daedelos Krata, and the Chapter repelled it. Minotaurs Strike Force Abrecht, under Captain Ulrech, served with Battle Group Faustus of Fleet Secundus in the Indomitus Crusade. The Chapter's most controversial action of the era was the Hexarchy Rebellion, an attempt by current and deposed High Lords, many removed by Guilliman, to seize Terra while the Lord Commander was away and restore the old order. The Minotaurs fought for the Hexarchy, which Imperial scholars take as proof of their long service to the High Lords as a body rather than to any one regent. The rebellion is dated to the first decades of M42.[3][b]

    Organisation and wargear

    [edit]

    The Minotaurs keep a full complement of about a thousand battle-brothers. Their Armoury is unusually rich: most of their First Company veterans fight in Tactical Dreadnought armour, including rare Tartaros and Indomitus patterns, and they field Contemptor and Leviathan Dreadnoughts and other ancient machines rarely seen outside the oldest Chapters. Imperial scholars take this as further evidence of patronage on Terra.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Asterion Moloc, Chapter Master, Satrap of the Daedelos Krata and Bringer of Wrath; known to have slain several renegade Chapter Masters.
    • Captain Ulrech, commander of Strike Force Abrecht in the Indomitus Crusade.
