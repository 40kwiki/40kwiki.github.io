    Saint Celestine, the Living Saint and Hieromartyr of the Palatine Crusade, is a warrior of the Adepta Sororitas held by the Imperial Cult to have been raised from death repeatedly by the will of the Emperor of Mankind. A Sister Repentia of the Order of Our Martyred Lady, she fell in battle during the Palatine Crusade, rose the next day unmarked, and was proclaimed a Living Saint after emerging from the hidden crypt of Saint Katherine clad in golden armour and bearing a flaming sword. She was believed lost centuries later at Forrax, but has since appeared again and again where the faithful were in desperate need.[1][2]

    Her greatest intervention came in 999.M41 at the Fall of Cadia. She arrived at Kasr Kraf with five companies of her order lost in the Warp for fourteen centuries, killed the daemon prince Urkanthos, and stabbed Abaddon the Despoiler in the back with the Ardent Blade to save Lord Castellan Ursarkar E. Creed. She then led the survivors of Cadia, the Celestinian Crusade, through the Webway to Macragge, where she witnessed the restoration of Roboute Guilliman, and followed him on the Terran Crusade to the Imperial Palace.[3]

    In the Era Indomitus she has continued to appear on scattered battlefields. On the beleaguered world of Tsadrekha in the Imperium Nihilus she fought for an astropathic beacon and was slain by Khârn the Betrayer. As with every previous death, the Ecclesiarchy holds that it will not be her last.[3]

    Origins

    [edit]

    Almost nothing is recorded of Celestine before her apotheosis, other than that she served as a Sister Repentia in the Palatine Crusade, a war of faith against the heretics of the Palatine Schism. She fought in the first wave of the assault on the capital of Eurytion and fell there with every other Repentia, having killed more than a hundred schismatics. When her sisters recovered her body to set it among the honoured dead, they found it flawless and still living, with a divine light in her eyes. The next day she led the renewed assault, and the city fell within hours.[1]

    The crusade's commander, Lord Militant Ansgar, intended to end the war on the sector capital, but Celestine insisted first on liberating the backwater world of Sanctus Lys. There her followers found a broken altar among ruins, beneath which lay a crypt untouched for millennia: the shrine of Saint Katherine of the Order of the Fiery Heart, visited in secret by that saint shortly before her own death. Celestine went down alone. At dawn she rose from the tomb on a column of light, attended by cherubs and doves, clad in golden power armour and carrying a blade too bright to look upon, the Ardent Blade. Ansgar and a conclave of priests declared her a Living Saint.[1][2]

    Wars of faith

    [edit]

    Celestine led numerous wars of faith after the Palatine Crusade, and hundreds of thousands of heretics died before her armies. Her influence on the Orders Militant was extraordinary; she became the object of their total veneration, and it was said that the Sisters would follow her into the Eye of Terror.[2]

    She was lost in battle against the renegade Warmaster of Forrax, who destroyed his own fortress and many kilometres around it by triggering the meltdown of its ancient atomic pile at the moment of her triumph. No one survived to report how the battle ended. The bell of the Tower of Heroes on Terra tolled once for her, and a billion souls paused in mourning. The Sisters at prayer in the Convent Prioris, it is said, rejoiced, for they knew that she now sat at the Emperor's right hand.[2]

    Sightings of the saint continued. In 980.M41, during the Promethean War on the cardinal world of Heletine, the Order of the Ebon Chalice and the Salamanders were being overrun by the possessed warriors of the daemon prince Lord Gralastyx of the Black Legion when Celestine appeared, cut her way to the daemon prince, drove her blade through his heart and vanished as suddenly as she had come.[2]

    The Fall of Cadia

    [edit]

    In 999.M41, as the 13th Black Crusade assaulted Cadia, Celestine appeared wreathed in flame over Kasr Kraf. She declared that she had been sent back by the Emperor in the hour of the Imperium's greatest need. With her came five companies of the Order of Our Martyred Lady, lost in the Warp for fourteen hundred years, whose ship her light had guided back into realspace. She immediately engaged the daemon prince Urkanthos, alongside two canonesses whom he had recently slain and whom she had raised from death by her power and their faith. These were Genevieve and Eleanor, who have since fought at her side as the Geminae Superia. The three killed Urkanthos and threw his body from the walls, routing his followers.[3]

    During the respite, the Archmagos Belisarius Cawl revealed that Abaddon's purpose was to destroy the ancient pylons of Cadia, which held the Eye of Terror in check. Lord Castellan Creed fortified the catacombs beneath the Elysion Fields while Cawl, secretly advised by the Necron Trazyn the Infinite, learned to turn the pylons into a weapon. When the pylons were activated they severed the region from the Warp and banished the daemons; but the same effect weakened Celestine, whose power flows from the faith of the Emperor's servants through the Immaterium. Diminished, she was beaten by Abaddon himself and left at his feet. She told him that humanity would one day be free of his masters. "There is no freedom," he replied.[3]

    The Ordo Hereticus Inquisitor Katarinya Greyfax, long convinced that Celestine was a fraud, assaulted the Despoiler with her psychic powers and drove him back. Abaddon then broke the charge of the Cadian 8th and was about to kill Creed when Celestine rose and drove the Ardent Blade into his back. "The Emperor protects," she told him. It had been millennia since Abaddon had been so wounded. With the Warp receding, he withdrew. He had already prepared a final blow: the crippled Blackstone Fortress Will of Eternity was hurled onto Cadia, destroying the planet and the pylons. The restoration of the Warp restored Celestine's strength.[3]

    The Celestinian Crusade

    [edit]

    Creed ordered the evacuation of the dying world. Its leaders, Cawl, Celestine, Greyfax and Marshal Marius Amalrich of the Black Templars, took the name of the Celestinian Crusade and carried some three million survivors out of the Cadia system at sub-light speed, pursued by the Black Fleet. Cawl's ark Iron Revenant was sacrificed against Abaddon's flagship so that the others could reach the ice moon Klaisus, where Celestine had foreseen that salvation waited. There the crusade was found by the aeldari of the Ynnari, who led it through a hidden Webway gate.[3]

    The Ynnari brought the Celestinians to Ultramar, then under Black Legion assault. In the Fortress of Hera Celestine explained her mission to Marneus Calgar and his officers. In the Temple of Correction, while Chaos forces assaulted the shrine, Cawl's technology and the power of the aeldari god Ynnead restored Guilliman to life. The Imperial Cult proclaims the event a miracle of the Emperor, and Greyfax, confronted by a living primarch, renounced her doubts about the saint. Celestine blessed Guilliman at his investiture as Lord of Ultramar.[3]

    The Terran Crusade

    [edit]

    Celestine accompanied Guilliman on the Terran Crusade through the Warp storms of the Maelstrom. When a cloud of corrosive spores and plague flies the size of frigates fell upon the fleet, she took her place in the Navigator's observation blister of the flagship Macragge's Honour and purged the daemon swarm with a shockwave of holy light. She was among the few veterans of Cadia to survive the voyage, which ended with the crusade's arrival on Luna through the Webway and the defeat of Magnus the Red and his Thousand Sons at the lunar gate.[3]

    On Terra, while Guilliman spent a day before the Emperor beyond the Ultimate Gate of the Sanctum Imperialis, Celestine ministered to the multitudes of pilgrims and penitents outside it. When the primarch emerged to proclaim the Indomitus Crusade, she pledged to stand at his side.[3]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    Tsadrekha

    [edit]

    During the Noctis Aeterna, the hive and ocean world of Tsadrekha in the Imperium Nihilus kept in contact with other worlds through a single psyker strong enough to serve as an astropathic beacon. The beacon drew the Archenemy. The Alpha Legion warband known as the Unsung under Captain Kassar, which had been trapped for ten thousand years on a daemon world where only a few years had passed, sought it; so did the Emperor's Children of the Chaos Lord Excrucias and the World Eaters under Khârn the Betrayer. A force of the Imperial Fists came to the world's defence, and Celestine appeared from the Warp soon afterwards. Rather than let the beacon fall to Khârn, she entrusted the psyker to Kassar, whose warriors had not yet given themselves to Chaos. Khârn then killed her in combat.[3]

    Status

    [edit]

    As of the latest Imperial records, Celestine has been seen again since Tsadrekha, as she has after every previous death, and the Adeptus Ministorum regards her as active. Reports of her appearances come from widely separated fronts of the Indomitus Crusade, often accompanied by the Geminae Superia. The Ecclesiarchy teaches that she will die only when the Emperor finally calls her to Him, and not before.[2][3][a]

    Powers and wargear

    [edit]

    Celestine shines with a holy radiance that fills the faithful with courage and the corrupt with terror. She can call down psychic fire on her enemies, heal the wounded, and fly on wings created by the power of her faith. Her abilities depend on the Warp: when Cawl's pylons cut Cadia off from the Immaterium, her strength failed.[1]

    • The Ardent Blade, a flame-wreathed power sword with a hilt in the form of the aquila, which can loose a jet of fire at her command.
    • The Armour of Saint Katherine, golden power armour anointed with a vial of Saint Katherine's blood.
    • The Wings of Faith, feathered wings manifested by her faith.
