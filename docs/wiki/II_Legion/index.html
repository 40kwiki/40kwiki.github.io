    The II Legion, the Second Legion of the Space Marine Legions, is one of two Legions of the Great Crusade that no surviving Imperial record names in full. It was raised in the First Founding of about 700.M30 and fought in the early campaigns of the Crusade. Its primarch was found in 821.M30, and by the time of the Horus Heresy it had been removed from the archives together with its commander. Nothing in the Imperial record shows that it turned against the Emperor. It simply stops appearing.

    The Imperium calls this removal the Excision, a name that appears in no formal edict but is used by Inquisitorial and Remembrancer staff alike. The Legion's name, number and primarch were struck from the rolls, its battle honours were reassigned or erased, and its gene-seed was never used again. Imperial archivists agree on that much. They disagree on everything else, including whether the Legion was destroyed, dishonoured, absorbed into another Legion or sent somewhere that no record can reach.

    The reasons are sealed. The Inquisition's policy on the II Legion is that it is not to be discussed, and the few references that survive are either damaged or annotated with a single word, expunged. Scholars who write on the subject do so from fragments, rumours and the absence of the Legion from documents where it ought to appear. This article is written in the same spirit. Where a claim rests on a rumour, it is marked as one.

    History

    [edit]

    Raising and early service

    [edit]

    The II Legion was among the twenty Legions raised on Terra in the First Founding. Like the others, it went to war under a Legion Master before its primarch was found. The earliest surviving reference to it is an Administratum muster from the first decade of the Crusade, which lists its strength in the same column as the Legions that survive today. Its recruits were drawn from Terra and the hardest worlds then within reach of the Emperor's armies, as those of the other Legions were.[1]

    The Legion's gene-seed was taken from its primarch, as was the custom, and the Emperor's Apothecaries recorded it as stable. No surviving Apothecarion record describes a flaw in the seed, and the Legion's later absence is not explained by any known genetic defect. This is the first of several points on which the Legion's history is unlike the histories of the Legions that fell.[2]

    The finding of the primarch

    [edit]

    The primarch of the II Legion was found in 821.M30, between the recovery of Jaghatai Khan and the finding of Ferrus Manus. The Imperial record of the event is a single line in the chronology of the Great Crusade: the primarch was found, and the entry carries a later annotation that his records were expunged. The date of that annotation is unknown. The name of the world on which he was found is not recorded.[1]

    This is the earliest point at which the Imperium's accounts diverge. The Legions' archives treat the finding as an ordinary event in the Crusade. The Excision, by contrast, is never recorded as an event at all. It is inferred from the absence of later entries, from the annotation, and from the gap in the Legion numbering.[3]

    The Rangdan Xenocides

    [edit]
    Main article: Rangdan Xenocides

    The only unambiguous evidence that the II Legion fought in the Great Crusade is a passage in a copy of the Imperial Infantryman's Uplifting Primer, revised in 990.M41, which was found with a misplaced section of an older archive. It states that the II Legion took part in the Rangdan Xenocides in the 860s.M30, and it says nothing about the nature or scope of that participation.[4] Scholars who read the passage differ on whether the Legion fought alongside the Dark Angels and the Alpha Legion or on a separate front. Since the Legion vanishes from the record after this, the passage is treated as the last clear mention of its active service.

    The Ultramarines' archive, which records the Crusade's campaigns in detail, does not mention the II Legion in any of the Rangdan campaigns. Its silence is not proof of absence, since the same archive is equally silent on several Legions that were certainly present.[5]

    The Excision

    [edit]

    The Excision is estimated to have taken place between c. 900 and 950.M30. The range is wide because no record fixes the event, and because the annotation of expungement on the primarch's entry could have been made at any point afterwards. Some accounts place it in the middle decades of the Crusade, and others in the years just before the Heresy. None of these can be confirmed.[a]

    Imperial accounts agree on the form of the removal. The Legion's name and number were stricken from the rolls of the Administratum, its primarch's statue was removed from the Investiary, and every record that named its officers was sealed or destroyed. The Imperial Fists' archive notes that a Legion's battle honours were redistributed to the Legions that fought beside it. The Ultramarines' archive mentions a sudden rise in the number of Astartes it enrolled in the same years.[6]

    Rumours of the Legion's fate

    [edit]

    The II Legion's fate is the subject of many rumours, and they are recorded here only so that the reader can see what has been said. Imperial accounts differ on the following points, and none has been confirmed.

    • Destruction. The earliest and simplest rumour holds that the Legion was destroyed by the Emperor's own forces, with the Space Wolves given the task that the Imperium has always reserved for its executioners. A remembrancer's note records the phrase "not the first time the Wolves were loosed", but its context is lost.[7]
    • Absorption. A long-standing rumour among the Legiones Astartes holds that the Legion's warriors were drafted into another Legion, and that the Ultramarines grew to outnumber every other Legion in the years of the Excision. Word Bearers captives in later campaigns repeated the claim, and the Inquisition has not been able to disprove it.[7]
    • Disease. A gene-flaw that threatened the Blood Angels is said by some scholars to have been the reason for the Excision of a Legion. The II Legion has been named as a possible example, although the claim rests on a conversation recorded in an Imperial Fists' dossier and no Legion's genetic records support it.[8]
    • A founding of the Blood Ravens. Some scholars have proposed that the II Legion's primarch was the founder of the Blood Ravens Chapter. The theory has little support. It does not explain why the Blood Ravens would survive into the 41st millennium when the Legion they might descend from was erased so thoroughly.[2]
    • Secret survival. A minority of inquisitors, mainly from the Ordo Malleus, have written that the Legion survives in hidden form and that its primarch may be among the figures the Imperium has named as traitors. No one has produced a warrior who can be shown to be of the II Legion.[5]

    The Horus Heresy and after

    [edit]

    The II Legion took no part in the Heresy as recorded by any Legion. It is not named at the Isstvan III massacre, the Drop Site Massacre or the Battle of Calth, and no loyalist or traitor report from the early war mentions its colours. Its warriors, if any survived, are not known to have fought under their old name. The Legion's absence from the Heresy is one of the strongest reasons for the belief that it had already been removed before the war began.[7]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    Nothing is known of the II Legion's internal structure. The Legions of the early Crusade were generally organised along the notional pattern set out by the Imperial staff, with Chapters, battalions and companies beneath a command structure headed by the primarch. Whether the II Legion followed that pattern, or organised itself differently, cannot be established from the record.[1]

    Heraldry

    [edit]

    No Legion badge, livery or battle cry of the II Legion survives in an Imperial archive. The emblem of the Adeptus Astartes is used in this article only because a Legion infobox requires an image. Any surviving Legionary heraldry would have been removed in the Excision, and no Chapter that claims descent from the II Legion is recognised by the Adeptus Astartes.
