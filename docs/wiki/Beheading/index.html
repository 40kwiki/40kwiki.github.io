    The Beheading was the murder of the twelve High Lords of Terra in a single solar day in 546.M32, on the order of the Grand Master of Assassins, Drakan Vangorich, and the political order that he built on their graves. It came at the very end of the War of the Beast, while the Lord Commander of the Imperium, Maximus Thane, was away on crusade with the reconstituted Imperial Fists. Vangorich, whom Thane had named Lord Protector, ruled the Imperium for nearly a standard century through a council of his own appointees.[1]

    Vangorich was an effective ruler and a cruel one. He rebuilt the fleets, fortified Terra, and carried out the Fourth Founding of the Adeptus Astartes that Thane had decreed. In the end his rule decayed into paranoia and purge, and Thane returned to Terra with the Imperial Fists to remove him. The assault on the Lord Protector's last refuge, the Eversor Temple, cost the Space Marines all but one of their number. Thane alone reached Vangorich and killed him.[1][2]

    The affair left the Imperium without a government for almost two solar decades, until the Chapter Master Agnathio of the Ultramarines brought fifty Chapters to Terra and compelled the factions to end their quarrel. The council that was then seated differed from the old in several respects, and the office of Lord Commander, created in the Great Crusade's aftermath, was abolished and not restored until the return of Roboute Guilliman in the 41st millennium.[3]

    Background

    [edit]

    The council and the war

    [edit]

    The Senatorum Imperialis of the early 32nd millennium was a twelve-member body whose members represented the great institutions of the Imperium: the Administratum, the Ecclesiarchy, the Adeptus Mechanicus, the Navy, the Astra Militarum, the Inquisition, the Custodes and the Assassinorum, with a Lord Commander at its head. It had become a place of delay and intrigue. Its record in the War of the Beast was poor. Its members quarrelled while the Orks overran the Segmentum Solar, it approved the disastrous Proletarian Crusade over the objections of several of them, and it showed little resistance when the Imperial Fists Chapter Master Koorland deposed the Lord Commander and took the office himself.[3]

    The Grand Master of Assassins, Drakan Vangorich, had sat on the council throughout the war and had come to hold most of his colleagues in contempt. He had taken part in Koorland's coup and supported the founding of the Deathwatch against the opposition of the Ecclesiarch and the Master of the Administratum. By the end of the war he had concluded that the council was incapable of reform.[1]

    The Lord Protector

    [edit]

    When Koorland was killed at the second battle of Ullanor, Maximus Thane, Chapter Master of the Fists Exemplar, succeeded him as Lord Commander of the Imperium and restored the Imperial Fists. After the third battle of Ullanor and the destruction of the Beast, Thane ordered the Fourth Founding, a Founding of as many Chapters as the gene-seed in the vaults of Terra could support, and decreed that the Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra but would sail as a crusading Chapter to help the shattered worlds recover.[1]

    Before he left, Thane named Vangorich Lord Protector of the Imperium, to be his voice and his hand, and to be heeded as if Thane were present. The High Lords, who had misgivings, were reminded that the threat of the Lord Commander's return would ensure the Lord Protector's good conduct. Thane had warned Vangorich against the Fabricator-General Kubik, whose ambitions he distrusted, and had assumed that the Grand Master of Assassins was a man who could be trusted with power because he had never sought it.[2]

    The coup

    [edit]

    Kubik's deception

    [edit]

    The immediate cause was a discovery. Thane had demanded that Ullanor be sterilised by Exterminatus once the Beast was dead. The Mechanicus had not complied. Using technology taken from the Ork Attack Moons, the Fabricator-General had teleported the Ork capital, Gorkogrod, aboard the immense Mechanicus ship Ark Majesty, intending to strip the city of everything useful. Vangorich learned of this soon after Thane's departure. It confirmed his view that the great institutions of the Imperium served only themselves, and that the war had changed nothing.[1]

    The murder of the High Twelve

    [edit]

    The Officio Assassinorum is the instrument by which the Emperor's justice is delivered to the powerful, and it had been founded, according to the tradition of its temples, to check the follies of empire. Vangorich, who understood this better than any, concluded that the time had come to use it. The killings were carried out across the Palace and the Senatorum Imperialis in one solar day. Eleven of the twelve High Lords were killed. The only survivor was the Inquisitorial Representative, Wienand, whom Vangorich spared out of a personal regard that Inquisition records are careful not to describe.[1]

    He installed new High Lords at once. They were men and women of his own choosing, and they sat as a council in name while the Lord Protector governed. It was understood, though never stated, that Thane's silence on hearing the news amounted to consent. Thane, then far from Terra, received reports of what had been done and did not move against his Lord Protector, preferring to see what the new order would achieve.[1][2]

    The Lord Protector's rule

    [edit]

    For nearly eighty standard years Vangorich ruled the Imperium. The reconstruction that followed the War of the Beast was the work of his council, as was the rebuilding of the Astra Militarum and the Navy, the refortification of Terra and the completion of the Fourth Founding, in which the Imperium raised scores of new Chapters, among them the Halo Brethren and the Sable Swords. By the standards of the age the Imperium was well governed, and its worlds recovered.[1]

    Vangorich's character did not survive his power. He began to see enemies on every side and responded with purges among the officials of the Adeptus Terra, then with mass executions, which grew in number each day. Reports of the Lord Protector's instability reached Thane on crusade. After a period of hesitation, he decided that the Imperial Fists should return to Terra and remove him.[2]

    Fury of the Space Marines

    [edit]

    The assault

    [edit]

    The Imperial Fists' crusade fleet arrived at Terra and put four hundred Space Marines on the surface. They were a company from each of two of the new Chapters, the Halo Brethren and the Sable Swords, and two hundred Imperial Fists of the First, Fourth and Fifth Companies. The commander of the strike force, Qublicus Amar, Chapter Master of the Sable Swords, was killed by a Vindicare Assassin's bullet soon after landing.[2]

    The Space Marines went on regardless. Hundreds of Assassins fell on them in the Palace, and in securing it from the Officio Assassinorum they lost half their number. In the course of the fighting Thane met Wienand, now elderly, who convinced him of her loyalty and gave him the intelligence he needed to find the Lord Protector: Vangorich had taken refuge in the Eversor Temple at the north pole of Terra.[2]

    The Eversor Temple

    [edit]
    Main article: Officio Assassinorum

    The surviving Space Marines moved at once on the Temple. Within it they were met by a hundred Eversor Assassins, the Officio's frenzied shock troops. The fighting was close and short. When it was over only Maximus Thane was alive, and he went on alone to the chamber where Vangorich waited. He gave the Lord Protector the Emperor's judgement with his bolt pistol.[2]

    Aftermath

    [edit]

    With the Lord Protector dead, the order he had imposed collapsed. For almost two solar decades Terra and its dependencies were riven by factions: the puppet High Lords and their retinues, the Assassins' temples, the Inquisition, the Mechanicus and the Administratum, each claiming the right to govern. The Imperial Fists, reduced to a handful, could not enforce peace, and Thane took no part in the quarrel.[3]

    The deadlock was broken by Agnathio, Chapter Master of the Ultramarines, who persuaded the Chapter Masters from all over the galaxy to join him. A combined armada of more than fifty Chapters came to Terra, the first such gathering since the end of the Horus Heresy, and gave the factions an ultimatum: cease fighting and form a unified government at once, or be purged as heretics. The strength of this force, equal to an entire Legion of old, was not in doubt, and the factions yielded. The Space Marines were present at a closed session of the Senatorum Imperialis, and when they left a new council of High Lords had been chosen.[3]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    The Beheading was the most serious injury to the government of the Imperium since the Heresy, and the High Lords have been conscious of it ever since. The new council was seated on different terms. The Captain-General of the Adeptus Custodes was given a place among the High Lords, and the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium was abolished. It was not revived until Guilliman's return.[3] The Inquisition formalised the Ordo Xenos and the Ordo Malleus in the period that followed, partly in response to the war that had preceded the crisis.[4]

    Assessments of Vangorich remain divided. The Imperial Fists and the Imperial Cult condemn him as a murderer and a tyrant. The Officio Assassinorum, whose temples were forced to rebuild their discipline, was thereafter placed under closer scrutiny. Its own tradition holds that Vangorich was right about the council and wrong about himself. The Ordo Sicarius, created four millennia later in the wake of the Age of Apostasy to police the Assassins, cites the Beheading as its chief precedent.[4]
