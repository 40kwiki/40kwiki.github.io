    Argel Tal, called the Crimson Lord, was a captain of the Word Bearers Legion and the first of the possessed Space Marines. On Lorgar's Pilgrimage into the Eye of Terror he and his company were joined to daemons, and they returned as the Gal Vorbak, the "Blessed Sons". He led them at the Drop Site Massacre (c. 006.M31) and his second formation, the Vakrah Jal, in the Shadow Crusade (007–008.M31). He was murdered by the First Chaplain Erebus at Nuceria c. 008.M31.[1][2]

    His name meant "the Last Angel" in the tongue of the southern Colchisian steppe. He is honoured with statues on the daemon worlds of the Word Bearers.[1]

    Early life

    [edit]

    Argel Tal was born in the village of Singh-Rukh on Colchis, the last child of his family. Erebus chose him for the Legion, meaning him for the Chaplaincy, but Argel Tal refused it and became a line officer. He rose to captain the 7th Assault Company of the Serrated Sun Chapter.[1]

    The Pilgrimage

    [edit]

    After the Emperor rebuked the Word Bearers at Monarchia on Khur c. 961.M30, Lorgar led his Legion on a search for gods worth worshipping. Aboard the cruiser Orfeo's Lament, guided by the daemon Ingethel the Ascended, the Serrated Sun entered the Eye of Terror c. 964.M30 and became among the first of the Imperium to see the Realm of Chaos. Their journey lasted seven months for them and moments for the Legion waiting outside. Each warrior came back bound to a daemon; in Argel Tal's case a being called Raum. The binding was less a conquest of the body than a merging of two souls. Argel Tal's account became part of the Book of Lorgar.[1][a]

    Lorgar made him commander of the Gal Vorbak, who were the first of the Legion to wear the crimson that the whole XVII Legion later took.[1]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]

    Argel Tal was the one Word Bearer trusted by the Custodians whom the Emperor had set to watch Lorgar. At Isstvan V he killed them when they learned of the Legion's treachery. He led the Gal Vorbak in the Drop Site Massacre against the Raven Guard, Salamanders and Iron Hands. Most of the original Gal Vorbak died on Calth in 007.M31, while Argel Tal was serving with the World Eaters.[2]

    With Lorgar's leave he formed the Vakrah Jal, the Chapter of Consecrated Iron, from Word Bearers left without companies, and trained them in the fighting pits of the Conqueror. There he became honour-brother to Khârn. He led the Vakrah Jal in the Shadow Crusade against Ultramar, including the attack on the war world Armatura.[3]

    Death

    [edit]

    At Nuceria c. 008.M31 Angron ordered his World Eaters and the Vakrah Jal to kill every living thing on his homeworld. The Ultramarines under Roboute Guilliman arrived during the slaughter. In the fighting Erebus stabbed Argel Tal in the back with his athame. Erebus later claimed that Argel Tal's friendship held Khârn back from his destiny in the service of Khorne, and that this would cost the traitors the war. A prophecy given on the Pilgrimage, that he would die "in the shadow of great wings", was fulfilled: he fell beneath the eagle on the Imperator Titan Corinthian.[3]

    On the Pilgrimage Lorgar had also seen a vision of Argel Tal, grown monstrous, dying at the hands of Sanguinius at the Palace's Eternity Gate. That future did not come to pass.[1]
