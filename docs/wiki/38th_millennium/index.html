    The 38th millennium (M38) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M38 to 999.M38. The Age of Redemption ended in its second century, in about 112.M38, with the return of the survivors of the Abyssal Crusade. The Waning followed, a long decline in which the Imperium lost ground on every frontier and the reach of the Astronomican seemed to shrink.[1]

    The record of the millennium is thinner than that of the crusading age. Its best-known events are the Ninth Black Crusade against Cancephalus and Antecanis, the Grim Harvest of 666.M38, when the ships of the Lost Crusade returned as space hulks, and new orders of the Adepta Sororitas. On the Eastern Fringe the T'au Empire fought its first long war against the Orks and allied with the Kroot.[1]
