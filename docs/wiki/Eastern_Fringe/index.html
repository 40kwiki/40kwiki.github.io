    Eastern Fringe
    Ordo Xenos chart of the Eastern Fringe in the late 41st millennium, with the approach routes of Hive Fleet Behemoth and Hive Fleet Kraken
    TypeFrontier region of the galaxy
    LocationGalactic east, beyond and bordering the Ultima Segmentum
    First Imperial presenceGreat Crusade, late M30
    Imperial realmsUltramar; scattered sectors such as Thramas and Aegis
    Xenos powersT'au Empire; Sautekh Dynasty; Orks of Charadon and Octarius; Tyranids
    Warp phenomenaHadex Anomaly (656.M40), eastern end of the Great Rift (999.M41)
    Notable warsThramas Crusade (007–009.M31); Damocles Gulf Crusade (742–745.M41); Battle of Macragge (745.M41); Achilus Crusade (from 777.M41); Plague Wars (c. 000–012.M42)

    The Eastern Fringe is the frontier region on the galactic eastern edge of the Imperium of Mankind, beyond and along the borders of the Ultima Segmentum. It lies so far from Terra that the light of the Astronomican is faint across much of it and absent from its outer reaches. Imperial settlement there is thin: apart from the realm of Ultramar and a few sectors along the main warp routes, its human worlds are isolated, and many have reverted to barbarism.[1][2]

    The Eastern Fringe has been the Imperium's busiest xenos frontier in the late 41st millennium. The T'au Empire arose there, and the Damocles Gulf Crusade of 742–745.M41 was fought to halt it. The Tyranids entered the galaxy across the Fringe, beginning with Hive Fleet Behemoth in 745.M41. Ork empires such as Charadon and Octarius, and Necron dynasties such as the Sautekh, hold large parts of it. In 999.M41 the Great Rift reached its eastern end in the Fringe, at the Hadex Anomaly.[1][3]

    Geography

    [edit]

    The Imperial chart, the Imperius Dominatus, treats the Eastern Fringe as the band of stars between the last fully administered sectors of the Ultima Segmentum and the Halo Stars at the galaxy's edge. It is not formally part of any segmentum. Its worlds that are in Imperial hands are administered from the Ultima Segmentum, and their fleets answer to the segmentum's fortress at Kar Duniash.[2][a]

    Its main regions, from galactic north to south, include the Ghoul Stars on its northern edge, the Jericho Reach and the Hadex Anomaly, the Ork empire of Charadon, the Damocles Gulf and the T'au septs beyond it, the realm of Ultramar, and the Thramas and Aegis sectors on the old Tithe Road. Much of the space between is wilderness that has never been charted.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    Great Crusade

    [edit]

    The Eastern Fringe was among the last regions reached by the Great Crusade in the late 30th millennium. Its principal Imperial power was then, as now, Ultramar, which Roboute Guilliman built up from Macragge into a realm of five hundred worlds by the end of the crusade. During the Horus Heresy, the Ruinstorm cut the Fringe off from Terra, and Guilliman briefly ruled the Imperium Secundus from Macragge. The Thramas Crusade of 007–009.M31, between the Dark Angels and the Night Lords, was fought in the Thramas Sector of the Fringe.[4]

    Discovery of the T'au

    [edit]

    The Adeptus Mechanicus Explorator ship Land's Vision catalogued the T'au home world in 789.M35, recording a primitive species. A warp storm then closed the region for thousands of years. When the Imperium came back, in the 41st millennium, the T'au had built an interstellar empire.[5] The Imperium's Damocles Gulf Crusade of 742–745.M41 failed to destroy it, and a truce was made when Hive Fleet Behemoth appeared.[1]

    The Tyrannic Wars

    [edit]

    The Tyranids reached the galaxy from the intergalactic void beyond the Eastern Fringe. Hive Fleet Behemoth destroyed Tyran Primus and fell on Ultramar in 745.M41, where it was destroyed at the Battle of Macragge. Hive Fleet Kraken struck in 992.M41, and was defeated at Ichar IV and by the craftworld Iyanden. Hive Fleet Leviathan came from beneath the galactic plane in 997.M41 and spread into the southern Ultima Segmentum. The Fringe has borne the heaviest losses of all the Tyrannic Wars.[5]

    Achilus Crusade

    [edit]

    The Jericho Reach, a region of the Fringe lost to the Imperium since the Age of Apostasy, was reopened when a stable warp gate linking it to the Calixis Sector was found. The Achilus Crusade passed through it in 777.M41 and has fought there ever since against the forces of Chaos, the T'au and the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Dagon. The Deathwatch has kept its watch fortress Erioch in the Reach for millennia.[6]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    When the Great Rift opened in 999.M41 its eastern end lay in the Fringe, at the Hadex Anomaly. Some worlds of the northern Fringe fell into the Imperium Nihilus. Ultramar itself remained in the Imperium Sanctus, but it was attacked by Mortarion and the Death Guard in the Plague Wars (c. 000–012.M42). The T'au launched new expansions into weakened Imperial space, and the Indomitus Crusade sent fleets to hold the Fringe against them.[3]

    Powers

    [edit]

    Imperial

    [edit]

    The Ultramarines and their realm of Ultramar are the Imperium's greatest strength in the Fringe. Other Chapters keep fortress-monasteries along the frontier, and the Astra Militarum maintains garrisons on the Damocles Gulf and the borders of the T'au Empire. The Imperial worlds outside Ultramar are mostly feudal and feral worlds, agri worlds and mining colonies.[1]

    Xenos

    [edit]

    The T'au Empire is the largest xenos power. The Farsight Enclaves, a breakaway state of the T'au, lie on its border with the Imperium. The Necrons of the Sautekh Dynasty, ruled by Imotekh the Stormlord from Mandragora, hold many tomb worlds. The Ork empires of Charadon and Octarius are the largest greenskin realms on the Imperial border. The Kroot, Vespid and other client species of the T'au are also present.[5]
