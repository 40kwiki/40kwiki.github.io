    The Timeline of the Great Crusade lists the dated events of the Great Crusade, the war of reconquest by which the Emperor of Mankind and the Space Marine Legions reunited the scattered worlds of humanity after the Age of Strife. It begins with the Emperor's preparations on Terra in the 30th millennium, follows the crusade from its departure from the Sol System in 798.M30 to the Triumph of Ullanor in 000.M31, and ends with the Isstvan III atrocity of 005.M31, which opened the Horus Heresy.[1][2]

    The crusade lasted a little over two centuries. Its records were kept by the fleets themselves, by the Remembrancer Order in its last years, and by the Administratum's Departmento Cartographicae on Terra. They often disagree. Warp transit distorted the logs of every Expeditionary Fleet, and many entries were sealed after the Heresy or lost with the Legions that kept them. The dates below follow the Administratum's reconciled chronology. Dates that rest only on the order of events are marked "c." and noted.[1][a]

    Late M29 – 712.M30The Unification Wars. The Thunder Warriors break the techno-barbarian kingdoms of Terra.TerraSee Unification Wars.
    c. 550.M30The Emperor begins the Space Marine project, creating the gene-template later used for the Legions.[a]TerraMechanicum records give no earlier date.
    669.M30Battle of Mount Ararat. The last great army of Terra's warlords is destroyed.Terra (Anatolia)The Thunder Warriors take heavy losses.
    c. 700.M30The First Founding. The first Legiones Astartes are raised from gene-seed taken from the unborn primarchs.[a]TerraThe Legions are numbered but not yet named.
    703.M30First Pacification of Luna. The early Legions take the Selenar gene-cults' moon.Sol SystemFirst operation of the Legiones Astartes.
    712.M30The Emperor declares the Unification Wars ended. The surviving Thunder Warriors are destroyed.TerraDate of the formal declaration.
    739.M30The Treaty of Olympus binds Terra to the Mechanicum of Mars. Mars builds the fleets and engines of the crusade.MarsAlso called the Treaty of Mars.
    c. 790.M30The Astronomican is lit on Terra, making long Warp journeys possible across the galaxy.[a]TerraSome records put the lighting nearer 798.M30.
    c. 792.M30The twenty primarchs are taken from the Emperor's laboratory by the powers of the Warp and scattered across the galaxy.TerraThe scattering is sometimes put a few years later.
    c. 798.M30Campaigns in the outer Sol System, among them the Unheard War on the stations of Uranus.Sol SystemThe last fighting before the departure.
    798.M30The Great Crusade departs Sol. The Emperor leads the fleet in person aboard the Bucephalus.Sol SystemThe traditional first year of the crusade.

    843.M30Sanguinius is found on Baal and takes command of the IX Legion, the Blood Angels.Segmentum UltimaHe has foreseen the Emperor's coming.
    846.M30Lion El'Jonson is found on Caliban and takes command of the I Legion, the Dark Angels.Segmentum ObscurusThe knightly orders of Caliban join the Legion.
    848.M30Suppression of Sarosh.Segmentum Pacificus
    849.M30Perturabo is found on Olympia and takes command of the IV Legion, the Iron Warriors. The Fall of the Black Judges follows.Segmentum TempestusHe orders the decimation of his Legion.

    c. 999–000.M31The Ullanor Crusade. The Emperor, Horus and several Legions destroy the Ork empire of Urlakk Urg.Segmentum UltimaThe largest campaign of the crusade.
    000.M31The Triumph of Ullanor. Horus is named Warmaster. The Emperor returns to Terra.UllanorThe Council of Terra takes up the civil government.
    000.M31The Remembrancer Order is chartered to record the crusade.TerraOlder traditions put it earlier.

    001.M31The Council of Nikaea. The Emperor forbids sorcery and disbands the Librarius of every Legion.NikaeaThe Sisters of Silence and Custodes enforce the decree.
    c. 001.M31The Emperor's Children destroy the Laer; the Luna Wolves of the 63rd Expedition take world 63-19.VariousRecorded by the remembrancers.
    c. 001.M31Compliance of 140-20 ("Murder"), where the crusade first meets the Interex.Segmentum Obscurus

    003.M31The Luna Wolves are renamed the Sons of Horus.UnrecordedAt Horus's own request.
    003.M31The First War of Xenobia. The Sons of Horus fight the Interex, and the Kinebrach Anathame is taken.Segmentum ObscurusContact ends in war.

    004.M31Magnus the Red warns the Emperor by sorcery and breaks open the Imperial Webway.TerraThe Webway War begins.
