    The ''Talon of Horus'', first known as the ''Warmaster's Talon'', is a large lightning claw fitted with an early custom-built combi-bolter. It is carried by Abaddon the Despoiler, master of the Black Legion and Warmaster of Chaos, who took it from the body of Horus at the end of the Horus Heresy. Abaddon wears it on his right hand as a chief emblem of his authority over the forces of Chaos.[1]

    To the Imperium the ''Talon'' is a symbol of the Traitors' cause. The sight of it unnerves Imperial troops, and it provokes an unyielding hatred in the Blood Angels, whose primarch it killed.[1]

    Description

    [edit]

    The weapon is a heavy lightning claw with a thumb blade. A combi-bolter runs from the wrist to the knuckles of the gauntlet. It was designed for the first Terminator armour project as a flexible weapon, and is in effect two bolters slaved to fire together. Two ammunition belts feed it from the right side, and the ejector casing is formed as the faces of howling daemons. It is bulky and crude by later standards, but it saw wide use in the years of the Heresy.[1]

    Imperial analysts assume that Abaddon's ranged attacks combine the combi-bolter with the daemon sword ''Drach'nyen'', and that in close combat the claw is at least equal to a standard lightning claw.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Forging

    [edit]

    The ''Warmaster's Talon'' was made by Master Adept Urtzi Malevolus, a follower of the traitor Fabricator-General Kelbor-Hal and of the Dark Mechanicum that sided with Horus. In the same forge Malevolus built a unique suit of Artificer Terminator armour, one of the earliest of its kind, and the claw was made to match it. It soon became Horus's favoured weapon and a symbol of his office as Warmaster of the Great Crusade.[1][2] Some apocryphal accounts say instead that it is a relic of the Age of Technology found on the world of Clthon. No record settles the question.[1]

    The Siege of Terra

    [edit]

    During the Siege of Terra, on the flagship ''Vengeful Spirit'', Horus used the claw to kill his brother Sanguinius and to mortally wound the Emperor. Ezekyle Abaddon, First Captain of the Sons of Horus, and his Justaerin Terminators fought through the Imperial Fists to the command deck but arrived too late. He took up the body of his gene-father and detached the claw. He is recorded as swearing to use it to strangle the Emperor himself, but he abandoned the assault on Terra and led the Traitors to the Eye of Terror.[1][3]

    The Long War

    [edit]

    In the ten thousand years since, Abaddon has led thirteen Black Crusades, and in each of them the ''Talon'' has been on his right hand. The 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41 was the last of these. In the Despoiler's own words, recorded by a dying Sigismund, the claw has killed two primarchs.[1][4]
