    Land Speeder
    TypeLight anti-gravity vehicle
    OriginStandard Template Construct recovered on Mars by Arkhan Land
    In serviceFrom the early 31st millennium (estimated) to the present
    Used byAdeptus Astartes, and the Forge World of Ryza and Adepta with rich resources
    RoleReconnaissance, scouting, resupply and fast attack

    The Land Speeder is a light anti-gravity vehicle. It is the primary reconnaissance, scouting, resupply and fast attack vehicle of the Adeptus Astartes. Its design is based on a Standard Template Construct (STC) recovered early in the 31st millennium (estimated) by the Techno-Archaeologist Arkhan Land during his expedition into the Librarius Omnis on Mars.[1]

    Land Speeders were produced widely after their rediscovery. Both the Space Marine Legions and the Imperial Army used them. Over the millennia, the plasma and anti-gravity technologies needed to build them became rare. The Imperial Army gradually stopped using them. Only the richest Imperial worlds, such as the Forge World of Ryza, and the Adeptus Astartes can now afford to build and use them.

    Design and handling

    [edit]

    Land Speeders use anti-gravity repulsion plates to skim above the ground at high speed. They can make turns that conventional vehicles could not manage. Their grav-drives do not work at high altitude, so they are usually dropped from orbit by a Thunderhawk or a similar gunship, and they make a controlled descent to the ground. They are not aircraft and cannot fly.

    Land Speeders are often deployed in squadrons of three. They are fast-response units and add firepower to the Space Marines who are already on the ground. Chapters such as the Dark Angels fielded them in large numbers, and the Dark Angels' Ravenwing are noted for their Land Speeder Tornado variant.[1]

    Use by the Heresy and after

    [edit]

    The design was rediscovered at the time of the Horus Heresy. Because of the cost and difficulty of maintaining them, the Traitor Legions had none. Renegade Space Marines who turned to Chaos from Chapters created after the Heresy can still use them.[2]
