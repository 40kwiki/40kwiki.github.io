    The Flame Falcons were a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes raised in the Twenty-first Founding, the "Cursed Founding". They are remembered for one thing: during battle their warriors burst into flame without harm, and fought on with fierce savagery. They took this as a gift of the Emperor. The Inquisition took it as possession, and the Grey Knights destroyed the Chapter on its homeworld Lethe.[1]

    Imperial records say little else. The Chapter was declared Excommunicate Traitoris, and its history was struck out.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    The Cursed Founding

    [edit]

    The Twenty-first Founding was the largest since the Second Founding. It came shortly before the Age of Apostasy, and the Adeptus Mechanicus used it to perfect the gene-seed by removing flaws and, under a project named Homo Sapiens Novus, to make warriors more like the primarchs. The Genetors were far less skilled than the Emperor, and the gene-seed they made was seriously flawed. The Founding ended when one of its Chapters, already corrupted, sent a signal through the Warp that drew the notice of Fabius Bile. The Chapters of the Founding suffered such persistent misfortune that it was called the Cursed Founding. Most were lost to treachery, mutation or the guns of the Inquisition.[2]

    The Emperor's blessing, a Chapter's curse

    [edit]

    The Flame Falcons began well. Their combat trials were a success, and they were put on active service on the Southern Rim from Lethe. Their first wars against Orks and Aeldari corsairs showed them hardy and courageous. At Raffenburg's World, in their largest battle to that date, the First Company was seen to burst into flame at the height of the fighting against rebel forces. Fearing witchcraft, the Chapter commander reinforced the line and sent for an Inquisitor who accompanied the Astra Militarum there. When the reinforcing Marines reported that the burning company was alive and fighting more savagely, the commander declared it a miracle.[1]

    The Inquisitor judged otherwise. He waited and watched the rest of the Chapter undergo the same change, and let the campaign end. The Flame Falcons returned to Lethe to celebrate. Soon after, the Grey Knights, acting for the Ordo Malleus, assaulted Lethe and destroyed the Chapter, believing it daemon-possessed. A small number are thought to have escaped, but their fate is not recorded.[1][a]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Chapter's flame is thought to come from an unforeseen psychic mutation in its gene-seed, caused by the Mechanicus' alterations to the gene-seed of the Cursed Founding. The Inquisition remains uncompromising toward any such mutants it meets.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Gherak: a Flame Falcon who survived the destruction as a Deathwatch Black Shield under Inquisitor Lord Otto Dagover.
