    The 42nd millennium, abbreviated M42, is the millennium of the Imperial Dating System that began after 999.M41 and is now in progress. By the Terran reckoning followed in this encyclopedia it is the present age. Its history so far is the history of the Era Indomitus, the period that opened with the Fall of Cadia, the birth of the Great Rift and the return of Roboute Guilliman at the end of the 41st millennium.[1][2]

    The millennium began in the darkness of the Noctis Aeterna, when the Astronomican could not be seen across most of the galaxy. Guilliman's Indomitus Crusade reconnected much of the southern half of the Imperium, the Imperium Sanctus, in a first phase that ended at the Battle of Raukos in about 012.M42, and the Primaris Space Marines of the Ultima Founding reinforced almost every Chapter. North of the Rift, the Imperium Nihilus has been governed from Baal by Commander Dante as Regent.[1]

    The first decades of the millennium also saw the return of a second loyal primarch, Lion El'Jonson; the reappearance of the daemon primarchs Mortarion, Angron, Fulgrim and Perturabo; the Psychic Awakening; the Arks of Omen crisis caused by Vashtorr the Arkifane; the Fourth Tyrannic War against Hive Fleet Leviathan; and the war for the Pariah Nexus. The dates of most of these events are uncertain, because time has passed unevenly across the galaxy since the Rift opened.[1][3]

    Dating

    [edit]

    The 42nd millennium is the first in Imperial history in which no single calendar is in universal use. Warp distortion after 999.M41 left worlds and fleets years apart from Terran time, and the Chronostrife revealed that the Imperium's own calendars no longer agreed. By decree of the High Lords of Terra, issued after the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade, each region keeps its own chronology anchored on the arrival of the Great Rift, while Terra and the Administratum continue the old count. Fleets of the crusade use the Tempus Indomitus, keeping time by their flagships' chronometers.[2][1]

    As a result, most events of the millennium are known only to the nearest decade or as "early M42". The dates below follow the records of the Lord Commander, revised in 003.M42 and later, and the reconciled tables of the Logos Historica Verita.[2]

    Overview

    [edit]

    The Indomitus Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Indomitus Crusade

    The Indomitus Crusade was proclaimed on Terra in 999.M41 and launched across the Imperium Sanctus in its first years. Its fleets broke the sieges of thousands of worlds, and its first phase ended in about 012.M42 when the crusade defeated a traitor host at the Pit of Raukos. Guilliman then returned to defend Ultramar, where Mortarion had begun the Plague Wars (c. 012.M42), and drove the Death Lord from Iax.[1]

    The Rift and the Gauntlet

    [edit]

    Crossings of the Great Rift are few. The most important, the Nachmund Gauntlet, was the scene of the War of Nightmares on Vigilus in the first years of the millennium, in which Marneus Calgar was gravely wounded by Abaddon the Despoiler, and of the later Nachmund Rift War. Vigilus is also the origin of the best-known local Rift calendar, the Vigilus Template.[4]

    Primarchs returned

    [edit]

    Lion El'Jonson, first primarch of the Dark Angels, woke again after ten thousand years of absence and has campaigned through the worlds of the Imperium Nihilus, gathering the Dark Angels and their successors to him. The daemon primarchs of four traitor Legions have reappeared: Angron in the Imperium Nihilus, Fulgrim at the head of a crusade of his own, and Perturabo at the Cadian Gate, where he opened the Infinite Citadel.[1]

    New and renewed threats

    [edit]

    The Psychic Awakening saw psychic births multiply across the galaxy. The daemon engineer Vashtorr the Arkifane drove the Arks of Omen crisis, assembling the pieces of a key in the battle of Wyrmwood. Hive Fleet Leviathan began the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Segmentum Pacificus, overrunning Oghram and besieging Sanctum. The Necrons expanded the Pariah Nexus in the Ultima Segmentum, and Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka returned for the Fourth War for Armageddon.[3][1]
