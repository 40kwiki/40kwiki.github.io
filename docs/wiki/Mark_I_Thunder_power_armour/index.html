    Mark I Thunder power armour, also called Thunder armour, was the first pattern of power armour made for the Emperor of Mankind's gene-warriors. It was worn by the Thunder Warriors in the Unification Wars on Terra in the 30th millennium, and by the first Space Marines before the forges of Mars made sealed suits for the Great Crusade. At the time it was simply called power armour. The name Mark I, and the name Thunder armour after the lightning device on its breastplate, were given later by the Mechanicum when it numbered the patterns.[1]

    Origins

    [edit]

    The armour came from the powered combat suits of the techno-barbarian warlords who ruled Terra at the end of the Age of Strife. The Emperor's armourers took that form and improved it for the Thunder Warriors, whose strength could make full use of it. No single design existed. Each suit was made locally, and suits differed widely, often by the taste of the warrior who wore it.[1]

    Design

    [edit]

    The heart of the suit was a massive powered torso that enclosed the chest and arms. Coiled energy cables under the plates carried power from a pack on the back to the arms and greatly increased the wearer's strength. This, more than anything, made the Thunder Warriors so deadly in close combat.[1]

    The legs were not powered. They were covered by tough padded breeches, sometimes with simple steel plates. The best-equipped warriors wore armoured greaves and boots. The suit was not sealed against vacuum or poison, and it was far noisier than later marks, which made stealth almost impossible. The helm was often open or fitted with goggles.[1]

    The breastplate bore the lightning and thunderbolt device of the Raptor Imperialis, the Emperor's banner in the Unification. The plates were painted in the colours of each Thunder legion.[2]

    Arms

    [edit]

    Before the suit was common, the first Thunder Warriors wore metal armour strapped on with leather and carried lasrifles. In armour they carried an early, heavy form of the bolter, power blades and thunder hammers, and the heavier guns of the field armies.[2]

    Service

    [edit]

    The armour served through the whole of the Unification Wars, to the final victory at Mount Ararat and the raising of the Banner of Lightning (c. 798.M30). The first Space Marine Legions were at first armed and armoured as the Thunder Warriors had been. When the Treaty of Olympus joined Mars to the Imperium, the Mechanicum began work on a sealed suit with full life support, the Mark II Crusade, which replaced Thunder armour early in the Great Crusade.[1]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    Few suits survive. Those that do are kept as relics in the armouries of Terra and Mars as the first power armour ever made. The lightning bolt of its breastplate lives on in the heraldry of several Chapters and of the Adeptus Custodes, and the Raptor Imperialis it bore is the forebear of the Aquila.[2]

    Timeline

    [edit]
    • c. 700.M30: the first suits are made for the Thunder Warriors in the Unification Wars.[b]
    • c. 739.M30: the Treaty of Olympus binds Mars to the Emperor; work on a sealed suit begins.
    • c. 798.M30: the Unification of Terra ends at Mount Ararat; the Thunder Warriors are destroyed.
    • c. 800.M30: Mark II Crusade armour replaces Thunder armour in the Legions.[c]
