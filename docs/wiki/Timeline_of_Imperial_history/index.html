    This is a timeline of Imperial history, the history of the Imperium of Mankind from the last wars of the Unification of Terra in the 30th millennium to the Era Indomitus of the early 42nd millennium. It lists the principal wars, reforms, schisms and disasters recorded by the Administratum and the other Adepta, arranged by millennium. Each section opens with a short summary of the age and is followed by a dated list of events.

    Imperial historians divide the ten thousand years after the Horus Heresy into a series of named ages: the Time of Rebirth, The Forging, the Nova Terra Interregnum, the Age of Apostasy, the Age of Redemption, The Waning and the Time of Ending. The Era Indomitus, which began with the opening of the Great Rift at the end of the 41st millennium, is the present age.[1]

    Dates are given in the Imperial Dating System, in which 005.M31 means the fifth year of the 31st millennium. The record is uneven. Whole centuries of the 33rd and 34th millennia survive only as entries in the Administratum's register marked "no further data", and many events have been sealed by the Inquisition or the Adeptus Mechanicus. Where the Adepta disagree, the date in the Administratum's reconciled chronology is given and the others are noted. Since the return of Roboute Guilliman the whole calendar has been under revision, for the temporal disruption of the Great Rift and the discovery of long-standing errors in the old reckoning mean that dates of the early 42nd millennium are approximate.[2]

    30th millennium

    [edit]

    The 30th millennium saw the end of the Age of Strife on Terra. The Emperor of Mankind united the planet with his Thunder Warriors, made an alliance with the Mechanicum of Mars, created the twenty Space Marine Legions and launched the Great Crusade to reunite the scattered worlds of humanity. By the close of the millennium the Crusade had reconquered much of the galaxy and the Emperor had recovered most of his lost sons, the primarchs.[3]

    DateEvent
    Late M29 – c. 712.M30The Unification Wars. The Emperor's armies conquer the techno-barbarian kingdoms of Terra; the final battle at Mount Ararat is dated 669.M30 in some records.
    c. M30The Thunder Warriors win the last battles of Unification and are then destroyed.
    Late M30The Treaty of Olympus binds Terra and the Mechanicum of Mars. The Astronomican is lit.
    Late M30The First Founding of the twenty Space Marine Legions.
    c. 798.M30The Great Crusade begins.
    c. 839–890.M30The Rangdan Xenocides, the costliest wars of the Crusade before the Heresy.
    c. 941.M30Davin brought to compliance.
    c. 999.M30 – 000.M31The Ullanor Crusade destroys the Ork empire of Urlakk Urg.

    31st millennium

    [edit]

    The 31st millennium opened with the Triumph of Ullanor and the Emperor's withdrawal to Terra. Within five years the Warmaster Horus had rebelled, and the Horus Heresy divided the Legions and ended at the Siege of Terra, where the Emperor was mortally wounded and placed on the Golden Throne. The rest of the millennium was spent in the Great Scouring, the reorganisation of the Imperium under the Codex Astartes and the first years of the Time of Rebirth.[4]

    DateEvent
    000.M31The Triumph of Ullanor: Horus named Warmaster.
    001.M31The Council of Nikaea forbids the use of sorcery.
    c. 001–003.M31Horus wounded on the moon of Davin.
    003.M31Destruction of the Interex at Xenobia.
    005.M31The Isstvan III atrocity opens the Horus Heresy.
    c. 005.M31The Burning of Prospero.
    c. 006.M31The Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V.
    007.M31The Battle of Calth; the Signus Campaign; the Battle of Phall; the Shadow Crusade against Ultramar begins.
    c. 012.M31The Battle of Beta-Garmon.
    013–014.M31The Siege of Terra. Horus slain; the Emperor placed on the Golden Throne.
    014–021.M31The Great Scouring drives the Traitor Legions into the Eye of Terror.
    c. 019.M31The Codex Astartes is completed.
    c. 021.M31The Second Founding breaks the Legions into Chapters. The Imperial Fists resist and are rescued from the Iron Cage.
    M31The Legion Wars among the Traitor Legions in the Eye of Terror; the Sons of Horus destroyed at Canticle City.
    M31The Third Founding is approved on the petition of Rogal Dorn.
    121.M31Roboute Guilliman mortally wounded by Fulgrim at Thessala and placed in stasis on Macragge.
    781.M31The First Black Crusade and the First Battle of Cadia. Rogal Dorn reported killed.

    32nd millennium

    [edit]

    The 32nd millennium belongs partly to the Time of Rebirth and partly to The Forging. The cults of the God-Emperor were united into the Adeptus Ministorum, which became the state church. The greatest war of the age was the War of the Beast, after which the High Lords were murdered in the Beheading and the government of the Imperium was reshaped.[1]

    DateEvent
    M32The Adeptus Ministorum recognised as the state church; Ecclesiarch Veneris II given a seat among the High Lords of Terra.
    544–546.M32The War of the Beast. The Ordo Xenos and Ordo Malleus of the Inquisition formed; the Deathwatch raised; the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium abolished.
    546.M32The Beheading: the High Lords murdered on the orders of the Grand Master of Assassins.
    After 546.M32The Fourth Founding raises as many Chapters as the gene-vaults of Terra allow.
    597.M32The Second Black Crusade strikes the Belis Corona Sector.
    646.M32Chapter Master Agnathio of the Ultramarines restores the council of twelve High Lords.
    888.M32The Astropath Wars.
    909.M32The Third Black Crusade; the tomb of Saint Gerstahl desecrated.
    910.M32The Firestorm.

    33rd millennium

    [edit]

    The 33rd millennium was the height of The Forging, the age the Ecclesiarchy calls the golden age of the Imperium. The Imperial Cult spread across the galaxy, the Ministorum fought its first Wars of Faith, and the Imperium pushed its borders beyond the frontiers of the Great Crusade.[1]

    DateEvent
    077.M33The War of the Confessor, first of the Ministorum's Wars of Faith. The Temple of the Saviour Emperor suppresses the Confederation of Light.
    265–285.M33Last voyage of Admiral Usurs into the intergalactic void.
    313.M33The Siege of Eternity's Gate.
    615.M33The Blade of Infinity emerges from the Warp, the harbinger of Chaos incursions.
    780–860.M33The War of the False Primarch.
    831.M33The Year of the Ghosts in the Segmentum Solar.

    34th millennium

    [edit]

    The Forging continued through the 34th millennium. The Imperium's astropathic network reached its widest extent and its commerce recovered with the rediscovery of an STC database in the Cana System, but the millennium also saw the Pale Wasting and the Howling, two of the worst disasters of the age.[1]

    DateEvent
    001.M34The Fourth Black Crusade: the citadel of El'Phanor stormed.
    Early M34The Pale Wasting from the Ghoul Stars.
    401.M34The Howling: the death scream of the Cacodominus kills a billion astropaths.
    666.M34The first Warp Stars appear after a storm from the Eye of Terror.
    934.M34The warnings of Ulthwé.

    35th millennium

    [edit]

    In the 35th millennium the Imperium was divided by the Nova Terra Interregnum, in which the Ur-Council of Nova Terra ruled the Segmentum Pacificus apart from Terra, while the Moirae Schism tore the Adeptus Mechanicus apart. The Ecclesiarchy reunited the Imperium in the Cataclysm of Souls and emerged as its most powerful institution.[1]

    DateEvent
    Early M35The Ur-Council of Nova Terra repudiates the High Lords; the Nova Terra Interregnum begins.
    M35The Moirae Schism in the Adeptus Mechanicus; the forge world of Moirae destroyed.
    257.M35The Dark Angels raid Nova Terra.
    495.M35The Hrakon Campaign.
    975.M35The Cataclysm of Souls ends the Interregnum.
    980.M35The Hrud Rising.
    991.M35The Cursed Founding, the twenty-first.

    36th millennium

    [edit]

    The 36th millennium was the Age of Apostasy, the second great civil war in the Imperium's history. The Master of the Administratum, Goge Vandire, seized the office of Ecclesiarch and ruled by terror in the Reign of Blood, until he was overthrown in the movement led by Sebastian Thor. The Plague of Unbelief of the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis followed.[5]

    DateEvent
    104.M36The War of Recovery in the Mortuam Chain begins.
    Early M36The Reign of Blood of Goge Vandire.
    c. 288.M36Vandire killed by Alicia Dominica; Sebastian Thor becomes Ecclesiarch; the Decree Passive; the Adepta Sororitas and the Ordo Hereticus founded.[a]
    c. 310.M36The Plague of Unbelief; the siege of Fenris; the martyrdom of Confessor Dolan Chirosius.
    350.M36Death of Sebastian Thor.
    723.M36The Fifth Black Crusade, the "Tide of Blood", in the Elysia Sector.
    754.M36The Web of Intrigue disaster.
    901.M36The Sixth Black Crusade.
    989.M36The Hunger of the Throne: the tithe of psykers to the Golden Throne increased.

    37th millennium

    [edit]

    The 37th millennium began the Age of Redemption, in which a newly zealous Imperium launched crusade after crusade to atone for the Apostasy. So many fleets and Chapters were drawn into distant wars that worlds at home were left defenceless.[1]

    DateEvent
    010.M37The Wrath of the Chaos Sun in the Maxil Beta System.
    020.M37The Great Cull purges the Adeptus Terra.
    M37The Sons of Medusa ratified as a Chapter.
    321.M37The Abyssal Crusade: thirty Chapters sent into the Eye of Terror.
    555.M37The Occlusiad War against the Apostles of the Blind King.
    754.M37The Lost Crusade of the young.
    811.M37The Seventh Black Crusade, the Ghost War.
    956.M37The Heavenfall Massacres.
    999.M37The Eighth Black Crusade and the Skullgather.

    38th millennium

    [edit]

    The Age of Redemption ended in the 38th millennium and The Waning began, a long decline in which the Imperium lost ground on every frontier.[1]

    DateEvent
    112.M38Return of the Abyssal Crusade; the cult of Saint Basilius destroyed.
    537.M38The Ninth Black Crusade against Cancephalus and Antecanis.
    666.M38The Grim Harvest: the lost pilgrims of the Lost Crusade return as space hulks.

    39th millennium

    [edit]
    DateEvent
    001.M39The Tenth Black Crusade, with Perturabo, in the Helica Sector.
    103.M39The Mausolean Cataclysm.
    131.M39The Redemption Crusades push the borders of the Imperium outward in every segmentum.
    301.M39The Eleventh Black Crusade, the Doom of Relorria.

    40th millennium

    [edit]

    The record of the 40th millennium is scattered, and much of it comes from the frontier sectors of Calixis and the Jericho Reach.[1]

    DateEvent
    433.M40Krieg declares independence; the Atonement of Krieg begins.
    656.M40The Hadex Anomaly consumes the hive world of Verronus.
    Late M40The Bastion Founding, the twenty-fifth.

    41st millennium

    [edit]

    The 41st millennium saw the last centuries of The Waning and, from about 744.M41, the Time of Ending. The Imperium faced the Tyranids, the reawakening Necrons, the expansion of the T'au Empire and the greatest Ork invasions since the War of the Beast. It ended with the Thirteenth Black Crusade and the fall of Cadia.[1]

    DateEvent
    139–160.M41The Gothic War, the Twelfth Black Crusade.
    392–399.M41The Macharian Crusade.
    500.M41The Tears of the Emperor.
    742–745.M41The Damocles Gulf Crusade against the T'au Empire.
    745.M41Hive Fleet Behemoth and the Battle of Macragge.
    813–830.M41The Siege of Vraks.
    901–913.M41The Badab War.
    941.M41The Second War for Armageddon.
    993–997.M41The Second and Third Tyrannic Wars.
    998.M41The Third War for Armageddon.
    999.M41The Thirteenth Black Crusade and the Fall of Cadia; the Great Rift opens; Roboute Guilliman returns.

    42nd millennium

    [edit]

    The Era Indomitus began with the opening of the Great Rift, which cut the Imperium in two, and with the return of Roboute Guilliman, who took up the office of Lord Commander again and launched the Indomitus Crusade with the Primaris Space Marines of the Ultima Founding. The Crusade lasted more than a century by the old reckoning and was followed by new wars against the Chaos Legions, the Tyranids and the Necrons. Because the Imperial calendar was disrupted by the Rift, the dates below follow the reconstructed reckoning of the Lord Commander's office.[2]

    DateEvent
    c. 999.M41The Noctis Aeterna. The Battle of Lion's Gate on Terra; the Ultima Founding revealed.
    From c. 999.M41The Indomitus Crusade. The Devastation of Baal is lifted; the Imperium Nihilus is cut off.
    Early M42The War of Beasts on Vigilus.
    c. 012.M42The Plague Wars in Ultramar; Mortarion driven from Iax.
    Early M42The Battle of Raukos ends the Indomitus Crusade. The Lion returns.
    Early M42The Arks of Omen; the Pariah Nexus; the Fourth Tyrannic War; the Fourth War for Armageddon.
