    33rd millennium
    000.M33 – 999.M33
    AgeThe Forging
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra, restored in 646.M32
    Key eventsWar of the Confessor (077.M33); Siege of Eternity's Gate (313.M33); War of the False Primarch (780–860.M33)
    Preceded by32nd millennium
    Followed by34th millennium

    The 33rd millennium (M33) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M33 to 999.M33. It was the high point of The Forging, the age that the Adeptus Ministorum calls the golden age of the Imperium. The Imperial Cult spread to almost every human world, the Ministorum fought its first Wars of Faith against rival creeds, and new Foundings of the Adeptus Astartes raised Chapters such as the Howling Griffons and the Sons of Guilliman.[1]

    The age was not peaceful. The Dark Angels fought a long series of campaigns against xenos and in pursuit of the Fallen, and the War of the False Primarch threw the Segmentum Pacificus into anarchy for eighty years. By the close of the millennium the Administratum had won back much of the influence it had lost to the Ecclesiarchy, at a cost to the efficiency of Imperial government.[2]

    Chronology

    [edit]
    DateEventNotes
    Early to mid-M33The political power of the Adeptus Ministorum grows while that of the Administratum declines.
    M33The High Lords pass over the gene-seed of the Dark Angels in every Founding of the age.The reason is not recorded.
    M33The Apocrypha of Davio attempts to list every Chapter of the Second Founding.
    077.M33The War of the Confessor, first of the Ministorum's Wars of Faith: the Temple of the Saviour Emperor suppresses the Confederation of Light.Some tables place the first War of Faith late in M33.
    112.M33The Feral War on Aggaros, in which Adeptus Mechanicus forces are thrown back by the shamans of a feral world.
    118–418.M33The Sorrowful Pursuit: the Black Wings hunt the killers of their Chapter Master Khardin Gol for three centuries.Begins with the Hour of Shadow on Lorin Alpha.
    c. 220.M33The Howling Griffons founded from Ultramarines gene-stock.
    c. 250.M33The Sons of Guilliman founded from Ultramarines gene-stock.[a]The Founding is unnamed.
    265–285.M33Last voyage of Admiral Usurs, sent by the High Lords into the void beyond the galactic rim.He never returns.
    313.M33The Siege of Eternity's Gate.
    340.M33The "Mother Gullet" affair: a Callidus Assassin takes the heir of Governor Thygmus van Spracht.See Officio Assassinorum.
    344–346.M33The Blackstar Liberation: more than half the Blood Angels free the Blackstar System after the Warp storm Korinthus abates.
    454.M33The Rogue Trader Apollyon Maestrich-Nova founds a mining fief on a moon of fire-diamonds.See Rogue Trader.
    560.M33The Second Mortis Gate Campaign; all three Sacred Standards of the Dark Angels fly together.
    615.M33The pre-Heresy cruiser Blade of Infinity emerges from the Warp, apparently twenty thousand years after it left.Held to herald Chaos incursions.
    732–822.M33The Red Stars Campaign clears the Kulgotha System of xenos.Dark Angels at full strength.
    780–860.M33The War of the False Primarch throws the Segmentum Pacificus into anarchy.Much of the record is purged.
    822.M33Battle of Vermilac Prime: the Fallen Angel Cypher keeps the Lion Helm and the Sword of Secrets from the Orks after the death of Supreme Grand Master Alloken.
    831.M33The Year of the Ghosts in the Segmentum Solar.
    860.M33The Pentarchy of Blood, closing episode of the War of the False Primarch.
    870.M33The Hellion War: the Hellion Sector secedes and attacks its neighbours.
    882.M33First True War of Veonid: a Khornate daemon legion falls on the weapons-testing world of Veonid.
    953.M33Siege of Lakadamon: the Sons of Guilliman hold a hive world against the Warboss Vorhgad Bloodfang.
    997.M33The Angels of Redemption find that Cypher, as the "Saviour of Praxis", has been shaping their recruits.
    Late M33The Administratum regains much of its lost influence; Imperial governance becomes slower and more tangled.
