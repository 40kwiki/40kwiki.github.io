    The Plague Wars were the invasion of the Realm of Ultramar by the daemonic and Heretic Astartes forces of Nurgle, the Plague God, in the early years of the Era Indomitus. They were led by the Daemon Primarch Mortarion, with his Death Guard and the Plague Legions of the Great Unclean One Ku'Gath. Their object was to add the richest and best-ordered realm of the southern Imperium to the growing domain of Nurgle in the material universe. They were fought by the Ultramarines, the Ultramar Auxilia and the Lord Commander of the Imperium, Roboute Guilliman, who had been forced to leave his own realm to the care of his officers while he fought in the Imperium Sanctus.[1][2]

    The war was fought in three stages: a long, creeping war of attrition called the War of Flies; a bold offensive in which three spearheads attacked along a hundred fronts; and a counter-offensive led by Guilliman himself, which ended in a duel of primarchs on the hospital world of Iax. It was not an Imperial triumph. Ultramar was held but not freed, and the Scourge Stars, from which the invasion came, are still in the hands of Nurgle.[1]

    Background

    [edit]

    The Scourge Stars

    [edit]

    When the Great Rift opened and the Noctis Aeterna fell, the Warp storms that crossed the southern Ultima Segmentum carried with them a pestilence unlike any before it. Where the cults of the Plague That Walks had planted seeds of corruption, a new corruption grew. The dead rose to claim the living, and when they were destroyed they released swarms of nurglings. Hive worlds fell. Cults came out of their hiding to call on the Realm of Chaos for aid. Three star systems, once rich and populous, fell wholly to Nurgle, and became the Scourge Stars. The Imperial records of these systems list the diseases that broke out: the Crawling Pustulence, Eyerot, the Plague That Walks and the Oozing Pox. Planetary defence regiments, led by demagogues who spoke filth and lies, turned on their comrades and joined the plague armies, and the pyres of corpses burned across the star systems until the last of them was silent.[1]

    From the Scourge Stars, Mortarion made his base. It was the first time in ten thousand years that the Daemon Primarch had come to the material universe in strength, and his hatred of his living brother was a part of the reason. Since the Battle of Kornovin in 901.M41, where Mortarion killed the Supreme Grand Master of the Grey Knights and was struck down in turn by his successor Kaldor Draigo, he had been unable to return. The return of Guilliman and the opening of the Rift gave him the occasion, and the Imperial Inquisition records that his anger seeded seven plagues on Imperial worlds.[3]

    The state of Ultramar

    [edit]

    Ultramar, the realm of the Five Hundred Worlds, had been ruled by Marneus Calgar and the tetrarchs in the absence of the primarch since the Rift's opening. It was a rich realm, with a Defence Fleet, star fortresses and a well-trained auxiliary, and it had already been attacked by the Black Legion during the Ultramar Campaign that preceded the revival of Guilliman. By the time Mortarion's forces entered it, its defences were stretched and its Space Marines and its Auxilia were committed to a dozen other fronts. The Ultramarines' Primaris reinforcements, raised in the Ultima Founding, had come to Macragge after the end of the Terran Crusade aboard Fleet Avenger, and had increased the garrison, but not by enough.[2]

    Course of the war

    [edit]

    The War of Flies

    [edit]

    The first stage was a stop-and-start war of attrition, so called because of the bloated flies that accompanied every Nurgle force in Ultramar. From Talassar to Iax, from Espandor to Circe to Laphis, the Plague Marines made strikes. Some were squad actions, intended only to put a contaminant on a loyalist world or to enact a ritual sacrifice that would undermine its defences. Others were offensives by entire vectoriums of Death Guard. The defenders, Ultramarines and Auxilia, outnumbered and with no word of the Lord Commander, threw back each attack and burned out the corruption. Among the weapons of the plague was the Zombie Plague, which raised the dead of Ultramar to fight against the living. The Ultramarines found it hard to maintain the morale of their troops when the enemy was the shambling remains of their own allies.[1]

    The three spearheads

    [edit]

    Mortarion's plan was drawn in seven parts, and the first was the War of Flies, followed by the assault on the Three Planets, the siege of the hive world Ardium and the Creeping Doom offensive against Espandor and Drohl. In the final phase of this offensive three spearheads attacked along a hundred fronts, with virus bombardments before them. Once-glorious hive cities became pits. Agri-worlds became flyblown wastes. Communications were cut by fresh Warp storms, and relief forces could not pass. Meanwhile a Plague Fleet under Typhus, formerly Calas Typhon of the Death Guard, systematically destroyed the Ultramar Defence Fleet and the star fortresses that had guarded it, until, about the twelfth year of the Indomitus Crusade, the Imperial defenders were depleted in dozens of ground campaigns.[1][2]

    Guilliman returns

    [edit]

    Guilliman returned to Ultramar after the end of the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade, and at once he began to check the invasion. His first measure was to re-establish the Tetrarch Council and to reorganise the defence by a series of deft, defensive manoeuvres that bought time. At Ardium, a hive world that had been held by a Death Guard garrison, he cleansed the planet and removed the garrison. In the Spear of Espandor, a counter-attack of great skill, he retook the system of that name and killed the Great Unclean One Qaramas with the Emperor's Sword. At the Gates of Parmenio he defeated the Greater Daemon Septicus and his Plague Guard. Mortarion, who had expected to meet a brother who was weakened and uncertain, found himself blocked at every turn by the precise counter-attacks of the Ultramarines. For the first time since his return the offensive of Nurgle had stalled.[1][3]

    Iax

    [edit]

    The hospital world

    [edit]

    Iax had been the Garden of Ultramar, a death world to no one and an agri-world of such fertility that its farms and cultivated woods covered the planet. The oldest city was the fortress of First Landing. During the Plague Wars, Guilliman, still at the head of the Indomitus Crusade, redesignated it a hospital world, to which the sick and wounded of the war could be sent. The plagues of Nurgle were potent, and the quarantine was strict. Shuttles landed their patients at the Hortusia spaceport, where decontamination crews were waiting. The wounded were separated from the sick, and the sick by their illness, whether physical, mental or spiritual. Those suffering from daemonic illness were held in orbit on specially commissioned void stations. The star fort Korsteel was anchored above the planet to decontaminate the passage from orbit. As few as possible of the patients were put to death.[2]

    The Plague God took offence at the hospital. It disrupted his gifts to mankind. Ku'Gath launched an invasion of Iax at the head of seven Plague Legions. Seven soldiers on the world, marked by Nurgle by trauma, by scratch or by the bite of a fly, were drawn one evening to a lake, where they were transformed. When they died, insects and maggots broke out of them, and the rite opened a gate into the Garden of Nurgle. Through it came seven plague arks, built from the carcasses of void whales, each surrounded by clouds of flies carrying disease.[2]

    The duel

    [edit]

    Mortarion, having been repulsed by Guilliman at Espandor and at Parmenio, joined Ku'Gath on Iax. Guilliman followed. The two primarchs fought at last in the ruins.[3]

    The accounts of the duel differ. The earlier Imperial report, which was in circulation before the revision of the Lord Commander's records, holds that the two brothers fought to a standstill and that the Death Guard then withdrew under the cover of a virus bomb. The more detailed account, now accepted in Ultramar, is that Guilliman was beaten, as he had been by Fulgrim and Magnus on the road to Terra, and was infected with the Godblight, the daemon's plague. In the moment between life and death he had a vision of his meeting with the Emperor on Terra, and understood that the Emperor had been conscious in that hour. The power of the Emperor burned the plague out of his body, and, as a conduit of that power, he struck through the Warp at the Garden of Nurgle itself. Mortarion fled, and the Death Guard were recalled by their god.[1]

    Both accounts agree on the final act. The Death Guard left Iax under cover of the Virus Bomb, which scoured every living thing from the planet. A world that had been the Garden of Ultramar was now a place without life.[2]

    End of the war

    [edit]

    The Imperial scholars say two things of the retreat of Mortarion. First, that the defence of Ultramar was now so strong that he could not have continued the offensive. Second, that in this same period the forces of the other Chaos Gods invaded the Scourge Stars as part of the Great Game, and that Nurgle was obliged to withdraw his forces from Ultramar to protect his new possession. The Imperial records name the Blood God among the powers that fell on the new possession of the Plague God.[2]

    In the respite that followed, Guilliman ordered the decontamination and rebuilding of Ultramar and the creation of new procedures for raising Ultramarines. It was a short respite. New Imperial crusades soon called the Lord Commander away from his realm, and the Ultramarines began the preparation of their revenge.[1]

    Chronology

    [edit]

    The date of the Plague Wars is in dispute. The earliest Imperial reports, written in the first decade of the Era Indomitus, say that the Indomitus Crusade lasted about a century and ended with the war in Ultramar. These accounts put the duel at Iax at the end of the first phase of the crusade, at the Battle of Raukos in c. 012.M42, and have Guilliman return to Ultramar after the triumph in which he dispersed the fleets. The Lord Commander's own records, revised in 003.M42, say that the first phase lasted only twelve years, place the Plague Wars within the early years of the crusade, and set no end date. The revised chronology is the accepted one, and in it the fighting of the Plague Wars overlaps with other campaigns of the crusade, including the campaign in the Nephilim Sector, where Guilliman was too occupied with Mortarion to send the aid that was asked of him.[4][5]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    The Plague Wars did not end the threat. The Scourge Stars are still held by Nurgle, and the Ultramarines regard the war as unfinished. The war cost the realm billions of lives and many worlds. Iax is a dead world. The Ultramarines have been forced to take on a policy of vigilance, and they have been strengthened by the Primaris Marines who came with Guilliman. For Mortarion, the war was a defeat against a brother whom he had hated for ten thousand years. The Death Guard have been called back to the Plague Planet in the Eye of Terror and remain a threat to every part of the Imperium.[3]
