    34th millennium
    000.M34 – 999.M34
    AgeLate The Forging
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra
    Key eventsFourth Black Crusade (001.M34); Pale Wasting (early M34); the Howling (401.M34); first Warp Stars (666.M34)
    Preceded by33rd millennium
    Followed by35th millennium

    The 34th millennium (M34) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M34 to 999.M34. The Forging continued through it. The astropathic network of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica reached its widest extent, and the rediscovery of a STC database in the Cana System revived Imperial industry. The High Lords confirmed the Imperial Cult as the official religion of the Imperium.[1]

    The millennium opened with the Fourth Black Crusade and saw two of the worst disasters of the age: the Pale Wasting from the Ghoul Stars, whose records are largely destroyed, and the Howling of 401.M34, when the death scream of the psychic tyrant called the Cacodominus killed about a billion astropaths. The Traitor Legions in the Eye of Terror fought among themselves, while the first Warp Stars began to appear. By its end the Imperium was drifting towards the division of the Nova Terra Interregnum.[1]

    Early M34The Administratum continues to win back influence from the Adeptus Ministorum.
    c. M34The High Lords and the Ecclesiarchy declare the Imperial Cult the official state religion.
    c. M34An STC database recovered in the Cana System.
    c. M34The Warp storm Gae-sann swallows the Kai System into the Eye of Terror.
    c. M34The Blackstar Crusade into the Ganglax Sector; the champion Ekrak makes M'laan XIII a daemon world.
    085.M34The terraforming of Atasca IV.
    101.M34Death of Armaros, twelfth Supreme Grand Master of the Dark Angels, of a plague taken in the Bloodpox Campaign against the Death Guard.
    Early M34The Pale Wasting spreads from the Ghoul Stars.Some records place it late in M34.
    216.M34Clan Raukaan of the Iron Hands enforces the quarantine of the Ghoul Stars during the Pale Wasting.
    230.M34The Trachesai Massacre: Blood Angels heavy-weapon squads seen to charge into close combat.Early sign of the Red Thirst.
    303.M34The Grand Hunt on Geminex: House Raven and the Skitarii fight the Aeldari of Biel-Tan.
    401.M34The Howling: the Black Templars end the Catelexis Heresy by killing the Cacodominus, whose death scream kills about a billion astropaths.
    438.M34A single strike cruiser of the Black Wings returns from the outer void.Survivors speak only to the Ordo Malleus.
    456.M34Inquisitor Halleck captures the heretic called the Angel of Truth, then vanishes.
    490.M34The Dabenlar III Campaign: the 6th Company of the Rampagers crosses the Burning Plains.
    Mid-M34The Eighth Founding, probably including the Mantis Warriors and the Angels Revenant.One table gives 598.M35.
    498–601.M34The Sin of Pride: the Shining Blades win the Berillia campaigns, then turn on their allies.
    600–730.M34The Dispute of Iron: civil war among the Iron Warriors on Medrengard.
    647.M34Death of the documentarian Lasko Pyre, the only known escaped slave of Commorragh.
    666.M34The first Warp Stars sighted after a storm out of the Eye of Terror.
    764.M34The Shattering of Lugganath: the Emperor's Children invade the craftworld.
    843.M34The Night Lords exterminate the people of Grendel's World in the Ysobael Cloud.
    934.M34The warnings of Ulthwé.
    979.M34The North-South conflict on Mars between rival Tech-priest factions.
    Late M34The Obscuran Uprisings: the Sons of Vengeance put down separatist risings across the Segmentum Obscurus.Four centuries of revolt end.
    Late M34Unrest in the Segmentum Pacificus prepares the Nova Terra Interregnum.Some sector records date its start to 401.M34.
