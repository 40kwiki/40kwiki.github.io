    Stygies VIII, called the Ever-Staring Cyclops, is a forge world of the Adeptus Mechanicus on the seventh and largest moon of a ringed gas giant in the Vulcanis System of the Segmentum Pacificus. It is the least trusted forge world of the Imperium of Mankind, because a powerful faction of its priesthood, the Xenarites, studies and uses the technology of alien races, which orthodox doctrine forbids.[1][2]

    The forge world nearly fell to the traitors in the Horus Heresy, when both of its Titan Legions joined Horus, and was saved by the intervention of the Aeldari. Its output is so important that the High Lords of Terra have ruled its continued existence vital, but its quarrels with the Ordo Xenos, beginning with the Xenarite Schism of 335.M36, have never ended.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]
    Further information: Horus Heresy

    Before the Horus Heresy Stygies VIII was the rare forge world that maintained two Titan Legions, the Legio Vulcanum I and II. Both turned traitor in the early years of the war, c. 006–008.M31, and the forge world came close to falling to the Dark Mechanicum.[3][b] It was saved by the Aeldari, who for their own reasons wished to deny the forge world to the traitors. Since that time the Vulcanis System has been home to the Xenarites, who hold that the Aeldari's aid proved that even aliens are subject to the will of the Omnissiah and that their technology may be studied as sacred knowledge.[1]

    After the Heresy the forge world raised a new Titan Legion, the Legio Honorum, known as the Death Bolts.[1]

    The Xenarite Schism

    [edit]

    The Xenarites pursued their studies covertly, garrisoning alien sites with Skitarii rather than bringing artefacts home as doctrine required. Their expeditions were attacked by aliens, planetary garrisons and the Skitarii of rival forge worlds. In 335.M36 the Ordo Xenos learned of their activities and sent dozens of Deathwatch kill-teams against Stygies VIII, beginning the Xenarite Schism. Later in the 36th millennium the forge world answered the growing record of charges against it by using viral programs, machine canticles and infiltration to erase or alter the files of the Administratum and even of the Inquisition.[2][1]

    Battles between Deathwatch kill-teams and radical tech-priests in the forge world's reliquary-halls have continued intermittently to the present day.[2]

    The 41st millennium

    [edit]
    Further information: 41st millennium

    The loss of the forge world of Tigrus to the Ork warlord Arrgard the Defiler in the 35th millennium and of Gryphonne IV to Hive Fleet Leviathan in 997.M41 left Stygies VIII the only known producer of the Vanquisher cannon.[1] In 991.M41 Stygian Skitarii were sent to the ocean world of Magogue, where industry had drained the seas to reveal Necrontyr architecture.[1]

    During the Thirteenth Black Crusade in 999.M41, Stygies VIII and Agripinaa received refugees from the Fall of Cadia, and the forge world's armies were drawn into the defence of the Segmentum Obscurus.[1]

    The Webway expeditions

    [edit]
    See also: Webway

    The most radical Stygian magi have breached ancient Webway portals on Vulcanis III, a neighbouring world of the system, and sent war processions into the Labyrinth Dimension of the Aeldari. Their aim is to find the Black Library, the hidden repository of Aeldari lore, and bring its knowledge back to Stygies VIII. These expeditions have fought Harlequins, Chaos Space Marines and worse, and are disowned by the forge world's official voice.[2][c]

    Geography

    [edit]

    Stygies VIII is the seventh and largest moon of a ringed gas giant on the outer edge of the binary Vulcanis System. Its surface is covered by forges and reliquary-halls. Vulcanis III, another world of the system, holds the ancient webway portals used by Xenarite expeditions.[1]

    Society

    [edit]

    The priesthood of Stygies VIII is famous for its curiosity: it makes war on alien races not to conquer or exterminate them but to study their technology. The Xenarites are tolerated by the rest of the forge world because of the value of what they find, but are regarded as near heresy by orthodox magi. Stygian Skitarii wear black and red. The forge world's goods carry its sigil, the "cyclops" eye of its name.[1]
