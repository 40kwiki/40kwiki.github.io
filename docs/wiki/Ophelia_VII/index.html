    Ophelia VII
    Cardinal world of the Segmentum Tempestus
    Ophelia VII from orbit

    PopulationTens of billions

    Ophelia VII is the oldest of the cardinal worlds of the Imperium of Mankind, held by the faithful of the Imperial Cult to be second in holiness only to Terra. It lies in the Segmentum Tempestus and is given over entirely to the worship of the Emperor of Mankind as a god. Its surface is covered by cathedrals and bell towers, many a mile high, linked by avenues lined with the statues of thousands of Imperial saints, and its dungeons run deep beneath them. It is the seat of the Synod Ministra and of the Convent Sanctorum, one of the two great convents of the Adepta Sororitas.[1][2]

    The world was brought into the Imperium by the Emperor in person during the Great Crusade, at the head of a hundred Custodians and ten thousand Imperial Fists. For a time in the 34th millennium it rather than Terra was the capital of the Ecclesiarchy, after the Ecclesiarch Benedin IV, who had been its cardinal, moved the whole apparatus of the church there. The move placed the Ministorum far from the other High Lords of Terra, and in the centuries that followed it raised vast tithes and rooted out rival cults. A later Ecclesiarch, Greigor XI, took the church back to Terra, though Ophelia VII has kept its rank as the second centre of the faith.[1][3]

    After the Age of Apostasy the world took on a second function. The Synod Ministra was founded there to guard the church against a second Goge Vandire, and the Adepta Sororitas was divided between Ophelia VII and Terra so that no single hand could control the whole of it. The Convent Sanctorum became the mother house of many Orders of the Sisters of Battle, and two of the best known saints of the Ecclesiarchy are said to have been born on the world.[2][3]

    The opening of the Great Rift after the Fall of Cadia in 999.M41 left Ophelia VII exposed to the Warp. The world was seized by a greater daemon of Tzeentch, the Tyrant of Blueflame, and its Sisters fought until they were all but destroyed. It was freed by the Indomitus Crusade and the Black Templars, although the daemon itself escaped, and in the Era Indomitus the Black Templars guard it and other shrine worlds against renewed assault.[4]

    History

    [edit]

    The Great Crusade

    [edit]
    Further information: Great Crusade

    Ophelia VII was claimed for the Imperium in the 30th millennium in a battle led by the Emperor himself, who charged the enemy alongside a hundred Custodians and ten thousand Imperial Fists Legionaries. The Ecclesiarchy treats the battle as one of the founding events of the world's sanctity.[1][a]

    Seat of the Ecclesiarchy

    [edit]

    By the 34th millennium Ophelia VII was said to be the richest world in the Imperium after Terra and Mars, and it was the diocese of the Cardinal Benedin before he became Ecclesiarch. In about 200.M34, as Ecclesiarch Benedin IV, he moved the Holy Synod and the whole apparatus of the Adeptus Ministorum from Terra to Ophelia VII. Three reasons are given: his own tie to the world, its wealth, and the distance it put between the Ministorum and the other High Lords of Terra.[1][3]

    Benedin built cathedrals over ninety thousand square miles of ground and four thousand metres high; only the Imperial Palace on Terra surpassed them as works of Imperial Gothic architecture. From its distant seat the Ministorum raised huge tithes, encouraged cardinals across the galaxy to compete in building cathedrals and monuments to the God-Emperor and His saints, and rooted out rival cults through its zealous cardinals, preachers and confessors. Its control of the faith of Mankind grew rapidly.[1]

    The Ecclesiarch Greigor XI later judged that the Ministorum's position was strong enough for it to return to Terra and take its seat in the Senatorum Imperialis. The return took twelve years and much of the church's treasure before the doors of the Ecclesiarchal Palace on Terra were opened again. Ministorum records date Greigor's announcement to about 300.M34, a century after Benedin's move, while other accounts give the stay on Ophelia VII as nearly three centuries.[1][3][b]

    The Age of Apostasy

    [edit]
    Main article: Age of Apostasy

    When Goge Vandire rose to be both Ecclesiarch and Master of the Administratum, he had a power that no other institution in the Imperium could rival, and he brought his fist down on the cardinals of the Ministorum. Some of his enemies resolved to escape the corridors of Terra, and chose Ophelia VII as their refuge, but their ship never came out of the Warp and was lost for ever. After the Reign of Blood and the fall of Vandire in c. 288.M36, the new Ecclesiarch, Sebastian Thor, created the Synod Ministra on Ophelia VII as a second governing body for the Ministorum, far from Terra. It would spread the laws and acts of the Holy Synod, and it would stand as a defence against any faction, or any single Ecclesiarch, who tried to seize the church for his own purposes. The Astra Ministra, the administrative body of the Ministorum's functionaries on the starships of the Imperium, is based on the world and forms part of the Synod.[1][3]

    The Convent Sanctorum

    [edit]

    The Daughters of the Emperor, who had served Vandire as the Brides of the Emperor, were reorganised after his fall under the Decree Passive. They were divided between two convents, the Convent Prioris on Terra and the Convent Sanctorum on Ophelia VII, so that no one person could control the whole. From then on the Ophelian convent has been one of the two centres of the Adepta Sororitas.[2][3]

    The Great Rift and the Battle of Ophelia VII

    [edit]
    Main article: Great Rift

    The opening of the Great Rift after the Fall of Cadia in 999.M41 left Ophelia VII exposed to the Warp, and the world was invaded by daemons under the Tyrant of Blueflame, a Lord of Change in the service of Tzeentch. The Sisters of the Order of Our Martyred Lady resisted fiercely but were facing total defeat when the Indomitus Crusade reached the system, c. 000–005.M42. The Crusade, with the Black Templars, freed the whole population, although the daemon itself escaped. The Sisters on the world had suffered almost half their number in casualties by the time they were relieved.[4][c] At the request of Roboute Guilliman, the Black Templars later sent a crusade fleet to guard Ophelia VII and other shrine worlds against further attack.[4]

    Geography

    [edit]

    Ophelia VII is a temperate world whose surface is covered by cathedrals, chapels and bell towers, many over a mile high, joined by avenues lined with statues of thousands of Imperial saints. Beneath them deep dungeons hold heretics, who are made to repent by methods of soul-cleansing such as arco-flagellation, death-masking, soul-scouring and the Trial of Castigation. Imperial records place the world either in the galactic south or near the Eastern Fringe, depending on the source, and it is sometimes wrongly entered as "Ophelia IV".[1]

    Society

    [edit]

    The whole of Ophelian society is devoted to the Imperial Cult. The planet is governed by a Cardinal of the Ministorum, and its population of tens of billions serves the church as priests, pilgrims, artisans and labourers. The Synod Ministra, which meets there, is the most important assembly of the Ecclesiarchy outside Terra, and the world remains one of the main centres of Ministorum power.[1]

    The Adepta Sororitas

    [edit]

    The Orders of the Adepta Sororitas, which have been the armed forces of the Ecclesiarchy since the Age of Apostasy, were split between Terra and Ophelia VII. There are extensive facilities on both worlds, and each Order has a home on both if need be. Ophelia VII is the primary base of half the Orders Militant, Dialogous, Famulous and Hospitaller. The Convent Sanctorum is home to the Order of the Bloody Rose, the Order of Our Martyred Lady and the Order of the Valorous Heart, and its Canoness is one of the two highest officers of the Sororitas.[2]

    Two of the best known Sisters of the Imperial record, Saint Praxedes and Saint Helena the Virtuous, are said to have been natives of the world.[5]
