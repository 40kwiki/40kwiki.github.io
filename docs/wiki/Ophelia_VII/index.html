    Cardinal world of the Segmentum Tempestus

    Ophelia VII is the oldest of the cardinal worlds of the Imperium of Mankind, held by the faithful of the Imperial Cult to be second in holiness only to Terra. It lies in the Segmentum Tempestus and is given over entirely to the worship of the Emperor of Mankind as a god. It is the seat of the Synod Ministra and of the Convent Sanctorum, one of the two great convents of the Adepta Sororitas.[1][2]

    The world was brought into the Imperium by the Emperor in person during the Great Crusade. In the 34th millennium the Ecclesiarch Benedin IV moved the whole Ecclesiarchy there, and for a time Ophelia VII rather than Terra was the capital of the church. After the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41 it was overrun by daemons and was freed by the Indomitus Crusade.[1][3]

    History

    [edit]

    The Great Crusade

    [edit]

    Ophelia VII was claimed for the Imperium in the 30th millennium in a battle led by the Emperor Himself, who charged the enemy at the head of a hundred Custodians and ten thousand Imperial Fists Legionaries. The Ecclesiarchy treats the battle as one of the founding events of the world's sanctity.[1][a]

    Seat of the Ecclesiarchy

    [edit]

    By the 34th millennium Ophelia VII was said to be the richest world in the Imperium after Terra and Mars, and it was the diocese of the Cardinal Benedin before he became Ecclesiarch. In about 200.M34, as Ecclesiarch Benedin IV, he moved the Holy Synod and the whole apparatus of the Adeptus Ministorum from Terra to Ophelia VII. Three reasons are given: his own tie to the world, its wealth, and the distance it put between the Ministorum and the other High Lords of Terra.[1][4]

    Benedin built cathedrals over ninety thousand square miles of ground and four thousand metres high; only the Imperial Palace on Terra surpassed them as works of Imperial Gothic architecture. From its distant seat the Ministorum raised huge tithes, encouraged cardinals across the galaxy to compete in building cathedrals, and rooted out rival cults, and its control of the faith grew rapidly.[1]

    The Ecclesiarch Greigor XI later decided that the Ministorum's position was strong enough for it to return to Terra and take its place in the Senatorum Imperialis. The move took twelve years and much of the church's treasure. Ministorum records date Greigor's announcement to about 300.M34, a century after Benedin's move, while other accounts give the stay on Ophelia VII as nearly three centuries.[1][4][b]

    The Convent Sanctorum

    [edit]

    After the Reign of Blood and the fall of Goge Vandire in c. 288.M36, the Daughters of the Emperor, who had served Vandire as the Brides of the Emperor, were reorganised under the Decree Passive and divided between two convents: the Convent Prioris on Terra and the Convent Sanctorum on Ophelia VII. From then on the Ophelian convent has been one of the two centres of the Adepta Sororitas and the mother house of many of their orders.[2][4]

    The Great Rift

    [edit]

    The opening of the Great Rift after the Fall of Cadia in 999.M41 left Ophelia VII exposed to the warp, and the world was invaded by daemons under a greater daemon known as the Tyrant of Blueflame. When the Indomitus Crusade reached the system, c. 000–005.M42, it freed the whole population, although the daemon itself escaped.[3][c] At the request of Roboute Guilliman, the Black Templars later sent a crusade fleet to guard Ophelia VII and other shrine worlds against further attack.[3]

    Geography

    [edit]

    Ophelia VII is a temperate world whose surface is covered by cathedrals, chapels and bell towers, many over a mile high, joined by avenues lined with statues of thousands of Imperial saints. Beneath them deep dungeons hold heretics, who are made to repent through arco-flagellation, death-masking, soul-scouring and the Trial of Castigation. Imperial records place the world either in the galactic south or near the Eastern Fringe; it is sometimes wrongly entered as "Ophelia IV".[1]

    Society

    [edit]

    The whole of Ophelian society is devoted to the Imperial Cult. The planet is governed by a Cardinal of the Ministorum and its population of tens of billions serves the church as priests, pilgrims, artisans and labourers. The Synod Ministra, which meets there, is the most important assembly of the Ecclesiarchy outside Terra, and the world remains one of the main centres of Ministorum power.[1]

    The Convent Sanctorum dominates the world's martial life. Many Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas, including the Order of Our Martyred Lady, have their preceptories there, and its Canoness is one of the two highest officers of the Sororitas.[2]
