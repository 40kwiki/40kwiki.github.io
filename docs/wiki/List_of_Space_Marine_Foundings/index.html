    Space Marine Foundings
    Foundings of the Adeptus Astartes, late M30 to c. 999.M41
    Fafnir Rann, first Master of the Executioners, raised in the Third Founding
    AuthorityThe High Lords of Terra
    RegisterRegister of Foundings
    FirstFirst Founding, the twenty Space Marine Legions
    LatestUltima Founding, c. 999.M41 onward

    This is a list of the Foundings, the groups in which the Adeptus Astartes raised new Chapters. A Founding could be ordered only by the High Lords of Terra, and each drew its gene-seed from an existing Chapter under a writ of succession. The Registers number the Foundings, though many are recorded without a number, and the dates of the earliest and latest are the least certain.

    The table below lists the Foundings in order, with the principal Chapters assigned to each. The Register is incomplete, so a Founding may have raised Chapters not named here. Where the Register gives only a period, that period is shown, and where the date rests on the sequence of events it is marked "c.".[a]

    The Foundings

    [edit]
    FoundingDateNotable Chapters and notes
    First FoundingLate M30The twenty original Space Marine Legions of the Great Crusade.
    Second Foundingc. 021.M31The loyal Legions divided into Chapters of about a thousand warriors, including the Black Templars, Crimson Fists and Angels of Absolution.
    Third FoundingM31Raised on the petition of Rogal Dorn. Includes the Executioners, the Scythes of the Emperor, the Angels Erythrean and the Death Wardens.
    Fourth FoundingAfter 546.M32Ordered by Maximus Thane after the War of the Beast. Includes the Halo Brethren, Sable Swords and Tome Keepers.
    Unnumbered Foundingc. 220.M33Raised from Ultramarines gene-stock. Includes the Howling Griffons.
    Unnumbered FoundingBefore 401.M34Includes the Red Scorpions.
    Eighth FoundingMid-M34Includes the Mantis Warriors and the Angels Revenant.[b]
    Tenth FoundingMid-M35Includes the Astral Claws and the Angels of Wrath.
    Thirteenth ("Dark") FoundingLate M35 or early M36Includes the Death Spectres and the Exorcists.
    Nineteenth FoundingMid-M36Includes the Avenging Sons.
    Twenty-first ("Cursed") Founding991.M35The Cursed Founding. Flawed gene-seed; includes the Lamenters, Black Dragons, Flame Falcons and Minotaurs.
    Unnumbered FoundingEarly M37Includes the Disciples of Caliban, and the Sons of Medusa, whose Chapter was ratified in this Founding.
    Twenty-third ("Sentinel") FoundingLate M37 or early M38Includes the Star Phantoms, the Marines Errant and the Celestial Lions.
    Twenty-fourth Foundingc. M39Includes the Hospitallers.
    Twenty-fifth ("Bastion") FoundingLate M40Includes the Emperor's Spears and the Fire Angels.
    Twenty-sixth Founding738.M41Includes the Storm Giants and the Mentors.
    Ultima Foundingc. 999.M41 onwardRaised the first Primaris Space Marines. Includes the Knights of the Chalice, the Castellans of the Rift and the Wolfspear.

    Notes on the series

    [edit]

    The numbered series in the Register stops at the Twenty-sixth Founding. The Ultima Founding is recorded separately, as the work of the Era Indomitus under the Lord Commander of the Imperium.[1]
