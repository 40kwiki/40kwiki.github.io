    Space Marine Foundings
    Foundings of the Adeptus Astartes, late M30 to c. 999.M41
    Fafnir Rann, first Master of the Executioners, raised in the Third Founding
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    AuthorityThe High Lords of Terra
    FirstFirst Founding, the twenty Space Marine Legions
    LatestUltima Founding, c. 999.M41 onward

    This is a list of the Foundings, the groups in which the Adeptus Astartes raised new Chapters. A Founding could be ordered only by the High Lords of Terra, and each drew its gene-seed from an existing Chapter under a writ of succession. Imperial records number some of the Foundings, but many are recorded without a number, and several dates are known only as a period of the millennium.[1][2]

    The table below lists the Foundings in order, with the principal Chapters assigned to each. The records are incomplete, so a Founding may have raised Chapters not named here. Where the records give only a period, that period is shown, and where the date is approximate it is marked "c.".[a][1]

    The Foundings

    [edit]
    Main article: Third Founding
    FoundingDateNotable Chapters and notes
    First FoundingLate M30The twenty original Space Marine Legions, created from the gene-seed of the twenty primarchs during the Unification Wars, after the Age of Strife.
    Second Foundingc. 021.M31The loyal Legions divided into Chapters of about a thousand warriors, including the Black Templars, Crimson Fists and Angels of Absolution.
    Third FoundingM31Raised on the petition of Rogal Dorn. Includes the Executioners, the Scythes of the Emperor, the Angels Erythrean and the Death Wardens.
    Fourth FoundingAfter 546.M32Ordered by Maximus Thane after the War of the Beast. Includes the Imperius Reavers, Halo Brethren, Sable Swords and Tome Keepers. The Sable Swords are also placed after 926.M41, and that date is disputed.
    Unnumbered Foundingc. 220.M33Raised from Ultramarines gene-stock. Includes the Howling Griffons.
    Unnumbered FoundingBefore 401.M34Includes the Red Scorpions.
    Eighth FoundingMid-M34Includes the Angels Revenant and, unconfirmed, the Mantis Warriors.
    Tenth FoundingMid-M35Includes the Astral Claws and, suspected, the Angels of Wrath.
    Thirteenth ("Dark") FoundingLate M35 or early M36Includes the Death Spectres and the Exorcists.
    Nineteenth FoundingMid-M36Includes the Avenging Sons.
    Twenty-first ("Cursed") Founding991.M35The Cursed Founding. Flawed gene-seed; includes the Lamenters, Black Dragons, Flame Falcons and Minotaurs.
    Unnumbered FoundingEarly M37Known to precede the Great Cull of 020.M37. Includes the Disciples of Caliban and the Dark Hunters. The Sons of Medusa belong to no Founding: their Chapter was ratified by a special Imperial edict in 011.M37.
    Twenty-third ("Sentinel") FoundingLate M37 or early M38Includes the Star Phantoms, the Marines Errant and the Celestial Lions.
    Twenty-fourth Foundingc. M39Includes the Hospitallers.
    Twenty-fifth ("Bastion") FoundingLate M40Includes the Emperor's Spears and the Fire Angels.
    Twenty-sixth Founding738.M41Includes the Storm Giants and the Mentors.
    Ultima Foundingc. 999.M41 onwardRaised in the wake of the fall of Cadia, from Primaris Space Marines only. Includes the Knights of the Chalice, the Castellans of the Rift and the Wolfspear.

    Notes on the series

    [edit]

    The numbered series stops at the Twenty-sixth Founding. The Ultima Founding is recorded separately, as the work of the Era Indomitus under the Lord Commander of the Imperium.[3]
