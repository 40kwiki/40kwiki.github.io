    The Doom Eagles are a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes, raised from the gene-seed of the Ultramarines in the Second Founding. Their home is Gathis II, a storm-wracked feral world, and their fortress-monastery, the Eyrie, stands on the Ghostmountain, the tallest peak of its Razorpeak Mountains. In the Era Indomitus they are one of the nine Shield Chapters of Ultramar, assigned by Roboute Guilliman to defend and extend the realm at the direction of the tenth Chapter, the Ultramarines.[1]

    The Doom Eagles hold that every Marine is already dead and so has nothing left to lose. The doctrine makes them nearly impossible to turn from their duty, and it also makes them dour, pious and aloof. They use stealth and speed, and spearhead attacks by massed jump troops and gunships, delivering a series of crushing blows that may cost many lives but leave the enemy shattered.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Founding

    [edit]
    See also: Second Founding

    The Doom Eagles are one of the Primogenitor Chapters, created by Guilliman himself from his own Legion at the Second Founding of c. 021.M31. He gave command to Captain Tulian Aquila of the 24th Chapter of the XIII Legion, the Exitium, a strategic reserve with an unusually large complement of ordnance, mobile artillery and munitions trains. Aquila had been a sergeant at the Battle of Calth (007.M31), stationed with his company at the city of Ithraca. When the Word Bearers took the planet's orbital defences and bombarded it, only Aquila and two other battle-brothers survived. The fatalism he drew from the day became the Chapter's creed.[1][a]

    The High Lords of Terra gave the Chapter Gathis II as its demesne. The Chapter built the Eyrie on the Ghostmountain, whose peak the early tribes named for the dead who haunt it. The Reclusiam of the Eyrie is a great octagonal tower holding relics of defeats and calamities from across the Imperium, gathered by battle-brothers on pilgrimage, with at its heart a chamber holding pieces of the shattered Imperial Palace, a feather from the wing of Sanguinius and a shard of the Emperor's armour.[1]

    Wars of the 41st millennium

    [edit]

    In 075.M41, in the campaign called the Torment, sixty Doom Eagles joined sixty thousand Guardsmen on the hive world Pirody, on the order of the Warmaster Getus, to cleanse the eastern continent of Chaos. A supernatural fever like a haemorrhagic plague struck the army, and even six of the Marines fell to it. In 991.M41 the whole Chapter assaulted the supposedly dead world Faugris, and found a Necron tomb world; after sixteen solar weeks of bombardment and invasion the legions were still pouring out of stasis, and the Doom Eagles withdrew before reinforcements could arrive. During the Achilus Crusade its 3rd Company took the world of Wrath with a high-altitude drop of the entire company in jump packs onto the capital island.[1]

    13th Black Crusade and the Indomitus Crusade

    [edit]
    See also: Black Crusade and Indomitus Crusade

    At least five companies of the Doom Eagles defended the Cadian Sector in the 13th Black Crusade (999.M41). Captain Luctus of the 3rd Company led the largest force to the Belis Corona Sub-sector, where the Chapter met the Curse of Unbelief, the undead plague. No Doom Eagle was touched by it, and the Chapter also destroyed the dead on Yayor and aboard the carrier Deliverance. After the Great Rift opened, the Chapter contributed to the Indomitus Crusade of its resurrected primarch.[1][2]

    Organisation and recruitment

    [edit]

    The Chapter follows the Codex Astartes, with a special liking for Assault Marines in jump packs and a larger use of Dreadnoughts than the Ultramarines, which suits its belief that its warriors already fight beyond death. The Chapter Master bears the title Commander and sits with a Council of Eagles, modelled on the Senatorum Imperialis, of the company captains, senior Chaplain, Apothecary, Forge Master and Librarian.[1]

    Every thirteen years, by Imperial command, every boy of thirteen on Gathis II must go to Table City, and gliders gather youths from the villages. The Chapter does not choose the strongest or bravest. It chooses those who have known loss, grief and exile, and who already think themselves dead. They are led to a cave that floods with boiling water and must pass a series of trials, through thorn-webs, hot coals and a chasm, then glide to the Ghostmountain. A hidden Librarian examines the survivors, and those who accept their own mortality are admitted. The rest are left on the ledge.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Tulian Aquila, first Chapter Master.
    • Hearon, Commander of the Chapter in the Era Indomitus.
    • Luctus, Captain of the 3rd Company, who defended Belis Corona.
    • Secundus Thryn, Librarian, known for the Chapter's saying that all of its sons are already dead.
