    The Harlequins are a kindred of the Aeldari, warrior-performers devoted to Cegorach, the Laughing God, who travel the Webway in companies called masques. They belong to no craftworld and no city, and are welcomed by every Aeldari people: the Asuryani of the craftworlds, the Drukhari of Commorragh, the Exodites and the Corsairs. They perform the sacred dances that tell the history of their race, guard the Black Library and its knowledge of Chaos, and fight with an acrobatic speed and grace that Imperial observers describe as impossible to follow.[1][2]

    Aeldari belief holds that Cegorach alone of the old gods escaped Slaanesh at the Fall, and that he laughs at the Dark Prince from hiding in the Webway. The Harlequins are his servants and the keepers of his secret purpose. In 999.M41 the masque of the Midnight Sorrow took part in the ritual meant to wake Ynnead, and in the first years of the Era Indomitus the Shadowseer Sylandri Veilwalker set the Archmagos Belisarius Cawl on his path to Cadia and helped free Roboute Guilliman from the Maelstrom.[1][3]

    Cegorach

    [edit]

    In Aeldari myth Cegorach is the trickster among the gods, whose jests and wit outwitted even Khaine and the Great Enemy. When Slaanesh was born at the Fall and devoured the gods of the Aeldari pantheon, Cegorach escaped, and is said to hide in the labyrinth of the Webway, mocking the god who could not catch him. The Harlequins believe that he has a plan to save the Aeldari, which he has revealed to no one. Their performances, their wars and their sudden interventions in the affairs of other races all serve it.[1][4]

    History

    [edit]

    The Fall

    [edit]

    According to Harlequin tradition, the first Players were Aeldari who fled into the Webway at the Fall, in the early 30th millennium, and were saved by Cegorach. Since then they have lived as wanderers, carrying the memory of their race's ruin in their dances. The Harlequins' great performances retell the creation, the War in Heaven, the coming of the Great Enemy and the Fall.[1][a]

    Recorded deeds

    [edit]

    Harlequin records obtained by the Ordo Xenos list many campaigns. In 641.M33 the Laughing God's followers returned to war after centuries of isolation. In 666.M33 the Dance Without End gave the first performance by a Solitaire, the lone dancer who plays the Great Enemy. In 764.M34 the craftworld Lugganath was invaded and defended. In 984.M35, according to these records, Asdrubael Vect seized power in Commorragh with consequences the Harlequins had foreseen. The Silent Shroud fought the Orks of WAAAGH! Gutrippa on Sheng's World in 358.M40. In 056.M41 the Frozen Stars destroyed the Knights of House Terryn, who had claimed the maiden world Velos. In 113.M41 a Solitaire duelled the Bloodletter called Skulltaker. In 215.M41 the Veiled Path offered to help defend the Imperial naval base at Roth against pirates, then turned on the defenders in mid-battle and vented the survivors into space, for reasons never explained.[1]

    Late 41st millennium

    [edit]
    See also: 41st millennium

    The Harlequins grew more active in the last decades of the 41st millennium. In 899.M41, as the Thousand Sons sorcerer Ahzek Ahriman learned the first secrets of the Webway, masques began to watch him. In 990.M41 an unknown Great Harlequin won the Commorrite Dance of the Blinding Blade and vanished; Commorrite whispers held that he was Cegorach himself. In 992.M41 Harlequins fought at Iyanden during the war against Hive Fleet Kraken, and on Deshil a masque drove back a Tyranid swarm from an Ultramarines strike force, which then woke on the tables of Drukhari Haemonculi. In 993.M41 several masques joined in a grand masque to topple the Echospire on the shrine world Baedros, earning the enmity of the Space Wolves. In 996.M41 the Midnight Sorrow trapped sixty-six Heralds of Slaanesh in runic stones, for a purpose still unknown.[1]

    The Thirteenth Black Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Thirteenth Black Crusade

    In 999.M41, during the Thirteenth Black Crusade, Ahriman broke into the Webway and forced his way into the Black Library itself, seeking its knowledge of Chaos. A great host of Harlequins and Aeldari warriors drove him out. In the same year the Midnight Sorrow joined the Farseer Eldrad Ulthran in his ritual on the moon Coheria to wake Ynnead; the ritual was interrupted by the Deathwatch, and the Harlequins with Eldrad were killed. A fragment of the god's consciousness reached Commorragh instead, and the Ynnari were born.[1][5]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Sylandri Veilwalker, the Shadowseer of the Masque of the Veiled Path, appeared to Archmagos Cawl before the Fall of Cadia and directed him to the Cadian Pylons. During Guilliman's Terran Crusade, she and the Fallen Angel Cypher freed the primarch from the Red Corsairs' Blackstone Fortress in the Maelstrom; the Veiled Path guided the Crusade through the Webway to Luna, fought beside it against Magnus the Red, and sealed the gate behind it.[3][6]

    Since the opening of the Great Rift the Harlequins have been reported across the galaxy, fighting alongside craftworlds, Drukhari and Ynnari alike, and more rarely beside Imperial forces against Chaos. Some masques have performed for the Ynnari, and the Ordo Xenos believes that the Harlequins regard Yvraine with interest but have not declared for her.[5][2]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    A masque is a company of Harlequins under a Great Harlequin. It is made up of troupes of Players, each led by a Troupe Master, and its members take the roles of the characters in the Harlequin dances. The Shadowseers are psykers who weave illusions and guard their companions' minds against the Warp. The Death Jesters play the part of death in the dances and bear heavy weapons in battle. The Solitaires play Slaanesh. They are said to be the only Aeldari whose souls the Great Enemy can claim at will, they dance alone and are shunned even by other Harlequins, and they are among the deadliest warriors known.[1][2]

    Harlequins wear masks, many of them holo-suits that shatter their outline into a storm of colour. They fight with shuriken pistols, power swords, Harlequin's kisses and caresses, and ride jetbikes and grav-skimmers. Each masque bears its own patterns and colours.[7]

    The Black Library

    [edit]

    The Black Library, also called the Black Library of Chaos, is a hidden realm within the Webway, said to resemble a craftworld that exists only within the labyrinth. It holds the Aeldari's most precious knowledge, above all what they know of Chaos: grimoires sealed in warded rooms, relics, and the psychic lore of the dead. Its entrances are hidden by riddles and illusion, and it is guarded by sentinels whose nature its few visitors refuse to describe. The Harlequins are its keepers. A handful of human Inquisitors have been admitted under close supervision, always in the company of Harlequins; none has spoken of it.[1][8]

    Relations

    [edit]

    The Harlequins move freely between all Aeldari peoples, and even the Kabals of Commorragh do not openly hinder them; Vect alone is said to have defied them publicly. They have helped and harmed the Imperium as their purposes required. The Inquisition treats them as an unpredictable xenos power, but some of its members, sworn like the Harlequins to the destruction of Chaos, have found them reliable allies.[2][8]
