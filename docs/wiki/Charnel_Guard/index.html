    Charnel Guard
    Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes
    Badge of the Charnel Guard
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    FoundingSuspected Third Founding
    Successor ofBlood Angels (not acknowledged by the parent Chapter)
    PrimarchSanguinius
    HomeworldNone; fleet-based Chapter
    AllegianceImperium of Mankind
    ColoursBlack and maroon; some accounts say sombre grey
    AlliancesMaelstrom Warders; the Triarchy

    The Charnel Guard are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. They are a reclusive, fleet-based Successor of the Blood Angels, although the parent Chapter does not acknowledge them as a member of the Sanguinary Brotherhood. The Charnel Guard are believed to have been raised during the Third Founding.[1]

    When not on campaign, the Charnel Guard seal themselves away in vast stasis crypts. Their Armoury holds many weapons that are rarely seen in the 41st millennium. Among their relics are a Fellblade and at least two other super-heavy tanks of similar age. Their fleet includes many Fire Raptor gunships, a specialised variant of the Storm Eagle.[1]

    The Chapter's badge is a black fleur-de-lis cross with lily-petal flourishes at the arm-ends, outlined in white. A maroon lozenge at its centre holds a fanged skull. Their armour is said to be finished to the gloss of black volcanic glass, though accounts disagree on the colour: some record black and maroon, while the First Captain Paracelius was described as wearing sombre grey during the defence of Baal.[2][3]

    Alliances

    [edit]

    The Charnel Guard have been members of the Maelstrom Warders, an alliance of Space Marine Chapters that guards the dangerous region of the Ultima Segmentum known as the Maelstrom Zone, where Chaos Renegades gather around a Warp rift of the same name.[1]

    The Charnel Guard also belong to the "Triarchy", a partnership of Blood Angels successors that includes the Angels Numinous and the Red Seraphs. The last two are the successors of the Blood Eagles Chapter, which tore itself in two during an unknown conflict. The Triarchy has a ritual phrase, "We are Three, drawn from Two, born of One", which is spoken before any conclave begins. Its representatives meet at the abandoned homeworld of the Blood Eagles.[3]

    The Triarchy in the Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    The Triarchy was formed to fight the enemies of the Emperor wherever they could be found in the Imperium Nihilus. It did so after the Great Rift split the Imperium in two during the Era Indomitus. At one such conclave, the representatives of the three Chapters, Ynoch of the Red Seraphs, Jagudir of the Angels Numinous, and Mordath of the Charnel Guard, discussed how to cope with their growing isolation. The Chapters could no longer cross the Great Rift and faced rising threats from xenos and the forces of Chaos.[3]

    They chose to set aside their traditions. Rather than defend their shrinking domains, they would seek out the enemies of the Emperor in the Dark Imperium and fight without restraint, even at the cost of their own deaths.[3][a]

    Defence of Baal

    [edit]

    In the closing days of the 41st millennium, the Charnel Guard were among the Blood Angels successors who went to defend the parent Chapter's homeworld of Baal against Hive Fleet Leviathan. Their flagship, the battle barge Invictrix, was destroyed in a void battle with the hive ships. Whether First Captain Paracelius survived its loss is not recorded.[3]
