    Battlefleet Gothic
    Battlefleet of the Imperial Navy
    Badge of the Imperial Navy
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    TypeBattlefleet
    Parent formationBattlefleet Obscurus
    AreaGothic Sector, Segmentum Obscurus
    Known forThe Gothic War (the 12th Black Crusade), 139–160.M41
    Notable engagementDefence against the 13th Black Crusade (999.M41)

    Battlefleet Gothic is a large fleet of Imperial Navy vessels charged with protecting the Gothic Sector in the Segmentum Obscurus. It forms one of the Bastion Fleets of the greater Battlefleet Obscurus, alongside its neighbours Battlefleets Tamahl and Odessa and the more distant Battlefleets Cadia, Agripinaa and Scarus. The Gothic Sector lies beside the Eye of Terror. It was the scene of the 12th Black Crusade, known as the Gothic War, fought from 139.M41 to 160.M41. The forces of Chaos were defeated in that conflict. Abaddon the Despoiler managed to seize several Blackstone Fortresses, which he used to bring a more successful conclusion over eight centuries later in the 13th Black Crusade of 999.M41.[1]

    The Segmentum Obscurus

    [edit]

    The Segmentum Obscurus lies to the galactic north of Terra. It is the home of the Eye of Terror, the main base of the forces of Chaos in the galaxy. The Imperial Navy's Segmentum fleet headquarters is at Cypra Mundi. The Segmentum is the area of the Imperium most often under threat from the Eye of Terror, and it is the site of all of Abaddon's Black Crusades. It therefore holds the greater part of the Imperium's armed forces.[2]

    Battlefleet Obscurus must be far more active than most Segmentum battlefleets. The Cadian Gate is a constant source of turmoil, and the whole sector lives in an almost permanent state of readiness. Many new, inexperienced ratings must be recruited to replace those lost in the fighting. Those who survive their harsh first years of service near the Cadian Gate mature quickly into some of the most capable leaders in the Imperium.[2]

    Reserve fleets

    [edit]

    As with other militarised zones, the Imperial Navy keeps large reserves of vessels around the Eye of Terror. The fleet does not have the manpower to keep reserve crews aboard them. Instead, whole orbital shipyards are filled with rows of silent, inactive ships, often of outdated or scarce classes. In times of great need these ships are brought into service and crewed with ratings from lost or crippled vessels, or with hastily mustered recruits. Crews rarely know their new vessel. A posting to a reserve fleet is considered an unnerving duty. Much superstition surrounds such fleets, and strange behaviour is common in both crew and ship.[2]

    Reserve fleets are used only reluctantly, in the most desperate circumstances. The invasion of the 13th Black Crusade is held to be such a circumstance. In a reserve fleet, mass panic, mutiny, unexplained noises and sensor readings and failures of machinery are all more likely, and against Chaos Warfleets the followers of the Dark Gods can use their influence to unsettle their already nervous opponents further.[2]

    The Gothic War

    [edit]

    The Gothic Sector's most important feature was that it held six known Blackstone Fortresses. These are xenos artefacts built by the Old Ones as weapons against the Necrons during the War in Heaven, over 65 million standard years ago. When Mankind rediscovered the Gothic worlds during the Great Crusade, the Imperium found the Blackstone Fortresses and in time turned them into naval bases.[1]

    During the Gothic War, capturing the fortresses became Abaddon's main objective. He took control of three of the six. He used their xenos technology to strip one planet of its atmosphere and to turn another star into a supernova that destroyed its planetary system. Unknown to the Imperium, Abaddon escaped into the Warp with two of the three captured fortresses, which later played a pivotal part in the 13th Black Crusade.[1]

    A catastrophic Warp storm broke over the sector in 142.M41 and cut it off from outside communication and from Imperial reinforcements. The defence fell to the Imperial ground and naval forces already present. Battlefleet Gothic, the principal Imperial Navy force in the sector, fought for the following two decades against a series of Chaos Warfleets numbering from eight to twenty, led by a variety of Chaos Warmasters, Abaddon among them. The fleet also had to guard against Aeldari, Ork and human pirates who sought to profit from the crisis.[1]

    In 151.M41 the Warp storms over the sector began to abate, as the grip of the Ruinous Powers weakened after several Imperial victories. Segmentum Command rushed in reinforcements, including detachments from several Space Marine Chapters. Elements of the Craftworld Aeldari, who feared Chaos taking the Blackstone Fortresses, joined the Imperium against the forces of Chaos. Abaddon's own warfleet was pursued into the Schindelgeist System, where the last great battle of the war ended in a famous victory for the Imperial Navy. By 160.M41 the sector was stable and cleansed of the remaining Chaos, Ork and pirate forces.[3]

    The 13th Black Crusade

    [edit]

    In 999.M41 Abaddon launched the 13th Black Crusade, which opened in the Belis Corona Sub-sector with plague-ridden Space hulks entering Imperial space. A fleet of a size not seen since the Gothic War was dispatched from Cypra Mundi to Belis Corona, and its battlegroups were assigned to the sectors most in need. Lord Admiral Quarren, commanding Navy assets in the Cadian System, defeated Chaos warfleets many times the size of his own, and the whole of Battlefleet Gothic entered the fight, with the chance to avenge the losses it had suffered at Abaddon's hands in the Gothic War. The Navy held space at the end of the campaign's formal operations, though Cadia remained besieged.[3]

    Notable vessels

    [edit]

    The flagship of the battlefleet is the Divine Right, an Emperor-class battleship built at the Cypra Mundi dockyards between 234.M36 and 298.M36. She served as flagship of Battlefleet Artemis from 306.M36 to 457.M37 and has led Battlefleet Gothic since 458.M37. Her prizes include the Chaos battleship Harbinger of Anarchy and the Chaos grand cruiser Restless Vengeance. She took part in the defence of the sector in the Gothic War.[3]
