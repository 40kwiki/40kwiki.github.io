    The Marines Malevolent are a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes founded in the 32nd millennium from gene-seed of unknown origin. They are loyal to the Emperor of Mankind and devoted to the destruction of the Imperium's enemies, and their war record is, by any count, exemplary. They are better known for something else: a callous disregard for the civilians and fellow Imperial forces who stand in their way.[1]

    That disregard has brought the Chapter into conflict with the Salamanders and other Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes, and into censure by the Inquisition. Several Imperial commanders refuse to serve beside them. The Chapter does not answer the charge, and its own sayings say plainly that it does not accept the idea of innocence.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Macharian Crusade and Heresy

    [edit]
    See also: Macharian Crusade

    The Marines Malevolent committed the whole Chapter to the Macharian Crusade (392–399.M41) under Lord Solar Macharius, and fought through many of its deadliest actions beside the Star Phantoms. After Macharius died in 400.M41 the conquests fell into civil war, and the two Chapters turned on each other. The Star Phantoms fought the Marines Malevolent in a long and bloody conflict that nearly destroyed them. The Chapter was in the end cleared of heresy by the Ordos but censured for its conduct.[1]

    Archimedes Rex

    [edit]

    In an undated incident of the 41st millennium, a squad of Marines Malevolent under Veteran Sergeant Lorkar boarded the derelict Mechanicus forge ship Archimedes Rex to take salvage from its armouries, against the traditional agreement that such wargear be returned to the Adeptus Mechanicus. They found a Salamanders rescue force already aboard. Captain Vinyar's men withdrew only when the Salamanders' strike cruiser Vulkan's Wrath threatened them. After the cruiser crashed on the world Scoria, Vinyar came to the aid of the Salamanders' 3rd Company, but only after its captain, N'Keln, agreed to return the salvage. Tu'Shan, the Salamanders' Chapter Master, later faced Vinyar on Nocturne.[1]

    Third War for Armageddon

    [edit]
    Main article: Wars for Armageddon

    In 998.M41 two companies of the Chapter fought in the Third War for Armageddon. The 2nd Company under Vinyar was censured by the Inquisition for attacks that killed thousands of civilians. At the Emperor's Deliverance camp for refugees from Hades Hive, Vinyar waited for an Ork warband to break through the Imperial line and begin the slaughter, and then shelled the camp with Whirlwind artillery. The shrapnel pinned and shredded the Orks, and the Chapter destroyed the horde in six solar hours, at the cost of thousands of refugees. The Chapter's elite Vilifiers Company under Captain Kastor also took part in a rescue of captured Imperial officers from the ruins of Hades Hive, beside a force of Black Templars.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Captain Vorr's company has taken part in cleansing the daemonic taint from the Fenris System. In an undated action of the early 42nd millennium called Duty Unto Death, a relief force of Marines Malevolent drove two wayward Tyranid hive ships from the death world Loqe II, where a single wounded Custodian Warden still held a fortress of dead Tyranids around a store of Primaris gene-technology that his fellows had died to protect.[1]

    Organisation and beliefs

    [edit]

    The Chapter holds that there is no innocence, only degrees of guilt, and that those who will not fight for themselves have no right to live, since their freedom was bought with others' blood. It salvages gear from the fallen of other Chapters when it finds it. It follows some of the anti-psyker tenets laid down at the Council of Nikaea, so its Librarians hold no command.[1]

    The Chapter's armour is mostly ancient Mark VI Corvus pattern, with old bolters. Its raid on the Archimedes Rex suggests that it cannot easily replace or repair them, and may lack the means to produce newer wargear. Captain Vinyar's strike cruiser is the Purgatory.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Vinyar, Captain of the 2nd Company, responsible for the shelling at Emperor's Deliverance and the Archimedes Rex incident.
    • Kastor, Captain of the Vilifiers Company.
    • Vorr, Captain, who cleansed the daemonic taint of the Fenris System.
    • Lorkar, Veteran Sergeant, and Harkane, Techmarine, who boarded the Archimedes Rex.
    • Ballack, a Veteran of the Vilifiers who fell in a blood feud with a Black Templars sergeant's brother.
