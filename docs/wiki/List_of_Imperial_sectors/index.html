    List of Imperial sectors
    Sectors and regions of the Imperium of Mankind
    DivisionsFive segmenta, with the Imperium Sanctus and Imperium Nihilus as the two halves of Imperial space since the Great Rift
    EntriesAbout seventy named sectors and regions, of which some sixty are placed in a segmentum
    Compiled byThe Administratum, from the Departmento Sectorum registers
    Status dateImperial Dating, M42, as recorded in the registers of the Era Indomitus

    This list names the sectors, regions and frontier zones of the Imperium of Mankind that appear in the registers of the Administratum. Entries are grouped under the five segmenta to which they belong, followed by a final section for sectors whose segmentum is disputed or unrecorded, and for the notes that apply to the Imperium Sanctus and the Imperium Nihilus. The status column gives the condition of each sector as the most recent registers of the 42nd millennium record it.[1]

    The list is not complete. Sectors are redrawn often, as worlds are lost to Chaos and xenos, reconquered by crusades, or cut off by the Warp. Many sectors on the frontiers appear in the registers only by name, with no recorded worlds or dates. Where a name is known only from a fragmentary record, the entry says so rather than guess. Where a sector is the subject of its own article, the name links to it. Other names are linked as red links, marking articles still to be written.[1]

    Dates are Imperial. A date marked c. is an estimate drawn from the sequence of events in the registers, and the note at the foot of each table explains how it was arrived at. Where archives disagree, the Administratum's accepted date is given, with the earlier or disputed record named in the notes.[a]

    Segmentum Solar

    [edit]
    Main article: Segmentum

    The Segmentum Solar holds the Throneworld and the most densely settled sectors of the Imperium. Its sectors are the best defended and the most regularly inspected, and its battlefleet is the largest of the five. The sectors below lie in or around the Sol System and along its western and northern borders.[1]

    Segmentum Obscurus

    [edit]
    Main article: Segmentum

    The Segmentum Obscurus lies to the galactic north, beside the Eye of Terror. Its sectors have borne the brunt of the Black Crusades since the Age of Apostasy, and most of the Imperium's armed forces were raised from them. The Cadian Gate and the Calixis Sector are among its best-known regions.[1]

    Segmentum Ultima

    [edit]
    Main article: Segmentum

    The Segmentum Ultima is the largest of the five segmenta and the most thinly settled. It holds the Realm of Ultramar, the Eastern Fringe and the Maelstrom Zone, and roughly half of it lies beyond the Great Rift in the Imperium Nihilus. Its sectors face Ork empires, T'au expansion and Tyranid incursions.[1]

    Segmentum Tempestus

    [edit]
    Main article: Segmentum

    The Segmentum Tempestus lies to the galactic south. Its Fortress is Bakka, and its sectors have a long reputation for wealth and for the independence, and sometimes the corruption, of their officials. The southern edge is the Veiled Region, a stretch of unmapped space whose sectors are often cut off for years at a time.[1]

    Segmentum Pacificus

    [edit]
    Main article: Segmentum

    The Segmentum Pacificus lies to the galactic west, and despite its name it has been among the most troubled regions of the Imperium. Its Fortress is Hydraphur. It borders the Halo Stars and the Veiled Region, and its sectors were made famous by the campaigns of Macharius.[1]

    Imperium Nihilus and Imperium Sanctus notes

    [edit]

    Since the formation of the Great Rift (the Cicatrix Maledictum) at the climax of the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41, the Imperium has been divided into two halves. The Imperium Sanctus lies to the south and west, where the Astronomican is still visible to Navigators. The Imperium Nihilus, the "Dark Imperium", lies to the north and east, mostly in the Segmentum Obscurus and Segmentum Ultima. The sectors on either side are listed above. The two halves are joined only by the Nachmund Gauntlet, one of the few known passages through the Rift.[1]
