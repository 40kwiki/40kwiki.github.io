    Ordo Scriptorum
    Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition
    The Inquisitorial I, the symbol of the Holy Ordos
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    Organisation
    Known as"The Unsleeping Eye"
    RemitImperial records and communiqués; the "Wars of the Quill"
    SeatTerra, one of seventeen Ordos based there
    FoundedUnrecorded
    Sibling ordosOrdo Originatus, Ordo Redactus

    The Ordo Scriptorum, known as the Unsleeping Eye, is an Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition dedicated to examining and investigating Imperial records and communiqués. It is one of the seventeen minor Ordos that have their seat on Terra, and it also oversees the occasional conflicts between rival sects of Imperial scribes, called the Wars of the Quill.[1]

    The work can seem small beside the labours of the Ordo Malleus or Ordo Xenos. The Ordo's Inquisitors would say that it is not.

    Remit

    [edit]

    The weight of the record

    [edit]

    The Imperium strains under its own bureaucracy, and the Administratum is the heaviest part of it. Vital information is lost or misfiled through human error. A Planetary Governor whose world is in uproar may look to the sky for help, not knowing that his plea has been bound into the spine of an eight-hundred-page report on lumen design. A fleet of hundreds of void ships may hang motionless in space because the orders updating it have gone from desk to desk and never reached anyone with the authority to sign them.[1]

    The Ordo can catch only a handful of such failures. A single one may be the difference between life and death for billions.[1]

    The Wars of the Quill

    [edit]

    Rival sects of scribes and archivists sometimes fall to quarrelling over precedence, interpretation and control of a record, until the quarrel disturbs the work of the Imperium. The Ordo oversees these Wars of the Quill. Records name no particular war and give no date.[1]

    Methods

    [edit]

    The Ordo's chief weapon is attention. It is enough for a scribe to learn that an Inquisitor is reading his work to make him careful. The records say that the mere rumour of supervision reduces errors for years to come. This is what the Ordo is for: not to catch every fault, but to make faults rarer.[1]

    Relations

    [edit]

    The Ordo is a natural partner of the Ordo Originatus, which tries to establish the truth of the Imperium's past, and a natural rival of the Ordo Redactus, which hides it. Imperial rumour says that the Ordo Scriptorum is thought to keep copies of records others would like to see lost. It has not been confirmed. The Ordo's relations with the Logos Historica Verita are unrecorded.[1]

    The Ordo's Inquisitors are few, and one does not hear of them sitting in conclave. Its Terran seat is held to put it close to the records of the Administratum, where it works.[1]

    Place among the Ordos

    [edit]
    See also: Ordos Minoris

    The Ordo is one of the seventeen minor Ordos said to be seated on Terra itself, which indicates that its task is as old as the Imperium's bureaucracy and that the Inquisition sees it as lasting. Most Ordos Minoris are formed against a particular threat and may lie idle for years once it passes. The Ordo Scriptorum has no such end, since there will always be records.[1]

    Members

    [edit]

    No Inquisitor of the Ordo is named.

    Legacy

    [edit]

    The size of the Imperium's records is greater than any Ordo can read. In the Era Indomitus, with the Rift cutting the Imperium in half and the Astronomican's light failing, the delay in communications has grown. The Ordo's work has become, if anything, more important, though no record says what it has done.[1]
