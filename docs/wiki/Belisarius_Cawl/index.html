    Belisarius Cawl is an Archmagos Dominus of the Adeptus Mechanicus, one of the ruling lords of the Priesthood of Mars, and a man who has lived for more than ten thousand years. He is the creator of the Primaris Space Marines, the architect of the Ultima Founding, a maker of new weapons and vehicles for the Imperium, and one of the two persons chiefly responsible for the resurrection of the primarch Roboute Guilliman in 999.M41. He played a decisive part in the events of the 13th Black Crusade, the Celestinian Crusade and the Terran Crusade, and in the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade. He is now at work in the Nephilim Sector, where he seeks to adapt the blackstone technology of the Necrons to close the Great Rift.[1][2]

    Cawl is a figure of fascination and suspicion. His work has saved the Imperium on several occasions, and it has made him many enemies on Mars. The traditionalists of the Priesthood regard him as an arch-heretek who has dealt with xenos, used forbidden technology and rejected the commandments of the Machine God. The Lord Commander, who owes him his life, is wary of him for the same reasons. Cawl holds that it is envy that moves them.[3][1]

    Appearance and nature

    [edit]

    Cawl is a monster of flesh and iron, wrapped in the red robes of Mars, who walks on a multitude of metal legs and stands several times the height of a man. His many arms end in energy weapons, piston claws and instruments that can peel a tank like a ration tin. His eyelenses glow beneath a heavy cowl, and they study the weaknesses of those he fights with a cool, insect gaze. He speaks the binharic cants of the Lingua-technis so well that he can reinvigorate a machine spirit or stop a reactor with a phrase. In his youth he rejected the augmetic changes that others made, and held to the Emperor's teaching of the superiority of the human form. Over ten thousand years he changed his body until it was more machine than man, and he added to his mind, by soul-merging, so many other minds that he is now best described as a mesh of souls rather than a single person. He has lost much of his memory, for his intelligence core could hold only so much and he had to dump data every five hundred years or so, and twice he has been mindwiped. He remains a savant in whatever field he turns his mind to.[3][2]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins and the Heresy

    [edit]

    Cawl was born on Mars, in the birthing facilities of Xanthe Terra, under the Magos Biologis Hammareth, early in the 31st millennium. Like most Martian children he was grown in a vat, and was an adolescent in body and mind at the time of his birth. Unlike most, he could speak, for he had absorbed more fully the data that is uploaded into every Mechanicum foetus. His first recorded appearance is as a tech-acolyte on the Mechanicum world of Trisolian-A4, under the Magos Domina Hester Aspertia Sigma-Sigma, a master of soul-merging, the duplication of a person's mind and its fusion with another by cybernetic implants. His closest companion was the tech-priest Friedisch Adum Ship Qvo. He believed that the Emperor was the Omnissiah, as the orthodoxy of the Mechanicum held, but he disagreed with most of the rest of its dogma. He believed that the quest for knowledge meant innovation and not the ritual maintenance of what was already known. He privately had illegal surgery to raise his own intelligence.[3]

    When Horus attacked Trisolian, Aspertia went over to the Warmaster. Cawl feigned submission, killed her with Friedisch in the course of the battle for the world, and, before he left, merged his mind with her intelligence core, and so inherited her art of soul-merging and cloning. He and Friedisch fled to Terra. There he was recalled under a pretext by Ezekiel Sedayne, the second director of the Biotechnical Division, who had grown so old that the rejuvenat could no longer keep him alive. Sedayne wanted Cawl's art, in the hope that his own mind, merged with Cawl's, would be dominant. To compel him, he had his servant shoot Friedisch, and promised to restore him only if Cawl agreed. The merger was made. Sedayne's mind was overwhelmed. Cawl woke with the memory of the day when the Emperor first saw Sedayne, early in the Unification Wars, and called him Belisarius Cawl, not by his own name, for the Emperor had foreseen the end of Sedayne hundreds of years before. In the memory the Emperor told the future Cawl that it was his to continue the Emperor's work, even on the day when he thought that he had betrayed all that the Emperor stood for.[3]

    The two tasks

    [edit]

    After the Heresy, Guilliman, then the first Lord Commander and Regent, gave Cawl two charges in case the primarch should fall. One was the making of the Armour of Fate, a suit of artificer armour that would sustain its wearer through any wound. The other was the keeping of the Emperor's Sword, the blade given to Guilliman by the Emperor, which held a fragment of his power. A third charge, the making of a new kind of Space Marine, was begun in the same period. It took ten thousand years, in the underground complexes of Mars, and used the Sangprimus Portum, a device that held genetic material taken from the primarchs, and the knowledge of Sedayne. The Primaris Space Marines were kept in stasis until the 41st millennium.[3]

    Prime Conduit

    [edit]

    In the Priesthood of Mars, such beings as Cawl, who hold in themselves the learning of many fields, are called Prime Conduits of the Omnissiah, or Dominatus Dominus, the Master of Masters. Of the few who survive, he is by far the most active. He has dozens of projects at the same time, from the construction of the largest macro-cannon on Mars to the perfection of stasis fields, and he has added to himself many independent minds to track them and keeps them hidden from his rivals in the Priesthood. After a period of reloading some of his own memories, he is subject to forgetfulness, and spends solar decades in his stasis crypts. When he emerges he takes the field as an archmagos dominus, with Skitarii legions and maniples of the Legio Cybernetica, and other forge worlds may place troops under his command if he promises to share what he learns. He has invented dozens of weapons, found scores of Standard Template Construct databases, and once went into the Eye of Terror.[2][3]

    The 13th Black Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Thirteenth Black Crusade

    In 999.M41, at the time of the 13th Black Crusade, Cawl was in command of the Ark Mechanicus Iron Revenant and a large fleet, with Skitarii and Kataphron servitors. The Harlequin Shadowseer Sylandri Veilwalker guided him to Eriad VI, an old Navy base abandoned in the 4th Black Crusade, which was also a Necron tomb world. There he recovered an ancient Necron artefact, and learned of the nature of the Cadian pylons, which the Necrons had made to hold back the eruptions of the Warp, the Eye of Terror among them. He went on to Cadia on the instructions of the Shadowseer, and arrived at a time when it was besieged by Abaddon and his forces. His arrival brought Lord Castellan Ursarkar Creed much needed reinforcements.[3]

    On Cadia he studied the pylons. There the Necron Overlord Trazyn the Infinite, who had decided to aid mankind against the enemy in the Warp, made himself known. Cawl was wary of the xenos, but his interest in the advanced technology overcame his hostility, and he accepted the help. With Trazyn's knowledge, he found how to use the pylons to cut a region of space from the Warp, and at the height of the battle between Abaddon and Creed's defenders he activated them. The Necron technology worked as expected. Cadia was cut off from the Immaterium, daemons were banished, and the Eye of Terror shrank. It gave the defenders a chance of yet another unlikely victory.[3]

    Abaddon would not be denied, and had foreseen the need for a second plan. He had the damaged bulk of the Blackstone Fortress Will of Eternity, whose systems had been disabled by the star fort Phalanx of the Imperial Fists, thrown from orbit as a meteor onto the surface of Cadia. The impact destroyed the world's defences and left it in the grip of the expanding Eye. The Lord Castellan ordered the evacuation of the world. The survivors, led by Cawl, Saint Celestine, Inquisitor Katarinya Greyfax and Marshal Marius Amalrich of the Black Templars, who called themselves the Celestinian Crusade, loaded some three million people on every voidship they had, including the Iron Revenant. They could not enter the Warp and fled at sub-light speed, with the Chaos fleets after them. The Iron Revenant, the largest and most powerful of the ships left, gave itself in a battle with the Vengeful Spirit, so that the others could reach the ice moon of Klaisus, near Kasr Holn. There, at the last, the survivors were saved by the arrival of the Ynnari through a hidden Webway gate. Cawl had lost his flagship, but he had saved its most precious cargo, the Armour of Fate.[3]

    The resurrection of Guilliman

    [edit]
    See also: Roboute Guilliman

    The Ynnari led the Celestinians through the Webway to the Realm of Ultramar, which was also under attack from the forces of the Despoiler. After some distrust, they were taken in by the Ultramarines on the capital world, Macragge. In the Ultramarines' Fortress of Hera, Cawl revealed the contents of the auto-reliquary he carried. He declared that they were to bring Roboute Guilliman back to life from the mortal wound that had held him in stasis for ten thousand years in the Temple of Correction. With the Chaos forces attacking the temple, Cawl worked with Yvraine, Daughter of Shades and priestess of Ynnead, the god of the Aeldari dead, to restore the primarch's life-force, while his own technology healed the wound. Guilliman rose from his throne.[3][1]

    Cawl went with the primarch in the Terran Crusade, and his skill and technical knowledge helped to bring the Crusade through the Maelstrom. At the end, when the survivors reached the Sol System by the Webway at Luna, Cawl was one of the few who had survived the Celestinian and Terran crusades both. After the defeat of Magnus the Red on the Moon, he went with the crusade to the Imperial Palace. Before the Eternity Gate he spoke to his fellow Mechanicus and to the primarch, in terms that were cryptic, of secret pacts and of long works at last drawing to completion. He then went back to Mars.[3]

    The Ultima Founding

    [edit]
    Main article: Ultima Founding

    The works that Cawl spoke of were the Primaris Space Marines. For ten thousand years he had worked on the task that Guilliman gave him before the Battle of Thessala, a new body of transhuman warriors. When it was finished, in the days after the Terran Crusade, he released them from their stasis. This was the Ultima Founding. Approximately half the Primaris Marines formed new Chapters, and the other half were formed into nine Legion-sized armies, the Unnumbered Sons. He also designed the Mark X power armour, the Mark II bolt rifle that bears his name, the Mark III plasma incinerator, the Redemptor Dreadnought, the Overlord gunship and the Repulsor grav-tank. See Primaris Space Marines.[3]

    The Indomitus Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Indomitus Crusade

    In the Indomitus Crusade, Cawl was given free rein to innovate. He re-fitted Guilliman's flagship, the Macragge's Honour, with new technologies, and made several vehicles and weapons for the crusade. Aboard his Ark Mechanicus Zar-Quaesitor he took with Fleet Primus a vast store of Primaris Marines in stasis, which his workers woke as the crusade advanced, working them at such speed that their circuits were at risk. When the holds of the ship were empty he left, for he had other vaults to open. At Baal, the Mechanicus sent by Cawl installed the apparatus by which the Blood Angels could make Primaris Marines of their own.[1][4]

    He sought to persuade Guilliman to let him use the gene-seed of the nine Traitor Legions and of the two lost Legions, the Second and the Eleventh. He argued that there was nothing wrong with their gene-seed, and that the fault was in their primarchs. Guilliman refused, and ordered him to go no further. He knew that Cawl would very likely carry on without him, for all magi hunger for knowledge and cannot easily keep from using it, and on this subject he did not trust him at all.[3]

    Tensions in the Mechanicus

    [edit]

    Cawl has asked repeatedly for Guilliman to make him Fabricator-General of Mars. When the primarch took the Regency, he replaced five of the High Twelve of the High Lords of Terra and hundreds of the lesser lords, and Cawl asked why he should not replace one more. Guilliman answered that the Treaty of Mars forbade the Imperium to impose its will on the Mechanicus, and that the priests would not accept an appointee. He added that such an act would cause a civil war among the forge worlds. A small faction of tech-priests, who are Cawl's disciples and who style innovation as a faith, has formed around him. They have made enemies of the conservatives of the Priesthood, and Guilliman has had to consider whether the path of innovation could lead Cawl to the same damnation as the original Dark Mechanicum.[3]

    The Cawl Inferior

    [edit]

    To free himself for his research, Cawl built at least two machines, each known as the Cawl Inferior. One is hidden in the depths of the Macragge's Honour, in a chamber where only the primarch is allowed to go. The other is on his own ark. It consists of the living heads of servitors, deck-sized psychically active cogitators and parts of xenos origin, and it is operated by a lone master astropath, Guidus Losenti, who prefers never to be in its presence. Through the Inferiors Cawl and Guilliman talk without the corruption that has plagued astrotelepathy since the Rift. The machine mimics Cawl's voice and manner, insists that it is a separate entity and sometimes shows a sardonic humour. Cawl says that it is a limited expression of his own augmented mind, whose answers he worked out beforehand, and that it breaks none of the Mechanicus's prohibitions on artificial intelligence. Guilliman does not believe it, and thinks that it is a machine intelligence in disguise.[3]

    Sotha

    [edit]

    The opening of the Great Rift woke an ancient Necron beacon, the Pharos, on the world of Sotha in the Ultima Segmentum. It was found by the Scythes of the Emperor, who guarded the ruins of Sotha after its destruction by a Genestealer cult and Hive Fleet Kraken in the Second Tyrannic War. Cawl led an expedition there, with Ultramarines and the Scythes, to study the Pharos and perhaps to use it to close the Rift. The expedition was attacked by Genestealer cultists among the Chapter's serfs, and the Chapter Master, Thracius, and all the remaining Firstborn Marines of the Chapter were killed with the Genestealer Patriarch. At the Pharos, Cawl found that the beacon was powered by a shard of the C'tan Zarhulash the Potentate. He made a bargain with the star-god, in which Zarhulash would destroy the device by removing its heart, and Cawl would remove the necrodermis shackles that held the shard and open a portal for him. Cawl agreed, and instead teleported the shard to a part of the galaxy surrounded by hostile tomb worlds. Zarhulash has sworn revenge. Cawl escaped with data from the Pharos' heart, including the places of other Pharos devices and of large deposits of blackstone.[3]

    War with the Necrons

    [edit]
    See also: Necrons

    After the Rift opened, the great influx of Warp energy set off the anti-Warp protocols of every Necron tomb world, and the Mechanicus holdings, which were often on sites of blackstone, came under attack across the Imperium. The Technomandrites of Magistrakh, who had been put down in the early days of the Necrontyr, returned to seek vengeance. Cawl, who alone understood the reason for the attacks, sent armies of Mars and House Taranis to hold the line, and hundreds of battles were fought. Many of the tech-priests, who feared being called Hereteks, would not ask the Imperium for help.[3]

    Guilliman, in the closing stage of the first phase of the crusade, gave Cawl a new task: to find how the blackstone technology of the Necrons could be used to seal the Rift. In the Nephilim Sector Cawl has now taken command of Battlegroup Hephaestus, which has a large fleet of the Mechanicus. His order, the Noctilith Decree, divides his ships between the aegis fleets that hold the ground and the accretion fleets that gather blackstone for the liminal abraisers he is building. He has brought with him radical tech-priests who are ready to bring experimental and forbidden weapons into the Pariah Nexus. When the Necron civil war began, he deduced its cause, and tried to end the fighting, but some of his magi paid no heed. See Pariah Nexus.[5]

    Wargear

    [edit]
    • Arc Scourge: a weapon of technobanishment that exorcises the machine spirits of Heretic war engines with jolts of lightning.
    • Solar Atomiser: a melta weapon of Cawl's design that focuses melta-waves into a short, searing beam.
    • Master-crafted Omnisian Axe: the Mechanicus version of the power axe.
    • Mechadendrite Hive: a cluster of mechanical tentacles that throttle and disassemble the victim.
    • Scryerskull: a servo-skull that transmits the strengths and weaknesses of the battlefield.
    • Refractor Field: a field that refracts attacks into harmless bursts.
    • Cawl Inferior: the digital construct described above.

    Assessment

    [edit]

    The creation of the Primaris Space Marines and the resurrection of Guilliman are the greatest works of the Era Indomitus, and both are Cawl's. He is also a danger. He has dealt with xenos, used forbidden technology, built a machine that may be an artificial intelligence, and wished to use the gene-seed of the Traitors. Guilliman has told him in no uncertain terms where the limit lies, and has said that he knows that he will have to be harsh with him one day. Cawl himself regards the Emperor's charge to him, to continue his work even if he comes to think he has betrayed it, as the justification for all that he has done.[3][1]
