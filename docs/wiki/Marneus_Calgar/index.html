    Marneus Augustus Calgar, styled Lord Macragge and since the return of his primarch the Lord Defender of Greater Ultramar, is the Chapter Master of the Ultramarines. He has led the Chapter through the most dangerous two and a half centuries of its history since the Horus Heresy, including the first invasion of the galaxy by the Tyranids at the Battle of Macragge in 745.M41. The deeds recorded in his name fill twenty-eight volumes in the archive of the Fortress of Hera, twice as many as any other Ultramarine except Roboute Guilliman himself.[1][2]

    Calgar is regarded across the Adeptus Astartes as the model of a Chapter Master who follows the Codex Astartes: a patient strategist who spends his warriors carefully and leads in person when the decisive blow falls. Among his recorded feats are the boarding of the hive ship Behemoth Primus, the defence of a gate at Zalathras against an Ork horde for a night and a day, the slaying of an Avatar of Khaine at Orar's Sepulchre, and the defeat of the daemon prince M'kar the Reborn.[1]

    In 999.M41 Calgar commanded the defence of Macragge when the Black Legion struck at Ultramar and permitted the Celestinian Crusade to bring its reliquary to the Temple of Correction, where Roboute Guilliman was restored to life. He surrendered his authority over the Chapter and the realm to his primarch. In the Era Indomitus he was the first of the Firstborn Space Marines to cross the Rubicon Primaris, an operation that killed him for twenty minutes, and he has since commanded the defence of Ultramar, the Imperial forces on Vigilus, and the eastern front against the renewed advance of Hive Fleet Leviathan.[3][4]

    Early life and rise

    [edit]

    Calgar was born on Macragge, the capital world of Ultramar, and was taken into the Ultramarines as an aspirant from among its people. The Chapter keeps few public records of his early service, and those that exist are partly contradictory. Ultramarines tradition holds that he served in the battle companies and rose through command of a company before his election as Chapter Master, an office he already held when the Tyranids reached the Eastern Fringe in the mid-41st millennium.[1][a]

    Chapter Master

    [edit]
    Main article: Chapter Master

    The First Tyrannic War

    [edit]
    Main article: Battle of Macragge

    When Hive Fleet Behemoth fell upon Ultramar in 745.M41, Calgar placed the defences of Macragge at full readiness and divided his forces into three armies. Two garrisoned the polar fortresses; the third, under his personal command, fought a mobile campaign of ambushes and fighting retreats across the ice fields. At Cold Steel Ridge the Ultramarines met the creature later named the Swarmlord, whose grasp of tactics matched their own. The beast overran the Ultramarines' positions and struck Calgar down. His Honour Guard closed around him and held the creature back until gunships could lift him to the battle-barge Octavius; Brother Aloysius was cut down covering the withdrawal.[1]

    Calgar resumed command from orbit within hours. He broke the Tyranid fleet against the guns of the polar fortresses and pursued it to the system of Circe, where Battlefleet Bakka trapped the bio-ships between two fleets and the Emperor-class battleship Dominus Astra destroyed the remnant by detonating its own warp drive. When Calgar's ships returned to Macragge, the 1st Company had died to the last man in the defence of the northern fortress. The Ultramarines' victory came at the cost of the Chapter's veteran company, and the survivors were reformed, against the strict letter of the Codex, into the Tyrannic War Veterans, specialists in fighting the Great Devourer.[1][2]

    Campaigns of the 41st millennium

    [edit]

    The Chapter recovered under Calgar's command over the following century. In 762.M41 he led its remnants against the daemon-corrupted forge world of Thrax, recovered its data-vaults and then ordered Exterminatus. In 797.M41, at the hive city of Zalathras, he held a breached gate alone against an Ork horde for a night and a day. In 888.M41 he led the entire Chapter to the world of Commrath to guard the tomb of the Ultramarines hero Captain Orar against the Aeldari of Alaitoc and Iyanden. On the second day of the battle an Avatar of Khaine led the assault up the stairs of the sepulchre. Calgar challenged it, was driven to one knee, caught its fourth blow on his gauntlet and drove his other fist through the creature's molten body. The Aeldari withdrew, and Calgar had the relic they sought removed to Macragge.[1]

    In 921.M41 his ship was ambushed by a Night Lords warband and crippled. Calgar came down on the feudal world of Barathred and raised its people to defeat the several hundred Traitor Astartes who followed him. In 935.M41 he boarded the star fort Indomitable, seized by the daemon prince M'kar the Reborn, a former Word Bearers Chaplain, and defeated him in single combat. The Chapter's public record stated that Calgar had torn the daemon apart and cast him into the Warp. In truth he could not be banished, and was bound into the fort's warp core, which was sent on a secret course through the uncharted margins of Ultramar.[1]

    In 993.M41 the Ultramarines met Hive Fleet Kraken on Ichar IV. Calgar and Chaplain Ortan Cassius severed the swarm's synaptic control, and Calgar killed the reborn Swarmlord with his own hands. The deception about M'kar came to light in 997.M41, when the Iron Warriors Warsmith Honsou freed the daemon and joined him in an assault on Ultramar. Calgar met M'kar on the world of Talassar and destroyed him with a blade known as a Shard of Erebus, a weapon dating from the Battle of Calth. Ultramarines chroniclers record that the Chapter Master emerged from the affair a humbler man, and that his officers' loyalty to him was undiminished.[1]

    The return of the primarch

    [edit]

    In 999.M41, as the 13th Black Crusade destroyed Cadia, a fleet of the Black Legion and its allies descended on Macragge. Calgar had recalled the Ultramar Defence Fleet on the strength of visions received by his Chief Librarian, Varro Tigurius, and was fighting the invaders inside the walls of the Fortress of Hera when the survivors of Cadia arrived through the Webway. Among them were the Archmagos Belisarius Cawl, the Living Saint Celestine, the Inquisitor Katarinya Greyfax and the aeldari of the Ynnari.[3]

    First Captain Severus Agemman counselled that the newcomers be confined and their aliens destroyed. Tigurius instead vouched for them, declaring that his visions had foretold their coming. Calgar ruled that the pilgrims could bring Cawl's reliquary to the Temple of Correction under heavy guard, and that the Ynnari might accompany them. When the reliquary opened and the aeldari priestess Yvraine cut the cables that powered Guilliman's stasis field, Calgar believed that he had been betrayed, and ordered Tigurius to stop them. The Librarian refused. Calgar was borne down by a mass of Chaos Terminators and did not see his primarch rise; when the enemy fell back he found Guilliman standing over him.[3]

    In the strategium of the fortress Calgar, wounded and supported by two of his Honour Guard, knelt before Guilliman and gave him command of the Chapter in perpetuity. The Chapter Master's sanctum became the primarch's, and Guilliman was invested as Lord of Ultramar and Master of the Ultramarines. Over the following seven months Calgar fought under his primarch to free Macragge, leading the strikes that retook the orbital batteries of Magna Macragge Civitas. When Guilliman departed on the Terran Crusade, he left Calgar, Tigurius and Agemman to complete the reconquest of Ultramar.[3]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Lord Defender of Greater Ultramar

    [edit]
    See also: Ultramar

    Calgar remained Chapter Master throughout the Indomitus Crusade, since Guilliman's duties as Lord Commander of the Imperium kept him far from Macragge, but his title was changed from Master of Ultramar to Lord Defender of Greater Ultramar. Only the primarch is recognised as the realm's ruler. The new title reflected Guilliman's restoration of Ultramar to its borders of the Great Crusade, an expansion that placed many more worlds under Calgar's protection.[3]

    During the Plague Wars, when Mortarion and the Death Guard invaded Ultramar from the Scourge Stars, Calgar put down a series of uprisings and mutinies among the Ultramar Auxilia in the Illyrian region of Macragge, and later slowed an Ork offensive toward the forge world of Metalica with rapid strikes.[3]

    Crossing the Rubicon

    [edit]

    At some point in the early years of the Era Indomitus, Calgar volunteered to be the first of the Firstborn Space Marines to receive the additional organs of the Primaris Space Marines. The operation was performed by Belisarius Cawl himself, who warned the Chapter Master that it carried a 61.6 per cent probability of death. Calgar was opened from crown to heel, and the Magnificat, the Belisarian Furnace and the sinew coils were implanted. His metabolism had to be stopped and restarted to begin the transformation; he was clinically dead for more than twenty minutes before the Belisarian Furnace woke and returned him to life.[4]

    The procedure has since been refined and offered to other veterans, among them Commander Dante, Mephiston and Azrael. Calgar's survival was taken across the Adeptus Astartes as proof that the Firstborn and Primaris need not be separate brotherhoods.[4] After his ascension he took a suit of Mark X Gravis armour, the Armour of Heraclus, made to his larger frame.

    Vigilus

    [edit]
    Main article: War of Nightmares

    Calgar was given command of the Imperial forces on the sentinel world of Vigilus, which guards the Nachmund Gauntlet, one of the few stable passages across the Great Rift. In the multi-sided war remembered as the War of Beasts he faced Orks, Genestealer Cults, aeldari and Drukhari raiders, and at last the Black Legion force of the Chaos Lord Haarken Worldclaimer and the Despoiler himself. Calgar organised the world's defence as the "Vigilus Defiant", and the planet, though gravely damaged, remained in Imperial hands.[5]

    The war against Leviathan

    [edit]
    See also: Hive Fleet Leviathan

    The return of Hive Fleet Leviathan in the middle years of the crusade, striking from beneath the galactic plane, has drawn Calgar back to the war against the Great Devourer, the foe that first tested him at Macragge. Crusade records place him at the head of the Ultramarines' defence of the Bastior sub-sector, directing Imperial forces including the Adepta Sororitas to threatened worlds such as Derenis IV.[6]

    Status

    [edit]

    As of the latest Imperial records Calgar is alive and in active command. He remains Chapter Master of the Ultramarines and Lord Defender of Greater Ultramar, answering directly to Roboute Guilliman, and is a Primaris Space Marine. He continues to command the Chapter's campaigns in the defence of Ultramar and the eastern Imperium against Leviathan and the forces of Chaos.[3][6]

    Character

    [edit]

    Imperial accounts describe Calgar as fearless but never reckless. Under fire he assesses the situation before he seeks cover, and he leaves cover only when no tactically sound alternative remains. He is known for his reluctance to waste the lives of his warriors and for his command of information: Ultramarines witnesses describe him listening to petitioners while absorbing a stream of reports and issuing orders at the same time. The M'kar affair, in which he allowed a false account of his victory to stand for more than sixty years, is the only stain on his record that the Chapter acknowledges, and its exposure is said to have tempered his pride.[1]

    The people of Ultramar regard him with something close to reverence, and even after Guilliman's return the population treats Calgar as the embodiment of the realm's defiance. The Space Wolves, who are hostile to the Codex, regard him with a mixture of respect and irritation.[2]

    Wargear

    [edit]
    • The Gauntlets of Ultramar, a pair of ancient power fists with built-in bolters. Guilliman took them from a Chaos champion after the Gamalia Reclusiam massacre, and no artificer has been able to open their housings.
    • The Armour of Heraclus, a suit of Mark X Gravis armour made for him after his ascension, and the Armour of Antilochus, an ornate suit of Indomitus-pattern Terminator armour with a teleport homer, crafted by the tech-priests of Ademax Primus.
    • An Iron Halo, and a relic power sword from the Chapter armoury.
    • The Maximus, an ornate Land Raider believed to have served the Ultramarines since the Great Crusade, inscribed with passages from the Codex Astartes.

    Notes

    [edit]
    1. ^ A widely copied but discredited Departmento Munitorum broadsheet of the late 41st millennium claims that Calgar was rescued as a boy from a Tyranid attack, joined the Chapter as a menial in its armoury, and later lost all four limbs to the minor hive fleet Perseus in 976.M41. The Chapter's own records place Calgar in command at Macragge decades before any recorded Tyranid incursion, and no later Imperial account repeats the claim of near-total augmetic replacement. He does bear an augmetic eye.
