    Ordo Astartes
    Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition
    The Inquisitorial I, the symbol of the Holy Ordos
    Artwork © Games Workshop

    The Ordo Astartes is a minor order of the Inquisition founded in the 32nd millennium. The roll of the Ordos Minoris gives its area of vigilance as the Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes, and its established strength as fifty Inquisitors.[1]

    Remit

    [edit]

    The Space Marines are not under the authority of the Inquisition. The Chapters answer to their Chapter Masters and, in theory, to the High Lords of Terra, and they are the strongest force of the Imperium. A Chapter that fell to heresy would be a catastrophe. The Horus Heresy showed it, and the Ordo Astartes exists to see that no Chapter reaches that point unobserved.[2]

    The Ordo is one of three bodies in the record with a claim to this duty. The Inquisition's article records, besides it, the Ordo Militum, which monitors the armed bodies of the Imperium and is given the Adeptus Astartes in a qualified way, and an unnamed Ordo that develops ways to limit the independence of the Space Marines.[2]

    The unnamed ordo

    [edit]

    The record describes a minor Ordo, nameless and almost forgotten, founded in the Horus Heresy to devise means of limiting the independence of the Adeptus Astartes. One account mentions an assassin, Xenothan, who used a poison made from the first blossoms of the Mecurian Swamp Orchid on the Space Wolves. It disrupts a Space Marine's poison-neutralising gland and temporarily paralyses him, turning the Marine's own enhancement against him. Whether this was the work of the Ordo Astartes, of the Ordo Militum, or of another, the record does not say.[2]

    The date makes it unlikely that the Ordo Astartes is that Ordo, since the Ordo Astartes is dated to M32 and the unnamed Ordo to the Heresy, in M31. It is possible that the Ordo Astartes inherited the work of its predecessor, and the unnamed Ordo's papers.[2]

    Relations with the Chapters

    [edit]

    The Chapters are proud and secretive and keep their own councils. After the opening of the Great Rift the Inquisition relaxed its policy of keeping the Space Marines ignorant of daemons. The Ordo Astartes's work with the Chapters, the Grey Knights and the Deathwatch is not recorded.[3]

    Organisation and members

    [edit]

    Fifty Inquisitors is a number large enough to put an observer near each of the Chapters of the larger foundings and small enough to be spread thin. No Inquisitor of the Ordo is named in the record.[2]

    Purpose and limits

    [edit]

    The Ordo has no Chamber Militant and no force of its own, and it cannot oppose a Chapter by arms. Its power must therefore lie in what it knows: in the knowledge of a Chapter's gene-seed, its recruiting and its Master. In the Era Indomitus, with Chapters scattered by the Rift and many cut off from Terra for decades, the Ordo's task has become both harder and more pressing, though no record describes how it has been carried out.[2]

    Rumour

    [edit]

    Imperial rumour says that the Ordo keeps a file for every Chapter, and that the files grow thickest for the Chapters that keep the most apart. It also says that the Ordo has never succeeded in watching a Chapter without the Chapter's knowledge. Neither claim has support.[2]
