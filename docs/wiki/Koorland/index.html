    Koorland, known by his wall-name Slaughter and remembered as the Last Son of Dorn, was the Second Captain of the Imperial Fists and, after the destruction of his Chapter at Ardamantua in 544.M32, believed to be its only survivor. In the War of the Beast (544–546.M32) he invoked the Last Wall Protocol that called the successor Chapters of Rogal Dorn together at Phall, and when the failing council of Terra would not lead, he displaced it and became Lord Commander of the Imperium.[1][2]

    As Lord Commander he reduced the power of the High Lords of Terra, brought the lost Primarch Vulkan back into the Imperium's wars, and created the multi-Chapter kill-teams that became the Deathwatch. He also found the surviving Sisters of Silence on Nadiries, a discovery that was to decide the war. He was killed fighting the Orks of the Beast at Ullanor in the second assault on that world, in about 545.M32.[3][4]

    His death left the Imperial Fists extinct for the second time in a year. His closest ally, Maximus Thane, rebuilt the Chapter in his honour from the other scions of Dorn, and he became the symbol that the Chapters of the Last Wall rallied to. The Imperium remembers him as a mortal soldier who proved worthy of his primarch's name.[5]

    Ardamantua

    [edit]
    Main article: Ardamantua

    At the opening of the war in 544.M32 Koorland served as Second Captain of the Daylight Wall Company under Chapter Master Cassus Mirhen. The Imperial Fists had been sent to Ardamantua, six solar weeks from Terra, to exterminate the insectoid Chromes, whose migration, in the order of eighty-eight billion strong, threatened to pass through the core of the Segmentum Solar. The High Lords sent the Fists' Shield Company first, then the whole Chapter, with a rescue force under the Lord Commander Militant Heth. The Chapter deployed under First Captain Algerin, Koorland and Captain Sauber.[1][6]

    Gravitational storms heralded the arrival of an Ork attack moon, which phased into orbit, destroyed Heth's fleet and landed Orks of enormous size. Mirhen was slain by the Chromes. Of the whole Chapter only four warriors survived, and three of them died under the care of Mechanicus tech-priests. Koorland, rescued by the tech-priest Phaeton Laurentis and healed by the Magos Biologis Eldon Urquidex, found himself the only Imperial Fist alive and so, by default, its Chapter Master. The Imperium thereafter named him the Last Son of Dorn.[1][6]

    The Last Wall

    [edit]
    Main article: Battle of Phall

    Rather than return to Terra, Koorland took up a secret contingency of Rogal Dorn's. Following the adoption of the Codex Astartes Dorn had secretly laid down the Last Wall Protocol, by which the successors of the VII Legion would reunite, in defiance of the Codex, if Terra itself were gravely threatened. The place of meeting was the Phall system, a world long remembered by Dorn's sons. The protocol was controversial even among its beneficiaries. The Black Templars' High Marshal Bohemond obeyed only reluctantly, for he held that his Chapter's duty lay in its endless crusade, while Zerberyn of the Fists Exemplar disliked a scheme that undid the work of his Chapter's founder. The Last Wall has been enacted only once.[2][3]

    At Phall Koorland met the Black Templars, Crimson Fists, Excoriators and Fists Exemplar, and was joined by the Iron Knights, while the Soul Drinkers could not be reached. Their combined fleet of twenty battle barges and strike cruisers, with some 2,800 Space Marines, fell on the Ork attack moon that had materialised over Terra, supported by the Mechanicus of Mars once the Fabricator-General Kubik agreed to help. The assault disabled the moon's teleportation portal but cost the Iron Knights their Chapter Master, Malfons. Afterwards the Lord Commander Udin Macht Udo claimed the victory, scolded the Chapter Masters for assembling a Legion-sized force, and ordered the loss of the Imperial Fists kept secret. Though Bohemond and Thane urged him to defy the council, Koorland urged his brothers to keep faith with the Emperor's order.[3][6]

    When Udo then moved to expel the Inquisition from the Senatorum Imperialis, Koorland concluded that the High Lords were too feeble to win. With the Grand Master of Assassins Drakan Vangorich, the Inquisitorial Representatives Wienand and Veritus, and the Grand Provost Marshal Vernor Zeck, he carried out a coup, and the rest of the council fell into line and named him Lord Commander.[6][3]

    Lord Commander

    [edit]
    Main article: Lord Commander of the Imperium

    Mars and the Deathwatch

    [edit]

    His first act was to curb the Priesthood of Mars. The Mechanicus had been experimenting with Ork teleportation technology in the hope of moving the Red Planet out of the Sol system, and Eldon Urquidex, now half a servitor, knew the Beast's origins. Koorland sent Thane and the Fists Exemplar to take him, and a bloody skirmish with the Skitarii was averted only when Kubik, swayed by Koorland's plea for unity, ordered his forces to stand down. From Urquidex the Lord Commander learned that the Beast's home was Ullanor, the world where Horus had been raised to Warmaster, and called the Imperium to Terra for a crusade.[6][3]

    After the first assault on Ullanor failed, Koorland, with Vangorich, proposed small elite kill-teams drawn from many Chapters under one command. The Master of the Administratum, Tobris Ekharth, and the Ecclesiarch Mesring opposed it as contrary to the Codex, and the first vote was lost. When the attack moon over Terra reawakened, the High Lords relented, and the black-armoured survivors of Ullanor became the Deathwatch. Koorland struck a bargain with Wienand that the new body would answer to the Inquisition while an Astartes served as its Chapter Master.[7][6]

    The kill-teams' first mission planted Mechanicus beacons on the attack moon so that it could be teleported out of the system. The Mechanicus mastered the technology only in part, and only half of the moon was moved. The other half shattered and fell on Terra, killing hundreds of millions and damaging the Imperial Palace.[7]

    Vulkan and the first Ullanor

    [edit]

    Veritus told Koorland that Vulkan had been found waging a one-man war against the Orks on Caldera. Koorland led an expedition to the world, helped destroy the Ork attack moon over it, and persuaded the primarch, who had sworn to save Caldera first, to join the crusade. Back on Terra Vulkan rebuked the High Lords but spared them. At Ullanor the primarch withdrew to the Alcazar Remembered and left the burden of command to Koorland and the Magos Dominus Gerg Zhokuv. When the Beast's stratagems drove the Imperial host back to its supply depots, it was Koorland who ordered the strike on the Orks' food stores, which drew out the Beast's guard. Bohemond challenged his authority, and Vulkan set both a test: whoever could lift his hammer, Doomtremor, would command. Bohemond and the Crimson Fists' Quesadra failed. Koorland and Thane's squad lifted it together, and Vulkan declared that the Last Son's willingness to share a burden, not any single prowess, made him worthy.[8][6]

    In the temple-Gargant at the heart of Gorkogrod the Beast revealed himself, and Vulkan chose to face him alone. Koorland, understanding the primarch's sacrifice, ordered a withdrawal and struck down Bohemond when he objected. The temple exploded with Vulkan and the Beast inside it, and the broken host limped home to a chant of "I am Slaughter" on every Imperial frequency.[6]

    Nadiries and the Ecclesiarch

    [edit]

    To counter the Ork psykers whose Waaagh! power had maddened the Librarians, Koorland followed Veritus's guidance to Nadiries in the Segmentum Pacificus, where the last Sisters of Silence had remained for fifteen centuries. Having lifted the Orks' siege, he won over the sullen Sisters, who had resolved to see the Imperium burn rather than aid it, by telling them he carried Vulkan's blessing. His plan was to set the Sisters' null-field against a captured Ork psyker and make a reversed Waaagh! that would destroy the Orks around it, a method proved at Incus Maximal.[4][6]

    On Terra he treated the High Lords ever more harshly. The Ecclesiarch Mesring, by now mad, declared for the Beast, and Koorland executed him, proclaimed the return of the Imperial Truth and stripped the Ecclesiarchy of its seat on the council.[4]

    Death

    [edit]

    Koorland led an assassination team in the second assault on Ullanor, with Bohemond, Asger Warfist, the Raven Guard Sergeant Tyris and his Deathwatch kill-team, the Venenum assassin Esad Wire, and six Sisters of Silence under Knight-Abyssal Kavalanera Brassanas. Warships sacrificed themselves to overload the shield of Gorkogrod, and the team teleported into the palace. They reached the throne room, where six thrones stood, and the Sisters' ritual turned the Ork psyker's death into a reverse Waaagh! that swept across Ullanor. With the last effort of the Space Marines, Koorland and his team killed the Ork on the throne.[5][6]

    It proved to be one of six Prime-Orks, each a Beast in its own right. A larger Ork, the Beast who had fought Vulkan, swept the team aside and crushed Koorland underfoot. His comrades carried his body from the world. He was buried on Inwit, at the Feast of Blades, and the Chapters of the Last Wall resolved to refound the Imperial Fists in his memory under Thane.[5][6]

    Character

    [edit]

    Koorland began the war as a loyal captain who held that the Astartes must obey the High Lords, and he urged this on Bohemond and Thane even after the council had shown itself unfit to rule. The war changed him. He overthrew a Lord Commander, executed an Ecclesiarch and struck an ally who challenged his order, and Vulkan judged that his willingness to share a burden, rather than any feat of arms, was what made him fit to lead. His wall-name, Slaughter, was taken up by the Orks themselves, whose chant after Ullanor was a mockery of it.[4][6]
