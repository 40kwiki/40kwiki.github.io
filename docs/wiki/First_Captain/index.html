    A First Captain is the senior captain of a Legion or Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. He commands the 1st Company, the most experienced warriors of the host, and acts for the Primarch or Chapter Master when that commander is absent or has fallen. In the Legions of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy the rank was one of the offices of the praetors, the lords who stood second only to the primarchs in generalship, and several of the Legions gave it a name of their own. In the Chapters formed after the Heresy it is the first of the company commands, and the title is given to the captain of the 1st Company by usage and not by a separate commission.[1][2]

    The office has marked some of the most celebrated, and most hated, warriors in the Imperium's records. Sigismund, First Captain of the Imperial Fists, held the walls of the Imperial Palace and founded the Black Templars. Ezekyle Abaddon, First Captain of the Sons of Horus, became the Warmaster of Chaos and has led thirteen Black Crusades against the Imperium. Calas Typhon of the Death Guard and Ahzek Ahriman of the Thousand Sons also held it, and both became servants of the Dark Gods. On the loyal side Raldoron of the Blood Angels stood beside Sanguinius in the last hours of the Heresy, and in the Era Indomitus Severus Agemman of the Ultramarines rules Macragge as regent in the absence of the Chapter Master.[3][4]

    In Imperial law the First Captain is not a ruling office outside the Chapter, and no Codex gives him power over a world. His real authority is in the Chapter's own tradition: he is the strongest warrior who is not the commander, the one on whom the commander most relies, and the first who is named when the command falls vacant. In practice the choice of a Chapter's next master has not always fallen on him, and the records of several Chapters speak of the rivalry between the First Captain and the captain whom the master favoured.[1][4]

    Origins

    [edit]

    In the Legions

    [edit]
    Main article: Space Marine Legions

    In the Great Crusade (c. 798.M30–005.M31) the Legions were vast hosts of tens or hundreds of thousands of warriors, and their primarchs delegated command to a few high officers. These praetors were the masters of the Legion's formations, of chapters or great companies of about a thousand warriors, and had control of fleets and armies. Their titles followed the customs of the Legions: archein, warsmith, khan, tribune, lord commander, chapter master, wolf lord or first captain.[2]

    The rank is attested in the records of many Legions. The Space Wolves held their first captain to be a Wolf Lord. The Imperial Fists, whose 1st Company was drawn from the Templar Brethren, named Sigismund First Captain after he had won a trial of two hundred single combats. The Blood Angels' Raldoron held it with the then-new honorific of Chapter Master, serving Sanguinius in a role like that of the Mournival of the Sons of Horus. In the Thousand Sons the 1st Fellowship was led by Ahriman, in the Iron Warriors by Forrix, and in the Night Lords the title passed from Sevatar to Zso Sahaal.[2][3]

    In the Ultramarines the same duties were held by the First Master of the 1st Chapter, Marius Gage, and in the Dark Angels by the First Master and by the Master of the Deathwing. In the Sons of Horus the title was said to be newly made for Ezekyle Abaddon, who led the 1st Company, the Justaerin, in black Cataphractii plate, and whose captains were chosen after his example.[5]

    The Council of Nikaea

    [edit]

    The ranks of the Legions were set out in public at the Council of Nikaea (001.M31), where the primarchs and their chief officers came before the Emperor. Raldoron attended with a retinue of captains, sat with First Captain Typhon of the Death Guard, the Custodian Constantin Valdor and the primarchs Fulgrim and Leman Russ, and gave his voice to the debate. The presence of the first captains showed that they were counted as the equals of the Legion's chief officers.[3]

    The Chapters

    [edit]
    Main article: Codex Astartes

    After the Heresy the Legions were divided by the Codex Astartes into Chapters of a thousand warriors in ten companies, and the old praetorial offices were reduced. The captain of the 1st Company, the Veteran company, became the senior captain, and in many Chapters he was given the title of First Captain. In the Dark Angels the commander of the 1st Company is the Grand Master of the Deathwing, in the Space Wolves the commander of the 1st Great Company is a Wolf Lord, and in the Black Templars the senior captains are Marshals. The Chapters keep their own names and customs, and most of the variants are listed in the article on the rank of captain.[1][6]

    In the Chapters that follow the Codex, the commander of the 1st Company is also the Master of the Keep, the second-in-command of the Chapter. He serves as regent of the Chapter's homeworld when the Chapter Master is off-world or incapacitated. In the Ultramarines the same captain bears the title of Regent of Ultramar. He is the official successor of the Chapter Master if the master should be killed. Elsewhere in the Chapter the titles that fall to other captains by tradition, the Master of the Watch for the 2nd Company, the Master of the Arsenal for the 3rd and the Master of the Fleet for the 4th, are not held by him.[6][4]

    The First Captain is the senior commander of the Veterans, who wear Terminator armour or the finest of the artificer plate and carry the relic weapons of the Chapter. He leads them into the hardest fighting, often the first assault on a defended objective. When a Chapter fights a campaign beyond its homeworld and a captain is made Force Commander, the First Captain may be given precedence over the other captains. He is traditionally a master of the Chapter Master's war council, and speaks for the 1st Company there.[6]

    Primaris Chapters and the Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    The Chapters of the Primaris foundings and the First Founding Chapters that have taken Primaris recruits use the same structure. A Primaris captain does the same work as his Firstborn counterpart and differs only in his enhancement and wargear, as in the Gravis plate of the 1st Company's veterans. In the Era Indomitus the Ultramarines' First Captain, Severus Agemman, was named one of the Tetrarchs of Ultramar by Roboute Guilliman in about 012.M42 and made the Tetrarch of Konor. He had commanded the 1st Company since the Battle of Macragge in the First Tyrannic War, having been promoted from the 2nd Company after the death of his predecessor Saul Invictus, and he led his warriors against the Bloodborn of the Daemon Prince M'kar in the Invasion of Ultramar (999.M41), where he was struck down and later recovered.[4]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    The First Captains of the Heresy divided on the same lines as the Legions. Sigismund, Raldoron and Santor remained loyal and fought in the wars that followed, while Abaddon, Typhon, Ahriman, Kaesoron and Sevatar followed their primarchs into Chaos, often with the whole of their companies. In the Imperial view the first captains of the Traitor Legions were the more dangerous, because their loyalty to their primarchs was the better example of the bond that the Legions were built on, and when it was bent, the whole Legion went with it. The lesson was a part of the reasoning of the Codex Astartes, which gives the First Captain no command of the Chapter beyond the 1st Company and keeps the Chapter Master the sole master of the whole.[5][6]

    The Traitors, for their part, have kept the rank. Abaddon is still recorded as First Captain of the Sons of Horus in the traditions of the Black Legion, which numbers the lords who held the office in the Great Crusade among its leaders.[5]
