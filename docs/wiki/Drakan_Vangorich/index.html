    Drakan Vangorich was the twelfth Grand Master of Assassins of the Officio Assassinorum and one of the High Lords of Terra in the 32nd millennium. He sat on the Senatorum Imperialis through the War of the Beast (544–546.M32), the largest Ork invasion since the Great Crusade, and held most of his colleagues in contempt for their quarrels while the Throneworld was threatened. He worked with Koorland and later with Maximus Thane, the two Imperial Fists who in turn took the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium, and he used the influence of his Officio to bend other High Lords to the war effort.[1]

    In 546.M32 Thane, leaving Terra on crusade, named Vangorich Lord Protector of the Imperium, to speak and act in the Lord Commander's name. Within the year Vangorich learned that the Fabricator-General Kubik had deceived Thane, and concluded that the High Lords would never put the survival of Mankind before their own ambitions. In a single day his assassins murdered the High Lords, an act the records call the Beheading. Only the Inquisitorial Representative Wienand was spared.[2]

    Vangorich ruled the Imperium through a council of his own choosing for nearly a standard century. He refortified Terra, rebuilt its armies and completed the Fourth Founding of the Space Marine Chapters that Thane had ordered. In his last decades his rule decayed into paranoia, purges and massacres. Thane returned c. 646.M32 with a retribution force of four hundred Space Marines, and killed the Lord Protector in the Eversor Temple at the north pole of Terra. The Imperium then passed through almost two decades of civil disorder before the Chapters themselves restored a lawful council.[2]

    Imperial assessment of the man remains divided. Some scholars see a murderer who destroyed the lawful government of the Imperium and then became the tyrant he had meant to prevent. Others note that he rebuilt the defences of Terra and the strength of the Chapters, and that the council he destroyed had done little against the Orks. The Officio Assassinorum itself holds that its twelfth Grand Master read the council correctly and misjudged himself.[1][2]

    Early life and the Grand Mastership

    [edit]

    Almost nothing is recorded of Vangorich before the War of the Beast. He was born in the 32nd millennium, in a year the Imperial archives do not give, and was trained as an assassin of the Venenum Temple, the poisoners of the Officio. He rose to lead the temple, and in that office became something close to a father to a young assassin named Esad Wire, who followed him through the events of the war.[1]

    He became the twelfth Grand Master of Assassins at a date before 544.M32 that is not recorded. The office is among the oldest in the service of the Emperor. The Grand Master directs the Officio's temples, the Callidus, Culexus, Eversor, Vanus, Venenum and Vindicare, each of which trains its killers for a different manner of work, and holds a seat on the Senatorum Imperialis as one of the High Lords of Terra. The seat gives the Officio a voice in policy that its secrecy would otherwise deny it, and gives the Grand Master a daily view of the High Lords at work.[1]

    The War of the Beast

    [edit]
    Main article: War of the Beast

    The council in crisis

    [edit]

    When the Orks of the Beast fell on the Imperium in 544.M32 the High Lords of Terra were already a divided body. Vangorich held most of them in contempt and was on good terms only with the Inquisitorial Representative Wienand and the Lord Commander Militant Heth. As the Orks destroyed fleet after fleet and the disaster of the Proletarian Crusade left Terra almost undefended, he watched the High Lords quarrel over blame. It was Vangorich who obtained a hearing before the Senatorum for the Ork envoy who landed on the Throneworld to demand its surrender.[1]

    The Aeldari raid on the Imperial Palace, in which a troupe of Harlequins reached the heart of the fortress with a message from the farseer Eldrad Ulthran, widened the divisions. Vangorich afterwards made an uneasy alliance with the Inquisitor Lord Veritus, and kept his own counsel about what the intruders had said.[1]

    Koorland and the kill-teams

    [edit]

    The last Imperial Fist, Koorland, was named Lord Commander after he and several allies, among them Vangorich, Veritus, Wienand and the Grand Provost Marshal of the Adeptus Arbites, deposed Udin Macht Udo, who had proved unable to command the war. Koorland shared Vangorich's low opinion of the High Lords, and the Grand Master supported his plan for small kill-teams of Space Marines drawn from many Chapters. The plan met bitter opposition from the Master of the Administratum, who held that it broke the Codex Astartes, and from the Ecclesiarch Mesring. It was passed only when the Attack Moon over Terra woke again and the High Lords had no other course. The kill-teams became the foundation of the Deathwatch.[1]

    During the campaign for Ullanor, Vangorich sent Esad Wire, also called the Beast Krule, to the capital world on a secret mission to find and kill the Beast. When Koorland fell, Vangorich turned to Thane, then Chapter Master of the Fists Exemplar. The two, with Kubik of the Adeptus Mechanicus, formed the inner circle that planned the last advance. To assemble the fleets and armies, Vangorich used his office to intimidate the Lord Commander Militant Verreault and the Lord High Admiral Lansung into committing every resource they held. The Ork capital on Ullanor fell in 546.M32, and the war ended.[1]

    The Beheading

    [edit]
    Main article: Beheading

    The Lord Protector

    [edit]

    After the victory Thane decreed that the Imperium would raise as many new Chapters as its stores of gene-seed allowed. All Chapters were to give a portion of their veterans to the new Founding, and the gene-seed tithe was doubled for a century. The Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra, but would sail as a crusading fleet to free the worlds that the war had left helpless. Knowing that he might never see the Throneworld again, Thane made Vangorich Lord Protector, to be treated by all as if Thane were present. To calm the High Lords he reminded them that the threat of his return would be enough to keep the Grand Master loyal.[2]

    The murder of the High Lords

    [edit]

    Vangorich meant at first to work with the High Lords, but Thane had warned him of Kubik, and he soon found the full extent of the Fabricator-General's plan. Instead of carrying out an Exterminatus on Ullanor as ordered, Kubik had used machines taken from the Orks' Attack Moons to teleport the Ork capital city, Gorkogrod, aboard the vast Mechanicus ship Ark Majesty, so that its technology could be plundered. The Grand Master concluded that the Senatorum was beyond repair, and that the Imperium had grown corrupt from within.[2]

    In 546.M32 he struck, and the High Lords were killed in a single solar day. Only Wienand was spared, because the Lord Protector held a private regard for her that the Inquisition's records do not explain. He hid the murders behind excuses and impersonators and installed new High Lords of his own choosing, who served as a council of puppets. Thane received word of the killings on crusade but did not move against him, for the Lord Commander judged that the Imperium might at last be ruled effectively. Wire, who tried to kill his master, was defeated and turned into an Eversor, the most frenzied of the Officio's killers.[2][1]

    Rule as Lord Protector

    [edit]

    For some decades the Lord Protector governed better than his predecessors, if with great cruelty. His rule saw the refortification of Terra, the rebuilding of the Imperial armies and the fleets, and the Fourth Founding of the Adeptus Astartes, which Thane had commanded and which raised hundreds of new Chapters. The last Ork hordes of the Beast were hunted down in the same years.[2]

    About eighty Terran years into his rule the Lord Protector began to show signs of fear and instability. He saw enemies everywhere and ordered purges and massacres in the Adeptus Terra, which grew more frequent from day to day. Reports of his condition reached Thane in his crusade, and the Lord Commander judged that the Imperial Fists must return to Terra to remove him. Wienand, who still stood at Vangorich's side, reached the same judgment.[2][a]

    Fall and death

    [edit]

    The retribution fleet reached Terra with four hundred Space Marines: two hundred Imperial Fists of the 1st, 4th and 5th Companies, and one company each of the Halo Brethren and the Sable Swords, two Chapters of the new Founding. Their commander, Qublicus Amar, Chapter Master of the Sable Swords, was killed by a Vindicare Assassin's bullet soon after landing. The survivors went on regardless. Assailed by hundreds of assassins, they lost half their number while securing the Imperial Palace.[2]

    Wienand then convinced Thane of her loyalty, and told him that the Lord Protector had taken refuge in the Eversor Temple at the north pole of Terra. A hundred Eversors met the Space Marines in its halls, and at the end only Thane, Vangorich and one Eversor were left alive. Vangorich ordered the Eversor to kill the Chapter Master; the assassin, who was Wire, held his hand. Vangorich asked whether they wished to hear how Konrad Curze had died, and Thane shot him with his bolt pistol. Wire was not seen again.[2]

    Aftermath

    [edit]

    With the Lord Protector dead and the office of Lord Commander vacant, Terra slid into factional strife that lasted nearly two decades. It ended when Agnathio of the Ultramarines gathered Chapter Masters from across the galaxy, and an armada of more than fifty Chapters came to Terra, the first such gathering since the Horus Heresy. Agnathio gave the factions an ultimatum to unite or be purged as Heretics. The Astartes sat in a closed session of the Senatorum, and when they left a new council of High Lords had been chosen. The date is not recorded; it falls in the 660s.M32 by the sequence of events.[2]
