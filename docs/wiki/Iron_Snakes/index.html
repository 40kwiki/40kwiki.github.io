    Iron Snakes
    Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes
    FoundingSecond Founding (date not recorded)
    Parent LegionUltramarines (XIII Legion)
    Notable campaignSabbat Worlds Crusade

    The Iron Snakes are a Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and one of the Successor Chapters of the Ultramarines, the XIII Legion. Like the other Successors, the Chapter was founded in the Second Founding under the Codex Astartes, with the Ultramarines' doctrine as its base.[1]

    The Chapter takes its name from its founding traditions. Its warriors are recorded as steady and methodical in their campaigns, in keeping with the Ultramarines' doctrine.

    Sabbat Worlds Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Sabbat Worlds Crusade

    The Iron Snakes took part in the Sabbat Worlds Crusade alongside the Astra Militarum, the Imperial Navy and the White Scars. The crusade's commanders also drew on four further Chapters and the Titan Legions of the Mechanicus. The Chapter's part in the crusade's campaigns is recorded in the Sabbat Worlds registers.[2]

    Successor Chapters of the Ultramarines

    [edit]

    The Ultramarines have more than fifty Successor Chapters, including the Black Consuls, Novamarines, Doom Eagles, Silver Skulls, Aurora Chapter and Marines Errant. The Iron Snakes are among the better-recorded of them.
