    The 31st millennium (M31) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M31 to 999.M31. It opened at the height of the Great Crusade with the Triumph of Ullanor, where Horus was named Warmaster. Within five years Horus had turned to Chaos, and the Horus Heresy split the Space Marine Legions and ended at the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, where the Emperor of Mankind was mortally wounded and placed on the Golden Throne.[1]

    The rest of the millennium belongs to the recovery. The Great Scouring drove the Traitor Legions into the Eye of Terror; Roboute Guilliman wrote the Codex Astartes, and the Second Founding broke the loyal Legions into Chapters. The primarchs who remained disappeared or fell one by one, and the High Lords of Terra took over the government. In 781.M31 Abaddon the Despoiler led the first Black Crusade out of the Eye. Imperial historians count this long recovery as the Time of Rebirth.[2]

    Chronology

    [edit]

    Dates for the Heresy years follow the dedicated articles on each campaign, which use the reconciled reckoning of the Logos Historica Verita. Many entries for the later centuries are only dated to the millennium.

    DateEventNotes
    000.M31The Triumph of Ullanor: Horus named Warmaster; the Emperor of Mankind returns to Terra.The Council of Terra takes over the Crusade's administration.
    001.M31The Council of Nikaea forbids the use of sorcery by the Legions.Some Legion records give a later year.
    c. 002–003.M31The 63rd Expedition makes contact with the Interex.
    003.M31The Interex destroyed by the Luna Wolves in the First War of Xenobia; the Luna Wolves renamed the Sons of Horus.
    c. 004.M31Horus wounded on the moon of Davin and turned to Chaos.The Imperial Truth still forbids worship of the Emperor.
    004.M31The Burning of Prospero: the Space Wolves destroy the homeworld of the Thousand Sons.The Timeline of Imperial history gives c. 005.M31.
    005.M31The Isstvan III atrocity opens the Horus Heresy.
    005–006.M31The Schism of Mars: Kelbor-Hal takes the Dark Mechanicum into rebellion.Occupation lasts to 014.M31.
    c. 005–006.M31The Signus Campaign traps the Blood Angels.
    c. 006.M31The Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V; Ferrus Manus killed.
    007.M31The Battle of Calth; the Ruinstorm raised; the Shadow Crusade against Ultramar begins.
    007.M31The Battle of Phall.
    007–010.M31Early phase of the Battle of Beta-Garmon.The great Titan battle follows in c. 012.M31.
    c. 009.M31Imperium Secundus proclaimed on Macragge; Sanguinius named its emperor.Dissolved c. 011.M31.
    009.M31The Battle of Molech: Horus seizes the Emperor's ancient path into the Warp.
    010–011.M31The Battle of Tallarn, the greatest armoured battle of the Heresy.
    c. 011.M31The Ruinstorm weakens after the Second Battle of Davin.
    c. 012.M31The Adeptus Mechanicus is formed from the loyal Mechanicum.
    013–014.M31The Siege of Terra. Horus slain; the Emperor placed on the Golden Throne.
    c. 014.M31The Fall of Caliban; the Fallen scattered through time and space.Only The Rock survives.
    014–021.M31The Great Scouring drives the Traitor Legions into the Eye of Terror.
    014.M31Roboute Guilliman takes up the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium.He holds it to 121.M31.
    c. 014–021.M31The Iron Cage: the Imperial Fists are trapped by the Iron Warriors and saved by other Legions.Recorded with the Second Founding disputes.
    c. 019.M31The Codex Astartes completed.Some records give 021.M31.
    c. 021.M31The Second Founding breaks the loyal Legions into Chapters.
    c. 022.M31Corvus Corax leaves the Raven Guard and goes into the Eye of Terror.[a]Last words "Nevermore".
    Early M31The Legion Wars among the Traitor Legions in the Eye; the Sons of Horus broken at Canticle City.No exact dates are known.
    c. 084.M31Jaghatai Khan disappears into the Webway while pursuing the Dark Eldar.
    M31The Third Founding approved on the petition of Rogal Dorn.See the note in Third Founding.
    121.M31Guilliman mortally wounded by Fulgrim at Thessala and placed in stasis on Macragge.
    211.M31Leman Russ leaves Fenris and is not seen again.On the eve of the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension.
    Mid-M31Vulkan vanishes from Nocturne.
    781.M31The First Black Crusade and the First Battle of Cadia. Rogal Dorn reported killed.Abaddon unites the Black Legion.
    c. 900.M31The Temple of the Saviour Emperor founded by the officer called Fatidicus.[a]Root of the later Adeptus Ministorum.
