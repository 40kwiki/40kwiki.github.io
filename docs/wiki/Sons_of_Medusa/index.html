    The Sons of Medusa are a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes descended from the Iron Hands. They were not created in any regular Founding. The Chapter grew out of the Moirae Schism, the religious division that split the Iron Hands and the Adeptus Mechanicus in the 36th and 37th millennia, and its warriors are the heirs of the third of the Iron Hands who held to the dissident doctrine and were exiled from Medusa. The High Lords of Terra ratified the exiles as a Chapter by special edict in 011.M37.[1][2]

    The Chapter is fleet-based. Its fortress-monastery is a network of fortified asteroids in the Taelus System, and it has no single Chapter Master: three Iron Thanes govern it as a council. Its warriors are known for the extreme severity of their doctrine, which treats the flesh as a weakness to be purged even more harshly than their parent Chapter does, and for a long record of service from the Great Cull of 011.M37 to the Badab War and the wars of the Era Indomitus.[2][3]

    History

    [edit]

    The Moirae Schism

    [edit]
    Main article: Moirae Schism

    The Moirae Schism began as a dispute within the Cult Mechanicus over the nature of the Omnissiah and spread to the Iron Hands, whose close ties with Mars left them open to it. By the early 37th millennium the Chapter was divided between the old orthodoxy and the Moirae doctrine. Imperial records state that the Iron Hands' Iron Council, wishing to avoid a war among brothers, chose exile rather than bloodshed. Those who had embraced the dissident creed were to leave Medusa, and both parties bound themselves by treaty never to raise arms against each other. About a third of the Chapter departed, and some battle-brothers from other Iron Hands successors who shared the same belief later joined them. The exact year of the exile is not recorded; it is placed in the last decades of M36 from the sequence of events.[1][a]

    Ratification

    [edit]

    The exiles spent their first years as a body without status, in effect a renegade fleet. Their warriors were nonetheless of proven loyalty, and the Imperium of the time, recovering from the Age of Apostasy, had urgent need of them. In 011.M37, by special edict of the High Lords of Terra, they were reorganised and ratified as the Sons of Medusa. The edict was opposed by parts of the Adeptus Mechanicus, and several Chapters recorded misgivings at a Chapter born of schism.[1]

    Wars of M37–M41

    [edit]

    The new Chapter's first campaign was the Great Cull of 011.M37, the systematic destruction of the organisations judged to have supported the apostate Goge Vandire. Between 182.M38 and 453.M38 it fought in the Bellrath Crusade, which brought the Laanah Rifts in the north-west of the Segmentum Pacificus under Imperial rule. Within a century of the crusade's launch the Sons of Medusa had entered the folk tales of the new sector, whose people called them the "Emerald Death". In 322.M39 the Chapter fought in the Angevin Crusade, which conquered the Calixis Sector, beside the Black Templars, the Tigers Argent and the Charnel Guard.[2]

    The Badab War

    [edit]
    Main article: Badab War

    The Sons of Medusa answered the call of the Legate Inquisitor for reinforcements against the Secessionist Chapters of the Badab War, and their first strike cruisers reached the Maelstrom in 907.M41. The Chapter's worst loss of the war was the strike cruiser Warspite, destroyed with all hands by the Executioners near the Grief System in 911.M41. Its last great action was the assault on the orbital fortress of Sentinel-Sigma in 912.M41, which broke the "Ring of Steel" around the Badab System and opened the way for the final Loyalist assault on Badab Primaris.[4]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    After the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, a Torchbearer task force of the Indomitus Crusade brought the Chapter the means to create Primaris Space Marines. Imperial records note that the gene-seed used for those reinforcements came from the Iron Hands, and that the old enmity of the Moirae Schism made the gift a difficult one to accept. The Sons of Medusa took it nonetheless. In the War of Beasts on Vigilus, dated to about 012.M42, the Chapter deployed five companies to hold the planet against the Orks and the Genestealer Cults.[3][b]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    The Sons of Medusa are ruled by a Chapter Council of three Iron Thanes, who share the authority that other Chapters give to a single Chapter Master. Like the Iron Hands they make wide use of bionic replacement, but their creed of purging the weakness of the flesh is said to go further, and their Iron Priests hold a correspondingly high status. The Chapter's warriors fight in mechanised formations, with heavy use of armour and drop assaults.[2]

    Beliefs

    [edit]

    The Chapter's creed derives from the Moirae doctrine, which held that the Omnissiah's will could be read in the patterns of the machine and that the flesh was to be shed in its pursuit. The Sons of Medusa keep the treaty that their founders made with the Iron Hands, and records of the Badab era report no armed clash between them. Relations remain cold.[1]
