    Grand Provost Marshal
    Head of the Adeptus Arbites
    Sigil of the Adeptus Arbites, the fist and the scales
    IncumbentUnrecorded since early M42[a]
    StyleGrand Provost Marshal; High Lord
    Member ofHigh Lords of Terra (permanent seat)

    Term lengthFor life or until removed

    The Grand Provost Marshal is the head of the Adeptus Arbites, the enforcers of Imperial law, and holds a permanent seat among the High Lords of Terra. From the Hall of Judgement on Terra, where the Book of Judgement containing the Lex Imperialis is kept, the Grand Provost Marshal represents the Emperor's law to the council, advises it on legal matters and directs the Arbites of a million worlds through their judges and marshals.[1]

    The office is one of the four oldest in the Imperium. A Grand Provost Marshal sat among the High Lords Civilian of the Unification Wars in the late 30th millennium, and another, Harr Rantal, on the Council of Terra during the Horus Heresy. After the Heresy the scattered offices of the Marshals were united into the Adeptus Arbites under the Grand Provost Marshal. The most recent holder named in the record, Aveliza Drachmar, joined the Hexarchy against the Lord Commander of the Imperium and was killed in its failed coup in early M42.[2][3]

    Powers and duties

    [edit]

    The Grand Provost Marshal is concerned mainly with the politics of Terra and leaves the daily work of the Arbites to subordinates: the judges of sectors and systems and the marshals who command the precinct-fortresses. The Arbites enforce only the laws that touch the Imperium itself, such as the tithe, loyalty and the security of Imperial worlds, and leave common crime to local enforcers. The Grand Provost Marshal is the final authority on the interpretation of that law within the Arbites and the council's chief source of legal counsel.[1][4]

    The office carries great weight in a crisis. When a planetary government fails or rebels, the local precinct is often the last Imperial authority left, and the Grand Provost Marshal's word decides whether the Arbites hold, withdraw or call for the Astra Militarum or the Inquisition.[4]

    History

    [edit]

    Unification and the Heresy (late M30 – 014.M31)

    [edit]

    The earliest recorded holder is Uwoma Kandawire, one of the four High Lords Civilian who governed for the Emperor at the time the First Legion took the field, late in the Unification Wars and before the Great Crusade began in c. 798.M30.[2][b] During the Heresy (005–014.M31) the office was held by Harr Rantal, who sat on the Council of Terra and, after the Siege of Terra, on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. In the reforms that followed, the laws and edicts of the Emperor and the Council were gathered into the Lex Imperialis, and their enforcement given to a single adepta under the Grand Provost Marshal.[5][4]

    War of the Beast and the Beheading (544–546.M32)

    [edit]

    In the War of the Beast (544–546.M32) the Grand Provost Marshal was Vernor Zeck. He was one of the few High Lords who openly supported the Imperial Fists Chapter Master Koorland when he deposed the Lord Commander Udin Macht Udo in c. 545.M32. After the Beheading of 546.M32 the seat in the Lord Protector Drakan Vangorich's council was held by Galatea Haas, nominally as Lady Speaker for Zeck.[6][c]

    Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

    [edit]

    Aveliza Drachmar held the office at the Thirteenth Black Crusade in 999.M41 and kept it when Roboute Guilliman restored the council. She was among the serving High Lords who joined the Hexarchy of the former Master of the Administratum Irthu Haemotalion, and she was killed by the agents of the Grand Master of Assassins Fadix when the conspiracy moved to seize Terra in early M42.[3][d]
