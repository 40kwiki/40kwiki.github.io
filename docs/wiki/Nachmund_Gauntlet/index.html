    The Nachmund Gauntlet is a corridor of stable space through the Great Rift, linking the hive world of Vigilus in the Imperium Nihilus with the world of Sangua Terra in the Imperium Sanctus. It is the best known, and for long years after the Rift opened the only confirmed, route by which Imperial fleets can cross from one half of the Imperium to the other without risking the full fury of the storms. Other crossings have been reported but not confirmed.[1][2]

    The Gauntlet exists because both worlds contain great spears of blackstone, or noctilith, a warp-resonant mineral, polarised by some ancient xenos power to repel the Warp. The field between them holds the storms back. Its importance made both ends the targets of Abaddon the Despoiler and the Black Legion in the early Era Indomitus, in the War of Nightmares for Vigilus and the later Nachmund Rift War. The ruined hulks of thousands of ships that failed to run it hang along its length.[1]

    Geography

    [edit]

    The Gauntlet lies in the Segmentum Obscurus, in a region close to the Eye of Terror that Navigators had avoided since the Great Crusade because of its storms and anomalies. When the Great Rift opened in 999.M41, it engulfed the region, but as the storms settled a narrow lane of calm was found between Vigilus on its northern side and Sangua Terra on its southern. Nearby worlds include Cadia, destroyed in 999.M41, the shrine world Chinchare, the Mordian system and the segmentum fortress of Cypra Mundi.[1][3]

    The corridor is not wholly safe. Its walls press in and fall back, warp predators and daemons hunt along it, and the voids at its edges are filled with wrecks. Convoys that run it are escorted by Navy squadrons where possible.[2]

    Cause

    [edit]

    Noctilith, called blackstone, is a rare mineral that resonates with the Warp and can be charged either to attract or to repel its energies. It is the same material as the Blackstone Fortresses and the pylons of Cadia. The spear-like deposits in the crust of Vigilus had been polarised to repel warp energy by some technology of great age, probably xenos. Sangua Terra was found to contain identical spears, held in a strange suspension so that they always point towards Vigilus. The field that runs between the two holds the Rift apart.[1][a]

    If either set of spears were destroyed or depolarised, the Gauntlet would close. This is why the forces of Chaos have tried so hard to take or ruin both worlds.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Opening and Dharrovar

    [edit]

    At the opening of the Gauntlet, ships seeking passage had to pass close to the Nachmund system, whose principal planet, the Knight world of Dharrovar, was ruled by the Tyrant King Kaligius. His house, Mandrakor, had not been in contact with the Imperium since the Horus Heresy. Kaligius declared that Old Night had returned, refused all contact with the Imperium and made pacts with renegades and pirates. Ships that kept their distance were allowed to pass, while those that approached under a flag of parley were attacked. He was declared Excommunicate Traitoris as the term Imperium Nihilus was first coming into use, around 000.M42.[1]

    Imperial reinforcement

    [edit]

    Roboute Guilliman, as Lord Commander of the Imperium, recognised the Gauntlet's value at once. He sent the Castellans of the Rift, a Chapter of the Ultima Founding, to drive back the daemon engines and renegade Knights that infested the route, and at least two of its companies fought in the defence of Sangua Terra. Vigilus became the staging post for every Imperial force sent into the Imperium Nihilus by that route and a refuge for billions of refugees fleeing south through the Gauntlet.[2]

    War of Nightmares

    [edit]
    Main article: War of Nightmares

    In the first decades of the 42nd millennium Vigilus was attacked in turn by an Ork Speedwaaagh!, a rising of the Genestealer Cult in its hives, Aeldari and Drukhari raiders and finally the Black Legion. Marneus Calgar of the Ultramarines commanded its defence and fought Abaddon in single combat. Abaddon withdrew, and the Gauntlet remained open.[1]

    Nachmund Rift War

    [edit]

    Abaddon did not abandon his aim. In the years after the War of Nightmares the Black Legion and its allies turned on Sangua Terra and the systems along the Gauntlet, in a wider struggle recorded as the Nachmund Rift War. The Imperium held both ends, at great cost, and the passage remains open, although contested along its whole length.[2][b]
