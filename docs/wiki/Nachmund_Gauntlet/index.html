    The Nachmund Gauntlet is a corridor of stable space through the Great Rift, linking the hive world of Vigilus in the Imperium Nihilus with the war world of Sangua Terra in the Imperium Sanctus. It is one of only two confirmed stable passages through the Rift, and the one best known, by which Imperial fleets can cross from one half of the Imperium to the other without risking the full fury of the storms. Other crossings have been reported but not confirmed.[1][2]

    The Gauntlet exists because both worlds contain great spears of blackstone, or noctilith, a warp-resonant mineral that some ancient xenos power polarised to repel the Warp. The anti-psychic field that hums between the two sets of spears holds the storms back from the lane. For the Astronomican's light to reach the far side by even this uncertain road, and for refugees, reinforcements and supplies to pass the other way, the spears must hold.[2][3]

    Its importance made both ends the targets of Abaddon the Despoiler and the Black Legion in the early Era Indomitus. Roboute Guilliman, as Lord Commander of the Imperium, set a Chapter of the Ultima Founding, the Castellans of the Rift, to guard the lane against renegade Knights and daemon engines, while the War of Nightmares and the later Nachmund Rift War were fought for the worlds at its ends. The ruined hulks of countless ships that failed to run it hang along its length.[2][4]

    Geography

    [edit]

    The Gauntlet lies in the Segmentum Obscurus, in a region close to the Eye of Terror that Navigators had avoided since the Great Crusade because its storms and anomalies made it too dangerous to cross. When the Great Rift opened in 999.M41 it engulfed the region, but as the swirling nebulae dissipated a narrow lane of calm was found between Vigilus on its northern side and Sangua Terra on its southern. The Rift itself is a serpentine mass, and the channel of the Gauntlet runs across it. Imperial astropaths on Vigilus noted that the shape of the Rift, with the Gauntlet cut through it, began to appear in the daubings of madmen and the idle scrawlings of those who believed they were thinking of other things.[2][5]

    The Nachmund system lies on the route, and its principal world, the Knight world of Dharrovar, is the only inhabited place the lane passes close to. Vigilus is the terminus on the Nihilus side. It is a desert hive world of the Vigilus system, famed before the Rift as a linchpin of the Segmentum Obscurus and a stronghold of the Adeptus Mechanicus, whose bore-hives on its forge continent of Megaborealis mined the very blackstone that now protects it.[5][2]

    The corridor is not safe. Some Imperial ships have run it successfully, but many more have been destroyed in the attempt, and the voids at its edges are filled with wrecks.[2]

    Cause

    [edit]

    Noctilith, called blackstone, is a rare mineral that resonates with the Warp and can be charged either to attract or to repel its energies. It is the same material as the Blackstone Fortresses and the pylons of Cadia. The spear-like deposits in the crust of Vigilus had been polarised to repel warp energy by some technology of great age, probably xenos. Sangua Terra was found to contain identical spears, held in a strange black suspension so that they always point towards Vigilus. The anti-empyric field that thrums between the two worlds keeps the channel open and holds the Rift apart.[2][a]

    If either set of spears were destroyed or depolarised, the storms around the Gauntlet would close in and the corridor would be lost, leaving the Imperium Nihilus to the mercy of the forces of Chaos. This is why Abaddon and his Black Legion were so determined to seize or ruin both worlds in the first years of the Era Indomitus.[2][3]

    History

    [edit]

    Opening and Dharrovar

    [edit]

    At the opening of the Gauntlet, ships seeking passage had to pass close to the Nachmund system. Its principal planet, Dharrovar, was ruled by the Tyrant King Kaligius over the renegade Knights of House Mandrakor, who had not been seen by the Imperium since the Horus Heresy. Kaligius declared that Old Night had returned, refused all contact and made pacts with renegades and pirate fleets. His defenders said he had good cause for his fear, for on either side of the lane a seething tide of Chaos pressed constantly to break its bonds and spill into realspace.[2][3]

    Ships that gave Dharrovar a wide berth were allowed to slink past without challenge, while those that sought haven or approached under a flag of parley met hostility, suspicion or violence. Some Imperial vessels ran the Gauntlet and survived. Many more were destroyed, and their hulks became a vast graveyard of voidcraft. A staunch independent already convinced that nothing good could come of being drawn back into the fold, Kaligius turned to tyranny and oppression, and as the term Imperium Nihilus was first coined he was declared Excommunicate Traitoris.[2][3]

    Imperial reinforcement

    [edit]

    Guilliman recognised the Gauntlet's value at once. He sent the Castellans of the Rift, an all-Primaris Chapter of the Ultima Founding raised from Ultramarines lineage in about 999.M41, to drive back the daemon engines and renegade Knights that infested the route. Their duty, given by the Lord Commander himself, is to maintain and defend the channel, and they have made repeated raids on the fallen Knight world of Dharrovar. When war later came to Vigilus the greater part of the Chapter remained engaged against the heretics of Dharrovar, though two companies fought in the defence of Sangua Terra and two more travelled to Vigilus.[4][2]

    Vigilus became the staging post for every Imperial force sent into the Imperium Nihilus by this road, and the refuge of the billions of refugees who fled south through the Gauntlet from the Dark Imperium. Its governors imposed curfews and forbade the watching of the Rift in the night sky, and the repression sowed unrest that later broke into open revolt.[5]

    War of Nightmares

    [edit]
    Main article: War of Nightmares

    In the early Era Indomitus Vigilus was attacked in turn by an Ork Speedwaaagh! out of the Rift, a rising of the Genestealer Cult in its hives, Aeldari and Drukhari raiders and finally the Black Legion under the Chaos Lord Haarken Worldclaimer and Abaddon himself. Marneus Calgar of the Ultramarines commanded its defence and fought Abaddon in single combat. In 025.M42 the Despoiler and his Legion were driven from the surface and the world was saved, and the Gauntlet remained open.[2][5]

    Nachmund Rift War

    [edit]

    Abaddon did not abandon his aim. Almost at once word came of an attack on Sangua Terra, where a vast voidship matching the descriptions of the Planet Killer was reported in low orbit. In the years after the War of Nightmares the Black Legion and its allies turned on the worlds at the southern end of the Gauntlet and the systems along it, in a wider struggle recorded as the Nachmund Rift War. The Imperium held both ends, at great cost, and the passage remains open, although contested along its whole length.[5][b]

    Importance to the Imperium

    [edit]

    Control of Vigilus allows the Imperium to just barely keep its beleaguered worlds in the Imperium Nihilus reinforced and resupplied. To lose it would cripple the war effort against the forces of Chaos. Tigurius, Chief Librarian of the Ultramarines, said that if Vigilus fell and the Gauntlet collapsed the Imperium Nihilus would be all but lost, and that the empire of Terra would soon follow.[5][2]

    Dharrovar

    [edit]

    Dharrovar is the only world the lane passes near. The renegade Knights of House Mandrakor, who had not been seen since the Horus Heresy, took the opening of the Rift as the return of Old Night, and their tyrant king saw in every ship that approached a servant of the Imperium that had abandoned them. The Castellans of the Rift have made repeated raids on the fallen Knight world.[4][2]
