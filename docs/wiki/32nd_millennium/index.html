    32nd millennium
    000.M32 – 999.M32
    AgesLate Time of Rebirth; early The Forging
    RulersThe High Lords of Terra; Lord Commander Maximus Thane (546.M32); Lord Protector Drakan Vangorich (546–c. 626.M32)
    Key eventsWar of the Beast (544–546.M32); Beheading (546.M32); Second and Third Black Crusades (597 and 909.M32)
    Preceded by31st millennium
    Followed by33rd millennium

    The 32nd millennium (M32) is the span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M32 to 999.M32. In its first centuries the cults of the God-Emperor were drawn together into the Adeptus Ministorum, whose Ecclesiarch joined the High Lords of Terra in 200.M32. The Imperial Army had already been divided into the Imperial Guard and the Imperial Navy, and the lasgun became the standard arm of the Guard.[1]

    The defining war of the age was the War of the Beast of 544–546.M32, the greatest Ork invasion since the Great Crusade, which nearly destroyed the Imperial Fists and saw the founding of the Deathwatch and the Ordos of the Inquisition. It ended with the Beheading, in which the Grand Master of Assassins Drakan Vangorich had the High Lords murdered and ruled for most of a century. Order was restored in 646.M32 by Chapter Master Agnathio of the Ultramarines. The rest of the millennium marks the start of The Forging.[2]

    Early M32The Imperial Army is divided into the Imperial Guard and the Imperial Navy.A reform of the Codex Astartes era.
    Early M32The lasgun replaces the autogun as the standard weapon of the Imperial Guard.
    Early M32The cults of the Emperor are united as the Adeptus Ministorum, grown from the Temple of the Saviour Emperor.See Imperial Cult.
    Early M32Asdrubael Vect founds the Kabal of the Black Heart in Commorragh.Ordo Xenos record.
    Early M32Some summary tables of the Administratum place a Third Founding here.The Register of Foundings puts it in M31.
    c. 100.M32The Cursus of Alganar on Tallarn: a daemon incursion breaks out during an Aeldari raid.
    161.M32The Ravening Storm cuts off the mining colonies of Ichtar IX for nine centuries.
    200.M32Ecclesiarch Veneris II becomes a High Lord, the first to sit on the Senatorum Imperialis.The Imperial Cult becomes the state religion in all but name.
    243.M32Inquisitor Damakso first sets out how the Chaos Gods act through mortal hosts.See Inquisition.
    c. 300.M32The Ecclesiarch's seat on the council is made permanent.
    522.M32The Ork space hulk called Da Iron Worm ravages the Uhulis Sector.A forerunner of the Beast's invasions.
    544.M32The War of the Beast begins; the Imperial Fists are almost wiped out at Ardamantua.
    544–545.M32Koorland, last of the Imperial Fists, becomes Lord Commander and calls the Last Wall council of Dorn's successors.
    545.M32Vulkan reappears to defend Caldera; the Deathwatch is created; the Sisters of Silence are restored.
    545.M32The Ordo Xenos and Ordo Malleus of the Inquisition take shape.
    546.M32The Beast is slain at the third battle of Ullanor; Maximus Thane succeeds Koorland and orders the Fourth Founding.
    546.M32The Beheading: the twelve High Lords murdered on the order of Drakan Vangorich, who rules as Lord Protector.
    After 546.M32The Fourth Founding raises the Halo Brethren, the Sable Swords and many other Chapters.See Third Founding.
    597.M32The Second Black Crusade curses the worlds of the Belis Corona Sector.
    c. 626.M32Thane returns with the Imperial Fists and kills Vangorich in the Eversor Temple; almost two decades of anarchy follow.Nearly eighty years after the Beheading.
    646.M32Chapter Master Agnathio brings fifty Chapters to Terra and restores the council of twelve High Lords; the office of Lord Commander is abolished.The office returns only in 999.M41.
    742.M32First Battle of The Fang: Magnus the Red and the Thousand Sons attack the Space Wolves on Fenris.
    751.M32The Rogue Trader Xiatal catalogues Naogeddon.
    888.M32The Astropath Wars in the Adeptus Astra Telepathica.
    909.M32The Third Black Crusade: the Daemon Prince Tallomin assaults the Cadian Gate and the tomb of Saint Gerstahl is defiled.
    910.M32The Firestorm.Some tables give 910.M33.
    990.M32The Culexus Assassin Dranos kills the Thousand Sons sorcerer Xantaka.See Officio Assassinorum.
    Unknown date.M32The Jericho Sector is re-established after twelve centuries of disorder.Later the Jericho Reach.
    Late M32Nearly every Imperial world outside the holdings of the Astartes and the Mechanicus has a cathedral; The Forging begins.
