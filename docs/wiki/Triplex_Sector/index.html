    Triplex Sector
    Sector of the Imperium of Mankind
    SegmentumUltima Segmentum
    RegionEastern Fringe
    Principal systemTriplex System
    Forge WorldsTriplex Galatia, Triplex Thule, Triplex Phall
    Titan LegionLegio Victorum
    Known forThe Horus Heresy and the Thramas Crusade

    The Triplex Sector was a sector of the Imperium of Mankind in the Eastern Fringe of the Ultima Segmentum. It held the three Forge worlds of the Triplex System and the Legio Victorum Titan Legion. Most of the sector was made up of barren and largely uninhabitable star systems.[1]

    At the time of the Horus Heresy in the early 31st Millennium, the sector was a wasteland of ore-rich worlds around old and dying stars. A few clans of Abhuman miners and prospectors lived there. It was far from any established trade route and was cut off from the more populous parts of the region by fierce Warp storms.[1]

    The Triplex System

    [edit]

    At the heart of the sector lay the Triplex System. Its three Forge Worlds were the centre of all industry in the sector and the greatest prize in the Eastern Fringe. A general who controlled Triplex could command the eastern half of the Imperium. Its forges rivalled those of Anvillus in power, and many aspiring warlords looked to it when the Heresy broke out.[1]

    Triplex was the industrial heart of the east during the last years of the Great Crusade. It was home to the Legio Victorum and several hundred militant taghma, making it one of the great concentrations of military force in the east. The few inhabited worlds and mining colonies in the sector were held in thrall to Triplex. They depended on it for protection against the terrors of unknown space.[1]

    Triplex Galatia

    [edit]

    The first of the three Forge Worlds to be founded was Triplex Galatia. Its birth is lost to history, from the time when the Explorator fleets of the Mechanicum sailed blindly into the void in search of new lands. For long centuries it was the overlord of local space. Its power rested on the ''Legio Victorum I'', the Foe Slayers, and on its own strength.[1]

    When the Emperor arrived, Mars moved to curb this rival. It petitioned him to take only the ''Legio Victorum II'' on the Great Crusade, leaving the rest of the Legio sealed to the defence of Triplex. Cut off from the spoils of the Crusade, Galatia turned inwards, and its tech-adepts began to walk paths forbidden by both Mars and Terra. When envoys of Horus and of Triplex Phall approached them, they found a willing ally. Galatia stockpiled weapons built to new specifications and sent only orthodox work to the Imperium, to keep up a pretence of loyalty.[1]

    Imperial accounts suggest that Galatia took no hand in the death of Thule and kept its Titans out of that war. It is nonetheless held guilty of standing by, and of stripping the ruin of Thule once the battle was decided. Its triumph was short. Lion El'Jonson and his Dark Angels arrived, and the Dreadwing utterly destroyed Galatia. The ruin was largely left unvisited even at the end of the Heresy, since few wished to draw the wrath of the I Legion by plundering it.[1]

    Triplex Phall

    [edit]

    Triplex Phall was the second Forge World. It began as an adjunct of Galatia, and grew swiftly in the years of Old Night into a notable industrial and martial power, defending Triplex against xenos and widening its small domain. The ''Legio Victorum II'', the Foe Hammers, came to rival the Legio of Galatia and won the honour of representing Triplex in the Great Crusade. The spoils of that service raised Phall above its siblings and made it one of the main shipyards of the Crusade outside the Sol System.[1]

    Under the patronage of Horus, Phall became the foremost power of the Eastern Fringe, and its pride angered Mars. The archmagi of Mars isolated Phall and Triplex from the technology and influence of the Cult Mechanicus, which pushed Phall to research forbidden technologies with help from Horus. In the end the Dark Angels brought Phall to heel. The Lion spared it so that it could repay the harm done with the labour of its manufactoria and the lives of its warriors. Its archmagi worked under the oversight of Dark Angels forge masters, and the princeps and Titans of the Foe Hammers were sent into the most dangerous war zones. By the end of the Thramas Crusade it was much reduced.[1]

    Triplex Thule

    [edit]

    Triplex Thule was the smallest of the three. It was founded by tech-magi cast out of Galatia for an extreme doctrine that condemned even the primitive servo-automata still made in the Mechanicum's worlds. They grew slowly, starved of resources and shunned by their siblings, and had reached only the lowest grade of Primaris ranking when the Imperium arrived. Thule fought a hidden war against the other forges, using a small Taghmata of modified servitors and the agents of the Malagra sect rather than the Titans of the ''Legio Victorum III'', the Foe Breakers.[1]

    Thule spurned the emissaries of Horus as servants of Phall and its Hereteks. The hidden war grew savage, with bombs and data-phage infections. Thule's retaliation was the murder of a Phallian archmagus by three Malagra adepts. In the opening months of Horus's rebellion Phall made open war on Thule, setting the Legios Victorum I and III against one another. By the time the Night Lords reached the system there was little left of Thule but ruins.[1]

    After the Heresy

    [edit]

    After the battles of the Heresy, only one of the three Forge Worlds, Triplex Phall, survived into the 41st millennium. Its Tech-priests and Titan Legion remained loyal servants of the Emperor. Phall became the primary homeworld of the ''Legio Victorum'', whose ranks swelled with newly built Titans over its ten thousand years of service. The oldest of its Titans, the ''Primus Malachi'', still fights the enemies of the Machine God.[1]
