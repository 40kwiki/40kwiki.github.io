    Inquisition
    Dated chronology of the Inquisition
    The Ordo Malleus, one of the three principal Orders of the Inquisition
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    Span544.M32 to the present
    Principal OrdersOrdo Malleus, Ordo Xenos, Ordo Hereticus
    Founded inThe War of the Beast, 544–546.M32

    This is a timeline of the Inquisition, the secret order of the Imperium of Mankind that holds authority over every other institution of the Imperium. The Orders of the Inquisition were formed in the War of the Beast, and from that time they took part in the great wars of the Imperium, in the hunt for heresy and in the defence against the Tyranids and the Chaos Legions.[1]

    Many of the Inquisition's records are sealed, and the Orders are known to keep their own calendars. Where the sources differ, the date in the Administratum's reconciled register is given. Dates marked "c." rest on the sequence of events.[a]

    32nd and 36th millennia

    [edit]
    DateEvent
    544.M32The Ordo Xenos traces its inception to the period of the Time of Rebirth, as the Inquisition takes its first shape during the War of the Beast.[1]
    544–546.M32During the War of the Beast the Ordo Malleus and Ordo Xenos are formed, and the Deathwatch is raised.
    546.M32The Beheading. The High Lords are murdered on the orders of Drakan Vangorich, and the Inquisition takes a leading part in the restoration of the council.
    c. 288.M36The Ordo Hereticus is founded, in the aftermath of the Age of Apostasy.[b]

    37th to 39th millennia

    [edit]
    DateEvent
    020.M37The Great Cull purges the Adeptus Terra.
    999.M37During the Eighth Black Crusade the Inquisition discovers the ritual pattern of the Chaos campaign and breaks it.

    41st millennium

    [edit]
    Further information: 41st millennium
    DateEvent
    139–160.M41The Gothic War. Inquisitor Horst of the Ordo Malleus supports the Imperial defence of the Gothic Sector.
    c. 160.M41Inquisitor Horst writes his testimony on the Hand of Darkness, which the Ordo Xenos seals.[2]
    447.M41Two brotherhoods of the Grey Knights are sent to the Godjera Incursion, led in part by the Ordo Malleus inquisitor Appoloyon.
    850.M41The Ordo Xenos inquisitor Solomon Lok is believed slain on Beta Anphelion IV.
    997.M41Inquisitor Lord Fidus Kryptman identifies the signs of Hive Fleet Leviathan, which begins the Third Tyrannic War.
    c. 999.M41The Chancellor of the Imperial Council, Lev Tieron, seeks a Dissolution Act. The High Lords, among them the Ecclesiarch Baldo Slyst, oppose it at the time of the Thirteenth Black Crusade.[c]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus
    DateEvent
    999.M41 – presentThe Indomitus Crusade is launched against the Chaos Legions and the xenos threats of the Rift years.[3]
