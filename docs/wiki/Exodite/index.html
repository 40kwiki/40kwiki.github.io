    Exodite
    People of the Aeldari
    HomeworldsExodite worlds
    LifestyleSettled, agrarian and pastoral
    RelationsShared kinship with the Asuryani, Drukhari and Harlequins

    Exodites are the Aeldari who chose to live on worlds of their own rather than in the Craftworlds or the Webway. Their worlds are called the Exodite worlds. The Exodites are close in blood to the Asuryani of the craftworlds, and Imperial observers record that they share many customs with them.[1]

    Unlike the craftworld Aeldari, the Exodites live on the surface of planets and farm, herd and build in the old style. They are welcomed by every Aeldari people, and the Harlequins travel among them. Their fighting strength is small, but their worlds are well defended.[1]

    Way of life

    [edit]

    The Exodites hold that the old ways of the Aeldari are best kept on the land. Their settlements are small, and they build few great machines. Imperial accounts describe their warriors as skilled with the bow and the blade, and as fighting in close bands.[a]
