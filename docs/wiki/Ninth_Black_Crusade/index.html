    Part of the Black Crusades

    The Ninth Black Crusade was a Black Crusade led by the Chaos Champion Abaddon the Despoiler and his Black Legion. It was launched out of the Eye of Terror in 537.M38. Abaddon's intended target was the naval fortress of Cancephalus, but he first struck the nearby world of Antecanis. The campaign lasted about seventeen standard years, and it stripped both worlds of their most valuable resource, manpower.

    Siege of Monarchhive

    [edit]

    Abaddon's vanguard besieged Monarchhive, the hive city that was the seat of Antecanis' Imperial Commander. The Black Legionaries burned and butchered their way through the lower levels of the hive, while Abaddon himself stormed its inner sanctums. The population was slowly destroyed.

    When the Astra Militarum arrived from Cancephalus to reinforce the survivors, Abaddon and his favoured Legionaries had already left orbit. Before leaving, they dropped a dozen cyclonic warheads onto the ruins of Monarchhive as a last gesture of contempt.

    War in the sector

    [edit]

    The war that followed lasted seventeen standard years. Without the fleets of Cancephalus to stop him, Abaddon ravaged the rest of the sector at will. His Sorcerers performed the rituals that weakened the boundary between realspace and the Warp in the region.

    The High Lords of Terra recalled several Crusading Chapters to deal with the rising threat from the Eye of Terror, including the Lamenters. Fierce fighting followed across the sector. Two Chapters named the Celestial Swords were destroyed in it. Imperial records give the shared name as a clerical error of the Adeptus Administratum.

    Defence of Corillia

    [edit]

    The best known episode of the war was the defence of the Hive world of Corillia by the Lamenters. The Mortifactors refused to fight beside the Lamenters, whom they considered cursed, and abandoned the planet. The Lamenters stayed alone and held out for six solar weeks against the Black Legion's onslaught, suffering heavy losses. Relief came only when the Ultramarines and White Scars led an Imperial battle group that broke through the Chaos Warfleet.

    The sacrifice left the Lamenters with barely 200 Battle-Brothers. Their fleet vanished into a Warp Storm soon afterwards and was given up as lost. More than a century later it reappeared on the edge of the Segmentum Solar, having slowly fought its way back from the outer void.[1]
