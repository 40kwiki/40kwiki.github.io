    Euphrati Keeler, venerated as Saint Euphrati, was an imagist of the Remembrancer Order attached to Horus's 63rd Expeditionary Fleet who became the leading prophet of the Lectitio Divinitatus, the cult that worshipped the Emperor as a god. Her faith was such that daemons fled from it. She escaped the Isstvan III atrocity of 005.M31 aboard the Eisenstein, preached on Terra through the Horus Heresy, and in the last hours of the Siege of Terra in 014.M31 led the prayer that drove back the Death Guard at the Hollow Mountain and relit the Astronomican.[1][2]

    After the Siege Sigismund hid her away, and her later life is unrecorded. She was named among the first saints of the Imperial Cult after the founding of the Ecclesiarchy.[3]

    Early life

    [edit]

    Keeler was a young Terran picture-maker chosen for the Remembrancer Order, founded by the Emperor to record the Great Crusade. She was attached to the 63rd Expedition aboard the Vengeful Spirit c. 001.M31. Her images included the Luna Wolves captain Garviel Loken swearing his oath before the battle of the Whisperhead Mountains on the world called Sixty-Three Nineteen, and pictures of a comet near El'Phanor, later named the Keeler Comet in her honour.[4]

    Conversion

    [edit]

    On Sixty-Three Nineteen c. 001.M31 Keeler and the Iterator Kyril Sindermann slipped away from their escort into the rebel tunnels and saw a daemon, which called itself Samus, take the body of a Luna Wolves sergeant and kill his squad. Horus suppressed the incident. Some time later, in the archive of the Vengeful Spirit, Sindermann read from the Book of Lorgar and summoned a daemon by accident. Keeler stood before it holding an aquila and banished it with her faith, then fell into a long trance. Word spread through the fleet's secret believers, who called her "the Saint." Horus's agents tried to kill her.[1][4]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]
    Further information: Horus Heresy

    Escape

    [edit]

    Keeler woke shortly before the bombing of Isstvan III in 005.M31 and warned that something dark had taken root in the Legion. With Sindermann and Mersadie Oliton she reached Captain Iacton Qruze, who got them off the flagship, and they fled on the Eisenstein with Nathaniel Garro. On Luna the Sisters of Silence held her as a possible psyker, but found none of the witch's gift in her and let her go.[1]

    Terra

    [edit]
    Main article: Terra

    On Terra Keeler preached openly, against the Imperial Truth, and converted many thousands. Horus sent the turned assassin Eristede Kell to kill her and set off religious riots before his invasion. Garro stopped Kell at the last moment. In that fight Keeler shared visions of the future, healed wounds and seemed to stop time. Malcador the Sigillite took her into his keeping beneath the Imperial Palace, less as a prisoner than as a test of whether faith in the Emperor could be a weapon against the Warp. Garro, Sindermann and Sigismund visited her there; to Sigismund she gave the vision that kept him on Terra.[5]

    The Siege

    [edit]

    In the Siege (013–014.M31) her followers sheltered in the Inner Palace among the refugees, under an immunity granted by Malcador. The daemon Cor'bax Utterblight, called up by the traitor Zardu Layak and Typhus, used her cult's own fervour to manifest in the Palace. Keeler learned from it the danger of blind faith, and helped the Custodian Amon Tauromachian and Malcador to defeat it. She was then held in a prison beneath the Hegemon, where Sindermann enlisted her in a new order of remembrancers.[2]

    She left the prison to help an investigation of the despair spreading through the defenders, and was separated from her escort in an ambush. In the ruins she led bands of armed faithful, a forerunner of the Frateris Militia. Garro died in 014.M31 buying her time to escape the Death Guard. In the last hours Keeler led a pilgrimage of survivors out of the Sanctum Imperialis, following a light she could not explain. Sigismund saved them from the Sons of Horus and brought them to the Hollow Mountain, where they stood with Corswain's Dark Angels against Typhus. Keeler led a prayer of the faithful that threw back the Death Guard in a psychic backlash and rekindled the Astronomican.[2]

    Later life and canonisation

    [edit]

    When Roboute Guilliman and the other primarchs reached Terra after the Siege, Sigismund hid Keeler. Where she went, and when she died, are not recorded. After the Imperial Cult became the state faith and the Ecclesiarchy was founded in the 32nd millennium she was named a saint. The image-relic called the "Keeler Image", a picture of Horus before his fall, was found on Pallik in the 41st millennium by the Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn. A Warp phenomenon in the Sabbat Worlds, Euphrate's Warp, is believed to be named for her.[3][6]
