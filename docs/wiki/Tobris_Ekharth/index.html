    Tobris Ekharth
    Master of the Administratum
    Master of the Administratum
    TenureIncluding the War of the Beast (544–546.M32)
    AllegianceImperium of Mankind
    Known forOpposing the campaign against the Ork Beast on grounds of the Codex Astartes

    Tobris Ekharth was the Master of the Administratum during the War of the Beast (544–546.M32). He held that the proposed campaign against the Ork Beast violated the Codex Astartes, since it would send several Chapters under a single command. He was allied with Udin Macht Udo and the Lord High Admiral Lansung.[1]

    Ekharth's opposition is recorded as one of the reasons the council was slow to act against the Orks. His views are preserved in the minutes of the council, which the Administratum keeps.

    War of the Beast

    [edit]

    The proposal was to send several Chapters under one command to hunt the Orks' strategic assets and, eventually, the Beast itself. Ekharth held that this breached the Codex Astartes. Erekart Mesring, the Ecclesiarch, called it blasphemy. A first vote went against the proposal, with Drakan Vangorich alone in favour. The dormant moon above the Ork world was then found to be a threat, and the council's position changed.[1]

    Legacy

    [edit]

    Ekharth is recorded in the Administratum's histories as a defender of the Codex Astartes at a time when its authority was tested. The Administratum's own doctrine has since treated the Codex as the basis for the Chapters' command.
