    The Master of Sanctity, also styled High Chaplain or First Chaplain, is the head of the Chaplaincy in a Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and the head of the Chapter cult, its body of beliefs and customs. He is one of the Masters of the Chapter and oversees its spiritual health by assigning Chaplains to the companies.[1]

    Directly beneath him stands the Reclusiarch, head of the Reclusiam. In some Chapters the duties of the two offices are held by one man.[1]
