    Incubi
    Drukhari mercenary warrior order
    AllegianceDrukhari, sworn to Khaine and to their contracts
    Based inShrines in the Dark City of Commorragh
    LeadersHierarchs, and Klaivex squad leaders
    FounderArhra, the Fallen Phoenix (disputed)
    Signature weaponKlaive
    Notable membersDrazhar, Morr

    The Incubi are an order of elite mercenary warriors of the Drukhari, who live in the Dark City of Commorragh. They serve as bodyguards and shock troops for anyone who can pay for their loyalty. Their craft is the killing stroke, and they practise it with the Klaive, a two-handed greatsword.

    Each Incubi order is organised into a shrine dedicated to Khaine, the Aeldari god of war. The shrine is led by a Hierarch. Incubi are very similar to the Aspect Warriors of the Craftworld Aeldari, but where the Aspect Warriors seek calm and purity, the Incubi seek murder and contract.

    Induction and training

    [edit]

    Becoming an Incubus is dangerous. Aspirants are trained through long and gruelling practice. The weak are cut down, and their bodies are burnt as offerings to the statue of Khaine that stands at the heart of every shrine. An aspirant who defeats an Incubus and takes his armour begins a final trial. He must kill an Aspect Warrior of a craftworld in single combat, take the prey's Soul Stone and turn it into a psychic torture device for a Tormentor Helm.[1]

    Promotion comes through combat too. An Incubus who wants a higher place must defeat its holder in personal combat. A Hierarch can be replaced only in a fight to the death among the surviving Incubi of the shrine. Assassination is not tolerated within the order, unlike almost everywhere else in Commorragh.[1]

    Service and contracts

    [edit]

    Archons pay the Incubi to guard them, and the Incubi surround their charge in a ring of blades. They are also hired as enforcers against rival kabals. Because they follow their contract to the letter and stay neutral in kabal politics, they are sought after as bodyguards. A subordinate who wants an archon's place must first find a loophole that voids the contract, or face the Incubi in combat.[1]

    Wargear

    [edit]

    Almost every Incubus carries a Klaive, a masterwork of Aeldari craft. Their Klaivex leaders sometimes prefer the Demi-klaive, two paired blades that can be used separately or joined into a single sword. Some shrines favour the Punisher, a two-handed power weapon in the form of a halberd. Their Incubus Warsuit is protected by a close-guarded manufacture process, and its helm holds the Tormentor Helm. Some Tormentor Helms carry Bloodstones, which can boil an enemy's blood with a pulse of psychic energy.[1]
