    The Planetary Defence Force, usually abbreviated PDF, is the principal military force of a settled Imperial world. It is also called the planetary militia, or the planetary guard. The PDF is under the control of the local government, which is led by the Planetary Governor. The raising, training, arming and leadership of its units rest with the governor, and the Imperial Army does not command them directly.[1]

    Every Imperial world is required to keep a standing force of this kind. The purpose is to keep the world under the control of its governor, and so under the control of the Imperium, against invasion or revolt. The PDF is the first line of defence. It holds the world until Imperial reinforcements from the Astra Militarum or the Imperial Navy arrive.[2]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    During the Great Crusade (798.M30 – 000.M31) it fell to the Imperialis Militia to hold the worlds that the crusade had brought into compliance. These regiments formed the lowest and most common part of the Imperialis Auxilia. When a world was declared compliant, its governor was given the duty of raising a militia to protect the world and keep the Imperial administration in place.[3]

    The militia enforced the Imperial Truth where that was needed. It also protected the administrators left behind to oversee the long transition to full obedience. When the crusade fleets departed, these worlds stood alone, and the militia was their only defence against rebellion and raiders.[1]

    After the Heresy

    [edit]

    The reforms that followed the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) placed the militias under the Departmento Munitorum. The reforms are dated to c. 015–021.M31 (estimated). The Munitorum set the standards of training and equipment, while the governor kept command of the force on his or her world.[4][a]

    The Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    In the Era Indomitus the PDF of many worlds has been reduced to a few hundred soldiers, or to none at all, by the Great Rift and by wars. On some worlds the militia has been replaced by storm troopers hired or kept by the governor. Where the Indomitus Crusade has reclaimed a world, its PDF is usually the first Imperial force to be restored.[1]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    The PDF has no single standard. Its organisation depends on the size and the needs of the world. Frontier worlds may have a single company of militia. Hive worlds may keep regiments and professional corps, and a few worlds keep special forces. Some PDF units are house troops of a noble family, and they serve under the governor's command.[1]

    Command

    [edit]

    The planetary governor commands the PDF, or appoints a commander to lead it. Regiments in larger militias answer to a commander who reports to the governor. Where a sector command is nearby, it may send officers to advise the militia or to inspect its units.[2]

    Recruitment

    [edit]

    The PDF is the chief source of recruits for the Astra Militarum. Its best soldiers are sent to Imperial regiments for further training and then shipped to their posts across the galaxy. This gives the PDF a second role, as a school for the Guard.[1]

    Loyalty

    [edit]

    Imperial accounts describe the PDF as crucial but unreliable. The militia is rarely respected by the Imperial Army, because it has little experience of war. Its loyalty to its governor is also not always assured, and many militia regiments have turned on their governors when the offer of reward was high enough.[b][4]

    [edit]
