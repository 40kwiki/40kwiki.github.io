    Logan Grimnar, the Great Wolf, also called the Old Wolf and the High King of Fenris, is the Chapter Master of the Space Wolves. A warrior of Fenris taken into the Chapter in 313.M41, he became the Wolf Lord of the Champions of Fenris in 415.M41 and was elected Great Wolf, without a single dissenting vote, in 440.M41. He has ruled the Chapter for more than five and a half centuries and has lived for more than seven hundred years, making him one of the longest-serving commanders in the history of the Adeptus Astartes.[1]

    Grimnar is famous across the Imperium for his victories, among them the defeat of Angron at the First War for Armageddon, and equally for his readiness to fight other Imperial institutions when he judges their actions dishonourable. He has defied the Inquisition repeatedly, most violently in the Months of Shame after Armageddon and in the siege of Fenris by the Inquisitor Lord Kysnaros, whom he beheaded on the bridge of his own flagship. He was the supreme commander of Imperial forces at the Cadian Gate in the 13th Black Crusade and led the defence of the Fenris System against Magnus the Red, whom he wounded with the Axe Morkai.[1][2]

    In the Era Indomitus Grimnar remains Great Wolf, and leads the Chapter from The Fang in Terminator armour as he has for centuries. Unlike those of Marneus Calgar, Commander Dante and Azrael, no Imperial record reports his crossing of the Rubicon Primaris. Under his rule the Space Wolves have accepted Primaris warriors of Russ' line and tested them in the Fenrisian manner.[1][3]

    Early life

    [edit]

    Grimnar was born among the Iron Blood, a tribe of the Fenrisian seas, and was known in his youth as a fearless fighter and a bold sailor. When the land mass called the Kraken's Spur rose from the Boiling Ocean, the tribes fought for it in the battle remembered as the Sea of Blades. In the ruins of the Temple of Morkai the leader of the Sea Devils, Thorgil Icetooth, ambushed Grimnar's crew with his Kraken Guard and cut them down until Grimnar stood alone on the altar with an axe in either hand. A Wolf Priest who had followed his raids for days watched him kill a dozen tribesmen and then Thorgil, and later swore that he had seen the shadow of Leman Russ rise behind the young warrior. Grimnar was taken to the Fang in 313.M41.[1]

    The Wolf Priest Ulrik the Slayer tested him from the start. In the Trial of Morkai Grimnar killed the ice troll Frostblood, which had slain dozens of aspirants, by driving a broken bone into its eye. The Canis Helix bonded with him without difficulty, and he joined the Great Company of Wolf Lord Asvald Stormwrack as a Blood Claw. His charm earned him friends even among the cynical Long Fangs.[1]

    Wolf Lord and Great Wolf

    [edit]

    At the Battle of Blood Falls on Hesperia in 357.M41, the Grey Hunter Grimnar stood alone over the trapped Asvald for long minutes against the renegades of the Bloody Crescent, and was raised to the Wolf Guard. In 415.M41, after a decade of war against the pale Orks of the Cyclopean Rift on Zylor IX, Asvald and the Ork warboss Dakfang mortally wounded each other and fell together into the dark beneath the hive. Grimnar was named Wolf Lord in his place.[1]

    In 440.M41, on the battlefields of Xor, a Drukhari succubus killed the Great Wolf Sigvald Grimhammer. Ulrik put forward Grimnar's name, though he was younger than every other Wolf Lord by centuries. When the rune-stones were counted, every vote had been cast for him.[1]

    As a young Great Wolf he also slew, alone and unarmed, the Thunderwolf Fellclaw that terrorised the northern plains of Fenris, biting out its throat. In its lair he found two orphaned cubs, which he carried back to the Fang in the wolf's pelt. They grew into the Thunderwolves Tyrnak and Fenrir, which have been at his side ever since. He wears Fellclaw's pelt and gilded skull to this day.[1]

    The First War for Armageddon and its aftermath

    [edit]

    In 444.M41 the World Eaters and a daemonic host led by the Daemon Primarch Angron invaded the hive world of Armageddon. The newly elected Great Wolf led the Space Wolves to its relief, broke the World Eaters before the hastily raised Imperial lines, and saw Angron and his twelve Bloodthirsters banished by the Grey Knights.[1]

    The Inquisition then began to purge the Astra Militarum survivors and the population who had witnessed the daemonic invasion. Grimnar demanded that the purges stop. In the conflict that followed, known to the Space Wolves as the Months of Shame (445–451.M41), Space Wolves fought Inquisitorial forces and Grey Knights, and it ended only when the ancient Dreadnought Bjorn the Fell-Handed intervened. The feud was revived more than once. When the Inquisitor Lord Kysnaros brought an armada, Grey Knights and the Red Hunters Chapter to bombard the Fang, Grimnar returned with the Chapter fleet, teleported onto the Inquisitor's flagship Corel's Hope and beheaded him at his command throne. Bjorn again imposed a truce. The eight-month engagement left the Chapter fleet and the Fang badly damaged and the trust between the Space Wolves and the Inquisition all but destroyed.[1][a]

    Later reign

    [edit]

    Grimnar's sagas record campaigns in every segmentum. He ended the Palacia Heresy in 499.M41 by taking command of the bickering Imperial regiments and halting the execution of the planet's population; he purged the space hulk Well of Darkness; he rescued a lost warrior of the Wolf Brothers from the Black Legion before Fabius Bile could experiment on him; he destroyed an Ecclesiarchy envoy ship that tried to force its way into the Fang; and he led the 30th Great Hunt for the lost primarch in 900.M41. He fought the Necrons of Trazyn the Infinite on Midgardia in 933.M41 and broke the phalanxes of Imotekh the Stormlord on Vhaloth IV in 988.M41.[1]

    In 998.M41 he sent five Great Companies to the Third War for Armageddon and fought the Orks of Grukk Face-Rippa in the Sanctus Reach. In 999.M41 he mustered the whole Chapter for the 13th Black Crusade and was named supreme commander of the Imperial forces of the Cadian Gate. There the Space Wolves fought beside the Dark Angels of Supreme Grand Master Azrael at Kasr Tyrok, a temporary truce that ended in insults as soon as the battle was won.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    The siege of the Fenris system

    [edit]

    As the galaxy convulsed at the end of the 41st millennium, the return of the Wulfen and the plots of Magnus the Red brought war to Fenris itself. The Thousand Sons invaded the system to take vengeance for the Burning of Prospero. Grimnar led the reclamation of the world of Midgardia with the Wolf Lord Egil Iron Wolf against the Nurglish daemons of the Infernal Tetrad, and was stranded on the planet before Egil rescued him shortly before Magnus destroyed it.[2]

    In the final battle against the Daemon Primarch, Grimnar led the Space Wolves, the Grey Knights and the Dark Angels together. Egil Iron Wolf gave his life to open the way, and Grimnar struck Magnus with the Axe Morkai. The wound bought the Grey Knights time to banish the Daemon Primarch to the Warp. Fenris was saved, though the Fang and the system's worlds were left scarred.[2]

    The Indomitus Crusade

    [edit]
    Main article: Indomitus Crusade

    Fenris lies in the Imperium Sanctus, on the near side of the Rift. In the years that followed, the Space Wolves received Primaris Space Marines of Russ' line from the Indomitus Crusade. Many of the Chapter's older warriors doubted that these were true sons of the primarch. Grimnar accepted them into the Great Companies on the condition that they prove themselves in the Fenrisian way, and the Chapter has since raised new Primaris successors from its gene-seed for the first time in many millennia.[3][1]

    Whether Grimnar has undergone the Rubicon Primaris, as most of his peers among the great Chapter Masters have, is not stated in any record released by the Fang. The sagas are silent on the matter; Fenrisian tradition holds that the Allfather and Russ judge a warrior by his deeds, not his organs.[1]

    Status

    [edit]

    As of the latest records Logan Grimnar is alive and in command of the Space Wolves as Great Wolf. He still wears his ornate Terminator armour and is attended by Tyrnak, Fenrir and his champion Arjac Rockfist. He leads the Chapter's wars across the Imperium Sanctus, and the hunt for Leman Russ, from the Fang.[3][1]

    Character

    [edit]

    Grimnar is loud, charismatic and generous, more likely to reward a warrior with a slap on the back and a horn of mead than with a formal commendation. Ulrik still calls him "young Grimnar", and the Great Wolf lets it pass. He has no patience with bureaucracy or with authorities he considers faithless, and the Administratum has learned that it is wiser to have the Space Wolves as allies than as enemies. Beneath the bluster, his chroniclers stress, he is a cunning strategist with an iron will.[1]

    Wargear

    [edit]
    • The Axe Morkai, a two-headed frost axe of great age, named for the two-headed wolf that guards the gates of the Fenrisian underworld.
    • Ornate Terminator armour with the Belt of Russ, which projects a protective field, and the pelt of Fellclaw.
    • A storm bolter, and the chariot Stormrider, drawn by Thunderwolves.
