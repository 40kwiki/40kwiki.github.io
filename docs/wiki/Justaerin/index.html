    Justaerin
    Sub-formation of the Sons of Horus Legion
    LegionXVI Legion, formerly the Luna Wolves
    UnitElite of the 1st Company
    CommanderEzekyle Abaddon, the First Captain
    ArmourCataphractii Pattern Terminator Armour

    The Justaerin were one of two sub-formations within the elite 1st Company of the Luna Wolves, later the Sons of Horus, of the XVI Legion. They were led by the Legion's First Captain, Ezekyle Abaddon. They wore black armour that marked them as specialists in harrying actions, shock assaults and decapitation strikes.[1]

    Role

    [edit]

    The Justaerin formed the point of the spear for the XVI Legion. They went where the fighting was thickest, and aimed their attacks at the heart of an enemy force.[1]
