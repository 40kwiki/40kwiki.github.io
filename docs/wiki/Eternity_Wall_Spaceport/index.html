    The Eternity Wall Spaceport is one of the great spaceports of the Imperial Palace on Terra, named for the Eternity Wall, the rampart of the outer Palace beside which it stands. Together with the Lion's Gate Spaceport, it was one of the two ports nearest to the Palace at the time of the Horus Heresy, and both were among the first objectives of the traitors in the Siege of Terra.[1][2]

    The port was lost at the start of the ground war in 014.M31 and stayed in traitor hands for the rest of the Siege. It served as a landing ground for the armies of Horus, and a loyalist attempt to retake it failed. At the end of the Siege the broken traitor armies fled Terra through it. It was rebuilt after the Heresy and serves the Palace still.[1][3]

    History

    [edit]

    Construction

    [edit]

    The spaceport was built during the Great Crusade, when the Palace grew into the capital of the Imperium and needed landing fields large enough for the traffic of the expedition fleets. Its date of construction is not recorded. It lay outside the inner walls of the Palace but inside the outer defences laid down by Rogal Dorn in the years before the Siege.[2][a]

    Siege of Terra

    [edit]
    Main article: Siege of Terra

    The traitor fleet reached Terra in 013.M31 and bombarded the Palace for weeks. When the bombardment ended in 014.M31, traitor Astartes landed by drop pod around the Palace and advanced on the two nearest spaceports to secure them for the main landings. Elements of five traitor Legions took part, aided by cultists and renegade troops already on the surface. Both the Eternity Wall and the Lion's Gate spaceports fell within hours. Cultists made blood sacrifices around the captured ports and called greater daemons onto Terra, and the main traitor armies, with their Titans and the forces of the Dark Mechanicum, landed through them.[1]

    Imperial accounts of the loss differ. The older chronicles say that both ports were taken by storm. The fuller Palace archives opened in later millennia record that Dorn, as Praetorian of Terra, judged that the Eternity Wall port could not be held, and gave it up so that its defenders could strengthen the walls. The Captain-General of the Adeptus Custodes, Constantin Valdor, is recorded as having objected to the decision.[3][1][b]

    Some days later, Jaghatai Khan and the White Scars retook the Lion's Gate Spaceport in a night attack and turned its guns on the traitors' landing craft. Encouraged by this, the loyalists also struck at the Eternity Wall Spaceport, but the traitors had reinforced its garrison after the loss of the Lion's Gate, and the attack was driven back.[1][3]

    Some older chronicles also praise the Blood Angels of Sanguinius for their defence of the Eternity Wall during the Siege. Later scholars read these accounts as referring to the rampart and the inner defences rather than the spaceport, which was already lost.[1]

    End of the Siege

    [edit]

    After the death of Horus aboard the Vengeful Spirit in 014.M31, the traitor armies broke. Many fell back on the Eternity Wall Spaceport to escape Terra. The White Scars, with remnants of the Imperial Army's 1st Terran Tank Division and several infantry regiments, attacked the traitors' supply lines as they fled, but many escaped from the port into the Great Scouring and the Eye of Terror.[1]

    After the Heresy

    [edit]

    The spaceport was rebuilt during the reconstruction of the Palace, which went on for decades after the Siege. In the following millennia it served the traffic of the Adeptus Terra and the Navy. In the Era Indomitus it handled part of the arrivals of the Terran Crusade of Roboute Guilliman in 999.M41, and the Palace's ports have since been among the busiest in the Segmentum Solar.[2][4][c]
