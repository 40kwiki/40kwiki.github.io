    Szarekh, the Silent King, is the last of the Silent Kings of the Szarekhan Dynasty, who ruled the Necrontyr through the council called the Triarch. In his reign the Necrontyr accepted the bargain of the C'tan and were made into the undying Necrons. After the War in Heaven, which ended about sixty million years before the 41st millennium, he led his people in revolt against the C'tan, ordered the dynasties into the Great Sleep and left the galaxy.[1]

    He returned in 774.M41, having seen the Tyranids in the void between galaxies, and has since tried to bring the scattered dynasties back under one rule. In the Era Indomitus he leads his forces in person and is held by the Inquisition to be behind the Pariah Nexus, an attempt to cut the galaxy from the Warp.[1][2]

    Reign before the Sleep

    [edit]

    The Silent Kings were named for a law that forbade them to speak to any but the Triarch's members and their heralds. Szarekh inherited an empire weakened by its failed war on the Old Ones and by the civil wars of its dynasties. To hold it together he renewed the war, and when the C'tan offered his people immortality, he accepted. The Necrontyr went through biotransference into bodies of living metal. Szarekh's own mind survived intact, but most of his people lost their souls, and most their minds as well.[1]

    Szarekh came to see what he had done. When the Old Ones had fallen, he led the revolt that shattered the C'tan into shards. Too weakened to fight the Aeldari, he ordered the dynasties to sleep until the younger races had faded. As his last act he released his people from the command protocols that bound them to his will, and he left the galaxy for the void beyond.[1]

    Return

    [edit]

    In the void between galaxies Szarekh saw the hive fleets of the Tyranids approaching. If they consumed all life in the galaxy, there would be no bodies of flesh left for the Necrons to claim, and the hope of restoring them would die. In 774.M41, by Ordo Xenos reckoning, he re-entered the galaxy.[1][a]

    For more than two centuries he worked largely in secret. Imperial records attribute to his forces a war on Gehenna in 955.M41, where the Blood Angels under Commander Dante fought a Necron host until a Tyranid splinter fleet forced both sides to fight it together; some Inquisitors doubt that the Silent King himself was present.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    After the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, Szarekh revealed himself. He took back the authority of the Triarch and called on the dynasties to unite under him against the Tyranids and the powers of the Warp, which he blames for the Old Ones' war and for the ruin of his people. Many dynasties obeyed; others, led by Imotekh the Stormlord of the Sautekh, refused, and the Necrons have since fought a civil war alongside their wars with the younger races.[2][3]

    The Inquisition holds Szarekh responsible for the Pariah Nexus, a region in the south of the Imperium Nihilus where a lattice of blackstone pylons has severed worlds from the Warp and drained their people of will, an affliction called the Stilling. His stated purpose is to free the galaxy from the Warp, whatever the cost to the living. Imperial forces have fought him in the Nexus throughout the early 42nd millennium.[2]

    Character and wargear

    [edit]

    Szarekh is described by those who have dealt with him as cold, patient and burdened by guilt for what he did to his people. He rides into battle on the Dais of Dominion, a floating throne carried with his two Triarch companions, and is guarded by Triarch Praetorians. He carries the Staff of Stars and the Sceptre of Eternal Glory, and directs his armies through the old command protocols of the Triarch.[1][3]
