    The Skitarii, collectively the Legiones Skitarii and sometimes called the Tech-Guard, are the soldiers of the Adeptus Mechanicus. They are men and women whose bodies have been extensively replaced with machinery, armed with the most advanced weapons of the forge worlds and directed in battle by the tech-priests of the Cult Mechanicus. Every forge world maintains its own Skitarii Legions, which guard it in peace and march with the Mechanicus's armies in war.[1][2]

    The Skitarii are not part of the Astra Militarum and do not answer to its commanders. They serve the Mechanicus alone, in accordance with the Treaty of Olympus, and fight beside the Titan Legions and Knight houses of the forge worlds, or as part of the great expeditions of the Explorator fleets. They are regarded in the Imperium with a mixture of respect and unease, because their discipline is absolute and their humanity uncertain.[2]

    Each Skitarius is connected to his commanders through the noosphere, the data-field of the Mechanicus. Through it the tech-priests can alter their soldiers' behaviour in the midst of battle by broadcasting doctrina imperatives, overriding fear, pain and even the will to survive.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    The Skitarii existed on Mars before the Imperium. During the Age of Strife, when Mars was cut off from Terra and torn by wars between rival forges, the tech-priests armed and augmented their followers to defend their domains. When the Treaty of Olympus joined Mars to the Imperium, these forces became the guardians of the Mechanicum and its forge worlds.[1]

    Great Crusade and Heresy

    [edit]
    See also: Great Crusade and Horus Heresy

    During the Great Crusade the armies of the forge worlds were organised into Taghmata, the war hosts of the Mechanicum, in which the Skitarii fought beside battle-automata, the Titans of the Collegia Titanica and the Knight houses. They took part in many of the crusade's campaigns, and the Mechanicum's Explorator fleets travelled with every major expedition.[1][3]

    When the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) divided the Mechanicum, the Skitarii divided with it. On Mars, Fabricator General Kelbor-Hal sided with Horus, and Skitarii fought Skitarii across the Red Planet in the Schism of Mars. Traitor forge worlds sent their Skitarii to the armies of the Warmaster, and many of those who survived the Heresy fled with the traitors into the Eye of Terror, where their descendants serve the Dark Mechanicum.[3]

    Late 41st millennium

    [edit]

    In the ten thousand years after the Heresy, the Skitarii fought wherever the interests of the Mechanicus were threatened: to defend forge worlds, to recover ancient technology, and to support Imperial campaigns. They were prominent in the defence of the forge world of Ryza against the Orks, in the wars of the Armageddon system, and against the Necrons, whose awakening threatened worlds that the Mechanicus prized.[2]

    One Legion has stood guard for millennia around the Noctis Labyrinthus of Mars, an area forbidden to all by the Mechanicus, and is said to have destroyed a Necron force that landed there.[4]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Since the opening of the Great Rift, the Skitarii have been fully committed to the defence of the forge worlds, many of which lie in the Imperium Nihilus and have been cut off from Mars. The Legions of Archmagos Belisarius Cawl accompanied the Indomitus Crusade, and Skitarii forces have fought in the Pariah Nexus against the Necrons, and against Chaos forces seeking to capture the forges.[5]

    Creation

    [edit]

    Skitarii are drawn from the populations of the forge worlds, from children raised in the Mechanicus's schools, and in some cases from bodies grown in vats. Each is altered by stages throughout his service. Limbs are replaced with mechanical prostheses, organs with augmetics, and senses with electronic equivalents. Ultimately even the mind is reshaped: memories are removed or rewritten, and implants allow the tech-priests to control the soldier through the noosphere. A veteran Skitarius may have little of his original body left, and his augmetics are a mark of honour, not of injury.[1]

    Despite this, Skitarii remain human in important respects. Unlike servitors, they keep their own minds and some of their own will, and they are capable of courage, initiative and loyalty. They worship the Omnissiah devoutly, and many hope to rise into the priesthood.[1]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    The Skitarii are organised in Legions, each attached to a forge world and named for it. A Legion is divided into cohorts and maniples. The basic unit is the squad, led by an Alpha; the senior Alpha of a maniple is the Alpha Primus. A Skitarii Marshal commands a larger force, but above every Skitarius stands the tech-priest who directs his formation, usually a Tech-priest Dominus, whose commands are law.[1]

    Each forge world arms and equips its Legions according to its own traditions and specialisations. The Legions of Mars are the most numerous and the most orthodox. Those of Lucius specialise in the use of its sun-forge; those of Ryza in plasma weapons; those of Stygies VIII in stealth and infiltration; those of Graia in unbreakable defence; those of Metalica in relentless attack, accompanied by a din of machine noise; and those of Agripinaa, near the Eye of Terror, in long attrition against the forces of Chaos.[1][2]

    Forces

    [edit]
    Skitarii Vanguard
    Infantry armed with radium carbines, whose weapons and own bodies are so radioactive that their very presence sickens the enemy.
    Skitarii Rangers
    Infantry trained for long campaigns in hostile terrain, armed with galvanic rifles whose rounds can overload a target's nervous system.
    Sicarian Ruststalkers and Infiltrators
    Elite hunters, tall and swift, built for close combat and assassination. Infiltrators carry neurostatic emitters that scramble the senses of their foes.
    Ironstrider Ballistarii and Sydonian Dragoons
    Mounted on long-legged walkers, they act as the Skitarii's cavalry. The Dragoons charge with taser lances, while the Ballistarii carry heavy cannon.
    Onager Dunecrawlers
    Four-legged war engines that carry heavy weapons and protect the Skitarii with shield generators.

    Doctrine

    [edit]

    The Skitarii fight with precision and without fear. Their commanders can send doctrina imperatives through the noosphere, compelling their troops to adopt one form of behaviour at the expense of another. Protector imperatives make them steadfast and accurate at range; Conqueror imperatives drive them forward to close and destroy. The cost of an imperative is the Skitarius's freedom of action, and in extreme cases his life, because the commands override the body's natural instinct to survive.[1]

    The Skitarii are equipped with weapons that the rest of the Imperium rarely sees, including arc rifles, transuranic arquebuses, plasma calivers and phosphor weapons, and they are among the best-armed soldiers of the Imperium. Their weakness is their number: no forge world can raise Skitarii in the quantities of the Astra Militarum, and the Mechanicus guards its Legions jealously.[2]

    Heraldry and appearance

    [edit]

    Skitarii wear hooded robes over their armour plates and augmetics, and the colours of both are fixed by the traditions of their forge world. Most Legions wear some form of the red of Mars as a mark of loyalty to the Red Planet. Others depart from it: the Legions of Agripinaa reverse the Martian red and black, those of Lucius wear plate scorched almost black by the artificial sun at the heart of their world, and those of Ryza wear ochre. Stygies VIII, a forge world long suspected of heresy, uses the colours of Mars redistributed to proclaim its orthodoxy. Each Skitarius also bears glyphs that identify his maniple and cohort, and the purity seals of blood-wax and parchment that mark him as free of scrapcode.[1]
