    From 40,000 Wiki, the Imperial encyclopedia

    40,000 Wiki is an encyclopedia of the Imperium of Mankind: its history, its institutions, its wars and the people who shaped them. Articles are written in a neutral voice, summarise their subject in the opening paragraphs, and cite the archives they draw on.

    Using the encyclopedia

    • Blue links lead to written articles. Red links lead to subjects that do not have an article yet; the Contents page lists the most wanted ones.
    • Hover a link to preview the article, or a footnote number to read the source without scrolling.
    • The Appearance menu changes text size, page width and light or dark colour.

    Sources

    Articles draw on the records of the Adeptus Terra, the Remembrancer Order, the Logos Historica Verita, the Codex Astartes and the archives of the Chapters and Adepta. Imperial accounts often contradict each other, and many records are sealed or were deliberately destroyed. Where sources disagree, articles say so rather than picking one.
