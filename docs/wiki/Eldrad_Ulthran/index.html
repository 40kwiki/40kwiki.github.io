    Eldrad Ulthran is an Aeldari Farseer, for millennia the High Farseer of the craftworld Ulthwé and perhaps the most powerful seer born to his race since its Fall. More than ten thousand years old by the end of the 41st millennium, he has spent his life reading the threads of fate and cutting them to save Aeldari lives. Imperial records hold him responsible for some of the worst disasters of the age, among them the Second War for Armageddon, which he arranged to spare a few thousand Aeldari at the cost of millions of human lives.[1][2]

    In 999.M41 Eldrad led the ritual that partially woke Ynnead, the Aeldari god of the dead, which led to the birth of the Ynnari. Ulthwé's Seer Council exiled him for acting for the whole race without its consent, and he has since served the Ynnari and their prophet Yvraine.[3]

    Early history

    [edit]

    Nothing certain is known of Eldrad's youth. He was already a Farseer at the end of the Great Crusade. On the Maiden World Tarsus, shortly before the Horus Heresy in 005.M31, he tried to warn the Imperium of Horus' treachery, but he spoke to the primarch Fulgrim, who was already corrupted. Fulgrim turned on him, and Eldrad barely escaped.[2][4]

    Manipulations of the 41st millennium

    [edit]

    Eldrad's foresight has guided Ulthwé through countless crises. Among the actions attributed to him:

    • In 783.M41 he foresaw that an Imperial Explorator fleet would wake the Necron tomb world Maedrax.
    • In 941.M41 Ulthwé raids on Ork worlds, guided by his visions, raised the warlord Ghazghkull Thraka to prominence and diverted his Waaagh! towards the hive world Armageddon and away from the craftworld Idharae. The Second War for Armageddon followed. Eldrad judged ten thousand Aeldari lives worth more than the human dead.
    • He led a long campaign that closed a Warp rift over the Exodite world Haran, now called Haranshemash, the world of blood and tears, at great cost.
    • On Andante IV Abaddon the Despoiler attacked a webway gate to draw out Ulthwé's seers, and fought Eldrad himself; Abaddon escaped.
    • In 992.M41 he warned Iyanden of Hive Fleet Kraken in time for it to prepare.

    The dates of the Haran and Andante IV campaigns are not recorded in Imperial archives; both are placed in the 41st millennium.[1][2]

    Ynnead

    [edit]
    Main article: Rise of the Ynnari

    For centuries Eldrad sensed a slow pulse among the souls of Ulthwé's infinity circuit and came to believe that the dead of his race could become a god, Ynnead, able to defeat Slaanesh. In 999.M41 he gathered crystal seers from many craftworlds on the crystal moon Coheria and began a ritual to draw the souls of the dead together, while Ulthwé's warriors attacked Port Demesnus as a feint. The ritual was interrupted, but Ynnead partly woke. In Commorragh the Succubus Yvraine died and rose again as Ynnead's emissary, an event the Aeldari call the Night of Revelations.[3]

    The Seer Council of Ulthwé exiled Eldrad for taking such a decision for the whole race. He joined Yvraine. Accounts of what happened to him in these years differ: some Aeldari sources say that his body had died and that his spirit was restored by Ynnead's power, while Imperial observers record only that he was active among the Ynnari.[3][a]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Eldrad accompanied the Ynnari in their search for the Croneswords, the five blades of the Aeldari goddess of fate. He was with them in the Fall of Cadia and in their part in the return of Roboute Guilliman in 999.M41. He is still at Yvraine's side in the early 42nd millennium, and he believes that the Ynnari are his people's only hope against Slaanesh.[3][5]

    Writings

    [edit]

    Eldrad is credited with the Treachery of Vision, a set of texts on the beginning of the universe, the Old Ones and the War in Heaven. In it he suggests that the Old Ones' use of the Warp in that war gave rise to the first daemons, and that the Harlequins guard Old One secrets in the Black Library. Copies are held by the Ordo Xenos.[6]

    Wargear

    [edit]
    • The Staff of Ulthamar, carried by the chief Farseer of Ulthwé and linked to its infinity circuit.
    • The Armour of the Last Runes, whose runes speak of enduring until the light dies.
    • A ghosthelm, a witchblade, a shuriken pistol and runes of warding and witnessing.
