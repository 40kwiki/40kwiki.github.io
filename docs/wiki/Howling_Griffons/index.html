    The Howling Griffons are a Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes descended from the Ultramarines, created in an unrecorded Founding of the 33rd millennium. Their homeworld is Mancora, near the Ork empire of Charadon in the Eastern Fringe, and their fortress-monastery is the Proud Eyrie. The Chapter is almost always engaged in at least one campaign, and often divides its companies between several wars at once.[1]

    For six thousand years the Howling Griffons have waged a vendetta against the Word Bearers and the warband of Periclitor the Foresworn, who massacred the Chapter Master and the First Company at Arios Point in 220.M38. The Chapter avenged the massacre in the last years of the 41st millennium, when Chapter Master Alvaro left the war against the Thirteenth Black Crusade to pursue Periclitor and his First Company banished the daemon prince.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Founding and early wars

    [edit]

    The reason for the Chapter's creation is lost; Imperial scholars suppose it met a need for more Space Marines in some forgotten crisis. From the date of its five-thousandth anniversary in 220.M38, its Founding is placed in about 220.M33. In the 35th millennium the Howling Griffons joined the Salamanders and the Silver Skulls in a raid on the Drukhari city of Commorragh to rescue the Salamanders' strike cruiser Forgehammer.[1][b]

    Arios Point

    [edit]

    In 220.M38 the Chapter's battle barge, carrying Chapter Master Orlando Furioso, his Honour Guard and the whole First Company home to Mancora for the five-thousandth anniversary celebrations, stopped to resupply in the Arios Point System. There the Chaos Lord Periclitor the Foresworn ambushed it with Word Bearers and Night Lords. None survived, and the raiders took much of the First Company's irreplaceable wargear. Months later the Chapter tracked down the ship and recovered Furioso's body and gene-seed. Every Howling Griffon since has sworn vengeance on Periclitor, who later rose to become a daemon prince, and on the Word Bearers.[1]

    The 40th and 41st millennia

    [edit]

    In 109.M40 the Chapter suppressed a Chaos cult on the agri-world of Dennar IV. In 143.M41 it led the Jorun Retaliation against General Jorun's 15th Heraklon Ironclads, an Astra Militarum battlegroup that had turned traitor in 109.M41 instead of reinforcing the Gothic War. In 905–906.M41 the Fourth Company and parts of others fought a search-and-destroy campaign in the Caradryad Sector, and in the Badab War the Chapter took on convoy duties so that the Red Scorpions and Fire Hawks could fight. The Executioners inflicted catastrophic losses on it at the lunar outposts of Khymara in 907.M41. In 951.M41 it crushed the Amar Secession, and with the 901st Penal Legion it freed Vanqualis from the Orks.[1][2]

    In 999.M41 the Howling Griffons were one of twelve Chapters that went with the Dark Angels to the Siege of the Fenris System, the crisis in which the Space Wolves were also attacked by Magnus the Red and the Thousand Sons. In the same years Alvaro diverted the battle barge Forge of Destiny from the Thirteenth Black Crusade to hunt Periclitor, and the daemon prince was banished in a great void battle against the First Company.[1]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    Led by Alvaro, the Chapter served in the Indomitus Crusade of the returned Guilliman. It fought in the War of Beasts on Vigilus in about 001–025.M42 and in the Plague Wars in Ultramar in about 012.M42, and later at the Battle of Malak during the Arks of Omen. Mancora itself has come under heavy assault by Orks from Charadon.[3]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    The Chapter follows the Codex Astartes but, unlike most Ultramarines successors, does not show company colours on its armour. Its quartered heraldry is among the most recognisable in the Imperium.[1]
