    Tu'Shan is the Chapter Master of the Salamanders and, like every master of that Chapter, Regent of Prometheus, the moon of Nocturne that the Salamanders hold as a feudal fief of the Imperium. He took office in 938.M41 and won the respect of the Adeptus Astartes and the gratitude of Armageddon's people in the Second and Third Wars for Armageddon. He is known above all for a concern that his Chapter shares and that is rare among Space Marine commanders: the protection of the ordinary citizens of the Imperium.[1][2]

    His saying "My sword is at the Emperor's command. I answer to no other man, living or dead" is recorded in the Chapter's annals. In the Era Indomitus he has led the Salamanders through the wars that followed the opening of the Great Rift and the return of Roboute Guilliman, whose Primaris Space Marines now serve in the Chapter.[3]

    Second War for Armageddon

    [edit]
    Main article: Wars for Armageddon

    When the Ork warlord Ghazghkull Thraka invaded Armageddon in 941.M41, Tu'Shan had been Chapter Master for only three years, and many doubted that he was equal to such an enemy. He willingly placed himself under the senior Chapter Master in the war, Dante of the Blood Angels. While the Ultramarines defended the hive cities and the Blood Angels struck at the Orks directly, the Salamanders protected supply convoys and refugee columns, a duty that the other Imperial forces often neglected. Tu'Shan rallied scattered troops of the Planetary Defence Forces and the Astra Militarum, and with the First Company, the Firedrakes, he held one of the few bridges over the Stygies River against a thousand-strong Ork Speed Freek column for three days and four nights.[2]

    At the end of the war Dante sought out the young Chapter Master and praised him before the assembled Blood Angels. For the Salamanders no greater honour exists than the respect of their brothers in arms.[2]

    Third War for Armageddon

    [edit]
    Main article: Wars for Armageddon

    When Ghazghkull returned in 998.M41, fifty-seven years later, Tu'Shan was one of the first to answer, leading six of the Chapter's seven companies to Armageddon and the Firedrakes in person. Commissar Sebastian Yarrick, the hero of the Second War, welcomed his offer gladly, and the two are said to have respected each other at once. The Salamanders directed their attacks against the Rok fortresses the Orks had dropped from orbit, where their skill in close firefights told heavily.[2]

    In one notorious incident the Marines Malevolent captain Vinyard ordered his Whirlwinds to bombard a refugee camp that five thousand Orks had overrun; nearly four thousand refugees died. Tu'Shan rebuked Vinyard in front of the whole Armageddon command staff and reminded him that the first duty of the Space Marines is to protect the people of the Imperium. At Hive Tempestora he stopped the Orks from taking the Khatrin water purification plant; the hive fell, but most of its people escaped. Once more the Salamanders held the Stygies bridge.[2]

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    Further information: Era Indomitus

    Since the Great Rift opened in 999.M41, Tu'Shan has led the Salamanders in the Indomitus Crusade and in the defence of Nocturne and its region, and the Chapter has taken in Primaris reinforcements of Vulkan's line. The Salamanders continue to fight in small, self-reliant forces wherever the Imperium's people are most in danger, true to the Promethean Cult of their primarch.[3][a]

    Character and appearance

    [edit]

    Tu'Shan is humble, steadfast and unusually gentle in his dealings with mortals. Like all Salamanders his skin is coal-black and his eyes red, the result of a reaction between the Chapter's gene-seed and Nocturne's radiation. He wears a cloak of salamander hide, an older and more honoured version of the cloaks of the Firedrakes.[1]

    Wargear

    [edit]

    Tu'Shan fights in artificer armour forged by the Chapter's own smiths, who are counted among the finest in the Adeptus Astartes. Over it he wears the Firedrake Mantle, the hide of one of Nocturne's legendary firedrakes, whose iron-hard scales few weapons can pierce; tradition holds that the beast was slain by a Chapter Master in single combat. His thunder hammer, Stormbearer, is said by Chapter legend to be made of the same material as the weapons of the primarch himself. He has also borne Deathfire, a master-crafted power weapon that Vulkan wielded in the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V in 005.M31, and Prometheus, an ornate flame weapon of ancient design. Like all the Salamanders' relics, these are tended by the Chapter's Forgefathers and are as much objects of devotion as weapons.[1]
