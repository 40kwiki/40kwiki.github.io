    The Imperial Regent, also called the Regent of Terra or the Lord Regent, is a title of high nobility that has been given to a powerful official of the Imperium of Mankind several times, in the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy and throughout the Age of the Imperium. Imperial doctrine holds that the bearer of the title is second only to the Emperor of Mankind in civil authority across the galaxy, and that he speaks with the Emperor's voice in whatever he commands.[1]

    The office is civil and administrative. Its functions have at times overlapped with those of the Lord Commander of the Imperium, a military rank created to give supreme command of the Imperial armed forces and the chair of the Senatorum Imperialis. The Lord Commander is a soldier, while the Regent is expected to attend to civil administration, and the two titles have often been held together by one person. Imperial records do not agree on which title took precedence in each age.[1][2]

    The Regent is one of the few officials of the Imperium whom the Adeptus Custodes guard, a mark of his importance to the Imperium and to Humanity. The title has had four principal holders. Malcador the Sigillite bore it from the Great Crusade until his death in the Siege of Terra. Roboute Guilliman held it for the first time from 014.M31 until he was struck down at the Battle of Thessala in 121.M31, and took it up again in 999.M41. In the mid-32nd millennium the Chapter Master Maximus Thane is recorded as Regent in the confused years after the War of the Beast. The primarch Sanguinius held a related style, Imperator Regis, in the short-lived Imperium Secundus.[3][4]

    Since 999.M41 the title has belonged again to Guilliman, who holds it together with the office of Lord Commander and has used both to reorder the government of the Imperium in the Era Indomitus.[3]

    Powers and limits

    [edit]

    The Regent has supreme civil authority below the Emperor but is not himself a commander of armies. The title confers the right to speak for the Emperor on matters of state, and in practice the Regent acts through the Senatorum Imperialis, the Administratum and the Adeptus Terra. He directs the flow of tithes, supplies and recruits that feeds the armies of the Imperium, and he sets the policy that the planetary and sector governors carry out.[1]

    The Regent cannot overrule the Emperor, whose will is held to be expressed through the Regent's commands, and the holder is bound by the same law as any other official of the Imperium. The Custodes who guard the office also watch it. Imperial accounts record that the title has been given only to men who held the Emperor's complete trust, which is why holders such as Malcador or Guilliman have commonly been drawn from his closest circle.[1][5]

    History

    [edit]

    Creation under Malcador

    [edit]
    See also: Malcador the Sigillite

    The office was first created for Malcador the Sigillite, the Emperor's most trusted counsellor. Imperial accounts give the origin as the aftermath of the Ullanor Crusade, after the triumph at Ullanor, when the Emperor withdrew to the Imperial Palace on Terra to begin the work that would open the next stage of his plan for a new golden age of Mankind. This was the Webway project. The Emperor did not wish to spend his time on the business of state, and he left it to Malcador, who became his regent.[5]

    Earlier records give a different date. Some Great Crusade rolls, which are the oldest, speak of Malcador as regent when the Emperor first left Terra for the stars in c. 798.M30, while the later histories that are most widely read place the creation of the title at the end of the Ullanor Crusade, c. 000.M31. The later account is the one this article follows.[1][a]

    Under Malcador the Regent governed the Throneworld and directed the supply of the Legions while the Emperor's sons campaigned across the galaxy. He held the office until his death. Imperial tradition records that Malcador also secretly held the post of Grand Master of Assassins, so that the greatest civil and the most secret powers of the Imperium sat in one man.[1][5]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]
    Further information: Horus Heresy

    The authority of the Regent was tested by the Horus Heresy, which began in 005.M31. When the Warmaster Horus marched on Terra, Malcador held the Palace together while the Emperor and the loyal Legions prepared to meet him. The Siege of Terra lasted from 013.M31 to 014.M31. In the last days of the siege the Emperor fought Horus aboard the Vengeful Spirit, and the Regent is said to have given the Emperor the last of his psychic strength. Malcador died at the end of the siege, in 014.M31.[3]

    His death left the Imperium without a Regent. Rogal Dorn, who had commanded the defence of the Palace, gave the powers of the military command to Roboute Guilliman, and the civil power of the Regent passed to the same hands.[3][2]

    The Imperium Secundus

    [edit]
    Main article: Imperium Secundus

    During the Heresy the loyal primarch Sanguinius held a separate title, Imperator Regis, in the Imperium Secundus, which was proclaimed in c. 009.M31 on Macragge. Imperial records treat it as a regency in a rival state and list it with the Regents of the Imperium for completeness only. The Imperium Secundus did not outlast the war.[1][b]

    Guilliman and the Time of Rebirth

    [edit]
    See also: Roboute Guilliman and Time of Rebirth

    After the Heresy, Guilliman took the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium and became Imperial Regent as well. From 014.M31 he used both titles to rebuild the government of the Imperium in the Time of Rebirth, and he held them until the Battle of Thessala in 121.M31, where the Daemon Primarch Fulgrim wounded him mortally. His body was placed in stasis on the throne of the Temple of Correction on Macragge, and he did not wake for ten thousand standard years.[2]

    The mid-32nd millennium

    [edit]

    The title was held again in the 32nd millennium. After the War of the Beast, the greatest Ork invasion since the Great Crusade, the Chapter Master Maximus Thane of the Imperial Fists was Lord Commander of the Imperium and, in the later rolls, Imperial Regent and first among equals in the Senatorum. In 546.M32 the Lord Protector Drakan Vangorich, whom Thane had named to govern in his absence, had the High Lords murdered in the Beheading, and he ruled for most of a century through men of his own choosing. Thane returned to Terra and killed him in c. 626.M32.[4][c]

    The office of Lord Commander was abolished afterwards, and no later roll names a Regent until the return of Guilliman. For nearly ten thousand years the civil power of the Imperium belonged to the High Lords of Terra as a council.[4]

    Restoration in 999.M41

    [edit]

    In 999.M41, during the Terran Crusade, Guilliman fought his way to Terra and stood before the Emperor for the first time since the Heresy. Afterwards he told the sitting High Lords that he was again Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent. He removed five of them from office and several hundred of the lesser lords, and replaced them with men of his own choosing. He warned the council that a great wound in the Warp, the Great Rift, was opening across the galaxy, and he told them that the Emperor still watched over his people.[3][6]

    Holders

    [edit]

    Malcador the Sigillite

    [edit]

    Malcador was the first Regent and is the one most often meant when the older rolls speak of the Regent of Terra. He served the Emperor from the earliest days of the Imperium, and by the time of the Great Crusade he was the one man to whom the Emperor entrusted the business of the Throneworld without reservation. He founded or fostered several of the institutions that the Regent still oversees, and tradition holds that he kept his hand on the most secret of them, the Officio Assassinorum, as its Grand Master. His regency lasted from the end of the Ullanor Crusade to his death at the end of the Siege of Terra, some fourteen years, and the Imperium of the Great Crusade was governed in that time almost entirely through his office.[1][5]

    Roboute Guilliman

    [edit]

    Guilliman is the only holder who has borne the title in two ages. In the first, from 014.M31 to 121.M31, he was both Regent and Lord Commander, which made him the only man to command the whole of the armed forces of the Imperium after the Heresy. He used the double authority to carry out the reforms of the Time of Rebirth, restructuring the administrative and military apparatus of the Imperium after the Emperor's internment in the Golden Throne. In the second, begun in 999.M41, he has used it to remove officials he found wanting and to reshape the High Lords of Terra on his own authority.[2][6]

    Maximus Thane

    [edit]

    Thane, a Chapter Master of the Imperial Fists and formerly of the Fists Exemplar, is the least certain of the four. The later rolls call him Regent, but earlier ones give him only the style of Lord Commander and name him the first among equals of the High Lords. Whichever style is correct, his authority did not outlast the crisis of the Beheading. It is Thane who is credited with the Fourth Founding, in which the Imperium raised as many new Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes as its stores of gene-seed could support.[4]
