    Kubik
    Fabricator-General of Mars
    The Icon Mechanicus, seal of the Fabricator-General

    Kubik was the Fabricator-General of Mars, head of the Adeptus Mechanicus and a member of the High Lords of Terra, during the War of the Beast (544–546.M32). He is remembered for two things: his reluctance to bring the power of Mars to the defence of Terra while the Orks held its skies, and his decision, once the war was won, to keep the Ork capital for study instead of destroying it as the Lord Commander had ordered.[1]

    That decision, when it was discovered, persuaded the Grand Master of Assassins Drakan Vangorich that the High Lords served only themselves. It was the immediate cause of the Beheading, in which the council was murdered in a single solar day in 546.M32. Kubik did not survive it.[2]

    Background

    [edit]

    Nothing is published about Kubik's early life, forge or rise through the priesthood of the Cult Mechanicus. The Mechanicus keeps such records to itself, and the annals of the Fabricator-General that are open to scholars name him only in the context of the war.[3] By 544.M32 he governed Mars and its forges, commanded the Skitarii legions and the other fighting arms of the Red Planet, and held the Mechanicus seat on the Senatorum Imperialis beside the Lord Commander of the Imperium Udin Macht Udo and the Lord High Admiral Lansung. Imperial accounts describe him as allied with Lansung during the first half of the war.[1]

    War of the Beast

    [edit]
    Main article: War of the Beast

    Mars withholds aid

    [edit]

    In the first months of the war the Mechanicus took little visible part. Orks overran the Segmentum Solar, an Attack Moon hung over Terra and the High Lords barricaded themselves in the Imperial Palace, and the Fabricator-General refused to send help from Mars. Inquisitorial and Assassinorum agents reported that Mechanicus personnel were present among the Orks from the war's earliest months. The reasons were not understood until later.[1][4]

    The Last Wall

    [edit]
    Main article: Koorland

    The refusal ended when the Space Marines of the Last Wall, the successors of the Imperial Fists gathered by High Marshal Bohemond of the Black Templars, came to Terra and pressed him. Kubik then sent a fleet of Basilikon Astra, five regiments of Skitarii and seven cohorts of the Legio Cybernetica to join the assault on the Attack Moon.[5] After the Lord Commander Koorland took the chair of the council, he confronted Mars. The Mechanicus had been experimenting on the Orks' teleportation technology, in the hope of moving the Red Planet out of the Sol System, and a force of the Fists Exemplar met the Skitarii at Pavonis Mons, where an errant shot began a brief firefight. Kubik, who was on Terra at the time, and an appeal from Koorland for unity ended it.[4]

    The Deathwatch and Ullanor

    [edit]

    When the council voted on the founding of the Deathwatch, Kubik abstained, as did the Inquisitorial Representative Veritus. Later, when Maximus Thane decided to act without the High Lords, Kubik was one of those who joined him, with Vangorich, in planning the third assault on Ullanor in 546.M32. The Mechanicus dropped asteroids on the Ork world and sent its own attack group under a commander named Zhokuv.[1][6]

    The Ullanor deception

    [edit]

    Thane had decreed that Ullanor be destroyed by Exterminatus once the Beast was dead. Kubik did not carry out the order. Using technology taken from the Ork Attack Moons, the Mechanicus teleported Gorkogrod, the Ork capital, aboard the immense Mechanicus ship Ark Majesty, intending to strip the city of everything useful. The Mechanicus tradition, which the High Lords have never confirmed, holds that the whole of Ullanor Prime was moved through the Warp and resettled.[1][7]

    Thane had warned Vangorich against Kubik's self-interest before he left Terra to begin the Fourth Founding. Vangorich learned the full extent of the plan soon after.[2]

    Death

    [edit]

    On the day of the Beheading in 546.M32 Vangorich's assassins killed eleven of the twelve High Lords. Cornered by the Grand Master of Assassins, Kubik shut himself down rather than allow a virus to be loosed into the Mechanicus noosphere. His successor is not named in the records open to Imperial scholars.[1][2]
