    Ordo Necros
    Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition
    The Inquisitorial I, the symbol of the Holy Ordos
    Organisation
    FoundedM37
    Strength5 Inquisitors (established)
    Area of vigilanceNot recorded
    Watched byOrdo Vigilus

    The Ordo Necros is a minor order of the Inquisition founded in the 37th millennium. The roll of the Ordos Minoris gives it an established strength of five Inquisitors and leaves its area of vigilance blank, marked only as unknown. It is one of the smallest Ordos recorded, and one of the least understood.[1]

    Only one fact about its work is on record: it is watched by another Ordo. The Ordo Vigilus, founded in the same millennium, is listed with the Ordo Necros as its area of vigilance.[1]

    Name and remit

    [edit]

    The record does not state the remit. The name comes from the High Gothic root for death. It would be natural to suppose that the Ordo has to do with the dead: with the undead, the necromancy of the Ruinous Powers, or the burial rites of the Ecclesiarchy. This is inference from the name alone, and the Ordo may be concerned with something else. The Ordo Sepulturum, which studies plague zombies and was formed in 999.M41, is a separate body, and no record connects the two.

    Imperial rumour associates the Ordo with the Necrons, the ancient xenos whose name is similar. The Necrons are the proper concern of the Ordo Xenos, and the likeness of names is likely a coincidence. The Ordo's date of foundation is M37, and the Imperium's knowledge of the Necrons has changed since.[2]

    Organisation

    [edit]

    Five Inquisitors cannot do much as a body. The Ordo is probably better described as a cabal of Inquisitors of one mind, of the kind the Inquisition's records say makes up many of the lesser Ordos. Such cabals gather around a threat in a given region, and some are specialised sub-ordos of the greater Ordos.[3] Which description fits the Ordo Necros is not stated.

    The Ordo Vigilus

    [edit]
    Main article: Ordo Vigilus

    That the Ordo has its own watcher is the strongest hint that its work is dangerous. The Inquisition has always had Inquisitors who watch other Inquisitors, and rumours persist of Ordos whose sole purpose is to purge the Inquisition itself.[3] An Ordo that watches a single other Ordo of five Inquisitors suggests that either the Ordo Necros handles something that corrupts, or that someone fears it will.

    Members

    [edit]

    No Inquisitor of the Ordo is named. A small Ordo, in a century of existence, might lose its whole roll to the dangers of its work, and be remade by new recruits. Neither the Ordo's survival to the 42nd millennium nor its end is attested.

    Status in the Era Indomitus

    [edit]

    The record does not say whether the Ordo survives. The upheaval of the Great Rift and the rise of the Imperium Nihilus have cut many Inquisitorial cells from Terra, and an Ordo that was obscure in the 37th millennium may have been forgotten by those who held its records. The roll of the Ordos Minoris is revised from time to time; the Ordo Necros has so far kept its place on it.[1]
