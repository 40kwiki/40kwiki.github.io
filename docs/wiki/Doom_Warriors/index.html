    The Doom Warriors are a loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes that follows the Codex Astartes, of unknown Founding and genetic origin. Their gene-seed carries a defect in the Catalepsean Node, an organ that lets a Space Marine sleep in part, and Doom Warriors cannot truly sleep. The lack has made them morose, moody and prone to dark moods. Imperial scribes say it is the reason the Chapter specialises in Exterminatus campaigns, where it can put its unbalanced temper to constructive use.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The Chapter's early history is not recorded. It appears in Imperial records chiefly in the long years of the 41st millennium, when its Exterminatus teams were called to some of the Imperium's worst wars.[1]

    Euxine Incident

    [edit]

    After the death of Lord Solar Macharius in 400.M41 the lands he had won fell into civil war and anarchy, and Space Marine Chapters drew their swords on each other in the Macharian Heresy. The High Lords of Terra called in several fresh Chapters to bring the conflict to an end, among them the Minotaurs. In the Euxine Incident, one of their most infamous acts of the period 400–425.M41, the Minotaurs intervened in a feud between the Doom Warriors and the Inceptors and left both Chapters nearly destroyed.[1][2]

    Wars against the Orks and the Warp

    [edit]

    In an undated action the Doom Warriors fought street by street against Orks in the Conquest of Truan City. In 951.M41 a task force noticed sorcery in the ruins of a dead city on Truan IX and diverted to investigate. It found a psi-cult trying to speak with sleeping minds of immense power, believed buried far beneath the surface, and broke the rite with a devastating assault before it could be completed.[1]

    Exterminatus of Carnage

    [edit]

    A squad led by Captain Grimmer Slayne was sent to execute Exterminatus on the world of Carnage, in an undated action of the 41st millennium. Slayne used a virus bomb named the Emperor's Tears, an evolved strain of the Life-eater virus. The weapon had been designed by a Magos Biologis of the Adeptus Mechanicus, Hieronym Rottle, who was later found to be possessed by Nurgle. When the bomb was released the entire population rose as plague zombies and became undead servants of Nurgle, so that the planet, by accident, fell under the control of Chaos.[1]

    Organisation and heraldry

    [edit]

    The Doom Warriors wear yellow armour with a black Aquila. The right shoulder carries the black specialty symbol with a white numeral, and the helmet stripe gives the company colour by the Codex: white for the 1st, yellow for the 2nd, red for the 3rd, green for the 4th. The badge is a pair of black angular wings, one raised and one inverted, with a large white skull between them and a yellow lightning bolt below, on yellow.[1]
