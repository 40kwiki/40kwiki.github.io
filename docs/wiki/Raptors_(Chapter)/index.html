    The Raptors are a loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and a Second Founding successor of the Raven Guard. They have fought in many of the Imperium's recent wars, among them the Badab War, the Third War for Armageddon and the Taros Campaign. The Chapter has been nearly destroyed several times, and has adapted so well that it has survived where others would have been struck from the rolls.[1]

    The Raptors distrust martial glory. They put pragmatism, adaptability and completion of the mission above honour, having been reduced to a handful of battle-brothers too often to hold any illusions about war. They prize marksmanship and independent thought, treat the Codex Astartes as a set of guidelines rather than doctrine, and field large formations of Scout Marines supported by waves of combat skimmers. The Ultramarine Captain Ardias is quoted as saying that "their reckless disregard for the Codex Astartes is legendary".[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    The Raptors say they descend from a company of new initiates on the Raven Guard homeworld of Deliverance at the time of the Drop Site Massacre at Isstvan V (005.M31), which spared them the worst of the mutation that afflicted the Legion's hurried rebuilding. After the Heresy this company served against the remnants of Chaos and was made a Chapter at the Second Founding of c. 021.M31. The Raven Guard's own chronicles tell a different story: a new generation of Legionaries, called the Raptors under Captain Branne, was made from undifferentiated primarch gene-stock taken from the laboratories beneath the Imperial Palace, and the hurried process produced many mutants. Imperial scholars have not settled the matter.[1][2]

    The young Chapter was at first fleet-based and hunted Traitors in the Great Scouring. Some centuries later it was given a world on the edge of the Eye of Terror to guard against Chaos. That homeworld was destroyed, probably in the 4th or 5th Black Crusade of Abaddon the Despoiler, in the 34th or 36th millennium (the date is not recorded), and with it went the Chapter's early records, trophies and many ancient arms.[1]

    Lost and found

    [edit]

    Imperial records of the 37th millennium list the Raptors among the Chapters missing and presumed dead, and for more than two thousand years nothing further is known. The Chapter may have been rebuilding, or may have been lost in the Warp. It re-emerged at full strength in the Caberiad Wars of the early 39th millennium with an ample supply line, a new homeworld and a fortress it calls only Prime. The world is said to lie deep in the stellar drifts of the Sutter Spiral Nebula on the northern border of the Segmentum Solar. No battle-brother has told even the Administratum its name or position.[1]

    Notable campaigns

    [edit]
    • Origo Blockade (701–711.M36). A force built around the 5th Company sealed the world of Origo for almost ten years while war raged over the other settled bodies of the system. The surface was found scoured clean when the blockade lifted, and several Inquisitors of the Ordo Xenos who went to learn why have vanished.
    • Sancta Angelis Campaign (339–370.M39). A task force cut off by Warp storms fought a thirty-year guerrilla war against WAAAGH! Irongol in the heart of the Ork domain, and ended it by killing the Warboss and his hundred mega-armoured bodyguard in a single six-hour fight.
    • Crucible of Endikon IX (236.M40). With the Red Scorpions, the Raptors helped free a hive world from Aeldari corsairs. Captain Kel Vamar proposed a campaign of ambush and attrition, but the Red Scorpions' Commander Oren Saker refused it as "cowardice" and made a direct drop assault in which only sixteen of eighty-three Red Scorpions returned.
    • Sulsalid Campaign (775–777.M41). The Raptors, Dark Angels, Skull Bearers and Red Wolves ended the Fourth Quadrant Rebellion.
    • Badab War (906–908.M41). Chapter Master Lias Issodon brought four companies to the Maelstrom Zone with the battle barge War Talon and two strike cruisers at the call of the Inquisitor Legate. He offered himself for command of the Loyalist Marines, and deferred without resentment when Carab Culln of the Red Scorpions was chosen. The Chapter lost between 35 and 60 per cent of its strength by the end of 907.M41, much of it against the Mantis Warriors and Astral Claws in the Gargathea System, where the strike cruiser Arias Vex was destroyed, and withdrew in 908.M41.
    • Third War for Armageddon and Taros (998.M41). Five companies fought in the jungles of Armageddon beside the Armageddon Ork Hunters and the Catachan regiments. The 3rd and 6th Companies served in the Taros Campaign against the T'au Empire.

    Era Indomitus

    [edit]
    See also: Indomitus Crusade

    The Raptors' home on the northern edge of the Segmentum Solar made them among the first to answer Roboute Guilliman's call for the Indomitus Crusade. Issodon sent strike forces to Fleets Primus, Secundus and Tertius. Lieutenant Seranis's force served as scouts for Battle Group Orphaeus and boarded the Aeldari corsair flagship Valanith. With Fleet Secundus the Raptors fought beside the Black Templars and the Adepta Sororitas on the Sanctus Reach, where their kill-teams eliminated enemy commanders; Veteran Sergeant Octor Guerris's team killed seven fallen nobles of House Mandrakor on Dharrovar. Captain Kassyan's 1st Company served in Fleet Tertius against the Khornate hosts.[1][3]

    Organisation and heraldry

    [edit]

    Company numbers are shown by numerals on the right knee plate. Raptors Librarians use psychic powers suited to the Chapter's way of war: Farsight, which gives a hunter's gaze; Raptor's Wings, which lets them leap and descend on a foe from above; and Screaming Eagles, a swarm of shadow birds that blind the enemy. The Chapter's colours are now a dull green, replacing its original blue and yellow.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Lias Issodon, Chapter Master, expert in covert war.
    • Kel Vamar, Captain at Endikon IX; Kassyan, Captain of the 1st Company; Seranis, Lieutenant and scout.
    • Octor Guerris, Veteran Sergeant of a kill-team on Dharrovar.
