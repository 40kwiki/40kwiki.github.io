    The Battle of Thessala was fought on 121.M31 during the Horus Heresy. The Ultramarines and five other successor Chapters fought the Emperor's Children on Thessala in Ultramar. It is the battle in which Roboute Guilliman was struck down by the Emperor's Children's primarch, Fulgrim.[1]

    The battle is recorded as a defeat that the Loyalists turned into a withdrawal. Guilliman was wounded and infected, and the Ultramarines were forced to leave the field. The Imperial records treat it as one of the darkest moments of the Realm's defence.[1]

    Course of the battle

    [edit]

    The Emperor's Children attacked the Ultramarine positions with a force that the Loyalists had not expected. Guilliman led the Ultramarines in person and was struck in single combat by Fulgrim. The Novamarines and the other successor Chapters fought beside the Ultramarines, and a stele on Thessala records their names.[1]

    Aftermath

    [edit]

    Guilliman was carried from the field and treated in the Ultramarine fleet. His recovery took many years. The Ultramarines continued to guard Ultramar under the command of Chapter officers during his absence.[a]
