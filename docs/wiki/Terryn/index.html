    Terryn, properly House Terryn and known as the Marshalls of Serenity, is an Imperial Knight house of the Questor Imperialis that rules the Knight world of Voltoris in the Ultima Segmentum. It is one of the most celebrated of the houses loyal to the Emperor of Mankind, and its name is commonly used in Imperial registers as shorthand for its world, which the Administratum cites as a model Knight world.[1][2]

    Voltoris is a rich and peaceful world, known in the Great Crusade as the "Tranquil World", whose life is dominated by ceremony. Its Knights, by contrast, are famed for their eagerness to campaign far from home. Detachments of the house fight across the Eastern Fringe, from the Ghoul Stars to the Damocles Gulf, and its Knights served in the Indomitus Crusade.[1][3]

    History

    [edit]

    Foundation

    [edit]

    House Terryn was founded at the end of the Dark Age of Technology, in the 25th millennium, by Maximillian Terryn, one of the first settlers of Voltoris. The founder was said to have been warned of danger in visions of a white stallion, and the white horse's head on a blue field has been the house's emblem ever since. The Knights of Terryn cleared Voltoris of the great beasts that roamed it and drove the survivors into the wilds.[1]

    Warp storms shielded the system through the Age of Strife (M25–M30), and Voltoris knew no war for about three thousand years. In the last years of the Age of Strife, c. M30, Lord Brutus Terryn led the house in a war against the Great Kroktar, a beast that had preyed on his people for a decade, and killed it at the Battle of the Six Swords.[1]

    The Great Crusade

    [edit]
    Further information: Great Crusade

    The world was reached by the 6th Expeditionary Fleet in the 30th millennium. By the house's tradition the Emperor hailed its cities as His dream made real, a story told by Voltoris's playwrights but impossible to verify. The house's warrant of ancient domain and arms was recognised in 861.M30, when Seuitonius Thucidides Terryn swore allegiance to the Emperor, and ratified in 017.M31.[1][a]

    The house's Scions were so eager to join the Crusade that they had to draw lots to decide who would go and who would stay to guard Voltoris. Those who left seldom came home, and Terryn Knights often left fleets assigned to garrison duty to seek battle elsewhere.[1]

    The Horus Heresy

    [edit]
    Further information: Horus Heresy

    When the Horus Heresy broke out in 005.M31, Voltoris was left with only about three dozen Knights for its defence, and its wealth drew raiders of both sides. The house held its world. Its detachments fought in the long campaign of Beta-Garmon (c. 007–012.M31), and its Knights stood with the loyalists until the death of Horus in 014.M31.[1][4]

    The 41st millennium

    [edit]
    Further information: 41st millennium

    In the late 41st millennium House Terryn's Knights fought in the Eastern Fringe, among other wars against the T'au Empire in its Third Sphere Expansion, which struck the hive world Agrellan in 999.M41.[1] After the opening of the Great Rift, Terryn Knights joined the Indomitus Crusade. Around 012.M42, Baron Hrothwyn of House Terryn, serving with the crusade's Fleet Quintus, led six Knights against fifteen hundred heretics on Ashloth II and carried the position known as the Jade Span.[3][b]

    Geography

    [edit]

    Voltoris is a tropical world of picturesque cities and well-ordered countryside. Its great beasts survive only in remote regions. The system lies in the Ultima Segmentum close to the Eastern Fringe, and its warp storms, which once isolated it, have long since abated.[1]

    Society

    [edit]

    Voltorian society is governed by convoluted ritual and ceremony, and its people are given to art, theatre and culture. Its peace and wealth made it, in the house's account, a model of what the Emperor intended for humanity. Imperial observers have suggested that the ferocity of Terryn's Knights and their love of long campaigns are a reaction against the tedium of life at home.[1]

    Like other Knight worlds, Voltoris is feudal. Its nobles pilot the house's Knight suits, bound to them through the Throne Mechanicum, and owe service to the High King. Under the Oath of Fealty the house pays its tithe to the Imperium in military service.[1][2]
