    A Sector Governor, styled Lord Sector, is the civil ruler of an Imperial sector. The office governs the sector in the name of the Emperor of Mankind, collects the tithes owed to Terra and answers to the Administratum. Most holders are also the planetary governor of the sector capital or its most important world. The title is sometimes given as "sector lord" or "sector governor", and the two forms are used interchangeably in Imperial records.[1]

    The office sits between the Segmentum and the Sub-sector in the chain of rule, and below it the planetary governors. Its authority is therefore broad but thin. It is broad in that a sector may hold several million cubic light years of space. It is thin in that most of that space is uncharted, and the sector governor can reach only its inhabited worlds along the warp routes.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    The sector was not a formal division during the Great Crusade (798.M30 – 000.M31). Conquered worlds were placed under the expedition fleets that had taken them, and their governors answered to the commanders of those fleets. Records of the late 30th millennium use the word "sector" for groups of worlds that were tithed together.[2]

    After the Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) the reforms of Roboute Guilliman fixed the sector as a level of government, probably in the years c. 015–021.M31. The reforms separated civil government from naval and military command. A sector governor was not to command both a fleet and an army.[3][a]

    Some holders of the title have been drawn from the Ecclesiarchy. In a few sectors a cardinal was made Lord Sector, usually because the sector had no noble house able to take the office.[2]

    Powers and duties

    [edit]

    Civil government

    [edit]

    The sector governor oversees the sub-sector governors and the planetary governors of the sector. He or she settles disputes between them, confirms their appointments when the High Lords allow, and reports the sector's condition to the Administratum. The governor also sets tithe assessments within the limits fixed by Terra.[2]

    Military affairs

    [edit]

    The sector governor does not command the sector's battlefleet. That fleet is led by a Lord High Admiral of the Imperial Navy, who answers to the Lord High Admiral of the Segmentum. The governor may, however, call on the regiments of the Astra Militarum raised on the sector's worlds, and in time of war the High Lords may appoint a Lord Commander Militant to direct every Imperial force within the sector.[4]

    Oversight

    [edit]

    The Adeptus Arbites keeps a precinct in the sector capital that is answerable to Terra rather than to the governor. The Inquisition may act in the sector without his leave. A governor who ignores these limits is liable to be replaced.[1]
