    The Black Guard are a loyalist Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and one of the few successors of the Raven Guard raised in the Second Founding. They hold that the timely use of overwhelming firepower defeats a foe, and they have little taste for half-measures. Their victories leave wide, cratered ruins. Much of their early history has been lost.[1]

    Their combat doctrine centres on the ambush. The Chapter masses Thunderfire cannons and Vindicators to destroy an enemy in a single blow.[1]

    History

    [edit]

    Origins

    [edit]

    The Black Guard began as the Shadow Wardens, the honour guard of the primarch Corvus Corax, commanded by Gherith Arendi. Arendi was believed to have been killed in the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V in c. 006.M31, but he rejoined the Legion after the liberation of Scarato. He was met with suspicion and then cleared. He kept one secret until Corax confronted him: on Isstvan V he had used loyalist survivors of the Salamanders and Iron Hands as bait to seize a shuttle and escape.[2]

    Acting on rumour and suspicion, Arendi led a Raven Guard force against the prison world of Carandiru in the campaign remembered as the Day of Vengeance. He disobeyed his orders to go to Corax's aid when the primarch was captured by the "New Men", Legionaries remade by the Emperor's Children Chief Apothecary Fabius Bile. Arendi's arrival probably saved the primarch's life, and Corax repaid it by sparing Arendi from being crushed by the last New Man.[2][a]

    Corax then gave Arendi command of a new veteran unit drawn from the Shadow Wardens, the Black Guard, which fought in the Battle of Yarant. After the Heresy, the Raven Guard were split into three Chapters, the Black Guard, the Revilers and the Raptors. Arendi became first Chapter Master of the Black Guard at the Second Founding in 021.M31. Corax himself had gone into the Eye of Terror, weighed down by guilt for what he had done to save his Legion.[2][1]

    Gene-seed degeneration

    [edit]

    A few centuries after Corax's disappearance the gene-seed of the Raven Guard and their successors began to fail, perhaps from the harvesting forced on the Legion after Isstvan V. The Black Guard do not have the Mucranoid, which lets a Space Marine survive heat, cold and vacuum, or the Betcher's gland, which spits a venom. A mutation of the Melanochromic organ drains the pigment from their skin, which becomes white, while their hair and eyes darken to black, as Corax's did.[1]

    Recorded campaigns

    [edit]

    In 990.M41 the Black Guard's 3rd Company held the world of Doom Gorge against a splinter of Hive Fleet Kraken, during the Second Tyrannic War. The Master of the Forge, Germanicus, directed a battery of Thunderfire cannons that destroyed three waves of Tyranids in a narrow gorge, and the Chapter's counterattack then ended the Tyranid force with few Black Guard losses.[3]

    Elements of the Chapter took part in the failed defence of Cadia during the Thirteenth Black Crusade in 999.M41.[4] In the 42nd millennium (no year is recorded), in the Charadon campaign, Strike Force Krenzig of the Black Guard and Strike Force Vorgha of the White Scars fought a guerrilla war in the Dereen Canyon on Dryoch's Reach against vectoriums of the Death Guard. They delayed the advance long enough for the nearby hive city to hold off Nurgle cultists, but the world fell in the end.[5]

    Appearance

    [edit]

    The Black Guard wear white armour with black helms, pauldrons, greaves and gauntlets and a red Aquila on the chest. A red squad symbol on the right shoulder shows the squad's role, with a white numeral for its number. The right knee plate shows the company colour in the manner of the Codex Astartes.[1]

    Notable members

    [edit]
    • Gherith Arendi, commander of the Shadow Wardens and first Chapter Master.
    • Germanicus, Master of the Forge, who commanded the defence of Doom Gorge in 990.M41.
