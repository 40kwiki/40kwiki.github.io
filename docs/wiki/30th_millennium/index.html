    000.M30 – 999.M30
    AgesLast centuries of the Age of Strife; Unification Wars; Great Crusade
    RulersEmperor of Mankind; Malcador the Sigillite as regent on Terra

    The 30th millennium (M30) is the thousand-year span of the Imperial Dating System from 000.M30 to 999.M30. It saw the end of the Age of Strife on Terra, the Unification Wars in which the Emperor of Mankind conquered the techno-barbarian nations of the Throneworld, the alliance with the Mechanicum of Mars sealed by the Treaty of Olympus, the creation of the primarchs and the Space Marine Legions, and the first two centuries of the Great Crusade.[1]

    Imperial records of the early millennium are thin. Most of what survives was written down after the event by the Administratum and by the Remembrancer Order, and dates before about 700.M30 are usually given only to the century. From the launch of the Great Crusade in about 798.M30 the record improves, for the Expeditionary Fleets kept their own logs and counted years from the Crusade's start. The millennium closed with the Ullanor Crusade against the greatest Ork empire of the age.[2]

    Chronology

    [edit]

    Dates follow the reconciled chronology of the Administratum. Where the Adepta disagree, the variant is given in the notes.

    DateEventNotes
    Early M30The Age of Strife reaches its depth on Terra; the planet is divided among techno-barbarian warlords such as the Ethnarch and the Panpacific Tribes.Warp storms isolate Terra from most human worlds.
    Late M29 – c. 712.M30The Unification Wars. The Emperor of Mankind wins Terra nation by nation.The start is dated only to the century.
    c. M30The Thunder Warriors are created as the Emperor's shock troops.Their gene-craft foreshadows the Adeptus Astartes.
    c. 650.M30The Emperor's companion Malcador the Sigillite begins the administration that will become the Adeptus Terra.[a]The Council of Terra grows from his staff.
    669.M30The battle of Mount Ararat, recorded in some accounts as the last great battle of Unification.Other records place the end of the wars in 712.M30.
    c. 700.M30The First Founding of the twenty Space Marine Legions from the gene-stock of the lost primarchs.Date from the Dark Angels and Salamanders rolls.
    c. 712.M30Terra is united under the Emperor; the Thunder Warriors are destroyed in the last battles.See Unification Wars.
    c. 730.M30The Pacification of Luna: the gene-covens of Luna are subdued and their sciences put to the Legions' use.[a]Before the treaty with Mars.
    739.M30The Treaty of Olympus binds Terra and the Mechanicum of Mars; the Emperor is recognised as the Omnissiah by the Martian priesthood.Also called the Treaty of Mars.
    Late M30The Astronomican is lit, giving the Navigators a beacon across the galaxy.See Astronomican.
    792.M30The twenty primarchs are created in the gene-vaults beneath the Imperial Palace and scattered across the galaxy by the powers of Chaos.The scattering is recorded as the same year.
    c. 798.M30The Great Crusade begins; the Imperial Truth becomes the doctrine of the Imperium and the Imperial Army is raised.Crusade years are counted from this date.
    c. 798.M30The Remembrancer Order is established.Its numbers grow sharply after Ullanor.
    c. 800.M30Horus is found on Cthonia, the first of the primarchs to be recovered.[a]Given command of the Luna Wolves.
    819.M30Leman Russ found on Fenris.Second primarch found.
    830.M30Fulgrim found on Chemos.
    832.M30Vulkan found on Nocturne.
    835.M30Rogal Dorn found; his fleet comes to Terra from Inwit.
    837.M30Roboute Guilliman found on Macragge.
    839–890.M30The Rangdan Xenocides, three campaigns on the galactic rim, the costliest wars of the Crusade before the Heresy.The Dark Angels bear the heaviest losses.
    840.M30Magnus the Red found on Prospero.
    843.M30Sanguinius found on Baal Secundus.
    846.M30Lion El'Jonson found on Caliban.
    849.M30Perturabo found on Olympia.
    865.M30Jaghatai Khan found on Chogoris; in the same year the Imperial Fists fight a great Ork host at Rennimar.
    899.M30The Ultramarines near the strength of the largest Legion, after the losses of the Dark Angels.
    903.M30The Word Bearers bring Khur into compliance; the city of Monarchia is raised to the Emperor's worship.Monarchia is later destroyed on the Emperor's order.
    922.M30Corvus Corax found on Deliverance.
    c. 941.M30Davin brought to compliance by the Luna Wolves.143rd year of the Crusade.
    984.M30Konrad Curze destroys his homeworld Nostramo with the guns of the Night Lords fleet.See Night Lords.
    c. 999.M30The Ullanor Crusade against the Ork empire of Urlakk Urg opens.Ends with the Triumph of 000.M31.
