    Ordo Originatus
    Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition
    The Inquisitorial I, the symbol of the Holy Ordos
    Artwork © Games Workshop
    Organisation
    RemitThe true history of the Imperium and of the Inquisition itself
    FoundedUnrecorded
    Opposed byOrdo Redactus
    Allied bodyLogos Historica Verita (since c. 012.M42)[a]

    The Ordo Originatus is an Ordo Minoris of the Inquisition dedicated to unravelling ten Terran millennia of myth, exaggeration and lie in the Imperium's records. It is a quiet body, and it has an enemy within the Inquisition itself, the Ordo Redactus, whose work is to do the opposite.[1]

    Remit

    [edit]

    The Imperium's history has been damaged by Edicts of Obliteration, by the Historical Revision Units, by the faith of the Imperial Cult and by simple neglect. The origins of the Inquisition itself are not exempt. Records have been sealed, destroyed or falsified, and the Inquisition is not sure of its own beginnings. One of the notes to the Inquisition's own article records that the Ordo Originatus exists to settle the question and the Ordo Redactus to obscure it, and that the two are said to have reached opposite conclusions.[1][2]

    Only a part of the work can be done, and the part that is done is often hard to prove. It is said to have been "raised to impossibility" by the efforts of the Ordo Redactus.[1]

    The Logos Historica Verita

    [edit]
    Main article: Logos Historica Verita

    The Ordo's work has been made lighter by the foundation of the Logos Historica Verita by Roboute Guilliman in the Era Indomitus. The Logos is a corps of historitors whom the Primarch trained to collect and compare evidence about Mankind's history since the Horus Heresy. By about 012.M42 the Logos had taken up much of the work the Ordo had been doing alone.[3] It is not recorded whether the Ordo and the Logos share information, or whether the Ordo regards the Logos as an ally or a rival.

    The Chronostrife

    [edit]
    Main article: Logos Historica Verita

    The Ordo cannot recover the order of events without a reliable calendar. The Chronostrife of the Ordo Chronos over the true date was therefore of direct concern to it. How the Ordo reacted is unrecorded. By the decree that allowed each region to keep a local calendar, the task of the Ordo became harder: the evidence must now be placed in many local reckonings, not one.[3]

    Methods and rumour

    [edit]

    The Ordo's methods are not recorded. It would be reasonable to suppose that it uses the same sources as any historian: the archives of the Administratum, the memoirs of Inquisitors, and the testimony of the old. According to Imperial rumour its Inquisitors cultivate friendships among the Remembrancers and the keepers of Chapter libraries, and the Ordo is held to be the reason why some Chapters have opened their archives. Neither claim is supported.[1]

    Members

    [edit]

    No Inquisitor of the Ordo is named in the open record.

    Relations

    [edit]

    The Ordo has a natural ally in the Ordo Scriptorum, which examines records for error. Its opponent is the Redactus. The Inquisition's article on its own origin sets out the position in neutral terms: the Inquisition itself does not know which of the two will prove right.[1]
