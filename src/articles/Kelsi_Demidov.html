Kelsi Demidov was Speaker for the [[Chartist Captains]] in the last years of the [[Horus Heresy]] and sat on the [[Council of Terra]], the civil body that governed the Imperium before the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. The archives preserve little beyond that.

Office and character

The [[Chartist Captain|Chartist Captains]] held the Emperor's charters to trade between the stars, and their Speaker carried their interests to the Council. [[Malcador the Sigillite]], [[Imperial Regent|Regent of Terra]], recorded that Demidov bore herself like someone used to being obeyed but would still listen to others.

The Council in the Heresy

The Council sat only for a short time before [[Horus]]'s approach to the [[Sol System]] ended its ordinary work. Demidov attended the meeting of the Council's members in the early days of the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31), alongside [[Ossian]], [[Sidat Yaseen Tharcher]] and the other councillors.Estimated; no record gives an exact year. Whether she survived the siege is not recorded. A later Speaker, [[Juskina Tull]], appears in the [[32nd millennium]], and the names of the Speakers between them are not preserved.
