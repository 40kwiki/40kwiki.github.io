The Ordo Sicarius is an [[Ordos Minoris|Ordo Minoris]] of the [[Inquisition]] charged with monitoring and controlling the [[Officio Assassinorum]]. It decides, in practice, when the Imperium's assassins are sent, and it watches the Temples for corruption. Its work is little known outside the highest halls of the Imperium, and even there it is a quiet presence in the politics of [[Terra]].

The Ordo was founded by the Inquisitor Jaeger after his investigation of the Wars of Vindication in the [[36th millennium]]. Like the [[Ordo Hereticus]], it is a child of the [[Age of Apostasy]], and it was created for the same reason: so that the Imperium should not suffer the same disaster twice.

History

The Wars of Vindication

Main article: [[Reign of Blood]]

The Wars of Vindication followed the death of the High Lord [[Goge Vandire]] at the end of the Reign of Blood, which the [[Administratum]] reckons to 288.M36. Vandire had bribed, blackmailed or corrupted most of the [[Adeptus Terra|Adepta]], and the [[Officio Assassinorum]] was not spared. His chief agent there was Tziz Jarek, a [[Callidus Assassin]] who used the shape-changing drug polymorphine to kill the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] and take his place.

The Grand Master had foreseen the plot. A decoy had taken his place in his chambers and he had survived, and he gathered the Assassins who were still loyal to the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. A secret war was fought between the factions inside the [[Imperial Palace]] itself. Weapons that the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] had long banned were taken from the arsenals of the Officio and used. In the end the true Grand Master killed Jarek and withdrew into a self-imposed exile, leaving a shattered hierarchy that had to be rebuilt.

Foundation of the Ordo

Jaeger examined the war and the conflicting acts of the Officio during the civil war, and found that the Temples needed constant oversight. His proposal was accepted and the Ordo Sicarius was created. Because the Officio is itself secret, the only way to monitor it was to place Inquisitors and Assassin-[[Acolyte]]s within its ranks. As a further guard, the operations of the Temples were scattered to separate places across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] away from [[Terra]], so that if one fell to [[Xenos|xenos]] influence, daemonology or heresy, another would stay clean. Rules were laid down to ensure that no single person could turn the Officio's power to his own ambition.

Role

By ancient decree no Imperial Assassin may be sent without the consent of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. That is impractical in a galaxy-wide Imperium, and [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] of the Ordo will often sanction the Officio under cover of an edict from Terra. Some hold this to be an abuse of power. The Ordo's answer is that it keeps the Imperium's response to its many threats fast enough to matter.

Acolytes of the Ordo are often placed in the circles of high-ranking figures, or lent to Inquisitors of other Ordos who need their skills. Missions are sent either on the request of the High Lords or another Inquisitor, or on the Ordo's own judgement of what matters.

The Culexus Temple

Main article: [[Officio Assassinorum]]

The Ordo also oversees the [[Culexus Temple]], whose assassins are psychic blanks, bearers of the [[Pariah Gene]]. Because such people are so rare in the human population, an Inquisitor of the [[Cato Sicarius|Sicarius]] has a better chance than any other of meeting, and sometimes studying, an Untouchable. Access is granted only every few standard years.

Relations

The Ordo's oversight of a body that can kill an [[Inquisitor]] is unusual, and Inquisitors of other Ordos have reason to want to stay in its favour. It is reasonable to suppose that it works most closely with the [[Ordo Hereticus]], which shares its origin in the [[Age of Apostasy]]. No record says so.
