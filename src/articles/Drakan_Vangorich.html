Drakan Vangorich was the twelfth [[Grand Master of Assassins]] of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] and one of the [[High Lords of Terra]] in the [[32nd millennium]]. He sat on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] through the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32), the largest [[Orks|Ork]] invasion since the [[Great Crusade]], and held most of his colleagues in contempt for their quarrels while the Throneworld was threatened. He worked with [[Koorland]] and later with [[Maximus Thane]], the two [[Imperial Fists]] who in turn took the office of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], and he used the influence of his Officio to bend other High Lords to the war effort.

In 546.M32 Thane, leaving Terra on crusade, named Vangorich Lord Protector of the Imperium, to speak and act in the Lord Commander's name. Vangorich learned that the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kubik]] had deceived Thane, and concluded that the High Lords would never put the survival of Mankind before their own ambitions. In a single day his assassins murdered the High Lords, an act the records call the [[Beheading]]. Only the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] [[Wienand]] was spared.

Vangorich ruled the Imperium through a council of his own choosing for nearly eighty standard years. He refortified [[Terra]], rebuilt its armies and completed the Fourth Founding of the [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] that Thane had ordered. In his last decades his rule decayed into paranoia, purges and massacres. Thane returned, about eighty years into the Lord Protector's rule (c. 626.M32, estimated), with a retribution force of four hundred [[Space Marine]]s, and killed the Lord Protector in the [[Officio Assassinorum|Eversor Temple]] at the north pole of Terra. The Imperium then passed through almost two decades of civil disorder before the Chapters themselves restored a lawful council.

Early life and the Grand Mastership

Almost nothing is recorded of Vangorich before the War of the Beast. He was born in the 32nd millennium, in a year the Imperial archives do not give, and was trained as an assassin of the Venenum Temple, the poisoners of the Officio. He rose to lead the temple, and in that office became something close to a father to a young assassin named [[Esad Wire]], who followed him through the events of the war.

He became the twelfth Grand Master of Assassins at a date before 544.M32 that is not recorded. The office is among the oldest in the service of the Emperor. The Grand Master directs the Officio's temples, the Callidus, Culexus, Eversor, Vanus, Venenum and Vindicare, each of which trains its killers for a different manner of work, and holds a seat on the Senatorum Imperialis as one of the High Lords of Terra.

The War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

The council in crisis

When the Orks of the Beast fell on the Imperium in 544.M32 the High Lords of Terra were already a divided body. Vangorich held most of them in contempt. As the Orks destroyed fleet after fleet and the disaster of the Proletarian Crusade left Terra almost undefended, he watched the High Lords quarrel over blame.

The [[Aeldari]] raid on the [[Imperial Palace]], in which a troupe of [[Harlequin]]s reached the heart of the fortress with a message from the farseer [[Eldrad Ulthran]], widened the divisions.

Koorland and the kill-teams

The last [[Imperial Fists|Imperial Fist]], [[Koorland]], was named Lord Commander after he and several allies, among them Vangorich, Veritus, Wienand and the Grand Provost Marshal of the [[Adeptus Arbites]], deposed [[Udin Macht Udo]], who had proved unable to command the war. Koorland shared Vangorich's low opinion of the High Lords, and the Grand Master supported his plan for small kill-teams of Space Marines drawn from many Chapters. The plan met bitter opposition from the Master of the [[Administratum]], who held that it broke the [[Codex Astartes]], and from the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Erekart Mesring|Mesring]]. It was passed only when the Attack Moon over Terra woke again and the High Lords had no other course. The kill-teams became the foundation of the [[Deathwatch]].

During the campaign for Ullanor, Vangorich sent Esad Wire, also called the Beast Krule, to the capital world on a secret mission to find and kill the Beast. When Koorland fell, Vangorich turned to Thane, then Chapter Master of the [[Fists Exemplar]]. The two, with Kubik of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], formed the inner circle that planned the last advance. To assemble the fleets and armies, Vangorich used his office to intimidate the Lord Commander Militant [[Abel Verreault|Verreault]] and the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]] into committing every resource they held. The Ork capital on Ullanor fell in 546.M32, and the war ended.

The Beheading

Main article: [[Beheading]]

The Lord Protector

After the victory Thane decreed that the Imperium would raise as many new Chapters as its stores of [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] allowed. All Chapters were to give a portion of their veterans to the new Founding, and the gene-seed tithe was doubled for a century. The Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra, but would sail as a crusading fleet to free the worlds that the war had left helpless. Knowing that he might never see the Throneworld again, Thane made Vangorich Lord Protector, to be treated by all as if Thane were present. To calm the High Lords he reminded them that the threat of his return would be enough to keep the Grand Master loyal.

The murder of the High Lords

Vangorich meant at first to work with the High Lords, but Thane had warned him of Kubik, and he soon found the full extent of the Fabricator-General's plan. Instead of carrying out an [[Exterminatus]] on Ullanor as ordered, Kubik had used machines taken from the Orks' Attack Moons to teleport the Ork capital city, Gorkogrod, aboard the vast Mechanicus ship Ark Majesty, so that its technology could be plundered. The Grand Master concluded that the Senatorum was beyond repair, and that the Imperium had grown corrupt from within.

In 546.M32 he struck, and the High Lords were killed in a single solar day. Only Wienand was spared, because the Lord Protector harboured romantic feelings for her. He hid the murders behind excuses and impersonators and installed new High Lords of his own choosing, who served as a council of puppets. Thane received word of the killings on crusade but did not move against him, for the Lord Commander judged that the Imperium might at last be ruled effectively. Wire, who tried to kill his master, was defeated and turned into an Eversor, the most frenzied of the Officio's killers.

Rule as Lord Protector

For some decades the Lord Protector governed better than his predecessors, if with great cruelty. His rule saw the refortification of Terra, the rebuilding of the Imperial armies and the fleets, and the Fourth Founding of the Adeptus Astartes, which Thane had commanded and which raised hundreds of new Chapters. The last Ork hordes of the Beast were hunted down in the same years.

About eighty Terran years into his rule the Lord Protector began to show signs of fear and instability. He saw enemies everywhere and ordered purges and massacres in the Adeptus Terra, which grew more frequent from day to day. Reports of his condition reached Thane in his crusade, and the Lord Commander judged that the Imperial Fists must return to Terra to remove him. Wienand, who still stood at Vangorich's side, reached the same judgment.Imperial sources differ on the length of the Lord Protector's rule. The Imperial record gives his rule as nearly a standard century in one place and as nearly eighty Terran years in another. This article follows the eighty-year reckoning, which gives c. 626.M32 for his fall (an estimate, as no record names the year); see [[Beheading]].

Fall and death

The retribution fleet reached Terra with four hundred Space Marines: two hundred Imperial Fists of the 1st, 4th and 5th Companies, and one company each of the [[Halo Brethren]] and the [[Sable Swords]], two Chapters of the new Founding. Their commander, Qublicus Amar, Chapter Master of the Sable Swords, was killed by a [[Vindicare Assassin]]'s bullet soon after landing. The survivors went on regardless. Assailed by hundreds of assassins, they lost half their number while securing the Imperial Palace.

Wienand then convinced Thane of her loyalty, and told him that the Lord Protector had taken refuge in the Eversor Temple at the north pole of Terra. A hundred Eversors met the Space Marines in its halls, and at the end only Thane, Vangorich and one Eversor were left alive. Vangorich ordered the Eversor to kill the Chapter Master; the assassin, who was Wire, held his hand. Vangorich asked whether they wished to hear how [[Konrad Curze]] had died, and Thane shot him with his bolt pistol. Wire was not seen again.

Aftermath

With the Lord Protector dead and the office of Lord Commander vacant, Terra slid into factional strife that lasted nearly two decades. It ended when Agnathio of the [[Ultramarines]] gathered Chapter Masters from across the galaxy, and an armada of more than fifty Chapters came to Terra, the first such gathering since the [[Horus Heresy]]. Agnathio gave the factions an ultimatum to unite or be purged as [[Heretic]]s. The Astartes sat in a closed session of the Senatorum, and when they left a new council of High Lords had been chosen. The restoration is dated 646.M32.
