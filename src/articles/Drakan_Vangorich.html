Drakan Vangorich was the twelfth [[Grand Master of Assassins]] of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] and a [[High Lords of Terra|High Lord of Terra]] in the [[32nd millennium]]. Through the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) he was one of the few High Lords to act while his colleagues quarrelled, and he helped [[Koorland]] and [[Maximus Thane]] take the office of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. Thane named him Lord Protector of the Imperium in 546.M32. Within months he had murdered almost every other High Lord in a single day, the [[Beheading]], and he ruled the Imperium as a tyrant for about a century until Thane returned and killed him c. 646.M32.

Early life

Vangorich trained as an assassin of the Venenum Temple, the poisoners, and rose to lead it. As a temple master he became a father figure to the young assassin Esad Wire. He was the twelfth to hold the Grand Mastership, which carries a seat among the High Lords.

The War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

When the [[Orks]] of the Beast struck in 544.M32, Vangorich held most of his fellow High Lords in contempt; he was on good terms only with the Inquisitorial Representative Wienand and the Lord Commander Militant Heth. To force the Lord High Admiral Lansung to send the fleets against the Orks, he disguised himself as a priest, poisoned the Ecclesiarch Mesring, and offered the antidote only if Mesring declared a war of faith against the Beast. He sent Wire against the Inquisition as a warning, then to rescue Wienand when she was in danger, and sent assassins of the Vanus Temple to learn what the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] was hiding about the Orks.

After the Eldar raid on the Palace he made an uneasy alliance with the Inquisitor Lord Veritus. He told the last [[Imperial Fists|Imperial Fist]], [[Koorland]], what he thought of the High Lords, and helped Koorland depose the Lord Commander Udin Macht Udo. He was angered when Koorland, and later [[Vulkan]], did not purge the council. With Koorland he devised the kill-teams that became the [[Deathwatch]]. After Koorland's death at [[Ullanor]] he allied with Maximus Thane, and secured the support of Lansung and the Lord Commander Militant Verreault for the last assault on Ullanor by placing Callidus assassins in their own bodyguards.

The Beheading

Main article: [[Beheading]]

In 546.M32 Thane, now Lord Commander, sailed on crusade and named Vangorich Lord Protector, to speak and act in his name. Vangorich learned soon after that the [[Fabricator-General]] Kubik had not destroyed Ullanor as Thane ordered, but had stolen the Ork capital to study its technology. He concluded that the High Lords would never put the survival of humanity above their own ambitions. In a single day his assassins killed every High Lord except Wienand, whom he spared out of a personal regard the Inquisition's records do not describe. He hid the killings with excuses and impersonators and named a council of his own creatures. Wire tried to kill him, was defeated, and was turned into an Eversor.

Rule and death

For some decades Vangorich ruled well, if cruelly. He refortified Terra, rebuilt the armies, completed the Fourth Founding that Thane had ordered, and destroyed the last Ork hordes of the Beast. About eighty years into his rule he grew unstable, and massacres and random purges followed. Wienand, still at his side, judged that his time was over; Thane, on crusade, reached the same judgment. He returned to Terra with four hundred [[Space Marine]]s of the Imperial Fists, the Halo Brethren and the Sable Swords. Wienand told him that the Lord Protector had taken refuge in the Eversor Temple at Terra's north pole.

A hundred Eversors met the Space Marines in the temple, and at the end only Thane, Vangorich and one Eversor were left alive. Vangorich ordered the Eversor to kill Thane; the assassin, who was Esad Wire, held his hand. Vangorich asked whether they wished to hear how [[Konrad Curze]] had died, and Thane shot him. Wire was not seen again.Imperial sources differ on the length of the Lord Protector's rule. Assassinorum records speak of a century; other accounts date his fall c. 646.M32, which is used here.

Legacy

The office of Lord Commander was left vacant after the Beheading and civil disorder followed on Terra. Imperial assessments of Vangorich remain divided between those who see a murderer and a tyrant and those who credit him with saving an Imperium his colleagues had nearly lost. The Officio Assassinorum holds that he was right about the council and wrong about himself.
