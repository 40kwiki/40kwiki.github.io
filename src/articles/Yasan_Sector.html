The Yasan Sector is a [[sector]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]], near the [[Warp rift]] known as the [[Maelstrom]]. It holds [[Chogoris]], the [[Space Marine Chapter Homeworld|Chapter homeworld]] of the [[White Scars]].

Horus Heresy

During the [[Horus Heresy]] in the early 31st millennium, the sector was ravaged by the Traitor forces of [[Horus]]. Later, [[Drukhari]] slave raiders attacked it.

The Great Scouring

In the campaigns of the [[Great Scouring]], the White Scars returned to their home sector. They cleared out the remaining Traitors and drove the Drukhari back into the [[Webway]]. In one battle, on the world of [[Corusil V]] in 084.M31, the White Scars' [[primarch]], [[Jaghatai Khan]], disappeared while pursuing Drukhari raiders into a Webway portal.

Era Indomitus

After the [[Great Rift]] opened, the sector came under constant attack from the [[Heretic Astartes]] of the [[Red Corsairs]]. During the [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, the Red Corsairs invaded out of the Maelstrom and almost completely took the [[Gartuli System]] and the [[Thaxis System]]. The White Scars threw back the assault on Chogoris, but at a steep cost, and Chaos outposts in the sector keep the threat alive.
