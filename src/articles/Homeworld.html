A Space Marine Chapter Homeworld, also called a Chapter planet, is an Imperial classification for a world that is the base of a [[Space Marine Chapter]]'s [[fortress-monastery]]. Some Chapters recruit only from their homeworld. Others draw aspirants from several worlds.

Status

A Chapter homeworld is held by the Chapter as a feudal possession of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Only [[Terra]] is more important to a Chapter, and only the [[Emperor of Mankind]] is owed greater loyalty by its people. The Chapter Master is often also the [[planetary governor]] of the world.

Recruitment

Many homeworlds are [[Death world]]s, [[Feral world]]s or [[Feudal world]]s. Their harsh conditions produce hardy populations, and the Chapter seeks aspirants who can survive there. Chapters such as the [[Imperial Fists]], [[Black Templars]] and [[Blood Ravens]] recruit from more than one world. Some demand aspirants from a world they have saved from invasion.

Forms

Not every homeworld is a planet. Some are orbital stations, deserted moons or asteroids, or voidcraft. Chapters that are [[fleet-based]] keep their homeworld aboard their flagship.
