A space hulk is the term the [[Imperium of Mankind]] gives to the wreckage of a starship, or to a mangled twist of many voidships and artificial debris, found drifting through the void without apparent direction. Many are so huge that they have their own atmosphere and gravity. Hulks often leave and re-enter the [[Warp]] seemingly at random, so searching or travelling within them is dangerous in the extreme.

The [[Imperial Navy]] and all Imperial forces and authorities have standing orders to survey such objects and report their location. A hulk often consists of voidships and technology many Terran millennia old, and its recoverable technology can be of immense value to the Imperium, above all to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Elite Imperial forces are therefore sent aboard to clear out [[xenos]] or [[Chaos]] infestations and to judge whether anything within is valuable enough to recover.

Formation and behaviour

Age and nature

Many hulks have been adrift in the void for hundreds or even thousands of standard years, forming an unrecognisable mass in which technological and scientific treasures of the [[Age of Technology]] may lie. Most hulks fall apart given time or sufficient massed firepower, or vanish back into the Warp and are never seen again. A few last far longer: the hulk [[Mortis Thule]], the largest known in the [[Jericho Reach]], was already there before the expeditionary fleets of the [[Great Crusade]] arrived, and its longevity and growing mass are thought to be tied to the Reach's other mysterious artefact, the Warp Gate.

Realspace and the Warp

See also: [[Realspace]]

A hulk's jump into or out of the Warp is made in an essentially random direction. This gives those who use hulks the advantage of surprise over [[Imperial]] and [[Asuryani]] forces, and the [[Orks]], the [[Genestealers]] and even renegade [[Heretic Astartes]] have used them to invade worlds. A hulk may carry [[Chaos Space Marines]], Orks or Genestealers in [[stasis]], who wake to attack the unsuspecting planets of the Imperium.

Genestealer infestations

See also: [[Genestealer Cults]]

Many hulks are known to be breeding grounds for the Genestealers of the [[Tyranids]]. Often a hulk is a voidship that suffered a Genestealer infestation, whose Genestealers took the ship and killed its crew. The magi of the Adeptus Mechanicus suspect that the [[Hive Mind]] seeds Genestealers in stasis on hulks to serve as unwitting vanguards for a nearby [[Hive Fleet]] that is looking for new worlds to consume. Such hulks are doubly dangerous, for the cargo they may hold and for the hive fleets they may call down on an inhabited planet.

The hulk [[Spawn of Damnation]] shows how a hulk can seed a world. In 781.M41 it appeared briefly in the Viridia System, long enough for the Genestealers aboard to spread their taint into the human population, and in 928.M41 the resulting [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]] rose against the planetary governor.

Boarding actions

Because of these dangers the task of boarding and exploring a hulk is normally given to [[Terminator armour|Terminators]], the most experienced veterans of an [[Adeptus Astartes]] Chapter, heavily armed and protected by Tactical Dreadnought Armour. Space Marines are usually sent to purge a hulk of alien or Chaos infestation, depending on the strategic situation: the troops available, the hulk's distance from vital Imperial systems and the safety margins.

In some cases boarding is impossible, because of powerful enemy resistance, too few Imperial troops or a hulk that lies too close to a vital system. The only alternative is then to bombard and destroy it. Hulks are huge and durable, however, and even [[Nova Cannon]] fire is not always enough to destroy one.

The Sin of Damnation

The most famous hulk in Imperial records is the [[Sin of Damnation]]. In 996.M40, in the Secoris System, the [[Blood Angels]] assaulted an unnamed hulk. The campaign was a disaster: the Chapter faced an unending tide of Genestealers and withdrew with only fifty battle-brothers surviving of the whole Chapter. In 589.M41 the Blood Angels made a second assault on a hulk, this time confirmed as the Sin of Damnation. Under the command of Captain Michaelus Raphael of the 1st Company, eighty Terminators slew more than forty thousand Genestealers and this time captured the hulk for the Imperium.

The Death of Integrity

In 887.M39 the hulk [[Death of Integrity]] was sighted near the world of Vol Secundus. The [[Blood Drinkers]] and [[Novamarines]] answered the call for aid with their first companies, and the two Chapters together deployed nearly two hundred Terminators aboard the wreck. Over two solar months they cleared a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer]] infestation from its ruins. Deep in the hulk they found a well-preserved [[Standard Template Construct]] database, which held data on many lost technologies. The Adeptus Mechanicus accepted it gladly and repaid both Chapters with a newly commissioned strike cruiser.

Orks and hulks

The Orks use hulks to invade worlds. The hulk Alveus Alpha Alpha Sextus first bore the Orks to [[Armageddon]] in the [[Second War for Armageddon]]. The Ork-crewed [[Da Iron Worm]] ravaged the Uhulis Sector in the middle of the [[32nd millennium]], destroying Battlegroup Azazel, sacking the forge world of Temaxia and ravaging the fortress worlds of Armstrong and Velgagrad, until a Warp storm carried it into the Dark Marches of the Segmentum Tempestus. It is believed to have been destroyed there when its Orks disturbed the Necrons of the Manarkh Dynasty.

The Ork hulk Da Kollosus served as the base of the pirate Kaptin Krashkrooz in the Gothic Sector at the time of the [[Gothic War]]. Battlefleet Gothic, at the behest of Lord Admiral Ravensburg, severely damaged it, and Krashkrooz in defeat detonated a cyclonic torpedo that destroyed his own hulk.

Recovery and the Adeptus Mechanicus

See also: [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]

The Adeptus Mechanicus values hulks for the ancient technology they may hold, and a cleansed hulk is rendered to its keeping for study. The hulk [[Fury (space hulk)|Fury]] began as an ambitious project of the Mechanicum of Mars, which planned to turn a small planet taken from the [[Eye of Terror]] into a spacefaring vessel. The planet was lost in the Warp and became a hulk, and its Mechanicus crew survived inside it for millennia. In the 41st millennium the Fury emerged near Iax on the borders of [[Ultramar]], where the [[Ultramarines]] and their allies in the Mechanicus and the [[Inquisition]] boarded it and purged its Chaos taint. It was returned to its original owners in the Mechanicus, and both the Mechanicus and the [[Ordo Hereticus]] have blocked inquiries into its fate.

Famous hulks

  • [[Sin of Damnation]]: the hulk the [[Blood Angels]] captured in 589.M41, after their failed assault on an unnamed hulk in 996.M40.
  • [[Death of Integrity]]: the hulk that held an [[Standard Template Construct|STC database]], cleared in 887.M39.
  • [[Spawn of Damnation]]: a conglomeration of wrecked voidships some five kilometres across, first sighted in 447.M36 and captured by the [[Reclaimers]] after the Viridia uprising of 928.M41.
  • [[Mortis Thule]]: the largest hulk known in the [[Jericho Reach]], older than many hulks of its kind.
  • [[Forsaken Doom]]: a Genestealer-infested hulk cleared by [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] of the Blood Angels near the [[Forge world|forge world]] of Gorgonum.
  • [[Judgement of Carrion]]: a hulk boarded by the [[Blood Ravens]] in the Aurelian Sub-sector during the Second Aurelian Crusade.
