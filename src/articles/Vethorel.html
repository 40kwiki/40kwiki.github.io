Vethorel was the envoy of the loyalist [[Fabricator-General]] [[Zagreus Kane]] on the [[Council of Terra]]. Kane, master of the Mondus Occulum forge, usually sent Vethorel to represent the loyalist Mechanicum in the council during the [[Horus Heresy]].

In 012.M31 the loyalists were formally recognised as the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], separate from the corrupted Mechanicum. Kane became its first Fabricator-General and first High Lord. Vethorel's service as envoy belongs to the years before and during that change.
