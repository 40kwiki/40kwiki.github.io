Varro Tigurius is the [[Chief Librarian]] of the [[Ultramarines]] [[Space Marine]] Chapter. As a [[Psyker|psyker]] he has always stood apart from his battle-brothers, and even the Chapter's company captains regard him as a figure of mystery. Imperial records describe a knowledge in him that goes beyond the datastacks of the Chapter's archive.

When the Aquiliarian Council called for help, reinforcements came from several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], led by [[Marneus Calgar]] and some of the Ultramarines' finest officers. Among them was Tigurius, who served as Chief Librarian in the campaign.

Service

Tigurius was among the Ultramarine officers who answered the call for help at [[Vigilus]], where [[Marneus Calgar]] was cut down at [[Saint's Haven]] by [[Drach'nyen]]. His part in the campaign is recorded in Ultramarine chronicles.The extent of Tigurius's role is recorded in Ultramarine chronicles and is not fully confirmed by other sources.
