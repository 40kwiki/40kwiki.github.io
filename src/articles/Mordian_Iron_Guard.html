The Mordian Iron Guard are the elite [[Astra Militarum]] regiments that hail from [[Mordian]], a [[Hive World]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Their regiments are superbly drilled and well equipped. They are mainly deployed in the [[Segmentum Solar]] and the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], where they are especially skilled at suppressing revolts against Imperial rule and at urban warfare.

Their colourful, parade-like uniforms have misled many enemies into thinking they face amateurs. Enemies who have made this mistake have discovered that every Mordian soldier is a determined and experienced professional. Their recruits are told that nothing less than full commitment to the Emperor will be tolerated.

Tactics

In battle the Iron Guard present perfectly formed ranks to the enemy. They unleash precisely timed volleys of las-fire from behind a hedge of bayonets. They hold their ranks even against overwhelming odds. The regiments coordinate with attached artillery, which provides pinpoint shelling, and with armoured tank squadrons, which make rapid advances while the infantry platoons advance in a choreographed manner.

Origins

The Iron Guard are raised from the populations of the dark side of Mordian. Newly raised regiments commonly have several standard years of experience in putting down insurrections, riots and uprisings before they leave the world. The oppressive atmosphere, overcrowding and scarcity of resources make Mordian a hotbed of antagonism. The Iron Guard is the force that keeps order on the planet.
