The Mordian Iron Guard are the elite [[Astra Militarum]] regiments that hail from [[Mordian]], a [[Hive World]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Their regiments are superbly drilled and well equipped. They are mainly deployed in the [[Segmentum Solar]] and the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], where they are especially skilled at suppressing revolts against Imperial rule and at urban warfare.

Their colourful, parade-like uniforms have misled many enemies into thinking they face amateurs. Enemies who have made this mistake have discovered that every Mordian soldier is a determined and experienced professional. They are unusual in their disdain for camouflage, and take the field in flak-reinforced dress uniforms, brightly coloured and pristine.

Tactics

In battle the Iron Guard present perfectly formed ranks to the enemy. They unleash precisely timed volleys of las-fire from behind a hedge of bayonets. They hold their ranks even against overwhelming odds. The regiments coordinate with attached artillery, which provides pinpoint shelling, and with armoured tank squadrons, which make rapid advances while the infantry platoons advance in a choreographed manner.

Origins

The Iron Guard are raised from the populations of the dark side of Mordian. Newly raised regiments commonly have several standard years of experience in putting down insurrections, riots and uprisings before they leave the world. The oppressive atmosphere, overcrowding and scarcity of resources make Mordian a hotbed of antagonism, with queue wars and hospice riots a common event. The Iron Guard are both the military force and the enforcers of order on their world, outnumbered by the populace by hundreds of thousands to one, and they serve the oligarchic [[Tetrarchy]] with the same unwavering discipline.

Mordian has few resources, so part of its tithe to the Imperium is paid in regiments of the Iron Guard. These serve in several roles but most often as line infantry. At the founding of a regiment a lavish banner, made in the Tetrarchal Palace, is presented in a ceremony on the parade ground, where every trooper swears never to let the colours fall in battle or be captured. Their vehicles and troops carry an Iron Eagle emblem beside the usual unit markings.

Doctrine and beliefs

Mordians seldom endure the open sky, and they have difficulty adjusting outside an enclosed place such as a hive city or a starship. They are at ease among crowds, and weave through the densest mobs. They present perfectly formed firing lines under any conditions and do not leave the line. Their fortitude is near superhuman beside other Astra Militarum regiments, though the death of an officer can weaken their ability to take the initiative. Their tank crews are drilled as strictly as the infantry.

History

Battle of Mordian

The regiments' greatest victory was the defence of their own world against an invasion of Chaos from the [[Eye of Terror]]. The Iron Guard held the capital, [[Vanandra]], street by street, until they stood around the Tetrarchal Palace. They bought the time that Imperial psykers needed to break the rite that held the invaders in the material world, and the daemons dissolved. After this the regiments were counted among the best of the Astra Militarum and were sent to many wars.

The Spinward Front

Mordian lies in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], so its regiments are often sent to the [[Calixis Sector]] and its surrounds. A sizeable contingent serves on the [[Spinward Front]], where the regiments' experience as enforcers has been valued in rear areas and in multi-regiment deployments. The Mordians are often set as provosts for the [[Commissar]]s. This has caused resentment among more independent regiments such as those of [[Catachan]], [[Elysia]] and [[Tallarn]]. The Iron Guard has served well on [[Nox]], at the mustering yards of [[Kalf]] and on the shifting defence lines of [[Kulth]]. Their resolve has been costly, for regiments cut off without an order to withdraw fire and reload mechanically, then fight with bayonets until they die where they stand.

Stygius Crusade

After the Great Rift opened in 999.M41, [[Magnus the Red]] and the [[Thousand Sons]] invaded the [[Stygius Sector]] in the [[Stygius Crusade]]. Mordian was the only world attacked that held out. The siege was relieved after a long campaign.
