Gryphonne IV was a [[Forge World]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the home of the [[Legio Gryphonicus]], a [[Titan Legion]]. It was overrun by [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] in 997.M41, and it is now recorded as destroyed.

The world's loss is one of the most serious for the Mechanicus in the [[41st millennium]]. Its forges supplied the Imperium with weapons and war machines, and its loss shifted that work to a small number of surviving forges.

Loss to the Tyranids

See also: [[Tyranids]]

The [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] invasions destroyed several [[Forge world|forge worlds]] in the late [[41st millennium]], and Gryphonne IV was among them. Its research outpost on [[Tyran Primus|Tyran]] was the first to meet the Tyranids, and the [[Tyrannic Wars]] brought the swarm to the forge world itself.

The loss of Gryphonne IV left Stygies VIII as the only known producer of the Vanquisher cannon. The [[Stygies VIII]] forges were already under strain after the loss of Tigrus to the Ork warlord Arrgard the Defiler in the [[35th millennium]].
