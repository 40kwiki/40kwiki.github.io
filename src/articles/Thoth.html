Thoth is a world of the [[Macharian Crusade]]. In 395.M41 Arrian's 6th Army Group took the last enemy stronghold on Thoth. The [[Star Phantoms]], who had turned on Guard formations they judged to have betrayed the Imperium, had been among the enemies on the world.

The Star Phantoms fought a war with the [[Marines Malevolent]] that nearly destroyed that Chapter, and they were investigated by the [[Ordo Hereticus]] afterwards.
