A servitor is a cyborg made from a human body whose mind has been largely erased and replaced by machine programming. Servitors are the Imperium's mechanical labour force: they work in forges and mines, crew starships, guard doors, carry loads, keep records and fight in battle. They are found wherever the Imperium needs a worker who will do one task without rest and without thought. Countless billions of them are in service across the galaxy.

The servitor exists because the [[Imperium of Mankind]] forbids thinking machines. After the revolt of the [[Men of Iron]] in the [[Dark Age of Technology]], humanity swore never again to make an artificial mind, and the servitor was developed as the alternative. It is built around a human brain, however reduced, and so is not regarded as an [[Abominable Intelligence]]. Most servitors are made from criminals condemned to servitude, from bodies grown in vats, or, in some orders, from the faithful dead who wish to continue their service.

History

Records preserved on [[Mars]] state that servitors were first built at the end of the Dark Age of Technology, after the Cybernetic Revolt had made true robots unthinkable. The method survived the [[Age of Strife]] (c. M25 – M30) on Mars and other forge worlds, and by the time of the [[Treaty of Olympus]] in 739.M30 the Mechanicum was producing servitors in vast numbers.The date of the first servitors is estimated from the sequence of events. Mechanicus doctrine says only that they were made "when the iron ones had fallen".

The [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31) spread the servitor across the Imperium. Every expedition fleet carried servitors to crew its ships and to build on newly conquered worlds, and the Mechanicum's armies used combat servitors alongside the robots of the [[Legio Cybernetica]]. Since then, the manufacture of servitors has become one of the main industries of the forge worlds.

Manufacture

A servitor is made by removing or destroying most of the higher functions of the brain, a process known as mind-wiping, and replacing them with a cortex implant that holds a fixed set of instructions. The limbs and organs needed for its function are replaced or supplemented with bionics: a drill or crane in place of an arm, a weapon mount, an auspex in place of an eye. Many servitors have their lower bodies removed entirely and are fixed to tracks or to the machine they tend.

The quality of the work varies greatly. A servitor made by a skilled magos on a great forge world may serve for centuries; one made cheaply on a poor world may last a few years. The process does not always erase the subject completely, and accounts of servitors that recall fragments of their former lives, weep or speak are common, although the Mechanicus discourages their repetition.

Sources of bodies

On most worlds, condemnation to servitude is a sentence of law, imposed for serious crimes and considered more merciful than death. Others are vat-grown on the forge worlds from cloned flesh and never had a mind to lose. Some orders of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] accept the bodies of devout servants who ask, before death, to be made into servitors so that their service may continue.

Types

Labour servitors

The commonest servitors do manual work: lifting cargo, loading guns aboard ships, running machinery in manufactoria, tending crops on [[Agri world|agri worlds]] and maintaining the systems of hive cities. Many are built into the machines they tend and never move from them.

Combat servitors

Combat or gun servitors carry heavy weapons and are used to guard buildings, starship decks and Mechanicus installations, and to support tech-priests in battle. They respond slowly to anything outside their programming and are most useful in defence. Tech-priests of the [[Astra Militarum]], the Enginseers, take combat servitors into battle with them.

Kataphron servitors

The heaviest Mechanicus battle servitors, the Kataphron, are mounted on tracked lower bodies and carry weapons that no man could lift. Kataphron Breachers are armoured for close assault and the storming of fortifications. Kataphron Destroyers carry plasma culverins, heavy grav-cannon or phosphor blasters. They fight alongside the [[Skitarii]] in the [[Cult Mechanicus|Cult Mechanicus's]] armies.

Specialist servitors

Other servitors serve as scribes and record-keepers (lexmechanic or scribe servitors), as medical assistants (chirurgeon servitors), as ship's crew controlling turrets or sensors, or as bearers and attendants. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] uses servitors as choristers and incense-bearers. The Mechanicus's Cenobyte servitors carry relics and chant in its processions.

Arco-flagellants

Main article: [[Arco-flagellant]]

The [[Arco-flagellant]] is a specialised servitor made by the Ecclesiarchy from condemned heretics, pacified by a helmet and driven into a killing frenzy by drugs in battle. It is treated as a sentence of redemption rather than labour.

Status and doctrine

In law, a servitor is property and not a person. To the Mechanicus it is a machine with a spirit like any other, and it receives the rites due to a machine. Imperial citizens rarely think of servitors at all. Some have questioned the practice: the Inquisitor [[Gregor Eisenhorn]] recorded his unease at servitors that seemed to retain fragments of personality, and preachers have from time to time condemned the use of the faithful dead. The practice has never been seriously challenged.

Because the servitor is the Imperium's answer to the forbidden thinking machine, the Mechanicus regards any attempt to give servitors greater intelligence with suspicion. Tech-priests accused of making servitors that think for themselves have been condemned as [[Heretek|hereteks]].
