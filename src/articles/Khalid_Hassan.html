Khalid Hassan was a [[Grand Master of Assassins]], the head of the [[Officio Assassinorum]], the Imperial order of assassins. He is one of the recorded holders of the office, which was founded in the [[Great Crusade]] and whose first holder was [[Malcador the Sigillite]].

The Grand Master of Assassins is appointed for life and answers only to the Emperor's direct authority, as the Officio's doctrine holds. Records of Hassan's tenure are sparse.

Office

The Grand Master directs the Officio's assassins and sets the rules for their missions. The office was held for life, and its holders were chosen from among the Officio's most experienced operatives. [[Drakan Vangorich]] is recorded as another holder, and his name is recorded beside Hassan's in the lists of the office.

Succession

Successors to the Grand Master are chosen from the Officio's own ranks, and the Officio's records describe the choice as a matter for the Grand Master alone. The [[Officio Assassinorum|Assassinorum]]'s sealed rites are not open to outside scholars, and the lists of holders are the only fully public record of the office.
