The Tyrant Star, also known as Komus and the Hereticus Tenebrae, is an uncategorised stellar anomaly in the [[Calixis Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It appears in the sky as a black dwarf star. Its arrival over a world is taken by many in the sector as a herald of imminent catastrophe.

Imperial astronomers cannot agree on whether the body is a star in the usual sense. The [[Adeptus Astronomica]] records its position but not its mass, and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] treats its appearance as a sign of heresy in the region. The [[Inquisition]] keeps a file on the star and on the worlds where its light has been reported.

Hive worlds

Main article: [[Hive world]]

The star is most often seen from [[Hive world]]s of the sector, where its light is visible through the smog of the hive cities. Population records for those worlds note a rise in unrest in years when the star was seen, although the link is not proven.
