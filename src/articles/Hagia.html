Hagia is an Imperial [[agri-world]] in the [[Sabbat Worlds Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]]. It was the birthplace of [[Saint Sabbat]], and it is now the burial place of her remains. For this reason it is one of the most important [[Shrine world]]s of the Ecclesiarchy.

During the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] the forces of [[Chaos]] attacked Hagia and mocked its people by calling themselves "Infardi", the local word for "pilgrim". The regiment of [[Ibram Gaunt]], the [[Tanith First and Only]], fought there against them.

Saint Sabbat

Main article: [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]

[[Sabbat Worlds|Sabbat]] was a shepherd girl of Hagia who led the crusade that freed the Sabbat Worlds from 500 to 605.M35. Her remains were brought back to the world after her death, and the planet became a site of pilgrimage. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] holds her to be a patron of the Sabbat Worlds.The dates of Sabbat's crusade are recorded in the Ecclesiarchy's registers.
