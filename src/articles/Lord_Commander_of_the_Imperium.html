The Lord Commander of the Imperium, known for millennia simply as the Lord Commander and in the [[32nd millennium]] as Lord Guilliman, is the highest military office of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and the chair of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Its holder is the supreme commander of every Imperial armed service and ranks second only to the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. It has almost always been held together with the title of [[Imperial Regent]], which grants the same supremacy in civil affairs.

The office was created after the [[Horus Heresy]] for the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]], who used it between 014 and 121.M31 to rebuild the shattered Imperium, divide the [[Space Marine Legions]] into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and reorganise its armies, fleets and bureaucracy. After Guilliman was struck down at [[Thessala]] in 121.M31 the title passed to mortal chairs of the council, who were addressed as "Lord Guilliman" in his honour. It was last held in that age by the [[Imperial Fists]] [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]] [[Koorland]] and [[Maximus Thane]] during and after the [[War of the Beast]], and it was abolished in the disorder that followed the [[Beheading]].

For more than ten thousand years the office stood empty. In 999.M41 Guilliman, restored to life on [[Macragge]], reached [[Terra]] at the end of the [[Terran Crusade]], spoke with the Emperor in the Throne Room and took up the titles of Lord Commander and Imperial Regent once more. He has held them since, at the head of the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. He is the only person to have held the office twice.

Powers

The Lord Commander is the commander-in-chief of the [[Astra Militarum]], the [[Imperial Navy]] and every other armed service of the Imperium, and the only officer since the Heresy entitled to command them all at once. The [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]], which the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] cannot command, treat the Lord Commander as their sole legitimate superior below the Emperor. The office also confers the chair of the [[Senatorum Imperialis]], its agenda and, in the case of a divided vote, the final word.

As [[Imperial Regent]] the holder speaks with the Emperor's voice in civil matters. The title of Regent is older than the office of Lord Commander. [[Malcador the Sigillite]] held it, as Regent of Terra, from the Emperor's departure on the [[Great Crusade]] until his death on the [[Golden Throne]] in 014.M31, and [[Sanguinius]] ruled the short-lived [[Imperium Secundus]] during the Heresy as Imperator Regis. The two titles are distinct in law: the Lord Commander's authority is military and the Regent's is civil. Since [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] held both from the first, they have been treated in practice as one office. In the [[Era Indomitus]] Guilliman also styles himself [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]] of the council.

The holder is guarded by the [[Adeptus Custodes]], a protection given to few others.

History

Origins (005–014.M31)

Before the Heresy the armies of the Imperium were directed by the Emperor's War Council and, from the Triumph of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] in 000.M31, by the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]]. When Horus rebelled in 005.M31 no loyal commander held a comparable rank. The defence of [[Terra]] was given to [[Rogal Dorn]] as Praetorian, and Palace tradition holds that the title of Lord Commander was first used for him during the war. The civil government of Terra remained with the Regent [[Malcador the Sigillite|Malcador]].

Guilliman's first tenure (014–121.M31)

When the [[Siege of Terra]] ended in 014.M31, the Emperor was entombed and Malcador dead, and a Grand Council of Reconstruction took charge of the Imperium. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]], who had reached Terra after the Siege, took its chair as Lord Commander and [[Imperial Regent]]. The title of Warmaster, for evident reasons, was set aside. Under his command the [[Great Scouring]] (014–021.M31) drove the [[Traitor|traitors]] into the [[Eye of Terror]].

Guilliman used the office to remake the Imperium. He wrote the [[Codex Astartes]] and imposed the [[Second Founding]], separated the [[Imperial Navy]] from the army, split the provinces so that no one commander held ships, soldiers and territory together, and gathered the civil services into the [[Administratum]]. The council of mortal officials that later became the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] took shape under his chairmanship. His tenure ended in 121.M31, when his brother [[Fulgrim]], by then a [[Daemon|daemon prince]], wounded him with a poisoned blade at the [[Battle of Thessala]]. Guilliman was placed in stasis in the [[Macragge|Temple of Correction]] on Macragge.

The Lords Guilliman (121.M31 – c. 646.M32)

See also: [[High Lords of Terra]]

The office did not die with Guilliman's command. For centuries after 121.M31 the chair of the council kept the title and its military supremacy, and its holders were addressed as "Lord Guilliman" in memory of the first. The names of most are lost.

At the outbreak of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 the Lord Commander was [[Udin Macht Udo]], a former [[Lord High Admiral]] who was in practice the instrument of the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]]. Udo's inaction as the [[Orks]] overran the [[Segmentum Solar]], and his attempt to expel the [[Inquisition]] from the council, led [[Koorland]], the last surviving Imperial Fist, to depose him in c. 545.M32 with the support of the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] [[Wienand]] and the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Drakan Vangorich]]. Koorland is one of only two [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] to have chaired the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]. He was killed during the second assault on [[Ullanor]] in 546.M32 and was succeeded by Maximus Thane of the [[Fists Exemplar]].

Thane ordered the [[Fourth Founding]] and left Terra on crusade with the rebuilt [[Imperial Fists]], naming Vangorich Lord Protector to act in his name. Vangorich had the High Lords murdered in the Beheading of 546.M32 and ruled through appointees for some eighty years, until Thane returned and killed him in the [[Eversor Temple]] c. 646.M32. The Imperium then went without a government for almost two decades, until the [[Ultramarines]] [[Chapter Master]] [[Agnathio]] brought more than fifty [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] to Terra and compelled the factions to seat a new council. That council abolished the office of Lord Commander.

Vacancy (c. 646.M32 – 999.M41)

For nine and a half millennia no one held the office. The Senatorum ruled as a body of equals, with the [[Master of the Administratum]] and, in some ages, the [[Ecclesiarch]] as its most powerful members. No High Lord had the power to command all the Imperium's forces, and none of the councils of the period sought to revive an office that had served as the lever of the Beheading. Records of the [[Age of Apostasy]] in the [[36th millennium]] no longer list it among the seats.

Guilliman's second tenure (999.M41 – present)

Guilliman was restored on Macragge in 999.M41, during the [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the [[Great Rift]]. After fighting his way to Terra and speaking with the Emperor, he declared to the sitting High Lords that he was again Lord Commander and Imperial Regent. He dissolved the council, removed five High Lords and hundreds of lesser lords, ruled in person for a time, and then restored the High Twelve with himself in the chair. He launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]] (from c. 000.M42), on a scale not seen since the Great Crusade.

His authority has not gone unchallenged. Officials of the old order, who called it the Imperium Eterna, formed the [[Hexarchy]] and attempted to seize Terra in his absence in early M42; the coup was broken by the Grand Master of Assassins, [[Fadix]]. Guilliman also named the [[Blood Angels]] Chapter Master [[Commander Dante|Dante]] Regent of [[Imperium Nihilus]], a deputy for the half of the Imperium cut off by the Rift.
