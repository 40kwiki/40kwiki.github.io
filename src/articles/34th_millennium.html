The 34th millennium (M34) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M34 to 999.M34. [[The Forging]] continued through it. The astropathic network of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] reached its widest extent, and the rediscovery of a [[Standard Template Construct|STC]] database in the Cana System revived Imperial industry. The High Lords confirmed the [[Imperial Cult]] as the official religion of the Imperium.

The millennium opened with the Fourth [[Black Crusade]] and saw two of the worst disasters of the age: the [[Pale Wasting]] from the [[Ghoul Stars]], whose records are largely destroyed, and the Howling of 401.M34, when the death scream of the psychic tyrant called the Cacodominus killed about a billion [[astropath]]s. The [[Traitor Legions]] in the [[Eye of Terror]] fought among themselves, while the first [[Warp Stars]] began to appear. By its end the Imperium was drifting towards the division of the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]].
