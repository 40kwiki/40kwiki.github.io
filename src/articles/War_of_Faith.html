A War of Faith is a religiously inspired military conflict fought by Imperial forces. It is ordered and blessed by the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], the state church of the Imperium of Mankind. Only the [[Ecclesiarch]] may declare one, calling the faithful of whole sectors to arms against a heretic or xenos foe.

A War of Faith is often confused with an [[Imperial Crusade]]. The two differ in who commands them and who takes part. An Imperial Crusade is declared by the secular rulers of the Imperium, the [[High Lords of Terra]] or the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. A War of Faith is declared by the church, and its forces are drawn from the church's own clergy and the faithful.

History

The office of the Ecclesiarch was created in the early 32nd millennium, when the High Lords recognised the Temple of the Saviour Emperor as the state religion. The power to declare a War of Faith dates from the same period. The first Wars of Faith are recorded in the 33rd millennium.The dates of the first Wars of Faith are estimated from the church's registers.

During the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32, the Ecclesiarch [[Erekart Mesring]] declared a War of Faith against the Beast. The war was the most famous such conflict of the 32nd millennium.
