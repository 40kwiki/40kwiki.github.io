Askellon Sector is a region of Imperial star systems in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], lying between the [[Eye of Terror]] and the [[Halo Stars]]. It sits at the end of a stellar cluster that includes the [[Calixis Sector]] and the [[Ixaniad Sector]]. The sector was once prosperous and well governed, but by the 41st millennium it is isolated, and few travel there except the desperate, the mad and the outcast.

Geography

The sector is affected by a near-constant [[Warp storm]] known as the [[Pandaemonium]]. The storm rises and falls over the millennia, but has grown more intense with each generation. Its discharges seem to act independently of other storms, and Imperial observers describe it as a creature seeking to consume the whole sector. Several of the major [[Navigator]] clans have withdrawn their holdings from Askellon, and the [[astropath]] choirs of its lynchpin worlds report that the storm drowns out the signals of other sectors.

History

Imperial records give few certain dates for the founding of the sector. Most copies of the sector's records place its beginnings in the [[Age of Technology]], before the [[Imperium of Mankind]] rose. The epic [[Lay of Askellios]] gives the common account, though many scholars treat it as allegory. In 731.M41, soon after the [[Vaxi Atrocity]], an Imperial Navy patrol squadron completed a three-year sweep of the outer systems of the Asphodel Deeps Sub-sector and found a string of ruined settlements on a dozen moons of the Phobetor System. The damage showed that the Aeldari had raided them.The founding date is not recorded in any surviving source and is given here as "Age of Technology" only.

The sector's ruling classes have held power for longer than the [[Adeptus Terra]] has existed in its present form. Its nobles are said to have been undone by madness, hubris or failure, and the sector's great men and women are remembered for their falls as much as their deeds. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] preachers of the sector now teach that the [[Emperor]] has turned His face from Askellon, although the masses continue to honour Him.
