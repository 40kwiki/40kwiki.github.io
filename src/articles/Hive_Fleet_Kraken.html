Hive Fleet Kraken was a [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] hive fleet that entered [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] along the [[Eastern Fringe]] in the last decade of the [[41st millennium]]. Its assault, which the [[Administratum]] officially dated to 992.M41, is known as the Second Tyrannic War. Unlike the slow, single advance of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] half a century before, the Kraken split into hundreds of fast-moving tendrils that struck many worlds at once, and thousands of Imperial worlds were lost to it.

The fleet's main strength was broken in 992–993.M41 at two places. At the [[Hive world|hive world]] [[Ichar IV]] the [[Ultramarines]] under [[Marneus Calgar]] crushed a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer]] rebellion and the swarms that came to feed on it, and at the craftworld [[Iyanden]] the [[Aeldari]] threw back the fleet's heaviest waves at the cost of most of their people. The surviving tendrils fled inward, towards the galactic core, where they remained a menace for decades. Imperial strategists now regard the Kraken as the forerunner of the larger invasion by [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] that began in 997.M41.

Background

The Imperium first met the [[Tyranids]] in 745.M41, when Hive Fleet Behemoth was destroyed at the [[Battle of Macragge]]. In the years after, the [[Inquisitor]] [[Kryptman]] warned that Behemoth had been only a vanguard. The Imperium did not heed the warning until Kraken arrived. Its tendrils had in fact been moving into the galaxy since at least early 990.M41, preceded, as with every [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]], by the [[Shadow in the Warp]] and by [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer]] infiltration of the worlds in its path.The Administratum's date of 992.M41 marks the first confirmed loss of worlds; Ordo Xenos estimates of the fleet's arrival are earlier.

Course of the war

Ichar IV

On Ichar IV, a [[Hive world|hive world]] of the Eastern Fringe, a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]] had grown in secret for generations. In 992.M41 it rose in open rebellion and seized much of the planet just as the Kraken arrived overhead. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]], led by [[Marneus Calgar]], landed to break the cult, then faced the full weight of a tendril of the [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]]. The battle lasted months and was won by a combination of the Ultramarines' assaults, the [[Imperial Navy]]'s victories in orbit and the defenders' destruction of the hive ships' synaptic creatures. Ultramarines who fought there were later counted among the Chapter's [[Tyrannic War Veterans]].

Radnar and the Eastern Fringe

After Ichar IV, Imperial forces including the Justus regiments known as the Emperor's Shield were redeployed to the nearby hive world Radnar, where they burned out the [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] nests around Jourmall Hive in a six-week bombardment and armoured sweep. Elsewhere [[Mephiston]] of the [[Blood Angels]] held the world Hollonan against a Kraken tendril in 992.M41.

Iyanden

Main article: [[Craftworld]]

Craftworld Iyanden learned of the Kraken from its Rangers in 992.M41. [[Farseer]] Kelmon called the whole craftworld to arms, woke the Avatar of [[Aeldari|Khaine]] and raised the spirits of its dead as wraith-constructs, a step that risked the souls of its ancestors. The craftworld's fleet defeated the first swarms but was ground down; later waves landed on the craftworld and took the Forests of Silence and the Shrine of Asuryan. The exiled Prince [[Yriel]] returned with his corsairs, broke the last waves in space, and then fought through the craftworld to destroy the swarm's Hive Tyrant. Iyanden survived, but four-fifths of its people were dead, Kelmon among them, and countless spirit stones were lost.

The T'au

Main article: [[T'au Empire]]

An outer wing of the fleet approached the [[Damocles Gulf]] in 991.M41 and struck the [[T'au Empire]], which suffered heavy losses before driving it off.

Aftermath

The losses at Ichar IV and [[Craftworld|Iyanden]] broke the Kraken's coordinated advance. Imperial and [[Aeldari]] scholars both concluded that the two victories depended on each other: had the Kraken not divided its strength between them, neither defence would have held. The scattered remnants fled towards the [[Milky Way|galactic core]], past the defence lines built to stop them, and fed on poorly defended worlds far from the war zones. Running battles with these splinter fleets continued for many years.

Kraken splinter fleets have been reported in the [[Era Indomitus]]. One struck the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] world Fecundia, where an Imperial garrison eventually drove it off; by 058.M42, after three decades without a confirmed [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] sighting, the world was declared cleansed.

Imperial response

The speed of the Kraken's advance overwhelmed the Imperium's usual methods. Requests for aid from the Eastern Fringe took months to reach the [[Segmentum]] command at [[Macragge]] and the [[Ultramar]] fleets, and by the time a major task force had been raised, many of the worlds that had called for help had been consumed. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] raised new regiments in great numbers across the eastern Ultima Segmentum, and the [[Imperial Navy]] set up picket lines across the routes the tendrils had followed, with the aim of catching the swarms in the void before they could reach inhabited worlds.

The [[Ordo Xenos]] drew three lessons from the war, which it circulated to [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]]. The first was that [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer cults]] prepare the way for a [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]], and that an uprising like that on Ichar IV should be read as a warning of an approaching swarm. The second was that the [[Shadow in the Warp]] that precedes a fleet can isolate a world or a [[Craftworld|craftworld]] for weeks before the first creatures arrive, as it did at Iyanden. The third was that a hive fleet that has been broken is not destroyed: its scattered tendrils remain dangerous for decades, and may feed and grow again far from the original war zone.

Imperial records count the cost of the Second Tyrannic War in thousands of worlds, most of them small or lightly defended colonies whose populations had no warning. Many were struck from the Administratum rolls without any record of their last days.

Characteristics

The Kraken favoured speed and numbers. Its swarms were rich in fast creatures, Genestealers, [[Lictor]]s and Raveners, and its tendrils moved independently, so that worlds were overrun before reinforcements could arrive. Its bioforms were usually coloured bone white with red, orange or brown carapace. Imperial tacticians drew from the war the lesson that the [[Tyranids]] could adapt their methods from one fleet to the next.
