A warp storm, also written warpstorm, is a region of violent turbulence in the [[Warp]], comparable to a tempest in an ocean. Warp storms are born of the emotions of living beings, and they swell with war, terror and psychic power. Some last a few days. Others have lasted thousands of years, and a few, such as the [[Eye of Terror]], have torn the boundary between [[Realspace|realspace]] and the [[Immaterium]] open. Within a storm the currents of the Warp are unreadable, [[Navigator|Navigators]] are blinded, and ships are thrown far off course or destroyed.

A warp storm obscures the light of the [[Astronomican]] and cuts off [[Astropathy|astropathic]] messages, so that the worlds inside it are isolated for years, decades or centuries. It is also the means by which [[Chaos]] enters the material universe. Where a storm lies upon a world, the veil is thin, [[Daemon|daemons]] may cross, and the minds of the population are open to corruption. The Imperium treats the approach of a storm as a grave threat, and the region around a persistent storm is often a place of [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]], [[Mutant|mutation]] and war.

The greatest warp storm in recorded history is the [[Great Rift]], the Cicatrix Maledictum, which opened in 999.M41 and divided the [[Imperium of Mankind]] in two.

Nature and causes

[[Warp|The Warp]] is formed of the thoughts and passions of living minds, and it responds to them. A great battle, a plague, a massacre or a mass of worshippers sends ripples through the Immaterium, and where the ripples meet they form a storm. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] compares the process to weather and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] to the wrath of the Emperor. The [[Inquisition]] regards both comparisons as insufficient, because some storms are made, not born, by the rites of sorcerers.

A storm can be caused by a single event. The Fall of the [[Aeldari]] gave birth to [[Slaanesh]] and opened the Eye of Terror, and the sacrifice performed by the [[Word Bearers]] at [[Battle of Calth|Calth]] raised the Ruinstorm. A storm may also grow out of a persistent source, such as a great concentration of [[Psyker|psykers]], a fortress of [[Chaos]] or a world on which a god of Chaos is worshipped. The storms of the [[Age of Strife]] are believed to have been caused by the psychic upheaval of the humans' own decline.

The effect of a storm on the Warp is strongest at its centre and fades with distance. The surrounding region, called the storm's margin, is turbulent but often passable.

Effects

On travel

A ship that enters a storm cannot be steered. The [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]]'s third eye is dazzled by the storm, and the light of the Astronomican is lost. Ships may be thrown tens of thousands of light-years from their course, or held in the Warp for years, or destroyed outright. Those that survive may emerge in a different century. Even a ship that does not enter a storm may be diverted by it, because a storm changes the currents around it and ends the stable routes that crossed its region.

On communication

[[Astropathy|Astropathic]] messages are scattered by storms, and a world inside a storm may hear nothing from the Imperium for generations. In the worst cases the astropaths of the world are driven mad by the noise of the Warp.

On worlds

The inhabitants of a world under a storm suffer a rise in [[Mutant|mutation]] and psychic outbreaks. Their dreams are disturbed, and cults arise with great speed. The Imperium's governors report spectral lights, the appearance of daemons in the streets and sudden changes in the weather and the seasons. A world cut off by a storm may revert to barbarism, fall to Chaos or be forgotten for ten thousand years.

Daemonic incursions

Main article: [[Daemon]]

Daemons cross into [[Realspace|realspace]] most easily where a storm touches a world. The Imperium records frequent incursions on worlds near the Eye of Terror and the Maelstrom, and in the regions of the [[Great Rift]]. A world that suffers one may be reclaimed by the [[Inquisition]] or the [[Adeptus Astartes]], or it may be declared lost and subjected to [[Exterminatus]]. Where a storm lasts for centuries, a world may become a [[Daemon world]], in which the laws of realspace no longer hold.

Notable storms

The storms of the Age of Strife

See also: [[Age of Strife]]

In the late [[Dark Age of Technology]], the rise of psychic power in the human species produced great storms that cut off the human worlds from one another. These storms, which are blamed for the [[Age of Strife]] (M25 – M30), did not clear until the Fall of the Aeldari opened the Eye of Terror and the Warp's energy drained into it. The [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] houses and the Emperor's [[Astronomican]] later restored contact.Estimated; no record gives exact years.

The Eye of Terror

Main article: [[Eye of Terror]]

The Eye of Terror is a permanent rift in the north-west of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], born of the Fall of the Aeldari in the 30th millennium. It is the stronghold of the [[Traitor Legions]] and the starting point of the [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]]. The region was kept under constant watch from the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of [[Cadia]] until that world fell in 999.M41.

The Ruinstorm

Main article: [[Ruinstorm]]

The [[Ruinstorm]] was raised in 007.M31 by [[Erebus]] on the surface of [[Battle of Calth|Calth]], at the command of [[Lorgar]]. It grew with the suffering of the Heresy and in time cut the galaxy in two, isolating [[Terra]] from [[Ultramar]] and the rest of the Imperium. It began to wane in about 011.M31, after the Second Battle of [[Davin]], and dispersed after the death of [[Horus]] at the end of the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31.

The Storm of the Emperor's Wrath

Main article: [[Reign of Blood]]

The Storm of the Emperor's Wrath is a persistent storm in the Caradryad Sector that began in the [[Age of Apostasy]], in the [[36th millennium]]. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] holds that the Emperor raised it to show his anger at the tyranny of [[Goge Vandire]] and the [[Reign of Blood]]. The [[Administratum]] records only that it appeared in the years of the Reign and has since swallowed much of the sector.

The Maelstrom

Main article: [[Maelstrom]]

The [[Maelstrom]] is a great storm in the [[Ultima Segmentum]] that has been the domain of the [[Red Corsairs]] since the [[Badab War]]. It is a rift into the Warp, from which [[Daemon|daemons]] and pirates raid the surrounding [[Sector|sectors]], and the Imperium has fought to contain it for millennia.

The Hadex Anomaly

Main article: [[Hadex Anomaly]]

The [[Hadex Anomaly]], in the [[Jericho Reach]], appeared in 656.M40. Unlike other storms it disturbs time as well as space. Ships leave the Warp before they enter it, and worlds in its glow suffer days of changing length. It forms the eastern end of the Great Rift.

The Great Rift

Main article: [[Great Rift]]

The [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, at the climax of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] and the destruction of Cadia. It crosses the galaxy from the Eye of Terror to the Hadex Anomaly and is a chain of storms rather than a single one. It brought the [[Noctis Aeterna]] and the division of the Imperium, and it has caused the opening of many new [[Daemon world|daemon worlds]].
