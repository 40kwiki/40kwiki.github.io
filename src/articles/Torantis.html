Torantis is a world of the Necron wars. A war fleet commanded by Oradi used an [[Ark of Oblivion]] there, destroying three tomb ships. The weapon harmed the ships of the Necron Behzt as well.

The war between the Necrons grew more terrible after a decree of [[Szarekh]] allowed the use of superweapons to both sides. The Ark's use at Torantis is one of the earliest uses recorded under that decree.The date of the Torantis battle is estimated from the order of events in the sources.
