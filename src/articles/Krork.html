The Krork are a fungoid, symbiotic xenos species that the [[Old Ones]] created as a soldier race more than sixty million Terran years ago. The Krork are the ancestors of the [[Orks]], and the [[Ordo Xenos]] classes them as Orkoids in its catalogue.

The Krork were bred for war in the [[War in Heaven]], the conflict between the Old Ones and the [[C'tan]]. Imperial records describe them as the Old Ones' tools, made without the subtlety of the [[Aeldari]] or the [[Necrons]].

Origin

[[Old Ones|The Old Ones]] made the Krork as one of several servant races. The [[Ordo Xenos]] records describe the Krork as a species that spread quickly and adapted to many conditions, which is consistent with the later spread of the [[Orks]] across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]].Dates for the Old Ones are estimates based on the xenos records. No Imperial record gives an exact date for the creation of the Krork.

Descendants

The [[Orks]] are recorded as the Krork's descendants, though the [[Ordo Xenos]] treats the link as a matter of classification rather than certain fact. The Krork's symbiotic biology is held to explain many of the traits of the Ork species.
