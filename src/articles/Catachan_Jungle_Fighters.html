The Catachan Jungle Fighters are the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised on [[Catachan]], the most dangerous [[Death world|death world]] settled by humans. Bred in an environment where survival to adulthood is itself a trial, Catachans are larger, stronger and hardier than most humans, and the Imperium uses them above all as light infantry for jungles, swamps and other hostile terrain, where they excel in ambush, infiltration, booby traps and survival.

Catachan regiments have fought in most of the great wars of the late 41st millennium, from the [[Macharian Crusade]] to the [[Tyrannic Wars]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] their homeworld's regiments defeated a daemonic invasion on their own before the [[Indomitus Crusade]] arrived. Their heroes, among them Colonel "Iron Hand" Straken and the lone scout Sly Marbo, are famous throughout the Imperial Guard.

History

Origins

The [[Departmento Munitorum]], founded in the reforms that followed the [[Horus Heresy]], c. 020.M31, recognised that the descendants of Catachan's marooned colonists would make soldiers for the Imperium's most hostile battlefields, and raised the first Jungle Fighter regiments.The first founding is not dated in open records. It falls after the creation of the Munitorum and before the 34th millennium (estimated). Since then Catachan has paid its tithe almost entirely in soldiers.

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

Large numbers of Catachan regiments served in the Macharian Crusade of 392–399.M41, and in the First Tyrannic War of 745.M41 they were deployed across the galaxy against [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]]. Colonel Gator's Catachan XVIII, the "Swamprats", fought a Tyranid horde on Koralkal VIII for seven years, from 762 to 771.M41, and in 762.M41 the Catachan MXIV "Unseen Lurkers" under Colonel "Steel Eye" Black fought the Orks on Kator.

When WAAAGH! Grax fell on [[Ryza]] in 925.M41, Catachan regiments were among the first sent to its aid. In 926.M41 the Catachan II, under Colonel Straken, was the only regiment to survive planetfall on Dulma'lin. In 930.M41 the Catachan XXIV, the "Waiting Death", destroyed WAAAGH! Krakskull on Arandra V by luring the whole horde into a booby-trapped ravine. In 938.M41 Straken personally led the Catachan II in the charge at Moden's Ridge on Yaquit 27, defeating the L'Huraxi invaders although outnumbered more than ten to one.

In 972.M41 the Night Lords Chaos lord Ratathrax invaded Catachan itself and was driven off. Catachan regiments fought [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] in the Second Tyrannic War (990–993.M41) and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] from 997.M41, held Jorn V with the 12th Army Group in 997.M41, drove the Aeldari of Alaitoc from Fortuse III the same year, fought the [[Orks]] in the Third War for Armageddon in 998.M41 and the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] on Cytheria in 999.M41.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Catachan regiments fought in the Thirteenth Black Crusade of 999.M41. After the [[Great Rift]] opened, Catachan was invaded by daemons; when [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s fleets reached the system, c. 000–001.M42, the local regiments had already destroyed the invaders. The Crusade took on new Catachan regiments and left with more soldiers than it had brought.

Organisation and tactics

Catachan regiments are light infantry with little armour of their own, though they field heavy weapon teams, Sentinels and artillery. They prefer close terrain, where they ambush the enemy, lay mines and traps, and fight at knife range. Each regiment is known by a Roman numeral and usually a nickname, such as the Catachan II "Catachan Devils". The regiments are informal in manner: officers lead from the front, and rank and parade-ground discipline are lightly regarded, which has sometimes brought them into conflict with the [[Officio Prefectus|Commissariat]].

Catachans have a reputation for distrusting psykers and machinery, and for contempt for soldiers raised on gentler worlds.

Equipment and appearance

Catachans fight in green fatigues and often without armour or shirts, which the heat of their native jungles makes intolerable. Many wear a red bandana. Every Catachan carries a Catachan fighting knife, a heavy blade that serves as weapon, tool and badge of honour. They are armed with lasguns, flamers and heavy weapons, and use improvised traps such as spike pits, snares and shredder mines.
