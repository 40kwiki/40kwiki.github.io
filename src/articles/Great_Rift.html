The Great Rift, called the Cicatrix Maledictum in High Gothic, is a tear in the fabric of reality and a galaxy-wide chain of [[Warp storm|warp storms]] that opened across the middle of the Imperium in 999.M41. It runs from the [[Eye of Terror]] in the galactic west to the Hadex Anomaly on the eastern fringe, and it has divided the territory of the Imperium into two parts. The worlds on the side of [[Terra]], the Imperium Sanctus, can still see the light of the [[Astronomican]]. Those beyond it, the Imperium Nihilus, were plunged into a darkness that the [[Administratum]] has named the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]], the Eternal Night, and have been left to fight alone.

The Rift is the greatest [[Warp|warp]] event in recorded history, and it has transformed the Imperium. It claimed countless worlds, fleets and armies in the first years. It is the reason for the [[Indomitus Crusade]], for the new [[Imperial Dating System|Imperial calendar]], and for a surge in psychic ability across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] that scholars have called the Psychic Awakening and that common speech calls the Age of Witches. To the [[Traitor|Traitors]] of the Eye it is the Crimson Path, which [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] strove for thirteen crusades to open.

Names

Every culture has its own name for the Rift, and most reflect fear. The Imperium uses the [[High Gothic]] Cicatrix Maledictum, "the accursed scar", or the Great Rift. Mariners speak of the Mouth of Ruin or the Warpscar. The [[Aeldari]] call it Dathedian, a name from their own tongue that no Imperial scholar has been able to translate with certainty. The [[Orks]] see it as the grin of their god [[Gork and Mork|Gork]]. The Orks have only two gods, brutal but cunning Gork and cunning but brutal Mork, and the greenskins who saw the Rift open took it to be Gork's wide mouth, about to swallow the stars. That the Rift pours [[Chaos]] worshippers and [[Daemon|daemons]] into the galaxy did not disturb them, since the cleverer among them reasoned that Gork was vomiting out unnatural filth so that the Orks could fight it. Tribes whose worlds were swallowed concluded that Gork had found them soft and sent them to the warpath.

Origins

The state of the Warp

Before the Rift opened, the Warp had been growing restless for decades. In the last years of the [[41st millennium]], travel and communication through it suffered increasing disturbance, and the [[Astropathy|astropathic]] choirs recorded visions and storms that they could not explain. The events of the Thirteenth [[Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 made matters worse. In the end, Imperial scholars hold, the material universe could bear no more, and a vast rift tore across the stars.

Theories of cause

No scholar knows why the Rift opened. The principal theories, as set out by the [[Inquisition]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], include the following.

  • The fall of Cadia. The destruction of the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] and of the [[Necrons|Necron]] pylons that had held the Eye of Terror in check, in the final battle of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], is the explanation most often given. [[Eye of Terror|The Eye]] expanded when the pylons fell. See [[Fall of Cadia]].
  • The sorcery of Magnus. The [[Daemon|Daemon Primarch]] [[Magnus the Red]] and his [[Thousand Sons]] invaded the [[Fenris|Fenris system]] in the same period to take revenge for the burning of Prospero, and some hold that the sorcery of the invasion weakened the Warp.
  • Catastrophe in the Webway. The [[Aeldari]] believe that a breach in the Webway was involved, and in particular the sundering of the craftworld Biel-Tan and the partial awakening of [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnead]], their god of the dead.
  • The breaking of the Amethal daemon cage, the bloodshed between the Imperium and the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] in the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade|Damocles Gulf]], and a dozen similar events of the last years of the millennium.
  • The resurrection of [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The return of a [[Primarch|primarch]], and the psychic disturbance that went with it, is held by some to have been a contributing cause.

The Inquisition's official position is that no single event was the cause, and that all of these contributed to a collapse that had been in preparation for a long time. The [[Ordo Malleus]] adds that the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]], who have been working toward such a moment for ten thousand years, would not have been idle.

The opening

The Noctis Aeterna

Main article: [[Imperium Nihilus]]

When the Rift tore open it unleashed a wave of empyric energy and supernatural darkness that dimmed the psychic light of the Astronomican. The Imperium named it the Noctis Aeterna. The effect alone claimed countless worlds, fleets and armies. [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] lost their way. Ships that had entered the Warp did not come out. [[Astropathy|Astropathic communication]] between the two halves of the Imperium ended, and worlds that had been in contact with [[Terra]] for ten thousand years heard no word for years. On many of the worlds beyond the Rift, the Astronomican was not simply obscured, it was replaced by what survivors called a psychic [[Maelstrom|maelstrom]] of nightmares, and the gates of Hell were said to have opened.

Storms of unprecedented fury now circle the Rift, and others move across the galaxy as if guided by a will, obeying no known law of solar current. They cut worlds off from the Astronomican and have enslaved, terrorised or destroyed countless populations. The Noctis Aeterna has since receded. Its beacon is visible again in the [[Imperium Sanctus]], but the storms, the Rift itself and the Imperium Nihilus remain.

The distortion of time

The fraying of the barrier between worlds and the Warp has also disturbed time. The temporal fluctuations that were once confined to the great storms such as the Eye of Terror now spread across the galaxy. Some worlds on the edge of the Rift felt standard centuries pass in an instant, and others were almost frozen. Others experience constant shifts. The result was a revision of the [[Imperial Dating System|Imperial calendar]] ordered by [[Roboute Guilliman]]. It was made to correct the growing inaccuracy of the previous chronology, and it takes the opening of the Rift as the point from which all later dates are reckoned. The [[Era Indomitus]] is named for the response.

The divided Imperium

The Imperium Sanctus

Main article: [[Imperium Sanctus]]

The Imperium Sanctus, the Holy Imperium, includes Terra, [[Mars]] and most of the worlds of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Solar]]. [[Astronomican|The Astronomican]] can be seen from it. After the Noctis Aeterna receded, the light returned across the Imperium Sanctus, and travel by Warp became easier, though never safe. The Sanctus has been the base of the Imperium's recovery. It is governed by the [[High Lords of Terra]] under the authority of the returned [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], [[Roboute Guilliman]].

The Imperium Nihilus

Main article: [[Imperium Nihilus]]

The Imperium Nihilus, the Dark Imperium, lies beyond the Rift. The light of the Emperor cannot be seen there, and the Navigators of the [[Navis Nobilite]] cannot navigate by it. Many planets near the Rift disappeared. Others were corrupted so that they are now classed as [[Daemon world|Daemon Worlds]]. Among the many [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] whose homeworlds lay in the Nihilus was the [[Blood Angels]], whose world of [[Baal]] was already under attack by [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]. Cut off from Terra, the Blood Angels and their successors stood against the fleet in the [[Devastation of Baal]]. They were saved in the end only by aid from the servants of the Emperor and, unexpectedly, from his enemies. The Lord Commander has appointed the Blood Angels' [[Chapter Master]] [[Commander Dante|Dante]] as Regent of the Imperium Nihilus, charged with holding together a realm that cannot be reached by ordinary means.

Responses

The Indomitus Crusade

Main article: [[Indomitus Crusade]]

[[Roboute Guilliman]], newly resurrected, launched the Indomitus Crusade to restore contact and fight the forces emerging from the Rift. He assembled an armada, with elements of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], a small contingent of the [[Sisters of Silence]] and a vast host of [[Primaris Space Marines]] drawn from the newly founded [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Second Founding]]. Strike forces from more than a dozen older Chapters, led by the [[Imperial Fists]], joined it. The fleets travelled to besieged worlds, broke sieges and cleared invaders. News of the crusade spread by [[Astropathy|astropath]] wherever messages could still be received, and the return of a hero from the age of myth became known across the Imperium.

The task was too great for any single crusade. The first phase ended with the repulse of the forces of [[Nurgle]] from Guilliman's own realm of Ultramar in the [[Plague Wars]]. Since the Rift opened, no world of the Imperium has been without war, and many thousands still call for aid.

The Psychic Awakening

Main article: [[Psychic Awakening]]

The Rift has also enhanced the number and power of [[Psyker|psykers]]. Worlds that had no psykers for generations began to produce them in large numbers, and the strength of established psykers increased. The [[Inquisition]] and the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] have found their resources overwhelmed. The same effect has made the daemonic more dangerous, since the Warp spills through any weakness into the material universe. Some speak of an Age of Witches. The [[Ynnari]], a cult among the [[Aeldari]] that serves the god Ynnead, has appeared in the same period, and the Imperium's scholars note that the Rift is a place of rebirth for several of the galaxy's peoples as well as of destruction.

Effects on other powers

Chaos

Main article: [[Chaos]]

For the [[Traitor|Traitors]] the Rift is the Crimson Path, the goal of the [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]]. The expanded [[Eye of Terror]] has become a larger realm of Chaos, and the wreck of [[Cadia]] has been resettled by the [[Black Legion]] as a fortress. The [[Death Guard]], the [[Thousand Sons]] and other [[Traitor Legions]] have launched campaigns across the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]] and beyond, and daemonic incursions have multiplied on worlds in the [[Imperium Nihilus|Nihilus]].

The xenos

Main article: [[Xenos]]

The Rift has also changed the war with the xenos. The [[Orks]] have spread through the galaxy in greater numbers, and their stronger tribes have claimed [[Gork and Mork|Gork]]'s Grin as a sign. [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] [[Hive Fleet|hive fleets]] have taken advantage of the Imperium's weakness on its [[Eastern Fringe|eastern fringe]]. The [[Necrons]] have woken in larger numbers, and the [[T'au Empire]] has expanded where Imperial garrisons have been stripped for the defence of the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]].

Current state

The Rift has not stopped growing. It roils and spreads, devouring entire species with its power, and the Imperium has not found any way of closing it. Imperial scholars regard it as the defining event of the age, as the Heresy was of the [[31st millennium]]. The [[Era Indomitus]], the age that it opened, continues.
