The White Scars, called the Vth Legion in the age of the [[Great Crusade]] and the Star Hunters before their primarch was found, are one of the original [[Space Marine Legions]] and a loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. They are the Imperium's acknowledged masters of the lightning strike: fast columns of bikes, jetbikes, skimmers and gunships that appear without warning, break a foe's weakest point and are gone before an answer can be organised. Their warriors take their name from the ritual scar each bears on the face and their culture from the nomad tribes of their homeworld, [[Chogoris]].

The Legion's [[Primarch]] was [[Jaghatai Khan]], the Khagan or Khan of Khans, who had united the steppe tribes of Chogoris before the [[Emperor of Mankind]] arrived. Through the Great Crusade the White Scars ranged far beyond the frontier. In the [[Horus Heresy]] they refused to be drawn into the wars of the [[Traitor|Traitors]], fought their way to [[Terra]] and took part in the [[Siege of Terra]]. The Khan vanished in pursuit of the [[Drukhari]] in the years after the [[Great Scouring]], and the White Scars hold to the belief that he hunts still and will one day return.

After the [[Second Founding]] the White Scars were divided under the [[Codex Astartes]] and gave rise to a number of Successor Chapters, yet the parent Chapter kept its home on Chogoris, its brotherhoods and its customs. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it has survived the attack of the [[Red Corsairs]] on the Yasan Sector and the near death of its Great Khan, and it has been reinforced by [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

History

Origins as the Vth Legion

The Vth Legion was raised during the last years of the [[Unification Wars]], when the Emperor's first Legions were recruited from the warriors of [[Terra]]. Its earliest recruits came in large part from the technomadic clans of the Thulean Basin, who had survived the [[Age of Strife]] in mechanised crawlers on the frozen salt flats of the north, and later from every region of Terra. In the wars of Unification the Vth was given the lonely task of scouting. Small cadres, sometimes a dozen warriors, went ahead of the main armies to find the strongholds of Terra's warlords, test their defences and leave them weakened. Few of these deeds were recorded, and the Legion won a name for independence rather than renown.

When the Great Crusade began in 798.M30, the Legion was broken into a hundred Pioneer Companies and sent out along the strongest currents of the [[Warp]] to find lost human worlds and map alien realms. For more than fifty years, until c. 865.M30, each company fought alone, so far apart that they lost all sense of being one Legion. It was during this period that the Pioneer Company of Captain Kornelius Dure surveyed the [[Planetary classification|mining world]] of [[Cthonia]] in c. 800.M30 and reported it to be a nest of serpents. [[Horus]], who would later claim Cthonia as his homeworld, remembered the report with amusement and favoured the Vth Legion's pioneers ever after. The Legion lived by one [[Ursarkar Creed|creed]], that each new day was a victory.

Jaghatai Khan and the Blooding

The Legion's missing [[Primarch|primarch]] was found on [[Chogoris]], a [[Feudal world|feudal world]] in the Yasan Sector then known to the Imperium by the cold name Mundus Planus. His gestation capsule had fallen on the steppe as an infant, and [[Jaghatai Khan|the khan]] of the Talskar tribe, Ong Khan, adopted him. When rivals killed his foster father, the young Jaghatai avenged him by destroying the Kurayed tribe in a single night. He was elected khan, subdued each tribe in turn on the choice of submission or death, mixed their warriors into new units so that loyalty would follow him instead of old blood feuds, and was acclaimed Khagan. He then broke the cult-ridden realm that had ruled the western plains and overthrew the city-dwelling Palatine empire of the east. Chogorian tradition says the steppe ran red for years.

The Emperor reached Chogoris in c. 865.M30, in a fleet that included Horus and the XVIth Legion, only a few solar months after the Khan's conquest was complete. The Khan weighed the offer he was given, a place in the Emperor's Imperium or defeat, as one who had made the same offer to others, and he knelt. Records written by the Khan himself show a more calculating motive than the accounts of the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]]: he valued the Emperor's goal of unity and understood the cost of refusing it. Only Rogal Dorn and [[Roboute Guilliman]] are recorded as objecting to the brief instruction he received about the Imperium's workings.

The Khan called the Pioneer Companies back to Chogoris. It took nearly a solar decade, until c. 875.M30, for most of them to arrive, and they came as strangers, each with its own heraldry. He bound them together with Chogorian ritual. On the open plain more than fifty thousand Legionaries cut a mark into their own faces, each choosing the pattern and depth, and each took a name. The rite, known as the Blooding or the Ascension, gave the Legion its new title. The Vth Legion became the White Scars, and the Star Hunters ceased to exist. Chogorians were then recruited in numbers, and the Legion took on the Khan's tribal customs, though many Terran veterans remained.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The White Scars fought across the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]] and beyond in campaigns that the Remembrancers largely neglected. They took part in the destruction of the Ork empire of [[Waaagh!|WAAAGH!]] Mashogg alongside the [[Space Wolves]] and the [[Iron Warriors]]; in the Compliance of the Araaki Spiral, whose people built fortresses in narrow passes; and in the Compliance of [[Battle of Molech|Molech]] in 869.M30, led by the Emperor himself. At the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 the Khan argued that [[Psyker|psykers]] were a necessary danger, and Imperial records say that he drew up many of the rules for the Legions' [[Librarian|Librarius]], though his name does not appear in the official texts. He had no illusions about the Warp, and the Chogorians, who never lost belief in spirits, understood better than most why the Emperor feared it.

The Legion was at the [[Triumph of Ullanor]] in 999.M30–000.M31, where Horus was named [[Warmaster]]. A [[Long War|long war]] against an Ork realm in the Chondax System followed, and it was still being fought in 007.M31.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

News of Horus's revolt reached the Khan in the closing stages of the Chondax Campaign as fragments of [[Astropathy|astropathic]] signals, some of them months old. The [[Alpha Legion]] was sent to pin down the Space Wolves and the White Scars piecemeal, and the Khan had to choose between answering [[Leman Russ]] and obeying the order of [[Rogal Dorn]] to return to Terra. He chose Terra. The Legion lost a fifth of its strength in four years of raiding, 007–011.M31, against the [[Traitor|Traitors]]' supply lines, and was trapped by the Ruinstorm, the storm in the Warp raised by the [[Word Bearers]]. Some records name the stratagem that freed them. At the ruined Kalium Gate the Khan's Keshig Master, Qin Xa, wore the Khagan's armour and drew the [[Death Guard]] and [[Emperor's Children]] task force under [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Eidolon]] onto himself. He died of his wounds aboard the flagship Lance of Heaven while the Khan, free of pursuit, went in search of the [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] Pieter Achelieux and a passage through the storm.

On Terra, which they reached in c. 013.M31, the White Scars joined the [[Imperial Fists]] and the [[Blood Angels]], the only loyalist Legions at the Throneworld before the siege began. The Khan did not hold a wall. He took the mobile reserve, the White Scars and the Terran armoured divisions, and in 014.M31 raided the [[Battle of Lion's Gate|Lion's Gate Spaceport]] at dawn, retaking it from a garrison that did not expect an attack. The defence lasers were turned on the Traitors' own landing craft, and the flow of men and machines to the Palace was halved in a single day. A later attempt on the [[Eternity Wall Spaceport]] was beaten back. The latest Imperial chronicles of the siege record that the Khan was gravely wounded in the final fighting and carried from the field near death, an account that supersedes the earlier version in which his Legion emerged from the siege untouched in its leadership.

Great Scouring and the disappearance of the Khan

The Khan spent the years after the Heresy rebuilding in the Yasan Sector, which had been raided without mercy by [[Drukhari]] while the Imperium was at war with itself. Thousands of Chogorians were taken through the Webway as slaves. The campaign that followed was a vengeance. In c. 084.M31, at Corusil V on the edge of the Maelstrom, the Khan and his 1st Brotherhood fought several Kabals. The Khan followed an Archon through a portal into the Webway and was not seen again. Imperial records state only that he vanished. The Chapter's own accounts say that he is hunting still.

Chapter era

The White Scars survived the [[Second Founding]] of 021.M31 with their identity intact, dividing into several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] under the [[Codex Astartes]] while the parent Chapter retained its home. Its history is a long list of hunts and mobile campaigns. In the Time of Two Emperors, when the Imperium was divided in the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], the Chapter led the war against [[Orks]] in the Scorched System. Its brotherhoods fought in the [[Sabbat Worlds]], the [[Macharian Crusade]] and the wars of the Fourth Quadrant. A series of misfortunes known as the Cursed Year, in which three successive Khans died on their first campaigns, led the Stormseers to recall the Chapter to Chogoris and to demand a trial for every candidate. Only Jubal Khan, in the Chapter's latest accounts, survived such an ordeal and was made Great Khan.

In 943.M41 Great Khan Kyublai Khan set out after a Drukhari raid on Chogoris and did not return; Jubal Khan was installed in his place and proclaimed a Great Hunt for the Khan's killers. Kor'sarro Khan, the Master of the Hunt, spent much of that war tracking the Drukhari warlord known as the Shatterling Prince. Another hunt followed the [[Daemon|Daemon Prince]] Kernax Voldorius, an Alpha Legion warlord, across ten years of campaigns that ended in 871.M41. Several brotherhoods fought at the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]] in 998.M41, where they were deployed to the frozen Deadlands. At the Fall of [[Cadia]] the brotherhood of Khajog Khan used the moors around the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]] as a field for hit-and-run raids until it was ordered back to Chogoris.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Shortly before the Great Rift opened, the [[Red Corsairs]] of [[Huron Blackheart]] emerged from the Maelstrom against the Yasan Sector in three prongs. Gartuli and Thaxis fell. With the Great Khan and much of the Chapter abroad, Barutai Khan, Captain of the 2nd Brotherhood, took command at Quan Zhou and fought on the ground, using Chogoris's storms and canyons as weapons while the invaders divided into raiding parties whose champions vied for the favour of the [[Chaos|Dark Gods]]. When daemons manifested after the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the sector was overrun and the Chapter seemed to face its last stand. Battle Group Delphi II of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] arrived in time, bringing [[Primaris Space Marines]], [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] support and the means to produce more.

With the brotherhoods reinforced, Jubal Khan led a fleet action against the station Seethnar, the dockyard Blackheart had seized. The assault succeeded, but the Great Khan was captured. His warriors ambushed the ship that held him and recovered him tortured and close to death. He now lies in an [[Apothecary|Apothecarion]] cradle in his sanctum on Chogoris, directing the Chapter in battle while his body heals. The struggle with the Red Corsairs, called the Blackheart War, continues in the Yasan Sector with allies from several Successor Chapters, among them the [[Marauders (Chapter)|Marauders]], Rampagers and [[Destroyers (Chapter)|Destroyers]].

Organisation

The Legion

Main article: [[Space Marine Legions]]

The Vth Legion was organised as hordes or ordu and as brotherhoods, which were named rather than numbered. The Horde of the Stone and the Horde of the [[Terra|Earth]] are recorded; among the brotherhoods were those of the Black Axe, the Blooded Spear, the Dying Sun, the Golden Star, the Hunter's Star, the Jade Scorpion and the Summer Lightning, which was commanded by Jubal Khan. The Legion's Terminator elite was the Keshig, the Khan's household guard, led by Qin Xa. A formation known as the Destroyer Corps, or Karaoghlanlar, carried out the Khan's most savage assignments. Despite a popular belief to the contrary, the Legion used [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]]: the Uhaan Solban brotherhood is recorded as having fielded them.

The Chapter

Main article: [[Space Marine Chapter]]

The present Chapter follows the [[Codex Astartes]] in broad form. It has ten companies, called brotherhoods, each commanded by a Captain with the title of Khan. The 1st Brotherhood, the Spearpoint, is the veteran company. The [[Chapter Master]] is the Great Khan, who sits at [[Chogoris|Quan Zhou]] and is attended by the khans in a council called a kurultai. The Reclusium, led by the Voice of the Storm, provides spiritual guidance through its [[Chaplain|Chaplains]]. The [[Apothecary|Apothecaries]], called emchi, guard the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. The [[Librarian|Librarius]] is the Zadyin Arga, whose Stormseers practise a discipline of their own, the Stormspeaking, which invokes the elemental winds, lightning and ghosts of Chogoris.

The Chapter is notably lean in heavy weapons. It fields few Dreadnoughts, and its battle companies rely on bikers, jetbikers, [[Land Speeder]]s, [[Thunderhawk]]s, assault bikes and light armour. Its scouts ride bikes as well.

Combat doctrine

The White Scars fight like the horsemen of [[Chogoris]]. A brotherhood studies the enemy for some time before an operation, then strikes where the foe is weak, pulls back and strikes again. They avoid contest with the enemy's strongest point, meeting brute force with emptiness, and move around it. Other [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] sometimes call them unreliable, since they will not fight where they have not chosen; but they have a way of arriving in the most dangerous part of a battle unexpectedly, laughing as they do so. The doctrine is not berserk fury but planning: the White Scars scout each objective at length and coordinate each strike with great care. [[Raven Guard]] sources describe a rivalry with the White Scars over who may claim the better hunters and scouts, though the two Chapters have fought side by side often.

Homeworld and culture

[[Chogoris]] is a fertile [[Feudal world|feudal world]] in the Yasan Sector. When the Khan left for the Great Crusade, the empire he had built dissolved back into warring tribes, and it is said that he intended it, so that the Legion would always have a supply of skilled warriors. The tribes roam the steppes behind great herds of grazing animals. Of the cities that he captured, only Quan Zhou was left standing, a marble fortress-monastery high on the Khum Karta, whose approach is lined with the heads of fallen foes.

The White Scars honour the Emperor as the Great Uniter but do not worship him, and the [[Imperial Cult]] has little hold on them. Every battle-brother wears a long ritual scar on the face, the Honour Scar, received at the Blooding, and many tribal customs persist among them. The Chapter's fear of the Warp is as strong as its use of the Stormseers' powers. They do not entomb their dead in [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] in the usual way, since a warrior's spirit should be free to ride the plains of the afterlife.

Gene-seed and recruitment

The Legion's [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] is stable. Its one noted weakness is cultural: the [[Chogoris|Chogorian]] savagery that entered the Legion with the tribal recruits, which flares into feuds between tribal groups within a squad and, in a few recorded cases such as the Red Highway Massacre, a brotherhood exceeding its orders. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] have sought to learn whether the cause is genetic or cultural. Recruits are taken from the tribes of Chogoris, by trial on the steppe, and a Scout wears the lightning bolt without the bar that marks a full Initiate.

Successors

The [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] descended from the White Scars include the Astral Bears, Dark Hunters, [[Destroyers (Chapter)|Destroyers]], Iron Talons, Jade Scorpions, [[Mantis Warriors]], [[Marauders (Chapter)|Marauders]], Rampagers, [[Solar Hawks]], Storm Lords and [[Storm Reapers]]. The Griffon Lords and the Sons of Jaghatai are suspected but unproven descendants.

Heraldry

The Chapter's badge is a stylised red thunderbolt over a horizontal bar, said to represent both the White Scars' speed and the scars on their faces. White and red are the Chapter colours. Brotherhoods wear their own symbols, and veterans of the Legion's early campaigns were allowed to display personal marks. Pre-Heresy armour bore the tribal icons of the brotherhoods: an axe, a hawk, a wolf or a star.
