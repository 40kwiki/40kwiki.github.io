The Enslavers, known in different parts of the galaxy as Psyrens, Krell, Dominators or Puppeteers, are an intelligent xenos species of other-dimensional psychic entities native to the [[Immaterium]]. They look like a hybrid of Terran jellyfish and arachnids, and they can cross into real space through the Warp.

Enslavers take control of any psychic being left unprotected by warding or training. Their victims are used as puppets, and the Imperium records cases in which entire crews were controlled through a single captured psyker. This makes the creature one of the most feared threats to the [[Astronomican]] and to Imperial navigation.

Imperial response

The [[Ordo Xenos]] treats the Enslavers as a priority threat. The [[Imperial Navy]] is ordered to ward its [[Psyker|psykers]] with [[Geller field]]s and to keep them away from any region where Enslaver activity is reported.
