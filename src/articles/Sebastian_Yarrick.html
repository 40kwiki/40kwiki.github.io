Sebastian Yarrick, born Sebastian Vaarden and known as the Hero of Hades Hive and the Old Man of Hades, is a [[Commissar]] of the [[Officio Prefectus]] and one of the most famous soldiers of the late [[41st millennium]]. He led the defence of Hades Hive on [[Armageddon]] against the Ork warlord [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] in the [[Second War for Armageddon]] (941–943.M41), and he has hunted Ghazghkull ever since.

Yarrick lost an arm and an eye at Hades. He replaced the arm with the power klaw of the warboss who took it and the eye with a laser implant, the Bale Eye, and the Orks came to fear him as a man who could kill with a look. He commanded the defence of Armageddon again in the [[Third War for Armageddon]] from 998.M41, then followed Ghazghkull off-world. In the [[Era Indomitus]] he has returned to Armageddon, where the war still goes on.

Early life

Yarrick was born Sebastian Vaarden on Taos III and orphaned at the age of seven. His grandfather, a veteran sergeant of the [[Imperial Guard]], raised him near New Caedon Hive and taught him to fight, set traps and hide. When Orks invaded Taos III, the boy survived alone for months, killing Gretchin and setting traps, until Imperial regiments landed and a patrol found him at the age of ten. He took his grandfather's name as his surname and was sent to the [[Schola Progenium]], from which he entered the Commissariat.No record gives Yarrick's birth year. He was an old man by 941.M41 and is estimated to have been born in the 860s.M41; his survival into the 42nd millennium is attributed to juvenat treatment and bionics.

Second War for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

When Ghazghkull's [[Waaagh]]! struck Armageddon in 941.M41, Yarrick sent an astropathic call for help over the objection of the planetary governor, Herman von Strab, who banished him to Hades Hive. Von Strab's defence collapsed and the Orks reached Hades. There Yarrick took command and held the hive for months against every attack Ghazghkull could devise.

In the first assault the warboss Ugulhard reached Yarrick and severed his right arm at the elbow with his power klaw. Yarrick killed him, and is said to have allowed himself to pass out only after the attack was beaten. He had Ugulhard's klaw adapted as his own arm and later replaced his left eye with a pulse-laser implant. The Orks came to fear the "bale eye", and Ghazghkull himself joined the siege. Hades held out for six months, and when it finally fell, Yarrick was among its few survivors. The time it bought allowed the [[Blood Angels]], [[Salamanders]] and Ultramarines to arrive and break the Waaagh!.

Golgotha

After the war Yarrick swore to kill Ghazghkull and pursued him for nearly ten years. He forced the warlord to battle on the former Imperial world Golgotha, only to find that it was a trap: a second Ork army struck the Imperial force from behind, and a space hulk drove off the fleet. The Imperial army was destroyed, and Yarrick was captured. Ghazghkull let him go, judging by Ork reasoning that a worthy foe was worth keeping alive, and Yarrick returned to Imperial service.

Third War for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

When Ghazghkull returned to Armageddon in 998.M41, Yarrick came out of retirement and took command of its defence with the rank of Lord Commissar. No Imperial commander knew the warlord better, and he anticipated all but the strangest of his assaults. The war became a war of attrition across the whole planet. Ghazghkull then left Armageddon to follow what he believed to be a vision from the Ork gods, and Yarrick went after him with a [[Black Templars]] crusade.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the [[Era Indomitus]] Ghazghkull leads his Great Waaagh! against the Imperium. Yarrick has returned to Armageddon, where the Third War has never ended, and is reported to direct its defence again while following his enemy's movements across the galaxy. He remains the Imperium's foremost authority on Ghazghkull and on Ork warfare.

Legacy

Yarrick is a hero of the Imperium. His memoir, Imperial Commissar: My Life, is read by Schola Progenium cadets, and his image is used on Officio Prefectus posters across the Segmentum Solar. Among the Orks he has a place of his own: Ork warlords who have fought him speak of him with something close to respect.
