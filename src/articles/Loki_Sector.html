The Loki Sector is a region of Imperial space in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], to the galactic south-east of [[Terra]] and close to the border with the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It is best known for the invasion of 989.M41, when the Ork warlord [[Snagrod]] led his WAAAGH! out of the [[Charadon Sector]] and fell upon the sector's worlds. The war that followed, which included the defence of [[Rynn's World]], is remembered as one of the most costly campaigns of the late [[41st millennium]].

The sector has been an Imperial frontier for many millennia, and its worlds were settled and governed under the Administratum. Its losses of 989.M41 are still recorded in the garrisons of its worlds.The sector's early settlement dates are not recorded in the surviving sources.

History

Settlement

The sector's worlds were settled as part of the Imperial colonisation of the Segmentum, and [[Rynn's World]] itself was named for the Imperial Reclamator Saldano Malverro Rynn, who led the first colonists to its system and reclaimed it for Mankind. The world was settled as an [[Agri-world]], and it became the homeworld of the [[Crimson Fists]], a [[Space Marine Chapter]] created in the [[Second Founding]] from the [[Imperial Fists]].

The Ork invasion of 989.M41

In 989.M41 Snagrod, the Ork warlord known as the Arch-Arsonist of [[Charadon]], led his WAAAGH! out of the Charadon Sector and into the Loki Sector. A strike force of the Crimson Fists' 3rd Company was sent to kill him before he could attack, but the attempt failed when a scout's shot missed. Only twenty-eight of the company's eighty-four warriors returned.

Snagrod then invaded Rynn's World. For eighteen months, from mid-989 to 990.M41, the surviving Crimson Fists and the planetary defence forces held New Rynn City against the Orks. They were relieved in 990.M41 by a large Imperial Navy fleet and six Space Marine Chapters, who drove the Orks from the world. Fewer than one in ten of the Chapter's warriors survived, and Snagrod escaped.

Aftermath

The Ork WAAAGH! seized many worlds of the sector, and the Imperium spent many years reconquering them. Imperial chronicles say that Chapter Master [[Pedro Kantor]] foresaw this, and that it would take many years of fighting to retake the worlds that Snagrod had taken. [[Badlanding]], an Imperial Agri-world, was captured by Ork Warboss Uzrog on Snagrod's orders, and it remains in Ork hands.

Geography

The sector lies on the frontier with the Segmentum Ultima. Its surviving survey records little of its layout, and much of it has not been surveyed since the Ork invasion of 989.M41.

Notable worlds

[[Rynn's World]] is an Imperial Agri-world that is also the homeworld of the Crimson Fists. It is mountainous and devoted to intensive farming. Its system, the Rynnstar System, is named for the world's first colonist. It was the scene of the sector's most famous battle, and its ruined cities are kept as memorials.

[[Badlanding]] is an Imperial Agri-world on the sector's border. It was invaded and captured by Ork Warboss Uzrog in 989.M41, and it has not been retaken.

Governance

The sector's worlds are governed by planetary governors under the Administratum, who answer to the sector's Imperial Navy command. The Crimson Fists hold their homeworld of Rynn's World under their own command.

Military

The sector is defended by the planetary defence forces of its worlds, Imperial Navy squadrons and the Space Marine Chapters that have fought there. The Crimson Fists' losses are recorded in the Chapter's own memorials, and the Officio Militaris keeps the Imperial account of the defence of Rynn's World.
