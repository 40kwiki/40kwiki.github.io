The Grendl Stars are a constellation on the far [[Eastern Fringe]] of the galaxy, within the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. The region was once known as the "Wings of Sanguinius", and it is sometimes called "the Fourteen". It is home to the violent alien race of the [[Barghesi]].

History

For three thousand standard years the [[Iron Lords]] have kept the [[Barghesi]] confined to the Grendl Stars. The Chapter also stopped the [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] from entering the region and harvesting the Barghesi's genetic potential for new [[Tyranid]] bioforms. The Barghesi are held within the Iron Lords' "Iron Grip", and the Imperium had planned a Crusade to destroy them. The birth of the [[Great Rift]] has made such a Crusade all but impossible.

Imperial beacons around the Grendl Stars warn travellers to "avoid at all costs". Pirates and fugitives still enter the region seeking sanctuary, but most die within solar months, often slain by the xenos. Each star is watched from one of the fourteen fortresses of the Iron Grip, called the "Holdfasts".
