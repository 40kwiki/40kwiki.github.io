The Valkyrie is a vertical take-off and landing assault carrier of the Imperium. It serves the [[Astra Militarum]] as a gunship and as a transport for airborne regiments such as the [[Militarum Tempestus]] Scions and the [[Elysian Drop Troops]]. A Valkyrie and its crew belong to the Aeronautica Imperialis of the [[Imperial Navy]], and the Navy lends them to the Guard as they are needed.

It is a manoeuvrable, twin-engined craft with strong armour and a flexible payload. Its sealed cockpit and promethium-fed turbojets let it fight in the upper atmosphere and on worlds with hostile air. Its transport bay holds as many guardsmen as a [[Chimera]], and the crews use it to move squads quickly, to strike key targets and to reinforce a failing line.

Role

Airborne assault

Vectored engines let a Valkyrie twist through low-level dogfights or hover while troops rappel from its hold. Every craft carries enough grav-chutes for its passengers, which allows a fast and dangerous drop. The shock lands hardest on the enemy, who find a new front opening behind their line with no warning but the roar of the aircraft overhead. Artillery and psykers working far from the front can be surrounded and brought down.

Commanders usually load Valkyries with veteran Guardsmen or Tempestus Scions, because the risk is high and so is the reward. Some have drawn rank-and-file squads from a Valkyrie drop, as Company Commander Jetta Bacchi of the Athonian 992nd, the "Vagrant Blades", did in city fights across the Imperium, seeding the field with his Tunnel Rats before the main attack.

Gunship

When troops are not carried, the Valkyrie makes hit-and-run attacks and strafing runs and fights enemy flyers. Its multi-laser and Hellstrike missiles let it stay at a landing zone and cover the drop. The multi-laser may be swapped for a lascannon. The missiles may be replaced by extra fuel tanks or multiple rocket pods. For long-term service with a regiment the Aeronautica fits extra armour, at the cost of range and ceiling.

Instead of infantry, a Valkyrie can carry Drop Sentinels, Cyclops demolition vehicles or sentry guns. The extra weight requires two external fuel tanks. It cannot carry large groups of Ogryns.

Variants

  • Vendetta, with three twin-linked lascannons for tank-hunting while keeping the door gunners. Some carry Hellfury missiles in place of two of the lascannons.
  • Vulture, a similar airframe built as a heavy gunship to support the Valkyries as their troops disembark, and to remain over the drop zone.
  • Sky Talon, stripped of its troop bay and fitted with magnetic clamps to drop light vehicles such as the Tauros or Drop Sentinels into the middle of a fight.

Users

All military aircraft of the Imperium fall under the Aeronautica Imperialis, and Valkyrie crews are experienced Aeronautica pilots. Most Guard formations have a few Valkyries attached for a campaign. They are normally repainted for the regiment, and their pilots report to its senior officer. Some regiments, such as the [[Elysian Drop Troops]], have their own Aeronautica squadrons permanently attached. The [[Inquisition]] may requisition any craft it needs, and many Inquisitors use Valkyries as personal transports and to deploy Inquisitorial Storm Troopers.

Recorded users include the 2nd Imperial Navy Tactical Wing, which fought in the defence of [[Cadia]] in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, the 184th and 71st Tactical Wings in the [[Taros Campaign]] against the [[T'au Empire]], and the 181st Elysian Drop Troop Regiment and the 5136th Wing, which provided 80 Valkyries, in the Raid on Kastorel-Novem.The records of the campaigns other than Cadia give no year in the source.
