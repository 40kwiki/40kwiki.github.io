Arhra, once called the "Father of Scorpions" and later the "Fallen Phoenix", was the first [[Phoenix Lord]] of the [[Striking Scorpions]] [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. He is the only Phoenix Lord known to have been lost entirely to the [[Asuryani]] and to their [[Aspect Shrine]]s. His murderous nature overwhelmed him, and his soul was taken by [[Chaos]], which transformed him into a destroyer of life in the manner of the [[Drukhari]].

Arhra's fall is still disputed. The craftworld accounts and the traditions of the [[Drukhari]] [[Incubi]] of [[Commorragh]] describe the same events in very different ways, and no single account can be verified.

Origins

The first Phoenix Lord was [[Asurmen]], who led the [[Asuryani]] away from the lost homeworlds of the [[Aeldari Empire]] and founded the first shrine, the Shrine of [[Asur]], on a barren moon in the [[Webway]]. Arhra was one of the first Asurya, the children of Asurmen, who were the greatest of his students. Arhra founded the Striking Scorpions Aspect Shrine, and it came to reflect its founder's dark nature. Its warriors were patient and deadly, and they stalked their prey before striking like the wrath of [[Khaine]].

The Fall

Arhra's darkness grew until he let [[Daemon]]s into the First Shrine to wage war on his fellow Phoenix Lords. Those who stayed loyal to [[Asurmen]] were defeated and scattered through the stars. Arhra fled into the Webway and became "the Fallen Phoenix who burns with the dark light of Chaos". The Asuryani hold that he then walked the [[Path of Damnation]].

The Drukhari tell a different story. In their version, Arhra opposed the other Phoenix Lords, who wanted the Aeldari to take up the warrior's role only at need and to abandon the [[Drukhari]] of Commorragh. Arhra refused this compromise, went his own way, and tested his followers for their worth. In this telling, he stood alone against Chaos to the end and was struck down by its dark light, after which his face returned to the first [[Incubi]] shrine wreathed in unholy fire.

Later reports

Many Asuryani believe that Arhra survived and became [[Drazhar]], the Master of Blades and champion of the Drukhari Incubi. Those reports are unconfirmed.The identification of Arhra with Drazhar is a matter of rumour in the sources. No record confirms it. His last known appearance is set in 928.M41, when his successor [[Karandras]] duelled him for seventeen solar days amid the ruins of [[Zandros]]. Karandras goaded Arhra into a killing frenzy. At the critical moment, the Fallen Phoenix melted into shadow and vanished, leaving his nemesis to slay his own Incubi disciples in a rage.
