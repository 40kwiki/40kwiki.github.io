Arhra, once called the "Father of Scorpions" and later the "Fallen Phoenix", was the first [[Phoenix Lord]] of the [[Striking Scorpions]] [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. He is the only Phoenix Lord known to have been lost entirely to the [[Asuryani]] and to their [[Aspect Shrine]]s. His murderous nature overwhelmed him, and his soul was taken by [[Chaos]], which transformed him into a destroyer of life in the manner of the [[Drukhari]].

Arhra's fall is still disputed. The craftworld accounts and the traditions of the [[Drukhari]] [[Incubi]] of [[Commorragh]] describe the same events in very different ways, and no single account can be verified.

Origins

The first Phoenix Lord was [[Asurmen]], who led the [[Asuryani]] away from the lost homeworlds of the [[Aeldari Empire]] and founded the first shrine, the Shrine of [[Asur]], on a barren moon in the [[Webway]]. Arhra was one of the first Asurya, the children of Asurmen, who were the greatest of his students. Arhra founded the Striking Scorpions Aspect Shrine, and it came to reflect its founder's dark nature. Its warriors were patient and deadly, and they stalked their prey before striking like the wrath of [[Khaine]].

The Fall

Arhra's darkness grew until he let [[Daemon]]s into the First Shrine to wage war on his fellow Phoenix Lords. Those who stayed loyal to [[Asurmen]] were defeated and scattered through the stars. Arhra fled into the Webway and became "the Fallen Phoenix who burns with the dark light of Chaos". It is believed that after he began to walk the [[Path of Damnation]] he fled to the [[Dark City]] of [[Commorragh]], where he is said to have become the first Hierarch of the [[Incubi]] and founded their first shrine.

The Drukhari tell a different story. In their version, Arhra opposed the other Phoenix Lords, who wanted the Aeldari to take up the warrior's role only at need and to abandon the [[Drukhari]] of Commorragh. Arhra refused this compromise, went his own way, and tested his followers for their worth. In this telling, he stood alone against the [[Daemon]]s of Chaos for solar days and nights when the other Phoenix Lords failed to aid him, and was at last pierced through the heart by its dark light. What returned to the first [[Incubi]] shrine bore his face but burned with unholy fire. Arhra's voice bade his terrified students slay his corrupted mortal form, and they then took his untouched spirit into themselves, so that his way would live on as the way of the Incubi.

Later reports

Many Asuryani believe that Arhra survived and became [[Drazhar]], the Master of Blades and greatest champion of the Drukhari Incubi. Those reports are unconfirmed.The identification of Arhra with Drazhar is a matter of rumour in the sources. No record confirms it. Arhra is suspected to have been seen last in 928.M41, when Drazhar and [[Karandras]], the greatest student of Arhra and later master of the Striking Scorpions, duelled for seventeen solar days amid the shattered ruins of [[Zandros]]. Outnumbered and unable to win by strength, Karandras goaded his former master into a blind killing frenzy. At the critical moment, the Shadow Hunter melted into a patch of darkness and vanished, leaving his nemesis to slay every one of his own Incubi disciples in a murderous rage.
