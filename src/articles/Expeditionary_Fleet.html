An Expeditionary Fleet was a numbered battle fleet of the [[Great Crusade]]. Each fleet was a force that could cross the galaxy and bring worlds into [[Compliance]]. The War Council gave each fleet a number, and that number was used to track its conquests. The world that a fleet found first was given the fleet's number and a count, so the [[63rd Expeditionary Fleet]] named its first discovery 63-19, the nineteenth world found by that fleet.

At the peak of the Great Crusade, in the early 31st millennium, there were 4,287 primary fleets in the field. Another 372 fleets were regrouping, refitting or resupplying in the [[Sol System]] or other hub systems. Their secondary deployment groups numbered more than 60,000. Imperial accounts give these figures about 203 standard years after the Crusade began.

Origins

The first fleet was the [[Principia Imperialis]], which the [[Emperor of Mankind]] commanded himself. As the Crusade spread, more fleets were launched and trusted generals were given command of them. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] assembled the original fleets in Terran space. The fleets were then dispersed across the Milky Way to reunite the human colony worlds.

After several [[Primarch]]s were found, the fleets were divided into three main thrusts. One was led by [[Horus]], one by [[Ferrus Manus]], and one by the Emperor. The rediscovery of the Primarchs is recorded in the [[Great Crusade]] article.

Composition

The make-up of each fleet was never fixed. Imperial Army regiments, [[Titan Legions]] and [[Space Marine Legions]] could join or leave as strategy required. The numbers stayed the same. Each fleet was a major armada, usually under a Lord Commander of the Imperial Army. Its naval elements were commanded by a Master of the Fleet. Each Primarch also commanded a larger fleet, with the main body of his Legion attached.

Most fleets had no Space Marines at all. The largest fleets with a Legion attached made the great thrusts into new space. Smaller fleets followed them and secured worlds that offered less resistance. Some Primarchs split off companies of Space Marines to support smaller fleets. In those cases the senior Astartes officer commanded the fleet, since Astartes outranked mortal troops in any action where they were present.

Every fleet also carried [[Iterators]], who spread the Imperial Truth to the worlds it conquered, and [[Remembrancers]], who recorded its actions.

Legacy

The numbering system let Imperial administrators follow a fleet's path through the galaxy by the order in which it found worlds. Many of these records are lost, and some fleets are known only from a few surviving entries. Imperial scholars disagree on how many fleets were lost in the Heresy and how many were simply renamed.
