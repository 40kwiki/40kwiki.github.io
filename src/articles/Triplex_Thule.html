Triplex Thule was the smallest of the three [[Forge world|Forge Worlds]] of the [[Triplex System]] in the [[Triplex Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Its tech-priests belonged to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and it was the home of the Titan Legion known as the Foe Breakers, the [[Legio Victorum]] III. The world was ruined during the [[Horus Heresy]] in a war with its sibling, [[Triplex Phall]]. It is now a dead world.

History

Foundation

Triplex Thule was founded by tech-magi who had been cast out of [[Triplex Galatia]] in its early years. They followed an extreme branch of the orthodoxy of [[Mars]], which condemned even the primitive servo-automata still made on the scattered worlds of the Mechanicum. They prized the purity of mechanical devices, and their subtle blending of man and machine, in the form of [[Servitor]]s and MIU links, set them at odds with the rulers of [[Triplex Galatia]], who followed an increasingly deviant path in their research and lust for power.

The world grew slowly. Shunned and starved of ore and of new subjects for its labours, it had reached only the lowest grade of Primaris by the time the Imperium arrived in the sector.

The hidden war

Triplex Thule struggled for every shipment against its two siblings. For long ages it fought a hidden war with the other Forge Worlds of the system, using a small Taghmata of heavily modified servitors and the agents of the Malagra sect, not the Titans of the Foe Breakers. The conflict sharpened after the Emperor took the warriors of Triplex Phall into the [[Great Crusade]] and gave them the wealth of the wider Imperium. Phall kept that wealth out of reach of Thule, a reminder of the power it held and a promise of what Thule might share should it bow to Phall, as Galatia had done.

The rulers of Thule refused. When emissaries of the Warmaster [[Horus]] came in secret to offer his favour for their loyalty, the archmagi turned them away as servants of Phall and its "Hereteks". The hidden war became more savage, and bombs and data-phage infections struck outposts of both worlds and Thule itself.

The Horus Heresy

The end began when three Malagra adepts killed a Phallian archmagos in retaliation. In the opening months of the Heresy, open war broke out in the Triplex System. [[Triplex Phall]] took up arms against its smallest sibling, and [[Legio Victorum]] I and III met in battle.No record gives a date for the war. It is placed in the opening months of the Heresy by the account's own wording, c. 005–006.M31, as an estimate. The war was fought without mercy. By the time the [[Night Lords]] came to the system in the [[Thramas Crusade]] (007–009.M31), little remained of Thule but ruins.

Legacy

Thule shared the fate of Triplex Galatia, which was also destroyed in the campaign. The three worlds had supplied most of the Loyalist warship capacity of the Ultima Segmentum, and only Triplex Phall survived to serve the Imperium in later ages.
