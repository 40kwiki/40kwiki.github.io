Tobris Ekharth was the [[Master of the Administratum]] during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). He held that the proposed campaign against the Ork Beast violated the [[Codex Astartes]], since it would send several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] under a single command. He was allied with [[Udin Macht Udo]] and the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]].

Ekharth's opposition is recorded as one of the reasons the council was slow to act against the [[Orks]]. His views are preserved in the minutes of the council, which the [[Administratum]] keeps.

War of the Beast

The proposal was to send several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] under one command to hunt the [[Orks]]' strategic assets and, eventually, the Beast itself. Ekharth held that this breached the [[Codex Astartes]]. [[Erekart Mesring]], the [[Ecclesiarch]], called it blasphemy. A first vote went against the proposal, with [[Drakan Vangorich]] alone in favour. The dormant moon above the Ork world was then found to be a threat, and the council's position changed.

Legacy

Ekharth is recorded in the [[Administratum]]'s histories as a defender of the [[Codex Astartes]] at a time when its authority was tested. The Administratum's own doctrine has since treated the Codex as the basis for the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]' command.
