The Ghoul Stars are a remote region of space at the far north-east of the galaxy, beyond the [[Eastern Fringe]] and the northern edge of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. They form part of the outer [[Halo Stars]]. The region was once home to many human worlds, but most of them are now dead, and their suns are dying. Imperial records blame an ancient threat for the ruin, and the region's name is taken from the ghoulish things that are said to walk among its dead worlds.

Almost the whole region lies beyond the light of the [[Astronomican]]. Without the psychic beacon, [[Navigator]]s cannot find [[Warp|the warp]] routes, and most Navigators refuse to take a ship into the Ghoul Stars for that reason. Those who enter must navigate blind, and few return.

Environment and inhabitants

The worlds of the Ghoul Stars are lit by cold rays from dying suns. Their native fauna include the bat-like humanoid [[xenos]] known as the [[Cythor Fiends]] and the [[Togoran Bloodreeks]]. Imperial reports describe other creatures so alien that they are thought to be drawn from the nightmares of mankind. Some of these are said to live in the dead cities of the region, and the Imperium's accounts of them are sparse.

The most feared of the region's inhabitants are the [[Necrons]] of the [[Flayed Ones]]. Their home is the [[Bone Kingdom of Drazak]], a [[Dead World]] where the Flayed Ones, afflicted by a hunger for living flesh, fight over scraps of rotting meat and shards of bone. They are ruled by the [[Necron Lord]] [[Valgûl]], the Fallen Lord, who is the only one of his kind to have kept his reason in the Flayer plague. He maintains the Flayed Ones' loyalty by declaring periodic "Times of Bounty", when the Necron fleets of Drazak raid nearby worlds for blood and flesh.

History

Early Imperial contact

Human worlds in the Ghoul Stars were settled in the Imperial era and remained a frontier for millennia. The [[Imperial Navy]] never held more than a light footprint there, and the region was largely lost during the [[Horus Heresy]], when the Imperium's attention was on [[Terra]].No surviving Great Crusade chart details the Ghoul Stars' settlement, so the dates of their early colonies are not known.

In 002.M31 elements of the [[Raven Guard]] were sent on a crusade into the Ghoul Stars. Some of them turned renegade and founded the human realm of the [[Ashen Claws]], which still holds worlds in the region.

Nova Terra Interregnum

Main article: [[Nova Terra Interregnum]]

During the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], when the Imperium split into warring factions in the [[34th millennium]], scattered and veiled references were made to a great threat that rose from the Ghoul Stars. The threat was called the [[Pale Wasting]]. Imperial accounts disagree on its nature. Some call it a "star-spawned plague" that swept away scores of worlds. Others describe "nightmare engines" that slaughtered the worlds of whole sectors. Much of the evidence has been censored or destroyed, and the sealed record is incomplete.

The [[Novamarines]] took part in the campaign against the Pale Wasting, and it is one of the Chapter's earliest recorded battle honours. The Chapter's own chronicles, however, say nothing about it.

Covenant of Ecale

In 390.M38 the [[Marines Errant]] entered into the Covenant of Ecale with the [[Rogue Trader]] Sia'hadn Ecale of House Ecale. The Chapter added its strength to the Rogue Trader's army, and the two made a voyage of war and exploration into the Ghoul Stars that lasted until 433.M38. The exact terms of the pact remain secret.

The Tyranid attack on Orask

In 977.M41 a splinter of the eastern [[Hive Fleet]]s of the [[Tyranids]], disoriented and twisted by its passage through the Ghoul Stars, fell on the [[Sentinel World]] of [[Orask]] near the region's border. The defence was held by the Orask garrison, backed by a company of the [[Red Talons]] and a [[Titan]] maniple of the [[Legio Magna]]. Tens of thousands died in a few solar days, but the attackers were pushed back and purged from the surface. Some [[Genestealers]] escaped into the outer systems.

Ghoul Stars Crusade

Main article: [[Helbrecht]]

In 990.M41 [[High Marshal]] [[Helbrecht]] of the [[Black Templars]] proclaimed a Crusade against the Cythor Fiends. Within eight standard years the xenos of the outlying systems had been exterminated, and the Crusade pushed on to the Cythor homeworld, the gas giant [[9836-18 Grave Core]]. Its core systems were found empty of any trace of the Cythor. Before the mystery could be solved, the Crusade was called to Armageddon in 998.M41, when [[Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka]] returned during the [[Third War for Armageddon]].Imperial sources differ on the date of the Crusade's proclamation. Some Black Templars records give 989.M41.

In the [[42nd millennium]] the Black Templars' Oparian Crusade is recorded as having disappeared into the Ghoul Stars. No later Imperial expedition has found it.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The Ghoul Stars were largely cut off from the Imperium by the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41. The [[Death Spectres]] keep a constant vigil on the region's borders, guarding against the threats that might spread out of it. No Ultima Founding Chapter is recorded as having been given duties in the Ghoul Stars, and the Imperium's records of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] make no mention of a campaign there.The Ghoul Stars and the [[Ultima Founding]] are not linked in any consulted Imperial source. Readers who want the later Ultima Founding campaigns should consult that article.

Geography

The Ghoul Stars are a broad, dark tract of worlds around dying suns, separated by long stretches of [[Wilderness space]]. Orask, the [[Sentinel World]] on the region's edge, lies near the Iquathan Deeps of the northern [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]]. The Imperial charts give no detailed map of the interior.
