A Farseer is the most potent and respected form of [[Craftworld]] [[Aeldari]] [[Psyker|psyker]], or Seer. A Farseer was once a Warlock, but has become lost upon the Path of the Seer for ever, in the same way that Exarchs are wed to the Path of the Warrior. Imperial records describe them as the strongest Aeldari minds in the Warp.

Farseers guide their craftworld by reading the future, and they are consulted on every major decision of their people. The most powerful of them sit on a council that governs the craftworld and its relations with other Aeldari. The council is often shown in Imperial records with [[Eldrad Ulthran]] as its most famous member.
