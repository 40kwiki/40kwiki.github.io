Ahzek Ahriman is a [[Chaos]] Sorcerer of the [[Thousand Sons]] [[Traitor Legion]], held to be the most powerful in that Legion after its [[Daemon|daemon primarch]], [[Magnus the Red]]. A Terran by birth, he was [[Chief Librarian]] of the Legion during the [[Great Crusade]], captain of its 1st Fellowship, and a leader of the Corvidae, the cult of seers. He was Magnus's closest adviser at the time of the [[Burning of Prospero]].

In exile on the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] Ahriman cast the Rubric of Ahriman, a great spell meant to end the [[Mutant|mutation]] that afflicted the Legion. It reduced almost every warrior without strong psychic gifts to dust sealed in armour, the Rubric Marines. Magnus banished him, and for ten thousand years he wandered [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] seeking the knowledge to undo his work, above all the hidden [[Black Library]] of the [[Aeldari]].

Before the 13th Black Crusade of 998.M41 Magnus recalled him for the assault on [[Fenris]], and after the fall of [[Cadia]] Ahriman saw proof, in the power of the Aeldari god Ynnead, that the Rubric could be reversed. In the [[Era Indomitus]] he is again rising in the Legion's favour.

Early life

Ahriman was born on [[Terra]] into the techno-barbarian Achaemenid peoples, whose kings allied with the Emperor in the [[Unification Wars]] and were spared much of their destruction. He and his twin brother Ohrmuzd were chosen for the [[Space Marine Legions|Legions]] and fought in the first years of the Crusade with the [[Thousand Sons|XV Legion]].

Within five years the Legion's psychic gifts began to emerge, and with them the flesh-change, a [[Mutant|mutation]] that turned many legionaries into monsters who had to be killed by their brothers. Ohrmuzd was among them. Ahriman wore a pendant of his brother's, a gift from their mother, on his shoulder guard, and the fear of mutation stayed with him for the rest of his life. The finding of [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] on [[Prospero]] halted the flesh-change, and the Legion took the name Thousand Sons.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

Ahriman rose to be [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]] of the [[Thousand Sons]], captain of its 1st Fellowship and leader of the Scarab Occult, the Legion's elite veterans in Terminator armour. He was one of the leaders of the Corvidae, the cult of seers who read the paths of the future. He served five years with the [[Word Bearers]] and befriended their Chaplain [[Erebus]]. He trained the [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancer]] Lemuel Gaumon in psychic arts at [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]]'s direction.

At Heliosa Ahriman saw the flesh-change return in the Legion, though Magnus had promised that it was banished for ever, and he felt betrayed. At the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 the Emperor banned sorcery in the Legions, and the Thousand Sons felt themselves on trial. There Ahriman learned that Magnus had very likely struck a bargain with the [[Chaos|powers of the warp]] to end the mutations, and his trust in his [[Primarch|primarch]] was shaken. Magnus removed the details of the bargain from his Chief Librarian's mind.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Prospero

Main article: [[Burning of Prospero]]

[[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] foresaw the fall of [[Horus]] and, in breaking the Decree to warn the Emperor, destroyed the wards around the Emperor's [[Webway|webway]] project. The Emperor sent [[Leman Russ]] to bring Magnus to Terra; Horus persuaded Russ to destroy Prospero instead. Magnus hid the planet's fate from his own seers and let the attack come. Ahriman fought in the defence of Tizca against the [[Space Wolves]], the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]]. At the end Magnus took the survivors through [[Warp|the warp]] to the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] in the [[Eye of Terror]].

Siege of Terra

Main article: [[Siege of Terra]]

Ahriman came to the [[Siege of Terra]] with the [[Thousand Sons]]. His forces took part in the first assaults on the [[Sol System]], and their [[Daemon|daemons]] were defeated at the Colossi Gate by the [[White Scars]] stormseers. During the Siege he went to the Palace archives and spoke there with the [[Iterators|iterator]] Kyril Sindermann.

The Rubric

On the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] the flesh-change returned. Ahriman distilled the knowledge in the Book of [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] into a spell to purge the [[Mutant|mutation]] and gathered a cabal of the Legion's strongest sorcerers to cast it, among them his former mentor Amon. The Rubric worked. Every legionary with weak or no psychic power was reduced to dust within his armour, his spirit trapped inside; the sorcerers who survived were made stronger. The Legion was left with fewer than a hundred sorcerers set over thousands of automaton warriors.

Magnus came to destroy Ahriman for what he had done to his sons. [[Thousand Sons]] accounts say [[Tzeentch]] stayed his hand, and Magnus instead banished Ahriman and his cabal from the Planet of the Sorcerers.

Exile

Ahriman spent the next ten thousand years in exile, at times hidden in other warbands. Amon, who had also been banished, founded the Brotherhood of Dust, a warband of exiled sorcerers and [[Thousand Sons|Rubric Marines]], and spent a thousand years hunting for the one man who knew enough of the Rubric to undo it. Ahriman had hidden among a Khornate warband, The Harrowing. When Amon found him at last, Ahriman destroyed him by releasing the energy of the Rubric bound in his flesh, and took his armour and his followers.

The Harvest of Calliope

Calliope was an archive world. Ahriman is held to have planted among its indexers the seed of the Summation, a formula that unified every index of the archive, and to have harvested its fruit millennia later. When an Imperial ship arrived a solar decade afterwards, it found the archives blank and a few starved survivors who could remember nothing for more than a few seconds.

The Black Library

Main article: [[Harlequins]]

For many centuries Ahriman sought the [[Black Library]], the [[Aeldari]] repository of knowledge of Chaos and sorcery hidden in the [[Webway]]. On his last attempt he passed its Harlequin guardians by projecting himself into its halls, while his body transcribed what he saw, and so copied the Tome Labyrinthus, the Harlequins' map of the webway. It is rumoured that if he ever gained full entry to the library he could become a new power of the warp.

Late 41st millennium

Before the Thirteenth [[Black Crusade]] of 998.M41, [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] summoned Ahriman to the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] for the first time since the Rubric. In the Siege of the [[Fenris]] System, in 999.M41, Ahriman brought back many exiled [[Thousand Sons]] through [[Webway|the webway]] to fall on the [[Space Wolves]]. With Magnus he used the nine Silver Towers of [[Tzeentch]] to draw power from Fenris, a psychic harvest that ended with the destruction of the planet Midgardia. It gave Magnus the power to return the Planet of the Sorcerers to realspace above the husk of Prospero.

During the Thirteenth Black Crusade, in 999.M41, Ahriman breached the webway twice. The first time he used knowledge taken from the captured Inquisitor Bronislaw Czevak. The second time he entered through a long-forgotten portal on Etiamnum III and was repulsed by the Asuryani under Eldrad Ulthran. Soon afterwards he found a webway portal to Arcadia, a hidden [[Harlequins|Harlequin]] world with a great librarium, but he was ejected from the webway without his goal.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After the fall of [[Cadia]] Ahriman's agents in [[Webway|the webway]] brought him word of the Ynnari, [[Aeldari]] who serve the god of the dead, Ynnead. He ambushed them in the webway and took their leaders into a pocket reality. The priestess [[Yvraine]] restored a dozen [[Thousand Sons|Rubric Marines]] to life. When the Aeldari escaped, the restored warriors were lost into a chasm, but Ahriman now knew that the Rubric could be undone and who held the power to do it.

Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] he has gathered his forces and turned his foresight on the [[Drukhari]] city of [[Commorragh]], where he now knows the knowledge he seeks can be found. His star has risen again with [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] because of his battles in the webway and his capture of Czevak.

Character and abilities

Ahriman is a [[Psyker|psyker]] of the highest grade, skilled in telekinesis, divination and the binding of [[Daemon|daemons]]. He has sought the keys to save what he destroyed by his own hubris. To the Craftworld Aeldari he is the carrion scribe who feeds on the souls of their dying race; to the [[Ordo Malleus]] he is a lightning rod for daemonic incursions.

Wargear

  • The Black Staff of Ahriman, a force weapon that focuses his power; its blade is a remnant of the Spear of Shadows.
  • An enchanted inferno bolt pistol.
