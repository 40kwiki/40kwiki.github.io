The Mortifactors are a loyal [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. They are a [[Second Founding]] Successor of the [[Ultramarines]], one of the Primogenitor Chapters. They recruit from the austere [[feral world]] of [[Posul]], where the sun never rises over frigid plains and nomadic tribes are constantly at war with one another. Their [[neophyte]]s are chosen from the most vicious and skilled warriors of the planet, who grow up with a dark view of life.

The Mortifactors are a grim and brooding Chapter. They venerate their honoured dead to the point of obsession. Their open celebration of death makes some of their Imperial allies uneasy, especially the superstitious. Their fearsome appearance is a weapon in its own right. They have been seen in skeletal war paint and hooded robes, and they carry [[Power Scythe]]s inlaid with bone.

History

The Mortifactors were created during the Second Founding in the 31st millennium. Their first [[Chapter Master]], Sasebo Tezuka, was a [[Captain]] of the Ultramarines. He led the new Chapter to the dark and frigid world of [[Posul]], which the Imperium had granted to it. Tezuka used the [[Emperor's Tarot]] for guidance. He established the Chapter's [[fortress-monastery]] in orbit over the planet and earned many honours before his death.

Posul was later destroyed by the [[Tyranids]]. The Chapter's fortress-monastery is now the ''[[Basilica Mortis]]'', a space station. The date of Posul's destruction is not recorded in the sources.
