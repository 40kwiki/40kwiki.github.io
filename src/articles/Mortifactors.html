The Mortifactors are a loyal [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]]. They are a [[Second Founding]] Successor of the [[Ultramarines]], one of the Primogenitor Chapters. They recruit from the austere [[feral world]] of [[Posul]], where the sun never rises over frigid plains and nomadic tribes are constantly at war with one another and practise cannibalism. Their [[neophyte]]s are chosen from the most vicious and skilled warriors of the planet, who grow up with a dark view of life.

The Mortifactors are a grim and brooding Chapter. They venerate their honoured dead to the point of obsession. Their open celebration of death makes some of their Imperial allies uneasy, especially the superstitious. Their fearsome appearance is a weapon in its own right. They have been seen in skeletal war paint and hooded robes, and they carry [[Power Scythe]]s inlaid with bone.

Posul

Posul is a bleak [[feral world]] lying so far from its star that it is wrapped in permanent darkness, and is counted among the [[Night World]]s. Its surface is craggy, frigid and mountainous. Its barbaric tribes know neither sunlight nor joy and live in constant bloodshed marked by cannibal rites. A Posulan youth may have taken hundreds of skulls before he is chosen as a neophyte. The tribes worship the [[Emperor]] as a great spirit called the "Ultimate Warrior".

The Chapter's [[Chaplain]]s do the recruiting, and many were once the shamans or chieftains of the tribes. Neophytes who fail the trials are disposed of. Each must kill his first prey and take its skull to earn a name in the Chapter, and those who have not yet killed are called the "Faceless".

Beliefs and doctrine

The Mortifactors honour the [[Codex Astartes]] as the foundation of their way of life, but they hold that following it to the letter, without regard for what they have learned and observed, is blind orthodoxy. They think rigid adherence to any dogma the sign of a weak mind, and they follow the counsel of their Chaplains. They still venerate their primarch and swear their oaths to him and to the Emperor.

On the eve of battle they seek a trance akin to death, in which they believe they commune with the primarch and their ancestors. Chaplains teach that death unites a warrior with the Emperor as the Ultimate Warrior, and that the fallen sit at his feast table in the Halls of the Victors. After battle they have been known to drink the blood and eat the flesh of the enemy, and to sever the head and flense the skull. To outsiders the Chapter seems to verge on heresy and has brought unwelcome [[Inquisition|Inquisitorial]] scrutiny, but their deep honour for the fallen of the Imperium has generally eased suspicion.

Fortress-monastery

The ''Basilica Mortis'' is a vast space station in orbit of Posul, like a mountain range adrift in the void. It houses the Chapter's thousand battle-brothers and some seven and a half thousand serfs and servitors when it is full. Its halls are lined with the bleached skulls of the Chapter's dead, and seated along its docking tunnel are the preserved bodies of fallen battle-brothers. The main chapel is filled with the skulls of the Chapter's enemies.

History

The Mortifactors were created during the Second Founding in the 31st millennium. Their first [[Chapter Master]], Sasebo Tezuka, was a [[Captain]] of the Ultramarines. He led the new Chapter to the dark and frigid world of [[Posul]], which the Imperium had granted to it. Tezuka used the [[Emperor's Tarot]] for guidance. He established the Chapter's [[fortress-monastery]] in orbit over the planet and earned many honours before his death.

The surface and biosphere of Posul were later destroyed by [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] during the [[Third Tyrannic War]]. The Chapter's fortress-monastery, the ''[[Basilica Mortis]]'', is a space station.

Notable campaigns

In the [[Ninth Black Crusade]] (537.M38) the Mortifactors and the [[Lamenters]] answered the call of the hive world of Corillia. The Mortifactors considered the Lamenters cursed and would not fight beside them, leaving them to hold Corillia alone for six standard weeks until the [[White Scars]] and [[Ultramarines]] broke through the Chaos fleet.

In the [[Achilus Crusade]], from 777.M41, the Mortifactors served three times in the [[Jericho Reach]], in the ash-choked forests of Ravacene on the [[Greyhell Front]]. They smeared themselves with volcanic ash to hunt unseen. They were enraged to find that the [[Kroot]] eat the flesh of the fallen, which they saw as an insult to their own rites of respect for the dead. Several Mortifactors have learned the ways of the Kroot and serve in the [[Deathwatch]], and keep a trace of the ash on their faces. In 819.M41 a perceived slight brought the Mortifactors and the [[Crimson Fists]] to blows, and only [[Marneus Calgar]] prevented open war. The matter was kept from the [[Adeptus Terra]]. At an unrecorded date the Chapter joined the [[Raven Guard]] against daemons and Blood Cults of [[Khorne]] on Rhorsch.

In 997.M41 the Mortifactors sent a company under [[Chaplain]] Astador in the strike cruiser ''Mortis Probati'' to help the Ultramarines' 4th Company, under [[Uriel Ventris]], and the Deathwatch defend [[Tarsis Ultra]] against a splinter of Hive Fleet Leviathan. They proved worthy but reckless. In 998.M41, on the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension, the whole Chapter entered a mass meditation. They awoke with a shared vision that the [[Third War for Armageddon]] would be the "Last Battle", and recruited every tribesman of Posul who could be a Mortifactor. All ten companies sailed for [[Armageddon]], leaving the fortress-monastery to their serfs. Afterwards the Chapter rebuilt its losses.

In 999.M41 the Mortifactors fought in the Chaeros taskforce of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], and sent a contingent to join [[Roboute Guilliman]] on the [[Terran Crusade]]. Later, during the [[War of Beasts]], they sent four companies to defend [[Vigilus]]. In the [[Plague Wars]], about 012.M42, they contributed three companies to the "Spear of Espandor" campaign that retook the [[Espandor]] system.

Notable members

The Chapter's first Chapter Master, Sasebo Tezuka, is dead. The last recorded Chapter Master, Lord Magyar, was a very old warrior in armour of bleached bone who wielded a great [[Power Scythe]]. He was beheaded by an Exarch of the Aeldari craftworld [[Biel-Tan]], when its Swordwind opened a [[Webway]] gate inside the ''Basilica Mortis''. Most of the Chapter's Librarians perished in the psychic onslaught that accompanied the attack, and Chief Librarian Zdeno alone endured it. [[Captain]] [[Artemis]] was seconded to the Deathwatch, where his gift for sensing alien influence suits him to the hunting of xenos.
