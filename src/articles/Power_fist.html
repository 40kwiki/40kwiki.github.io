A power fist, also called a power glove, is a large armoured gauntlet. It projects an [[energy field]] that breaks up any solid matter it touches. It is a favoured close combat weapon of the [[Imperium]], of the [[Forces of Chaos]] and above all of the [[Space Marines]].

Use

A power fist is slow and unwieldy, so its bearer must accept the first blow before he can strike back. It greatly increases the wearer's strength, in the same way as [[power armour]]. With the disruptive field, a Space Marine can tear through the armour of a vehicle and kill a monstrous creature in close combat.

It is most often fitted to [[Tactical Dreadnought Armour|Tactical Dreadnought]] (Terminator) armour. Its weight and bulk make it hard to use outside heavy armour.

Other users

Some [[Sisters of Battle]] carry power fists, as they also wear power armour. Senior officers and [[Lord Commissar]]s of the [[Astra Militarum]] may use smaller versions, although these need a bulky exoskeletal frame and are rare. [[Heretic Astartes]] warriors also use power fists.

The [[Orks]] have a cruder version, the [[Power Klaw]]. [[Commissar Yarrick]] took an Ork Power Klaw from a defeated [[Warboss]] and kept it as his own weapon. It has become a symbol of his record in the long war against the Orks.
