The Ordo Xenos, sometimes called the "Alien Hunters", is one of the three [[Ordos Majoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. Its purpose is to protect the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from threats caused by intelligent alien species, or by their influence on Humanity. It works alongside the [[Ordo Malleus]], which hunts daemons, and the [[Ordo Hereticus]], which hunts heresy and witchcraft.

The Ordo is served by its Chamber Militant, the [[Deathwatch]]. The Deathwatch draws Veteran [[Astartes]] from [[Chapters]] across the Imperium to form kill-teams, which are sent against xenos threats to the survival of Mankind. The Ordo's [[Inquisitors]] are held to be extremely knowledgeable about their enemies, and they are known for their hostility to non-Human species.

History

Origins of the Inquisition

The Inquisition itself is far older than the Ordo Xenos. It was founded in the early 31st millennium on the Emperor's order, at the start of the [[Horus Heresy]]. The first members were a small group of Imperial agents drawn from the adepta and the nobility, together with loyal Space Marines from both sides of the Heresy. [[Malcador the Sigillite]] chose them at the end of the Heresy, and gave them the task of finding every threat to the Imperium, from without and within.

Creation of the Ordo

The Ordo Xenos was created in the mid-32nd millennium. Its inception is traced to the [[Time of Rebirth]] and to the arrival in Imperial space of the greatest [[Ork]] [[WAAAGH!]] since the [[Ullanor Crusade]]. This invasion was led by a warlord known only as "The Beast", and it threatened [[Terra]] itself. It is remembered as the [[War of the Beast]], and it is dated 544.M32.

During the war, the newly elected [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and [[Chapter Master]] [[Koorland]] devised the concept of small elite Astartes kill-teams for use against xenos threats. That concept became the basis of the Deathwatch, and the Ordo Xenos was formed to direct it. The exact origins of the Deathwatch are not fully recorded, and the Imperial accounts differ on details.

Role

The primary remit of the Ordo Xenos is to defend the Imperium against the alien. This duty goes beyond confronting non-Human creatures in open war alongside the [[Astra Militarum]] or the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. The Ordo must investigate and study intelligent alien species, learn their weaknesses and the nature of their strengths, and destroy any species judged to be a threat to Humanity. Its agents must also be watchful against the smuggling of xenos artefacts and the spread of alien influence within the Imperium.

The Ordo's work is directed against xenos species such as the [[Orks]] and the [[Tyranids]], and against human agents who serve alien interests. Its Inquisitors investigate the traces that alien species leave in the Imperium, and they pursue alien influence through trade, cults and corrupt officials. Where an alien threat cannot be handled by the Imperial armies, the Ordo calls on the Deathwatch.

Imperial records describe the Ordo's methods as the best human and alien technology available, applied with a consistent and deep hostility to non-Human species.

Notable members

The Ordo's records name [[Gregor Eisenhorn]] and [[Solomon Lok]] among its Inquisitors. Lok was reported slain on [[Beta Anphelion IV]] in 850.M41, though his fate is disputed in later accounts. Other Inquisitors of the Ordo appear throughout the rolls.
