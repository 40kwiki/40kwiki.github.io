The Ordo Xenos, called the Alien Hunters in common speech, is one of the three [[Ordos Majoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. Its purpose is to protect the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from every internal and external threat that springs from intelligent alien species, or from their influence on Humanity. It works beside the [[Ordo Malleus]], which hunts [[Daemon|daemons]], and the [[Ordo Hereticus]], which hunts heresy and witchcraft.

The Ordo is served by its [[Chamber Militant]], the [[Deathwatch]], an unusual [[Space Marine Chapter]] that draws veteran [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] from the Chapters of the whole Imperium for fixed terms of service and forms them into [[Kill-team|kill-teams]]. Its Inquisitors are expected to know their enemies in great depth, and they are known for the hatred of non-human life that colours so much of Imperial thought. They are also, in their studies and their methods, the Holy Ordos most familiar with the alien.

The Ordo took formal shape in the mid-32nd millennium, after the [[War of the Beast]] of 544.M32, though it grew out of a long-standing group of Inquisitors who studied the alien. Lord Commander [[Koorland]] proposed the kill-teams that became the Deathwatch, and the Ordo was set up to direct them. Since then its Inquisitors have taken part in nearly every major war against the xenos, from the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] to the flight before [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]].

Not all of its members hold the same view. Most believe that the alien must be driven to extinction, while a few hold that a species that does the Imperium no harm, or that shares a common enemy, may be used. These opinions are held to be [[Radical]] within the Ordo, and their adherents can be shunned or even hunted down by their brethren.

History

Origins of the Inquisition

Main article: [[Inquisition]]

The Inquisition itself is far older than the Ordo Xenos. It was set up in the early 31st millennium at the Emperor's express order, after the betrayal of the [[Horus Heresy]] had begun. It began as a small and secret body of skilled agents from the Imperial adepta and the [[Highborn|nobility]], with select loyal [[Space Marine Legions|Legionaries]] from both loyalist and traitor Legions. [[Malcador the Sigillite]] chose them, and the task given them was to uncover and destroy every threat to the Imperium from within and without, with a particular emphasis on the servants of [[Chaos]].

The Ordo Xenos had no separate existence in those years. The Inquisitors whose interest lay in the alien species of the galaxy worked as part of the whole, and for more than a thousand years the study of the xenos remained one concern among many. It is said to have grown, before it was recognised, out of that group of Inquisitors who were chiefly interested in studying the alien and in removing the threat that xenos species posed.

The War of the Beast and the Deathwatch

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

The Ordo's formal creation is traced to the [[Time of Rebirth]] and to 544.M32, when the greatest [[WAAAGH!]] of the [[Orks]] since the [[Ullanor Crusade]] of the Great Crusade fell upon Imperial space. Its warlord, known only as the [[The Beast|Beast]], threatened [[Terra]] itself. During that war the newly elected [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], [[Koorland]], the [[Imperial Fists]] Chapter Master and the sole survivor of his Chapter, devised the use of small, elite kill-teams of Astartes to destroy the Orks' key strategic assets and, in the end, the Beast.

The [[High Lords of Terra]] were reluctant to approve the plan, since it would only increase the power of the Space Marines in the Imperium, but desperate times prevailed. The first recruits came from the survivors of the Chapters that had assaulted the Beast's capital, [[Ullanor Prime]], and had been bled almost white. They stood watch over their fallen brothers, and from that vigil the force took its name. Koorland reached an agreement with the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] [[Wienand]] to reassure the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] that the Deathwatch would fall under the Inquisition, while an Astartes would hold the office of Chapter Master.

It was after this war that the Inquisition was formally divided into its first two Ordos Majoris, the Ordo Malleus and the Ordo Xenos. The Deathwatch became the Chamber Militant of the new Ordo, as the [[Grey Knights]] were of the Ordo Malleus. The exact origins of the Deathwatch are not fully recorded, and Imperial accounts differ on details.

The wars of the 41st millennium

The Ordo has been drawn into almost every great alien war of recent millennia. It was slow to act when the [[T'au Empire]] made first contact with Imperial governors on the [[Eastern Fringe]], and the aliens' technology passed along the trade routes as far as Terra before it was checked. Later the Ordo acted with force. In 742.M41 an Ordo-led fleet of the [[Navis Imperialis]] spearheaded the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] deep into T'au space, and its advance stalled before [[Dal'yth]], one of the T'au Sept Worlds. The arrival of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] in 745.M41 forced the crusade to break off the siege and withdraw.

The most famous act of an Ordo Xenos Inquisitor is the work of Lord Inquisitor [[Kryptman]], who first identified the [[Genestealer]] infestation that heralded [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] in the late 41st millennium. He authorised what is held to be the greatest single act of genocide ever inflicted by the Imperium on itself, the abandonment or destruction of every world in the hive fleet's path. For this he was issued a ''Carta Extremis'' and stripped of his office, yet he went on to lead loyal Deathwatch to capture live Genestealers and used them as bait to lure the hive fleet into the Ork empire of Octavius, delaying its march on Terra.

Role

Duty and remit

The Ordo Xenos's task is to defend the Imperium against the alien, which goes far beyond fighting non-human enemies in open war alongside the [[Astra Militarum]] or the Astartes. Its Inquisitors must study intelligent alien species, learn their weaknesses and the nature of their strengths, and destroy any that is judged a threat to Humanity. They must be watchful too against the dangers nearer home: renegade humans, some corrupt and others merely opportunistic, who trade in alien technology or hide alien species within the Imperium for experiment, profit or sport. This commerce is known as the [[Cold Trade]].

A great part of the Ordo's work faces inward. Its records are filled with atrocities and losses of life that followed where the alien took hold of an Imperial world by stealth, infiltration or slow taint, and with disasters caused by misunderstood artefacts and xeno-lore. The [[Calixis Sector]], on the border of the Imperium and flanked by uncharted space, is a particularly hard post. [[Rogue Trader|Rogue Traders]], [[Explorator]] fleets, merchants, smugglers and pirates cross its edge continually, and any of them may carry alien influence into the heart of the Imperium.

Methods

An Inquisitor's methods depend on the threat. Where the taint is subtle, the Ordo works by guile and stealth, using its power like a scalpel to cut out a malignancy. Where the alien menace is great, an Inquisitor may command whole regiments of the Astra Militarum and the [[Imperial Navy]] and may call on the Deathwatch. Some of the Ordo's greatest Inquisitors, such as Lord Inquisitor [[Kryptman]], have sacrificed entire worlds to deny them to the enemy, and an Inquisitor in that position makes decisions that cost millions of lives.

The Ordo makes distinctions among species. Those that are primitive or confined to remote worlds are often tolerated, and dealing with starfaring aliens is routine on many frontier worlds. Other species are so dangerous that the Ordo brands them with a High Gothic stigma, the ''Xenos Horrificus'', which marks them as targets to be hunted and purged at every opportunity. The brand applies also to anyone who has dealt with them, and more than one Rogue Trader has been marked with it.

Not all Inquisitors of the Ordo hold the same view of alien life. Some practise alliance with aliens, holding that their knowledge of alien methods and minds makes the risk worth taking. The Imperium shares enemies with some xenos species, and some alien technology, such as the [[Jokaero]] digital weapons, far surpasses its own. The Ordo's Radicals were famous in the Calixis Sector; Staven Arcturos and Jena Orechiel are named among those who kept aliens in their retinues. More conservative members of the Ordo denounce such practice.

The Deathwatch

Main article: [[Deathwatch]]

When an alien threat is too great for the Astra Militarum or the planetary defence forces an Inquisitor may requisition, the Inquisitor calls on the Deathwatch, which operates in kill-teams of veteran Astartes from every Chapter. Deathwatch Space Marines wear black power armour, with one shoulder plate in the colours of their original Chapter and the other bearing the Deathwatch badge. A Space Marine who leaves the Deathwatch after his term keeps the silver badge on the left shoulder. A kill-team commonly numbers five or ten Astartes, and rarely as many as twenty.

Chapters are chosen for the experience they bring. The [[Ultramarines]] fought the [[Tyranids]] at the [[Battle of Macragge]], and the [[Crimson Fists]] fought the Orks at [[Rynn's World]], and Deathwatch teams are shaped around the enemy they must face. A team may specialise against the T'au, Tyranids, [[Necrons]], Orks or Aeldari. The Deathwatch serves either an Inquisitorial conclave charged with protecting a sector, or is assigned to an Inquisitor's retinue for the length of a mission.

Notable members

[[Gregor Eisenhorn]] was an Amalathian Inquisitor of the Ordo Xenos who brought down the Radical Quixos and the Glaw family by means of the daemonhost Cherubael, and who was afterwards declared a heretic by Inquisitor Osma of the Ordo Malleus. [[Solomon Lok]] was sent in 850.M41 to investigate the silence of an Imperial research facility on [[Beta Anphelion IV]], where he found that tech-priests studying captured Tyranid sub-species had let them escape; he destroyed a Hierophant bio-titan in a last stand with the Elysian and Cadian troops that served with him, and is presumed slain. Inquisitor Lord Quate'maz Knael compiled the Calixian ''Black Grimoire'', a field manual of the xenos, which is disputed within the Calixis conclave.

[[Hezika Carmillus]], Inquisitor of the Chamber and mistress of the Tower of Brass at [[Watch Fortress Erioch]] in the [[Jericho Reach]], has led purges on a dozen worlds and has condemned Lord Commanders and Admirals. Inquisitor Kalistradi, a specialist in Genestealer infestations, died on Avalos while hunting a broodlord. Inquisitor Oriel's mistaken attempt to defend the Typhon Sector from a hive fleet by releasing a Genestealer into the fortress city of Coritanorum caused a two-year civil war, and Inquisitor Horosa Barnabas, whose career was one of lost artefacts and failed missions, was killed by an Aeldari fire lance on Adronicus.
