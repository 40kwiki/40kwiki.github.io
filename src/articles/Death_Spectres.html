The Death Spectres are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], descended from the [[Raven Guard]] and created in the [[Thirteenth Founding]], the so-called "Dark Founding" of the late 35th or early [[36th millennium]]. They are one of only two Chapters known to have come from that Founding, the other being the [[Exorcists]]. Their homeworld is the cemetery world of Occludus, and their fortress-monastery is the necropolis of Logopol.

The Chapter's charge is the [[Ghoul Stars]], a region on the far eastern edge of the galaxy, where it watches over the deathless things that dwell there, among them [[Necrons|Necron]] Flayed Ones and the xenos called the Cythor Fiends. It holds that duty still in the [[Era Indomitus]]. The Death Spectres are known for their sombre, death-centred culture and for the honour that they pay the fallen.

History

The Dark Founding

The Thirteenth Founding took place in the troubled period after the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], at some point between the 35th and 36th millennia. It is one of the very few Foundings that were not recorded in full. Since the [[Horus Heresy]] the [[Adeptus Terra]] has kept a bank of original gene-seed from every Chapter it has created, with the single exception of the Thirteenth Founding, and no reliable record states how many Chapters were made in it or what became of them.The Founding cannot be dated more closely than the turn of M35 and M36; Imperial scholars infer its date from the end of the Nova Terra Interregnum.

Chapter tradition holds that its first Megir, as the Death Spectres call their [[Chapter Master]], Merrin Corcaedus, received a vision while swearing his oath of service at the foot of the [[Golden Throne]]. The vision, the Chapter says, showed him the place where it was to serve and the death it was to guard against, and it led the Chapter to Occludus and the Ghoul Stars.

Campaigns

In 140.M40 the Death Spectres fought on Vern IV against the [[Drukhari]] Kabal of the Envenomed Thorn and the Void Dragons, an [[Aeldari]] corsair band. Between 392 and 399.M41 they served in the [[Macharian Crusade]] of Lord Solar [[Macharius]]. From 777.M41 they sent forces to the [[Achilus Crusade]], the long war to retake the Jericho Reach.

Between 857 and 859.M41 the Chapter joined four others, among them the [[Howling Griffons]] and the Silver Skulls, in the Lazar Blockade, a campaign to purge the Necrons from the Lazar System in revenge for losses suffered by the Silver Skulls. The campaign failed: the Silver Skulls' Chapter Master was slain, the blockade was broken, and the Chapters involved withdrew.

From 990 to 998.M41 the Death Spectres fought alongside the [[Black Templars]] in the Ghoul Stars Crusade, the first crusade of the new High Marshal [[Helbrecht]]. After eight years of fighting the Cythor Fiends were nearly destroyed, and the crusaders pushed toward their gas-giant homeworld, catalogued as 9836-18 Grave Core. In 995.M41 the Chapter also met the first tendrils of Hive Fleet Jormungandr on the Imperium's north-eastern frontier.

Thirteenth Black Crusade and the Era Indomitus

In 999.M41 the Death Spectres fought in the defence of the [[Cadia|Cadian Sector]] during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], including the Siege of Lelithar. After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] the Chapter returned to its watch over the Ghoul Stars, which lie in the [[Imperium Sanctus]], and it has since been reinforced with [[Primaris Space Marines]].The date of the Chapter's Primaris reinforcement is not recorded; it is placed in the early years of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] by analogy with other Chapters.

Organisation

The Death Spectres keep the organisation of the [[Codex Astartes]] in outline, but use their own titles. The Chapter Master is the Megir, and the Mesazar, the Chief [[Librarian]], commands in his absence. The Chapter also maintains a body of Librarians of unusual strength, and its fallen are said to be honoured by the War-Geists, Dreadnoughts and veterans entombed and kept in the Chapter's service. Recruits are drawn from breeding worlds established near Occludus.

Beliefs

The Death Spectres find peace in the silence of the tomb-cities of their homeworld, where they believe the dead watch over the living. Their rites are centred on death and remembrance, and their Chaplains open battle with the call that gives the Chapter its battle cry.
