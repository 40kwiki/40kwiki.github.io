Deimos, called the Steel Forge, is a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Sol System]]. It was once the outer of the two moons of [[Mars]]. At some time after the founding of the [[Grey Knights]], the Mechanicus tore it from its orbit, moved it across the Sol System and set it above [[Titan (moon)|Titan]], the hidden fortress-moon of the Chapter at [[Saturn]]. Since then its forges have made the arms, munitions and war machines of the Grey Knights and of no one else.

Deimos has been mined and rebuilt so far that it is now more a star fort than a moon. It is home to many Skitarii macroclades and to three [[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]], among them [[House Steel]], which has kept a vow of silence in the service of the Mechanicus since the [[Great Crusade]]. A chain of weapons platforms that once stood around its old orbit, the Deimos chain, still forms part of the defences of Mars.

History

Moon of Mars

Like [[Phobos]], Deimos was settled and industrialised during the [[Dark Age of Technology]]. By the time of the [[Treaty of Olympus]] in 739.M30 it was one of the forges of the Martian Mechanicum, and the vehicle patterns that bear its name, among them versions of the Rhino, Predator and Vindicator, were in service with the [[Space Marine Legions]] during the Great Crusade. Its forges are believed to have been held for the loyalists during the [[Schism of Mars]] in 005–006.M31, though the records are incomplete.The loyalty of Deimos during the Schism is inferred from its later gift to the Grey Knights; no open record states it.

Gift to the Grey Knights

The Grey Knights were founded in secret late in the [[Horus Heresy]], about 013–014.M31, and are recorded as a Chapter of the [[Second Founding]] of about 021.M31. To fight daemons they needed a steady supply of arms that no outsider could study, and they made a pact with the Mechanicus. Under it the Mechanicus gave them Deimos. Using technology whose nature has never been disclosed, the moon was pulled from its orbit, carried across the system and placed in orbit above [[Titan]]. Its mass was then mined and converted until little of the original moon remained.

The date of the move is not recorded in any open archive. Mechanicus and Inquisitorial records place it "upon the inception" of the Chapter, and Imperial scholars generally date it to the early 32nd millennium.Date estimated from the founding of the Grey Knights and the reform of the Mechanicus after the Heresy.

Among the gifts that came with the moon was a voidship loaded with weapons of the finest make, among them the storm bolter Fury of Deimos, said to have been made by the first [[Fabricator-General]] of the Mechanicum. The secret of its making has since been lost.

Later history

Deimos has stood above Titan for about ten thousand years. Its armies guard the Chapter's supply lines and, when the Grey Knights ask for them, march beside the Chapter in battle. In 998.M41, when a Necron squadron raided Mars, the Deimos chain of platforms in Mars orbit was the second line that the raiders ran past.

In the [[Era Indomitus]], a war fleet from Deimos helped break the siege of the forge world [[Metalica]] during the [[War of Slime and Metal]], in which the forces of [[Nurgle]], among them companies of the [[Death Guard]] and the Titans of the Legio Morbus, were driven off with the help of [[House Raven]].The war is dated only to the early 42nd millennium in open records.

Secrecy

Only the [[Nemesis force weapon|Nemesis force weapons]] of the Grey Knights are made on Titan, by the Chapter's own [[Techmarines]]. Everything else comes down from Deimos in guarded transports. [[Servitors]] pack and carry every consignment and are then mind-wiped before they return to the forge, so that no tech-priest of Deimos knows the full extent of the Chapter's arsenal. The same measures keep the psychic Grey Knights from learning the secrets of the Mechanicus. Each weapon is given psychic wards against daemonic taint on top of the blessings of the [[Omnissiah]].

Forces and heraldry

The Skitarii of Deimos wear armour of sinopia red with brass trim, over sky-blue robes edged in a white cog-tooth pattern. Their sign is a hexagonal tech-glyph whose meaning is not explained to outsiders. Three Knight houses are stationed on the moon permanently.
