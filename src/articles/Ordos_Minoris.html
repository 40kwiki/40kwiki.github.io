The Ordos Minoris (singular Ordo Minoris), or minor ordos, are the lesser branches of the [[Inquisition]]. Each is dedicated to a more particular or more transient danger than the three [[Ordos Majoris]], which are the [[Ordo Malleus]], the [[Ordo Xenos]] and the [[Ordo Hereticus]]. The records speak of "many scores" of them. Most are unknown even to other [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]].

The Ordos Minoris are, in theory, of equal rank and power to the Ordos Majoris. In practice they rarely command the same resources, and none has a Chamber Militant of its own. Some police the [[Officio Assassinorum]]'s death cults, and others tend to the workings of the [[Black Ships]]. Rumour holds that other Ordos watch the officers of the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Navis Imperialis]], and that others still exist only to purge the Inquisition itself.

Nature

An Ordo can lie fallow for years, a historical curiosity, until its subject becomes relevant again. The Ordos Majoris are called "majoris" because the dangers they face, the [[Daemon|daemon]], the [[Xenos|xenos]] and the [[Heretic|heretic]], are never absent. The minor Ordos face dangers that come and go.

Many are no more than cabals of [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] who unite against a common danger in one region. Others are in effect specialised sub-ordos of the greater Ordos, and a few concern themselves with matters so strange that nobody else knows of them. The [[Inquisition]] has no ranks and no formal hierarchy, and the minor Ordos are no exception.

The Ordo Hydra is often counted as an Ordo Minoris, but it was a group defined by a tool, the Hydra, not by a threat, and is perhaps better described as an Inquisitorial cabal. The Ordo Obsuletus is similarly listed, though it may be a regional conclave that calls itself an Ordo. The [[Inquisition]]'s records also mention an unnamed Ordo, founded in the [[Horus Heresy]], that develops ways to limit the independence of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]; see [[Ordo Astartes]].

Duties of the Ordos Minoris

Watching other institutions

The [[Ordo Machinum]] watches the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], the [[Ordo Sicarius]] the Assassins, the Ordo Militarum the [[Astra Militarum]], the Ordo Sanctorum the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]], and the [[Ordo Astartes]] the [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]]. The [[Ordo Vigilus]] watches another Ordo.

Watching history and records

The [[Ordo Originatus]], the [[Ordo Redactus]] and the [[Ordo Scriptorum]] are concerned with the Imperium's records. The [[Ordo Chronos]] with its date.

Specific threats

The [[Ordo Sepulturum]] opposes the [[Zombie Plague]], the [[Ordo Maledictum]] the [[Great Rift]], and the [[Ordo Excorium]] the misuse of [[Exterminatus]].
