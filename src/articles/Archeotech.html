Archeotech is the term used throughout the [[Imperium of Mankind]] for lost advanced [[Human]] science and technology that has been recovered from the past. The term covers devices from the [[Dark Age of Technology]], the [[Age of Strife]] and even the Imperium's own [[Great Crusade]], when the [[Imperial Army]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] recovered great stores of knowledge from worlds long forgotten.

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] takes the strongest interest in archeotech. It treats every device built from a recovered [[Standard Template Construct (STC)]] database as archeotech, and it holds that the rediscovery of lost designs is a sacred duty. Some archeotech is nonetheless forbidden, most notably any device that could be said to possess machine sapience.

Forbidden forms

The most serious limit on archeotech is the ban on [[Silica Animus|artificial general intelligence]]. Imperial scholars point to the [[Men of Iron]], a rebellion of machine intelligences at the close of the [[Age of Technology]], as the reason for this law. That rebellion nearly destroyed the star-spanning Human civilisation of its era. Mechanicus doctrine therefore treats any machine that thinks for itself as an abomination, and any archeotech that shows signs of awareness is sealed away or destroyed.

Imperial accounts differ on how strict the ban should be. Some [[Tech-priest]] orders keep dormant thinking engines in reliquaries for study, while the [[Inquisition]] has ordered several of these sealed on the grounds that even a dormant mind may wake.The treatment of dormant machine intelligences is a matter of dispute between Mechanicus orders. No single doctrine is recorded for all forges.

Recovery and sources

Archeotech is most often found in frontier regions where the Imperium once held worlds but lost contact with them for millennia. Examples include the [[Koronus Expanse]] and the [[Jericho Reach]]. It is also found in the deep [[Hive world]]s, such as [[Necromunda]], where people have lived since before the [[Age of the Imperium]] began. Recovery teams range from [[Explorator]] expeditions to [[Rogue Trader]] ventures.

The holy grail of archeotech scholars would be a complete STC database, containing the schematics of every technology once thought necessary to found a new colony during the height of the Age of Technology. No complete copy is known to survive.

Notable examples

Archeotech is recorded in a wide range of forms. The following examples appear in Imperial records:

  • Viewer: a hand-held device that shows the scene from any point, including areas behind doors or walls.
  • Cutting Beam: a heavy directed-energy tool able to cut through the densest building materials. It is of little use as a weapon.
  • Lifter: a device that manipulates gravity in a small area, so that its operator can rise or descend slowly.
  • Holo Projector: an image of the operator, projected a short distance away, used to draw enemy fire.
  • Shadowsource Drive: an ancient device able to blot out the sun over an entire world.
  • Icarus Skyrule Engine: an advanced form of the Icarus pattern [[Lascannon]], used against aircraft.
  • Teleportation Hub: a [[Teleportarium|teleporter]] with several pads, able to move a number of people at once.
  • Hyper-deceleration Field: a field that slows incoming fire to a fraction of its speed.
