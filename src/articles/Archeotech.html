Archeotech is the term used throughout the [[Imperium of Mankind]] for lost advanced [[Human]] science and technology that has been recovered in the present age. The term covers devices from the [[Dark Age of Technology]], the [[Age of Strife]] and even the Imperium's own golden age during the [[Great Crusade]].

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] takes the strongest interest in archeotech. It treats every device built from a recovered [[Standard Template Construct (STC)]] database as archeotech. Some archeotech is nonetheless forbidden, most notably any device that could be said to possess machine sapience.

Forbidden forms

True [[Silica Animus|artificial general intelligence]] is strictly forbidden within the Imperium. Imperial scholars point to the [[Men of Iron]], a rebellion of machine intelligences at the close of the [[Age of Technology]] that nearly destroyed the star-spanning Human civilisation of that era.

Recovery and sources

Archeotech is often recovered in frontier regions of the galaxy such as the [[Koronus Expanse]], in regions such as the [[Jericho Reach]] that were once part of the Imperium but have been cut off for millennia, and in the depths of [[Hive world]]s such as [[Necromunda]], which [[Mankind]] has inhabited since before the [[Age of the Imperium]] began.

The holy grail of archeotech would be a complete STC database, holding the schematics of every advanced technology once thought necessary to found a new Human colony at the height of the Age of Technology.

Notable examples

Archeotech is recorded in a wide range of forms. The following examples appear in Imperial records.

  • Viewer: a portable hand-held device that shows the scene from any point, even behind closed doors or solid walls.
  • Cutting Beam: a bulky directed-energy tool able to cut through the densest building materials. It is of little use as a weapon, being heavy and demanding great concentration.
  • Lifter: a device that manipulates gravity in a small area, so that its operator can rise or descend slowly.
  • Holo Projector: an image of the operator, projected a short distance away, used to draw enemy fire.
  • Shadowsource Drive: an ancient device able to blot out the sun on any world where it is deployed.
  • Icarus Skyrule Engine: an advanced form of the Icarus pattern [[Lascannon]], used against aircraft.
  • Teleportation Hub: a [[Teleportarium|teleporter]] with several pads, able to move several people at once.
  • Hyper-deceleration Field: a priceless field that slows incoming fire to a fraction of its former speed, leaving those inside all but safe from ranged attacks.
