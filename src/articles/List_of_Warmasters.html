This is a list of the Warmasters, the holders of the title of [[Warmaster]], the highest military command of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. The title was first given to [[Horus]] by the Emperor in the last years of the [[Great Crusade]]. It was not used again until the High Lords granted it in the 41st millennium, and it has since been rarely granted, because of its ties to the [[Horus Heresy]].

In the 41st millennium the rank is officially proclaimed as Lord Solar, or Lord Commander Solar, when the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] confers it. Subordinates still address the holder as Warmaster. The Chaos Gods grant the same title to the Chaos Lord who can unite their servants, and the Warmaster of Chaos is listed separately below.

Notes on the office

The Imperium regards the title as cursed, for it was the rank Horus held when he turned against the Emperor. For this reason the High Lords have granted it rarely, and in the 41st millennium the Senatorum Imperialis has preferred the name Lord Solar. The Administratum keeps the title on its register even when no holder is named.
