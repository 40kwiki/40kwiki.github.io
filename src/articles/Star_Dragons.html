The Star Dragons are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of unknown Founding and genetic origin. They follow the [[Codex Astartes]] closely and recruit from the world of Draconith, where the warriors are scarred in ritual and the cuts inlaid with circuitry that glows like caged stars. Some Imperial scholars suspect a descent from [[Ferrus Manus]] and the [[Iron Hands]], though the Chapter shows little of that line's cold logic. It is guided instead, observers say, by furious passions held in check by careful tactical sense.

The Chapter's long friendship with the Blood Swords, a neighbour with a nearby homeworld, led to a joint patrol fleet near the [[Eye of Terror]] in the late 41st millennium.

History

Containment Fleet Kappa

For centuries before 998.M41 the Star Dragons and the Blood Swords fought alongside each other whenever the need arose. When the Blood Swords suffered a shameful loss of their homeworld and undertook a Penitent Crusade, the Star Dragons stood by them without thought of debt or honour. The act made a rare bond between two Chapters, and the two later formed the joint Containment Fleet Kappa to watch the edges of the Eye of Terror. It was commanded by Captain Tanek of the Star Dragons' 6th Company from the strike cruiser Ladon.

The Accursed Eternity

In 998.M41 Inquisitor Shadrach Remigius of the [[Ordo Malleus]] summoned Kappa to the Balanor System, where a Chaos warfleet and the ships of the Inquisition were fighting and a space hulk matching the legendary Accursed Eternity had been sighted. The fleet translated from the Warp into the heart of the battle, and two of its escorts were crippled by Inquisition guns in the confusion, which led to angry words on both sides. The Star Dragons and Blood Swords then boarded the hulk. At least one Star Dragon, Brother Arion of the 3rd Scale, died aboard when a Chaos entity compromised him.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, the world of Darkhold, home of the Dark Sons, was cut off in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] and besieged. The Star Dragons set out with the Iron Hands to break the siege, a detail that supports the belief in a shared descent. A Star Dragons sergeant, Zymiskes, served with the [[Deathwatch]] at the watch fortress Eye of Octos and fought the [[Tyranids]] of a splinter of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] on the world Death of Bianzeer during the [[Octarius War]].The Siege of Darkhold and the Octarius War service are dated only to M42 and the years after the opening of the Great Rift.

Organisation and beliefs

The Star Dragons keep close to the Codex, with draconic names for a few offices: squads are called scales, and the daily meeting of a company's senior officers is called the Clutch. In the Clutch aboard the Ladon, every problem of the fleet is laid out and orders are shared with the Blood Swords. The meeting is businesslike, and Captain Tanek, who is grim in battle but open in council, encourages his officers to speak freely.

The Chapter's Chief Apothecary, Sol Konna, is known for the saying "Even he who dies, lives, in the blood of his brothers and the deeds of the Chapter". Its Chaplains wear cloaks stitched from the skins of the draconae of Draconith, as thick as chainmail.

Notable members

  • Tanek, Captain of the 6th Company and commander of Containment Fleet Kappa.
  • Sol Konna, Chief Apothecary.
  • Hetor Iakodos, Chaplain of the 6th Company, bearer of a dragon-skin cloak.
  • Korydon, Veteran Sergeant of the 3rd Scale, a young but sharp-tongued squad leader.
  • Zymiskes, sergeant seconded to the Deathwatch.
