Aveliza Drachmar was the most recent holder of the office of [[Grand Provost Marshal]] named in the Imperial record. She joined the [[Hexarchy]] against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and was killed in its failed coup in early M42.

The office she held is one of the four oldest in the Imperium. The Grand Provost Marshal leads the [[Adeptus Arbites]], which were formed after the [[Horus Heresy]] when the scattered offices of the Marshals were united.

Career

The records give little of her career. Her office is unrecorded after her death, and no successor is named in the sources consulted. Imperial accounts describe the coup as the last of the Hexarchy's open attempts against the Lord Commander.The date of the coup is given as early M42. The sources do not give her date of appointment.
