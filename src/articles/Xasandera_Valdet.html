Xasandera Valdet is the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]] and High Priest of the [[Cult Mechanicus]]. She holds the permanent Mechanicus seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] as restored in the [[Era Indomitus]], and she succeeded [[Oud Oudia Raskian]], who vanished soon after the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41.

Very little about her is published. The year of her accession, her forge of origin and her earlier career are not recorded in the sources open to Imperial scholars. She is noted in the Rolls of the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]] only as the holder of the office, and the [[Imperial Regent]] has treated her seat as one that the Treaty of Olympus guarantees.

Office

The [[Fabricator-General]] rules [[Mars]] and presides over the Synod, the assembly of the senior magi of the priesthood. The office directs the forges of the Red Planet, its [[Skitarii]] legions and its share of the [[Titan Legions]], and makes the holder the supreme interpreter of the doctrine of the Machine Cult. The Fabricator-General does not command the other [[Forge world|forge worlds]] directly, whose rulers owe Mars reverence but rule as they choose.

Under the Treaty, the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] cannot appoint or remove the Fabricator-General. Valdet therefore sits on the council as the head of an allied power, not as a subject.

Accession

Raskian disappeared after the [[Fall of Cadia]] and was presumed dead. A faction of the Synod then petitioned the representatives of the [[Archmagos|archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] to take the office and to lead Mars in a direction more independent of [[Terra]]. Cawl declined. The [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], [[Roboute Guilliman]], whom Cawl had repeatedly pressed to appoint him, held that the Treaty forbade the Imperium to impose its will on the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], and that an imposed appointment would provoke a second civil war among the [[Forge world|forge worlds]]. The office passed to Valdet in the early [[42nd millennium]].

On the council

Valdet sits among the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] alongside the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Eos Ritira]], the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] [[Kleopatra Arx]] and the [[Paternoval Envoy]] [[Kadak Mir]], after [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] replaced a number of the former High Lords. She is not recorded among the High Lords who joined the [[Hexarchy]], the conspiracy that tried to seize Terra in early M42, and her seat remained after it was broken.

The open record names no decision of hers on the council. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] keeps its counsel, and Valdet's own annals, if she has made any, are held on Mars.
