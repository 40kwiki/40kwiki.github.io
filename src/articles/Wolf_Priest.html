A Wolf Priest is a specialist officer of the [[Space Wolves]] [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]]. The role combines the duties of the [[Chaplain]] and the [[Apothecary]] found in other Chapters that follow the [[Codex Astartes]]. Wolf Priests heal the body and mind of the Chapter's warriors, and they choose aspirants from among the tribes of [[Fenris]]. They answer only to the [[Great Wolf]].

History

The role dates from the [[Great Crusade]], in the last two centuries of the 30th millennium. Wolf Priests were one of several orders of the Priests of Fenris, which provided medical, spiritual and technical care to the Legion. The office has kept its place in the Chapter since the [[Horus Heresy]].

Duties

A Wolf Priest trains in the arts of healing and in the lore of genetics. He also keeps the rites and songs of the Chapter. The Wolf Priests keep the Chapter's [[Rune Priest]]s and [[Iron Priest]]s in their company, and they hold seats in the Chapter's councils.
