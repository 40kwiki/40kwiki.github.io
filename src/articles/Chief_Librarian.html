The Chief Librarian is the strongest [[Psyker|psyker]] in a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the master of its [[Librarius]] and the keeper of its lore. Every other [[Librarian]] of the Chapter reports to him, takes instruction from him and defers to him. If no psyker is fit for the office, the post is often left vacant.

Duties

The Chief Librarian sits on the council of the Chapter's senior officers and is among the closest advisors of the [[Chapter Master]], whose decisions rely on his gift for foretelling. He presides over the training and selection of new Librarians and over the cultivation of psykers in the ranks, and he teaches his lesser brothers the dangers of the [[Warp]]. Chief Librarians seldom take the field, but when they do they may command a force or assist the Chapter Master in battle.
