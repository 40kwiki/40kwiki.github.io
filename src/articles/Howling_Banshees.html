The Howling Banshees are the all-female [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. They specialise in fast, close-range combat. Their name comes from the [[banshee]], a spirit of Aeldari myth whose cry announces death. The Banshees are lightly equipped, and what they lack in raw strength they make up in precision and efficiency. Their piercing war cry has heralded the coming doom of countless enemies of the Aeldari. They are almost always female, because the banshees of legend, the daughters of the Crone Goddess Morai-Heg, are female spirits. A few males walk the Path of the Howling Banshee, usually [[Autarch]]s who pause on the Path of Command to master another aspect of Khaine.

Each Banshee is trained in the [[Path of the Warrior]] within a Banshee shrine, which stresses swiftness of foot and mobility in hand-to-hand combat. Banshees wear helmets called Banshee Masks that hold psychosonic amplifiers, which magnify their keening battle cries into shock waves. The shock can cause mortal terror and a moment of paralysis, and a full squad that activates its masks together can cripple an enemy unit before a blow is struck.

Myth and origin

Aeldari tradition holds that the banshee is a harbinger of grief and death, and that its cry calls the dead from the Warp. Some stories go further and claim that a banshee's cry can draw the spirits of the dead from their [[Spirit Stone]]s and deliver them to [[Slaanesh]]. Imperial scholars record this as a belief, not a fact, but they note that Aeldari souls are known to disperse into the [[Immaterium]] during transfer between Spirit Stones.

Legend holds that the Aspect was created when the Crone Goddess [[Morai-Heg]], the goddess of fate, wished to drink the knowledge in her own divine blood. She sent her daughters, the banshees, to haunt their father, the war god Kaela Mensha Khaine, who alone could harm another god. To end the wailing, Khaine severed her hand, and in exchange for his help Morai-Heg granted him the Howling Banshee Aspect. It came into being after the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], when the first Aspect Warriors were raised. When [[Asurmen]] raised them, he chose [[Jain Zar]] for her ferocity and speed. She was the first of her species to lose herself on the Path and become an Exarch, and then the Phoenix Lord of the Banshees.

Organisation and shrines

Each Banshee shrine is led by an [[Exarch]], a warrior who can no longer leave the Path and who wears more ornate armour holding the souls of earlier wearers. Banshee Exarchs may wield the Executioner, a powered polearm, or the Triskele, or replace sword and pistol with twin Mirrorswords. Squads number five to ten warriors, sometimes led by an Exarch, and may ride in [[Wave Serpent]] transports. Banshee armour is usually bone white and flaming red, which stands for ferocity and for the eternal death that the banshee brings. Several shrines are known. The Ebon Witch Shrine wears black armour in place of the usual red and bone, and the Exarchs of the Shrieking Blade Shrine favour the Mirrorsword. Jain Zar is the most devoted of the Phoenix Lords to the shrines of her Aspect, and she travels the Webway from craftworld to craftworld to visit them.
