The Howling Banshees are the all-female [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. They specialise in fast, close-range combat. Their name comes from the [[banshee]], a spirit of Aeldari myth whose cry announces death. The Banshees are lightly armoured and carry blades rather than heavy weapons, and they trade raw strength for speed and precision. Their piercing war cry has heralded the coming doom of countless enemies of the Aeldari.

Each Banshee is trained in the [[Path of the Warrior]] within a Banshee shrine. Their discipline is measured by the flawless execution of a strike, not by endurance. The Banshees are among the most feared Aspects of the Asuryani because they are fast enough to reach an enemy wherever it stands, and because their strikes are often over before the target can respond.

Myth and origin

Aeldari tradition holds that the banshee is a harbinger of grief and death, and that its cry calls the dead from the Warp. Some stories go further and claim that a banshee's cry can draw the spirits of the dead from their [[Spirit Stone]]s and deliver them to [[Slaanesh]]. Imperial scholars record this as a belief, not a fact, but they note that Aeldari souls are known to disperse into the [[Immaterium]] during transfer between Spirit Stones.

The Asuryani legend of the Aspect's founding tells that the Crone Goddess Morai-Heg sent her daughters, the banshees, to haunt the war god Kaela Mensha Khaine. Khaine was the only being able to harm another god, so Morai-Heg made him sever her hand. In return she gave him the Howling Banshee Aspect. The legend is cited across several craftworlds, although the sequence of its events differs between sources.

Organisation and shrines

The Banshees are divided into shrines, and each shrine has its own colours and style. The Ebon Witch Shrine wears black Aspect armour where other Banshees wear red and bone. The Shrieking Blade Shrine favours the [[Mirrorsword]], and its Exarchs fight in a flurry of strikes. The Crimson Scream Shrine is distinguished by its banners. Each shrine is led by an [[Exarch]], and the whole Aspect answers to the [[Phoenix Lord]] [[Jain Zar]].

A typical Banshee squad is small. It moves quickly into position and uses its speed to surround or cut off an enemy unit, then strikes in close combat. The Banshees rely on their [[Shuriken pistol]]s only when a close attack is not possible.
