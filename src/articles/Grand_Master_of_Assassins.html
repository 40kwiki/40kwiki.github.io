The Grand Master of Assassins, formally the Grand Master of the Officio Assassinorum, is the head of the [[Officio Assassinorum]], the Imperium's order of assassins, and one of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The holder directs the [[Assassin temples|temples]] of the Officio and the deployment of their operatives against the enemies of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], including, when the High Lords so decide by a two-thirds vote, its own governors, commanders and nobles.

The office was created during the [[Great Crusade]] by [[Malcador the Sigillite]], who founded the Officio on the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]]'s order and was its first Grand Master. He was succeeded at his death in 014.M31 by [[Khalid Hassan]], the first of his Chosen. The twelfth Grand Master, [[Drakan Vangorich]], ordered the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32, in which the High Lords were murdered in a single day, and then ruled the Imperium as Lord Protector for some eighty years. No other office has done the council such harm.

The incumbent, [[Fadix]], sat on the council at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41. In early M42 he feigned membership of the [[Hexarchy]], a conspiracy against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], and had its leaders killed at the moment of their coup.

Powers and duties

The Grand Master commands the temples of the Officio, among them the [[Vindicare Temple|Vindicare]], [[Callidus Temple|Callidus]], [[Eversor Temple|Eversor]] and [[Culexus Temple|Culexus]], each led by a director primus. Operatives are dispatched on the authority of the High Lords; the Grand Master chooses the means. He is said to be chosen from among the directors, his loyalty must be beyond question, and he is rarely seen by the officials of the [[Adeptus Terra]]. Some accounts hold that he has personal access to the [[Golden Throne]].

His place on the council is unusual. Grand Masters seldom take a side in the High Lords' disputes, which makes them useful as a deciding vote. The other High Lords fear the office, with reason, and its seat has twice been taken away: before the [[War of the Beast]] it was given to the Inquisitorial Representative, though Vangorich continued to attend every session, and the office was put under scrutiny after his fall. Since the [[Age of Apostasy]] the Officio has been watched by the [[Ordo Sicarius]] of the [[Inquisition]].

History

Malcador and the clades (M30 – 014.M31)

Malcador, Regent of Terra, created the Officio during the Great Crusade from six clades, each led by a director primus, and was its first Grand Master. He held the office alongside his regency, and in secret. When the Warmaster [[Horus]] rebelled in 005.M31, the directors met in council with the Captain-General of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], [[Constantin Valdor]], and on his suggestion sent an [[Execution Force]] of four assassins, one from each of the major clades, against Horus, instead of a single operative as was the custom. It failed.

Khalid Hassan (014.M31 – unrecorded)

Khalid Hassan, a former officer of the Imperial Army's Clandestine Orta, had been recruited by Malcador into his Chosen and by the last stage of the [[Siege of Terra]] was the first among them. Before Malcador took the Emperor's place on the Golden Throne, he passed his unfinished projects to Hassan and two others. Hassan survived the Siege, carried out Malcador's last projects in its aftermath and became the second Grand Master of Assassins and master of the Sigillites. He sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31.

Drakan Vangorich and the Beheading (544–646.M32)

Drakan Vangorich, the twelfth Grand Master, held the office at the outbreak of the War of the Beast in 544.M32. He had lost his formal seat to the Inquisitorial Representative but attended the council regardless, allied with [[Wienand]] of the Inquisition and the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Heth]] against the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]]. He poisoned the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Erekart Mesring]] to force a War of Faith against the Orks, and in c. 545.M32 backed [[Koorland]]'s removal of the Lord Commander [[Udin Macht Udo]].

After the war the Lord Commander [[Maximus Thane]] named Vangorich Lord Protector of the Imperium and left Terra on crusade. When Vangorich learned that the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kubik]] had defied Thane's order to destroy [[Ullanor]], he concluded that the council could not be reformed, and in 546.M32 he had the High Lords killed in one day, sparing only Wienand. He ruled through a council of his appointees for some eighty years and carried out the [[Fourth Founding]], but grew paranoid and murderous. Thane returned and killed him in the Eversor Temple at Terra's north pole, c. 646.M32.

The Age of Apostasy (M36)

In the Wars of Vindication that followed the death of [[Goge Vandire]] in c. 288.M36, a renegade Callidus assassin, [[Tziz Jarek]], tried to kill the Grand Master of the time, and a series of atrocities followed within the [[Imperial Palace]]. In response the temples, until then all on Terra, were dispersed across the galaxy, and the Ordo Sicarius was formed to watch them. In the 35th millennium the Ecclesiarch Alexis XXI had already shown how the Officio could be suborned, by corrupting assassins to remove governors who opposed him.

Fadix (M41 – present)

Fadix, who as an operative left a strip of scarlet silk at each kill, held the office by the Thirteenth Black Crusade in 999.M41. He was a typical Grand Master, rarely seen and rarely involved in the council's quarrels, and he abstained on the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]]'s proposal to release the Adeptus Custodes from their oath to remain on Terra. He kept his seat when [[Roboute Guilliman]] restored the council. When the former [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Irthu Haemotalion]] formed the Hexarchy against Guilliman, Fadix appeared to join it. At the climax of the coup in early M42 he revealed the pretence and loosed a dozen Callidus and Vindicare assassins on the other five conspirators, killing them all.The year of the coup is not given in the available records. It is estimated as c. 000–012.M42.
