Noum Retraiva is the earliest recorded holder of the office of [[Master of the Administratum]], the head of the [[Administratum]]. He held the office in the late [[30th millennium]], during the [[Unification Wars]], as one of the four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian of [[Terra]].

Administratum tradition counts [[Malcador the Sigillite]] as the founder of the office, since the bureaucracy of the Unification Wars answered to him. The records of Retraiva's tenure are the earliest with a name attached to the post.

Unification Wars

Main article: [[Unification Wars]]

When the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]] was brought into the field late in the Unification Wars, the Lords Civilian of Terra were headed by four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian. Retraiva was one of them, alongside [[Pelops Dravagor]], Chancellor of the Estate Imperium, and [[Uwoma Kandawire]], [[Grand Provost Marshal]]. [[Kli-San Weia]] held the military seat.

Legacy

The [[Administratum]] regards Retraiva as the first of its Masters to hold the civil administration of the Imperium together through the wars that set the Imperium's shape. His successor is not recorded in the earliest lists.
