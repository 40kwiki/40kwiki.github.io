The Thaxis System is an Imperial [[star system]] in the [[Yasan Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], close to the [[Maelstrom]] [[Warp rift]]. It is controlled by the [[Red Corsairs]] [[warband]] of [[Heretic Astartes]] and takes its name from its principal world, the former [[Forge world]] of Thaxis.

History

In 999.M41, the Chaos Lord [[Huron Blackheart]] of the Red Corsairs invaded the Yasan Sector out of the Maelstrom. The Red Corsairs conquered Thaxis during the invasion. The [[White Scars]] held their fortress-monastery of [[Quan Zhou]] against them, and the Chaos forces later withdrew from [[Chogoris]] when the [[Noctis Aeterna]] ebbed.

The Red Corsairs have since claimed the [[Thaxis System]] and the neighbouring [[Gartuli System]] for their own, and use these footholds to attack the White Scars' homeworld.
