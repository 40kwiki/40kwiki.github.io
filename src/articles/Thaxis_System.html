The Thaxis System is an Imperial [[star system]] in the [[Yasan Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], close to the [[Maelstrom]] [[Warp rift]]. It is controlled by the [[Red Corsairs]] [[warband]] of [[Heretic Astartes]] and takes its name from its principal world, the former [[Forge world]] of Thaxis.

History

In 999.M41, the Chaos Lord [[Huron Blackheart]] of the Red Corsairs invaded the Yasan Sector out of the Maelstrom. The bulk of the [[White Scars]] was deployed to other conflicts, and the Chaos forces occupied several systems of the sector and nearly overwhelmed the garrison left on [[Chogoris]]. Thaxis fell to them during the invasion. The White Scars rallied and held their fortress-monastery of [[Quan Zhou]], but the Red Corsairs withdrew only when the [[Noctis Aeterna]] ebbed and their daemonic allies were lost.

The Red Corsairs kept a presence on the Chogoris System world of Shovith and have claimed the Thaxis System and the neighbouring [[Gartuli System]] outright. They use these footholds in the Yasan Sector to launch new attacks on the White Scars' homeworld.

System worlds

  • Sanctum (Defence platform): a former Imperial defence platform that orbited the Thaxis star, now held by the Red Corsairs and their allies
  • Thaxis (Forge world): the principal world and namesake of the system, once an [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] forge world, conquered during the invasion of 999.M41
  • [[Vydoria]] (Agri-world): besieged by the Red Corsairs and still contested with Loyalist forces
  • Shetlock Belt (Asteroid belt): the ring of asteroids at the outer edge of the system, contested between the Red Corsairs and the remaining Loyalist forces
