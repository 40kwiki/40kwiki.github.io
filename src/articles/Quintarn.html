Quintarn is an [[agri-world]] of the [[Realm of Ultramar]]. Like the other agri-worlds of the realm, it is given over to farming to feed the hive and [[Forge world|forge worlds]] and the armies of [[Ultramar]]. It is one of the worlds of the [[Ultima Segmentum]] that the [[Ultramarines]] have guarded since the [[Great Crusade]].

Its produce is sent along the realm's supply routes, and its farms and settlements are watched by Chapter garrisons. Its neighbours in the realm include [[Iax]], [[Masali]] and [[Tarentus]].

Context

The Imperial records list Quintarn among the [[Agri-world|agri-worlds]] that supplied the realm before the destruction of [[Prandium]] in 745.M41. Its supply role made it one of the realm's most important worlds for the Ultramarines' campaigns.The records of Quintarn's production are general. No exact figures are recorded.
