The Chancellor of the Estate Imperium is the highest official of the [[Estate Imperium]], the division of the [[Adeptus Administratum]] that keeps the records of the Imperial bureaucracy. The chancellor also has the duty of overseeing the [[Office of the Imperial Pursary]] within the [[Departmento Munitorum]]. That office keeps a record of all funds and materiel taken in [[tithes]] by the Munitorum and all funds spent to meet the demands laid out by the [[Officio Tactica]]. Its bureaucrats are in effect the paymasters of most of the Imperial armed forces.

At some points in Imperial history the chancellor has held a seat among the twelve [[High Lords of Terra]], who form the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. The office is one of the oldest on that council. It was one of the four original [[Magisters Temporal]], the civilian officials who governed the [[Emperor]]'s regime during the [[Unification Wars]] in the [[30th Millennium]].

Notable chancellors

  • Kallander: the first chancellor and a Magister Temporal during the Unification Wars. He remarked in office that he saw no need for an edifice the size of the still-planned [[Imperial Palace]].
  • Pelops Dravagor: a Magister Temporal in the later stages of the Unification Wars. He had to find the revenue for the [[Great Crusade]], which was to follow the conquest of [[Terra]]. He was described as being under the control of the [[Master of the Administratum]], [[Noum Retraiva]].
  • Ossian: a High Lord of Terra on the [[Council of Terra]] during the [[Horus Heresy]]. He attended the early meeting of the High Lords during the [[Siege of Terra]], survived the Traitor Legions' assault on [[Terra]], and sat on the [[Grand Council of Reconstruction]], convened by [[Rogal Dorn]] and [[Roboute Guilliman]] to rule the Imperium during the [[Great Scouring]].
  • Hektor Rosarind: chancellor during the [[War of the Beast]] in the mid-32nd millennium. He was of little political importance and was not given a seat among the "[[High Twelve]]" in that era. He had less prestige among his colleagues than even [[Drakan Vangorich]], the [[Grand Master of Assassins]], whom the other High Lords distrusted.
  • Seward Rosencranz: chancellor in 995.M41.
  • Brach: succeeded Rosencranz and held a seat among the High Lords of Terra by 999.M41. He died in the early years of the [[13th Black Crusade]]. The [[Captain-General]] [[Trajann Valoris]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] replaced him on the council in the fifth standard year of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].
