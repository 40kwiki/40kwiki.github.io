The Imperial Infantryman's Uplifting Primer is a standard-issue text covering service in the [[Astra Militarum]]. Every Guardsman carries a copy as part of his equipment. A Guardsman who loses his copy faces severe punishment.

Contents

The Primer is a basic guide. It covers the principles and regulations of the Astra Militarum, the arms, attire and equipment issued to each trooper, and the structure of the Astra Militarum. It also gives basic battlefield medical instruction and a guide to the foes of the Imperium.

History

The Primer is a small, black-covered book full of prayers and instructions for basic tasks. Troopers are encouraged to read and memorise much of it. Some troopers call it "toilet paper", a remark that has been recorded in Imperial reports.
