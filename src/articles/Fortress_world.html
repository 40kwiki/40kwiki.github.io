A fortress world is a class of planet of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that serves as a bastion of Imperial defence against some constant and persistent threat to Imperial space. Its cities are fortresses studded with artillery, its industry is given almost wholly to the making of war machines, and its people are trained as soldiers. Fortress worlds are uncommon but necessary. They are as much roadblocks as bastions, and several of them linked together form a barrier that prevents an enemy from spreading into Imperial space.

Their garrisons are very large and are usually made up of regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] and squadrons of the [[Imperial Navy]]. A fortress world is as valuable for its soldiers as for its position, since its armies are trained and equipped to the high standards of the Imperial Guard, and it is a rare fortress world that does not raise its armies in the expectation that they will be called to serve in the Guard. So the Tactica Imperialis teaches.

The most famous fortress world was [[Cadia]], which guarded the [[Cadian Gate]], the only stable passage out of the [[Eye of Terror]], against the forces of [[Chaos]] and the [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. It fell in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in c. 999.M41, and its fall was followed by the opening of the [[Great Rift]]. Not all fortress worlds belong to the Imperium: several, among them [[Medrengard]], have fallen to the [[Traitor Legions]], and the [[Iron Warriors]] hold a number of them.

Characteristics

Defences

Fortress worlds are established as military strongholds, staging points and physical deterrents. They are often set on habitable planets that are then built up for war, though the class is defined by purpose, not by kind of world. The cities of such a world are giant fortresses studded with artillery weapons, and its industry is directed almost entirely to the manufacture of the machines of war.

Government

On [[Cadia]] the supreme authority lay with the [[Lord Castellan]], who was both the planetary governor and the leader of its armed forces.

Society

Life under arms

Life on a fortress world is steeped in military discipline and doctrine. From the moment the citizens wake until they sleep they train and operate as a military unit, and each prepares for the day when he is called on to push back the darkness beyond the crenelated walls. Those old enough to enlist in the Astra Militarum or the planetary defence forces are often required to do so, and even those too young or infirm find ways to serve. Whole generations of one family may be in service at one time, and many regiments of the Imperial Guard boast a rich and proud history.

Cadia was so heavily militarised that its people were said to be taught to field-strip and fire a [[Lasgun|lasgun]] before they could read. Cadians were much sought after by commanders throughout the galaxy, and Cadian gear and training became the model for the regiments of the Astra Militarum.

Armed forces

Soldiers raised on fortress worlds are brought up to war, and by the time they are grown they are well versed in the arts of war and the doctrines of the Tactica Imperialis. Each is long trained to destroy the enemies of the Imperium, above all those whose threat first caused the world to be fortified. Fortress-worlders are known as disciplined, honourable and loyal, and each is a proficient combatant long before he is taken into the Imperial armed forces.

Fortress worlds that fall are a loss to the Imperium and a prize for its enemies. The Iron Warriors in particular hold several, among them [[Kasr Partox]] in the [[Cadian Sector]].

History

The long watch

Fortress worlds guarded the approaches to the Eye of Terror and the borders of the Imperium for ten thousand years. Their defence was tested many times, above all in the Black Crusades of Abaddon, the first of which began in 781.M31. In the 41st millennium some Imperial fortress worlds in the Jericho Reach, among them Alphos, Hethgard, Karlack and Pyrathas, served as bases in the [[Achilus Crusade]], which began in 777.M41.

The Era Indomitus

The destruction of Cadia in c. 999.M41 and the opening of the Great Rift ended the long watch at the Cadian Gate. Some three million of Cadia's citizens were evacuated before the world broke apart, and the surviving Cadian regiments continue to serve in the Astra Militarum.

Notable fortress worlds

The registers record dozens of fortress worlds, in the keeping of the Imperium and of its enemies. The greatest of the Imperial fortress worlds after Cadia is Coronis Agathon in the Segmentum Obscurus, a world of some 120 billion people, and the Cadian Sector held Kasr Partox. In the Jericho Reach the Imperium holds Alphos, Karlack, Hethgard, Pyrathas, Spite and Wrath, with estimated populations of between 57 and 338 million where they are recorded. In the Sabbat Worlds Jago and Morlond stand as fortress worlds, and the Inquisition holds two of its own, Talasa Prime in Ultramar and the moon Titan.

Some are lost. Khirab, Mortrex and Sybari are now dead worlds, and Mortain was destroyed. Others serve the enemy: the Iron Warriors hold Brigannion IV, Burjan's World, Castellax, Forgefane and Malodrax, along with Medrengard in the Eye of Terror and Kasr Partox, while Lutoris Epsilon is held by the forces of Chaos and Magog in the Jericho Reach by Chaos.

Belis Corona, a dead world near the Eye of Terror, is not itself classed as a fortress world. It is the primary base of Battlefleet Obscurus and the administrative capital of the Segmentum Obscurus, and in the Thirteenth Black Crusade it served as the headquarters of the defence of the segmentum and of the Cadian Gate.

Fortress worlds of the Inquisition

Not every fortress world answers to the Astra Militarum. The [[Inquisition]] keeps fortress worlds of its own. The moon [[Titan (moon)|Titan]], in orbit about Saturn in the [[Sol System]], is the home of the [[Grey Knights]] and of the Chamber Militant of the [[Ordo Malleus]], and its fortress-monastery is carved of basalt. [[Talasa Prime]] in [[Ultramar]], a fortress world gifted in perpetuity by the [[Ultramarines]] to the [[Ordo Xenos]] and the [[Deathwatch]], is the Deathwatch's foremost watch fortress and one of its main training worlds.
