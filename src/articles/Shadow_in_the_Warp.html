Shadow in the Warp
Psychic phenomenon generated by the [[Tyranids]]
SourceThe [[Hive Mind]] of a [[Tyranid]] [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]]
EffectBlocks connections to the [[Immaterium]] in the local space around the fleet
Affects[[Psyker]]s, [[Astropathy]] and psychic manifestations

The Shadow in the Warp is a psychic phenomenon generated by the [[Tyranids]]' [[Hive Mind]]. It blocks all connections to the [[Immaterium]] in the space around a [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]], so that psychic communication and the manifestation of psychic powers become extremely difficult. Imperial psykers describe it as a silence in the Warp that spreads with the fleet.

The Shadow is one of the main reasons the Tyranids are so hard to warn against. Astropaths sent ahead of a fleet go silent, and the warnings they carry are lost. Imperial records state that the phenomenon is strongest at the centre of a fleet and weakens at its outer edges.
