The Angels of Redemption are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], raised in the [[Second Founding]] from the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of the [[Dark Angels]]. They are counted among the Unforgiven, the Chapters that share the Dark Angels' secret penance. Their deeds are nearly as celebrated as those of their progenitors, and so is their habit of standing apart from their allies.

Nothing deters the Angels of Redemption from their pursuit of the Fallen Angels, the former sons of the Legion whose treachery the Unforgiven hunt. The Chapter has on occasion left a campaign to follow its own undisclosed ends, and [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] have refused its aid because of it. The consequences have been postponed by the [[Great Rift]], which has cut off the reports that would otherwise reach [[Terra]].

History

Forgotten Wars

From 580.M31 to 632.M32, during an unspoken period of the [[Dark Angels]]' history, rumours of several Fallen led the Dark Angels and three of their successors, the Angels of Redemption, the Angels of Vengeance and the Lions Sable, on a campaign through the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], the Gothic Sector and at last the [[Eye of Terror]]. The [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]] had baited them. The Dark Angels escaped through sheer resolve at a high cost in casualties, and the Lions Sable were annihilated.

Some time in the [[32nd millennium]], with the arms of [[The Rock]] at their disposal, the Angels of Redemption refitted their 1st and 2nd Companies as the Redwings, in imitation of the Deathwing and Ravenwing. The other Unforgiven [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] followed. In 997.M33 the Chapter found sedition on one of its own recruiting worlds: the notorious Fallen [[Cypher]], styled the "Saviour of Praxus", had been hand-picking and training feral warriors to improve their chances of selection. He escaped before he could be taken.

The Defence of Gaitlinghive

In an undated action of the [[41st millennium]], the Angels of Redemption were fighting beside seven brigades of Gaitlinghive militia against an Ork horde three million strong. At the moment of the [[Orks]]' final assault the Chapter withdrew to pursue a Fallen Angel. The militia died, the [[Hive world|hive city]] fell and its people were massacred. No survivor remained to demand an inquiry. The Angels took their quarry, the Fallen Baalakai, a former captain of the Legion, and counted the price a fair one.

Sepulchre Sigma

In 811.M41 elements of the 1st and 2nd Companies came to the [[Jericho Reach]] without the request of the [[Achilus Crusade]]'s High Command. They went into orbit around Fortress Spite and demanded entry to the dungeons of Sepulchre Sigma, including an [[Chaplain|Interrogator-Chaplain]]. The garrison refused and a short, brutal firefight killed thirty enlisted troops and three senior officers. The Marines emerged three solar days later with a stasis-casket and left.

Third War for Armageddon and after

See also: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

In 998.M41 the Chapter sent four companies to the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]], and was diverted to the homeworld of the Monglor [[Abhuman|Ogryn]] [[Imperialis Auxilia|Auxilia]] to put down an Ogryn insurrection. In the same year it joined the Dark Angels, the Consecrators, the Knights of the Crimson Order, the [[Angels of Absolution]], the Repentant Brotherhood and the Angels of Vengeance in the Battle of the [[Caliban]] System, against a [[Death Guard]] plague fleet and the fleet of the Fallen. The battle followed the surrender of Cypher to the Inner Circle of the Dark Angels. In 999.M41 the Chapter came to the defence of the [[Cadian Sector]] in the 13th [[Black Crusade]]. In the early [[42nd millennium]] it took part in the failed defence of the Stygius Sector against the [[Khorne|Blood God]]'s host, and sent a company with the other Unforgiven to the War of Beasts at [[Vigilus]].

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter follows the [[Dark Angels]]' structure: the 1st and 2nd Companies are the Redwings, and an Inner Circle of its elite veterans guards the knowledge of the Fallen and of the true events of the [[Luther|Fall of Caliban]]. That knowledge is sealed by edict and is not set down here. Veterans and the Inner Circle commonly wear red-hooded cloaks and tabards, a sign of shame at what the Fallen did to bring about the fall of their homeworld.

Its armour is halved horizontally, dark green on the right in memory of the forests of the lost Legion homeworld and bone white on the left, with a red [[Aquila]]. The Chapter badge is a white skull with black stylised wings on bone white. Its relics include a great storm shield, the Bastion Unwavering, which a battle-brother named Asmodel carried to the watch fortress Erioch of the [[Deathwatch]]. When its protection failed him, the Chapter demanded the shield back, and the watch commander kept it in the armouries of Erioch, where it is a continuing cause of dispute.
