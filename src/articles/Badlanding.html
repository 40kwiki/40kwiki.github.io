Badlanding is an arid and isolated Imperial world in the [[Loki Sector]], to the galactic south-east of [[Terra]]. It was originally colonised in an unknown period, most likely before the [[Age of Strife]]. It was rediscovered by the [[Rogue Trader]] [[Uzieth Pallandaro]] in 238.M34, and Imperial colonial settlements were established on it shortly after.

Despite its harsh environment, Badlanding became an agri-world that exported water filtration and extraction machinery and respirators. It lies on the border of the Ork empire of [[Charadon]], and it is only a few solar weeks by [[Warp travel|warp travel]] from [[Rynn's World]], the homeworld of the [[Crimson Fists]].

Snagrod's attack

In mid-989.M41 the Ork warlord [[Snagrod]] attacked the isolated world with the largest [[Waaagh!|WAAAGH!]] that the region had seen since the Great Crusade. Its defence and its fate in the campaign are recorded in the [[Loki Sector]]'s registers.The date of the attack is recorded as mid-989.M41. The outcome of the siege is not recorded in full.
