The Third Sphere Expansion was a series of campaigns that the [[T'au Empire]] began in the [[Eastern Fringe]] in 997.M41. The campaigns were launched in the wake of the Great War of Confederation against the [[Orks]], and they were led by Commander [[Shadowsun]]. The T'au call the expansion the spread of the Greater Good.

The expansion won extraordinary victories over the forces of the Imperium. Among them were the Battle of Mu'gulath Bay and the [[Taros Campaign]] of 998.M41. In 999.M41 the expansion struck the [[hive world]] of Agrellan, and the Imperium answered with the Second Agrellan Campaign.

Course of the expansion

The [[T'au Empire|T'au]] moved through the Eastern Fringe world by world. They used peaceful annexation where they could, winning local populations by offering the Greater Good, and they supported the annexation with [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]] of [[Kroot]] and [[Vespid]]. Where persuasion failed, their forces fought with precision and speed.The campaigns are recorded as a series. Dates for individual actions are taken from Imperial registers.
