Barbarus was a [[Feral world|feral world]] of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], wrapped in a toxic atmosphere, on which the [[primarch]] [[Mortarion]] was raised. Its human settlers lived in the low valleys beneath the poison fog, preyed on by sorcerous Overlords who ruled from the mountain heights. Mortarion, adopted by the greatest of them, rebelled, led the people of the valleys in war against their masters, and was found by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] during the Great Crusade, in a year that the records do not give. Barbarus then became the homeworld and recruiting ground of the XIV Legion, the [[Death Guard]].

During the [[Horus Heresy]], after the Death Guard had joined [[Horus]] and fallen to [[Nurgle]], Barbarus was destroyed with [[Exterminatus|cyclonic torpedoes]] by the [[Dark Angels]], in about 013.M31, shortly before the [[Siege of Terra]]. Mortarion later remade a [[Daemon world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]] in its likeness.

Geography

Barbarus orbited a dim yellow star inside a dark nebula. Its atmosphere was a thick layer of poisonous chemicals that rose with height, so that only the lowest moors and valley basins held breathable air. Its days were short and grey, and the sun never fully broke through the fog. The jagged mountain ranges that crossed the planet rose into the toxic heights, where the Overlords built keeps of grey stone that no ordinary human could reach.

History

Settlement and the Overlords

Human colonists reached Barbarus before or during the [[Age of Strife]]; the date of settlement is unrecorded. The harsh environment reduced them to a pre-feudal peasantry. The Overlords who ruled them were immune to the poison air and wielded powers drawn from the [[Warp]]. They raised armies of stitched-together dead and shape-shifting monsters, and they fed on the valley people and experimented on them. Their true nature is unknown. Most of what is known of Barbarus comes from one source, the Stygian Scrolls of the [[Remembrancer]] Lackland Thorn, who travelled with the expeditionary fleet that found the world; Imperial scholars believe that parts of the account were edited at the Emperor's order.The settlement of Barbarus is undated. The Overlords' rule is known only from the Great Crusade period.

Mortarion

Main article: [[Mortarion]]

Mortarion's capsule came down on a battlefield moor in about 792.M30. The greatest Overlord, Necare, found the infant alive in poison air no mortal could breathe, took him as his heir and named him Mortarion, the child of death. He raised the [[Primarch|primarch]] in a keep set at the highest altitude the boy could survive, below his own fortress.

Mortarion grew curious about the valley people his master preyed upon, and finally fled down the mountain, renounced by Necare. Among the villagers he was feared at first, until he defended a village against a lesser Overlord's dead army and pursued its master up into the mists to kill him. He then trained the settlers in war, fortified their villages and built a fighting force able to bear the poison heights in sealed armour. With this army he carried the war to the Overlords' keeps and destroyed them one by one, until only Necare's fortress remained.The war against the Overlords is dated only to the years before the Emperor's arrival.

The Emperor and the Death Guard

See also: [[Death Guard]]

The Emperor reached Barbarus, in a year that is not recorded, in the last stage of this war. Mortarion refused to accept him until Necare was dead and demanded the right to kill his foster father himself. The Emperor agreed. Mortarion went alone to the summit and was overcome by the poison air before he could reach Necare; the Emperor followed him and slew the Overlord. Mortarion took command of the [[Death Guard|XIV Legion]], the Dusk Raiders, renamed it the Death Guard and merged it with his Barbaran warriors.

Barbarus became the Legion's recruiting world. Its people, hardened by generations under the fog, made aspirants of unusual endurance, and the Legion's culture of grim stoicism, toxin resistance and gas warfare came largely from the Barbaran war. Mortarion kept the Emperor's part in Necare's death to himself and resented it for the rest of the [[Great Crusade]].

The Heresy and destruction

The Death Guard declared for Horus at [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31. On the voyage to Terra, the Legion's fleet was becalmed in the Warp and struck by the Destroyer Plague, and Mortarion surrendered his Legion to [[Nurgle]] in about 012.M31. In the same period the [[Dark Angels]] under [[Lion El'Jonson]], fighting the Traitors in the Segmentum Tempestus, reached Barbarus and destroyed it with cyclonic torpedoes. Mortarion is reported to have received the news without visible emotion; many of his warriors swore to repay it on [[Caliban]].An earlier Imperial record says Barbarus was destroyed with virus bombs in the [[Great Scouring]] after the Heresy. Accounts preserved by the Dark Angels place its destruction before the Siege of Terra, and are followed here. The date c. 013.M31 is estimated.

The Plague Planet

Main article: [[Plague Planet]]

After the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31), Mortarion claimed a [[Daemon world|Daemon World]] in the [[Eye of Terror]] and shaped it into a likeness of Barbarus: a world of fog and plague, with a fortress on its highest peak in the manner of Necare's. From there he has commanded the Death Guard for ten thousand years. In the [[Era Indomitus]] he led the Legion out of the Eye to wage the [[Plague Wars]] against [[Ultramar]] (c. 000–012.M42).
