The Angels of Wrath were a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Dark Angels]] and one of the Unforgiven. Their Founding is unrecorded, though they may have been raised in the [[List of Space Marine Foundings|Tenth Founding]] in the middle of the [[35th millennium]], for in the 36th they were still counted a young Chapter.

During the [[Age of Apostasy]] the Chapter refused to allow priests of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] to take the place of its [[Chaplain|Chaplains]], and the usurper [[Goge Vandire]] sent a fleet of [[Frateris Templar]] to destroy it. It is not known whether any part of the Chapter survives in the [[42nd millennium]].

History

Refusal

[[Goge Vandire|Vandire]], who ruled both the [[Administratum]] and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]], wished to bring institutions that were exempt from the Ecclesiarchy to heel. He meant to replace the Angels of Wrath's company [[Chaplain|Chaplains]] with Ecclesiarchy missionaries, to teach the divinity of the Emperor to its [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] and give the church control of them. The Chapter was to be an experiment and an example. It would have none of it, for its loyalty was to the Emperor and the memory of its [[Primarch|primarch]], and it chose to die rather than have its ranks diluted by men who were not of its [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]].

Decimation

Vandire sent a large fleet of Frateris Templars to the Chapter's homeworld. When the fortress-monastery's sensors detected the fleet, each of the thousand warriors knew what it was. Thousands of troop transports landed. The Angels of Wrath fought fiercely, but in five hours ninety per cent of the Chapter fell, including its Grand Master Tristan Dare. Captain Cornelius Makallan, commander of the Tenth Company, found himself the last commanding officer. He ordered the remnants to withdraw so that word of Vandire's betrayal could reach the Dark Angels and the other Unforgiven. They fought through the Templars to their last ship and jumped into the Warp. Vandire then ordered an Edict of Obliteration and the Chapter was struck from the record in a Damnatio Memoriae.The year of the decimation is not recorded. It fell in the Reign of Blood, which the Administratum places in the 36th millennium.

Return

Most of the survivors were Scouts of Makallan's own company. Their officers were dead and their armour, weapons and vehicles lost, so he reorganised the remnant around the Scouts as combat squadrons. After what seemed a year in the Warp, their battle barge emerged near the sun of the [[Death world|death world]] Megiddio Primus. The [[Planetary Governor]] Ezbesde Konnar hailed them from the Metroplex, the world's only settlement. He had taken a great risk, for the ship might have been tainted. They did not know that the voyage had lasted four thousand years, that Vandire was long dead, and Konnar did not tell them. He needed them: the faceless Keepers of the Faith of the Ecclesiarchy ruled his world through the Arbitrators of the [[Adeptus Arbites]], and his [[Planetary Defence Force|planetary defence force]] could not stand against them. He played on their hatred of the Ecclesiarchy, and Makallan, believing Vandire's tyranny still ruled the Imperium, agreed to help.

Covert X

On Megiddio Primus the survivors fought a seven-month underground campaign, which Makallan called Covert X. The [[Librarian|Epistolary]] Julius Zenobi, the only Librarian, preached sedition and turned the people of the Metroplex against the Ecclesiarchy. The [[Techmarine]] Aurellius Fabricus, "the Fixer", took readily to civilian life. Konnar's other allies included an enhanced [[Abhuman|Ogryn]] named Buzbox and a young Callidus assassin, Tandi Chann, who had not been sanctioned by the [[Officio Assassinorum]]. The outcome of their war is not recorded.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter followed the [[Dark Angels]]' pattern. After its fall it fought as Scouts: its Covert X formations were made of one to eight Scout squads, with two Arkangel Scout squads in support. Makallan refused the title of Grand Master in memory of Dare and took the title of Commander.

Notable members

  • Tristan Dare: last Grand Master, killed in the decimation.
  • Cornelius Makallan: Captain of the Tenth Company, then Commander of the survivors.
  • Radfael: captain killed in the last stand.
  • Julius Zenobi: [[Librarian|Epistolary]] of the Tenth Company.
