A Librarian is a [[psyker]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]: a Space Marine whose psychic gift has been trained and turned to war. Librarians serve in the Librarius of their Chapter, which keeps its records and lore as well as its psychic strength. The Codex grades of the Librarius are, from lowest to highest, Lexicanium, Codicier, Epistolary and Chief Librarian, who leads the order.

The Librarius was first raised in the [[Space Marine Legions]] during the [[Great Crusade]]. It was disbanded by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] at the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31, after a dispute over the use of sorcery, and restored after the [[Horus Heresy]] in the [[Codex Astartes]], when the Imperium recognised that it could not face the powers of the [[Warp]] without psykers of its own. Every Chapter save those, like the [[Black Templars]], that refuse psykers on principle now keeps a Librarius.

Librarians fight with psychic powers and [[force weapon]]s that channel the psyker's will, and are protected by a [[psychic hood]] that shields them from enemy witches and from the Warp. They also serve their Chapters as communicators, seers and judges of the psychic fitness of recruits.

History

The Legion Librarii

Imperial records of the origin of the Librarii are murky. The [[Psyker|psykers]] of the Legions were called Librarians because they became the keepers of their Legion's records and history. The [[Thousand Sons]] under [[Magnus the Red]], who was himself a powerful psyker, had an unusually high rate of psychic mutation and used their powers freely. Other primarchs accepted Magnus's ideas in their own Legions, and several Legions kept a Librarius, among them the [[Ultramarines]], the [[Dark Angels]], the [[White Scars]] and the [[Salamanders]]. [[Leman Russ]] of the [[Space Wolves]] refused to deal with what he saw as unnatural witchery, and his Legion kept instead its Rune Priests, who drew on the traditions of [[Fenris]].

The Council of Nikaea

Main article: [[Council of Nikaea]]

The growing use of psychic powers by the Thousand Sons, whom many accused of sorcery, led the Emperor to call the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31. A group of Librarians from the other Legions proposed a compromise, in which psykers would be trained to serve mankind and sorcery would be outlawed as heresy. The Emperor rejected it when Magnus was shown to have gone on studying sorcery despite an earlier warning. The Emperor ordered every Legion to disband its Librarius and its Librarians to return to the ranks and never use their powers again.

The Horus Heresy and restoration

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the traitor Legions used sorcery freely, and the loyalist commanders found themselves without defence against it. Several loyal Legions restored their Librarians in defiance of the edict, among them the Raven Guard under [[Corvus Corax]]. The Imperium found that Chaos could not be defeated unless the loyalists could draw on the power of the Warp as well.

After the Heresy the compromise proposed at Nikaea was adopted as law, and it has stood ever since as the policy of the Imperium on psychic powers. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s [[Codex Astartes]] of c. 021.M31 set the Librarius in its place in a Chapter, with its grades and duties.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Ultima Founding]] have Librarians of their own. A Primaris Librarian serves as his Firstborn counterpart does, as a combat psyker and keeper of lore, and differs mainly in his wargear.

Grades

The [[Codex Astartes]] divides the Librarius into four grades. A Librarian advances by the growth of his power and the approval of his seniors:

GradeRole
LexicaniumThe novice grade. A Lexicanium learns to master his powers and serves as an assistant in the archives and on the battlefield.
CodicierA trained warrior-psyker who has proved his control. Codiciers organise the work of the Lexicani and usually go on to train as Epistolaries.
EpistolaryA Librarian of great power and experience, able to use more than one power at a time in battle. Epistolaries are also the Chapter's psychic communications officers, directing its fleets and landings.
Chief LibrarianThe master of the Librarius and keeper of the Chapter's lore, a member of the council of its senior officers and the closest adviser of the [[Chapter Master]].

Powers and wargear

A Librarian's powers depend on his gifts and his Chapter's teaching. The Librarius classifies them by discipline. Common disciplines include divination, the reading of the future; telepathy, the reading and control of minds; telekinesis; pyromancy; and the manipulation of the Warp's energy in bolts and shields. Some [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] keep disciplines of their own, drawn from the powers of their primarch's [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]: the [[Blood Angels]]' Sanguinary discipline and the [[Space Wolves]]' tempest magic among them.

The [[psychic hood]] is a crystalline matrix worn about the head, which focuses the Librarian's power and lets him block the powers of hostile [[Psyker|psykers]]. The force weapon, a sword, staff, axe or mace with a psychically conductive core, kills by channelling the Librarian's will into the foe. Codex Librarians wear blue armour, the colour of the Librarius, with their Chapter's insignia on one shoulder; many Chapters keep their own colours.

Chapter variants

  • The [[Space Wolves]] keep Rune Priests, whose masters claim to draw on the spirits of [[Fenris]]. The greatest of them in the 41st and 42nd millennia is Njal Stormcaller.
  • The [[White Scars]] call their Librarians Stormseers, after the shamans of [[Chogoris]].
  • The [[Dark Angels]] are led in psychic matters by Ezekiel, Grand Master of Librarians, keeper of the Book of Salvation.
  • The [[Black Templars]] have no Librarius, because they hold [[Psyker|psykers]] to be unclean.
  • The [[Grey Knights]] are a Chapter in which every warrior is a psyker.
