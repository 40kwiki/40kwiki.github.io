A Techmarine is a Space Marine of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] who has been trained by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] to maintain, repair and command his Chapter's war machines. Techmarines serve in the Chapter's Armoury, which keeps its tanks, aircraft, [[Dreadnought]]s, wargear and the systems of its [[fortress-monastery]]. The Armoury is led by the Master of the Forge, who sits in the Chapter Command.

Techmarines hold a double loyalty. They are sworn brothers of their Chapter but have also been initiated, on [[Mars]], into the mysteries of the [[Cult Mechanicus]], whose god is the [[Omnissiah]]. The arrangement rests on the ancient alliance between [[Terra]] and Mars, the [[Treaty of Olympus]] of 739.M30, and on the [[Codex Astartes]], which made the Armoury a part of every Chapter. Most [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] regard their Techmarines with respect and some unease, as men who are not wholly of the Chapter.

Techmarines wear red armour, or red markings on their Chapter's colours, and are recognised by the [[servo-harness]] they bear, a set of mechanical arms, tools and weapons mounted on the back of their armour.

History

The Legions and the Mechanicum

Under the [[Treaty of Olympus]], signed in 739.M30, the [[Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]] agreed to arm the Emperor's armies, and the [[Space Marine Legions]] were among the first to benefit. The Legions' own artificers, called Techmarines or forge-masters in several Legions, were trained on Mars and returned to their Legions with the knowledge to maintain their vehicles and armour. The [[Iron Hands]] under [[Ferrus Manus]] and the [[Iron Warriors]] under [[Perturabo]] were famed for their close bonds with the forges, while the [[Salamanders]] kept their own tradition of smithcraft from [[Nocturne]].

The [[Schism of Mars]], which began in 005.M31, split the Mechanicum between loyalists and the [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Dark Mechanicum]] of [[Kelbor-Hal]]. Techmarines of the loyal Legions fought against the traitor [[Forge world|forge worlds]], and some of the traitor Legions' Techmarines became the first [[Warsmith]]s and [[Heretek]]s of the [[Chaos]] Legions.

The Codex Astartes

Main article: [[Codex Astartes]]

The [[Codex Astartes]] of c. 019.M31 confirmed the arrangement. Each Chapter was to send chosen battle-brothers to Mars, where they would spend years in the forges learning the rites of the machine before returning to serve in the Armoury. In return the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] owed the Mechanicus respect for its doctrines and a share of their [[gene-seed]] for its vaults. The Chapters' Techmarines thus became one of the few lasting bonds between the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] and the priesthood of Mars.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The [[Ultima Founding]] of 999.M41 was itself a work of the Mechanicus, overseen by [[Belisarius Cawl]], and brought with it new vehicles, weapons and armour that the Chapters' Armouries had to learn. [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] Techmarines, trained in Cawl's own forges, joined the Chapters from the first years of the [[42nd millennium]].

Training

A battle-brother chosen for the Armoury is usually one who has shown an affinity for machines. He travels to Mars, or to a [[Forge world|forge world]] allied to his Chapter, and spends years in apprenticeship to the [[Tech-priest]]s. He learns to tend [[Omnissiah|machine spirits]], to perform the rites of repair and activation, and to understand the technology of the [[Standard Template Construct]]. He receives bionic implants and the servo-harness, and is initiated into the lower mysteries of the Cult Mechanicus. Imperial records note that some Techmarines return to their [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] changed in manner and speech, and that the Chapters' [[Chaplain|Chaplains]] watch them closely for signs of divided loyalty.

Duties

  • Maintenance. Techmarines perform the rites that keep a Chapter's [[Rhino]]s, [[Land Raider]]s, [[Thunderhawk]]s and [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] in service, and the repairs to its [[Power armour|power armour]] and weapons.
  • Battle. In the field they repair damaged vehicles under fire, command batteries of artillery such as the Thunderfire cannon, and lead the [[servitor]]s of the Armoury.
  • Fortress. They tend the systems of the fortress-monastery, its defences and its void shields.
  • Relics. They are the keepers of the Chapter's relic armour and weapons, whose [[Omnissiah|machine spirits]] they must placate.

Master of the Forge

The senior Techmarine of a [[Space Marine Chapter|Codex Chapter]] is the Master of the Forge, a member of the Chapter Command beside the [[Chief Librarian]], the [[Chaplain|Master of Sanctity]] and the [[Chief Apothecary]]. He commands the Armoury and all its Techmarines, [[Servitor|servitors]] and serfs, and he is responsible for the Chapter's whole stock of war machines. Many Masters of the Forge are also the Chapter's chief liaison with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] and with its allied [[Forge world|forge worlds]].

Chapter variants

  • The [[Iron Hands]] give their Techmarines, the Iron Fathers, great authority; the Chapter is ruled by an Iron Council in which they hold many seats.
  • The [[Space Wolves]] call their Techmarines Iron Priests. They are trained on [[Fenris]] and not on Mars, in an arrangement the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] has tolerated rather than approved.
  • The [[Salamanders]] hold the craft of the forge as a sacred duty of every warrior; their Techmarines lead a Chapter of smiths.

Standing in the Chapter

A Techmarine's double oath is a frequent cause of tension. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] regards him as an initiate of its own cult, owing obedience to Mars on matters of technology, while his Chapter regards him as a brother whose first loyalty is to his [[Chapter Master]]. Most [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] resolve the matter by custom: the Techmarine keeps the rites of the Omnissiah in the Armoury but takes his orders in battle from his commanders. In the [[Schism of Mars]] of 005.M31 and in later disputes between the Chapters and the [[Forge world|forge worlds]], some Techmarines have had to choose between the two.

Techmarines are also among the few Space Marines who regularly deal with outsiders: tech-priests, the [[Collegia Titanica|Titan Legions]], the Navy's shipwrights and the magi of the forge worlds that supply their Chapters.
