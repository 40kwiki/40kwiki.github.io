Thorian Reformation
Reform of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]]
Datec. 379.M36 (estimated)
Context[[Age of Apostasy]]
Led byThe preacher [[Sebastian Thor]]
EffectEnded the Ecclesiarchy's right to maintain armies

The Thorian Reformation was a reform of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] carried out after the [[Age of Apostasy]] in the [[36th millennium]]. It took from the Ecclesiarchy the right to maintain armies of "men under arms", which is why the Ecclesiarchy's own warriors are all women.

The reform followed the tyranny of [[Goge Vandire]], the [[Master of the Administratum]] who had seized the office of [[Ecclesiarch]] as well. Vandire used regiments loyal to him to enforce his [[Reign of Blood]] across the Imperium. Other regiments marched under the banner of the preacher [[Sebastian Thor]], and with the aid of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Adeptus Custodes]] they brought the tyrant down.
