The Fire Angels are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], raised in the Twenty-fifth Founding of the late [[40th millennium]] from [[Ultramarines]] [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. They hold the [[Hive world|hive world]] of Lorin Alpha in the western [[Segmentum Solar]] and are best remembered for their part in the [[Badab War]]. They have no special links with the wider family of Ultramarines successors.

They are unusually active and open in their faith in the [[Imperial Cult]], which has won them backing in the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and allies among the cardinals. Their detractors in other Chapters see this as unseemly and a threat to the Space Marines' independence.

History

Founding

The Fire Angels were raised by order of the [[High Lords of Terra]] in the Twenty-fifth Founding and given fief over Lorin Alpha. Their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] is [[Ultramarines]], but they name no ancestral Chapter. Their first officers and trainers were chosen from several Ultramarines successors. Initiates are drawn from the [[Planetary Defence Force|planetary defence force]]'s military caste and from the kill-gangs of the sub-hives, and are chosen three times a year in the Test of Fire, a trial of combat, fear and endurance run by the Master of Recruits under the eyes of the [[Chaplain|Chaplains]].

Wars of the Chapter

The first great campaign was the Fenright Tithe Wars, a crusade against the civil wars of the Cal-Sek, Nepthis and Sadusyne Sectors, in which the Fire Angels fought on the heretic world of Jhoppa against rebel "juggernaut" companies, matching Predators and Razorbacks against rebel Leman Russ and Malcador tanks.The Wars are dated 760.M39 to 411.M40 in the records, before the Chapter was founded. Scholars think a clerical error has misdated them by about a millennium. In 143–151.M41, in the Magdellan Prime Civil War, they relieved the [[Adepta Sororitas]] [[Alicia Dominica|Order of the Ebon Chalice]], whose preceptory had held for years against the Cult of Balthalamus. In 666.M41 the [[Inquisition]] called them to fight the warband The Sanctified on the cemetery worlds of Grand Al'gul; the [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]] Mathias Dee burned himself to avoid possession.

The Badab War

Answering the Inquisition, the Fire Angels came to the [[Badab Sector]] with seven companies. Their largest and costliest battle was for Sagan, after which they could no longer serve as a front line force. With a contingent of the [[Sons of Medusa]] they were sent to suppress the [[Mantis Warriors|Endymion Cluster]]. The exterminations there by the Carcharodons led them to lodge a formal grievance with the Loyalist command. In 910.M41, reduced and unwilling to back down, they withdrew from the war rather than risk fighting a fellow Chapter.

Organisation and doctrine

The Fire Angels follow the [[Codex Astartes]] and treat it as a military treatise, as does the aristocratic military tradition of their world. They are young and own few relics, so they rely on easily made Codex patterns such as the [[Rhino]] and Predator, and field most of their First Company as Sternguard. The few Terminator suits, [[Land Raider|Land Raiders]] and [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] given by [[Ultramarines]] successors at their founding are used only in the direst emergencies. The factories of Lorin Alpha turn out the vehicles, and the Chapter prefers mobile mechanised war to Drop Pod assault.

Their faith has taken them to [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] crusades and wars of faith, beside the Sisters, and the Synod and the [[Ordo Hereticus]] often seek their help. Their relic flamer, the Light of Castigation, was given after the Hengistus Crusade by a grateful cardinal.

Notable members

  • Consul-Master Uriaens: present [[Chapter Master]].
  • Consul-Master Haran Stark: died in the mid-41st millennium fighting a [[Daemon|Lord of Change]] to defend the Narthex of Penitents.
  • Praetor Tarnus Vale: captain of the Third Company, a master of mechanised war.
  • Malghrav: Assault Marine who led a [[Deathwatch]] strike force in the Hadex Anomaly.
