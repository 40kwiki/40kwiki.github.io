The Iron Ravens are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] made up entirely of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. They were created from the gene-seed of the [[Raven Guard]] in the [[Ultima Founding]] of c. 999.M41. The Chapter specialises in sudden drop assaults: its 10th Company scouts may spend solar months gathering intelligence, and then the main body descends in Drop Pods with such precision that the war is decided by one blow. The Chapter calls the tactic the Blade of Corax.

The Iron Ravens are not the first Chapter of the name. The earlier Iron Ravens were a Second Founding successor of the Raven Guard that was lost to the Warp.

History

The earlier Iron Ravens

The first Iron Ravens were raised in the [[Second Founding]] and made their home on the world of Taltos. They vanished when Taltos was swallowed by a Warp storm, and Imperial records do not say when. After the [[Great Rift]] opened, the [[Inquisition]] and the Raven Guard had to consider that Taltos had returned, and that the Chapter on it had turned to the worship of [[Nurgle]], many becoming Chaos Space Marine Raptors. Whether any of the old Chapter lives is not known, and whether the new Chapter descends from it by name only or by inheritance is not recorded.Earlier Imperial accounts treated the first Iron Ravens as lost with Taltos and gave no later history. The Chapter of the Ultima Founding carries the name and badge and is the one that appears in modern records.

The Unnumbered Sons

Main article: [[Primaris Space Marines]]

Many Iron Ravens Primaris Marines first served in [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s Unnumbered Sons, and fought in the early years of the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. The experience taught the Iron Ravens to be more flexible in tactics and doctrine than other Raven Guard successors, since they fought for years beside Marines of other lineages. They are not wedded to stealth as the parent Legion is. They hold that the true skill is to gather enough intelligence to fit tactics to any enemy and any ground.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

As a newly raised Chapter, the Iron Ravens are determined to master their own fate. A Chaplain of the Chapter, Kaledir, serves in the [[Deathwatch]] at the watch fortress Eye of Octos, where he took part in the fighting against a splinter of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] on Death of Bianzeer in the [[Octarius War]].

Organisation and heraldry

The Iron Ravens' colours are silver, blue and red. The higher an Iron Raven rises in rank, the more blue he wears, and veterans have blue arms. Every rank is shown by helmet colour: battle-brothers have a single red stripe, and sergeants, lieutenants and other officers other patterns of stripes. The shoulder trim shows company by the [[Codex Astartes]] scheme, and the white specialty symbol is on the right pauldron, with squad number on the left knee plate. The Chapter badge is a stylised white raven on a field of blue.
