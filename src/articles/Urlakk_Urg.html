Urlakk Urg was the Overlord of the greatest [[Ork]] empire known to the [[Great Crusade]]. The empire was centred on [[Ullanor]]. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] and nine [[Legiones Astartes|Legions]] destroyed it in the [[Ullanor Crusade]], c. 999.M30 – 000.M31, and the victory was marked by the Triumph of Ullanor.

Urlakk Urg's empire was the largest Ork realm that the Imperium had met. Its warbands were gathered under a single overlord, and its fleets and armies made it a threat to the Crusade's conquests in the region.

Ullanor Crusade

The Crusade against the Ork empire opened c. 999.M30. [[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]] fought across [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] and the surrounding systems, and the empire was broken in the fighting. The Triumph of Ullanor marked its end in 000.M31.The dates of the campaign are estimated from the Great Crusade's records; the Triumph is recorded as 000.M31.
