The First Black Crusade was the first of the great assaults led by [[Ezekyle Abaddon]], later known as [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], out of the [[Eye of Terror]] into Imperial space. It was the first major [[Chaos]] incursion since the defeat of the [[Traitor Legions]] at the [[Siege of Terra]], and the campaigns of the [[Great Scouring]] had driven them back into the Eye.

Purpose

Abaddon meant the Crusade to show the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and the [[Chaos Gods]] that he was the worthy successor to [[Horus]]. Through his deeds he won the favour of the [[Ruinous Powers]] and a powerful [[Daemon Weapon]], which made him the mortal champion of [[Chaos Undivided]].
