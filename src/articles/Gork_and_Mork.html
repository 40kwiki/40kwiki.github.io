Gork and Mork are the gods of the [[Orks]]. In Ork tradition they are the echoes of the Greenskin species in the [[Empyrean]], and the fathers of the [[Waaagh!]] and of all Ork warfare. Gork is held to be brutal but cunning, while Mork is held to be cunning but brutal. Ork belief holds that the two are the same god seen from two sides.

Imperial scholars record the Orks' belief that the two gods reward strength in battle and punish those who fail to fight. Imperial theologians treat the pair as a case of the [[Warp]] giving form to the beliefs of a warlike species, and they do not accept the claim that the gods are real beings.
