St. Josmane's Hope was the ninth world of [[Cadia]] and a prison world of the [[Cadian Sector]]. It was destroyed during the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41, one of the most debated losses of the Imperium's recent history.

The world held prisoners sentenced by the Cadian courts, and its defences were kept by the Cadian garrison under the [[Lord Castellan]] [[Ursarkar E. Creed]]. Its name became a byword in the Imperium for a sacrifice made to hold back the enemy.

The Fall of Cadia

Main article: [[Fall of Cadia]]

During the Fall of Cadia, the [[Black Legion]] and their allies attacked the world. The defenders held for months. When the world was about to fall, [[Ursarkar Creed|Creed]] ordered a generatorium on the planet overloaded, and the world was destroyed. Most Imperial accounts record this as the act that ended the defence.

Some accounts differ. A strike force of Adeptus Astartes and Inquisitors is also recorded as having destroyed the world, and the records of the Cadian Sector do not agree on which account is correct. The Imperium has treated the question as open.Imperial accounts differ on who destroyed the world. The Creed account is the most common in Ecclesiarchy and Astra Militarum records.

Legacy

The destruction of St. Josmane's Hope is cited in Imperial doctrine as an example of an [[Exterminatus]] carried out by the defenders of a world rather than by an Inquisitorial fleet. The planet's name appears on the memorial rolls of the [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian regiments]].
