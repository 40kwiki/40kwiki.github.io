A magos (plural magi) is a [[Tech-priest]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] who is recognised as a master of a technological or scientific discipline. The title is earned through many years of service to the [[Omnissiah]] in one field of study. Magi keep the advanced technology of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] working, and they carry out the little scientific research that still takes place in the Imperium after the [[Horus Heresy]].

The rank is a mark of mastery, not of rule. A magos is judged by his peers within his field, and a Tech-priest who is seen to have fully mastered a discipline may be called an [[Archmagos]]. Imperial records treat the meaning of the higher titles as unclear, but they agree that they indicate greater seniority, standing and experience.

Divisions and specialisations

The Adeptus Mechanicus is divided into many specialist groups, called divisiones. A magos of each division takes a title that names his specialisation. A magos biologis studies biological and genetic systems as if they were organic machines, and believes they are as blessed by the Machine God as any inorganic device. A magos dominus oversees the operation of [[Titan]]s and is more common in wartime, when many senior Tech-priests turn their efforts to military matters and take on the title. Other divisions include the magos explorator, who leads expeditions to recover lost technology, the magos logis, who handles logistics and calculation, and the magos cybernetica, who designs and maintains servitors and cybernetic bodies.

Magi are fully Human, although many replace much of their flesh with bionics. They are not servitors, which are created to perform heavy labour. Imperial scholars regard a magos as a Tech-priest who has advanced far enough to become a holder of knowledge and authority within his discipline.

Rank and authority

Within the magi, the ranks are shifting and complicated. Imperial records list the highest rank as the Prime Hermeticon and the lowest as the Data-Predator, with many intermediate titles. An Overseer-Imperator is recorded as a rank of uncertain position.

Above the magi stand the [[Fabricator-General]], who leads the Adeptus Mechanicus across the galaxy and serves as its Magos Mechanicus, and the [[Fabricator Locum]], who is the second-highest Tech-priest of [[Mars]] and of the [[Forge World]]s.
