A Radical is an [[Inquisitor]] who uses methods, tools and ideas that the [[Imperial Cult]] forbids. Radicals turn the darkness of [[Chaos]], the xenos, the witch and the daemon against themselves. They walk a path that few of their peers will follow, and they risk their souls on it in the hope of saving a million human worlds.

The label is almost always given by others. Inquisitors seldom choose the path on purpose. The horrors they meet lead them to make a decision for the need of a moment, and then another, until a doctrinal compromise, a forbidden text consulted or the babble of a condemned heretic heeded has changed their method for good. Whatever they believe themselves to be, whether realist, relativist or keeper of a truth others cannot bear, they share the belief that all they do is done for the Imperium.

Radicals and Puritans

The Inquisition divides itself into two broad schools. The Puritans hold to strict dogma and see any deviation as suspect. Radicals say that every Inquisitor begins as a Puritan and ends as a Radical, as the realities of the galaxy wear down their certainty. Puritans answer that the extreme Radicals have been corrupted by the power they use and the knowledge they carry.

Most Radicals hide their views in mixed company and pursue their aims away from the sight of the more puritan. A few are outspoken and make enemies trying to convert others. Inquisitors are usually wary of Radical views in their acolytes until those acolytes have proved themselves in many investigations.

Factions

Radical belief varies widely, from Inquisitors who turn the Warp against its inhabitants to those who would shatter the Imperium itself.

Xanthism and the Horusians

Xanthism is the most powerful and openly Radical grouping. It advocates weapons of the Warp, such as daemon-possessed swords and daemonhosts, to turn Chaos against itself. It is one of the oldest philosophies of the Inquisition and is named after Inquisitor-Master Zaranchek Xanthus, who was executed as a heretic in the 32nd millennium for knowing too much of the Warp. Open Xanthites alone, among Inquisitors, may be refused the service of the [[Grey Knights]] when they request them, for Puritans fear they would lead the Grey Knights into a trap.

The Horusians are a branch of Xanthism. They wish to create a new leader for Mankind and hold that the power of [[Horus]] was wasted. They believe that the limitless power of Chaos could be bound into a ruler who would unite humanity. Even other Radicals regard them as dangerous.

Recongregators and Istvaanians

The Recongregators believe that the Imperium has grown corrupt and stagnant after millennia, and that only change can save it. They work quietly to engineer events toward progress and are among the least visible of the major factions. The Puritan Amalathians oppose them most. The Istvaanians go further. They take their name from the atrocity on [[Isstvan III]] and hold that the Imperium needs constant strife to survive, so they provoke conflict and revolt wherever they go.

Oblationists

The Oblationists were born in the "War of Brass" that wracked the [[Calixis Sector]] in the late 40th millennium. They are an oath-bound society of fanatics who wield Warp-forged weapons and bind daemons with a self-hating fury, and who believe that all other Inquisitors are impure. They accept no other Radical's use of such methods.

Other factions

  • Ocularians, diviners of the Calixis Sector obsessed with prophecy and omens. Their origins are lost in myth.
  • Polypsykana, who believe Mankind will ascend into a psychic species and shelter psykers from Puritan pyres. They are especially active in the Calixis Sector, where the Tyrant Star raises the number of psykers born.
  • Xenos Hybris, who seek alien technology and cooperation with xenos against Chaos, and clash with the Monodominants.
  • Plutonians, a sect of the [[Ordo Malleus]] allied with the [[Exorcists]], who carried out deliberate daemonic possession of Exorcist initiates in the Halls of Tempering beneath the Basilica Malefex on Banish. The Chapter's Imperial Fists gene-seed resisted the possession, and the survivors became exceptional daemon-fighters. The sect is now gone.
  • Antiquarti, Casophilians, Libricarans and Phaenonites, smaller sects. The Phaenonites have long been declared Excommunicate Traitoris by the Ordo Malleus.
  • Ultra-radicals, an unnamed sect of resurrectionists who hold that the Emperor must die in body to rise as a force against Chaos. The [[Adeptus Custodes]] hunt them down whenever they are found.
