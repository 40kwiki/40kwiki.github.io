Ortan Cassius was a [[Chaplain]] of the [[Ultramarines]] during the [[Tyranid]] invasions of the late 41st millennium. He fought in the [[Battle of Macragge]] during the First [[Tyrannic War]], and he is recorded as one of the warriors who held the Chapter together in the defence of the Chapter's homeworld.

Cassius is remembered in the Ultramarines' records for his skill with the Chapter's rites and his steadiness under pressure.

Battle of Macragge

Main article: [[Battle of Macragge]]

During the Battle of Macragge, Cassius served alongside the [[First Captain]] [[Saul Invictus]] and the Third Company, in a battle that annihilated the First Company. The Chapter's losses in that battle were among the heaviest of its history.

Ichar IV

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Kraken]]

In the Second Tyrannic War, in 992–993.M41, Cassius returned to the field with [[Marneus Calgar]] on the hive world of [[Ichar IV]]. The two cut the [[Swarmlord]]'s synaptic control, and Calgar killed the Swarmlord with his own hands. The defeat of the Kraken assault on the planet followed.
