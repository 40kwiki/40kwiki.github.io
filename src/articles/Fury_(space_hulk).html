Fury is a [[Space hulk|space hulk]] whose Mechanicus crew kept a hold on its core for many millennia. It is one of the hulks that the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has claimed for itself rather than leave to the Imperial fleets or to the [[Orks]].

The hulk is cited in Imperial records as an example of the Mechanicus practice of keeping a hulk in service instead of destroying it. Its crew held the core against raiders and against the Imperial Navy, and the core's technology was taken into the Mechanicus archives.

Mechanicus claim

Hulks often carry technology from the [[Age of Technology]] that the Imperium cannot make again. The Mechanicus takes part in almost every hulk operation, and it has sent its own forces to hulks it wished to keep. Fury is the clearest case of this. Its crew did not leave the wreck after its first capture, and the date of that capture is not recorded.The date of the first capture is not recorded. Mechanicus records say only that the crew held the hulk for "many millennia".
