Dimmamar is a [[Cardinal world]] on the edge of the Imperium of Mankind. It is known to Imperial historians as the home of the preacher [[Sebastian Thor]], whose opposition to the rule of [[Goge Vandire]] began the first major challenge to the Administratum's control of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] in the 36th millennium.

Thor was born on Dimmamar c. 238.M36. He spoke against the injustice of Vandire's rule and called on the faithful to restore the church's independence. His followers protected him from the assassins sent to kill him, and many of the assassins were themselves converted. The world's cardinal was unable to stop the movement.Dates for Thor's life are estimated from the Ecclesiarchy's records of his preaching.

Background

At the start of the 36th millennium the [[Ecclesiarch]] was [[Paulis III]], a weak man controlled by the [[Master of the Administratum]], Goge Vandire. Vandire had him executed for heresy c. 218.M36 and took the Ecclesiarchy for himself. Dimmamar lay far from the centres of power, and it was there that the first open resistance began.
