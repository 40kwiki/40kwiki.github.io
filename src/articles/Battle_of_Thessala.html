The Battle of Thessala was fought on 121.M31 during the [[Horus Heresy]]. The [[Ultramarines]] and five other successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] fought the [[Emperor's Children]] on [[Thessala]] in [[Ultramar]]. It is the battle in which [[Roboute Guilliman]] was struck down by the Emperor's Children's [[Primarch|primarch]], [[Fulgrim]].

The battle is recorded as a defeat that the Loyalists turned into a withdrawal. Guilliman was wounded and infected, and the Ultramarines were forced to leave the field. The Imperial records treat it as one of the darkest moments of the Realm's defence.

Course of the battle

The [[Emperor's Children]] attacked the Ultramarine positions with a force that the Loyalists had not expected. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] led the Ultramarines in person and was struck in single combat by [[Fulgrim]]. The [[Novamarines]] and the other successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] fought beside the Ultramarines, and a stele on Thessala records their names.

Aftermath

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] was carried from the field and treated in the Ultramarine fleet. His recovery took many years. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] continued to guard [[Ultramar]] under the command of Chapter officers during his absence.Guilliman's recovery is recorded as lasting several years. The precise length is estimated from later records.
