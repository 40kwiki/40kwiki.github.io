Nemeton is an [[Imperial]] ocean world and [[feral world]] in the [[Elara's Veil]] sector of the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It lies in the Annwyn Reaches sub-sector of the Elara's Veil nebula. Its star system is called the Ophion System by some records and the Avalon System by others, and Imperial scholars do not agree on which name is correct. Nemeton is also the homeworld of the [[Emperor's Spears]], a [[Space Marine Chapter]] tasked with protecting the twenty inhabited worlds of Elara's Veil.

Nemeton is a world covered by enormous oceans. Its land is small, mountainous and heavily forested, with marshy jungle near the equator. Rain falls constantly across most of the surface, and the climate is temperate to nearly tropical across the globe. The Human population holds the rain to be sacred.

History

In the late 30th millennium the [[Ultramarines Legion]] brought Nemeton into Imperial compliance during the [[Great Crusade]]. The people of the world later rejected the advanced technology and urban life brought by the Imperium. They returned to the pre-industrial tribal life of the [[Age of Strife]].

The Human population is divided among the Twelve Tribes of Nemeton, a monoculture of interacting tribes who share beliefs, customs and a language. Because Elara's Veil, a crimson nebula, fills most of Nemeton's night sky, the tribes believe that the rain is a sacred gift born from the great lights of the heavens. Their mythology centres on the legendary hero Elara.

Society and the Emperor's Spears

Elara is worshipped by most peoples of the Veil. Imperial records say the figure may be the name of an early Imperial commander or a saint of the [[Imperial Cult]] who first brought the region into compliance. To the tribes she is the Emperor's bride, whose crimson funeral veil became the nebula that reddens the stars.

The relationship between the Chapter and the people of Nemeton is unlike that of most Space Marine Chapters and their recruiting worlds. The tribes see the Space Marines as the spirits of lost children, who take their young before their time and leave ghosts to be mourned. The Emperor's Spears are nonetheless the foremost defenders of the region.

Defences

Nemeton is well defended. A large orbital minefield guards its approaches, and many of the rocks and ice fragments of its rings have been turned into weapons platforms that bombard attackers with torpedoes. The [[Forge Moon]] of [[Bellona]] adds further defence. The Emperor's Spears work closely with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] leaders of Bellona, who build much of the Chapter's wargear and voidships, and together they guard the Elara's Veil region.
