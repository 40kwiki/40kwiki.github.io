Hive Fleet Gorgon was a splinter of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] that attacked the [[T'au Empire]] and the Imperium's frontier between 899 and 903.M41. Its colonies at Sha'draig, Ho'sarn and Ka'mais were devastated, and the T'au and the [[Astra Militarum]] fought the swarm together.

Gorgon is one of the fleets of the [[Tyranid]] invasions recorded between the First and Second [[Tyrannic Wars]]. Imperial records count it among the swarms that tested the Imperium's frontier before the wider invasions of the late [[41st millennium]].

War with the T'au

From 899 to 903.M41 the [[T'au Empire|T'au empire]] faced Hive Fleet Gorgon on its eastern flank. The T'au are not friends of the Imperium, but the two forces fought the swarm together in the same years. The [[Astra Militarum]] sent forces to the region in support.
