Hypnoth is a [[Forge World]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It was besieged by the [[Necrons]] of [[Imotekh the Stormlord]] in 910–911.M41, and the siege is one of the major campaigns of Imotekh's wars in the Imperium.

The forge world's defence was held for several months by its own forces, which Imperial accounts describe as the chief reason the Necron campaign did not reach further into the sector.

Siege of Hypnoth

Imotekh's campaign was held for several months by the defence of Hypnoth. Imperial records count the siege as one of the first major checks on the Necron advance in the 41st millennium. The forge world's defenders were supported by Imperial forces from the surrounding systems.

Schrödinger VII

In 930.M41 Imotekh's army fell on the ice world of Schrödinger VII. The [[Black Templars]] Marshal [[Helbrecht]] had sworn an oath of vengeance after that attack, and his oath is one of the reasons the Black Templars took part in the later campaigns against the Necrons.
