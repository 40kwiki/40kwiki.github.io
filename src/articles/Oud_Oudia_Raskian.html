Oud Oudia Raskian was the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]] at the time of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41. He was among the most heavily augmented of all the [[High Lords of Terra]], so much machine that he could be brought to the Great Chamber of the council only after special works of construction. For more than five hundred years he led the investigations of the Mechanicus into the failing mechanisms of the [[Golden Throne]] without finding a remedy.

Raskian disappeared soon after the [[Fall of Cadia]] and was presumed dead. [[Xasandera Valdet]] later became Fabricator-General in his place.

Background

No record open to Imperial scholars describes where Raskian was made or how he rose in the priesthood of the Machine God. The date of his accession is likewise unrecorded. The span of his work on the Throne, more than five centuries before 999.M41, places the start of his work before the 500th year of the 41st millennium, long before the Thirteenth Black Crusade.The five centuries of investigation are recorded as Raskian's own work. The record does not say when he became Fabricator-General within that span.

The Fabricator-General speaks for Mars among the High Lords as an ally and not a subject, bound to Terra only by the [[Treaty of Olympus]]. It is a post of great authority within the priesthood and little power over the other forge worlds.

The Golden Throne

Main article: [[Golden Throne]]

The Golden Throne has sustained the [[Emperor of Mankind]] since 014.M31, and the Mechanicus has tended it ever since alongside the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]]. By the late 41st millennium its mechanisms were failing. Raskian was the Fabricator-General charged with finding the cause. He did not succeed. Imperial records give no account of what his investigations found, and the Mechanicus has not published them.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In 999.M41 the Chancellor [[Alexei Lev Tieron|Lev Tieron]] sought to release the Adeptus Custodes from their oath to remain on Terra, so that they could be sent against [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. Raskian opposed him. The reasons he gave are not published.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade ended with the destruction of [[Cadia]]. Soon afterwards Raskian vanished. The records do not say where he went or how he was lost. He was presumed dead.The Imperial record states only that he vanished and was presumed dead. No account of how, or where, has been published.

Succession

A faction of the Synod of Mars then petitioned the representatives of [[Belisarius Cawl]] to take the office and to lead Mars in a direction more independent of Terra. Cawl declined, and [[Roboute Guilliman]], who had returned to rule as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], refused every urging that he be appointed, since an appointment imposed from Terra would have provoked a second Martian civil war. The office passed to Valdet in the early 42nd millennium.
