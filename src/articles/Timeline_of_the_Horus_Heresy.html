The Timeline of the Horus Heresy lists the dated events of the [[Horus Heresy]], the civil war in which the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] and nine of the eighteen known [[Space Marine Legions]] rebelled against the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. It begins with the fall of Horus on [[Davin]] in 004.M31, follows the war from the [[Isstvan III]] atrocity in 005.M31 to the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31, and ends with the [[Great Scouring]], the [[Codex Astartes]] and the [[Second Founding]] in 021.M31.

No complete record of the war survives. Warp storms, above all the [[Ruinstorm]] of 007–011.M31, broke the reckoning of time across half the galaxy, so that fleets and worlds kept calendars that differ by months or years. Many records were sealed after the war by the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and later by the [[Inquisition]]. The dates below follow the Administratum's reconciled chronology. Where the Adepta disagree, the other dates are noted. Dates that rest only on the order of events are marked "c.".Where no record gives a year, the date is estimated from the sequence of events.
