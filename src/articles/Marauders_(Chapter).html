The Marauders are a loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a [[Second Founding]] successor of the [[White Scars]], according to the Apocrypha of Davio, written in the [[33rd millennium]]. From the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of the Marauders was raised the [[Mantis Warriors]], the Chapter that grew infamous in the [[Badab War]].

The Marauders are isolationist. They will fight alongside other Imperial forces readily enough, but they stay aloof and share only the information they must to carry out the campaign. They are known for marksmanship: they set ambushes, strike the enemy with massed firepower, and then sweep down on the ragged survivors.

History

Imperial records of the Chapter's early centuries are few. Like its parent Legion it was shaped by the lightning warfare of the [[Chogoris]] steppes, and it later produced the successor that became the [[Mantis Warriors]]. The Marauders appear in the record chiefly in joint campaigns.

Relief of Ferrax Hive (071.M41)

When Hive Ferrax on [[Thracian Primaris]] fell to a cannibal cult that rose out of the lawless underslums, a number of nearby [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] mustered to the hive's relief. Under Commander Zha'Khan of the Marauders, the Thracian Response Force deployed to the wastes around it. The [[Chaos cult|cultists]], maddened by hunger, surged out in a vast horde and were destroyed by the massed guns of the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]].

Reconquest of the Zypher Sub-Sector (204–224.M41)

See also: [[Sub-sector]]

For almost three centuries the Ork empire of [[Waaagh!|WAAAGH!]] Arbuttz held the Zypher [[Sub-sector]] undisturbed. A campaign of reconquest began in 204.M41, led by the [[Raven Guard]], Marauders, [[Marines Errant|Eagle Warriors]] and Revilers with more than two dozen [[Astra Militarum]] regiments. It lasted twenty years, and in 224.M41 the sub-sector was declared free of the [[Xenos|xenos]] taint.

Diata Purge (858.M41)

The [[Chapter Master]] of the [[White Scars]], Kyublai Khan, led the combined White Scars and Marauders against a host of [[Chaos Space Marines|Chaos Space Marine]] renegades in the Diata Purge.

Orphean War (992.M41)

When the [[Necrons]] awoke in the Orpheus Sector, the Marauders sent a battle company to join Battlegroup Salvation. With the whole of the [[Minotaurs]] it fought at the Battle of Amarah and held the Necron advance at a standstill, but the losses were so great that the war was counted lost and the [[High Lords of Terra]] struck the whole sector from the Imperium by special edict.

Organisation and heraldry

The Marauders wear yellow armour with a black [[Aquila]]. A black symbol on the right shoulder marks the squad's specialty, with a white [[High Gothic]] numeral for the squad, and a black numeral on the left knee guard gives the company. The Chapter badge is a [[Sigismund|black sword]] whose blade is wreathed in red flame, on yellow.

Notable members

  • Zha'Khan, Commander at Ferrax Hive (071.M41).
  • Vaas, [[Dreadnought]] of the 9th Company, once a veteran sergeant, nicknamed "Old Painless" and marked with a large letter M on his left knee, the meaning of which is unknown.
