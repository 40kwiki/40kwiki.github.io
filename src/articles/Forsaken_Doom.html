Forsaken Doom was a [[Space hulk|space hulk]] found drifting near the [[Forge World of Gorgonum]]. Its interior was infested with [[Genestealers]]. It was cleared by [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] of the [[Blood Angels]], who entered the wreck and destroyed the brood within.

The operation is one of many recorded against Genestealer-infested hulks in the late 41st millennium. The hulk was a threat to Gorgonum, since a drifting hulk can be steered into a system and used as a weapon.

Clearance

The Blood Angels' Terminators boarded the hulk through its breaches and fought through the corridors to its brood chambers. The hulk was secured after the infestation was destroyed. The Mechanicus then examined the wreck for technology, as it does with every hulk.
