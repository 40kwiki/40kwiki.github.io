The Aurora Chapter is a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] raised from the [[Ultramarines]] in the [[Second Founding]]. Its name is a byword for armoured assault across the [[Segmentum Ultima]], its main sphere of operations. Its armoury holds more [[Land Raider]]s and [[Predator]]s than most Chapters, many of them ancient machines that have served since the Founding, and its homeworld, Firestorm, is covered with industrial macrofactories that rival the larger [[Forge world|forge worlds]]. Its tanks never lack ammunition.

History

Origins in the XIII Legion

The Aurora Chapter traces its descent to the [[Great Crusade]], when it was the 4th Chapter of the [[Ultramarines|XIII Legion]], known to the Legion as the Aurorans. After [[Roboute Guilliman]] was reunited with his Legion, he reorganised it on a doctrine of structured adaptability. Some [[Space Marine Chapter|chapters]] kept notable differences in equipment, and the Aurorans operated about five times the usual number of armoured vehicles. Their armoured formations stopped an Ork advance on the ash plains of [[Cypra Mundi]] and saved a city of millions, and to commemorate it they wore the emerald light of the aurora borealis that had split the sky that day on the right pauldron. The Legion wore storm grey and gunmetal, taken after the retaking of Sedna, at the edge of the [[Sol System]], at the start of the Great Crusade.

At the [[Second Founding]], decreed seven years after the death of [[Horus]], the existing armoured formations of the 4th Chapter became the Aurora Chapter. Imperial accounts of the Heresy also record a small Auroran detachment among the [[Imperial Fists]]' garrison of the Astagar Garrison on [[Cthonia]], besieged by the [[Sons of Horus]] in 013.M31 while the [[Siege of Terra]] was fought and left without relief.

The Eukari Insurrection

In 330.M41 the world of Eukari rose in a Chaos-led rebellion. It lay in the Elara's Veil region, the duty of the Emperor's Spears, whose 4th Warhost under Captain Trystance arrived to find the Aurora Chapter already fighting. The Aurora commander told the Spears that they had failed in their duty and let others fight their wars. The Spears made a planetary drop on the enemy capital, took heavier losses than the Aurora Chapter's careful counter-offensive and, after three solar days and nights of urban war, chained the warlord's skull to Trystance's pauldron and claimed the victory.

An honour duel was agreed, but each Chapter says its champion won, and each says the other fired first in the skirmish that followed, damaging several warships. A delegation of Ultramarines ruled for the Aurora Chapter, and held that the glory should be shared. Trystance swore never to set foot on [[Macragge]], even if the world were threatened. There is a story that every ten years a cargo ship brings a hold of seawater and several hundred abyssal vine-serpents, a despised sea vermin of the Spears' home world Nemeton, to Firestorm, "a tribute to the Spears' beloved cousins".

Later wars

The Chapter broke an Ork invasion of Grylos with massed armoured spearheads in 282.M36. In 650.M41 it ended the Goru Heresy, in which a nihilist cult had taken the system, in ten days of lightning war, but the [[Chapter Master]], Lord Herak Nhuson, was killed when the [[Heretic|heretic]] leader released a psi-vortex as a last spite. In 744.M41 the Chapter joined the Knights of the Raven against the [[Alpha Legion]] warband behind the Redemption Rebellion, and afterwards the two fell out; [[Marneus Calgar]] ended the feud (see [[Knights of the Raven]]). In 999.M41 it supplied Predator support to the Zeist Campaign against the [[T'au Empire]] and took part in the assault on Augura, and joined the Revilers and the Death Eagles, with a demi-legio of the Legio Astraman, in the reconquest of the Forsarr Sector from the [[Waaagh!|Ork WAAAGH!]] Garaghak.

Organisation and heraldry

The Chapter favours massed spearheads led by heavy main battle tanks. Its armour is forest green with a gold [[Aquila]]. The pauldron trim shows company in the order of the [[Codex Astartes]], and sergeants wear a red skull on the helm. The badge is a black alpha, turned on its side, in a white twelve-pointed star on green. The Chapter also holds a thunder hammer called Remembrance. A task force of the Chapter was lost to [[Xenos|xenos]] in the [[Jericho Reach]] during its Age of Shadow, and the [[Deathwatch]] rescued the survivors and returned the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of [[Dark Angels|the fallen]]. The [[Techmarine|Forge Master]] of Watch Fortress Erioch forged the axle of a [[Land Raider]] ruined in that action into the hammer, so that the machine might avenge itself.
