Vaxanide is a [[frontier world]] at the edge of the [[Calixis Sector]] in the [[Malfian Sub-sector]]. It is a poorly supplied and desperate world that struggles to enter the inner circle of sector planets. Its three billion people answer to Lord Vaxanide, of [[House Vaxanide]], whose holdings include [[Scintilla]], [[Malfi]] and [[Regulus]].

Geography and society

Vaxanide has decent mineral output and exports of meat and fish. It is famed for its porcelain, glass and fish products. Its economic troubles make it a lawless and dangerous frontier world, especially outside the central hive city of Vaxanhive. It is also the site of a shrine to [[Saint Drusus]], where miracles are said to occur.

Rumour says that a hidden city lies in the canyons of the equatorial jungle, a mirror of the [[Lucid Palace]] peopled by the phantoms of the real palace's denizens. Every expedition into the jungle has failed, and those who return are usually insane, raving of a "grey death" that stalks them.

History

In 507.M41 the Second Siege of Vaxanide came when an Ork space hulk, the Pinnacle of Savagery, pushed into the Vaxanide System. [[Battlefleet Calixis]] and two Explorator fleets of the Calixian [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] met it at the edge of the system and broke it apart. An attack some ninety Terran years earlier had cost the system dearly. The Mechanicus claimed the largest pieces of the wreck for the [[The Lathes|Lathe Worlds]].
