This is a list of the Fabricators-General, the holders of the office that rules [[Mars]] and leads the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] as the secular head of the [[Cult Mechanicus]]. The holder is also one of the [[High Lords of Terra]], and so has a permanent seat on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. The title is also given more generally to the archmagos who governs any other [[Forge World]].

The record of the office is uneven. The Mechanicus keeps its own registers, and many of its holders are known only from the forge-world archives. The list below gives the holders that Imperial records name, with the dates given in those records. Where a date is estimated, it is marked.
