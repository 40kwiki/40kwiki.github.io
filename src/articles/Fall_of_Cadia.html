The Fall of Cadia was the destruction of the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of Cadia in 999.M41, the final act of Abaddon the Despoiler's Thirteenth [[Black Crusade]]. For ten thousand years Cadia had stood at the mouth of the only stable route out of the [[Eye of Terror]], the Cadian Gate, and it had held against twelve Black Crusades and many thousands of lesser attacks. It supplied the Imperium with the regiments that set the standard of the Astra Militarum, and its fortifications made it the most heavily defended world in the Segmentum Obscurus. At the end of the [[41st millennium]] it was struck from orbit by a captured Blackstone Fortress and broken apart, and the Eye, no longer restrained, spread across the stars.

The loss was the worst in the Imperium's modern history. Imperial scholars rank it among the gravest defeats the Imperium has suffered at the hands of the Archenemy since the Heresy. Its immediate consequences were the evacuation of the Cadian survivors, the end of the Gate as a defensive line, and the opening of the [[Great Rift]], which divided the Imperium and darkened the [[Astronomican]] across half [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The words Cadia stands were chanted by its soldiers in the last days. They have since passed into the speech of the whole Astra Militarum.

Background

The world

[[Cadia]] was a cold, windswept world of sundered plains and ash-grey mountains, on the edge of the Eye. Imperial records state that about forty years before the Heresy it was home to a primitive human people with violet eyes who worshipped the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]], and that the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Lorgar]] of the [[Word Bearers]] came there on his pilgrimage to learn whether the old gods were real. A priestess named Ingethel is said to have led his fleet's scout ship into the Eye. After the pilgrimage Lorgar ordered a bombardment that killed the [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadians]] and left the world empty. The account depends on Word Bearer testimony and was sealed by the [[Inquisition]] until the [[41st millennium]].

After the first [[Black Crusade]] in 781.M31 the Imperium recognised that Cadia was the keystone of the defence of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]]. It settled the world with colonists, whose descendants soon showed the violet eyes of the earlier inhabitants. The first settlements were laid out in the broad avenues of Terran cities and proved easy to attack. After the second crusade, in 597.M32, they were rebuilt as Kasrs, fortress-cities whose streets and buildings were planned by siege engineers as fields of fire. Every Cadian child was taught to field-strip a weapon and drilled in tactical doctrine from the age at which other children learned to read.

The pylons

Scattered over Cadia's surface were black monoliths of unknown age, the Cadian pylons. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] had studied them for millennia and suspected that they were the reason that the Gate was stable, but it could not make them answer. In the last days of the world, [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] and the [[Necrons|Necron]] lord [[Trazyn the Infinite]] established that they were Necron works, built millions of years earlier to shut out the Warp, which the Necrons' masters had feared. The Imperium had been relying on an alien defence for ten thousand years without knowing it.

Prelude

The Time of Ending

Further information: [[Time of Ending]]

By the last decade of the [[41st millennium]] the Imperium was beset on every side. [[Orks]] multiplied, the [[Necrons]] rose from their tomb worlds, the [[T'au Empire]] spread on [[Eastern Fringe|the eastern fringe]], and [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] [[Hive Fleet|hive fleets]] approached from beyond the galaxy's edge. The Warp was restless. [[Astropathy|Astropaths]] received visions of angels and [[Daemon|daemons]] fighting beneath a ghostly firmament, and the choirs of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Solar]] were flooded with cries from every other corner of the galaxy while the Segmentum Pacificus fell silent. Historians call these years the Time of Ending. On the Cadian side, the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] was already gathering around the Eye.

The Plague of Unbelief

Main article: [[Plague of Unbelief]]

The first signs were [[Space hulk|space hulks]], which dropped out of the Warp in great numbers around the [[Fortress world|Belis Corona]] and [[Agripinaa]] systems. Boarding parties of the Navy and the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] found them full of disease and corruption. At the same time, Navy ship crews began to sicken, and the Navy reported sightings of the plague ship Plagueclaw. The [[Death Guard]] lord [[Typhus]], the Herald of Nurgle, brought a fleet from the Eye to the Subiaco Diablo system, and a disease called the Plague of Unbelief spread across a dozen worlds. The dead rose from graves in numbers that the Guard could not contain, and flagellant cults grew on every afflicted world. Several Navy battle groups were paralysed by sickness before the [[Chaos]] fleet arrived.

The treachery at Kasr Tyrok

On [[Cadia]] itself the Imperium was gathering an army. Regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] mustered at the fortress world beside [[Titan Legions]] and [[Skitarii]]. During the muster, the Volscani Cataphracts, a regiment of the Astra Militarum, rose in revolt. They killed thousands of loyal Guardsmen and swarmed aboard the command [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|leviathan]] Excubitoi Castellum, where they killed the Governor Primus, Marius Porelska, and the senior command of the Cadian Gate. At the darkest moment Colonel [[Ursarkar Creed|Ursarkar E. Creed]] of the 8th Cadian Regiment, known as the Castellan's Own, took control of the surviving troops and put down the revolt. His soldiers demanded that he lead them. After refusing three times he accepted the office of Lord Castellan, and he held it to the end.

Creed promoted officers he thought capable and dismissed those he did not, and made many enemies among the Cadian officer class. By the opening of 999.M41 the garrison of Cadia and the surrounding systems stood at several hundred regiments, with the Titans of the [[Metalica|Legio Metalica]] and other Legions, regiments of [[Kasrkin]] and the Youth Corps, and companies of the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the Astartes Praeses, the [[Space Wolves]] and many others.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

The storm breaks

In early 999.M41 Cadian [[Kasrkin]] sent into the outer reaches of the Eye searched the blighted world of [[Urthwart]] for the enemy's first move. They found nothing alive. While they prepared to withdraw, the Black Fleet emerged from the Eye with the [[Planet Killer]], a ship that the Navy had believed lost since the [[Gothic War]]. It destroyed Urthwart in minutes, with the Kasrkin on it. A fleet of hundreds of ships, with two [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]], advanced on [[Cadia]]. [[Chaos Space Marines]] walked on worlds they had not touched for ten thousand years.

The [[Black Legion]] took the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of Nemesis Tessera in the first phase, perhaps because it was the seat of an Inquisitorial conclave, and the Fortresses scoured the Demios Binary to bare rock. [[Traitor]] Volscani regiments took the mining stations of Solar Mariatus. The first inner world of the Cadian system to fall was the prison world of [[St. Josmane's Hope|Saint Josmane's Hope]], where an uprising had been prepared by a cult front and the freed prisoners proved to be the followers of the Violators, a [[Slaanesh]] warband. When the situation could not be recovered, [[Ursarkar Creed|Creed]] ordered the world destroyed. The [[Inquisition]]'s ships carrying [[Exterminatus]] weapons were weeks away, and [[Logan Grimnar|Grimnar]] reported that his fleet had none. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] devised a method, which was to overload the generatorium of the prison complex so that its discharge would tear through the crust. A team went to the surface to do it, and the world broke apart.

Kasr Kraf and the arrival of the Saint

[[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s ground assault on Cadia itself fell first on the fortress-city of Kasr Kraf, the principal bastion of the defence, under the command of one of his Chosen, [[Urkanthos]], the Lord Purgator, with a horde of Chaos Space Marines, [[Chaos cult|cultists]] and [[Daemon|daemons]]. The city was close to falling when [[Saint Celestine]], the Living Saint, arrived and killed Urkanthos in the breach. Kasr Kraf held, and Creed used the time to move the main defence to the [[Elysion Fields]], the largest cluster of pylons on the planet. It had by then become evident that the pylons were the object of the whole campaign.

The pylons awakened

[[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]] had come to Cadia led by a [[Harlequins|Harlequin]] Shadowseer, [[Sylandri Veilwalker]]. With the knowledge of [[Trazyn the Infinite|Trazyn]], who had been present when the pylons were first built, he mastered their systems in the catacombs beneath the Elysion Fields. As Abaddon launched his last great assault on the field, Cawl activated the network. The pylons projected a beam of dark light into the Warp, the daemons of the assault faded, and the Eye of Terror began to shrink. For a time Cadia seemed to have been saved.

The fall

The Blackstone strike

When the pylons were lit, [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] abandoned his wish to defeat the defenders in person. He turned to a plan he had prepared for the case. The [[Blackstone Fortress]] Will of Eternity, damaged by an attack from the [[Imperial Fists]]' mobile fortress-monastery the [[Phalanx]] and by a [[Space Wolves]] fleet, was sent down on [[Cadia]] from orbit as a kinetic weapon. Reports describe the impact as a gouge hundreds of kilometres across. Mountains turned to dust, seas boiled into steam, and tidal waves and pyroclastic flows swept across the continents. The continent of Cadia Tertius was obliterated, Cadia Primus half drowned, and Cadia Secundus ruined by fire. The Krian Fault, which had threatened the world since the [[Age of Strife]], ruptured. The planet's crust split.

The tremors and winds reached the [[Elysion Fields]], half a world away, and toppled the pylons. The beam of dark light flickered and went out, and the Eye began to expand again. [[Daemon|Daemons]] poured from rifts around the wreck of the Blackstone Fortress. As the world died, [[Ursarkar Creed|Creed]] organised an evacuation that carried about three million of its people off the surface before it came apart. Creed was not among them. He is recorded as missing, and the circumstances are not known.

Orders of battle

The [[Administratum|Departmento Munitorum]]'s reconstruction divides the last defence into five engagements. At Kasr Trunch some 127 regiments of [[Cadian Shock Troops]], 65 of [[Kasrkin]] and 12 of the Youth Corps, with five [[Skitarii|Skitarii legions]], a [[Titan Legions|Titan]] demi-legion, two preceptories of the Order of the Bloody Rose and companies of the [[Angels of Absolution]] and the Black Consuls, held the first line. A counterattack by the Black Consuls and Watchguards with an [[Officio Assassinorum]] team followed. In the Long Retreat the defenders withdrew under cover of the Legio Ignatum and three preceptories of the [[Alicia Dominica|Order of the Ebon Chalice]]. In the high-orbit offensive the [[Novamarines]], Reclaimers, Excoriators, Cerulean Guard and [[Iron Hands]] recaptured the orbital batteries of Mos Khazner, which allowed the retreat to proceed. At the Lord Castellan's last stand Creed held the Elysion Fields with 99 Cadian regiments, [[Penal Legion|Penal Legions]] from Fort Drokz, two Knight households, the Relictors and a host of [[Harlequins|Harlequin]] troupes, until the end.

Aftermath

The Great Rift

Main article: [[Great Rift]]

The destruction of the pylons coincided with the opening of the Great Rift. A tear in reality, stretching from the Eye of Terror to the Hadex Anomaly in the east, divided the Imperium into the Imperium Sanctus, which could still see the Astronomican, and the Imperium Nihilus, which could not. The storms in the Warp that followed isolated worlds for years in the period called the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]]. Imperial scholars disagree about the cause. [[Cadia]]'s fall is the most commonly cited, but the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] lists a dozen other events of the same period, from the resurrection of the primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]] to the fall of the craftworld Biel-Tan, that may have contributed.

The Cadian Gate after Cadia

The surviving garrison of the Cadian Gate did not break. The worlds of the Cadian, [[Fortress world|Belis Corona]] and [[Agripinaa]] systems held, many with veterans of the last battles, and the Chaos host fell to quarrelling, since its warlords had expected a victory that would end the war. The Imperial commands exploited each overreach. The Imperium also stripped its [[Eastern Fringe|eastern fringe]] garrisons to reinforce the Gate, which weakened the defences against the [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] hive fleet Leviathan and allowed the [[T'au Empire]]'s latest expansion until the Zeist campaign stopped it. The wreck of Cadia was resettled by the [[Black Legion]] and has become a stronghold of the [[Traitor|Traitors]] at the centre of a new Chaos realm. The [[Adeptus Custodes]] sent a small, fast force to the wreck after the Rift opened. Details of its mission are sealed, even among the Custodes.

The soldiers who escaped Cadia, among them the force known as the [[Celestinian Crusade]], fled by a [[Webway|Webway gate]] on the ice moon of Klaisus with the aid of the [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnari]]. Their allegiance with the [[Aeldari]] produced an unlikely alliance that ended with the resurrection of the primarch Roboute Guilliman on [[Macragge]], at the hands of [[Belisarius Cawl|Archmagos Cawl]].

Legacy

The [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian regiments]] that remain are scattered across the galaxy. New Cadian Shock Troops are raised on transport ships between war zones, drawn from soldiers of other worlds who can stand the training. The motto [[Cadia|Cadia stands]] has passed into use among the officers and platoons of the service, and holds that the world is not lost while a single Cadian lives. In the view of some regiments, the planet broke before the Guard did. Many Cadian survivors consider the fall a personal failure and fight with a hatred that the [[Commissar|Commissariat]] has had to restrain, and those who were not present resent those who survived, while others hold that the soldiers of other worlds who failed to come to Cadia's aid should not be allowed into Cadian regiments.
