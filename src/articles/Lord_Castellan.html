The Lord Castellan, also called Lord General, Lord Marshal and Captain-General, is the highest rank on the General Staff of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The title varies with the culture of its holder.

Duties

A Lord Castellan usually commands an entire army group. That army group serves under the campaign of a [[Lord General Militant]] or another [[Imperial Crusade]]. The duties of the rank depend on the needs of the theatre commander.

Cadia

See also: [[Cadia]]

On the lost [[Fortress world]] of [[Cadia]], the title was granted in times of planetary emergency. Cadia had two [[planetary governor]]s, a Primus and a Secundus, so that one could take over if the other fell. A Lord Castellan could take command of the world's defence in an emergency.
