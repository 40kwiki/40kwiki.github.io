A Wolf Lord is a senior officer of the [[Space Wolves]] who commands one of the twelve Great Companies of the Chapter. He is the equivalent of a [[Captain (Adeptus Astartes)|captain]] in a Chapter that follows the [[Codex Astartes]], and his only master is the [[Great Wolf]]. The number of twelve includes the Great Wolf himself, who is chosen from among the Wolf Lords.

Role

Each Great Company is a self-sufficient brotherhood with its own quarters and territory in the fortress-monastery of [[The Aett]], its own ships, Armoury and forges. It is usually named after its ruling lord. When a Wolf Lord is slain his body is burned at sea or sent into a star, and the next day his [[Wolf Guard]] elect a successor, who takes the Great Company's name as his own and chooses a badge from the legends of [[Fenris]]. The badge is set into the [[Grand Annulus]], the stone table at which the twelve lords gather.

A Wolf Lord may be attended by Fenrisian wolves and is always guarded by a contingent of Wolf Guard loyal to him. He leads from the front, and the losses among Wolf Lords are high because of the Chapter's taste for close combat.
