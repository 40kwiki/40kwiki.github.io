Cypher, called the Lord of the Fallen, is the most notorious of the [[Dark Angels#The Fallen|Fallen]], the warriors of the I Legion who turned against [[Lion El'Jonson]] on [[Caliban]] at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]] and were scattered through time and space by the Warp storm that destroyed that world. For ten thousand years he has appeared across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], always ahead of the Dark Angels and their successor [[Chapters]], the [[Unforgiven]], who hunt him above all others. His true name and purpose are unknown.

In 999.M41 Cypher freed the resurrected [[Roboute Guilliman]] from captivity in the [[Maelstrom]] in return for a promise that he would be brought before the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]]. He fought beside the [[Primarch|primarch]] all the way to [[Terra]], was imprisoned in the Imperial Palace on Guilliman's order, and escaped within hours. In the [[Era Indomitus]] he is pursued by the Dark Angels, now led again by their returned primarch, and by a force of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]].

Identity

Cypher is said to have been a warrior of the Legion's oldest ranks, perhaps one of the Terran-born veterans of the First Founding. Accounts of the Heresy era record that within the [[Dark Angels|I Legion]] "Cypher" was a title, held by the keeper of the Legion's rites and lore, rather than a name; Imperial scholars dispute whether the renegade of later ages is one of its holders, a successor to the title, or a series of different men. The Dark Angels' own records list his kills as including [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]], [[Chaos Space Marines|Chaos Space Marine]] warlords, [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]], sorcerous covens, cult leaders and high-ranking [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], but also report that he has intervened in battles to save Imperial forces.

Sightings

The [[Dark Angels]] probably first met him in the last years of M31, during their search for new recruiting worlds. Between 580.M31 and 632.M32, in the Forgotten Wars, the Dark Angels and the Angels of Vengeance, [[Angels of Redemption]] and Lions Sable pursued the Fallen, and the Lions Sable were lost on Cocytus in a trap that some attribute to him. In 822.M33, at the Battle of Vermilac Prime, Cypher's intervention stopped [[Orks]] from looting the Lion Helm and the Sword of Secrets from the body of [[Chapter Master|Supreme Grand Master]] Alloken. He was seen during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] in 257.M35, and in 624.M36 he was first seen carrying a [[C'tan]] phase knife.

In 665.M38 he impersonated the cowled [[Inquisitor]] Arnuldus on a secret mission. The Dark Angels lost track of him between 200 and 500.M39, a period in which he is believed to have been within the [[Eye of Terror]]. Further sightings are recorded throughout M40 and M41, often at moments when the Unforgiven could not seize him.

The Terran Crusade

Main article: [[Terran Crusade]]

During [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s [[Terran Crusade]] in 999.M41, the primarch's fleet was defeated in the Maelstrom by the [[Daemon|Greater Daemon]] [[Kairos Fateweaver]], and he and his surviving forces were imprisoned in a [[Blackstone Fortress]] that [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] had given to the [[Red Corsairs]]. Cypher came to the fortress with the [[Harlequins|Harlequin]] Shadowseer [[Sylandri Veilwalker]] and offered to free Guilliman if the primarch would let him stand before the Emperor on [[Terra]]. Guilliman agreed. Cypher fought at his side through the escape from the fortress, through the [[Webway]] and on [[Luna]], where they faced [[Magnus the Red]]. When the crusade reached the [[Imperial Palace]], Guilliman had him taken into custody, unwilling to trust him until he understood his aims; within hours Cypher had escaped.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Soon after his escape the [[Dark Angels]] picked up his trail again. The pursuit led to the Massacre at Darkmor, in which companies from half a dozen Unforgiven [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], among them the Angels of Vengeance, the Consecrators and the Guardians of the Covenant, followed their [[Librarian|Librarians]]' scrying to the [[Shrine world|shrine world]] of Darkmor, found it ruined and its people slain, and were ambushed by [[Daemon|daemons]], [[Chaos Space Marines]] and renegades as the Warp storms closed again. Fewer than a hundred of a thousand [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] escaped, and they did so only by following Cypher to a hidden teleportation device. It was the Unforgiven's worst single loss since the Forgotten Wars.The Massacre at Darkmor is dated only to the opening years of M42.

His jailers on Terra are also in pursuit. Shield-Captain Daryth of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], with his Shield Company and a cadre of [[Sisters of Silence]], followed his trail across the [[Great Rift]] into the [[Imperium Nihilus]] aboard the frigate Sol's Arrow, unaware that an unmarked black strike cruiser was following them. The return of [[Lion El'Jonson]] in the [[42nd millennium]] has given the hunt a new urgency, since the Lion is the one being in the galaxy who could answer the question of what happened on [[Caliban]]; whether Cypher seeks him or flees him is not recorded.

Wargear

Cypher fights with two ancient pistols, a plasma pistol and a [[Bolter|bolt pistol]], and carries a sword on his back that he is never seen to draw. Some among the Unforgiven believe it is the Lion Sword, lost when the primarch vanished; the [[Dark Angels|Inner Circle]] whispers that it may instead be the black Sword of [[Luther]], said to have struck the Lion down. From 624.M36 he has also carried a [[C'tan]] phase knife.
