The Rhino is the armoured personnel carrier of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It carries squads of Space Marines across the battlefield and is among the most widely used Imperial vehicles. Its design follows a [[Standard Template Construct]] pattern held by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]].

The Rhino is used for transport, and it is often adapted for other roles. Its variants, which share its hull, are numerous across the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]].

History

The Rhino's pattern was in service with the [[Space Marine Legions]] during the [[Great Crusade]]. The forge of [[Deimos]] on [[Mars]], which was then one of the forges of the Martian [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], produced the vehicle patterns that bear its name, among them versions of the Rhino, [[Predator]] and [[Vindicator]]. The forge's records were held for the Imperium through the Heresy.

Service

Every Chapter maintains Rhinos in its armoury, and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] keep them in service alongside the newer [[Land Raider]] and the Razorback. Their transport capacity and armour are recorded as the Rhino's main strengths.
