The Vengeful Spirit is a Gloriana-class battleship, built at [[Mars]] in the first years of the [[Great Crusade]], that served as the flagship of the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] and his XVI Legion, the Luna Wolves, later the [[Sons of Horus]]. From her command deck Horus led the Great Crusade after [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], and then the traitor Legions in the [[Horus Heresy]].

In the final hours of the [[Siege of Terra]] Horus lowered her shields, and the [[Emperor of Mankind]] teleported aboard with his bodyguard and two of his loyal sons. The Emperor destroyed Horus in Lupercal's Court but was himself mortally wounded, and has been kept alive on the [[Golden Throne]] since. The crippled ship was abandoned by the loyalists and later recovered by [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]], for whom she has served as flagship of the [[Black Legion]] ever since.

Design

The Gloriana-class vessels were the largest warships of the Great Crusade, built by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] after the [[Treaty of Olympus|Treaty of Mars]] as flagships for the Emperor and his [[Primarch|primarchs]]. Each combined the firepower of a battleship with the capacity to carry a large part of a Legion. The Vengeful Spirit was built to a variant of the Scylla pattern.

Contemporary accounts describe her as "a heavy ingot of iron", lined with hundreds of turrets and accelerator cannon longer than most battleships. Her prow bore a great golden ring around a narrow ellipse, [[Eye of Terror|the eye]] of the [[Warmaster]]. The Strategium, later rebuilt as [[Sanctum Imperialis|the throne room]] called Lupercal's Court, was the command centre of the 63rd Expeditionary Fleet and of the thousands of Imperial personnel who lived and worked aboard.

Because she was among the first ships built for the Imperium, the ship's core was old and largely abandoned. It was a labyrinth of disused decks where crew rarely went, and in those spaces the warrior lodges of the Legion met.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The Vengeful Spirit carried [[Horus]] through the Great Crusade, including the campaigns of the 63rd Expedition recorded by the [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancers]] [[Iterators|Kyril Sindermann]] and Mersadie Oliton. After Horus was named [[Warmaster]] at [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], the ship became the seat of command of the whole Crusade.

Aboard the ship the Warmaster kept the Mournival, the council of four captains who advised him, among them [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]] and [[Sons of Horus|Garviel Loken]]. The remembrancers lived and worked on her decks, and Sindermann's accounts describe the Strategium as a place of open debate where Horus heard his officers and his chroniclers alike. Those accounts are now valued by Imperial historians as the fullest record of the Warmaster's court before his fall.

The [[Word Bearers]] [[Chaplain|chaplain]] [[Erebus]] used the empty depths of the ship to hide the [[Kinebrach Anathame]], which he had stolen from the [[Interex]], before it was used to wound Horus on [[Davin]]. In the same depths Erebus later held the rites by which Horus spoke with the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]].

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

After [[Isstvan III]] the ship became less a warship than a temple to the gods [[Horus]] now served. Loyalists aboard were purged. [[Sons of Horus|Garviel Loken]] was sent to his intended death on Isstvan III, and the [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancers]] were hunted down or fled; a few escaped with the loyalist [[Death Guard]] captain [[Nathaniel Garro]] aboard the frigate Eisenstein, which carried the first warning of the rebellion to Terra. The lodges' meeting rooms in the ship's depths became shrines, and sacrifices were made there to the [[Chaos|powers of the warp]].

The ship took part in the [[Drop Site Massacre]] and in the campaigns of the Sons of Horus that followed, among them the invasion of Molech, where Horus sought a way into [[Warp|the warp]].

Siege of Terra

Main article: [[Siege of Terra]]

The Vengeful Spirit opened the bombardment of [[Terra]] with a single shell aimed at the [[Sanctum Imperialis]]. Through the Siege she stayed in orbit as Horus's throne and fortress.

In the last days, Horus ordered her shields lowered entirely. The Emperor teleported aboard with [[Rogal Dorn]], [[Sanguinius]] and the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] of [[Constantin Valdor]]. The interior of the ship, saturated with warp power, merged with the Palace into what survivors called the Inevitable City, and the loyalists were scattered through it. Horus turned the ship itself against the invaders. The [[Blood Angels]] crippled her, Sanguinius was killed by Horus, and the Emperor destroyed Horus in Lupercal's Court, purging the ship and Terra of the warp's corruption in the process.

As reality was restored, Terra and the ship were forced apart. Dorn and Valdor found the Emperor alive in Lupercal's Court and carried him back to the Palace with the body of Sanguinius. Valdor ordered that Horus's corpse be left aboard, expecting the ship to be destroyed.

After the Heresy

The ship was not destroyed. With most of her mortal crew dead or insane, the surviving [[Sons of Horus]] made the repairs themselves. [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] gathered the Legion's survivors aboard, took up [[Horus]]'s Talon and assumed command of the Legion. The Vengeful Spirit carried them in their retreat from [[Terra]].

In the [[Eye of Terror]] the Sons of Horus suffered heavily in the [[Legion Wars]], and the fortress of Maleum fell. The Vengeful Spirit, altered by long exposure to the warp, became embedded beneath Asa'ciaral, a dead [[Aeldari]] world in the Eye, and Abaddon withdrew from command. An expedition led by Falkus Kibre and the sorcerer Iskandar Khayon found both. Abaddon agreed to lead the Legion again, and used the ship to destroy the [[Emperor's Children]] on the [[Daemon world|daemon world]] of Harmony, together with the clone of Horus they had grown there.

After the Battle of Harmony Abaddon renamed his warriors the [[Black Legion]] and took the title of Warmaster of Chaos. The Vengeful Spirit became the heart of the Black Fleet.

Black Crusades

Main article: [[Black Crusade]]

Under Abaddon the ship has been rebuilt many times with the aid of the [[Dark Mechanicum]], and Navy observers describe her hull as warped and darkened by the Eye. Her armament has grown, and her decks hold a standing garrison of the Black Legion's chosen warriors. Lupercal's Court remains her command deck, where Abaddon is reported to hold council with the lords who follow him.

The ship has appeared in [[Realspace|realspace]] during several of Abaddon's [[Black Crusade]]s, including the Tenth, the Twelfth and the Thirteenth. During the Thirteenth, in 999.M41, she fought Imperial fleets in the [[Cadia|Cadian system]] before the [[Fall of Cadia]].

Nearly all the other Gloriana-class ships were lost during the Heresy or soon after. Among those still known to exist are the Black Legion's Harbinger of Doom and the [[World Eaters]]' Conqueror. The [[Imperial Navy]] rates the Vengeful Spirit as the most powerful capital ship of the Black Legion.

Legacy

The Vengeful Spirit holds a unique place in Imperial history as the site of the Emperor's last battle. To the [[Imperial Cult]] the ship is accursed, and to the [[Black Legion]] she is a relic of [[Horus]] and a symbol of [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s claim to his inheritance.
