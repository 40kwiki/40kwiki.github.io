Ibram Gaunt, known as the People's Hero, is an Imperial [[Commissar]] and the commanding officer of the [[Tanith]] First-and-Only regiment of the [[Astra Militarum]], better known as Gaunt's Ghosts. He later became a [[Lord General Militant|Lord Militant]], and he was appointed First Lord Executor Militant of the [[Sabbat Worlds]].

Gaunt is remembered in Imperial regimental records as a commander who valued the lives of his soldiers and who held his regiment together through many hard campaigns. Records of his service are preserved by the [[Departmento Munitorum]] and by the regiment's own archive. Some details of his early career are disputed between accounts.
