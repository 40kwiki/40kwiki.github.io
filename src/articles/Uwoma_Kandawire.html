Uwoma Kandawire was the earliest recorded [[Grand Provost Marshal]], the head of the [[Adeptus Arbites]], the enforcers of [[Imperial law]]. He held the office during the [[Unification Wars]] in the late [[30th millennium]], and he sat as one of the [[High Lords of Terra]].

Kandawire is recorded in the Arbites' lists as the first holder of the office. The Imperial records later name him as the leader of the [[Imperial Palace]] Coup, in which the surviving [[Thunder Warriors]] took part.

Unification Wars

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

When the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]] was brought into the field late in the [[Unification Wars]], the Lords Civilian of [[Terra]] were headed by four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian. Kandawire was [[Grand Provost Marshal]] among them, alongside [[Pelops Dravagor]], Chancellor of the Estate Imperium, [[Noum Retraiva]], [[Master of the Administratum]], and [[Kli-San Weia]], [[Lord Commander Militant]].

Imperial Palace Coup

See also: [[Imperial Palace]]

The [[Thunder Warriors]], who had been under the command of [[Ushotan]], the [[Primarch|primarch]] of the [[Iron Warriors|IV Legion]], took part in the [[Imperial Palace]] Coup. The coup was led by Kandawire, a former Provost Marshal, who hoped to call [[Constantin Valdor]] to account for the destruction of the Thunder Warriors. The surviving warriors joined it for revenge and for an honourable death in battle.

Imperial accounts do not agree on the date of the coup or on its outcome. The records are sealed in parts.The date of the Imperial Palace Coup is not recorded in the surviving sources. Some accounts place it in the Unification Wars and others in the Heresy.
