Aesoth Koumadra was [[Trajann Valoris|Chief Custodian]] and [[Captain-General]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] at some point between the 33rd and 39th millennia. No record narrows the date, and nothing survives of his origins or death.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

The Ominous Gift

Augurs in the Halo belt detected a [[space hulk]], the Ominous Gift, drifting steadily toward [[Terra]]. Koumadra used his standing as a [[High Lords of Terra|High Lord]] to overrule the objections of the [[Imperial Navy]] and ordered several Shield Companies of the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] to enter the vessel and purge it. The Lockwarden of the [[Shadowkeepers]] led the attack with a band of black-armoured companions. The hulk was destroyed. Those outside the Custodes did not understand why it mattered, and the Imperium at large was told nothing more.
