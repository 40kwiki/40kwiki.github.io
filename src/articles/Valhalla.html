Valhalla is an Imperial Ice World, also classified by the Administratum as a Civilised World. It lies in the Ultima Segmentum. The planet is extremely cold and its ice never thaws. It is famous across the Imperium as the homeworld of the [[Valhallan Ice Warriors]], the regiments of the Astra Militarum who are known for holding their ground against hopeless odds.

History

Valhalla was once a green Paradise World. No record of its colonisation survives, but Valhallan legend tells of a world that was ripe for conquest and settlement.

Late in the [[Age of Strife]] a rogue comet struck the planet. Its defence lasers fired on the comet but only broke it into fragments. The comet was made almost entirely of iron, which explains why the lasers failed to stop it. One large fragment struck the northern continent. The impact threw up enough dust to cover the planet in a blanket of dirt. It also changed the planet's orbit, so that it lay farther from its sun. The amount of warmth and light reaching the surface fell, and the ecosystem was permanently changed into an icy wasteland.

The Valhallans survived by carving homes deep beneath the ice, in massive subterranean caverns. Hive cities grew in those caverns, and the people built their farms in the vats they kept below the surface.

Ork invasion

Not long after the comet strike, a damaged [[Ork]] warfleet landed on Valhalla and fought the people for every scrap of their stored food. The Valhallans held their hive cities for a long time before they were pushed back to their food vats. They then used ice-boring machines to strike at the heart of the Ork mob. The machines burned and melted most of the Orks and scattered the rest.

Valhallan Ice Warriors

The Valhallan Ice Warriors were raised from the hive cities and trained to fight in the cold. They are known throughout the Imperium for enduring heavy losses without breaking. Valhallan regiments are sent to the hardest wars in the Astra Militarum, and they are often found on the front lines of the Imperial Crusades.
