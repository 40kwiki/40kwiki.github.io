Prandium was a [[Agri-world|garden world]] of the [[Ultramar|Realm of Ultramar]]. It was known as the "Jewel of Ultramar", and its gardens and forests were reckoned a wonder of the Imperium. It was destroyed in 745.M41 by the alien fleet known to the Imperium as Behemoth.

The world was defended by the [[Ultramarines]] under [[Marneus Calgar]]. Calgar's first plan followed the [[Codex Astartes]] and sent the Ultramarine fleet to stop Behemoth at Prandium. The defence failed, and the alien fleet overran the world's orbital defences and its surface.

Destruction

The attack began with the Behemoth fleet's approach through the outer system. The fleet overran the planet before the Ultramarine squadrons could reach it. Imperial records describe the loss as a shock to the realm, and the world's gardens were lost in the fighting. The defence was the subject of later debate in the Ultramarine records.The date of the attack is recorded as 745.M41. The exact course of the battle is reconstructed from fragmentary records.
