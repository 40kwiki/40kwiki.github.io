The Battle of Macragge was fought in 745.M41 between the [[Ultramarines]] and the tendrils of Hive Fleet Behemoth, the first full [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] of the [[Tyranids]] that the Imperium met, in the system of the Chapter's homeworld, Macragge. It was the climax of the First Tyrannic War. The Ultramarines, under their Chapter Master [[Marneus Calgar]], held the world's polar fortresses against the swarm on the ground and destroyed the fleet at Circe in the void. It was the severest test of the Chapter since the [[Horus Heresy]], and it cost the Chapter its entire First Company.

The victory did not end the Tyranid threat. Behemoth's survivors split into smaller fleets that grew again by feeding on the worlds they found, and the Hive Mind had learned a great deal about the Imperium's methods. It would return in the Second Tyrannic War, beginning in 992.M41, with [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] and later with [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]. At Macragge, though, the Imperium had shown that a hive fleet could be stopped, and the Chapter drew from the battle a doctrine it has used against every hive fleet since.

Background

The Ultramarines and Ultramar

[[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] are the successors of the XIII Legion, and [[Macragge]] is their home, the capital of the [[Ultramar|Realm of Ultramar]], a cluster of some five hundred worlds the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] ruled before the Heresy. In the ten millennia since he was wounded by [[Fulgrim]] the Chapter has defended Ultramar against [[Orks]], rebels, the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] and [[Necrons|Necron]] raiders. The Chapter's stronghold, the Fortress of Hera, stands on Macragge. Its principal defences include two polar fortresses, the northern and southern, which command the planet's orbital approaches with laser silos and missile batteries.

Tyran Primus and the warning

The first full encounter between the Imperium and a [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] came at the [[Planetary classification|research station]] on [[Tyran Primus]], a small [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] outpost on [[Eastern Fringe|the eastern fringe]]. The [[Tyranids]] stripped the world of all life before help could arrive. The [[Ordo Xenos]] sent [[Inquisitor]] [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Fidus Kryptman]] to investigate, and he arrived too late to save anything. The Mechanicus adepts who went with him found a data-codex hidden by Magos Varnak in a bore shaft three kilometres beneath the surface, in which he had recorded what he had learned of the attackers before the station fell.

From the codex and the trail of dead worlds he followed, Kryptman concluded that the Imperium was facing a vast, hungry and coordinated alien fleet. He named it Behemoth, following the custom of naming alien invaders for great beasts of Terran myth. Behemoth then devoured the observatory moon of Occulus, defeated the Ork fleet of the Star Krumpas at Jagga, and took the world of Thandros, whose telepathica matrix was found with its defence lasers burned out. The fleet also destroyed the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of Sybari, the stronghold of the Death Shadows, a [[Chaos Space Marines|Renegade Chapter]]. Two minor tendrils that approached the tomb world of [[Solemnace]] avoided it, and, finding no suitable prey, went dormant.

Kryptman reached the ruins of Thandros and with great effort sent an [[Astropathy|astropathic]] warning through the shadow of the fleet in the Warp. A single voice replied from Macragge. Kryptman reached Ultramar ahead of the fleet and was received by [[Marneus Calgar|Calgar]] under the portico of his palace.

The first defence

Prandium and Calth

[[Marneus Calgar|Calgar]]'s first plan followed the [[Codex Astartes]]. The Ultramarine fleet was deployed to stop Behemoth at the [[Agri-world|garden world]] of [[Prandium]], the Jewel of [[Ultramar]], whose gardens and forests were reckoned a wonder of the Imperium. It was a slaughter. The Codex prescribes mobile warfare and harrying actions, and the [[Tyranids]]' spore cysts and tentacles ruptured the Thunderhawks and other craft that attacked at short range, which was the only range at which the Imperial weapons were effective. The fleet could do nothing, and Prandium was consumed. A smaller swarm seeded by the [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] then struck [[Calth]].

Calgar withdrew to meditate. For a solar week he took neither food nor water, and when he emerged he told the Chapter that it had been guilty of the sin of pride. The Codex was written for the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]], and the Space Marines had not been made for the Codex. Against the Tyranids they would have to abandon their habit of harrying actions and choose a place to defend with concentrated firepower. He chose the [[Macragge|Fortress of Hera]] on Macragge, the heart of Ultramar.

Preparations

Calgar improved the defences of Macragge for several solar weeks. A dozen warships already lay in orbit, and more arrived each day: strike cruisers, Navy [[Destroyers (Chapter)|destroyers]] and the Chapter's Battle Barges. Orbital defence stations were readied. On the ground he divided his forces into three armies: two to hold the polar fortresses, composed largely of the First Company's veterans and the Ultramaran [[Planetary Defence Force|Planetary Defence Forces]], and a third under his own command to fight a delaying action and kill the synapse creatures that directed the swarm. Titans of the Legio Praetor stood on the ice fields before the northern fortress.

The battle

The fight on the ground

Within solar weeks the outriders of the fleet attacked, and the bio-vessels swept aside the Ultramarines' strike cruisers and swarmed over the planet. Mycetic Spores landed thousands of creatures that spread across the surface. Calgar's army fought a long rearguard action and bought time at the price of blood for the polar fortresses. When Genestealers came up from the sewers beneath the Sirocco Starport, the Chapter lost much of its shuttle fleet and the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Auxilia]] forces that made up the bulk of its defence.

At Cold Steel Ridge, Calgar chose to stand. The [[Tyranids|Hive Mind]] had by then learned him, and sent against him a new bioform, a Hive Tyrant whose cunning matched that of a commander. The Imperial records name it the Swarmlord. Its plan used the whole range of Tyranid forms: Raveners and Trygons erupted from the frozen ground in the middle of the trenches, cutting off the defenders from the main force, and Carnifexes destroyed the Baneblade Pride of Hera, which had been left without infantry support, and with it the Imperial western flank. Hormagaunts that had been lying still among the dead rose at the Swarmlord's will to fall on the Ultramarines from the side. Calgar, who had tried to meet the Swarmlord himself, was struck down and badly wounded. His Honour Guard drove the creature off, and Brother Aloysius, the commander of the Guard, held the Swarmlord and its Tyrant Guard alone while Thunderhawks landed and took off with the survivors. Aloysius fell, but Calgar lived.

The polar fortresses

The army of [[Ultramar]] was gone, and the polar fortresses stood alone. The Tyranid fleet, having defeated the orbital defences, released thousands of spores directly over them. The veterans of the First Company and the Auxilia held every wall and trench until it was overrun, then fell back through the fortress step by step. The Titans of the Legio Praetor killed Tyranids by the thousand and were dragged down by the weight of the swarm. At the northern fortress the Tyranids climbed the walls using heaps of their own dead. Defenders' gun barrels glowed red and jammed in the cold, and the ammunition that had been intended to last months of siege ran low.

The war in space

The fight in orbit went no better. The first wave of the Tyranid fleet destroyed the Battle Barge Caesar. The third wave, which included swarms of Void Fiends that came through the gap, destroyed most of Ultramar's [[Chartist Captain|merchant fleet]], and by the ninth the Starnova stations of [[Macragge]] were wrecks. After the twelfth wave the orbital defences were gone. Calgar, who had returned to the Battle Barge Octavius and would accept only the medical care that kept him on his feet, took command of what remained of the fleet and harried the Tyranids as they spread to surround the world.

The Hive Mind withdrew its ships to lure the defenders from the world, and Calgar followed them toward the ringed world of Circe at the edge of the system. This was part of his own plan. The [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Bakka|Battlefleet Tempestus]], from Bakka, arrived at that moment and caught the Tyranid fleet between two fleets. Even so, the Imperial ships were outnumbered. The battleship Dominus Astra, flagship of Battlefleet Tempestus, rammed into the heart of the [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] and detonated her warp drives. The resulting vortex dragged her and the greater part of the Tyranid fleet into the Warp. A few battered ships slipped away.

Kryptman had in the meantime found a weakness in the fleet. When a major bio-ship was destroyed, the nearby ones became unable to coordinate until another arrived to take its place. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] exploited this at a high price in ships.

The last stand of the First Company

The battle was won at Circe, but the world was not yet safe. When the fleet returned to Macragge, the Third and Seventh Companies landed at the poles by Drop Pod and [[Thunderhawk]]. The Seventh found the southern fortress held, and joined the survivors to clear its lower tunnels. The Third landed in the north and was attacked at once. Captain Fabian waited for the company's three [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] before he led his Space Marines through corridors that were already changing, dripping with mucus and filled with the Tyranid smell, where Lictors lay in ambush among the dead. The company burned its way forward with flamers until it reached the lower Penitorium, where the last [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] of the First Company had made their stand. Tyranid dead lay six deep about the doors. Inside, a circle of Terminators lay where they had fought, back to back. The First Company had been destroyed to the last man.

The body of a Hive Tyrant believed to be the Swarmlord lay on the ice field outside the northern fortress, but the damage to it made identification impossible. Rumours that it survived were later proved correct.

Aftermath

The Tyrannic War Veterans

The Chapter needed a century to recover. Its names of [[Dark Angels|the fallen]] are read each year at the Feast of Days. The task of rebuilding the First Company was given to the [[Chaplain]] [[Ortan Cassius]], whose hatred of the [[Tyranids]] was boundless. He petitioned [[Marneus Calgar|Calgar]] to raise a new body of veterans drawn from those who had survived the Tyranids on [[Macragge]], on the principle that only the tested could understand the enemy. Calgar agreed, and for the first time in its history the Chapter altered the dictates of the [[Codex Astartes]]. The Tyrannic War Veterans, who wear upgraded armour and receive additional duties, form the core of the reconstituted First Company. Calgar tolerated the departure from the Codex because he expected that the Ultramarines and the Tyranids would fight again.

The splinter fleets

Behemoth was not finished. Kryptman learned that the [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] had been in two parts, leapfrogging each other as they fed, and Macragge had destroyed only one. The survivors split into smaller fleets that scattered in all directions, grew by consuming biomass, and appeared in many parts of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] for solar decades. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] found a Carnifex that the Imperium called Old One Eye, which had been left on [[Ultramar]], and it was a long task to scour the Tyranids from Macragge. In the [[41st millennium]]'s last century it became clear that the scattered forces on [[Eastern Fringe|the eastern fringe]] were the vanguard of two new fleets, [[Hive Fleet Kraken|Kraken]] and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]]. The Second Tyrannic War opened with Kraken in 992.M41.

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] drew its own conclusions. A splinter of Behemoth that attacked the world of Daugel Helix was met by Cohort Commander Alpha 9-Thyrrc, who used the codex from [[Tyran Primus]] to saturate the upper atmosphere with promethium gas from the refineries and ignited it with a neutron laser. Each wave of spores burned in the sky. The method was recorded in a data-codex on the [[Forge world|forge world]] of Accatran and was soon forgotten.

The Imperium's knowledge

The Imperium gained a great deal of knowledge about the Tyranids in this war. It learned what a hive fleet was, how the synapse creatures coordinated the swarm, and where they were vulnerable. The [[Ordo Xenos]] used the same knowledge to design the plan that Kryptman would later propose against the next fleet. In the Second Tyrannic War that knowledge was put to hard use.

Legacy

The battle is remembered in [[Ultramar]] as a moment when its defenders were tested beyond expectation and did not despair. The Chapter Banner of Ultramar, which was not carried into battle by a full-strength Chapter for a hundred years, flew once again in force after the Chapter was rebuilt. [[Marneus Calgar|Calgar]]'s decision to set aside the Codex in the face of an enemy it had not anticipated is remembered in the Chapter as a lesson that the Codex serves the Chapter, and not the reverse.

In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Ultramarines have faced the [[Tyranids]] again, as tendrils of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] have reached into the Realm of Ultramar, and the returned primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]] has taken up the defence of his realm.
