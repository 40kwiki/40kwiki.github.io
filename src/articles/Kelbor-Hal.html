Kelbor-Hal was Fabricator-General of [[Mars]] and master of the Martian Mechanicum in the last years of the [[Great Crusade]]. At the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] he took the side of [[Horus]], opened the forbidden Vaults of Moravec and began the [[Schism of Mars]], the civil war that tore the Red Planet apart. He became the first lord of what the Imperium later named the [[Dark Mechanicum]].

Kelbor-Hal had never accepted the [[Emperor of Mankind]] as the Omnissiah, the incarnation of the Machine God. He regarded the [[Treaty of Olympus|Treaty of Mars]] as an ultimatum that had made Mars a vassal of Terra, and he sold the Mechanicum's loyalty to Horus in return for an end to the Emperor's limits on forbidden research. His forces held Mars for most of the Heresy. His fate after the failure of the [[Siege of Terra]] is not recorded.

Early life and the Treaty of Mars

Kelbor-Hal was a magos of great ability and sincere faith in the Machine God. He was present at Olympus Mons when the Emperor first came to Mars after the [[Unification Wars]] to seek an alliance with the priesthood. Many tech-priests hailed the Emperor as the Omnissiah. Kelbor-Hal did not. He judged that the peace on offer was a demand backed by force, and that by accepting it Mars had bargained away its independence to Terra, its ancient rival.

Under the Treaty of Mars the Mechanicum built the fleets and arms of the Great Crusade in return for the autonomy of its forge worlds and the freedom to practise its faith, though the Emperor sealed whole fields of research and had the Vaults of Moravec beneath Olympus Mons closed. The vaults held artefacts made or tainted by the powers of the warp.

Fabricator-General

Over the two centuries after the Treaty Kelbor-Hal rose, by about 939.M30, to become Fabricator-General and master of Olympus Mons, the greatest forge on Mars. He gathered a circle of allied magi, among them Urtzi Malevolus, Lukas Chrom of the Mondus Gamma forge, and his envoy Melgator. By the end of the Crusade more than four-fifths of his body was machine, a fact in which he took pride, and his reign saw the longest run of rising production quotas in the Mechanicum's history.

Relations among the forges were already poor. Espionage and violence between rival magi were common, and the [[Titan Legions]] of the Collegia Titanica were suspected of having chosen sides in advance of any conflict.

Horus Heresy

The pact with Horus

At the outbreak of the Heresy Horus sent the adept Regulus, who had already joined the Warmaster, to Mars. Regulus reminded the Fabricator-General that the Mechanicum already favoured the fleets loyal to Horus with arms and shipments. He offered a database of [[Standard Template Construct|STC]] data recovered by the [[Sons of Horus]] from the Auretian Technocracy, a promise to withdraw all non-Mechanicum forces from the forge worlds after victory, and the end of every restriction on research, including into [[Abominable Intelligence]]. As a pledge, Horus gave him the codes to open the Vaults of Moravec. Kelbor-Hal accepted.

The Schism of Mars

The opening of the vaults released scrap code, a corrupted data plague that spread through the networked cogitators of Mars. It halted production lines, caused reactor failures and turned machines against their keepers. Under cover of the disorder Kelbor-Hal's allies used sabotage and assassination against magi who would not join them.

He needed a pretext for open war, and found one when Koriel Zeth, mistress of Magma City, stated that she did not believe the Omnissiah existed. Kelbor-Hal declared her and her allies heretics against the Cult Mechanicus. Fighting spread across the planet between forge cities, skitarii and Titan Legions, in a civil war that mirrored the war between the Legions. Magma City became the centre of loyalist resistance and was destroyed.

Loyal tech-priests who escaped, led by Zagreus Kane, continued the Mechanicum's government in exile, and Kane was recognised by the Imperium as Fabricator-General. Kelbor-Hal held Mars, which was blockaded for much of the war by loyalist forces.

The Furious Abyss

Kelbor-Hal secretly sanctioned the building of the battleship Furious Abyss at the orbital yard of Thule, an asteroid towed into orbit around Jupiter. The ship was designed to destroy a Legion and was meant as the new flagship of the [[Word Bearers]] for their attack on [[Ultramar]]. When it was finished he had the yard destroyed with everyone aboard, so that no worker could reveal it. The Furious Abyss was later lost in a battle with the [[Ultramarines]] before it could reach its target.

Siege of Terra

After the [[Solar War]] broke the loyalist blockade of Mars, Kelbor-Hal took direct command of the Dark Mechanicum's war effort and joined the traitor host at Terra. Imperial records name him among the traitor commanders at the [[Siege of Terra]]. Survivors of the traitor councils described him as arrogant and eager to claim credit for the siege works of [[Perturabo]] and the magos Sota-Nul against the Palace defences.

Beliefs

Kelbor-Hal's rebellion was not only political. Mechanicus doctrine holds that its adepts approach the Machine God through knowledge and the perfection of the machine. In his view the Emperor's restrictions on research stood between the priesthood and its god, and an ally who would remove them was to be preferred to a master who would not. Mechanicus accounts record that the choice of allegiance "required only nanoseconds of computation" once Horus made his offer.

He also held that the Emperor had deceived Mars by allowing himself to be hailed as the Omnissiah. To Kelbor-Hal and those who followed him, the Emperor was a false god, and the war against him a war of faith. The loyalists who opposed him came to the opposite conclusion. The Adeptus Mechanicus that emerged after the Heresy accepted the Emperor as the Omnissiah as a matter of doctrine, a position Imperial scholars regard partly as a reaction to the Fabricator-General's heresy.

Fate

After the failure of the Siege the traitor tech-priests were driven off Mars by the loyalists of the newly formed [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and fled to the edges of the galaxy and into the [[Eye of Terror]]. There the Dark Mechanicum fell further under the power of the warp. No loyal record describes what became of Kelbor-Hal himself. Mechanicus histories of the period are among the most heavily sealed in its archives, and the question of his end remains open.

Legacy

To the Adeptus Mechanicus Kelbor-Hal is the archetype of the heretek. Imperial scholars still find it remarkable that a magos of such ability could have brought the Mechanicum into rebellion, and the Schism he began is the reason the Mechanicus guards forbidden knowledge as closely as it does. The ruin of the Martian forges during the Schism destroyed knowledge the Imperium has never recovered.
