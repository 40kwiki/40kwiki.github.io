Yriel, Prince of House Ulthanash, is the [[High Admiral]] and an [[Aeldari]] [[Autarch]] of the [[craftworld]] [[Iyanden]]. He is a brilliant if reckless commander in the craftworld's grand fleet. He is not of pure Iyanden blood, but he is honoured as one of the greatest defenders and servants of Iyanden.

Yriel wields the cursed Spear of Twilight, and his fleet is known as the Eldritch Raiders. Imperial records describe him as a consummate Aeldari fleet commander whose choices shaped many of the battles that Iyanden fought.

Iyanden

Main article: [[Craftworld]]

Iyanden is one of the great craftworlds of the [[Aeldari]]. Its exiles, led by Yriel, have fought the [[Tyranids]] and the [[Genestealer Cults]] in the Imperium's [[Sector|sectors]], and they have been among the allies of the [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Blood Angels]] in some campaigns.The campaigns named here are recorded in Imperial intelligence. Their dates are not recorded in full.
