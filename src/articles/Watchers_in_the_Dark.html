The Watchers in the Dark are a secret order of the [[Dark Angels]]. They kept knowledge of the survival of their [[primarch]], [[Lion El'Jonson]], from all but the Emperor and a few of the Chapter's leaders. The Watchers were the Chapter's guardians of this secret for ten thousand years.

The Lion was spirited from the Warp into the heart of the [[Rock]], the fragment of [[Caliban]] that survives as the fortress-monastery of the Dark Angels. He slept there for ten thousand years, and his survival was known only to the Emperor and to the Watchers.

Background

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Lion fought on the outer marches of the Imperium and could not reach Terra before the end of the [[Siege of Terra]]. He returned to Caliban and was betrayed there by his foster brother [[Luther]], who had come under the sway of the powers of [[Chaos]]. The duel between the two destroyed Caliban and shattered the Legion that Luther had suborned into the Fallen.
