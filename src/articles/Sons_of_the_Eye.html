The Sons of the Eye were a large warband of [[Chaos Space Marines]] dedicated to [[Chaos Undivided]]. They broke from the [[Sons of Horus]] when [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] made himself master of the Legion, and were led by the Chaos Lord Drecarth the Sightless, the Blind Butcher of Irridous VII, a former battle-brother and captain of the Legion. Always jealous of the favour [[Horus]] had shown to Abaddon, the warband held him a usurper. Abaddon destroyed them in the Sixth [[Black Crusade]], in 901.M36, and absorbed what was left into the [[Black Legion]].

History

After the Heresy

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

After the death of [[Horus]] aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]], [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] and the surviving [[Sons of Horus]] broke orbit and fled to the [[Eye of Terror]], during the Imperial [[Great Scouring]]. The Legion had been reduced to a fraction of its size, and its captains turned to each of the [[Chaos|Chaos Gods]] in turn for power and invited daemonic possession. Abaddon, sickened by war, wandered alone into the Eye. His brothers carried the stasis-preserved body of Horus to the [[Daemon world|daemon world]] of Maeleum, a graveyard world of steel and rust, and built a fortress from the wrecks of ships lost to the Warp. The Legion interred its [[Primarch|primarch]] in a great tomb and many fell to worshipping it.

During the Slave Wars, the Traitor Legions' wars of the Eye, the [[Emperor's Children]] took the body of Horus from the tomb, some say to clone him and raise a new Warmaster. The Sons of Horus fell into killing each other. Abaddon returned from his Dark Pilgrimage in time to watch the fighting, and judged that Horus's failure had brought the Legion to this. He killed the captains one by one, took command, and destroyed the stolen body and its clones. The Legion put on black in mourning and became the Black Legion.

Drecarth's Folly

Not every Son of Horus swore to Abaddon. Some held that Horus was a god who would come again. Others held that they had finished with all masters. Most of them were lost to the Warp, but a few prospered. Drecarth gathered those who refused Abaddon's oath, and fled deeper into the Eye. His warband raided the daemon worlds and other renegades, and in 892.M32 won the Battle of Keening Deep against the Chaos Lord Voslok and his [[World Eaters]] warband, took the fortress and threw the skulls of its defenders into the world's soulfires. Drecarth tore the daemon axe from Voslok's grip and used it on him.

Abaddon had heard of Drecarth's escape from the Daemon Prince [[Be'lakor]] and from his own sorcerers. In the guise of a truce he allied with the Sons of the Eye in the Sixth Black Crusade of 901.M36. The two forces besieged the Imperial Forge World of Arkreach for months, bombarding its forge cities from orbit, and Abaddon promised Drecarth an equal share of the plunder. When the ruins were won and the dead lay over the manufactoria, Drecarth held out his hand. Abaddon took it and drove the claws of the Talon of Horus into his stomach. Drecarth lived long enough to see his warband bow to Abaddon, and then Abaddon tore out his skull and spine. The Sons of the Eye were taken into the Black Legion as a warning to any who would challenge the Despoiler.Be'lakor's part in the matter is unclear. Imperial scholars of the Ordo Malleus think that his advice to Abaddon served his own plans rather than those of the Chaos Gods.

Organisation and heraldry

The Sons of the Eye still wore the dark verdigris green-black of the [[Sons of Horus]], and the gang-sigils of [[Cthonia]] graven on their armour to record kills and deeds. When Drecarth broke with [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] his warriors cut a line across the Eye of [[Horus]] they wore on their armour, a mark of the blindness of Horus that had brought the Legion to ruin.

Notable members

  • Drecarth the Sightless, [[Chaos]] Lord and founder, killed by [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] at Arkreach in 901.M36.
  • Voslok, [[World Eaters]] warlord, killed by Drecarth at Keening Deep (892.M32).
