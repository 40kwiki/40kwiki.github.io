Vernor Zeck was the Grand Provost Marshal during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). He was one of the few High Lords who openly supported the [[Imperial Fists]] [[Chapter Master]] [[Koorland]] when Koorland deposed the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Udin Macht Udo]] in c. 545.M32.

After the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32 the seat in the council of the Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]] was held by [[Galatea Haas]], nominally as Lady Speaker for Zeck. The record does not say whether Zeck survived the Beheading. Haas's title suggests the seat was kept in his name.
