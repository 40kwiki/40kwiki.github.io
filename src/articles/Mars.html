Mars, called the Red Planet and in Mechanicus usage the Forge World Principal, is the fourth planet of the [[Sol System]] and the homeworld of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is the first and greatest of the Imperium's [[Forge world|forge worlds]], the seat of the [[Fabricator-General]], and to the [[Cult Mechanicus]] the holiest place in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. Its surface is covered by forge-cities, refineries and temple-manufactoria, and the shipyards of the [[Ring of Iron]] in its orbit build the greater part of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s capital ships.

Mars was colonised and terraformed during the [[Dark Age of Technology]] and survived the [[Age of Strife]] as an isolated technocracy, the Mechanicum. In c. 739.M30 its rulers recognised the [[Emperor of Mankind]] as the Omnissiah, the Machine God made flesh, and bound Mars to [[Terra]] by the [[Treaty of Olympus]]. The planet armed the [[Great Crusade]], and Imperial doctrine holds that the two heads of the [[Aquila]] stand for Terra and Mars. During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] sided with [[Horus]], and Mars was torn apart by the [[Schism of Mars]].

Since the Heresy Mars has been the centre of the reformed Adeptus Mechanicus, whose Fabricator-General holds a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it supplied the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of [[Belisarius Cawl]], and its forges now work at a rate not seen since the Great Crusade.

Name

The planet's name is older than the Imperium and is preserved in Terran records from before the [[Age of Strife]]. The adepts of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] also use the names Forge World Principal, the Red Planet and, in older binaric liturgy, the First Altar. Across the Imperium, many [[Forge world|forge worlds]] were founded and terraformed on the Martian model, and the word "Martian" is used loosely of the Mechanicus as a whole.Mechanicus scholars distinguish between "Martian", meaning born on Mars or ordained in one of its forges, and "Mechanicus", meaning any member of the priesthood. Outside the priesthood the two are generally treated as the same.

History

Colonisation and the Dark Age of Technology

Mars was one of the first worlds settled by humanity beyond [[Terra]]. In the [[Age of Technology|Dark Age of Technology]] it was terraformed, given a breathable atmosphere and open seas, and became one of the chief centres of human industry and research. [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] charts from this period, preserved in the [[Librarius]] Omnis, show green lowlands and a northern ocean.

The Mechanicum's own histories begin in the late years of that age, when the planet's engineers and data-priests, cut off from Terra by war and disaster, organised themselves into a priesthood dedicated to the preservation of knowledge. The doctrines of the [[Quest for Knowledge]] and of the [[Machine God]] date, in their earliest form, to this time. Imperial and Mechanicus accounts agree that some of the technologies of the age were sealed in vaults beneath the planet's surface, and that some of those vaults were sealed for good reason.

Age of Strife

Further information: [[Age of Strife]]

[[Warp|The warp]] storms of the [[Age of Strife]] isolated Mars from the wider [[Milky Way|galaxy]]. The planet's terraformed climate collapsed under the pressure of constant warfare and unrestrained industry. Mechanicum records speak of the revolt of thinking machines, which the Mechanicus calls the rising of the [[Men of Iron|Iron Men]], and of wars between rival forges in which whole regions of the planet were rendered uninhabitable. By the end of the age the seas had dried, the atmosphere had thinned and poisoned, and the population had withdrawn into sealed forge-cities.

The Mechanicum emerged from the Age of Strife as the undisputed master of the planet, governed by a [[Fabricator-General]] and a parliament of high adepts sitting at [[Olympus Mons]]. As the warp storms waned, its fleets travelled outwards to raid for technology and to found colonies of tech-priests. Some of these became the first daughter [[Forge world|forge worlds]]; others were lost.

The Treaty of Olympus

Main article: [[Treaty of Olympus]]

When the Emperor had ended the [[Unification Wars]] on Terra, he travelled to Mars. The Mechanicum recognised in him the Omnissiah foretold in its doctrine, and in c. 739.M30 the two powers concluded the Treaty of Olympus, called on Terra the Treaty of Mars. Mars kept its own laws, its own priesthood and its faith in the Machine God, and was exempted from the [[Imperial Truth]]. In return it placed its forges, its [[Titan Legions]], its [[Skitarii]] and its knowledge at the service of the Emperor.

The treaty made Mars an ally of Terra and not its subject. No other world of the Imperium was ever given the same standing, and Imperial jurists have argued over the extent of Martian autonomy ever since.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

Mars armed the [[Great Crusade]]. The shipyards of the Ring of Iron built the expedition fleets, the forges of [[Tharsis Montes|Tharsis]] made the [[Power armour|power armour]] and bolters of the [[Space Marine Legions]], and the Titan Legions marched with the expeditions. Forge masters such as [[Koriel Zeth]] of the Magma City and [[Zagreus Kane]] of Mondus Occulam became powers in their own right. The Mechanicum calls the period the Great Expansion, since hundreds of lost forge worlds and [[Knight world|Knight worlds]] were rediscovered in it.

The priesthood's support was not given without reservation. The [[Kelbor-Hal|Fabricator-General Kelbor-Hal]] resented the Emperor's restrictions on Martian research, in particular the sealing of certain vaults beneath Olympus Mons, among them the Vaults of Moravec, which the Emperor had judged too dangerous to open.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

In 005.M31 [[Horus]] promised Kelbor-Hal that the Emperor's prohibitions would be lifted, and the Fabricator-General declared for the [[Warmaster]]. Kelbor-Hal released a corrupting scrapcode into the planet's noosphere, which drove machines and their operators mad across Mars. The forge-cities fell to fighting one another in the [[Schism of Mars]]. Koriel Zeth's Magma City was destroyed; the loyalist Legio Ignatum and [[Legio Tempestus]] fought the [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Legio Mortis]] across the Tharsis plains; and a force of the [[Imperial Fists]] under [[Sigismund]] landed to recover what it could from Mondus Occulam before the forge was overrun.

By the end of the Schism the traitors held most of the planet. Those who followed Kelbor-Hal became the [[Dark Mechanicum]], and their forges supplied the traitor host in the [[Siege of Terra]]. Mars was blockaded by loyalist forces from Terra, and loyalist adepts under Zagreus Kane fought on in the deserts and the deep catacombs.

Imperial records do not agree on the fate of Kelbor-Hal. Some accounts place him at Terra during the Siege, where he claimed credit for the work of [[Perturabo]]'s engineers against the [[Imperial Palace]]; others say that he fled with the traitors. No reliable record of his death survives.

The Scouring and the Age of Rebirth

After the Siege, loyalist forces retook Mars in the course of the [[Great Scouring]]. Zagreus Kane became Fabricator-General, and the Mechanicum was reformed as the Adeptus Mechanicus. Its Fabricator-General was given a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]], and the priesthood took charge of the maintenance of the [[Golden Throne]], which none but the tech-priests of Mars could understand. The planet's forge-cities were purged, rebuilt and rededicated, a process that Mechanicus records say took more than a century.

From the 32nd to the 41st millennium

Mars was attacked during the [[War of the Beast]] in M32, when Ork forces entered the [[Sol System]], and its shipyards worked without pause to replace the fleets lost in that war. In M34 the old rivalry between the forges of the northern and southern hemispheres broke into open war, which ended only when both sides united against a renegade adept, remembered as the Prophet of Cogs, who had bound millions of abandoned war machines to his will. The [[Moirae Schism]] of early M35, during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], divided the priesthood across the galaxy and took more than two thousand years to suppress.

In M39 the Fabricator-General's claim to the treasures of the Culexus Temple brought Mars and Terra close to war in the affair of the Golden Cog, which was resolved by the [[Officio Assassinorum]]. In the [[Macharian Crusade|Macharian Conquests]] of 392–399.M41, Mars led the forge worlds that supplied the armies of [[Lord Solar Macharius]].

In 998.M41 a squadron of [[Necrons|Necron]] cruisers passed the outer defences of the Sol System, evaded the defence lines at [[Deimos]] and [[Phobos]], and attacked the deep mines of Mars before they were destroyed. The Mechanicus then sealed the [[Noctis Labyrinthus]] behind an exclusion zone held by an entire Skitarii Legion. The purpose of the raid has never been explained in any record open to the wider Imperium.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The [[Great Rift]] of 999.M41 cut Mars off from many of its daughter worlds. At least seven forge worlds were destroyed in the years that followed and others were lost in the warp storms. Mars contributed Skitarii Legions, maniples of the [[Legio Cybernetica]], Knights of [[House Taranis]] and the person of [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] to the [[Indomitus Crusade]] of [[Roboute Guilliman]]. Cawl's [[Primaris Space Marines]], prepared over ten thousand years in vaults that some sources place beneath the Martian surface, were released at this time.

Since the opening of the Rift, Mars has ordered every forge world to raise its output to the limit. Vast fleets have been sent out to strip resources from whole systems, among them the Rust Fields, an asteroid region whose interior has been found to be held by [[Orks]]. Mars and its allies have also fought a series of wars against the Necrons over deposits of [[Noctilith|noctilith]], the black stone from which the [[Cadian Pylons]] were made. Despite the Rift, the [[Temple of All Knowledge]] still receives [[Astropathy|astropathic]] reports from forge worlds across the galaxy every day.

Geography

Mars is a small rocky planet with about two-fifths of Terra's surface gravity. Its natural surface is a desert of iron-red dust and basalt, crossed by canyons and broken by some of the largest volcanoes known in the Imperium. The atmosphere is thin and so heavily polluted that it cannot be breathed outside the sealed forge-cities. Surface temperatures range from far below freezing at night to mild in the equatorial day.

Very little of the natural surface remains visible. Forge-cities, manufactoria, refinery fields, reactor complexes and temple-cities the size of continental plates cover most of the planet, joined by elevated transit tubes and power lines. Between them lie wastes of slag and industrial ruin, some dating to the Age of Strife and still haunted by malfunctioning servitors and abandoned war machines.

Principal regions

  • [[Olympus Mons]], the highest mountain on Mars, is the capital and the site of the largest forge temple in the Imperium. It is the seat of the Fabricator-General and contains the Temple of All Knowledge, whose roof stands about two kilometres high on moving pistons that alter its acoustics for the hymns of the Machine God. Within the temple is the High Altar of Technology, to which every new discovery of the priesthood is dedicated.
  • Tharsis, the volcanic upland around Olympus Mons, contains the [[Tharsis Montes]] (Arsia Mons, Pavonis Mons and Ascraeus Mons) and many of the oldest forge temples, including Mondus Occulam and Mondus Gamma. Mastery of a Tharsis forge is still among the highest honours of the priesthood.
  • [[Noctis Labyrinthus]], the "Labyrinth of the Night", is a maze of canyons between Tharsis and the Valles Marineris. Mechanicus legend says that a being called the Dragon of Mars sleeps beneath it. The region has been closed by an exclusion zone since 998.M41.Some sealed Mechanicus and Inquisitorial sources identify the Dragon of Mars with the [[C'tan]] called the Void Dragon, and state that the Emperor imprisoned it there before the Age of Strife. The Adeptus Mechanicus neither confirms nor denies these accounts, and the matter is [data expunged].
  • The Librarius Omnis, a continent-wide subterranean archive, where the explorer [[Arkhan Land]] recovered the patterns for the [[Land Raider]] and for anti-gravitic plating at the dawn of the Imperium.
  • The fortress temples of the Titan Legions and of [[Imperial Knight|Knight House]] Taranis, the Navis Imperialis assembly yards, and the great space ports of Esperanos and Deus Manus.

Orbit and moons

The Ring of Iron is a band of thousands of orbital shipyards, docks and foundries that circles Mars above the equator. Most of the expedition fleets of the Great Crusade were built there, and it still produces the greater part of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s capital ships. Elements of [[Battlefleet Solar]] are based in its docks.

Mars has two moons. [[Phobos]] carries a line of orbital defences. [[Deimos]] has been absent from Mars' orbit for many thousands of years. It was moved to the orbit of Saturn and serves as a [[Forge world|forge world]] for the [[Grey Knights]] of [[Titan]]; how and when it was moved is not recorded in any open archive.

Government

Mars is a theocracy governed by the [[Fabricator-General]], the head of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. The Fabricator-General rules from [[Olympus Mons]] with the counsel of the high adepts and [[Archmagos|archmagi]] of the planet's forges, and holds a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Under the [[Treaty of Olympus]], Mars is not subject to Imperial governors, [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] outside the treaty's terms, or the tithe, and its rulers have always guarded those privileges. Each forge on the planet is ruled by its own [[Techmarine|forge master]], and rivalries between forges, and between the northern and southern hemispheres, are an old feature of Martian politics.

Society

Martian society is strictly stratified. At its base are the unaugmented citizens, who make up most of the human population and labour in vast numbers at sorting scrap, tending hydroponics, breaking ore and stoking furnaces. Above them are the [[Skitarii]], whose service brings the first battle-grade augmetics and a chance of ordination, then the tech-priests, magi and [[Archmagos|archmagi]] of the priesthood proper. [[Servitor|Servitors]], built from criminals and the condemned, perform much of the planet's mechanical work and are not counted among its people.

Almost every Martian carries electoos, circuits implanted beneath the skin through which the planet's masters can monitor them and, in time of war, drive them to fight. Visitors describe Martian citizens as burly, shaven-headed and dour. Binaric cant is the language of the priesthood, though [[Low Gothic]] is spoken among the workers.

Travel to Mars is restricted for those who are not members of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], and pilgrims of the Cult Mechanicus travel there from every [[Forge world|forge world]]. To its adherents, Mars is the holiest of worlds: the place where the Machine God first spoke to humanity, where the Omnissiah walked, and where every discovery is offered on the [[Temple of All Knowledge|High Altar]]. The priesthood's archives on the planet are believed to rival those of the [[Adeptus Terra]], though they are disordered, and a crystal of genetor-grade data may be found inside a manual for military locomotives.

Military

Mars is among the most heavily defended worlds of the Imperium. Its forces include [[Skitarii|Skitarii Legions]], [[Titan Legions]], the war robots of the [[Legio Cybernetica]] and [[Imperial Knight|Knight House]] Taranis, whose fortress temple stands on the planet. The Titan Legions historically associated with Mars include the Legio Ignatum and the [[Legio Tempestus]]; the [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Legio Mortis]] was also raised there before the Heresy. The planet's orbital defences, the Ring of Iron and the elements of [[Sol System|Battlefleet Solar]] stationed there make it a [[Segmentum Fortress|segmentum fortress]] in its own right.

Legacy

Mars is the model for every other [[Forge world|forge world]], and many were deliberately terraformed and built to resemble it. The double-headed [[Aquila (symbol)|Aquila]] is taught in [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] schola as a symbol of the union of Terra and Mars, and the [[Treaty of Olympus]] remains the legal foundation of the priesthood's place in the Imperium. The [[Schism of Mars]] is remembered by the Mechanicus as its darkest hour, and the [[Dark Mechanicum]] that arose from it is still hunted across the galaxy.
