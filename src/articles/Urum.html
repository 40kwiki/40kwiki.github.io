Urum is a [[crone world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]]. It is the seat of the Consortium of Urum, a [[Chaos]] power that deals in flesh, gene-craft and the trade in forbidden things. The renegade [[Fabius Bile]] is recorded as its master in several accounts.

Imperial scholars treat Urum as one of the notable worlds of the Eye. Its ruling Consortium is held to be the source of much of the gene-craft used by Chaos forces in the Eye and beyond.Accounts of the Consortium's role come from captured records and from Imperial agents. The sources do not give dates for its founding.
