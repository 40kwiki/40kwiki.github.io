The Mythos Angelica Mortis is a tome of ancient knowledge. It lists the twenty [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]]s that make up the [[Astartes Praeses]], a fraternity founded to guard against incursions from the [[Eye of Terror]] after the [[Great Scouring]].

Membership

By the late 41st millennium, seventeen of the twenty Chapters still serve this duty. One Chapter has been destroyed by the [[Archenemy]]. Two others have been branded [[Excommunicate Traitoris]]. No complete list has ever been compiled, but the tome names the known and former members.

Among the listed Chapters is the [[Excoriators]], a Successor of the [[Imperial Fists]].
