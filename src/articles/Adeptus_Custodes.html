The Adeptus Custodes, formerly the Legio Custodes and also called the Custodian Guard, are the bodyguard of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. They are warriors of great size, speed and strength, created by the Emperor himself from his own genetic knowledge, and they serve as the Emperor's guard, his emissaries and his last line of defence. They are far fewer than the Legions of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], but each is the equal or superior of a [[Space Marine]], and they are considered among the most dangerous warriors in the Imperium.

The Custodes have guarded the Emperor from the Unification to the present day. During the [[Horus Heresy]] they were at the heart of the defence of [[Terra]] and went with the Emperor to the final duel against [[Horus]]. They stand watch at the [[Golden Throne]], where the [[Sisters of Silence]] serve beside them.

History

Foundation

The Emperor created the Custodes before the [[Thunder Warriors]] and before the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]]. Accounts of the [[Unification Wars]] of the late 29th and early 30th millennia, which ran until 712.M30, show him flanked by "giants of crimson and gold", and later the Custodes, by then hundreds strong, led the cull of the Thunder Warriors.Date estimated from the sequence of events. Only about one candidate in many thousands could survive their making, which took decades of augmentation and training. The first Chief Custodian and [[Captain-General]] was [[Constantin Valdor]].

The Custodes were intended as the Emperor's personal guard, not an army. Their number was small, and each was trained for a lifetime. They accompanied the Emperor wherever he went, including on the early campaigns of the Great Crusade.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

During the Great Crusade the Custodes served as the Emperor's household and bodyguard. They also took on the secret tasks that the Emperor did not entrust to the Legions, among them diplomatic missions, the investigation of threats to the Palace and, from 000.M31, the guarding of the Emperor's work in the [[Webway]]. They were seldom seen outside Terra, and, with the exception of Valdor, rarely spoke of their business.

One force of Custodes fought at the [[Battle of Gyros-Thravian]], where, with the Emperor himself, they broke an Ork horde that had nearly beaten the Legions of [[Horus]], [[Rogal Dorn]] and [[Mortarion]]; no more than a thousand have ever been seen together.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the Heresy the Custodes became the core of the Palace's defence. In 004.M31 Valdor and a force of Custodians went with [[Leman Russ]] and the [[Space Wolves]] to Prospero, and from 004.M31 the Custodes also fought the [[Webway War]] beneath the Palace. In 014.M31 they defended the Palace in the [[Siege of Terra]]. Only a handful of them went with the Emperor to the [[Vengeful Spirit]], and they could not prevent Horus from crippling him. They bore the Emperor's failing body back to Terra, set it within the [[Golden Throne]], donned black mourning cloaks and swore a penitent's oath. They have not left him since.

The Legio Custodes was reformed as the Adeptus Custodes before the [[Second Founding]], and kept its strength of ten thousand. At some time in these years Valdor vanished from the histories, along with his weapons and armour, and the Custodians elected a new Captain-General from their own ranks.

The long watch

For ten thousand years the Custodes have remained on Terra. They almost never left the Palace and, until the [[Era Indomitus]], took little open part in the affairs of the Imperium, though they ended the rule of the High Lord Goge Vandire in the Age of Apostasy. In about 999.M41, after the opening of the [[Great Rift]], a host of Khornate daemons assailed the Lion's Gate of the Palace, and the Custodes, with [[Roboute Guilliman]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]], destroyed it. Afterwards Captain-General Trajann Valoris and Guilliman agreed that the Custodes should take the fight beyond Terra. The three hundred Companions still guard the Throne, but Shield Companies have since joined the [[Indomitus Crusade]], garrisoned the Sol System's outer defences and hunted heretics across the galaxy.

Organisation

The Custodes are led by the Captain-General. The [[Captain-General]] named in the records of the [[Era Indomitus]] is [[Trajann Valoris]], in office by 999.M41, who has overseen the Custodes' wider role since the Great Rift. Beneath him are the Custodians, who are organised in orders and hosts.

  • The Shield Hosts are the Custodes' fighting formations, each commanded by a Shield-Captain. Among them are the Solar Watch, the Emissaries Imperatus and the Shadowkeepers.
  • The Dread Host is a formation of Custodian veterans entombed in Contemptor-Achillus [[Dreadnought|dreadnoughts]].
  • The Emissaries Imperatus are diplomats and agents who carry the Emperor's will to the Imperium's lords.
  • The Companions are the three hundred warriors charged with the direct defence of the Emperor on the Golden Throne.
  • The Sisters of Silence, though not Custodes, serve with them as watchers over the Throne and the Palace.

Powers and equipment

A Custodian is larger than a Space Marine, and he has been shaped by the Emperor to be stronger, faster and more durable than any other human warrior. Each carries a guardian spear, which is both a long blade and a bolt-weapon, or a sentinel blade and shield. They wear armour of auramite, a golden alloy, and fight in the Emperor's name with no regard for their lives. The Custodes also have a number of vehicles, of which the best known are the Caladius grav-tanks and the Allarus [[Terminator armour|Terminators]], and are served by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] on request.

The Custodes receive a form of training that has been described as the most demanding in the Imperium. They are tested in the Palace's arenas against each other and against the Emperor's other warriors, and their dedication is believed to be absolute. The mental assaults of sorcerers are said to rebound from their conviction, for they share the Emperor's blood.

Relations with others

The Custodes' relations with the other institutions of the Imperium are formal and distant. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] respect them as the Emperor's own, and the [[Inquisition]] works with them on matters that concern the Palace. The [[Captain-General]] sits among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Since his return [[Roboute Guilliman]] has worked with Valoris to widen the role of the Custodes.
