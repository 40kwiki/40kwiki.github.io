The Nachmund Sector is a war-torn sector of Imperial space in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It holds one end of the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], one of the only known routes through the [[Great Rift]] between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]].

Notable systems and worlds

  • [[Nachmund System]]: an Imperial system near the [[Eye of Terror]] that contains one end of the Nachmund Gauntlet.
  • [[Vigilus System]]: centred on the star Astravigilia, on the Imperium Nihilus side of the Gauntlet. It is the only confirmed navigable passage through the Rift, and the system has been attacked by many factions.
  • [[Vigilus]]: an Imperial [[Hive world]] and the terminus of the route. It became the primary war zone of the [[War of Beasts]] in the early [[Era Indomitus]], and was then designated a [[War World]] and a [[Sentinel World]].
  • [[Daethe]]: an [[Exodite]] maiden world near the Gauntlet, attacked by a [[Black Legion]] warband called the Forgeborn under the [[Warpsmith]] D'vok.

History

Not long after [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] withdrew from Vigilus following the War of Beasts, the Black Legion Forgeborn assaulted Daethe. The attack was contested by the [[Asuryani]] [[Autarch]] Ghaelyn of [[Craftworld Saim-Hann]].
