The 36th millennium (M36) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M36 to 999.M36. It is the [[Age of Apostasy]], the second great civil war of the Imperium. [[Goge Vandire]], Master of the [[Administratum]], made himself [[Ecclesiarch]] as well and ruled by terror for about seventy years in the [[Reign of Blood]], until the preacher [[Sebastian Thor]] and the [[Confederation of Light]] rose against him and [[Alicia Dominica]] beheaded him in c. 288.M36.

Thor's reforms reshaped the Imperium. The [[Decree Passive]] forbade the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] to keep "men under arms", the [[Adepta Sororitas]] and the [[Ordo Hereticus]] were founded, and the [[Holy Synod]] was reformed. The [[Plague of Unbelief]] of the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis followed within a few decades. In the later centuries the Fifth and Sixth [[Black Crusade]]s struck the Imperium, the [[Jericho Reach|Jericho Sector]] was lost behind [[Warp storm|Warp storms]], and the [[T'au]] were united under the [[Ethereal|Ethereals]].
