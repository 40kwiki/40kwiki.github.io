This is a timeline of the Inquisition, the secret order of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that holds authority over every other institution of the Imperium. The Orders of the Inquisition were formed in the [[War of the Beast]], and from that time they took part in the great wars of the Imperium, in the hunt for heresy and in the defence against the [[Tyranids]] and the [[Chaos Legions]].

Many of the Inquisition's records are sealed, and the Orders are known to keep their own calendars. Where the sources differ, the date in the Administratum's reconciled register is given. Dates marked "c." rest on the sequence of events.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.
