Penitence is a form of punishment in the [[Imperial Cult]] in which a repentant sinner is required to atone by fighting, and usually dying, against the enemies of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. It is a sentence of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and of the [[Inquisition]], and it is reserved for those whose offences are grave but who have confessed and repented. The penitent is held to earn forgiveness only through a heroic death in battle.

Penitence takes two main forms. The first is the [[Penitent Engine]], a combat walker in which the sinner is bound as the pilot. The second is the Repentia, Sisters of Battle who fight in the same spirit. Both are considered a sacred penance, and both are watched by the [[Adepta Sororitas]] as a reminder of the cost of sin.

Penitent Engine

A Penitent Engine is a large bipedal combat walker used by the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] to punish those [[Heretic|heretics]] who have repented their sins. The Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]] also use Penitent Engines as heavy infantry fire support, in the way that [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] use [[Dreadnought]]s.

Crime and punishment

To be a pilot of a Penitent Engine is to have committed a crime so grave that imprisonment, exile or [[Arco-flagellant|arco-flagellation]] would be too lenient. The penitent is wired into the front of the walker, and, unlike the sealed Dreadnought, is exposed to enemy fire. Many of the souls punished this way were once members of the Ecclesiarchy, [[Imperial Priest]]s who fell from grace, or Battle-Sisters whose failures caused the deaths of others.

Design

The penitent is bound to the walker through fibre-optic neural connectors, and chemical injectors are implanted in the pilot's brain and spine. The pilot is kept alive by stimulants, which let the penitent survive several wounds while feeling the pain of each one. The walker charges the enemy with little regard for its own safety, because its pilot believes that only a heroic death against the Emperor's foes can earn forgiveness.

Armament

Penitent Engines are armed only with close-combat weapons, because the pilot is too frantic to aim at range. Each arm carries a Dreadnought-class close-combat weapon, such as a power saw, a chainfist or a power blade. The arms also carry flamers or heavy flamers. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has not made the technical specifications of the walker publicly available.

Repentia

The Repentia are Sisters of Battle who have been sentenced to penance and who fight with the Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] holds that a death in the Emperor's service can earn forgiveness for such a Sister. The surviving records give few details of their organisation, and this article does not describe them further.
