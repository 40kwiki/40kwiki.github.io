The Noctis Labyrinthus, in [[Low Gothic]] the Labyrinth of the Night, is a region of [[Mars]] made up of a maze of steep-walled canyons, lying between the [[Tharsis Montes|Tharsis]] uplands and the great rift valley of the Valles Marineris. It is one of the few parts of the planet with no forge-cities. Every attempt by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] to settle it has failed, and since 998.M41 it has been closed behind an exclusion zone held by a whole [[Skitarii]] Legion.

Mechanicus legend says that a being called the Dragon of Mars sleeps in the deep tunnels beneath the region. Sealed Inquisitorial and Mechanicus sources identify the Dragon with the [[C'tan]] called the [[Void Dragon]], and say that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] imprisoned it there in a remote age. The priesthood neither confirms nor denies these accounts.

Geography

The canyons of the Labyrinth were formed by faulting, set off by the volcanic activity of the Tharsis region long before humanity first reached Mars. Many are graben valleys whose floors still carry the old upland surface. Elsewhere the floors are broken by landslides, sinkholes and deep crevasses. Beneath the canyons lies a large complex of mines dug during the [[Dark Age of Technology]].

History

The Great Purge

Mechanicum histories say that the mines under the Labyrinth were poisoned with radiation and with chemical and biological weapons during the wars that the priesthood calls the Great Purge, fought between the Mechanicum and its rivals for the surface of Mars early in the [[Age of Strife]]. The contamination is said to be lethal to flesh and machine alike. Some Mechanicus records describe this account as a fabrication by the ruling magi, made to keep curious [[Tech-priest|tech-priests]] out of the tunnels and away from what lies in them.Dating to the early Age of Strife is the Mechanicum's own; no exact year is recorded.

Failed settlements

Through the [[Great Crusade]] and the millennia after it, several forge masters tried to found forge-cities in the Labyrinth. All of them are recorded as having suffered severe and unexplained failures of their machines, and all of the settlements were abandoned. The region remained empty while the rest of the planet was built over.

The Guardians

Sealed records describe a watch kept over the Dragon since the [[Schism of Mars]] of 005–006.M31 by a single Guardian, a Terran woman named [[Dalia Cythera]], and her companion, an ancient Mechanicum war machine of the Protector class called [[Ro-Mu 31]]. According to these records, the Emperor granted both an unnaturally long life so that they could keep the watch. Their existence has never been confirmed in any open archive.These records are held under Inquisitorial seal. The account here follows the summary released to the Logos Historica Verita.

The raid of 998.M41

In 998.M41 a squadron of [[Necrons|Necron]] light cruisers passed the outer defences of the [[Sol System]], ran past the weapons platforms in Mars orbit and the [[Phobos]] line, and attacked the deep mines of Mars before they were destroyed by the [[Imperial Navy]]. The purpose of the raid was never explained in any open record. The [[Fabricator-General]] then declared the Noctis Labyrinthus a perdita exclusion zone, and an entire Skitarii Legion was posted to hold it.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The exclusion zone has been kept in force since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41. The Mechanicus has given no public account of what, if anything, the Legion guarding the Labyrinth has encountered.
