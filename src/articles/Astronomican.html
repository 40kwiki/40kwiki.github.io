The Astronomican is a psychic beacon of immense power that shines from [[Terra]] through the [[Warp]] across most of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. It is the means by which the Imperium's ships navigate the Immaterium, and it has been a foundation of Imperial travel since the [[Great Crusade]]. It is calibrated and projected by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] from the [[Golden Throne]], through a vast apparatus in the Chamber of the Astronomican beneath the Hollow Mountain in the Himalazian range, and it is fuelled by the life forces of ten thousand specially chosen [[Psyker|psykers]], of whom up to a thousand die every solar day.

The Astronomican is invisible to the ordinary eye, but any psyker can sense it as a silver light and a chorus of voices that begins with one voice and swells into a choir. To the [[Navigator|Navigators]], the mutated humans whose third eye can look into the Warp, it is a fixed point by which a ship's course can be plotted through the changing currents of the Immaterium. Without it, a ship is limited to jumps of four or five light-years calculated by cogitator, and long-range [[Warp travel|warp travel]] across the Imperium would be impossible.

Origins

At the opening of the Great Crusade, in c. 798.M30, the worlds near Terra could be reached by blind jumps through the Warp, which had only lately calmed after the Age of Strife. To go further the Navigators needed a stable point in the Immaterium against which to fix their position. After the [[Unification Wars]] the Emperor ordered the building of the Astronomican on Terra, and a great host of tech-priests was brought from [[Mars]] to oversee the work while most of the population of Terra was set to raising the machine-buildings that support it. The machine is only a focus. The signal itself is the Emperor's own psychic power, shaped and sent through the Warp.

Before the Astronomican, the work of a beacon had been done by older devices of xenos make, such as the Pharos that the [[Ultramarines]] later found beneath Mount Pharos on Sotha. Imperial savants hold that these were made by the [[Necrons]] and that they formed a network of waymarks across the human part of the galaxy, but a Pharos is a lantern for a single place and not a beacon seen from afar.

Few knew that the Emperor Himself powered it. In the early years of the Great Crusade His strength was great enough to sustain the beacon while He was off Terra at the head of the expeditionary fleets, but the task wore on Him, and it was one reason He gave the command of the Great Crusade to [[Horus]] after the Ullanor Crusade and returned to Terra to finish the Webway Project, which was meant to make the Astronomican all but unnecessary.

Operation

The Emperor and the Chosen

As the Webway Project made greater and greater demands on the Emperor before the [[Horus Heresy]], the Astronomican's signal weakened across the galaxy. [[Malcador the Sigillite]] proposed a compromise: the Emperor would still guide the beacon, but most of its energy would come from the psychic and life energies of ten thousand sacrificed psykers, leaving the Emperor free to complete the Webway. He agreed only with great reluctance and as a temporary measure.

It did not stay temporary. The wounds the Emperor took from Horus in the [[Siege of Terra]] left Him bound to the Golden Throne. His mind still sets the frequency of the beacon, but He cannot again power it alone, and the sacrifice of psykers has become permanent. Their lives are spent in a matter of solar months, so the ranks must always be filled with fresh recruits from the tithe of psykers that the [[Black Ships]] carry to Terra.

The Adeptus Astronomica

The upkeep of the Astronomican is the duty of the [[Adeptus Astronomica]], a branch of the [[Adeptus Terra]]. It trains the sanctioned psykers who power the beacon, and recruits them through the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]]. They enter as acolytes, and learn their gifts together with the lore and the philosophy of the adepta. Some reach a mystic state and are named the Chosen, and these are called in time to serve in the Chamber of the Astronomican. There their strength is drawn by the Emperor to feed the beacon, and they slowly fade and die. The Adeptus reckons this a sacrifice for the survival of mankind.

Navigators and the fleets

Imperial ships carry [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] of the great Houses, who use the Astronomican to plot their route. It lets fleets cross great distances in predictable time. Its range is about seventy thousand light-years, though some Imperial charts give fifty thousand and Warp storms can shorten it. Terra lies in the galactic west, so the beacon only just reaches the Eastern Fringe, and the arrival of the Warp-smothering [[Tyranids|hive fleets]] has made it fainter there. In effect, the reach of the Astronomican sets the true borders of the Imperium.

Pious [[Space Marines]] are said to hear it, and to fall into a meditative trance when they listen. Because of its place in the faith of the [[Imperial Cult]], it is also called the Emperor's Light, the Divine Light, the Ray of Hope and the Golden Path.

Importance

The Imperium could not survive as a star-spanning society without the Astronomican. It lets Terra keep contact with distant worlds and lets the [[Imperial Navy]] gather its fleets, and the whole structure of [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial government]] depends on it. Its destruction would badly cripple all Imperial Warp travel and bring the Imperium to its knees. The Imperial scholars add that the light also draws the Tyranids, which were alerted to the galaxy by the waking of the Pharos on Sotha during the Heresy, and which now move toward Terra.

The Great Rift

Main article: [[Great Rift]]

In 999.M41 the fall of [[Cadia]] at the end of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] was followed by the eruption of the [[Great Rift]], which cut the Imperium in two. The Noctis Aeterna, also called the Blackness or the Days of Blinding, took the Astronomican from the Navigators and from astropathic communication. Across the Rift, in the half that became the [[Imperium Nihilus]], the light could not be sensed, and the Blackness there lasted for solar decades or even centuries. On Terra it lasted only thirty-three solar days.

Over time the effects of the Rift faded on Terra's side, and Warp travel became more dependable in the [[Imperium Sanctus]]. This, with the onset of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], restored the hold of the Emperor on the Sanctus, but the Imperium Nihilus remained beyond His light. The price was heavy: the fate of nearly half of the thousand Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] was unknown at the end of the Blackness, and at least twelve Chapter homeworlds were reported destroyed.
