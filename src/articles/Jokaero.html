The Jokaero are a sentient [[alien]] species that resembles the orange-furred great apes of Old Earth. Although they have no known language, they can build and manipulate advanced technology, and they excel at weapons design, especially miniaturised weapons. They are not a naturally evolved species. The [[Old Ones]] created them during the [[War in Heaven]], more than sixty million Terran years ago, to aid their struggle against the [[C'tan]] and the [[Necrons]].

The Jokaero were made alongside several other intelligent races, among them the [[Aeldari]], the [[Rashan]], the [[K'Nib]] and the green-skinned [[Krork]], possibly the ancestors of the [[Orks]].

Technology

The defining trait of the Jokaero is their extraordinary, seemingly innate skill at building advanced technology from even the most basic parts. The skill may be related to the similar gift of the [[Orks]]. The difference is that Jokaero technology works for members of other species, whereas greenskin devices usually function only for Orks.

The Jokaero are also renowned for their work in miniaturising advanced weapons. Their [[Digital Weapons|Digi-weapons]] are highly prized across the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Their form resembles a large ring, yet the most powerful examples match the punch of an [[Inferno Pistol]]. [[Highborn]] nobles, [[Rogue Trader]]s and [[Inquisitor]]s have paid large sums to obtain them.

Service to the Inquisition

In recent centuries, some Inquisitors, most often the more radical members of the [[Ordo Xenos]], have taken Jokaero into their retinues of [[Acolyte]]s. Their innate affinity with technology lets them alter the weapons and armour of the retinue in battle. Many of these changes are cosmetic. Some Jokaero modifications change the munitions of a weapon, increasing its damage in ways that are hard to predict. They can also build force fields and imaging systems on the spot to improve the accuracy and rate of fire of their companions.

Anatomy and culture

Jokaero are heavyset, orange-furred humanoids who recall the orangutans of Old Earth, but with retractable fingers and toes. Their appearance misleads humans, for they are unparalleled masters of advanced technology. Imperial savants note the resemblance to Terran apes and speculate that the [[Old Ones]] may have uplifted that primate to sentience, since they are known to have visited [[Terra]] and to have had a hand in the evolution of [[Mankind]].

Their skill seems innate, a gift at the genetic level, much as the gift of the [[Orks]] known as [[Mekboyz]]. Given enough components, even damaged ones, a working group of Jokaero can build anything from an advanced starship to a [[Lascannon]]. They are forever devising new inventions, though seldom the same way twice. None can say whether they are truly sentient or whether they carry out an inherited instinct without understanding it.

Jokaero live in family groups of about a dozen, led by a matriarch or patriarch, which roam the galaxy at random with survival as their only aim. They arrive on Imperial worlds to scavenge technology, and sometimes band together for well-coordinated raids on lone power stations, farmsteads or mining vessels. They escape with their loot before a response can come. They cannot be reasoned with, and how they speak to each other is unknown. Attempts to hold them captive to work on set projects fail, because they simply invent a way out.

Inventions

Besides [[Digital Weapons]], the Jokaero are known for [[Defence Orb]]s and for the Spy-flies, tiny crystalline automatons that resemble flying insects and carry out reconnaissance by sight and sound. Because the Spy-flies need psychic input, [[Inquisitor]] [[Jaq Draco]] suspected that the Jokaero may have psychic powers. Their force-field emitters are adapted from Imperial ones and work on a principle like the [[Gellar Field]] of a starship, so [[Ordo Malleus]] operatives prize them against daemonic threats.

Jokaero starships are open lattices in the shape of an unusual polyhedron, unlike any vessel of another species. They appear to draw on currents of energy unknown to other peoples, and the ships move and enter the [[Warp]] by changing their own shape.
