The Black Wings is a loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] whose genetic origins and Founding are not recorded. It follows the rules of the [[Codex Astartes]] in its organisation and in its conduct of war, as most Chapters of the Imperium do.

In 700.M35 half of the Chapter renounced its vows to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] for reasons that Imperial records do not establish. The renegade half turned on its former brethren. In the war that followed, much of the Chapter's fortress-monastery was devastated and a number of its fleet vessels were destroyed. The Chapter was not reconstituted as a single force for a long period afterwards.

Renunciation of 700.M35

The cause of the renunciation is recorded as unknown. Some [[Administratum]] records suggest a corruption by the [[Warp]], while Chapter records list a dispute over the succession of its leadership. Both accounts agree that the war was fought between the two halves of the same Chapter, and that neither side received aid from other [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]].
