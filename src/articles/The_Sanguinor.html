The Sanguinor, also called the "Exemplar of the Host" and the "Golden Angel", is a mysterious entity of unknown origin associated with the [[Blood Angels]] [[Chapter]]. It is named after the martyred [[primarch]] [[Sanguinius]]. Little is known of its origin. What is known is mostly speculation by the Chapter's [[Librarium]] and by Imperial scholars who study the lore of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

The Sanguinor appears when the Blood Angels are in their greatest need, usually in combat. To many battle-brothers it is a myth, because few survive the battles in which it appears. The initiates of the [[Reclusiam]] know otherwise. They have kept detailed records of its appearances across the centuries.

Debate over its identity

The deeds of the Sanguinor are not disputed, but its identity is. The Blood Angels' [[Chapter Council]] holds that the Sanguinor is the manifestation of the nobility of [[Sanguinius]] himself. In this view, it is the part of the primarch that kept his darkness in check, and it was lost to the Chapter with his death at the hands of [[Horus]] aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] at the climax of the [[Siege of Terra]].

The [[Sanguinary Guard]] does not accept that the Sanguinor is the primarch. They hold that it is [[Azkaellon]], the original founder of their order, preserved beyond time by the will of the [[Emperor]]. The legend has long since spread beyond the Blood Angels. It has added to the unease that parts of the Imperium feel towards them.

Inquisitorial scholars have their own theories. Some suggest that the Sanguinor is a psychic construct born of the [[Warp]] from the collective desires and needs of Blood Angels in extreme duress. Others consider it a darker empyric manifestation. Some Inquisitors take these theories as proof that the Blood Angels have come close to [[mutation]], corruption and [[heresy]].The sources do not record a single Inquisitorial doctrine on the Sanguinor. The theories listed are those held by individual scholars.

The Sanguinor is said to fight an unending battle in the Warp against the [[Black Angel]], a psychic entity that embodies the Flaws of Sanguinius and of the Blood Angels, such as the [[Black Rage]]. The Sanguinor grows stronger each time a son of [[Baal]] protects an innocent, and the Black Angel grows each time one of them falls to the Black Rage.

Origins

Blood Angels tradition offers two possibilities. One is that the Sanguinor is [[Azkaellon]], last of the [[Sanguinary Guard]] to survive the [[Horus Heresy]], though this seems implausible even for a Space Marine. The other, more widely accepted, is that when [[Horus]] slew Sanguinius his soul split in two. The anguished half became the Flaws that torment the Chapter, and the noble half took form in the [[Immaterium]] as the Sanguinor.

The title itself is recorded as older. During the [[Imperium Secundus]] of the Heresy years, Sanguinius served as regent but could not appear in public for fear of assassination. Azkaellon persuaded him to name a herald. Ten Sanguinary Guard drew lots, two marked in blood, and the lot of Alatron was drawn over that of Haratial. To hide the herald's identity, Azkaellon executed Haratial and seared a helm in the likeness of Sanguinius onto Alatron's head. Alatron became the Sanguinor, Herald of Sanguinius. Whether he survived the Heresy, and whether the present Sanguinor is the same being, is not recorded.

Notable appearances

The Sanguinor appears rarely, and only when the situation is dire and of great weight to the Chapter and the Imperium. He does not hand the Blood Angels victory, but makes the impossible possible. In the legend of the assault on the [[Night Lords]] battle barge Terrorclaw, twelve battle-brothers boarded to slay its master as penance, and the Sanguinor joined them as their boarding torpedo struck. He cut a path through the ship, but only the sergeant in command survived to return to Baal.

In 823.M41, at Khartas, the Sanguinor appeared as the Bloodthirster [[Ka'Bandha]] attacked the survivors of Captain Zorael's 4th Company. He broke the daemon's axe, stabbed it and carried it high into the sky, where he let it fall. Only six battle-brothers lived to be recovered, and the Sanguinor had vanished.

In 997.M41, in the Cryptus Campaign against [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], he descended on the Tyranids beset around the Death Company on Tartaros. Afterwards he spoke for the first time on record to [[Commander Dante]], answering his question whether Baal could be saved with the words "There is yet hope." He appeared again at the climax of the [[Devastation of Baal]] in c. 999.M41.
