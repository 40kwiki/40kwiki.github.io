Crypteks are a caste of the [[Necrons]] who build and maintain the dynasty's weapons, tomb ships and machines. They are the Necron equivalent of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and Imperial records describe them as the most dangerous of the Necron castes to fight, because their weapons are the ones most often turned on whole fleets.

Crypteks served under the overlords of their dynasties, and their weapons were the chief tools of the Necron war against the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and the other races of the galaxy.

Superweapons

By a decree of [[Szarekh]], the use of superweapons was permitted to both sides of the Necron wars. Crypteks were then free to use them. A war fleet commanded by Oradi used an [[Ark of Oblivion]] at [[Torantis]], destroying three tomb ships. The weapon harmed the ships of the Necron Behzt as well, and her counter consumed her forces and a third of the mass of the world.

At [[Iash'Uddra]] the Eyes of the Void broke the planet into swarms of canoptek scarabs. At [[Veridian]] a Hyper-Alembic killed the legions of the Overlord Khutmecc. Crypteks of the Nihilakh released shards of the [[C'tan]] at [[Zeta IIX Hespus]] in the middle of the 42nd millennium.The dates of these events are estimated from the order of the sources. Imperial accounts give the sequence but not exact years.
