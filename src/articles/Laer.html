The Laer were a non-humanoid, reptilian [[xenos]] species from the [[Ocean World]] of [[Laeran]]. They worshipped the [[Chaos God]] [[Slaanesh]]. They were first encountered by the [[Emperor's Children]] [[Legion]] during the [[Great Crusade]] in the early [[31st Millennium]]. The Laer were exterminated in the campaign that Imperial records call the [[Cleansing of Laeran]], and the exposure of the Legion to their corruption is recorded as the start of its fall to Chaos.

Culture

Like the Emperor's Children, the Laer prized perfection in every part of life. From birth they were shaped by chemical and genetic manipulation, so that each Laer was suited to a particular role. Some were workers, some soldiers, some diplomats, and some artists.

First contact

Shortly after the Emperor's Children began the Great Crusade on their own terms, after leaving the [[Luna Wolves]], their [[expeditionary fleet|28th Expeditionary Fleet]] met the serpentine aliens who called themselves the Laer. Analysis of captured scouts and envoys showed that the Laer were concentrated in a single star system, on a homeworld covered entirely by a global ocean. Imperial records code the world as Twenty-Eight-Three, while its own name was Laeran.

The Laer were a formidable foe. Imperial observers accompanying the fleet suggested that the Laer might be made a protectorate of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Conquering so efficient a species would be long and costly. [[Fulgrim]] rejected any co-operation with a xenos race. In his view only [[Humanity]] was perfect, and an alien species that claimed ideals comparable to Mankind's deserved only annihilation. He ordered his [[Praetor (Space Marine)|Lord Commanders]] to attack at once.

The Cleansing of Laeran

The Council of Terra predicted that the war would last several solar decades. Fulgrim is recorded as having said that the Imperium would rule Laeran within a single solar month. The campaign was brutal and bloody. The Emperor's Children destroyed the Laer species, but at a cost. The Astartes' exposure to Laer corruption is recorded as the first step in the downfall of the Emperor's Children and their primarch into servants of [[Slaanesh]].
