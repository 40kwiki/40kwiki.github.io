The Laer were a non-humanoid, reptilian [[xenos]] species from the [[Ocean World]] of [[Laeran]]. They worshipped the [[Chaos God]] [[Slaanesh]]. They were first encountered by the [[Emperor's Children]] [[Legion]] during the [[Great Crusade]] in the early [[31st Millennium]]. The Laer were exterminated in the campaign that Imperial records call the [[Cleansing of Laeran]], and the exposure of the Legion to their corruption is recorded as the start of its fall to Chaos.

Earlier Imperial accounts placed the invasion of Laeran in 893.M31, long after the [[Second Founding]]. The later and accepted record places it in the closing years of the Great Crusade, within the first decade of the 31st millennium.

Culture

Like the Emperor's Children, the Laer prized perfection in every part of life. From birth they were shaped by chemical and genetic manipulation, so that each Laer was suited to a particular role. Some were workers, some soldiers, some diplomats, and some artists.

The Laer lived on massive coral platforms that floated above their world's ocean on anti-gravity technology. They had moved onto the platforms after rising seas sank every continent of Laeran.

Physiology

The Laer were wildly diverse in form, because each was engineered for its own theatre of war. The Emperor's Children met winged, aquatic and many other variants of the basic Laer body. A typical Laer was tall and sinuous, with a snake-like lower body and a muscular thorax sheathed in silver armour. Warriors carried long power blades in their upper hands and gauntlets that fired green energy bolts in their lower hands. Their heads were bulbous and insect-like, with glossy faceted eyes and jutting mandibles that screeched as they attacked.

Their engineering gave them many advantages. Their warships were linked directly to the minds of their crews by [[cybernetic]] means, their amphibious warriors breathed water through gills, their scouts moved as fast as a [[Land Speeder]], and their gunners could pick out single [[Space Marines]] at a distance of kilometres.

First contact

Shortly after the Emperor's Children began the Great Crusade on their own terms, after parting from the [[Luna Wolves]], their [[expeditionary fleet|28th Expeditionary Fleet]] met the serpentine aliens who called themselves the Laer. Analysis of captured scouts and envoys showed that the Laer were concentrated in a single star system, on a homeworld covered entirely by a global ocean. Imperial records code the world as Twenty-Eight-Three, while its own name was Laeran.

The Laer were a formidable foe. Imperial observers accompanying the fleet suggested that the Laer might be made a protectorate of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Conquering so efficient a species would be long and costly. [[Fulgrim]] rejected any co-operation with a xenos race. In his view only [[Humanity]] was perfect, and an alien species that claimed ideals comparable to Mankind's deserved only annihilation. He ordered his [[Praetor (Space Marine)|Lord Commanders]] to attack at once.

The Cleansing of Laeran

The [[Council of Terra]] predicted that the war would last several solar decades. Fulgrim is recorded as having said that the Imperium would rule Laeran within a single solar month. The Emperor's Children, with the Archite Palatine regiments of the [[Imperial Army]] under Lord Commander Fayle, attacked the Laer in orbit, on the world's surface, beneath its oceans and over the hulls of its orbital platforms.

The campaign was brutal and bloody. Casualties on both sides were severe, and the Legion's [[Apothecaries]] are credited with saving more than half of the warriors who might otherwise have died of their wounds. The Laer never surrendered. Their last warriors died in the ruins of their capital, defending the central temple of their worship of Slaanesh. One solar month after the attack began, Fulgrim planted a standard bearing the [[Palatine Aquila]] over the last of the Laer dead. More than seven hundred of his warriors had fallen and some four thousand were wounded.

Aftermath

The Emperor's Children destroyed the Laer species, but at a cost. Fulgrim, and several of the civilian [[Remembrancer]]s who entered the Laer temple, were exposed to the touch of [[Slaanesh]]. This is recorded as the first step in the downfall of the Legion and its primarch into servants of the Chaos God.

The Legion's Chief [[Apothecary]], [[Fabius Bile]], used the knowledge of genetic engineering he took from Laer corpses to develop a series of modified [[gene-seed]] organs, with the consent of Fulgrim. These organs were later implanted into every warrior of the Legion who followed Fulgrim against the Emperor. The early experiments led to the first [[Noise Marines]] and to other mutant variants of the [[Chaos Space Marines]]. Laeran has since been settled by Humanity and holds three Imperial cities and a dozen mining colonies. No trace of the Laer remains.
