The Ordo Hydra is an extremist offshoot of the secret society of the [[Illuminati]]. It is dedicated to extreme measures that would destroy the existence of [[Chaos]] in the universe once and for all. Its membership is made up mostly, though not entirely, of Illuminati who are also [[Inquisitor]]s. Some of them hold very high rank in the [[Inquisition]].

Origins

The Ordo Hydra grew from the deep hatred of Chaos felt by these Illuminati. Their impatience with the mainstream Illuminati was a second cause. The mainstream plans were slow to bear fruit, and their outcome was uncertain. In their haste, the Ordo Hydra's members used [[sorcery]] to summon a [[Warp]] entity called the Hydra. It was a psychic "virus" that could enter a human mind and dominate it completely.

The Hydra plan

The Ordo Hydra planned to infect as many human worlds as possible. Its infected masses would then act as a single hive mind, delivering a concentrated psychic blow against Chaos in the [[Immaterium]]. The Ordo believed that the [[Dark Gods]] could not resist such a blow. It held that the blow could be struck in a matter of Terran centuries, rather than the several millennia that the mainstream plans required.

The mainstream Illuminati opposed the plan. It would have amounted to the psychic enslavement of much of Mankind, even if it succeeded. There was also a real danger that the plan would backfire. It could have created a fifth [[Chaos God]] out of the collective unconscious of the infected, which would have ensured the victory of the Ruinous Powers.

Secret war

The rift between the Illuminati factions led to secret operations. Rival Inquisitor-Illuminati were assassinated, and several double agents were used. In one case, the mainstream Illuminati used a double agent, [[Zephro Carnelian]], to manipulate the [[Ordo Malleus]] Inquisitor [[Jaq Draco]] into joining the Ordo Hydra. They hoped he would see the danger of its plans and act against it.

Draco was appalled by the plans. He travelled secretly to [[Terra]], where he became one of the few mortals to enter the Hall of the [[Golden Throne]] in the [[Imperial Palace]]. There he told the [[Emperor of Mankind]] of the Ordo Hydra's plan. Imperial records do not show what measures were taken against the Ordo Hydra after Draco's revelations.
