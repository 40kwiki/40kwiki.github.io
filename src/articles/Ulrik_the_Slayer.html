Ulrik the Slayer is a [[Wolf Priest]] of the [[Space Wolves]]. In the last days of the 41st millennium he declared the return of the [[Wulfen]] an omen that the [[Leman Russ|Primarch]] Russ's return was near.

The Wulfen of the Thirteenth had emerged from Warp storms to save the Great Company of Harald Deathwolf at the Battle of Nurades. They were sent to [[Fenris]] for judgement before the [[Great Wolf]] [[Logan Grimnar]].

Hunt for the Wulfen

Ragnar Blackmane argued that every Wulfen must be gathered before the [[Inquisition]] found them. The Great Companies set out across the galaxy on the Hunt for the Wulfen, recovering them from daemon worlds and warzones. The hunt brought the [[Dark Angels]] and their allies to Fenris in fear of what the Space Wolves were hiding.The dates of the Hunt are not recorded. Imperial accounts place it at the very end of the 41st millennium.
