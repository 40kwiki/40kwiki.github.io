A sub-sector is the division of an Imperial [[Sector|sector]] that holds a cluster of star systems close enough to be patrolled by one squadron of [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] ships. It is the lowest level of territorial government that has its own administrative seat. Below it lie the individual star systems and the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]] who rule them.

The sub-sector is defined by the reach of Imperial starships, not by the volume of space it holds. Its systems share [[Warp travel|warp routes]], trade and defence, and most are within a few weeks' voyage of one another. Many sub-sectors are named after their principal world.

History

During the [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31) worlds were grouped by the expedition fleets that conquered them. The grouping that later became the sub-sector was first used to share out the tithe between nearby worlds. Records of the late [[30th millennium]] use the word for such groups, but the term was not fixed as an office.

The reforms that followed the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) fixed the sub-sector as a unit of government below the sector. The reforms are placed in c. 015–021.M31 by most Administratum records. The first boundaries of each sub-sector were drawn by survey fleets, and many were redrawn later.The dates of the reforms are estimated from the sequence of events. The Administratum's first survey registers are lost.

In M36, [[Warp storm|warp storms]] of the [[Age of Apostasy]] cut many sub-sectors off from their sector capitals. Some were not reached again for centuries. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has restored several sub-sectors, while others remain uncharted.

Organisation

Star systems

A sub-sector holds between two and eight star systems in most cases, although some hold more and a few hold only one. Systems that lie between sub-sectors are called inter-sector space, or [[Wilderness space|wilderness space]]. Many such systems are unknown to the Imperium.

Civil government

Each sub-sector has a governor who answers to the [[Sector Governor]]. In some sub-sectors the governor is also the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]] of the principal world. In others the post is given to a cardinal of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] or to an official of the [[Administratum]].

Naval and military

A sub-sector is patrolled by squadrons of the [[Imperial Navy]], which are drawn from the sector battlefleet. Its regiments are raised by the [[Departmento Munitorum]] and kept on the worlds of the sub-sector, and they answer to the sector's command in time of war.
