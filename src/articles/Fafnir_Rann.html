Fafnir Rann was a captain and lord seneschal of the [[Imperial Fists]] Legion during the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. He was known as a ruthless and bellicose officer, and he fought in many of the Legion's most notable campaigns. Imperial records describe him as a commander who valued the discipline of the siege, which the Imperial Fists were famous for.

During the Heresy he took part in the [[Battle of Phall]], the [[Battle of Pluto]] and the climactic [[Siege of Terra]]. Accounts of the Siege record his unit as one of the forces that held the walls of the Imperial Palace against the traitor [[Legiones Astartes|Legions]].

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Fafnir Rann's conduct during the Heresy is preserved in the Imperial Fists' own records. Those records praise his stubbornness in defence and his refusal to withdraw while the Legion was still in the field. Some later accounts question details of his service, and these are listed as disputed in Administratum registers.
