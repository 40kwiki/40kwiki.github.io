The Kasrkin, pronounced "KAH-sir-kin", are elite troops of the [[Astra Militarum]]. They are dedicated to the security of the [[Fortress world]] of [[Cadia]], from which they were drawn. Because they are elite special forces, they are recruited from the same world as the [[Cadian Shock Troops]] regiments, and they share many of that regiment's doctrines.

Kasrkin are trained to fight in small units and to carry heavy weapons, including the heavy plasma gun. Their selection is strict, and Imperial records describe their discipline as the highest of any regiment drawn from Cadia. Their deployments are set by the [[Imperial Guard]] high command and are often kept secret.
