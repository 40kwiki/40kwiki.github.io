The Adeptus Terra, also called the Priesthood of Terra, is the vast body of adepta that governs the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from [[Terra]]. It comprises the departments, offices and orders through which the will of the [[High Lords of Terra]] is carried out: the record-keeping and tithe-taking [[Administratum]], the telepathic communicators of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]], the keepers of the [[Astronomican]] in the [[Adeptus Astronomica]], and the judges of the [[Adeptus Arbites]]. It is less an agency than an umbrella over many autonomous departments, and its greatest figure in practice is the Master of the Administratum. Its adepts number in the many millions.

The Adeptus Terra grew from the civil government that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] built during the [[Unification Wars]] and the [[Great Crusade]]. After the Emperor was interred within the [[Golden Throne]], the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] of the High Lords took over the government of the Imperium, and it has directed the Adeptus Terra ever since.

The [[Inquisition]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Adeptus Ministorum|Ecclesiarchy]] stand outside it, each with its own hierarchy. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Adeptus Terra has been reformed in part by [[Roboute Guilliman]], the restored [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], whose purge of the bureaucracy met resistance from within the High Lords themselves.

History

Unification and the Great Crusade

See also: [[Unification Wars]] and [[Ullanor Crusade]]

The first adepts of [[Terra]] were bureaucrats who followed the Emperor's armies through the [[Unification Wars]] to build the foundations of his new regime. They were known as the Magisters Temporal, and as the realm grew they were reorganised as the Lords Civilian. The four greatest of them, the Master of the Administratum, the Chancellor of the Estate Imperium, the Grand Provost Marshal and the Lord Commander Militant, became the High Lords Civilian. [[Malcador the Sigillite|Malcador]] was the Emperor's greatest ally in those wars and stood at his left hand in the War Council.

As the Great Crusade carried the Imperium across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], the civil departments multiplied. The [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial Administration]] assessed tithes and kept records; the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica|Astra Telepathica]] gave the expeditions their means of communication; the [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]]' houses gave them the means of travel. After the [[Ullanor Crusade|Triumph of Ullanor]], the Emperor left the war to his primarchs under the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], returned to Terra, and created the [[Council of Terra]] to handle civil affairs, with Malcador as regent in his absence. The primarchs had no place in the Council, and several of them resented it. Horus and the powers of Chaos exploited that grievance, and Imperial historians count it among the causes of the [[Horus Heresy]].

The Heresy and the High Lords

The Council of Terra was disbanded in the months before the [[Siege of Terra]], when the military took control of every part of Imperial life. During the Siege, Malcador willingly took the Emperor's place upon the [[Golden Throne]], using his psychic power to hold back the Warp-spawn that sought to enter Terra. When the Heresy ended, the Emperor was interred in the Throne and could no longer rule directly.

A temporary Grand Council of Reconstruction ruled the Imperium through the chaos of the [[Great Scouring]]. Early in the [[Time of Rebirth]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] set up the Senatorum Imperialis, the High Lords of Terra, with twelve seats, and took the chair himself as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and [[Imperial Regent]]. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] remained outside the civil government, bound by the [[Treaty of Olympus]] (the Treaty of Mars), and the [[Adeptus Astartes]], though formally part of the Adeptus Terra, kept wide autonomy from it.

Growth and decay

In the early [[32nd millennium]] the rise of the Imperial Creed won the Ecclesiarch a seat among the High Lords, which soon became permanent. In 546.M32, during the [[War of the Beast]], the Grand Master of Assassins [[Drakan Vangorich]] had the whole Senatorum assassinated in the coup called the [[Beheading]], and Chapter Masters of the [[Imperial Fists]] held the office of Lord Commander for a time.

In the 36th millennium the [[Master of the Administratum]], [[Goge Vandire]], seized control of the Ministorum as well and ruled the Imperium as a tyrant in the [[Age of Apostasy]], dissolving the Senatorum. The Fabricator-General and four Space Marine Chapters marched on Terra in the Terran Crusade of 378.M36, the reforming preacher [[Sebastian Thor]] rose against him, and Vandire was killed by his own bodyguard, [[Alicia Dominica]]. The Senatorum was restored, and the reforms that followed included the creation of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] and of the [[Adepta Sororitas]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The return of Roboute Guilliman at the end of the 41st millennium brought the first sustained effort in ten thousand years to reform the Adeptus Terra. Guilliman, as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] and [[Imperial Regent]], pledged to deal peacefully with the sitting High Lords, but soon removed and replaced five of them for corruption and incompetence, and ordered a general purge of the bureaucracy called the Primarch's Scourge. He found much of Mankind's history lost to superstition and suppression, and among his remedies was the [[Logos Historica Verita]], an office charged with recovering an accurate history of the Imperium.

Many High Lords continued to oppose him, longing for the stability of the old order. Some current and deposed High Lords, led by the former Master of the Administratum Irthu Haemotalio, formed the Hexarchy and attempted a coup, which the Grand Master of Assassins Fadix foiled.

Organisation

The Adeptus Terra is an umbrella organisation, and each of its divisions works largely autonomously from the others. The most powerful figure in it, and in practice in the whole Imperium, is the [[Master of the Administratum]]. Imperial authorities sometimes disagree about who holds authority over which adepta.

[[Administratum]]

By far the largest of the adepta. It assesses and collects the [[Imperial tithe]], keeps the records of every world and allocates resources among them.

Through the [[Departmento Munitorum]] it also supplies and directs the [[Astra Militarum]]. Its Master sits among the High Lords and is regarded as the most politically powerful of them.

[[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]]

The Adeptus Astra Telepathica recruits and trains the Imperium's [[Psyker|psykers]], and provides the [[Astropath|astropaths]] on whom all interstellar communication depends. Its [[Black Ships]] range across the galaxy in search of rogue psykers to capture and carry back to Terra.

[[Adeptus Astronomica]]

The Adeptus Astronomica maintains the [[Astronomican]], the psychic beacon that guides Imperial navigation, a duty it has held since before the Heresy. Its head, the [[Master of the Astronomican]], represents it as a High Lord of Terra.

[[Adeptus Arbites]]

The Adeptus Arbites enforces [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]], as opposed to the laws of individual worlds, and guards against rebellion by [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]]. Its head is the Lord Marshal of the Arbites.

[[Adeptus Custodes]]

The Emperor's guardians are formally listed among the adepta of the Adeptus Terra, and are led by their [[Captain-General]].

The Administratum itself holds a great many lesser bodies, among them the Officio Agricultae, the Officio Medicae, the Astra Cartographica, the [[Navis Nobilite]], the [[Officio Assassinorum]], the Imperial Fleet and the planetary governors. The Adeptus Astra Telepathica holds the Sisters of Silence, and the [[Logos Historica Verita]] is a recent office of the Adeptus Terra. The [[Inquisition]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] (with its [[Adepta Sororitas]]) are not part of it.

Powers and relations

The Adeptus Terra rules the Imperium through the [[Imperial Commander|Imperial Commanders]], the planetary governors and sector lords who answer to the Administratum for their tithe. Its orders reach them from the High Lords of Terra, who pass them down to the departments that carry them out.

Relations with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] are governed by the Treaty of Mars, which left the Mechanicum its autonomy. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarch]] holds a seat among the High Lords, and the Inquisition answers to none of the adepta. Some Space Marine Chapters have clashed with the Inquisition and the Ministorum so bitterly that the quarrel has become open war.
