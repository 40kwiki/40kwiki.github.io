The Adeptus Terra, also called the Priesthood of Terra, is the vast body of adepta that governs the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from [[Terra]]. It comprises the departments, offices and orders through which the will of the [[High Lords of Terra]] is carried out: the record-keeping and tithe-taking [[Administratum]], the telepathic communicators of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]], the keepers of the [[Astronomican]] in the [[Adeptus Astronomica]], and the judges of the [[Adeptus Arbites]]. Its adepts number in the uncounted billions, and its seat is the [[Imperial Palace]], much of which is given over to its offices.

The Adeptus Terra grew from the civil government that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and [[Malcador the Sigillite]] built during the [[Unification Wars]] and the [[Great Crusade]]. After the Emperor was interred within the [[Golden Throne]], the Council of Terra became the High Lords, and the institutions they directed became the permanent government of the Imperium. In the ten thousand years since, it has grown into a bureaucracy of such size that no single mind can comprehend it, and much of its work continues by precedent long after its original purpose has been forgotten.

The term "priesthood" reflects the reverence with which its work is regarded, not a religious function: the adepts are not clergy, and the [[Adeptus Ministorum|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] stand outside it as partners, not departments. In the Era Indomitus the Adeptus Terra has been reformed in part by [[Roboute Guilliman]], the restored Lord Commander, whose efforts to make it efficient have met a resistance of its own kind.

History

Unification and the Great Crusade

The first adepts of Terra were the officials who followed the Emperor's armies through the Unification Wars, counting what had been won and organising its people. The Emperor's chief minister was Malcador, who directed the civil service, the intelligence networks and many of the secret works of the Imperium on his master's behalf. By the time of the First Legion's founding, the Lords Civilian of Terra were already a recognised body.

As the Great Crusade carried the Imperium across the galaxy, the civil departments multiplied. The Imperial Administration assessed tithes and kept records; the Astra Telepathica gave the expeditions their means of communication; the Navigators' houses gave them the means of travel. After the [[Ullanor Crusade|Triumph of Ullanor]], the Emperor withdrew to Terra and created the [[Council of Terra]] to govern the Imperium in his name, with Malcador as its master. The Emperor's decision to leave his sons and Legions out of the Council was resented, most of all by [[Horus]], and Imperial historians have long counted it among the causes of the [[Horus Heresy]].

The Heresy and the High Lords

During the Heresy the Council of Terra governed what remained of the loyal Imperium from the besieged Palace. Malcador died on the Golden Throne during the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31), holding the gateway to the [[Webway]] closed in the Emperor's stead. When the Siege ended, the Emperor was entombed in the Throne, and his government passed to the Council.

In the years of reconstruction the Council became the Senatorum Imperialis, the High Lords of Terra, and the departments it controlled were set in the form that they have kept since. It was in this period that the term Adeptus Terra came into general use, distinguishing the adepta of the Throneworld from the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], whose independence the [[Treaty of Olympus]] had confirmed, and from the [[Adeptus Astartes]], whom Guilliman's [[Codex Astartes]] placed outside the civil chain of command.

Growth and decay

Over the following millennia the Adeptus Terra swelled with each new crisis. Each emergency created a new department, and few were ever dissolved. In the 32nd millennium, during the [[War of the Beast]] and the [[Beheading]], the High Lords themselves were murdered and rebuilt. In the 36th, the Master of the Administratum, [[Goge Vandire]], used the bureaucracy and the Ecclesiarchy together to rule the Imperium as a tyrant during the [[Age of Apostasy]]. The reforms that followed his fall bound the Adeptus Terra more tightly to the [[Imperial Cult]], and the image of the adept as a holy servant of the God-Emperor dates from that time.

By the late 41st millennium the Adeptus Terra was proverbial for its slowness. Orders could take centuries to pass through its departments. Regiments were raised for wars that had long ended, and worlds that had been destroyed continued to be assessed for tithe. Yet it continued to function, and Imperial scholars have pointed out that the Imperium's survival through ten thousand years of crisis owes as much to its patient clerks as to its armies.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The return of Roboute Guilliman at the end of the 41st millennium brought the first sustained effort in ten thousand years to reform the Adeptus Terra. Guilliman, as Lord Commander and Imperial Regent, removed several High Lords, reorganised the Senatorum, and ordered the Administratum to concentrate its resources on the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. He found the adepta crippled by duplication, secrecy and fear, and records in which, he complained, not even the date could be trusted. Among his remedies was the [[Logos Historica Verita]], an office charged with recovering an accurate history of the Imperium.

The reforms have had mixed success. Some departments were made more efficient. Others proved impossible to change, and the opening of the [[Great Rift]] has cut off many of the Adeptus Terra's offices in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] from Terra entirely. Those offices continue to collect tithes and keep records with no way of sending them anywhere.

Organisation

The Adeptus Terra has no single head beneath the High Lords. Each of its great adepta is led by a master who usually holds a seat on the Senatorum Imperialis, and each answers to the Senatorum as a whole. Its divisions are not neatly bounded; departments overlap, compete for resources and occasionally wage bureaucratic war on one another.

[[Administratum]]
By far the largest of the adepta. It assesses and collects the Imperial tithe, keeps the records of every world, allocates resources and, through the [[Departmento Munitorum]], supplies and directs the [[Astra Militarum]]. Its Master is traditionally among the most powerful of the High Lords.
[[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]]
Recruits and trains the Imperium's psykers, and provides the [[Astropath|astropaths]] on whom all interstellar communication depends. Its [[Black Ships]] gather psykers from every world.
[[Adeptus Astronomica]]
Maintains the [[Astronomican]], the psychic beacon that guides Imperial navigation. Its Master, the Master of the Astronomican, has always held a seat among the High Lords.
[[Adeptus Arbites]]
Enforces Imperial law, as opposed to the laws of individual worlds, and guards against rebellion by planetary governors. Its head is the Lord Marshal of the Arbites.
[[Adeptus Custodes]]
The Emperor's guardians are listed among the Adeptus Terra in older registers, but in practice answer to no authority but the Emperor and their Captain-General.

Other bodies associated with the Adeptus Terra include the Officio Agricultae, the Officio Medicae, the Adeptus Astra Cartographica and the many lesser offices whose functions are known only to their own adepts. The [[Navis Nobilite]], the [[Inquisition]], the [[Officio Assassinorum]] and the Ecclesiarchy all work closely with the Adeptus Terra but are not considered part of it.

Adepts

An adept of the Adeptus Terra is recruited young, usually from the scholae of the Administratum or from the families that have served the same department for generations. Most spend their whole lives at a single task, in a single office, and never learn what purpose their work serves. Their status is marked by their robes and by the numbered tattoos or brands of their department. Senior adepts may be granted juvenat treatments and live for several centuries. The great majority are faceless scribes, and Imperial proverb holds that a world's governor is less to be feared than a junior adept with a grudge.

Powers and relations

The Adeptus Terra rules the Imperium through the [[Imperial Commander|Imperial Commanders]], the planetary governors who hold their worlds as fiefs and who answer to Terra for their tithe. It intervenes in a world's affairs only when the tithe goes unpaid or when Imperial law is broken, and most citizens will never see an adept from Terra. Its power lies in its records: a world that the Administratum does not record does not exist, and a regiment the Munitorum has not raised cannot be supplied.

Relations with the Adeptus Mechanicus are governed by treaty and by mutual dependence. The Adeptus Terra cannot build or maintain the Imperium's machines without Mars, and Mars cannot feed its forge worlds without the Imperium. Relations with the Ecclesiarchy have been close since the Age of Apostasy, and with the Inquisition watchful on both sides.
