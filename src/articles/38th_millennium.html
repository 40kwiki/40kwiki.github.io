The 38th millennium (M38) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M38 to 999.M38. The [[Age of Redemption]] ended in its first century: the [[Administratum]] closes the age in about 034.M38, the [[Ecclesiarchy]] with the return of the survivors of the [[Abyssal Crusade]] in 112.M38. [[The Waning]] followed, a long decline in which the Imperium lost ground on every frontier and the reach of the [[Astronomican]] seemed to shrink.

The record of the millennium is thinner than that of the crusading age. Its best-known events are the Ninth [[Black Crusade]] against [[Cancephalus]] and Antecanis, the Grim Harvest of 666.M38, when the ships of the Lost Crusade returned as [[space hulk]]s, and new orders of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. On the Eastern Fringe the [[T'au Empire]] fought its first long war against the [[Orks]] and allied with the [[Kroot]].
