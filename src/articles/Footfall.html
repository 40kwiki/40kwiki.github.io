Footfall is a palace-city world in the [[Expanse]]. It was founded in 410.M41 by the [[Rogue Trader]] Parsimus Dewain, who governed it until his death in 498.M41. The city he raised gave the world its name in the records of the Expanse.

Footfall is one of the few settled worlds of the Expanse that appear in the [[Administratum]]'s registers. Its rulers were Rogue Traders, and the world was later held under the tyranny of another ruler of the same kind.

History

Parsimus Dewain founded the city of Footfall in 410.M41. He ruled from its palace until his death in 498.M41, and his successors inherited a world that depended on its palace and its trade. The records of the period describe the city as a centre of the wider Expanse trade.The date of the city's foundation is recorded. The later rulers of Footfall are not dated in the sources consulted.

  • 410.M41: Parsimus Dewain founds the city of Footfall
  • 498.M41: Parsimus Dewain dies, having governed the world
