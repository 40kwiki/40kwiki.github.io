This is a list of the Foundings, the groups in which the [[Adeptus Astartes]] raised new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. A Founding could be ordered only by the [[High Lords of Terra]], and each drew its [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] from an existing Chapter under a [[writ of succession]]. Imperial records number some of the Foundings, but many are recorded without a number, and several dates are known only as a period of the millennium.

The table below lists the Foundings in order, with the principal Chapters assigned to each. The records are incomplete, so a Founding may have raised Chapters not named here. Where the records give only a period, that period is shown, and where the date is approximate it is marked "c.".Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

Notes on the series

The numbered series stops at the Twenty-sixth Founding. The [[Ultima Founding]] is recorded separately, as the work of the [[Era Indomitus]] under the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]].
