The Forge World of Arkreach is a [[Forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is known to Imperial scholars chiefly as the target of the sixth [[Black Crusade]], which the [[Traitor|Traitors]] launched against it in 901.M36.

The attack was one of a series of campaigns that the Traitors fought against Imperial worlds during the middle crusades. Its defence destroyed a splinter warband, the [[Sons of the Eye]], and ended the threat to the forge.

Black Crusades

Main article: [[Black Crusade]]

The middle Black Crusades ranged across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The fifth struck the [[Elysia Sector]]. The sixth struck [[Sons of the Eye|Arkreach]]. The seventh opened the [[Ghost War]]. The eighth was a ritual campaign dedicated to [[Tzeentch]]. The records give these campaigns in order, and the order is not disputed.
