A Chapter serf is a Human bondsman or bondswoman who serves a [[Space Marine]] Chapter for life. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] are too few to carry out every task needed to keep a Chapter at full efficiency, so the serfs perform most of the menial but essential work. Their service frees the battle-brothers to train and to fight. Serfs are formally part of [[Chapter Command]] in most Chapters that follow the [[Codex Astartes]].

Serfs are inducted into the [[Chapter cult]] and its customs. They are generally well treated and are educated well beyond most servants of the Imperium. Every Chapter serf can read and write [[High Gothic]], which allows them to keep the records and manuals that Chapter operations require.

Recruitment and status

Most serfs come from the population of the Chapter's [[homeworld]]. Some are young Humans selected as [[aspirant]]s who were found unfit for the [[Space Marine]] gene-seed trials, and some are born into the status to serve as the children of earlier serfs. A small number are Humans rescued on the battlefield who asked to serve their saviours out of gratitude. Imperial records say that the serfs do not resent their station, because they understand that the Astartes cannot fulfil their duty without them.

Serfs live within the Chapter's [[fortress-monastery]]. Their families often remain in service for generations, and the Chapter treats them as loyal members of its community, even though their station is humble.

Duties

When the Space Marines go to war, the serfs maintain the fortress-monastery and defend it from attack. They are normally never deployed in battle beside the Astartes, whose prowess their mortal frames cannot match. Most are nonetheless trained at least to the standard of an [[Imperial Guardsman]] and are expected to help defend Chapter assets such as fortresses and vessels. When the enemy boards a battle barge, it may be serfs who man its gun turrets.

Common offices include the Artificers, who are given basic training by the [[Techmarine]]s and perform routine maintenance of wargear, and the Master of the Refectorium, who runs the kitchens of the fortress-monastery. The Medicae assist the [[Apothecary|Apothecaries]], the Sacratium tend relics and assist the [[Chaplain]]s, and the Scribellum assist the [[Librarian]]s in recording the Chapter's deeds. Monitors are trained messengers who are made to memorise a message perfectly and can recall it only when given the correct verbal code. The Ordinator of the House serves as personal seneschal of the [[Chapter Master]] and is the nominal leader of all the serfs.

Treatment by Chapter

Chapters differ in how they treat their serfs. The [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Salamanders]] treat their serfs most fairly, as important if still mortal extensions of the Chapter, while the [[Imperial Fists]] tend to treat them as if they were unimportant, and the [[Marines Malevolent]] treat them as little more than slaves. Some Chapters have unique roles for their serfs. Each [[Salamanders]] battle-brother is assigned his own Brander-priest or Brander-priestess, who brands his flesh with a red-hot rod while reciting the Promethean Cult's sacred passages. Brander-priests often become close confidants of their masters, and many immolate themselves on the funeral pyre of a fallen Astartes.

The [[Excoriators]] assign three personal serfs to each Astartes. The Seneschal maintains the master's power armour and leather whip, the Lictor performs the ritual flagellation of the shoulders and back during the donning of Dorn's Mantle, and the Absterge tends wounds and provides food and drink. The Space Wolves have no Space Marine captain in overall charge of the Chapter's fleet. Each Great Company has its own flagship, which is manned by serfs, and the Helmsmaster serves as its captain.
