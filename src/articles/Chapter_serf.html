A Chapter serf is a Human bondsman or bondswoman who serves a [[Space Marine]] Chapter for life. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] are too few to carry out every task needed to keep a Chapter at full efficiency, so the serfs perform most of the menial but essential work. Their service frees the battle-brothers to train and to fight. A Chapter could not sustain its [[fortress-monastery]], its fleet or its armoury without them.

Serfs are inducted into the [[Chapter cult]] and its customs. They are generally well treated and are educated well beyond most servants of the Imperium. Every Chapter serf can read and write [[High Gothic]], which allows them to keep the records and manuals that Chapter operations require.

Recruitment and status

Most serfs come from the population of the Chapter's [[homeworld]]. Some are young Humans selected as [[aspirant]]s who were found unfit for the [[Space Marine]] gene-seed trials, and some are born into the status to serve as the children of earlier serfs. A small number are Humans rescued on the battlefield who asked to serve their saviours out of gratitude. Imperial records say that the serfs do not resent their station, because they understand that the Astartes cannot fulfil their duty without them.

Serfs live within the Chapter's [[fortress-monastery]]. Their families often remain in service for generations, and the Chapter treats them as loyal members of its community, even though their station is humble.

Treatment by Chapter

Chapters differ in how they treat their serfs. The [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Salamanders]] are often cited as examples of the most considerate practice, while other Chapters impose stricter discipline. Some Chapters have unique roles for their serfs. Each [[Salamanders]] battle-brother is assigned his own Brander-priest or Brander-priestess, who brands his flesh with a red-hot rod while reciting the Promethean Cult's sacred passages. Brander-priests often become close confidants of their masters, and many immolate themselves on the funeral pyre of a fallen Astartes.

The [[Excoriators]] assign three personal serfs to each Astartes. The Seneschal maintains the master's power armour, the Lictor performs the ritual flagellation of the shoulders and back during the donning of Dorn's Mantle, and the Absterge tends wounds and provides food and drink. The [[Space Wolves]] have a serf of a different kind, the Helmsmaster. Each Great Company of the Space Wolves is commanded by a Helmsman, a serf who captains the company's flagship, since the Chapter has no Space Marine captain over its fleet.
