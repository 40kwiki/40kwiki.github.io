The Disciples of Caliban are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Dark Angels]] and one of the Unforgiven. The circumstances of their raising are unusual. Late in the [[37th millennium]] the Dark Angels' Supreme Grand Master Anaziel asked the [[High Lords of Terra]] for a new Chapter from the Dark Angels' [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. A Chapter Master had never before made such a request, and it was debated in the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] for some time before it was granted. Some say Anaziel must have held leverage over one of the High Lords.

The Chapter is fleet-based, fast and very active. It is believed to have been raised to hunt the Fallen Angel [[Cypher]], and it has the highest standards of recruitment of any Unforgiven Chapter.

History

Founding

Few [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] are made from [[Dark Angels]] [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], because the line is held to be pure and free from [[Mutant|mutation]]. The seed used for the Disciples was of the highest pedigree the Dark Angels held, and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] examined it to a standard well beyond that required by the [[Adeptus Terra]], since the [[Inquisition]] has never ceased to suspect the motives of the Unforgiven. The Genetors of the Mechanicus still watch the Chapter's seed for the slightest sign of corruption. What happens to a battle-brother who falls short of its demands of purity is not known.

The Unforgiven set down no purpose for the new Chapter, but the world has noticed that the Dark Angels could bring so much influence to bear on the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]]. The Chapter's Grand Master Orias, the first, bore a Heavenfall Blade, the Sword of Sanctity, and was given the honour of visiting the captive Arch-heretic [[Luther]] with the [[Chapter Master|Supreme Grand Master]]. He fell fighting [[Orks]] in the Quolon Pass.

The hunt for the Fallen

In the short time of its existence the Chapter has fought in many war zones across the Imperium. Many of those wars seem to have been stirred up by the Fallen Angel [[Cypher]], who sets worlds alight with a single cryptic transmission. The Disciples have grown skilled at following the traces of the Fallen and acting on the least evidence. How many they have taken to the excoriation chambers of the Dark Angels is not known.

Deathwatch service

In the late [[38th millennium]] a strike force of the Chapter reached the Jericho Reach and met a force of black-armoured [[Chaos Space Marines]] of more than a company, who knew the terrain and took a heavy toll. The [[Deathwatch]] intervened. The episode, called the Lion's Mane, is among the few the Chapter has allowed to be recorded.

Organisation and beliefs

Each company has several Relic Bearers who carry the Chapter's most sacred artefacts into battle, and the flagship holds reliquaries that celebrate their forebears' deeds. Imperial scholars do not know whether the relics are only symbolic. The Lionus Censum, a sacred scroll listing the names and deeds of the Chapter's most famous brothers, is the ambition of every member.

Notable campaigns

  • Quolon Pass (M38): Grand Master Orias killed fighting [[Orks]].The date is not recorded.
  • Lion's Mane (late M38): a strike force in the Jericho Reach fights a company of [[Chaos Space Marines]].

Notable members

  • Orias: first Grand Master, who carried a Heavenfall Blade.
