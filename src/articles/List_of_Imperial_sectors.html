This list names the sectors, regions and frontier zones of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that appear in the registers of the [[Administratum]]. Entries are grouped under the five [[Segmentum|segmenta]] to which they belong, followed by a final section for sectors whose segmentum is disputed or unrecorded, and for the notes that apply to the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. The status column gives the condition of each sector as the most recent registers of the [[42nd millennium]] record it.

The list is not complete. Sectors are redrawn often, as worlds are lost to [[Chaos]] and [[xenos]], reconquered by crusades, or cut off by the [[Warp]]. Many sectors on the frontiers appear in the registers only by name, with no recorded worlds or dates. Where a name is known only from a fragmentary record, the entry says so rather than guess. Where a sector is the subject of its own article, the name links to it. Other names are linked as red links, marking articles still to be written.

Dates are Imperial. A date marked c. is an estimate drawn from the sequence of events in the registers, and the note at the foot of each table explains how it was arrived at. Where archives disagree, the Administratum's accepted date is given, with the earlier or disputed record named in the notes.Imperial archives do not agree on the dates of many frontier wars, and the Administratum has revised several registers since the Indomitus Crusade. The dates below follow the most recent revision of each register.

Segmentum Solar

Main article: [[Segmentum]]

The [[Segmentum Solar]] holds the Throneworld and the most densely settled [[Sector|sectors]] of the Imperium. Its sectors are the best defended and the most regularly inspected, and its [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]] is the largest of the five. The sectors below lie in or around the [[Sol System]] and along its western and northern borders.

Segmentum Obscurus

Main article: [[Segmentum]]

The [[Segmentum Obscurus]] lies to the galactic north, beside the [[Eye of Terror]]. Its [[Sector|sectors]] have borne the brunt of the [[Black Crusade]]s since the [[Age of Apostasy]], and most of the Imperium's armed forces were raised from them. The [[Cadian Gate]] and the [[Calixis Sector]] are among its best-known regions.

Segmentum Ultima

Main article: [[Segmentum]]

The [[Segmentum Ultima]] is the largest of the five segmenta and the most thinly settled. It holds the [[Realm of Ultramar]], the [[Eastern Fringe]] and the [[Maelstrom]] Zone, and roughly half of it lies beyond the [[Great Rift]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. Its [[Sector|sectors]] face [[Ork]] empires, [[T'au Empire|T'au]] expansion and [[Tyranid]] incursions.

Segmentum Tempestus

Main article: [[Segmentum]]

The [[Segmentum Tempestus]] lies to the galactic south. Its Fortress is [[Bakka]], and its [[Sector|sectors]] have a long reputation for wealth and for the independence, and sometimes the corruption, of their officials. The southern edge is the [[Veiled Region]], a stretch of unmapped space whose sectors are often cut off for years at a time.

Segmentum Pacificus

Main article: [[Segmentum]]

The [[Segmentum Pacificus]] lies to the galactic west, and despite its name it has been among the most troubled regions of the Imperium. Its Fortress is [[Hydraphur]]. It borders the [[Halo Stars]] and the [[Veiled Region]], and its [[Sector|sectors]] were made famous by the campaigns of [[Macharius]].

Imperium Nihilus and Imperium Sanctus notes

Since the formation of the [[Great Rift]] (the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]]) at the climax of the [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, the Imperium has been divided into two halves. The [[Imperium Sanctus]] lies to the south and west, where the [[Astronomican]] is still visible to [[Navigator|Navigators]]. The [[Imperium Nihilus]], the "Dark Imperium", lies to the north and east, mostly in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] and [[Segmentum Ultima]]. The [[Sector|sectors]] on either side are listed above. The two halves are joined only by the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], one of the few known passages through the Rift.

The sectors below have no segmentum fixed in the registers, or are outside the five segmenta altogether. Their entries are provisional.
