A Maiden world, called Lilaethan in the [[Aeldari Lexicon]], is a lush paradise world that the [[Aeldari]] made from a lifeless world through advanced terraforming. The work was done in the late 30th millennium, before the [[Fall of the Aeldari]] ended their interstellar empire. The Aeldari seeded barren planets with life and planned the worlds as a long-term answer to their need for living space.

Few Maiden worlds stayed in Aeldari hands. Many were settled by other intelligent species, notably humans and [[Ork]]s. The Aeldari regard these colonists as invaders, and they have repeatedly driven them out or destroyed them. The worlds survived the Fall and developed on their own, and in the [[Age of the Imperium]] many are verdant paradises.

Creation

Creating a Maiden world was slow. Simple bacteria first produced a stable, oxygen-rich atmosphere. More complex life was then added to stabilise the ecosystem. The process took thousands of Terran years, but once started it was largely self-sustaining. The worlds were prepared for Aeldari colonists of a distant age, and many were not settled until the Aeldari began the Exodus.

Exodus and the Fall

As the Aeldari empire neared its Fall, groups of Aeldari left their decadent homeworlds in a series of migrations called the Exodus. These [[Exodites]] settled the still barren Maiden worlds. After the Fall, the surviving Aeldari returned to the worlds they had made. Where another species had settled, they offered the colonists a choice between leaving peacefully and extermination. Feral humans and Orks with no means of leaving were still exterminated.

Notable Maiden worlds

  • '''Alayran''' (631.M41): Ghost Warriors of [[Alaitoc]] ambushed and destroyed an [[Ork]] invasion that the craftworld's [[Farseer]]s had foreseen.
  • '''Kiliak''' (838.M41): Imperial xenologists who plundered its buried artefacts were killed to a man by the nearby craftworlds of [[Biel-Tan]] and [[Ulthwé]].
  • '''Asmorylia''' (881.M41): An [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[Explorator]] force attacked the world and captured a Farseer, who was taken to the [[Forge world]] of [[Stygies VIII]].
  • '''Lethidia''' (999.M41): During the attack of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], the [[Drukhari]] abducted the entire planet into the [[Webway]]. Lethidia now hangs above the [[Dark City]] as a warped shadow of its former self.
  • '''Rasilena''' (777.M34): [[Biel-Tan]] fought a long war to reclaim the world from human settlers, and [[Iyanden]] refused to help.
