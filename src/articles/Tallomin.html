Tallomin is a [[Daemon Prince]] of [[Chaos]] who led a frontal assault on the [[Cadian Gate]] during the Third Black Crusade in 909.M32. The attack drew in Imperial regiments and [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] from across the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], and it is one of the best-recorded daemon assaults of the early 32nd millennium.

Imperial accounts describe Tallomin as a daemon of great strength, who fought in person at the head of his host. His name is recorded in the Imperial Navy's lists of Chaos commanders beside those of the Black Crusades.

The Cadian Gate

Main article: [[Fall of Cadia]]

The Cadian Gate was the stellar gateway through which Cadia could be reached from the Imperium's inner worlds. Tallomin's assault was launched to break it, and the attack was driven back only after the [[Space Wolves]] engaged the daemon in person.

The defence drew in regiments and Chapters from across the Segmentum Obscurus, which the Imperium's records count as a tactical success but a costly one. Tallomin was driven back into the Warp, and the relics of [[Saint Gerstahl]] at the [[Shrine World of Gerstahl]] were destroyed in the fighting.
