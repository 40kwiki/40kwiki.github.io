Thracian Primaris is an Imperial world of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] that gives its name to a group of worlds on the [[Cadian front]]. Its neighbours include [[Vraks]], which was lost to [[Chaos]] and retaken in a campaign directed from [[Cadia]].

The world's industry supplies the front. Its stores were needed by the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial army]] in the late [[41st millennium]], and its loss would have broken a supply chain that reached as far as the [[Cadian Gate]].

Vraks campaign

See also: [[Siege of Vraks]]

The [[Departmento Munitorum]] judged that [[Siege of Vraks|Vraks]] had to be retaken, because its stores were needed and its loss broke the supply line. The [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]] high command on [[Cadia]] gave the task to [[Lord General Zuehlke]]. The campaign was fought in the worlds of the Thracian group, and Thracian Primaris served as one of its supply bases.The date of the Vraks campaign is not recorded in the surviving sources.
