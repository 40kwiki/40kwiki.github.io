Haldane Ma'lon was a Lord Commander Militant of the [[Imperialis Auxilia]], the mortal Imperial Army of the [[Great Crusade]], who shared the office with [[Tabor Ludovicia]] at the time of the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31. The Acta of the council list both under the title Lord Militant, Muster of the Imperialis Auxilia, among the officers summoned to Nikaea to hear the question of psykers.

The record on Ma'lon is slight. Nothing is published of his origins, campaigns or death, and no statement of his at Nikaea survives. This article therefore sets out only what is recorded of him and of the office he held.

The office in the Great Crusade

The Imperial Army of the Great Crusade held ground, air and void forces in one chain of command, so that a Lord Militant could command regiments, fighter wings and battleships together. Its soldiers were ordinary men and women, raised from the worlds the Crusade conquered, who fought beside the [[Space Marine Legions]], held what the Legions had won and crewed most of the ships that carried the Crusade. The title Lord Commander Militant, already old, was borne at its head. By 001.M31 it was shared by two officers.

Ma'lon sat on the [[Council of Terra]] with Ludovicia, with [[Malcador the Sigillite]], the Captain-General [[Constantin Valdor]] and the other officers of the Emperor's government. By the time of the [[Siege of Terra]] (c. 013–014.M31) the office was held by a single officer, [[Adreen]].

Nikaea

The Emperor summoned the primarchs, the Fabricator-General, and the officers of the Council of Terra to Nikaea in 001.M31 to decide whether the psychic gift was a resource or a mortal danger. Malcador acted as convener. The Acta list the stance of each officer present: the Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] was vehemently against psykers, and the Grand Admiral of the Imperialis Armada, Constansa Suati-Falkan, likewise. The two Lords Militant of the Auxilia, Ma'lon and Ludovicia, are recorded as open to either side. The council's judgment, the Decree Absolute, sanctioned the Navigators and astropaths under strict control, forbade sorcery and disbanded the Librarius of every Legion.

The Acta do not say whether either commander spoke.
