The Captain-General, also called the Chief Custodian, is the commander of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], the warriors created by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] to guard his person, his palace and his throne. The Captain-General decides who may approach the [[Golden Throne]], answers for the security of the [[Imperial Palace]] and the [[Sol System]], and directs the forces of the Ten Thousand with no fixed remit. When the Custodes deal with the [[Adeptus Terra]], they deal through the Captain-General.

Seventeen Custodians have held the office since it was created for [[Constantin Valdor]] in the [[Unification Wars]] of the [[30th millennium]]. Most died in battle; several became Eyes of the Emperor, his envoys beyond Terra; and three, Valdor among them, vanished in circumstances that the Custodes themselves have not explained.

The office carries a right to a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]], but by long tradition Captains-General have taken it only in times of extreme danger, such as the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). In 999.M41 the seventeenth Captain-General, [[Trajann Valoris]], took the seat at the urging of the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]], and after the battle of the [[Battle of Lion's Gate|Lion's Gate]] led the Custodes beyond Terra into the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Powers and duties

The Captain-General's authority within the order is complete. Below him the two Tribunes govern the ritual life of the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]], and Shield-Captains command its Shield Hosts. The three hundred Companions, who stand within the Throne Room itself, answer to him directly. He alone decides who may come into the physical presence of the Emperor.

Imperial custom treats the Captain-General as the Emperor's personal representative. To mock a High Lord is a matter for gossip; to mock the Captain-General is held to be an insult to the Emperor. Few Captains-General have chosen to use their right to sit among the High Lords, preferring to give all their attention to their duty to the Emperor.

Each Captain-General inherits the armour and weapons of his predecessor, unless they are lost with him.

History

Constantin Valdor (M30 – M31)

See also: [[Constantin Valdor]]

Valdor commanded the Custodians from the [[Unification Wars]] on [[Terra]] through the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. He led the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] beside the [[Space Wolves]] at the [[Burning of Prospero]] in 004.M31, sat on the [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]] during the war, and in 005.M31 advised the directors of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] to send an [[Execution Force]] against [[Horus]]. During the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31) he led the Ten Thousand in its defence, and aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] he wounded [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]]. After the Heresy he became a High Lord of Terra, but he later stood down of his own accord, and some say he serves the Emperor still. Records conflict as to how and when he disappeared from Imperial history, and his armour and weapons never entered the Hall of Armaments. The Custodians elected his successor from among their ranks.

The Custodes on Terra (M31–M41)

After the Heresy the Custodes stood watch on Terra and for ten thousand years seldom fought beyond the [[Sol System]]. The Captains-General of this long watch are recorded only in part:

  • [[Beyreuth]] held the office during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) and sat among the High Lords. He led the defence of the [[Throne Room]] when the [[Aeldari]] [[Harlequins|harlequins]] of the Joyful Tears broke into the inner Palace, and was prevented from executing their surviving seer only by the intervention of the [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Inquisitor Veritus]].
  • [[Aesoth Koumadra]] held it at an unrecorded date between the 33rd and 39th millennia.
  • [[Galahoth]], the fifteenth, was a conservative who let the order stagnate. He disappeared in the late 40th or early 41st millennium, opening the period called the Years of Madness.
  • [[Andros Launceddre]], the sixteenth, was sent to his death at the Battle of the Gilded Pyre, later called the Black Pyre, by the false prophecies of possessed doomscryers in the Palace, in the late 41st millennium.

Trajann Valoris (late M41 – present)

See also: [[Trajann Valoris]]

[[Trajann Valoris|Valoris]], a former Allarus Custodian, Companion and Shield-Captain, was raised to command after Launceddre's death and inherited his arms. Under him the Blood Games increased tenfold, the defences of the stable Warp routes into the Sol System were strengthened, and long-hidden cults were purged from the underhives of Terra. In 999.M41 Tieron pressed him to take the seat left vacant by the death of Lord Brach, [[Chancellor of the Estate Imperium]], to win a majority for suspending the laws that bound the Custodes to Terra. Valoris accepted, and he was in session when Guilliman arrived at the end of the [[Terran Crusade]].

When the birth of the [[Great Rift]] let a host of [[Khorne]]'s [[Daemon|daemons]] into the Palace, Valoris commanded its defence and led four thousand Custodians out of the Lion's Gate beside Guilliman. After the battle the High Lords suspended the old restrictions, and the Custodes have since taken part in the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. At the Battle of Gathalamor Valoris led Custodians and [[Grey Knights]] against the [[Word Bearers]] and the daemons they had summoned.
