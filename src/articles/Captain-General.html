The Captain-General, also called the Chief Custodian, is the commander of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], the warriors created by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] to guard his person, his palace and his throne. The Captain-General decides who may approach the [[Golden Throne]], answers for the security of the [[Imperial Palace]] and the [[Sol System]], and directs the forces of the Ten Thousand with no fixed remit. When the Custodes deal with the [[Adeptus Terra]], they deal through the Captain-General.

Seventeen Custodians have held the office since it was created for [[Constantin Valdor]] in the [[Unification Wars]] of the late 30th millennium. Most died in battle; several became Eyes of the Emperor, his envoys beyond Terra; and three, Valdor among them, vanished in circumstances that the Custodes themselves have not explained.

The office carries a right to a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]], but by long tradition Captains-General have taken it only in times of extreme danger, such as the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). In 999.M41 the seventeenth Captain-General, [[Trajann Valoris]], took the seat at the urging of the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]], and after the battle of the [[Battle of Lion's Gate|Lion's Gate]] led the Custodes beyond Terra into the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Powers and duties

The Captain-General's authority within the order is complete. Below him the two Tribunes govern the ritual life of the Custodes, and Shield-Captains command its Shield Hosts. The three hundred Companions, who stand within the Throne Room itself, answer to him directly. He alone may grant leave to approach the Emperor, and High Lords, Inquisitors and primarchs have all been refused at his word.

Imperial custom treats the Captain-General as the Emperor's personal representative. To mock a High Lord is a matter for gossip; to mock the Captain-General is held to be an insult to the Emperor. The office has used this standing sparingly in the politics of the council. Its interventions have been rare and decisive: the protection of the Throne, the deposition of a tyrant, or the choice of an Ecclesiarch.

Each Captain-General inherits the armour and weapons of his predecessor, unless they are lost with him.

History

Constantin Valdor (late M30 – c. 014.M31)

Valdor commanded the Custodians from the Unification Wars on Terra through the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. He led the Custodes beside the [[Space Wolves]] at the [[Burning of Prospero]] in 004.M31, sat on the Council of Terra during the war, and in 005.M31 advised the directors of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] to send an Execution Force against [[Horus]]. During the [[Siege of Terra]] (c. 013–014.M31) he defended the Palace, and aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] he wounded Ezekyle Abaddon. He sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. Soon after the Emperor was placed on the Throne he resigned his office to an elected successor and disappeared. His armour and the Apollonian Spear never entered the Custodes' Hall of Armaments.

A Captain-General presented the Emperor's Sword to [[Roboute Guilliman]] during the [[Great Scouring]], before the battle of Thessala in 121.M31. The Custodes' chronicles do not say whether this was Valdor or his successor.One Imperial account placed the gift of the sword after Guilliman's resurrection in 999.M41, from Valoris's hand. The later and accepted account, from the Ultramarines' archives, places it in the Scouring.

The Custodes on Terra (M31–M41)

After the Heresy the Custodes withdrew into the Palace and for ten thousand years seldom fought beyond the Sol System. The Captains-General of this long watch are recorded only in part:

  • [[Beyreuth]] held the office during the War of the Beast (544–546.M32) and sat among the High Lords. He led the defence of the Throne Room when the [[Aeldari]] harlequins of the Joyful Tears broke into the inner Palace, and was prevented from executing their surviving seer only by the intervention of the Inquisitor Veritus.
  • Excelsor held it in the [[Age of Apostasy]]. After the High Lords acquitted [[Sebastian Thor]] in c. 288.M36, Excelsor delivered the verdict and persuaded Thor to accept the office of [[Ecclesiarch]], which he had refused.
  • [[Aesoth Koumadra]] held it at an unrecorded date between the 33rd and 39th millennia.
  • [[Tybanus Lencilius]], of the 40th or 41st millennium, spent twenty years tracing a conspiracy of radical Thorian Inquisitors who were stealing psykers destined for the Golden Throne and selling them through the High Lord Sennaca. With Custodians, [[Sisters of Silence]] and Imperial Assassins he destroyed them all.
  • [[Galahoth]], the fifteenth, was a conservative who let the order stagnate. He disappeared in the late 40th or early 41st millennium, opening the period called the Years of Madness.
  • [[Andros Launceddre]], the sixteenth, was sent to his death at the Battle of the Gilded Pyre, later called the Black Pyre, by the false prophecies of possessed doomscryers in the Palace, in the late 41st millennium.

Trajann Valoris (late M41 – present)

Valoris, a former Allarus Terminator and Shield-Captain, was raised to command after Launceddre's death and inherited his arms. He purged the cults of Terra, multiplied the Blood Games that test the Palace's defences and struck at threats beyond the Sol System. In 999.M41 Tieron pressed him to take the seat left vacant by the death of Lord Brach, [[Chancellor of the Estate Imperium]], to win a majority for suspending the laws that bound the Custodes to Terra. Valoris accepted, and he was in session when Guilliman arrived at the end of the [[Terran Crusade]].

When the birth of the [[Great Rift]] let a host of [[Khorne]]'s daemons into the Palace, Valoris commanded its defence and led four thousand Custodians out of the Lion's Gate beside Guilliman. After the battle the High Lords suspended the old restrictions, and Valoris has since led the Ten Thousand into the Indomitus Crusade (from c. 000.M42), while keeping the watch over the Throne and the breaches into the [[Webway]] beneath the Palace. He sits among the High Lords restored by Guilliman.
