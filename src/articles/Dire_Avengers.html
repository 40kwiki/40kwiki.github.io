The Dire Avengers were the first of the [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Aeldari]]. They represent [[Khaine]] in his aspect as the noblest and most merciless of warriors. They are devoted to their people and show no mercy to their foes. Their battle cry is said to be "War is my master, death is my mistress!"

Of all the Aspect Warriors, the Dire Avengers are the least specialised and the most tactically flexible. They serve Aeldari armies as elite ranged infantry. They are also the most common of the Aspects, and their shrines are the largest on any craftworld.

Origins

The Dire Avengers trace their origins to [[Asurmen]], the first of the [[Phoenix Lord]]s. After the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], Asurmen led the craftworlds away from the ancient Aeldari homeworlds and founded the first [[Aspect Shrine]]. Many Aeldari walk the [[Path of the Warrior]] only because their species is on the brink of extinction. Most who take up arms do so in a crisis, as members of the [[Guardian]] militia. Those who walk the Path devote themselves wholly to the art of war.

The Dire Avengers' shrine is a place of meditation and study. When they are not on campaign, its members study the tactics of their ancestors and practise their skills at arms.

Role in battle

The Dire Avengers are patient and methodical. They are as fierce in attack as they are unyielding in defence. They have a sharp sense of the ebb and flow of battle. A Dire Avenger squad might unleash a storm of fire, then charge into the enemy lines with [[Power Weapons]] drawn, or fall back to draw the enemy into a prepared ambush.

The [[Farseer]]s turn to the Dire Avengers for their most important missions. They recover ancient relics, defend abandoned [[Webway]] portals, and clear [[Maiden World]]s of intruders. When a Farseer meets with agents of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], the Dire Avengers serve as his honour guard. They are rarely seen beyond their craftworld, but they are the Aspect Warriors most often encountered by outsiders.
