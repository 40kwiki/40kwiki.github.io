Zagreus Kane was an [[Archmagos|archmagos]] of the [[Mechanicum]], [[Techmarine|forge master]] of Mondus Occulum and Fabricator-Locum of [[Mars]], second only to the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kelbor-Hal]]. When Kelbor-Hal sided with [[Horus]] in 005.M31 and began the [[Schism of Mars]], Kane held to the Emperor. With [[Sigismund]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] he saved the arms of his forge for [[Terra]] and escaped the Red Planet. In exile he claimed the office of Fabricator-General, and in 012.M31 the Council of Terra recognised the loyal Mechanicum as the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], with Kane its first Fabricator-General and first [[High Lords of Terra|High Lord]].

Kane survived the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31) and sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction that governed the Imperium after it. The date of his death is not recorded in the open archives of Mars.

Early life

Nothing is recorded of Kane's origins except that he was born on [[Mars]] in the [[30th millennium]] and rose through the priesthood of the [[Cult Mechanicus]]. By the end of the [[Great Crusade]] he was [[Techmarine|forge master]] of Mondus Occulum, a great forge-temple in the [[Tharsis Montes|Tharsis]] region below the caldera of Pavonis Mons, and Fabricator-Locum, deputy to [[Kelbor-Hal]]. His forge made a great share of the armour and weapons of the [[Space Marine Legions]].

The Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

When the [[Horus Heresy]] broke out in 005.M31, [[Kelbor-Hal]] took the [[Warmaster]]'s side and released a scrapcode plague into the noosphere of Mars. Kane saved most of his systems by cutting his forge off from the planet's data highways, and Mondus Occulum became one of the three centres of loyal resistance, with the forges of [[Koriel Zeth]] and Ipluvien Maximal. [[Traitor]] forces soon laid it under siege.

[[Malcador the Sigillite]] ordered [[Rogal Dorn]] to secure the forges, and Dorn sent his [[First Captain]] [[Sigismund]] with four companies. Sigismund landed at Mondus Occulum and found Kane loyal. Kane set his [[Servitor]]s to load the forge's stocks onto Imperial transports, nearly twelve thousand suits of [[Power armour|Mark IV]] power armour and twice as many weapons, and warned Sigismund that if Mondus Occulum and [[Tharsis Montes|Mondus Gamma]] both fell the Imperium would have no means to replace its losses. Both did fall within hours. The transports escaped with Kane, his surviving adepts and the cargo that armed the Legions in the defence of [[Terra]].

Fabricator-General in exile

See also: [[Fabricator-General]]

On Terra the loyalists named Kane [[Fabricator-General]], but [[Kelbor-Hal]] still held Mars and the title, and Kane ruled in little more than name. The Mechanicum called the problem of two Fabricators-General the binary succession, an unresolved equation. Kane grew bitter and more mechanical. Accounts of the late Heresy describe him with almost no human features left, moving on treads in place of legs, and contemptuous of his own followers.

During the [[Webway War]] (c. 005–014.M31) Kane served as a loyalist commander beneath the [[Imperial Palace]] and sought, with the artificer [[Arkhan Land]], a way to reach Mars through the [[Webway]]. In the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]' council his envoy [[Vethorel]] proposed that the loyal priesthood become a new Adeptus of the Imperium. The Council resisted, fearing a Martian bid for power, until the [[Titan Legions]] threatened to leave the [[Battle of Beta-Garmon|war]] and the Imperator Titan Magnificum Incendius of the Legio Ignatum marched on the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]]. The measure passed in 012.M31. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] was recognised as separate from the corrupted Mechanicum of Mars, Kane became its Fabricator-General and took a seat among the High Lords, and the captive followers of Kelbor-Hal were executed in their thousands.

The Siege of Terra

Main article: [[Siege of Terra]]

In the [[Siege of Terra]] Kane moved his library deep beneath the Palace. Learning from the [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] Esha Ani Mohana that [[Horus]] had been wounded at [[Battle of Beta-Garmon|Beta-Garmon]], he carried the news to [[Sanguinius]]. [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] judged him loyal but proud. In the last days of the siege in 014.M31 he wept with his adepts for the god-engines destroyed on the walls.

Later life

Kane survived the Siege and sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction. There he supported [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s plan to consolidate the Imperium and retake [[Luna]] and [[Mars]] before pursuing the fleeing [[Traitor|traitors]], against [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]'s call for a war of vengeance. He worked with [[Constantin Valdor]] to study the [[Golden Throne]], on which the Emperor's life now depended. Mars was retaken in the [[Great Scouring]] (014–021.M31). Kane's death, and the name of his successor, are not given in any archive open to Imperial scholars.Mechanicus records place the next Fabricator-General of note, Gastaph Hediatrix, in the [[Age of Apostasy]] of the early 36th millennium; the holders between them are not published.
