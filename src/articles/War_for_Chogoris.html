The War for Chogoris was a campaign of 999.M41 to early M42 on the [[Feral world|feral world]] of [[Chogoris]], in the [[Yasan Sector]]. It was fought by the [[White Scars]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]], who had a long connection to the world, and by the [[Red Corsairs]], a renegade warband of [[Chaos Space Marines]].

The war is recorded as part of the wider campaigns of the Era Indomitus. Its course is recorded in the White Scars' chronicles and in the [[Indomitus Crusade]]'s records.

Background

[[Chogoris]], recorded by Imperial astrocartographers as Mundus Planus, was a [[Feral world|feral world]]. Its people were known to the [[White Scars]] through the Chapter's earlier contact with the world. Imperial records list the [[Necrons|Necron]] attack of 890.M41 as one of the world's major events before the war.

Course of the war

The [[White Scars]] fought the campaign alongside their allies, and the [[Red Corsairs]] joined the war against them. The Chapter's records describe the fighting as heavy, with many of its warriors lost in the long campaign. The war ended in early M42, and the world was then recorded as held by the Imperium.
