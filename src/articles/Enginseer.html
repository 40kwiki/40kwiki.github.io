An enginseer, also called a Tech-priest Enginseer, is a [[tech-priest]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] who specialises in the maintenance and repair of the machines of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Enginseers are dedicated to the [[Machine God]] and are the members of the [[Cult Mechanicus]] that ordinary citizens most often see, because they are posted wherever a settlement has any technology at all.

By the ancient [[Treaty of Mars]], many enginseers are attached to the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Navis Imperialis]], where they tend vehicles, aircraft and starships. They soothe the [[Machine Spirit]]s of these machines with binary prayer and the rites of the cult. Without them, Imperial armies and fleets would grind to a halt.

History

At the foundation of the Imperium, after the [[Unification Wars]] ended in 712.M30, the [[Emperor of Mankind]] sealed the Treaty of Mars with the Cult Mechanicus in 739.M30. The cult gained political and religious autonomy, and in return agreed to supply the Imperium's war machine. As a result the Mechanicum began training a large number of aspirants as enginseers.

Unlike most of the [[Priesthood of Mars]], who stay on their [[Forge world]]s or in [[Explorator]] fleets, enginseers serve in almost every Imperial institution, and most often in the Astra Militarum and other parts of the [[Adeptus Terra]]. The one exception is the Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], which rely on no outside aid and keep their own technical priests, the Techmarines. A Techmarine is a genetically enhanced warrior; an enginseer is a fully baseline human. Their duties are otherwise much the same.

Role

Enginseers are conspicuous among Guard regiments in their red robes, with bodies altered by the augmetics that bring them nearer to the [[Omnissiah]]. They command mono-task [[servitor]]s, carry incense burners and sacred oils, and move among the regiment's armouries making sure that the proper obeisance is paid to the machines. They serve at regimental, company and squad level, and are expected to fight and, if needed, die in defence of the Imperium.

Enginseers also accompany Titan Legions and the crews of Navy warships. In battle they ease the pain of a damaged machine, carry out damage control and make field repairs. They rush into danger to calm the spirit of a crippled [[Chimera]] or to bring their knowledge to bear on enemy fortifications.

They are respected by the units they serve, but within the Cult Mechanicus they are held in low regard. Their work seldom advances the [[Quest for Knowledge]], and the cult views them as necessary but lowly cogs. Guardsmen who modify their own vehicles in the field are a standing grievance, since every deviation makes the enginseer's rites harder.

Wargear

Enginseers wear sanctified [[power armour]] adapted to their needs, along with augmetic protections. They carry a laspistol and an Omnissian axe, a long two-handed halberd that is tool, weapon and holy symbol at once. Many have mechadendrites, robotic limbs wired to the spine, which help with repair work.
