Throne Gelt, commonly called thrones, is the most widely recognised currency of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is not a single galactic currency but a family of sector and segmentum currencies, each issued under the authority of the [[Administratum]] and backed by the audited value of the [[Imperial tithe]] owed by the worlds of its region. Throne Gelt circulates as metal coins, also called thrones, and as ledger credit carried on [[data-slate]]s and transferred between accounts by [[cogitator]].

The name "gelt" for Imperial money is attested as early as the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), and "throne" coinage is recorded throughout the [[41st millennium]], above all in the Calixis Sector of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] and the neighbouring Koronus Expanse. Most worlds of the Imperium keep their own local money for everyday trade, and many use barter, scrip or no money at all; Throne Gelt is the currency in which tithes are reckoned, in which [[Rogue Trader]]s and off-world merchants deal, and into which local currencies are converted when the Administratum comes to collect.

Name

"Gelt" is an old Terran word for money that survived into the Imperial vernacular, [[Low Gothic]]. "Throne" refers to the [[Golden Throne]] of the [[Emperor of Mankind]], whose image or emblem appears on most issues. The fractional coin called the aquila, after the Imperial [[Aquila (symbol)|eagle]], is an older [[Great Crusade]] unit; a throne coin of the [[41st millennium]] is of about the size of an old five-aquila piece.

History

Great Crusade and Heresy

See also: [[Great Crusade]]

The [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30–005.M31) spread Imperial weights and measures, and with them a common unit of account, to the worlds brought into compliance. [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancer]] accounts of the period mention payment in "gelt" in the markets of compliant worlds and in the fleets of the expeditions. The coinage of the time used the [[Aquila (symbol)|aquila]] as its basic unit.

Sector currencies

The collapse of central control during and after the Horus Heresy, and later during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] (early M35 to 975.M35), left monetary matters to the sector and segmentum offices of the Administratum. Each came to issue its own throne, valued against the tithe of its worlds. The result is a currency that shares a name and a backing principle across much of the Imperium but whose value differs from one sector to the next. Imperial merchants and the [[Departmento Munitorum]] keep conversion tables between sector thrones, many of them centuries out of date.

The Calixis Sector and the Koronus Expanse

The best-documented throne economy is that of the Calixis Sector, founded during the Angevin Crusade of the 39th millennium. There, Throne Gelt circulates alongside precious shell tokens and coins of rare strategic metals, all of which are legal tender on the sector's [[civilised world]]s. Beyond the Maw, in the Koronus Expanse, Rogue Traders carry thrones as their standard of value, though most frontier trade is conducted in goods, ammunition and favours.The founding of the Calixis Sector is dated by its own archives to the late 39th millennium; the date is not reconciled with the Terran chronology.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 cut many sector economies off from their tithe-auditing offices. Where a sector's tithe could no longer be audited, as across much of the [[Imperium Nihilus]], its throne lost the backing on which its value rested. The [[Indomitus Crusade]] supplied itself chiefly by direct requisition under the authority of the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] rather than by purchase.

Forms

Coinage

Throne coins are struck at sector mints in base or precious metal, usually with the [[Aquila (symbol)|Aquila]] on one face and the sector's arms or a portrait of the Emperor on the other. Fractional coins and local denominations circulate alongside them. [[Hive world|Hive worlds]] often strike token coinage for particular levels or guilds.

Ledger credit

Larger sums are held and transferred as ledger credit, recorded on data-slates and in the cogitator archives of merchant houses and [[Administratum]] counting-halls. Ledger transfers leave records that can be traced by the [[Inquisition]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and for that reason criminal and [[Heresy|heretical]] organisations prefer coin, goods or barter.

Economic role

The Imperium is not a market economy. Its central institution is the [[Imperial tithe]], under which each world supplies goods, soldiers and [[psyker]]s to the Imperium according to its grade. Throne Gelt serves chiefly as the unit in which that tithe is measured and as the medium of trade between worlds and merchant houses. The local currencies of mankind, which include government coinage, digital ledgers, ammunition, gold, and shells picked from a beach, are converted into thrones only when the tithe is assessed.

Outside the Imperium, [[Renegade]]s and pirates of human descent generally recognise the throne but favour hacksilver, technology, ammunition and slaves. [[Xenos]] peoples use their own systems of value: [[Orks]] reckon wealth in teeth, and the [[Aeldari]] are said to have no money at all, though they prize the lost artefacts of their own kind.
