Caliban was a forested [[Death world|death world]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] and the homeworld of the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Lion El'Jonson]] and of the I Legion, the [[Dark Angels]]. Its forests were inhabited by monstrous creatures, the Great Beasts, and its people lived in fortress-monasteries ruled by orders of knights. The Lion grew up there, was found by the knight [[Luther]], and led the crusade that cleared the forests of the Beasts before the Emperor came.

Caliban was destroyed at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]], in a battle between the Lion and the forces left on the planet under Luther, who had turned against him. Only a fragment survived, holding the fortress-monastery of Aldurukh, which became the Dark Angels' mobile fortress, [[The Rock]]. The circumstances of the planet's end are the most closely guarded secret of the Dark Angels and their Successors, and the source of their name, the Unforgiven.

History

The forest world

Caliban was cut off from the rest of Mankind during the [[Age of Strife]], and its people declined into a feudal society. By the late [[30th millennium]] its population lived in fortified settlements in the forest clearings, governed by knightly orders whose members trained from childhood to fight the Great Beasts. The orders lived in fortress-monasteries and observed codes of honour and chivalry; the common people farmed, hunted and served them.

The Great Beasts were huge predators of many kinds, and the forests were deadly to anyone who entered them. Imperial scholars hold that the Beasts were warped and mutated by [[Chaos]], and ascribe this to the Caliban system's nearness to the [[Eye of Terror]].

The Lion and the Order

The infant Lion came to Caliban through the Warp from Terra. He grew up alone in the forest, surviving by his own strength, and was found as a young man by a hunting party of knights led by [[Luther]]. Luther brought him to the fortress of the Order, a knightly order that took in men of any birth, and the two became brothers in arms.

The Lion, with Luther at his side, led the Order in a crusade to exterminate the Great Beasts. The other knightly orders joined it or were absorbed, and by its end the forests had been cleared of the Beasts and the Lion and the Order ruled Caliban. The orders' fortresses, including Aldurukh, the Order's own seat, became the centres of government.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

When the Emperor reached Caliban in 846.M30, the Lion took command of the I Legion, and Caliban became its homeworld. The Legion recruited from the knights and youths of the planet, who brought their codes and orders into the Legion. Luther followed the Lion into the Legion and served at his side in the early years of the Great Crusade. Later, he was sent back to Caliban to command the garrison and oversee recruitment. Dark Angels accounts describe this as the result of the Lion's suspicion, Luther's ambition or both, and Luther came to resent it.

The Horus Heresy and the destruction

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

During the [[Horus Heresy]], Caliban was cut off from the Legion's main fleet. Luther, influenced by agents of Chaos, and the Calibanite warriors who had been left with him, turned against the Lion. When the primarch returned to Caliban after the [[Siege of Terra]], his fleet was fired on from the surface. The Lion ordered a bombardment and landed to confront Luther at Aldurukh, where the two fought.

The bombardment and the warp storm that broke out during the duel tore the planet apart. Most of the surface was destroyed. The traitors who had followed Luther were scattered through space and time by the warp, becoming the Fallen. The Lion was lost, and Luther was found by the Dark Angels in the ruins, insisting that the Watchers in the Dark had taken the primarch away and that the Lion would return. A fragment of the planet's crust, held together by Aldurukh's defences, survived and was converted into the Rock.

Accounts differ on almost every point of this story. The Dark Angels' own tradition blames Luther and the Fallen alone; the Fallen claim the Lion destroyed his world in his anger; and records preserved by the [[Inquisition]] describe divisions among the Dark Angels that began before the Lion's return. The full account is known only to the Supreme Grand Master and the Inner Circle.

Aftermath

The remains of Caliban form an asteroid field in a system whose location the Dark Angels have kept secret. The Rock, propelled by warp engines, has carried the Chapter's Inner Circle, its Deathwing and its Ravenwing across the galaxy ever since. Beneath it, Luther was kept a prisoner for more than nine thousand years, and the Watchers in the Dark guarded a chamber in its depths.

The Dark Angels and their Successors have hunted the Fallen ever since, and that hunt has shaped the Chapter's history and its relations with the rest of the Imperium. Caliban itself survives in their memory, in the names of their companies and relics, and in the customs of their knightly orders.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the Era Indomitus, during the [[Arks of Omen]] campaign, the Lion woke from a sleep of some ten thousand years in the heart of the Rock. Before his return became known, tales spread from world to world of a figure who came out of the mist to kill monsters. Some hold that, in his dreams, he was carried through the forests of Caliban to other worlds.How the Lion travelled in his sleep is known only to the primarch himself.

Geography

Caliban was a world of deep, ancient forests broken by clearings, rivers and mountains. Its climate was temperate, and its forests were so dense and so dark that they were rarely entered except by armed parties. The fortress-monasteries of the knightly orders, many of them built into mountains and protected by walls and towers, were the planet's principal settlements, and towns and villages grew up around them.

Society

Calibanite society was feudal. The knightly orders held power, recruiting both noble and common boys, though some orders admitted only the high-born. Knights were trained from childhood in swordsmanship, riding and the hunting of the Great Beasts, and were bound by oaths of fealty and codes of chivalry. The orders often fought one another. The Order, the Lion's own, was notable for admitting candidates of any birth.

Much of the [[Dark Angels]]' identity descends from these traditions: the Deathwing and Ravenwing companies, the use of swords and robes, the Chapter's hierarchy of circles and secrets, and the knightly titles of its officers. The Chapter's Successors, the Unforgiven, preserve the same customs.
