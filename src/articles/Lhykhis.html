Lhykhis, called the "Whispering Web", is the [[Phoenix Lord]] and founder of the [[Warp Spiders]] [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. She is rarely seen in the battlefield records of the [[41st millennium]], and Imperial scholars long considered her apocryphal. At one point she had been absent for so long that even the [[Aeldari]] believed her lost.

Lhykhis has since returned. She has become one of the most active Phoenix Lords of the [[Era Indomitus]]. She travels across the [[galaxy]] to defend the [[Asuryani]] wherever they are most needed. She has unmatched mastery of the short leaps through the [[Warp]] that her Aspect uses to ambush its prey, surpassing even her [[Exarch]]s. The suddenness and ferocity of her attacks explain why Imperial commanders took so long to confirm that she existed.

Character

Lhykhis embodies the willingness of [[Khaine]] to risk everything for victory. She leaps from nothing into the midst of the enemy and slips back into the Warp, where a very different enemy awaits. Even among the Phoenix Lords she is considered a daredevil. Her keen sense of navigation lets her cross the [[Immaterium]] further and more accurately than even the oldest Warp Spider [[Exarch]]s. Such frequent forays would give pause to the most hardened [[Autarch]]s, but she enters that realm again and again without hesitation.

When the Asuryani need her, she always emerges from the Warp to save them from doom. Her unique [[Aspect Armour]] has four additional arms. Two carry a pair of [[Death Spinner]]s, and the other two end in monofilament blades in the style of the [[Harlequin's Kiss]]. Her most dangerous weapon is the crackling [[power sword]] ''Vhattarkan'', which can cut through the fabric of reality and open microrifts into the Warp.

Wargear

Lhykhis wears a unique suit of [[Aspect Armour]], the [[Phoenix Armour]] of the Warp Spiders. It houses the [[Spirit Stone]]s of every Warp Spider Exarch who has become Phoenix Lord since the first Lhykhis took the mantle, many Terran millennia ago. Besides the four extra arms and the sword ''Vhattarkan'', the armour carries the pair of [[Death Spinner]]s.

Disappearance and return

Lhykhis often vanishes into the Warp for long periods. Many [[Craftworld Aeldari]] therefore came to believe that the Warp Spiders had no living Phoenix Lord. In the [[Era Indomitus]] a company of the [[Astra Militarum]] reported finding a suit of ancient Aeldari relic armour. The [[Craftworld Aeldari]] recovered it before the guardsmen could burn it, leaving only scant [[Human]] remains that pointed to Warp Spiders as the attackers. The company commander was summarily executed for failing to guard the relic. It is likely that this was the armour of Lhykhis, long thought lost, and that its recovery allowed the Phoenix Lord to be reborn after her long absence.

The Wraith Spider

Because Lhykhis was so seldom seen, some Aeldari who dimly remembered the truth named another figure as the Warp Spider Phoenix Lord. He is Lhykosidae, the "Wraith Spider", a legendary Aspect Warrior of the craftworld [[Kaelor]]. Kaelorian legend holds that he is a rogue Exarch who appears once every few Terran millennia to bring justice and peace to the craftworld, and that he rises from the ranks of the Warp Spiders with a power beyond any Exarch. A copy of the ''Arc of Destiny'' in the House Library of Ansgar on Kaelor claims that Kaelor won the Craftwars through his timely arrival.

Kaelorian scholars disagreed about him. Some saw in his description a Phoenix Lord, while others held that he carries the essential soul of the Warp Spiders and is a spiritual awakening rather than a wearer of Phoenix armour. The Warp Spiders of Kaelor believe that he is a force of purification, because their Aspect draws its name from the tiny crystalline creatures of the [[Webway]] and the Infinity Circuit, which cleanse them of non-Aeldari psychic energy. In this view the Wraith Spider returns at the moment of greatest pollution. The wraithsmith Vhaalum the Silver instead holds that he is a personification of [[wraithbone]], a ''sha'iel'' that has taken organic form.
