The Feast of Blades is a tournament of honour held about once in a hundred standard years between the [[Imperial Fists]] and their successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. Each Chapter chooses a champion, usually one of its Company Champions, who fights the champions of the others in a series of duels until a single warrior stands. The victor and his Chapter win great honour, the artefact called the Dornsblade, and the right to host the next Feast where they choose.

The Feast was instituted by [[Rogal Dorn]] after the [[Horus Heresy]] to keep the bond between the scions of the VII Legion. The Imperial Fists' successors differ widely in temperament and culture, and several are fleet-based with no permanent base, so that centuries can pass without two of them meeting. The Chapters' own reckoning counts the Feast as having been held for 8,140 standard years, which does not agree with the sequence of its numbered Feasts, and Imperial scholars have not settled the discrepancy.The Feast's founding is dated here to the years after the Iron Cage, as tradition holds. The Chapters' own count of years and Feasts does not agree with this date or with itself.

History

[[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] is said to have set up the Feast after the battle of the [[Iron Cage]] against the [[Iron Warriors]] on Sebastus IV (019.M31), where the Legion was nearly destroyed. It commemorates his decision to divide the Legion into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] at the [[Second Founding]], as the [[Codex Astartes]] and Imperial security required. Dorn presided over the first Feast in person. The aim was to build bonds between the new Chapters, tested and tempered by competition. The winner of each Feast names the site and timing of the next, to suit his own champions' strengths and the weaknesses of his rivals. A Chapter that cannot attend because of war may ask another to take its place.

It is the custom for the host to choose a site of recent victory. The 816th Feast was held on Samarquand IV, a world lately won back from the Urk Empire, an Ork realm of two thousand years' standing in the Samarquand Sector. The Great Tusk who ruled it had never joined the invasions of neighbouring [[Sector|sectors]]. The [[Space Wolves]], [[Dark Angels]] and [[Imperial Fists]] had all been committed to destroy it, and only the Imperial Fists succeeded.

The Cage and the rules

The combat is fought in an arena called the Cage, an architectural echo of the Iron Cage whose layout changes between rounds and even within them. Each champion wears carapace armour like a Scout's and no helm, with a yellow shoulder guard for the old VII Legion on one side and his own Chapter's colours on the other. He may use only his body and a ceremonial gladius hidden in the Cage, one for each fighter. Every blade is coated with a paralytic toxin made by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], so that the more he cuts, the likelier a fighter is to be the first to fall. Each round ends with a knock-out. Accidental deaths are overlooked, while deliberate maiming is condemned by all the Chapters.

Participants

The [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] that attend regularly are the [[Black Templars]], [[Celestial Lions]], [[Crimson Fists]], Death Strike, Emperor's Warbringers, [[Excoriators]], [[Executioners]], Fire Lords, [[Maximus Thane|Fists Exemplar]], Hammers of [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]], [[Imperial Fists]], [[Iron Knights]], Knights of Dorn, Sons of Dorn, [[Soul Drinkers]] and White Templars. The Shadow Wolves no longer take part, having been destroyed by a [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] splinter fleet in 987.M41, and the Venom Thorns were declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]].

Notable Feasts

  • 546.M32, Inwit. During the [[War of the Beast]], [[Chapter Master]] [[Maximus Thane]] of the Fists Exemplar called a Feast at the funeral of [[Koorland]], last of the [[Imperial Fists]], and fought as his Chapter's champion. After he defeated Sergeant Dathan Tychor of the Excoriators, Thane was named the winner.
  • The Feast of a Hundred Duels (undated). A great [[World Eaters]] host invaded the Feast. The Dorn-sons united against the Khornate warbands, and the finest bladesmen of the age matched power swords against chainaxes and flails. The [[Traitor|Traitors]] fell to numbers, and the skies rumbled with [[Khorne]]'s approval.
  • 812th Feast, Ganath (749.M41). All twelve [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] attended. Hervald Strom, the champion of the [[Iron Knights]], won.
  • 813th Feast (849.M41). Strom won again, though wounded in the Vinculus Crusade: no Chapter, and no champion, had won twice in a row.
  • 814th Feast (949.M41). Only eight of twelve Chapters came because of war. The Imperial Fists and the [[Crimson Fists]] were tied for most wins.
  • 816th Feast, Samarquand IV (date not recorded). The Excoriators, having lost seven champions early, woke their Chapter Champion Zachariah Kersh from stasis, where he had been held for losing the Chapter's standard to the [[Alpha Legion]]. His victories were not recognised by the other Chapters, so Master Fortinbrase of the Imperial Fists ordered a three-way final between Kersh, the [[Black Templars]]' Castellan Alighieri and the Imperial Fists' champion Montalban.
