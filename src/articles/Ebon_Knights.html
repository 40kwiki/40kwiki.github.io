The Ebon Knights were a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is recorded about this reclusive Chapter except its last campaign, on the [[Planetary classification|industrial world]] of Iriad IV in 437.M35, where it fought a warband of the [[World Eaters]]. Both forces were destroyed, and the Chapter's one survivor was corrupted by [[Khorne]].

History

The Red Dawn of Iriad IV

In 437.M35 a tide of [[World Eaters]] [[Chaos Space Marines]] invaded the fortified [[Planetary classification|industrial world]] of Iriad IV. Captain Revellion of the Ebon Knights led his men against their [[Traitor|traitor]] cousins. The war crossed the planet, and the dead mounted quickly, until less than a company remained on either side. The World Eaters cared little for their losses, but Revellion, driven to desperation by the death of so many of his men, murmured a prayer in the night.

Hours later he strode from the shadows covered in blood with a dripping chainsword in each hand. His warriors cried out in joy as the last World Eater fell, but the victory turned to ashes. Revellion could not be stopped. He hacked his way through the remaining Ebon Knights and attacked anything that moved, even his own reflection. District after district of the world was emptied. The survivors of the population were evacuated, and the [[Inquisition]] declared the planet Perditas. Revellion's fate is unknown.

Background

Iriad IV lay in the middle of the [[35th millennium]], when the Imperium was divided by the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] and the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Pacificus]] was ruled apart from [[Terra]]. Raids by the [[Traitor Legions]], among them the [[World Eaters]], were frequent on the fringes of the divided realm, and loyal [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] were often the only force within reach. The Ebon Knights are one of the Chapters that were set against them. Imperial records say nothing of where the Chapter was raised or from whose [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], or whether it had fought before Iriad IV.

Aftermath and legacy

The [[Inquisition]]'s declaration that Iriad IV was Perditas means the world is lost and forbidden. Its industrial works, which had made it worth defending, were left empty. The fate of Captain Revellion is not known, and the Inquisition has recorded no sighting of him. Imperial scholars cite his pact with [[Khorne]] as an example of how a warrior in despair can be turned by a single prayer, and the entry for the Ebon Knights in the lists of lost [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] is brief for that reason: the Chapter ended in a day, and no one remained to write its history.

Organisation and beliefs

Nothing is recorded of the Chapter's organisation, colours or beliefs.The date of Iriad IV is the only one given for the Chapter's existence; no earlier act is recorded.

Notable members

  • Revellion: captain and sole survivor of Iriad IV, who made a pact with [[Khorne]] before the final action and then slaughtered the rest of his Chapter and everyone on the world.
