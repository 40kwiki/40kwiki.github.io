The Lex Imperialis, also called the Dictates Imperialis or simply Imperial Law in [[Low Gothic]], is the civil and criminal code of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is a vast body of laws, decrees and rulings compiled over ten thousand years. It is enforced chiefly by the [[Adeptus Arbites]] and by the [[Inquisition]], and it binds every world, Chapter and institution within the Imperium.

The code is held to be the word of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. Much of it derives from the decrees the Emperor issued during the [[Great Crusade]], and from the rulings of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and of the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] after the [[Horus Heresy]].

History

The Great Crusade

The first laws of the Imperium were issued in the years of the [[Great Crusade]], which began in 798.M30. These decrees governed the conquered worlds, set the duties of the Legions and fixed the relations between the Legions and the Imperial administration. Their texts survive in the oldest sections of the archive on Terra, written on parchment in several languages that have since been lost.

The Heresy and the Time of Rebirth

The [[Horus Heresy]] tested the code as nothing had before. After the Heresy, [[Roboute Guilliman]] held the offices of [[Imperial Regent]] and Lord Commander from 014.M31, and he reshaped the law during the [[Time of Rebirth]]. Under his direction the [[Codex Astartes]] was promulgated, and the Legions were divided into smaller Chapters.

Guilliman also issued the Edict of Restraint, in consultation with the Captain-General of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], [[Constantin Valdor]]. It required the Custodes to remain on Terra as guardians of the Emperor until the start of the [[Era Indomitus]] in 999.M41.

The Era Indomitus

In 999.M41 Guilliman returned to Terra and resumed his offices. The Imperial records of the Indomitus Crusade describe a continuing reform of the law under his direction.

The Hall of Judgement

The body of law is kept in the Hall of Judgement on Terra. The Hall is a complex covering many acres, with kilometres of corridors and levels. Its shelves rise a hundred metres towards the vaulted ceilings, and each row holds ten thousand volumes. Scholars and law lords work among the bookcases, and new volumes are added daily. Every decree and ruling of the High Lords of Terra is held there, along with the words of the Emperor Himself.

Because no one can read the whole code, the Imperial courts rely on abridged collections. Most [[Arbites Precinct Fortress]]es keep a cogitator stack holding a short selection of rulings and precedents. These are drawn from the judgements of segmentae judges, High Marshals and the Lords Marshal, and are used to speed up the work of individual Arbitrators.

Enforcement

The Adeptus Arbites are the galactic police force of the Imperium and enforce the code on every human-settled world. The Inquisition may override the code wherever it concerns heresy, xenos or Chaos, and its agents are not bound by ordinary procedure. The [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] designation is the most severe penalty the code can impose.

Content

The code covers crime, property, trade, the duties of officials, the rights of citizens and the relations between the Imperium's institutions. It is separate from the [[Imperial Cult]]'s doctrine, which governs faith, although the two overlap in practice wherever heresy is a civil crime.
