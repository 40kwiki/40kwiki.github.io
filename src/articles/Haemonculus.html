The Haemonculi (singular Haemonculus), also called the "Lords of Pain", are horrific and insane [[Drukhari]] flesh-sculptors. They have lived in the depths of [[Commorragh]] for many Terran centuries, and some for millennia. They are the Drukhari's master torturers and their most skilled artists of pain. Even other Drukhari fear them, because they can reshape the soul as well as the body.

The Haemonculi are organised into covens. The covens are central to Commorrite society. They are the true masters of the regenerative processes that keep the Drukhari alive, and they extract the sustaining psychic essences of their victims through torture. Even among the [[Aeldari]], they remain figures of terror and paranoia.

Practice

Haemonculi specialise in body modification and flesh-sculpting. They prefer to work with a new flesh canvas. A client who asks for barbed quills on the shoulders, the scaled face of an alien reptile, or the eyes of a wraithspider will receive them. Imperial scholars note that no request is too difficult or strange for a Haemonculus to fulfil.

Each Haemonculus reshapes its own body to suit its tastes and madness. Their pale frames are stretched and twisted. Some of them keep their organs outside the body, in ornaments, to present a fashionably horrific appearance.

Origins

How a Drukhari becomes a Haemonculus is not recorded. The oldest of them, known as the Haemonculi Ancients, are of an age that even the long-lived Aeldari find remarkable. Some Imperial scholars suggest that these Ancients may include those who founded the first cults of pleasure and pain before the [[Fall of the Aeldari]]. The matter is unresolved, since the Haemonculi modify themselves so heavily that they hardly resemble their own kind.
