Sicarus is a [[Daemon world]] deep within the [[Eye of Terror]]. It is the homeworld of the [[Word Bearers]] [[Traitor Legiones Astartes|Traitor Legion]]. The Word Bearers took the world after the [[Horus Heresy]], when the forces of Chaos were driven into the Eye by the Imperium. It has been held by the Legion ever since.

From Sicarus, the veteran [[Dark Apostle]]s of the Legion form the [[Dark Council]]. They rule the Word Bearers in the absence of their primarch, [[Lorgar]], and they direct the Legion's efforts to corrupt the Imperium.

History

The Imperial records of Sicarus are fragmentary. The world was known to Imperial astropaths as a place of great warp activity. During the [[Great Scouring]] the Word Bearers took it and began to build a monument to their gene-father.

In the early Heresy years the Dark Apostle [[Kalta-Ar]] began the monument. Chaos legends say that something then began to kill the Legion's warriors, and that the planet's dead were given to the warp in its service.The dates of these events are estimated. The Word Bearers' own records are not available to the Imperium.
