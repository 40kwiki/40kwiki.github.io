The Siege of the Fenris System was an attack on the [[Fenris]] system in 999.M41 by the [[Thousand Sons]], led by their [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Magnus the Red]]. Fenris is the homeworld of the [[Space Wolves]] and of the primarch [[Leman Russ]], and the Space Wolves defended it against the [[Chaos]] force.

The Thousand Sons had attacked Fenris before, most seriously in 742.M32. In 999.M41 Magnus led the siege himself, and the Space Wolves defended their world against the Chaos forces.
