Agnathio was a [[High Lord of Terra]] who restored the council of the [[High Lords of Terra]] in 646.M32. The restoration is recorded as the start of a new age of government after the disorder that followed the murder of the High Lords in 546.M32.

The murder of the High Lords in 546.M32 left the government of the Imperium in disorder. The [[Grand Master of Assassins]] ruled as [[Lord Protector]] for several decades, and the struggle for the empty seats continued after his death. Agnathio's restoration of the council ended that period.

Restoration

Agnathio gathered the surviving offices and filled the empty seats with officials of the Terran courts. The council that followed is the first whose full roll is known in the Imperial records. Its members held their offices for life, and they were answerable only to the council as a body.Agnathio's own career before 646.M32 is not recorded. The date of the restoration is taken from the Administratum's registers.
