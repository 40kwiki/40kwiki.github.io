Lethe is a former colony world and [[Space Marine Chapter Homeworld|Chapter homeworld]] of the [[Flame Falcons]], a [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] that is now extinct. It lies on the border of the [[Manachean Commonwealth]], an association of populated and advanced worlds at the junction where the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] ends and the northeastern reach of the [[Ultima Segmentum]] runs on. At the dawn of the [[Horus Heresy]], the region was the most strategically vital part of the [[Coronid Deeps]].

The Manachean Commonwealth was a centre of trade and a powerhouse of industry and military strength. Many of its worlds had held [[Human]] populations since before the [[Age of Strife]]. It anchored much of the force projection of the [[Imperial Army]] and the [[Imperialis Armada]] in the region during the [[Great Crusade]], and so held the shape of the Imperium's borders.

Early history

Lethe was home to a Human remnant civilisation that had declined to a near pre-industrial state during the long night of the [[Age of Strife]]. The world was first contacted in 824.M30 by the [[Rogue Trader Militant]] Vaspperly Elgin, before the [[Great Crusade]] advanced into the region in force. The mist-shrouded, largely forested world, with its many rivers around which the population clustered, became an early staging point for Crusade [[expeditionary fleet]]s. It never rose above the status of a [[Frontier world]], with no major industrial or economic power, but it supplied several elite pioneer light infantry cohorts to the [[Imperialis Auxilia]].

The famous 905th [[Solar Auxilia]] Cohort, the "Ash Scorpions", was raised from the warrior tribes of Lethe. The Cohort was known for its aggression and dedication, and it won many battle honours in pacification wars across the Coronid Deeps. Its last stand, a glorious sacrifice, came at [[Port Maw]] during the Heresy.

The Flame Falcons

About five standard millennia after the Heresy, Lethe became the homeworld of the Flame Falcons. The Chapter was raised in the Twenty-first Founding, the "Cursed Founding", shortly before the [[Age of Apostasy]], and the largest Founding since the [[Second Founding]]. Next to nothing is known of the Chapter in official records, for it was declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] within a standard century of its creation. Its combat trials succeeded and it went on active service on the southern rim of the galaxy, based on Lethe. Its first wars were against [[Orks]] and Aeldari corsairs.The Founding is dated 991.M35. Records give the fall of the Chapter only as within a century of its creation, with no exact year.

At the battle of Raffenburg's World, the Chapter's largest engagement to that time, the 1st Company burst into flame at the height of the fighting, and fought on with renewed savagery, unharmed by the fire. The commander took this as a gift of the [[Emperor]]. An [[Inquisitor]] attached to the [[Astra Militarum]] forces judged otherwise, waited, and watched the rest of the Chapter undergo the same change. The Chapter returned to Lethe in celebration.

Soon after, the [[Grey Knights]], the [[Chamber Militant]] of the [[Ordo Malleus]], assaulted Lethe at the behest of the [[Inquisition]] and destroyed the Chapter. To ensure that its taint did not spread to the rest of the world, the Inquisition ordered an [[Exterminatus]] of Lethe. A few Flame Falcons may have escaped, but nothing is known of their fate.
