Lethe is a former colony world and [[Space Marine Chapter Homeworld|Chapter homeworld]] of the [[Flame Falcons]], a [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] that is now extinct. It lies on the border of the [[Manachean Commonwealth]], an association of populated and advanced worlds at the junction where the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] ends and the northeastern reach of the [[Ultima Segmentum]] runs on. At the dawn of the [[Horus Heresy]], the region was the most strategically vital part of the [[Coronid Deeps]].

The Manachean Commonwealth was a centre of trade and a powerhouse of industry and military strength. Its worlds had held [[Human]] populations since before the [[Age of Strife]]. Its force projection helped define the Imperium's borders during the [[Great Crusade]].

Early history

Lethe was home to a Human remnant civilisation that had declined to a near pre-industrial state during the long night of the [[Age of Strife]]. The world was first contacted in 824.M30 by the [[Rogue Trader Militant]] Vaspperly Elgin, before the [[Great Crusade]] advanced into the region in force. The mist-shrouded, largely forested world, with its many rivers, became an early staging point for Crusade [[expeditionary fleet]]s.

The famous 905th [[Solar Auxilia]] Cohort, the "Ash Scorpions", was raised from the warrior tribes of Lethe. The Cohort was known for its aggression and dedication, and it won many battle honours in pacification wars across the Coronid Deeps. Its last stand, a glorious sacrifice, came at [[Port Maw]] during the Heresy.

The Flame Falcons

About five standard millennia after the Heresy, Lethe became the homeworld of the Flame Falcons. The Chapter developed a dangerous [[mutation]] that caused its members to burst into flame. The [[Inquisition]] sent a large [[Grey Knights]] taskforce to deal with the Chapter. The mutated Chapter was wiped out, and the Inquisition ordered an [[Exterminatus]] of the doomed world to ensure that nothing remained.The date of the Flame Falcons' fall is estimated at c. 036.M36 from the phrase "five standard millennia later" in the sources. No record gives an exact year.
