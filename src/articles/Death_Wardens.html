The Death Wardens are a little-known loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Blood Angels]] raised in the [[Third Founding]] of the [[31st millennium]]. They stand sentinel over an unnamed [[Shrine world|Shrine World]] in the [[Eastern Fringe]], a world whose people have worshipped the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Sanguinius]] since he walked it in the [[Great Crusade]], and whose devotion pre-dates the rise of the [[Ecclesiarchy]]. The Chapter ensures the priests of the Ecclesiarchy do not bend that worship to the orthodoxy of the [[Imperial Cult]].

Almost nothing else is set down. Imperial records leave the Chapter's colours, badge, Master and fortress unrecorded. What is known is why it was made: to preserve a secret.

History

Origins

The Death Wardens were raised from the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of the Blood Angels in the [[Third Founding]]. Their first warriors came from the Blood Angels and from several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the Sanguinary Brotherhood: the Angels Encarmine, [[Angels Numinous]], Angels Sanguine, [[Flesh Tearers]] and Blood Drinkers. The founding officer cadre, a [[Chapter Master]], his Sanguinary Guard, a [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]], a Sanguinary High Priest, a High [[Chaplain]] and a [[First Captain|first captain]], were all veterans of the [[Horus Heresy]] who knew of the forgotten [[Signus Campaign]] and where the Signus Cluster lay.

The secret of Signus

In 005.M31 the Blood Angels went to the Signus Cluster at the order of the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], not knowing it was a trap. Signus Prime had become a [[Daemon|daemon]] realm ruled by a Greater Daemon of [[Slaanesh]], Kyriss the Perverse. Horus hoped to turn the Blood Angels' hidden flaws, later called the [[Black Rage]] and the [[Red Thirst]], against them and bind the Legion to [[Khorne]]. The Blood Angels cleansed the cluster, but [[Sanguinius]] ordered every trace of their presence removed. The dead were carried home to [[Baal]], and warning beacons were set about the system, which was declared Mortae Perpetua, forever dead. The order was written in the Legion's Book of Hours in the primarch's own hand.

Organisation and beliefs

The Death Wardens keep the knowledge of Signus out of every written record, including from the [[Inquisition]]. It is held by the senior officers and the [[Techmarine|Master of the Forge]], whose duty to the Omnissiah makes his silence doubly bound. When an officer dies, his successor ritually eats his body, so that the secret passes with the flesh. The custom comes from the early [[Great Crusade]], when the Legion, then called the Revenant Legion, fought in conditions so extreme that the eating of fallen captains preserved their skill. Recruits took the names of those they absorbed, and lieutenants those of their captains, so that few outsiders noticed that each warrior resembled his flawless brothers.

When the body cannot be recovered, the surviving officers must allow themselves to be consumed by the new command cadre. The Chapter once tried to pass the secret to an aspirant who was then sacrificed to a new officer; every attempt failed, for reasons the commanders have not understood. As a last safeguard the Master of the Forge is bound to wake a [[Dreadnought]] deep in the fortress-monastery. It holds the partly devoured body of the first [[Chapter Master]], still alive in the sarcophagus, who would tell the new command what Signus was.

Notable campaigns

No campaign of the Death Wardens is recorded. The Chapter's records, like its colours and its badge, have been withheld or lost.Imperial sources differ on whether this Chapter and the Angels Erythrean, a second obscure Blood Angels successor of the same Founding, are separate commands. The Administratum lists them separately.
