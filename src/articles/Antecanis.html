Antecanis is a [[Hive world|hive world]] that was the seat of a monarch hive, a great hive that ruled the lesser hives of its system. It lay beside the naval fortress [[Cancephalus]]. In 537.M38 it became the target of the [[Ninth Black Crusade]], led by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]].

Imperial accounts describe the world as a place of little value to the Imperium apart from its position. Its loss is recorded less for the population killed than for the war it started.

The Ninth Black Crusade

[[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] launched the Ninth [[Black Crusade]] against both [[Cancephalus]] and Antecanis. The assault destroyed the monarch hive of Antecanis, and the naval fortress was drawn into a seventeen-year war. Both worlds lost much of their manpower in the fighting.

Abaddon's sorcerers used the war to weaken the Warp barriers in the region. Imperial astrographers noted that the rifts there grew wider through the seventeen years of fighting, and the Inquisition later counted the Antecanis war among the causes of the Imperium's losses in the area.Imperial records differ on how much of the Warp weakening was caused by the war. The Inquisition's figures are the most cited.
