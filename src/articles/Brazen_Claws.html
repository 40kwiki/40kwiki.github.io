The Brazen Claws are a loyalist Chapter of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], founded in the [[Second Founding]] from the [[Iron Hands]]. They share the stoicism of their parent Legion and fight on without pity for the enemy or for their own losses. Their homeworld, Talus IV, was torn apart by a daemonic horde in the late 41st millennium, and since then they have been a fleet-based Chapter.

The Chapter is best known for a crusade of vengeance that it carried into the [[Eye of Terror]] in 955.M41. It lasted twenty years of the Chapter's own time and almost forty years in the rest of the galaxy. Some parts of the Chapter were corrupted in the Eye. The greater part emerged in 994.M41 and has since fought on the [[Cadian Gate]] and elsewhere.

History

Origins

The Brazen Claws trace their lineage to the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy, when they were the 34th Clan Company of the [[Iron Hands]], within Iron Lord Autek Mor's Clan Morragul. The Clan Company was garrisoned on the [[Knight world]] of Fabris Callivant in payment of a debt that the X Legion owed to House Callivant. For that reason it escaped the heavy losses that the rest of the Legion took in the [[Drop Site Massacre]] on [[Isstvan V]].

After the Heresy and the Great Scouring, the Chapter was formed from the Astartes of the 34th Clan Company in 021.M31, during the Second Founding.

Ten thousand years of service

The Chapter's records are quiet for most of the following millennia, but certain actions are noted. The Brazen Claws took part in the defence of the Imperium against the Orks of the Beast in 544-546.M32, in the [[War of the Beast]]. One of their Iron Fathers, Jurkim Bohr, became a founding member of the [[Deathwatch]] in that war.

In 416.M37 the Brazen Claws joined the Sons of the Raven and the Celestial Guard in an expedition led by the Inquisitor Lord Antonius Coil of the [[Ordo Malleus]] against the nomadic Hell-Forge of Sarum. Ambushed in the Golgotha Wastes by the Warp Ghosts, the Black Wings and Dark Mechanicum engines, the Imperial fleet was torn apart in seventeen hours. Only the Brazen Claws' fleet broke out without crippling losses. The Sons of the Raven were lost to the last warrior and declared martyred.

At Falax in 223.M39 the Chapter besieged a renegade stronghold in the Peligron Cluster. It lost three squads of the 3rd Company, and a daemonic surge destroyed the mind of the Librarian leading the assault. Only the firepower of the Devastator Centurions held the Daemons back long enough to allow an evacuation.

In 900.M41 the Brazen Claws and the Iron Hands, under Iron Father Kardan Stronos, fought the [[Necron dynasty]] of the Crimson King in the Crusade of Steel. Stronos refused to fall back, and with the Iron Hands' Garrsak Clan Company he struck the Crimson King a blow that broke the dynasty's command.

The Crusade of Vengeance

In 955.M41 Daemons overran Talus IV and left it in ruins. Chapter Master Caul Engentre swore an oath of vengeance among the burning fortress-monastery. Against orders from the [[High Lords of Terra]], he took the Chapter into the Eye of Terror. Its last astropathic message told of a battle against an [[Iron Warriors]] fleet.

The war cost the Chapter heavily. The 8th and 9th Companies followed an [[Emperor's Children]] warband into the depths of the Eye and were thought lost. In 994.M41, on Engentre's order, the survivors prepared to leave. At the moment of departure they were ambushed by a dozen warships and two full companies of their own brothers, now corrupted into Heretic Astartes. Engentre and many others escaped. Only twenty years had passed for them, but almost forty had passed outside.

Cadia and the Era Indomitus

The Chapter was sighted around the Cadian Gate during the Thirteenth Black Crusade in 999.M41, and Lord Castellan [[Ursarkar Creed]] testified to this. Most of the Brazen Claws came too late to help [[Cadia]] before its fall, and turned instead to defend the worlds around it. They rode out the opening of the [[Great Rift]] and defended Gudrun, Lapis VII and Thracian Primaris. Elements of the 2nd Company under Captain Erod served in the Fall of Medusa V in 999.M41.

In the early 42nd millennium three companies fought on Vigilus and helped quarantine the hive city of Dontoria (c. 001-025.M42, estimated).The war on Vigilus is dated here from the Imperial record of the Era Indomitus; no source gives exact years. At an unrecorded date the Brazen Claws also joined the Iron Crusade, in which the Iron Hands and several successors relieved Mordian a second time from the Night Lords.

Beliefs and organisation

Like their parent Legion, the Brazen Claws despise weakness of any kind, even in their own bodies. They hold that every organic body, including a transhuman one, is frail and subject to age and disease. A neophyte begins to replace flesh with cybernetics from the day he is initiated. The 1st Company is known as the Dionekean Guard.

Notable members

  • Caul Engentre, Chapter Master, who led the Chapter into the Eye of Terror and ordered it out again.
  • Macklen Eogh, First Captain; Erod, Second Captain, who abandoned the crusade and returned to realspace alone.
  • Julas Imbolkh, Seventh Captain, whose red hair turned grey in the Eye.
  • Firlus Ghad, Captain and Master of the Forge, whose throat was replaced with an augmetic after the Eye.
