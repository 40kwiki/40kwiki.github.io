The Red Talons are a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and one of the Successor Chapters of the [[Iron Hands]] Legion. Like its sister Chapters, it was founded in the [[Second Founding]] from the clans of [[Medusa]], the Legion's homeworld. The Red Talons are recorded among the Successor Chapters of the Medusan clans, which include the [[Brazen Claws]], the [[Iron Fists]] and the [[Sons of Medusa]].

The Chapter takes its name from the clan that produced its founders. Its colours and devices are recorded as red and iron, and its warriors are known for an aggressive style of close assault.

Notable actions

The Red Talons took part in the defence of the [[Sentinel World]] of [[Orask]] during the [[Ghoul Stars]] campaign. A company of the Chapter stood with the Orask garrison and a [[Titan Legions|Titan]] maniple of the [[Legio Magna]]. Tens of thousands died in a few solar days, but the attackers were pushed back and purged from the surface.

The Chapter also fought in the [[Moirae Schism]], on the side of the Loyalist clans of the [[Iron Hands]] against the Moirate [[Heretic|heretics]]. Records of that conflict are sealed.
