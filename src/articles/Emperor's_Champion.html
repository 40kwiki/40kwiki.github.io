Emperor's Champion
Title in the [[Imperial Cult]] and the [[Adeptus Astartes]]

Associated with[[Black Templars]], [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] and the Emperor's forces

The Emperor's Champion is a title given to the foremost warrior of the Emperor's forces. The title belongs to the doctrine of the [[Imperial Cult]] and is used by the [[Adeptus Astartes]] in their own writings. It was first described in the early 36th millennium, in the [[Mythos Angelica Mortis]].

The title is applied to a warrior who is held to stand for the Emperor in battle. The Imperium does not record a single holder of the title for the whole of its history. Several figures are named in the sources, and accounts differ on which of them held it at each point.

Crusades

In the late 41st millennium [[Roboute Guilliman]] gathered his forces at Macragge for the [[Terran Crusade]] and the defence of Ultramar. The forces he gathered included contingents of many Chapters, the [[Grey Knights]] under Grand Master Aldrik Voldus, the [[Black Templars]] under Marshal Marius Amalrich, and the Emperor's Champion, who is named in the list without further detail.The sources do not name the holder of the title in this campaign, and the record may use the title as a description rather than a formal office.
