A First Captain is the senior captain of a [[Space Marine Legions|Legion]] or [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He commands the 1st Company, the most experienced warriors of the host. In the Legions of the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]] the rank was one of the offices of the [[Praetor (Space Marine)|praetors]], the lords who stood second only to the primarchs in martial skill and generalship. In many Chapters formed after the Heresy the commander of the elite 1st Company is the senior captain, and in some he is the [[Master of the Keep]], the Chapter Master's second-in-command.

The office has marked some of the most celebrated, and most hated, warriors in the Imperium's records. Sigismund, First Captain of the [[Imperial Fists]], was the first Emperor's Champion and the first High Marshal of the [[Black Templars]]. Ezekyle [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]], First Captain of the [[Sons of Horus]], became the [[Warmaster]] of [[Chaos]] and has led thirteen [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]] against the Imperium. Calas Typhon of the [[Death Guard]] and Ahzek [[Ahriman]] of the [[Thousand Sons]] also held it, and both became servants of the Dark Gods. On the loyal side [[Raldoron]] of the [[Blood Angels]] stood beside [[Sanguinius]] in the last hours of the Heresy, and in the Era Indomitus [[Severus Agemman]] of the [[Ultramarines]] rules [[Macragge]] as regent in the absence of the Chapter Master.

Origins

In the Legions

Main article: [[Space Marine Legions]]

In the Great Crusade (c. 798.M30–005.M31) the [[Legiones Astartes|Legions]] were vast hosts of tens or hundreds of thousands of warriors, and their primarchs delegated command to a few high officers. These praetors were the masters of the Legion's formations, of chapters or great companies of about a thousand warriors, and had control of fleets and armies. Their titles followed the customs of the Legions: archein, warsmith, khan, tribune, lord commander, chapter master, wolf lord or first captain.

The rank is attested in the records of many Legions. The [[Space Wolves]] held their first captain to be a [[Wolf Lord]]. In the Imperial Fists Sigismund led the Templar Brethren of the 1st Company, the most exalted position beneath [[Rogal Dorn]]. In the Blood Angels Raldoron, First Captain of the 1st Company, also held the honorific of Chapter Master, a special seniority that few other warriors of the Legion could claim. In the Thousand Sons the 1st Fellowship was led by Ahriman, in the Iron Warriors by Forrix, and in the Night Lords the title passed from Sevatar to Zso Sahaal.

In the [[Ultramarines]] the same duties were held by the First Master of the 1st Chapter, [[Marius Gage]], and in the [[Dark Angels]] by the First Master and by the Master of the Deathwing. In the Sons of Horus, then the Luna Wolves, [[Ezekyle Abaddon]] rose to be First Captain of the 1st Company.

The Council of Nikaea

The [[Council of Nikaea]] (001.M31), called after the Triumph at Ullanor, brought the primarchs and some of their chief officers before the Emperor. [[Raldoron]] attended at the side of [[Sanguinius]] and sat among giants: First Captain Typhon of the Death Guard, the Custodian [[Constantin Valdor]] and the primarchs [[Fulgrim]] and [[Leman Russ]].

The Chapters

Main article: [[Codex Astartes]]

After the Heresy the Legions were divided by the [[Codex Astartes]] into Chapters of a thousand warriors in ten companies, and the old praetorial offices were reduced. The captain of the 1st Company, the [[Veteran]] company, became the senior captain, and in many Chapters he was given the title of First Captain. In the Dark Angels the captain of the 1st Company is the Grand Master of the [[Deathwing]], and in the Ultramarines the same captain is the Regent of [[Ultramar]]. The Chapters keep their own names and customs, and most of the variants are listed in the article on the rank of [[Captain (Adeptus Astartes)|captain]].

The title of [[Master of the Keep]] is the Chapter Master's second-in-command, who serves as regent of the Chapter homeworld when the Chapter Master is off-world or incapacitated, and it is usually borne by the commander of the elite 1st Company. In the Ultramarines the same captain bears the title of Regent of [[Ultramar]], and he is the official successor of the Chapter Master if the Master should fall.

Primaris Chapters and the Era Indomitus

The Chapters of the [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] foundings and the First Founding Chapters that have taken Primaris recruits use the same structure. A Primaris captain does the same work as his [[Firstborn]] counterpart. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Ultramarines' First Captain, [[Severus Agemman]], was named one of the [[Tetrarchs]] of Ultramar by [[Roboute Guilliman]] in about 012.M42 and made the Tetrarch of [[Konor]]. He had commanded the 1st Company since the Battle of Macragge in the [[First Tyrannic War]], having been promoted from the 2nd Company after the death of his predecessor Saul Invictus, and he led his warriors against the Bloodborn of the Daemon Prince [[M'kar]] in the [[Invasion of Ultramar]] (999.M41), where he was struck down and later recovered.

Legacy

The First Captains of the Heresy divided on the same lines as the Legions. Sigismund, Raldoron and Santor remained loyal, while Abaddon, Typhon, Ahriman, Kaesoron and Sevatar followed their primarchs into Chaos.
