A First Captain is the senior captain of a [[Space Marine Legion|Legion]] or [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He commands the strongest company and acts for the [[Primarch]] or [[Chapter Master]] when that commander is absent. The office was held in each Legion from the earliest years of the Great Crusade.

The most notable First Captain in Imperial records is Ezekyle [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]], First Captain of the [[Sons of Horus]]. He led the Legion's assault on [[Luna]] in the [[Solar War]] of 014.M31, which was the opening campaign of the [[Siege of Terra]].

Duties

A First Captain commands the Legion's or Chapter's largest formation, and he speaks for the commander in councils of war. Imperial accounts describe him as the second in authority in the Chapter, and the first to be named as successor if the commander falls. The rank was often held by the Legion's most experienced captain.
