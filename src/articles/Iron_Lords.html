The Iron Lords are a rare [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a Successor of the [[Iron Hands]] created during the 23rd Founding. Their character is marked by an exceptionally stern demeanour and an absolute hatred of xenos life. They have played major roles against the [[Barghesi]], the [[Tyranids]] and the [[T'au Empire|T'au]], and they have never accepted the peace that other Chapters have made with the alien.

The Grendl Stars

Since the 38th millennium (M38) it has been the duty of the Iron Lords to maintain the blockade of the [[Grendl Stars]]. The blockade confines the [[Barghesi]], a hyper-aggressive alien species, within that region of space until the Imperium can muster the resources to exterminate them as a threat to Mankind. The Chapter drove the Barghesi back to their home in the Grendl Stars during the Grendl Stars Campaign, whose exact date is not recorded.

In the 41st millennium the Chapter has faced the [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] as it sweeps through the Grendl Stars. The tendrils of the hive fleet threaten to absorb the genetic potential of the Barghesi, and Imperial scholars regard that outcome as potentially disastrous for the galaxy. The Iron Lords have vowed to prevent it at any cost, and they have suffered heavily in keeping that oath.

Notable campaigns

In 801.M41 the Iron Lords took part in the Judgement of Hellenus. [[Inquisitor]] [[Hector Rex]] took control of the 279th Assault Armada of the [[Navis Imperialis]] and ordered the mutant population of Hellenus purged. A combined strike force of Space Marines from the Iron Lords, the [[Skull Bearers]] and the [[Sons of Guilliman]] gathered to confront the mutants holding the enemy capital. During the fight the Iron Lords contingent was tainted by the mutants' gene-taint, and their fellow Space Marines had to purge them as well.

In 992.M41 the Chapter was recorded as having so far kept Hive Fleet Kraken out of the Grendl Stars. In 997.M41 the [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] overran the Ecclesiarchy world of [[Valedor]] within solar hours, killing its pilgrims and priests, and overwhelmed a battle barge of the Iron Lords. A small force of Battle Sisters of the [[Order of the Sacred Rose]] were the only Humans to witness it. In 999.M41 the Chapter answered the summons of [[Marneus Calgar]] and took part in the punitive [[Zeist Campaign]] against the expansion of the [[T'au Empire]] in the [[Eastern Fringe]].

Other operations include the Cleansing of Eurydice, in which four squads of the Iron Lords' 3rd Company destroyed the nesting-crypts of an unknown xenos species on the frontier of the [[Eurydice Sector]].
