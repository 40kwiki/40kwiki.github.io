The Chief Apothecary, formally the Master of the Apothecarion, is the most senior [[Apothecary]] of a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. As head of the [[Apothecarion]] he assigns the Chapter's other Apothecaries to its companies, so that they may heal their battle-brothers or recover their [[gene-seed]] after death.

In the [[Blood Angels]] the equivalent office belongs to the Sanguinary High Priest, since [[Sanguinary Priest]]s serve in the place of Apothecaries.
