The Ashen Claws are a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] descended from the [[Raven Guard]] Legion. They are one of the Raven Guard's Successor Chapters, formed in the [[Second Founding]], and they keep the Raven Guard's tradition of swift, shadow-borne attack.

Among the Raven Guard's Successor Chapters are the Ashen Claws, the [[Black Guard]], the [[Death Spectres]], the [[Iron Ravens]], the [[Knights of the Raven]], the [[Necropolis Hawks]] and others. The [[Carcharodons]] and [[Dark Eagles]] are suspected to descend from the same stock.

Campaigns

In 002.M31 elements of the [[Raven Guard]] were sent on a crusade into the Ghoul Stars. The Ashen Claws took part in later campaigns against the [[Ghoul Stars]] and the raiders of the region, as the records of the Chapter describe.The dates of the Chapter's individual campaigns are not recorded in the sources consulted.
