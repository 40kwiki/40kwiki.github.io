The rise of the Ynnari is the emergence, from 999.M41, of the Ynnari, who call themselves the Reborn: a religious sect of the [[Aeldari]] devoted to Ynnead, the Aeldari god of the dead, who was partly awakened in that year. The sect is led by the prophet [[Yvraine]], once a gladiator of [[Commorragh]], whom a fragment of the god's power raised from death in the arena. Unlike the other Aeldari peoples, the Ynnari draw their members from every kindred: the [[Craftworld|craftworld]] Asuryani, the [[Drukhari]], the [[Harlequins]], the Corsairs and the Exodites. They seek to wake Ynnead fully, so that the god may defeat [[Slaanesh]], the [[Chaos]] power that devours Aeldari souls, without the extinction of the whole race.

The Ynnari's rise was one of the events that brought about the [[Era Indomitus]]. In their search for the relics called the Croneswords they shattered the craftworld of Biel-Tan, and with the Imperium's survivors of the [[Fall of Cadia]] they restored the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] to life. The Imperium has since treated them sometimes as allies of convenience and more often as enemies, while among the Aeldari they are both a source of hope and a cause of schism.

Beliefs

The [[Craftworld|craftworlds]] have long held that the souls of their dead, kept from [[Slaanesh]] in the infinity circuits of the world-ships, will one day unite into a new god, Ynnead. In the traditional belief this will happen only at the Rhana Dandra, the final battle, when the last [[Aeldari]] has died: the god will then rise from the gathered souls, destroy Slaanesh, and allow the race to be reborn. The Ynnari teach that it need not wait for the death of every Aeldari. They hold that the five Croneswords of Morai-Heg, the crone goddess of fate, united in the right hands, will give their bearers power over life and death and serve as a conduit through which Ynnead may wake. They call this the Seventh Path.

The Reborn draw on the energies released at death. In battle they are said to grow faster and stronger as those around them die, and they carry spirit stones whose souls lend them power. Many Aeldari regard this as a corruption, and say that the Ynnari are already dead inside.

History

The ritual at Coheria

For centuries the High [[Farseer]] [[Eldrad Ulthran]] of the craftworld Ulthwé had sensed a slow pulse, like a sleeping heartbeat, among the souls of his [[Craftworld|craftworld]]'s infinity circuit. In 999.M41 he found a place to wake it: Coheria, a moon of the Imperial war world Port Demesnus, whose crystal sands were steeped in psychic residue from the fall of the Aeldari empire. With the [[Harlequins]] of the Masque of the Midnight Sorrow he began a ritual that would draw the souls of every craftworld's dead to Coheria at once, while the warriors of Ulthwé attacked Port Demesnus as a feint.

The ritual was interrupted at its height by Watch Captain Artemis of the [[Deathwatch]], who had seen through the feint. Ulthran's Harlequin allies were killed and the Farseer fled. The ritual failed, save for one mote of the god's consciousness that crossed the void to [[Drukhari|Commorragh]].

The Night of Revelations

In the arena of the Crucibael, the Succubus [[Yvraine]], an exile of Biel-Tan who had walked every Path of the craftworlds and been a Corsair before falling to the Dark City, was fighting before a crowd that included the famed [[Lelith Hesperax]]. She had killed a captive [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] Hive Tyrant, been mortally wounded by Hesperax, and was dying in a last duel against a priestess of Morai-Heg when the mote of Ynnead struck her. At the threshold of death she was filled with the god's power. A wave of deathly energy swept the arena, a breach opened in the Webway and [[Daemon|daemons]] poured into Commorragh. With the mysterious swordsman called the Visarch at her side, Yvraine escaped the Dark City and began to preach.

Biel-Tan and the Yncarne

Yvraine travelled through the Webway to [[Exodite]] worlds and craftworlds, leaving schism behind her. On her return to Biel-Tan, as daemons of [[Slaanesh]] invaded, she drew the second Cronesword from the [[Wraithbone|wraithbone]] spine of the craftworld. Its infinity circuit shattered, and from the vortex of souls emerged the Yncarne, the Avatar of Ynnead, bearing the great Cronesword Vilith-zhar, the Sword of Souls. The daemons were destroyed, but Biel-Tan began to break apart, and the storm of psychic energy released by its fracture joined the storms that formed the [[Great Rift]].

The Seers of Ulthwé rescued the Ynnari from Biel-Tan, which was close to civil war, and called them to account before their council along with Eldrad Ulthran. Emissaries of Altansar, whose craftworld had survived millennia in the [[Eye of Terror]], spoke for them, and the Ynnari were allowed to leave on condition that they enter the Eye. On the crone world Belial IV they found a third Cronesword, borne up from the [[Terra|earth]] by the Yncarne in the midst of a battle against daemons and Drukhari sent by Vect. They were rescued by a delegation from Iyanden.

Iyanden and Macragge

At Iyanden a Nurglite plague fleet attacked the craftworld. Prince [[Yriel]], the craftworld's Autarch, led a boarding action against the fleet's flagship and destroyed it at the cost of his life. Yvraine burned the daemonic plague from his corpse and raised him, claiming his Spear of Twilight, which proved to be a fourth Cronesword. Yriel and his Eldritch Raiders joined the Reborn.

The elders of Iyanden then concluded that the [[Aeldari]] could only survive the coming doom if humanity was given the strength to hold back Chaos. The Ynnari passed through the Webway, fighting [[Ahzek Ahriman]]'s [[Thousand Sons]] on the way, to the ice moon Klaisus in the [[Cadia]] System. There they met the survivors of the [[Fall of Cadia]] under [[Celestine]], [[Katarinya Greyfax]] and [[Belisarius Cawl]], and led them to [[Macragge]]. In the Fortress of Hera Yvraine killed the primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]], who had lain at the edge of death for ten thousand years, and raised him again with the power of Ynnead, while Cawl sealed him in the [[Artificer armour|Armour of Fate]]. The Ynnari helped to clear [[Ultramar]] of Chaos and then withdrew into the Webway.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The opening of the Rift was followed by the loss of two [[Craftworld|craftworlds]], whose psychic traces faded away. Some Spiritseers believe that their souls passed to Ynnead. Many [[Aeldari]] blamed the Ynnari for both disasters. Vect, whose hold on [[Drukhari|Commorragh]] the Night of Revelations had shaken, set off a hidden civil war in the Dark City against pro-Ynnari factions and put a vast bounty on [[Yvraine]]'s head. His agent [[Drazhar]], Master of Blades, came within a hand's breadth of killing her at the debating fires of Saim-Hann before the Phoenix Lord Jain Zar, who had joined the Reborn, parried the blow. Despite Vect's efforts, whole sub-realms of Commorragh have passed under Ynnari control.

[[Slaanesh]] released its [[Daemon|Keeper of Secrets]] [[Shalaxi Helbane]] to hunt Yvraine. At the council of Aeldari kindreds she convened on the maiden world of [[Iathglas]], Helbane attacked and was driven off by Yvraine, Jain Zar, [[Lelith Hesperax]], the Visarch and the Yncarne fighting together. Its presence there proved to be an illusion, and it revealed that the fifth and last Cronesword had been taken to the Palace of Slaanesh. The Seventh Path now appears closed, and Yvraine has turned to seek another way to defeat the Dark Prince.

The Ynnari have fought beside other Aeldari in defence of Iyanden and Ulthwé during the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]], and continue to win converts from every kindred. In the [[42nd millennium]] their warhosts have been reported across both halves of the Imperium.

Relations with the Imperium

The Imperium owes the Ynnari the restoration of its [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], a debt that [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] acknowledges and the [[Inquisition]] prefers not to discuss. Imperial records describe the Ynnari as a [[Xenos|xenos]] faction like any other, and most [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] have treated them as enemies. Some, especially those who served in the [[Celestinian Crusade]], have accepted their help. The [[Ordo Xenos]] considers the sect's aims to be no concern of mankind's, except in so far as a god of the [[Aeldari]] that can wake the dead is a power to be watched.
