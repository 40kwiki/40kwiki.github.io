The Achilus Crusade is an ongoing [[Imperial Crusade]] that began in 777.M41 to bring the [[Jericho Reach]], a region of the [[Eastern Fringe]], back under the rule of the [[God-Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]]. The Reach had been lost to the Imperium since the [[Age of Apostasy]]. Its recovery is one of the largest campaigns of the late [[41st millennium]].

The crusade began when a stable [[Warp Gate]], the Jericho-Maw [[Warp]] Gate, was found linking the Reach to the [[Calixis Sector]]. The [[Achilus Crusade|Achilus]] forces passed through it in 777.M41. Over four solar decades the crusade has cost millions of lives and seen dozens of worlds destroyed. Imperial records do not agree on when it will end.

Background

[[Jericho Reach|The Jericho Reach]] was once part of the wider Imperial territory on the edge of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. After the [[Age of Apostasy]], its worlds were cut off by [[Warp storm|warp storms]] and fell into the control of local warlords and [[Xenos|xenos]]. For centuries the Imperium knew only fragments of the region from [[Astropathy|astropathic]] messages and the reports of lost [[Sector Governor|sector governors]].

The Jericho-Maw Warp Gate changed this. It gave a route into the Reach that the warp storms did not close, and the [[Administratum]] judged that the region could be retaken for the cost of one crusade.

Course of the crusade

The crusade has been fought in waves. Each wave secures a group of worlds, sets up a [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]] and a [[Planetary Defence Force]], and then moves on. Some worlds have changed hands several times. The forces of [[Chaos]] and the [[Xenos|xenos]] of the region have fought fiercely, and the crusade has been slowed by the warp storms that surround the gate.
