Hive Fleet Hydra
[[Tyranid]] [[Hive fleet]]
ParentTendril of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]
Known forRapid generation of bio-organisms, including slimer maggots
StatusActive in the [[Era Indomitus]]

Hive Fleet Hydra is a recent tendril of the [[Tyranids]]' invasion of the galaxy. It drifts in the wake of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], which it follows through the regions the larger fleet has already consumed.

The fleet is noted for the generative power of its bio-organisms. Even its lowest creatures, such as the slimer maggots, can multiply with terrifying speed and infest the flesh of their victims. Imperial commanders warn that a world may be infested before its defenders see the enemy, because the fleet's smallest creatures spread so quickly.
