The Solar Hawks are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a rare successor of the [[White Scars]]. Their Founding is unrecorded, but they appear in Imperial records by the [[35th millennium]]. Imperial scholars describe them as aggressive and noble, and as embodying what is best in the sons of [[Jaghatai Khan]]. They honour the lineage by painting one shoulder guard or gauntlet white, the colour of the parent Legion.

As their name suggests, the Solar Hawks field an unusual number of airborne assets, and send whole wings of Stormtalons, Stormravens and other gunships into battle. Armour plays a large part as well, and their best-known campaign was won in a duel of tanks.

History

Wars of the 41st millennium

In 882.M41 the [[White Scars]] and two of their successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], the Solar Hawks and the Storm Lords, joined forces against the [[Waaagh!|Ork WAAAGH!]] Blackfist on the [[Agri-world|agri-world]] of Lycelle. Competition between the three was fierce, each striving to surpass the others' deeds in the name of their [[Primarch|primarch]]. The campaign is remembered as the Blackfist Scalping.

The Tetrahric Uprising began when a dozen once-loyal worlds rebelled against the Imperium. The Solar Hawks invaded them in turn and fought hundreds of tank-on-tank engagements. The best known is the Battle of Brokentread Plain on Haus K5, where a single Solar Hawks strike force of a hundred vehicles faced thousands of renegade [[Leman Russ]] tanks. For solar days the two sides duelled through dust thrown up by grinding treads, until only a graveyard of shattered rebel armour remained. The date of the uprising is not recorded.The Tetrahric Uprising is recorded only as an event of the 41st millennium.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the first decades of the [[Era Indomitus]] three demi-companies of the Solar Hawks went with a White Scars strike force to [[Vigilus]], to defend the link between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]] in the War of Beasts (c. 001–025.M42). [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris Marines]] now serve in its ranks.

Organisation and beliefs

The Solar Hawks wear sunburst-yellow armour with a golden [[Aquila]], a silver backpack and, often, a white left arm. Squad numbers are stencilled in black on the specialty symbol of the right shoulder, and the colour of the squad markings shows the company. Their badge is a hooded red hawk's head on white.

The Chapter is rich in psychically gifted battle-brothers. The [[Deathwatch]] draws many of its [[Librarian|Librarians]] from three [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] for this reason: the [[Blood Angels]], the [[Blood Ravens]] and the Solar Hawks.

Imperial scholars also note the Chapter's shared inheritance with the other [[White Scars]] successors. Where the White Scars of [[Chogoris]] mark squads with tribal signs, the Solar Hawks use the plain numerals of the [[Codex Astartes]]. The Chapter's rivalry with the Storm Lords, seen on Lycelle, is kept friendly by their common debt to the Khan.

Notable members

No [[Chapter Master]] or captain of the Solar Hawks is named in Imperial records. Their [[Librarian|Librarians]] are better known than their commanders, through their service in the [[Deathwatch]].
