Menazoid Epsilon is a world of the [[Sabbat Worlds]] that fell to [[Chaos]] during the wars of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]. It is recorded chiefly for two events in c. 765.M41: the death of the [[Lord Militant]] [[Hechtor Dravere]] there and the destruction of a [[Men of Iron]] fabricator by [[Ibram Gaunt]].

The world's name is recorded in the Sabbat Worlds lists beside those of [[Tanith]], [[Verghast]] and [[Balhaut]].

Men of Iron fabricator

In about 765.M41 an Imperial force found a [[Standard Template Construct]] fabricator for [[Men of Iron]] on the Chaos-held world. Dravere and the [[Inquisition|Inquisitor]] Golesh Heldane both hoped to use it. [[Commissar|Colonel-Commissar]] [[Ibram Gaunt]] destroyed it after the fabricator was taken, because he judged that its use would bring the machines back into Imperial service.

Death of Dravere

Dravere had plotted against the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]'s commander. He was killed on Menazoid Epsilon in 765.M41, and [[Macaroth]] took over his command in the crusade.
