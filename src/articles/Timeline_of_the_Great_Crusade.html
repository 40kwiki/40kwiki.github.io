The Timeline of the Great Crusade lists the dated events of the [[Great Crusade]], the war of reconquest by which the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and the [[Space Marine Legions]] reunited the scattered worlds of humanity after the [[Age of Strife]]. It begins with the Emperor's preparations on [[Terra]] in the [[30th millennium]], follows the crusade from its departure from the [[Sol System]] in 798.M30 to the [[Ullanor Crusade|Triumph of Ullanor]] in 000.M31, and ends with the [[Isstvan III]] atrocity of 005.M31, which opened the [[Horus Heresy]].

The crusade lasted a little over two centuries. Its records were kept by the fleets themselves, by the [[Remembrancer Order]] in its last years, and by the Administratum's Departmento Cartographicae on Terra. They often disagree. Warp transit distorted the logs of every Expeditionary Fleet, and many entries were sealed after the Heresy or lost with the Legions that kept them. The dates below follow the Administratum's reconciled chronology. Dates that rest only on the order of events are marked "c." and noted.Where no record gives a year, the date is estimated from the sequence of events.
