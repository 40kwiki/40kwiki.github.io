The Oriente Sector is an Imperial sector in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. It lies at the very edge of the [[Astronomican]]'s reach. Imperial control over the sector is tenuous, and it is constantly attacked by xenos forces.

History

A [[Genestealer Cult]] has infiltrated the [[Hive world]] of [[Cryobuur]] and is slowly working to corrupt the sector capital, [[Khamrang Prime]]. Imperial records do not give a date for the infiltration.
