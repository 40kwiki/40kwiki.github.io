The Fire Hawks were a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] created in the "Cursed" [[Twenty-First Founding]] of the [[36th millennium]]. The Chapter claimed descent from the [[Ultramarines]], although defects in the gene-seed samples held by the [[Adeptus Terra]] cast doubt on that claim. The Fire Hawks lost two homeworlds in their history and were driven to the edge of extinction several times, most nearly in the [[Badab War]] of 904–912.M41.

In 963.M41 the Chapter's mobile fortress-monastery, the void fortress Raptorus Rex, and five ships of the line vanished in the [[Warp]] with about eight hundred battle-brothers aboard. They have not been seen since. A persistent belief among Imperial scholars holds that survivors of the Chapter became the [[Legion of the Damned]], but no record confirms it.

History

Age of Apostasy

Further information: [[Age of Apostasy]]

The Fire Hawks fought from their earliest years. In 378.M36 they joined the [[Black Templars]], the [[Imperial Fists]], the [[Soul Drinkers]] and [[Skitarii]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the assault on [[Terra]] that ended the [[Reign of Blood]] of [[Goge Vandire]]. The cost was severe: their first homeworld, Zhoros, was destroyed by a thermonuclear bombardment unleashed by a fleet of the Ecclesiarchy's Frateris Templar.

The middle millennia

Between 770 and 791.M38 the Chapter was one of the five Chapters, collectively called the Manus Irae, that carried out the Great Malagantine Purge of the heretical Malagant Sector in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. The Fire Hawks later settled the world of Cousteau XI, which was in turn rendered uninhabitable; from the 228th year of M40 they were once more a fleet-based Chapter. Between 228.M40 and 902.M41 they waged the long series of campaigns remembered as the Castigations of Golgotha.The loss of Cousteau XI is not dated; it preceded 228.M40, when the Chapter is first recorded as fleet-based again.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

The Fire Hawks were among the first Loyalist Chapters drawn into the [[Badab War]]. In 904.M41 their ship Red Harbinger was attacked and captured by the [[Mantis Warriors]], the first recorded clash between Loyalist and Secessionist Space Marines. The Chapter then fought throughout the war under Grand Master Stibor Lazaerek and suffered losses so heavy that it ended the war with only a fraction of its strength. Imperial scholars record that the Fire Hawks fought with particular bitterness against the Mantis Warriors.

Disappearance

In 963.M41, some fifty years after the war, the Raptorus Rex and five ships of the line left the Piraeus System for the Crow's World Sub-sector. They carried about eight hundred battle-brothers and some two thousand other personnel, and were expected to arrive within a few weeks. They never did. In 987.M41 a patrol ship in the Maran Sub-sector narrowly avoided collision with a vessel at the Cift jump-point, and debris recovered there was identified as belonging to the lost fleet. In 988.M41 the scorched banner of the Fire Hawks was found, bearing the High Gothic motto in dedicato imperatum ultra articulo mortis, "for the Emperor beyond the point of death".

Some accounts hold that about two hundred Fire Hawks survived the Warp transit and became the Legion of the Damned, the spectral warriors in black armour marked with flames who appear in the Imperium's darkest hours. The [[Inquisition]] has neither confirmed nor denied this, and the origin of the Legion's apparent supernatural powers remains unexplained.

Organisation and heraldry

The Fire Hawks followed the [[Codex Astartes]], with the variant title Grand Master for their [[Chapter Master]]. Their colours were red and yellow, and their emblem was a phoenix rising from flame, chosen, the Chapter said, because it had so often risen again from near-destruction.
