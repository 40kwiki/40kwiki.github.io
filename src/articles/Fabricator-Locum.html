The Fabricator-Locum, also written Fabricator Locum, is the second-highest-ranking [[Tech-priest]] of [[Mars]] and of the other [[Forge world]]s of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. The Locum assists the [[Fabricator-General]] in governing the world, in meeting its production quotas and in seeing that the devotions owed to the [[Machine God]] are kept at all times.

The most celebrated Locum is [[Zagreus Kane]]. Master of the Mondus Occulum forge, he took the Emperor's side in the [[Horus Heresy]] and the [[Schism of Mars]], helped the [[Imperial Fists]] carry arms and armour off the planet before the blockade, and was recognised on [[Terra]] as Fabricator-General of the loyalist Mechanicum.

Duties

The title is used on Mars and on the other forge worlds, where the Locum stands second to the ruling fabricator. Of all the fabricators only the Fabricator-General of Mars holds a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]], and the Locum has none.
