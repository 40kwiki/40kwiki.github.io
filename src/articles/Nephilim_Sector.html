The Nephilim Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. Its name is shared with the [[Nephilim]], the xenos species that first subjugated the human world of [[Melchior]] during the [[Great Crusade]]. Imperial records do not state whether the sector's name refers to that species. In the early [[42nd millennium]] the sector became the scene of a major war, after a region within it was found to have lost contact with the [[Warp]]. Imperial records call that region the [[Pariah Nexus]], and its effect on the Warp the Stilling.

The sector lies beyond the core of the Ultima Segmentum and is reached by the crusade routes of the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. The Imperial survey of the sector is incomplete, and its warp routes became unreliable during the Stilling, so much of what is known comes from crusade fleets that entered the sector and reported back.The sector's surviving survey is partial. Its boundaries are taken from Indomitus Crusade reports and are subject to revision.

History

Early records

The sector was surveyed in the Imperial settlement of the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]], and its worlds were recorded in the [[Administratum]]'s registers. Little of its early history survives, and the sector's records for the millennia before the Stilling are fragmentary.The early history of the sector is not recorded in detail. Dates for its early settlement are not known.

The Stilling

Main article: [[Pariah Nexus]]

In the early [[42nd millennium]], [[Imperial Crusade|Imperial crusade]] fleets found that the Warp had gone silent in a region of the sector. [[Astropaths]] could not reach its worlds, and the people of the region were said to suffer an affliction of the will that Imperial doctors called the Stilling. The crusade fleet that entered the region named it the Pariah Nexus.

The [[Necrons]] under the [[Silent King]], [[Szarekh]], were found to have built a lattice of blackstone pylons across the Pariah Nexus. The pylons severed the region from the [[Warp|Immaterium]], and their effect on the people who lived there is recorded as a drain on their will. The Stilling is thus both a natural and a constructed effect, a matter which Imperial scholars still dispute.

The Nephilim War

The war that followed is called the Nephilim War, or the Pariah Crusade. It began as a standard Imperial expedition, sent to investigate the fate of the sector and to restore contact with its worlds. Battle Group Kallides was sent to investigate the Pariah Nexus, and Battlegroup Hephaestus arrived later as a reinforcement.

The war has since become a four-way conflict between the Imperium, [[Chaos]] and two rival [[Necron dynasty|Necron dynasties]]. Chaos forces entered the sector to seek the blackstone pylons and to exploit the Stilling, and the Necron dynasties have fought each other and the Imperium over the pylons. The war is recorded across the [[Indomitus Crusade]]'s campaign archives as one of its most dangerous fronts.

Vashtorr

Main article: [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]]

The Necron Lord [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]] is recorded in the sector's war as having emerged from the remains of the [[Wyrmwood]], according to some accounts, and to have torn a rift in the Pariah Nexus. Imperial accounts of his role are contradictory, and some scholars treat them as legend.

Geography

The sector's principal feature is the Pariah Nexus, a region in which the Warp is stilled and in which [[Warp travel|warp travel]] is impossible. The Nexus is surrounded by [[Sector|sectors]] where the Warp is weaker than usual, and its borders are marked by blackstone pylons. Crusade fleets approach the Nexus from its edges, and the Imperial charts of the region show its boundaries in dotted lines.

Notable worlds

The Pariah Nexus is not a single world but a region of the sector, containing the [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb worlds and the blackstone pylons that cause the Stilling. Its worlds are the focus of most of the war.

Governance

The sector is not governed by a [[Sector Governor]] in the usual sense. Its affairs are run by the [[Indomitus Crusade]] under the Office of the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], and Imperial authority is exercised by the crusade fleets. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has a large presence in the sector, and [[Belisarius Cawl]] commands Battlegroup Hephaestus there under the [[Noctilith|Noctilith]] Decree.

The [[Inquisition]] is represented in the sector by the [[Ordo Malleus]] and the [[Ordo Xenos]], who investigate the Stilling and the [[Necrons|Necron]] presence. Their findings are sealed.

Military

The Imperial forces in the sector are drawn from the crusade fleets of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], Battlegroup Hephaestus of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] companies of several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. The Chaos forces of the Nexus are drawn from the warbands that entered the sector to seize its pylons, and the [[Necron dynasty|Necron dynasties]] fight for control of the Nexus from their own tomb worlds.

The war is one of several fought in the early [[42nd millennium]] along the [[Great Rift]]. Its crusade fleets are often drawn from the same forces as the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]] campaigns, which are fought on the far side of the Rift.
