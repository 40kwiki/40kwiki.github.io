A praetor was a senior [[Astartes]] officer of the [[Space Marine Legions]] during the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. The rank belongs to the late [[30th Millennium]] and the early [[31st Millennium]]. Praetors were the mightiest warriors and battle-leaders of the ancient [[Legiones Astartes]]. Only the [[primarch]]s surpassed them in martial skill and generalship.

Praetors commonly commanded chapter-sized formations of about 1,000 [[Space Marines]]. They held the power of life and death over whole worlds, and they controlled entire Imperial war fleets and armies. Each was an experienced warlord, with a legend written in blood, and carried the finest wargear known to [[Mankind]] at the time.

Titles

Praetors appeared under the titles their Legions chose, in line with each Legion's culture and traditions. Titles included [[Chapter Master]] and [[Lord Commander]], [[Archein]], [[Warsmith]], [[First Captain]], [[Khan]] and [[Tribune]]. Whatever the title, these officers were known across the Legiones Astartes as praetors.

Role in the Great Crusade

The Legions of the Great Crusade were vast engines of war, with transhuman warriors numbering in the tens or hundreds of thousands. Each Legion's full panoply could destroy a civilisation by many means. These included brute force, armoured assault, siege craft and relentless attacks from close orbit. The high commanders of the Legions oversaw these campaigns. They brought long experience and tactical skill to the ruin of [[Mankind]]'s foes, and they were called praetors.

Praetors led the hosts that conquered worlds beyond counting during the Great Crusade. They fought on both sides of the Horus Heresy. Their clashes at Heresy-era sieges, such as the [[Siege of Cthonia]], are recorded in several accounts.
