The Battle of Molech was the invasion of the [[Knight world|Knight World]] of Molech by the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] in 009.M31, during the [[Horus Heresy]]. Molech was among the most strongly garrisoned worlds on the approaches to [[Terra]], defended by its Knight houses, three loyal Titan Legions, more than a hundred regiments of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] and detachments of the [[Ultramarines]] and [[Blood Angels]]. Horus attacked with his own [[Sons of Horus]], the [[Death Guard]] under [[Mortarion]], the [[Emperor's Children]] under the [[Daemon|daemon primarch]] [[Fulgrim]], and four traitor Titan Legions.

The battle was decided by treachery. The ruling Knight house of Molech, House Devine, long corrupted by a serpent cult of [[Slaanesh]], turned on the defenders at the height of the fighting for the capital, Lupercalia, and brought down the loyal Imperator Titan that anchored the line. The loyal army was destroyed almost to the last man. Horus, meanwhile, left the battle to pursue his true purpose. Beneath Lupercalia lay an ancient gateway into the [[Warp]], which the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] had once used and which he had garrisoned the world to guard. Horus passed through it and returned with far greater power than before.

Background

Molech

Molech was a verdant [[Knight world|Knight World]] on the routes toward the [[Sol System]]. It was brought into the Imperium in 869.M30 in a compliance led by the Emperor in person, with three [[Primarch|primarchs]]: [[Horus]], [[Lion El'Jonson]] and [[Jaghatai Khan]]. The compliance was bloodless. The capital was renamed Lupercalia in honour of Horus. The world was ruled thereafter by House Devine, its high house, from the fortress called the Dawn Citadel, and a large Imperial garrison was left in place, far larger than such a world would seem to need.

The people of Molech never learned why. The Emperor had come to the world to secure a gateway into the Warp hidden beneath the land on which Lupercalia stood. Inquisitorial sources hold that long before the founding of the Imperium the Emperor had used the gate to enter the Warp, and that there he had faced the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]] and won from them the power he needed for his work. Horus, who had been present at the compliance, knew of the gate.

The Knight houses

Main article: [[Imperial Knight]]

Molech's nobility was divided among several houses. House Devine of the Western Marches held the rule of the world as the old seneschals of the vanished High Kings of Molech. House Tazkhar came from the southern steppes and was known for its ferocity. House Kaska of the western peninsula was proud and plain-living. House Indra of the southern swamps was called the Mad House by its peers. House Mamaragon held the islands of the northern Azure Sea. House Donar held lands in the east.

Under its noble surface House Devine had long been rotten. The worship of an old serpent god of Molech, a deity of excess and vengeance, had been suppressed by the [[Imperial Truth]] but survived in cults that the house protected. The last true seneschal, Cyprian Devine, knew nothing of the plotting of his wife and his second son, Raeven. His eldest son and heir, Albard, had been rejected by his Knight armour through their schemes and held for years as a captive.

Prelude

When loyal commanders learned that a [[Traitor|traitor]] fleet was approaching, they reinforced Molech. Three loyal [[Titan Legions]] mustered on the world: [[Ryza|Legio Crucius]], Legio Gryphonicus and Legio Fortidus. More than a hundred [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] regiments joined the [[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]], and detachments of the Ultramarines and [[Blood Angels]] were already present. It was intended as one of the strongest stands on the road to [[Terra]].

[[Malcador the Sigillite]] also sent a party of his [[Knights-Errant]] under [[Sons of Horus|Garviel Loken]], a former captain of the Luna Wolves. Their task was to infiltrate the [[Warmaster]]'s flagship, the [[Vengeful Spirit]]. The Vindicare assassin Eristede Kell, the last survivor of an earlier attempt on the Warmaster's life, was also aboard the flagship, hunting [[Horus]].

Course of the battle

The landings

[[Horus]]'s first assault devastated cities and strongholds across Molech. In the confusion Raeven Devine murdered his father and took his place as lord of the world. Horus himself took the field with his sons and faced a host of [[Imperial Knight|Imperial Knights]]. He destroyed them almost alone. Only Raeven Devine is recorded to have escaped.

With a beachhead won, the main [[Traitor|traitor]] host landed: the [[Sons of Horus]], [[Death Guard]] and [[Emperor's Children]], with their [[Primarch|primarchs]], and four [[Titan Legions]].

Mortarion in the east

See also: [[Mortarion]]

[[Mortarion]] led the Death Guard against the eastern city of Ophir, defended by Lord Bolmar of House Donar, who made a desperate last stand there. The Death Guard released the [[Exterminatus|life-eater virus]] on the jungles of Kush, an early example of the plague warfare for which the XIV Legion would become infamous. Mortarion then took the Preceptor defence line with the help of traitor Titans.

Fulgrim and House Devine

See also: [[Fulgrim]]

[[Fulgrim]]'s task was the corruption of House Devine. During the invasion Albard Devine escaped his captivity, killed his half-brother Raeven and reclaimed his birthright and his Knight armour, Banelash. But the armour had been tainted by Raeven and by the serpent cult, and when Albard joined his broken mind to it, the corruption took him. Fulgrim, now a [[Daemon|daemon]] of [[Slaanesh]], completed the work. Albard became obsessed with visions of a great serpent, and many of his retainers followed him into the god's service.

Lupercalia

The loyal armies were pushed back toward Lupercalia. The Knight houses led the defence, rallying the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], the Legion detachments and the Titans, but each line was broken in turn. The first great blow fell on the Iron Fist, the mountain on the city's western flank that housed the Titan Legions' fortresses. Horus redirected an orbital defence laser against it and destroyed it in a single strike.

The final blow was struck by House Devine. During the battle for Lupercalia, the Knights of Devine turned their weapons on the Imperator-class Titan Paragon of Terra of [[Ryza|Legio Crucius]], the anchor of the Imperial line. Its plasma reactor failed and exploded, killing thousands of defenders and leaving a crater nearly a kilometre wide. Traitor Titans marched through the gap. Caught between the betrayers and the advancing traitor host, the defenders were destroyed. Records state that only one in a hundred of the Imperial Army soldiers on Molech survived the campaign.

The Warp gate

Horus did not stay to see the city fall. He left the battle before its end and went to the gate beneath Lupercalia. What happened there is known only from sealed accounts. Horus entered the Warp, as his father had done before him, and returned. Inquisitorial scholars hold that he made his own bargain with the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]] and was invested with far greater power, at the price of what remained of his soul. Imperial scholars hold that much of the power he showed in the last years of the war was won there.

The [[Nathaniel Garro|Knights-Errant]] and the assassin aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] did not kill the [[Warmaster]]. Loken survived the mission and returned to the loyalist cause.

Aftermath

Molech fell to the [[Traitor|traitors]] and was held by them until the end of the Heresy. [[Imperial Knight|House Devine]] became the first Knight house to fall to [[Chaos]]. Houses Kaska and Indra were destroyed, along with others. A few survivors of House Mamaragon lived through the occupation, and the Tazkhar Steppe became the heart of a loyal resistance.

During the [[Great Scouring]], in about 015–020.M31,Date estimated from the sequence of events. a combined force of Houses Cadmus, [[Terryn]] and Borgius reclaimed Molech and destroyed what remained of House Devine. Some of its Knights were never accounted for and are thought to have fled to the [[Eye of Terror]] with the traitor Legions. The surviving loyal houses rebuilt their world after the war.

Significance

The Battle of Molech is held by Imperial scholars to mark a turning point in the Heresy. [[Horus]] came out of the Warp gate transformed, with powers he used through the rest of the war and in his final confrontation with the Emperor aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] at the end of the [[Siege of Terra]]. The betrayal of [[Imperial Knight|House Devine]] also showed that the Imperial Knights, bound to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] and to their own codes, were not proof against the [[Traitor|traitors]]. Since that first fall, only a handful of Knight houses have been corrupted by Chaos.
