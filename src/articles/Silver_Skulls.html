The Silver Skulls are a [[Codex Astartes]]-compliant Loyalist [[Chapter]] of [[Space Marines]]. They are a successor of the [[Ultramarines]], founded in the [[Second Founding]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] they are one of the nine [[Shield Chapters of Ultramar]], which defend the [[Realm of Ultramar]] under the Ultramarines.

History

The Silver Skulls fought beside the other Primogenitor Successors of the Ultramarines as if they were kin. For many Terran years they believed they were Ultramarine successors of the Second Founding. Over time, all records of their true origins were lost.

After the [[13th Black Crusade]], the Chapter's beliefs were confirmed. [[Roboute Guilliman]] had been resurrected, and the Chapter was officially recognised as one of the Avenging Son's own.
