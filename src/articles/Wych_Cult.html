The Wych Cult, also called the Hekatarii, is a gladiatorial cult of the [[Drukhari]] in [[Commorragh]]. In Drukhari society the cults are second in prestige only to the [[kabal]]s that sponsor them. The Drukhari thrive on displays of bloodletting, and the Wyches are the most skilled of the [[Aeldari]] in the pursuit of murder.

The nightly battles between the gladiatrixes of the Wych Cults draw crowds to every Commorrite arena. Wyches fight with weapons that whip out, extend, enmesh and split, and their duels are the chief entertainment of the city's lower districts.

Role in Commorragh

The Imperium knows the [[Drukhari]] as a [[Scourge Stars|scourge]] without territory. Their raiding parties fall on worlds through [[Webway]] portals, take slaves and captives, and vanish before a defence can be organised. The Wych Cults supply the warriors for these raids, and they fight in the kabals' wars as well as in the arenas.
