The Chancellor of the Imperial Council is the bureaucratic officer who manages the daily business of the [[Senatorum Imperialis]], the council that governs the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from [[Terra]]. The office is not one of the twelve [[High Lords of Terra]], and it carries no vote of its own in the recorded procedure. Its holder is nonetheless a powerful figure, because the Chancellor controls the agendas, the minutes and the archives on which the council acts.

The title is recorded in the lists of the Senatorum as a lesser member, sitting alongside officials such as the Commandant of the [[Schola Progenium]] and the Lord Constable of the Synopticon when they are admitted as full members. Imperial sources do not agree on whether the Chancellor was a full member of the council in every period.

Office and standing

The Senatorum is led by a chair, who holds the offices of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and [[Imperial Regent]] when those are filled. The chair is first among equals. The Chancellor's place is below the chair. Imperial records describe the Chancellor as a senior bureaucrat rather than a military or political leader, with the [[Administratum]] as the wider civil service the office draws on.

The record does not give the Chancellor a fixed term. Holders are named in the rolls when a session is recorded, and the sources checked for this article name only one holder by name.

History

Origins

The Senatorum traces its origin to the Council of Terra, which governed the Imperium while the Emperor led the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Malcador the Sigillite|Regent]] held Terra in the Emperor's name. The post of Chancellor appears in the records only after the Senatorum was established by [[Roboute Guilliman]] in the aftermath of the [[Horus Heresy]], c. 014.M31 to 021.M31, when the council was reorganised and its members were set at twelve.

The mid-32nd millennium

After the [[War of the Beast]] and [[The Beheading]], the Senatorum passed through a period of political upheaval, dated c. 546–646.M32 in the Imperial record. The Chancellor's role in the rolls of this period is poorly documented, and no full list of holders survives.

Alexei Lev Tieron

The one holder named in the sources consulted is [[Alexei Lev Tieron]], who is recorded as Chancellor of the Senatorum on Terra at the time of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]. The sources do not give his years in office, and the date of the Thirteenth Black Crusade is estimated from the records rather than fixed in them.

Return of the Lord Commander

In 999.M41 [[Roboute Guilliman]] returned to Terra, took the chair as Lord Commander and Imperial Regent, and removed five High Lords from office along with hundreds of lesser lords.
