The Third Founding was the first creation of new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] after the [[Second Founding]] had divided the [[Space Marine Legions]]. It took place in the [[31st millennium]], in the years after the [[Great Scouring]], and was made at the request of [[Rogal Dorn]], who put the need for new Chapters to the [[High Lords of Terra]]. After more than forty years of preparation the High Lords granted over a hundred writs of succession, nine of them to the [[Ultramarines]] alone.

The Chapters of the Third Founding are among the oldest in the Imperium. They include the [[Executioners]] of the Imperial Fists' line and the [[Scythes of the Emperor]] of the Ultramarines', and probably the [[Angels Erythrean]] and the [[Death Wardens]] of the [[Blood Angels]]' line. The records of many others have been lost or deliberately concealed.

A Founding is the creation of a group of new Chapters by edict of the High Lords. The Imperium has held at least twenty-six since the First Founding of the Legions, the most recent being the [[Ultima Founding]] of the [[Primaris Space Marines]] at the end of the [[41st millennium]]. A list of the known Foundings is given [[#List of Foundings|below]].

Foundings

New [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] are not raised one by one as the need arises but in deliberate groups called Foundings. Only the [[High Lords of Terra]] can order one, for it requires the mobilisation of many parts of the Imperium's government, and decades or centuries of planning may precede the announcement. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] creates, tests and cultures the [[gene-seed]] from which the new Chapters will be made, and whole [[forge world]]s may be turned over to producing their arms, armour and ships. A homeworld must be found and its people studied over generations to make sure their stock is pure, a fortress-monastery built or, for a fleet-based Chapter, a great Chapter barque. Even once a Founding has been declared, it is usually a century before its Chapters are ready to fight.

The gene-seed of each new Chapter is drawn from an existing Chapter, which is granted a "writ of succession" for the purpose. The donor Chapter's Masters may help plan Foundings from their own line; the [[Disciples of Caliban]], for instance, were raised on the direct appeal of the [[Chapter Master|Supreme Grand Master]] of the [[Dark Angels]], an unusual request. Foundings made in haste in times of crisis have often gone wrong, for gene-seed cultured too fast is liable to degrade or mutate.

Background

The [[Second Founding]] of about 021.M31 had replaced each loyal Legion with a group of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of about a thousand warriors. The [[Codex Astartes]] limited the size of every Chapter. The [[Great Scouring]] and the wars that followed it cost the new Chapters heavily, and the Imperium's frontiers were long. The only remedy was to create more Chapters.

[[Rogal Dorn]], the Praetorian of Terra, had been the most stubborn opponent of the division of the Legions, and he had accepted it only after the [[Iron Cage]]. Having accepted it, he became the advocate of the next step. He put the case for a further Founding to the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] himself.

The Founding

The preparation took more than forty years. When it was done, the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] issued over a hundred writs of succession to [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of both the First and the Second Foundings. The Ultramarines, whose [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] was the most stable and whose Chapter had already supplied many [[Second Founding]] Chapters, received nine writs.

The Register singles out two of the new Chapters, one of Dorn's line and one of Guilliman's, as highly aggressive. Both were commanded from the first by warriors who had served in the Legions.

The Executioners

Main article: [[Executioners]]

The [[Executioners]] are of the line of [[Rogal Dorn]]. Their first master was [[Fafnir Rann]], reputedly the most ruthless of Dorn's later captains, a veteran of the [[Siege of Terra]]. The Chapter was created not for the defence of a region or a strategic post but for the single task of finding and killing the enemies of mankind, and it has kept that character. Its homeworld is the binary world of [[Stygia-Aquilon]].

The Scythes of the Emperor

The [[Scythes of the Emperor]] trace their history to the [[Horus Heresy]], when they were the 199th Company of the [[Ultramarines]] Legion, called the Aegida, set to guard the restricted world of [[Sotha]] and the alien device known as the [[Pharos]]. After the Second Founding [[Roboute Guilliman]] kept the survivors of the Aegida as an eleventh "ghost company" of the Ultramarines Chapter to continue the watch, in breach of his own Codex. In the Third Founding their last surviving member, Captain [[Oberdeii]], was made the first [[Chapter Master]] of a new Chapter, which he named the Scythes of the Emperor.

The Scythes kept their watch over Sotha and the surrounding [[Sector|sectors]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]] for ten thousand years. In 992.M41 [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] destroyed their homeworld and nearly the whole Chapter. The survivors swore to destroy the Kraken's remnants, and in the [[Era Indomitus]] they were rebuilt with [[Primaris Space Marines]] from the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Other Chapters

The records of the Third Founding are incomplete, and only a few other Chapters can be assigned to it. The [[Angels Erythrean]], a fleet-based Chapter of the [[Blood Angels]]' line, are listed in the Register, as are the [[Death Wardens]], raised from the gene-seed of the Blood Angels and their Successors. The [[Dark Paladins]], whose origins are unknown, were destroyed in the late [[41st millennium]] at the fall of [[Jarrman Primus]] to the [[Necrons]]. The fleet-based [[Charnel Guard]], whose relics are of great age, and the [[Iron Knights]] of Dorn's line, who existed at the time of the [[War of the Beast]], are believed by some scholars to date from the same Founding. An allegorical treatise called the Tears of the Gorgon, now prohibited, is associated with a Chapter of the [[Iron Hands]]' line from the Third Founding whose name has been struck from the record.

Legacy

Imperial scholars regard the Third Founding as the pattern for later Foundings: a petition to the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], a long preparation, and writs of succession to existing [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. It also established that the Codex limit of a thousand warriors would be kept by adding Chapters rather than enlarging them. That principle held until the [[Era Indomitus]], when the [[Ultima Founding]] swelled many Chapters beyond it.

[[Rogal Dorn]] did not live to see the age of Foundings that followed. He was reported killed in the First [[Black Crusade]] of 781.M31.
