Lugganath is a [[Craftworld]] of the [[Aeldari]]. In 764.M34 it was invaded and defended in the Shattering of Lugganath, when the [[Emperor's Children]] attacked the craftworld in a campaign of pleasure and bloodshed. It is one of the best-recorded Aeldari losses of the [[34th millennium]].

The craftworld's people survived the attack in part, and the events of the Shattering are among the reasons the [[Harlequins]] later took a close interest in the Emperor's Children.

The Shattering

The [[Emperor's Children]] invaded the [[Craftworld|craftworld]] and held it for a period. Imperial records do not give the number of defenders or the length of the occupation. Imperial accounts describe the campaign in terms of Slaaneshi revelry, and they count it among the early acts of the Emperor's Children after the Heresy.

Harlequins and the Dance

In the [[Harlequins|Harlequin]] records, the Dance Without End gave its first performance by a Solitaire in the years around the Shattering. The Harlequins' accounts record the [[Craftworld|craftworld]]'s loss as a warning, and they tie it to later Slaaneshi campaigns.
