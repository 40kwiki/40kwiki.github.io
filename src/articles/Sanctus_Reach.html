Sanctus Reach is a region of Imperial space that was invaded in 998.M41 by the Red Waaagh!, a [[WAAAGH!]] of [[Orks]] led by the [[Goff]] warboss [[Grukk Face-Rippa]]. The campaign is one of the largest Ork invasions of the late [[41st millennium]], and it is recorded in Imperial accounts as the start of a [[Long War|long war]] that spread across the region. The fighting centres on the knight world of [[Alaric Prime]].

The region has been fought over repeatedly, and the Imperial records of its governance are incomplete. Its worlds have been drawn into the wider Ork wars of the era, and the Red Waaagh! is the best recorded of its campaigns.The segmentum to which Sanctus Reach belongs is not fixed in the sources consulted. Its place on the Imperial charts is recorded only by sector.

History

Background

Sanctus Reach was settled by the Imperium in the [[40th millennium]] and held as a frontier of Imperial sector government. Its worlds were tithed through the [[Administratum]], and they supported the [[Knight world|knight worlds]] that guarded the region's approaches. The [[Imperial Knights]] and the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of several [[Chapters]] kept garrisons on its border.The settlement dates of Sanctus Reach's worlds are not recorded in any surviving source and are not given here.

The Red Waaagh!

In 998.M41 the Red Waaagh! fell on the Reach. The warband of Grukk Face-Rippa, a Goff warboss, struck the region's systems in a series of raids that grew into a full invasion. The Ork warbands moved from one side of the Reach to the other, and what began as a raid became a [[Waaagh!]] that burned across the region.

The defence of [[Alaric Prime]] was the campaign's pivotal struggle. The knight world held out against the Ork hordes, and its defenders are remembered in Imperial dispatches as the centre of resistance. The fighting spread from there to the surrounding systems, and the Imperial garrisons were pushed back across the region.

Later campaigns

After the first invasion, the war in the Reach continued in a series of smaller campaigns. The Imperial forces struggled to hold the systems they had lost, and the Ork warbands repeatedly returned to the region. The Red Waaagh! was not destroyed in a single battle. Instead, its warbands were broken up and driven back in a long series of actions.

The [[Stormclaw]] campaigns that followed are recorded as part of the same struggle. Their outcomes are contested in the sources, and the Imperium has not yet declared the region free of [[Orks]].

Geography

Sanctus Reach is a region of stellar systems, many of them rich in mineral resources. Its [[Knight world|knight worlds]] sit on the approaches to the region, and its inhabited planets are clustered along the main [[Warp travel|warp routes]]. Few parts of the region have been charted in full.

Notable worlds

  • '''[[Alaric Prime]]''': a [[Knight world|knight world]] and the centre of the defence against the Red Waaagh!.
  • '''[[Stormclaw]]''': the site of later campaigns in the region.

Governance

The region is administered by [[Sector Governor|sector governors]] under the [[Administratum]], with military command in the hands of the [[Imperial Knight|knight houses]] and of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] that hold fortresses on its border. In wartime the [[High Lords of Terra]] may appoint a commander to direct all forces in the region.
