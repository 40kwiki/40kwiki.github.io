The Angels Erythrean are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Blood Angels]] raised in the [[Third Founding]] of the [[31st millennium]]. Their fleet roams the [[Ghoul Stars]], a dim region beyond the [[Eastern Fringe]], and they are unknown to the Chapters of the Sanguinary Brotherhood.

Imperial scholars regard them as a deliberate secret: a Chapter created to be out of reach, so that the line of [[Sanguinius]] could survive if the Imperium ever turned against it.

History

A hidden safeguard

The [[Third Founding]] was proposed by the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Rogal Dorn]], who asked the [[High Lords of Terra]] for new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. The Angels Erythrean were drawn from [[Blood Angels]] [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. Over the following millennium their progenitors' flaws, the Red Thirst and the Black Rage, became known beyond the Sanguinary Brotherhood. The [[Inquisition]] and the new [[Adeptus Ministorum]] looked with suspicion on the Chapters' blood rituals and inherited curses. The Blood Angels therefore sent one successor beyond the reach of Imperial authority, forbidden any contact with Imperial forces, so that the bloodline of the [[Sanguinius|Great Angel]] could endure whatever came.

Exile in the Ghoul Stars

In time even the Blood Angels forgot them. The Chapter remained in the outer dark, remembering its purpose and fighting for Sanguinius against threats that came out of the region, which also kept it from returning. Its isolation made recruiting and resupply hard. The Chapter relies on copied and recovered fragments of Imperial technology held on its flagship, and it recruits subhuman and [[Abhuman|abhuman]] aspirants, whom its technology raises into [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]]. Its Sanguinary Priests are troubled by the question of whether such recruits are free of the taint of [[Chaos]].

The Chapter raids Imperial shipping that strays into its region for supplies and salvage, and several vessels have gone missing. It trades with other exile Chapters, among them the Carcharodons and others that follow the nomad-predation pattern in the outer dark. By such trade it recently obtained the gene-seed, knowledge and equipment to make its own [[Primaris Space Marines]], without a visit from a Torchbearer fleet of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter is fleet-based and follows the practice of the [[Blood Angels]] in its worship of [[Sanguinius]]. Its colours, badge and [[Chapter Master]] are unrecorded. Its [[Apothecary|Apothecarion]] carries the heaviest burden, since it must both make new warriors and judge their purity.

Notable campaigns

No named campaign of the Angels Erythrean is recorded. The Chapter's engagements are reports of lost Imperial ships and rumours among the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the outer dark.Imperial scholars disagree whether the Angels Erythrean replaced an earlier obscure Blood Angels command, the Death Wardens, or serve alongside it.

Notable members

No individual of the Chapter is named in Imperial records.
