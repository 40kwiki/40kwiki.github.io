Huron Blackheart, once Lugft Huron (also recorded as Lufgt Huron), is a renegade [[Space Marines|Space Marine]] and the master of the [[Red Corsairs]], the largest pirate power of the [[Maelstrom]]. As Chapter Master of the [[Astral Claws]] and lord of [[Badab Primaris]] he was a celebrated defender of the Imperium's eastern marches, until his ambition and suspicion led him to lead the secession of the [[Badab Sector]] in 901.M41. The [[Badab War]] that followed lasted until 912.M41 and ended with his near death. He survived by a bargain with the Ruinous Powers, and has ruled the Maelstrom ever since.

Half his body is bionic, and he is attended by a daemonic familiar, the Hamadrya. Huron honours the [[Chaos Gods]] without serving any one of them, and has so far kept his own will. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 he has captured the primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]] for a time, invaded the [[White Scars]]' homeworld of [[Chogoris]], and survived a challenge from his own lieutenant.

Early life

Most of Huron's early life has been erased by the [[Inquisition]]. The records that survive show him as a Scout of the Astral Claws in the last stages of the Ribos Crusade (early M41, estimated). On the world of Lylogir he fought through psychic attacks to kill an [[Aeldari]] Warlock with his bare hands and save his squad. He served next in the Chapter's Devastator reserve, where he became known for his skill at plotting fields of fire, then in the Third Company and the First, where he rose over far older veterans. As Captain of the Fourth Company he defeated much larger enemy armies with new tactics of concentrated firepower, and gathered a following that made some in the Chapter uneasy.

In 697 or 715.M41 (the records give both dates) Chapter Master Rovik Blake was killed fighting the Ork warboss Vorg Manburna, and Huron was elected to succeed him.The two dates appear in the same Imperial record; neither has been confirmed.

The Tyrant of Badab

Main article: [[Badab War]]

As Chapter Master, Huron led the [[Maelstrom Warders]], the [[Chapters]] that guarded the Maelstrom. He destroyed Ork invasions, and his defence of Khirab (mid-M41, date unrecorded) made him a hero to the people of the Endymion Cluster. He also became lord of Badab Primaris and the dominant power in the sector. As conditions in the Maelstrom worsened, he grew paranoid and distanced himself from Terra. He began to keep the sector's tithes for its own defence and petitioned for more Chapters and a new Founding under his command.

In 901.M41 he destroyed an Imperial investigation fleet sent to Badab. The [[Executioners]], [[Lamenters]] and [[Mantis Warriors]] joined his secession, and the Imperium sent loyal Chapters against him. The war is described in the article [[Badab War]]. In 912.M41 the loyalists stormed the Palace of Thorns on Badab Primaris, and Huron was struck down by a melta blast from Captain Zhrukal Androcles of the [[Star Phantoms]], who was dying. The blast destroyed the right side of his body. The surviving Astral Claws carried him into the Maelstrom.

Blackheart

In the Maelstrom, Lord Apothecary Garreon and Forge Master Valthex rebuilt Huron with bionics. Imperial savants hold that he would not have lived if he had not made a bargain with the powers of the [[warp]] while he lay between life and death. When he woke he was accompanied by the Hamadrya, a small creature of unknown nature that gives him psychic powers and is said to read the minds of those around him, which has saved him from many assassins. He took the name Blackheart and renamed his warriors the Red Corsairs (912.M41).

Over the following century Huron built the largest pirate fleet in the eastern Imperium. He took in renegade Astartes from every Chapter, killed the [[Salamanders]] Chapter Master Neh'Tar in 938.M41, and raided across the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. Before the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] (c. 999.M41), [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] gave him a [[Blackstone Fortress]], which he named Corsair's Keep, in return for alliance with the [[Black Legion]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the [[Terran Crusade]] (999.M41–000.M42) Huron's lieutenant Verngar, aided by the daemon [[Kairos Fateweaver]], captured Guilliman and his flagship Macragge's Honour and held them aboard Corsair's Keep. [[Cypher]] and the [[Harlequins]] freed most of the prisoners, and Huron blamed Verngar for the loss.

Huron then led the Red Corsairs against Chogoris and the Yasan Sector (999.M41–early M42). On Chogoris Verngar tried to usurp him with the relic called the Ebon Talon. Huron cut off the hand that held it, refused the relic's temptation and gave it to Valthex for safe keeping. The Corsairs captured and tortured the White Scars' Chapter Master [[Jubal Khan]] and held many worlds for a time, but were driven back.

Later in the Era Indomitus (early M42, dates unrecorded) [[Typhus]] defeated Huron in a duel atop the Endless Spire on Danasar, while the warp storm Fomori engulfed that world, and left him alive. Huron has since fought Tyranid splinters in the Maelstrom, raided the Crimson Eyrie against [[Yriel]], and opposed the Black Legion during the [[Arks of Omen]] (c. M42).

Character and abilities

Huron is a gifted strategist, a master of fire support and an exceptional duellist. He is proud, vengeful and convinced that the Imperium betrayed him, and he presents his realm as one where the strong prosper free of weak old men. He uses the Chaos Gods as he uses his allies, and avoids lasting obligation to any of them.

Wargear

  • The Tyrant's Claw: a huge bionic power fist with a built-in heavy flamer, part of his rebuilt body.
  • A power axe.
  • The Hamadrya: his daemonic familiar.
  • Before 912.M41: Terminator armour, an artificer heavy flamer and the lightning claw called the Ghost Razors.
  • Vessels: the battle barge Spectre of Ruin and the Blackstone Fortress Corsair's Keep. His followers also gave him an [[Imperial Knights|Imperial Knight]], Little Huron, stolen from House Terryn.
