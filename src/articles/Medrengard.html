Medrengard is a [[daemon world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]] and the seat of the [[Daemon Primarch]] [[Perturabo]] and his [[Traitor Legion]], the [[Iron Warriors]]. It is a world of fortresses: thousands of citadels built by the Legion's [[Warsmiths]] on the labour of slaves, under a lightless black sun that draws the life out of everything on the surface. The Iron Warriors made it their home after the [[Horus Heresy]], when they withdrew into the Eye with the other Traitor Legions (c. 015.M31, estimated).

The planet's fortresses have been the battlefields of the Legion's own wars, in which Warsmiths besiege one another for rank and favour. Perturabo rules from the greatest of them, the Kolasikon. Medrengard has also been entered at least once by Imperial agents, when two [[Ultramarines]] on a Death Oath reached the fortress of Khalan-Ghol at the end of the [[41st millennium]] (c. 999.M41, estimated).

History

Settlement

After the failure of the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31, the [[Iron Warriors]] fell back with the other [[Traitor Legions]] through the [[Great Scouring]]. [[Perturabo]], by then a [[Daemon|Daemon Primarch]] of [[Chaos]] Undivided, took the majority of his Legion to Medrengard, which became their fortress. The world's earlier history, if it had one in the material universe, is not known. The date of the settlement is not recorded; it is estimated from the Legion's withdrawal into the Eye at the end of the Scouring (c. 015–021.M31).All M31 and M32 dates for Medrengard are estimates from the sequence of the Iron Warriors' history; Imperial records hold no direct dating.

Perturabo set the Legion's captains to building, and each Warsmith raised his own fortress as a demonstration of his skill. The slaves taken in raids from the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] died in their millions on these works. The [[Primarch|primarch]]'s own fortress, the Kolasikon, was built on an isle of onyx in a lake of molten rock.

Wars of the Warsmiths

The Iron Warriors' internal wars began soon after the Legion settled on Medrengard (from c. M32, estimated). The Legion has no tradition of unity beyond obedience to Perturabo, and the Warsmiths have fought each other repeatedly for slaves, territory and their primarch's attention. Many fortresses have been razed and rebuilt in these wars, and some ruins are of uncertain age. Perturabo has allowed the struggles, which Imperial savants believe he uses to test his commanders.

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

Late in M41 (c. 990s.M41, estimated) a [[T'au Empire|T'au]] expeditionary force reached Medrengard and attempted to explain the doctrine of the Greater Good to the Iron Warriors. It was destroyed.

At the end of the millennium (c. 999.M41, estimated), the Warsmith [[Honsou]], who had inherited the fortress of Khalan-Ghol, was besieged there by rival Warsmiths. During the siege the Ultramarines Captain [[Uriel Ventris]] and his sergeant [[Pasanius Lysane|Pasanius]], exiled from their Chapter on a Death Oath, made their way to Medrengard and destroyed the daemonculaba beneath Khalan-Ghol, the wombs in which the Iron Warriors grew new Legionaries. Honsou survived the siege and kept his fortress.

Iron Warriors from Medrengard marched with [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] (999.M41) and have since raided across the [[Imperium Nihilus]] in the [[Era Indomitus]]. Perturabo himself is rarely reported outside Medrengard.

Geography

Medrengard orbits a black star that gives no light and draws vitality from the world below. Its natural sky is white, stained by the smoke of the forges. Rivers of lava cross the surface and drive the Legion's machines. The tallest fortresses reach above the atmosphere and serve as docking spires for [[Iron Warriors]] ships, so that the Legionaries rarely need to set foot on the surface among the slaves.

  • The Kolasikon, [[Perturabo]]'s fortress, the largest structure on the planet. Its cogitator networks feed him intelligence from across the Legion's holdings.
  • Arazikon, a fortress-city in a valley between parallel mountain ranges, reachable only through a handful of narrow passes and defended in every layer.
  • Pyriphon, a great cluster of underground [[Hell-forge|hell-forges]] run by [[Warpsmith]]s and magi of the [[Dark Mechanicum]], where the Legion's [[Daemon Engine]]s are bound.
  • Khalan-Ghol, the fortress held by [[Uriel Ventris|Honsou]] in the late [[41st millennium]].

Outside the fortresses the hills and gullies are roamed by the Unfleshed, [[Mutant|mutants]] who escaped the Legion's experiments, and by beasts of metal and flesh. Iron trees are reported to grow there.
