Beyreuth was Chief Custodian and [[Captain-General]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] in the middle of the 32nd millennium. He commanded the Emperor's guard during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32), and after the slaughter of the [[High Lords of Terra]] in [[The Beheading]] he became the first Captain-General since [[Constantin Valdor]] to take a seat on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] and join in the rule of the Imperium.

Nothing is recorded of his birth, his rise through the Ten Thousand or the date he took the office. His tenure is known only through the years of the war.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

Defence of the Throneroom

While the [[Ork]] warlord known as [[Beast, The|the Beast]] rampaged through the Imperium, a troupe of [[Harlequins]], the Troupe of Joyful Tears, broke into the [[Imperial Palace]] to deliver a message to the Emperor. Beyreuth led the [[Custodian Guard]] against them. The defence cost the lives of dozens of Custodians. Only the [[Shadowseer]] Lhaerial Rey and the Death Jester Bho reached the outer chamber before the [[Golden Throne]]. The Death Jester was killed, and Lhaerial was pinned to the floor with a guardian spear.

Lhaerial held that the [[Aeldari]] came in peace, bearing word from the [[Ulthwé]] Farseer [[Eldrad Ulthran]]. Beyreuth would not hear it and prepared to order her death. [[Drakan Vangorich]] and the Inquisitorial Representative [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Veritus]] arrived and claimed her for interrogation. Veritus used the authority of his seat to force the Captain-General to yield.

Seat among the High Lords

The Captain-General's office has carried a seat among the High Lords since the earliest days of the Council of Terra, but its holders have rarely used it, preferring to guard the Emperor and leave politics to others. After the Beheading in 546.M32 only Vangorich remained of the High Lords. He reminded Beyreuth that the Aeldari had come close to the Eternity Gate itself, and argued that the Custodes could not stay aloof from a Senatorum in ruins. Beyreuth was persuaded and took his place beside Vangorich.

The seat stood vacant for long ages afterwards. In 999.M41 [[Lev Tieron]] had to press [[Trajann Valoris]] to take it.
