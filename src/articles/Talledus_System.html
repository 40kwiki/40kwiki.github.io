Talledus System
Star system of the [[Veritus Sub-sector]]
LocationNear the centre of the [[Segmentum Solar]]
Known forLong-standing Imperial faith; the [[Talledus War]]
StatusContested since the opening of the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]]

The Talledus System lies near the centre of the [[Segmentum Solar]], within the [[Veritus Sub-sector]]. For centuries it was a prosperous bastion of the Imperial faith. Since the opening of the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]] and the resurgence of [[Chaos]] forces, it has become the site of the bitter and bloody [[Talledus War]], fought for the souls of its mortal population.

The system is small, but it is well connected to the trade routes of the [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Veritus]] [[Sub-sector]] and has a large population of [[Ecclesiarchy]] clergy. Imperial records describe its worlds as a centre of pilgrimage before the war, and as a target of [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]] after it began.
