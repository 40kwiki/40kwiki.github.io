T'au is the homeworld of the [[T'au Empire]], in the eastern fringe of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It is the seat of the [[Ethereal High Council]], which governs the empire under the [[Ethereal]]s. The Imperium refers to the world as the source of the T'au's expansion.

The planet is the centre of the empire's castes and of its religious and political life. Its cities are built to the T'au's own designs, and its Ethereal leaders are regarded by the empire's citizens as guides of the Greater Good.

Leadership

In the closing years of the 41st millennium the Ethereal Supreme, known as the Aun'o, presided over the High Council on T'au. Aun'Va, styled the Master of the Undying Spirit, held that office in those years and was regarded by the empire as its spiritual leader.Aun'Va's dates in office are estimated from Imperial intelligence reports of the late 41st millennium.
