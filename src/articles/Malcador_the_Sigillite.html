Malcador the Sigillite was the first First Lord of the Council of Terra, forerunner of the [[High Lords of Terra]], and the Emperor's trusted regent and counsellor. A [[Psyker|psyker]] of extraordinary power and an administrator of great ability, he governed [[Terra]] and the Imperium while the Emperor and his sons were abroad. At the climax of the [[Siege of Terra]] he took the Emperor's place on the [[Golden Throne]], and the Emperor named him Malcador the Hero. The [[Inquisition]] treats him as its founder.

Early life

His origins were known to no one but perhaps the Emperor. By the dawn of the 31st millennium he was over 6,700 years old, and could recall the moment of his birth. His title of Sigillite, "seal-bearer", also recalled the Sigillites, a secret order that had gathered the greatest relics of humanity's past during the Age of Strife, and of which he was probably the last survivor. Their fortress in the mountains of Terra was the foundation on which the Imperial Palace was later raised. The Khan claimed that Horus had learned his true name, Brahm al-Khadour, and that he was a Perpetual like the Emperor, but the claim is unproven.

Service to the Emperor

The Unification and the Great Crusade

See also: [[Ullanor Crusade]]

Malcador served at the Emperor's side from the early days of the Unification, as a counsellor and, when it was needed, a fighter, and was said to have urged Him to take the title Emperor of Mankind. His skills lay in administration, counsel and the covert management of an empire. He came to administer the Imperial Palace, and through it the conquered Terra. As the Great Crusade, which began in 798.M30, consumed the Emperor's time, he was left as Regent of Terra and oversaw the Imperial Tithe and the bureaucracy of the Administratum. He also acted as the Emperor's representative: it was Malcador who was sent to Monarchia on Khur, where Lorgar struck him across twenty metres before the Emperor Himself brought the Word Bearers to heel.

After the Triumph of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] in 000.M31 the Emperor named [[Horus]] Warmaster and returned to Terra. There He convened the first Council of Terra, which administered the Imperial Tithe and the civil government, and named Malcador its First Lord to lead it in His absence. The Council was the forerunner of the High Lords of Terra. In 001.M31 Malcador delivered the edict of the [[Council of Nikaea]] that created the Chaplains.

Founder of the Inquisition

See also: [[Inquisition]]

The Inquisition traces its origin to Malcador, who in the early years of the Heresy (c. 006–014.M31)Date estimated from the sequence of events. set out, at the Emperor's order, to find people of inquisitive nature to hunt the witch, the traitor, the mutant and the xenos. At [[Luna]] he met the Death Guard captain Nathaniel Garro, who had carried word of Horus's treachery to Terra, and gave him his personal seal, later the symbol of the Inquisition, with the task of finding seven more loyal Astartes. These warriors, the Knights-Errant, became the seed of the Grey Knights. Malcador also used his secret position as Grand Master of the Officio Assassinorum to attempt to have Horus killed.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

When [[Horus]] rebelled in 005.M31, the Emperor was already shut away in the vaults beneath the Palace, and only Malcador was given free access to Him. The Primarchs resented his authority as regent. He counselled [[Rogal Dorn]], who feared that he would fail in the defence of Terra, and helped him prepare the Palace, and he was close to [[Leman Russ]], whom he had tutored in the ways of the Imperium and tried to keep on Terra during the Heresy. He sent a party of Knights-Errant under Garviel Loken to try to kill Horus at Molech, which failed.

Magnus's warning had broken the wards of the Emperor's Webway project, and the Warp Gate beneath the Palace demanded all of His strength. The Emperor chose Malcador to replace Him on the Golden Throne. Before the Siege of Terra in 014.M31 Malcador brought twelve hooded recruits before the Emperor, eight Astartes and four mortals, the foundation of the Inquisition and the Grey Knights, and then ascended the Throne, whereupon the Emperor declared that he should be known as Malcador the Hero. The Emperor went to fight Horus on the [[Vengeful Spirit]]. When Dorn brought the dying Emperor back, Malcador was wasted by the strain; he died as the Techpriests removed him from the Throne, after giving the last of his strength to keep the Emperor alive to give His final orders.His body crumbled to ash when it was taken from the Throne.

Legacy

Malcador's death left the Imperium without a regent, and the office was not refilled. The Council of Terra he had led continued as the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Before the Siege he also hid the moon Titan in the Warp, where the Knights-Errant chosen as the first [[Grey Knights]] founded their Chapter.

The Emperor's own words, that he should always be Malcador the Hero, remain the Imperium's epitaph for him.

Character and abilities

Malcador wore the plain robes of an Administratum adept and looked a slender, elderly man. His mark was the staff topped by a flaming [[Aquila (symbol)|aquila]] that showed his office as First Lord and Regent.

His psychic gifts were extraordinary even among the Imperium's greatest [[Psyker|psykers]]. He was a telepath and telekinetic who could speak across interstellar distances, and a scholar with a deep knowledge of human history, which he had lived through. He was thought the second most powerful natural psyker in history after the Emperor, perhaps with Magnus the Red above him. His body was also tougher than its look: struck by Lorgar, he rose with only cracked bones.

Relations with the primarchs

Malcador's relations with the [[Primarch|primarchs]] were uneven. He was a companion and tutor to Leman Russ and worked with Dorn on the defence of Terra. Others resented him: the Primarchs felt insulted that they had no seats on the new Council of Terra, and during the Heresy they openly questioned his right to command them.

In a sealed account, Malcador told a dying friend that he and the Emperor had meant to set the Primarchs against one another, then said that this was a lie told to comfort her.
