Sotha was a world close to the edge of the galaxy in the [[Eastern Fringe]]. The [[Ultramarines]] Legion found it during the [[Great Crusade]] in 882.M30. It was notable for the [[Pharos]], a potent [[xenos]] artefact.

History

Sotha later became the [[Space Marine Chapter Homeworld|Chapter homeworld]] of the [[Scythes of the Emperor]], a Loyalist [[Chapter]] and successor of the [[Ultramarines]]. The Scythes and their allies, the [[Lamenters]], were nearly destroyed when the [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] invaded the Imperium in 992.M41. The hive fleet consumed the biosphere of Sotha, and the world became a lifeless rock, a new [[Dead world]].

Recovery

In the [[Era Indomitus]], [[Belisarius Cawl]] began to terraform Sotha as part of his work on the [[Pharos]] and its technology.The sources give only the start of this work. Its progress is not recorded.
