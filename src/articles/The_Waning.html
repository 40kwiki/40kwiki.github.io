The Waning is the name [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperial]] historians give to the long decline that followed the [[Age of Redemption]], from the [[38th millennium]] to about 744.M41. In those three thousand years the Imperium's armies, fleets and Chapters were worn down by the crusades of the previous age and by endless new wars, and star system after star system fell to [[Orks|Ork]] invasion, [[Chaos]] uprisings, xenos infestation or rebellion. The Waning ended with the prophecy that opened the [[Time of Ending]].

The age is not defined by a single event. It saw four [[Black Crusade]]s, the Redemption Crusades of the 39th millennium, the founding of the [[Calixis Sector]], the [[Gothic War]] (139–160.M41) and the conquests of Lord Solar [[Macharius]] (392–399.M41), as well as the first meetings between the Imperium and the [[T'au Empire]]. Its last centuries are the best recorded; much of the 40th millennium survives only in the archives of frontier sectors.

Origins

The Age of Redemption had been an age of crusading zeal, in which the Imperium sent its warships, regiments and Chapters against heretics and aliens across the galaxy. By the early 38th millennium those crusades had drawn so many forces from their posts that thousands of worlds were left undefended. Historians differ on where the Waning begins: the Annals of the Imperium close the Age of Redemption around 034.M38, others in 112.M38, the year the survivors of the [[Abyssal Crusade]] returned from the [[Eye of Terror]].

Character of the age

Through the Waning the [[Adeptus Terra]] answered disorder with harsher law and heavier punishment. In many lawless regions the [[Administratum]] handed whole systems to the direct rule of [[Space Marine Chapter]]s, since only the Astartes had the strength to hold them. Seers and astropaths spoke of great disturbances in the [[Warp]], like a swell before an unseen storm. Imperial historians agree that the Imperium entered the 41st millennium weaker than at any time since the [[Age of Apostasy]], with much of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] and the [[Ultima Segmentum]] thinly held.

Principal events

38th millennium

Main article: [[38th millennium]]

In 537.M38 [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] launched the Ninth Black Crusade against the naval fortress [[Cancephalus]] and the neighbouring world of Antecanis. The seventeen-year war that followed stripped both of their manpower, and Abaddon's sorcerers worked unopposed to weaken reality in the region. In 666.M38, in the event called the Grim Harvest, a fleet of space hulks came out of the Warp near [[Terra]]: some could be identified as ships of the Lost Crusade of 754.M37, the pilgrims of the Children's Crusade, now carrying mutants and daemons. The Inquisition hid the event.

39th millennium

Main article: [[39th millennium]]

In 001.M39 the Tenth Black Crusade struck the Helica Sector, while [[Perturabo]]'s [[Iron Warriors]] attacked the Iron Hands' world of [[Medusa]]. The Mausolean Cataclysm followed in 103.M39. In 131.M39 the Redemption Crusades began: a great hero arose in each segmentum, and their combined crusades pushed the Imperium's borders further than at any time in five hundred years. Some fifty years later all five vanished without trace, and the Ecclesiarch Inovian III declared them saints. In 301.M39 the Eleventh Black Crusade, the Doom of Relorria, ended in a war between the Black Legion and an Ork Waaagh!. The [[Angevin Crusade]] (322–384.M39) conquered the Calyx Expanse and founded the Calixis Sector in 384.M39.

40th millennium

Further information: [[40th millennium]]

The record of the 40th millennium is scattered. It includes the waking of [[Necrons]] of the Oltep Dynasty at Cocholos in 052.M40, the Long Night of Verronus in 249.M40, the rebellion of [[Krieg]] in 433.M40 and its Atonement, and the first battle between Imperial ships and Necron warships, in the Yuctan System in 666.M40. The future Chapter Master of the [[Blood Angels]], [[Commander Dante|Dante]], was born in 447.M40.

41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

The Waning's last centuries saw some of the Imperium's largest wars. The Twelfth Black Crusade, the [[Gothic War]] (139–160.M41), cost the Imperium four of the six Blackstone Fortresses. The [[Macharian Crusade]] (392–399.M41) took a thousand worlds, which collapsed into the Macharian Heresy after the death of Macharius. In 444.M41 the daemon primarch [[Angron]] led the First War for [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]]. In 500.M41 visions of the Emperor weeping, the Tears of the Emperor, were reported on worlds across the Imperium. The [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] against the T'au began in 742.M41.

End

Imperial historians close the Waning in about 744.M41, when the seer [[Taggarath]] of Corrinto prophesied the coming of the end times. The arrival of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] in 745.M41 and the waking of the Necrons soon after seemed to confirm the prophecy, and the age that followed is called the [[Time of Ending]].
