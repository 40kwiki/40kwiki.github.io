Legio Tempestus is a [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is one of the [[Titan Legions]] of the Martian Triad, founded with the [[Legio Ignatum]] and the [[Legio Mortis]] in the Great Crusade. During the [[Schism of Mars]] it fought for the loyalist side against the [[Traitor|traitor]] Legio Mortis.

The Legio's history is closely tied to the forges of Mars. Its Titans were built in the Martian forge-cities, and its service is recorded in the Mechanicus's own registers.

Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

During the Schism, the Martian forge-cities fought one another. The loyalist Legio Ignatum and Legio Tempestus fought the [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Legio Mortis]] across the [[Tharsis Montes|Tharsis plains]]. A force of the [[Imperial Fists]] under [[Sigismund]] also landed to recover what it could from the forges.

The fighting set forge against forge and [[Titan Legions|Titan]] Legion against Titan Legion. The traitors used [[Exterminatus|virus bombs]] on [[Koriel Zeth]]'s forge-city and killed fourteen million people in minutes. Its course is described in the article on the [[Schism of Mars]].
