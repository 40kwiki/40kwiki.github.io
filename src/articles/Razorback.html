The Razorback is an armoured personnel carrier used mainly by the Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and also by the [[Inquisition]] and the [[Adeptus Arbites]]. It is a variant of the [[Rhino]] chassis that gives up part of the troop space in order to carry a remote-controlled weapon on the roof, usually twin-linked heavy bolters or lascannons.

Unlike the Rhino, which has no more than a pintle-mounted storm bolter, the Razorback can stay on the field after landing its cargo and serve as heavy fire support. The range of turret weapons lets it be used against targets from light infantry to heavy armour.

History

The design was recovered from an [[Standard Template Construct]] database found in the 36th millennium in the Southern Rim by Chief Artisan Tilvius. A second template was found at the same time, but its schematics were never put into production. After nearly two hundred Terran years of field trials, Tilvius gave the full template to [[Mars]], with records showing that the Razorback had carried Human troops in the [[Age of Technology]].The sources give only the 36th millennium. The field trials ran for nearly two centuries after the recovery, so the approval falls in the later part of M36 or the early part of M37. The date is inferred.

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] judged the omens good and approved the template, and production began on the [[Forge world]]s. Imperial scholars had long suspected that a troop-carrying form of the [[Predator]] existed. The Razorback fits that description, and its extra firepower filled the place left when the Predator became a pure battle tank.

Design and use

The turret is controlled from inside through the same logic-engine as the [[Land Raider]], which cuts the crew to one, though a second crew member can help when the driver is busy. Older patterns have a manned weapon. The Razorback carries six battle-brothers.

Standard doctrine places Razorbacks as fire support alongside Rhinos or infantry in an assault, since even a single Razorback escort sharply improves a strike. Some Chapters use them as mobile command posts or as the screen for heavy reconnaissance with bikes and attack bikes. Some Chapter Masters say that it fails both as a transport, since it cannot carry a full squad, and as a tank, since it lacks armour and heavy weapons.

Armament and patterns

Because the Razorback is a newer design than the Rhino and Predator, it has less sanctity and fewer taboos against change. It has the largest range of weapon options of any Space Marine vehicle, and many Chapters add unapproved weapons that then spread to others. Alongside twin heavy bolters and twin lascannons, it can mount twin heavy flamers, twin multi-meltas or a single multi-melta. The [[Blood Angels]] fit twin assault cannons or a flamestorm cannon, and the [[Grey Knights]] and [[Ordo Malleus]] use a pattern with twin [[Psycannon]]s.

The Infernum Pattern, a multi-melta variant, is used by some Chapters of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. The [[Fire Hawks]] used it in the [[Badab War]]. The Vortimer Pattern was made by the Grey Knights' Master Armourer Regis Vortimer for the Deliverance of Vulgate, and each deployment needs the sanction of the Chapter Council. The oldest pattern, the Mark 1 Stronos, carried twin plasma cannons and a lascannon. Production stopped for reasons that are unknown, and the records on it are sealed.
