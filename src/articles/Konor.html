Konor is an [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperial]] [[Forge world]] and former research station of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is the second world of the [[Konor System]], in the heart of [[Ultramar]] and the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It is ruled by the [[Ultramarines]] [[Chapter]]. Its moon, [[Gantz]], is also a powerful forge world, and at various times in the politics of the Mechanicus it has outranked Konor.

History

In the age of the [[Great Crusade]] Konor was one of the founding worlds of the [[Realm of Ultramar]]. It hosted a large population of [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] adepts, and its pre-Compliance orbital yards, with those of [[Calth]], turned out cruisers and strike craft for the [[Ultramarines]] Legion. Its cantons eventually covered about three quarters of the world, and its output far exceeded that of [[Gantz]], which held the title of forge world by ancient treaty with [[Mars]]. In the waning years of M30 the magi of Konor repeatedly petitioned the Synods of Mars for a charter as a full forge world. [[Kelbor-Hal]] denied each petition, citing the world's ties to the rulers of Ultramar. Konor was home to one of the four Tetrarchs, Eikos Lamiad, the Iron Warden, and to a garrison of the Ultramarines. Imperial accounts hold that this refusal bred resentment, and that during the [[Battle of Calth]] some of the magi of Konor wished to side with the Warmaster, but the world held to the Ultramarines and sent forces to Calth. Only after the Heresy, in the early years of the [[Great Scouring]], was Konor given the formal status of a forge world.

During the [[Great Crusade]], Konor was one of the four ruling worlds of the [[Tetrarchy]] that governed Ultramar. It lost that status in the millennia after the [[Horus Heresy]], but regained it in the [[Era Indomitus]] after the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The Ultramarines' First Captain, [[Severus Agemman]], was made Tetrarch of Konor and was tasked with expanding Ultramar northward.

Konor supplied munitions and war machines to the Ultramarines and to the [[Astra Militarum]] regiments raised in Ultramar. During the [[Plague Wars]] the world was attacked by the forces of Chaos in service to [[Nurgle]], but Imperial forces rallied to take it back.

In c. 012.M42, after the end of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and Guilliman's return to Ultramar with reinforcements, the resurrected primarch announced that he had erred ten thousand years before in letting Ultramar shrink to a handful of systems. He declared that the Five Hundred Worlds would be reborn and re-established the Tetrarchy, restoring Konor as one of the four primary worlds.
