A hive fleet is a large armada of living, biomechanical starships created by the [[Tyranids]] to gather the raw material and biomass that their species needs to reproduce, evolve and expand. It strips a star system of life using the full range of Tyranid bioforms, and every world it consumes adds to its growth. Only three primary hive fleets and a host of smaller splinter fleets have made direct contact with the [[Imperium of Man]], but all have brought devastating damage and loss of life.

The Imperium's Segmentum Ultima Command designated the first three as [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]], [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], and each made first contact with Imperial space on the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy. Behemoth arrived in 745.M41, Kraken in 993.M41 and Leviathan in 997.M41. Each fleet is governed ultimately by the collective psychic consciousness of the [[Hive Mind]], though at the heart of any fleet are the [[Norn-Queen]]s, who interpret its will to other organisms and birth almost every Tyranid bioform in the fleet, save for its [[Hive Ship]]s.

The fleet's psychic bond with the Hive Mind produces the [[Shadow in the Warp]], which prevents travel or communication through the [[Immaterium]] in its vicinity. This is among the most dangerous of its effects, for Imperial worlds depend on [[Astropath]]s and Navigators to call for help. By the time the Shadow falls, a world is already alone.

Imperial scholars regard the fleets as an existential threat. The Hive Mind has fed on countless worlds across many galaxies, and when a fleet strikes it does not do so for territory or vengeance but to harvest biomass. Every action of every creature is bent to a single goal: the swift and total absorption of a world's population, ecosystems and resources. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 further fleets have been reported approaching the galaxy, and the fight against Hive Fleet Leviathan continues in the [[Fourth Tyrannic War]].

Structure

The Magi Biologis categorise each hive fleet as a separate force that competes with the others for resources. Each is self-sufficient and appears to follow its own strategy and breed its own bioforms to overcome its prey. The truth is more complex, for every fleet is but a splinter of one greater assemblage. Every creature is a single cell in the body of a super-organism, linked into one consciousness that stretches across hundreds of light-years. The ships themselves are living creatures, each home to countless lesser organisms grown in its reproductive chambers, and each exists only as part of the entity that is the fleet.

Each fleet is built around a command vessel that serves as a synaptic link for the other bio-ships and for the Hive Mind. If it is destroyed and no other synaptic creature is near to take its place, the fleet's subordinate ships turn feral and disorganised and become easy prey. While the command ships survive, their subordinates remain under perfect control. Such ships are powerful psychic entities and some emit a psychic scream, a shockwave that can break the minds of an enemy crew, and hive ships emit a weaker one on dying.

Void-warfare bioforms have evolved weapon biomorphs comparable in effect to Imperial naval armament. Pyro-acid cannons fire balls of acid like macrocannon, acid spray cannons sheet a short cone of burning acid over a target, and bio-plasma cannons loose a stream of superheated plasma that passes through void shields by some unknown means. Larger ships carry spore shields, a cloud of spores that acts as a living secondary armour and eats the hull of an enemy that closes. Tentacles and devourer claws grapple a target for boarding by lictors or for consuming it outright. Capital-class bio-ships carry crew hatcheries and regrow lost crews almost at once, and fleets build hatchery structures in the void to repair themselves.

History

First contact

The first recorded contact with the Tyranids came in early 745.M41 on the eastern fringe, at the ocean world of [[Tyran Primus]], after which the Imperium named the species. For decades the fleets had fed on worlds without intelligent life, replenishing the biomass spent on their long crossing of the intergalactic void. The Adeptus Mechanicus Explorator base on Tyran Primus was overwhelmed, and its commander, [[Varnak|Magos Varnak]], hid a data-codex 3,000 metres below the surface before destroying the outpost in a plasma reactor explosion. This was the beginning of the [[First Tyrannic War]].

A year later the Inquisitor [[Fidus Kryptman]] of the [[Ordo Xenos]] reached the silent world and found it stripped of every plant and drop of water, its cruiser fleet reduced to acid-eaten hulks. He recovered Varnak's codex, whose images of scythe-limbed aliens and of living voidships so numerous that they veiled the stars made him understand what he had found. He set out to warn the galaxy, and in time the Inquisitors [[Bronislaw Czevak]], Agmar and Solomon Lok would also come to recognise the threat.

The principal fleets

[[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] was the first major fleet encountered and defeated. The war it started was the First Tyrannic War, and the fleet was shattered as a coherent force in 745.M41 by the [[Ultramarines]], the [[White Scars]] and the battlefleets of the Imperial Navy at [[Macragge]], though splinters of it ravaged the galaxy for centuries. [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] began the Second Tyrannic War in 992.M41 and was broken only when the Ultramarines won at [[Ichar IV]] and the Aeldari of [[Craftworld]] [[Iyanden]] held their home at great cost. [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], the largest, entered the galaxy from beneath the galactic plane in 997.M41. Kryptman's Kryptman Census, which burned out dozens of astropaths, charted its course, and the left half of the fleet was crushed at [[Tarsis Ultra]] by the Ultramarines, the Mortifactors, Imperial Guard and a [[Deathwatch]] kill-team under Kryptman. This was the Third Tyrannic War.

Era Indomitus

The Fourth Tyrannic War began in the Era Indomitus with Leviathan's coordinated assault from above and below the galactic plane by tendrils the Imperium named Nautilon, Promethor and Grendyllus, aimed at [[Terra]]. A tendril was defeated at [[Baal]] by the [[Blood Angels]], the Sanguinary Brotherhood and Guilliman's Indomitus Crusade, helped by the Khornate daemons of the Bloodthirster [[Ka'Bandha]], and the main mass struck the Western Reaches of the Segmentum Pacificus.

Other fleets have emerged from the void since the Great Rift opened. [[Hive Fleet Hydra]] was found dormant in 999.M41 by the Drukhari of the Poisoned Fang Kabal at the extremity of the eastern spiral arm. Garmr and Ladon drifted towards the Vultis Sector and devoured death worlds, and a splinter of Garmr attacked the Caligari Sector in the Segmentum Tempestus. The Imperium also counts Moloch, advancing from the north through the Ghoul Stars, and the twin fleets [[Hive Fleet Scylla|Scylla]] and [[Hive Fleet Charybdis|Charybdis]], which carve parallel paths up through the Segmentum Pacificus and Segmentum Solar, closest of all to Terra.

Notable hive fleets

The older fleets are named in Imperial records of the 36th and 38th millennia, among them Ouroboris, recorded by Cardinal Miriamulus of Thracian Primaris in the Helican Sector, and the nomad stone-ships of Colossus. [[Hive Fleet Naga]] invaded from the Eastern Fringe in 801.M41, devoured several Asuryani worlds and was destroyed in 812.M41 by the craftworlds of Idharae, Iyanden and Malan'tai. The Aeldari called it Shai'naid, the Endlessly Winding Serpent. [[Hive Fleet Dagon]], believed a splinter of Behemoth, first struck the Jericho Reach in 812.M41. [[Hive Fleet Gorgon]], a splinter of Behemoth, invaded the space of the [[T'au Empire]] in 899.M41 and was destroyed in 903.M41 by the Fire Caste and the Imperial Guard, leaving a region now called the Zone of Silence. Scarabus was annihilated by the 9th Cadian regiment at Fortress Carcasson in 937.M41, Locust stripped Ulmore in 945.M41 before the Ultramarines drove it back, and Perseus fought the Ultramarines in 976.M41 and vanished in 982.M41. Jormungandr entered the north-east of the Ultima Segmentum in 993.M41, and Medusa and Ophidia were both met in 998.M41.

Hive Fleet Lotan is known from the Doom of Hesp, in which the Death Guard fought it in the jungles of Hesp and the contest of tyranid toxins and plague sorcery reduced the world to a bubbling slurry.

Splinter fleets

When Kraken's assault on Ichar IV failed, its scattered remnants fled towards the galactic core and drove within the Imperial defensive perimeters. These splinter fleets have become an even greater threat as they feed on ill-defended worlds far from the main war zones. A splinter can comprise as few as a dozen hive ships, but even that is enough to overrun a world, and some have grown so large that the Imperium treats them as fleets in their own right, as with Magalodon. Every battle teaches the Hive Mind more about the galaxy's defences, and some Imperial scholars believe that gaining this information was the true aim of Kraken's invasion.
