Hive Fleet
Swarm fleet of the [[Tyranids]]
Species[[Tyranids]]

WarsThe [[First Tyrannic War]] to the [[Fourth Tyrannic War]]

A Hive Fleet is a swarm of the [[Tyranids]], a vast body of bio-ships and organisms that moves through [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] consuming worlds. Each fleet is directed by a hive mind, and each consumed world adds biomass to the fleet's growth. The Imperium's records list the major fleets by name and track their courses where they are known.

Hive fleets are the main form of the Tyranid threat. Their size is recorded in Imperial estimates as beyond count, and a fleet can divide into splinter fleets that spread across several regions at once.

Structure

A hive fleet is organised around a central [[Tyranids|hive mind]], with bio-ships and swarm creatures that carry out its orders. The fleets' organisms are adapted to a wide range of worlds, and each fleet is recorded as having its own character in battle. Imperial records describe the fleets as sharing a common command in the hive mind, which the Tyranids' tendrils extend across the galaxy.

Principal fleets

The principal fleets recorded by the Imperium include [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]], [[Hive Fleet Kraken]], [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]], [[Hive Fleet Gorgon]] and [[Hive Fleet Hydra]]. Gorgon is a splinter of Behemoth. Each has been fought in a series of wars, from the First Tyrannic War at [[Macragge]] in 745.M41 onwards.
