The Master of the Astronomican is the head of the [[Adeptus Astronomica]], the order of adepts and [[Choir Astronomica|choristers]] that tends the [[Astronomican]], and holds one of the permanent seats among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Because every [[Navigator]] and every ship of the [[Imperial Navy]] depends on the beacon, the Master has a quiet power out of proportion to the size of the adepta.

The earliest recorded Master, [[Cohran Hursula]], sat on the [[Council of Terra]] during the [[Horus Heresy]]. In 544.M32 the office was held by [[Volquan Sark]]. At the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 the Master was [[Leops Franck]], who joined a cabal with the Lord Inquisitor [[Adamara Rassilo]] and the High Lord Kania Dhanda to bring a [[Drukhari]] haemonculus to Terra in the hope of mending the failing [[Golden Throne]]. In the early [[42nd millennium]] the seat is held by [[Lucius Throde]], named among the High Lords whom [[Roboute Guilliman]] restored.

Duties

The Master directs the adepts who care for the machinery of the Astronomican, the soul-binding rites that feed it and the Choir Astronomica that guards it. The Master answers for the beacon but does not control its source, which is the Emperor on the Golden Throne. Where the beacon wavers, as it has since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, the Master is the High Lord who must explain the failure to the council.

History

Heresy and reconstruction (005–014.M31)

Hursula sat on the Council of Terra throughout the siege of the Palace, and with the other mortal officials he took part in the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31.

War of the Beast (544–546.M32)

Sark held the seat when the first full roll of the High Lords was recorded at the outset of the [[War of the Beast]]. His part in the war is not described in the records consulted.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 onward)

Franck was Master at the time of the Thirteenth Black Crusade. The cabal that he joined failed when the haemonculus escaped and loosed havoc on Terra. When Guilliman rebuilt the council, the seat passed to Throde. Imperial records do not give the manner of Franck's leaving the office.
