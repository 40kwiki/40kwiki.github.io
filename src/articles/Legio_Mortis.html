The Legio Mortis, the Death's Heads, is a [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Titan Legions|Titan Legion]]. It was raised on [[Mars]] in the [[Age of Strife]] as one of the three oldest Legios, served as the personal guard of the [[Fabricator-General]], and was assigned to [[Horus]] in the [[Great Crusade]]. When Horus rebelled in 005.M31 it followed him, the first Titan Legion to do so. It fought in nearly every great battle of the [[Horus Heresy]] and, at the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31), destroyed its old rival the Legio Ignatum and breached the walls of the [[Imperial Palace]].

After the Heresy the Legio fled to the [[Eye of Terror]]. Its god-engines, changed by the Warp into daemonic shapes, have returned to Imperial space many times since, most recently in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] of 999.M41 and the wars of the [[Era Indomitus]].

History

Origins

According to the Martian lexmechanics, the Legio Mortis was one of the Triad Ferrum Morgulus, the three [[Titan Legions]] raised by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Cult Mechanicus]] to retake [[Mars]] from the Cy-Carnivora and the [[Mutant|mutant]] hordes of the [[Age of Strife]]. Its partners were the [[Legio Tempestus]] and the Legio Ignatum. Its seal names its two duties: to stand guard at the gates of the [[Fabricator-General]]'s domain, and to destroy whatever threatened the cult. It kept both. For centuries the Fabricators-General used it to punish rebellious magi and even other Titan Legions, and it was one of the few Legios to have wiped out another entirely.

Its princeps wore onyx skull-masks and black robes, kept exact tallies of their kills, and filled their command decks with [[Servitor|servitor]] choirs that chanted the names of the dead. It is not recorded whether the name "Death's Heads" came from this custom, from the skull of its heraldry, or from the bones it hung on its engines.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The Imperium's commanders distrusted a Legio whose loyalty seemed to lie with Mars alone. Partly to bind it to the Imperium, most of its active strength was assigned to the 63rd Expeditionary Fleet of [[Horus]] and his [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]]. It fought in more than a hundred campaigns at his side, among them the [[Ullanor Crusade]] (c. 999.M30 – 000.M31). The bond meant to tie it to the Emperor tied it instead to Horus.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

At [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31, the Legio Mortis was given the task of finishing the loyalist [[Space Marine]]s who survived the virus-bombing. Led by the Imperator Titan Dies Irae, it became the first Titan Legion to fight the [[Adeptus Astartes]] in open battle. On Mars it sided with [[Kelbor-Hal]] in the [[Schism of Mars]] (005–006.M31); its contingents there attacked [[Tharsis Montes|Magma City]] and were destroyed by the Legio Tempestus, though the Imperator [[Aquila (symbol)|Aquila]] Ignis killed the last Tempestus engines before it fell.

The Legio then fought at the [[Drop Site Massacre|Drop Site Massacre]] (c. 006.M31), at [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]], at [[Battle of Tallarn|Tallarn]] and at [[Ryza]], which it invaded with two other [[Traitor|traitor]] Legios in 013.M31. In the [[Battle of Beta-Garmon]] (007–013.M31) it led Horus's final attack on Beta-Garmon III.

The Siege of Terra

Main article: [[Siege of Terra]]

By the [[Siege of Terra]] the Legio was deeply corrupted. Its engines regenerated their damage and some moved without a princeps. Led again by Dies Irae, it marched on the Mercury-Exultant killzone of the Palace in 014.M31 and there destroyed the Legio Ignatum, its enemy since the Age of Strife. It was given the honour of breaching the outer walls and did so at the cost of more than thirty Titans in a single night.

When Horus fell in 014.M31 the Legio's survivors were hunted from system to system until they reached the Eye of Terror.

After the Heresy

Engines of the Legio Mortis have appeared in Imperial space throughout the following ten thousand years. They fought in the [[Wars for Armageddon|First War for Armageddon]] (444.M41), and Dies Irae was finally destroyed at the siege of [[Hydra Cordatus]] in the late [[41st millennium]]. The Legio marched in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] (999.M41), on [[Vigilus]] (c. 999.M41 – 000.M42) and in the [[Nachmund Gauntlet|Nachmund war]] of the early [[42nd millennium]].
