A Hive Ship is an enormous biomechanical [[bioship]] that forms the heart of every [[Tyranid]] [[hive fleet]]. The Hive Ship acts as a telepathic synapse node for the bioships around it, and it is also a vast bio-factory. From it come the endless swarms that characterise the Tyranid forces. Although their size seems impossible, Hive Ships are living creatures made from millions of bioengineered organisms.

Each Hive Ship is a biological manufactory. It can generate, gestate and deploy millions of Tyranid organisms. It can replicate known genomes and splice new ones, so that the fleet can produce creatures suited to each world it meets.

Combat

Beyond their support role, Hive Ships are formidable in battle. Their morphology varies widely, but most have stone-like armour plating and many weapon growths. They are protected by thick clouds of defensive spores. Their armaments include pyro-acid batteries, bio-plasma spines, launch bays and gigantic claws. They can match the largest [[battleship]]s of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and of the [[xenos]].

Rumours

Some hold that most fleets carry several Hive Ships, yet only one keeps central control of a swarm assaulting a single system. This cannot be confirmed. There are also unsubstantiated reports of indescribably huge Hive Ships that arrive in the last stages of a world's consumption. They would explain how a fleet can strip a planet's whole biosphere, atmosphere and hydrosphere.

Command and debate

Within a hive fleet there is believed to be a single mature Hive Ship that coordinates the [[Hive Mind]] across the fleet, much as [[synapse creature]]s coordinate a swarm on the surface of a world. Analysts of the [[Ordo Xenos]] generally accept this view. The escort vessels around it may be immature forms of the same bioform. The [[Tech-priest]]s of the Divisionis Biologis of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], the [[Genetor|genetors]], debate this, because the differences between Hive Ships make genetic sampling difficult.

In three recorded battles where a particular Hive Ship was destroyed, parts of the Tyranid fleet broke away and fled, rather than fall into the uncoordinated assault that usually follows the loss of the Hive Mind. Some Mechanicus theorists suggest that the death of a synapse node may hasten the maturation of other nodes in the fleet. The result could be smaller splinter fleets that attack more vulnerable targets.

Tens of thousands of [[Tyranid Warrior]]s are carried aboard a Hive Ship, the cocooned officers of nightmare hordes yet to be born.
