The XI Legion, the Eleventh Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]], is the second of two Legions of the [[Great Crusade]] that no surviving Imperial record names in full. It was raised in the [[First Founding]] of about 700.M30, like the other eighteen Legions known to history, and its primarch was found late in the Crusade, in 927.M30. Within a few years the Legion had been removed from the archives along with its commander. Unlike the [[II Legion]], the XI Legion has become the subject of rumours that reach beyond the Imperium, since its number is used by [[Chaos cults]] for their own purposes.

[[The Imperium]] calls this removal the Excision. The Legion's name, number and primarch were struck from the [[Administratum]] rolls and the Investiary, and the record of its service was sealed. The [[Imperial Fists]]' archive preserves a remark by [[Rogal Dorn]] that the second and eleventh plinths of the Investiary were empty for a long time and that nobody spoke of the two absent brothers. Beyond that the record is silent.

The reasons for the Excision are sealed by edict and it is an offence to enquire into them. This article draws only on what may be stated openly and on rumours that have been recorded in Imperial archives. Rumours are marked as such.

History

Raising and the early Crusade

The XI Legion was raised on [[Terra]] during the First Founding and went to war under a Legion Master, as the other Legions did before their primarchs were found. The Administratum's muster of the early Crusade lists its strength in the same column as the other Legions, and gives it no unusual designation. Its recruits came from the hardest worlds within reach of the Emperor's armies.

No surviving Apothecarion record describes the XI Legion's gene-seed as flawed. The seed was taken from the primarch when he was found, and the Emperor's geneticists are recorded as having approved it. The reasons for the Legion's removal are therefore not, on the available evidence, medical, although a minority of scholars continue to suspect a genetic fault.

The finding of the primarch

The primarch of the XI Legion was found in 927.M30, one of the last recoveries of the Great Crusade, before that of [[Alpharius Omegon]] in 981.M30.The Legion's name was already sealed by then, according to a later annotation on the entry, although the date of that annotation is unknown. The world on which the primarch was found is not recorded.

Because the finding and the expungement fall so close together, historians have asked whether the primarch was ever brought into the Legion at all. The Imperium's answer is that he was found, and that the record of what happened next was taken away.

Service before the Excision

No surviving campaign history records the XI Legion's service before its removal. Its number appears in a few Remembrancer indexes of the Crusade, but the entries are too brief to say where or when the Legion fought.

The Excision

The Excision is estimated to have taken place between c. 930 and 950.M30. The start date follows from the finding of the primarch, and the end date is the last year in which any record mentions the number XI in a non-expunged context. Both dates are estimates, and it is possible that the Excision was a single act or a long process.

Imperial accounts describe the removal in the same terms as the removal of the II Legion. The Legion's name and number were stricken, its officers' records were sealed, its battle honours were redistributed, and its primarch's statue was removed from the Investiary. The Ultramarines' archive is cited by some researchers as the only Legion source that notes a rise in recruitment during these years, although the Ultramarines' own record is not clear on the point.

Rumours

The XI Legion's fate is the subject of more rumour than any other Legion's, and some of that rumour has a religious character. The following are recorded here as rumour and have not been confirmed.

  • Absorption. The belief that the XI Legion's warriors were drafted into the [[Ultramarines]] is the most widespread. It is not supported by any Ultramarines record, and the Ultramarines' own accounts of the period say only that their numbers grew. Word Bearers captives in later campaigns have repeated the claim, and scholars read it as a warning rather than a fact.
  • Lorgar's Eleventh. Chaos cults among the [[Word Bearers]] have at times claimed the Eleventh as a lost brotherhood of [[Lorgar]], and some of their scriptures describe an Eleventh Legion that was taken from the Emperor. The Imperium treats these claims as heresy and has no means of checking them. No Word Bearer has been shown to be of the XI Legion, and the Word Bearers' own records do not name it.
  • A Lord of the Lost. A speculative note in Ordo Hereticus files, of uncertain date, suggests that one of the two lost primarchs is the figure the Chaos cults call the Lord of the Lost. The title is attested elsewhere in heretical texts, but nothing links it to a named primarch with confidence.
  • Survival. Some inquisitors have written that the XI Legion was not destroyed but hidden, and that its warriors were placed among the loyal Legions. The claim has been applied to several Chapters and has never been proven.
  • The Legion's place in the Heresy. Some Traitor Legion records describe an "Eleventh" present at the outbreak of the Heresy. Such records are fragmentary and are generally treated as propaganda, since the Traitor Legions had every reason to claim numbers that the Imperium had lost.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The XI Legion does not appear in any account of the Heresy. It is absent from the nine Legions that declared for [[Horus]] at [[Isstvan III]] and from the loyal Legions that survived the [[Drop Site Massacre]]. The [[Siege of Terra]] was defended and attacked by Legions whose numbers are all accounted for, and the Imperial Fists' account of the defence mentions the two empty plinths without naming a Legion for either.

Organisation

The internal organisation of the XI Legion is unknown. It may have followed the notional pattern of Chapters and companies set out by the Imperial staff, but no record confirms this.

Heraldry

No badge or livery of the XI Legion survives in an Imperial archive. The Adeptus Astartes emblem is used here only because a Legion infobox requires an image.
