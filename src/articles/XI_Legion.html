The XI Legion, the Eleventh Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]], is one of two Legions of the [[Great Crusade]] that no surviving Imperial record names in full. It was raised in the [[First Founding]] of about 700.M30, like the other Legions of the original eighteen and two, and its primarch was found late in the Crusade, after Corvus Corax (922.M30) and before Alpharius Omegon (981.M30); the year is not recorded. Both the Legion and its commander were later removed from every record by an edict of obliteration, passed before the [[Horus Heresy]].

The [[Imperium]] calls such a removal a damnatio memoriae. The Legion's name, number and primarch were struck from the archives, and the record of its service was sealed. The [[Imperial Fists]]' archive preserves a remark by [[Rogal Dorn]], who noticed in the Imperial Palace that the second and eleventh plinths of the Investiary had stood empty for a long time, and that nobody ever spoke of the two absent brothers. Beyond that the record is silent.

The reasons for the erasure are sealed and it is an offence to enquire into them. This article draws only on what may be stated openly and on rumours that have been recorded in Imperial archives. Rumours are marked as such.

History

Raising and the early Crusade

The XI Legion was raised on [[Terra]] during the First Founding. Almost nothing of its service survives. A stray record, found by chance in a printing of the Astra Militarum's Imperial Infantryman's Uplifting Primer, places both the II and the XI Legions in the Rangdan Xenocides of the 860s.M30, though the scope of their part is not recorded.

The finding of the primarch

The chronology of the Crusade places the finding of the XI primarch, one of the last recoveries of the Great Crusade, after that of Corvus Corax in 922.M30 and before that of [[Alpharius Omegon]] in 981.M30. No year is given. The world on which the primarch was found is not recorded.

The erasure

No date is given for the erasure. It is known that it came before the Heresy, and by a wide margin: when the Emperor rebuked [[Lorgar]] and the [[Word Bearers]] at Monarchia on Khur, only eighteen Legions were named, at least forty-three years before the [[Drop Site Massacre]]. Lorgar, speaking privately to [[Magnus the Red]], feared being "cast alongside the brothers we no longer speak of". At the [[Ullanor Crusade|Triumph of Ullanor]] Magnus spoke of "nine sons of the Emperor" gathered on one world, and [[Mortarion]] reminded him that the Emperor had forbidden them to speak of the last such gathering. Later, Kasper Hawser of the [[Space Wolves]] wrote that the Emperor's Legions numbered eighteen when [[Prospero]] burned.

The only earlier account suggests that the primarchs' absence was not yet known in the 840s.M30: in that decade the Librarian Israfael of Caliban told an initiate that [[Lion El'Jonson]] had nineteen brothers. Later in the Crusade, [[Sanguinius]] told [[Horus]] that he feared the erasure of the Blood Angels, and the "third empty plinth" that would be their only memorial.

Rumours

The fate of the XI Legion is the subject of rumour, which is recorded here and has not been confirmed.

  • Absorption. It is said that the Ultramarines swelled in number until they outnumbered every other Legion at about the time the II and XI Legions were forgotten, and that the missing Legions were inducted into them. The Ultramarines' own records do not confirm this. Warriors of the Word Bearers, in a recorded conversation, spoke of the rumour as something that everyone knew and no one could prove.
  • A flaw in the blood. Sanguinius, afraid that the Flaw in his own Legion's gene-seed would bring about its erasure, implied that one of the missing Legions might have been purged for a genetic fault. No record states the cause, and the suggestion has never been confirmed.
  • An earlier censure. A remark of Leman Russ before the Burning of Prospero, that Astartes had fought Astartes before, may refer to the destruction of the II and XI Legions, or to the Night of the Wolf, when the Space Wolves were sent to bring the World Eaters to heel.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The XI Legion does not appear in any account of the Heresy. It is absent from the four Legions that turned on their loyal brothers at [[Isstvan III]] and from the loyal Legions that survived the [[Drop Site Massacre]], and the Legions that defended and attacked Terra in the [[Siege of Terra]] are all accounted for.

Organisation

The internal organisation of the XI Legion is unknown. It may have followed the notional pattern of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and companies set out by the Imperial staff, but no record confirms this.

Heraldry

No badge or livery of the XI Legion survives in an Imperial archive. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] emblem is used here only because a Legion infobox requires an image.
