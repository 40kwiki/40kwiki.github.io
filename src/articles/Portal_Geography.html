The [[Imperium of Mankind]] spans the [[Milky Way]], a galaxy of billions of stars divided into [[Segmentum|segmenta]], [[Sector|sectors]] and [[Sub-sector|subsectors]]. Its geography is charted by Imperial [[Navis Nobilite|navigators]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], and it changes with every war and crusade. This portal gathers the articles on its regions, its worlds and its great divisions.

Selected article

[[Milky Way]] is the galaxy in which the [[Imperium of Mankind]] lies, as charted by Imperial science. Its stars are grouped into the great divisions of the Imperium, from the [[Segmentum Solar]] around [[Terra]] to the frontier of the [[Eastern Fringe]]. Much of it remains uncharted, and the [[Great Rift]] has cut many regions off from the rest.
