Cancephalus is a [[naval fortress]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. In 537.M38 [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] launched the [[Ninth Black Crusade]] against it and its neighbouring world of [[Antecanis]]. The fortress was drawn into a war that lasted seventeen years and stripped both sides of their manpower.

Antecanis, the monarchhive of the system, was destroyed in the first assault. Cancephalus held out for the rest of the war, and its garrison is recorded as one of the most stubborn in the Imperium's records of the period.

The Ninth Black Crusade

The Ninth [[Black Crusade]] was one of the [[Chaos]] incursions of the late 36th and 38th millennia. [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s forces struck the naval fortress with the strength of the [[Traitor Legions]]. The siege of Cancephalus was the longest engagement of the crusade, and Abaddon's forces were worn down by it as much as the defenders.The dates of the seventeen-year war are recorded from Imperial registers. Its close is estimated from the withdrawal of Chaos fleets.
