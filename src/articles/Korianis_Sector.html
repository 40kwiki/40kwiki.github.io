The Korianis Sector was an Imperial sector in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It contained Sub-sector Aurelia, the most important recruiting region of the [[Blood Ravens]] [[Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It was also the site of the lost Chapter world of [[Aurelia]].

History

In the 41st millennium, before the [[Era Indomitus]] began, the Korianis Sector was the scene of the [[Aurelian Crusades]]. The [[Blood Angels]] and their Imperial allies fought there against the [[Tyranids]], the [[Orks]] and the forces of Chaos.
