Agripinaa is an Imperial [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[forge world]] in the Agripinaa System of the [[Agripinaa Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It is known as the "Orb of a Million Scars" for its surface, which is covered almost entirely by industrial works, pipelines, factories and refineries.

Like the other worlds of its sector, Agripinaa provides materials for the [[Cadian front]]. Its industrial heartland is almost wholly engaged in producing war matériel. It is the chief supplier of [[Cadia]] and of the Cadian Gate.

Role in the war

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 cut Mars off from many of its daughter worlds. Agripinaa remained on the Imperial side of the Rift and became a front-line forge world. Its production was sent to the Cadian front until the fall of Cadia.The forge's output after 999.M41 is recorded only in general terms.
