The Pale Wasting was an obscure conflict fought by the Imperium against a mysterious and probably [[Xenos|xenos]] threat from the [[Ghoul Stars]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It took place in the late [[34th millennium]]. The only known participant in the surviving records is the [[Novamarines]] Chapter, although eleven other [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] are recorded as lost.

The plague spread through [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] in M34. The Novamarines were among the first to meet it, and their records describe a wasting sickness that robbed the victims of colour and then of life. The Imperium's understanding of the threat is incomplete, and the nature of the enemy is not recorded.

Course of the conflict

When the plague reached the [[Forge world|forge world]] of Grammachus Beta, the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] asked [[Clan Raukaan]] of the [[Novamarines]] to help enforce a quarantine. The Chapter's warriors sealed the world and fought the infected in its ruins. Later Imperial records place the Chapter's service in the following decades as constant and hard.The sequence of the conflict and the numbers of lost Chapters are estimated from fragmentary records. No record gives an exact year for the quarantine.

Legacy

The conflict is cited in later Imperial accounts of the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], when the Imperium fractured into warrior factions. Its records are largely lost, and the disappearance of the eleven [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] is still disputed.
