The Predator Destructor, usually called simply the Predator, is the most common battle tank of the [[Space Marines]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and of the [[Traitor Legions]]. It is a more heavily armed and armoured relative of the [[Rhino]] personnel carrier. The Destructor carries a long-barrelled Syrtis Pattern autocannon, also called the Predator cannon, which makes it most effective against infantry.

All Rhino and Predator variants descend from designs made in the [[Age of Technology]] and preserved in [[Standard Template Construct]] records. They are built on the [[Forge world]]s of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] or in a Chapter's own forges, then held in the Chapter's [[Armoury]] until needed.

Organisation and use

A [[Space Marine Chapter]] usually keeps twenty to thirty Predators of all types. The [[Master of the Forge]] is responsible for all of the Chapter's vehicles and issues them as requested. Tanks are not attached to any one company. They form a pool from which a commander may draw them as needed.

Each tank is given a name that reflects its duty to protect the Chapter's battle-brothers. Over the years its victories are celebrated as much as those of the Chapter's warriors. The Space Marines treat each Predator as a member of the Chapter.

History

The Predator first served in the Age of Technology as the main battle tank of Mankind's armies. It was developed when Humanity met a warlike xenos species later known as the [[Orks]], whose headlong attacks required heavy armour and strong weapons to survive. The first Predators carried a small number of troops.

In the [[Great Crusade]], in the late 30th and early 31st millennia, the troop space was given over to ammunition, especially for vehicles with sponson weapons. That practice became the standard, and all Predators now carry sponsons.The Great Crusade is dated 798.M30 to 005.M31 in Imperial reckoning. The sources describe the change as gradual and give no single year.

Design

The hull has three layers. The inner layer is bonded [[ceramite]] and [[adamantium]], over five times more protective than steel but much lighter. The middle layer is reinforcing Thermoplas with a mesh that dissipates heat, cold and radiation. The outer layer is a non-magnetic acrylic sheath that hides the vehicle from enemy scanners. Like all Rhino variants it can ford deep water but is not amphibious.

The Mars Pattern Mark IVb Destructor mounts a turret autocannon with an automatic feeder, a flash suppressor and a discharge extractor. A multi-spectral surveyor and accuracy talisman link the gun to the commander, who is also the gunner. The sponsons are fired from the turret and may carry heavy bolters or lascannons. Common additions are a dozer blade, extra armour, a hunter-killer missile, a pintle storm bolter, a searchlight and smoke launchers.

Patterns and formations

Older patterns include the Skarath Pattern Mark IId and the Phaeton Pattern Mark IIIb and IIIc, based on the Mark I Rhino chassis. Both have a square turret mounted forward. The [[Blood Angels]] and their successors use the Baal Pattern, which replaces the autocannon with twin assault cannons. The Deimos Pattern is an ancient Great Crusade design with curved turret and sponsons, each one made by hand on [[Mars]].

A Predator Assassin Squadron groups three to five Destructors or Annihilators and is tasked with destroying a single target. The [[Dark Angels]] field the Hammer of Caliban, which joins a [[Techmarine]], a [[Land Raider]] and a squadron of Predators, Whirlwinds or Vindicators against monstrous enemies.
