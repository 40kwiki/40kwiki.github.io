Demetrian Titus, called the Warden of Ultramar, is a captain of the [[Ultramarines]] who has served the Chapter for nearly three centuries, though not continuously. He first rose to command the 2nd Company in the late 41st millennium and led the relief of the [[forge world]] of [[Graia]] against an [[Orks|Ork]] invasion and a Chaos summoning. During that campaign he showed an unexplained resistance to the powers of the [[Warp]], and the [[Inquisition]] took him into custody on suspicion of heresy.

Titus vanished from the Chapter's records for about a century. He was held by an Inquisitor who hated the Adeptus Astartes, was later found by [[Red Hunters]] and sent to the [[Deathwatch]], where he served in secret under the name Nullus. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[Tyranids]] wounded him so badly on the world of Kadaku that he was returned to the Ultramarines and made a [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris Space Marine]]. He was restored as a lieutenant, and after the Recidious Campaign and a series of costly missions was named captain of the 2nd Company again.

His record divides Ultramarines opinion. His supporters hold that his resistance to corruption is a gift. His critics remember that he deviated from the [[Codex Astartes]] in the field. The Chapter's examiners, among them Chief Librarian [[Varro Tigurius]], found no taint in him, and the Chapter Master accepted their finding.

Early life

Titus was born on the agri-world of Tarentus in the realm of [[Ultramar]], the only child of a modest family, c. 665.M41. A Chapter selector named Metaurus found him as a small child carrying a combat knife across a battlefield. Metaurus noted that the boy had never known fear and recommended him as an aspirant, and he remained Titus's mentor throughout his career.

Titus joined the 10th Company as a Scout and excelled in every trial. In his youth he found his temper hard to hold. Early in his service he befriended Veteran Sergeant Sidonus. In a campaign against Chaos his squad met a sorcerer whose warp-fire killed every other battle-brother. Titus took direct blasts of that power, survived, and killed the sorcerer alone. The Apothecaries could not explain his recovery. Some Ultramarines asked whether he had been touched by Chaos, and he had to defend his reputation more than once afterwards.

Captain of the 2nd Company

Titus rose through the ranks of the 2nd Company under Captain Lucian Trajan. On the world of Beta-Arcturus the company met warriors of [[Craftworld Biel-Tan]], and Trajan slew the [[Autarch]]. Titus's squad killed three of the Aspect Warriors who guarded the xenos commander. The Aeldari took a young battle-brother, Leandros, as they withdrew. Trajan led a combat squad to recover him and was drawn into an ambush and killed. Titus led the counterattack that recovered Trajan's body and then drove the Aeldari off the world.

[[Marneus Calgar]] made the brevet command permanent. Titus brought Trajan's body to Macragge for interment in the Fortress of Hera, and he led the company to victory against mutant, xenos and heretic for about a quarter of a century. First Captain Severus Agemman and the Chapter Master regarded him as a model of Ultramarines humility. He treated the spirit of the Codex as more binding than its letter.

The liberation of Graia

About ten years after taking the company, in c. 880.M41, Titus led a strike force to the forge world of Graia, which an Ork host of about a million, the Waaagh! of the Warboss Grimskull, had invaded to take Imperial weapons, among them a Warlord Titan. The invasion was part of a plan by the Chaos sorcerer Nemeroth, leader of the Chosen of Nemeroth warband, who sought an experimental power source on the world to make himself a [[Daemon Prince]].

Titus fought with Sergeant Sidonus, Brother Leandros and the survivors of a [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian]] regiment under Second Lieutenant Mira, and killed Grimskull. The Ordo Xenos Inquisitor Drogan, who had aided the Ultramarines, proved to be a dead servant of Nemeroth. Drogan used a weapon, the Psychic Scourge, powered by the new source, and opened a gate to the Warp through which daemons and Heretic Astartes came. Titus again withstood the sorcerer's powers, a fact that Leandros, a puritan, noticed with alarm. After Nemeroth killed Sidonus, Titus hunted him down and killed him as he began his ascent.

Inquisition custody

Leandros reported his captain's resistance, and his deviations from the Codex, to the Inquisitor Thrax, who arrived with a retinue of [[Black Templars]]. Thrax took Titus into custody. He held a long grudge against the Adeptus Astartes dating from the [[Badab War]]. For about a century he interrogated Titus with mind-trawls and torture, keeping him in stasis between sessions. Thrax found no sign of corruption and still refused to release him. Calgar petitioned the Inquisition repeatedly for the return of his captain and received no answer. Cato Sicarius, who believed he had been passed over after the [[Battle of Macragge]], took the company.

Deathwatch service

Thrax was killed in the 990s.M41 during an operation against the corrupted Grey Slayers, after he had been possessed by a daemon. [[Red Hunters]] sent to secure his holdings found about twenty Space Marines in stasis in a remote watch station. The Inquisitor who took over his affairs sent them to Watcher Keep of the Deathwatch to be examined. Titus was cleared of taint and disloyalty.

He had heard nothing from his Chapter for a century and believed he had brought it shame. He stripped the Ultramarines heraldry from his armour and served as a Black Shield, a battle-brother whose Chapter is not named, under the name Nullus. He did not know that [[Roboute Guilliman]] had been restored in 999.M41 and that the [[Indomitus Crusade]] was under way.

Return to the Ultramarines

In the early Era Indomitus Nullus was a member of the Deathwatch Kill-team Kasaeran on the jungle world of Kadaku in the Recidious System, where [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] had arrived. The kill-team's task was to fire a virus bomb from a local launcher into the main swarm. Their gunship was brought down and the kill-team was killed one by one. A Carnifex gravely wounded Titus.

An Ultramarines force led by Agemman and Tigurius arrived too late to save Kadaku. Tigurius had sensed a soul he knew in the Warp. The wounded Black Shield was taken to the battle barge Resilient, and the Apothecaries judged that only the additional organs of a Primaris Space Marine could save him. Titus underwent the procedure, which the Ultramarines call the Calgarian Rites. When he woke he was a Primaris Space Marine, in new Mark X armour, and a lieutenant under Captain Sevastus Acheran. He was ordered to keep his Deathwatch service secret. Tigurius examined him, found no taint and spoke with Calgar, who authorised his reinstatement.

The Recidious Campaign

Titus fought on Kadaku and the hive world of Avarax alongside Sergeant Valorem Gadriel and Brother Meduras Chairon, who learned his history from a third party and distrusted him for it. A Mechanicus programme called Project Aurora was building a warp weapon from the Chaos artefact he had destroyed on Graia. The [[Thousand Sons]] sorcerer Imurah, who had influenced the programme, took the artefact on Demerium, a cemetery world that proved to be a [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb world. Calgar came to the fight, and Titus turned the polarity of the artefact as Imurah drew on it, which destroyed the sorcerer.

Calgar gave Titus the Laurels of Victory. Titus learned that the Chaplain who had escorted him from the Apothecarion was Leandros, now Chaplain Quintus, who told him that the Chapter would execute him without hesitation if he turned.

Later service

Titus next took part in a mission to Zsah'Uj, a world held by a [[Tzeentch]] cult, with Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant Metaurus, his old recruiter, and two other Bladeguard. Expected to die, the squad had to find and destroy an idol while a portable Gellar field held back the Warp. A mutant sorcerer froze them and killed the other two Bladeguard in their minds. Titus, who had no fear she could use, broke free and killed her. The idol was destroyed by an orbital strike, and he carried Metaurus out.

Acheran then died defending the hive world of Trygg against a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]], having replaced Sicarius in the command. Titus was made captain of the 2nd Company for a second time, and Guilliman named him Master of the Watch, responsible for the defence of the Fortress of Hera, and Warden of Ultramar. His task is to help restore the realm to its pre-Heresy extent of five hundred worlds. He chose a command squad of Astartes and mortals, called the Wardens of Ultramar.

Status

As of the latest records Titus is alive and in active command of the 2nd Company. He is still the subject of doubt in parts of the Chapter, but his promotion was made on the authority of the Chapter Master and the Lord Commander.
