The Palace of Thorns was the fortified palace that [[Huron Blackheart]] built on [[Badab Primaris]] during the [[Badab War]]. It was the seat of the Secession, and its storming in 913.M41 ended the war on the planet.

The palace was a city-fortress built for war. Its walls and defences were intended to hold out a siege, and it was the last major position of the Secessionist [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] in the Badab System.

Storming

The Loyalist armada broke the [[Ring of Steel]] at Sentinel-Sigma, and the [[Star Phantoms]] stormed the palace. [[Huron Blackheart|Huron]] was struck down by a melta blast from the dying Captain Zhrukal Androcles of the Star Phantoms. The surviving Secessionists were carried away from the palace alive by their followers, and the city collapsed in the fighting.

Some older records date the storming to 912.M41.Imperial records differ on whether the palace fell in 912.M41 or 913.M41. The later date follows the Consistorial Court acts and is used throughout this encyclopedia; the war as a whole is dated 901–913.M41.

Aftermath

The surviving Secessionist [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] were sentenced to [[Penitent|penitent]] crusades, and the [[Badab Sector]] was returned to Imperial control. [[Huron Blackheart|Huron]] escaped into the [[Maelstrom]] with more than two hundred of his followers, and the palace was not rebuilt.
