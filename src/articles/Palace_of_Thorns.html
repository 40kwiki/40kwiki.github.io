The Palace of Thorns was the fortified palace that [[Huron Blackheart]] built on [[Badab Primaris]] during the [[Badab War]]. It was the seat of the Secession, and its storming in 912.M41 ended the war on the planet.

The palace was a city-fortress built for war. Its walls and defences were intended to hold out a siege, and it was the last major position of the Secessionist Chapters in the Badab System.

Storming

The Loyalist armada broke the [[Ring of Steel]] at Sentinel-Sigma, and the [[Star Phantoms]] stormed the palace. Huron was struck down by a melta blast from the dying Captain Zhrukal Androcles of the Star Phantoms. The surviving Secessionists were carried away from the palace alive by their followers, and the city collapsed in the fighting.

Some records date the storming to 913.M41 rather than 912.M41.Imperial records differ on whether the palace fell in 912.M41 or 913.M41. This article follows the Badab War entry.

Aftermath

The surviving Secessionist Chapters were sentenced to penitent crusades, and the Badab Sector was returned to Imperial control. Huron escaped into the [[Maelstrom]] with more than two hundred of his followers, and the palace was not rebuilt.
