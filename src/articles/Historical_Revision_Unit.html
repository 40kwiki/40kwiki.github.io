The Historical Revision Unit is one of the many bureaucratic departments of the [[Administratum]]. Its adepts are indentured [[Human]] servants and [[servitors]], known as "historitors", who work within the [[Imperial Palace]]. Their task is to revise records of Imperial history that are judged subversive. The revised versions are meant to be more reverent towards the [[Emperor]] and to glorify His name. The chief adept of the unit is called the "historicus".

Revision and deletion

A key duty of the unit is to find historical records and, when ordered, to destroy them. Specially designated groups, the Deletion Teams, carry out this work. Their most common task is an [[Edict of Obliteration]]. This is the deliberate destruction of every record concerning a person or organisation declared ''[[Excommunicate Traitoris]]'' by the [[Emperor of Mankind]], the [[High Lords of Terra]] or the [[Inquisition]].

Most deletion missions take place in the catacombs of the [[Librarium Terra]], an endless repository of Imperial records. It lies deep beneath the crust of Terra. The sound of its bureaucracy hums through its corridors, as it does through the rest of the [[Hive World]] of Terra.

The Deletion Teams

A Deletion Team has ten adepts under the command of an officer of [[Captain]] rank. The adepts wear black bodysuits with hoods that leave only the eyes visible, and rebreathers filter out the dust that gathers in the Librarium. Each carries a narrow-nozzled [[flamer]] fed from [[promethium]] canisters on the belt. The captain usually carries a silenced [[Auto Weapons|autogun]]. Imperial scholars who meet a team on a deletion mission are silenced at once, because no witness may survive a historical deletion.

The members of these teams are drawn from the Terran masses and are often put to the task almost from birth. After decades in the Librarium, they hold an instinctive sense that its records contain a dangerous and especially pure history. No one knows the full size of the holdings. Estimates vary, because no one has reached its furthest reaches and returned.

Sources and status

The unit is recorded in the [[Imperial Inquisition]]'s files as an active department. Its work is not acknowledged in public Imperial records. Scholars note that the Historical Revision Unit appears in the sources in very different forms depending on the period, and that its records are themselves subject to revision.The date of the unit's founding is not recorded. Its work is attested in sources from the late 41st millennium.
