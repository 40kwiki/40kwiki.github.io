The Halo Brethren are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] who follow the [[Codex Astartes]]. Their genetic lineage is unknown. They were raised in the [[Fourth Founding]] after the [[War of the Beast]], in or soon after 546.M32, and their one recorded act is the part they played in the fall of the Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]].

History

The Fourth Founding

The War of the Beast, 544–546.M32, left the Imperium shattered a thousand years after the Horus Heresy. The lord commander [[Maximus Thane]], formerly Master of the Fists Exemplar and now Master of the restored [[Imperial Fists]], told the [[High Lords of Terra]] that a Fourth Founding must be raised, as many Chapters as the gene-seed of Terra could sustain. Every Chapter released veterans to start the new ones, and the gene-seed tithe was doubled for a century. When it was done, hundreds of Chapters ringed the Imperium instead of dozens.

Thane also ordered the Imperial Fists to leave Terra and become a crusading Chapter, speeding the recovery of the stricken worlds. He left [[Drakan Vangorich]] as Lord Protector, his personal voice. Soon after, Vangorich had the Officio Assassinorum murder eleven of the twelve High Lords in the [[Beheading]] and installed puppets in the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. For some eighty years he ruled with an iron hand while the Founding went on and the Halo Brethren were made.

Retribution on Terra

By the time Thane received the reports of Vangorich's purges and massacres, the Lord Protector was unstable. Thane returned to Terra c. 626.M32 with the Imperial Fists.The year is estimated from the eighty years of rule after 546.M32. A strike force of four hundred went to the surface: a company of the Halo Brethren, a company of the [[Sable Swords]], and two hundred Imperial Fists from the First, Fourth and Fifth Companies. The Sable Swords' Master, Qublicus Amar, was shot dead soon after landing, and the force pressed on. The Assassinorum fell on it in hundreds and it lost half its strength securing the palace. An aged Inquisitorial representative named Wienand convinced Thane of her loyalty and told him that Vangorich had taken refuge in the Eversor temple at the north pole. A hundred Eversor assassins met the force there. Only Thane survived to reach the Grand Master and shoot him.

Imperial records say nothing more about the Halo Brethren.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter is described only as Codex-compliant. Its colours, badge, Master, homeworld and fortress are not recorded.

Notable campaigns

  • Retribution on Terra (c. 626.M32): a company of the Chapter joined the strike that removed Vangorich from power.

Notable members

None of the Chapter's members is named in Imperial records.
