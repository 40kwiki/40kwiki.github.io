The Venerators were a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. They were destroyed with the [[Warhawks]] in 723.M36, during the Fifth [[Black Crusade]], the Tide of Blood, led by the [[Daemon Prince]] Doombreed.

On the world of [[Tarinth]] the [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Despoiler]] lured the two Chapters into the ruins of Kasyr Lutein. They were destroyed there between the [[Black Legion]] and a daemon host of [[Khorne]].
