The Siege of Terra, also called the Battle of Terra or the Siege of the Imperial Palace, was the final battle of the [[Horus Heresy]]. It was fought in 014.M31, after the Solar War of 013–014.M31, on [[Terra]] and in the space above it, where the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] led the traitor Legions, the [[Dark Mechanicum]] and the armies of the Warp against the [[Imperial Palace]] and the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. The Palace held, but only barely. The Warmaster was killed aboard his flagship the [[Vengeful Spirit]], and the Emperor was mortally wounded and placed on the [[Golden Throne]]. The [[Traitor|traitors]] fled to the [[Eye of Terror]] and the [[Great Scouring]] followed.

The Siege is reckoned the largest and most terrible battle in human history. Its dead on Terra alone numbered tens of millions, and the city-sized Palace was reduced in places to ruin. Its outcome fixed the shape of the Imperium that followed: the Emperor withdrew from the affairs of mankind, the [[Imperial Cult]] took root, and the [[Space Marine Legions]] were reformed into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]].

Background

After the [[Drop Site Massacre]] the [[Warmaster]] commanded the strength of eight Legions, and he took up the work of the war in earnest. For seven years he pursued the loyalists across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] while the [[Dark Mechanicum]] raised war machines on [[Mars]], [[Word Bearers]] and [[World Eaters]] ravaged [[Realm of Ultramar|Ultramar]], and the Ruinstorm kept the loyalist Legions from reaching one another. Of the loyalists, only the [[Imperial Fists]], [[White Scars]] and [[Blood Angels]] were able to return to [[Terra]] before the [[Traitor|traitors]] came. The [[Space Wolves]], [[Dark Angels]] and [[Ultramarines]] were held back by the Ruinstorm and by blockades, and reached the Throneworld only in the closing days.

The defence of Terra

The defence was led by [[Rogal Dorn]], the Praetorian of Terra and primarch of the Imperial Fists, who had been planning for the siege since the first news of Isstvan. He built the Palace's walls and bastions into a system of killing grounds, ordered the destruction of outlying districts and arranged the garrison in depth. Under his direction the Palace was held by the Imperial Fists, the Custodians under [[Constantin Valdor]], [[Sisters of Silence]], a loyalist [[Titan Legions|Titan]] force of four Legions, the [[Imperialis Auxilia]] and the surviving mortal regiments of Terra, close to two million in all. [[Malcador the Sigillite]] directed the civil defence and maintained the Emperor's work beneath the Palace.

The traitor host

The host that gathered on [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] and passed through Mars was drawn from the [[Sons of Horus]], World Eaters, [[Death Guard]], [[Emperor's Children]] and [[Thousand Sons]], with the [[Iron Warriors]], the [[Night Lords]] and part of the Word Bearers, all under the Warmaster. They brought the Titan Legions of the Dark Mechanicum, among them [[Legio Mortis]], and a mass of [[Daemon|daemons]] that followed them through the Warp. The [[Alpha Legion]] had withdrawn after delivering a detailed plan of the defences to [[Horus]].

Course of the siege

Orbital battle and landing

The [[Traitor|traitor]] fleet engaged the Mars blockade and the lunar defences, and destroyed Terra's orbital platforms after some thirty days of fighting. The bombardment of the [[Terra|Throneworld]] began on the thirteenth day of the second month. When it ended, the traitor Legions landed in drop-pods around the Palace and seized the two nearest spaceports, the Lion's Gate and the [[Eternity Wall]]. [[Chaos cult|Cultists]] and renegade [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]] invoked [[Daemon|greater daemons]], and the veil between Terra and the Warp thinned across the continent.

The walls

The defenders were forced back to the Palace walls, where [[Angron]], now a daemon, demanded their surrender. [[Sanguinius]] answered him by action, and the [[Blood Angels]] three times threw the traitors from the walls. Outside, [[Jaghatai Khan]] led the [[White Scars]] on a lightning raid that retook the Lion's Gate Spaceport and turned its defence lasers on the traitor landing-craft. The traitors replied with their Titans, and the [[Legio Mortis]]'s war machines broke open the walls in several places. The defenders withdrew to the Eternity Gate, the final gate to the inner Palace.

At the Eternity Gate the daemon [[Ka'Bandha]] challenged Sanguinius. The Angel, wounded and fatigued, broke the Bloodthirster and cast it down, and the gate was closed on the last defenders. It was the last victory of the primarch of the Blood Angels.

The Webway breach

Beneath the Palace the Emperor continued his labour, the opening of the Webway to human use. The Imperial records state that the warning sent by [[Magnus the Red]] from the ruins of [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]] shattered the wards of the work and let daemons into the Palace's lower levels. The [[Sisters of Silence]], Custodians and [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] tech-priests fought them in the vaults. [[Malcador the Sigillite|Malcador]], in the final days, took the seat of the Golden Throne to hold the breach shut at the cost of his own life.

The Vengeful Spirit

Main article: [[Vengeful Spirit]]

The newest account

The account of the last day accepted by the [[Logos Historica Verita]] since the opening of the Siege archives in the late [[41st millennium]] is as follows. As the [[Traitor|traitors]] broke the outer Palace, the Emperor rose from his work beneath it and took the field in person with three companions: his Angel, Sanguinius; his Praetorian, [[Rogal Dorn]]; and his [[Captain-General]], [[Constantin Valdor]]. With a company of Custodians and picked [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]], they teleported to the Vengeful Spirit, the [[Warmaster]]'s flagship, where Horus waited. The Warmaster had by then drawn so deeply on the powers of the Warp that he stood at the threshold of godhood, and the boarding was in no small part his design. The strike force was scattered through the vast ship by the sorcery that surrounded it, and each member fought alone.

Sanguinius, already wounded in the defence of the Eternity Gate, reached Horus first. Horus offered him a place at his side, and Sanguinius refused. The two fought, and Horus killed him. On the Palace walls the [[Blood Angels]], who felt the death of their [[Primarch|primarch]], were seized by a rage that has since been named the Black Rage, and surged forward to scatter the attackers. The Emperor then reached the Warmaster. Father and son fought in the material realm and in the Warp, and the Emperor was gravely wounded.

The [[Imperial Cult]] honours as Saint Ollanius Pius the ancient companion of the Emperor whom the sealed records of the court call Ollanius Persson. He stood between the Emperor and Horus at the last and died in the act. The Emperor, putting aside the human compassion that had held his hand, struck the Warmaster down and destroyed his soul entire, so that it could not be raised by the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]]. In the same moments the daemonic tide in the [[Sol System|Sol system]] broke, and the traitor host was routed.

The earlier Imperial account, still told in many cathedrals, holds that the Warmaster himself lowered the shields of the Vengeful Spirit out of a last moment of regret or defiance, that the Emperor teleported aboard and that Sanguinius was strangled, and that the warrior who held the line was an unnamed soldier, a Custodian or an [[Imperial Fists]] Terminator. The Imperial Fists maintain their own version. These are now treated as earlier or disputed recensions.

The last hours

While the Emperor fought on the flagship, the defence of Terra reached its limit. A vanguard of [[Dark Angels]] under the Seneschal [[Corswain]], sent ahead of the Lion, [[Lion El'Jonson]], who had not yet reached Terra, held the Hollow Mountain against the [[Death Guard]] and its sorcerer [[Typhus]]. The [[Space Wolves]] arrived in the same hours. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]], under [[Roboute Guilliman]], came last, as the traitors were already withdrawing.

Rogal Dorn carried the broken Emperor from the flagship to the Golden Throne beneath the Palace. The Throne's machinery, devised for the Webway project, was set to sustain him. [[Malcador the Sigillite]], who had held the Throne in the Emperor's stead, died in the act. The Emperor's body was entombed in the Throne's life support, and his mind, according to the Imperial Cult, endures.

Aftermath

With the [[Warmaster]] dead the [[Traitor|traitor]] host broke up. The [[Sons of Horus]] were first to flee, with their primarch's body, and the [[Death Guard]] were the last to leave. The loyalists, led by the [[Blood Angels]], who were in the grip of the Black Rage, and the [[Imperial Fists]], slaughtered many of the fleeing traitors in front of the [[Sanctum Imperialis]]. Over the following years the loyalist Legions hunted the traitors to the Eye of Terror in the [[Great Scouring]].

The Siege left the Imperium wrecked. The Emperor sat on the Golden Throne, no longer able to speak or command, and [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] and [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] bore the work of rebuilding. The consequences were the [[Codex Astartes]], the breaking up of the Legions in the [[Second Founding]], the rise of the [[Imperial Cult]] and the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The date of the Siege is regarded as the beginning of the [[Age of the Imperium]].

Legacy and commemoration

The Palace was repaired in the decades that followed, from 014.M31 into the 020s.M31,, and the [[Eternity Wall]] still bears the scars of the siege. The [[Imperial Fists]], [[Blood Angels]] and [[White Scars]] honour the defence; the Blood Angels keep the death of [[Sanguinius]] as the central tragedy of their [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. The [[Sons of Horus]], the [[Death Guard]] and the other traitor Legions remember the Siege as the war's greatest failure. Imperial scholars count it the most important single battle in the history of the Imperium.
