Scintilla is the capital world of the [[Calixis Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It lies in the Golgenna Reach [[Sub-sector]] and is a thriving Imperial hub that supports the largest planetary population in the sector. The world is dominated, and some observers say shared, by two vast [[Hive world|hive cities]], Hive Sibellus and Hive Tarsus. The great majority of the planet's population is crowded into them.

The sector government is seated on Scintilla, and the rival hives have long been a source of tension. [[Administratum]] records describe the planet's politics as a contest between the two cities for control of trade and the sector's tithe. The [[Inquisition]] keeps a substantial presence on the world.

Hive cities

Main article: [[Hive world]]

Hive Sibellus and Hive Tarsus are each stacked hundreds of levels high and house millions of citizens. Their lower levels are rarely visited by the upper classes. Imperial census records show that most births and deaths on the planet take place within the hives.
