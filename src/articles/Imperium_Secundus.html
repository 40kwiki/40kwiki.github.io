The Imperium Secundus, also called the Unremembered Empire or the Second Imperium, was a contingency empire founded at [[Macragge]] during the [[Horus Heresy]], in about 009.M31. When the Warp storm called the [[Ruinstorm]] cut the eastern half of the galaxy off from [[Terra]], the primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]] could no longer tell whether the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] lived or whether the Throneworld had fallen to the Warmaster [[Horus]]. He resolved to preserve what remained of the Emperor's realm around the Five Hundred Worlds of [[Realm of Ultramar|Ultramar]], so that the Imperium would not die with its capital.

The empire was ruled by three primarchs. [[Sanguinius]] of the [[Blood Angels]] was crowned Imperator Regis, regent-emperor in his father's stead. Guilliman took the title of Lord Warden and governed its civil affairs. [[Lion El'Jonson]] of the [[Dark Angels]] was named Lord Protector, with command of its armies in a role that Imperial scholars compare with that of Warmaster. The arrangement was troubled from the start. The Lion distrusted Guilliman's motives, the Night Lords' primarch [[Konrad Curze]] waged a campaign of murder on Macragge, and the alien beacon that guided the empire's fleets was lost at the [[Battle of Sotha]].

The Imperium Secundus was dissolved once it became clear that the Emperor still lived. Its three rulers led their Legions into the Ruinstorm, destroyed the world of [[Davin]] and opened a passage that allowed the Blood Angels to reach Terra before the siege. Guilliman afterwards counted the empire among the gravest errors of his life. Its records were largely sealed after the [[Great Scouring]], and it is still rarely spoken of on Macragge.

Background

Calth and the Ruinstorm

In 007.M31 the [[Word Bearers]] attacked the [[Ultramarines]] at the muster on Calth. The [[Battle of Calth]] cost the XIII Legion roughly half its strength and most of its fleet. In its last hours the Dark Apostle [[Erebus]] completed a rite on the planet's surface that raised the Ruinstorm, a Warp tempest of a size not seen since the [[Age of Strife]]. The [[Shadow Crusade]] of the Word Bearers and [[World Eaters]] across Ultramar fed it further. The storm divided the galaxy. No astropathic message could cross it, the light of the [[Astronomican]] could not be seen from the Eastern Fringe, and Warp travel became a gamble with every jump.

The Pharos

Ultramar was not wholly blind. On the world of Sotha, the Ultramarines had found an ancient xenos device, buried in the heart of a mountain, which they called the Pharos. Mechanicum adepts learned to make it cast a light into the Warp that Navigators could follow much as they followed the Astronomican, though over a smaller span. With it the fleets of Ultramar could still move between their own worlds, and loyalists stranded elsewhere behind the storm could find their way to Macragge.

The question of Terra

By 009.M31 Guilliman had no word from Terra for two years. The few reports that reached Macragge spoke of traitor fleets on the route to the Sol System and of loyal worlds falling silent. He judged that Terra might already have fallen, and that if it had not, he had no means of learning so. His advisers urged him to declare himself the Emperor's heir or at least regent. He refused. Ultramarines chroniclers record that he feared to be seen doing what Horus had done, taking a throne in his father's absence, and that he believed one man could not both command a war and rule a realm.

Guilliman nevertheless began to build the structures of a second empire, first in secret and then openly. He prepared the government of Ultramar to administer worlds beyond its borders, gathered survivors of the loyal Legions broken at the [[Drop Site Massacre]], and raised in the Macragge Civitas a council chamber with a granite table and twenty-one seats, one for the Emperor and one for each of his sons. Two of the seats were draped in blank cloth for the [[Lost Primarchs]] of the II and XI Legions.

Founding

Arrival of the Lion

The first primarch to reach Macragge was Lion El'Jonson. The Dark Angels had spent the first years of the war fighting the [[Night Lords]] in the [[Thramas Crusade]], and the Lion's fleet followed the light of the Pharos out of the Thramas Sector. Guilliman received him with full honours. Some forty-seven thousand warriors of the XIII Legion formed his honour guard, and more than a million citizens filled the streets of the capital.

The meeting between the two primarchs was cordial in public and strained in private. The Lion suspected that Guilliman meant to make himself a second Emperor, as Horus had tried to do, and Guilliman believed his brother secretive and hard to trust. In the council chamber Guilliman set out his plan and offered the Lion the regency of the new empire. The Lion declined. Later Terran sources hold that [[Malcador the Sigillite]] had feared before the Ruinstorm that Guilliman might found a realm of his own if cut off, and had hoped that the First Legion would restrain him if he did. Whether the Lion carried any such charge is not recorded.

The Dark Angels brought an unwanted passenger. Konrad Curze, primarch of the Night Lords, had boarded the Lion's flagship, the Invincible Reason, at the end of the Thramas campaign and hidden in its depths. He reached Macragge with the fleet.

The coming of Sanguinius

Sanguinius and the Blood Angels came next. The IX Legion had been trapped by the Warmaster in the [[Signus Campaign|Signus Cluster]], where it lost a great part of its strength to daemons, and had since been unable to reach Terra. Guided by the Pharos, Sanguinius led the surviving Legion to Macragge.

Guilliman saw in Sanguinius the candidate he needed. The Angel was loved by his brothers and by the people of every world he had visited, and Guilliman held that he shared more of the Emperor's nobility than any other primarch. Sanguinius was reluctant to accept, but at length agreed. He was crowned in Macragge Civitas as Imperator Regis, ruler of the Imperium Secundus until contact with Terra was restored. Popular accounts on Macragge simply called him the new Emperor.

Offices of state

The three primarchs divided the government between them. The arrangement is sometimes called the Triumvirate of Macragge.

OfficeHolderDuties
Imperator Regis[[Sanguinius]]Head of state and regent for the Emperor. Final authority over the empire.
Lord Warden[[Roboute Guilliman]]Head of the civil government. Administration, supply, law and the Pharos. Some records style him chief adviser to the Imperator Regis.
Lord Protector[[Lion El'Jonson]]Supreme commander of the empire's armies and fleets and guardian of its security, with powers comparable to those of the Warmaster.

The title of Lord Protector belonged to the Lion, not to Guilliman. Some later summaries confuse the two offices, but the surviving Ultramarines and Blood Angels records agree on the division.The title "Lord Protector" was later used for unrelated offices, notably by [[Drakan Vangorich]] after the [[Beheading]] in the 32nd millennium. The Imperium Secundus title has no connection with them.

Government and forces

The Imperium Secundus covered the Five Hundred Worlds of Ultramar and such loyal worlds of the Eastern Fringe as could be reached by the Pharos. Its administration was in practice the government of Ultramar, extended. Guilliman's officials supplied the fleets, raised regiments, and kept the census of worlds that had declared for Macragge. The Vigil Opertii, the intelligence service of Ultramar, watched for traitors and agents of the Warmaster.

Its armies were the three Legions present: the Ultramarines, still the largest Legion despite Calth, the Blood Angels, and the Dark Angels. To them were added the Ultramar Auxilia, the Praecental Guard of Macragge, loyal [[Imperial Army]] regiments, Mechanicum forces and Titans, and detachments of the [[Shattered Legions]] and other Legions that had come to Macragge by chance or by design. Small bodies of [[White Scars]], [[Space Wolves]], [[Imperial Fists]], [[Salamanders]], [[Iron Hands]] and [[Raven Guard]] were among them.

The empire kept the [[Imperial Truth]] as its creed. It made no claim of divinity for the Emperor or for Sanguinius, though some of the people of Macragge treated the Angel's coronation as a holy event.

History

Curze on Macragge

The first trial of the new empire came from within its capital. Konrad Curze slipped off the Invincible Reason and began a campaign of terror on Macragge. Officials were murdered and their bodies displayed, bombs were set in the city, and riots broke out in districts that had lost faith in the Lord of Macragge. Curze is believed to have attempted the life of Guilliman himself.

In the same days [[Vulkan]] of the [[Salamanders]], believed dead since the Drop Site Massacre, appeared on Macragge, by means that the surviving records do not explain. He warned his brothers of Curze and joined the hunt. Vulkan fell in the fighting on Macragge in a state that his sons took for death, and the Salamanders who were present afterwards carried his body back to [[Nocturne]].Salamanders tradition holds that Vulkan rose again on Nocturne. The details are kept by the Chapter and are not part of the Ultramarines record.

As Lord Protector the Lion took charge of the security of the capital. He placed parts of Macragge under martial law and used the Dark Angels to restore order, in some districts with weapons that the Ultramarines thought far too harsh to use on their own people. Guilliman protested, and the two primarchs came close to blows. The Lion at last captured Curze in the Illyrium region of Macragge, and the Night Haunter was imprisoned to await judgement.

The Battle of Sotha

The traitors knew the value of the Pharos. A Night Lords force attacked Sotha in an attempt to seize or destroy it. The loyalists defended the world, and the attackers were defeated. The Pharos itself did not survive the battle in working order. Its loss left the empire's fleets once more with no light to steer by in the storm. Accounts differ on whether the device was destroyed by the enemy, turned against the Night Lords fleet by its keepers and burned out, or deliberately silenced.One Mechanicus report from Sotha states that before the end the Pharos showed its keepers a faint image of the Astronomican, the first sign seen behind the Ruinstorm that the Emperor still lived. The Logos Historica Verita has not confirmed this.

Strains within the triumvirate

The empire's rulers did not agree on how it should be run. The Lion's command of its armies sat poorly with Guilliman, who had built most of the forces he now commanded. Guilliman's habit of planning for every outcome, including the empire's own survival without Terra, seemed to the Lion to show ambition. Sanguinius stood between them. He was troubled throughout by visions of his own death on Terra, and by his own unease at sitting on a throne in his father's place.

The question of Curze divided them most. Guilliman wanted a trial before the people of Macragge. The Lion wanted the Night Haunter dead. Sanguinius, who is said to have foreseen that Curze would meet his fate on his own world, did not demand his execution. Curze remained a prisoner for a time and later escaped, in circumstances that Blood Angels records attribute to Sanguinius's tacit consent.

Dissolution

Proof that the Emperor lived

By 011.M31 the rulers of the Imperium Secundus had come to believe that the Emperor lived and that Terra still held. The evidence came from several sources: reports from the last workings of the Pharos, visions granted to Sanguinius, and the thinning of the Ruinstorm. With the reason for the empire gone, the three primarchs agreed to abandon it. Sanguinius set aside the title of Imperator Regis. The territories of the empire returned to their former governments, and the Realm of Ultramar was left in the care of a regency under Valentus Dolor.

The road to Davin

The primarchs then turned their Legions toward Terra. Their course led through the heart of the Ruinstorm to [[Davin]], the world where Horus had first been corrupted, which they found transformed into a daemon realm and the anchor of the storm. In the [[Second Battle of Davin]] the three Legions fought on its surface and in the air above it, and then destroyed the planet with cyclonic torpedoes. Its destruction opened a passage through the storm.

Horus had foreseen the opening and placed a blockade across the route. Guilliman and the Lion agreed to engage the traitor fleet while Sanguinius took the whole of the Blood Angels Legion straight for Terra. The Angel reached the Throneworld before the siege began, and died aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] at the end of the [[Siege of Terra]]. The Ultramarines and Dark Angels did not reach the Sol System until the closing hours of the war.

Legacy

The Imperium Secundus left almost no trace in the official histories. Imperial scholars of the following centuries treated it as an embarrassment: an empire raised by loyal sons in their father's lifetime, whose existence could be read as a lesser form of the treason it was meant to oppose. Many of its records were sealed at the end of the Scouring, and some appear to have been destroyed. The name "Unremembered Empire" reflects this neglect.

Guilliman himself regarded the empire as a grievous error made in good faith. After his return in the Era Indomitus as [[Roboute Guilliman|Lord Commander of the Imperium]], he spoke of it rarely, and only to admit that he had been wrong to despair of his father. Imperial commentators have noted that the regency he exercises in the 42nd millennium, ruling in the name of an Emperor who sits silent on the [[Golden Throne]], resembles the arrangement he once tried to build for Sanguinius.

The empire also shaped the later history of the Dark Angels and the Blood Angels. The long absence of the Lion and his Legion behind the Ruinstorm is held by some to have given [[Luther]] the opportunity to turn [[Caliban]] against him. For the Blood Angels, Sanguinius's coronation is remembered as the moment their primarch came closest to his father's place, and the Chapter's histories record it with reverence.
