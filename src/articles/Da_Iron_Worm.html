Da Iron Worm was a [[Space hulk|space hulk]] crewed by [[Orks]]. It ravaged the [[Uhulis Sector]] in the 32nd millennium, and it was lost when a Warp storm carried it away.

Orks use hulks as ships, bases and battle-sites. An Ork crew will break into a hulk, claim it as a rok or hulk, and use it to carry a Waaagh! across the stars. Imperial records describe Ork hulks being sent into systems in the path of Imperial fleets, where the ship's own engines and weapons are used to batter a defence.

Loss

Da Iron Worm was lost when a Warp storm carried it off. Imperial accounts record that the hulk was not seen again after the storm, and they do not give a date for its loss.The sources place Da Iron Worm's raids in the 32nd millennium but give no exact dates for them.
