Orikan the Diviner is a [[Crypteks|Cryptek]] of the [[Necrons]], an astromancer and chronomancer of the Sautekh Dynasty and adviser to its [[Necron dynasty|phaeron]], [[Imotekh the Stormlord]]. Before the Great Sleep he was astromancer to the Triarch of the Silent King [[Szarekh]], and he was the only voice on the council to oppose the [[C'tan]] bargain that turned the Necrontyr into Necrons. He submitted to biotransference out of loyalty, and his powers grew in his new body.

Orikan reads the future from the stars, and Imperial accounts credit him with foreseeing the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], the rise of mankind, the [[Horus Heresy]] and the coming of the [[Tyranids]] long before they happened. When events go against his predictions he can step back along his own timeline and change them. The Ordo Xenos believes he is working towards a plan of his own that will come to fruition when the stars reach a certain alignment.

Before the Sleep

As astromancer of the Triarch, Orikan foresaw that biotransference would end in disaster and argued against it. He was ignored. He accepted the process with the rest of his race and, unusually, kept his mind and found his powers increased, since his calculations were no longer limited by a failing body.

Service to the Sautekh

Orikan serves the [[Imotekh the Stormlord|Sautekh]] as a seer. In 910.M41 his prophecies guided Imotekh's strikes on the supply worlds of the [[Forge world|forge world]] [[Hypnoth]], which broke the Imperial defence; the world fell in 911.M41. The nobility distrust him for his scorn and for the knowing manner in which he treats them, but no lord has been able to act against him, since he knows their plans before they do.

In 999.M41 Sautekh forces from Mandragora, Gidrim and Trakonn answered the call of [[Anrakyr the Traveller]] to the world of Carnac, which was held by [[Exodite]] [[Aeldari]]; Orikan's presence in that campaign is recorded in [[Ordo Xenos]] reports.

Chronomancy

Orikan's powers are not flawless. [[Warp travel]] and chance events upset his calculations. When this happens, he can walk back down his own timeline and change the past enough to restore his predicted outcome. The best recorded example is the Helios VI affair. The [[Imperial Navy]] dockyards on Helios VI had survived the [[Waaagh!|Ork Waaagh!]] Skullkrak, against his prediction, thanks to the [[Silver Skulls]]' 4th Company. Orikan arranged for that company to be ambushed weeks earlier, and Helios VI fell as foreseen. The change had consequences: five [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], among them the Silver Skulls, the [[Howling Griffons]] and the [[Death Spectres]], fell on the Lazar System in revenge and destroyed the [[Necrons|tomb world]] that had commissioned his prophecy.The date of the Helios VI affair is not recorded. It is placed in the late 41st millennium from the Chapters involved.

Feud with Trazyn

See also: [[Trazyn the Infinite]]

Orikan and [[Trazyn the Infinite]] have been rivals since before the Sleep. Their longest quarrel, lasting some ten thousand years, was over the supposed tomb of the [[Necron dynasty|phaeron]] Nephreth on the world of Serenade on the [[Eastern Fringe]]. Trazyn released a Genestealer into the tomb to kill Orikan; the creature failed, but founded a [[Genestealer Cults|cult]] that led to the Imperium destroying the planet by [[Exterminatus]]. Orikan, buried, spent more than a century digging himself out and lived more than two thousand subjective years in loops of his own making. The two were eventually forced to work together when they learned that Nephreth's tomb was a myth planted by a [[C'tan|C'tan shard]].

Wargear

  • Staff of Tomorrow, which exists a fraction of a second ahead of the present, so that it strikes before the target moves.
  • A phase shifter, which lets blows and shots pass through him.
  • Temporal snares, which slow time around enemies, and a transdimensional beamer.
  • When the stars align, Orikan is briefly restored to a fraction of his ancient power, and the [[Necrons]] say of these moments that "the stars are right".
