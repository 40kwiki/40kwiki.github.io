Peregrim's World was an industrial [[agri-world]] of the Imperium in the [[Affluix Sector]]. It produced vast quantities of [[grox]] meat for the sector. It was infested by a gene-sect of the [[Innerwyrm]] [[Genestealer Cult]], and its meat exports carried the infection across the local stars.

History

After the [[Great Rift]] formed, the Cult of the Innerwyrm spread across the Affluix Sector along the grox-meat export network of Peregrim's World. The beasts were selectively bred into enormous size, so large that Innerwyrm abattoir workers could sew [[Genestealer]]s into their guts.

The infected carcasses were sent to a dozen worlds in the [[Fort Adere]] system, which was famously watched by paranoid bio-scanners. The scanners did not detect the tissue of the smuggled Genestealers, and a new infestation took hold on each world.
