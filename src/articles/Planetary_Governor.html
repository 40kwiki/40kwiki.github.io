A Planetary Governor, formally styled Imperial Commander, is the ruler of a world of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] on behalf of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. He has almost complete authority over the internal affairs of his world, its laws, its armies, its industry and its people, provided that he pays the world's tithes to the [[Administratum]], raises regiments for the [[Astra Militarum]] when required, surrenders its [[Psyker|psykers]] to the [[Black Ships]], and keeps the world loyal to the Imperium and to the [[Imperial Cult]].

The office dates from the [[Great Crusade]], when the Emperor's armies installed governors on worlds brought to compliance. Over the millennia most governorships have become hereditary in noble houses, whose succession the Administratum confirms. Governors of several worlds answer to a Lord Sector or sector governor, and the greatest governors rule whole [[Sub-sector|subsectors]] or realms.

The Imperium relies on the loyalty of its governors more than on any other single office, and their rebellions have caused some of its worst wars. A governor who fails in his duties may be removed by the Administratum, executed by the [[Adeptus Arbites]] or the [[Inquisition]], or deposed by a [[Space Marine Chapter]] acting on their behalf.

History

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

During the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30 – 000.M31) the expeditionary fleets brought tens of thousands of worlds into the Imperium. Where a world submitted, its own rulers were often confirmed in power and sworn to the Emperor; where it resisted, the crusade installed new rulers, frequently officers of the [[Imperial Army]] who remained when the fleets moved on. These rulers were called Imperial Commanders, and the [[Imperial Truth]] and the laws of [[Terra]] were imposed through them. The [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancers]] recorded that some governors were installed within days of a world's compliance.

The Horus Heresy and after

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) many governors sided with the [[Warmaster]], and whole [[Sector|sectors]] fell to rebellion. In the reforms that followed, under [[Roboute Guilliman]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], the military and civil powers on each world were separated: governors were to keep a [[Planetary Defence Force]] but could not command the [[Astra Militarum]] regiments they raised, nor the ships of the [[Imperial Navy]]. The [[Adeptus Arbites]] were established on governors' worlds to enforce [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] above them.

The Age of Apostasy and later millennia

See also: [[Age of Apostasy]]

The [[Age of Apostasy]] in M36 showed the danger of governors who obeyed a corrupt High Lord: many supported the [[Reign of Blood]] of [[Goge Vandire]], and the [[Plague of Unbelief]] saw governors declare themselves gods. In later millennia governors' rebellions remained a constant threat. The secession of Overlord [[Herman von Strab]] of [[Armageddon]], who betrayed his world to [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] in 941.M41, was one of the most damaging of the [[41st millennium]].

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 left the governors of the [[Imperium Nihilus]] cut off from Terra. Many have ruled alone since, raising their own forces and in some cases their own creeds, and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has had to restore Imperial authority over worlds whose governors had become all but independent.

Powers and obligations

An Imperial Commander's rights over his own world are close to absolute. He makes its laws, sets its taxes, commands its [[Planetary Defence Force]] and decides its religion within the limits of the [[Imperial Cult]]. The Imperium does not ask how he governs, only that he meet his obligations:

  • The tithe. The [[Administratum]] assigns every world a tithe grade, set by its population and industry, and the governor must pay it in goods, materials, soldiers or labour.
  • The founding. When the Departmento Munitorum requires it, the governor must raise regiments for the [[Astra Militarum]], trained and equipped at the world's cost.
  • The psyker tithe. The governor must gather the world's [[psyker]]s and surrender them to the [[Black Ships]] of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]].
  • Defence. The governor must defend his world against invasion until relieved, and report any threat to Imperial authorities.
  • Loyalty. He must permit the Imperial Cult and the presence of the [[Adeptus Terra|Adepta]], and suppress heresy and [[Mutant|mutation]].

Variant forms

  • Hereditary rule. Most governorships pass within a noble house, such as House Helmawr, which has ruled [[Necromunda]] since about M34.
  • Sector and subsector governors. A Lord Sector rules a sector of worlds, with the governors of its worlds below him; his authority is political and military, and he may raise armies from his sector in a crisis.
  • Chapter rule. On worlds held by the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the [[Chapter Master]] is usually recognised as Imperial Commander. The master of the [[Ultramarines]] is the Lord of [[Macragge]] and rules the realm of [[Ultramar]].
  • Ecclesiarchal and Mechanicus worlds. On [[shrine world]]s a cardinal or other priest may rule, and on [[forge world]]s the ruler is a [[Fabricator-General|Fabricator]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], who answers to [[Mars]], not to the [[Administratum]].
  • Military governors. Worlds under martial law, such as [[Fortress world|fortress worlds]], may be governed by a military officer; [[Cadia]] was held in its last years by its Lord Castellan, [[Ursarkar Creed]], until its fall in 999.M41.
