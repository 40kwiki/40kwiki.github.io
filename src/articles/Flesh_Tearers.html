The Flesh Tearers are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created from the [[Blood Angels]] in the [[Second Founding]] of 021.M31. They are the most savage of the sons of [[Sanguinius]]: their gene-seed carries the [[Red Thirst]] and the [[Black Rage]] in a severe form, and their history is marked by massacres that have more than once brought them close to being declared renegade. Their homeworld is the death world of Cretacia, a name taken from a Baalite word meaning "Birth of Wrath".

By the end of the 41st millennium the Chapter had fallen to some four hundred warriors under its Chapter Master, Gabriel Seth. At the [[Devastation of Baal]] in 999.M41 Seth took command of the defence of the Arx Angelicum after [[Commander Dante|Dante]] was struck down, and held until the [[Indomitus Crusade]] arrived. The Chapter has since been restored to full strength with [[Primaris Space Marines]], an outcome Seth received with mixed feelings.

History

Founding

At the Second Founding the first Chapter Master was Nassir Amit, captain of the Blood Angels' 5th Company, whose company had the most bloodthirsty reputation in the IX Legion. For over two centuries the new Chapter crusaded without a home. Its record began in blood: in 021.M31, the year of its creation, it carried out the Zurcon Massacres. Amit later settled the Chapter on Cretacia, whose feral human tribes provided strong recruits.The settlement of Cretacia is dated only to the two centuries after the Founding.

Between 544 and 546.M32 the Flesh Tearers fought in the [[War of the Beast]]. In M36, at Kallern, Flesh Tearers overcome by the Black Rage slaughtered hundreds of civilians along with the enemy. In 201.M37 they were condemned for the Tosovic Pacification, a war of such brutality that nothing has grown since on that planet's plains, and in 540.M38 their name was linked again to atrocity in the Eypharos campaign.

Gabriel Seth

Gabriel Seth became Chapter Master at a low point and has fought ever since to keep his Chapter from destruction or condemnation. In 837.M41 the [[Ordo Hereticus]] Inquisitor Corvin Herrold boarded the Victus to question him about events on the shrine world of Lucid Prime. The Chapter's excesses at length brought it before the Carmine Court, a trial before Dante and every Chapter Master of the Blood Angels' line, and only the intervention of the Blood Angels' High Chaplain Astorath saved it from censure.

The Flesh Tearers fought in the [[Achilus Crusade]] in 813.M41, in the Siege of Hypnoth in 910–911.M41 and in the Stromark Civil War in 991.M41. In 998.M41, during the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]], Seth and the Inquisitor Nerissa Lekkas found an abandoned Imperator-class [[Titan Legions|Titan]] of the Legio Annihilator at the bottom of a borehole in the Ephesus ore mine.

Cryptus and Baal

In 998.M41 a tendril of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] reached the Cryptus System, the shield worlds that guard [[Baal]]. The Flesh Tearers joined the Blood Angels and their successors in its defence, hoping to win redemption, and then fell back to Baal for the final siege in 999.M41. When the Swarmlord wounded Dante, Seth led the retreat into the Arx Angelicum and commanded the defence until the fleets of [[Roboute Guilliman]] broke the Tyranid swarm.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The Primaris reinforcements that followed brought the Flesh Tearers to full strength for the first time in centuries. The new warriors appear less afflicted by the Flaw, but Seth feared that Guilliman's gift would turn the sons of Sanguinius into copies of the [[Ultramarines]] in crimson armour. The Chapter has since fought in the Red Scar Campaign.The Red Scar Campaign is dated only to M42.

Gene-seed

Main article: [[Gene-seed]]

The Chapter's gene-seed is among the most unstable of Sanguinius's line. The Red Thirst drives its warriors into a killing frenzy that makes them feared even by allies, and many fall to the Black Rage. Seth himself is known for keeping his fury in check by an effort of will.
