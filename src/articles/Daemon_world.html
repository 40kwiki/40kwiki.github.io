A daemon world is a planet that has been overrun by the power of the [[Warp]], so that the laws of reality no longer hold on it and it is ruled by a [[Daemon prince]] or other servant of [[Chaos]]. The physical nature of such a world is shaped by the will of its master: mountains may be built of skulls, seas may run with blood or plague, and time and distance may change from one moment to the next. Most daemon worlds lie within the great warp rifts, above all the [[Eye of Terror]], where the boundary between realspace and the warp is thin.

Daemon worlds are not part of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], and the [[Administratum]] removes a world from its registry when it is recorded as a daemon world. The [[Inquisition]] keeps their records under seal. The most famous are the worlds of the [[Daemon Primarch]]s: the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] of [[Magnus the Red]], [[Medrengard]] of [[Perturabo]], [[Sicarus]] of [[Lorgar]] and the [[Plague Planet]] of [[Mortarion]]. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Imperial reports record new daemon worlds forming along its length.

Nature

The servants of the Dark Gods regard a daemon world as a reward. A champion who has risen to become a daemon prince may receive a world of his own as a domain, which he reshapes according to his desires and those of his patron. On such a world the master's will is close to law: he may raise fortresses from the ground, alter the seasons or call on daemonic hosts. Mortals who live there are changed by the warp over generations, and few remain recognisably human.

Daemon worlds reflect the god to whom they are given. Worlds of [[Khorne]] are scenes of endless battle; worlds of [[Nurgle]] are swamps of disease and decay; worlds of [[Tzeentch]] are crystalline and ever-changing; worlds of [[Slaanesh]] are places of excess and cruelty. Worlds dedicated to Chaos Undivided, or to a master who serves no one god, may show elements of all four.

Creation

A world becomes a daemon world in several ways. Some were human or Aeldari worlds swallowed by the Eye of Terror when it formed at the birth of [[Slaanesh]] in M30, and remain within it. Others are drawn into the warp when a warp storm engulfs them, or when a ritual of great power tears open the boundary of realspace. Some are given to a new daemon prince by the gods. In the Era Indomitus, Imperial worlds in the path of the Great Rift have been transformed in this way, and some daemon worlds have extended their influence into neighbouring systems.

Relations with the Imperium

The Imperium has no means of reclaiming a daemon world. Imperial forces seldom attempt to land on them, since even the presence of mortals is dangerous and many who return are found to be corrupted. The [[Grey Knights]] and the [[Ordo Malleus]] have carried out raids on daemon worlds to destroy particular threats, and some Space Marine Chapters have entered the Eye of Terror on crusades, but such expeditions are rare and their records are sealed.

Where a world is in danger of becoming a daemon world, the Inquisition may order its destruction by [[Exterminatus]] before the transformation is complete.

Inhabitants

The population of a daemon world consists of the servants of its master: the warriors of his warband or Legion, cultists who have come to serve him, and the mutants and slaves who labour in his domain. Daemons walk openly, since the warp is close enough to sustain them in realspace. On the worlds of the Daemon Primarchs, whole Legions have made their homes for ten thousand years. The Iron Warriors on Medrengard keep vast numbers of slaves to labour in their fortresses, and the Thousand Sons on the Planet of the Sorcerers live in a city built to resemble the lost Tizca of [[Prospero]]. Captives taken in raids on the Imperium often end their days on such worlds.
