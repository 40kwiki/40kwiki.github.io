Heresy is, in Imperial law and doctrine, any belief, teaching or act that opposes the [[Emperor of Mankind]], the [[Imperial Truth]] or the lawful order of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is the gravest of crimes after open treason, and in most cases the two are not distinguished. A person who commits heresy is a [[Heretic]]. The Imperium's courts, priests, [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] and tech-priests each claim the right to judge it, and in practice the pursuit of heresy is the largest single labour of the Imperial state after war.

The Imperium treats heresy as a contagion. A single heretic may corrupt a household, a household a hive, a hive a world. The Imperial doctrine holds that most heresy is the work of [[Chaos]], either directly, through the worship of the [[Ruinous Powers]], or indirectly, through the weakness of the human mind. The punishment is therefore not only for the crime but for the protection of the many from the infection of the few, and it is severe. Sentences range from public penance to death, and in the worst cases a whole world may be destroyed.

The word is also used for the great civil war of 005–014.M31, the [[Horus Heresy]], in which the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] turned against the Emperor. That war is the origin of the Imperium's present fear of the crime, and every later judgement refers to it.

Doctrine and law

Heresy is defined by three bodies of text. The first is the Imperial Truth, the teaching that the Emperor is the sole [[Emperor of Mankind|master of mankind]] and that the Imperium alone gives it safety. In its early form the Truth was a statement of the Emperor's own secular [[Ursarkar Creed|creed]], which rejected all religion. After the [[Horus Heresy]] the Emperor's divine status was taken up by the [[Ecclesiarchy]], which now teaches the worship of the Emperor as the divine Saviour of Mankind. The second is the [[Lex Imperialis]], the body of Imperial law, which sets out the offences and penalties in civil terms. The third is the Liber Ecclesiasticus, in which the Ecclesiarchy states its orthodoxy and the limits of permitted opinion.

The question of what is heresy has changed with the centuries. In the [[Great Crusade]] the Imperial Truth forbade religious worship, and the cult of [[Lorgar]]'s Lectitio Divinitatus was condemned by the Emperor as a mistake. After the Emperor's entombment in the [[Golden Throne]] in 014.M31, the veneration of him was embraced as orthodoxy and the earlier prohibitions became themselves a heresy of a kind. Today a person who denies the divinity of the Emperor is liable to be charged as a [[Heretic|heretic]].

Kinds of heresy

The Imperium classifies heresy in many ways, and the lists differ by body. The following divisions are those most often used.

  • Heresy against the Emperor. Denial of his divinity, claims to speak with his voice without authority, the worship of another power, or the teaching of the Imperial Truth in unapproved forms.
  • Tech-heresy. The use or study of technology in ways forbidden by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]: the alteration of sacred machines without rite, the use of forbidden knowledge, the creation of thinking machines, and the denial of the [[Omnissiah]]. [[Tech-heresy]] is judged by the Mechanicus under its own law.
  • Xenophilia. Friendship with or aid to [[Xenos|xenos]], the study of their works without sanction, or the adoption of their customs. It is judged by the [[Ordo Xenos]] and by the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]].
  • Mutation. The bearing of a mutation is held to be a sign of corruption, and mutants are treated as [[Heretic|heretics]] by their very existence. The [[Mutant]] may be tolerated, quarantined or put to death, according to the world on which he is born.
  • Psychic heresy. The use of psychic power without sanction. The [[Psyker]] is the Imperium's unavoidable heretic, and unsanctioned psykers are taken by the [[Black Ships]] or put to death.
  • Chaos worship. Allegiance to the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]], in a [[Chaos cult]] or alone, is the worst heresy and is punished without mercy. The [[Ordo Malleus]] pursues the worst cases.

Judgement

Ecclesiarchy

Main article: [[Imperial Cult]]

The Ecclesiarchy judges heresy of doctrine and belief. Its confessors, priests and the [[Frateris Templar]] watch for unorthodox preaching, and it holds courts of faith that may order public penance or, for the gravest offences, hand the [[Heretic|heretic]] to the secular arm for execution. The Ecclesiarchy's own warrior orders, among them the [[Adepta Sororitas]], serve as its swords.

Inquisition

Main article: [[Inquisition]]

The [[Inquisition]] has the power to judge any heresy anywhere in the Imperium, and an [[Inquisitor]] answers to no court below the High Lords. Its [[Ordo Hereticus]], founded c. 380.M36Estimated; no record gives an exact year. in the wake of the [[Age of Apostasy]], is charged above all with the heresies of the Imperial Cult and the Imperial state, and it is known as the Witch Hunters. The [[Ordo Malleus]] hunts the [[Chaos]] worshippers and the daemonic, and the [[Ordo Xenos]] the [[Xenos|xenos]] heresies.

Adeptus Arbites

Main article: [[Adeptus Arbites]]

The [[Adeptus Arbites]] enforces the [[Lex Imperialis]] on every Imperial world. Its precinct-fortresses hold the records of crimes, and the Arbitrators arrest heretics on the order of a Judge. A heresy that is a crime against the law is theirs to try, and one that is a crime against the faith they pass to the Ecclesiarchy or the Inquisition.

Adeptus Mechanicus

Main article: [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]

The Mechanicus judges [[Dark Mechanicum|tech-heresy]] through its own officers. A tech-heretic may be declared Abominable, the Mechanicus term for an outcast, and driven out of the [[Forge world|forge worlds]] or hunted by the [[Skitarii]]. The Mechanicus rarely surrenders its heretics to the other bodies.

Punishment

The penalty for heresy depends on the gravity of the offence and the repentance of the offender.

  • Penance. A [[Heretic|heretic]] who repents may be sentenced to penance, a term of confinement, labour or ritual humiliation. A penitent may be enrolled among the [[Penitent]] or placed in a [[Penitent Engine]].
  • Servitude. The heretic may be sent to a penal world or a [[Penal Legion]] or made a servant of the Imperial machine, as a [[Servitor]] or an [[Arco-flagellant]]. The arco-flagellant is a condemned criminal or heretic whose mind has been burned away, leaving a killing machine for the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] to command.
  • Death. Most heretics are executed. The means vary by world, from the hanging of a [[Mutant|mutant]] to the burning of a [[Psyker|witch]].
  • Excommunicate Traitoris. The highest sentence of the Imperium, in which the accused is struck from every record and banished from the protection of the Imperium. It was pronounced on [[Horus]], and on the [[Traitor Legions]].
  • Exterminatus. When a world is judged beyond saving, an [[Inquisitor]] may order its destruction. [[Exterminatus]] is the final penalty for a heresy that has become a world.

Notable heresies and trials

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The largest heresy in the history of the Imperium began in 005.M31 at [[Isstvan III]], when [[Horus]] ordered the virus-bombing of the world and the massacre of the loyalists of four Legions. It ended in 014.M31, when the Emperor killed Horus in the [[Siege of Terra]]. The [[Traitor Legions]] were declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] and driven into the [[Eye of Terror]] in the [[Great Scouring]].

Goge Vandire and the Reign of Blood

In the 36th millennium the Master of the Administratum [[Goge Vandire]], who had also made himself [[Ecclesiarch]], ruled the Imperium in a tyranny called the Reign of Blood (c. 218–288.M36).Estimated from the length of the reign; no record gives an exact year. He set his own authority above the Emperor's and executed all who disagreed. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] teaches that his rule was a heresy and that the Emperor punished it with the [[Reign of Blood|Storm of the Emperor's Wrath]]. The Reign ended with his death and the Decree Passive of [[Sebastian Thor]], and the [[Ordo Hereticus]] was founded to guard against a repetition.

The Moirae Schism

Main article: [[Moirae Schism]]

The [[Moirae Schism]], in the 35th millennium, was a doctrinal war of the Adeptus Mechanicus over the nature of the Omnissiah, which split the [[Iron Hands]] and created the [[Sons of Medusa]]. It was fought as a tech-heresy, and its last holdouts were not purged until the 37th millennium.

The Plague of Unbelief

Main article: [[Plague of Unbelief]]

The [[Plague of Unbelief]] was the rebellion of the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis of Gathalamor, who denied the Ecclesiarchy's teaching and gathered a large following. The Administratum records it under 300–310.M36, and Ecclesiarchy tradition dates it only to some decades after the elevation of Thor. Other records give c. 410.M36.Imperial records disagree on the date; the Administratum register and the Ecclesiarchy chronicles differ by about a century. It was brought down by the [[Space Wolves]], and it was the last war of the [[Age of Apostasy]].

The Macharian Heresy

Main article: [[Macharian Crusade]]

The generals of the [[Lord Solar]] [[Macharius]] began the Macharian Heresy in 400.M41, when they fought over his conquests after his death. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] took some seventy years to put it down.

Cypher

Main article: [[Cypher]]

[[Cypher]], the Lord of the Fallen, is hunted by the [[Dark Angels]] as a [[Heretic|heretic]] and a [[Traitor|traitor]] to his Chapter. In 999.M41 he freed the resurrected [[Roboute Guilliman]] from his captivity in the [[Maelstrom]], in return for a promise that he would be brought before the Emperor. His case is regarded by the [[Inquisition]] as one of the strangest of the age.

The Great Rift

Main article: [[Great Rift]]

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 gave rise to a great number of apocalyptic cults, which are classed as heresy and have been pursued by the Ordo Hereticus ever since.
