Uixot was the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]] at the time of the Eighth [[Black Crusade]], in c. 999.M37. The records of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] describe him as an insular ruler, deeply withdrawn from the affairs of [[Terra]], who at first refused to receive the Custodes' envoy and who gave way only to an embassy that flattered him.

He is also recorded as the Fabricator-General who oversaw the addition of new power systems to the [[Golden Throne]]. Little else about him is open to Imperial scholars, and the dates of his accession and death are not given.

Background

By the late [[37th millennium]] the office of [[Fabricator-General]] had been held by a long line of rulers of whom the open record names almost none. Between the [[Age of Apostasy]], when [[Gastaph Hediatrix]] held it, and the Eighth [[Black Crusade]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] lists no holder, and the interval is nearly seven centuries. The Mechanicus keeps its rolls private, and its priesthood has long held that the affairs of [[Mars]] are the business of Mars alone. Under the [[Treaty of Olympus]] the Fabricator-General sits among the [[High Lords of Terra]] but cannot be appointed or removed by them.

The Eighth was one of the middle Black Crusades, in which [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] tested the defences of the Imperium with campaigns whose purposes were seldom visible at the time. The [[Inquisition]] later identified it as a ritual campaign, because the places and numbers of its massacres fell into a pattern.

The Custodes' request

During the crusade, [[Traitor|traitor]] forces held the Andromax System. The [[Adeptus Custodes]], who by long custom do not leave Terra, sought the help of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] against them. Uixot refused at first to receive the envoy. In the Custodes' account he was a ruler who preferred his own counsel, and he saw little reason to put the strength of Mars at risk for the defence of a system in which the Machine Cult had no direct interest.

The embassy that finally moved him did so by flattery. The Custodes recorded that appeals to his learning and to the greatness of Mars gained what the earlier approach had not, and that he then yielded the aid that was asked of him. The record does not state what forces Mars committed, nor the outcome at Andromax, and it is silent on whether Uixot's decision was shaped by anything other than pride.

The Golden Throne

Main article: [[Golden Throne]]

The same records credit Uixot with overseeing the addition of great new power systems to the Golden Throne, the machine that has sustained the [[Emperor of Mankind]] since 014.M31. The Throne is tended jointly by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]], and its engineering is among the most closely guarded work of the Mars priesthood. No details of Uixot's additions are published.

Legacy

Uixot's reputation rests on two short entries. He is the [[Fabricator-General]] whom the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] had to flatter, and he is one of the few whose name is attached to work on the Golden Throne. Later Fabricators-General, among them [[Oud Oudia Raskian]], continued to labour on the Throne's failing mechanisms without lasting success.
