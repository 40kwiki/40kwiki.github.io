The Ordo Chronos is one of the [[Ordos Minoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. It exists to investigate the consequences of travel through time by way of the [[Warp]], and above all the danger that someone might manipulate it on purpose. It is among the most obscure bodies of the Holy Ordos. Few [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] have heard of it, and fewer still know what it is for.

The Ordo is said to have been raised in the [[Jericho Reach]] to deal with the temporal accidents of [[Warp travel]]. It studied them for several centuries, then vanished. Every Inquisitor linked to it disappeared or went into hiding in the same moment, and no record says why. By 999.M41 the Ordo was active again, either returned from wherever it had gone or rebuilt from new members. Its best-known act in the Imperial record is not an investigation but a quarrel: the [[Chronostrife]], its internal dispute over the true date.

Remit

Warp-induced time slips

Time does not pass in the Warp as it does in [[Realspace|realspace]]. A vessel becalmed in the [[Immaterium]] may emerge after its crew have lived a few solar months, while centuries have gone by outside. Rarer, and more feared, are voyages that end with the ship expelled from the Warp at a time before it departed. The Ordo's founding concern was that such travellers, appearing in the past, might alter the proper course of events and so the fate of [[Mankind]] and the intentions of the [[Emperor of Mankind]].

Deliberate manipulation

Over the centuries its inquiries widened from accidents to cases that might have been intended, and the Ordo came to watch for any attempt to bend time on purpose. Imperial records of what it found are almost wholly absent.

The Imperial calendar

Main article: [[Imperial Dating System]]

The [[Imperial Dating System]] is listed among the matters the Ordo maintains alongside the [[Administratum]]. After the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41 the subjective flow of time on many worlds departed wildly from that on [[Terra]], and the Ordo took part in the decision that ended the single Imperial calendar (see below).

Matters outside the record

Imperial rumour links the Ordo to two further concerns. The first is the manipulation of time by the [[Daemon|daemons]] of [[Tzeentch]], the Changer of Ways, and by the sorcerers who serve him. The second is the time-keeping arts of the [[Necrons]], whose tomb worlds are said to hold devices that slow, fold or stop time. No sealed or open record confirms that the Ordo has ever studied either. They are given here as rumour only.

Secrecy and disappearance

The Ordo is secret even by the standard of the [[Inquisition]]. Its numbers are unknown and no [[Inquisitor]] of the Ordo is named in the open record, which is itself a mark of how thoroughly it has kept its work from the other Ordos. When it vanished, no body of Inquisitors claimed to know why. Imperial rumour holds that [[Renegade]] factions of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] were involved, pursuing technologies the [[Cult Mechanicus]] has long forbidden. The rumour is unsupported. It is repeated chiefly by those few who remember that the Ordo ever existed, and some of them say that its members remain watchful, ready to return if the doom they feared should come.

The Chronostrife

Main article: [[Logos Historica Verita]]

When the Ordo returned, it came with a feud. The Chronostrife, also called the Chronostrife of Terra, was a bitter and continuing dispute among its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] over the [[Imperial Dating System|Imperial calendar]]. In the ten thousand years since the [[Great Crusade]] the dating system had been worn by dogmatic adherence on the one side and thoughtless revision on the other, and rival calendars had grown out of it.

The dispute became known beyond the Ordo after [[Roboute Guilliman]] returned and founded the [[Logos Historica Verita]] to recover Mankind's history. Near the end of the first phase of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], he calculated the year by the five principal calendars and found that they set it anywhere from the early [[41st millennium]] to a full millennium later, not counting the lesser and more [[Heresy|heretical]] reckonings. After conference with his historitors and with the Ordo Chronos, he put before the [[High Lords of Terra]] a decree that ended the demand for a single reckoning. Each region now keeps a chronology anchored to the moment the Rift opened over it. The Vigilus Template is the best known of these local calendars.

Relations with other Ordos

The Ordo Chronos has no Chamber Militant and no recorded dealings with a Chamber Militant. Its link with the [[Ordo Malleus]], which studies the Warp, is natural but unrecorded. The [[Logos Historica Verita|Chronostrife]] touches the work of the [[Ordo Originatus]], which tries to establish the Imperium's true history, and of the [[Ordo Redactus]], which tries to bury it, since neither task is possible without a date. The [[Ordo Machinum]] would be the Ordo to ask about the forbidden technologies named in the rumour. All of this is inference, and the Ordos themselves are silent.

Known members

None are named. The roll of the Ordo is sealed, lost, or never written.
