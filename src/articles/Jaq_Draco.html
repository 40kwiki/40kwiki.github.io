Jaq Draco was a secret [[Inquisitor]] of the [[Ordo Malleus]], active in the late 38th and early 39th millennia. According to the surviving reports he uncovered a conspiracy of senior Inquisitors known as the [[Ordo Hydra]], and learned from it something about the condition of the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor's]] mind that the Imperium has never admitted.

The Inquisition officially acknowledges no record of him. Some scholars of the [[Adeptus Terra]] hold that he never lived and that his deeds were an invention of the [[Aeldari]] or of the [[forces of Chaos]], meant to unsettle faith in the Inquisition and the Throne. The Inquisition encourages that view, and any new mention of the name is liable to be purged as hostile disinformation. This article records the account as it survives in sealed Ordo files and in the testimony of those who claim to have known him.

Early life

Draco was born in the late 38th millennium on [[Xerxes Quintus]], a low-technology agri-world full of [[psyker]]s and mutants. The planet had been cut off for thousands of years and was recontacted by the [[Imperium of Mankind]] about a century before his birth. After his parents, Imperial geneticists posted to the world, were murdered by possessed local psykers, he was raised in an [[Adeptus Ministorum]] orphanage.

At twelve he showed psychic gifts of unusual purity: he could perceive the [[Astronomican]] and hide his presence from [[Warp]] entities. A [[Black Ships|Black Ship]] took him, and he entered the Hidden Chambers of the Ordo Malleus. By thirty-five his body was covered from the neck down in ritual markings, one for each daemon he had overcome. He was never a strict member of the [[Puritan]] faction and was willing to entertain doubt, particularly where the lives of civilians and his retinue were at stake.

The Ordo Hydra

Early in the 39th millennium Draco was sent to [[Stalinvast]], where the [[Illuminati]] agent [[Zephro Carnelian]] approached him. Farseers allied to that cabal had foreseen that Draco would be needed to prevent a disaster for the whole galaxy. The Illuminati steered him into the Ordo Hydra, a faction hidden in the Inquisition, as an unwilling double agent.

He learned that the Ordo Hydra meant to bind all of [[Humanity]] into a single psychic mind and turn that power against [[Chaos]]. Such a working would very probably have created a fifth Chaos God. Draco travelled to [[Terra]], entered the [[Imperial Palace]] and spoke mind to mind with the Emperor in the [[Golden Throne]] chamber. He asked Him to stop the plot. The audience earned him the honorific Auditorii Imperator, and it left him doubtful of the Emperor's stability and of the Imperium's chances of survival. The records do not say what was done to the Ordo Hydra, but the plot failed.

The Black Library

Draco then spent a hundred years in stasis. On waking he learned that the Inquisition had declared him Renegade for the ''Exterminatus'' on Stalinvast and for his dealings with the Illuminati. He then did what no mortal is known to have done: without Aeldari leave he entered the [[Webway]], found the [[Black Library]] and took from it the ''[[Rhana Dandra|Book of Rhana Dandra]]'', an Aeldari prophecy of the end of the galaxy.

He learned the Aeldari tongue from a captured [[Harlequin]] and used the book to locate a crossroads in the Webway where time can be reversed. [[Magnus the Red]] of the [[Thousand Sons]] sought the same book for [[Tzeentch]] and attacked Draco's refuge on Sabulorb, which was later lost to anarchy and disaster.

Death

When events at the crossroads went against him, Draco tricked a companion, the Imperial Fists captain [[Lexandro d'Arquebus]], into believing he was possessed by a daemon of Tzeentch. The companion killed him as they had agreed beforehand. His mind may have survived as a disembodied presence in the Webway.The sources place his death in the early 39th millennium, but also place a century of stasis before it. Imperial accounts do not settle the spacing of these events.

Retinue and works

His retinue included the [[Callidus Assassin]] [[Meh'lindi]], the Squat enginseer Grimm and several Navigators and astropaths. He travelled in the stealth ship ''Tormentum Malorum'', and wrote the ''Liber Secretorum'', his account of the Ordo Hydra, now held in the ''Librarium Obscurum'' of the Ordo Malleus.
