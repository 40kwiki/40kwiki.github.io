The Lamenters are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Blood Angels]] created in the "Cursed" [[Twenty-First Founding]] of the early [[36th millennium]]. Their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] was altered on [[Mars]] at their making, and they appear to suffer far less than other sons of [[Sanguinius]] from the [[Red Thirst]] and the [[Black Rage]], though the secret of that change may be lost.

The Chapter's history is one of misfortune. It fought on the side of the Secessionists in the [[Badab War]] out of loyalty to the [[Astral Claws]], was all but destroyed by the [[Minotaurs]] at Optera in 908.M41, and on the [[Penitent|penitent]] crusade imposed on it afterwards ran into [[Hive Fleet Kraken]], which reduced it to fewer than four hundred warriors by 993.M41. It survives in the [[Era Indomitus]] under its Chapter Master, Malakim Phoros, and has been rebuilt in part with [[Primaris Space Marines]].

History

Founding and early history

The Lamenters were raised in the early [[36th millennium]], in the Founding that later became infamous for the dark ends of so many of its [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. When renegade Chapters of that Founding rose against the Imperium in the early [[37th millennium]], the Lamenters withdrew to the Imperium's fringes rather than fight their fellows, and operated for long periods as a crusading force near the [[Milky Way|galactic core]]. In 537.M38, at the Siege of Corillia during the ninth [[Black Crusade]], they were relieved by a battle group of the [[Ultramarines]] and [[White Scars]], a debt the Chapter never forgot.

In 587.M41 the [[High Lords of Terra]] named the Lamenters one of the Maelstrom Warders, the Chapters charged with guarding the [[Maelstrom]]. In 698.M41 they joined a force under [[Marneus Calgar]] with the Angels of Absolution, the [[Marines Errant]] and the Silver Skulls in the Charadon campaign, and in 701.M41 they answered Calgar's call again for the Corinth Crusade, fighting to free Slaughterhouse III from the Orks. In 812.M41 a Lamenters force escorting the Rogue Trader Kazandus Lan to the Jericho Reach was stranded at Falon's Lament when its ship failed during translation.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

Among the Maelstrom Warders, the Astral Claws under [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]] gave the Lamenters a respect and a supply of war materiel they had seldom received from other Chapters. When Huron declared the secession of the Badab Sector, the Lamenters joined him, believing that they defended the traditional autonomy of the Adeptus Astartes. By 908.M41 the Chapter had suffered heavy losses. In that year the Minotaurs discovered the battle barge Mater Lachrymarum taking on supplies above the feral world of Optera, drew the Lamenters' fleet back to defend it, and destroyed it in a close-quarters void battle. The Minotaurs' Reclusiarch Ivanus Enkomi led the boarding of the strike cruiser Dawn Reaper. Phoros was at first reported slain.

In 913.M41, after the fall of Badab Primaris, the surviving Secessionists were tried before a court of five Chapter Masters who had taken no part in the war, according to Astartes tradition and against the wishes of the Inquisitor Legate. The Lamenters, who had come to recognise Huron's corruption, were spared and sent on a hundred-year penitent crusade.

Hive Fleet Kraken

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Kraken]]

The penitent crusade led the Chapter into the path of Hive Fleet Kraken during the Second Tyrannic War in 992.M41. When the fighting ended in 993.M41, barely three companies' worth of battle-brothers remained.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The Lamenters have since received Primaris reinforcements. In the early 42nd millennium a Lamenters strike force named Latenzar fought in the Aberrus System alongside the Iron Lords, the Knights of the Raven and the [[Scythes of the Emperor]].The Aberrus campaign is dated only to M42; the date of the Primaris reinforcement is not recorded.

Organisation and gene-seed

See also: [[Gene-seed]]

The Lamenters keep closer to the standard panoply of the [[Codex Astartes]] than other [[Blood Angels]] successors, probably for ease of supply as a fleet-based Chapter. The full extent of the changes made to their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] on [[Mars]] was hidden even from the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] of the day. Surviving records show that very few Lamenters have fallen to the Red Thirst over the centuries.
