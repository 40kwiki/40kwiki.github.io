The Pharos was a biotechnological device built in the distant past by a xenos civilisation, later identified as the [[Necrons]]. It harnessed the psychic energy of empathy to guide ships safely through the [[Warp]]. It worked on a far smaller scale than the later [[Astronomican]], which was built by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] to serve the whole of the Imperium.

Imperial records state that the device was buried in a mountain. The mountain was later found to hold the device's core, and the Imperium studied its workings. The Pharos is treated in Imperial scholarship as an early proof that the Warp could be mastered by means other than psykers.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the mountain holding the Pharos was at the centre of the [[Battle of Sotha]]. Both loyalist and traitor forces wanted control of the device, and the fighting around the mountain was among the fiercest of the campaign.
