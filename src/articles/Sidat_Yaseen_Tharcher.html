Sidat Yaseen Tharcher was the Chirurgeon-General of the [[Orders Hospitaller]] and a member of the [[Council of Terra]] during the [[Horus Heresy]]. In those days the office was held by a man; the [[Adepta Sororitas]] did not yet exist, and the Orders Hospitaller were a secular body of public health.

The health crisis

At a council meeting in the early days of the [[Siege of Terra]] (013.M31), Tharcher reported a severe health crisis in the cities of the [[Terra|Throneworld]]. The effects of the fighting, and the taint of the [[Warp]] brought by the [[Traitor Legions]], were spreading among the population.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

Nothing else is preserved of his life, his order or his death.
