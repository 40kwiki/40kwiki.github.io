The Legio Victorum, whose name means "Foe Slayers", is a Loyalist [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Collegia Titanica]], one of the military arms of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Imperial records say very little about its founding, but it was raised before the [[Great Crusade]] began, and its origins are traced to the [[Age of Strife]]. Its war cry is "For the alien, nothing!" Over time the Legio grew so large that it divided into three separate Titan Legions, each based on one of the three [[Forge World]]s of the [[Triplex System]].

The three divisions are Legio Victorum I, the original Foe Slayers, based on [[Triplex Galatia]]; Legio Victorum II, the Foe Hammers, based on [[Triplex Phall]]; and Legio Victorum III, the Foe Breakers, based on [[Triplex Thule]]. In the wake of the [[Horus Heresy]], only the Loyalist elements of Legio Victorum I survived and kept the original name and cognomen.

Origins and the Great Crusade

Imperial histories say that the first Legio Victorum built a petty empire for its parent forge in the Age of Strife. It subdued the isolated pockets of Humanity it found and destroyed the xenos marauders that infested that edge of the galaxy, and it earned the epithet "Foe Slayers" by the count of the dead it left. As [[Triplex Phall]] and [[Triplex Thule]] were founded beside [[Triplex Galatia]], the Legio split. Victorum II, the Foe Hammers, was formed of the most veteran princeps and built around the Apocalypse-class Titan Apex Maxis. Victorum III, the Foe Breakers, was filled by the most orthodox and militant tech-adepts of the system.

With the Great Crusade the Legio lost its unchallenged place. Victorum II went to the stars with the Warmaster [[Horus]], perfecting planetary assault with overwhelming firepower, and was composed almost wholly of Battle Titans. Victorum I stayed in the Eastern Fringe as a defensive force, because Mars sought to keep it from the riches of the crusade, and became expert at fighting in cities without ruinous damage. Victorum III was barred from the crusade by the schemes of both Mars and Galatia, and served as a force of raiders.

During the Great Crusade the Legio's Titans fought at [[Isstvan III]] during the Compliance of 994.M30, alongside the 27th Expeditionary Fleet under the primarch [[Corvus Corax]] and the [[Legio Adamantus]]. They crushed the sophisticated forces of the Istvaanians and were given a place of honour in the triumph of the Redarth Valley.

The Horus Heresy

When the [[Schism of Mars]] began in the opening days of the Horus Heresy (005–006.M31), the first Titans of the Legio were still on Mars, waiting for transport to other war zones of the Great Crusade. Civil war broke out between the loyalist [[Mechanicum]] and the forces of the [[Dark Mechanicum]]. The Legio held its positions on the Red Planet until Imperial forces relieved it after Horus was defeated at the [[Siege of Terra]].

The Legio itself was torn apart by the war. Victorum II returned at the command of Horus Lupercal and landed nearly its full strength on Triplex Thule to pacify that loyalist outpost and prove its loyalty to the approaching [[Night Lords]]. The Somnus Etema, a Reaver-class Titan of Victorum III, held the gate of the Thulian forge-fanes alone against a maniple of the Foe Hammers until it was shattered, and then closed with the Warlord Glory of Phall and destroyed both machines by detonating its core. Fewer than nine light Titans survived on Thule. They pledged themselves to [[Lion El'Jonson]] when the [[Dark Angels]] liberated the Triplex System, and they commanded the elements of Victorum II that surrendered. The Lion gave the Apex Maxis to the Foe Breakers, who renamed it Somnus Perpetua.

Titans of the Legio took part in the [[Thramas Crusade]] (007–009.M31), where traitor and loyalist elements of the Legio fought each other, and in the Battle of Ice World Tralsak in 011.M31, where the traitor Foe Slayers fought beside the [[Legio Magna]] against the loyalist [[Legio Atarus]] and [[Legio Agravides]]. Neither side won. The Legio also took part in the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, one of several Titan Legions in the battle that decided the fate of the Throneworld.

After the Heresy

Imperial records contain nothing further about the Legio for ten thousand years. In 992.M41 the Legio was mandated to cover the general retreat from the [[Orpheus Sector]] after the Imperial losses in the Orphean War, and it sent a full demi-legio to the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. In the same year [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] threatened the [[Ultima Segmentum]] and the shadow of the swarm fell on the Legio's home forge world of Triplex Phall. The Legio survived, though at a high cost. In 998.M41 Legio Victorum Titans were among those deployed to defend [[Armageddon]] against the second Ork WAAAGH! led by [[Ghazghkull Thraka]], and they are known to have fought on the Plains of Anthrand and along the South Primus Front.

The Legio has never wavered in its devotion to the Emperor, whom it reveres as the [[Omnissiah]] embodied in each Titan. Its crews hold that Mankind alone has the right to exist in the galaxy, and the Legio's hatred of the alien has extinguished species the Administratum had listed as harmless.
