The Legio Victorum, whose name means "Foe Slayers", is a Loyalist [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Collegia Titanica]], one of the military arms of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Imperial records say very little about its founding, but it was raised before the [[Great Crusade]] began. Over time the Legio grew so large that it divided into three separate Titan Legions, each based on one of the three [[Forge World]]s of the [[Triplex System]].

The three divisions are Legio Victorum I, the original Foe Slayers, based on [[Triplex Galatia]]; Legio Victorum II, the Foe Hammers, based on [[Triplex Phall]]; and Legio Victorum III, the Foe Breakers, based on [[Triplex Thule]]. In the wake of the [[Horus Heresy]], only the Loyalist elements of Legio Victorum I survived and kept the original name and cognomen.

The Horus Heresy

When the [[Schism of Mars]] began in the opening days of the Horus Heresy, the first Titans of the Legio were still on Mars, waiting for transport to other war zones of the Great Crusade. The civil war then broke out between the Loyalist [[Mechanicum]] and the traitor forces of the [[Dark Mechanicum]]. The Legio fought on the Red Planet until Imperial forces relieved it after Horus was defeated at the [[Siege of Terra]].

Earlier in the war, the Legio fought at [[Isstvan III]] during the Isstvan III Compliance of 994.M30, alongside the 27th Expeditionary Fleet under the [[Corvus Corax|Primarch Corvus Corax]]. The Legio also took part in the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, one of several Titan Legions in the battle that decided the fate of the Imperial Throneworld.

After the Heresy

Imperial records contain nothing further about the Legio for ten thousand years. In 992.M41 the Legio was mandated to cover the general retreat from the [[Orpheus Sector]] after the Imperial losses in the Orphean War, and it sent a full demi-legio to the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. In 998.M41 Legio Victorum Titans were among those deployed to defend [[Armageddon]] against the second Ork WAAAGH! led by [[Ghazghkull Thraka]], and they are known to have fought on the planet's battlefields.
