Fenris is a [[Death world|death world]] of ice, ocean and volcanic islands on the border of the [[Segmentum Solar]] and the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], and the homeworld of the [[Space Wolves]], who call themselves the Vlka Fenryka. It is the world on which the primarch [[Leman Russ]] grew up among the tribes and the wolves, and it holds the Fang, the Chapter's mountain fortress on the continent of Asaheim. The planet is one of the harshest inhabited worlds of the Imperium, and its people, who know the Space Wolves as the Sky Warriors, live in a constant struggle with the cold, the sea and the planet's predators.

Fenris has been attacked many times since the Heresy, most seriously by the [[Thousand Sons]] in 742.M32 and again in 999.M41, when [[Magnus the Red]] led the [[Siege of the Fenris System]]. The Space Wolves have defended it against Chaos, against renegade crusades and, more than once, against other Imperial institutions.

History

Before the Imperium

Fenris was settled in the [[Dark Age of Technology]], but its people lost almost all knowledge of their origins during the [[Age of Strife]]. By the time the Imperium found the planet, it was inhabited by feuding tribes of seafarers and mountain folk who lived by raiding and hunting. The tribes preserved their history in sagas, and spoke of a time when the ancestors had come from the sky.

Leman Russ

The capsule carrying the infant Russ landed on Fenris in the age of Old Night. According to the sagas he was raised by a she-wolf, grew up among the wolves, and came among men as a wild creature. He was captured by King Thengir of the Russ tribe, who adopted him, and in time he became king of all the tribes of Fenris. When the Emperor came to Fenris, the sagas say, he challenged Russ to a contest of strength and won it by knocking the primarch down, after which Russ swore loyalty to him and took command of the VI Legion. The Legion then made Fenris its home and recruited its warriors from the tribes.

The Horus Heresy

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Space Wolves were sent against Prospero and destroyed the homeworld of the Thousand Sons in the [[Burning of Prospero]], a deed that made the two Legions enemies for ever after. Fenris itself was not taken in the Heresy, and the Wolves fought at the [[Siege of Terra]] before taking part in the [[Great Scouring]].

In 211.M31, at the 197th Feast of the Emperor's Ascension held in the Fang, Russ fell into a vision, left the hall with his closest companions and departed for the Eye of Terror. He left [[Bjorn the Fell-Handed]] to lead the Chapter as the first Great Wolf. The Space Wolves await his return at the Wolftime, which their prophecies place at the end of the age.

From M32 to M41

In 742.M32, in the First Battle of the Fang, Magnus the Red drew the Great Wolf Harek Ironhelm and eleven Great Companies away from Fenris and then attacked the planet with the Thousand Sons. Bjorn, now a Dreadnought, led the defence of the Fang until the rest of the Chapter returned. Ironhelm was killed by Magnus in single combat before the daemon primarch was driven off.

In the [[Plague of Unbelief]] of M36, the renegade cardinal Bucharis of Gathalamor besieged Fenris for three years. The Fang held, and Wolf Lord Kyrl Grimblood returned from the Eye of Terror to break the siege. In 444.M41, after the First War for [[Armageddon]], the Space Wolves' defence of Armageddon's citizens against Inquisitorial purge led to the Months of Shame, in which Imperial ships fought each other in the orbit of Fenris until Bjorn arbitrated a peace. In 886.M41 the Ecclesiarchy, sending envoys to examine the Chapter's beliefs, was turned away by force, and an expedition of three Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]] was repelled after three weeks of fighting.

Fenris was also raided by the [[Drukhari]] in 641.M41, when tribespeople and aspirants were taken from Asaheim, and in 933.M41 the Necron [[Trazyn the Infinite]] attacked Midgardia, a neighbouring world of the system.

The Siege of the Fenris System

In 999.M41 Magnus the Red returned with the Thousand Sons and an army of daemons. The whole Fenris System was attacked, and Fenris itself was invaded. The Imperial response was divided: forces acting for the Inquisition, supported by other Chapters, moved to purge the tribes who had witnessed the daemon primarch, and the Space Wolves fought them as well as the invaders. The Wolves under Logan Grimnar defeated the invasion, but the deaths on Fenris allowed Magnus to complete a ritual that drew his [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] out of the Eye of Terror and into realspace beside the ruins of Prospero.The Inquisition's records of its own part in the siege are sealed. The Space Wolves' account, preserved in the sagas of the Fang, describes the purge of the tribes as an atrocity, and it has further worsened the Chapter's relations with the Ordo Malleus.

The Era Indomitus

The Space Wolves have received [[Primaris Space Marines]] and have fought across both halves of the divided Imperium. The tribes of Fenris, much reduced by the siege, have been rebuilt from survivors and from settlers brought from other islands. Early in the Era Indomitus the Wolf Priest Ulrik the Slayer avenged the aspirants taken by the Drukhari in 641.M41, killing the haemonculus responsible.

Geography

Fenris follows an elongated orbit around its star. For most of its year it is held in a long winter, when its seas freeze, its land masses are covered in ice and storms are almost constant. As the planet approaches its sun, it enters a brief summer, which Fenrisians call the Time of Fire: the ice melts, the seas rise, volcanoes erupt and whole islands are lost to earthquakes and floods. The returning cold is called Helwinter.

Most of the planet is covered by a single ocean, the World-Sea, broken by island chains and drifting ice. Its one permanent continent is Asaheim, a land of mountains and glaciers, where the Fang stands. The tribes live on the islands, on the mountains of Asaheim and on the glaciers.

The Fang

The Fang is the fortress of the Space Wolves, carved into the highest mountain of Asaheim. Its halls, armouries and forges extend deep into the rock, and its upper levels contain void shields and landing platforms. The great hall of the Fang, where the Chapter holds its feasts, is also where Russ departed in 211.M31. The fortress also contains the Chapter's gene-seed vaults, its trophy halls, and the tombs of its heroes.

Wildlife

Fenris is known for its predators: the great Fenrisian wolves, from which the Chapter takes its name and its symbols, the larger Thunderwolves, giant sea serpents, krakens and mammoth-like beasts. Ice trolls and other creatures are recorded in the sagas. Its plant life is sparse and mostly confined to the islands during the short summer.

Society

The people of Fenris live in clans and tribes ruled by jarls. They are seafarers, hunters and raiders, and fight one another over the scarce resources of the islands. They know almost nothing of the Imperium, and regard the Space Wolves as the Sky Warriors, the chosen of the All-Father, who descend from the heavens to take the bravest of the dead and dying to live among the gods. The tribes speak Fenrisian dialects and keep their history in sagas, recited by skjalds.

The Space Wolves recruit by watching the tribes' wars and taking the warriors who show the most courage, often at the point of death. Recruits are tested by ordeals in the wilderness of Asaheim before they are accepted. The Chapter's culture, its sagas, its feasts and its Wolf Priests, preserves the customs of the tribes, and the Space Wolves have resisted every attempt by the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] to impose a more conventional faith on them.

The Chapter rules Fenris but does not govern it in any Imperial sense. There is no planetary governor, no tithe and no Administratum presence, and the tribes are left to their wars, which the Space Wolves consider a means of producing warriors.
