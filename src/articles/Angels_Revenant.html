The Angels Revenant are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a successor of the [[Ultramarines]]. They follow the [[Codex Astartes]] closely. Raised in the Eighth Founding during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], they spent most of their history on crusade in the western [[Segmentum Tempestus]], and became the guardians of the Orpheus Sector after the Orphean [[War of Faith]].

In 991.M41 the [[Necrons]] of the Maynarkh Dynasty cracked open their homeworld Libethra. The Chapter was believed lost, and a day of requiem was decreed across the segmentum. During the [[Indomitus Crusade]] a Torchbearer task force found survivors still fighting beneath the surface, and reinforced them with [[Primaris Space Marines]].

History

Crusade and the Orpheus Sector

The Angels Revenant were by reputation noble and aloof even among [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]]. In the Rising Storm that followed the [[Age of Apostasy]], the [[Segmentum|segmentum]] authorities launched the Dark Marches Crusade under Lord Tempestus Hal Orpheus, a descendant of the [[Rogue Trader]] house that first explored the region. The Chapter gave four companies to it in the period 610–759.M36.

In the Orphean [[War of Faith]], 903–922.M39, the Orpheus Sector was brought to the brink by the military and ruling classes fighting each other under the influence of [[Chaos]], until a crusade of zealots, militia, Guardsmen and pilgrims under the saint Marduk arrived. At Colkasth in 921.M39 a [[Hive world|Hive World]] corrupted by the Death Mongers stood against the victorious Imperials, and an Imperial fleet attacking it was destroyed by vessels now recognised as [[Necrons|Necron]]. The Angels Revenant strike cruiser Intercessor saw the loss, and carried fresh scars from void combat with them. After the war the minor colony of Libethra, which Marduk had favoured, became a [[Shrine world|Shrine World]] and a cemetery for the war's dead. The Chapter, until then fleet-based, was given it as a domain and the care of its reliquaries. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]'s Orphean branch paid for a fortress-monastery, and the [[Feral world|feral world]] of Patara supplied recruits.

The Orphean War and the fall of Libethra

In 990.M41 the Warp turbulence over the western Orpheus Sector, which had lasted three centuries, was broken by a shock in the [[Warp|Immaterium]]. [[Psyker|Psykers]] and [[Astropathy|astropaths]] died or were mind-burned, and signals bore a single phrase: "Maynarkh comes." Ships crossing a line of darkness between Tlaloc and Epirus did not return. Libethra fell silent. When the sector learned its fate in 991.M41 the world was a sea of ash and lava, and the system was littered with wreckage. A tomb ship later seen above Amarah bore scars of bombardment cannon fired at point-blank range, and it is thought the Necrons broke through Libethra's crust and loosed the molten core only after the Chapter had resisted to the last. The Synod Tempestus decreed a day of requiem and bells tolled on a thousand worlds.

Era Indomitus

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the [[Indomitus Crusade]] sent a task force of Torchbearers to find the Chapter, expecting to refound it wholly from [[Primaris Space Marines]]. They found Libethra dead and airless, but their instruments picked up fighting deep within it. The last Firstborn survivors were still at war with the Necrons below the crust. The Torchbearers joined them, the enemy was defeated, and the Chapter was rebuilt with Primaris reinforcements and the gift of the technology to make more. Its warriors are now called the Angels of Libethra.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter held closely to the Codex in organisation and wargear. Because its crusades often cut it off from resupply, it kept to common patterns of Astartes equipment and valued the repair of a fallen brother's wargear as highly as the veneration of the dead, which bound each generation to the last. It kept a core of relic [[Power armour|Mark IV]] armour from the days of the Great Crusade and the Heresy. A strong supporter of the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the Imperial Creed, the Chapter received generous Ministorum help for its fortress.
