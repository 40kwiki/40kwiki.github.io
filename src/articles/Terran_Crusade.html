The Terran Crusade was the journey of the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] from [[Macragge]] to [[Terra]] in 999.M41, shortly after his restoration to life. Guilliman set out to consult the [[Emperor of Mankind]] on the state of the Imperium he had found on waking. His fleet was thrown into the [[Maelstrom]] by his brother [[Magnus the Red]], captured by the Greater Daemon [[Kairos Fateweaver]], and imprisoned in a Blackstone Fortress held by the [[Red Corsairs]]. Freed by the Fallen Angel [[Cypher]] and the [[Harlequins]], the survivors fought their way through the [[Webway]], defeating the Bloodthirster [[Skarbrand]], and emerged on [[Luna]], where they drove back the [[Thousand Sons]] and sealed the gate behind them.

On Terra Guilliman was admitted alone to the Emperor's [[Sanctum Imperialis|throne room]] and remained there for a full day. When he came out he summoned the [[High Lords of Terra]], took up again the office of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] that he had held after the [[Horus Heresy]], and announced the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. Imperial historians treat his arrival on Terra as the true beginning of the [[Era Indomitus]].

Background

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] had lain in stasis in the [[Macragge|Temple of Correction]] on Macragge since the [[Battle of Thessala]] in 121.M31, poisoned by the blade of [[Fulgrim]]. After the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41, the survivors of the [[Celestinian Crusade]], led by the living saint [[Celestine]], [[Inquisitor]] [[Katarinya Greyfax]] and [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]], were brought to Macragge through the Webway by the Ynnari prophet [[Yvraine]]. While the fleets of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] attacked the world, Yvraine and Cawl restored the primarch to life, Yvraine by the power of the [[Aeldari]] death-god [[Ynnead]] and Cawl by the life-sustaining [[Artificer armour|Armour of Fate]].

Guilliman broke the siege of Macragge and, over some seven months, cleared [[Ultramar]] of the forces of [[Chaos]]. In that time he studied the history of the ten thousand years he had missed. The Imperium he found was a theocracy, ruled by fear and ignorance, that worshipped his father as a god against his father's own teaching. He was close to despair, and resolved to go to [[Terra]] and stand before the Golden Throne to learn what the Emperor wished.

Course of the crusade

Departure and the Maelstrom

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] gathered every force that could reach [[Macragge]] through the worsening [[Warp]]: his own [[Ultramarines]] under Captain [[Cato Sicarius]], contingents of a dozen [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], the [[Grey Knights]] of Grand Master Aldrik Voldus, the [[Black Templars]] of Marshal Marius Amalrich, the [[Emperor's Champion]], and [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]], [[Katarinya Greyfax|Greyfax]] and [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]]. He left the defence of [[Ultramar]] to [[Chapter Master]] [[Marneus Calgar]].

As the fleet passed near the Maelstrom it was intercepted by Magnus the Red, who had lately struck at [[Fenris]] and now came to stop his brother. The Crimson King worked a ritual that tore the fleet from realspace and cast it into the Maelstrom. For months the crusade wandered from daemon world to daemon world, losing ships to every crossing. The Ultramarines' records describe Guilliman in this period as burdened by guilt, a weakness the daemons of the Maelstrom learned to use.

Captivity

A psychic message from the Farseer [[Eldrad Ulthran]] gave the fleet a chain of landmarks to follow to the Maelstrom's edge. There, at a graveyard of wrecked ships, the crusade was ambushed by the Red Corsairs and Kairos Fateweaver, a Lord of Change whom Abaddon had bound and sent to stop the primarch. Fateweaver bound Guilliman in chains formed from his own guilt and forced the fleet to surrender by threatening his life. The prisoners were taken to a Blackstone Fortress hidden in the Maelstrom, held by the Red Corsairs lord Verngar the Apostate, where Fateweaver meant to leave Guilliman to rot.

The Blackstone Fortress

Main article: [[Blackstone Fortress]]

The Fortress was then attacked by Skarbrand the Exiled One, a Bloodthirster who had come for the primarch's skull. In the confusion Cypher, the hooded Fallen Angel, and the Shadowseer Sylandri Veilwalker of the Masque of the Veiled Path reached the cells. Cypher freed Guilliman on condition that he would be brought before the Golden Throne. Veilwalker's dance broke Fateweaver's sorcery and the crystal fetters shattered.

With their ships lost, the Imperials had only one way out: a Webway gate at the heart of the Fortress. They fought through its corridors against Red Corsairs and daemons of both Khorne and Tzeentch, assisted at one point by warriors of the [[Legion of the Damned]]. At the heart of the Fortress Skarbrand fell on them. Marius Amalrich fought the daemon alone and was killed, but his Black Sword left a deep wound. Guilliman then fought Skarbrand in single combat while the daemon's rage overcame his own warriors, and at last threw him from the platform with his body torn apart.Ultramarines records say Skarbrand was slain; the Grey Knights hold that a Greater Daemon of his rank cannot be destroyed outside the Warp, and record him as banished.

The Webway and Luna

Veilwalker led the survivors into the Webway toward a forgotten spar that ended on [[Luna]], Terra's moon. [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] had foreseen their road. The [[Thousand Sons]] harried the crusade through the [[Webway|Labyrinth Dimension]], and as the gate opened on Luna's Mare Tempestus the Crimson King's Legion came through behind them, meaning to fall on the [[Terra|Throneworld]] itself.

The Battle of Luna was fought among the macrohives and scrapyards of the moon. Guilliman was joined by [[Imperial Fists]] from the Sol defences, a cadre of the [[Sisters of Silence]] and warriors of the [[Adeptus Custodes]]. When Magnus stepped through the gate in person, the primarch fought him and, with the [[Harlequins]] and the null-maidens beside him, drove him back through. The gate was sealed permanently so that it could never again be used against Terra. [[Cypher]]'s dark-armoured companions fought with the Imperials throughout.

Arrival on Terra

The survivors were carried from [[Luna]] to the [[Eternity Wall Spaceport|Eternity Wall spaceport]] by an [[Aquila (symbol)|aquila]] lander of the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] under Shield-Captain Ty Adronitus, and from there in procession to the [[Imperial Palace]]. Billions watched them pass. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s own account records his shock at what Terra had become: its oceans gone, its surface buried in gothic construction, its people queuing for generations before the offices of the [[Administratum]].

At the door of [[Sanctum Imperialis|the throne room]] the Aquila Commander Kalim Varanor ruled that the primarch alone might enter. Guilliman ordered [[Cypher]] and his warriors arrested; he had recognised the sword on Cypher's back. [[Dark Angels|The Fallen]] Angel submitted, and a few hours later escaped from a warded prison block without trace. [[Belisarius Cawl]] exchanged coded words with a Martian priest at the door and left at once for [[Mars]].

Guilliman passed alone into the throne room. He remained there for one full day. No record of what passed between the primarch and the Emperor has been made public. When he came out he called an immediate assembly of the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]].

Aftermath

In the days that followed, [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] addressed the [[Senatorum Imperialis]], claimed the Emperor's mandate, removed several [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] from office and replaced them with his own appointees. He warned the council of a Warp disaster spreading across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]; the [[Astropathy|astropathic]] reports that arrived soon after, of the [[Great Rift]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]], bore him out. He announced that new armies would be raised, new fleets built, and that [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]]'s forges would bring forth new warriors, the [[Primaris Space Marines]]. Soon after, the Warp storm that had followed him reached Terra, and he defeated a Khornate host at the Lion's Gate of the Palace. Then, rather than defend Terra from behind its walls, he led the [[Indomitus Crusade]] out into the galaxy.

The companions of the crusade went their separate ways at the Palace. [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]] left to carry her blessing to the war zones; [[Katarinya Greyfax|Greyfax]] went to report to her Ordo. The survivors of the [[Black Templars]] carried Amalrich's [[Sigismund|Black Sword]] home.

Legacy

The Terran Crusade is remembered in the Imperium as a pilgrimage as much as a campaign. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]'s account treats the primarch's journey through the Warp as a trial set by the Emperor, and his audience as a [[Emperor of Mankind|revelation]]; the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] has never confirmed either reading. Among the Ultramarines, the names of those who fell on the road are kept in the [[Macragge|Temple of Correction]]. Inquisitorial studies of the campaign note how many of its outcomes turned on the aid of [[Xenos|xenos]] and of the Fallen, and the question of what [[Cypher]] sought at the Throne remains unanswered.
