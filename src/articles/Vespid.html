The Vespid are an insectoid alien species who are members of the [[T'au Empire]]. In the T'au language they are called Mal'kor, from the words for "insect" and "air". The soldiers of the Imperium often refer to them as "Stingwings".

The Vespid homeworld is a gas giant three light-years to the galactic south of the T'au's D'yanoi Sept. When the T'au first encountered the Vespid, they saw a species of great use in war, and the Vespid became [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]] of the empire.

In the T'au Empire

See also: [[T'au Empire]]

Vespid serve as [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]] alongside the [[Kroot]] and the [[Gue'vesa]], human defectors who fight for the [[T'au Empire|T'au]]. Their Stingwing squads use neutron blasters, and Imperial reports note them as among the most dangerous units of the T'au's forces in the [[Third Sphere Expansion]].
