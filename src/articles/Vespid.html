The Vespid are an insectoid alien species who are members of the [[T'au Empire]]. In the T'au language they are called Mal'kor, from the words for "insect" and "air". The soldiers of the Imperium often refer to them as "Stingwings".

The Vespid homeworld is a gas giant three light-years to the galactic south of the T'au's D'yanoi Sept. When the T'au first encountered the Vespid, they saw a species of great use in war, and the Vespid became auxiliaries of the empire.

In the T'au Empire

Vespid serve as auxiliaries alongside the [[Kroot]] and the Gue'vesa, human defectors who fight for the T'au. Their Stingwing squads use neutron blasters, and Imperial reports note them as among the most dangerous units of the T'au's forces in the [[Third Sphere Expansion]].
