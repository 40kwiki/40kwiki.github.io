The Iron Halo is the insignia of the [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], worn as a symbol of office. Guilliman is recorded as wearing it as part of his wargear, which includes the [[Armour of Fate]], the [[Emperor's Sword]] and the [[Hand of Dominion]].

Within the Chapters, the Iron Halo is worn by the Chapter Master in ceremony and in battle. It is recorded alongside the laurels of victory and the artificer armour that mark the Chapter's highest rank.

Insignia of the Chapter Master

The Codex Astartes defines the Chapter Master's insignia as a group of marks of office. The Iron Halo is among them, together with the laurels of victory. A Chapter Master's right to wear the halo is granted by the Chapter's own rites and is kept for life.

Guilliman's halo

[[Roboute Guilliman]] wears the Iron Halo as part of his wargear, and the Imperium's records describe it as his mark of command. Imperial accounts differ on whether the halo was given to him at the Second Founding or whether it was his own from the Great Crusade.The origin of Guilliman's Iron Halo is disputed in the Imperial records. No single account is accepted by all Chapters.
