Eternity Wall
Fortification of the [[Imperial Palace]], [[Terra]]

Location[[Terra]], around the [[Imperial Palace]]
Notable event[[Siege of Terra]], c. 013–014.M31

The Eternity Wall is the great defensive wall that encloses the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Terra]]. It is the outermost line of the Palace's defences, and it was the principal point of attack when the forces of [[Horus]] assaulted the Palace during the [[Siege of Terra]].

The wall stands with its spaceport, the [[Eternity Wall Spaceport]], which was contested during the siege. The loyalist defence held the wall, and the Traitors' attempts to take the spaceport were beaten back.

Siege

During the siege the [[White Scars]], led by [[Jaghatai Khan]], did not hold a section of the wall. They took the mobile reserve and raided the [[Battle of Lion's Gate|Lion's Gate Spaceport]] in 014.M31. A later attempt on the Eternity Wall Spaceport was beaten back.
