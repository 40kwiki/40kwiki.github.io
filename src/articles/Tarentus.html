Tarentus is an [[agri-world]] of the [[Realm of Ultramar]]. It is one of the worlds that feed the forge and hive worlds of the realm, and it is best known as the birthplace of the Ultramarine [[Titus]], born c. 665.M41.

The world is ruled by the [[Ultramarines]] and its farms are watched by Chapter garrisons. Its settlements are small and its population is modest. Its records are kept in the realm's archives on [[Macragge]].

Titus

Main article: [[Demetrian Titus]]

Titus was the only child of a modest family on Tarentus. A Chapter selector named Metaurus found him as a small child carrying a combat knife across a battlefield, and took him into the Chapter's service. Imperial records give his birth date as c. 665.M41, estimated from the records of his service.Titus's years are estimated from the records of his service: about a decade as captain before Graia, a custody of "almost a standard century", and a return in the early Era Indomitus. Sources disagree on the details.
