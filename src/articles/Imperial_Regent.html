The Imperial Regent, also called the Regent of Terra or the Lord Regent, is a title of high nobility held by the most powerful civil official of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Imperial doctrine holds that the holder speaks with the voice of the [[Emperor of Mankind]] in matters of state, and that the office ranks second only to the Emperor in civil authority across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The title has been held several times, during the [[Great Crusade]], the [[Horus Heresy]] and the [[Age of the Imperium]].

The office is civil and administrative in character. The [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] commands the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armed forces]], and the two titles have often been held by the same person. Imperial records describe the Regent as the officer who manages the Imperial bureaucracy and the [[Administratum]], while the Lord Commander presides over the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. The two offices have overlapped in practice, and accounts differ on which took precedence in each period.

History

Creation under Malcador

The office was first held by [[Malcador the Sigillite]], the Emperor's most trusted counsellor. Imperial accounts disagree on when the title began. The [[Great Crusade]] records give c. 798.M30, when the Emperor is said to have left Terra and entrusted the Palace to Malcador. Other records place the creation of the office after the [[Ullanor Crusade]], at the end of c. 000.M31, when the Emperor withdrew to the [[Imperial Palace]] to begin work on the [[Webway]] project and left his affairs to Malcador. The later account is the one given in the most widely circulated histories.

Under Malcador the Regent governed the [[Terra|Throneworld]], directed the flow of tithes and supplies to the Legions, and managed the political affairs of the Imperium while the Emperor's sons conquered the galaxy. He also served as head of the Council of Terra, the body that preceded the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]].

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The Regent's authority was tested by the [[Horus Heresy]], which began in 005.M31. When the [[Warmaster Horus]] marched on Terra, Malcador held the Palace together while the Emperor and the loyal Legions fought. The [[Siege of Terra]] lasted from 013.M31 to 014.M31. In the last days of the siege, Malcador gave the last of his psychic strength to the Emperor, who was fighting Horus aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]]. Malcador died at the end of the siege, in 014.M31.

Malcador's death left the Imperium without a Regent. The powers of the office passed to the surviving [[High Lords of Terra]], and the title was not filled again until the mid-32nd millennium.

Imperium Secundus

Main article: [[Imperium Secundus]]

During the Heresy the loyal [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Sanguinius]] held a separate title, ''Imperator Regis'', in the short-lived [[Imperium Secundus]], which was proclaimed in 009.M31. Imperial records treat this as a separate regency in a rival state, and it is listed in the table of holders above only for completeness.

Guilliman and the Time of Rebirth

After the Heresy, [[Rogal Dorn]] gave the position of Lord Commander to [[Roboute Guilliman]], who was also made Imperial Regent. From 014.M31 Guilliman used both offices to reform the [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial government]] in the [[Time of Rebirth]]. He held them until he was mortally wounded by the [[Daemon|Daemon Primarch]] [[Fulgrim]] at the [[Battle of Thessala]] in 121.M31. His body was placed in stasis in the [[Temple of Correction]] on [[Macragge]].

The mid-32nd millennium

After Guilliman's internment the title passed to other holders. Records from the mid-32nd millennium name [[Maximus Thane]], a [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Imperial Fists]], as Imperial Regent and first among equals in the Senatorum after [[The Beheading]]. The Beheading, a coup attempt by the [[Officio Assassinorum]], is dated c. 546–646.M32 in the Imperial record, though some accounts give 626.M32 for the end of the crisis. Thane's regency is recorded only in the later rolls, and some historians regard it as uncertain.

Restoration in 999.M41

In 999.M41, Guilliman returned to Terra during the [[Terran Crusade]], spoke with the Emperor, and resumed the offices of Imperial Regent and [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. He held both titles from that date into the [[Era Indomitus]]. He removed five of the High Lords from office on his return and reformed the Senatorum.

Powers and limits

The Regent has supreme civil authority, but is not a military commander. The [[Adeptus Custodes]] guard the office, which is held to have been one of the few Imperial positions that warrants such protection. The Regent cannot overrule the [[Emperor of Mankind]]; the Emperor's will is held to be expressed through the Regent's commands. In practice the Regent works through the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]], the [[Administratum]] and the [[Adeptus Terra]], and is bound by the [[Lex Imperialis]] like any other official.
