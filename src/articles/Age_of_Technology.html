The Age of Technology, often called the "Dark Age of Technology", was the zenith of humanity's scientific knowledge and technological prowess in the [[Milky Way]]. It lasted from the 15th millennium until the onset of the [[Age of Strife]] in the 25th millennium. It saw the development of the first true human interstellar civilisation and the first warp-capable spacecraft.

Much of what the Imperium knows of the Age comes from fragments of records and from the ruins of its worlds. Many of its technologies were lost in the Age of Strife, and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] still studies its surviving machines.

Luna

Main article: [[Luna]]

Humans first landed on [[Luna]] in 969.M2 and soon built domed cities on and below its surface. During the Dark Age it became a centre of biomedical research and zero-gravity manufacture. In the [[Age of Strife]] it fell almost to ruin, but its domed cities survived in part.
