Drazak is a [[tomb world]] of the [[Necrons]] in the [[Ghoul Stars]], in the far northeast of the galaxy. In ancient legend it is known as the Bone Kingdom of Drazak. Its streets are now inhabited by [[Flayed Ones]], a Necron strain so insane and savage that even other Necrons loathe them. The planet is ruled by the Necron overlord [[Valgûl]].

History

The Flayed Ones stalk the desolate streets of Drazak and fight over scraps of rotting meat and shards of bone. Valgûl, called the Fallen Lord by his own kind, is the only one of the world's population who has stood apart from the madness. He seems untouched by the [[Flayer virus]] that has consumed his people, and he rules from a throne of splintered bone and tanned skin, with one good eye fixed on keeping what order he can. Imperial scholars cannot tell whether he stays out of duty or from a subtler madness of his own.

His rule is not founded on reason, since his subjects have lost it. He keeps their allegiance by providing the gory bounty in which they delight. Every few solar months, when no meat remains, Valgûl announces a new Time of Bounty and sends the Necron fleets of Drazak to raid nearby worlds. The raiders seek no riches, only tithes of gore and blood.
