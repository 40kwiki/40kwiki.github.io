Drazak is a [[tomb world]] of the [[Necrons]] in the [[Ghoul Stars]], in the far northeast of the galaxy. In ancient legend it is known as the Bone Kingdom of Drazak. Its streets are now inhabited by [[Flayed Ones]], a Necron strain so insane and savage that even other Necrons loathe them. The planet is ruled by the Necron overlord [[Valgûl]].

History

Valgûl is the only one of the world's population who has kept his reason. He is said to be unaffected by the [[Flayer virus]] that has consumed his people, and he is said to rule from a throne of bone and tanned skin. His rule relies on providing the gory tribute that the Flayed Ones crave. Imperial scholars cannot tell whether he keeps order out of duty or from a madness of his own.

The Imperium has few records of Drazak. Imperial agents of the [[Deathwatch]] have recorded it among the hazards of the Outer Reach.
