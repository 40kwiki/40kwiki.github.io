Udin Macht Udo was the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] at the outbreak of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32. The first full roll of the [[High Lords of Terra]] as it is known today sits at the start of that war, and Udin Macht Udo is named as the Lord Commander on it.

The war began during the Imperium's period of disorder after the murder of the High Lords. Udo's position was contested by the [[Grand Provost Marshal]] [[Vernor Zeck]], who openly supported the [[Imperial Fists]] when their [[Chapter Master]] [[Koorland]] deposed him. Udo's deposition is recorded as part of the events that led to the war's end.

Deposition

[[Koorland]] deposed the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] with the support of part of the council. Imperial records describe the move as a response to the Lord Commander's failure to deal with the Beast's threat. Udo's later fate is not recorded.The circumstances of Udo's removal differ between sources. Imperial accounts agree only on the date, which is estimated from the war's opening.
