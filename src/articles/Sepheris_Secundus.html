Sepheris Secundus is a feudal [[Mining world]] in the [[Golgenna Reach]] sub-sector of the [[Calixis Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It holds immense mineral wealth, city-sized mines and billions of serfs. It is also the sharpest division between elites and masses in the sector. The Imperium's wish to exploit its wealth makes it both the richest and the poorest world in the sector.

Geography

The planet is cold and stormy, and its surface is obscured by cloud. It has no oceans save small polar seas, so its moisture comes from below the crust, spewed from failed mine workings and natural geysers. Its surface is split between open mines and snow-laden forests, which hold the ruins of failed kingdoms that once tried to claim the world.

History

Feudalism on Sepheris Secundus is taken to an extreme. Imperial records describe a "Suffering Kingdom" in which the birth of each person settles their place, and in which the nobles of the Knight Houses rule the serfs who work the mines. Its mineral wealth is the reason the Imperium keeps it in service.
