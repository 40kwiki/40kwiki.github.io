Sepheris Secundus is a feudal [[Mining world]] in the [[Golgenna Reach]] sub-sector of the [[Calixis Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It holds immense mineral wealth, city-sized mines and billions of serfs. It is also the sharpest division between elites and masses in the sector. The Imperium's wish to exploit its wealth makes it both the richest and the poorest world in the sector.

Geography

The planet is cold and stormy, with an arctic climate and frequent global blizzards, and its surface is obscured by cloud. It has no oceans save small polar seas, so its moisture comes from below the crust, spewed from failed mine workings and natural geysers. Its surface is split between open mines and snow-laden forests, which hold the ruins of failed kingdoms that once tried to claim the world.

The Suffering Kingdom

Feudalism on Sepheris Secundus is taken to an extreme. Everyone born on the planet must have a master, to whom they pay nine tenths of their income. Weapons and other technology are a monopoly of the Crown, so the culture is primitive by Imperial standards. The queen's Royal Scourges, the world's [[Planetary Defence Force]], carry lasguns, grenades and chainswords and wear gaudy armour like stained glass. They are few, but their equipment has put down many uprisings in the mines, and the planet's history is littered with massacres of serfs by lasgun volley. The barons keep their own troops, mostly with primitive but well-made arms, and each baron enforces his own laws. The Scourges answer to the queen alone, which also lets her keep the barons in line.

The planetary governor is Queen Lachryma III, an elderly ruler who has led the world through numerous revolts, baronial uprisings and rising tithe demands from the [[Administratum]]. Her body is failing, and some barons say she is losing her ruthlessness. She has ordered her barons to investigate what goes on in the deep mines, and they have resisted.

Places

The capital, Icenholm, is a city of stained glass built into the crags above the pit of the Gorgonid Mine and hung between three peaks on cables and chains. Mirrors concentrate light on it. Imperial tradition holds that it grew around a warship that docked during the [[Angevin Crusade]]. Red is reserved for royal use, and the hereditary servants of the city keep the barons' tithes in order.

The Gorgonid is one of the largest mines, a pit hundreds of metres deep. Its serfs live in the wooden tenements of the Commons, pay the barons nine tenths of their output, and mine claims on the Face that they mark with their own blood. The Shatters are its deepest and most dangerous parts, where the dispossessed and the mutants dwell. Above it lies the Tumble, a spoil-heap wasteland where black-market trade takes place. The Fathomsound is the most unusual mine: its serfs live on rafts and barges on an underground lake called the Flotsam.
