The Master of the Forge is the most senior [[Techmarine]] of a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. He oversees all of the Chapter's Techmarines and its Armoury, and is its chief engineer and weaponsmith, charged with the upkeep of its armoured vehicles such as [[Rhino]]s, [[Land Raider]]s and [[Predator]]s. He is always part of the Chapter council, yet is often an outsider to all save his own Techmarines.

His learning rivals that of the senior tech-priests of [[Mars]]. He alone can rouse the oldest [[Dreadnought]]s from sleep, and he keeps the Chapter's most ancient technological secrets. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] often counts him a [[Heretek|heretek]], because he is willing to modify a machine to the Chapter's need and does not wait for approval.

Variants

The [[Dark Angels]] call the post the Master of the Rock. Its holder is permanently wired into the machine banks deep in [[The Rock]], where he placates the machine spirits of its engines, warp drive and force field; his predecessors, withered, remain in place until they are collected for the Alcoves of Honour.
