The Ordo Machinum, known to its few acquaintances as the Judicators of the Forge, is an [[Ordos Minoris|Ordo Minoris]] of the [[Inquisition]] that scrutinises the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is one of the Inquisition's most delicate bodies, since the Mechanicus is an ally the Imperium cannot do without and a power that does not take watching kindly.

Its records give no founding date. It appears in the listings of the lesser Ordos as the one that watches the Machine Cult, and in the Inquisition's own account of the long and secret struggle with the Cult Mechanicus over the control of records.

Remit

The Ordo has two principal concerns. The first is the reintegration of recovered [[Standard Template Construct]] variants into the armies and industry of the Imperium. A recovered pattern may be a lost marvel or a trap, and the Ordo wants to know which before it is built in the millions. The second is the rare adoption of alien technology into accepted Mechanicus protocols, which the [[Cult Mechanicus]] forbids in principle and permits in practice more often than it admits.

[[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] of the Ordo often accompany technoarchaeological expeditions of the Mechanicus to distant worlds, to see for themselves what has been dug up. When the world was once held by [[Xenos|xenos]], or when the find may not be of human origin, they work with the [[Ordo Xenos]].

Relations with the Mechanicus

See also: [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]

The [[Treaty of Olympus]], signed in 739.M30, protects the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] and sets the terms on which the forges of [[Mars]] serve the Imperium. The [[Inquisition]] has never formally broken it. Even so, the Ordo works at its edge, and Mechanicus records speak of a hidden war of purge-programs fought with the Inquisition for control of data. The Ordo Machinum is the part of the Inquisition that fights it.

The Mechanicus knows of the Ordo and, so far as is known, tolerates it. How the two sides address each other is not recorded. A tech-priest may refuse an [[Inquisitor]], and the Inquisitor may then decide that the refusal is itself evidence.

Rumour

Imperial rumour credits the Ordo with a long interest in the [[Schism of Mars]] and in the renegade tech-priests who left the Imperium's service. This is not in any record. It is also said that the Ordo would be the first to be told if the [[Ordo Chronos]] were right about forbidden temporal technologies in [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] hands, but that too is only a rumour.

Members

No [[Inquisitor]] of the Ordo is named in open records. Its Inquisitors are said to be chosen for their ability to speak the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]]'s own idiom, so that they can examine a forge-master without offending the Omnissiah. That too is an unconfirmed report.

Legacy

The Ordo's task has grown since the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. [[Belisarius Cawl]] and the new creations of the forges of [[Mars]] have made the question of what to build, and what to leave unbuilt, urgent. How the Ordo has met it is not known.
