The Order of Broken Claws (also the Order of the Broken Claw) was one of the secretive Orders Militant of the [[Dark Angels]] Legion, a small body of Legionaries whose duty was to guard the only full account of the [[Rangdan Xenocides]] and of the xenos strain known as the [[Rangda]]. It was formed in the wake of the Second Xenocide and was the smallest order of the Legion's Inner Circle, not because it was unimportant but because the knowledge it kept was too dangerous to be shared widely.

Its adepts were trained to stand against the worst of the Rangdan bio-forms in open combat. They won renown in the sieges of the Third Xenocide, and by the time of the [[Horus Heresy]] their numbers had dwindled to a handful, with a single chantry station still in service over the ruined world of [[Advex-mors]]. During the Heresy their duty widened to the guarding of stasis vaults that held relics and weapons taken from the enemies of the Legion.

Background

The Hekatonystika

Within the formal structure of wings and hosts, the Dark Angels kept a second, hidden system of organisation: the [[Hekatonystika]], called the Orders of Battle. Each order was an autonomous brotherhood, dedicated to protecting one portion of the Legion's hard-won secret lore, most often the weakness of a particular foe or the mastery of a certain terrain or weapon. The leader of an order bore the title of preceptor, and the preceptors together formed the Conclave of Preceptors. Before a battle that would profit from their skills, cells of an order were gathered into a temporary unit called a cenobium, which led the assault and advised the commanders.

The Hekatonystika was divided into the [[Orders Militant]], made up of Legionaries with specialist battlefield knowledge, and the Orders Civilis, which were mostly mortal support staff. The Order of Broken Claws belonged to the first division. Its members were bound by cipher and ritual, and only those inducted knew the full meaning of the order's insignia.

The Rangdan Xenocides

Between 839 and 890.M30 the [[Imperium of Mankind]] fought three wars against the Rangda, which cost the Dark Angels dearly. The Legion ordered most records of the wars sealed or destroyed, and the Order of Broken Claws was created from the survivors of the Second Xenocide to keep what remained.

History

Foundation

The order was founded in 882.M30, after the last battle of the Second Xenocide.The founding date follows the account of the Rangdan wars in [[Rangdan Xenocides]]. The sources for the order itself say only that it was formed "in the wake of" the Second Xenocide. It was given the only complete record of the war and of the Rangda, together with the spoils of the victory. Its adepts gained access to the catalogue of anatomical data compiled by the operators of the [[Firewing]] and the cheirophages of the [[Dreadwing]], and trained to defeat the Rangda's bio-forms in combat.

The Third Xenocide

In the sieges of the Third Xenocide (c. 889–890.M30) the order won much renown, both for its prowess in battle and for its skill in anticipating and countering the strategies of the Rangdan warmasters. After the Rangda were apparently annihilated, the order shrank. A chantry station over [[Advex-mors]], where the relics of the Rangda were entombed, remained in service afterwards.

The Horus Heresy

By the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] in 005.M31 few members of the order remained. At an unrecorded date in the war the Legion fought the Battle of Perditus against a force of the [[Death Guard]] on Umbral-51, a barren world that held a research hold of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] devoted to [[Archaeotech|archaeotech]].The Battle of Perditus has no recorded year. The sources say only that it happened during the Heresy (005–014.M31); the date c. 006.M31 is an estimate used for the timeline. After the battle the magi reluctantly handed over the contents of their forge-fanes, which were carried to the Dark Angels ships in orbit. The knights of the Broken Claws judged the world too dangerous to leave standing and destroyed the facility with tectonic bombs.

The relic weapons of Umbral-51 might have tipped the war for either side. They passed into the order's keeping and their fate is uncertain. Some accounts say that the order sent them to its chantry houses across the galaxy and hid them there.

As the Heresy went on, the order's duties were extended. Small groups of its knights took charge of stasis vaults holding relics and weapons taken from the Legion's defeated enemies. Each knight swore to keep these artefacts from the hands of the enemies of the Imperium, and the oath could be broken only by the guardian's death.

Organisation

The Broken Claws were led by a preceptor, who sat in the Conclave of Preceptors with the heads of the other orders. The order had the smallest membership of any Order Militant in the Legion's Inner Circle. Its members were trained to fight the Rangdan bio-forms, and as with the other orders each tier of membership gave an initiate deeper knowledge of what the order protected, and a heavier duty to keep it ready.

Its sanctum was the chantry above [[Advex-mors]]; some accounts add that it kept further chantry houses across the galaxy. The [[Lion El'Jonson|Lion]] himself held the rank of high preceptor of every order in the Legion.

Legacy

The Rangdan wars left little record outside the keeping of the order and the Legion. Some Imperial reports confuse the chantry on Advex-mors with a Rangdan homeworld, and some historitors suspect the error was deliberate. The sentinels of the First Legion are said still to keep watch there in case the Rangda have not been wholly destroyed.
