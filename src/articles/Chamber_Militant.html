A Chamber Militant is the formal name given by the [[Adeptus Terra]] to the military arm of each of the three major ordos of the [[Inquisition]], the secret police and intelligence service of the [[Imperium of Mankind]].

Each ordo has its own. The [[Ordo Xenos]], the Alien Hunters, is served by the [[Deathwatch]]. The [[Ordo Malleus]], the Daemonhunters, is served by the elite and highly secret [[Grey Knights]]. The [[Ordo Hereticus]], the Witchhunters, is served by the [[Adepta Sororitas|Orders Militant]] of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], often called the Sisters of Battle.

Use

An Inquisitor works through [[Acolyte]]s and other agents, and the Chambers Militant are usually called on by their ordo only in cases of dire need, when every other option has been exhausted.

The ordos have other forces besides. Inquisitors commonly draw on the elite [[Inquisitorial Storm Troopers]], and some regional conclaves keep dedicated forces of their own. The [[Stormwatchers]] Space Marine Chapter, for instance, are the sanctioned blades of the [[Caligari Conclave]].

The three chambers

Deathwatch

The [[Deathwatch]] is a force of Space Marines that serves the Ordo Xenos.

Grey Knights

The Grey Knights are a highly secret Chapter of [[Astartes]] that serves the Ordo Malleus. The Grey Knights are called on only when a daemonic infestation has grown past what a lone Inquisitor and the Inquisitor's agents can contain.

Orders Militant

The Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas, better known as the Sisters of Battle, serve the Ordo Hereticus.
