A Chamber Militant is the formal name given by the [[Adeptus Terra]] to the military arm of each of the three major ordos of the [[Inquisition]], the secret police and intelligence service of the [[Imperium of Mankind]].

Each ordo has its own. The [[Ordo Xenos]] is served by the [[Deathwatch]], the [[Ordo Malleus]] by the [[Grey Knights]], and the [[Ordo Hereticus]] by the [[Adepta Sororitas|Orders Militant]] of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], often called the Sisters of Battle.

Use

An Inquisitor works through [[Acolyte]]s and personal agents, and the Chambers Militant are called on only when those means are exhausted. The Grey Knights, for example, are committed to a daemonic outbreak only when it has grown beyond what a lone Inquisitor and a retinue can contain.

The ordos have other forces besides. Inquisitors commonly draw on the elite [[Inquisitorial Storm Troopers]], and some regional conclaves keep dedicated forces of their own. The [[Stormwatchers]] Space Marine Chapter, for instance, are the sanctioned blades of the [[Caligari Conclave]].

Place in the Inquisition

The Chambers Militant are the only standing armies the ordos command openly, and they are kept apart from the [[Imperial Army|Astra Militarum]] and the Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] in command and loyalty. Their members answer first to the ordo they serve. The Inquisition calls on them only in dire need, once every quieter measure has failed.

The three chambers

Deathwatch

The [[Deathwatch]] draws Space Marines from many Chapters to serve the Ordo Xenos against alien threats. The Chambers Militant answer to an ordo and not to the Chapters they recruit from.

Grey Knights

The Grey Knights are a secret Chapter of [[Astartes]] whose purpose is the destruction of daemons and the service of the Ordo Malleus.

Orders Militant

The Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas serve the Ordo Hereticus, whose remit is heresy and the control of the psychic. They are the armed sisterhood of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] lent to the Inquisition.
