The Marines Errant are a fleet-based [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created late in the [[Twenty-Third Founding]] of the [[37th millennium]] from the gene-seed of the Eagle Warriors, a Chapter of the [[Ultramarines]] line. The Chapter is well named: it has never kept a homeworld or a formal fortress-monastery, and its companies have fought across the whole Imperium and far beyond its borders, usually dispersed on separate crusades.

The Chapter's only fixed holding was its gene-seed repository on the world of Vilamus. In 999.M41 [[Huron Blackheart]] and the [[Red Corsairs]], allied with [[Night Lords]] under the Chaos champion called the Exalted, sacked Vilamus and carried off its gene-seed to make new [[Chaos Space Marines]].

History

Founding

The Twenty-Third Founding was one of a series of linked Foundings intended to repair the Adeptus Astartes after the losses of the preceding millennia. According to the Requiem Malesent of Saint Kybra, no fewer than fifty-seven Chapters had been destroyed, turned renegade or lost in that period, which included the [[Age of Apostasy]] and the "Cursed" [[Twenty-First Founding]]. Why the Eagle Warriors were singled out as the source of the new Chapter is not recorded.

Crusades

From 390 to 433.M38 the Marines Errant held a covenant with the Rogue Trader house of Ecale under Sia'hadn Ecale, serving with its fleets in the unknown regions. In the 40th millennium five companies formed the core of the Kadyron Voyage into the Hazeroth Abyss of the [[Calixis Sector]]. Between 698 and 701.M41 the Chapter served in the Corinth Crusade, a combined force under [[Marneus Calgar]] of the Ultramarines with the Angels of Absolution, the [[Lamenters]], the Silver Skulls, the [[Scythes of the Emperor]] and fifty regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]], which broke the Ork empire of Charadon for decades.The Kadyron Voyage is dated only to M40.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

In 904.M41 the [[High Lords of Terra]] recalled the Marines Errant from the [[Eastern Fringe]] to reinforce the [[Fire Hawks]] in the [[Badab War]]. They fought on the Loyalist side until 908.M41.

The fall of Vilamus

Huron Blackheart, the former Chapter Master of the [[Astral Claws]], needed gene-seed to refill the ranks of his Red Corsairs. With the Exalted's Night Lords he assaulted Vilamus, broke its defences and took the Chapter's stored gene-seed. Imperial accounts differ on the date: some place the raid in 960.M41, but most, including the Chapter's own records, give 999.M41. The loss left the Chapter with no reserve from which to rebuild, and it has since depended on the Primaris reinforcements of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].The date of the Primaris reinforcement is not recorded.

Organisation

As a fleet-based Chapter, the Marines Errant rarely gather in force except when the Chapter Master calls a major crusade. Its companies otherwise operate independently, often far apart, and its warriors serve in numbers with the [[Deathwatch]]. The Chapter's creed is the crusade itself: it has been devoted since its Founding to taking the Emperor's light to the darkest regions of the galaxy.

Heraldry and service

The Chapter's armour is halved blue and white, a scheme that marks its descent from the Ultramarines while setting it apart from the Codex colours of its progenitors. Its badge is a golden comet on a blue field, a symbol of the endless journeying from which the Chapter takes its name. Company and squad markings follow the [[Codex Astartes]].

Because its companies spend so long beyond the Imperium's borders, the Marines Errant have unusual experience of xenos warfare, and the Chapter has long been a steady source of battle-brothers for the [[Deathwatch]]. Its Librarians are noted for skill in navigating the Warp in regions where the light of the [[Astronomican]] is faint, a gift that has saved companies of the Chapter more than once. Without a homeworld, the Chapter recruits from the worlds it passes through and from the human populations it frees on crusade, and its neophytes are trained aboard the fleet.
