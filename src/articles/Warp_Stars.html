Warp Stars are phenomena of the [[Warp]] that draw ships and small planets into the [[Immaterium]]. They were first recorded in about 666.M34, after a storm drifted out of the [[Eye of Terror]].

Imperial [[Navis Nobilite|navigators]] treat the Warp Stars as a hazard of the Warp routes near the Eye. Their appearance is one of the events recorded in the [[34th millennium]] that changed the Imperium's navigation.

Origin

The first Warp Stars appeared in a storm that drifted from the Eye of Terror. Their formation is not fully understood, and Imperial records describe them as a product of the Eye's disturbances rather than as natural objects of the Warp.The date of 666.M34 is an estimate from the records of the storm. No record gives an exact year for the first sighting.

Effects

A Warp Star can draw in ships and small planets, which are carried into the Warp and lost. The phenomenon is recorded as having taken much from the outer worlds of the region, and the Navy's charts mark the areas where Warp Stars are known to appear.
