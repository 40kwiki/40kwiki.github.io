Wraithbone is a material used by the [[Aeldari]]. It is psychically responsive, and it is grown rather than built. Its properties make it the basis of much Aeldari equipment, including the weapons and the war constructs of the [[Craftworld|craftworlds]].

The Aeldari build their constructs from wraithbone in which the spirit of a dead Aeldari is housed. Imperial analysts compare the great Wraithknights with the [[Imperial Knight|Imperial Knights]], although the Aeldari have no equivalent of the Imperial Knight houses.

Use in war

A [[Craftworld|craftworld]]'s war host is made of a small number of Guardians, a few Aspect Warrior shrines and the Rangers. Its key advantage is speed through the [[Webway]] to strike an isolated point, and the quality of its equipment, which is made of wraithbone.Imperial descriptions of Aeldari materials are drawn from captured equipment and from encounters. No record gives dates for the growth of individual items.
