Gene-seed, also written geneseed, is the genetic material from which the warriors of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] are made. It is held in the progenoid glands of every [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine]], and from it [[Apothecary|Apothecaries]] grow the nineteen organs that turn a boy into a Space Marine. All gene-seed descends from the [[primarch]]s, the twenty sons that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] created on [[Terra]] in the late 30th millennium, and each strain carries the strengths and flaws of the primarch it came from.

Gene-seed is the most precious resource of any Chapter. Without it no new warriors can be made, and a Chapter that loses its gene-seed is extinct whatever its strength in the field. The [[Codex Astartes]], set down in c. 019.M31, binds every Chapter to recover the progenoids of its dead and to send samples to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] for testing and storage, a duty known as the gene-seed tithe. Corruption, mutation and wear have damaged many lines over ten thousand years, and the tithe exists above all to catch them.

At the end of the 41st millennium the Archmagos [[Belisarius Cawl]] revealed an enhanced pattern with three more organs, the basis of the [[Primaris Space Marines]]. Since the [[Ultima Founding]] of 999.M41 most Chapters have taken warriors of the new pattern, and the old and new gene-seed now exist side by side.

Origins

The Emperor drew on earlier work to make the Space Marines. The [[Thunder Warriors]] of the [[Unification Wars]] were his first gene-forged soldiers, made in the M30 laboratories of the Himalazian mountains and Luna, and they were powerful but short-lived. The primarchs were his second and greater work, made in c. 792.M30 in the gene-vaults beneath the [[Imperial Palace]].

When the infant primarchs were scattered across the galaxy by the powers of the [[Warp]], the Emperor still held the genetic patterns he had taken from them. From these he made the gene-seed of the twenty [[Space Marine Legions]], which were raised in the closing years of the Unification Wars and the first years of the [[Great Crusade]] (from c. 798.M30). Imperial accounts describe the gene-seed as a set of tailored organ cultures, each keyed to the pattern of one primarch, not as a direct copy of his body.

The implants

The classic pattern of the Adeptus Astartes has nineteen organs, implanted in a fixed order over several years. Some grow only in an adolescent body, which is why aspirants are taken young. The organs are listed in Imperial texts as follows.

The Primaris pattern adds three organs: the sinew coil, a mesh of fibre around the muscles and organs; the magnificat, which strengthens the action of the other implants; and the Belisarian furnace, which floods the body with healing chemicals when it is badly wounded.

Progenoid glands

The two progenoid glands, one in the neck and one in the chest, are what make the Chapters self-sustaining. They grow germ cells of each implant from the Marine's own gene-seed. The neck gland matures about five years after implantation and the chest gland after about ten. When a Marine falls, his Chapter's Apothecaries cut out the progenoids with a reductor, and each mature gland can supply organs for a new aspirant. A Chapter that cannot recover its dead cannot replace them.

The gene-seed tithe and the Mechanicus

After the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), the [[Second Founding]] of c. 021.M31 broke the Legions into Chapters, and the Codex required each Chapter to send part of its gene-seed to the Adeptus Mechanicus. The Mechanicus tests it for corruption and keeps it in sealed gene-vaults on [[Mars]] and elsewhere, from which the High Lords draw stock for new [[Foundings]]. A Chapter that fails the tithe can be declared renegade; the [[Badab War]] of 901–912.M41 began when the Astral Claws withheld theirs.

The Mechanicus has also tried to improve gene-seed, with mixed results. The Twenty-First Founding of M36, called the [[Cursed Founding]], used gene-seed that Martian genetors had altered, and several of its Chapters, among them the [[Lamenters]] and the [[Minotaurs]], are said to bear hidden defects.No surviving record gives the exact year of the Twenty-First Founding. Most Administratum tables place it in M36, after the [[Age of Apostasy]].

Flaws and mutations

Gene-seed changes over time, and most lines have drifted from the primarchs' pattern. Some flaws are known to the whole Imperium:

  • The sons of [[Sanguinius]] suffer the [[Black Rage|Red Thirst]], a craving for blood, and the Black Rage, a madness in which the warrior relives the primarch's death on the [[Vengeful Spirit]] in 014.M31.
  • The [[Space Wolves]] carry the [[Canis Helix]], which can turn a warrior into a feral Wulfen.
  • The [[Imperial Fists]] line lacks a working Betcher's gland and sus-an membrane, and the [[Raven Guard]] line has lost several organs, including the melanochrome's normal function.
  • The gene-seed of the traitor Legions has been changed by [[Chaos]] since the Heresy, and the Mechanicus burns any sample of it that it finds.

The [[Primaris Space Marines]] were in part an answer to this drift. Cawl claims to have worked from purer samples given to him in M31, and the Imperium reports that the new warriors suffer the old flaws less, although Primaris sons of Sanguinius still feel the Thirst.

The Primaris gene-seed

[[Roboute Guilliman]] gave Cawl the task of improving the Space Marines in the last years of the Heresy era, and Cawl worked on it in secret beneath Mars for some ten thousand years. In 999.M41, when Guilliman returned to life, Cawl revealed the new gene-seed and the first Primaris warriors, who had been kept in stasis for centuries. The [[Ultima Founding]] that followed (999.M41 to the early 42nd millennium) raised new Chapters and sent Primaris reinforcements to nearly all the old ones. Existing Marines can now cross the [[Rubicon Primaris]], a surgery that adds the three new organs to an adult body.
