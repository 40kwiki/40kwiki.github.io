A battleship is the largest and most powerful warship that a space-faring military fleet can field. Battleships are protected by strong defences, carry enough firepower to destroy a lesser warship, and usually carry launch bays for attack craft. Their weaknesses are their cost and their poor manoeuvrability. When they are escorted by cruisers and smaller vessels, which offset these weaknesses, a battleship becomes a formidable addition to any fleet.

Battleships formed the core of the [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[Adeptus Astartes]] fleets throughout the [[Great Crusade]]. At the height of the Imperium's expansion in the early 31st millennium, more than a thousand battleships were in service. They are intended to form the line of battle in a fleet engagement, and their guns can bombard continents from orbit and control whole star systems.

Design and scale

A battleship is a vast construction, commonly between 8 and 12 kilometres long, although some much larger vessels have been built. Its hull carries tens of thousands of crew, and it can transport troops, hundreds of war machines and swarms of smaller craft, depending on the design. Imperial designs must also endure the transit of the [[Warp]] and the forces of the [[Empyrean]] and the deep void.

Imperial battleships are named by class. Among the best known are the Emperor-class, the Victory-class, the Apocalypse-class, the Retribution-class and the Oberon-class, as well as several Great Crusade classes such as the Gloriana, Goliath and Ironclad. Space Marine fleets rely on the [[Battle Barge]], which serves a similar role for an Astartes Chapter.

Combat role

In ordinary circumstances, battleships were threatened only by other battleships and by natural celestial hazards such as [[Warp storm]]s. This made them the mainstay of both the expeditionary fleets of the Great Crusade and the wider [[Armada Imperialis]]. In the 41st millennium they remain the foundation of the combat power of the segmentum and sector battlefleets of the [[Navis Imperialis]], and they serve as the ships of the line in any major void engagement.

Other powers field their own battleships. Chaos fleets include the Abyss-class and Despoiler-class battleships, and the [[Planet Killer]], which is the largest weapon of its kind. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] uses the [[Ark Mechanicus]]. The [[Orks]] have no true battleship class, but some individual Ork vessels rival the size and firepower of Imperial battleships. Battleships of the [[Tyranids]] are the [[Hive Ship]]s.
