Kaldor Draigo is the [[Supreme Grand Master]] of the [[Grey Knights]], the Chapter's leader and first among its eight Grand Masters. He has held the title since 901.M41, when his predecessor was killed at the Battle of Kornovin.

Draigo is best known for the fight at Kornovin, where he struck down the [[Death Guard]] primarch [[Mortarion]]. The Grey Knights' records describe him as a warrior of great discipline who kept the Chapter together through the early years of the 41st millennium.

Battle of Kornovin

At Kornovin, Mortarion killed the Supreme Grand Master of the Grey Knights, Geronitan. Draigo struck the primarch down in turn, and Imperial accounts record that he carved his predecessor's name on Mortarion's heart. Mortarion survived, but was unable to return to the material universe for many years.

Later years

The Grey Knights' records describe Draigo's later years in the Chapter's fortress of Titan. Some accounts say that Draigo himself was later cast into the Warp, and that the Chapter's rites were kept by his successors in his absence. These accounts are not confirmed by the Chapter's public records.The records of Draigo's later years are sealed or disputed. No public source confirms the account of his fate.
