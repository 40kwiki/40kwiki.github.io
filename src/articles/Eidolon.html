Eidolon is a [[Daemon world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]]. It is one of the worlds that the [[Emperor's Children]] took for their own after their fall, and it is linked with the [[primarch]] [[Fulgrim]] and with the Chaos renegade [[Fabius Bile]].

Eidolon is a Plague Planet in the sense that its corruption is held to be the work of Chaos. Imperial records describe it among the notable worlds of the Eye, alongside [[Cthonia]], [[Medrengard]] and [[Sortiarius]].

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Emperor's Children and their allies used Eidolon as a stronghold. The Legion's warriors, Lucius and others, are recorded as fighting from the world, and the Imperial sources say the world was lost to the Legions' own rivalries.The sources name Eidolon's role in the Heresy but do not give the dates of its individual battles.
