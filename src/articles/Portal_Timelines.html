The [[Imperial Dating System|Imperial calendar]] counts the years from the [[Unification Wars|Unification of Terra]] in M30 onward, and every event of the Imperium is dated in it. Where records disagree, the [[Administratum]] keeps the accepted date and notes the others. This portal gathers the chronologies of the Imperium, from the Great Crusade to the [[Era Indomitus|present age]].

Selected article

[[Timeline of Imperial history]] sets out the chronology of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from the [[Unification Wars|Unification of Terra]] in M30 to the [[Era Indomitus]] of M42. It is built from the records of the [[Administratum]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Estimated dates are marked where no record survives.
