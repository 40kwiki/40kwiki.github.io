The Furious Abyss was one of three [[Imperial Navy|Imperial]] Abyss-class battleships of special configuration. It was built in the Jovian shipyards of Thule, which orbited the gas giant [[Jupiter]] in the [[Sol System]], in the early [[31st millennium]]. The class takes its name from the Gothic battleship design used in the older naval records of the Imperium.

The vessel was built to a special configuration that set it apart from the standard Abyss class. Its exact fittings are not described in the surviving public registers. Imperial scholars note that the three sister ships are not recorded as operating together in any campaign.
