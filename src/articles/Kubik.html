Kubik was the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]], head of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and a member of the [[High Lords of Terra]], during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). He is remembered for two things: his reluctance to bring the power of Mars to the defence of [[Terra]] while the [[Orks]] held its skies, and his decision, once the war was won, to keep the Ork capital for study instead of destroying it as the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] had ordered.

That decision, when it was discovered, persuaded the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Drakan Vangorich]] that the High Lords served only themselves. It was the immediate cause of the [[Beheading]], in which the council was murdered in a single solar day in 546.M32. Kubik did not survive it.

Background

Nothing is published about Kubik's early life, forge or rise through the priesthood of the [[Cult Mechanicus]]. The Mechanicus keeps such records to itself, and the annals of the [[Fabricator-General]] that are open to scholars name him only in the context of the war. By 544.M32 he governed [[Mars]] and its forges, commanded the [[Skitarii]] legions and the other fighting arms of the Red Planet, and held the Mechanicus seat on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] beside the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] [[Udin Macht Udo]] and the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]]. Imperial accounts describe him as allied with Lansung during the first half of the war.

War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

Mars withholds aid

In the first months of the war the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] took little visible part. [[Orks]] overran the [[Segmentum Solar]], an Attack Moon hung over [[Terra]] and the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] barricaded themselves in the [[Imperial Palace]], and the [[Fabricator-General]] refused to send help from Mars. Inquisitorial and [[Officio Assassinorum|Assassinorum]] agents reported that Mechanicus personnel were present among the Orks from the war's earliest months. The reasons were not understood until later.

The Last Wall

Main article: [[Koorland]]

The refusal ended when the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] of the Last Wall, the successors of the [[Imperial Fists]] gathered by High Marshal Bohemond of the [[Black Templars]], came to Terra and pressed him. Kubik then sent a fleet of Basilikon Astra, five regiments of [[Skitarii]] and seven cohorts of the [[Legio Cybernetica]] to join the assault on the Attack Moon. After the [[Koorland|Lord Commander Koorland]] took the chair of the council, he confronted Mars. The Mechanicus had been experimenting on the Orks' teleportation technology, in the hope of moving the Red Planet out of the [[Sol System]], and a force of the Fists Exemplar met the Skitarii at [[Tharsis Montes|Pavonis Mons]], where an errant shot began a brief firefight. Kubik, who was on Terra at the time, and an appeal from Koorland for unity ended it.

The Deathwatch and Ullanor

See also: [[Deathwatch]] and [[Ullanor Crusade]]

When the council voted on the founding of the [[Deathwatch]], Kubik abstained, as did the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Veritus]]. Later, when [[Maximus Thane]] decided to act without the High Lords, Kubik was one of those who joined him, with [[Drakan Vangorich|Vangorich]], in planning the third assault on [[Ullanor]] in 546.M32. The Mechanicus dropped asteroids on the Ork world and sent its own attack group under a commander named Zhokuv.

The Ullanor deception

Thane had decreed that [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] be destroyed by [[Exterminatus]] once the Beast was dead. Kubik did not carry out the order. Using technology taken from the Ork Attack Moons, the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] teleported Gorkogrod, the Ork capital, aboard the immense Mechanicus ship Ark Majesty, intending to strip the city of everything useful. The Mechanicus tradition, which the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] have never confirmed, holds that the whole of Ullanor Prime was moved through the Warp and resettled.

Thane had warned [[Drakan Vangorich|Vangorich]] against Kubik's self-interest before he left Terra to begin the [[Fourth Founding]]. Vangorich learned the full extent of the plan soon after.

Death

On the day of the Beheading in 546.M32 [[Drakan Vangorich|Vangorich]]'s assassins killed eleven of the twelve [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]. Cornered by the [[Grand Master of Assassins]], Kubik shut himself down rather than allow a virus to be loosed into the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] noosphere. His successor is not named in the records open to Imperial scholars.
