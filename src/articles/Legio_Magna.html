The Legio Magna, whose name means "Flaming Skulls", is a [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Mechanicum]]. During the [[Horus Heresy]] it repudiated its oaths to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and followed the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] into the service of the [[Ruinous Powers]]. Imperial records suspect that the Legio was founded before the [[Age of Imperium]], during the [[Age of Strife]].

The Legio's homeworld was [[Estaban VII]], in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Its Mechanicum forges were isolated and beset by political infighting, and for most of the Great Crusade the Legio was a minor force.

The Great Crusade

During the Great Crusade the Flaming Skulls campaigned alongside the [[Sons of Horus]] (the XVI Legion) and the [[Word Bearers]] (the XVII Legion). The Legio was first sent to minor warfronts. By the middle of the second standard century of the Great Crusade it had been deployed with twenty-one expeditionary fleets, in units ranging from two dozen Titans to a single machine. The losses this caused outstripped the ability of Estaban VII to replace them.

The Mechanicum of Estaban VII then converted the world to supply wargear for the Imperial Army. The Legio was ordered to serve alongside Army units. Within a decade more than half of the Legio was on more prestigious assignments, acting in the service of the primarchs [[Horus]] and [[Lorgar]] and their Legions. Its princeps, who once cared little for honour, came to revel in the praise they received.

The Heresy

After Horus became Warmaster, a demi-legio of the Flaming Skulls was sent to Mars. Because of their close ties with Horus, Lorgar and [[Kelbor-Hal]], the [[Fabricator-General]] of Mars, the Legio followed its allies into treachery. The first blow of the [[Schism of Mars]] was struck against [[Mattias Kefra]], a Magos whose forge lay in the Sinus Sabaeus region of Mars. Titans of the Legio marched from the southern Noachis region to join the attack.

After the Heresy

The part of the Legio that had not been sent to Mars stayed loyal to the Imperium after the Heresy. It fought for the Imperium in the [[Orask Wars]] in the 41st millennium. Imperial records do not say whether the traitor elements of the Legio survived, because the Imperium has not encountered them in recent centuries.
