This is a timeline of the Era Indomitus, the age of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that began with the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41 and the opening of the [[Great Rift]], known in [[High Gothic]] as the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]]. The age is named for the [[Indomitus Crusade]] of [[Roboute Guilliman]], the Lord Commander of the Imperium, who returned from stasis in the same year and led the Imperium's recovery through the early [[42nd millennium]].

The rows below are arranged oldest first. Because the Great Rift disrupted the galactic calendar, many dates of the Era are approximate. Entries that the Office of the Lord Commander gives only as a season or a decade are marked "early M42" or "M42", and entries whose year rests on the reconstructed reckoning are marked "c.".Estimated; no record gives an exact year. Where the [[Adepta]] disagree, the [[Administratum]]'s reconciled date is given and the others are noted.

999.M41

The year 999.M41 holds most of the events that opened the Era Indomitus. It began with the Thirteenth Black Crusade and ended with the Imperium reformed around a single Lord Commander.

M42

Further information: [[42nd millennium]]

The first years of the 42nd millennium were the most dangerous of the Era. Campaigns were fought against the Chaos Legions, the [[Tyranids]], the [[Necrons]] and the [[Orks]], while the Lord Commander made the Imperium's first recovery.
