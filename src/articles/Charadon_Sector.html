The Charadon Sector is the Imperial designation for the region of the [[Eastern Fringe]] of the [[Segmentum Ultima]] that is held by the Ork Empire of [[Charadon]]. Charadon is one of many small, independent and mutually hostile Ork domains scattered across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], and it is the largest and most organised of them. It is ruled by the warlord [[Snagrod]], who holds the title Arch-Arsonist of Charadon.

The Ork empire lies near the Imperial world of [[Badlanding]], and it is only a few solar weeks away by [[Warp travel|warp travel]] from [[Rynn's World]], the homeworld of the [[Crimson Fists]]. Snagrod's wars of conquest have made the empire the most powerful state in the anarchic confines of Ork space, and the Imperium regards it as one of the gravest greenskin threats on its border.

History

Rise of the Arch-Arsonist

Charadon's Ork domain has been a power on the Eastern Fringe for many centuries. Its rulers are known as Arch-Arsonists, a title that marks the leader of the greenskin realm. [[Snagrod]] gained the title through a series of wars, and he used his position to build the most organised Ork state in the region.

Invasion of Rynn's World

In 989.M41 Snagrod led his WAAAGH! out of the Charadon Sector and invaded the Loki Sector. The invasion struck the Imperial world of [[Rynn's World]], the homeworld of the Crimson Fists. The defence of the world lasted eighteen months, and Snagrod escaped the battle, although the Imperial account records his Rok as destroyed.

The Charadon campaign

During the [[Indomitus Crusade]], the Daemon Prince [[Be'lakor]] invaded the Charadon Sector with his army, the Disciples of Be'lakor. [[Typhus]] of the [[Death Guard]] was already in the sector, and Be'lakor followed him there and allied with the [[Word Bearers]]. Inquisitorial records describe the campaign as a stealing of knights: while most of [[House Raven]]'s knights were away, Be'lakor corrupted the house's Sacristans and, through them, the Thrones Mechanicum of some of its Knights. He then took the house's homeworld, which was remade under the new name of [[Korvosi]].The campaign is dated c. 010.M42 in the Inquisitorial vision-record. The name of the house's homeworld, Kolossi, is recorded only in older sources, and the later name of the world is disputed.

Geography

The Charadon Sector lies on the Eastern Fringe, with the Imperial world of [[Badlanding]] on its border and [[Mancora]], the homeworld of the [[Howling Griffons]], nearby. Its worlds are held by Warbosses who answer to [[Snagrod]], and its [[Warp travel|warp routes]] are controlled by Ork pirates and [[Chaos]] raiders.

Notable worlds

[[Charadon]] is the capital world of the Ork empire and the seat of [[Snagrod]]. Its wars of conquest are the source of the empire's power. [[Badlanding]] is an Imperial [[Agri-world]] on the sector's border. It was captured by Ork Warboss Uzrog in 989.M41 under Snagrod's orders, and it remains in Ork hands.

[[Kolossi]], the [[Knight world]] of [[Imperial Knight|House Raven]], was taken during the Charadon campaign and remade under the name Korvosi. Its status in M42 is recorded as disputed.

Governance

The empire is ruled by [[Snagrod]], who holds the title Arch-Arsonist of Charadon. Under him, Warbosses hold their own domains and fight among themselves for power. The Imperium has no authority in the sector, and it regards its Warbosses as enemies to be kept out of the Imperial worlds on its border.

Military

The Ork forces of Charadon are organised into WAAAGHs, which are led by Warbosses and draw on the empire's industry and its Mekaniaks. The empire's raiding fleets have struck the Imperial worlds of the Eastern Fringe, and the [[Imperial Navy]]'s stations along the border watch its movements. Chaos forces, including the [[Death Guard]] and the [[Word Bearers]], have also fought in the sector.
