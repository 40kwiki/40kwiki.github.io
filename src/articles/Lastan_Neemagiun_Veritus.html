Lastan Neemagiun Veritus was an [[Inquisition|Inquisitor Lord]] of the Puritan school and the Inquisition's second voice on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). He was one of the four founders of the Inquisition, and his seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] was the means by which he steered the Imperium's response to the Ork invasion.

A restricted Inquisitorial record states that Veritus was the same man as [[Kyril Sindermann]], chief [[Iterators|Iterator]] of the 63rd Expedition in the Great Crusade, who survived the Horus Heresy and took a new name. If true, he lived for roughly 1,500 years, sustained by an ornate suit of power armour and arcane devices. He died in 546.M32, poisoned, but not before he and [[Wienand|Inquisitor Wienand]] set out the division of the Inquisition into the [[Ordo Xenos]] and [[Ordo Malleus]].

Background

As Sindermann, Veritus was an orator and a firm adherent of the [[Imperial Truth]]. After the possession of the Legionary [[Xayver Jubal]] on [[Sixty-Three-Nineteen]] and a daemon summoned by accident in the archives of the flagship Vengeful Spirit, he gave up the Truth and turned to the [[Lectitio Divinitatus]]. He escaped the ship before the [[Istvaan III Atrocity]] with the remembrancers [[Mersadie Oliton]] and [[Euphrati Keeler]], and reached [[Terra]] aboard the Eisenstein to warn the Emperor.

After the Heresy he became one of the four original members of the Inquisition raised by [[Malcador the Sigillite]]. The record gives no more of the intervening centuries than that he became a [[psyker]] and a cold, convinced authoritarian who held that the Inquisition should rule humanity for its own good.

Tenure on the Senatorum Imperialis

By the mid-32nd millennium Veritus was a senior Puritan and known for fanatical devotion to the Emperor. In the war against the Ork [[Beast, The|Beast]] he judged the sitting Inquisitorial Representative, Wienand, negligent and corrupted by the intrigues of the Senatorum. A conclave he called against her failed. He then sent agents to kill her; the [[Venenum Temple]] assassin [[Esad Wire]] saved her, and she faked her death and went into hiding. Veritus took the seat.

In the Senatorum he opposed the [[Proletarian Crusade]] of [[Juskina Tull]], and condemned the High Lords as incompetent. After a band of [[Harlequins]] reached the [[Imperial Palace]], he and [[Drakan Vangorich]] persuaded [[Beyreuth|Captain-General Beyreuth]] to spare the [[Shadowseer]] Lhaerial Rey, and Veritus took her for interrogation. Vangorich then brokered a truce between Veritus and Wienand, and the two declared themselves joint representatives.

Both supported [[Koorland]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] when he removed [[Udin Macht Udo]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. Veritus then told Koorland that the lost primarch [[Vulkan]] was fighting the Orks on Caldera, and that a last redoubt of the [[Sisters of Silence]] survived on Nadiries. Vulkan appeared to know him.

The Ordos and the Grey Knights

After the victory at [[Ullanor]], Veritus and Wienand proposed that the Inquisition be formalised without being made a rigid hierarchy. Two great ordos would stand beside each other: the Ordo Xenos under Wienand, with the [[Deathwatch]] as its military arm, and the Ordo Malleus under Veritus, served by the [[Grey Knights]]. A council of Inquisitor Lords of uneven number would set overall direction.

Death

Veritus told Wienand that the Grey Knights had been made from the Emperor's own gene-seed and were hidden on [[Titan]]. He admitted to having ordered [[Exterminatus]] three times to keep that secret. He also suspected that Vangorich had poisoned him through a gas that turned lethal when it met the fluids of his armour. He collapsed before her, named her his successor as keeper of the Citadel of Titan, and told her to work with Vangorich until she could remove him. Then he disclosed that he had once been Sindermann. He died soon afterwards.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.
