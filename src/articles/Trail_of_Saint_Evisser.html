The Trail of Saint Evisser is a neglected part of Imperial space on the western edge of the [[Segmentum Solar]]. It is a loose chain of star systems visited in ancient times by the pilgrimage of [[Saint Evisser]], one of the [[Ecclesiarchy]]'s most obscure saints. It spans about a dozen inhabited systems and winds around nebulae and asteroid fields.

Status

The Trail's official status is unclear. It has too few worlds to be a [[Sector]] and is not listed as a sub-sector. It has been neglected for several thousand years and is now a shadow of its former self.

History

During the 13th Black Crusade of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], the [[Grey Knights]] and the [[Inquisition]] investigated the Trail. The investigation, led by [[Alaric]], then a Justicar, uncovered a series of linked [[Chaos Cult]]s. These cults were working to bring the Greater Daemon [[Ghargatuloth]], the Prince of a Thousand Faces, one of the most powerful lieutenants of [[Tzeentch]], into the material realm.
