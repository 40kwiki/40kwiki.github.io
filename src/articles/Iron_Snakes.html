The Iron Snakes are a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and one of the Successor Chapters of the [[Ultramarines]], the XIII Legion. Like the other Successors, the Chapter was founded in the [[Second Founding]] under the [[Codex Astartes]], with the Ultramarines' doctrine as its base.

The Chapter takes its name from its founding traditions. Its warriors are recorded as steady and methodical in their campaigns, in keeping with the Ultramarines' doctrine.

Sabbat Worlds Crusade

Main article: [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]

The Iron Snakes took part in the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] alongside the [[Astra Militarum]], the [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[White Scars]]. The crusade's commanders also drew on four further [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and the [[Titan Legions]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]]. The Chapter's part in the crusade's campaigns is recorded in the [[Sabbat Worlds]] registers.

Successor Chapters of the Ultramarines

See also: [[Ultramarines]]

[[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] have more than fifty Successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], including the [[Black Consuls]], [[Novamarines]], [[Doom Eagles]], [[Silver Skulls]], [[Aurora Chapter]] and [[Marines Errant]]. The Iron Snakes are among the better-recorded of them.
