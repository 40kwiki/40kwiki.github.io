Shalaxi Helbane is a [[Keeper of Secrets]] of [[Slaanesh]], one of the greater daemons of the Dark Prince. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it was sent to hunt the Aeldari seer [[Yvraine]], and its pursuit of her drew the Aeldari into the wars that led to the rise of the [[Ynnari]].

Imperial records describe Helbane as an unusually persistent daemon. Its hunt of Yvraine is one of the best recorded daemonic campaigns of the 42nd millennium.

The hunt for Yvraine

Slaanesh released Helbane from the Palace of Punishments to pursue Yvraine. At a council of the Aeldari kindreds on the maiden world of [[Iathglas]], which Yvraine had convened, Helbane attacked. It was driven off by Yvraine, [[Jain Zar]], [[Lelith Hesperax]], the [[Visarch]] and the [[Yncarne]], who fought together. Helbane was defeated, but its presence on Iathglas revealed that the Aeldari were being watched by the Dark Prince.
