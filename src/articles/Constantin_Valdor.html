Constantin Valdor, called the Shield of the Emperor and the First of the Ten Thousand, was the first Captain-General of the Legio Custodes, the bodyguard of the [[Emperor of Mankind]] now known as the [[Adeptus Custodes]]. He served the Emperor from the [[Unification Wars]] on [[Terra]] through the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]], and was regarded by many of his contemporaries as the equal of a [[Primarch|primarch]] in all but name.

Valdor led the Custodian forces at the [[Burning of Prospero]], directed the Custodes' secret [[War Within the Webway]] beneath the [[Imperial Palace]], and sat on the Terran war council with [[Malcador the Sigillite]] and [[Rogal Dorn]]. In the last hours of the [[Siege of Terra]] he fought aboard the Warmaster's flagship, the [[Vengeful Spirit]], where he wounded [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]], and with Dorn he recovered the broken body of the Emperor after the death of [[Horus]].

Soon after the Emperor was placed on the [[Golden Throne]], Valdor laid down his office and vanished from the Imperial record with his arms and armour. His fate is unknown. Inquisitorial material sealed in the 41st millennium suggests that he may still be active under another name, a claim that the Custodes neither confirm nor deny.

Origins and the Unification Wars

The Custodians were the first of the Emperor's gene-crafted warriors, made one at a time from individual children rather than from a shared template, and older than any of the primarchs. Valdor's own records predate the primarch project, and Custodian tradition holds that he stood at the Emperor's side "from the first days", possibly before the Unification Wars began. He was given the Apollonian Spear, a weapon said to have been made and carried by the Emperor himself, when he became Chief Custodian, and no other warrior has borne it.

The Trans-Nordyc Akashic archives credit a cadre of Custodians under Valdor with the capture of the Priest-King of the Maullan Sen Confederacy at the Battle of the Red Frost, fought alongside the [[Thunder Warriors]]. Later chronicles place him at the close of Unification, in 669.M30, when the Thunder Warriors turned on their maker. Official histories long recorded that they died heroically at the Battle of Mount Ararat; the Custodian record states that Valdor led the force of Custodians and early Space Marines of the [[Dark Angels|I Legion]] that destroyed them.

Valdor's duties were not limited to war. Imperial accounts record that early in the Great Crusade, in about 800.M30Date estimated from the sequence of events., he personally executed Koja Zu, Minister of the Anuatan Steppes, whose crime in the Emperor's eyes was the theft of Terra's water. Her son was taken into the Custodes and became the Custodian Ra Endymion.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

For most of the Great Crusade Valdor remained on Terra, where the Emperor withdrew after the [[Ullanor Crusade]] in 000.M31 to work on a secret project beneath the Palace. The Custodes held the Magisterium Lex Ultima, a mark of office that made them answerable only to the Emperor, and Valdor used it sparingly. Courtiers who called him a primarch in all but name noted that he took no part in the politics of the Palace except where the Emperor's safety was concerned.

He attended the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 as the Emperor's guard. He took no side in the debate over the use of psychic powers, which ended in the Decree Absolute against sorcery and the disbanding of the Legion Librarius.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Prospero

Main article: [[Burning of Prospero]]

When [[Magnus the Red]] broke the wards of the Emperor's [[Imperial Webway|webway project]] with a sorcerous warning of [[Horus|Horus's]] treachery, the Emperor ordered that Magnus be brought to Terra for judgement. In 004.M31 Valdor led the Custodian contingent of the censure host, which also included the [[Sisters of Silence]] and the [[Space Wolves]] of [[Leman Russ]]. Russ, swayed by Horus, came as an executioner rather than a gaoler. Valdor resisted his intent at first, but committed his warriors once the sorcery of [[Thousand Sons|Prospero]] was revealed. In the battle for Tizca he is recorded as having killed more than thirty of Magnus's precognitive bladesmen while taking a single wound.

The war beneath the Palace

The breach on Terra let the daemonic hosts of the warp into the unfinished human webway under the Palace. For most of the Heresy the bulk of the Ten Thousand fought there, in what the Custodes call the War Within the Webway, alongside the Sisters of Silence and the Mechanicum's Ordo Reductor. Their absence from the war outside was not explained to the wider Imperium at the time.

Valdor also took a seat on the war council that governed Terra, with Malcador, Dorn, the loyalist Fabricator-General Zagreus Kane, the Fabricator Locum of Mars and an Oblivion Knight of the Sisterhood. He proposed the first combined [[Execution Force]] of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] against Horus, and argued with Dorn over the morality of such methods until the Emperor ruled that there would be "no more shadows". The Execution Force was nonetheless sent, and struck at Horus on Dagonet in 008.M31.

Siege of Terra

Main article: [[Siege of Terra]]

During the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31 Valdor led the Custodes against the traitor assaults on the Palace. He fought the [[Death Guard]] at the Colossi and objected to Dorn's decision to give up the Eternity Wall space port. In the final days, with daemons loose inside the Sanctum Imperialis, he hunted the intruders in the Emperor's own halls while preparing contingencies of his own in case the Emperor fell.

When Horus lowered the shields of the Vengeful Spirit, the Emperor teleported aboard with Dorn, [[Sanguinius]] and the Custodes. In the warped interior of the ship, where Terra and the flagship had merged into what survivors called the Inevitable City, Valdor's Custodians fought the [[Sons of Horus]] Justaerin under Ezekyle Abaddon, aided by the sorcery of [[Erebus]], and took heavy losses. Valdor wounded Abaddon with the Apollonian Spear. Custodian testimony records that at the moment of the blow he saw a vision of Abaddon's future as the Warmaster of [[Chaos]].

Recovery of the Emperor

After Horus was destroyed, Valdor and Dorn reached Lupercal's Court and found the Emperor barely alive, guarded by the former Luna Wolf Garviel Loken. The Custodians made a bier from the ship's deck plating to carry him. Valdor at first judged that moving the Emperor might kill him, and was ready to kill the surviving [[Blood Angels]] as a threat to his master until Dorn talked him down. He agreed, reluctantly, that the Emperor should be taken to the Golden Throne. He ordered that Horus's corpse be left aboard the crippled ship, which he expected to be destroyed with it, and the Emperor and the body of Sanguinius were teleported back to the Palace.

Disappearance

The Custodes took no part in the [[Great Scouring]]. They swore a penitent's oath, and for a time wore mourning black, standing watch over the Throne. Without the Emperor's voice, they had to interpret his will for the first time, and Valdor's place in that order was uncertain.

Within a short time, in about 014–015.M31Date estimated from the sequence of events., Valdor resigned the office of Captain-General to an elected successor and disappeared. Imperial records disagree on how and when. His armour and the Apollonian Spear never entered the Hall of Armaments, where the arms of fallen Custodians are kept, which Custodian scholars take as evidence that he did not die in the Palace.

An Inquisitorial file sealed in the late 41st millennium concerns the figure known as the King in Yellow, who has acted in secret on the [[Imperial Palace|Throneworld]] and beyond for centuries. A book recovered by the agents of Inquisitor Gideon Ravenor names that figure as Constantin Valdor. The Ordo Hereticus has not accepted the identification as proven, and the possibility that the name was placed to mislead has not been ruled out.The record dates to some centuries before the opening of the [[Great Rift]]. The Adeptus Custodes have made no statement on it.

Character and abilities

Valdor is described as stoic, watchful and highly intelligent, and as an inspiring leader when he chose to be. Contemporaries judged that he could have matched a primarch with the blade. He held himself apart from glory and conquest; his duty, as he saw it, was the Emperor's survival and nothing else, and Custodian tradition records that he would sooner have taken his own life than put the Emperor at risk.

That single purpose set him against others. His disputes with Dorn on Terra turned on the difference between guarding the Emperor and guarding the Imperium, and his conduct in the final hours of the Siege, when he would have killed loyal Space Marines to protect his master, shows the same narrow devotion.

Wargear

  • The Apollonian Spear, a guardian spear said to have been made by the Emperor, combining a power blade with a bolter of unusual strength.
  • The Corinthine warplate, Custodian armour made by the artificers who forged the Emperor's own armour.
  • Misericordia, a dagger, an iron halo, and grenades.

Legacy

Valdor is the model by which the Custodes judge their captains. The Valdor tank hunter, a heavy war machine of the Great Crusade armies, was named in his honour. His successors as Captain-General, of whom [[Trajann Valoris]] holds the office in the Era Indomitus, have been elected from the order's ranks; none has borne his spear. For most of the ten thousand years after him the Custodes stayed within the Palace, and they did not again take a full part in the wider wars of the Imperium until the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]].
