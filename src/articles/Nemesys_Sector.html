The Nemesys Sector is an Imperial sector in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. It borders large regions of [[Wilderness Space]], which are home to pirates and Renegades. The [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Battlefleet Nemesys]] patrols its edges constantly.

History

The [[space hulk]] known as the [[Gallowdark]] emerged from the [[Warp]] in the Nemesys Sector. Parties from several factions boarded it to seize the archeotech it might hold, before it crashed onto the [[Forge World]] of [[Bheta-Decima]].

The magi of Bheta-Decima used a capital-ship-scale [[graviton cannon]] to pull the passing hulk into orbit so they could plunder it at leisure. The tidal forces tore the hulk apart. Debris fell across the surface of Bheta-Decima, damaged most of its settlements and killed millions.
