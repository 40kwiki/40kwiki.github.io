The Butcher's Nails were the implants fitted to the [[primarch]] [[Angron]] on [[Nuceria]], the world on which he was raised as a gladiator-slave. They were driven into his flesh and his mind, and their effect was to bring on the battle-fury that later defined the [[World Eaters]].

Imperial accounts describe the Nails as a tool of the High Riders, the aristocracy of slavemasters who ruled Nuceria. Their purpose was to make the infant Angron a fighter who could not be stopped in the arena.

Removal

The Nails were removed by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] in 899.M30, when Angron was taken from Nuceria. The removal did not end the rage they had caused. Angron led the World Eaters for the rest of the [[Great Crusade]] and into the [[Horus Heresy]], and his fury is recorded as one of the causes of the Legion's later ruin.The date of the removal is given in the sources. The effect of the Nails on Angron's later conduct is disputed in Imperial accounts.
