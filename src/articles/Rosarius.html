A Rosarius is a sacred symbol of office worn by the clerical members of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]]. It is also a symbol of office for the [[Chaplain]]s of the [[Space Marines]], alongside the [[Crozius Arcanum]]. It is worn around the neck or waist on a sash, a cord or a string of prayer beads.

A Rosarius is usually made of adamantium in the form of the [[Imperial Aquila]] or a Gothic Cross, with a jewel at the centre. It houses a protective field generator. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] itself grants the Rosarius to the Chaplains of the Astartes as a sign of their faith.

Use

Chaplains are recognised by their black armour, their skull-faced helms, the Crozius Arcanum they carry as both mace and badge of office, and the Rosarius that marks their rank. Unlike the priests of the [[Imperial Cult]], Chaplains carry the Rosarius as part of their combat equipment.
