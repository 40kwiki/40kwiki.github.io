Irthu Haemotalion was a [[High Lord of Terra]] who had served as [[Master of the Administratum]]. He led the [[Hexarchy]], the conspiracy of serving and deposed [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] that tried to seize [[Terra]] in early M42. He is recorded as the conspiracy's chief organiser.

Haemotalion was among the High Lords removed by [[Roboute Guilliman]] after the [[Primarch]]'s return to Terra. He joined the deposed [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Baldo Slyst]] in the Hexarchy, and the two of them gathered the High Lords who had supported the old order.

Career

As [[Master of the Administratum]], Haemotalion was responsible for the registers of the Imperium's tithe and for the [[Sector|sectors]] that paid it. The office had been held by several powerful men before him, and Imperial records describe its rivalry with the [[Ecclesiarchy]] as a constant feature of the 36th and 41st millennia. Details of his earlier career are not recorded.

Downfall

After the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] restored the council and removed those who had opposed him. Haemotalion joined the [[Hexarchy]], which moved on Terra in early M42. The coup failed, and the [[Administratum]]'s records show that he was no longer in office after it.The date of Haemotalion's removal is estimated from the sequence of the Hexarchy's coup. Later records differ on whether he was killed or executed.
