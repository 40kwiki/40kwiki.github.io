A Warpsmith is an engineer of the [[Chaos Space Marines]], a Traitor Astartes who holds the war machines of his warband together. Warpsmiths are the [[Traitor|Traitors]]' counterpart to the [[Techmarine]] of the loyal [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. They are among the few Chaos Space Marines who keep a link with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] techniques that the [[Traitor Legions]] carried out of Imperial service.

Their craft is a blend of forge work and [[Warp]] sorcery. Their machines are often daemon-touched, and a Warpsmith is judged by how well he can bind a [[Daemon|daemon]] into a war engine.

Role in the warbands

[[Chaos Space Marines]] are organised into a small number of roles. The Sorcerers are the [[Traitor|Traitors]]' [[Psyker|psykers]], the Dark Apostles priests of the [[Chaos|Dark Gods]], and the Warpsmiths engineers who hold the warbands' war machines together. The Possessed share their bodies with [[Daemon|daemons]], and the Chosen are elite veterans.

Warpsmiths are most often found with the [[Iron Warriors]], whose Legion was the [[Traitor Legions|Traitor Legion]] most closely tied to siege engineering. Many have left the loyal forges entirely and have replaced their own flesh with machinery.

Relations with the Dark Mechanicum

See also: [[Dark Mechanicum]]

Warpsmiths often serve alongside, and sometimes under, the leaders of the [[Dark Mechanicum]]. Their orders are the same in principle as those of the loyal [[Techmarine|Techmarines]], but they answer to the [[Chaos|Chaos Gods]] rather than to the Omnissiah. Some Warpsmiths have joined the cult of [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]] and fought beside him in the [[Arks of Omen]].
