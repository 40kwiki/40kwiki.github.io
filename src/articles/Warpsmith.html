A Warpsmith is an engineer of the [[Chaos Space Marines]], a Traitor Astartes who holds the war machines of his warband together. Warpsmiths are the Traitors' counterpart to the [[Techmarine]] of the loyal Chapters. They are among the few Chaos Space Marines who keep a link with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] techniques that the Traitor Legions carried out of Imperial service.

Their craft is a blend of forge work and Warp sorcery. Their machines are often daemon-touched, and a Warpsmith is judged by how well he can bind a daemon into a war engine.

Role in the warbands

Chaos Space Marines are organised into a small number of roles. The Sorcerers are the Traitors' psykers, the Dark Apostles priests of the Dark Gods, and the Warpsmiths engineers who hold the warbands' war machines together. The Possessed share their bodies with daemons, and the Chosen are elite veterans.

Warpsmiths are most often found with the [[Iron Warriors]], whose Legion was the Traitor Legion most closely tied to siege engineering. Many have left the loyal forges entirely and have replaced their own flesh with machinery.

Relations with the Dark Mechanicum

Warpsmiths often serve alongside, and sometimes under, the leaders of the [[Dark Mechanicum]]. Their orders are the same in principle as those of the loyal Techmarines, but they answer to the Chaos Gods rather than to the Omnissiah. Some Warpsmiths have joined the cult of [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]] and fought beside him in the Arks of Omen.
