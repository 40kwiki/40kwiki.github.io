Yvraine, styled the Emissary of Ynnead and the Daughter of Shades, is an [[Aeldari]] prophet and the leader of the [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnari]], the Reborn. Born on the craftworld [[Biel-Tan]], she walked many of the craftworld Paths, became a Corsair, and fell at last to the gladiatorial arenas of [[Drukhari#Commorragh|Commorragh]]. In 999.M41, at the moment of her death in the arena, she was struck by a fragment of [[Ynnead]], the Aeldari god of the dead, and rose with the power to command the energies of death. The event, the Night of Revelations, opened the city to [[Daemon|daemons]] and began the Ynnari movement.

Yvraine leads the Ynnari with the swordsman called the [[Visarch]] and the [[Yncarne]], the Avatar of Ynnead; together they are the Triumvirate of Ynnead. Her search for the Croneswords of Morai-Heg, through which she hoped to wake Ynnead fully, led to the shattering of Biel-Tan, and with [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] she restored the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] to life on [[Macragge]] in 999.M41. In the [[Era Indomitus]] she is hunted by [[Asdrubael Vect]] of Commorragh and by the daemon [[Shalaxi Helbane]] of [[Slaanesh]].

Early life

Yvraine was born on [[Craftworld|Biel-Tan]], the craftworld that more than any other wished to restore the [[Aeldari]] empire. Like all Asuryani she followed the Paths, the disciplines by which the craftworld Aeldari master one calling at a time to hold their passions in check, and she is said to have walked more of them than most. In time she left the craftworld on the Path of the Outcast and took up the life of a Corsair. Her wanderings ended in [[Drukhari|Commorragh]], where she became a Succubus of a [[Wych Cult]], one of the gladiatorial societies of the Dark City.

The Night of Revelations

In 999.M41 the [[Farseer]] [[Eldrad Ulthran]] attempted on [[Luna|the moon]] Coheria to wake [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnead]] by drawing together the souls of the [[Craftworld|craftworld]] dead. The ritual was interrupted, and only a mote of the god's consciousness passed across the void to [[Drukhari|Commorragh]]. There, in the arena of the Crucibael, Yvraine had killed a captive [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] Hive Tyrant before a crowd that included [[Lelith Hesperax]], had been mortally wounded by Hesperax, and was dying in a last duel with a priestess of Morai-Heg. The mote struck her at the threshold of death. A wave of deathly energy swept the arena, a breach opened in the [[Webway]], and [[Daemon|daemons]] poured into the city.

Yvraine escaped with a mysterious swordsman who had appeared at her side, the Visarch, whose identity she has never publicly revealed, and began to preach the cult of Ynnead.

Prophet of Ynnead

Biel-Tan

Main article: [[Craftworld]]

Yvraine travelled through the Webway to [[Exodite]] worlds and craftworlds, winning converts and leaving schism behind. On her return to Biel-Tan, as [[Daemon|daemons]] of [[Slaanesh]] invaded, she drew a Cronesword from the craftworld's [[Wraithbone|wraithbone]] core. The craftworld's infinity circuit shattered, and from the storm of released souls rose the [[Rise of the Ynnari|Yncarne]], bearing the great Cronesword Vilith-zhar. The daemons were destroyed, but Biel-Tan began to break apart, and the psychic storm of its fracture added to the storms that formed the [[Great Rift]].

Ulthwé, Belial IV and Iyanden

Called to account before the seers of [[Ulthwé]], Yvraine was allowed to leave on condition that she enter the [[Eye of Terror]]. On the crone world Belial IV the Yncarne found a third Cronesword during a battle against daemons and a [[Drukhari]] force sent by Vect. At Iyanden Yvraine raised Prince [[Yriel]], killed in destroying a Nurglite plague ship, and claimed his Spear of Twilight, which proved to be a fourth Cronesword.

Macragge

Main article: [[Macragge]]

The elders of Iyanden concluded that the [[Aeldari]] could survive only if humanity held back [[Chaos]]. Yvraine led the Ynnari through the Webway, fighting the [[Thousand Sons]] on the way, to the ice moon Klaisus, where they rescued the survivors of the [[Fall of Cadia]], among them [[Saint Celestine]], [[Katarinya Greyfax]] and [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]]. She led them to Macragge. In the Temple of Correction she ended the stasis in which [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] had lain at the edge of death for ten thousand years, so that he died, and then raised him with the power of Ynnead while Cawl sealed him in the [[Artificer armour|Armour of Fate]]. The Ynnari helped clear [[Ultramar]] of Chaos and then withdrew into the Webway.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Many [[Aeldari]] blame Yvraine for the loss of Biel-Tan and of two craftworlds whose psychic traces faded after the opening of the [[Great Rift]]. Vect, whose hold on [[Drukhari|Commorragh]] the Night of Revelations had shaken, put a bounty on her head. His agent [[Drazhar]], Master of Blades, came within a hand's breadth of killing her at the debating fires of [[Saim-Hann]] before the Phoenix Lord [[Jain Zar]] parried the blow; Jain Zar then joined the Reborn. On Ghodri Sekmet the Haemonculi of Vect's court trapped the [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnari]] between an [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial army]] and a Drukhari strike force, and Yvraine escaped only through the manifestation of the Yncarne.

At a council of the Aeldari kindreds that Yvraine convened on the maiden world [[Iathglas]], the Keeper of Secrets Shalaxi Helbane, sent by Slaanesh to hunt her, attacked and was driven off by Yvraine, Jain Zar, Lelith Hesperax, the Visarch and the Yncarne together. The daemon revealed that the fifth Cronesword lay in the palace of Slaanesh. The Seventh Path, by which the Ynnari hoped to wake their god, now appears closed, and Yvraine has turned to other means.Dated to the early 42nd millennium from the order of Ynnari campaigns; Aeldari sources give no human date.

Powers and character

Yvraine is a [[Psyker|psyker]] of great power, whose gifts draw on the energies released by death; around her the Reborn grow stronger as those nearby die. She can raise the newly dead, as she raised [[Yriel]] and [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]. She carries Kha-vir, the Sword of Sorrows, a Cronesword, and is accompanied by a gyrinx, a psychically bonded [[Aeldari]] feline. Imperial observers who have met her describe her as calm, regal and certain of her purpose. The [[Inquisition]] regards her as among the most dangerous [[Xenos|xenos]] leaders alive, and the Imperium's debt to her as a matter it prefers not to discuss.
