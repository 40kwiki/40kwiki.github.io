Lev Tieron was the [[Chancellor of the Imperial Council]], the officer who presided over the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] in the closing years of the [[41st millennium]]. He is remembered for proposing, during the [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, a measure that would have released the [[Adeptus Custodes]] from their oath never to leave [[Terra]].

The Dissolution Act

The [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] pressed on the Imperium and the [[Terra|Throneworld]] itself. Tieron judged that the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]], the strongest warriors Terra possessed, should be free to fight the enemy outside the [[Imperial Palace]]. His plan required the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]] to vote to abolish the [[Lex Imperialis]], the binding legal code of the Imperium, which holds the Custodes to their post.

He gathered support among the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], among them [[Kania Dhanda]], the [[Speaker for the Chartist Captains]]. The [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Baldo Slyst]] and others opposed him. To tip the vote Tieron pressed [[Trajann Valoris]], [[Captain-General]] of the Custodes, to take the seat that had stood empty since the death of the Chancellor of the [[Estate Imperium]], Brach. Valoris relented after long persuasion.

Legacy

The record does not state how the vote ended or what became of Tieron. Later records name Dhanda among those who supported him and show the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]] divided over the measure.
