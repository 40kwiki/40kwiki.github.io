Civitas Imperialis is a term in High Gothic that means the rule of Imperial law. Its exact meaning is more specific. Imperial scholars have learned from the personal diaries of [[Slaydo|Warmaster Slaydo]] that it was a concept of particular importance to him. Slaydo was the first Imperial Commander of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]].

At the foundation of the Imperium in the late 30th millennium, the [[Emperor of Mankind]] laid down the Civitas Imperialis. He intended it to "guarantee the safety and assurance of any citizen of the Imperium of Man, wherever he or she travels or sets foot within the length and breadth of the Imperium". The Civitas was meant to be the mark of refined Imperial culture and the measure of its security and power.

Slaydo's restoration

Slaydo held that the Civitas was about the quality of human civilisation, not simply the rule of law. He was determined to restore it to the [[Sabbat Worlds]]. Imperial records describe the Civitas as the perfection of human society, which Slaydo wished to see reborn in the Sabbat Worlds.

Suspension in the Sabbat Worlds

Before their conquest by the Imperium in the 35th millennium, the Sabbat Worlds lay beyond the bounds of the Civitas and were an extremely dangerous frontier. Later, in the Sabbat Worlds Sector, the Civitas was suspended. The region was classed as "unstable/hazardous" and was no longer considered under Imperial governance. The court of the sector's [[Sector Governor|Lord Governor]] was moved to a neighbouring sector.

Etymology

The term is drawn from two Latin words. The first is civitas, meaning state, citizenship or city-state. The second is imperiosus, meaning imperious, domineering or powerful.
