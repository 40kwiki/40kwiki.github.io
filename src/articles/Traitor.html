A Traitor is, in the law of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], a person or organisation that has betrayed the Emperor or the Imperium. Treason is the highest civil crime, and the [[Lex Imperialis]] treats it as the gravest offence that a citizen can commit. A traitor is judged by the Imperial courts, by the [[Inquisition]], or by the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]], and the penalty is death.

The status of Traitor is distinct from that of [[Heretic]]. A heretic holds or spreads a doctrine that opposes the [[Imperial Cult]] or the law. A traitor acts against the Imperium, by rebellion, by aiding its enemies, or by serving the [[Ruinous Powers]]. Imperial law holds that a heretic who acts against the Imperium becomes a traitor, and that the two charges are often brought together.

Legal status

Treason

Treason is the act of betraying the Emperor, the Imperium or its institutions. It includes rebellion, desertion in the face of an enemy, the disclosure of secrets to outsiders, and any service to the enemies of Mankind.

Traitor status

An individual who is judged a traitor loses the protection of the law. Their possessions are forfeit, and their names may be struck from the records. A captured traitor is subject to execution, and loyal servants of the Imperium are held to be expected to act against known traitors. The sentence is harsh because the Imperium regards treason as a threat to all of Mankind, not only to the people who are betrayed.

Excommunicate Traitoris

Main article: [[Excommunicate Traitoris]]

The most severe form of the status is the [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] designation. It is given by the [[High Lords of Terra]] or by an [[Inquisitor]], and it removes the accused from the Imperium and from its records. The designation is used against the greatest traitors, including whole [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] that have turned against the Emperor.

Traitor Legions

Main article: [[Traitor Legions]]

The largest body of traitors in Imperial history is the [[Traitor Legions]] of the [[Horus Heresy]]. The Heresy began in 005.M31, when the [[Warmaster Horus]] rebelled against the Emperor. Several Legions followed him, and their warriors were declared traitors. The Imperial account of the Heresy treats their betrayal as a crime against Mankind and not only against the Emperor.

[[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]]' descendants are still treated as traitors by [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]]. The [[Chaos Space Marines]] are the successors of the Traitor Legions, and they are hunted accordingly.

Trial and proof

[[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] requires that the accused be judged before the sentence is passed, except where an [[Inquisitor]] or the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] have declared an [[Excommunicate Traitoris]]. In practice, evidence is weighed by the [[Inquisition]], which may set aside ordinary procedure where heresy or [[Chaos]] is suspected. The [[Adeptus Arbites|Arbites]] and the military courts apply the ordinary procedure to lesser cases.

Imperial sources treat the proof of treason as a difficult matter. An accused person may be a victim of the Warp, of a cult or of a corrupt official, and the law allows for that possibility. Those who confess and repent may be spared the full penalty, and some are sent to the [[Penitent]] order to atone.

Historical cases

[[Horus Heresy|The Heresy]] is the largest case of treason in the Imperial record. After the Heresy, the [[Great Scouring]] hunted the surviving traitors across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], and the [[Black Crusade]] and later Chaos incursions brought new traitors to the Imperium's attention. The [[Siege of Terra]], which ended the Heresy in 014.M31, is the final stage of the same crime.
