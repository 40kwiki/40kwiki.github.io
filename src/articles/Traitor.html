A Traitor is, in the vocabulary of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], a person or body that once served the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and has turned against him, usually to serve [[Chaos]]. The word is used in two linked senses. In law it names the gravest offence an Imperial citizen can commit, for which the penalty is death and, in the worst cases, erasure from the record. In common speech it names an enemy: the defenders of the Imperium call the mortal and daemonic servants of the [[Ruinous Powers|Dark Gods]] the forces of Chaos, the Archenemy or, where they were once Imperial, Renegades and Traitors.

The archetype of the traitor is the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], who rebelled against the Emperor in 005.M31 and drew nine of the twenty [[Space Marine Legions]] into the [[Horus Heresy]]. When the rebellion failed on [[Terra]] in 014.M31, the survivors fled with other traitor forces into the region of the [[Warp]] that became the [[Eye of Terror]]. Their descendants and successors, the [[Chaos Space Marines]], have remained the most visible and powerful servants of Chaos for more than ten thousand years.

The status of traitor is distinct from that of [[Heretic]]. A heretic holds a doctrine that opposes the [[Imperial Cult]] or the law; a traitor acts against the Imperium. The two charges are often brought together, and the most severe censure the Imperium possesses, the Excommunicate Traitoris, is applied to those guilty of both. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the opening of the Great Rift (999.M41) has swelled the numbers of the Lost and the Damned, and the Traitors under [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] are considered the gravest threat to the continued existence of the Imperium.

Legal status

Treason and heresy

Imperial authorities treat treason as a crime against the whole of mankind and not only against its rulers. The accused may be rebels, deserters, those who aid the Imperium's enemies, or those who serve Chaos, and the same word covers all of them. Heresy is the more common charge; it includes mutation, apostasy, blasphemy, rebellion and corruption by Chaos, and it is the Holy Ordos of the [[Inquisition]] that most often decides when a heretic has become a traitor. The Inquisition may set aside ordinary procedure where Chaos is suspected, while the [[Adeptus Arbites]] and military courts handle lesser cases.

Excommunicate Traitoris

Main article: [[Excommunicate Traitoris]]

The ultimate censure is the High Gothic designation Excommunicate Traitoris, used by the [[Administratum]] and the Inquisition. It may be pronounced by the [[High Lords of Terra]] or by an Inquisitor, and it banishes the accused from the Imperium on pain of death, striking them from Imperial history. If the accused was an Imperial servant, his name is deleted from the scrolls of honour in the Hall of Heroes in the [[Imperial Palace]] and wiped from the archives of the Librarium Terra by the deletion teams of the Administratum's Historical Revision Unit. If the accused is a [[Space Marine Chapter]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] destroys its stored [[gene-seed]], and slain Traitor Marines are burned so that their blood no longer taints mankind.

A traitor who confesses and repents is granted a quick and clean death. More often the accused flees, usually toward the storms of the Eye of Terror, and is hunted for the rest of his life or executed on sight by any loyal servant of the Imperium.

History

The Horus Heresy

Main article: [[Horus Heresy]]

The largest betrayal in Imperial history began when Horus, primarch of the [[Luna Wolves]] (renamed the [[Sons of Horus]] by the Emperor's leave), was corrupted by Chaos and determined to replace the rule of the Emperor with his own. The Heresy set the eighteen known Legions against one another in two camps, the Loyalists who continued to serve the Emperor and the Traitors who pledged themselves to the four Ruinous Powers. Some Traitors dedicated themselves to all four Powers, a worship called Chaos Undivided, and others to one alone. The Traitor Legions formed the strongest part of the rebel host, which also included daemons of the Warp, human traitors from the Army, the infantry and walkers of the Dark Mechanicum, and cultists drawn from across the galaxy.

Flight and the Long War

When Horus died at the hands of the Emperor aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] at the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31), the remnants of the nine Traitor Legions withdrew into the Eye of Terror. The [[Great Scouring]] that followed pursued the surviving traitors across the galaxy, but it did not end them, and the Warp's instability has let many of the warriors who rebelled continue to fight today. The Traitor Legions have kept their old names, except the Sons of Horus, which Abaddon renamed the [[Black Legion]]. Of the Traitor primarchs, Horus, [[Alpharius Omegon|Alpharius]] and [[Konrad Curze]] died during or soon after the Heresy, and of the six survivors, [[Fulgrim]], [[Perturabo]], [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]], [[Mortarion]] and [[Angron]] have been raised by the Dark Gods to the state of Daemon Primarchs, while the fate of [[Lorgar]] is uncertain.

Black Crusades

See also: [[Black Crusade]]

From the Eye of Terror the Traitors have launched repeated assaults on the Imperium, known as Black Crusades, under warleaders such as Abaddon, and the contagion of Chaos has continued to spread through ordinary Imperial society in the form of cults and rebellion. Since the Eye of Terror opened, whole regiments, [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and noble houses have been counted among the Lost.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the wake of the [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, the forces of Chaos entered a new phase. Abaddon's Traitors stand as the most grave threat to the Imperium, and the Rift, which divides the galaxy, has let Chaos forces reach worlds that were once far from the Eye. The [[Indomitus Crusade]] and the Imperium's other forces now fight Traitors on every front, and the Inquisition and the [[Adeptus Arbites]] have been stretched to breaking in searching out the traitors within.

The forces of the Traitor

Heretic Astartes

The Chaos Space Marines, also called Traitor Marines, Renegade Marines or [[Heretic Astartes]], are former Space Marines who have abandoned the Emperor. Most belong to the nine original Traitor Legions, but Chapters founded long after the Heresy have also turned Renegade. They are driven by hunger for the pleasures, wealth and power that the Emperor's ascetic servants were denied. The Dark Gods reward carnage with fickle gifts: a favoured champion may gain a blood-red aura or find his limbs reshaped into axes of bone. His ultimate aim is to be raised as a Daemon Prince, and for each who succeeds hundreds fail and become mewling Chaos Spawn.

Champions, sorcerers and prophets

The warbands of the Traitor Legions are led by Chaos Lords, men and women sworn to the Dark Gods and consumed by desire for dominion, who may bind other Chaos forces to their will. Aspiring Champions follow them in the hope of rising to the same place. Chaos Sorcerers, usually former Librarians or other psykers, use powers so great that they resemble the magic of old human belief; only [[Khorne]] rejects them. Dark Apostles of the [[Word Bearers]] preach the worship of the Dark Gods. Greater Daemons may command whole armies when summoned, and Daemon Princes serve as lieutenants, though true daemons regard them as too tainted by their mortal origins.

Mortal traitors

The Imperium calls the mortal servants of Chaos who are not Astartes the Lost and the Damned. The most numerous are the Chaos cultists, who exist on nearly every kind of Imperial world, from the blood cults of feral worlds to the secret societies of jaded hive aristocracies. The Chaos cults are held to be the most dangerous of all the organisations that plot the overthrow of the Imperium from within. Another body, the Traitoris Militarum, consists of soldiers of the [[Astra Militarum]] and of its predecessors, including whole regiments that followed Horus; such turncoats retain military discipline and are a reliable core of professional troops compared with the rabble of the Lost. Companies and whole regiments of the Imperial Guard have gone over to Chaos in later centuries, bringing their armouries and vehicles with them.

Engines of the Traitor

Thousands of [[Imperial Knight|Knight]] suits and whole Knight houses have fallen to Chaos since the Heresy; the Questor Traitoris, or Chaos Knights, are among the most terrible of the traitor engines, and their treachery is a heavy blow because Knight nobles are reckoned among the Imperium's most loyal servants. Many [[Titan Legions]] sided with Horus, among them the infamous [[Legio Mortis]], and the survivors in the Eye have become possessed Daemon Engines, with their machine spirits replaced by daemonic intelligences. The remnants of the Dark Mechanicum, the Traitor adepts of [[Mars]], complete the roll.
