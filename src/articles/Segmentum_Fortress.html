A Segmentum Fortress is a massive orbital fleet base that serves as the headquarters of the [[Imperial Navy]] in one of the five [[Segmentum|segmenta]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Each fortress is a void station and starport with many docks, orbital shipyards and voidship repair facilities. It is the seat of the segmentum's battlefleet and the centre of its naval administration.

Most ships of a segmentum battlefleet never see their fortress. The fleet is divided among its sector battlefleets, which are based in their own [[Sector|sectors]], and only a small part of it is kept at the segmentum base. The fortress is therefore better understood as the administrative heart of the segmentum than as its main fighting force.

The fortresses

Mars

Main article: [[Mars]]

The [[Ring of Iron]] of [[Mars]] serves as the fortress of the [[Segmentum Solar]]. It stands in the [[Sol System]], so it is the only fortress that lies at the heart of the Imperium and not at its edge. Mars is also a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], so the fortress is run in close partnership with the Mechanicus.

Cypra Mundi

Main article: [[Cypra Mundi]]

[[Cypra Mundi]] is the fortress of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], the northern segmentum. It is a forge world, and its [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]] is known as [[Battlefleet Obscurus]].

Hydraphur

Main article: [[Hydraphur]]

[[Hydraphur]] is the fortress of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]], the western segmentum. It is also a forge world. Its battlefleet is known as [[Battlefleet Pacificus]].

Bakka

Main article: [[Bakka]]

[[Bakka]] is a [[Planetary classification|mining world]] and the fortress of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], the southern segmentum. Its battlefleet is known as [[Battlefleet Bakka]]. Its planetary estates have long been held by hereditary families.

Kar Duniash

Main article: [[Kar Duniash]]

[[Kar Duniash]] is the fortress of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], the eastern segmentum. It is a naval world, with a naval academy, and it is the seat of [[Battlefleet Ultima]].

Command

A Segmentum Fortress has two heads. The civil administration is led by a [[Master of the Segmentum]], an official of the [[Administratum]]. The naval forces are led by the [[Segmentum|segmentum]]'s [[Lord High Admiral]], a flag officer of the [[Imperial Navy]] who may hold a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Records differ on which of the two outranks the other, and the Administratum and the Navy have each claimed precedence.Imperial accounts differ. Administratum records give the Master precedence in the fortress, while Navy records give precedence to the Lord High Admiral in matters of fleet deployment.
