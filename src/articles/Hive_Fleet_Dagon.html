Hive Fleet Dagon was the first major [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] incursion into the [[Jericho Reach]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Its arrival was first heralded in 812.M41 by the [[Shadow in the Warp]] cast by the Tyranid Hive Mind in that volatile region of space. Communication between the Reach's worlds was cut before the fleet could be seen.

[[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] describe the fleet's assault as coordinated, with [[Overlord]]s leading the Tyranid forces into each system. The fleet struck where the defences were thinnest and used the Shadow to isolate each world from its neighbours. Its advance shaped the Imperial response in the Reach for years.
