The Battle of Raukos was the decisive engagement that ended the first phase of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], led by [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The earliest Imperial reports date it to about 012.M42. After the battle, Guilliman returned to the defence of Ultramar.

The battle is remembered for the dispersal of the enemy fleets. Guilliman's triumph after it is recorded as the close of the crusade's first part, and the crusade's later phases are counted from it.

The duel at Iax

Accounts of the crusade place a duel at [[Iax]] at the end of its first phase, shortly before the battle. The duel is recorded in several chronicles but is not described consistently across them. Some accounts place it in the same campaign as the battle, and others describe it as a separate engagement.

Dating

Imperial records do not agree on the date. The [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]'s own records were revised in 003.M42, and the [[Novamarines]] chronicles place the battle, and the death of their [[Chapter Master]] Dovaro at [[Galatan]], in c. 111.M42.The date of the battle is disputed. The c. 012.M42 date follows the earliest reports and the Lord Commander's records. The c. 111.M42 date follows the Novamarines chronicles, which are the only source that gives it.
