The Storm Wardens are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] that keeps to the [[Calixis Sector]] and the [[Halo Stars]] beyond it, in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. They follow the [[Codex Astartes]] closely, but their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] and Founding are not known: the Chapter lost its oldest records in the Nemesis Incident of c. 945.M36, and has never recovered them.

Their homeworld, [[Sacris]], is classed by the [[Adeptus Terra]] as a [[Forbidden world]] and is guarded by automated beacons and Chapter warships that open fire without warning. Few officials in the sector know that a Chapter lives there. Those who have fought beside the Storm Wardens describe them as stoic, insular and grimly proud, and the Chapter takes its manner from the warrior clans of Sacris.

History

The Nemesis Incident

In c. 945.M36, during the [[Age of Apostasy]], the Storm Wardens joined agents of the [[Inquisition]] against an infestation of [[Enslaver|Enslavers]], psychic parasites of the [[Warp]], in the Steropes Cluster. [[Chapter Master]] Owin Glendwyr took the field with the Veteran 1st Company, and three worlds were cleansed before an [[Inquisitor|Inquisitor Lord]] of the [[Ordo Xenos]] declared [[Exterminatus]] on seven more. What the Storm Wardens found in the black ruins of the cluster is not recorded.The date is an estimate. The Chapter's own sacred history, the Liber Tempest, survives in several versions that disagree, and the Chapter's Librarians chose one as true after a study lasting about a century.

Imperial accounts agree that the company returned to Sacris tainted. Glendwyr consulted the Inquisitor Lord and, by authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]], sealed much of the fortress-monastery. He and the entire 1st Company were placed in hidden stasis vaults, to wait until a cure was found. The Chapter's records of its Founding and progenitor were destroyed or walled away, and Sacris was closed to the wider Imperium. The Chapter's [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] guard the vaults under a vow of silence.

The Inheritors and the Halo Stars

After the incident the Storm Wardens rebuilt their 1st Company from new battle-brothers, who call themselves the Inheritors and hold the Chapter's honour until their predecessors wake. Beacons around Sacris still broadcast a false warning of plague, a deception ordered by Glendwyr and kept by every Chapter Master since. In the [[41st millennium]] the Chapter garrisoned the frontier worlds near the [[Halo Stars]], where [[Warp storm|Warp storms]] bring [[Xenos|xenos]] raiders and [[Chaos]] renegades.

Wars of the 41st millennium

In 331.M41 the strike cruiser Eternal Sentinel was ambushed in the Maw by a Chaos warfleet emerging from a Warp interface that Imperial augurs could not identify. In 777.M41 the Chapter sent seven companies to the opening battles of the [[Jericho Reach|Achilus Crusade]] under the name Operation Hammerfall, the largest contribution of any Chapter. In 784.M41 three companies accompanied the [[Rogue Trader]] Tyral Cathek into the Halo Stars on a voyage that ended in the razing of several isolated realms; in 789.M41 a company freed the Colhep systems from alien enslavement in a single week. Undated actions include the defeat of the Ork Warboss Kraazgrug on Keffik's World and the Cleansing of Vigil, a close-quarters war in the tunnels of a [[Planetary classification|dead world]] against the Slaugth.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter recruits from the clans of Sacris, whose warriors believe the Emperor is a stern father who judges men by strength at arms and the keeping of their word. Aspirants run a trial through swamp gas, leeches and quicksand, then compete in games of arms on the moors. Those who pass receive a Sacris claymore, which is returned to the clan if the Marine dies.

The Chapter's veteran ideal is the Tempest Blade, a warrior who has won the regard of the whole Chapter and who inherits the name, teaching and often the wargear of a named predecessor. Tempest Blades seek the strongest enemy in every battle and fight it alone, and they study the Lexicon Heroica, a record of heroes whose earliest volumes lie in the sealed vaults. They hold that a warrior learns his limit at the moment of death, and so honour [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] as men who have returned from it.

Notable members

  • Lorgath Maclir, Ward-Master, initiated in 229.M41 and raised to command in 629.M41; [[Planetary Governor|Imperial Commander]] of Sacris and a diligent student of the [[Tactica Imperium|Tactica Imperialis]].
  • Owin Glendwyr, [[Chapter Master]] at the time of the Nemesis Incident; in stasis since.
  • Kynar Half-Hand, Captain of the 7th Company, who came to the Jericho Reach with the vanguard of the [[Achilus Crusade]].
  • Tarran Cearr, Watch Captain of the [[Deathwatch]] at Watch Fortress Erioch, who is quietly seeking the truth of the Nemesis Incident despite Inquisitorial prohibition.
  • Brand Mac Lir, Watch Captain, famed at the Cleansing of Vigil and bearer of the Sacris claymore Morwenna.
