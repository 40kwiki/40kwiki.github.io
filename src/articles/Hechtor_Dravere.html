Hechtor Dravere was a [[Lord Militant]] of the [[Imperial Guard]] and one of the senior commanders who served under [[Warmaster]] [[Slaydo]] and then [[Macaroth]] during the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]. He was passed over for the position of Warmaster after Slaydo's death in 765.M41. He died during the assault on [[Menazoid Epsilon]] later that year.

Career

Dravere adhered closely to Imperial tactical tradition. For a long period he was the most decorated officer in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]]. Before the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] he won many victories with his favoured regiment, the Jantine Patricians. He took Parfelis from the [[Orks]] in 730.M41 and put down the Frateris Uprising on Skolnik in 737.M41. He directed the Balance Wars in the Kartheope System in 740–743.M41 and was one of the first Imperial commanders to meet the [[Tyranids]] and survive.

His peers and subordinates often described him as boastful and self-important. Warmaster Slaydo doubted that his command was as skilful as his decorations suggested. Like his contemporary [[Cybon]], Dravere was willing to use regular infantry as cannon fodder. His tactical rigidity and failure to grasp the value of mobility cost many lives.

Dravere took part in the [[Khulan Campaign]] (752–754.M41), though only in a supporting role. He expected to be named Warmaster of the Crusade because of his record. Slaydo was appointed instead. Slaydo disliked him but treated him with outward respect. Slaydo once rejected the world of Ambold Eleven as a primary target because Dravere had championed it. During Operation Redrake, Slaydo gave Dravere and General [[Akkensor]] the task of taking the objective at Long Halent.

Warmaster succession

Dravere pressed Slaydo until the Warmaster's death in 765.M41. He then fought against other claimants, among them Cybon, for the post. Macaroth was named as Slaydo's successor. Dravere is said to have joined a conspiracy against Macaroth soon afterwards. No evidence survives, and any such record would most likely have been suppressed by the [[Commissariat]].The conspiracy against Macaroth is recorded only as a rumour. Imperial accounts differ on whether it existed.

Passed over a second time, Dravere died in the Imperial assault on Menazoid Epsilon in late 765.M41, less than eight months after Slaydo's death.
