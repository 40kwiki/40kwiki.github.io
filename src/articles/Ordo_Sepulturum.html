The Ordo Sepulturum, also written Ordo Sepultura, is one of the smallest and newest of the [[Ordos Minoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. It was formed in 999.M41, during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], to study and fight the undead plague-creatures that had begun to multiply at the edge of the [[Eye of Terror]]: plague zombies, [[Poxwalkers]], and the [[Nurgle]]-spawned [[Zombie Plague]] and Walking Pox that make them.

Its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] are particularly devoted to the opposition of the [[Death Guard]], the [[Traitor Legions|Traitor Legion]] that has loosed so many diseases on the Imperium since it grew more active after the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]] and the opening of the [[Great Rift]].

History

Outbreaks of the [[Zombie Plague]] have been recorded for many millennia. They became a threat of the first order only after the [[Plague Wars]] and the fleets of [[Typhus]], the Herald of Nurgle. Before [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] launched the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], the number of outbreaks near the [[Cadian System]] rose sharply. Several [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], most of them [[Thorians]], noticed it and were soon in contact. Within a few standard years the Ordo had formed, and among its founders was the Inquisitor Aghastri.

For the Thorians the plague had always been a subject of study, because it is both a Warp-born corruption and a physical sickness. Specimens were hard to get, since outbreaks are scattered and sudden. The [[Black Crusade]], the Rift and the Plague Wars gave the Ordo more subjects than it could examine.

Interests

The Zombie Plague

Main article: [[Zombie Plague]]

The plague wears down the body of whoever it infects, by many routes, including a psychic one, but it keeps a portion of the victim's life-essence after death. [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] have noted a likeness between this and the working of the [[Golden Throne]]. Some Thorians believe that the plague in its unaltered form might teach something about the Emperor's own life-in-death. Every form of the "faith virus" studied so far has mutated, and the hope remains only a hope.

The Obliterator virus

Much rarer is the Obliterator tech-virus, a [[Chaos]] contagion that joins physical corruption to mental disruption. It lets a human merge with machinery on the physical, psychic and spiritual planes. The Ordo studies it as well.

Notable Inquisitors

  • Inquisitor Aghastri, a Thorian who studied the [[Zombie Plague]] and helped to found the Ordo.
  • Inquisitor Bastian Caius, active in the [[Cadian Sector]] in the [[41st millennium]]. In 999.M41 he took part in the Reclamation of Kathur, hoping to gather data on the outbreaks. He was killed there by a [[Daemon Prince]] of [[Nurgle]], the being that had caused the outbreak two solar months earlier.

Relations

The Ordo's work touches the [[Officio Medicae]] and the Departmento Contagio, whose duty is to contain epidemics, but the Ordo is concerned with the cause, and the others with the cure. Its closest Ordo is the [[Ordo Malleus]], since every plague of this kind is the gift of a [[Daemon|daemon]], but this is not recorded.
