The 30th millennium (M30) is the thousand-year span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M30 to 999.M30. It saw the end of the [[Age of Strife]] on [[Terra]], the [[Unification Wars]] in which the [[Emperor of Mankind]] conquered the techno-barbarian nations of the Throneworld, the alliance with the [[Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]] sealed by the [[Treaty of Olympus]], the creation of the [[Primarch|primarchs]] and the [[Space Marine Legions]], and the first two centuries of the [[Great Crusade]].

Imperial records of the early millennium are thin. Most of what survives was written down after the event by the [[Administratum]] and by the [[Remembrancer Order]], and dates before about 700.M30 are usually given only to the century. From the launch of the Great Crusade in about 798.M30 the record improves, for the Expeditionary Fleets kept their own logs and counted years from the Crusade's start. The millennium closed with the [[Ullanor Crusade]] against the greatest [[Ork]] empire of the age.

Chronology

Dates follow the reconciled chronology of the Administratum. Where the Adepta disagree, the variant is given in the notes.

Early M30The [[Age of Strife]] reaches its depth on [[Terra]]; the planet is divided among techno-barbarian warlords such as the [[Ethnarch]] and the [[Panpacific Tribes]].Warp storms isolate Terra from most human worlds.
Late M29 – c. 712.M30The [[Unification Wars]]. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] wins Terra nation by nation.The start is dated only to the century.
c. M30The [[Thunder Warriors]] are created as the Emperor's shock troops.Their gene-craft foreshadows the [[Adeptus Astartes]].
c. 650.M30The Emperor's companion [[Malcador the Sigillite]] begins the administration that will become the [[Adeptus Terra]].Date estimated from the sequence of events.The [[Council of Terra]] grows from his staff.
669.M30The battle of [[Mount Ararat]], recorded in some accounts as the last great battle of Unification.Other records place the end of the wars in 712.M30.
c. 700.M30The First Founding of the twenty [[Space Marine Legions]] from the gene-stock of the lost primarchs.Date from the [[Dark Angels]] and [[Salamanders]] rolls.
c. 712.M30Terra is united under the Emperor; the [[Thunder Warriors]] are destroyed in the last battles.See [[Unification Wars]].
c. 730.M30The [[Pacification of Luna]]: the gene-covens of [[Luna]] are subdued and their sciences put to the Legions' use.Before the treaty with Mars.
739.M30The [[Treaty of Olympus]] binds Terra and the [[Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]]; the Emperor is recognised as the [[Omnissiah]] by the Martian priesthood.Also called the Treaty of Mars.
Late M30The [[Astronomican]] is lit, giving the [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] a beacon across the galaxy.See [[Astronomican]].
792.M30The twenty [[Primarch|primarchs]] are created in the gene-vaults beneath the [[Imperial Palace]] and scattered across the galaxy by the powers of [[Chaos]].The scattering is recorded as the same year.
c. 798.M30The [[Great Crusade]] begins; the [[Imperial Truth]] becomes the doctrine of the Imperium and the [[Imperial Army]] is raised.Crusade years are counted from this date.
c. 798.M30The [[Remembrancer Order]] is established.Its numbers grow sharply after Ullanor.
c. 800.M30[[Horus]] is found on [[Cthonia]], the first of the primarchs to be recovered.Given command of the [[Luna Wolves]].
819.M30[[Leman Russ]] found on [[Fenris]].Second primarch found.
830.M30[[Fulgrim]] found on [[Chemos]].
832.M30[[Vulkan]] found on [[Nocturne]].
835.M30[[Rogal Dorn]] found; his fleet comes to Terra from [[Inwit]].
837.M30[[Roboute Guilliman]] found on [[Macragge]].
839–890.M30The [[Rangdan Xenocides]], three campaigns on the galactic rim, the costliest wars of the Crusade before the Heresy.The [[Dark Angels]] bear the heaviest losses.
840.M30[[Magnus the Red]] found on [[Prospero]].
843.M30[[Sanguinius]] found on [[Baal]] Secundus.
846.M30[[Lion El'Jonson]] found on [[Caliban]].
849.M30[[Perturabo]] found on [[Olympia]].
