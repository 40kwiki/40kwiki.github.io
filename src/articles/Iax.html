Iax is a planet of the [[Ultramar|Realm of Ultramar]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Once known as the "Garden of Ultramar", it was among the most productive agricultural worlds of the Imperium. Its farms and cultivated woodlands covered most of its surface. The world is now classed as a [[Death World]], and its climate and wildlife have changed greatly since its fertile period.

Iax had no large cities. Its population lived in many small towns, and its produce fed the forge worlds and hive worlds of the realm. The [[Ultramarines]] have guarded it since the [[Great Crusade]], and the Chapter keeps a fortress on the planet.

Plague Wars

Main article: [[Plague Wars]]

During the [[Plague Wars]] in the early 42nd millennium, the servants of [[Nurgle]] invaded [[Ultramar]], led by [[Mortarion]] and the [[Death Guard]]. The war ended on Iax, which the enemy had taken and turned into a stronghold. [[Roboute Guilliman]] met Mortarion in person there. Guilliman was beaten and infected with the daemon's Godblight in the struggle, and the Imperium records the event as a turning point in the war.

Imperial records give different dates for the end of the Plague Wars. The date of the battle on Iax is estimated from the sequence of events.Estimated; no record gives an exact year for the battle on Iax.

Present day

The planet's fields have been abandoned and its farms have reverted to wild growth. Imperial survey fleets consider it unsafe to settle without a full Ultramarines garrison. Its name is still used in the realm as a symbol of the loss of Ultramar's garden, and the Ultramarines' records describe the world as a warning.
