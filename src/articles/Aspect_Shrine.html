An Aspect Shrine is a cult of [[Aeldari]] [[Aspect Warriors]] devoted to one specialty of the [[Asuryani Path|Path of the Warrior]]. Each [[Craftworld Aeldari|craftworld]] keeps at least one shrine. Each shrine is dedicated to one aspect of the war god [[Khaine]], and each brings its own fighting techniques, weapons and abilities.

Aeldari of either sex may walk the Path of the Warrior. In the distant past the [[Phoenix Lord]]s taught the arts of war to both men and women, so Aspect Warriors are as likely to be one sex as the other.

Organisation and war

When the Aeldari go to war, the Aspect Warriors of each shrine fight in a set role that matches their aspect. Each shrine has its own warrior garb, a ritual battle suit, and distinctive weapons. Their minds and bodies are trained by endless exercise until they embody the aspect of Khaine that their shrine represents. In battle, each squad plays its part with the precision of a virtuoso, and the shrines go to war as three-pronged Aspect Hosts.

Imperial scholars record that the Aeldari fight in a sense of constant peril. Their civilisation is shattered and their generations are few. Each life lost in battle is, in the eyes of the Aeldari, a sacrifice made only because there was no other choice. Young Aeldari often believe they can rebuild their lost empire with passion. More and more of them take the Warrior Path with each passing Terran year.

Exarchs

When the Aspect Warriors put aside their ritual masks and wargear, they live at peace within their craftworld. Only the keepers of each shrine, the [[Exarch]]s, remain in the shrine at all times. Exarchs are the war aspects of Khaine embodied. They are the high priests of the Bloody-Handed God and teachers of his creed.

Only Aspect Warriors who become completely lost upon the Path of the Warrior can become Exarchs. The transformation is rare and cannot be reversed. It follows repeated battle-frenzy so strong that it leaves a permanent mark on the psyche. In theory, an Aspect Warrior learns to set aside his blood-hungry persona, a practice called "putting on the war mask". An Aspect Warrior who loses this ability becomes an Exarch, feared and honoured by those he once called kin.

An Exarch wears an elaborate and often ancient version of the Aspect armour, and carries arcane wargear. His suit holds [[Spirit Stone]]s that contain the spirits of the suit's previous Exarchs. The Exarch takes the sacred name of the suit, and his spirit joins with those of its earlier wearers. This pooled psychic energy gives the suit and the warrior their special powers.

Phoenix Lords

The founders of the shrines, the [[Phoenix Lord]]s, have a stranger form of immortality. When they die, their suits lie empty until worthy Exarchs find them. An Exarch who dons such a suit becomes part of its gestalt soul, and the Phoenix Lord rises again to lead the Aeldari in war. [[Arhra]], the Fallen Phoenix, is the only Phoenix Lord lost entirely to Chaos.
