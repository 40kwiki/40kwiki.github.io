Khur, originally designated Forty-Seven Ten by the Imperium of Man, was a Civilised World. It was brought into Imperial Compliance during the [[Great Crusade]] in 903.M30 by the [[Word Bearers]], the [[Space Marine Legions|Legion]] of [[Lorgar]]. Its destruction by the [[Ultramarines]] is one of the causes of the [[Battle of Calth]] and of the Word Bearers' turn to Chaos during the [[Horus Heresy]].

The world was the tenth brought into Compliance by the [[47th Expeditionary Fleet]]. The Word Bearers did more than bring Imperial civilisation. Their zealous faith in the divinity of the [[Emperor of Mankind]], whom they called the God-Emperor, took root among the Khurian people. The people embraced the Word Bearers as their saviours.

Monarchia

At the direction of the Word Bearers, the Khurians built their new capital, Monarchia. The city was meant to show their faith and devotion to the God-Emperor. Imperial records call it the "Perfect City" because of its model devotion. The people of Monarchia spoke a dialect known as Uhturlan.

The Word Bearers' Chaplains praised the divinity of the Emperor, and Lorgar preached often. Millions were converted to the worship of the Emperor by his words alone. The Compliant worlds paid their tithes in the Emperor's name, and the orders of Terra were obeyed without question.

Judgement of Khur

About six solar decades after its Compliance, the Emperor ordered the [[Ultramarines]] to destroy Monarchia. The order was given personally, as an example to Lorgar and his Legion that the secular Imperial Truth would not tolerate religious error or idolatry. Imperial accounts date the act to about forty standard years before the start of the Horus Heresy, which places it at c. 963.M30 (estimated).

The action did not have the effect the Emperor intended. It broke the faith of Lorgar and the Word Bearers in the Imperial project. Lorgar and his Legion began to look for new gods, and they turned to the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]].
