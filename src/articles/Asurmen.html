Asurmen, whose birth name was Iliathin and who is known as the Hand of Asuryan, is the first and oldest of the [[Phoenix Lord]]s of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. He is the founder of the [[Aspect Shrine]] that became the [[Dire Avengers]], the most common of the [[Aspect Warriors]]. Before the Fall he was regarded as the penultimate warrior of the Aeldari, placed at the pinnacle of might.

Asurmen's exploits are recorded in the Aeldari mythic cycle. Imperial accounts treat them as legend, but they agree on his main deeds: he survived the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], he killed his brother when Tethesis was possessed by a [[Daemon]], and he founded the shrine that carries his title.

Early life and the Fall

Asurmen was born Iliathin on [[Eidafaeron]], a world of the [[Aeldari Empire]]. Accounts describe him as a sceptical citizen of his world. He doubted the prophecies of doom spread by the [[Exodite]]s, who had withdrawn from the empire. When the [[Fall of the Aeldari]] began with the birth of [[Slaanesh]], Iliathin and his brother Tethesis survived the destruction of their world. Tethesis was possessed by a Daemon, and Iliathin was forced to kill him. Afterwards Iliathin lived alone at an old temple. The [[Daemon]]s would not approach it, because the presence of the dying Aeldari gods could still be felt there.

In the temple, Iliathin is said to have considered suicide before he chose to take up the Path of the Warrior. He took the name Asurmen, meaning the Hand of Asuryan, and began to gather survivors. From this beginning he founded the Shrine of Asur, which is the forerunner of the Dire Avengers Aspect Shrine.

The Sword of Asur and the Phoenix armour

Asurmen carries the first known Aeldari diresword, the [[Sword of Asur]]. Its [[Spirit Stone]] holds the soul of his brother Tethesis, so that the brother may continue to fight the enemies of the Aeldari. He fights with it one-handed or two-handed and uses a master-crafted [[Shuriken pistol]] as a secondary weapon. Imperial scholars regard him as a formidable close-combat fighter, even though his Aspect is known for short-range volleys of fire.

As a Phoenix Lord, Asurmen's soul is held in a Spirit Stone within [[Phoenix armour]]. When he falls, his Exarch successor takes up the armour and the memories and abilities of all who have worn it. Imperial scholars dispute this account, but the Asuryani accept it.

Legacy

The Dire Avengers are the most common Aspect of the craftworlds, and their shrines are the largest in the Asuryani. Imperial records credit this to Asurmen's founding. His rune represents the balance between the fury of emotion and the cold surety of skill, and it is synonymous with the Path of the Warrior.
