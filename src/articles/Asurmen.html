Asurmen, whose birth name was Iliathin and who is known as the Hand of Asuryan, is the first and oldest of the [[Phoenix Lord]]s of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. He is the founder of the [[Aspect Shrine]] that became the [[Dire Avengers]], the most common of the [[Aspect Warriors]]. In the Aeldari mythic cycles he is counted the penultimate warrior of all the Aeldari, placed at the pinnacle of might.

Asurmen's exploits are recorded in the Aeldari mythic cycle. Imperial accounts treat them as legend, but they agree on his main deeds: he survived the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], he killed his brother when Tethesis was possessed by a [[Daemon]], and he founded the shrine that carries his title.

Early life and the Fall

Asurmen was born Iliathin on [[Eidafaeron]], a world of the [[Aeldari Empire]]. Accounts describe him as a sceptical citizen of his world. He doubted the prophecies of doom spread by the [[Exodite]]s, who had withdrawn from the empire. When the [[Fall of the Aeldari]] began with the birth of [[Slaanesh]], Iliathin and his brother Tethesis survived the destruction of their world. Tethesis was possessed by a Daemon, and Iliathin was forced to kill him. Afterwards Iliathin lived alone at an old temple. The [[Daemon]]s would not approach it, because the presence of the dying Aeldari gods could still be felt there.

In the temple, Iliathin is said to have lived alone for several years and to have considered suicide. His life took a new purpose when he met Faraethil, a feral Aeldari girl. When Faraethil was attacked by the hedonistic survivors who became the [[Drukhari]], he took the name Asurmen, after the god [[Asuryan]], and saved her. She became his disciple, and the two left the ruins of Eidafaeron and gathered other survivors. Asurmen and his followers, among them [[Jain Zar]], [[Baharroth]], [[Fuegan]] and [[Arhra]], settled on a barren moon in the [[Webway]] that they named [[Asur]]. There he created the [[Path of the Warrior]] and the Shrine of Asur, which is the forerunner of the Dire Avengers Aspect Shrine. He later travelled among the craftworlds and taught the Dire Avengers' skills, and the first Exarchs he trained became the Phoenix Lords of the other aspects.

The Sword of Asur and the Phoenix armour

Asurmen carries the first known Aeldari diresword, the [[Sword of Asur]]. Its [[Spirit Stone]] holds the soul of his brother Tethesis, so that the brother may continue to fight the enemies of the Aeldari. He fights with it one-handed or two-handed and uses a master-crafted [[Shuriken pistol]] as a secondary weapon. Imperial scholars regard him as a formidable close-combat fighter, even though his Aspect is known for short-range volleys of fire.

The helmet of Asurmen's [[Phoenix armour]] holds the Spirit Stones of every Dire Avengers Exarch who has become Phoenix Lord since Asurmen left the first shrine on Asur. Asur was later destroyed by the consequences of the Fall, and Asurmen's first incarnation died defending it against the daemons of [[Slaanesh]]. The Asuryani hold that another hero always takes up the suit and becomes Phoenix Lord once more.

Legacy

In the 41st millennium Asurmen fought beside [[Jain Zar]], who is pledged to [[Ynnead]], and took part in the defence of the [[Ynnari]] against the [[Thousand Sons]] and Tzeentchian daemons in the Webway. He vanished from sight during that battle, and his whereabouts are unknown.

The Dire Avengers are the most common Aspect of the craftworlds, and their shrines are the largest in the Asuryani. Imperial records credit this to Asurmen's founding. His rune represents the balance between the fury of emotion and the cold surety of skill, and it is synonymous with the Path of the Warrior.
