The Koronus Passage is the route that leads from the [[Halo Stars]], on the rimward frontier of the [[Calixis Sector]], into the [[Koronus Expanse]]. It is the only recorded way into the Expanse, and Imperial navigators treat it as a hazard in its own right.

Calixian records describe the Expanse beyond the Passage as a region that must "ultimately fend for itself", since the great fleets of Terra rarely reach it. The Passage is the limit of the Imperium's regular presence in the region.

Navigation

The Passage is not charted in full. Navigators of the [[Navis Nobilite]] and the Calixian [[Rogue Trader]] dynasties have sailed it, and their accounts differ on its course. The route is held by the Calixian authorities to be safe only at certain seasons of the Warp.

Calixian claims

The Calixis Sector makes its claims in the Expanse through the Passage, and the Rogue Trader dynasties hold most of the charters for ventures beyond it. The Passage is therefore as much a matter of trade and privilege as of navigation.
