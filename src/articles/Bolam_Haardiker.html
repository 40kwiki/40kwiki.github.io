Bolam Haardiker was the [[Paternoval Envoy]] of the [[Navis Nobilite]], the officer who speaks for the Navigator Houses to the rulers of [[Terra]]. He sat on the [[Council of Terra]] during the [[Horus Heresy]], and on the [[Grand Council of Reconstruction]] after the [[Siege of Terra]].

The Envoy's role

The [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] Houses are governed by the Paternova, who balances the Houses against one another and defends them from the [[Administratum]], the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Inquisition]]. The Envoy carries the Paternova's voice into the Imperium's highest council. Navigators guide every voidship through the [[Warp]], so the Envoy's seat gave the Navis Nobilite a say in matters of war and trade alike.

Record

Haardiker is listed among the councillors of the Heresy and among the members of the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31.Estimated; no record gives an exact year. Nothing is preserved of his House, his opinions or his end. In the [[32nd millennium]] the [[Paternoval Envoy]]'s seat was held by a member of House Gibran during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32).
