Cadia, officially Cadia Prime, was a [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] and for ten thousand years the most important military bastion of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It lay at the heart of the Cadian Gate, the only stable route out of the [[Eye of Terror]], and was garrisoned to stop the [[Traitor|traitor]] forces within the Eye from reaching the rest of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. Its people were raised as soldiers from childhood, and the [[Cadian Shock Troops]] were the most famous regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]].

Cadia withstood twelve [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]] led by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. In 999.M41, during the Thirteenth, Abaddon drove the [[Blackstone Fortress]] Will of Eternity into the planet, which broke apart. About three million survivors were evacuated under the [[Lord Castellan of Cadia|Lord Castellan]] [[Ursarkar E. Creed]]. The destruction of Cadia was followed by the opening of the [[Great Rift]], and the phrase "Cadia stands" has become a byword for defiance across the Imperium.

History

Before the Imperium

The earliest Imperial record of Cadia dates to the last years of the [[Great Crusade]], when the 1301st Expeditionary Fleet under the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Lorgar]] reached the region of the Eye of Terror. The planet was then inhabited by a primitive people with violet eyes, who worshipped the [[Chaos]] gods and spoke a tongue related to that of Lorgar's homeworld, [[Colchis]]. A priestess of these people, Ingethel, led the primarch's ship into the Eye, an episode that sealed records treat as one of the first steps of the [[Word Bearers]] towards treason. Lorgar afterwards ordered the planet bombarded, and its native population was destroyed.These events are recorded only in sealed Inquisitorial and Custodian sources and were unknown to the planet's later inhabitants.

Foundation as a fortress world

After the [[Horus Heresy]] and the [[Great Scouring]], the defeated traitor Legions fled into the Eye of Terror. The stable passage through the [[Warp|warp]] storms around the Eye became known as the Cadian Gate, and it was the route by which the Traitors came out again. Cadia stood upon it. The planet's surface came to be covered with fortress-cities, the kasrs, and the [[Cadian Sector]] became a heavily militarised region around the Eye.

The First Black Crusade of 781.M31 convinced the Imperium that the world was vital to its defence, and in M32 Imperial colonists were sent to resettle it. The first kasrs were built in the broad High Terran style and proved impossible to hold, so after the Second Black Crusade in 597.M32 the cities were rebuilt as the fortresses of later centuries.

The Black Crusades

Main article: [[Black Crusade]]

For ten thousand years Cadia was the main target of the forces of Chaos. Abaddon the Despoiler led twelve Black Crusades from the Eye of Terror before 999.M41, and each was met at the Cadian Gate. In some the defenders turned the enemy back in space; in others the traitors landed on Cadia and were driven off only after years of fighting. In the Twelfth, the [[Gothic War]], Abaddon bypassed the Gate and struck at the Gothic Sector instead. Cadia was also attacked many times by lesser warbands, by Ork raiders and by the Aeldari.

Over these millennia Cadia raised and sent away more regiments than any other world of the Imperium. Its people came to regard their service as a calling and their planet's survival as proof of the Emperor's favour.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In 999.M41 Abaddon launched the Thirteenth Black Crusade. The forces of Chaos landed on Cadia in strength and occupied large parts of it. Ursarkar E. Creed, a colonel of the 8th Cadian Regiment raised to Lord Castellan after the High Command was assassinated, held the planet's defence together, and the living saint [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]] arrived in time to save Kasr Kraf. At the same time the Archmagos Dominus [[Belisarius Cawl]], aided by the [[Necrons|Necron]] collector [[Trazyn the Infinite]], learned how to use the Cadian Pylons to drive back the Eye.

Abaddon responded by bringing down the Blackstone Fortress Will of Eternity on Cadia. The impact and the destruction of the pylons broke the planet apart. A handful of Imperial survivors escaped through a webway gate on the ice moon of Klaisus, with the aid of the [[Ynnari]]. Creed arranged the evacuation of about three million people and then vanished. The full course of the battle is described in the article [[Fall of Cadia]].

After the fall

The destruction of Cadia was followed within days by the opening of the [[Great Rift]], which divided the Imperium into the Imperium Sanctus and the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. Abaddon's warfleets passed through the ruined Gate. The surviving Cadians joined the forces that gathered for the [[Indomitus Crusade]] or were scattered among the regiments of the Astra Militarum. Many Cadian regiments that had been serving elsewhere when the planet died continued to fight under the old name.

In the Era Indomitus, Imperial reports state that the remnants of the Cadian System have been settled by the forces of Chaos and have become the stronghold of a renegade realm close to the Eye of Terror, which has expanded since 999.M41. Creed disappeared during the evacuation and his fate is unrecorded.

Cadian refugees have been settled on many worlds. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] continues to raise [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian regiments]] from their descendants and from those who have adopted the Cadian doctrine, and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] has made a cult of the world's memory. The phrase "Cadia stands", once the planet's motto, is now used as a battle cry, an oath and a prayer.

Geography

Cadia was a temperate, Terran-like world with a breathable atmosphere, wide grassy uplands, cold seas and stormy skies coloured violet by the nearby [[Eye of Terror]]. Its surface was dominated by the kasrs, great fortress-cities built for siege warfare, linked by military roads and surrounded by bunker lines, minefields and trench works. Notable kasrs included [[Thirteenth Black Crusade|Kasr Kraf]] and Kasr Derth, the largest in 241.M41.

The planet was dotted with thousands of black obelisks, the Cadian Pylons, which predated human settlement. Their builders and purpose were long unknown to the Imperium, though they were seen to resist the touch of the warp and were protected by [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] edict. [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]]'s researches in 999.M41 showed that they were made of [[Noctilith|noctilith]], that they were raised by the [[Necrons]], and that they held the Eye of Terror in check, which is why the warp storms around the Gate were calmer than elsewhere. Their destruction is believed to have contributed to the expansion of the Eye.

The Cadian System included the ice moon of Klaisus. The neighbouring systems of Belis Corona and [[Agripinaa]] formed the rest of the Cadian Gate's defences and held out after Cadia fell.

Society

Cadian society was organised for war. Every child was taught to strip and assemble a [[Lasgun|lasgun]], and every adult was a soldier or a trained reservist. Many young Cadians served as Whiteshields, conscripts recruited at fourteen, before taking a place in a Shock Troop regiment. Military drill, discipline and the observance of rank governed public life, and the planet's few civilian occupations were all directed towards the support of the garrison. Cadians were known for their violet eyes, a sign of their world's nearness to the Eye of Terror.

Supreme authority on the planet lay with the Lord Castellan, the commander of the Cadian forces, who was answerable to the Imperium alone. The Cadian regiments were raised in great numbers and sent across the galaxy, and the Cadian Shock Troops became the model for many other regiments. The elite stormtroopers of Cadia were called the [[Kasrkin]]. Cadian doctrine taught that every soldier was expendable but that no position was to be given up without orders, and Cadian officers were valued throughout the [[Astra Militarum]].

The phrase "Cadia stands" was used as a greeting, an oath and a toast. Since the fall, Cadian survivors and their descendants have preserved the old customs, including the presentation of a lasgun to every child, on the worlds where they have been settled.

Legacy

Cadia's fall is regarded by the Imperium as the greatest military defeat since the [[Horus Heresy]], and the beginning of the age of darkness that followed. To the soldiers of the [[Astra Militarum]] it is also the supreme example of endurance, since the planet did not surrender and was not taken in battle. Shrines to the fallen of Cadia have been raised on many worlds, and the regiments that still bear the Cadian name fight in every theatre of the [[Era Indomitus]].
