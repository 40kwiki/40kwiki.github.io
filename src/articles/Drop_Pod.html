A Drop Pod is an atmospheric assault transport used by the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Chaos Space Marines]] for orbital insertion and planetary assault. It is one of the main ways that a Space Marine Chapter puts its forces onto a planet's surface quickly, alongside [[Thunderhawk]]s, [[Stormeagle|Storm Eagles]], teleportation and [[Caestus assault ram]]s. The pod is used where achieving tactical surprise is important.

The pod is based on the standard Imperial [[Life Pod]], which is used to evacuate the crews of badly damaged starships. A Drop Pod is an environmentally sealed armoured capsule that works only once. It carries its occupants into the heart of a battle or deep behind enemy lines, and then it is left behind.

Descent

A Drop Pod is fired with great acceleration from an orbiting strike cruiser or [[Battle Barge]]. It then falls through the atmosphere at speeds recorded as high as 12,000 kilometres per hour. Its oversized rocket thrusters increase its speed beyond terminal velocity, so that even advanced air defence systems find it difficult to lock on. The pod fires its retro-thrusters only at the last possible moment, and it strikes the ground with enough force to crush most structures.

The descent is dangerous. A pod has few defences against anti-aircraft fire or debris, and its passengers must trust the guidance systems of the pod's [[Machine Spirit]]. Space Marines often recite prayers to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and hymns of vengeance during the descent, to steel themselves for the fight ahead.

Tactical use

Many Chapters use Drop Pods for quick insertions and deep strikes. The Scouring of [[Tsagualsa]] by the [[Ultramarines]] and their Successor Chapters lit the sky with thousands of pod trails, and the Badab War saw Drop Pods used by both sides to land on many planets. The [[Invaders]] and the [[Black Consuls]] are recorded as using Drop Pod assaults to land most of their forces at once. A pod that lands in the middle of an enemy formation can be devastating, because its occupants disembark at once and attack before the enemy can react.

The [[Deathstorm]] pod carries assault cannons, and the dreadnought pod carries a [[Dreadnought]] directly into battle. During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Legions used Drop Pods of their own pattern. At the [[Istvaan III]] Atrocity, the traitor elements of the [[World Eaters]], [[Sons of Horus]], [[Death Guard]] and [[Emperor's Children]] landed forces with Drop Pods to exterminate their Loyalist brethren.
