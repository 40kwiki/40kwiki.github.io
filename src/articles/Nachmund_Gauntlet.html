The Nachmund Gauntlet is a corridor of stable space through the [[Great Rift]], linking the [[Hive world|hive world]] of [[Vigilus]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] with the war world of Sangua Terra in the [[Imperium Sanctus]]. It is the best known, and for long years after the Rift opened the only confirmed, route by which Imperial fleets can cross from one half of the Imperium to the other without risking the full fury of the storms. Other crossings have been reported but not confirmed.

The Gauntlet exists because both worlds contain great spears of blackstone, or noctilith, a warp-resonant mineral that some ancient [[Xenos|xenos]] power polarised to repel the [[Warp]]. The anti-psychic field that hums between the two sets of spears holds the storms back from the lane. For the [[Astronomican]]'s light to reach the far side by even this uncertain road, and for refugees, reinforcements and supplies to pass the other way, the spears must hold.

Its importance made both ends the targets of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and the [[Black Legion]] in the early [[Era Indomitus]]. [[Roboute Guilliman]], as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], set a Chapter of the [[Ultima Founding]], the [[Castellans of the Rift]], to guard the lane against renegade [[Imperial Knight|Knights]] and daemon engines, while the [[War of Nightmares]] and the later [[Nachmund Rift War]] were fought for the worlds at its ends. The ruined hulks of countless ships that failed to run it hang along its length.

Geography

The Gauntlet lies in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], in a region close to the [[Eye of Terror]] that [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] had avoided since the [[Great Crusade]] because its storms and anomalies made it too dangerous to cross. When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41 it engulfed the region, but as the swirling nebulae dissipated a narrow lane of calm was found between Vigilus on its northern side and Sangua Terra on its southern. The Rift itself is a serpentine mass, and the channel of the Gauntlet runs across it. Imperial astropaths on Vigilus noted that the shape of the Rift, with the Gauntlet cut through it, began to appear in the daubings of madmen and the idle scrawlings of those who believed they were thinking of other things.

Nearby worlds include [[Cadia]], destroyed in 999.M41, the [[Shrine world|shrine world]] [[Chinchare]], the [[Mordian]] system and the [[Segmentum Fortress|segmentum fortress]] of [[Cypra Mundi]]. The Nachmund system lies on the route, and its principal world, the Knight world of Dharrovar, is the only inhabited place the lane passes close to. Vigilus is the terminus on the Nihilus side. It is a desert hive world of the Vigilus system, famed before the Rift as a linchpin of the Segmentum Obscurus and a stronghold of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], whose bore-hives on its forge continent of Megaborealis mined the very blackstone that now protects it.

The corridor is not wholly safe. Its walls press in and fall back, warp predators and [[Daemon|daemons]] hunt along it, and the voids at its edges are filled with wrecks. Convoys that run it are escorted by Navy squadrons where possible, and the Imperium treats every ship that passes as a victory in itself.

Cause

[[Noctilith]], called blackstone, is a rare mineral that resonates with the Warp and can be charged either to attract or to repel its energies. It is the same material as the [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] and the pylons of [[Cadia]]. The spear-like deposits in the crust of Vigilus had been polarised to repel warp energy by some technology of great age, probably xenos. Sangua Terra was found to contain identical spears, held in a strange black suspension so that they always point towards Vigilus. The anti-empyric field that thrums between the two worlds keeps the channel open and holds the Rift apart.The Adeptus Mechanicus has not been able to determine who placed or polarised the spears. The forge world of [[Stygies VIII]], which held mining rights on Vigilus under a pact with the planet's governors, has been accused of concealing what it knows.

If either set of spears were destroyed or depolarised, the storms around the Gauntlet would close in and the corridor would be lost, leaving the Imperium Nihilus to the mercy of the forces of [[Chaos]]. This is why Abaddon and his Black Legion were so determined to seize or ruin both worlds in the first years of the Era Indomitus.

History

Opening and Dharrovar

At the opening of the Gauntlet, ships seeking passage had to pass close to the Nachmund system. Its principal planet, Dharrovar, was ruled by the Tyrant King Kaligius over the renegade Knights of House Mandrakor, who had not been seen by the Imperium since the [[Horus Heresy]]. Kaligius declared that [[Old Night]] had returned, refused all contact and made pacts with renegades and pirate fleets. His defenders said he had good cause for his fear, for on either side of the lane a seething tide of Chaos pressed constantly to break its bonds and spill into realspace.

Ships that gave Dharrovar a wide berth were allowed to slink past without challenge, while those that sought haven or approached under a flag of parley met hostility, suspicion or violence. Some Imperial vessels ran the Gauntlet and survived. Many more were destroyed, and their hulks became a vast graveyard of voidcraft. A staunch independent already convinced that nothing good could come of being drawn back into the fold, Kaligius turned to tyranny and oppression, and as the term Imperium Nihilus was first coined he was declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]], around 000.M42.

Imperial reinforcement

Guilliman recognised the Gauntlet's value at once. He sent the Castellans of the Rift, an all-[[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] Chapter of the Ultima Founding raised from [[Ultramarines]] lineage in about 999.M41, to drive back the daemon engines and renegade Knights that infested the route. Their duty, given by the Lord Commander himself, is to maintain and defend the channel, and they have made repeated raids on the fallen Knight world of Dharrovar. When war later came to Vigilus the greater part of the Chapter remained engaged against the heretics of Dharrovar, though two companies fought in the defence of Sangua Terra and two more travelled to Vigilus.

Vigilus became the staging post for every Imperial force sent into the Imperium Nihilus by this road, and the refuge of the billions of refugees who fled south through the Gauntlet from the Dark Imperium. Its governors imposed curfews and forbade the watching of the Rift in the night sky, and the repression sowed unrest that later broke into open revolt.

War of Nightmares

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

In the first decades of the 42nd millennium Vigilus was attacked in turn by an Ork Speedwaaagh! out of the Rift, a rising of the [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]] in its hives, [[Aeldari]] and [[Drukhari]] raiders and finally the Black Legion under the Chaos Lord Haarken Worldclaimer and Abaddon himself. [[Marneus Calgar]] of the Ultramarines commanded its defence and fought Abaddon in single combat. In 025.M42 the Despoiler and his Legion were driven from the surface and the world was saved, and the Gauntlet remained open.

Nachmund Rift War

Abaddon did not abandon his aim. Almost at once word came of an attack on Sangua Terra, where a vast voidship matching the descriptions of the [[Planet Killer]] was reported in low orbit. In the years after the War of Nightmares the Black Legion and its allies turned on the worlds at the southern end of the Gauntlet and the systems along it, in a wider struggle recorded as the [[Nachmund Rift War]]. The Imperium held both ends, at great cost, and the passage remains open, although contested along its whole length.Dates for the later war are uncertain. The Administratum places it within the first three decades of the 42nd millennium, and the fall of Abaddon's assault on Vigilus in 025.M42 is the last firm date.

Importance to the Imperium

Control of Vigilus allows the Imperium to keep the beleaguered worlds of the Imperium Nihilus reinforced and resupplied, and to maintain a line of astropathic communication with them, unstable and dangerous though it is. Imperial strategists hold that a truly divided Imperium would fall apart entirely, and that the Gauntlet is what keeps the northern half under a semblance of the rule of [[Terra]]. Tigurius, Chief Librarian of the Ultramarines, said that if Vigilus fell and the Gauntlet collapsed the Imperium Nihilus would be all but lost, and Terra's empire would soon follow.

The cost of the route is counted in hulls. Because no one can say what polarised the spears or how long the field will last, the Adeptus Mechanicus watches the blackstone deposits closely, and the Inquisition has taken an interest in anyone who seeks to mine or move them.

Dharrovar

Dharrovar is the only world the lane passes near. The renegade Knights of House Mandrakor, who had not been seen since the Horus Heresy, took the opening of the Rift as the return of Old Night, and their tyrant king saw in every ship that approached a servant of the Imperium that had abandoned them. The Castellans of the Rift have made repeated raids on the fallen Knight world, and the daemon engines and renegade Knights that trouble the lane are drawn from its defenders.
