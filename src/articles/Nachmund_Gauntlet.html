The Nachmund Gauntlet is a corridor of stable space through the [[Great Rift]], linking the [[Hive world|hive world]] of [[Vigilus]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] with the world of Sangua Terra in the [[Imperium Sanctus]]. It is the best known, and for long years after the Rift opened the only confirmed, route by which Imperial fleets can cross from one half of the Imperium to the other without risking the full fury of the storms. Other crossings have been reported but not confirmed.

The Gauntlet exists because both worlds contain great spears of blackstone, or noctilith, a warp-resonant mineral, polarised by some ancient [[Xenos|xenos]] power to repel the [[Warp]]. The field between them holds the storms back. Its importance made both ends the targets of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and the [[Black Legion]] in the early [[Era Indomitus]], in the [[War of Nightmares]] for Vigilus and the later [[Nachmund Rift War]]. The ruined hulks of thousands of ships that failed to run it hang along its length.

Geography

The Gauntlet lies in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], in a region close to the [[Eye of Terror]] that [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] had avoided since the [[Great Crusade]] because of its storms and anomalies. When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, it engulfed the region, but as the storms settled a narrow lane of calm was found between [[War of Nightmares|Vigilus]] on its northern side and Sangua Terra on its southern. Nearby worlds include [[Cadia]], destroyed in 999.M41, the [[Shrine world|shrine world]] [[Chinchare]], the [[Mordian]] system and the [[Segmentum Fortress|segmentum fortress]] of [[Cypra Mundi]].

The corridor is not wholly safe. Its walls press in and fall back, [[Warp|warp]] predators and [[Daemon|daemons]] hunt along it, and the voids at its edges are filled with wrecks. Convoys that run it are escorted by Navy squadrons where possible.

Cause

[[Noctilith]], called blackstone, is a rare mineral that resonates with the Warp and can be charged either to attract or to repel its energies. It is the same material as the [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] and the [[Cadian Pylons|pylons]] of Cadia. The spear-like deposits in the crust of [[War of Nightmares|Vigilus]] had been polarised to repel warp energy by some technology of great age, probably [[Xenos|xenos]]. Sangua Terra was found to contain identical spears, held in a strange suspension so that they always point towards Vigilus. The field that runs between the two holds the Rift apart.The Adeptus Mechanicus has not been able to determine who placed or polarised the spears. The forge world of [[Stygies VIII]], which held mining rights on Vigilus, has been accused of concealing what it knows.

If either set of spears were destroyed or depolarised, the Gauntlet would close. This is why the forces of [[Chaos]] have tried so hard to take or ruin both worlds.

History

Opening and Dharrovar

At the opening of the Gauntlet, ships seeking passage had to pass close to the Nachmund system, whose principal planet, the [[Knight world]] of Dharrovar, was ruled by the Tyrant King Kaligius. His house, Mandrakor, had not been in contact with the Imperium since the [[Horus Heresy]]. Kaligius declared that [[Old Night]] had returned, refused all contact with the Imperium and made pacts with renegades and pirates. Ships that kept their distance were allowed to pass, while those that approached under a flag of parley were attacked. He was declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] as the term [[Imperium Nihilus]] was first coming into use, around 000.M42.

Imperial reinforcement

[[Roboute Guilliman]], as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], recognised the Gauntlet's value at once. He sent the [[Castellans of the Rift]], a Chapter of the [[Ultima Founding]], to drive back the daemon engines and renegade [[Imperial Knight|Knights]] that infested the route, and at least two of its companies fought in the defence of Sangua Terra. Vigilus became the staging post for every Imperial force sent into the Imperium Nihilus by that route and a refuge for billions of refugees fleeing south through the Gauntlet.

War of Nightmares

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

In the first decades of the 42nd millennium Vigilus was attacked in turn by an Ork Speedwaaagh!, a rising of the [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]] in its hives, Aeldari and Drukhari raiders and finally the Black Legion. [[Marneus Calgar]] of the [[Ultramarines]] commanded its defence and fought Abaddon in single combat. Abaddon withdrew, and the Gauntlet remained open.

Nachmund Rift War

Abaddon did not abandon his aim. In the years after the War of Nightmares the Black Legion and its allies turned on Sangua Terra and the systems along the Gauntlet, in a wider struggle recorded as the [[Nachmund Rift War]]. The Imperium held both ends, at great cost, and the passage remains open, although contested along its whole length.Dates for the later war are uncertain. The Administratum places it within the first three decades of the 42nd millennium.
