Artificer armour
Armour of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Adeptus Astartes]]
Type[[Power armour|Power armour]], of the highest craft
Made by[[Adeptus Mechanicus]] forges on [[Mars]]
Notable suitThe Armour of Fate, kept by [[Belisarius Cawl]]

Artificer armour is the finest class of [[Power armour|power armour]] made by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is built by the artificers of the forges of [[Mars]] for the most important warriors of the Imperium, and it is more heavily armoured and more powerful than the standard pattern.

Artificer armour includes systems that are beyond the standard suits, such as life-support technology and advanced augmentation. Each suit is made to order, and the Mechanicus records each one with its craft and its bearer.

The Armour of Fate

The Armour of Fate is a unique suit of artificer armour made on [[Mars]]. It was kept by [[Belisarius Cawl]] for ten thousand years. When [[Cadia]] fell in 999.M41, Cawl escaped from the planet with an armoured reliquary and this suit, which he had with him.The Armour of Fate's keeping is recorded in the sources as ten thousand years. The date of the Fall of Cadia is fixed; the length of Cawl's custody is a figure given in his own records.
