Ardamantua is an Imperial world that was besieged during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). Its garrison was the [[Imperial Fists]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]], and the world became one of the places where the Imperium first learned of the scale of the Ork threat.

Records of the war list Ardamantua among its decisive battles, with [[Terra]] and [[Ullanor]]. The world's defence is remembered less for its outcome than for the warnings it sent back to the Throneworld as the siege grew worse.

The relief attempt

The [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Heth]] led an expedition in person to relieve the Imperial Fists. His fleet was destroyed by Ork forces in the system, and he was killed. Before his death he sent Terra the first warning of the [[Beast]] and its Attack Moons. That warning was later used by the Imperial Navy when it planned the defence of Terra.

Heth's successor took up the relief in the following years. The Imperial Fists held the world until the fleets of the wider war could be brought to bear.

Legacy

Ardamantua is one of the names inscribed on the memorial rolls of the Imperial Fists. Its place in the war is recorded in the Imperial Fists' own chronicles, which describe the siege at length. The Administratum counts the world among the early sources of the Imperium's knowledge of the Ork Beast.
