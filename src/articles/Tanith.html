Tanith is a forest world in the [[Sabbat Worlds]] of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]]. It is the homeworld of the [[Astra Militarum]] regiment known as the [[Tanith First and Only]]. Its forests cover most of its land, and its people were raised to fight in them.

The world was counted among the worlds of the Sabbat Worlds sector when the sector was held by the Imperium. Its population was drawn into the regiment during the wars that followed the fall of the sector capital.

Military history

Tanith sent its regiment to the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]], where the regiment fought for many years across the sector. The world itself was not the main theatre of the crusade, and Imperial records describe it as a source of troops rather than a battlefield for much of the war.

Tanith's snipers and scouts are recorded as among the regiment's best units. The forest-fighting skills of the people were valued by the Imperial command.
