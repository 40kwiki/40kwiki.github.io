Urkanthos
[[Daemon Prince|Daemon prince]], Lord Purgator of the [[Chosen]]

Died999.M41, at [[Kasr Kraf]], killed by [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]]

Urkanthos was a [[Daemon Prince|daemon prince]] and the Lord Purgator among the Chosen of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. He commanded the ground assault that fell first on the fortress-city of [[Kasr Kraf]], the principal bastion of the defence of [[Cadia]], with a horde of [[Chaos Space Marines]], [[Chaos cult|cultists]] and daemons.

Urkanthos was killed at Kasr Kraf in 999.M41 by [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]], the Living Saint, during the [[Fall of Cadia]].

Fall of Cadia

Main article: [[Fall of Cadia]]

[[Saint Celestine|Celestine]] arrived at [[Thirteenth Black Crusade|Kasr Kraf]] with five companies of her order, lost in the Warp for fourteen centuries. She killed Urkanthos there, and stabbed [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] in the back with the Ardent Blade to save [[Ursarkar E. Creed|Lord Castellan Creed]]. She then led the survivors of [[Cadia]] to [[Macragge]].The sources date the Fall of Cadia to 999.M41. The events at Kasr Kraf are not dated more precisely.
