The Iron Knights are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a crusading successor of the [[Imperial Fists]] who follow the [[Codex Astartes]]. Their homeworld is Brycantia. Their raising is unrecorded, but they existed at the outbreak of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 and so were made before the [[Maximus Thane|Fourth Founding]].

They are one of the twelve Imperial Fists successors that send a champion to the centennial [[Feast of Blades]], and the only Chapter to have won it twice running. A part of the Chapter broke away and fell to [[Chaos]] as the warband called the Iron Brethren.

History

War of the Beast

When the [[Imperial Fists]] called all their successors to Sol to defend [[Terra]] against the [[Orks]] of the Beast, the Iron Knights came. Their [[Chapter Master]] Malfons was killed, and the [[First Captain]] Verpall served in the war. Afterwards they gave a part of their strength to rebuild the shattered Imperial Fists.

Wars of the 41st millennium

In 226.M41 Captain Cadulon, known as the Saint of Blades, vanished while commanding the Fourth Company on Omeros when he fought a Drukhari Archon and a dozen Incubi alone. He had won the Feast of Blades twice and lost narrowly once to the [[Black Templars]]. He had been a Company Champion after his predecessor was killed by a slaver-queen on Cystan.

In 746.M41 the Sons of Guilliman freed [[Nova Terra]] from a Tyranid attack, and their Squad Constantinus found Genestealer Cults in the noble houses of the capital. The ruling class was executed. The enraged population rose against its liberators, and Sergeant Constantinus blamed the Imperium for making him a killer, denounced the Emperor and vowed to lead the people to a better future. The old order fell, and other servants of the Ruinous Powers came. When the rebel sergeant's ambition set the whole sector at war, the Iron Knights led the forces that restored it, with the [[Tome Keepers]]. The campaign lasted thirteen years and ended in 759.M41 when the Officio Assassinorum killed Constantinus.

The Feast of Blades of 749.M41 was won by the Iron Knights' champion Hervald Strom, who narrowly beat the Imperial Fists' champion Demitrius Valor in a ritual duel. Strom, wounded in the Vinculus Crusade, won again at the next Feast, in 849.M41.Imperial sources number these Feasts the 812th and 813th, which cannot be reconciled with a centennial schedule. The dates given here are the Chapter's own. In 928.M41, on Thrassos, the Iron Knights and the Liberators tried to trap [[Waaagh!|WAAAGH!]] Dregdakka on a refinery platform by destroying the bridges and sending in kill-teams. The Orks dug in, over three hundred [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] died, and more Ork vessels poured into the system. The Chapter also fought the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] [[Farsight Enclaves]] with the Hammers of [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] in the Nightfire Wars.

Thirteenth Black Crusade

See also: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

The Chapter was already on a self-imposed [[Penitent|penitent]] crusade for a number of decades, after a group of its Astartes had fallen to Chaos. In 999.M41 it fielded a single company in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]. The First Company held Amistel Majoris beside the [[Howling Griffons]] in a trench war against [[Death Guard]] Plague Marines. Its [[Chaplain|Chaplains]] performed daily rites of purification to keep the plague at bay and vowed that the world would fall only at the cost of the Chapter. By the third week the world was secure enough to spare part of the force for the [[Cadia|Cadian System]].

Organisation and doctrine

The Iron Knights are a small Chapter used chiefly as a full assault force in planetary sieges. They use many [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] and heavy armour, such as Predators and [[Land Raider|Land Raiders]]. They treat war as a sport and take pride in testing themselves against harsh conditions and fortifications.

Notable members

  • Malfons: [[Chapter Master]], killed in the [[War of the Beast]].
  • Cadulon: the Saint of Blades, Captain of the Fourth Company.
  • Hervald Strom: champion, victor of the [[Feast of Blades]] in 749.M41 and 849.M41.
  • Goedendag Morningstar: Commander who sealed a [[Warp]] rift on the [[Hive world|hive world]] Minea.
