Warmaster is a title and military office of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It was created by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] at the Triumph of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] in 000.M31, at the close of the [[Great Crusade]], when he named his son [[Horus]] commander of all the Imperium's armies and withdrew to [[Terra]]. Horus's treachery in the [[Horus Heresy]] made the title infamous.

Since the Heresy the office has been revived in a lesser form. The [[High Lords of Terra]] grant it to the commander of a great crusade drawing on the forces of many [[Sector|sectors]], such as the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] or the [[Achilus Crusade]], and the holder commands every Imperial service in the theatre. Because of its association with Horus, the title is granted rarely and is regarded by many as ill-omened.

The servants of [[Chaos]] use the title for a lord strong enough to unite their warring factions as Horus once did. [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] of the [[Black Legion]] has claimed it as Warmaster of Chaos since the early [[31st millennium]].

Origin

For most of the Great Crusade the Emperor led the war in person. After the defeat of the [[Orks]] of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] he announced that he would return to [[Terra]], where he had begun secret work beneath the [[Imperial Palace]]. At the Triumph that followed the campaign, before an assembly of [[Primarch|primarchs]], Legions and armies, he named [[Horus]] Warmaster and ordered that all his armies and generals take orders from Horus as they would from himself. On the advice of [[Sanguinius]], the Emperor also suggested that the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] take the name Sons of Horus to mark their primarch's new rank; the change was made in 003.M31.

The appointment placed Horus above his brother primarchs, several of whom resented it. Some at Ullanor had expected the honour to go to [[Sanguinius]] or [[Roboute Guilliman]]. On the same occasion the Emperor announced that the Crusade would be governed from Terra by the [[Council of Terra]], made up of civilians, and the Legions would answer to it. Imperial historians count the two announcements together among the causes of the Heresy.

Horus as Warmaster

[[Horus]] commanded the Great Crusade from his flagship, the [[Vengeful Spirit]], for some years, from 000.M31. He was wounded on [[Davin]] in 004.M31 by a daemon-bound blade and, in the rites that healed him, accepted the offer of the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]] to overthrow his father. As Warmaster he had the authority to direct the Legions and armies where he chose, and he used it to gather the traitor Legions and send loyalists into traps, including the virus bombing of [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31 and the [[Drop Site Massacre]] in 006.M31.

The war that followed ended at the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31, where the Emperor destroyed Horus aboard the Vengeful Spirit and was himself placed on the [[Golden Throne]]. No loyalist held the title during the Heresy. The supreme authority on Terra was exercised by the Regent [[Malcador the Sigillite]] and the Praetorian [[Rogal Dorn]], and after the Siege by [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]].

Imperial Warmasters

After the Heresy the title was not used for centuries. It was revived for the commanders of great crusades, when the resources of several [[Sector|sectors]] and every arm of the Imperium had to be placed under one hand. In the [[41st millennium]] the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] grant it, and by formula the holder is said to receive his command from the Emperor himself. There is rarely more than one Warmaster in the Imperium at a time.

A Warmaster commands the [[Astra Militarum]], the [[Imperial Navy]] and allied forces in his crusade, and may direct [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] and agencies such as the [[Inquisition]] and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] where they agree to serve. The office ranks below only the Lord Commander Militant of the Astra Militarum among the military commands of the Imperium. The title is usually borne by a general officer of the Astra Militarum, though a Navy officer or a Chapter Master leading a great force may hold it.

Holders recorded in the archives of the Officio Militaris include:

  • Demetrius, commander of the Salonika Crusade in 733.M38.
  • Ingenus, whose capture by [[Drukhari]] raiders in 845.M41 widened the Dimmamak War. The Munitorum raised a further forty thousand regiments in response and assigned personal bodyguards to all senior officers of the Astra Militarum from that time.
  • Slaydo, who led the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] from 755.M41 and won the victory at Balhaut in 765.M41 before his death.
  • Macaroth, his successor in the Sabbat Worlds from 765.M41.
  • Tiber Achilus, who gained command of the Achilus Crusade into the Jericho Reach, drawing on the forces of the Calixis Sector.
  • Hokaeto, Supreme Imperial Commander of the Desmaxian Crusade.

Relation to the Lord Solar

The office of Lord Solar, or Lord Commander Solar, is distinct. It is the supreme military command of the Segmentum Solar and a standing appointment, not a crusade command. Its most famous holder, Lord Solar [[Macharius]], is often called a Warmaster in popular histories of his crusade, and Administratum usage has at times treated the two titles as equivalent. Officio Militaris records keep them separate.Some Administratum documents of the late 41st millennium describe "Lord Commander Solar" as the formal name under which the Warmaster's rank is conferred, to avoid the name of Horus. The Officio Militaris does not accept this usage.

Warmaster of Chaos

Main article: [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]

In the last hours of the [[Siege of Terra]], when the Custodian [[Constantin Valdor]] wounded Ezekyle Abaddon aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]], he saw a vision of Abaddon's future as Warmaster of Chaos; [[Ordo Malleus]] analysts hold that the gods had chosen [[Horus]]'s successor before Horus was dead. After the Siege Abaddon took Horus's Talon and command of the [[Sons of Horus]], but did not take Horus's title at once.

The title was disputed in the [[Legion Wars]] that followed, in the [[31st millennium]], in the [[Eye of Terror]]. The [[Emperor's Children]] grew a clone of Horus from his stolen body, intending it to lead the [[Traitor|traitors]]; Abaddon destroyed it at the Battle of Harmony. He then renamed his Legion the [[Black Legion]] and declared himself Warmaster, a claim strengthened when he bound the [[Daemon|daemon]] sword [[Drach'nyen]] and later by the [[Black Crusade]]s he led against the Imperium.

Abaddon's claim has never been universally accepted. Other lords of the traitor Legions, and daemon princes such as the [[Primarch|primarchs]] who fell with Horus, have refused to follow him or followed him only for a single crusade. Any lord who unites a large host of the [[Chaos|Dark Gods]]' servants may take the title for a time. After the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41 and the opening of the [[Great Rift]], Abaddon's authority stands higher than at any time since Horus.

Legacy

In the Imperium the title carries both prestige and suspicion. Officers who hold it command more power than almost anyone outside the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], and the precedent of [[Horus]] is often raised against them by rivals. Some regiments and Navy crews regard the appointment of a Warmaster as an omen of hard fighting.
