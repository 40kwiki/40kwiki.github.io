This is a timeline of Imperial history, the history of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from the last wars of the [[Unification Wars|Unification of Terra]] in the 30th millennium to the [[Era Indomitus]] of the early 42nd millennium. It lists the principal wars, reforms, schisms and disasters recorded by the [[Administratum]] and the other [[Adeptus Terra|Adepta]], arranged by millennium. Each section opens with a short summary of the age and is followed by a dated list of events.

Imperial historians divide the ten thousand years after the [[Horus Heresy]] into a series of named ages: the [[Time of Rebirth]], [[The Forging]], the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], the [[Age of Apostasy]], the [[Age of Redemption]], [[The Waning]] and the [[Time of Ending]]. The [[Era Indomitus]], which began with the opening of the [[Great Rift]] at the end of the 41st millennium, is the present age.

Dates are given in the [[Imperial Dating System]], in which 005.M31 means the fifth year of the 31st millennium. The record is uneven. Whole centuries of the 33rd and 34th millennia survive only as entries in the Administratum's register marked "no further data", and many events have been sealed by the [[Inquisition]] or the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Where the Adepta disagree, the date in the Administratum's reconciled chronology is given and the others are noted. Since the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]] the whole calendar has been under revision, for the temporal disruption of the Great Rift and the discovery of long-standing errors in the old reckoning mean that dates of the early 42nd millennium are approximate.

30th millennium

The 30th millennium saw the end of the Age of Strife on [[Terra]]. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] united the planet with his [[Thunder Warriors]], made an alliance with the [[Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]], created the twenty [[Space Marine Legions]] and launched the [[Great Crusade]] to reunite the scattered worlds of humanity. By the close of the millennium the Crusade had reconquered much of the galaxy and the Emperor had recovered most of his lost sons, the [[Primarch|primarchs]].

31st millennium

The 31st millennium opened with the Triumph of Ullanor and the Emperor's withdrawal to Terra. Within five years the Warmaster [[Horus]] had rebelled, and the [[Horus Heresy]] divided the Legions and ended at the [[Siege of Terra]], where the Emperor was mortally wounded and placed on the [[Golden Throne]]. The rest of the millennium was spent in the [[Great Scouring]], the reorganisation of the Imperium under the [[Codex Astartes]] and the first years of the [[Time of Rebirth]].

32nd millennium

The 32nd millennium belongs partly to the [[Time of Rebirth]] and partly to [[The Forging]]. The cults of the God-Emperor were united into the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], which became the state church. The greatest war of the age was the [[War of the Beast]], after which the High Lords were murdered in the [[Beheading]] and the government of the Imperium was reshaped.

33rd millennium

The 33rd millennium was the height of [[The Forging]], the age the Ecclesiarchy calls the golden age of the Imperium. The [[Imperial Cult]] spread across the galaxy, the Ministorum fought its first [[War of Faith|Wars of Faith]], and the Imperium pushed its borders beyond the frontiers of the Great Crusade.

077.M33The War of the Confessor, first of the Ministorum's Wars of Faith. The [[Temple of the Saviour Emperor]] suppresses the [[Confederation of Light]].
265–285.M33Last voyage of Admiral Usurs into the intergalactic void.
313.M33The Siege of Eternity's Gate.
615.M33The Blade of Infinity emerges from the Warp, the harbinger of Chaos incursions.
780–860.M33The War of the False Primarch.
831.M33The Year of the Ghosts in the [[Segmentum Solar]].

34th millennium

[[The Forging]] continued through the 34th millennium. The Imperium's astropathic network reached its widest extent and its commerce recovered with the rediscovery of an [[Standard Template Construct|STC]] database in the Cana System, but the millennium also saw the Pale Wasting and the Howling, two of the worst disasters of the age.

001.M34The Fourth Black Crusade: the citadel of El'Phanor stormed.
Early M34The [[Pale Wasting]] from the [[Ghoul Stars]].
401.M34The Howling: the death scream of the Cacodominus kills a billion astropaths.
666.M34The first Warp Stars appear after a storm from the [[Eye of Terror]].
934.M34The warnings of [[Ulthwé]].

35th millennium

In the 35th millennium the Imperium was divided by the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], in which the Ur-Council of Nova Terra ruled the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] apart from Terra, while the [[Moirae Schism]] tore the Adeptus Mechanicus apart. The Ecclesiarchy reunited the Imperium in the Cataclysm of Souls and emerged as its most powerful institution.

36th millennium

The 36th millennium was the [[Age of Apostasy]], the second great civil war in the Imperium's history. The Master of the Administratum, [[Goge Vandire]], seized the office of Ecclesiarch and ruled by terror in the [[Reign of Blood]], until he was overthrown in the movement led by [[Sebastian Thor]]. The [[Plague of Unbelief]] of the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis followed.

37th millennium

The 37th millennium began the [[Age of Redemption]], in which a newly zealous Imperium launched crusade after crusade to atone for the Apostasy. So many fleets and Chapters were drawn into distant wars that worlds at home were left defenceless.

38th millennium

The Age of Redemption ended in the 38th millennium and [[The Waning]] began, a long decline in which the Imperium lost ground on every frontier.

41st millennium

The 41st millennium saw the last centuries of [[The Waning]] and, from about 744.M41, the [[Time of Ending]]. The Imperium faced the [[Tyranids]], the reawakening [[Necrons]], the expansion of the [[T'au Empire]] and the greatest [[Ork]] invasions since the War of the Beast. It ended with the Thirteenth Black Crusade and the fall of [[Cadia]].

42nd millennium

The [[Era Indomitus]] began with the opening of the [[Great Rift]], which cut the Imperium in two, and with the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]], who took up the office of Lord Commander again and launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]] with the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Ultima Founding]]. The Crusade lasted more than a century by the old reckoning and was followed by new wars against the Chaos Legions, the Tyranids and the Necrons. Because the Imperial calendar was disrupted by the Rift, the dates below follow the reconstructed reckoning of the Lord Commander's office.
