The Taros Campaign was an [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial military]] campaign of 998.M41 in which the 4621st Imperial Guard Army and elements of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] tried to reclaim the desert [[Planetary classification|mining world]] of [[Taros]] from the [[T'au Empire]]. The campaign failed. The Imperium suffered heavy casualties, and Taros stayed in T'au hands, where it was renamed T'ros.

Taros had been annexed peacefully by the T'au. Its [[Planetary Defence Force]] had sided with the Imperium, and the T'au used local grievances to win the planet's support before the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial army]] arrived. The campaign is one of the most cited Imperial defeats of the [[Third Sphere Expansion]].

Course of the campaign

The [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial army]] landed in the mining fields and was met by [[T'au Empire|T'au]] forces, with [[Kroot]] mercenaries and human defectors of the [[Gue'vesa]]. [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] found the T'au able to hold their positions under fire and to withdraw without loss. The [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] contingent was committed to the cities, but the campaign's casualties were high on both sides.Casualty figures are not recorded in a single source. Imperial accounts describe them as "heavy" without a total.

Aftermath

The loss at Taros is recorded in the Imperial registers as part of a series of defeats in the Eastern Fringe. Later in 998.M41 and in 999.M41 the Imperium fought the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] again in the Agrellan campaigns.
