The Zombie Plague was one of the weapons used by the servants of [[Nurgle]] in the [[Plague Wars]]. It raised the dead of Ultramar to fight against the living, turning the bodies of defenders and civilians into shambling servants of the Plague.

The Zombie Plague was used alongside the contaminants and ritual sacrifices of the Plague Marines and the offensives of the [[Death Guard]]. Its effect was among the most feared in the campaign, since a world that had been held could be lost from within.

Use in Ultramar

The first stage of the [[Plague Wars]] was a stop-and-start war of attrition. From [[Talassar]] to [[Iax]], from [[Espandor]] to Circe and Laphis, the [[Death Guard|Plague Marines]] made strikes, and the Zombie Plague was used against defenders in several of them. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] and the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Auxilia]] threw back each attack and burned out the corruption.The sources describe the plague's use in general terms. They do not give a date for each attack in the first stage of the war.
