The Adepta Sororitas, commonly the Sisters of Battle, are the armed sisterhood of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]]. They are warrior-women raised from infancy in the faith of the [[Imperial Cult]], clad in [[Power armour|ceramite]] power armour and armed with bolters, flamers and melta weapons, and they fight the wars of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] in every quarter of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. They are organised in Orders Militant, each with its own saint, banner and tradition, and they act as the Chamber Militant of the [[Inquisition]]'s Ordo Hereticus.

The Sisters came into being as a result of a tyranny. The Ecclesiarchy's own armies, the [[Frateris Templar]], were disbanded in the reformation that followed the [[Reign of Blood]] in the 36th millennium, and the Ministorum was forbidden to keep "men under arms". The Sisterhood, being women, was held not to fall within the ban. That evasion of the spirit of [[Sebastian Thor]]'s decree is the foundation on which the Orders were built, and the Inquisition has watched them ever since.

In their own account the Sisters are a sisterhood of the Emperor's saints. They believe that faith is a weapon as certain as any armament, and their battle liturgy consists of hymns, litanies and the invocation of the Emperor's name in the middle of combat. Their soldiers describe visions, miracles and an inner force that carries them through wounds that would drop a normal soldier. The Sisters call this divine grace. [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], who have watched it closely, call it something else in their private reports.

History

The Emperor's Truth and its heirs

The Sisters have no counterpart in the [[Great Crusade]]. The Emperor held that the Imperium should be secular and rational, and he forbade the worship of himself as a god. [[Imperial Truth|The Imperial Truth]] that his soldiers carried to the conquered worlds condemned all religion as superstition, and in the early years of the Crusade the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]] noted that the Truth was applied with a firm hand to cults of every kind. Yet even then there were men and women who regarded the Emperor as more than a man, and sects, the largest of them the [[Imperial Cult|Lectitio Divinitatus]], formed about the figure of the Emperor on a number of worlds. The Emperor ordered them put down.

[[Horus Heresy|The Heresy]] changed this. The [[Word Bearers]] had been among the first to worship the Emperor, and their [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Lorgar]] was rebuked by the Emperor for it. After the Emperor was interred in the [[Golden Throne]] and the Imperium had been reformed, belief in his divinity spread with great speed and was formalised in the Imperial Cult. A great organisation of priests grew up to serve it. The military arm of the Ecclesiarchy, of which the Sisters of Battle are the last survivor, comes from the same period.

One tradition holds that the Sisters are descended from the women who stood beside the Emperor in the Great Crusade, among them the pariah warriors known as the [[Sisters of Silence]] who served him as hunters of [[Psyker|witches]]. The Sisters do not themselves claim it, and the [[Inquisition]] holds that no connection exists, since the Silent were made by the Emperor and served no faith. Imperial accounts differ on the point.

The Age of Apostasy

Further information: [[Age of Apostasy]]

In the [[36th millennium]] the Ecclesiarchy held nearly total power. Its tithes took precedence over the [[Administratum]]'s, and its choice of governors and wars determined the policy of the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] withdrew from a government they could not trust. [[Warp storm|Warp storms]] isolated whole [[Sector|sectors]], and cults of penitents, flagellants and doomsayers rose across the Imperium. The enemies of Mankind exploited the confusion, and the [[Traitor Legions]], [[Orks]] and [[Drukhari]] struck at worlds that no help could reach.

[[Goge Vandire]], the [[Master of the Administratum]], took advantage of the disorder in late M35. He had installed a pliable [[Ecclesiarch]], Paulis III, and when Paulis proved incompetent he had him murdered and took the office for himself. His rule, the [[Reign of Blood]], was a tyranny in which billions died, and it was enforced by the [[Frateris Templar]] and by regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] loyal to him.

The Brides of the Emperor

Early in his rule Vandire heard of a convent of warrior-women on the [[Agri-world|agri-world]] of [[Daughters of the Emperor|San Leor]], the Daughters of the Emperor. He went there with thousands of retainers and demanded their loyalty. The Daughters refused to open their gates to any but the Emperor. Vandire entered with a small guard and had his bodyguard fire a laspistol at him. The shot was turned by the field of his [[Rosarius]], which the Sisters had never seen, and they took the survival of the High Lord for a miracle. They swore themselves to him, and Vandire named them the Brides of the Emperor and made them his personal guard and his [[Executioners|executioners]].

Alicia Dominica and the fall of Vandire

A reform movement, the [[Sebastian Thor|Confederation of Light]], rose against Vandire under the preacher Sebastian Thor. As Thor's crusade gained ground the Astartes and the Mechanicus, who had stood aside, joined it and closed on [[Terra]]. In 378.M36, according to the Ecclesiarchy's account, a strike force drawn from the [[Imperial Fists]], the [[Black Templars]] and other [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] attacked the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] and found their drop-pod assault checked by a corps of warrior-women in [[Power armour|power armour]] who fought to the last. The siege was long and without quarter.

It ended through the intervention of Centurion Longinus of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], who sought out the Brides' leader, [[Alicia Dominica]], and her five closest companions, Silvana, Mina, Lucia, Katherine and Arabella. He brought them to the [[Golden Throne]]. What they learned there has never been written down, but when they came out they went directly to Vandire. Dominica struck off his head, and it is said that his Rosarius, which had saved him at San Leor, was cloven by her blade. The Reign of Blood was over.

The Thorian Reformation

Main article: [[Thorian Reformation]]

Thor was made the 292nd Ecclesiarch and set about rebuilding. The Frateris Templar were disbanded and the Decree Passive forbade the Ministorum from maintaining a standing army of "men under arms". The Brides resumed their old name as Daughters of the Emperor and were divided between two new convents, the Convent Prioris on Terra and the [[Ophelia VII|Convent Sanctorum]] on Ophelia VII. They were reconstituted as the Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas, with Dominica as their founder. The Sisters swelled to over ten thousand, and Thor established the system of orders that remains in place.

Soon afterwards the [[Ordo Hereticus]] of the Inquisition, founded in the same crisis to stop another Age of Apostasy, signed with the senior Sisters the Convocation of [[Nephilim Sector|Nephilim]], binding the Sisterhood to the Ordo as its Chamber Militant. The terms were never published. Some believe the Convocation reflects what Dominica was told before the Throne, and some that it was a means for the Inquisition to hold the Ecclesiarchy's new weapon in check.

Wars of Faith

Alicia Dominica fell in battle at Frideswide's World in the middle of the 36th millennium, wounded by a single [[Lasgun|lasgun]] bolt after surviving hundreds of blows, and was declared a saint. The Ecclesiarchy established the office of Abbess Sanctorum to lead the Sisterhood in her place. In the early [[38th millennium]] the power of the Ministorum reached a new height and a thousand Wars of Faith were launched against [[Heretic|heretics]], in the course of which two more Orders were founded, the Bloody Rose and the Sacred Rose.

The Orders Militant have fought alongside the Astartes, the Guard and the Inquisition ever since. In 799.M41 an invasion of San Leor by the [[Red Corsairs]] was annihilated by the counter-attack of nine Orders, and in 833.M41 the Order of the Bloody Rose fought beside the Black Templars in the Vinculus Crusade. The [[Order of Our Martyred Lady]] was almost destroyed at [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]] in 998.M41, and its Sisters have sworn to retake the [[Hive world|hive city]] of Tempestora.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

At [[Cadia]], and in the wars that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the Sisterhood fought in numbers not seen since the Reformation, and many of its Orders have been lost. The faith of the Sisters has been strengthened by the return of [[Saint Celestine]], the Living Saint, who has appeared on many battlefields to lead the Orders and then vanished. The Abbess Sanctorum, Morvenn Vahl, commands the Sisterhood in the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and the Sisters have been among the forces that [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] has relied on in his campaigns.

Organisation

Convents and Orders

The Adepta Sororitas is divided between the two convents. Each is led by a prioress, and the Abbess Sanctorum, who is elected from among the leaders of the Orders, commands the whole Sisterhood and may sit among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Each Order is headed by a Canoness Superior, who answers to the Abbess alone. A preceptory is a single convent of up to a thousand Sisters and is about the size of a [[Space Marine Chapter]]. An Order may have a number of preceptories on different worlds.

The Orders Majoris are joined by many Orders Minoris, which are smaller and recent, and by the non-militant orders of the Ministorum. The Orders Hospitaller tend the wounded, the Orders Dialogous serve as clerks, preachers and orators, and the Orders Famulous attend the saints and their relics.

Recruitment and training

Sisters are recruited from the female orphans of the [[Commissar|Schola Progenium]], the children of men and women who died in the Emperor's service. They enter the Convents as novices and are taught the [[Imperial Cult|Imperial Creed]] in a strict form, a regime of drill, prayer and penance by which they come to regard the Emperor as their father. At the end of her novitiate a Sister swears her vows and is given armour and a [[Bolter|bolter]]. She is expected to serve until she dies.

Ranks and specialisations

The common warrior is the Battle Sister. Above her are the Sister Superior, the Canoness and the Canoness Superior, and above them the Prioress and the Abbess. Within each squad are specialist Sisters. Celestians are elite troops in heavier armour, Dominions carry special weapons, Retributors use heavy weapons, Seraphim and Zephyrim use jump packs, and the Sisters Repentia are penitents who have disgraced themselves and are sent to die in the thick of the enemy with chainswords.

Faith and wargear

A Sister's armour is of the same [[Power armour|ceramite]] as that of the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]], and is richly ornamented with the symbols of her Order. She is armed with a [[Bolter|bolter]], a weapon granted by special dispensation, and with flamers and meltaguns for the heavier work. The Orders fight from armoured transports and tanks. The Immolator carries squads into battle with flame weapons, the Exorcist tank fires a great organ of rockets, and the [[Penitent|Penitent Engine]] is a war machine made from a bound penitent. The largest Orders also use the Paragon Warsuit, a heavy battle-frame that carries a Canoness into combat.

The Sisters make heavy use of a doctrine of faith. A battle hymn is sung before the attack, and relics of saints are carried into battle. A Sister who dies in combat is held to have achieved martyrdom, and her death is believed to strengthen those left behind. An Order that has been reduced to a few survivors will continue to fight, because to leave the field would insult the dead. The Orders also have a tradition of the Acts of Faith, moments of heightened devotion in which the Sisters perform feats that are believed to be miraculous.

Relations with other institutions

[[Inquisition]]
The [[Ordo Hereticus]] has the Sisters under its authority by the Convocation of [[Nephilim Sector|Nephilim]], and [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] of the Ordo use the Orders in their investigations. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] tolerates this and does not like it.
[[Adeptus Astartes]]
The Sisters and the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] have fought side by side since the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace|Siege of the Ecclesiarchal Palace]], which each side remembers differently. The [[Black Templars]], who have a great respect for the faith of the Sisters, fight beside them most readily.
[[Astra Militarum]]
Regiments of the Guard are often placed beside the Sisters in the Wars of Faith, and the Sisters set them an example of piety that the regiments do not always welcome.
[[Adeptus Arbites]]
The Arbitrators and the Sisters are drawn from the same Scholae and respect one another's severity, though the Arbites say that the law is older than faith.

Notable Sisters

  • Alicia Dominica, founder of the Orders Militant and the Sisterhood's first saint.
  • Katherine, shield-bearer of [[Alicia Dominica]], patron of the [[Order of Our Martyred Lady]].
  • Saint Celestine, a Sister [[Penitent|Repentia]] who became the Living Saint and has risen from death several times in the sight of the Orders.
  • Morvenn Vahl, Abbess Sanctorum in the [[Era Indomitus]], who fights in a Paragon Warsuit.
