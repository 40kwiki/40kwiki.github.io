The Elysian Drop Troops are the airborne assault infantry regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]]. They are recruited from the verdant world of [[Elysia]] in the [[Segmentum Solar]], about 30 light years from [[Armageddon]]. They are deployed by grav-chute and are used for assaults behind enemy lines.

Drop infantry are rare within the Imperial Guard as a whole. Elysia produces a considerable quantity of them, which makes its regiments a strategic asset that many generals seek to obtain.

Organisation

The regiments are trained for high-altitude and grav-chute insertion. Each drop company is equipped for rapid deployment and for holding ground until the main force arrives. Their role is often to seize positions that a conventional army could not reach in time.The structure of the regiments varies by campaign. The description here is a general summary.
