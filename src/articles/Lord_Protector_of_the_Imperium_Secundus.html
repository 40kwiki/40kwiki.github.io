The Lord Protector of the Imperium Secundus was the supreme military office of the [[Imperium Secundus]], the contingency empire raised at [[Macragge]] behind the [[Ruinstorm]]. Its only holder was [[Lion El'Jonson]], primarch of the [[Dark Angels]], who commanded every armed force of the empire under the Imperator Regis, [[Sanguinius]]. Imperial writers compare the office with that of the [[Warmaster]].

Creation

When the Ruinstorm cut the east of the galaxy from [[Terra]], [[Roboute Guilliman]] judged that the Imperium might already have fallen and prepared a second empire in its place. He wanted no crown for himself and found his ruler in [[Sanguinius]], who arrived at Macragge from the [[Signus Campaign]] with the [[Blood Angels]] and was persuaded, unwillingly, to rule as regent for the Emperor.

The Dark Angels had already come to Macragge, guided through the Warp by the beacon of the [[Pharos]] on [[Sotha]]. Once Sanguinius had accepted the title of Imperator Regis, the three primarchs divided the government. The Lion was named Lord Protector, in command of all the empire's military forces, and Guilliman took a separate office, that of Lord Warden.

Powers and duties

The Lord Protector held the supreme command of the armies and fleets of the Imperium Secundus, among them the three Legions, the Ultramar Auxilia and the loyal regiments that had escaped the storm. He answered to the Imperator Regis, and with him and the Lord Warden he sat in the council that the chroniclers call the Triumvirate.

The security of Macragge fell to him. When [[Konrad Curze]] loosed suicide bombers and rebels on the world, the Lion placed it under martial law and used the Dark Angels to hold it. He urged an orbital bombardment of the rebel region of Illyrium. Sanguinius and Guilliman refused it for the civilian dead it would cause, and the Lion sent in his Legion's Dreadwing instead.

The Lord Warden

The Lord Warden was a separate office with its own holder. Guilliman took it when Sanguinius became Imperator Regis. Imperial accounts style him the chief adviser to the Imperator Regis, and he governed the civil affairs and supply of the Five Hundred Worlds. The Lion's command of the armies was therefore distinct from Guilliman's civil authority, and the two clashed over the security of the empire. Later summaries sometimes confuse them.

Abolition

The quarrel over Curze ended the office. Brought before Sanguinius and Guilliman, Curze accused the Lion of secretly ordering the bombardment that Guilliman had forbidden. The Lion tried to kill him and was stopped by Sanguinius, and Guilliman broke the Lion Sword across his thigh. Sanguinius then dismissed the Lord Protector, ended the Triumvirate and banished the Lion, and the Dark Angels left Macragge within hours.

By 014.M31 the loyalists behind the Ruinstorm saw that the Emperor and his Imperium had survived. The ruling triumvirate abandoned the Imperium Secundus, and its territories returned to the Imperium or to the [[Realm of Ultramar]] under the regency of [[Valentus Dolor]]. The title of Lord Protector ended with it.

Legacy

The Imperium Secundus left almost no mark on the official histories, and its offices with it. In the [[32nd millennium]] the [[Drakan Vangorich|Grand Master of Assassins Drakan Vangorich]] was named Lord Protector of the Imperium after the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32, but that office has no descent from the Lion's.

The Lion's tenure is remembered chiefly for its end. Dark Angels tradition holds that he never gave the order for the bombardment, and the Ultramarines record the matter otherwise. After his dismissal he turned back to Macragge and confronted Sanguinius at the moment of Curze's sentence, and the Dark Angels later followed the other primarchs into the Ruinstorm on the road to [[Davin]].
