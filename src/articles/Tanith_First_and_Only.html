Tanith First and Only is an [[Astra Militarum]] regiment raised from the people of the forest world of [[Tanith]] in the [[Sabbat Worlds]]. It fought in the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] and is one of the best-recorded Imperial regiments of the late [[41st millennium]], chiefly because of the memoirs of its [[Commissar|commissar]].

The regiment's name reflects its origin: it was the Tanith First, and it was the only regiment the world raised. Its troops fought as light infantry and as snipers, and they were known for moving quickly through the forests of the sector.

Sabbat Worlds Crusade

Main article: [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]

The regiment served under the [[Civitas Imperialis]] and the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] in the years after the fall of the sector capital. Its commander, [[Ibram Gaunt]], was made [[Commissar|Colonel-Commissar]] in 755.M41. Under Gaunt the regiment took part in campaigns across the [[Sabbat Worlds]], and it was one of the forces that opposed the Chaos-held worlds of the Eyeless and their allies.

The campaign on [[Menazoid Epsilon]] in 765.M41 is one of the best recorded. There the [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]] [[Hechtor Dravere]] was killed, and Gaunt destroyed a [[Standard Template Construct]] fabricator that the Imperial forces had found there.
