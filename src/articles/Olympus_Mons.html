Olympus Mons is the highest mountain on [[Mars]] and the capital of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Its flanks and summit are covered by the largest forge temple in the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]], the seat of the [[Fabricator-General]] and of the [[Temple of All Knowledge]], where every discovery of the priesthood is dedicated on the High Altar of Technology. To the [[Cult Mechanicus]] it is the holiest place in the galaxy.

It was here, in 739.M30, that the Mechanicum and the [[Emperor of Mankind]] concluded the [[Treaty of Olympus]] that bound Mars to [[Terra]]. In 005.M31 the Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] declared from Olympus Mons for [[Horus]], beginning the [[Schism of Mars]]. Recovered by the loyalists after the [[Siege of Terra]], it has been the seat of every Fabricator-General since.

Geography

Olympus Mons is a shield volcano about 22 kilometres high and some 600 kilometres across, standing at the north-western edge of the [[Tharsis Montes|Tharsis]] uplands. Its summit caldera, its flanks and the cliffs around its base have been built over so completely that the mountain is now hard to tell apart from the forge temple that covers it. Transit tubes, power lines and orbital lifts link it with the forges of Tharsis and with the [[Ring of Iron]] above.

History

Age of Strife

Further information: [[Age of Strife]]

Mechanicum histories say that Olympus Mons became the seat of the priesthood's rulers during the [[Age of Strife]], when the Mechanicum emerged from the wars between the forges as master of the planet. A Fabricator-General and a parliament of high adepts, the Mechanicum Parliament, ruled from the mountain. No date is recorded for the founding of the temple, which Martian scholars count among the oldest buildings on the planet.The date is estimated from the Mechanicum's own chronology of the Age of Strife.

Treaty of Olympus

Main article: [[Treaty of Olympus]]

When the Emperor came to Mars after the [[Unification Wars]], he was received at Olympus Mons. The Mechanicum recognised him as the [[Omnissiah]] and in 739.M30 the two powers signed the Treaty of Olympus there. The Emperor also ordered the sealing of certain vaults beneath the mountain, among them the Vaults of Moravec, a maze of tunnels and chambers nearly a kilometre deep in which a forbidden thinking machine was held. Kelbor-Hal resented the order for the rest of his rule.

Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

In 005.M31, with the promise from Horus that the Emperor's prohibitions would be lifted, Kelbor-Hal declared for the Warmaster. The vaults were opened, and a corrupting scrapcode was released from Olympus into the planet's noosphere. Machines and their crews across Mars went mad, and the forge temples fell to fighting each other. Olympus Mons stayed in traitor hands throughout the Schism and became the centre of the [[Dark Mechanicum]], whose forges supplied the traitor host during the Siege of Terra.

Recovery and the Adeptus Mechanicus

Loyalist forces retook Mars during the [[Great Scouring]] after 014.M31. [[Zagreus Kane]], who had held out in the forge of Mondus Occulam, became Fabricator-General and took his seat at Olympus. The temple was purged and reconsecrated, which Mechanicus records say took more than a century, and the Mechanicum Parliament gave way to the councils of the reformed Adeptus Mechanicus.The length of the purge is given in Mechanicus records only as "more than a century"; the end date is unrecorded.

Later history

In M39 the Fabricator-General's claim on the relics of the Culexus Temple of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] brought Olympus close to war with Terra in the affair of the Golden Cog. In 998.M41 a [[Necrons|Necron]] raid on the deep mines of Mars led the Fabricator-General to close the [[Noctis Labyrinthus]] by decree from Olympus. In the [[Era Indomitus]], the orders that set every forge world to its full output have been issued from Olympus Mons.

Buildings

  • [[Temple of All Knowledge]]: the great temple of the Cult Mechanicus, whose roof stands on moving pistons about two kilometres high.
  • High Altar of Technology: the altar within the temple to which every new discovery is offered.
  • Mechanicum Parliament: the assembly of the high adepts of Mars before the Heresy.
  • Vaults of Moravec: sealed by the Emperor, opened by Kelbor-Hal in 005.M31, and closed again after the Scouring.
