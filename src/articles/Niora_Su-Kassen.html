Niora Su-Kassen was an admiral of the Armada Imperialis, the fleet arm of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], from the Void Clans of the [[Jupiter|Jovian]] moons. She served on the Solar command staff of [[Rogal Dorn]] during the [[Siege of Terra]] (c. 013–014.M31), commanded the loyal ships hidden at the edge of the [[Sol System]] and led the counter-attack that came in the war's last hours. After the Siege she sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction as the first Lord High Admiral of the new [[Imperial Navy]].

Little else is recorded of her. The date and manner of her death are not given in any archive open to Imperial scholars.

Background

Su-Kassen was born in the [[30th millennium]] among the Void Clans of [[Sol System|Jupiter]], who live in ships and habitats in the system's outer reaches and have long served the Imperium as spacers. The year of her birth is not recorded. By the time of the [[Horus Heresy]] she held flag rank in the Armada, which at that time was part of the same service as the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]]'s ground forces. The Armada's senior officer at the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 was a Grand Admiral, Constansa Suati-Falkan, and the record does not say whether Su-Kassen had served under her.

The Siege of Terra

Dorn's staff

When the Heresy came to the Sol System, [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] drew Su-Kassen onto his Solar command staff. She fought through the [[Siege of Terra|Solar War]] (c. 013.M31) and the defence of [[Terra]]. In the course of the Siege she learned of the death of her daughter, a ship's captain who had refused to join [[Horus]].

The Phalanx

See also: [[Phalanx]]

During the Siege the fortress-ship [[Phalanx]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] was nearly lost to a daemonic possession set loose aboard it. Dorn, about to order the ship destroyed, spared it when the possession was ended, and gave the damaged vessel to Su-Kassen, ordering her to hide it at the edge of the system and to strike the enemy's rear. It lay hidden in the rings of [[Saturn]] with other loyal ships through the Siege.

When [[Corswain]] of the [[Dark Angels]] reached the edge of the Sol System, he made contact with Su-Kassen. He persuaded her to support a landing, and the fireship that broke the [[Traitor|traitor]] blockade belonged to that effort.

The last hours

When the [[Astronomican]] was rekindled in the final hours of 014.M31, Su-Kassen and the Imperial Fists officer Halbrecht took it as a sign. They attacked the traitor fleet, some hours before [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s fleet arrived. The Phalanx ended the Siege a half-ruin.

Lord High Admiral

See also: [[Lord High Admiral]]

After the Siege Su-Kassen sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. She is listed there as [[Lord High Admiral]] of the [[Imperial Navy|Armada Imperialis]], while the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Adreen]] commanded the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]]. When [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] divided the army from the fleets, the office she held became the supreme command of the new Imperial Navy, and she is recorded as its first Lord High Admiral.

The records say no more of her tenure. The next Lord High Admiral named in the Navy's registers is [[Javor]], in an unrecorded year of the [[32nd millennium]].
