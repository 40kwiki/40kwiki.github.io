Incaladion is an Imperial [[Forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It was once the home of the ''[[Legio Fureans]]'' ("Tiger-Eyes"), a [[Titan Legion]] with a sinister reputation. After the [[Horus Heresy]], when the [[Traitor Legions]] had been driven into the [[Eye of Terror]], the reformed Adeptus Mechanicus judged that the world's worth and industrial output were worth preserving.

Incaladion is a Production Grade III-Prima Forge World with a Tithe Grade of ''[[Aptus Non]]''. This means that it pays no tithe to the [[Administratum]]. Its output is considered to have great strategic value for the Imperium in its own right. In recent years the world has been implicated in a conspiracy linked to [[Heretek]]s and the worship of proscribed [[xenos]] lore.

History

Incaladion was founded by a [[Mechanicum]] Sleeper-Ark during the [[Age of Strife]]. The world was rich in rare minerals and held the organic compounds needed to sustain a biosphere. Its Warp currents had carried the Mechanicum to it, but the same currents later brought other, less welcome voyagers. Records of the early centuries are fragmentary. The Mechanicum colonists fought an almost constant war against invaders while they built vast works of macro-construction. As a result, the Incaladine Mechanicum became a warlike faction, separated from [[Mars]] by a great distance, and seldom united.

Xenos invasion pushed the colonists to unusual technological paths. They raised fortified cities around the richest mineral seams and the algae-rich seas. They sank hidden forge-fanes kilometres beneath the crust. Because they needed more human stock, the Incaladine raided the feral populations of the world. This brought barbaric and macabre cultures into the [[Machine Cult]] of the Incaladine Mechanicum, which grew schismatic over time.

Throughout this history the Legio Fureans were the chief military force of the ruling [[Archmagos]], known as the Preceptor-General, and their Titans were the avatars of her will. Whenever the fighting allowed, the Legion's strength was expanded. The name "Tiger-Eyes" is said to be a corruption of the name given to the Titans by the feral tribes of the nearby world of Humardu, who held them to be terrible gods come to reap a sacrifice of the young and strong. For centuries the Legion fought [[Ork]] marauders, Yldari corsairs, Tarellian war-packs and the carnoplasm infestations that fell from the Abyssal Reefs.

About fifty standard years before the [[Great Crusade]] reached the world, the Taghmata Omnissium, the feudal order of its defence, broke down after a brutal series of raids. The Preceptor-General was murdered and Incaladion split into warring or isolationist factions. When the Great Crusade arrived, the world was a global battlefield, fought over by a dozen armies of Mechanicum, renegades and xenos. The Tiger-Eyes refused to retreat and were ground down to a shadow of their strength, reduced to defending the last forge-fane of their dead regent.

Liberation

Mars wished to bring Incaladion into the Imperium, but most of the Mechanicum's forces were committed elsewhere. The liberation fell to the frontline forces of the [[IV Legion]], not yet known as the [[Iron Warriors]], of the 8th Expeditionary Fleet, with such help from the [[Legio Mortis]] and the [[Skitarii]] of Mars as could be spared. The campaign lasted from 842 to 843.M30 and was the bloodiest in the IV Legion's history to that date. After two Terran years of guerrilla war Incaladion joined the Imperium. Warship squadrons were based permanently in the system, and the world's mineral wealth was turned to feed the Great Crusade.

The Incaladine judged Mars and the Legio Mortis their saviours, rather than the Legion that had spent so many lives. The Legio Fureans were quickly reconstituted and rearmed. Reborn, they fought with an aggressive tempo matched by few other Titan Legions, in demi-legios and strike groups beside the Expeditionary Fleets.

The Horus Heresy

When the [[Horus Heresy]] broke out, the Legio Fureans sided with the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], as did the [[Fabricator-General]] of Mars and the Legio Mortis. Hindsight shows that the Legion knew of the treachery beforehand. A demi-legio of the Tiger-Eyes was sent to the [[First Battle of Paramar|Paramar]] assault force before blood was shed on [[Isstvan V]]. The Legion's darker rites soon developed into the arcana of what later became the [[Dark Mechanicum]]. With its Titan Legion gone, Incaladion's new Preceptor-General lost his chief means of overmatching his rivals, and the world fell into a civil war of its own.
