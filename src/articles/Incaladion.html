Incaladion is an Imperial [[Forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It was once the home of the ''[[Legio Fureans]]'' ("Tiger-Eyes"), a [[Titan Legion]] with a sinister reputation. After the [[Horus Heresy]], when the [[Traitor Legions]] had been driven into the [[Eye of Terror]], the reformed Adeptus Mechanicus judged that the world's worth and industrial output were worth preserving.

Incaladion is a Production Grade III-Prima Forge World with a Tithe Grade of ''[[Aptus Non]]''. This means that it pays no tithe to the [[Administratum]]. Its output is considered to have great strategic value for the Imperium in its own right. In recent years the world has been implicated in a conspiracy linked to [[Heretek]]s and the worship of proscribed [[xenos]] lore.

History

Incaladion was founded by a [[Mechanicum]] Sleeper-Ark during the [[Age of Strife]]. The world was rich in rare minerals and held the organic compounds needed to sustain a biosphere. Its Warp currents had carried the Mechanicum to it, but the same currents later brought other, less welcome voyagers. Records of the early centuries are fragmentary. The Mechanicum colonists fought an almost constant war against invaders while they built vast works of macro-construction. As a result, the Incaladine Mechanicum became a warlike faction, separated from [[Mars]] by a great distance, and seldom united.

Xenos invasion pushed the colonists to unusual technological paths. They raised fortified cities around the richest mineral seams and the algae-rich seas. They sank hidden forge-fanes kilometres beneath the crust. Because they needed more human stock, the Incaladine raided the feral populations of the world. This brought barbaric and macabre cultures into the [[Machine Cult]] of the Incaladine Mechanicum, which grew schismatic over time.

The world was liberated by the forces of the [[Great Crusade]]. Throughout its long history, the ''Legio Fureans'' were the chief military force of the ruling [[Archmagos]], known as the Preceptor-General. Their Titans were the avatars of her will. Whenever fighting allowed, the Titan Legion's strength was expanded. The name "Tiger-Eyes" dates from this early period.
