The Sol System, often called simply Sol and in old records the Solar System, is the star system in which humanity arose. It contains [[Terra]], the Throneworld of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], and [[Mars]], the homeworld of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], as well as [[Luna]], the gas giants Jupiter and Saturn, and Saturn's moon [[Titan]], fortress of the [[Grey Knights]]. It is the capital of the [[Segmentum Solar]] and the most heavily defended system in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]].

Every age of human history has passed through the system. It was unified by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] at the end of the [[Age of Strife]], besieged by the traitor Legions in the [[Siege of Terra]], invaded by [[Orks]] in the [[War of the Beast]], and has remained the heart of the Imperium for ten thousand years. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it lies close to the [[Great Rift]] and is the seat of the reforms of [[Roboute Guilliman]].

History

Ancient history

Humanity spread from [[Terra]] to the other worlds of the system in the deep past. [[Mars]], [[Luna]] and the moons of the gas giants were settled, and in the [[Dark Age of Technology]] Terra and Mars each ruled a great realm. When that age ended in the [[Age of Strife]], [[Warp storm|warp storms]] isolated the system. Terra fell to warring techno-barbarian tribes, Mars became a techno-empire of its own, and the outer worlds were lost, abandoned or seized by local powers, among them the Ringers of Saturn.

Unification

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

The Emperor first united Terra in the [[Unification Wars]], which ended in 712.M30, and in 739.M30 he went to Mars in peace and concluded the [[Treaty of Olympus]] (the Treaty of Mars) with the [[Mechanicum]]. The other worlds of the system were then brought under Imperial rule; in the early Crusade the Iron Warriors took Venus, and eight Legions drove an unremembered xenos species from Sedna. On Luna the Emperor's gene-laboratories made the breakthroughs that led to the [[Space Marine Legions]]. The Mechanicum's shipyards in the [[Ring of Iron]] and the orbital docks of Jupiter built the fleets of the [[Great Crusade]], which departed from the system in 798.M30.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

When the [[Horus Heresy]] broke out, the Praetorian of Terra, [[Rogal Dorn]], fortified the entire system. Its outer worlds and moons were turned into fortresses, its fleets were concentrated, and its approaches were mined. Mars fell to the Dark Mechanicum in the [[Schism of Mars]], and a loyalist fleet blockaded it. In 010.M31 the Alpha Legion struck at Pluto and the astropathic relay on its moon Hydra, in the first major engagement of the Solar War; [[Rogal Dorn]] killed the primarch Alpharius there.

After the Alpha Legion gave Horus a map of the system's defences, the traitor host gathered above Mars. For thirty solar days it fought the blockading fleet, and then it destroyed the orbital platforms of Terra and laid waste to the defences of Luna before it landed on the Throneworld. The [[Siege of Terra]] followed in 014.M31.

The Imperial age

After the Heresy the Sol System became the seat of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and of the [[Adeptus Terra]]. Saturn, which had never held a large population, became the home of the newly founded [[Inquisition]], and the [[Grey Knights]] were raised on [[Titan Legions|Titan]], which was moved into the Warp as they were made.

In 544–546.M32 the [[War of the Beast]] brought the [[Orks]] to the Throneworld. An Ork attack moon materialised above Terra while the High Lords quarrelled, the Mechanicum on Mars refused aid, and the Imperial Fists, reduced to a single captain, were remade under Lord Commander Koorland. The crisis exposed the Mechanicum's secret experiments with Ork teleportation technology, which were turned against the moon: half of it was teleported away and the rest shattered.

In 998.M41 five [[Necrons|Necron]] cruisers entered the system undetected by the monitoring station on Titan and landed on Mars for reasons that remain unknown.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, and the Sol System lies in the part of the Imperium that the Astronomican still reaches. [[Astronomican|The Astronomican]] shines from Terra and guides the fleets of the Imperium. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] returned to the system at the end of the [[Terran Crusade]], was received in the [[Imperial Palace]], and launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]] from Terra. On his order the moon Ganymede was made into Aquila Adamant, a hub-fortress that supplies and records the crusade, and his Throneworld Decree lets Terra and her sister worlds raise greater defence forces than before.

Mimas holds the Inquisition's prison complex, and Enceladus a citadel from which Lord Inquisitors of the [[Ordo Malleus]] hold court. The [[Phalanx]], the mobile star fortress of the [[Imperial Fists]], hangs in geostationary orbit above Terra.

The outer system is held by star forts garrisoned by [[Astra Militarum]] regiments, and by void mines, system monitors and hunter-servitors. The [[Adeptus Custodes]], among them the Solar Watch, patrol the system and vet every citizen who passes through the spaceports of Terra.

Government and defence

The Sol System is governed by the [[High Lords of Terra]], who sit in the [[Imperial Palace]]. Mars is governed by the [[Fabricator-General]] under the [[Treaty of Olympus]], and [[Titan Legions|Titan]] is the domain of the [[Grey Knights]]. The worlds and stations of the system are among the most closely administered in the Imperium, with every voyage into or out of the system logged and every ship inspected.

The system's defences include the ships of Battlefleet Solar, minefields, orbital battle stations, the fortress moons of the outer system, the Ring of Iron, and the garrisons of Terra. The [[Grey Knights]], the [[Inquisition]] and the Mechanicus each hold fortified bases of their own, and the Custodes are the keystone of the whole. Arriving ships must follow strictly controlled approach corridors.

Society

The populations of the Sol System are among the largest in the Imperium. Terra is a single city that holds hundreds of billions of people, and tens of billions more come each year on pilgrimage to the world their God-Emperor calls home. Pilgrims, merchants, adepts, refugees and envoys from every part of the Imperium arrive every solar day, and in the crowds rebellion and heresy can take root, which is why the Inquisition and the Custodes watch the system so closely.

To the faithful the whole system is holy, since it holds the Emperor on his [[Golden Throne]], and the journey to Sol is the greatest pilgrimage an Imperial citizen can make. To the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], Mars is the holier world. The relationship between Terra and Mars has defined the politics of the system since the [[Treaty of Olympus]].
