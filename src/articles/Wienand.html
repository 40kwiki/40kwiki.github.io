Wienand

Wienand was an [[Inquisitor]] who held the seat of the Inquisitorial Representative on the [[High Lords of Terra]] during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). He is recorded as a patron of the Imperial war effort against the [[Orks]], and as one of the men who founded the [[Deathwatch]] in the aftermath of that war.

Imperial records describe Wienand as a pragmatist. He was not a central figure in the war's campaigns, but his votes on the council mattered when the High Lords were split.

War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

When the [[Orks]] of the [[Beast]] reached the Imperium, the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] divided. Wienand joined the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Heth]] and the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] against the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]]. He had lost his formal seat to the [[Inquisitorial Representative]], but he attended the council regardless.

The Deathwatch

Main article: [[Deathwatch]]

In the middle of M32, after the war, the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Koorland]] founded the [[Deathwatch]] with Wienand's support. The Deathwatch was set up as a chamber of the [[Ordo Xenos]] to hunt alien threats, and its first fortresses were placed across the Imperium's frontier worlds.

Later nominations

Wienand later supported the nomination of [[Maximus Thane]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], in a council that also included [[Drakan Vangorich]]. The Imperial records give no other public acts for him after that council.
