The Gothic Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], north of [[Terra]] and on the border with the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It lies beside the [[Eye of Terror]], and its position made it the site of the twelfth [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], which is known as the [[Gothic War]] (139–160.M41). The sector is also notable for the six [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] that the Imperium found there, which are among the most powerful xenos artefacts ever recovered.

The sector's worlds are arranged in sub-sectors, each typically built around a Blackstone Fortress or a principal naval base. Its capital is [[Port Maw]], which is the principal naval base of the sector. The Gothic Sector remains one of the Imperium's most important frontier sectors on the spinward side of the Eye.Gothic sector records name Port Maw as the sector's capital. Other sources describe the capital as a matter of administrative convenience. The sub-sector names are taken from the sector survey.

History

Discovery

The worlds of the Gothic Sector were rediscovered during the [[Great Crusade]]. [[The Imperium]] found the [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] in the sector and, after study, transformed them into Imperial naval bases. The sector was thereafter governed as a naval province, and its fortresses were the chief reason for its importance to the [[Imperial Navy]].

The Gothic War

Main article: [[Gothic War]]

[[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s objective in the Gothic War was the capture of the Blackstone Fortresses. He seized three of the six, and used their power to strip one planet of its atmosphere and to turn a star into a supernova that destroyed its planetary system. A catastrophic [[Warp storm|warp storm]], which began in 142.M41, cut the sector off from outside communication and from reinforcements, and the sector's defence was left to the forces already in place.

The Imperium defeated the forces of [[Chaos]] in 160.M41, but Abaddon escaped into [[Warp|the warp]] with two of the fortresses. Imperial accounts record that these two were later used in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] of 999.M41, which was launched roughly eight hundred years later.

After the war

The sector was rebuilt in the decades after the Gothic War, but its fleets were thinned and its garrisons were rotated away to the wars of the Imperium. The Blackstone Fortresses that remained in Imperial hands were refitted, and the sector's worlds were resettled in the [[42nd millennium]], when the [[Great Rift]] opened and the sector's [[Warp travel|warp routes]] became unreliable.

Geography

The sector is organised around its [[Warp travel|warp routes]], and its worlds are grouped into [[Sub-sector|sub-sectors]] that are named for their principal systems. The Cyclops Cluster and the Gethsemane sub-sector lie nearest the Eye and are the most exposed. The Quinrox Sound is a region of nebulae and pulsars that interferes with [[Astropathy|astropathic communication]], and the Lysades sub-sector is the sector's oldest settled region.

The Orar Passage and the Port Maw sub-sector are the sector's principal routes into the wider [[Segmentum]]. The Bhein Morr sub-sector has been the site of several Blackstone-related incidents, and its records are partly sealed.

Notable worlds

[[Port Maw]] is the sector capital and the principal naval base of the sector. Its fleets form the core of the [[Imperial Navy|Battlefleet]] Gothic.

[[Bhein Morr]], [[Gethsemane]], [[Lysades]] and [[Orar]] are the names of the sector's [[Sub-sector|sub-sectors]], and each takes its name from its principal world or system. Their individual worlds are described in the sector survey.

Governance

The sector is governed by a [[Sector Governor]] who answers to the [[Administratum]] and the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The sector's naval command is based at [[Port Maw]], and the [[Imperial Navy|Battlefleet]] Gothic commander holds a seat in the sector's council. The Administratum's registers for the sector were partly lost in the [[Gothic War]] and have been rebuilt from surviving copies.

Military

The [[Imperial Navy]]'s Battlefleet Gothic is the sector's principal fighting force, and it is supported by regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised from the sector's worlds. The [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] are its most powerful assets. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Inquisition]] maintain a presence in the sector, and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] keeps its own forces to maintain the fortresses.
