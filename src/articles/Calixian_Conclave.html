The Calixian Conclave, also called the Ordos Calixis by common usage, is the conclave of the Holy Ordos of the [[Inquisition]] that operates in the [[Calixis Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. The Conclave is an alliance of the Ordos within the sector, and it answers to the wider Inquisition for matters that reach beyond the sector's borders.

The Conclave contains both Radicals and Puritans. The Radicals favour bold action and the use of alien technology against threats. The Puritans hold to a strict doctrine of purging all heresy and [[Xenos|xenos]] influence. Imperial accounts record that the two factions have often disagreed, and that their argument over methods has shaped the sector's policing for centuries.
