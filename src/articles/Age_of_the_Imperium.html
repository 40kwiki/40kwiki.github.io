The Age of the Imperium is the era of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that followed the [[Siege of Terra]]. The date of the siege is regarded as its beginning. The Imperium was left wrecked by the siege, and the era was shaped by the work of rebuilding it.

The Emperor sat on the [[Golden Throne]] after the siege, no longer able to speak or command. [[Rogal Dorn]] and [[Roboute Guilliman]] carried the work of rebuilding the Imperium in his name, and the era's institutions were set up in that period.

Institutions

The era produced the [[Codex Astartes]], which governs the organisation of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It saw the breaking up of the Legions in the [[Second Founding]], the rise of the [[Imperial Cult]], and the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. Each of these is recorded as a defining feature of the era.
