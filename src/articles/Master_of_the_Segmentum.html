The Master of the Segmentum is the civil official of the [[Administratum]] who governs one of the five [[Segmentum|segmenta]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from its [[Segmentum Fortress]]. The office is a deputy of the Administratum, not a naval rank. Its staff forms the command of the segmentum's civil affairs, and the name "Segmentum Command" is used for that staff in later usage.

The post is distinct from the naval command of the same segmentum. Each segmentum has a [[Lord High Admiral]] who leads its battlefleet, and in many records the Lord High Admiral is also styled a "Lord Admiral of the Segmentum". Where the two titles are used together, the Master governs the civil administration and the Lord High Admiral governs the fleet.

Office

Civil administration

The Master keeps the [[Segmentum|segmentum]]'s tithe registers, oversees the [[Sector Governor|sector governors]] and maintains the [[Administratum]]'s records for every world of the segmentum. The Master also sets the segmentum's returns to [[Terra]], which the [[High Lords of Terra]] use to assess the Imperium's resources.

Relations with the Navy

The Master has no authority over ships of the [[Imperial Navy]] in action, and the segmentum's fleets answer to the Lord High Admiral. The fortress is nonetheless shared by the two offices, and each one's rights within it are fixed by Administratum decree. Records of the late 41st millennium report frequent quarrels over docking rights, supplies and the repair of ships.Imperial accounts differ on who holds precedence within the fortress. Administratum records assign it to the Master, while Navy records assign it to the Lord High Admiral in all matters of fleet deployment.

Reporting

Each Master reports to the [[Master of the Administratum]] on Terra. The Master is expected to visit every sector capital of the segmentum in turn, but many such visits are recorded as incomplete.

Segmentum Masters

See also: [[Segmentum]]

The Imperial Decree of M31 that created the segmenta named the Master of each segmentum. The decree named Mars as the seat of the Segmentum Solar, and it required the Master of each segmentum to keep its fortress open to the [[Administratum]] at all times.
