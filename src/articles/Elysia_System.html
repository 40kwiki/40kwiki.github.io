The Elysia System is an Imperial [[star system]] in the north west of the [[Segmentum Solar]], about thirty light years from the [[Hive World]] of [[Armageddon]]. Its principal world is the idyllic [[Elysia]], home of the [[Elysian Drop Troops]] of the [[Astra Militarum]].

Geography

The system is young in galactic terms. Vast fields of debris and asteroids remain from its formation, and its many barren moons, gas clouds and asteroid fields make it a good ambush site for pirates. The surrounding wilderness space is notorious for the same reason.

History

The Elysian regiments became skilled at ship-to-ship boarding and at fighting alongside orbital support against isolated pirate bases. They are famed for their mobility, using [[Valkyrie]] assault carriers, rappelling lines and [[grav-chute]]s to strike behind enemy lines.

For a decade the system was blockaded by [[Orks]] under the [[Ork Kaptin]] Blacklaw and his Freebooterz. In 934.M41 the Elysian troops drove them from the Stranthium Orbital Space Docks, ending the blockade.
