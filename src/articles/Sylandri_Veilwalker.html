Sylandri Veilwalker is a Shadowseer of the Veiled Path among the [[Harlequins]], the Aeldari troupe that serves the god [[Cegorach]]. She leads the troupes of the Midnight Sorrow and the Frozen Stars, and she is recorded in Imperial intelligence as one of the more active Harlequin leaders of the late 41st millennium.

The Harlequins are the keepers of the Laughing God's secret purpose, and Veilwalker's role is to see the Webway routes and the Aeldari futures that the troupes must avoid. Her movements are rarely recorded in full.

Involvement

Veilwalker's name appears in Imperial intelligence on the Harlequins' movements in the late 41st millennium. Her part in the wider events of the period is not recorded in detail, and the Imperium's knowledge of the Harlequins comes largely from intercepted reports.Her activities are known only from intelligence summaries. The dates of her leadership are not recorded.
