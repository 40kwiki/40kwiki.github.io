The Tome Keepers are a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], raised in the [[Fourth Founding]] after the [[War of the Beast]]. They were ordered into being by [[Maximus Thane]], together with the [[Halo Brethren]] and the [[Sable Swords]].

The Fourth Founding was the last great reorganisation of the Chapters. It followed the upheaval of the War of the Beast, and it was carried out with the guidance of the Chapter Masters of the period.The number of Chapters raised in the Fourth Founding and their individual foundation dates are not recorded in the sources consulted.
