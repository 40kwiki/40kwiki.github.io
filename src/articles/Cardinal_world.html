A cardinal world is a planet of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] ruled directly by the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], the Ecclesiarchy of the [[Imperial Cult]]. Its [[planetary governor]] is a cardinal of the Ministorum, who often also heads the Church in the surrounding [[sector]]. Cardinal worlds are given over to the worship of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]: cathedrals, shrines and reliquaries may cover entire continents, and their populations are made up of clergy, devotees and pilgrims. They are the centres of the Ecclesiarchy's power outside [[Terra]] and serve as bases for the orders of the [[Adepta Sororitas]].

The class is recorded by the [[Administratum]] as a sub-class of [[Shrine world]]. The greatest cardinal world is [[Ophelia VII]] in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], whose spiritual importance is held by the Ecclesiarchy to be second only to Terra. In the Era Indomitus cardinal worlds have been among the chief targets of the traitor Legions, and the defence of [[Benediction]] in the [[Talledus War]] became one of the great Imperial victories of the [[Psychic Awakening]].

Government

A cardinal world is a theocracy. The cardinal rules both world and Church, appoints the officials of its government and commands its defences. Civil and religious law are one, and offences against the faith are treated as offences against the state. The [[Adeptus Arbites]] maintain a presence on most cardinal worlds, as do the Orders Militant of the Sororitas, who were forbidden after the [[Age of Apostasy]] (M36) to serve as the private army of any cardinal and answer instead to their own convents.

Cardinal worlds pay part of their tithe to the Ecclesiarchy rather than the Administratum, and the Ministorum also draws tithes from the worlds of the cardinal's diocese. This has made some cardinals very rich and has, at times, led to abuse. The [[Decree Passive]] and later reforms of the Ecclesiarchy were partly intended to limit the power of individual cardinals.

Society

Life on a cardinal world revolves around worship. The day is divided by services, the calendar by feasts and fasts, and pilgrims arrive by the shipload to visit holy relics and the tombs of saints. Much of the population works for the Church as clergy, servants, builders or makers of devotional objects. Preachers are trained on cardinal worlds before being sent to other planets, and the [[Schola Progenium]] often maintains institutions there.

History

Worlds governed by the Church existed from the early days of the Ecclesiarchy, which grew on Terra in the decades after the [[Horus Heresy]] (early M31). The power of cardinal worlds grew during the 35th millennium, when the Ecclesiarch Benedin IV moved the Holy Synod from Terra to Ophelia VII.The Synod's move is placed in M35 from its position in Ecclesiarchal histories before the Age of Apostasy; no exact year survives.

Cardinal worlds were at the heart of the [[Age of Apostasy]] in the early [[36th millennium]]. The High Lord [[Goge Vandire]] used the Ecclesiarchy's wealth and armies to rule the Imperium in the [[Reign of Blood]], and on [[Dimmamar]] the preacher [[Sebastian Thor]] led the [[Confederation of Light]] against him. Soon after, the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis of [[Gathalamor]] seized a great part of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] in the [[Plague of Unbelief]]. The reforms that followed, the [[Decree Passive]], required cardinals to acknowledge the authority of the Imperium and forbade the Ecclesiarchy to keep men under arms.

Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 the forces of [[Chaos]] have sought to destroy the holy places of the Imperium. In the early 42nd millennium the Dark Apostle [[Kor Phaeron]] led six traitor Legions against the [[Talledus System]], whose capital is the cardinal world of Benediction. The Adepta Sororitas, the [[Astra Militarum]] and several [[Space Marine Chapter]]s defended it in a war of several years, and the system was held.

Defence

Cardinal worlds are defended by their own [[Planetary Defence Force]]s and by the Frateris Militia, bands of armed faithful raised by preachers in times of crisis. The Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]] maintain convents and preceptories on many of them, and the greatest are guarded by whole Orders. Their populations, made fervent by constant worship, are known for fighting to the last in defence of their shrines. The traitor Legions value the desecration of a cardinal world as a blow to the faith of the whole Imperium, and in the Era Indomitus several have been attacked by the [[Word Bearers]] and their allies.
