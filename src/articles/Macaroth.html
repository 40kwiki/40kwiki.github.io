Macaroth was an Imperial [[Warmaster]] and the successor of [[Slaydo]] as commander of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]. He had distinguished himself as a young [[Astra Militarum]] commander at the end of the first decade of the Crusade, and he was named Slaydo's heir after Slaydo's death at [[Balhaut]] in 765.M41.

The Sabbat Worlds Crusade was a massive effort by the Imperium to retake the [[Sabbat Worlds]] in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] from the forces of Chaos in the late 41st millennium. Macaroth led it through the campaigns that followed Slaydo's death.

Command

Macaroth took command after the Imperial victory at Balhaut, which was won at the cost of Slaydo's life. The details of his later campaigns are recorded only in summary form.The details of Macaroth's campaigns are recorded in summary form. Their dates are estimated from the Crusade's registers.
