The Blood Drinkers are a [[Loyalist]] [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] and a [[Second Founding]] Successor of the [[Blood Angels]]. Their name comes from a craving for blood. This craving is a result of a mutated [[Omophagea]] organ in their [[gene-seed]]. Despite this name, the Chapter has an exemplary record and is widely considered one of the most honourable Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

The Blood Drinkers keep close company with other Chapters regarded as beyond reproach, among them the [[Crimson Fists]], the [[Novamarines]] and the [[Howling Griffons]]. Their [[Chapter Master]], [[Orloc]], fought beside his battle-brothers in the defence of the Blood Angels' homeworld of [[Baal]] against the forces of [[Chaos]] and the [[Tyranids]] in 999.M41.

Rites and the Flaw

What sets the Blood Drinkers apart is their willingness to take part in rites that involve the drinking of blood, sometimes in great quantities of [[Human]] blood. They do not hide these traditions. They practise them openly through ceremonies such as the ''Sanguis Excrucio'' and the Red Tears.

Many Imperial observers believe these rites help the Chapter resist the [[Red Thirst]] and the [[Black Rage]]. The Blood Drinkers do suffer fewer cases of the Black Rage among their battle-brothers than other Blood Angels descendants. Whether the rites are the cause, or merely a sign of the Chapter's control over the Flaw, is not settled. The Chapter's own leaders say that its acceptance of its nature has given it a unique degree of control. Its critics warn that this control may come at a greater cost.The link between the Blood Drinkers rites and their resistance to the Black Rage is disputed in Imperial records. The Chapter's own account is not independently confirmed.

History

The Blood Drinkers were founded in the early [[31st Millennium]], after the [[Horus Heresy]]. They were raised as a Second Founding Chapter before the truth about the flawed [[gene-seed]] of their progenitors came to light. Like their brother Successors, they are bound to the [[Blood Angels]] by the blood of [[Sanguinius]], which flows through their veins, and by ancient traditions that outsiders find hard to understand.

By the 37th millennium the Blood Drinkers were a dying Chapter, as battle-brothers succumbed to the Black Rage soon after their initiation. The hero-saint [[Holos]] left the Chapter Council against its will to search for a cure. Following a dream and the secret counsel of his [[Reclusiarch]] Shanadar, Holos climbed Mount Calicium, the highest volcanic peak on [[San Guisiga]]. There he fought [[Lo-tan]], lord of the astorgai, a vile [[xenos]] creature. The sources say the quest changed the Chapter's approach to the Flaw. Its exact details are recorded only in the Chapter's own accounts.
