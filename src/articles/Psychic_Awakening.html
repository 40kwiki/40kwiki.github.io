The Psychic Awakening, also called the Age of Witches, is the name given by the Imperium to the sharp rise in psychic activity across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41. The flood of [[Warp]] energy through the Rift made daemonic incursions stronger and longer-lasting, especially on worlds near it. It also caused new [[Psyker|psykers]] to appear in great numbers among humanity, strengthened the powers of existing psykers and of the [[Aeldari]], and gave new force to the miracles that the [[Imperial Cult]] attributes to the [[Emperor of Mankind]].

The phenomenon affected every power in the galaxy, and the term is used of the period as well as of the phenomenon itself. In the early [[Era Indomitus]] it shaped a series of wars, each of which turned in part on psychic power: the hunt for the Ynnari prophet [[Yvraine]], the [[Word Bearers]]' [[War of Faith]] on [[Benediction]], the war of the [[Blood Angels]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]], [[Magnus the Red]]'s gathering of psykers to the Prosperan Rift, and others. The [[Inquisition]] regards it as one of the gravest dangers of the age.

Nature

Imperial and [[Aeldari]] scholars agree that the Rift opened a wound through which the energies of the [[Warp|Immaterium]] bleed into [[Realspace|realspace]]. Where the bleed is strongest, the barrier between the two grows thin. Latent psychic potential, present in a minority of humans, has become active in far greater numbers. Children are born with powers, adults wake with them, and those who had them before find them grown. Without the [[Black Ships]] to gather them, many go untrained and unsanctioned, and every untrained [[Psyker|psyker]] is a door through which a [[Daemon|daemon]] can pass.

The same energies have made faith more potent. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] records manifestations of the Emperor as an angel, flocks of golden eagles, columns of holy fire that burn daemons and [[Heretic|heretics]] alike, and the appearance of saints at the moment of need. Among the [[Adepta Sororitas]] such signs have become common enough to shape the conduct of whole campaigns. The [[Inquisition]] does not agree on whether these phenomena are the Emperor's will, the collective psychic power of the faithful, or something else. The [[Ordo Hereticus]] regards the question as closed; the [[Ordo Malleus]] does not.

The Aeldari, who call the Rift the Dathedian, felt it most of all. Their seers report that their gifts have sharpened, and that the warriors of the Aspect Shrines fight with a new and terrible power. The disciplines of the Asuryani Path, designed to protect the mind from its own excesses, are believed to have spared the [[Craftworld|craftworlds]] the worst consequences.

War zones

The Aeldari and the hunt for Yvraine

The rise of the [[Ynnari]], the [[Aeldari]] sect devoted to the death-god [[Ynnead]], divided the Aeldari further. The [[Craftworld|craftworld]] of Biel-Tan, broken by the Ynnari's raising of the Yncarne, blamed them for the disaster, and the Supreme Overlord of [[Commorragh]], Asdrubael Vect, set bounties on Yvraine's head. [[Slaanesh]] released the [[Daemon|Keeper of Secrets]] [[Shalaxi Helbane]] from the Palace of Punishments to hunt her down. At a council of the Aeldari kindreds on the maiden world of [[Iathglas]], Helbane's daemons attacked and the quarrelling Aeldari were forced to fight together. Helbane was defeated, but revealed that its presence on Iathglas had been only a solid illusion, and that the last of the Croneswords Yvraine sought was already in the Palace of Slaanesh.

The Talledus War

In c. 999.M41 the [[Word Bearers]], with the [[Night Lords]], [[Iron Warriors]] and other [[Traitor Legions]], began a [[War of Faith]] against the [[Shrine world|shrine worlds]] of the Imperium, seeking to break the faith of its peoples by terror and sacrifice. Their greatest effort fell on the [[Talledus System]] and its shrine world, [[Benediction]], attacked by an armada of six Traitor Legions under the Dark Apostle [[Kor Phaeron]]. The Word Bearers nearly took the cathedral-city of the Grand Honorificum. Then the faith of those gathered in the cathedral called up from the Warp the spirits of the Imperium's honoured dead. This barrier, remembered as the Saints' Wall, surrounded the Honorificum and destroyed many of the daemons attacking it, allowing a demi-company of [[Salamanders]] to cut the enemy in two. On the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of Ghreddask a [[Black Templars]] strike force of the Rutherian Crusade destroyed the Soul Harvester fortress-factory at the cost of Castellan Dramos's life, and in the asteroid belt called the Tears of the Emperor [[White Scars]] of the 10th Brotherhood fought a void war against Night Lords raiders. The war continues.

War Zone Baal

When the Rift opened over the [[Devastation of Baal]], the defenders of [[Baal]] were weakened against the psychic voice of the [[Tyranids|Hive Mind]] just as unsanctioned [[Psyker|psykers]] began to appear among their thralls. The [[Blood Angels]]' [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]] [[Mephiston]], [[Commander Dante]] and [[Chapter Master]] [[Flesh Tearers|Gabriel Seth]] of the Flesh Tearers held the Sanguinary Brotherhood together, and with [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] reinforcements began to clear the surrounding systems.

The Prosperan Rift

After his attack on [[Fenris]], [[Magnus the Red]] summoned the [[Planet of the Sorcerers]] into [[Realspace|realspace]] above [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]], the ruined homeworld of the [[Thousand Sons]], and restored the world. He recalled the scattered warbands of his Legion and then called to the new psykers of the galaxy, promising them shelter from the persecution of the Imperium in the region now called the Prosperan Rift. The [[Ordo Malleus]] believes that he seeks to succeed where his father failed and make humanity a psychic species under his rule. The [[Dark Angels]] and the [[Grey Knights]], whose ranks swelled with new psychic recruits, formed an uneasy alliance to stop his rituals.

Other war zones

In the Chalnath Expanse of the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]], the [[T'au Empire]] under [[Commander Shadowsun]] and the [[Genestealer Cults]] fought for the loyalty of the Imperial population as much as for its worlds. [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] began to unite the Ork tribes into the greatest [[Waaagh!]] yet seen, and the [[Space Wolves]] under [[Ragnar Blackmane]] went out to meet it. On the fallen [[Forge world|forge world]] of Ordex-Thaag, cut off from [[Mars]], [[Chaos Knights]] tried to tear a new Warp rift with the techno-sorcery of its forges and were opposed by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and loyal [[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]].

In the War of the Spider, [[Fabius Bile]] stole the [[Death Guard]] relic called the Ark Cornucontagious and used it to reap [[Mutant|mutations]] around the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]]. He was pursued by [[Typhus]]'s plague fleet and by a force of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], the [[Sisters of Silence]] and Imperial Assassins, and escaped with Custodian and Silent Sister captives after the warring parties destroyed one another on Dessah.

Alone among these regions, the [[Nephilim Sector]] grew silent rather than louder. There the [[Necrons]] of the Silent King had built the [[Pariah Nexus]], an array that cut the region off from the Warp altogether.

Imperial response

The Imperium's institutions have struggled to meet the Awakening. The [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] has increased the tithe of [[Psyker|psykers]] demanded from each world, and the [[Black Ships]] sail in greater numbers, but cannot gather all who manifest. The [[Inquisition]] has expanded its purges, and the [[Ordo Malleus]] has warned that whole populations may need to be destroyed where unsanctioned psykers become too many to control. At the same time, the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]'s reforms have favoured the use of sanctioned psykers in the armies of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and the [[Librarian|Librarius]] of every Chapter has grown.
