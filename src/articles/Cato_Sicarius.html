Cato Sicarius is a hero of the [[Ultramarines]], for many years captain of the Chapter's Second Company, Master of the Watch and Knight Champion of [[Macragge]], and since 849.M41 Grand Duke of his homeworld of Talassar. Brilliant, proud and fearless, he is counted one of the finest swordsmen and commanders of his Chapter, though critics within it have long argued that his ambition costs the lives of his warriors.

Sicarius fought at Black Reach, against the [[Daemon|daemon prince]] M'kar the Reborn, and twice on the [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb world of Damnos. In 999.M41 he went with the resurrected [[Roboute Guilliman]] to [[Terra]] in the [[Terran Crusade]], and was then sent back to Ultramar with the first [[Primaris Space Marines]] of Guilliman's line, only to be lost in the Warp storms of the [[Great Rift]]. He returned, crossed the Rubicon Primaris, and in the [[Era Indomitus]] commands the Victrix Honour Guard.

Early life

Sicarius was born into the ducal household of Talassar, one of the leading noble dynasties of [[Ultramar]], and was trained in arms from infancy: by his fourth birthday he could wrestle, fence and shoot to his masters' satisfaction. The training masters of the Talassari agoge pressed him hard, seeing the glory he could bring their house, and he was accepted by the Ultramarines. As a captain he rose to Master of the Watch, and in 849.M41 he was named Grand Duke of Talassar and Suzerain of Ultramar.

As Knight Champion of [[Macragge]] he fought duels on the Chapter's behalf; in one, against the [[Heretic|heretic]] Orchaedes, he struck off the heretic's hand as it emerged from his robes holding a hidden pistol.

Black Reach and M'kar

In about 858.M41 Sicarius led an Ultramarines force in the Assault on Black Reach, which saved the world from the [[Orks]].Some Ultramarines rolls name Sicarius as captain of the Second Company at Black Reach. The company was then commanded by [[Demetrian Titus]], who held it from about 855.M41 until he was taken into custody in about 880.M41, so this encyclopedia dates Sicarius's captaincy of the Second Company from about 880.M41. A Terminator and twelve battle-brothers were killed, a toll that some in the Chapter council thought too high. In 878.M41 he first fought the [[Daemon|daemon prince]] M'kar, called the Reborn or Thrice-born, who had masterminded a [[Chaos]] raid; the enmity lasted more than a century. When Sicarius returned to [[Macragge]] in 999.M41 he found [[Ultramar]] under M'kar's attack, and with [[Marneus Calgar]] he defended Talassar until M'kar was slain and banished.Black Reach is dated from the statement that it took place about twenty years before 878.M41.

Damnos

In 974.M41 Sicarius led an assault on the [[Planetary classification|mining world]] of Damnos, which had been overrun by [[Necrons]] awakening from its tombs. He was to relieve Kellenport, the planetary capital and its last surviving city, while the [[Chief Librarian]] [[Varro Tigurius]] destroyed a Gauss pylon battery. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] could not hold the world. They evacuated what citizens they could and withdrew, and Damnos was declared a forbidden world. Sicarius was gravely wounded. The defeat changed him, and his courage afterwards often became recklessness.

In 999.M41, twenty-five years later, a [[Deathwatch]] team brought the decree of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and the [[Fabricator-General]] that the Ultramarines must return to Damnos and destroy the Necrons. [[Marneus Calgar|Calgar]] led five companies and elements of the Tenth, over five hundred [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]], to the world, and Sicarius trained for it so hard that he slept in the battle-cage. The Return to Damnos was a long and bitter campaign against an enemy that seemed to anticipate every move. In the same year Sicarius commanded the Imperial forces in the Fall of [[Medusa]] V, which ended in [[Exterminatus]], and led some thirty [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and [[Astra Militarum]] regiments in the Zeist Campaign against the [[T'au Empire]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] was restored at [[Macragge]] in 999.M41, Sicarius went with him to [[Terra]] in the [[Terran Crusade]]. Guilliman then sent him back to Macragge with a force of [[Primaris Space Marines]] of his own line, but the small fleet was caught in the Warp disturbances that marked the birth of the [[Great Rift]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]], and Sicarius's ship was lost. He eventually returned to Macragge and, at some time after, crossed the Rubicon Primaris. He now commands the Victrix Honour Guard, the elite guard of the [[Chapter Master]], while the title of Master of the Watch has passed to [[Demetrian Titus]].The length of his absence and the date of his ascension are not recorded.

Character

Sicarius is courageous, arrogant and certain of his destiny, and many believe that he intends one day to be [[Chapter Master]]. His warriors are fiercely loyal to him. His armour, the Mantle of the Suzerain, carries the honours of his career, among them the Victorex Alpha and the Eternium Ultra of the High Suzerain of [[Ultramar]].
