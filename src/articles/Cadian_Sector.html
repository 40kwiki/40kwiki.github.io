The Cadian Sector is a sector of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], to the galactic north of [[Terra]]. Its centre was the [[fortress world]] of [[Cadia]], the most important Imperial bastion of the Segmentum. Cadia guarded the only known navigable route, the [[Cadian Gate]], into and out of the [[Eye of Terror]]. The sector's worlds were the home of the [[Cadian Shock Troops]], one of the most famous regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The sector was largely lost in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] and the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41.

The sector's name is taken from Cadia, and its administration was always centred on the fortress world's Lord Castellan. Its neighbouring systems were the Cadia System's inner and outer worlds, which were tied to Cadia's defence. Imperial records are thorough for the sector's pre-war period, and many of its surviving documents are held in the sealed archives of the Administratum.

History

Pre-Heresy

Some forty standard years before the [[Horus Heresy]], Cadia was inhabited by a primitive people with violet eyes, who worshipped the four [[Chaos Gods]]. They were probably descended from humans who had turned to the [[Ruinous Powers]] during the [[Age of Strife]]. The primarch [[Lorgar]] of the [[Word Bearers]] visited the world during his pilgrimage to discover whether the gods of his homeworld's Old Faith were real, and the Imperial record of the visit comes from the 1301st Expeditionary Fleet of the [[Great Crusade]].

Post-Heresy

After the Heresy, Cadia was made into a fortress world, and its defences were built up over the following millennia. Its worlds were colonised and garrisoned under the reforms of [[Roboute Guilliman]]. By the 41st millennium, the Cadian Sector had become the Imperium's principal line against Chaos incursions from the [[Eye of Terror]].The dates of the Cadian garrison's organisation are not recorded precisely. The Heresy-era settlement is estimated from the order of the reforms.

Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In ca. 999.M41 [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] launched the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], the greatest Chaos assault since the Heresy. The attack began with the betrayal of the [[Volscani Cataphracts]] of the Astra Militarum, whose regiments assassinated the Cadian High Command, including the Lord Castellan. The colonel of the 8th Cadian Regiment, [[Ursarkar E. Creed]], was made Lord Castellan and contained the first assault. Abaddon then sent a second Chaos warfleet into the [[Cadian System]], and the planet was overrun.

The fall of Cadia broke the defences of the [[Cadian Gate]]. The pylons that held back the [[Eye of Terror]] were toppled, and the warp storm expanded into the [[Great Rift]]. Macharia was destroyed by Abaddon's warship [[Planet Killer]], and St. Josmane's Hope, the first of the inner worlds to fall, was destroyed by a strike-force of Adeptus Astartes and Inquisitors.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

With Cadia destroyed, the Cadian Sector was divided. The worlds of the Cadia System were lost, and the sector's neighbouring worlds held out for a time. Belis Corona and Agripinaa stood fast, and the defenders of the Cadian Gate fought on for the lost fortress world's sake. The Cadian Sector is now part of the wider struggle that the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has been fighting since 999.M41.

Notable worlds

  • '''[[Cadia]]''': the former lynchpin fortress world of the sector, destroyed in 999.M41.
  • '''[[Kasr Holn]]''' and '''[[Kasr Partox]]''': fortress worlds besieged during the Thirteenth Black Crusade.
  • '''[[Korolis]]''': an industrial world that produced weapons-grade materials.
  • '''[[Macharia]]''': a militarised hive world destroyed by the [[Planet Killer]] in 999.M41.
  • '''[[Pervigilium]]''': homeworld of the [[Angels of Vigilance]], just outside the Cadian Gate.
  • '''[[Prosan]]''': an uninhabited training world for the Astra Militarum.

Governance

The sector was governed by a planetary governor under the Lord Castellan, who commanded the Cadian forces from Cadia. The sector's worlds paid their tithe to the Administratum, and their fleets answered to the [[Imperial Navy]]. Inquisitorial conclaves kept watch on the Cadian Gate, and the [[Adeptus Arbites]] held its major cities.

Military

The Cadian regiments were among the best-known of the Astra Militarum. The [[Cadian Shock Troops]] were raised across the sector and fought in every major war on the Eye of Terror's frontier. Their defeat at Cadia, and the betrayal of the Volscani Cataphracts, is recorded as the point at which the sector's military structure broke down.
