Herman von Strab was the Overlord of [[Armageddon]], the planetary governor of the world. His failings in the defence of the planet allowed [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] to conquer the subcontinent of Armageddon Prime more easily than [[Angron]] had done in the First War. He is later recorded as a [[Traitor|traitor]] governor in the [[Third War for Armageddon]].

Imperial accounts describe von Strab as a governor more concerned with the planet's politics than its defence. His name is recorded in the Armageddon lists beside the governors of other Imperial worlds.

Ork invasion

In 941.M41 [[Ghazghkull Thraka|Ghazghkull]] invaded [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]] at the head of a massive [[Waaagh!]]. At that time the world was ruled by von Strab. His failings allowed the [[Orks]] to overrun the [[Hive world|hive city]] of Armageddon's great hives, and the subcontinent of Armageddon Prime was conquered more easily than it had been in the First War.

Third War for Armageddon

In the Third War, in 998.M41, von Strab is recorded as a [[Traitor|traitor]] governor who sided with the [[Chaos]] forces that attacked the world. The Third War is one of the best-recorded campaigns of the late [[41st millennium]], and the defence was led by [[Sebastian Yarrick]] and [[Dante]] of the [[Blood Angels]].
