Umberto II was an [[Ecclesiarch]] of the [[Imperial Cult]] in the [[39th millennium]]. He reasserted the authority of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] over the Imperium for the first time since the reign of [[Vandire]].

His predecessors include [[Deacis VI]], who spread the Sisterhood further in mid-M38 and founded the Orders of the Bloody Rose and the Sacred Rose, and [[Inovian III]], who canonised the heroes of the Redemption Crusades. Umberto's successor is not named in the sources consulted.
