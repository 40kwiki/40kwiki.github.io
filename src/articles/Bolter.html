The bolter, also called the boltgun, is a heavy hand-held weapon that fires small rocket-propelled shells, called bolts, which pierce a target and then explode inside it. It is the standard weapon of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and it is regarded in the Imperium as the arm that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] meant for his warriors. The bolt is a .75 calibre round with its own propellant, and a mass-reactive fuse that detonates it after it has penetrated armour or flesh. Its roar and its dull wet detonation are among the sounds the enemies of the Imperium know best.

The bolter has no one form. The Mechanicus has made hundreds of patterns of it, each with its own mechanism and fittings, and the Godwyn pattern of the Space Marines is based on ancient standard template construct technology from long before the Imperium. Smaller versions, called bolt pistols, are used as sidearms, and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] carry the longer bolt rifle. The weapon is far too heavy and recoils too hard for an unaugmented man, and it is made to answer only to the genetic code of a Space Marine. A citizen caught with a bolter or even a single bolt may be harshly sentenced by the [[Adeptus Arbites]]. A few patterns, such as the Godwyn-De'az, are made for mortal hands.

Design

A conventional firearm fires a bullet by the explosion of a charge in a case. A bolt carries its propellant in its body, and acts as a small rocket. The weapon uses two stages. A small charge forces the bolt out of the barrel at high speed and lights the rocket motor at the same moment, and the motor takes the bolt on to its target. This prevents the gas pressure of the motor from warping the barrel. The mass-reactive fuse, which is set to fire after the bolt has penetrated, makes the damage greatest inside the target.

A bolter of the standard [[Primaris Space Marines|Firstborn]] pattern is a large semi-automatic weapon without a stock, held like a pistol. A stock would be awkward in a [[Space Marine]]'s bulky armour, which is held to help absorb the recoil. It carries a sickle-shaped magazine, a counter that shows the number of bolts left, a biometric sensor in the grip that checks the user's genetic code, and an integral targeter that links to the auto-senses of the wearer's [[power armour]]. It can fire a burst of up to four bolts with one pull of the trigger.

The weapon needs far more care than a [[Lasgun|lasgun]]. The bolter is temperamental and demands regular servicing, and the Space Marines tend theirs with rites and prayers to its [[Machine spirit]], and treat it as an object of devotion. Its ammunition is heavy, and a Chapter on campaign uses a mountain of it. These are the reasons why the [[Astra Militarum]] does not issue it to ordinary soldiers.

History

Before the Crusade

The ancient [[Space Marine Legions]] once used volkite weapons, thermal ray guns of pre-Imperial origin that date back to the [[Age of Strife]]. They had great killing power, but they were difficult for even the best forges of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] to make, short in range and demanding to maintain.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The demands of the expanding Crusade in the late 30th millennium swiftly overwhelmed the supply of volkite weapons, and the Legions turned to the bolter. The Crusade pattern was the original bolter of the Legions. Several early patterns used other calibres: the Tigris pattern fired .60 calibre bolts, the [[Phobos]] pattern .70, and the Legions' Ikanos pattern of bolt pistol .50. The .75 calibre is now the standard. By the time the [[Horus Heresy]] began in 005.M31 the bolter had replaced volkite weapons in most of the Legions. The Umbra pattern was produced in large numbers early in the Crusade, and the Tigrus pattern appeared in its later years. Combi-weapons that joined a bolter to a plasma gun, melta gun or flamer were developed in the Heresy for the Terminators.

The Heresy and after

The [[Traitor Legions]] were issued their bolters before the Heresy, and their warriors therefore use older patterns than the Loyalists. The Mark Vb Godwyn pattern, the largest and most devastating of the Firstborn bolters, has served the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] for many millennia and fills the role the [[Codex Astartes]] gives to the sacred bolter, the primary weapon of a battle-brother. Many Chapters keep their own versions of it, such as the Angelus pattern of the [[Blood Angels]], a wrist-mounted, drum-fed bolter, and the Fenris pattern of the [[Space Wolves]].

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The Primaris Space Marines brought a new family of bolt weapons. [[Belisarius Cawl]] designed the [[Power armour|Mark II]] bolt rifle, called the Cawl pattern, which has a longer range and slightly more penetrating power than the Godwyn and is built to be modular. Its relatives include the auto bolt rifle, the stalker bolt rifle, the bolt carbine of the Reivers and the occulus bolt carbine of the Incursors, the Mark III Shrike bolt sniper rifle of the Eliminators and the executor bolt rifle of the Heavy Intercessors. The Reivers also carry the heavy bolt pistol. The Godwyn is still the common weapon of the Firstborn, and the two systems are used together in many Chapters.

Ammunition

The standard bolt is the mass-reactive round described above. The armouries also keep a large range of special bolts, of which the best known are the kraken round, which has an adamantium core for piercing heavy armour; the inferno bolt, which carries burning promethium; the hellfire round, which fills a target with mutagenic acid; the vengeance round, which has a volatile flux core; and the dragonfire round, which explodes in a gout of superheated gas. The [[Adeptus Arbites]] punishes the possession of even a single bolt by a citizen with a severe sentence.
