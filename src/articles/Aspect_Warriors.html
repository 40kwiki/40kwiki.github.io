Aspect Warriors are the specialist fighters of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]], the [[Asuryani]]. Each one has given a lifetime to a single style of combat, from close melee to long-range fire, and each style is organised as an Aspect Shrine. A warrior who joins a shrine trains under its [[Exarch]]s until they embody that aspect of the war god [[Kaela Mensha Khaine]]. The system is the core of the Asuryani way of war.

The shrines are sustained by the [[Path of the Warrior]]. Asuryani who feel called to it leave their craftworld duties and enter a long apprenticeship. Imperial accounts describe this path as a discipline with the intensity of a religious vow. Every craftworld keeps at least some shrines, but some, notably [[Biel-Tan]], keep more than others and place greater weight on the Path of the Warrior in their society.

Organisation and roles

Each shrine is named for its aspect and takes its battlefield role from that aspect. The [[Dire Avengers]] are the most common and act as the general combat core of an Asuryani force. The [[Howling Banshees]] and [[Striking Scorpions]] close with the enemy in melee, the Scorpions often by stealth. The [[Dark Reapers]] carry heavy long-range weapons for fire support, and the [[Fire Dragons]] carry melta weapons to destroy armour and fortifications at short range.

Mobility is the aim of the rest. The [[Swooping Hawks]] fly with jet packs and strike from the air, and the [[Shining Spears]] use jetbikes for hit-and-run attacks. The [[Warp Spiders]] carry a Warp jump generator that lets them step through the [[Immaterium]] and reappear across the battlefield. Imperial records warn that this jump is dangerous, as it may draw the attention of [[Daemon]]s. Aeldari air power is carried by the [[Crimson Hunters]] and the [[Eagle Pilots]], both of which fly as Aspect pilots.

Phoenix Lords

Each shrine is led by an [[Exarch]], a warrior who can no longer leave the Path and who dons an ancient suit of Exarch armour that bonds to the flesh. The soul of the new Exarch merges with those of earlier wearers held in the suit's [[Spirit Stone]]s. The first founder of an aspect is called a [[Phoenix Lord]]. The Phoenix Lord of the Dire Avengers is [[Asurmen]], the Phoenix Lord of the Howling Banshees is [[Jain Zar]], and the Phoenix Lord of the Warp Spiders is [[Lhykis]]. Asurmen founded the first Aspect Shrine, and the first Exarchs he taught went on to found the other aspects. The Phoenix Lord of the Crimson Hunters is unknown.
