Velk'Han is the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] sept of the [[Black Reef]], held by the T'au in the [[Jericho Reach]]. The [[Canis Salient]] opened the Greyhell Front in 785.M41 against the T'au-held domains of the Velk'Han Sept.

The crusade has been fought over three fronts since. It has been met by the [[Hadex Anomaly]], the Chaos domains of the Reach, and the newly arrived [[Hive Fleet Dagon]], which seeks to consume every inhabited world in the Reach.
