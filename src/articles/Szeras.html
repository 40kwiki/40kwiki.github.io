Szeras is an [[Illuminor]] of the [[Necrons]], the Necron class of technomancers who design and maintain the Necron war machines and their tombs. He is named in Imperial records as the architect of the [[Pariah Nexus]], the psychic and [[Warp]] structure that formed the centre of the [[Nephilim War]] in the [[Nephilim Sector]].

Imperial accounts describe Szeras as one of the more active Illuminors of the [[Era Indomitus]]. His work is known to the Imperium mainly through the technology it leaves behind, which the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has studied with great caution.

The Pariah Nexus

Main article: [[Pariah Nexus]]

The Pariah Nexus was a structure built in the [[Nephilim Sector]] during the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. It drew on the Warp in ways the Imperium's lore-keepers had not recorded before, and the war fought around it is known as the Nephilim War. Szeras is named as its architect in the Indomitus records, though the records are not in agreement about the details of his role.

Notes on his work

The Adeptus Mechanicus holds that Illuminors work through a process of reconstruction, rebuilding older Necron designs as needed. Szeras's role in the Pariah Nexus is one of the clearest records of that process in the Era Indomitus.
