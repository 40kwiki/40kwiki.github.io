House Cadmus is an [[Imperial Knight]] House that formerly had strong ties to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] as part of the Questor Mechanicus. It now owes allegiance directly to the Imperium as a House of the Questor Imperialis. The House comes from the forested Knight World of [[Raisa]], far to the galactic south. Its Knights have accepted the Imperium only as equals and never considered themselves wholly bound to the dictates of the [[High Lords of Terra]].

The first [[Rogue Trader]] to set foot on Raisa was given a cold welcome by its nobles. It was many Terran years before the first alliances were made. The nobles were equally reluctant when they were forced to swear oaths to the [[Forge world|Forge World]] of [[Gryphonne IV]], in return for skilled sacristans and the old technologies of the Mechanicum.

Relations with the Mechanicus

See also: [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]

The destruction of Gryphonne IV freed House Cadmus from its ancient obligations to the [[Tech-priest]]s. The current High King embraced the change of allegiance. His Knights now go to war whenever and wherever the lord of the House wishes. Imperial accounts describe this as a change of master, not a rejection of the Imperium.

Raisan history and the Cull

Raisa was isolated from the rest of Mankind for thousands of Terran years. For centuries, only the Knights protected the people from the creatures of its forests. The most feared of these were the golems, living constructs of wood and stone, animated by strange energies from the void. A golem could punch through the armour of a Knight with its granite fists.

The yearly event known as the Cull has been central to Raisan society for millennia. In the Cull the nobles compete to eradicate the [[Abhuman]] vermin that infest the homeworld. The contest hones the skills that the Knights later use against the greater threats of the Imperium.

Wars

House Cadmus has always answered the Imperium's call to war. Its fighting strength was badly hurt in the defence of Gryphonne IV. The Legio Gryphonicus, the Titan Legion of the Forge World, was overwhelmed by [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] in 997.M41. House Cadmus supported the Legion in that defence, and it lost many of its Knights.
