Andros Launceddre was the sixteenth Chief Custodian and [[Captain-General]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], the predecessor of [[Trajann Valoris]]. He came to command after [[Galahoth]] disappeared, in the Years of Madness, and died in the engagement the Custodes' chronicles call the Battle of Black Pyre, known elsewhere as the Battle of the Gilded Pyre.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

The doomscryers

The Palace kept psychic prognosticators, the doomscryers, to read what threatened the Emperor. During this period possession was found among a sub-sect of them. Their predictions were false, and they sent the [[Captain-General]] to his death.

Black Pyre

Launceddre was killed at the Battle of Black Pyre. The open archives record no more of the engagement. [[Trajann Valoris|Valoris]], who followed him, at once moved to restore firm control of [[Terra]]'s defences.
