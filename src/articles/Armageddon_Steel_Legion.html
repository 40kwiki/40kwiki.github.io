The Armageddon Steel Legion is the name given to the mechanised infantry regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised on the [[Hive world]] of [[Armageddon]]. Its soldiers ride to war inside [[Chimera]] transports and are best known for their long defence of their homeworld against the [[Orks]] of [[Ghazghkull Thraka]].

Mechanised regiments are rare in the Astra Militarum because few worlds can build and keep so many armoured vehicles. Armageddon builds Chimeras in vast numbers, so a high share of its regiments are Steel Legions.

Origins and recruitment

The resources for large formations of vehicles are hard to find even for [[planetary governor]]s, and they usually require an agreement with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], whose [[Tech-priest]]s and [[Enginseer]]s keep them running. Armageddon avoids the difficulty: its manufactoria turn out [[lasgun]]s, tanks and war machines of exemplary quality, and the demand for them has made the world an industrial giant.

The air beyond the hive cities is poisoned by centuries of industry, and a person breathing it for long would see their lungs rot. Even so, Armageddon is crowded with people. At the height of the Second War, it raised more regiments in a standard year than any two other worlds of the Segmentum Solar combined.

Many recruits come from the underhive, where gang wars select for the ruthless. Whole gangs are sometimes conscripted together and need little further training. Some such squads excel at inventive small-unit tactics.

Tactics and equipment

Steel Legion regiments redeploy convoys quickly. Often the vehicles simply overrun the enemy front line, and the infantry dismount to sweep the ground clear. They are skilled at fighting Orks and at assaults in hive cities, factory zones and chemical sumps.

Every soldier wears a toxin-resistant trenchcoat, gloves and visor, and carries an Armageddon pattern rebreather good for about two hours of air. Senior officers often wear rebreathers shaped like a grinning skull, to unsettle the superstitious Orks. Their vehicles are sealed against the ash wastes, and regimental banners are coated against acid decay.

Standard issue is the M36 pattern lasgun, flak armour, a mono-knife and grenades. Heavy weapons are grenade launchers and missile launchers, and the regiments are supported by Chimeras and Leman Russ Executioner tanks, sometimes with conscripted planetary defence troops and [[Ratling]] snipers.

Campaigns

Cassell Rebellion

In 776.M36 the Armageddon 16th Steel Legion, then stationed on the agri-world of [[Cassell]], put down the Cassell Rebellion. The planetary governor, Supreme Pontiff Skalin, had renounced the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and called for a crusade to spread his cult, the Way of the Emperor's Flesh. Colonel Kleist moved with his regiment already mobilised. Chimeras rolled on Port Cassell within a solar hour, and the Pontiff was captured. The whole rising lasted less than six solar hours.

Wars for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

The [[First War for Armageddon]] in 444.M41 was a daemonic invasion whose records the [[Inquisition]] has largely purged. In 941.M41 the Warboss Ghazghkull invaded, and the Steel Legions held with [[Commissar]] Yarrick at Hive Hades until reinforcements reached the world. More than fifty years later, in 998.M41, Ghazghkull returned with allies. The Steel Legions fought at Hive Helsreach, Hive Tempestora and Hive Acheron, where they are said to have lost fewer soldiers in their first hour than other regiments.

Spinward Front

Steel Legion regiments have also been sent to the [[Spinward Front]] in the [[Calixis Sector]], where they were mustered at [[Cyclopea]] and sent on to fight feral Orks on Ganf Magna.

Notable regiments

  • The 16th, "Tank Stormers": the Cassell Rebellion (776.M36).
  • The 339th, "Iron Heads": fought in the tank forges of Hive Tempestora in the Third War.
  • The 273rd, "Steel Vultures": an armoured regiment at Helsreach in the Third War.
  • The 81st: a rare assault regiment that made a costly orbital landing on Hive Infernus in the Second War.
  • The 8th Heavy Tank Company: a super-heavy company with Stormsword tanks in the Third War.

Prominent soldiers include Yarrick, Kleist, and Major Pious Korren, "Hero of Anthrand", who destroyed a column of Ork vehicles on the Anthrand Plain in 998.M41.
