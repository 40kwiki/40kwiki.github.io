The Battle of Mount Ararat was the last battle of the [[Unification Wars]]. It was fought in the Kingdom of [[Urartu]] on [[Terra]], and it closed the campaign that united the planet under the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. The battle is known mainly for the fate of the [[Thunder Warriors]] who fought in it.

The Imperial account

The Imperial historical chronicles say that the remaining Thunder Warriors were slain to a man at Mount Ararat. According to the chronicles, the famed Thunder Warrior [[Arik Taranis]] raised the Banner of Lightning at the final declaration of Unity, which established the Emperor's rule over all of Terra. He died of his wounds soon after. For centuries the Thunder Warriors' death was taught as the heroic price of the final victory.

Later accounts

Later testimony tells a different story. The Thunder Warriors were not destroyed in battle. They had been bred for war, and their enhanced bodies failed within a few decades of transformation, so they could not be allowed to outlive the conflict. According to this account, the Emperor ordered them culled, and the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and the newly made I Legion carried out the killing.

A further account holds that the Thunder Warriors turned on their creator before the wars ended, after they learned their short lives were the result of his flawed augmentation. In this version, a cadre of several hundred Custodians put the rebellion down. Imperial scholars do not agree on which account is correct.

Legacy

The early regiments that survived the Unification Wars were later called the "Old Hundred". They formed the core of the force that began the [[Great Crusade]] beside the new [[Space Marine Legions]].
