A Standard Template Construct, commonly STC, was an intelligent database built during the [[Dark Age of Technology]] that held the designs for every kind of machine human colonists might need, from simple tools to vehicles, weapons, shelters and starship components. Colony fleets carried STC systems so that settlers could build what they needed on any world, from local materials and without expert knowledge. Some systems could design new machines to meet new conditions.

No complete STC is known to be in the possession of the Imperium. Almost all were lost or destroyed in the [[Age of Strife]], and what survives are fragments, copies and print-outs, many corrupted by millennia of copying. To the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] these are the most sacred of relics, gifts of the [[Omnissiah]] to humanity, and the recovery of even a fragment is an event of great importance. Much of the standard equipment of the Imperium, including the [[Rhino]], the [[Land Raider]] and the [[Leman Russ (tank)|Leman Russ]], is built to patterns recovered from STCs.

The search for STCs drives much of the activity of the Mechanicus: its [[Explorator|explorator]] fleets, its technoarchaeologists and its alliances with [[Rogue Trader]]s. Because the Cult Mechanicus holds that all true knowledge was revealed in the past, a new design is acceptable only if it can be shown to derive from an STC. This doctrine shapes the Imperium's attitude to technology and has made innovation a matter of suspicion.

Origins

STC systems were created in the period of humanity's greatest expansion, when colony fleets left [[Terra]] and [[Mars]] to settle [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. Each fleet carried an STC so that its colonists could build shelters, power plants, transports and farming machines without technical training. The user asked for what was needed, and the system provided schematics in hololithic form, adapted to the materials at hand. The designs were robust and simple to repair rather than refined. Some Imperial historians believe that the makers of the STCs foresaw the coming collapse and built them to preserve human knowledge through it.

Most STCs held only the knowledge needed for an ordinary colony: farm machinery, simple aircraft and ground vehicles. Far fewer contained the designs for advanced devices such as [[Warp travel|warp engines]] or the war machines that colonists on dangerous worlds used. Among these were the Knight suits, which became the [[Imperial Knights]] of the [[Knight world]]s.

Loss

The war with the thinking machines known as the [[Men of Iron]], followed by [[Warp|the warp]] storms of the [[Age of Strife]], destroyed the interstellar civilisation of the Dark Age. Isolated colonies lost the ability to maintain their STCs, which failed, were destroyed in wars or were forgotten. On many worlds the surviving designs were debased over generations into crude copies. By the time of the [[Great Crusade]] no working STC system was known on any human world.

Recovery and doctrine

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] has searched for STCs since the [[Age of Strife]]. Its technoarchaeologists study ancient archives on [[Mars]], its [[Explorator|explorator]] fleets search lost worlds, and it rewards those who bring it fragments. The Martian technoarchaeologist [[Arkhan Land]] recovered the templates for the [[Land Raider]] and the Land Crawler from the [[Librarian|Librarius]] Omnis of Mars in M30, and his work on anti-gravitic plates led to the Land Speeder. Other recovered fragments produced the [[Rhino]] and the Leman Russ, which tradition holds was named by the [[Space Wolves]] for their [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Leman Russ]] during the Great Crusade.

[[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] requires that STC finds be surrendered to the Mechanicus, though this has not always been obeyed. The [[Blood Angels]] are said to have kept the designs of the Baal Predator, found during the Great Crusade, on their homeworld of [[Baal]]. The [[Dark Angels]] have recovered several fragments of their own and keep them within their Chapter.

Some recoveries have caused wars. Rumours of an STC have sent Mechanicus fleets across the galaxy and led to campaigns of decades, such as the War of Recovery that began in 104.M36. Fragments recovered in the Imperium are often incomplete, and the Mechanicus may judge a design too dangerous or too difficult to reproduce. A damaged STC core held by [[House Van Saar]] of [[Necromunda]] since M35 is said to have poisoned its keepers with radiation.

The doctrine of the Mechanicus holds that the STC represents the perfect knowledge of the Omnissiah, and that it is sacrilege to alter a design or to invent anew. Changes are permitted only as recovered knowledge. Tech-priests who innovate openly risk being condemned as [[Heretek|hereteks]], and disputes over whether a new design truly derives from an STC have divided the priesthood.

In the Era Indomitus

The [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]], creator of the [[Primaris Space Marines]] and of much of the new equipment of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], has been accused by rival magi of innovation. Cawl's supporters, and Cawl himself, maintain that his designs derive from recovered fragments and from knowledge preserved since the [[Horus Heresy]]. The question remains disputed within the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]].

The [[Leagues of Votann]], encountered again in strength in the late [[41st millennium]], are believed to preserve Dark Age knowledge of their own, which the Mechanicus covets. The [[Daemon|daemon]] of the forge [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]], active since the early [[42nd millennium]], has also sought ancient technology across the galaxy.
