The Shield Chapters of Ultramar are a group of nine [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] Chapters, all successors of the [[Ultramarines Legion]]. [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and [[Imperial Regent]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] charged them in the [[Era Indomitus]] with defending the [[Realm of Ultramar]] permanently and restoring it to its pre-[[Horus Heresy]] size of five hundred worlds. The [[Ultramarines]] are counted as the tenth member of the group, and they lead it and coordinate its actions.

Each Shield Chapter is therefore a defender of one part of the realm, while the Ultramarines coordinate the whole.

Organisation

Each Shield Chapter was granted full command and political control of one world within Ultramar. That world served as its base of operations and as its area of responsibility in defending that part of the realm. The [[Praetors of Ultramar]] secured their own Chapter homeworld, [[Howsbridge]]. The [[Scythes of the Emperor]] maintain their [[fortress-monastery]] on [[Sotha]], and the [[Avenging Sons]] were granted the Ultramarian world of [[Callimachus]].

The Ultramarines themselves were expected to secure their homeworld of [[Macragge]] and to serve as the leading Chapter in coordinating the actions of the other nine in defending and expanding Ultramar. The remaining members are the Aquiloan Brotherhood, the Doom Eagles, the Novamarines, the Silver Skulls, the Sons of Orar and the Reborn.

Membership and origins

The ten Chapters of the group are attested in the honour guard of [[Decimus Felix]], Tetrarch of [[Vespator]]. In Dark Imperium: Godblight, Felix explains that each member of his Chosen of Vespator Honour Guard came from one of the ten Shield Chapters of Ultramar. In Belisarius Cawl: The Great Work, Felix lists the name of each of his Chosen and the Chapter from which each came. In the revised edition of Dark Imperium, Roboute Guilliman names several of the worlds of Ultramar that the Chapters were granted as bases.
