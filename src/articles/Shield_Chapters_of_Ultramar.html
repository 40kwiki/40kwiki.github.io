The Shield Chapters of Ultramar are a group of nine [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] Chapters, all successors of the [[Ultramarines Legion]]. [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and [[Imperial Regent]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] charged them in the [[Era Indomitus]] with defending the [[Realm of Ultramar]] permanently and restoring it to its pre-[[Horus Heresy]] size of five hundred worlds. The [[Ultramarines]] are counted as the tenth member of the group, and they lead it and coordinate its actions.

Each Shield Chapter is therefore a defender of one part of the realm, while the Ultramarines coordinate the whole.

Organisation

Each Shield Chapter was granted full command and political control of one world within Ultramar. That world served as its base of operations and as its area of responsibility. Some Chapters were given the world on which they would build a homeworld of their own. The Praetors of Ultramar, for example, secured their own homeworld, [[Howsbridge]]. The Scythes of the Emperor maintain their [[fortress-monastery]] on [[Sotha]], and the Avenging Sons were granted the Ultramarian world of [[Callimachus]].

The Ultramarines themselves were expected to secure their home world of [[Macragge]] and to coordinate the other nine Chapters. In that role they defend and extend the realm together with the Shield Chapters. The member Chapters include the [[Aquiloan Brotherhood]], the [[Doom Eagles]], the [[Novamarines]], the [[Silver Skulls]], the [[Sons of Orar]] and the [[Reborn]].

Membership and origins

The members of the group are recorded in the honour guard of [[Decimus Felix]], Tetrarch of [[Vespator]]. In the novel Dark Imperium: Godblight, Felix explains that each member of his Chosen of Vespator Honour Guard came from one of the ten Shield Chapters of Ultramar. A later account, in Belisarius Cawl: The Great Work, lists the names of each Chosen and the Chapter from which each was drawn.
