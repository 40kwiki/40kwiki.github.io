Realspace, also called the Materium, is the material universe: the four-dimensional region of space and time that mortal beings inhabit and measure. It is the plane of existence of stars, planets, matter and the physical forces, and it behaves according to laws that the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the savants of every species can in principle observe. Imperial scholars set it against the [[Warp]], also called the [[Immaterium]], the realm of psychic energy and of [[Daemon|daemons]] that underlies the physical universe and presses against it from every side.

The Warp mirrors realspace and at the same time pervades it, as an unseen mist might. It is shaped by the emotions and thoughts of every sentient being, and since mankind is the most numerous intelligent species of the 41st millennium, its tides are shaped most strongly by the human race. The entities that dwell there long to cross into realspace to feed on the souls of mortals. Where the barrier between the two realms thins or fails, the result is a [[Warp storm]], a [[Warp rift]], a [[Daemon world]] or a possession.

Travel within realspace cannot exceed the speed of light, so a voyage between stars would take years or centuries. Every starfaring civilisation therefore moves through the Warp, where the geometry of space is not fixed, or through the [[Webway]] of the [[Aeldari]] and the dolmen gates of the [[Necrons]]. The [[Imperium of Mankind]] relies on the [[Navis Nobilite|navigators]] who steer its ships in the Warp and on the light of the [[Astronomican]] that guides them. In the Era Indomitus the barrier between the realms has been strained as never before, and the Imperium Nihilus is cut off from the light of Terra by the [[Great Rift]].

Nature of realspace

Realspace is subject to the natural laws that humanity has long known: gravity, electromagnetism, and the strong and weak nuclear forces. These laws build the elements of matter, the stars and the worlds. The physical universe does not respond to belief, and a weapon, a wall or a star behaves in the same way whatever its observers think. This stability is what allows the technologies of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and its enemies to work, and it is the reason that the [[Gellar field]] of a starship is so valued. The field is a barrier that protects a ship's crew and the ship itself from the Warp, and it is the one reliable defence against its denizens.

The Imperium treats realspace as the domain of the living, of the soldier, the governor and the factor, and the Warp as the domain of the psyker, the navigator and the sorcerer. The two interact, because [[Psyker|psykers]] draw power from the Warp and the Warp is fed by the souls of the living, but they are not equal. In Imperial doctrine the material world is a wall that must be defended, and the Warp is a flood.

Realspace and the Warp

Crossing between realms

A ship leaves realspace by a warp jump and returns at a planned point. Imperial navigators speak of "crossing into" the Warp and of "returning" to realspace. In the Warp, a journey of hundreds of light years may take a short time, because the Warp does not follow the geometry of realspace, but the journey is dangerous. Ships that cannot be steered in the Warp, or that leave it without a navigator, may emerge at the wrong point or not at all. A [[Space hulk]], a drifting mass of wrecked ships, can enter and leave the Warp without warning, and its destination cannot be forecast.

Thin places

Certain regions of the galaxy are scarred by past catastrophes and the barrier there is permanently thin. The [[Eye of Terror]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] opened when the Aeldari empire fell and the Chaos God [[Slaanesh]] was born, in the 30th millennium. It has since been the base of the Traitor Legions. The [[Maelstrom]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]] is another permanent storm. In these regions the nature of space itself is changed, and ships that enter them are rarely seen again.

The Ruinstorm

The Warp has also been used as a weapon. In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the Chaos powers raised a vast storm around [[Ultramar]] and, according to later Imperial reports, across much of the galaxy, which isolated [[Terra]] from the loyalist Legions and made the [[Astronomican]] fail. The storm, called the Ruinstorm, shut [[Roboute Guilliman]] and the [[Ultramarines]] away from Terra for the opening years of the war and subsided only as the Heresy ended. It remains the most recorded example of a Warp storm raised by design.

The Great Rift

Main article: [[Great Rift]]

In c. 999.M41 the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]], also called the Great Rift, opened across the galaxy from the Eye of Terror in the west to the [[Hadex Anomaly]] in the east. It divided the Imperium into two halves, the Imperium Sanctus and the [[Imperium Nihilus]], and made the barrier between the realms thinner than it has been since the fall of the Aeldari. Warp entities cross into realspace more easily, the powers of psykers have grown and become less stable, and the events of the following years are named the [[Psychic Awakening]] by the Imperial scholars.

In regions held by the servants of the Chaos powers the two realms have begun to merge. In the [[Scourge Stars]], held by the servants of [[Nurgle]], the environment has started to take on the nature of the Warp, as it long has within the Eye of Terror and the Maelstrom. Imperial savants warn that if the power of Chaos cannot be contained, the incursions into realspace will worsen, and that in the end the nature of reality itself may be threatened.

Realspace in practice

Distance and time

The scale of realspace shapes every Imperial institution. A message sent by lightspeed would take thousands of years to cross the galaxy, so the Imperium relies on the [[Astropath]]s of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] to send words through the Warp, and on its fleets to carry people and cargo. Even so, a ship's passage between worlds is measured in weeks or months of Warp time, and a planet may be out of contact for years. This delay is the reason the Imperium governs by [[Planetary governor|planetary governors]] and [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] with wide powers, and why the rule of Terra is felt differently in each segmentum.

Hazards of realspace

Dangers in realspace are of an ordinary kind: the vacuum, radiation, collisions, the gravity of stars and the guns of enemies. A fleet in orbit above a world is as vulnerable as any other force, and the [[Imperial Navy]] uses void shields and long-range weapons to guard its ships. Warp-born hazards also reach realspace, in the form of possession, a daemonic incursion or the arrival of a Space hulk. Imperial records show that a world which is quiet for centuries may in a single day be overrun by something that crossed from the other realm.

Other powers and realspace

The other species of the galaxy have their own relations with the boundary. The Aeldari built the Webway as a path between worlds that is neither realspace nor the Warp, and the Necrons built the dolmen gates to move their forces through realspace without crossing the Warp. The [[T'au Empire]] has no psykers and travels by Warp drives that its own engineers have built, and the [[Orks]] cross by fleets of crude vessels that somehow reach their destinations. The [[Tyranids]] cross the gulfs between galaxies in the darkness of realspace itself, sustained by hibernation, and are the most conspicuous exception to the rule that the Warp is the highway of the galaxy.

In Imperial doctrine

The [[Imperial Cult]] teaches that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] holds back the Warp from the soul of mankind through His sacrifice on the [[Golden Throne]], and that the light of the Astronomican is a reflection of His will. The Mechanicum, for its part, regards realspace as the domain of the [[Omnissiah]] and the Warp as a source of corruption that must be kept out of every machine. [[Tech-priest]]s refer to the barrier between the two as a matter of engineering. The [[Grey Knights]] and the [[Inquisition]] regard the defence of the boundary as their charge.
