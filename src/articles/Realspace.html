Realspace is the term the Imperium uses for the material universe, the stars, planets and voids that can be observed and travelled through by ordinary means. It is set against the [[Warp]], the realm of psychic energy and [[Daemon|daemons]] through which starships move faster than light. Imperial [[Navis Nobilite|navigators]] speak of "crossing into" or "returning to" realspace when a ship leaves the Warp.

The distinction is basic to Imperial navigation and to the [[Imperial Navy]]'s doctrine. A ship that is not in the Warp is in realspace, and most ships are only ever in realspace while in orbit or on station.

Realspace and the Warp

Ships pass between the two by a [[Warp travel|warp jump]], which takes them out of realspace and returns them at a planned point. The passage is governed by the [[Astronomican]] and by the navigators of the [[Navis Nobilite]] and the [[Imperial Navy]]. Most Imperial records treat the boundary as fixed, though some accounts argue it is thinner in certain regions.

A [[Space hulk]] can exit and re-enter the Warp without warning. Its jump direction is random, and a hulk far from a star system may arrive at a point of the Imperium's choosing only by accident. Realspace is therefore the place where such hazards are observed and recorded.
