The 37th millennium (M37) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M37 to 999.M37. It is the core of the [[Age of Redemption]], which began in about 010.M37. Shamed by the [[Age of Apostasy]], the Imperium launched crusade after crusade to prove its faith. The Great Cull purged the [[Adeptus Terra]], thirty Chapters judged wanting were sent into the [[Eye of Terror]] in the [[Abyssal Crusade]], and pilgrim fleets left the Imperium on crusades from which many never returned.

The crusading drew fleets and Chapters far from home and left many worlds bare. The Seventh [[Black Crusade]], the Ghost War of 811.M37, fell on the Imperium with little warning, and the millennium ended with the Eighth. Beyond the Eastern Fringe the [[T'au]], united by the Ethereals at the end of M36, built their first colonies and septs and took in their first client race.
