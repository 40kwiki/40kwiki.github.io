Fadix was the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] at the time of the [[Hexarchy]] conspiracy in early M42. The agents of the Grand Master moved against the conspirators when they tried to seize [[Terra]]. The conspirator [[Aveliza Drachmar]], a serving High Lord, was killed by Fadix's agents.

The Grand Master of Assassins is one of the High Lords of Terra, and Fadix's service to the council placed him among the most powerful officials on the planet during the crisis.

The Hexarchy

The [[Hexarchy]] was led by [[Irthu Haemotalion]] and [[Baldo Slyst]], and it moved on Terra while [[Roboute Guilliman]] was away from the capital. Its coup was broken by the council's agents, and Fadix's forces are recorded as the decisive element in its defeat.The date of the coup is recorded as early M42. Fadix's exact role is reconstructed from the Administratum's records of the crisis.
