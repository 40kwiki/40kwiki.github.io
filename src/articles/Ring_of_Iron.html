The Ring of Iron, also called the Iron Ring, is a band of thousands of orbital shipyards, docks, refineries and foundries that circles [[Mars]] above its equator. It is held by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and is said to be the largest structure ever built by humanity. Most of the expedition fleets of the [[Great Crusade]] were built there, and it still produces the greater part of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s capital ships.

The Ring was badly damaged during the [[Schism of Mars]] in 005–006.M31, when the [[Dark Mechanicum]] struck at it and the fires could be seen from the planet's surface. It was repaired after the [[Horus Heresy]] and has worked without a break since. Elements of [[Battlefleet Solar]] are based in its docks, and it forms part of the defences that make Mars a fortress of the [[Segmentum Solar]].

Structure

The Ring is not a single structure but a chain of stations, slips and dry docks, joined by transit spars and orbital tethers, which together run almost unbroken around the planet. Ore and refined metal come up from the forges of [[Mars]] and from the asteroid fields of the system, and finished hulls leave from its outer docks. The largest slips can hold several battleships at once. Below the Ring orbits [[Phobos]], whose batteries guard the space between the shipyards and the planet.

The Ring is the work of many ages. The oldest stations are believed to date from the [[Dark Age of Technology]], and others were added by the forges of Mars during the [[Age of Strife]]. Its present size dates from the late 30th millennium, after the [[Treaty of Olympus]] of 739.M30 put the shipyards at the service of the Emperor.Dates for the earliest stations are estimated from Mechanicus surveys; no foundation record survives.

History

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

During the [[Great Crusade]] the Ring of Iron built the expedition fleets of the [[Space Marine Legions]] and of the [[Imperial Army]], together with the battleships of the early Imperial fleets. [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] records speak of hulls leaving the Ring every day at the height of the Crusade, in the late M30 and the first years of M31.

Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

When the Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] declared for [[Horus]] in 005.M31, the Dark Mechanicum attacked the Ring of Iron, and large parts of it were wrecked. Witnesses on the surface described a blazing fire in the sky. Loyalist ships that escaped the Ring joined the blockade of Mars from [[Terra]], which lasted until the end of the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31.

After the Heresy

Once Imperial rule had been restored on Mars during the [[Great Scouring]], the Ring was repaired and set to building the ships of the new Imperial Navy and the merchant fleets. The work of restoration is dated to the late 31st millennium. In 544–546.M32, during the [[War of the Beast]], its shipyards worked without pause to replace the fleets lost against the Orks.Date of the restoration estimated from the sequence of the Great Scouring and the reform of the Mechanicus.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Mars ordered every forge to work at its full rate. Many of the new warships of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], launched from 000.M42, were built in the Ring of Iron, and it remains the chief shipyard of the Imperium.
