Chaos Space Marines, called in Imperial records the Heretic Astartes or Traitor Astartes, are [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] who have renounced their allegiance to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and serve the powers of [[Chaos]]. Most are the warriors of the nine Traitor Legions who followed the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] in the [[Horus Heresy]] of the [[31st millennium]]. Others are the warriors of loyal Chapters who have turned renegade in the ten thousand years since. They are among the most dangerous enemies the Imperium has: they have the strength, training and wargear of Space Marines, and the gifts of the Dark Gods besides.

After the defeat at the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31 and the [[Great Scouring]] that followed, the Traitor Legions fled into the [[Eye of Terror]], where time runs strangely and many veterans of the Heresy still live. There they fought one another in the [[Legion Wars]], and the Legions broke apart into warbands. From the Eye, and from other [[Warp|warp]] rifts such as the [[Maelstrom]], they have raided the Imperium ever since in what they call the Long War.

The Warmaster [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] has led thirteen [[Black Crusade]]s out of the Eye. The thirteenth, in 999.M41, destroyed [[Cadia]] and opened the [[Great Rift]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] the Traitor Legions and their allies have been at war on a scale not seen since the Heresy itself.

History

The Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Nine of the eighteen Legions of the [[Great Crusade]] turned against the Emperor between 005.M31, when the [[Warmaster]]'s rebellion was declared at [[Isstvan III]], and the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31. The causes differed: the [[Word Bearers]] had worshipped the [[Chaos|Dark Gods]] for decades, the [[World Eaters]] followed their [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Angron]] into rage, the [[Thousand Sons]] were driven to rebellion by the [[Burning of Prospero]], and the [[Alpha Legion]]'s reasons are still debated. Not every Legionary of the [[Traitor Legions]] turned; the loyalists among them were purged at Isstvan III and the [[Drop Site Massacre]], and some escaped to fight for the Emperor.

The [[Traitor|Traitors]] were defeated when [[Horus]] was killed aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]] in 014.M31. In the years that followed, the loyal Legions hunted them across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] in the [[Great Scouring]] until the survivors reached the Eye of Terror.

The Legion Wars

Main article: [[Legion Wars]]

In the Eye the Legions turned on each other. Imperial and captured accounts describe centuries of fighting between Legions over worlds, slaves, [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] and relics. The [[Emperor's Children]] and the World Eaters fought for the [[Daemon world|daemon world]] Skalathrax, where [[Khârn the Betrayer]] broke his own Legion; the [[Sons of Horus]] were driven from their stronghold, and Horus's cloned body was destroyed. From the ruin [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]] built the [[Black Legion]], which absorbed warriors from every Legion.

The Long War

Further information: [[Long War]]

The Traitors have raided the Imperium throughout its history. The great campaigns are the [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]], the first of which Abaddon launched in 781.M31. Most failed to win lasting territory, but each served a purpose that only became clear with the thirteenth. Between the crusades, countless warbands have struck at Imperial worlds on their own account.

Renegade Chapters

Not all Chaos Space Marines are Heresy veterans. [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] founded after the Heresy have fallen, among them the [[Red Corsairs]], once the [[Astral Claws]], whose master [[Huron Blackheart]] led them into rebellion in the [[Badab War]] of 901–912.M41, and the [[Purge]]. Renegades often begin by defying the Imperium for reasons of pride or grievance and fall to the Dark Gods later.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In 999.M41 Abaddon launched the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]. The Traitor Legions, renegades and [[Daemon|daemons]] broke through the Cadian Gate, and the [[Fall of Cadia]] destroyed the pylons that had restrained the Eye of Terror. The [[Great Rift]] opened across the galaxy, the [[Noctis Aeterna]] followed, and the Imperium was cut in two.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Since the opening of the Rift the Traitor Legions have struck at both halves of the Imperium. The [[Death Guard]] waged the [[Plague Wars]] against [[Ultramar]]; the daemon primarch [[Angron]] was reborn and led the World Eaters on a crusade aimed at [[Terra]]; Abaddon's Black Legion pursued the [[Despoiler's campaigns]] through the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. In the early [[42nd millennium]] the [[Arks of Omen]] campaign saw Abaddon seek the daemon-smith [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]] and the hidden [[Blackstone Fortress]]es. [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] report that renegade Chapters, cut off by the Rift, have fallen in numbers not recorded since the [[Age of Apostasy]].

Organisation

[[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]] that survived the [[Legion Wars]] intact are few. The [[Death Guard]], the [[Thousand Sons]], the [[World Eaters]] and the [[Emperor's Children]] each follow a single god and retain some of their Legion structure. The rest are scattered among warbands of a few dozen to a few thousand warriors, led by a [[Chaos]] Lord and kept together by loyalty, fear, plunder or the promise of glory. Warbands frequently mix warriors of several Legions, renegades, [[Mutant|mutants]], [[Chaos Cultist|cultists]] and [[Daemon|daemons]].

Among their specialists are [[Sorcerer]]s, the [[Psyker|psykers]] of the [[Traitor|Traitors]]; [[Dark Apostle]]s, priests of the Dark Gods who descend from the [[Word Bearers]]' [[Chaplain|Chaplains]]; [[Warpsmith]]s, who hold the Traitors' war machines together; the Possessed, who share their bodies with daemons; the Chosen, elite veterans; and the Obliterators and Mutilators, fused with their weapons by a techno-virus. Their armour ranges from Heresy-era patterns maintained for ten thousand years to plate warped by the Warp into living things.

Beliefs

Most Chaos Space Marines serve [[Chaos|Chaos Undivided]], honouring all four gods. Others follow one. [[Ordo Malleus]] [[Inquisitor|interrogators]] record a great range of belief, from fanatical worship to cold use of the gods' gifts in pursuit of revenge on the Emperor. Many Heresy veterans still describe their war as a rebellion against a false god and a tyranny, and some reject the name of "Chaos" altogether.

The gods reward those who please them with gifts, which may be power, [[Mutant|mutation]] or both. A favoured champion may rise to become a [[Daemon Prince]], immortal in the Warp. Most who seek that end become instead a mindless [[Chaos Spawn]].

Gene-seed and recruitment

The [[Traitor Legions]] lost their means of creating new [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] in the [[Great Scouring]], and [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] is the most valuable thing they possess. They recruit from captured or stolen Imperial gene-seed, from renegade Chapters, and with the help of [[Fabius Bile]], whose experiments have made many new Traitor Astartes. Their recruits are commonly taken from the populations of the worlds of the Eye. Some warbands raid Space Marine fortress-monasteries specifically to steal gene-seed.

Relations with the Imperium

The Imperium has never acknowledged that half the Legions betrayed the Emperor. Public Imperial teaching describes the [[Traitor|Traitors]] as [[Heretic|heretics]] and daemon-worshippers, and records of the Heresy are sealed by the [[Inquisition]]. The [[Ordo Malleus]] and the [[Grey Knights]] study them; the loyal [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], above all the [[Dark Angels]], who keep secrets of their own, hunt them.
