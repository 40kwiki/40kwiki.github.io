A Rogue Trader is the holder of a Warrant of Trade, a charter that frees its bearer from most of the laws of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and licenses him to travel beyond its borders: to explore, to trade with whomever he finds, including [[xenos]], to claim new worlds for the Emperor and to make war on those who resist. Rogue Traders command their own fleets of starships, with their own [[Navigators]], [[astropath]]s and armies, and are among the most powerful and least accountable individuals in the Imperium.

The first Warrants were granted by the [[Emperor of Mankind]] during the [[Great Crusade]] to explorers who would go ahead of the crusade fleets. Since the [[Horus Heresy]] they have been granted by the [[High Lords of Terra]] and by other powers of the Imperium. Most Warrants are hereditary and pass from one generation of a Rogue Trader dynasty to the next; a Warrant signed by the Emperor himself is the most valued and the most respected.

The Imperium's frontiers, such as the Koronus Expanse beyond the [[Calixis Sector]], have been explored and settled largely by Rogue Traders. Their freedoms make them both useful and suspect, and the [[Inquisition]] keeps close watch on dynasties suspected of heresy or of alien sympathies.

History

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30 – 000.M31) could not search the whole [[Milky Way|galaxy]] with its own fleets. The Emperor granted Warrants of Trade to captains who would scout ahead, find the lost colonies of mankind and report on the [[Xenos|xenos]] empires they met. Some served as advance agents of the expeditionary fleets, and their reports led the crusade to many worlds. The Emperor's own Warrants gave their bearers almost unlimited freedom and conferred on them an authority equal to that of a commander of the crusade.The earliest surviving Warrants are dated by the Navis Nobilite to the first decades of the Great Crusade; no exact year is recorded for the first grant.

After the Heresy

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

After the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), with the Emperor on the [[Golden Throne]], the power to grant Warrants passed to the [[High Lords of Terra]]. In later millennia other institutions have also granted them: the [[Navis Nobilite]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Inquisition]], each with its own purposes. A Warrant's authority depends on its signatory and its age, and older Warrants are often contested by heirs, rivals and the institutions themselves.

Rogue Traders took part in many of the Imperium's later crusades. The Rogue Trader Solomon Haarlock charted the [[Koronus Expanse|Calyx Expanse]] in M36, ahead of the [[Angevin Crusade]] that made it the [[Calixis Sector]] in M39, and in 997.M40 the Rogue Trader Purity Lathimon found a safe passage, the Maw, into the Koronus Expanse beyond. Rogue Trader dynasties remain the principal powers of that frontier. In the [[Era Indomitus]], after the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, some dynasties in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] have become the only link between isolated worlds and the wider Imperium.

The Warrant of Trade

The Warrant is a legal document, written on parchment or engraved on metal, that names its holder and the powers granted. Its terms vary. The oldest grant almost unlimited freedom; later Warrants may restrict their holder to a particular region or task. Common provisions are:

  • the right to travel beyond the Imperium and in regions where Imperial authority does not reach;
  • the right to trade with any power, including [[Xenos|xenos]], and to carry otherwise forbidden goods;
  • the right to claim worlds for the Imperium and to rule them, or to grant them to others;
  • the right to raise armed forces and to make war;
  • exemption from many tithes and taxes, and from the authority of [[Planetary Governor]]s and the [[Administratum]].

A Warrant does not exempt its holder from the authority of the [[Inquisition]], nor from the charge of heresy.

Dynasties and households

A Rogue Trader commands a household that may number in the hundreds of thousands. Its core is a flagship, often an ancient cruiser of the [[Imperial Navy]] or a vessel of unknown origin, and a fleet of escorts and transports. Its officers include a Navigator of the [[Navis Nobilite]], bound by contract to the dynasty, [[Astropathy|astropaths]], a seneschal, an arch-militant who leads the dynasty's armies, missionaries of the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and [[Explorator|explorators]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Many households keep alien servants or allies, which the [[Inquisition]] watches with suspicion.

Rogue Traders vary in temperament. Some are explorers, some merchants, some conquerors, and the Imperium's histories record dynasties of each. A Rogue Trader who leads mainly military expeditions is sometimes called a Rogue Trader Militant; in the [[41st millennium]] some such expeditions are accompanied by detachments of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

Relations with other powers

Rogue Traders work closely with the other powers of the Imperium and are watched by all of them. The [[Navis Nobilite]] values their contracts, since a dynasty's Navigator may travel with it for life. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] sends [[Explorator|explorators]] with them in search of archeotech and [[Standard Template Construct]] fragments. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] sends missionaries to convert the worlds they find. The [[Inquisition]] is their most dangerous partner: [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] make use of Rogue Traders' ships and knowledge of the frontier, but also investigate them for heresy, and dynasties have been destroyed for alien dealings. The [[Imperial Navy]] tolerates armed Rogue Trader fleets as long as they stay beyond its borders.

Within the Imperium, Rogue Traders often move among the highest nobility, and some dynasties hold estates on [[Hive world|hive worlds]] and seats at sector courts. Their wealth, drawn from the frontier, has made several of them richer than many [[Planetary Governor]]s.
