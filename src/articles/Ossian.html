Ossian was Chancellor of the [[Estate Imperium]], the branch of the [[Adeptus Administratum|Administratum]] that keeps the records of the Imperial bureaucracy and the accounts of the [[Departmento Munitorum]]. He sat on the [[Council of Terra]] during the [[Horus Heresy]] and on the [[Grand Council of Reconstruction]] after it.

Heresy and reconstruction

Ossian attended the meeting of the Council in the early days of the [[Siege of Terra]] (013.M31).Estimated; no record gives an exact year. He survived the siege, and he sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction, convened by the [[Primarch|primarchs]] [[Rogal Dorn]] and [[Roboute Guilliman]] to rule during the [[Great Scouring]] (014–021.M31). The record gives no word of his views, his departure or his death. Some fifteen centuries later the office was held by Hektor Rosarind, who sat outside the [[Senatorum Imperialis]].
