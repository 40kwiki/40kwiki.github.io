Alexis Polux, remembered by his sons as the Great Father, was a [[Captain (Adeptus Astartes)|captain]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] [[Legion]] and the first [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Crimson Fists]]. A giant even among [[Space Marine]]s, he was a master of void warfare and close-quarters fighting who rose to command of the Legion's Retribution Fleet during the [[Horus Heresy]].

He led the Retribution Fleet through the [[Battle of Phall]] against [[Perturabo]], was swept by the [[Ruinstorm]] to [[Macragge]] and [[Sotha]], and after the war was chosen by [[Rogal Dorn]] to head a new Chapter at the [[Second Founding]]. He led it for about eight centuries and fell on the edge of a campaign against the [[Scythians]].

Early life

Polux was born on the ice world of [[Inwit]], the same world that bore Rogal Dorn, among a tribal ice caste that lived in its frozen hives. Soon after the [[Emperor of Mankind]] recovered Dorn in the early [[Great Crusade]], Alexis and his twin Helias entered the Trials for the Imperial Fists.The Trials are placed in the early Great Crusade, c. M30, from the sequence of events. No record gives a year.

The Trials sent aspirants across the Splintered Lands, the lightless side of Inwit, in rotting furs. Alexis was gravely wounded by a six-legged predator, and Helias carried him on. When both hung over a crevasse and Alexis could no longer hold his brother, Helias let go. Polux passed the Trials and the loss marked him for the rest of his life.

He grew into a figure of stone, emotionless and unyielding. Even out of armour he stood taller than his battle-brothers, and he could deliver a [[power fist]] blow as easily as another Legionary swung a sword.

Horus Heresy

Retribution Fleet

By the time of the [[Great Betrayal]], Polux commanded the Legion's 405th Company and sat on Dorn's advisory council. He was a pupil of Captain Yonnad, the fleet master of the VII Legion. After [[Horus]] turned in the [[Isstvan System]], Dorn stayed on [[Terra]] as [[Praetorian of Terra]] and sent a third of his Legion, thirty thousand Legionaries and more than five hundred warships, to [[Isstvan III]] as a Retribution Fleet.

The fleet broke into reality inside a [[warp storm]]. Two hundred ships were lost with ten thousand Legionaries. Yonnad was among the dead, and command fell to Polux, who had been named his successor.

Battle of Phall

Main article: [[Battle of Phall]]

With his astropaths blind, Polux anchored the fleet near the ocean world of Phall II in the Phall System. He arranged the fleet in a sphere of looping battlegroups and drilled his shield-bearing breacher squads for the boarding actions he expected. When Perturabo's [[Iron Warriors]] arrived in force, about 007.M31, the fleet bent and then closed on the leading ships. A message from Terra then ordered the fleet home, and a gap opened in the storms. Polux broke off the action at heavy cost and withdrew.The date of the Battle of Phall is taken from the article on that engagement.

Macragge and Sotha

He never reached Terra. The Ruinstorm that followed the [[Battle of Calth]] scattered the fleet, and Polux's [[Navigator]] followed the psychic beacon of the [[Pharos]] on Sotha. He came to [[Macragge]], where [[Roboute Guilliman]] set him to strengthen the defence fleets and augur webs of [[Magna Macragge Civitas]].

Badly hurt at Phall, he fought little, but was attacked by [[Konrad Curze]] when the Night Lords primarch struck Macragge in secret. Polux was then working with the Iron Warriors [[Warsmith]] [[Barabas Dantioch]] through the empathic projector on Sotha. The Pharos let Dantioch aid him, and he held Curze long enough for Guilliman and [[Lion El'Jonson]] to arrive.

Crimson Fists

After the [[Siege of Terra]], Guilliman wrote the [[Codex Astartes]] and demanded the division of the Legions. Dorn first resisted, then conceded to avoid a second civil war, and split the Imperial Fists in the [[Second Founding]]. The [[Crimson Fists]] were made of the Legion's newest and most level-headed initiates under veteran officers, and Dorn chose Polux to lead them.

Polux reorganised the Chapter along the Codex but kept the defensive tactics of the VII Legion, the rare custom of naming an [[Emperor's Champion]], and a place in the [[Feast of Blades]]. He set aside sacred days to honour the Emperor, Dorn and himself, without worship.

Death

About eight centuries after the founding, Polux fell in the unnamed system coded HR8518, held by the alien [[Scythians]]. A venom-tipped projectile struck him in the temple and he fought the poison for many days. His forces carried out his last plans and drove the Scythians off in a short, bloody campaign.The year is estimated at c. 821.M31 from "eight Terran centuries" after the founding of the Chapter.
