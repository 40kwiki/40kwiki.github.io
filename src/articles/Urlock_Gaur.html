Urlock Gaur was an [[Archon]] of the [[Archenemy]] in the [[Sabbat Worlds]]. He is recorded as the magister who defended a long flank of the Khan Group against the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]].

Gaur was the rival of the warlord [[Anakwanar Sek]], whose forces he fought for control of the region. Sek later tried to replace him, and the sources describe a long contest between the two.

Contest with Sek

The crusade freed the worlds of the Newfound Trailing in its first decade and pushed into the Cabal Systems in its second. In the same period it defended its flank against Gaur's magisters and against the rival warlord Sek. Gaur was named Archon, and Sek is recorded as determined to replace him.The dates of the contest are not given in the sources consulted. Imperial accounts place it within the crusade's first two decades.
