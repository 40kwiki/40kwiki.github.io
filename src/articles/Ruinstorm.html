The Ruinstorm was a vast [[Warp]] storm that divided [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] during the [[Horus Heresy]]. It was raised in 007.M31 by the Dark Apostle [[Erebus]] in a rite performed on the surface of Calth during the [[Battle of Calth]], at the command of the [[Word Bearers]] [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Lorgar]]. It grew through the years that followed, fed by the slaughter of Lorgar's [[Shadow Crusade]], until it covered most of the eastern half of the galaxy. Within it, [[Warp travel]] became perilous and often impossible, [[Astropathy|astropaths]] could not send or receive, and the light of the [[Astronomican]] could not be seen.

The storm was a weapon of the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]]. It kept the [[Ultramarines]], the [[Blood Angels]] and the [[Dark Angels]], three of the strongest loyal Legions, away from [[Terra]] while the [[Traitor|traitors]] advanced on the Throneworld. The traitors themselves could pass through it by routes known to the Word Bearers. Cut off from the Emperor, [[Roboute Guilliman]] founded the [[Imperium Secundus]] at [[Macragge]]. The storm began to fail after the three loyal primarchs destroyed [[Davin]], its anchor, in about 011.M31, and it dispersed entirely after the death of Horus at the end of the [[Siege of Terra]].

Origin

The rite at Calth

See also: [[Battle of Calth]]

The Ruinstorm was planned long before it was raised. The [[Word Bearers]] had turned to the worship of the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]] decades before the Heresy, and [[Lorgar]]'s [[Erebus|First Chaplain Erebus]] and [[First Captain]] [[Kor Phaeron]] designed the attack on the Ultramarines at [[Battle of Calth|Calth]] as both a military blow and a great sacrifice. While the fleets fought above the planet, Erebus and a party of [[Argel Tal|Gal Vorbak]] and [[Chaos cult|cultists]] went to the Satric Plateau. There they had laid a circle of black stones taken from the slopes of Isstvan V. Into it they brought stasis flasks holding [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of every Legion that had fought at the [[Drop Site Massacre]], loyal and [[Traitor|traitor]].

The rite drew on the deaths of the battle above and below, and on the death throes of the Veridian star, which the Word Bearers bombarded with Calth's own orbital weapons until it flared and stripped the planet's atmosphere. When Erebus completed the rite, the Warp, which had been uneasy for a year, broke into a storm. It closed round the Veridian System within hours and spread outward over the Eastern Fringe.

Growth

The storm did not reach its full extent at once. The [[Shadow Crusade]] of the Word Bearers and [[World Eaters]] across [[Realm of Ultramar|Ultramar]] in 007–008.M31 was conducted in part to feed it. Millions died on worlds such as Armatura and [[Nuceria]], and their deaths were offered to the Ruinous Powers. Imperial scholars of the Warp hold that the storm grew in proportion to the suffering it fed on, and by 009.M31 it had cut the galaxy in two.

Nature and effects

In the Warp

Within the Ruinstorm the currents of the Warp were violent and unpredictable. Ships that attempted to cross it were scattered, lost, or thrown out of the Warp years after they had entered. [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] could not see the Astronomican through it, so that long jumps were made blind. [[Astropathy|Astropaths]] who tried to send messages across it went mad, died, or found their messages answered by voices that were not human. In practice the Imperium was divided into two halves that could not speak to each other.

The [[Traitor|traitors]] were not bound in the same way. [[Lorgar]] and his sons had been shown paths through the storm by the powers they served, and [[Word Bearers]] and other traitor fleets could cross it when loyal fleets could not. [[Horus]] made use of this to move his forces while his enemies were stranded.

In realspace

See also: [[Realspace]]

Though the storm existed mainly in the Warp, it bled into the material universe on many worlds. People saw the stars swallowed, or the sky filled with colours and shapes that seemed drawn from old religious images of damnation. On the worst-affected worlds the barrier between [[Realspace|realspace]] and the Warp failed and [[Daemon|daemons]] walked in numbers. Many of the first encounters of the [[Space Marine Legions|Legiones Astartes]] with daemons took place under the Ruinstorm, often among small detachments far from the main war, who had no doctrine and no knowledge for fighting such things.

The orphaned forces

The storm broke the chain of command of every Legion with forces in the east. Detachments that had been sent to distant campaigns could neither report nor receive orders. Some remained loyal and held the worlds where they were stranded. Others lost all knowledge of the war and had to decide their allegiance for themselves when they learned of it. Imperial historians who later tried to reconstruct the disposition of the Legions in these years concluded that a full account is impossible. Many actions fought under the Ruinstorm are known only from the survivors, and many from none.

The isolated Legions

Ultramarines

Main article: [[Ultramarines]]

The Ultramarines were the first and worst affected. After [[Battle of Calth|Calth]] they spent 007 and 008.M31 driving the [[Shadow Crusade]] out of [[Ultramar]], with no word from Terra and no means of sending any. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] could not tell whether the Emperor lived. The Ultramarines' discovery of the [[Pharos]], an ancient [[Xenos|xenos]] device on the world of Sotha that cast a beacon in the Warp, allowed them to navigate within Ultramar and to guide others to [[Macragge]]. On this basis Guilliman founded the Imperium Secundus in about 009.M31.

Blood Angels

Main article: [[Blood Angels]]

The Blood Angels had been sent by [[Horus]] to the [[Signus Campaign|Signus Cluster]], where they were drawn into a war with [[Daemon|daemons]] in which much of the Legion died. The storm then kept them from Terra and from their homeworld of [[Baal]]. Blood Angels detachments that had never reached Signus were scattered across the Warp, and for nearly five years many believed themselves the last of their Legion. [[Sanguinius]] brought the main body of the Legion to Macragge by the light of the Pharos.

Dark Angels

Main article: [[Dark Angels]]

The Dark Angels spent the first years of the war fighting the [[Night Lords]] in the [[Thramas Crusade]] on the Eastern Fringe. After the Lion's victories there, many Dark Angels strike forces were sent out to hunt the scattered Night Lords, and a number never returned. The main fleet followed the Pharos to Macragge, where the Lion became Lord Protector of the Imperium Secundus.

Terra

Main article: [[Terra]]

On Terra the storm meant blindness. The War Council of the [[Rogal Dorn|Praetorian]] and [[Malcador the Sigillite|the Regent]] could not learn the state of half the Imperium. The Council sent envoys and agents in person to learn the allegiance of distant worlds, knowing that most would not return. Many of those agents were the Sigillite's [[Knights-Errant]].

Breaking of the storm

Davin

Main article: [[Davin]]

By the time the [[Imperium Secundus]] was dissolved, the storm had come to centre on [[Davin]], the world where [[Horus]] had been corrupted in 004.M31. The three [[Primarch|primarchs]] at [[Macragge]], [[Sanguinius]], [[Lion El'Jonson]] and [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]], led their Legions through the storm toward it. They found Davin turned into a [[Daemon world|daemon world]]. After fighting on its surface and in the skies above it, they destroyed the planet with [[Exterminatus|cyclonic torpedoes]]. The destruction of Davin tore a breach in the Ruinstorm, through which a route to [[Terra]] lay open.

The blockade

Horus had foreseen the breach and had placed a [[Traitor|traitor]] fleet across the route. Guilliman and the Lion engaged the blockade, and Sanguinius took the whole of the [[Blood Angels]] Legion straight for Terra, as his visions had told him he must. He arrived in time to take part in the defence of the Throneworld. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] and [[Dark Angels]], delayed by the blockade and the remains of the storm, reached the [[Sol System]] only in the closing hours of the siege.

Dissipation

After Davin the storm weakened steadily. On Terra, [[Rogal Dorn]] came to believe that the Ultramarines might arrive in time, and the threat of their coming weighed on the [[Warmaster]]'s plans throughout the siege. In the final days, word that the fleets of Guilliman, the Lion and [[Leman Russ]] were only hours from Terra and needed the light of the Astronomican to guide them forced Horus to seek a decision. He lowered the shields of the [[Vengeful Spirit]] to draw the Emperor aboard. After the Warmaster's death the Ruinstorm, deprived of the champion whose cause it served, dissipated over the following months.

Legacy

The Ruinstorm shaped the end of the Heresy as much as any battle. It kept three loyal Legions away from Terra for most of the war, which allowed the [[Traitor|traitors]] to fight the [[Imperial Fists]], [[White Scars]] and [[Blood Angels]] alone in the Palace. It led directly to the founding of the [[Imperium Secundus]], an episode that [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] later regretted. Its isolation of the [[Dark Angels]] has been held to have given [[Luther]] the opportunity to turn [[Caliban]] against the Lion.

Imperial scholars of the Warp have compared the Ruinstorm with the [[Great Rift]] of the [[41st millennium]], which divided the galaxy in a similar way and cut off the [[Imperium Nihilus]] from Terra. The comparison was drawn by Guilliman himself after his return. The Rift is larger and has proved far more lasting, but in both cases a deliberate act of the servants of [[Chaos]] split the Imperium and left its halves to fight alone.
