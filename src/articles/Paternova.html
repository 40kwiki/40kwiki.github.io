The Paternova is the head of the [[Navis Nobilite]], the noble houses of [[Navigator]]s, and the most powerful Navigator alive. He rules from the Palace of the Navigators in the Navigators' Quarter of [[Terra]], which he never leaves from the moment of his installation, and is represented among the [[High Lords of Terra]] by his chief servant, the [[Paternoval Envoy]].

The Paternova's authority rests on more than rank. Through a power that no outsider understands, he sustains and amplifies the warp sense of every other Navigator. In the interval between the death of one Paternova and the rise of the next, the powers of all Navigators weaken, and the shipping of the Imperium falters. The office is filled not by election but by a transformation and a fight: when a Paternova dies, the Heirs Apparent of the great houses are changed into larger, stronger and more savage beings and fight until one remains, who becomes the new Paternova.

Few holders are named in records outside the Palace. A Paternova of the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) sent agents to destroy the Emperor's work on the [[Dark Glass]], which he feared would make Navigators unnecessary. The last Paternova named in the public record is [[Francisco M'edici XIV]] of House M'edici, of the 41st millennium.

Powers

The Paternova is the final authority in the affairs of the houses: their charters, their disputes and the punishment of any house whose treachery or excess endangers the others. His agents are known for their lack of mercy in such cases. Navigator tradition holds that if any living being knows the secret of the Navigators' origin in the [[Age of Strife]] or before, it is the Paternova.

His link with the other Navigators is uneven. Navigators of his own house find their powers strengthened, as if blood carried his influence further, while those of the previous Paternova's house lose that advantage at each succession. A succession can therefore change the fortunes of the great houses in a day, and the houses' politics turn on it.

The staff, soldiers and retainers of the Palace of the Navigators are all drawn from the Paternova's house and replaced at each succession. The chief of them is the Envoy, who speaks for him beyond its walls and on the council of the High Lords.

Succession

Each great house keeps one or more Heirs Apparent, its most powerful Navigators, in waiting for the death of the Paternova. When it comes, every Heir Apparent begins to change at once. They grow larger and stronger, the mutations common to Navigators become more extreme, and they gain the ability to live underwater, in poisonous air and even in vacuum. Their aggression grows and draws them into combat with one another. Each death changes the survivors further, until a single, vastly altered Heir remains and is installed in the Palace.

The interval is dangerous to the whole Imperium. While it lasts, every Navigator except the Heirs loses much of the ability to steer through the [[Warp]]; ships are delayed and many are lost. A long interregnum would bring the Imperium's commerce and war fleets to a halt. The ritual is accordingly brief, and the Navis Nobilite permits no outside interference with it. A Paternova commonly reigns for about a thousand years, so few living Navigators have seen a succession.

History

The Navigator houses predate the Imperium, and the origin of the Paternova's office is recorded only in the sealed stasis vaults of the Palace. During the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30 – 000.M31) the Paternova sat on the Emperor's War Council, and the Navigators' guidance of the Crusade's fleets made the houses indispensable. [[Esuin Rigantis]] of House Zuma, a Navigator of the Crusade, was among the Heirs Apparent of that time.

The Emperor's projects of the late Crusade alarmed the office. The Dark Glass, an artefact studied at his order by [[House Achelieux]], and the work beneath the [[Imperial Palace]] that would become the [[Golden Throne]], both promised ways through the Warp that needed no Navigator. During the Horus Heresy the Paternova sent agents to destroy the Dark Glass, c. 007–009.M31.The date is estimated from the course of the Heresy; Imperial records give no year. The Paternova was represented on the Council of Terra by the Envoy [[Bolam Haardiker]], who sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31.

The office has kept its place through every crisis since. Its Envoys sat on the council in the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) and at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] (999.M41). The Paternova of the late 41st millennium, Francisco M'edici XIV, came from House M'edici, one of the great Magisterial houses.
