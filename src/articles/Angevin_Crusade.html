The Angevin Crusade was an [[Imperial Crusade]] fought from 322.M39 to 384.M39 to bring the [[Calyx Expanse]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] under Imperial control. The region was a dangerous frontier close to the [[Eye of Terror]]. The crusade ended with its worlds organised into what became the [[Calixis Sector]].

The crusade is named for [[Golgenna Angevin]], a noble of the Terran Court. In 322.M39 she was raised to the rank of [[Lord Militant]] in the [[Astra Militarum]] and given a writ from the [[High Lords of Terra]] to lead the crusade. Imperial records describe the crusade as a long campaign of conquest rather than a single battle.

Course of the crusade

The crusade moved through the Calyx Expanse world by world. Each conquered world was placed under a [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]], and the governors answered to the crusade's headquarters. The administration was later based at Scintilla, which was named the seat of the sector. Imperial accounts describe the campaign as costly, with many worlds lost to Chaos and xenos raids during the decades of fighting.

Legacy

The crusade gave the Imperium the region that would become the Calixis Sector. Its governance was reformed several times after the crusade, and the sector remains one of the best recorded in the Segmentum Obscurus. The name of the crusade is still used for the sector's oldest noble houses, which claim descent from Angevin's officers.
