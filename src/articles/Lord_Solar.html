The Lord Solar, formally the Lord Commander Solar, is the supreme commander of the [[Astra Militarum]] in the [[Segmentum Solar]], the region of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] around [[Terra]]. He is one of the five Lord Commanders of the Astra Militarum, one for each [[Segmentum]], and as the commander of the segmentum that holds the throneworld itself he sits among the highest echelons of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Some holders have sat among the [[High Lords of Terra]].

The most celebrated Lord Solar was [[Macharius]], who held the office in the late 4th century of M41 and led the [[Macharian Crusade]] of 392–399.M41 as [[Warmaster]], conquering nearly a thousand worlds. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the office is held by Arcadian Leontus, a noble of Terra who has led the Emperor's armies for more than a Terran century and whose regiments have been drained by the demands of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] under [[Roboute Guilliman]].

The title is distinct from that of the [[Lord Commander Militant]], the High Lord who speaks for the Astra Militarum as a whole. "Lord Solar" is also used in place of [[Warmaster]], a rank given only with the consent of the High Lords to a leader of a crusade, because of the stigma that the title of Warmaster carries from [[Horus]].

History

Origins

After the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), in the reforms of the Time of Rebirth, the [[Imperial Army]] ceased to exist so that no army could again rise against the Imperium in such numbers. War in space became the work of the [[Imperial Navy]], and war on planetary surfaces the work of the [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]]. The Guard's armies are directed by five Lord Commanders, each responsible for all the Guard forces of one segmentum. The Lord Commander of the Segmentum Solar commands the armies around [[Terra]] and the [[Sol System]].

Macharius and the Macharian Crusade

See also: [[Macharius]]

The office's fame rests on Macharius. Born on Donia in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]], he rescued the Lord Commander Solar Phillips during the Roxane Rebellion; Phillips named him as successor, and in 386.M41, after Phillips was killed in the Lemort Landings, Macharius became Lord Solar, the youngest man in Imperial history to hold the office. He was also granted the rank of Warmaster, and led the crusade that bears his name. In seven years, 392–399.M41, his armies added nearly a thousand worlds to the Imperium at the galactic edge. He died in 400.M41, and his conquests collapsed into the Macharian Heresy as his generals fought over them.

Later holders

Few Lord Commanders Solar are as famous as Macharius. Eugenian held the office, possibly during the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]] in 998.M41.The dates of Eugenian's tenure are not recorded; the association with the Third War for Armageddon is only a possibility.

Arcadian Leontus

Arcadian Leontus is the Lord Solar of the [[Era Indomitus]]. He comes from one of the noblest families of Terra, a product of its hive spires and greatest military academies, and has spent more than a Terran century leading the Emperor's armies, kept at his physical peak by rejuvenat treatments. He leads from the saddle of his ancient warhorse Konstantin, now largely cybernetic, with the power sword Conquest and the pistol Sol's Righteous Gaze. Though his command is formally limited to the Segmentum Solar, Leontus has fought far beyond it, arguing that Terra must be defended against distant enemies as much as near ones. He would never gainsay the directives of [[Roboute Guilliman]], though the demands of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] have depleted the regiments under his command. During the crusade his ship was shot down by Orks over the world of Fortuna Minor, where he rallied the survivors and led the retaking of the spaceport.

Duties and powers

The Lord Solar is responsible for directing the operations of all [[Astra Militarum]] forces in the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Solar]]. Arcadian Leontus holds that his duties lie first with the defence of the throneworld, then the [[Sol System]], then its neighbouring sub-sectors and the wider frontiers of the segmentum. He is served by logisticians, priests, tacticians, readers of the [[Emperor's Tarot]] and a company of seers and prophets, the Collegiate Astrolex.

In theory the Lord Commander of a segmentum gives orders to the officers of its sectors, who pass them to the regiments through the [[Departmento Munitorum]]. In practice the distances of the Imperium make this a pretence, and operational control passes to the highest-ranking officer in each theatre of war. The rank of [[Warmaster]] stands outside this hierarchy and can be conferred only with the consent of the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], as it was for [[Macharius]].

The Lord Commanders

Main article: [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]

The Lord Solar is one of five Lord Commanders of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The others command the armies of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]], Pacificus and Tempestus and the Ultima Segmentum, and are usually styled by their segmentum, as Lord Commander Obscurus or Lord Commander Tempestus. Some holders have used variant titles such as Lord High General. All five answer in principle to the [[High Lords of Terra]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] to [[Roboute Guilliman]] as Lord Commander of the Imperium.

Other famous Lord Commanders include Lucellin Tyberius, whose tactical knowledge was preserved after his death in a gold-plated skull that later generals consult, and Morcar, remembered for his campaigns against the [[Aeldari]].
