The Lord Solar, formally the Lord Commander Solar, is the supreme commander of the [[Astra Militarum]] in the [[Segmentum Solar]], the region of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] around [[Terra]]. He is the senior of the five Lord Commanders of the Astra Militarum, one for each [[Segmentum]], and because the armies under his command defend the throneworld itself, he is often counted among the most powerful men in the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Some holders have sat among the [[High Lords of Terra]].

The most celebrated Lord Solar was [[Macharius]], who held the office in the late 4th century of M41 and led the [[Macharian Crusade]] of 392–399.M41 as [[Warmaster]], conquering nearly a thousand worlds. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the office is held by Arcadian Leontus, a noble of Terra who has led the Emperor's armies for more than a century and commands Astra Militarum forces in the [[Indomitus Crusade]] under [[Roboute Guilliman]].

The title is distinct from that of Warmaster, which is granted for the duration of a crusade, and from that of the [[Lord Commander Militant]], the High Lord who speaks for the Astra Militarum as a whole. Popular histories and some [[Administratum]] documents confuse them.

History

Origins

Before the [[Horus Heresy]] the [[Imperial Army]] was commanded through the expeditionary fleets of the [[Great Crusade]], under the [[Primarch|primarchs]] and the [[Warmaster]]. The Heresy (005–014.M31) showed the danger of an army and a fleet under one commander. In the reforms of about 021.M31 the Imperial Army was divided into the [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]] and the [[Imperial Navy]], and the Guard was placed under five Lord Commanders, each responsible for the armies of one segmentum. The [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] of the Segmentum Solar took precedence, because his armies held the defences of [[Terra]] and the [[Sol System]].

Macharius and the Macharian Crusade

The office's fame rests on Macharius. Born on Donia in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]], he rescued the Lord Commander Solar Phillips during the Roxane Rebellion; Phillips named him as successor, and Macharius became Lord Solar at about thirty, the youngest holder on record, after Phillips was killed in the Lemort Landings. The High Lords then named him Warmaster and gave him command of the crusade that bears his name. In seven years, 392–399.M41, his armies added nearly a thousand worlds to the Imperium at the galactic edge. He died in 400.M41, and his conquests collapsed into the Macharian Heresy as his generals fought over them.

Later holders

The records of the Officio Militaris name many later Lord Commanders Solar, few of them as famous as Macharius. Eugenian held the office in the last years of the 41st millennium, possibly during the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]] in 998.M41.The dates of Eugenian's tenure are not recorded; the Officio Militaris places him in the late 41st millennium, and the association with the Third War for Armageddon is inferred.

Arcadian Leontus

Arcadian Leontus is the Lord Solar of the [[Era Indomitus]]. He comes from one of the oldest noble houses of Terra, was educated in its military academies, and has spent more than a hundred years at war, kept in his prime by rejuvenat treatments. He leads from the saddle of his ancient warhorse Konstantin, now largely cybernetic, with the power sword Conquest and the pistol Sol's Righteous Gaze. Though his command is formally limited to the Segmentum Solar, Leontus has fought far beyond it, arguing that Terra must be defended against distant enemies as much as near ones. Under [[Roboute Guilliman]] he commands armies of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and in the defence of Imperial worlds he has fought beside [[Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

Duties and powers

The Lord Solar commands every regiment of the [[Astra Militarum]] in the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Solar]] not assigned to another command. He directs the defence of the segmentum's worlds, raises and moves armies against invasion and rebellion, and supplies regiments to crusades. In practice he shares power with the [[Lord High Admiral]]'s segmentum fleet, the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and other forces on Terra itself. He is served by a staff of generals, tacticians, logisticians, priests and seers, and by the [[Departmento Munitorum]], which supplies his armies.

The Lord Solar has no authority over the [[Imperial Navy]], the [[Adeptus Astartes]] or the forces of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], whose cooperation he must negotiate. When the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] appoint him [[Warmaster]] of a crusade, as with [[Macharius]], these limits are lifted for its duration.

The Lord Commanders

Main article: [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]

The Lord Solar is one of five Lord Commanders of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The others command the armies of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]], Pacificus and Tempestus and the Ultima Segmentum, and are usually styled by their segmentum, as Lord Commander Obscurus or Lord Commander Tempestus. Some holders have used variant titles such as Lord High General. All five answer in principle to the [[High Lords of Terra]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] to [[Roboute Guilliman]] as Lord Commander of the Imperium. Among them the Lord Solar has always held precedence, because his command includes the defence of Terra and of the [[Imperial Palace]].

Other famous Lord Commanders include Lucellin Tyberius, whose tactical knowledge was preserved after his death in a gold-plated skull that later generals consult, and Morcar, remembered for his campaigns against the [[Aeldari]].
