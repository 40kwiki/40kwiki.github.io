The Cadian Shock Troops are the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised on, and since its destruction from the people of, the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] [[Cadia]], which guarded the [[Cadian Gate]] out of the [[Eye of Terror]]. They are the most numerous and the most widely respected soldiers of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Their doctrine, equipment and drill set the standard for the Imperial Guard as a whole, and the Departmento Munitorum uses Cadian regiments as the model by which others are measured.

Cadians were trained for war from childhood and served in the defence of their own world against every [[Black Crusade]] from the 31st to the 41st millennium, while thousands of their regiments fought across the galaxy. When Cadia was destroyed in 999.M41, at the end of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], the regiments serving elsewhere survived it. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Cadian name lives on in regiments raised from refugees, their descendants and soldiers of other worlds trained in Cadian ways.

History

Origins

Cadia was settled and fortified after the [[Horus Heresy]] and the [[Great Scouring]] to block the Cadian Gate, and its garrison was first tested in the First Black Crusade of 781.M31. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] began raising regiments from its population for service off-world once the planet's own defence was secure, probably early in the 32nd millennium.No founding record survives in open archives. The date is estimated from the growth of Cadia's population after its fortification, and from the earliest Cadian regimental honours in the Munitorum rolls. Over the following ten thousand years Cadia raised more regiments than any other world.

The 41st millennium

Records of the late 41st millennium show Cadian regiments in almost every war zone of the Imperium. In 777.M41 they met a [[Black Legion]] incursion at the Cadian Gate; in 795.M41 the Cadian 23rd put down an uprising in the Krandor System. The armoured regiments were especially celebrated: in 863.M41 the Cadian 423rd, led by the tank ace [[Knight Commander Pask]], engaged traitor Titans at the Saint Cyllia Massacre, and in 883.M41 the same regiment led the largest armoured assault since the [[Battle of Tallarn]] on the Planus Steppes.

In 937.M41 the 9th Cadian destroyed Hive Fleet Scarabus at the walls of Fortress Carcasson without losing a single company. In 956.M41 Cadians were among the many regiments that crushed the Kieldar Rebellion. Between 975 and 997.M41 the young officer [[Ursarkar E. Creed]] made his name in the Drussite Crusade, the Hrud Migration of 983.M41 and the Youth's End campaign, and in 980.M41 the 8th Cadian, under Colour Sergeant [[Jarran Kell]], destroyed the Chaos lord Brule on Treconondal.

Cadian regiments also fought the [[Orks]] at [[Third War for Armageddon|Armageddon]] (998.M41), the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] at Taros (998.M41), where the 114th Cadian Armoured served with the 4621st Imperial Army, and the forces of [[Chaos]] on Medusa V in 999.M41.

The Fall of Cadia

In 999.M41 [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] launched the Thirteenth Black Crusade. Hundreds of Cadian regiments, with the Kasrkin and the Youth Army, held the kasrs of Cadia under Creed, who had been raised to Lord Castellan. They were joined by the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the [[Adepta Sororitas]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the living saint [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]]. Cadia did not surrender; it was destroyed when Abaddon brought down a [[Blackstone Fortress]] upon it. About three million Cadians were evacuated.

Era Indomitus

The destruction of Cadia ended its recruiting, but not the regiments. Cadian units serving elsewhere when the planet died went on fighting under the old name, and the survivors joined the [[Indomitus Crusade]] from 999.M41. New Cadian regiments are raised from refugee families on the worlds where they were settled, and even on troop transports between war zones, from soldiers of other worlds who can stand the Cadian training. The Cadians' grounding in the dangers of psychic taint and mutation, once a necessity so close to the Eye of Terror, has proved valuable on the many worlds now under the shadow of the [[Great Rift]]. Not every regiment has stayed loyal: the Cadian 917th was corrupted by a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer Cult]] on the forge world Bheta-Decima in the early 42nd millennium.

Organisation and training

On Cadia every child was taught to strip and assemble a lasgun, and children were drafted into the Youth Army. Its soldiers, called Whiteshields from the white stripe on their helmets, fought in the field before being admitted to a full regiment. The most capable soldiers were selected for the Kasrkin, Cadia's elite storm troops.

Cadian regiments follow the standard structure of the Astra Militarum and include infantry, armoured and artillery formations. Their doctrine stresses discipline, combined arms and the holding of ground: no position is given up without orders. Cadian officers are valued throughout the Guard, and many Cadians have been seconded to the staff of other regiments.

Equipment and appearance

The Cadian soldier wears green fatigues, flak armour and the Cadian-pattern helmet, and carries a Kantrael-pattern lasgun. Armour is painted according to the war zone. Many Cadians bear the violet eyes common on their homeworld. Kasrkin are equipped with carapace armour and hot-shot lasguns.
