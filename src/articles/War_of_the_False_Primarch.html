The War of the False Primarch was a conflict fought by the forces of the Imperium in 860.M33. The Segmentum Pacificus was plunged into anarchy by the war, and the [[High Lords of Terra]] convened the [[Pentarchy of Blood]] to enact their judgement. It is recorded in the Imperial registers under the years 780–860.M33 and is now largely lost to myth.

The war lasted for eighty years and threw the segmentum into disorder. Its records were purged after it ended, and the Imperium's accounts of it are fragmentary.The war's dates are recorded as 780–860.M33. Its exact course is not recorded.

The Pentarchy of Blood

Five [[Space Marine]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] were called before the Pentarchy to enact the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]' judgement. They were the Flesh Eaters, the Carcharodons, the Charnel Guard, the Death Eagles and the [[Red Talons]]. Their verdict ended the anarchy, although its details are not recorded in full.
