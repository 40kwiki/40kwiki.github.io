The Grand Master of the Ravenwing, also called the Leader of the Hunt, is the captain of the 2nd Company of the [[Dark Angels]], the [[Ravenwing]], and leads the Chapter's search for the [[Fallen Angels]], the Dark Angels who turned to [[Chaos]] in the [[Horus Heresy]]. The same title is used in the 2nd Company of the Dark Angels' successor Chapters, the [[Unforgiven]].

The Ravenwing is a mobile company that fights from fast attack vehicles, and its Grand Master is the foremost of its riders. He is a senior member of the Chapter's [[Inner Circle]] and so knows the dangers the Fallen present to the Chapter's honour.
