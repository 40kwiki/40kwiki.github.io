The Master of the Watch is the title of the [[Captain (Adeptus Astartes)|captain]] who serves as sentinel of the [[Fortress-monastery]] in a Chapter that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. He is responsible for the defence of the Chapter's base and homeworld when the [[Chapter Master]] is away.

The office is normally held by the commander of the 2nd Company, though which company actually garrisons the fortress-monastery changes on a rotation. Chapters that do not follow the Codex divide the duty in their own ways.
