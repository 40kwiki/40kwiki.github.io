Jubal Khan is the Chapter Master, or Great Khan, of the [[White Scars]] Chapter of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He took the title in 943.M41 after his predecessor [[Kyublai Khan]] vanished fighting the [[Drukhari]]. Weeks of torture in the hands of the [[Red Corsairs]] left him unable to fight, and he now commands from a life-support cradle in the fortress-monastery of Quan Zhou on [[Chogoris]].

Rise to Great Khan

Four candidates went into the Khum Karta mountains of Chogoris, to the Valley of Khans, to face the trials set by the Chapter's [[Stormseer]]s. Only Jubal came out alive. What the trials contain is not known, because survivors never speak of them. He was named Great Khan in 943.M41.

His early record includes the Jopal Uprising, when insurrectionists tried to take the world of Jopal out of the Imperium. Jubal led the 1st Brotherhood in raids on the enemy's supply and communication lines. The rebels had to pull large forces back to deal with the White Scars, and [[Astra Militarum]] regiments broke through the weakened front and ended the revolt.The date of the Jopal Uprising is not recorded.

Wars

Third War for Armageddon

When [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] invaded [[Armageddon Sector|Armageddon]] in the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]] (c. 998.M41), three brotherhoods of the White Scars answered the call. The whole Chapter went to the Deadlands in the south. Jubal's second, Suboden Khan, led a force that secured the promethium and water stations. Jubal took the rest north to the coast, where reconnaissance had found [[Orks]] building huge structures.

The Orks had raised a shipyard and were readying many ramshackle vessels and submersibles. Jubal first drew the Orks off with feints while kill-teams burned what they could. Construction outran the damage, so he took the fight into the yards and cleared them building by building. The Orks still launched their first fleets into the Tempest Ocean. Jubal then made it his work to destroy the remaining yards. In one action his warriors destroyed an entire Ork brigade without a loss, apparently by cutting it off from supply and reinforcement.

Battle of Chogoris and the Seethnar

While Jubal was away, [[Huron Blackheart]] and the Red Corsairs attacked Chogoris itself. The garrison under Barutai Khan of the 2nd Brotherhood could not turn the tide until a strike force under the Master of the Hunt, [[Kor'sarro Khan]], drove the Corsairs off. Jubal's forces returned in the aftermath.The date is estimated at c. 999.M41, from the sequence of events after the Third War for Armageddon.

Seeking revenge, Jubal led a boarding action on Seethnar, the station Blackheart used as his main dockyard. War parties entered from several points and planted melta bombs. Jubal and his Honour Guard reached the heart of the station and wrecked its plasma reactors, but collapsing corridors trapped them. The Corsairs took him alive.

Some weeks later the White Scars ambushed the warship that held him. They freed their lord, but he had been tortured close to death.

Confinement and command

Jubal was carried back to Chogoris and placed in an apothecarion cradle of calipers and stimm-dispensers. The Chief Apothecary, Ogholei, examines him daily. His body is beyond the Imperium's medicae arts to heal, and the Apothecaries can only sustain his mind. Some in the Chapter whisper that death in battle would have been kinder.

His judgement is unharmed. He sees through the eyes of White Scars outriders and directs strike forces across a whole segmentum, and his strategic sense may have sharpened without the demands of leading in person. His orders reach the field through his closest advisors.

Jubal and Kor'sarro Khan

Jubal was displeased to learn that Kor'sarro Khan had undergone the Rubicon Primaris treatment after the battle of Chogoris. The Master of the Hunt was summoned to the sanctum, knelt and begged forgiveness. What was said is not recorded. Kor'sarro then left to hunt the heretics in the Yasan Sector, and Jubal gave him the cyber-berkut Anzuq, the "Hawk of Fire and Water", a familiar of only a handful of khans. Some of the Chapter think the bird also records all that Kor'sarro does, as a leash.
