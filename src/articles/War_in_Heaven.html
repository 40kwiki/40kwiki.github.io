The War in Heaven was a war fought for millions of years between the [[Old Ones]] and the [[Necrons|Necrontyr]], who were later joined by the star-gods called the [[C'tan]]. It ended some sixty million years before the [[41st millennium]] with the fall of the Old Ones. It is the oldest war of which the Imperium has any knowledge, and its consequences, among them the existence of the [[Necrons]], the [[Aeldari]], the [[Orks]] and perhaps the [[Daemon|daemons]] of the [[Warp]], still shape the galaxy.

The Necrontyr began the war out of envy of the long-lived Old Ones and nearly lost it. Their acceptance of the C'tan, and the transfer of their whole race into undying metal bodies, turned the war in their favour. The Old Ones answered by making warrior races such as the Aeldari and the [[Krork]], and by drawing ever more deeply on the Warp. After the Old Ones' defeat, the Necrons rose against the C'tan and broke them into shards, then went into the Great Sleep in their tomb worlds.

Background

The [[Necrons|Necrontyr]] were a short-lived people of a world scorched by a fierce star. They envied the Old Ones' long lives, and when the Old Ones refused to share their secrets the Necrontyr went to war. They were beaten, and the defeat set their dynasties against each other in a long period of civil wars, the Wars of Secession. The Triarch, the council that ruled the Necrontyr, kept its empire together only by renewing the war against the Old Ones as a common cause.

Course of the war

The C'tan

Main article: [[C'tan]]

In the reign of [[Szarekh]], the last Silent King, the [[Necrons|Necrontyr]] found the C'tan: beings of star-energy who had fed on suns since the young universe. The first of them to take a body of living metal, the one later called the Deceiver, promised the Necrontyr immortality and victory. Szarekh accepted. In the biotransference, the whole Necrontyr race was moved from flesh into living-metal bodies, and the C'tan fed on the souls that were left behind. The race emerged as the Necrons, undying and fearless, but most of them had lost their minds as well as their souls.

Turn of the war

The Necrons and their gods proved immune to the Old Ones' psychic arts. Planet after planet fell. [[Old Ones|The Old Ones]] answered by creating or shaping new races to fight for them. The Aeldari were made to master the Warp; the [[Krork]] were made as a soldier race that bred and fought without pause. Aeldari myth records their gods fighting the C'tan in this period: [[Khaine]] against the Nightbringer, whose wound is said to be why mortal races fear death, and [[Cegorach]], the Laughing God, outwitting the Deceiver.

The Old Ones' reliance on the Warp is thought by many savants to have hastened their end. Storms rose in the [[Warp|Immaterium]], and parasites such as the [[Enslaver]]s came through their gates. By the end of the war the Old Ones were gone.

The Necron revolt

With the Old Ones defeated, the C'tan began to feed openly on the [[Necrons]] themselves. [[Szarekh]], who had come to regret the bargain, gathered weapons able to wound a star-god and led his people against their masters. [[C'tan|The C'tan]] were divided and greedy, and the Necrons broke each of them into many fragments, the C'tan shards, which they imprisoned in tesseract labyrinths. Some C'tan escaped this fate, among them the Void Dragon, which the Necrons left sleeping on a world that would later be called [[Mars]], although the truth of that account is disputed.

The Necrons were too weak to hold [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] against the [[Aeldari]], who now had the Old Ones' [[Webway|webway]] and their gods. Szarekh ordered the dynasties to enter stasis in their tomb worlds and to sleep until the younger races had faded, and then he left the galaxy.

Aftermath

The [[Aeldari]] ruled the galaxy for some sixty million years, until their [[Fall of the Aeldari|Fall]] in M30. The [[Krork]] declined into the [[Orks]]. The first [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb worlds began to wake in the late [[30th millennium]], and in 774.M41 [[Szarekh]] returned to the galaxy. In the [[Era Indomitus]] most of the surviving dynasties have woken, and some Imperial savants speak of a new war between the galaxy's oldest powers.
