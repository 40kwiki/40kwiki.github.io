Galahoth was the fifteenth Chief Custodian and [[Captain-General]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes]]. Custodian records call him notoriously conservative, and hold that the Ten Thousand stagnated under him. He vanished in the last years of the 40th millennium or the first of the 41st.Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

Disappearance

His vanishing marks the start of the Years of Madness, a period of omens and rumours on [[Terra]]. Cult activity, heretical and xenophile, rose steadily across the [[Sol System]]. Doomsday sects set pilgrim tribes against each other inside the [[Imperial Palace]], and the Custodians fought the bloodiest actions they had known in centuries. Galahoth's fate is not recorded. [[Andros Launceddre]] succeeded him.
