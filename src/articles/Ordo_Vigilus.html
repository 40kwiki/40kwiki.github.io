The Ordo Vigilus is a minor order of the [[Inquisition]] founded in the 37th millennium. Its only recorded area of vigilance is another Ordo, the [[Ordo Necros]]. It is the Inquisition's clearest example of the old question of who watches the watchers.

The Ordo is not connected with the [[Vigilus]] system, the Sentinel World at the mouth of the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]] where the War of Beasts was fought. The name is the High Gothic for watchfulness, and both took it for the same reason.

Remit

The roll of the [[Ordos Minoris]] states the remit in a single word: the Ordo Necros. Nothing in the record says what the Ordo Vigilus does to it. It may observe, audit, restrain or, if the Necros went wrong, destroy it. The Inquisition's own account notes that rumours persist of Ordos whose sole task is to purge the Inquisition's ranks, and the Ordo Vigilus is the only Ordo the record lists as having another Ordo for its subject.

Because the remit of the Ordo Necros is itself unrecorded, the Ordo Vigilus's work can be known only by its effects, and none are on record.

Founding

Both Ordos are dated to M37. The Ordo Vigilus was therefore either founded in answer to the Ordo Necros, or came into being with it. The first is more likely, but no record says so. The 37th millennium followed the [[Age of Apostasy]], which had shown the Inquisition what an unwatched power inside the Imperium could do, and the foundation of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] and the [[Ordo Sicarius]] in the previous millennium shows that the Holy Ordos had learned to build supervision into its structure.

Organisation and members

The record gives no strength for the Ordo and names no Inquisitor. It would be reasonable to suppose that it is small, since its charge is small. It is more reasonable to suppose that it is secret, since an Ordo that watched its fellows in the open would not be trusted by them.

Rumour

Imperial rumour holds that the Ordo Vigilus and the Ordo Necros are one Ordo, and that the double roll is a device to hide the fact. Another rumour says that the Ordo Vigilus is the Inquisition's answer to every Ordo, and that the Ordo Necros is merely the one the roll shows. Neither has support in the record.

Why it matters

The roll of the Ordos Minoris is mostly a list of threats: a plague, a heresy, a technology, a class of world. The Ordo Vigilus is on it because of a danger of another kind, which is that a body of Inquisitors, answerable to no one, may itself go astray. The Inquisition has no ranks and no court. An Ordo that watches another is thus the nearest thing it has to an internal check, and the Ordo Necros is the only subject the roll names. Whether the same arrangement exists for other Ordos, in forms that the roll does not show, is unknown.

Relations

The Ordo is on formal terms with the Ordo Necros, which is its subject. It has no recorded dealings with any other Ordo.
