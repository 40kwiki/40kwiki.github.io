A Geller field, also spelled Gellar field, is a protective energy field projected around a starship while it travels through the [[Warp]]. It maintains a small bubble of [[Realspace|realspace]] around the vessel, in which the laws of the physical universe continue to hold, and keeps out the energies of the [[Immaterium]] and the [[Daemon|daemons]] and other entities that live there. Without it no crew could survive a [[Warp travel|warp jump]]. The device that projects the field is formally called a Geller field generator.

Geller fields were developed together with the warp drive around the 18th millennium, during the [[Dark Age of Technology]]. Every warp-capable ship of the Imperium carries one, and its failure is among the most feared disasters in the void: a ship whose field fails in the Warp is usually lost with all hands, and may return centuries later as a [[Space hulk|space hulk]] or as a vessel of [[Chaos]].

Operation

A starship enters the Warp by using its [[Warp travel|warp drive]] to tear a passage between [[Realspace|realspace]] and the [[Warp|Immaterium]]. Once inside, it is surrounded by a medium that responds to thought and emotion and that is filled with predatory entities drawn to living souls. The Geller field projects a membrane of stable reality around the hull, sustained by the ship's sublight reactors, within which the crew can live and work. Entities of the Warp cannot cross the boundary except in rare circumstances.

For the field to work the ship must be completely sealed. No hatch, launch bay or hull breach may be open, because any gap in the envelope is a way in. The field must be kept up for the whole of the transit, and the Navy's manuals prescribe constant prayers and rites to the [[Machine spirit|machine spirit]] of the generator, which tech-priests maintain in shifts.

The Geller field is distinct from the void shields, which protect a ship against weapons in realspace, and from the [[Navigator|Navigator's]] art, which steers the ship by the light of the [[Astronomican]]. A ship needs all three to make a safe journey.

History

Dark Age of Technology

Main article: [[Age of Technology]]

According to the records of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], the warp drive and the Geller field were developed together around the 18th millennium, and made possible the great expansion of humanity across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The name is said to come from an artificer of that age, of whom nothing else is known.The spelling "Gellar" appears in many Mechanicus and Rogue Trader texts. The Navy and the Administratum standardised on "Geller" in the 41st millennium, but both forms are in use. The original designs were lost in the [[Age of Strife]] (c. M25 – M30), and the fields built since are reconstructions.

Great Crusade and Heresy

By the time of the [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31), the Mechanicum built Geller fields to several patterns. Some of the older patterns did not rely on machinery alone: they drew their field from the dreams of a [[Psyker|psyker]] held in a coma, whose mind projected an aura of normality around the ship. Such psykers died after a few voyages and had to be replaced.

The best-known Geller field failure in Imperial history is that of the frigate [[Nathaniel Garro|Eisenstein]], which fled the Isstvan system in 005.M31 carrying Battle-Captain Nathaniel Garro and the first warning of the treachery of [[Horus]]. Its field was damaged in the flight, and the ship survived its journey through the Warp only barely, with [[Daemon|daemons]] loose aboard.

Imperium

Main article: [[Imperium of Mankind]]

In the [[41st millennium]] Geller fields are built at many [[Forge world|forge worlds]]. The forge world of Belecane in the [[Calixis Sector]] is known for its void shields and Geller fields, among them the experimental Belecane-pattern 90.r field, which allowed fast passage through the Warp but suffered an unexplained flicker in long use and is rarely fitted. The Imperium accepts that a small but steady share of its warp traffic is lost every year, and much of that loss is attributed to failing fields.

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 placed new strains on every Geller field. The storms of the Rift are more violent than those of the old Warp, and many ships of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] were lost before the Mechanicus and the Navy revised their rites and settings for crossing it.

Failure

When a Geller field weakens, the first signs are cold spots, voices, apparitions and malfunctioning machinery. If it fails entirely, the Warp floods the ship. [[Daemon|Daemons]] may enter in force, crews may go mad or be mutated, and the ship itself may be torn apart or swallowed. Ships lost in this way sometimes drift back into [[Realspace|realspace]] after centuries, fused with other wrecks into [[Space hulk|space hulks]], or carrying crews that have become something else. Outbreaks of the mutating disease called Gellerpox, recorded among the crews of ships whose fields failed, are attributed by the [[Inquisition]] to the influence of [[Nurgle]].

Xenos equivalents

Other species either use their own forms of the field or avoid the Warp altogether. The [[Leagues of Votann]] use a field they call a Geller Rampart, of more advanced design and far more reliable than Imperial patterns. [[Orks]] cross the Warp in ships with no recognisable field, apparently protected by their own collective psychic presence. The [[Aeldari]] travel by the [[Webway]], and the [[Necrons]] by their own means, without entering the Warp. The [[T'au Empire]] has until recently travelled in [[Realspace|realspace]] only.
