The Red Corsairs are a renegade warband of [[Chaos Space Marines]] who command a vast pirate fleet of mortals and renegades that threatens the shipping and worlds of the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. They were once the [[Astral Claws]], a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of [[Badab Primaris]] charged with guarding the [[Maelstrom]], a [[Warp|warp]] rift of the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. Under their [[Chapter Master]], Lufgt Huron, the Tyrant of Badab, the Astral Claws led the secession of the [[Badab Sector]] that became the [[Badab War]] (901–913.M41). When the rebellion was crushed in 913.M41, about two hundred survivors fled into the Maelstrom with their maimed master, who bargained with the Ruinous Powers for his life and took the name [[Huron Blackheart]].

The Red Corsairs have since become the dominant power of the Maelstrom. They grow by taking in renegade Astartes from every Chapter and Legion, and they raid across the Segmentum Ultima. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 they have been sighted farther from the Maelstrom than ever, besieged the [[White Scars]]' homeworld of [[Chogoris]], and captured [[Roboute Guilliman]] himself for a time in the [[Terran Crusade]].

History

The Astral Claws

Main article: [[Astral Claws]]

The Astral Claws were raised in the Tenth Founding, in M35, to guard against the [[Daemon|daemons]], [[Orks]] and pirates that came out of the Maelstrom. Their homeworld was Badab Primaris, capital of the [[Badab Sector]], and they led the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the region, the [[Maelstrom Warders]]. After their fall they were scoured from the Imperial records.

In 715.M41 [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]] became [[Chapter Master]] by the acclaim of his peers, after his predecessor Rovik Blake fell in single combat with the Ork warboss Vorg Manburna in the Maelstrom. Huron became the Tyrant of Badab and ruled the sector, but he resented the Imperium's use of its resources, and the sector fell deep into debt in tithes, while the Chapter was tardy with its tithe of gene-seed. In 901.M41 an [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] explorator vessel, the Righteous Quest, entered orbit of Badab, and Huron ordered the planetary defences to fire on it. The ship was destroyed and the Badab War began. The [[Executioners]], [[Lamenters]] and [[Mantis Warriors]] joined his secession.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

The war lasted from 901 to 913.M41 and is described in the article [[Badab War]]. It ended in the storm of the [[Palace of Thorns]] on Badab Primaris in 913.M41, where Huron was fatally wounded by a melta blast from Captain Zhrukal Androcles of the [[Star Phantoms]]. The surviving Astral Claws carried him into the Maelstrom. The other secessionist Chapters were pardoned after [[Penitent|penitent]] crusades; the Astral Claws were declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] and erased from the record.

Lords of the Maelstrom

In the Maelstrom, Chief [[Apothecary]] Garreon, Apothecary Variel and [[Techmarine|Master of the Forge]] Armenneus Valthex rebuilt Huron's ruined body, replacing the lost side of it with bionics. Huron renamed himself Blackheart and his warriors the Red Corsairs, and turned them from wardens of the rift into its pirates. Recorded actions of the late [[41st millennium]] include:

  • 938.M41: the violent death of the [[Salamanders]]' Regent of Prometheus at Huron's hands, after which [[Tu'Shan]] took the office.
  • 959–961.M41: the [[Pandorax Campaign]].
  • 960.M41: the Fall of Vilamus, in which Huron and the Night Lords warband of the Exalted stole the gene-seed stock of the [[Marines Errant]].
  • 998.M41: a Red Corsairs strike force is destroyed by the [[Silver Skulls]] in the [[Gildar Rift]].
  • Undated: an attack on the shipyards of [[Bakka]], where a [[Grey Knights]] strike cruiser fell upon the fleet; the battle of Oldarian, where Huron destroyed a muster of Battlefleet Aquinas; and the capture of the [[Space Wolves]] strike cruiser Wolf of [[Fenris]] at Parenxes after the Badab War.

The warband grew quickly. Renegades from loyal Chapters, [[Traitor]] Legionaries from the Eye and mortal pirates all came to the Maelstrom, and many [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] defected to Huron after being captured. Shortly before the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] (c. 999.M41), [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] gave Huron a [[Blackstone Fortress]] to seal an alliance between the Red Corsairs and the [[Black Legion]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

During the Thirteenth Black Crusade the Maelstrom joined the [[Great Rift]] (999.M41). In the [[Terran Crusade]] (c. 999.M41), a large force of Red Corsairs was recruited by the daemon [[Kairos Fateweaver]] and [[Magnus the Red]]. It ambushed [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s fleet, and Huron's lieutenant [[Verngar]] held the [[Primarch|primarch]] and the [[Macragge]]'s Honour prisoner aboard the Blackstone Fortress, until [[Cypher]] and the [[Harlequins]] freed them. [[Skarbrand]] and his daemons attacked the fortress in pursuit of Guilliman during the captivity.

Verngar then left New Badab aboard the Macragge's Honour in search of the Tzeentchian relic called the Ebon Talon. After a temporary alliance, he tried to use it to usurp Huron and was maimed in the duel that followed. Huron took the relic, and the Ultramarines later recaptured the ship.

The Red Corsairs also invaded [[Chogoris]] and the Yasan Sector, as the Great Rift opened. The whole sector fell to Huron's attack. The Red Corsairs were driven back from Chogoris by the White Scars, led by [[Kor'sarro Khan]], but held strongholds in the sector, and the Great Khan [[Jubal Khan|Jubal]] was captured when he attacked the station Seethnar, before he was rescued. Imperial records give no later date for the war.

Later in the Era Indomitus (dates unrecorded) Huron was humbled by [[Typhus]] on Danasar, fought splinters of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] that reached the Maelstrom after the [[Devastation of Baal]], took part in the [[War of Beasts]] on [[Vigilus]], raided the Crimson Eyrie against the [[Aeldari]] prince [[Yriel]], and fought against the Black Legion in the [[Arks of Omen]], although some of their warriors marched under [[Angron]] at Malak.

Organisation and doctrine

The Red Corsairs are organised as a large pirate fleet that deploys small, elite warbands of [[Chaos Space Marines]] supported by larger groups of human reavers, in effect spaceborne [[Chaos cult|cultists]]. The Astartes are proficient at the boarding actions for which the Astral Claws were long infamous. Huron keeps a fleet of vessels captured in many engagements, from a single battle barge, the Spectre of Ruin, his flagship, to strike cruisers, escorts, interceptors and bombers. His fast attack force, the Hounds of Huron, is made up of Chaos Space Marine bikers, led by Chaos Lords called huntmasters who answer only to him.

The Red Corsairs fight as an expression of their faith in [[Chaos Undivided]]. With limited numbers they spend much of their time as space pirates, raiding Imperial shipping and outposts, and the looting of the regions they once swore to protect pleases Huron and his followers as much as it does their patrons. Their armour is red, painted over the old heraldry of the Astral Claws.
