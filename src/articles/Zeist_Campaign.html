The Zeist Campaign was a military conflict between the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and the [[T'au Empire]] in the [[Zeist Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], fought in 303.999.M41. It took place during the T'au Empire's Third Sphere Expansion, and it ran at the same time as the 13th Black Crusade of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and the Tyranid assault of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]].

The Imperium was heavily committed to stopping the forces of Chaos at the [[Cadian Gate]], and the T'au used the distraction to expand rapidly. The Imperium won the campaign, but it merely allowed the T'au commander [[Shadowsun]] to strike deeply into more valuable Imperial space elsewhere. By the time the campaign ended, the Third Sphere Expansion had enlarged the T'au Empire by 133 per cent of its former size.

Background

By 997.M41 the Third Sphere Expansion had reached Imperial worlds in the Eastern Fringe. This expansion was unusual, because the T'au annexed Imperial planets by force on occasion, rather than relying only on the cultural persuasion they usually preferred. By about 999.M41 the planetary governors of the [[Eastern Fringe]] were calling for massive reinforcements, but the attacks of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] and a rise in raids by Heretic Astartes from the [[Maelstrom]] left few defence forces able to answer.

Ultramarines intervention

The Ultramarines Chapter Master [[Marneus Calgar]] was one of the few commanders who could respond. He sent the Ultramarines' 2nd Company to the Zeist Sector to draw a defensive line that the T'au could not cross, and he asked other Space Marine Chapters for help. When the Ultramarines arrived they found many Imperial worlds abandoned to the T'au and others fortified against the xenos' lightning raids.

The 2nd Company's commander, [[Cato Sicarius]], attacked the [[Forge World]] of [[Praetonis V]] to break the T'au assault on that world, and then used that momentum to push the offensive back. He copied the T'au's own tactics, rapid strikes on weak positions to unsettle stronger ones, which suited a small force of Space Marines. The T'au advance slowed and stopped, and in the end only [[Augura]] remained in their hands, as their last logistical and repair base in the area. By this time the Ultramarines were no longer the only Space Marine force in the campaign. Warriors of the [[Iron Lords]], [[Silver Skulls]], [[Crimson Fists]], [[Aurora Chapter]], [[Night Watch]], [[Halo Dragons]], [[Sable Swords]], [[Eagle Warriors]], [[Knights of the Raven]] and others joined the Ultramarines in the attack on Augura. Its fortresses, shipyards and weapons factories were badly damaged or destroyed, and the T'au fell back.

Aftermath

The campaign was a victory for the Imperium, but the Space Marine forces were needed elsewhere against the Black Crusade, so no counterstroke into T'au space was possible. Sicarius returned the defence of the sector to the planetary governors. Later intelligence showed that the T'au had foreseen the counterattack and used the Zeist Sector as a diversion, tying down the Imperial rapid response forces while they struck at richer targets. Among the T'au gains was [[Shadowsun]]'s conquest of [[Agrellan]], a few solar months after the campaign, which was renamed [[Mu'gulath Bay]]. The T'au continued their expansion through the Eastern Fringe despite their losses in the Zeist Campaign.
