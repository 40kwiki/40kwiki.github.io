The Zeist Campaign was a military conflict between the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and the [[T'au Empire]] in the [[Zeist Sector]] of the [[Eastern Fringe]], fought in 999.M41. It took place during the T'au Empire's Third Sphere Expansion, and it ran at the same time as the 13th Black Crusade of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and the Tyranid assault of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]].

The Imperium was heavily committed to stopping the forces of Chaos at the [[Cadian Gate]], and the T'au used the distraction to expand rapidly. The Imperium won the campaign, but the T'au gained much of what they wanted. By the time the campaign ended, the Third Sphere Expansion had enlarged the T'au Empire by 133 per cent of its former size.

Background

By 997.M41 the Third Sphere Expansion had reached Imperial worlds in the Eastern Fringe. This expansion was unusual, because the T'au annexed Imperial planets by force on occasion, rather than relying only on the cultural persuasion they usually preferred. Few Imperial commanders could oppose this effectively in the east.

Ultramarines intervention

The Ultramarines Chapter Master [[Marneus Calgar]] was one of the few commanders who could respond. He sent the Ultramarines' 2nd Company to the Zeist Sector to draw a defensive line that the T'au could not cross, and he asked other Space Marine Chapters for help. When the Ultramarines arrived they found many Imperial worlds abandoned to the T'au and others fortified against the T'au's lightning raids.

The 2nd Company's commander, [[Cato Sicarius]], attacked the [[Forge World]] of [[Praetonis V]] to break the T'au assault on that world, and then used that momentum to strike further. By this time the Ultramarines were no longer the only Space Marine force in the campaign. Warriors of the [[Iron Lords]], [[Silver Skulls]], [[Crimson Fists]], [[Aurora Chapter]] and other Chapters joined the fighting.

Aftermath

The campaign was a victory for the Imperium, but it did not stop the T'au advance. The T'au commander [[Shadowsun]] used the time the Imperium had lost to expand her territory. The T'au continued their expansion through the Eastern Fringe despite their losses in the Zeist Campaign.
