A Waaagh! (also written WAAAGH!) is a great crusade of the [[Orks]]: a mass movement of Ork tribes, gathered behind a single powerful warlord, that sweeps across the stars in search of war. The word is also the Orks' battle cry, and the name Imperial scholars give to the psychic field that Orks generate together, which grows stronger the more Orks gather and the more they fight. A Waaagh! begins small, grows by conquest and by drawing in other Orks, and ends only when its leader dies or its momentum is broken.

Waaaghs are among the most destructive events in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The largest have threatened whole [[Segmentum|segmenta]]: the Ork empire of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] at the end of the [[Great Crusade]], the [[War of the Beast]] of 544–546.M32, and in the present age the Great Waaagh! of [[Ghazghkull Thraka]], which has advanced against the Imperium since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] advises that a Waaagh! must be destroyed while small, because a large one cannot be stopped.

Nature

The psychic field

Individual [[Orks]] are not [[Psyker|psykers]], apart from the rare Weirdboyz. Together, however, they generate a field of psychic energy that the [[Inquisition]] has studied for millennia. This field, the Waaagh! in its proper sense, strengthens Orks in battle, lets their crude machines work when they should not, and seems to turn what Orks collectively believe into fact. It grows as more Orks gather, and Weirdboyz who absorb too much of it may discharge it in bolts of green power, or have their heads explode.

Beginning

A Waaagh! begins when a Warboss of unusual size, cunning or charisma conquers the tribes around him. As he wins, more Orks are drawn to him, and the psychic field around his followers grows. Some Warbosses claim visions from the Ork gods [[Gork and Mork]] and proclaim themselves prophets. Once the Orks of a world have joined him, the Warboss leads them into space in captured, looted or crudely built ships, and other Ork tribes are drawn to the noise of the fighting.

Growth and end

A Waaagh! feeds on battle. Victory attracts more Orks, and each conquered world becomes a source of new Orks, because Ork spores remain in the ground of every world where Orks have lived and fought. A Waaagh! that is not checked may grow until it covers whole [[Sector|sectors]]. It ends when its warlord is killed, when it suffers defeats severe enough to break its momentum, or when its Orks fall to fighting among themselves. Even then, the spores left behind ensure that new Orks will rise on the worlds it passed through.

History

Ullanor

Main article: [[Ullanor Crusade]]

The greatest Ork empire known to the Great Crusade was that of the Overlord [[Urlakk Urg]], centred on Ullanor. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] and nine Legions destroyed it in the Ullanor Crusade, c. 999.M30 – 000.M31, and at the Triumph of Ullanor in 000.M31 the Emperor named [[Horus]] [[Warmaster]].

The War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

In 544.M32 a Warlord known only as the Beast led a Waaagh! of unprecedented power and technology against the Imperium, which was then still recovering from the [[Horus Heresy]]. The war lasted until 546.M32, when the Beast was killed on Ullanor. It changed the Imperium, and led among other things to the founding of the [[Deathwatch]].

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

The records of the 41st millennium list thousands of Waaaghs. Among the best known are Waaagh! Grax, which attacked the [[Forge world|forge world]] of [[Ryza]] in 925.M41; Waaagh! Garaghak, which overran the Forsarr Sector in 952.M41; and Waaagh! Snagrod, which destroyed the fortress-monastery of the [[Crimson Fists]] on [[Rynn's World]] in 989.M41. The [[Wars for Armageddon]] were fought against Waaaghs led by [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] in 941–943.M41 and again from 998.M41.

The Great Waaagh!

When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, Ghazghkull, who calls it "[[Gork and Mork|Gork]]'s Grin", declared that the Great Waaagh! had begun. He had already gathered the Ork empire of [[Octarius]] into his following, and he led a fleet that Imperial estimates put at more than five million vessels into the Imperium. At [[Ragnar Blackmane|Krongar]] in the early [[Era Indomitus]] he was beheaded by the [[Space Wolves]] Wolf Lord Ragnar Blackmane, but his head was grafted onto a new body and he returned to lead the Waaagh!. It is now regarded by the [[Ordo Xenos]] as the greatest Ork threat since the Beast.

Lesser Waaaghs have also exploited the [[Chaos|chaos]] of the Rift. A Speedwaaagh!, a Waaagh! of fast vehicles, crashed onto [[Vigilus]] at the start of the [[War of Nightmares]] in the first years of the [[42nd millennium]].

Imperial response

The Imperium has no policy towards the [[Orks]] other than war. Munitorum doctrine holds that a Waaagh! must be struck before it grows, preferably by killing its warlord, and that the Orks' own feuds should be exploited. Large Waaaghs are fought with crusades of their own, and worlds where Orks have been driven out are often kept under permanent watch because the spores in the soil will produce new Orks. The [[Inquisitor]] [[Fidus Kryptman]] went further in 997.M41, diverting part of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] into [[Octarius]] so that the [[Tyranids]] and the Orks would destroy one another.
