A genetor, sometimes called a magos biologis, is a high-ranking [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[Tech-priest]], usually holding the title of [[magos]]. The genetor is a geneticist, zoologist, xenobiologist, [[xenographer]] and molecular biologist in one, and studies all matters of genetics, anatomy and biology.

Genetors belong to the [[Priesthood of Mars]], alongside the [[logis]], [[artisan]] and [[magi]] ranks. They have access to knowledge and resources far beyond those of the lesser [[enginseer]]s and [[lexmechanic]]s. Their obsession with organic life often makes them seem strange to the mechanically minded majority of the Mechanicus.

Nature of the office

Genetors are much like other Tech-priests. They wear the same [[augmetic]] implants, value knowledge as the divine in the universe, and take part in the [[Quest for Knowledge]]. The difference is that they do not regard flesh and blood as inferior to steel. They see living creatures as complex and adaptable biomechanical machines. Some observe this from a distance, while others seek to improve their own forms with better engineered flesh and blood.

A genetor can look like any other Tech-priest in crimson robes. Where most Tech-priests gain their bulk from steel reinforcement and armour plate, a genetor may have added vat-grown muscle, tough carapace-like skin and organically reinforced bones.

Gene-seed duties

A primary duty of the genetors is to receive and study the [[gene-seed]] tithes of all [[Chapter]]s of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], checking them for corruption and deficiencies. This has been required since the [[Second Founding]]. The [[Horus Heresy]] revealed weaknesses in the gene-seed of several [[Space Marine Legions]]. These defects were made worse by the accelerated cultivation that was needed to keep the huge Legion formations in the field.

Becoming a genetor

A genetor differs from other Tech-priests in training, aptitude and focus. The calling needs a disposition that sees organic life as a kind of machine in its own right, rather than a weak shell to be cast off for steel. Beyond that it takes dedication and research to turn knowledge of living things to the service of the Imperium.

Xenobiologists

A genetor's interest reaches beyond the human form to [[xenos]] life. A sub-sect called the xenobiologists studies alien genetics in order to learn how best to kill the creatures concerned. The work is dangerous in a theocracy, and many genetors have been condemned as [[Heretic]]s for claiming that some xenos biology is superior to that of humans, against the doctrine of the [[Imperial Cult]]. Even so, [[Explorator]] fleets and [[Rogue Trader]]s prize a genetor, since one can judge the nature of a newly met species or a new strain of [[Abhuman]].

Notable genetors

During the [[Horus Heresy]], the Magos Genetor Nexin Orlandriaz helped the [[Raven Guard]] rebuild their Legion after the [[Drop Site Massacre]] at [[Isstvan V]], and accompanied [[Corvus Corax]] when he sought the dormant genetic assets of the [[Primarch Project]].
