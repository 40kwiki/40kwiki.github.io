The Burning of Prospero, also the Razing of Prospero or the Scouring of Prospero, was the Imperial reprisal against the [[Thousand Sons]] Legion on its homeworld in 004.M31, in the closing days of the [[Great Crusade]]. It was carried out by the [[Space Wolves]] under [[Leman Russ]], with a contingent of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and a vigil of the [[Sisters of Silence]], as punishment for the Legion's flagrant breach of the [[Council of Nikaea|Edicts of Nikaea]] against sorcery. The Emperor's writ called for the Legion's [[Primarch|primarch]], [[Magnus the Red]], to be captured and brought to [[Terra]] for judgement.

It did not go as the writ intended. Russ was influenced, by a message brought to him by emissaries of the [[Sons of Horus]], to seek Magnus's death rather than his arrest. The Space Wolves destroyed the capital, Tizca, and broke the Legion's defence. Magnus was struck down in a duel with Russ, and in the moment of his death he saved a fraction of his Legion by sending Tizca and its survivors through the Warp to the [[Eye of Terror]]. More than thirty million people died, nearly every person on Prospero. The Thousand Sons were declared renegades, and the Legion that emerged from the Eye was one of the [[Traitor Legions]] of the [[Horus Heresy]].

Background

Prospero and its people

Prospero was a [[Civilised world|civilised world]] on which the psychic gift was honoured, not hunted. When the infant [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] fell to it in a gestation capsule, from the laboratories under the Himalazian mountains of Terra by way of the Warp, he was raised by the sorcerers and scholars of its commune. His powers soon outstripped those of his teachers. He was elevated to rule the world, united its competing cults, and set about turning Prospero into a centre of learning, with a great library at its heart.

The [[Emperor of Mankind]] found him in 840.M30, during the Great Crusade, and incorporated Prospero into the Imperium. He warned Magnus of the dangers of the Warp and showed him something of its true nature. Magnus feigned shock, promised to give up sorcery and in secret did not. His Legion, the XV, which the Emperor named the [[Thousand Sons]], had been reduced by a genetic instability to about a thousand warriors. Imperial accounts say that Magnus found a way to cure it, restoring the Legion to strength and returning many who had been placed in stasis. The Legion made Prospero its homeworld. Its warriors were recruited from the sorcerer cults, and Magnus taught them the arts of the Warp in secret.

The Librarians and Nikaea

See also: [[Council of Nikaea]]

Magnus helped to design the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] [[Librarian|Librarius]], which turned the psychic [[Mutant|mutation]] seen in some warriors into a disciplined asset of the Legions. The Emperor allowed it. Other [[Primarch|primarchs]] objected. [[Leman Russ|Russ]] held Librarians to be [[Psyker|witches]], and [[Corvus Corax|Corax]] and [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] would not fight beside Legions that used them. [[Mortarion]] accused Magnus of sorcery of the kind practised by the overlords of Barbarus. Before leaving for the [[Imperial Palace]] to begin his work on the Webway, the Emperor called the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31. It judged the Librarians and tried Magnus. The Emperor's verdict forbade the Legions to use psykers and ordered Magnus to give up sorcery on pain of death.

Prelude

Magnus's vision and warning

[[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] broke his word. Soon after [[Council of Nikaea|Nikaea]], in 004.M31, while [[Horus]] lay mortally wounded on [[Davin]], Magnus saw in the Warp the pact the [[Warmaster]] made in the Temple of the Serpent Lodge. He saw the Heresy that would follow, and the death of some of his brothers. He did not see his own end. He gathered the cabal of his greatest sorcerers and resolved to warn the Emperor. [[Astropathy|Astropathic]] messages were slow and unreliable. He cast a sorcerous spell that carried the warning to the Emperor on Terra.

The spell crossed the wards of the [[Imperial Palace]] and struck the Emperor in his mind. It also broke the wards around the Webway project in the dungeons beneath the Palace and killed hundreds of tech-priests and [[Servitor|servitors]]. The Emperor, who recognised the taint of the Warp in the sorcery, took no notice of the warning. He saw in it a second breach of his decree and a proof of Magnus's lie. Imperial accounts say he thought the vision itself a trick of the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]]. He ordered [[Leman Russ]] to bring Magnus to Terra and to show the Legion no mercy.

The Censure Host

The [[Space Wolves]] were sent in full force. They gathered at Thardia and moved through three assembly points, adding strength at each. Some of the Space Wolves were drawn from the [[Battle of Beta-Garmon|Beta-Garmon cluster]], where they had been gathered for the campaign. [[Constantin Valdor|Valdor]], [[Captain-General]] of the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]], brought 982 Custodians, the [[Imperial tithe|Emperor's gift]] to Russ. A vigil of 3,000 [[Sisters of Silence]] was added to nullify the sorcery. A fleet of [[Black Ships]] followed to carry off any [[Psyker|psykers]] found alive.

Russ and his captains were uneasy. The [[Thousand Sons]] were Astartes, and Prospero was their home. A Legion fights best on its own ground. The Wolf King had other reasons for coming. He distrusted sorcery, and as the fleet approached a message reached him from the [[Sons of Horus]]. What it said has never been established. Imperial accounts say only that the Wolf King decided to end Magnus and that the Warmaster was believed to have written it. Whoever drafted it, Horus's plan to remove the Legion from the field before the Heresy came to the point was served by what followed.

Magnus's shame

When Magnus understood that [[Tzeentch]] had been using him, he decided to accept his punishment. He did not warn his Legion or his people. He laid a veil over Prospero so that no word of the fleet reached the planet, and he sent the Legion's fleet away from the system. Russ's armada arrived to find the defence grid dark, the planet's orbital platforms unmanned and a few civilian ships fleeing. The Thousand Sons had not been told what was coming. The sorcerers of the Raptora cult, who kept a telekinetic shield over Tizca, were the only ones to be prepared.

The battle

Bombardment

The fleet reached high anchorage at dawn and opened fire. [[Leman Russ|Russ]]'s flagship, the battle-barge Hrafnkel, began the bombardment. The Prosperine orbital defences were destroyed before most could fire. The first lance struck a kilometre north-east of Tizca, and the sky turned to fire. The Raptora shield held the city, at a terrible cost to the Astartes who maintained it, but the forests of the country around burned, and the grasslands with them. [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] watched the destruction of his world with grief, and accepted it as the price of his fault.

The ground assault

Wave after wave of landers followed. Russ was the first to set foot on Prospero, from a gunship that crashed into the port in Old Tizca, flanked by two great Fenrisian wolves and by his huscarls in [[Terminator armour]]. The citizen militia took up arms in the streets. The Skyguard Air Command flew its skimmers against the landing craft and was destroyed in the air. The regiments of the Prosperine Spireguard, guided by the Corvidae cult, held positions in the upper streets of Old Tizca and were swept away. The northern Palatine Guard under Katon Aphea was overrun in two minutes.

Then the [[Thousand Sons]] came out. A circle of Legionaries in crimson armour linked the six pyramids of the city, with the Occullum Square at its centre. Numbers told against them. The [[Space Wolves]] outnumbered them by about three to one, and the Wolf King's fury was beyond anything they had seen. The Thousand Sons used their sorcery to break the Wolves' advance, and by doing so they handed the Emperor's censure its justification. The sorcerers' fire, wind and lightning drove the attackers back.

The [[Sisters of Silence]] then closed in. The null maidens, who have no presence in the Warp, came down from the rubble in silence, and the conjurations of the Thousand Sons died in their throats. The Space Wolves and the Custodians took advantage, and the line failed. The Space Wolves' [[Wulfen]], the feral Astartes of the Legion's gene-curse, broke through. They tore into the Thousand Sons, who had not fought such creatures. The Legion fell back along the causeway to the Pyramid of Photep, the last refuge of the city.

Magnus's final command

Magnus appeared at last, in golden armour, his hair alight with psychic fire. All who met his gaze died or lost their minds. Only Russ and his wolves were unmoved. Magnus slowed time to speak to [[Ahzek Ahriman]], his [[First Captain]], and told him to take the survivors into the pyramid. There he would find Magnus's equerry Amon, bearing a gift. It was the Book of Magnus, the summation of the primarch's knowledge, and Ahriman was to guard it. He touched the jade scarab on Ahriman's breastplate, a crystal cut from the Reflecting Caves, of which every Thousand Son bore one. When the time came, Ahriman would know what to do with it.

Ahriman led the survivors in. By his count he had little more than a thousand Astartes, a tenth of the force that had stood in Tizca. Some of the Legion suffered the Flesh-Change at that moment and had to be put down by their brothers. The last count was 1,242.

The duel

Russ and Magnus fought alone, apart from their warriors, encircled by lightning. Russ shattered Magnus's breastplate, and Magnus burned Russ's armour and hair. The Wolf King's frostblade Mjalnar was turned aside by the primarch's axe. Magnus drove his fist into Russ's chest, and Russ broke Magnus's arm. Magnus then thrust a blade of pure thought through Russ's chest, and the wolves leaped at him. Russ struck blindly at the [[Eye of Terror|great eye]] of Magnus. It was the blow he needed. He seized Magnus about the waist, raised him and broke his back across his knee.

As the Wolf King raised his blade for the last stroke, Magnus said to Ahriman that this was his last gift. He spoke words of power. His body was unmade and the power flowed into Ahriman. Ahriman knew what to do. He put his hands on the scarab and pictured each of the others. The power spread to every Thousand Son. Tizca itself, the city of light with its libraries and its survivors, was torn out of Prospero and passed through the Warp to the Eye of Terror, where it came to rest on a [[Daemon world|daemon world]] that the Thousand Sons call [[Planet of the Sorcerers|Sortiarius]], the Planet of the Sorcerers. The Legion was saved. In the opinion of the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] it was saved at the cost of its soul.

Aftermath

Magnus and the Rubric

See also: [[Magnus the Red]]

[[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] had given up his flesh and returned as a [[Daemon|Daemon Prince]], wreathed in the light of stars, with wings of Warp fire. He became the first of the daemon primarchs and, later, a Greater Daemon of [[Tzeentch]]. The Legion found that the god he had served had made a gift of its own. The Flesh-Change returned, and threatened to turn every survivor into a [[Chaos Spawn]]. [[Ahzek Ahriman|Ahriman]] gathered a cabal of the strongest sorcerers and cast a spell, the Rubric of Ahriman. It succeeded. Those [[Thousand Sons]] who had no psychic gift were turned to dust and their spirits sealed in their armour, as Rubricae. Those who had it grew in power. Magnus was enraged to find what Ahriman had done to his sons and nearly killed him, but Tzeentch intervened. Ahriman and his cabal were exiled from [[Planet of the Sorcerers|Sortiarius]] and ordered to travel [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] in search of the meaning of [[Chaos]].

The cost

Over thirty million were killed on Prospero, nearly all of them the planet's own people. Imperial records count the Prosperine branch of humanity as extinct. The Legion's detachments abroad were treated as [[Traitor|traitors]] before they heard of the attack. The 9th Fellowship, 2,000 strong under Sentor Rahme, was killed in its camp by the Ultramarines who served beside it. Three hundred of the 7th Fellowship were left to die by the [[Imperial Fists]] at Maktor VIII. Most of the 4th Fellowship, about 5,000 Astartes, abandoned their posts and escaped. Part of the 6th Fellowship held the [[Forge world|forge world]] of Zhao-Arkhad. The Legion was struck from the rolls of the Imperium and assumed destroyed until the [[Siege of Terra]].

The Censure Host lost about 49,000. The [[Space Wolves]] had some 25,000 dead, and several thousand more were missing, mostly from the [[Wulfen|13th Great Company]]. Munitions were so depleted that they could fight only limited wars in the early Heresy. The Army [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]] lost 23,000. The Custodians and the Sisters suffered less. Some Custodians had been beaten by mere Space Marines, a lesson that certain Astartes remembered. The wiser among them noticed how easily the Custodians had disposed of their own kind in return, and wondered whether that was the task they had been made for.

Legacy

In [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] the Burning of Prospero was a just sentence, executed under the Emperor's seal. Few believe it so. The [[Imperial Cult]] counts the [[Thousand Sons]] as the first of the Legions to fall, and the [[Space Wolves]], who maintain that they obeyed the Emperor, do not speak of the duel in other Legions' company. [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]]'s vision of the Heresy was true, and Imperial scholars have debated ever since whether the Emperor could have averted the war by heeding it. The Beta-Garmon cluster, where the Space Wolves had mustered, was later the site of the war's great [[Titan Legions|Titan]] battle. See [[Battle of Beta-Garmon]].
