Drazhar, the Master of Blades, holds the position of Executioner and is the greatest champion of the [[Drukhari]] Order of [[Incubi]]. He is lethality personified, and many Craftworld [[Aeldari]] and Drukhari believe that he was once [[Arhra]], the first Phoenix Lord of the [[Striking Scorpions]].

As Executioner, Drazhar carries out the Drukhari's sentences on their own kind and is the champion of its gladiatorial games. Imperial records describe his skill with the blade as beyond that of any other living Aeldari. The identity claim linking him to Arhra is disputed, and the Aeldari of the Craftworlds treat it as a matter of belief.
