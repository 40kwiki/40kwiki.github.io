The Pale Stars are a region of blue-white stars within the [[Maelstrom Zone]], to the galactic southwest of the [[Maelstrom]] [[Warp rift]], in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. The region became an Imperial sector in the early years of the [[Great Crusade]]. Its most recent great conflict was the [[Badab War]], fought between 901 and 913.M41.

History

The region was once known to void-mariners as the "Sea of Pallid Stars". Its sickly blue-white suns were all born of one stellar nursery, and they are rich in minerals, although some regarded them as ill-fortuned. The 771st Expeditionary Fleet first charted it in the Great Crusade, meeting several devolved Human cultures and xenos strains, which the attached elements of the [[XIX Legion]] purged to extinction. The [[World Eaters]]' "Bloody" 13th Expeditionary Fleet later passed through, and its red wake left the people with a deep fear of the Legiones Astartes. At the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] in the early 31st millennium the region had yet to become a full Imperial sector. Its principal world, Phargos Rex, hosted a mission of the Terran Administration that was the de facto governing authority over several dozen frontier domains.

The Heresy and the shadow war

When word of the Heresy reached the planetary rulers of the region, most recoiled from Horus' betrayal but none dared declare against him. Then the worlds began to suffer creeping horrors, as astropaths heard strange echoes and navigators saw a Warp storm front advance. The tempest was the [[Ruinstorm]], called into being by [[Lorgar]]'s rite at Calth. Its shock wave struck the Pale Stars, choirs of astropaths perished, ships were lost, and populations rose in madness.

Two forces then converged on the region. One was a band of Astartes who had renounced their Legions and hidden their heraldry, called the [[Dark Brotherhood]] by the peoples of the Pale Stars. They gathered around a warrior called the Nemean Reaver, or the Nemean, and by 008.M31 he had bound them together and made a haven in the trinary system of the Eridayn Cataract. In late 007.M31 the Brotherhood assaulted the nearby system of 771/51-6 and purged the Human-xenos hybrid pirate clans that had preyed on the region since Old Night. The other force was a strike force of the [[Alpha Legion]] under Strike Master Ijax, a veteran of the Drop Site Massacre, sent to make the planetary rulers declare for the Warmaster. Ijax divided his command, sent his lieutenants to each ruler with the same offer, and then had the rulers place their musters under Alpha Legion command to destroy the Brotherhood.

The two sides fought across a dozen systems. Alpha Legion commanders used the militia armies as bait and the Nemean refused the bait, so thousands of troops starved or died of exposure, as on Larsa V. The war fed the Warp with blood, and Dissolution, Phargos Rex, Mirdath and Larsa were swallowed by Warp rifts. Daemons walked among Humanity, and great numbers of the people of Phargos Rex were turned to cannibals and murderers. Mobs overthrew planetary governments, and the rulers who survived depended on the Alpha Legion for protection. The Pale Stars had fallen to the Warmaster's cause by "Dark Compliance".

The Council of Terra sent an envoy, the Rogue Trader Militant Lorn Orpheus, to the region. Fewer than half his flotilla survived the Khymaran Drift, and his report that the Nemean led the Dark Brotherhood was routed to [[Malcador the Sigillite]], who already knew of the Nemean as one of the Astartes named by [[Severian]]. Malcador sent [[Nathaniel Garro]] and the Knights-Errant [[Callion Zaven]] and [[Tylos Rubio]] to the region to recruit him. They reached the system of Optera on the ship Eusebeian Herald at 017.008.M31. At the conclave on Optera IV the Alpha Legion attacked the gathering, the Brotherhood broke into rival groups, and the Nemean left with Garro to become one of the [[Knights-Errant]]. The rest of the Brotherhood fought its way across the Pale Stars and vanished into the Eridayn Cataract, not to be met again for an age.

The war of the Pale Stars is known only from scattered archival fragments. Phargos Rex was later declared Perditas by the Inquisition, possibly after an Exterminatus, and is now named Phargos Hex. Its records are classified at the highest security level.

The Badab War

The most recent war of the region, the [[Badab War]], was fought between 901 and 913.M41.

Notable worlds and systems

  • [[Phargos Hex]], formerly Phargos Rex: the sector capital, classified.
  • [[Crow's World]]: a civilised world.
  • [[Optera IV]]: a feral world.
  • [[Larsa II]]: a mining world with a mutant population.
  • [[Kyro System]]: includes Kyro IV, a mining world, and Kyro VI, a penal world.
  • [[Eridayn Cataract]]: a trinary star system and a primary source of resources, ringed by a dense shroud of asteroids that only the best void masters can navigate.
