The Pale Stars are a region of blue-white stars within the [[Maelstrom Zone]], to the galactic southwest of the [[Maelstrom]] [[Warp rift]], in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. The region became an Imperial sector in the early years of the [[Great Crusade]]. Its most recent great conflict was the [[Badab War]], fought between 901 and 913.M41.

History

The region was once known to void-mariners as the "Sea of Pallid Stars". Its systems share one stellar nursery and are rich in minerals, although some regarded them as ill-fortuned. The 771st Expeditionary Fleet first charted it and purged the xenos strains it found with the attached elements of the [[XIX Legion]]. The [[World Eaters]]' 13th Expeditionary Fleet later passed through, and the Pale Stars' people came to fear the Legiones Astartes.

In 007.M31, the [[Dark Brotherhood]] launched a sudden assault on a nearby system, and in 008.M31 its leader, the [[Nemean]], bound its warriors into a single domain. At the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] in the early 31st millennium, the region had not yet become a full Imperial sector.

The Badab War, from 901 to 913.M41, was the sector's most recent war on a large scale. Imperial sources record the conflict in the [[Imperial Armour]] volumes on that war.
