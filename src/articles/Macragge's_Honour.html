''Macragge's Honour'' is an Imperial [[Gloriana-class Battleship]] and the flagship of [[Roboute Guilliman]], the [[Primarch]] of the [[Ultramarines]] and [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. It was built during the [[Great Crusade]] as the second-largest vessel of its class. It fought in the [[Horus Heresy]] and in the [[Great Scouring]]. It is the largest [[battleship]] still in service with the [[Imperium of Mankind]].

The ship is 26 kilometres long, made of polished [[ceramite]] and steel armour. Imperial scholars regard it as the second largest of the Gloriana-class battleships used by the primarchs as flagships during the Great Crusade.

The Heresy

The ship led the flotilla of the Ultramarines' warships that formed the 12th Expeditionary Fleet of the XIII Legion. Guilliman coordinated and prepared his forces from this flagship, before and during the [[Battle of Calth]] at the start of the Horus Heresy.

At the opening of the Heresy, the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] attacked the world of [[Isstvan III]] against the Loyalist elements of four Legions under his command, among them the [[Sons of Horus]], the [[Emperor's Children]], the [[World Eaters]] and the [[Death Guard]]. The Ultramarines remained unaware of this. Horus then sent word to [[Lorgar]] and the [[Word Bearers]] that the time had come to strike against the Imperium. Lorgar hated Guilliman, because the Ultramarines had destroyed the Word Bearers' "Perfect City" of [[Monarchia]] on [[Khur]] on the Emperor's direct orders during the early Great Crusade.

The Ultramarines took no pleasure in this act. It was meant to teach the Word Bearers to adhere to the doctrines of the [[Imperial Truth]] rather than to spread the belief that the Emperor was a god.

Battle of Calth

Main article: [[Battle of Calth]]

In 007.M31 the Word Bearers struck the Ultramarines at [[Calth]] in the Veridian system. The Word Bearers first seized the fleet tender ''Campanile'' and used it to open the attack on the Ultramarine fleet in orbit. The ''Macragge's Honour'' served as the command headquarters of Guilliman and the whole Legion, and it helped coordinate the surviving forces in orbit and on the surface, during and after the battle.

Shortly after the battle began, Guilliman held a last conference with his brother Lorgar, who revealed his treachery. [[Daemon]]s then invaded the flagship and destroyed its command bridge. Guilliman was missing for most of the battle, flung into the void and kept alive only by his primarch physiology, until his warriors found him on the outer hull. A horde of daemons of [[Chaos Undivided]] overran much of the ship, and Word Bearers warships tried to board her, but surviving Ultramarines fought through the vessel and retook it.

When [[Kor Phaeron]] and his surviving warriors fled in the Word Bearers' flagship ''Infidus Imperator'', Guilliman ordered the Chapter Master [[Marius Gage]] to take command of the ''Macragge's Honour'' and give chase. This became one of the most infamous ship duels in the records of the Navy. Gage destroyed two of three intercepting cruisers in one volley and crippled the third, and the two flagships then exchanged broadsides and boarding assaults. Kor Phaeron used the ''Shard of Erebus'' to cut a hole in realspace and escape into the [[Warp]], and Gage followed. In the Warp the ''Macragge's Honour'' was struck by an anomaly and attacked by daemons, and then lost sight of its prey. Captain Aerion Mersaror, who had survived a daemonic ritual aboard the enemy ship, activated a homing tracer, and two volleys from the flagship destroyed the ''Infidus Imperator''. The ''Macragge's Honour'' was left badly damaged and stranded in the Warp. It returned to [[Ultramar]] only after a very long time, long after Guilliman had been placed in stasis after the [[Battle of Thessala]] in 121.M31.

Later service

After the [[Second Founding]], the ship remained part of the fleet of the Ultramarines Chapter. It returned to service most memorably under Guilliman after his resurrection in the [[Ultramar Campaign]] of the [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41. It then served as the restored Lord Commander's flagship throughout the [[Terran Crusade]], the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and the [[Plague Wars]] of the [[Era Indomitus]].

During the Ultramar Campaign [[Marneus Calgar]] commanded the Ultramar Defence Fleet from the ship's captain's throne at the naval battle over the shrine world of [[Laphis]]. Alpha Legion cultists crewed a flotilla of fleeing bulk carriers packed with explosives, which crippled several Ultramarine vessels. Calgar had expected treachery, and a reserve fleet of strike cruisers and frigates swept in from behind the moon Aurora while Techmarines woke the world's orbital defence grid. The Alpha Legion fleet was torn apart.

On the Terran Crusade Guilliman sailed in the ship, which gave him a welcome sense of familiarity in a changed galaxy. The fleet was trapped in the Maelstrom by a ritual of [[Magnus the Red]], and in the end the ''Macragge's Honour'' was lured into a trap set by the [[Red Corsairs]] and [[Kairos Fateweaver]]. It was boarded and Guilliman was taken. He later escaped with the aid of [[Cypher]] and the Harlequin [[Sylandri Veilwalker]], but the Red Corsairs kept the ship and sacrificed its human crews to the Dark Gods. Imperial forces later recaptured her, and she became Guilliman's flagship again, now as Imperial Regent and Lord Commander.

Before the Indomitus Crusade, the ship was refitted in the shipyards of the Ring of Iron around [[Mars]]. [[Belisarius Cawl]] reconfigured the command deck with new machines, to the outrage of more orthodox Tech-priests, and the changes raised the tactical responsiveness of the ship by about a tenth. Many Tech-priests of Cawl's faction of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] now serve aboard her. When Guilliman returned to Ultramar to drive back the Nurgle invasion of the Plague Wars led by [[Mortarion]], he commanded the counter-offensive from the ship.
