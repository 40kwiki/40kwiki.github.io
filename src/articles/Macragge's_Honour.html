''Macragge's Honour'' is an Imperial [[Gloriana-class Battleship]] and the flagship of [[Roboute Guilliman]], the [[Primarch]] of the [[Ultramarines]] and [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. It was built during the [[Great Crusade]] as the second-largest vessel of its class. It fought in the [[Horus Heresy]] and in the [[Great Scouring]]. It is the largest [[battleship]] still in service with the [[Imperium of Mankind]].

The ship is 26 kilometres long, made of polished [[ceramite]] and steel armour. Imperial scholars regard it as the second largest of the Gloriana-class battleships used by the primarchs as flagships during the Great Crusade.

The Heresy

The ship led the flotilla of the Ultramarines' warships that formed the 12th Expeditionary Fleet of the XIII Legion. Guilliman coordinated and prepared his forces from this flagship, before and during the [[Battle of Calth]] at the start of the Horus Heresy.

At the opening of the Heresy, the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] attacked the world of [[Isstvan III]] against the Loyalist elements of four Legions under his command, among them the [[Sons of Horus]], the [[Emperor's Children]], the [[World Eaters]] and the [[Death Guard]]. The Ultramarines remained unaware of this. Horus then sent word to [[Lorgar]] and the [[Word Bearers]] that the time had come to strike against the Imperium. Lorgar hated Guilliman, because the Ultramarines had destroyed the Word Bearers' "Perfect City" of [[Monarchia]] on [[Khur]] on the Emperor's direct orders during the early Great Crusade.

The Ultramarines took no pleasure in this act. It was meant to teach the Word Bearers to adhere to the doctrines of the [[Imperial Truth]] rather than to spread the belief that the Emperor was a god.

Later service

After the [[Second Founding]], the ship remained part of the fleet of the Ultramarines Chapter. It returned to service most memorably under Guilliman after his resurrection in the [[Ultramar Campaign]] of the [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41. It then served as the restored Lord Commander's flagship throughout the [[Terran Crusade]], the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and the [[Plague Wars]] of the [[Era Indomitus]].
