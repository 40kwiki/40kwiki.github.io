A Spirit Stone is a small gem that every [[Asuryani]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari|Craftworlds]] carries on the chest. It serves to save the soul of its owner from consumption by [[Slaanesh]] within the [[Immaterium]] after death. The stone is made from a psycho-receptive crystal called a Waystone, which is attuned to the mind of its owner. Imperial records say that the Spirit Stone works as a "psychic trap": at the moment of death it absorbs the soul.

The stone is an essential part of Asuryani life. Since the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], the stone has been the means by which the Asuryani protect their souls from the Warp.

The Infinity Circuit

After death, the Waystone is taken back to the owner's craftworld and embedded in the wraithbone core of the [[Infinity Circuit]], where it grows into a larger Spirit Stone. Once part of the circuit, the soul can move freely through the psychically reactive wraithbone and mingle with the souls of other Aeldari. Imperial records say that each soul continues to exist, safe from the Warp, as a potential within the circuit. The Infinity Circuit is therefore a place of refuge and rest, from which the dead watch over the living.

Uses in war

In times of need, the Asuryani may take the Spirit Stones of long-dead heroes and place them into [[Wraithguard]] and [[Wraithlord]] constructs. The Aeldari soul then pilots the wraithbone body and experiences reality as a dream. The process is regarded as little better than necromancy.

Spirit Stones are also placed in some Aeldari vehicles and [[Titan]]s, so that the captive soul can control the machine if its living crew is incapacitated. Imperial scholars compare this to the [[Machine Spirit]]s of Imperial vehicles such as the [[Land Raider]].

The [[Warlock]]s who specialise in this work are called [[Spiritseer]]s. The [[Phoenix Lord]]s keep the souls of their founders in Spirit Stones within their [[Phoenix armour]].
