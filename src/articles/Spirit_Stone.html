A Spirit Stone is a small gem that every [[Asuryani]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari|Craftworlds]] carries on the chest. It serves to save the soul of its owner from consumption by [[Slaanesh]] within the [[Immaterium]] after death. The stone is made from a psycho-receptive crystal called a Waystone, which is attuned to the mind of its owner. Imperial records say that the Spirit Stone works as a "psychic trap": at the moment of death it absorbs the soul.

The stone is an essential part of Asuryani life. Since the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], it has been the means by which the Asuryani protect their souls from the Warp. In Aeldari myth the first Spirit Stones were made by the smith god [[Vaul]] from the tears of [[Isha]], when she wept after [[Asuryan]] commanded that the gods be kept apart from mortals, and so the stones are called "Tears of Isha" in the Aeldari tongue. The Aeldari often take the tale as a metaphor for the crystallisation of the psychic energies of the [[Eye of Terror]] into Waystones.

The Infinity Circuit

After death, the Waystone is taken back to the owner's craftworld and embedded in the wraithbone core of the [[Infinity Circuit]], where it grows into a larger Spirit Stone. Once part of the circuit, the soul can move freely through the psychically reactive wraithbone and mingle with the souls of other Aeldari. Imperial records say that each soul continues to exist, safe from the Warp, as a potential within the circuit. The Infinity Circuit is therefore a place of refuge and rest, from which the dead watch over the living. It is not always at peace: at the core of each circuit lies a wraithbone chamber holding a murder-spirit whose battle-lust infects every Asuryani soul, a fragment of [[Kaela Mensha Khaine]] that can animate an [[Avatar of Khaine]] when the craftworld goes to war.

Uses in war

In times of need, the Asuryani may take the Spirit Stones of long-dead heroes and place them into [[Wraithguard]] and [[Wraithlord]] constructs. The Aeldari soul then pilots the wraithbone body and experiences reality as a dream. The process is regarded as little better than necromancy.

Spirit Stones are also placed in some Aeldari vehicles and [[Titan]]s, so that the captive soul can control the machine if its living crew is incapacitated. Imperial scholars compare this to the [[Machine Spirit]]s of Imperial vehicles such as the [[Land Raider]].

The [[Warlock]]s who specialise in this work are called [[Spiritseer]]s. [[Iyanden]], a craftworld with a very small population since the assault of [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] in the [[Second Tyrannic War]], is known for its greater use of these "living dead". The [[Phoenix Lord]]s keep the souls of their founders in Spirit Stones within their [[Phoenix armour]].

The primary source of Spirit Stones is the old Aeldari empire homeworlds within the Eye of Terror, now the [[Crone Worlds]]. Gathering new stones there is extremely dangerous, because the Crone Worlds lie within the Warp rift and are haunted by daemons.

Ynnead and the Ynnari

It is commonly believed that when enough craftworld souls have been gathered within the Infinity Circuits, a new god, [[Ynnead]], god of the dead, will emerge in the Warp, the first Aeldari god born since the Fall. Many [[Farseer]]s hope he will be strong enough to defeat Slaanesh. After the formation of the [[Great Rift]] and the partial awakening of Ynnead, his prophet [[Yvraine]] showed that she could merge the souls of the newly dead with his essence. The [[Ynnari]] among the Asuryani and the [[Drukhari]] need no longer rely on Spirit Stones.
