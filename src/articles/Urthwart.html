Urthwart was a blighted world on the edge of the [[Eye of Terror]]. In early 999.M41 it was destroyed by the [[Planet Killer]] of the Black Fleet, as the [[Traitor|Traitors]] advanced on [[Cadia]].

Cadian [[Kasrkin]] searching the world for the enemy's first move found nothing alive. While they prepared to withdraw, the Black Fleet emerged from the Eye with the Planet Killer, a ship that the Navy had believed lost since the [[Gothic War]]. It destroyed Urthwart in minutes, with the Kasrkin on it.
