The Ordo Excorium is a minor order of the [[Inquisition]] whose area of vigilance is the use of [[Exterminatus]]. The roll of the [[Ordos Minoris]] gives it an established strength of one hundred [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], which is large for a minor order, and no founding date.

Remit

[[Exterminatus]] is the sentence of death on a whole world. Any [[Inquisitor]] of the Ordos Majoris may order it, on a world beyond redemption, and the fleet that carries it out is expected to obey. The [[Inquisition]]'s own article describes the order as an extreme measure, and it is the one act by which the Holy Ordos can destroy a planet's entire population in a single decision.

The Ordo Excorium watches how that power is used. The record does not say how. It could review the orders after they are given, or insist that they be reviewed before. It could investigate the Inquisitors who gave the order, and perhaps punish those who gave it wrongly. A hundred Inquisitors is enough for a body that audits every Exterminatus in the Imperium.

Tension with the Ordos Majoris

An [[Inquisitor]] is, in theory, answerable to nobody. The [[Inquisition]] has no ranks, and no Ordo has a formal power over another. In theory the [[Ordos Minoris]] are of equal rank to the three great Ordos, but in practice they have fewer resources. The Ordo Excorium therefore holds a dangerous position: it is of equal standing to the Inquisitors whose acts it examines, and it has no army of its own.

No record shows how a quarrel between the Ordo and an Inquisitor Lord would be settled. The Inquisition has no court of appeal above its Inquisitors, and an Inquisitor of the [[Ordo Malleus]] who judged a world lost would hold, in his own eyes, the better knowledge of it. The Ordo's strength of one hundred suggests that its founders meant it to be heard. Whether it is obeyed is a separate matter that the roll does not answer.

The record of Exterminatus

[[Exterminatus]] has been ordered in every age of the Imperium, usually by [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] of the [[Ordo Malleus]] against daemon-ridden worlds and by the [[Ordo Xenos]] against worlds overrun by aliens. The Ordo's concern is with deliberate orders, not with the disasters of war such as the [[Fall of Cadia]] in 999.M41. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] a great many worlds have been judged beyond saving, and an Ordo that audits such judgements has more work in the [[Era Indomitus]] than ever. The Ordo is not recorded to have taken any public part in those decisions.

Why the Ordo is needed

The order for [[Exterminatus]] is final. There is no world to restore, no witness to question and no survivor to correct the record. Every other act of the [[Inquisition]] can be wrong and later put right. This one cannot, which is the only reason a body so large was founded to examine it. The Ordo is, in effect, the Inquisition's attempt to make sure its heaviest weapon is used only on worlds that deserve it.

Members

No [[Inquisitor]] of the Ordo is named in the record.
