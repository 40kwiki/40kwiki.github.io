Wilderness space is the name given in Imperial records to the star systems between the Imperium's organised [[Sector|sectors]] and the territories of the xenos. These systems are separated by wide stretches of empty space, and many are unknown to the Imperium. The region is marked on the Imperial galactic charts by [[Warp storm]]s that lie across its warp routes.

Its main regions, from galactic north to south, include the [[Ghoul Stars]] on its northern edge, the [[Jericho Reach]] and the Hadex Anomaly, the Ork empire of [[Charadon]], the [[Damocles Gulf]] and the T'au septs beyond it, and the realm of Ultramar.

Character

Wilderness space has no single government. Some of its systems are held by the Imperium, some by the [[T'au Empire]], [[Orks]] or the [[Aeldari]], and many are held by no one. Travel through it depends on the warp routes, which are unstable and often closed by storms.

Imperial scholars regard the wilderness as the place where the Imperium's survival will be decided. The [[Indomitus Crusade]] has moved into several of its regions, and its first phase ended at the Battle of Raukos in c. 012.M42.The end of the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade is recorded as c. 012.M42.
