The Veritus Sub-sector is a region of Imperial space in the [[Segmentum Solar]], near the centre of the segmentum. It holds many Imperial worlds and navigable [[Warp]] routes. For centuries it was a prosperous bastion of the [[Imperial Cult|Imperial faith]].

The sub-sector was a stronghold of the [[Ecclesiarchy]]. It was a symbol of the galaxy-spanning influence of the Imperial Creed. The Church ruled from the [[Shrine world]] of [[Benediction]], in the [[Talledus System]]. The sub-sector was governed by [[Cardinal]] Morst Bolifax, who was based on Benediction.

Star systems

The sub-sector's notable systems are the Talledus System, with its shrine world, and the Keyfire, Rantharos, Pavia, Oddreon and Leonus systems. The Warp routes between them made the sub-sector an important link for Imperial fleets. Its worlds kept the festivals and tithes of the Imperial Creed for centuries, and many of its people looked to the Church for guidance in times of trouble.

The Talledus War

The opening of the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]] and the resurgence of Chaos changed the sub-sector. It became the centre of a bitter and bloody struggle called the [[Talledus War]]. The war was fought for the souls of its mortal inhabitants. Forces of Chaos led by the [[Dark Apostle]] [[Kor Phaeron]] of the [[Word Bearers]] attacked the sub-sector during the Era Indomitus.

Imperial records describe the war as a struggle of faith as much as of arms. The Ecclesiarchy's defence depended on the loyalty of the people, as well as on the fleets and garrisons of the Imperium.Imperial accounts of the Talledus War disagree on the number of worlds that fell. This article does not give a figure.
