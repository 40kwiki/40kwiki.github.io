Judgement of Carrion is a named [[Space hulk]], a mass of wrecked starships and debris that the Imperium has catalogued as a single object. It is one of the hulks listed in the Imperial Navy's registers beside the [[Death of Integrity]], [[Spawn of Damnation]] and [[Mortis Thule]].

The records of the Judgement are sparse. The Imperium's knowledge of it rests mostly on the catalogue entries and on a small number of sighting reports.

Catalogue

[[Space hulk|Space hulks]] are formed when several vessels are caught together in a single mass, usually by a [[Warp storm]] or a failed jump. The Navy names the largest hulks and tracks their courses, since a hulk can exit and re-enter the Warp without warning.

Record

The hulk's name is recorded in the Navy's lists without a fixed position or a full account of its origin. Imperial [[Navis Nobilite|navigators]] have treated it as a hazard in the regions it has passed through, and the Navy keeps its course under review.
