Aun'Va, styled the Master of the Undying Spirit, was the Ethereal Supreme (Aun'o) of the [[T'au Empire]] in the closing years of the [[41st millennium]]. He presided over the Ethereal High Council on [[T'au (planet)|T'au]] and was regarded by the T'au of every caste as the nearest thing to a [[Saint Celestine|living saint]]. Imperial scholars regard him as the author of the Third Sphere Expansion, the largest offensive the empire has launched against the Imperium.

Aun'Va was killed on the world of Agrellan in 999.M41 by a Culexus assassin of the [[Officio Assassinorum]]. The Ethereal High Council concealed his death, and a hologram of the Ethereal Supreme is reported to continue to address the empire. The [[Ordo Xenos]] regards his loss as the most important single blow dealt to the T'au since the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]].

Rise

Aun'Va took the supreme office when his predecessor Aun'Wei made his Last Walk, the rite in which an aged [[Ethereal]] enters one of the caste's temple-domes and does not return. Custom required that the outgoing Supreme name his successor, and Aun'Wei named Aun'Va. The [[Ordo Xenos]] could not establish the date, and places it at c. 900.M41 by comparison with the empire's later campaigns. His reputation in the empire rested on an unusual ability to sway others without apparent effort. Imperial [[Inquisitor|interrogators]] who have spoken with captured [[T'au Empire|T'au]] report that a person who has agreed with Aun'Va is rarely sure afterwards that the idea was not his own. The [[Inquisition]] attributes this in part to pheromones, and the Ethereals' own methods are not recorded.

His counsel is traced in the empire's wars of the period. His speeches sent the [[T'au castes|Fire Caste]] against the Reek of the Si'coa System, and on the flanks of Mount Scion he pronounced the fate of the Ork host of the [[Waaagh!]] of Grognik. He was a major figure in the [[Orks|Ork]] war known as the Great War of Confederation, which the T'au won.

Farsight and the Third Sphere

See also: [[Farsight Enclaves]] and [[Third Sphere Expansion]]

The one [[T'au Empire|T'au]] who defied Aun'Va was [[Commander Farsight]], who left the empire in the aftermath of the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] and founded the [[Farsight Enclaves]]. Aun'Va's response was to retell Farsight's departure as a betrayal of the [[Greater Good]], and the Ethereal Supreme used the tale to bind the Fire Caste to the [[Ethereal|Ethereals]]. When he announced the [[Third Sphere Expansion]] in 997.M41, he named [[Commander Shadowsun]] as the empire's military leader and as the replacement for Farsight, and declared that he would himself go to the front. Imperial reconnaissance recorded him on his hover-throne at the front lines of the wars of the Prefectia and Agrellan campaigns, within range of Imperial artillery.

Death at Agrellan

In the Second Agrellan Campaign of 999.M41 the Imperium counterattacked to retake the [[Hive world|hive world]], which the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] called Mu'gulath Bay. Aun'Va was trapped beneath the ruins of the old hive city by the Imperial blockade. Inquisitorial records hold that an [[Officio Assassinorum]] [[Execution Force|execution force]] learned of his position and sent a Culexus assassin. The assassin killed [[T'au castes|Fire Warriors]], drones and the [[Ethereal]]'s honour guard, and the Supreme fled up into the hive city. His hover-throne failed, and he was killed on foot.

The Ethereal High Council hid the death. Within days a message from Aun'Va was broadcast across the empire, and [[Ordo Xenos]] analysis finds that it was a holographic construction. [[Commander Shadowsun|Shadowsun]] was herself wounded by a Callidus assassin during the campaign, and the same sources say that she then leaked to [[Farsight Enclaves|Farsight]] the Council's order that he be taken alive.

Legacy

The [[Ethereal|Ethereals]] have continued to rule without a Supreme, and the empire's expansion has not ceased. Some Imperial scholars hold that the loss of the person who controlled the [[T'au castes|Fire Caste]] has loosened the Ethereals' grip on it, and that the number of cadres following [[Commander Shadowsun|Shadowsun]]'s own judgement has risen. The [[Ordo Xenos]] cautions that the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] have always treated the death of an individual as less important than the Greater Good, and that the empire may be better able to bear the loss than the Imperium hopes.
