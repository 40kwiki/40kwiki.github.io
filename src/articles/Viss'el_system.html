The Viss'el system is a star system with an ice world, where the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] had fishery colonies. It was attacked by an Imperial force during the crusade that planned its crossing of the Gulf, c. 744.M41.

The attack had no strategic value. The Imperial ships bombarded the ice around the colonies and drowned them in flash floods.The date of the attack is estimated. The sources give the crusade's opening years but no exact date for these actions.
