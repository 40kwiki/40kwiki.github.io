The Dark Council is the ruling body of the [[Word Bearers]], the [[Traitor Legion]] that once was the XVII Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]]. It is made up of the eldest and most venerated [[Dark Apostle]]s of the Legion. From the [[Daemon]] world of [[Sicarus]] in the [[Eye of Terror]], the council governs the Legion in the name of its Daemon [[Primarch]], [[Lorgar]].

The council's members were Astartes of the XVII Legion when the Word Bearers were first corrupted by [[Erebus]] and [[Kor Phaeron]], some four solar decades before the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] in the early 31st millennium. They survived the Heresy and have governed the Legion ever since, spreading the worship of the [[Chaos Gods]] among untold billions of mortals.

Authority

Most Traitor Legions have lost their unity since the Heresy, and their operations are marked by near-anarchy. The Word Bearers are one of the few that retain a measure of cohesion. Imperial records attribute this to the Dark Council, whose members are held in deep reverence by their brethren. The council's influence is one of the main reasons the Word Bearers have kept their unity through the Long War against the Imperium.

Lorgar has isolated himself in meditation for long millennia in the Templum Inficio on Sicarus, and he plays no part in directing his Legion. The council rules in his absence from the Basilica of the Word, a cathedral-fortress crowned with hundreds of five-kilometre spires on which living sacrifices are impaled. It has a say in many decisions of the Legion, and above all it decides who rises to the rank of Dark Apostle when the reigning Dark Apostle of a Host can no longer command. A Dark Apostle may be unfit chiefly because he has died, and the Chaos Gods encourage rivalry within the Legion, so the council always makes the final choice of successor.

Members

[[Kor Phaeron]] was First Captain and Black Cardinal of the Legion in the Great Crusade, and he was foster father to Lorgar on [[Colchis]]. [[Erebus]] was First Chaplain and corrupted Lorgar, [[Horus]] and Calas Typhon. The two have long been rivals on the council for control of the Legion's direction. Imperial records say that Kor Phaeron ordered the secret sect called the Brotherhood to be formed a third time in the late 41st millennium to overthrow Erebus.

[[Ekodas]] served as Grand Apostle and senior member of the council after the Heresy, and he led the 7th Host in a crusade against the [[Black Consuls]]. He was a secret member of the Brotherhood and was killed by the [[Grey Knights]] at the Boros Gate System. Other members recorded include [[Ankh-Heloth]], who led the 11th Host, [[Marduk]], who led the 34th Host after the death of his master [[Jarulek]] on Tanakreg, and Sarabdal of the 18th Host, whom other Dark Apostles bowed to.

Sicarus

The council's seat is on Sicarus, whose surface is covered by temples and monuments to the Chaos Gods in the form of [[Chaos Undivided]]. Millions of slaves toil to build new structures, which rise into towering spires and sprawl into subterranean warrens. The temples are the physical expression of the Legion's devotion, and the council presides over them.
