The Dark Council is the ruling body of the [[Word Bearers]], the [[Traitor Legion]] that once was the XVII Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]]. It is made up of the eldest and most venerated [[Dark Apostle]]s of the Legion. From the [[Daemon]] world of [[Sicarus]] in the [[Eye of Terror]], the council governs the Legion in the name of its Daemon [[Primarch]], [[Lorgar]].

The council's members were Astartes of the XVII Legion when the Word Bearers were first corrupted by [[Erebus]] and [[Kor Phaeron]], some four solar decades before the outbreak of the [[Horus Heresy]] in the early 31st millennium. They survived the Heresy and have governed the Legion ever since, spreading the worship of the [[Chaos Gods]] among untold billions of mortals.

Authority

Most Traitor Legions have lost their unity since the Heresy, and their operations are marked by near-anarchy. The Word Bearers are one of the few that retain a measure of cohesion. Imperial records attribute this to the Dark Council, whose members are held in deep reverence by their brethren. The council's influence is one of the main reasons the Word Bearers have kept their unity through the Long War against the Imperium.

Lorgar himself is absent from the Legion's daily affairs. The council rules in his absence, and its decisions are carried out by the Dark Apostles and the Legion's warbands across the galaxy.

Sicarus

The council's seat is on Sicarus, whose surface is covered by temples and monuments to the Chaos Gods in the form of [[Chaos Undivided]]. Millions of slaves toil to build new structures, which rise into towering spires and sprawl into subterranean warrens. The temples are the physical expression of the Legion's devotion, and the council presides over them.
