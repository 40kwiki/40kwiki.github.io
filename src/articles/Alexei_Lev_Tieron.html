Alexei Lev Tieron is the one holder of the office of [[Chancellor of the Senatorum]] named in the sources consulted. He is recorded as Chancellor on Terra at the time of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]].

The office of Chancellor leads the [[Senatorum Imperialis]], the council that administers the Imperium's affairs on Terra. Tieron's tenure is not dated in the sources.

Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

The Thirteenth Black Crusade reached [[Terra]]'s council during the period of Tieron's office, but the sources do not record his actions in it.The date of the Thirteenth Black Crusade is estimated from the records rather than fixed in them. No record gives an exact year for Tieron's term.
