The 32nd millennium (M32) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M32 to 999.M32. In its first centuries the cults of the God-Emperor were drawn together into the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], whose [[Ecclesiarch]] joined the [[High Lords of Terra]] in 200.M32. The [[Imperial Army]] had already been divided into the [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]] and the [[Imperial Navy]], and the [[lasgun]] became the standard arm of the Guard.

The defining war of the age was the [[War of the Beast]] of 544–546.M32, the greatest [[Ork]] invasion since the Great Crusade, which nearly destroyed the [[Imperial Fists]] and saw the founding of the [[Deathwatch]] and the Ordos of the [[Inquisition]]. It ended with the [[Beheading]], in which the Grand Master of Assassins [[Drakan Vangorich]] had the High Lords murdered and ruled for most of a century. Order was restored in 646.M32 by Chapter Master [[Agnathio]] of the [[Ultramarines]]. The rest of the millennium marks the start of [[The Forging]].
