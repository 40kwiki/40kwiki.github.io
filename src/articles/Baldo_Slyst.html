Baldo Slyst was an [[Ecclesiarch]] of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] who was deposed after joining a conspiracy against the [[High Lords of Terra]] in the [[Era Indomitus]]. The office is held by the [[Holy Synod]] and passes by election within it, so his removal was a matter of the Synod as well as of the High Lords.

Slyst is remembered in Ministorum records as a churchman who put the Ecclesiarchy's interests above the Imperium's. Other accounts describe him as a loyal servant whose part in events was exaggerated by his enemies. Imperial accounts agree on the bare facts of his conspiracy and his fall.

Conspiracy

While [[Roboute Guilliman]] was away leading the [[Indomitus Crusade]], Slyst joined a plot with the former [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Irthu Haemotalion]]. The conspirators, some of them serving [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] and some deposed, attempted to seize control of [[Terra]]. To gain military strength they summoned the [[Minotaurs]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] to the Throneworld under a false pretext.

Imperial records date the plot to c. 999.M41. The exact month is not recorded.The date is estimated from the sequence of events. No record gives an exact year for the conspiracy.

Fall and legacy

The plot failed, and Slyst was deposed from the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]. The [[Ecclesiarch|Holy Synod]] chose [[Eos Ritira]] to succeed him. Ritira's appointment is recorded as the first act of the Ecclesiarchy under the restored [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], and the Synod has since treated Slyst's name as a warning against using the Ecclesiarchy's seat for private ends.

Slyst's name appears in the Ministorum's lists of deposed prelates. He is not commemorated in any Imperial shrine.
