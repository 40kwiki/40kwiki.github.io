A cardinal is one of the highest-ranking priests and prelates of the Adeptus Ministorum, the state church of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Cardinals serve directly under the [[Ecclesiarch]]. There are thousands of them. They belong to the Frateris clergy and form the Holy Synod, which elects the Ecclesiarch from among its own members.

Most cardinals are responsible for the spiritual guidance of a region of Imperial space called a diocese. A diocese can be as small as one hive city or as large as a star system or sub-sector. Each cardinal governs the spiritual and often the temporal affairs of the diocese. Some are called arch-cardinals, because their dioceses are of special size or strategic importance. An arch-cardinal often leads the Church within a whole sector, as head of the Sector Synod made up of all the cardinals in that sector.

Orders

Cardinals belong to one of three orders, and the orders differ in power.

Cardinals Palatine

The five Cardinals Palatine are the highest-ranking cardinals. They do not govern dioceses. They assist the Ecclesiarch in the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] on [[Terra]]. An Ecclesiarch often names one of them as his preferred successor, although the Holy Synod has the final say.

Cardinals Terran

The Cardinals Terran govern dioceses on Terra. Terra is the most sacred and politically important world in the Imperium, so a Terran diocese gives its cardinal great influence. Many Ecclesiarchs were chosen from among the Cardinals Terran.

Cardinals Astral

The Cardinals Astral govern dioceses on other Imperial worlds. Those in the [[Ophelia VII]] region are known as the Astral Ministra or Synod Ministra. Elsewhere they are called simply Cardinals Astral.
