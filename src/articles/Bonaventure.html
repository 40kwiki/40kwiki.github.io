Bonaventure is a world of the [[Calixis Sector]]. It is closely linked with the [[Inquisitor]] [[Gregor Eisenhorn]], whose agents came from its worlds. [[Alizebeth Bequin]] and the agent and scholar [[Kara Swole]] were both born there, and both served Eisenhorn in the years of his greatest investigations.

The world is also known for the ancient wheel-like tombs found on it, which may be the remains of the pre-Imperial [[Saruthi]]. Imperial scholars have studied them as a source for the history of the sector before the Imperium.

Eisenhorn's agents

Eisenhorn drew many of his agents from Bonaventure during his investigations of around 300.M41. The sources do not give exact dates for the individual cases, which are described only as the years of his greatest work.The date of the investigations is estimated from the records of Eisenhorn's career. No record gives an exact year.
