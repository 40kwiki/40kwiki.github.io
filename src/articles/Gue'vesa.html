The Gue'vesa ("Human Helpers" in the T'au tongue) are the humans who have chosen to join the [[T'au Empire]]. Some are descendants of Imperial soldiers left behind in T'au territory at the end of the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]], and others are citizens of Imperial worlds that the T'au have annexed or persuaded. Many serve in the Fire caste's cadres as [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxiliaries]], and they have shown that they will fight their fellow humans. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] holds that all Gue'vesa are [[Heretic|heretics]] and that any association with [[Xenos|xenos]] is a corruption, and the [[Inquisition]] treats any world on which Gue'vesa are found as lost.

Origin

The [[T'au Empire|T'au]] regard the absorption of Imperial worlds by agreement as the best means of defeating the Imperium. The [[T'au castes|Water caste]] sends missions to Imperial frontier worlds and offers them protection from Imperial retribution, trade without the Imperium's tithes, freedom of religion where it does not conflict with the Greater Good, and a place in a society in which every person is valued. The offer has been more successful than the Imperium likes to acknowledge.

The earliest Gue'vesa were [[Astra Militarum]] troops abandoned by the Imperial fleet when it withdrew from the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade|Damocles Gulf]] in late 745.M41 to meet [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]]. [[Commander Farsight]] offered them the choice of the empire, and most accepted. They were allowed to keep their worship of the Emperor in a form purged of its xenophobia. Their children, raised in the empire, have in the Imperial view been wholly lost to it.

Status

Gue'vesa live in the empire's western sept worlds and colonies, which border Imperial space, and some are found on [[T'au (planet)|T'au]] itself. They do not belong to a caste and it is rare for one to hold command in the [[T'au castes|Fire caste]]. They make their own tools and a copy of the [[Lasgun|lasgun]], which they use to protect their colonies from raiders. The [[T'au Empire|T'au]] show recordings of Gue'vesa at work to the population of Imperial worlds as a demonstration of the benefits of the Greater Good.

Notable actions

In 998.M41, when the desert [[Planetary classification|mining world]] of [[Taros Campaign|Taros]] was annexed by the empire, its [[Planetary Defence Force]] fought as Gue'vesa beside the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] against the Imperial force sent to retake it, in the Taros Campaign. The Imperial regiments met a determined defence and suffered heavy losses, and [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] reported with alarm that the enemy used [[Lasgun|lasguns]] and Imperial vehicles.

Role in the T'au army

Gue'vesa serve as light infantry and garrison troops, and Imperial scholars note that they are valued for their familiarity with Imperial tactics and equipment. They operate in cadres under a [[T'au castes|Fire caste]] officer, who is bound by the Code of Fire to protect them as he would his own caste. The [[T'au Empire|T'au]] arm them with pulse weapons where they can, and with locally made [[Lasgun|lasguns]] where they cannot. Some are trained to pilot the empire's light aircraft. They are not given battle-suits.

The loyalty of the Gue'vesa is not uniform. The first generation, taken from the [[Astra Militarum]], are reported to be more doubtful than their children, and Inquisitorial agents have used them as sources of information on conditions in the empire. The [[Ordo Xenos]] treats these reports with caution, because the Water caste is known to prepare such accounts for outsiders.

Imperial response

The Imperium has ordered that Gue'vesa be killed or reported, and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] preaches against them. The [[Ordo Xenos]] observes that the order has not stopped the movement and that the Imperium's own neglect of its frontier worlds is a cause of it. [[Inquisitor]] reports describe Gue'vesa who still hold the [[Aquila (symbol)|aquila]] sacred and refuse the title of [[Traitor|traitor]].
