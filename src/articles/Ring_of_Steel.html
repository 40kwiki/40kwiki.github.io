The Ring of Steel was a line of star forts and minefields built by the [[Secessionist]] leader [[Huron Blackheart]] around the [[Badab System]] during the [[Badab War]]. It was intended to keep the Imperial fleets out of the system while Huron's [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] fortified their homeworld.

The war began in 901.M41, when an [[Imperial tithe]] fleet was destroyed as it entered the Ring. The sector's worlds were then fought over for twelve years.

Construction and purpose

[[Huron Blackheart|Huron]] built the Ring after he withheld the planetary tithe of [[Astral Claws|Badab Primaris]], a dispute between the [[Administratum]]'s tithe and the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]' ancient rights. The star forts were linked by minefields, and the Ring made the system a fortress that the Loyalist fleets could not approach without heavy losses.

Breaking of the Ring

The Loyalist armada broke the Ring at the orbital fortress of Sentinel-Sigma in 912.M41. The [[Sons of Medusa]] took part in the assault, and the breach opened the way for the final assault on [[Astral Claws|Badab Primaris]]. Some accounts put the breaking one year later, in 913.M41.Imperial records differ on the year. This article follows the Badab War entry and notes the later date where it differs.
