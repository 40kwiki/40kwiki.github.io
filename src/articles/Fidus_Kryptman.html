Fidus Kryptman, also spelled Kryptmann, is an [[Inquisitor Lord]] of the [[Ordo Xenos]], one of the principal divisions of the [[Inquisition]]. He is remembered as the "Hero of the [[Macharian Heresy]]". A leading authority on the [[Tyranids]], he is credited with saving the Imperium during the [[Third Tyrannic War]]. He is also said to have discovered eighty-two new intelligent [[xenos]] species, all of which he judged a threat and ordered destroyed.

Kryptman is a divisive figure. His cordon against [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] cost the Imperium whole sectors of its own worlds, and the Ordo Xenos cast him out for it. He then drew Leviathan into the [[Octarius War]], a gamble whose outcome is still unknown.

Tyran and the First Tyrannic War

Kryptman was the first Inquisitor to see the aftermath of a Tyranid invasion. [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] destroyed all life on the [[Mechanicus]] research outpost world of [[Tyran Primus|Tyran]] in early 745.M41. Almost a year later Kryptman found the world a sterile [[Dead World]], stripped even of its water. Among the ruins he recovered a data-codex that held the outpost's records of the first attack. From those records the Inquisitor named the new enemy the Tyranids, after the first Imperial world they consumed.

Kryptman tried to warn the Imperium by astropathic message, but the hive fleet's [[Shadow in the Warp]] smothered the signal. At [[Thandros]] he found that the Tyranids had already passed through. He and his retinue repaired the system's telepathic relay and sent word of the threat. The Ordo Xenos then ordered him to [[Macragge]] to help [[Marneus Calgar]] of the [[Ultramarines]] meet Behemoth.

He went on to fight the Tyranids for more than 250 standard years. He was among the most active Inquisitors against them, and some of his measures were drastic enough to alarm his fellows.

Tarsis Ultra

In 997.M41 Kryptman noted the signs of a new Tyranid invasion. He ran what became known as the Kryptman Census, burning out dozens of [[astropath]]s as he sought answers from worlds on every fringe of the Imperium. The pattern of replies, and of silences, let him chart the new hive fleet, codenamed [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]], which approached from below the galactic plane in two arms like closing jaws.

In 999.M41 a tendril of Leviathan moved on the world of [[Tarsis Ultra]]. Kryptman, with a [[Deathwatch]] kill-team under Captain Bannon and the [[Magos Biologis]] Vianco Locard, joined the defenders: the Ultramarines 4th Company under Captain [[Uriel Ventris]], a detachment of the [[Mortifactors]], and Imperial Guard and planetary defence regiments. Kryptman wished to order ''[[Exterminatus]]'' on the next world in the Tyranids' path, Chordelis, to deny it as biomass. Ventris opposed him, but Kryptman deceived the captain and, with the help of the Mortifactors, carried it out.

During the fighting the Deathwatch captured a [[Lictor]] alive on Kryptman's order. Locard made a poison from its biology, and a kill-team led by Ventris used it to kill the [[Norn-Queen]] aboard the last hive ship of the tendril. This ended the battle at Tarsis Ultra and gave the Imperium its first victory of the third assault.

Kryptman's Cordon

Leviathan went on to destroy the [[Forge World]] of [[Gryphonne IV]]. To buy time for [[Battlefleet Solar]] and Battlefleet Tempestus to muster, Kryptman ordered a ''cordon sanitaire'': every world in the hive fleet's path was to be evacuated and, where possible, put to ''Exterminatus'', so that its biomass could not feed new Tyranid bioforms. It was the worst act of genocide the Imperium had inflicted on itself since the [[Horus Heresy]]. Many denounced him as a [[Radical]] and a traitor, Inquisitors among them.

When migrating [[Orks]] claimed a score of the evacuated worlds, the Ordo Xenos declared him ''Carta Extremis'': neither a heretic nor a Radical deserving execution, but too unstable and too ready to use destructive means to remain an Inquisitor. He was stripped of his title and cast out of the Inquisition.

The Octarius War

Kryptman kept loyal allies among the Deathwatch. When a [[Genestealer]]-infested [[space hulk]] drifted from the [[Warp]], they captured a brood still in stasis. With it the Inquisitor engineered a Tyranid invasion of the Ork-held Octarius System, close to Leviathan's line of advance. The teeming Ork cities drew the whole hive fleet into the [[Octarius Empire]], and the resulting [[Octarius War]] kept Leviathan from its course to [[Terra]].

The war bought the Imperium time, but the sources call it a temporary solution at best. If the Tyranids win, they will absorb the hardy Ork biology, and worlds on the edge of the conflict already report larger and more formidable Tyranid organisms.Imperial opinion treats Kryptman's actions as both a rescue and a crime.
