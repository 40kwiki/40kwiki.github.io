The [[Era Indomitus]] is the present age of the Imperium, which began when the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41. It is an age of war on every front, from the [[Imperium Nihilus]] to [[Eastern Fringe|the eastern fringe]]. This portal gathers the articles on its wars, its leaders and its changed [[Milky Way|galaxy]].

Selected article

[[Era Indomitus]] is the present age of the Imperium, from the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 to the wars of the [[42nd millennium]]. [[Milky Way|The galaxy]] was divided by the Rift into the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. Imperial scholars regard the age as the most dangerous since the [[Horus Heresy]].
