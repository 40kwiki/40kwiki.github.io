The Imperial Armed Forces are the many fighting services of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. They are not a single army under a single commander. They are a collection of separate institutions, each with its own history, law and chain of command, bound together by their loyalty to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and by the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The largest are the [[Astra Militarum]], the Imperium's mass army, and the [[Imperial Navy]], which carries it between the stars. Alongside them stand the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the Space Marines; the armies of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]; the [[Imperial Knight|Imperial Knights]]; the [[Adeptus Custodes]]; the [[Adepta Sororitas]]; and the military arms of the [[Inquisition]].

This division is deliberate. In the [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31) the Imperial Army, the Legions and the forces of the Mechanicum fought under a single [[Warmaster]], and in 005.M31 that Warmaster, [[Horus]], turned half of them against the Emperor. The reforms that followed the [[Horus Heresy]] broke up every great command so that no one commander could again hold such power. The resulting forces are vast and powerful, but slow to combine and often jealous of one another.

Since 999.M41 the forces of the Imperium have been led in the [[Indomitus Crusade]] by [[Roboute Guilliman]], who as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] is the first individual since the Heresy to command all of them.

History

Unification and the Great Crusade

The armed forces of the Imperium began with the armies of the [[Unification Wars]] on [[Terra]], fought from the late 29th millennium to about 712.M30. The Emperor's [[Thunder Warriors]] led these campaigns. They were succeeded by the [[Space Marine Legions]] and the mass army that became the Imperial Army.

In the Great Crusade the Imperial Army, made up of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Solar Auxilia]], the regiments of the Excertus Imperialis and the fleet that carried them, fought beside the twenty Legions of the [[Adeptus Astartes|Legiones Astartes]]. The Mechanicum of Mars, bound to the Imperium by the [[Treaty of Olympus]] in 739.M30, contributed its [[Mechanicum Taghmata|Taghmata]], its [[Titan Legions]] and the [[Legio Cybernetica]], and the Knight houses of the [[Knight world|Knight worlds]] marched with them. The whole effort was commanded, from 000.M31, by the Warmaster Horus.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The Horus Heresy (005–014.M31) divided every one of these services. Half the Legions, many Titan Legions and Knight houses and much of the Imperial Army followed Horus. The [[Schism of Mars]] split the Mechanicum. The surviving loyalist forces held Terra at the [[Siege of Terra]] and drove the Traitors back in the [[Great Scouring]].

The reforms

In the years after the Heresy, roughly 015–021.M31, [[Roboute Guilliman]] reorganised the armed forces. The [[Codex Astartes]] divided the Legions into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of about a thousand warriors in the [[Second Founding]]. The reforms also began the division of the Imperial Army into the Imperial Guard, now called the Astra Militarum, and the Imperial Navy, a division completed in the [[Time of Rebirth]] of the early 32nd millennium, so that no army commander controlled the ships to carry his troops and no admiral controlled an army. The Navy itself was divided among five [[Segmentum|segmentum]] fleets. The office of Warmaster was abolished.The reforms are dated from the sequence of events. They were introduced over many years, and some were resisted by the Legions for a generation or more.

The Ecclesiarchy's armies

In the [[Age of Apostasy]] of M36 the [[Ecclesiarchy]] raised armies of its own, the [[Frateris Templar]], which enforced the [[Reign of Blood]] of [[Goge Vandire]]. After Vandire's fall the [[Decree Passive]] of 288.M36 forbade the Ecclesiarchy to keep "men under arms". The [[Daughters of the Emperor]], being women, became the Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], which the Ecclesiarchy has kept ever since.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 brought the Imperium close to collapse. [[Roboute Guilliman]], restored to life in that year, was named [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. Its fleets combined every arm of the Imperium in battlegroups, and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Ultima Founding]] were distributed to every Chapter. The crusade remains the largest single military undertaking since the Great Crusade.

Command

In Imperial doctrine, the Emperor is the commander of every Imperial force, and all oaths are sworn to him. In practice, for ten thousand years, the forces of the Imperium were directed by the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The [[Lord Commander Militant]] and the [[Lord High Admiral]], who often sit among the High Lords, command the [[Astra Militarum]] and the Navy. The [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]], the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] and the [[Inquisition]] each answer to their own masters, and their co-operation with the rest of the Imperium depends on agreement, oath and custom rather than on command.

For a great campaign the High Lords appoint a supreme commander. Such commanders have been styled [[Lord Solar]], Lord Militant or [[Warmaster]] of a crusade, and the most famous of them, [[Macharius|Lord Solar Macharius]], led the [[Macharian Crusade]] of 392–399.M41. Since the Heresy, the title of Warmaster of the whole Imperium has never been revived. The return of [[Roboute Guilliman]] in 999.M41 restored a single commander over all the armed forces for the first time since the Heresy. He is styled not Warmaster but [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]].

Recruitment and supply

Most Imperial soldiers come from the [[Imperial tithe]], under which every world must provide a share of its population as soldiers, sailors and [[Psyker|psykers]]. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] raises regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] from these tithes and supplies them across the Imperium. The [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] recruit from their own homeworlds and recruiting worlds; the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] recruits its [[Skitarii]] from its [[Forge world|forge worlds]]; and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] draws its Sisters from the [[Schola Progenium]] and its own convents. The officers of the [[Militarum Tempestus]], the commissars of the [[Commissar|Officio Prefectus]] and many [[Adepta Sororitas|Sisters of Battle]] are orphans of Imperial servants raised in the Schola Progenium.

Strength

No complete count of the Imperial Armed Forces exists. The [[Administratum|Departmento Munitorum]] keeps records of millions of [[Astra Militarum]] regiments, and its estimates of the men under arms run to many billions. The [[Adeptus Astartes]] number roughly a thousand [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], perhaps one or two million warriors in all. The Navy fields many thousands of warships across its five [[Segmentum|segmentum]] fleets. To these must be added the forces of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]], the [[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]] and every world's own defence force.Munitorum figures are notoriously unreliable. Regiments destroyed in distant wars may remain on its rolls for centuries.
