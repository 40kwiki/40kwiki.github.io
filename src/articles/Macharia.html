Macharia is a [[shrine world]] of the [[Imperial Cult]]. It is one of the sacred worlds of the Ecclesiarchy, listed with [[Terra]], [[Hagia]], [[Herodor]], [[Ophelia VII]] and [[Benediction]] as a destination of pilgrimage.

The world is named in the same region of Imperial history as the Lord Solar [[Macharius]]. Its sacred status is recorded in the Ecclesiarchy's registers, and it is visited by pilgrims of the Imperial Cult.
