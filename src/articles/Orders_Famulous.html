The Orders Famulous are one of the non-militant Orders of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. Their members serve among the households of the Imperium's noble families as consuls and chamberlains. They broker alliances, trade deals and marriages between noble houses, and they use their diplomatic skill to keep the peace between rival households. The Sisters Famulous are skilled diplomats, able to reconcile the most bitter of rivals, though their true mission is often performed entirely behind the scenes. Where they have worked over several millennia, tithes have been maintained or increased and trade has improved, so they are in high demand among noble families across the Imperium.

Unlike most Orders of the Sisterhood, the Famulous are trained in etiquette, critical thinking and the economics of interstellar trade, as well as in devotional study and service. A Novice Sister who shows strong communication skills and the ability to stay calm under pressure may be drafted into the Order. Once trained, she is sent to a noble house or trade consortium to advise its rulers. The nobles see the Sister's role as helping their house. The Sisterhood intends her to steer a noble's decisions so that his work serves the Imperium as well as his house.

Service in the noble households

The Sisters Famulous serve the highest nobility as chamberlains and counsellors. Their influence is considerable, because the nobles they advise rule whole worlds and control the fates of billions. The Sisters are skilled enough to reconcile the most bitter of rivals, and they often perform their true missions entirely behind the scenes. Through their arranging of alliances and marriages they take a direct hand in the spiritual and genetic purity of Humanity. The Order does not openly discuss this influence, even within the Sisterhood, though many Sisters keep extensive contact with the [[Ordo Hereticus]].

The Sisters Famulous keep extensive records of the bloodlines and commercial dealings of nearly every noble family in their assigned sector. These are combined with the records of the [[Orders Hospitaller]] and the [[Orders Dialogous]], which gives the Order a long history of the noble families of its region. Ordo Hereticus Inquisitors, particularly the [[Thorians]], find these records valuable. Tracing the bloodlines of known heretics has often uncovered links to the wider Imperial nobility.

Saints and Living Saints

Much of the Sisters' work involves the study of the manifestations of saints. They help to decide whether an individual should be beatified, and they investigate the appearance of [[Living Saint]]s. Because of their links with the Thorians and their knowledge of noble bloodlines, the Sisters have on several occasions predicted the arrival of a Living Saint long before it happened.

Organisation

Like the other non-militant Orders, the Orders Famulous are divided between two convents. The Convent Sanctorum on [[Ophelia VII]] holds the Order of the Key and the Order of the Gate. The Convent Prioris on [[Terra]] holds the Order of the Holy Seal and the Order of the Sacred Coin.

In the [[Calixis Sector]], [[Canoness]] Goneril has offered the Order's services to every major noble family, and few refuse, because the offer is held to be a sign of future prosperity. One exception is the Lucid Palace, where within thirty years the number of Sisters advising Sector Governor [[Marius Hax]] and his subordinates has dropped by a third, a trend Goneril finds disturbing. Legatine Christal of the Abbey of the Dawn, who is often away from her fortress on Iocanthos, has been seen re-establishing a mission on [[Malfi]] and arranging a marriage between Friedrich Krin of House Krin and Josi Cestelle of the Cestelle Alliance on [[Scintilla]].

Sisters Famulous wear the armoured robes of the non-militant Orders, often tailored to local dress. They are likelier to ask questions and listen than to smite the unbeliever at once.
