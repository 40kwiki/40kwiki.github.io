The Orders Famulous are one of the non-militant Orders of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. Their members serve among the households of the Imperium's noble families as consuls and chamberlains. They broker alliances, trade deals and marriages between noble houses, and they use their diplomatic skill to keep the peace between rival households. The Sisters Famulous are among the most influential members of the Sisterhood, although most of their work is done out of public view.

Unlike most Orders of the Sisterhood, the Famulous are trained in etiquette, critical thinking and the economics of interstellar trade, as well as in devotional study and service. A Novice Sister who shows strong communication skills and the ability to stay calm under pressure may be drafted into the Order. Once trained, she is sent to a noble house or trade consortium to advise its rulers.

Service in the noble households

The Sisters Famulous serve the highest nobility as chamberlains and counsellors. Their influence is considerable, because the nobles they advise rule whole worlds and control the fates of billions. The Sisters are skilled enough to reconcile the most bitter of rivals, and they often perform their true missions entirely behind the scenes. The Order does not openly discuss this influence, even within the Sisterhood.

The Sisters Famulous keep extensive records of the bloodlines and commercial dealings of nearly every noble family in their assigned sector. These are combined with the records of the [[Orders Hospitaller]] and the [[Orders Dialogous]], which gives the Order a long history of the noble families of its region. Ordo Hereticus Inquisitors, particularly the [[Thorians]], find these records valuable. Tracing the bloodlines of known heretics has often uncovered links to the wider Imperial nobility.

Saints and Living Saints

Much of the Sisters' work involves the study of the manifestations of saints. They help to decide whether an individual should be beatified, and they investigate the appearance of [[Living Saint]]s. Because of their links with the Thorians and their knowledge of noble bloodlines, the Sisters have on several occasions predicted the arrival of a Living Saint long before it happened.

Organisation

Like the other non-militant Orders, the Orders Famulous are divided between two convents. The Convent Sanctorum on [[Ophelia VII]] holds the Order of the Key and the Order of the Gate. The Convent Prioris on [[Terra]] holds the Order of the Holy Seal and the Order of the Sacred Coin.

In the [[Calixis Sector]], every noble house is served or watched by Sisters Famulous, and the Order has a long record there. Imperial records describe the Sisters as valued sources of information for the [[Ordo Hereticus]], which makes the Order part of the Inquisition's network of contacts within the nobility.
