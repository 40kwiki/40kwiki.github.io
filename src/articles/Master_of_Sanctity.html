The Master of Sanctity, also styled High Chaplain or First Chaplain, is the head of the Chaplaincy in a [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the head of the Chapter cult, its body of beliefs and customs. He is one of the Masters of the Chapter and oversees its spiritual health by assigning [[Chaplain]]s to the companies.

Directly beneath him stands the [[Reclusiarch]], head of the [[Reclusiam]]. In some Chapters the duties of the two offices are held by one man.
