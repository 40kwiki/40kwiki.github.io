The Callidus Temple, called the Callidus Clade at the time of the [[Horus Heresy]], is the temple of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] whose assassins, the Callidus Assassins, work by deceit. They are the most subtle of the Imperium's secret operatives. Almost all of them are women, and they use the shape-changing drug [[polymorphine]] to pass as any humanoid.

The temple is used where open interference by the Imperium would upset the balance kept by the [[High Lords of Terra]]. It is also the temple least understood by outsiders, since its use depends on secrecy.

History

The Callidus Clade was founded on [[Terra]] in the late M30, in the [[Great Crusade]], at the direction of [[Malcador the Sigillite]]. Malcador and the six founders of the first clades swore an oath on Mount Vengeance to eliminate the Emperor's enemies by any means. The founder of the clade was the first Director Primus, called Siress Callidus, and the identities of the founders were hidden even from one another.

In the Heresy, the six clades combined in an [[Execution Force]] to slay [[Horus]], 008.M31. The attempt on Dagonet failed. The Callidus operative in it, Koyne, was a shape-changer so long at the work that the original sex was forgotten.The date follows the article on the Execution Force.

After the Heresy, M'Shen of the temple reached [[Konrad Curze]] on [[Tsagualsa]]. The primarch, who foresaw her coming, let her pass unopposed to his throne room and was killed.

Later records are scattered. In the early M36 the rogue Callidus assassin Tziz Jarek, foremost among the agents of [[Goge Vandire]] in the Officio, used polymorphine to assume the identity of the Grand Master of Assassins, but killed only a decoy. The true Grand Master rallied the loyal assassins, and a battle within the Imperial Palace ended when he killed Jarek. In 872.M40 the assassin U'var cut the shield power of the palace on Tullok for a few seconds, which let a squad of Blood Angels Terminators teleport in and end a fifty-year defiance by the renegade governor Urlos Grador.

Methods

Most missions are against a [[Chaos cult]] or heretical group. The assassin infiltrates it, kills the leader and the key members, and so lets the regular forces finish the rest without an [[Exterminatus]]. On the battlefield she may kill an isolated patrol, take the form and armour of a victim and approach enemy commanders or psykers to kill them at the moment of greatest disorder.

Polymorphine alone lets an assassin pass as any human, and as an Aeldari or a Dark Aeldari. For other species, [[Officio Medicae]] surgeons have made implants of flex-cartilage and synthskin that lie dormant until the drug wakes them. These allow the shape of an Ork, a [[Kroot]] and, in one legendary case, a Genestealer hybrid. The implants are often permanent and bind the assassin to that one species.

Callidus assassins train for many Terran years in secret martial arts. They prefer small, concealable weapons and can kill unarmed.

Wargear

The common kit is a synskin bodyglove that defeats passive sensors and holds hidden pockets. The signature weapon is the C'tan phase sword of necrodermis, which phases through any protection, including energy shields. They also carry poison needles able to drop an Ogryn, and the neural shredder, which tears apart a victim's nerve paths in a short cone. Some leave a psychoplas death card that shows the victim's face and screams when touched.
