Execution Force
Operation of the [[Officio Assassinorum]]
Organisation[[Officio Assassinorum]]
PurposeElimination of a designated target
First recorded008.M31, against [[Horus]]

An Execution Force is a group of [[Officio Assassinorum|Assassinorum]] operatives assembled to kill one named target. It is the Assassinorum's largest form of operation. Its members are drawn from several assassin cults and given a single objective, and it is recalled once the target is dead or the attempt has failed.

The Execution Force is known in Imperial records chiefly for the attempt on [[Horus]], which was proposed by [[Roboute Guilliman|Valdor]] during the war council of Terra and carried out on [[Dagonet]] in 008.M31.

Dagonet

[[Malcador the Sigillite]] and the loyalist council argued over the morality of such methods. The [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] ruled that there would be "no more shadows", but the strike was sent in any case. The Execution Force reached Dagonet and struck at [[Horus]] there. It failed, and the [[Warmaster]] escaped.The sources record the attempt but do not record the details of its planning. Accounts of its exact composition differ.
