A Dreadnought is a cybernetic combat walker of intermediate size, used by the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] as heavy infantry support for their companies. Its pilot is a Space Marine who has been too badly wounded to serve in the field, and who is interred within the machine's armoured body. Dreadnoughts bridge the gap between living warriors and the tanks of the [[Astra Militarum]].

The most common form of Dreadnought is built to carry heavy weapons and to walk into the fiercest fire. Chapters vary the design by their own doctrine, and a Dreadnought may be fitted with heavy cannons, flamers or close-combat weapons. Each machine is blessed by the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and tended by the [[Techmarine]]s of its Chapter.

Venerable Dreadnoughts

Dreadnoughts that have served for centuries are given the honorific of Venerable. Their pilots are the oldest and most respected of their Chapter, and the machine is often kept in the Chapter's reliquary when it is not in combat. The Imperium regards a Venerable Dreadnought as a living link to the Chapter's founding.
