The 31st millennium (M31) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M31 to 999.M31. It opened at the height of the [[Great Crusade]] with the Triumph of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], where [[Horus]] was named [[Warmaster]]. Within five years Horus had turned to [[Chaos]], and the [[Horus Heresy]] split the [[Space Marine Legions]] and ended at the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31, where the [[Emperor of Mankind]] was mortally wounded and placed on the [[Golden Throne]].

The rest of the millennium belongs to the recovery. The [[Great Scouring]] drove the [[Traitor Legions]] into the [[Eye of Terror]]; [[Roboute Guilliman]] wrote the [[Codex Astartes]], and the [[Second Founding]] broke the loyal Legions into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. The [[Primarch|primarchs]] who remained disappeared or fell one by one, and the [[High Lords of Terra]] took over the government. In 781.M31 [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] led the first [[Black Crusade]] out of the Eye. Imperial historians count this long recovery as the [[Time of Rebirth]].

Chronology

Dates for the Heresy years follow the dedicated articles on each campaign, which use the reconciled reckoning of the [[Logos Historica Verita]]. Many entries for the later centuries are only dated to the millennium.
