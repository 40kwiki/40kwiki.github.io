Bakka is a volcanic [[Mining world|mining world]] of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]] and the [[Segmentum Fortress]] of that segmentum. Its orbital shipyards, in use since the early years of the [[Great Crusade]], build capital ships for the [[Imperial Navy]], and the world is the headquarters of Battlefleet Bakka, also called Battlefleet Tempestus, which commands and supplies the Navy across the galactic south.

Bakka's fleet is best known for its arrival at the [[Battle of Macragge]] in 745.M41, where it caught the bio-ships of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] between two Imperial fleets. The world was also among those that fell to the heresy of [[Bucharis]] in the 36th millennium, and its yards have been attacked by the [[Red Corsairs]] and the [[Drukhari]].

History

Settlement and the Great Crusade

Bakka was settled during the [[Dark Age of Technology]] by colonists drawn by its mineral wealth.No date of settlement survives; it is described in Navy records only as "a relatively young world" at the time of its colonisation. The [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] began to build shipyards above it in the early [[30th millennium]], and Bakka became one of the main naval foundries of the southern Crusade.

During the [[Horus Heresy]], in about 006.M31, a combined force of [[Space Wolves]] and [[Ultramarines]] under the Ultramarines captain Cestus pursued the [[Word Bearers]] battleship Furious Abyss through the region of Bakka. The loyalists fought a traitor fleet of [[World Eaters]] and [[Thousand Sons]] ships and renegade vessels from Saturn in the attempt to stop the battleship before it reached [[Macragge]].The date is estimated from the vessel's launch shortly before the [[Battle of Calth]].

The Plague of Unbelief

Main article: [[Plague of Unbelief]]

In 300.M36, during the [[Age of Apostasy]], Bakka fell under the sway of the Apostate Cardinal [[Bucharis]] of Gathalamor, whose [[Plague of Unbelief]] held that the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] could no longer rule the galaxy. Bakka formed the southern limit of a pocket empire that stretched north as far as [[Fenris]]. The revolt was broken when Bucharis's forces failed in their assault on the [[The Fang|Fang]], the fortress of the Space Wolves, and the worlds of the heresy returned to the Imperium.

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

In 745.M41, during the [[First Tyrannic War]], Battlefleet Bakka was sent to help defend [[Macragge]], homeworld of the Ultramarines, against Hive Fleet Behemoth. It arrived as the defenders were breaking the Tyranid assault, trapped the bio-ships between itself and the Ultramarines fleet, and pursued the remnant to the system of Circe, where it was destroyed.

Later in the millennium the Chaos Lord [[Huron Blackheart]] and the Red Corsairs attacked the orbital yards to seize ships for their fleet. A [[Grey Knights]] strike cruiser under Grand Master Mordrak arrived in the middle of the battle and drove off the renegades, although Blackheart escaped.The date of the Red Corsairs' raid is not recorded; it occurred after the [[Badab War]] (901–912.M41). In 999.M41 Drukhari raiders struck the Navy's moorings above Bakka.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Bakka lies south of the [[Great Rift]], in the [[Imperium Sanctus]], and since c. 000.M42 has served as the main base for the resupply and repair of all Imperial fleets operating in the Segmentum Tempestus, including crusade fleets of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Geography

Bakka's surface consists of rafts of black basalt and granite floating on a sea of slowly cooling lava. Fresh magma bursts through the crust from time to time, throwing fountains of fire into the air. The atmosphere is hot and poisonous, a fog of nitrogen, sulphur and sodium oxides and ammonia. Tracked mining machines harvest ores when the convection of the lava sea brings them near the surface.

Permanent installations stand on the rafts, where ore is processed behind adamantium walls under roof-mounted laser batteries that destroy meteors and attackers. Most manufacturing, together with the yards and docks of the naval base, is in geostationary orbit.

Society

The lava mines are worked largely by penitents and convicted heretics, for whom the death rate is high. The Imperial Creed teaches that the penitent and the heretic suffer the same punishment, since even repented sin must be cleansed. The orbital population serves the Navy and the yards, and the world's government is in practice that of the segmentum command.
