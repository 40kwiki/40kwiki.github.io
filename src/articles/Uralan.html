Uralan is a world on which [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] recovered the [[Daemon|daemon]] sword [[Drach'nyen]]. Imperial accounts place the recovery in the same period as the [[First Black Crusade]], which Abaddon launched against the [[Cadian Gate]] in 781.M31.

The First [[Black Crusade]] failed, but it gave Abaddon the standing to lead the [[Traitor|Traitors]]. Imperial accounts record that his authority over the [[Traitor Legions]] grew after the recovery of the sword.
