The Shining Blades were a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] who were praised for the near-perfect purity of their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], together with the [[Red Scorpions]] and the Umbral Titans. Their record of victories convinced them that they could not fail. In the Berillia Massacres of 498.M34 their pride and the number of civilians killed in their campaigns led them to attack any who questioned them, and they became [[Chaos Space Marines|Heretic Astartes]] in the service of [[Slaanesh]].

They now call themselves the Flawless Host. They believe themselves the embodiment of justice and purity and the pinnacle of biological perfection, and have scarred the Imperium ever since as hedonistic servants of the Prince of Pleasure.

History

The Berillia Massacres

Before their fall the Shining Blades had an unshakeable faith in their own abilities, a result of the rigid mental training their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]'s purity encouraged. Over a dozen campaigns in the 498–601.M34 period they won a string of victories that became known as the Berillia Massacres, for the very high number of civilians who died. When the Chapter's methods were questioned it attacked its allies. The [[Red Scorpions]] came unheralded to bring them to justice, and in the war that followed the Chapter's real allegiance was shown. They became servants of [[Slaanesh]] and declared themselves the Flawless Host, an act of hubris for which the Red Scorpions swore punishment.Imperial sources give the Massacres as 498.M34 and also as a period lasting to 601.M34. The Chapter's turn is dated to the first year.

The Flawless Host

For a century the Host harried Loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], burned outposts and destroyed smaller detachments. It then seized the Red Scorpions strike cruiser Crux Puritatis as it returned from the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Tempestus]] and took the Chapter's dead to add to its stocks of uncorrupted gene-seed. The Red Scorpions' Lord High Commander Thay Kraun recalled all his combat units and searched the area. When the Host's asteroid fortress was found, he committed the whole Chapter to its destruction. The battle lasted six days, and the Red Scorpions retook the ship chamber by chamber. Kraun killed the Host's captain in single combat with the Blade of the Scorpion. The Host withdrew, vowing vengeance.

Later wars

A combined force of the Host and the [[Night Lords]] assaulted the [[Agri-world|agri-world]] Euthenia XX in 310.M41. The [[Chaos]] Lord Eleaxus the Flawless had his fealty bought by the [[Word Bearers]] [[Warmaster]] Davroth with the promise of the luxuries of Makenna VII, and led three squads of [[Emperor's Children]] [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] and [[Traitor]] Marines in Rhinos to take the world. In M42 the Silver Templars' new [[Chapter Master]] Akilios Zanaris found the people of Novaris enslaved by the Host. A quarter of the population died in a short war that ended when Captain Ducas killed the Host's leader Lyrius Soulslaver in a duel of more than a day. Novaris was given to the Silver Templars as their homeworld.

Organisation and beliefs

The Host's successor warbands are the Lashdrum Monarchs, the Luscious Few and the Villsid Skinsmen. Its warriors wear pink, purple and black.

Notable members

  • Eleaxus the Flawless: Chaos Lord of the Host.
  • Lyrius Soulslaver: leader killed at Novaris.
