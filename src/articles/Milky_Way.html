The Milky Way, in Imperial usage simply the galaxy, is the spiral of stars in which [[Terra]] lies and across which the [[Imperium of Mankind]] extends. Imperial science reckons it some 100,000 light years across, with several hundred thousand million stars. The Imperium claims rule over about a million worlds within it, but the [[Administratum]] admits that only a small fraction of the galaxy's systems have ever been charted, and that most of the galaxy has never been visited by any human vessel.

Imperial maps divide the galaxy into five [[Segmentum|segmenta]] centred on Terra: the [[Segmentum Solar]], the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] to the galactic north, the [[Ultima Segmentum]] to the east, the [[Segmentum Tempestus]] to the south and the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] to the west. Beyond them lie frontier regions such as the [[Halo Stars]] of the outer rim, the [[Eastern Fringe]] and the [[Ghoul Stars]]. Travel between the stars depends on the [[Warp]], and the reach of the Imperium has always been limited by the reach of the [[Astronomican]], the psychic beacon by which [[Navigator|Navigators]] steer.

The galaxy has been shaped by its warp storms. The [[Eye of Terror]], born in the Fall of the [[Aeldari]] around the 30th millennium, was for ten thousand years the gateway of the [[Traitor Legions]]. In 999.M41 the [[Great Rift]] opened from the Eye of Terror to the [[Hadex Anomaly]] and cut the galaxy in two, dividing the Imperium into the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. Its crossings, such as the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], are now among the most fought-over places in the galaxy.

Name and cartography

The name "Milky Way" comes from the language of ancient Terra, where the galaxy's disc was seen as a pale band across the night sky. In Imperial records the word galaxy is used alone, and the High Gothic Galaxia appears in older charts.

The standard Imperial chart of the galaxy is the Imperius Dominatus, maintained by the [[Astra Cartographica]], an office of the [[Administratum]] working with the [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[Navis Nobilite]]. It places Terra at the centre and names the directions of the compass from it, so that "north" means towards the Segmentum Obscurus and "east" towards Ultima. This is a convention of administration and not of astronomy. Terra lies far from the true centre of the galaxy, whose dense star-fields lie to the east of the Segmentum Solar.

Charts are made from the records of Navy patrols, [[Rogue Trader]] voyages and [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] [[Explorator]] fleets. Because warp travel distorts both distance and time, two charts of the same region seldom agree, and the Cartographica keeps many contradictory surveys of the same worlds.The Cartographica's own preface to the Imperius Dominatus warns that "no chart of the void is true for longer than the voyage that made it". Since the opening of the Great Rift, every chart of the northern galaxy has had to be revised.

History

Before Mankind

Mankind is a young species in an old galaxy. Aeldari tradition and the records of the [[Inquisition]] hold that, some sixty million Terran years ago, the galaxy was fought over in the [[War in Heaven]] between the [[Old Ones]] and the [[Necrontyr]], who became the [[Necrons]].Imperial dates before the Dark Age of Technology are estimates only. Figures for the War in Heaven are taken from Ordo Xenos digests of Aeldari tradition. The Old Ones are credited with the [[Webway]], with the creation of the Aeldari and, in some accounts, of the [[Orks]]. The Necrons, defeated, retreated into stasis on their [[Tomb world|tomb worlds]], where many slept until the late 41st millennium.

For millions of years afterwards the Aeldari Empire ruled much of the galaxy from the core outwards. Its fall, around the 30th millennium, brought the birth of [[Slaanesh]] and the creation of the Eye of Terror where the Aeldari heartland had been.

The age of human expansion

Humanity left the Sol System in the [[Dark Age of Technology]], from about M15 to about M25. With the warp drive and the first Navigators, human colony fleets reached across the galaxy, guided by the [[Standard Template Construct]] systems that let any colony build what it needed. Records of the age speak of settlement as far as the galactic core and the Halo Stars. The [[Men of Iron]] revolt of about M23 and the [[Age of Strife]] that followed, from about M25 to M30, shattered this realm. Warp storms isolated whole regions for thousands of years.

The Great Crusade and after

The [[Emperor of Mankind]] launched the [[Great Crusade]] from Terra in 798.M30 to reunite the scattered worlds. The crusade reached every segmentum and penetrated the Eastern Fringe as far as [[Ultramar]], but it never charted the whole galaxy. The [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) ended it. During the Heresy the [[Ruinstorm]] divided the galaxy between Terra and Ultramar for several years.

The [[Segmentum|segmenta]] were fixed in the reforms that followed the Heresy, about 015–021.M31. For the next ten thousand years the Imperium held roughly the same bounds, losing worlds to warp storms, rebellion and xenos and regaining others through crusades such as the [[Macharian Crusade]] (392–399.M41).

The Great Rift

In 999.M41, at the climax of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] and the [[Fall of Cadia]], the Great Rift tore across the galaxy. The [[Noctis Aeterna]] that followed blinded astropaths and Navigators across the Imperium, and when it lifted the Astronomican could no longer be seen north of the Rift. [[Roboute Guilliman]] launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]] around 000.M42 to restore contact with the lost half.

Regions

The Segmenta

Imperial space is divided into the five segmenta, each a military command with its own fortress world and fleet. They are divided in turn into [[Sector|sectors]] and [[Sub-sector|sub-sectors]]. The segmenta are described in their own article.

The galactic core

The core of the galaxy is a region of crowded stars, intense radiation and unstable warp currents. The Astronomican is faint there, and the Navy's charts show it largely blank. Imperial trade routes pass around it, not through it. The Jericho-Maw Warp Gate, found in the 41st millennium, links the [[Calixis Sector]] to the [[Jericho Reach]] on the far side of the core and opened the way for the [[Achilus Crusade]] in 777.M41. The core is the home of the [[Leagues of Votann]], whose ancestors were sent there in the Dark Age of Technology. Their Kindreds returned to Imperial notice in force in the early [[Era Indomitus]]. The [[Necrontyr]] are believed to have arisen near the core.

The Halo Stars

The Halo Stars are the ancient stars at the outermost edge of the galaxy, beyond which lies the intergalactic void. They are thinly scattered, poorly charted and dangerous, the haunt of pirates, renegades and unknown xenos. Few vessels that go far into them return. The [[Koronus Expanse]], beyond the [[Calixis Sector]] of the Segmentum Obscurus, is the best-known region of the Halo Stars and has been opened to [[Rogue Trader|Rogue Traders]] since the 40th millennium.

The Eastern Fringe

The [[Eastern Fringe]] is the frontier beyond the Ultima Segmentum, so far from Terra that the Astronomican barely reaches it. It contains the [[T'au Empire]], first catalogued in 789.M35, and it was the first part of the galaxy struck by the [[Tyranids]] in 745.M41. The Hadex Anomaly, the eastern end of the Great Rift, lies within it.

The Ghoul Stars

The Ghoul Stars, also called the Ghost Stars, are a region of the outer galaxy beyond the north-eastern reaches of the Ultima Segmentum. The Astronomican is all but invisible there, and Navigators refuse to enter it. The region contains many dead worlds, once settled by humans and destroyed by some unknown power. Fragmentary records of the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] in M34 describe a threat from the Ghoul Stars called the Pale Wasting, but most of them were destroyed.The Novamarines claim the defeat of the Pale Wasting as one of their earliest honours, but their own chronicles do not describe it. The Necron world of Drazak lies within the region. The [[Death Spectres]] Chapter keeps watch on its border. In 990.M41 [[Helbrecht|High Marshal Helbrecht]] of the [[Black Templars]] led a crusade into the Ghoul Stars, which was recalled in 998.M41 to fight in the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]].

The Veiled Region

The [[Veiled Region]] on the southern and south-eastern edge of the galaxy is a region of warp storms and nebulae where many vessels have vanished. Its storms are thought to hide Ork empires and other powers that the Imperium has never charted.

Warp storms and rifts

The galaxy is scarred by regions where the [[Warp]] spills into realspace. The largest are the Eye of Terror in the Segmentum Obscurus and the [[Maelstrom]] in the Ultima Segmentum. Lesser storms come and go, sometimes cutting off sectors for centuries. The Hadex Anomaly appeared in the [[Jericho Reach]] in 656.M40. The Great Rift of 999.M41 joined many of these regions into a single scar, the Cicatrix Maledictum, which now runs across the whole galaxy.

The Imperium has very few safe routes across the Rift. The [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], between [[Vigilus]] and [[Sangua Terra]], is the best known. Other crossings have been reported but not confirmed.

Other powers

The Imperium shares the galaxy with many other powers. The [[Aeldari]] survive in their craftworlds and in the Webway. The [[Orks]] are spread across the whole galaxy and gather in [[Waaagh!|Waaaghs]] that threaten entire sectors. The [[Necrons]] have woken on tomb worlds in every segmentum. The [[Tyranids]] came from outside the galaxy, beginning with [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] in 745.M41, and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] rose from beneath the galactic plane in 997.M41. The [[T'au Empire]] grows on the Eastern Fringe, and the [[Leagues of Votann]] hold the core. The forces of [[Chaos]] have held the Eye of Terror since the Heresy and have spread along the Great Rift.
