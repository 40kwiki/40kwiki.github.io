The Death Korps of Krieg are the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised on [[Krieg]], a world made uninhabitable by five centuries of atomic civil war. Masked, greatcoated and fatalistic, they are the Imperium's foremost siege troops and are prized for their willingness to accept any loss to achieve an objective. Their people believe that they must atone with their lives for their world's rebellion against the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] in 433.M40.

The Death Korps' best known campaign is the [[Siege of Vraks]] (813–830.M41), in which the 88th Siege Army, composed almost entirely of Krieg regiments, retook the armoury world of [[Vraks]] from the Apostate Cardinal [[Xaphan]] after seventeen years of trench warfare and the loss of some fourteen million Guardsmen.

History

The rebellion of Krieg

In 433.M40 the High Autocrat of Krieg's largest hive, chairman of its Council of Autocrats and in effect its governor, declared the planet independent of the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] and renounced the Emperor. His name has been struck from Imperial records. Colonel Jurten of the Krieg 83rd regiment refused to accept the secession. Rather than see the world fall to traitors, he unleashed the planet's atomic arsenals against the rebel hives, beginning a civil war that lasted five hundred years.

The war left the surface a poisoned wasteland. The loyalists survived in underground bunkers and fought on until, in 949.M40, Krieg was finally returned to the Imperium. The survivors, born and raised in a world of war, offered their entire population to the Imperium as soldiers in penance for the sin of their ancestors.

The Death Korps

From 949.M40 the [[Departmento Munitorum]] has raised the Death Korps from Krieg. Because the planet's population cannot sustain the demand, its soldiers are grown in vat-cloning facilities and raised in martial creches, and the world's only purpose is to make soldiers for the Imperium.The vat-growing of Krieg soldiers is attested in Munitorum records but is not acknowledged in all official accounts. Early deployments include the reconquest of Baran by the 127th regiment in 224.M41.

The Siege of Vraks

In 812.M41 the 88th Siege Army was assembled from Krieg regiments to retake Vraks Prime, which had fallen to Xaphan and his followers, and in 813.M41 it began the siege. Over seventeen years the Krieg regiments advanced trench line by trench line against the citadel. Thirty-four regiments were committed in all. In 830.M41 the 261st Siege Regiment under Colonel Tyborc was given the task of taking the citadel's main gates in the first assault, and after two days of fighting the 269th took over the final assault. The planet was retaken, a victory the Officio Militaris itself calls pyrrhic: some fourteen million Guardsmen died, and the forces of [[Chaos]] that the siege had drawn to Vraks were destroyed only with the aid of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

Later campaigns

Between 936 and 946.M41 Krieg regiments served as honour guard for a Munitorum investigation into the hive world of Derondis, which had withheld its tithes. In 992.M41 they fought the [[Necrons]] of the Maynarkh Dynasty in the Orphean War in the Segmentum Tempestus, and held a position on the forge world of Fecundia against [[Hive Fleet Kraken]]. In 998.M41 the 19th Armoured fought the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] at Taros, and Krieg regiments joined the Third War for Armageddon. In 999.M41 the 993rd under Colonel Trymon Stagler defended Tarsis Ultra against a splinter of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]].

Era Indomitus

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Krieg regiments were attached in great numbers to the fleets of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], where their siege skills were used to reduce fortified worlds held by traitors. Their reputation for accepting losses has made them a preferred force for the costliest assaults of the [[Era Indomitus]].

Organisation and doctrine

Krieg regiments are organised for siege warfare into Line Korps, Assault Korps and Bombardment Korps, which together form a siege army. Their regiments include siege infantry, siege artillery, tank regiments, engineers, grenadiers and the mounted Death Riders, who ride gene-bred steeds that can survive poisoned air. Doctrine favours methodical trench warfare, saturation bombardment and relentless assault, and accepts casualties that other regiments would refuse.

Krieg soldiers show little individual personality, rarely remove their masks before outsiders and speak of death as a duty. Their commissars and officers treat failure to advance as the only real crime. Krieg Quartermasters move among the wounded to recover usable equipment and to grant the mercy of a quick death to those who cannot be saved.

Equipment and appearance

The Death Korps wear long greatcoats, rebreather masks and distinctive helmets, protection against the poisoned air of their homeworld and the gas and fallout of the battlefield. Infantry carry the long-pattern Lucius lasgun with a bayonet. Their regiments are supplied in large part by the forge world [[Lucius]].
