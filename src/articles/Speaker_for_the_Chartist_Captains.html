The Speaker for the Chartist Captains is the official who commands the merchant fleets and speaks for the [[Chartist Captain]]s among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The merchant vessels make up most of the Imperial fleet, so in a great war the Speaker's weight can match that of the [[Lord High Admiral]]. The Speaker is among the richest people in the Imperium and keeps a fortress, the Nexus Axiomatic, on [[Terra]].

[[Kelsi Demidov]] sat on the [[Council of Terra]] in the closing years of the [[Horus Heresy]]. [[Juskina Tull]] held the office in 544.M32. At the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 it was held by Kania Dhanda, who afterwards lost her seat among the High Lords to the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Mar Av Ashariel]] when [[Roboute Guilliman]] turned the council toward military affairs, but kept the office of Speaker. She is the present Speaker in the records consulted.

History

Heresy (005–014.M31)

Demidov carried herself as one used to command, and also listened. She sat in the [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]] for a short time before it was disbanded, and attended the council in the first days of the [[Siege of Terra]].

War of the Beast (544–546.M32)

Tull raised the price of passage between the [[Segmentum|segmentae]] while refugees fled the war. After an Ork attack moon appeared over [[Terra]] she ousted [[Lansung]] with the help of the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Erekart Mesring]], then led a volunteer fleet of civilians and merchant ships called the Proletarian Crusade against [[Luna|the moon]]. It failed, and her ascendancy ended with it. She later backed [[Koorland]] against [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Udin Macht Udo]].

Era Indomitus (999.M41 onward)

Dhanda supported the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]] in his Dissolution Act, and joined the cabal of [[Inquisitor|Lord Inquisitor]] [[Adamara Rassilo]] and the [[Master of the Astronomican]] [[Leops Franck]] to bring a haemonculus to Terra. When the haemonculus ran amok she withdrew. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] later gave her rotating seat to the [[Lord Commander Militant]].
