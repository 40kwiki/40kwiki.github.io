A Rune Priest is a specialist officer found only in the [[Space Wolves]] [[Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. The Rune Priest is the Space Wolves' counterpart to the [[psychic]] [[Librarian]]s of other Chapters. Their powers are channelled through the ritual forms of the native shamanic traditions of the barbarian tribes of [[Fenris]].

Rune Priests are also the keepers of the Chapter's oral history. These secrets pass from one Rune Priest to the next. Each generation teaches the young skalds the complex and archaic chants of runic lore. Their [[Power Armour]] is marked with Fenrisian runes, and they wear runic fangs as pendants and badges of office. The runes are not only decoration. They channel and concentrate the Rune Priest's psychic powers.

History

The role dates to the [[Great Crusade]] in the last two centuries of the 30th millennium (late M30). The Rune Priests were one of several divisions of the Priests of Fenris, the interlinked orders that supplied the physical, spiritual and technological needs of the [[VI Legion]]. These orders were more than technicians. They held the true history of the Legion and were the masters of its lore.

One of the prominent divisions was the Casters of Runes, a warrior-mystic order of psykers who regarded themselves as a breed apart from the [[Librarian]]s of the other Legions. The Casters used a deliberate shield of cognitive discipline to reach for their power. They also wore a lattice of psycho-memetic field technology in their armour. This protected them from hostile forces and from drawing too much power from the [[Empyrean]]. They used runic matrices and adapted Fenrisian rituals. They gave the VI Legion prognostication and protection against [[Warp]] threats.

The bygone rank of the Legions evolved into the present-day Rune Priest of the Space Wolves.The line of descent from the Casters of Runes to the modern Rune Priests is given in the sources without a date. No exact year for the change is recorded.

Role

Among the priesthoods of the Space Wolves, the Rune Priests are the most arcane. They are distant and mysterious figures. They are learned in Fenrisian shamanism, and their powers are used to shield the Chapter and to reveal portents of doom and glory.

Powers and wargear

In battle the Rune Priests are masters of the storm. They can call a blizzard to drive shards of ice into their enemies, summon lightning from the sky, or open the rocky jaws of [[Fenris]] beneath their feet. Nobody can say how the gift of elemental command appears. Some Fenrisian tribes hold that all their people are sons of the storm, and the Chapter's warriors care only that the Rune Priests serve with honour.

A Rune Priest carries a runic weapon, often a staff or an axe, graven from end to end with runes that glow red-hot as psychic power runs through it. He also carries talismans, totems and fetishes. The runes on the fangs of the [[Fenrisian Wolf|wolves of Fenris]] are the most potent of these, and the fangs are used to divine the fate of men. Some Rune Priests are accompanied by a psybernetic familiar, usually a raven, whose augmetic relays give its master a psychic view of the battlefield.

Unlike the Librarians of other Chapters, Rune Priests draw on the rune magic of the Fenrisian shamans. Their powers work as a gestalt, and grow stronger the more of them are gathered in one place.

Notable Rune Priests

[[Njal Stormcaller]], called the Tempest that Walks, is the Chapter's greatest Rune Priest and is accompanied by his psyber-raven Nightwing. Irnist the Wise, twin brother of [[Erik Morkai]], advises the [[Great Wolf]] [[Logan Grimnar]]. [[Torvald]] of Red Kraken Hold is recorded among the Chapter's first Rune Priests, having fought beside [[Leman Russ]] in the [[Great Crusade]]. In the [[Horus Heresy]] era, [[Ohthere Wyrdmake]] befriended [[Ahriman]] of the [[Thousand Sons]] to learn the tenets of that Legion's sorcery. Imperial accounts link his testimony to the ban on Librarians at the [[Council of Nikaea]], and record that Ahriman killed him once the Space Wolves' manipulation by [[Chaos]] was understood.
