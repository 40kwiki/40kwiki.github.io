The Jericho Reach, once known as the Jericho Sector, is a region of space on the [[Eastern Fringe]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It was part of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from the [[Great Crusade]] onwards, until it fell into isolation, decay and corruption in the [[Age of Apostasy]]. It was formally dissolved as a sector in 889.M36, and it has been lost to the Imperium for more than four thousand standard years.

Many of its worlds were tainted by [[Chaos]], and others were infested by [[Xenos|xenos]]. At the heart of the Reach, a domain of [[Daemon|Daemon Princes]] and Chaos Lords is said to have grown up, where the laws of the [[Warp]] rather than those of [[Realspace|realspace]] govern space and time. The [[Achilus Crusade]], launched in 777.M41, is the Imperium's attempt to retake the Reach, and it has been fought on several fronts ever since.

History

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The volume of space later called the Jericho Reach was conquered during the [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30–005.M31), by Imperial fleets chiefly from the [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Night Lords]] Legions. The region was sparsely settled by pre-Imperial Humanity, but many of its systems had life-sustaining worlds, and the Crusade set up a sector government there.The Great Crusade dates are standard for the period. The sector's earliest administrative records do not survive.

Age of Apostasy

Further information: [[Age of Apostasy]]

The fall of the Jericho Sector followed the seizure of power by [[Goge Vandire]] during the [[Age of Apostasy]] (c. 100–600.M36). Vandire placed the sector under two puppet governors, the Lady Sector Castagar Murella and the Arch-cardinal Udo Asterus, whose authority was enforced by the Navis Imperialis and the [[Frateris Templar]]. Local commanders resisted, and the sector was cut off by [[Warp storm|warp storms]] soon afterwards.

The Age of Shadows

From M37 to M41, darkness and dissolution spread through the sector. The [[Administratum]] declared the sector a political entity no more in 889.M36, and the region reverted to the name Jericho Reach. Its records are fragmentary, and the [[Deathwatch]] has maintained the only continuous Imperial presence there.

In 380–405.M37 an Ork freebooter fleet of more than one hundred voidships ravaged the north of the Reach, attacking Castobel, Aurum and Alphos. Deathwatch [[Librarian|Codicier]] Kiataino destabilised the Ork forces before they could settle, and they were driven off.

The Achilus Crusade

Main article: [[Achilus Crusade]]

The discovery of the stable [[Jericho-Maw]] [[Warp]] Gate, which links the [[Calixis Sector]] to the Reach, made a crusade possible. The [[High Lords of Terra]] ordered the Achilus Crusade in 777.M41 under [[Tiber Achilus]], a [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]] who did not live to see its first decade out. The crusade was mustered from the [[Astra Militarum]], the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the [[Adepta Sororitas]] and a large Navis Imperialis fleet, and it was launched under strict secrecy from a staging point far from the Reach.

The Canis Salient opened the Greyhell Front in 785.M41 against the T'au-held domains of the [[Velk'Han Sept]] in the Black Reef. The crusade has been fought over three fronts since, and it has been met by the Hadex Anomaly, the [[Chaos]] domains of the Reach and the newly arrived [[Hive Fleet Dagon]], which seeks to consume every inhabited world in the Reach.

Geography

The Reach is organised around the Well of Night, a region of unstable space at the Warp Gate, and the Iron Collar, a belt of reclaimed systems that the crusade holds. Its salients are named for the Imperial commands that advance into them. The Orpheus Salient runs towards the northern edge of the Reach, the Acheros Salient lies near the Hadex Anomaly, and the Canis Salient pushes south towards the Black Reef.

The Hadex Anomaly is a region of warped space in the Reach's centre. Its stars lie in a dense red nebula, and Imperial ships have been lost there in large numbers. The Cellebos Warzone and the Charon Stars are the most heavily fought areas of the Reach, and the Outer Reach is largely unexplored.

Notable worlds

[[Erioch]] is a system of the Deathwatch, whose [[Watch Fortress Erioch]] holds the Reach's records and its Meageran Data-Looms. [[Vanir]] is a system of the Orpheus Salient. [[Velk'Han]] is the T'au-held sept of the Black Reef, and [[Castobel]] and [[Aurum]] were the Ork targets of 380–405.M37.

The Blood Trinity is a named system in the Cellebos Warzone, where the crusade's campaigns have been among its most costly.

Governance

There is no sector government in the Reach. The [[Achilus Crusade]] is run by a [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]] under the [[High Lords of Terra]], and each salient is governed by the [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commander]] who holds it. Civil rule in reclaimed worlds falls to the [[Administratum]], which appoints governors as worlds are retaken.

The Deathwatch keeps its own watch in the Reach from [[Watch Fortress Erioch]], and its Battle-Brothers act with the crusade under their own command. The [[Inquisition]] is present through agents of the [[Ordo Malleus]] and the [[Ordo Hereticus]], who investigate the Chaos domains.

Military

The crusade's forces include the [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]] regiments of the Astra Militarum, [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] companies of several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], [[Adepta Sororitas]] convents and a Navis Imperialis [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]]. The [[T'au Empire]] has also sent fleets into the Reach, and it has contested the [[Velk'Han|Velk'Han Sept]] with the Imperium since 785.M41.

[[Hive Fleet Dagon]], a [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] force that entered the Reach from the void, has forced the crusade to fight on a further front against the Great Devourer.
