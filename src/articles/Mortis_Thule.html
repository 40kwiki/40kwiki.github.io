The Mortis Thule is a notorious [[space hulk]] in the [[Jericho Reach]]. It is known even on primitive worlds that have never left their own system. Its dark form blots out local suns, and it has caused civilisations to fall into barbarism or new faiths to rise.

Names

[[Navigator]]s call it "The Doom that Awaits". The [[Deathwatch]] records it as Warp Artefact Aleph-Omicron-002. In the [[Achilus Crusade]] and the [[Orpheus Salient]] it is called the Mortis Thule, or "Death From Beyond the Horizon". It is the largest space hulk known.

Danger

Its appearances are sudden and catastrophic, and it has destroyed countless ships.
