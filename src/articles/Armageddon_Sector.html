The Armageddon Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Solar]], lying to the galactic north-east of [[Terra]]. It is named for the Hive World of [[Armageddon]], which lies at its centre and is the fourth planet of its star's ten-planet system. Armageddon is one of the Imperium's great industrial powers, and its weapon factories have supplied the [[Astra Militarum]] for millennia. Its strategic importance has made it the target of several major invasions.

The sector's wars are among the best known in the Imperium. The [[Wars for Armageddon]] were fought in the First War, the Second War and the Third War against the forces of [[Chaos]] and the [[Orks]]. The Third War, fought against the [[Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka|Warlord Ghazghkull Thraka]], was the largest Ork invasion recorded in the Imperium's history.

History

Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

The planet of Armageddon has been one of the Imperium's main industrial worlds for millennia. Its vast [[Hive world|hive cities]] produce arms and materiel in great quantity, and its stockpiles have made it a target. The hive world is governed by an Overlord, who answers to the [[Administratum]], and it maintains a large [[Planetary Defence Force]].

First War for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

The First War for Armageddon is the least well known of the three. It began with a wave of strange events and armed rebellion in the hive cities, which were quickly put down on the continent of Armageddon Secundus but proved harder to end on Armageddon Prime. The Planetary Defence Force was caught unprepared when the space hulk Devourer of Souls entered the system carrying a Chaos army.The First War is dated 444.M41 in the Armageddon records. A Calixian chronicle gives 474.M41, which is treated as a disputed record.

The forces of the [[World Eaters Legion]], led by [[Angron]], invaded the planet in the course of the war. Angron was halted at the river lines of Armageddon Prime, which later became the defensive positions the Imperium held in later wars.

Second War for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

The Second War for Armageddon began in 941.M41, when [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] invaded the world at the head of a massive [[Waaagh!|Ork WAAAGH!]] At that time Armageddon was ruled by Overlord [[Herman von Strab]], whose failings allowed Ghazghkull to conquer the subcontinent of Armageddon Prime more easily than Angron had. Ghazghkull's forces overran the hive city of Infernus and, after bitter fighting, the hive city of [[Helsreach]] fell to the [[Orks]].

Third War for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

The Imperium believed Ghazghkull defeated, but on the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension, 998.M41, his hordes landed on Armageddon once more. The Third War was the greatest of the Armageddon wars. Ghazghkull led an Ork WAAAGH! many times larger than any before it, and his fleets smashed the defending Imperial fleet and landed across the planet's surface.

The defenders of Armageddon were better prepared than before, but the war was long and costly. The [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armies]] of the sector, supported by several [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]], fought the Orks across the hive cities and the ash wastes, and the war lasted well into the [[Era Indomitus]].

Geography

The sector surrounds the star that gives [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]] its name. Its worlds are arranged as in a normal system, with inner worlds of ash and industry and outer worlds of ice and ports. The Armageddon Sub-sector is the industrial heart of the region, and the Golgotha Sub-sector and the Voss Sub-sector hold the sector's outer worlds.

Notable worlds

[[Armageddon]] is the [[Hive world|Hive World]] at the centre of the sector and the industrial hub of both the Armageddon [[Sub-sector]] and the wider region. [[Pelucidar]] is a sub-surface jungle world on the sector's outer edge, and [[St. Jowen's Dock]] is an [[Imperial Navy]] facility in the outer system. [[Chosin]] is an ice world that has been infested by [[Orks]], and [[Mannheim]] is a monitor station on the system's outer edge.

Governance

The Overlord of [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]] rules the [[Hive world|hive world]], and the [[Administratum]]'s sector registers record its tithe. The [[Imperial Navy]] keeps its facilities at St. Jowen's Dock and Mannheim, and the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Planetary Defence Force]] are raised from the sector's worlds.

Military

The sector's defence is shared between the [[Planetary Defence Force]], the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Imperial Navy]]. Its regiments fought through the Third War alongside [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] that were sent to the sector.
