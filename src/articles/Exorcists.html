The Exorcists are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] created in the [[Thirteenth Founding]], the obscure "Dark Founding" of the late 35th or early 36th millennium, and are the second known Chapter of that Founding beside the [[Death Spectres]]. Their homeworld is Banish. They are famous for an incorruptibility against daemonic influence that is matched by few outside the [[Grey Knights]], and they serve as one of the Imperium's foremost forces against the [[Daemon|daemonic]].

The source of that resistance is one of the Imperium's best-kept secrets. Every Exorcist is deliberately possessed by a lesser daemon during his initiation and then exorcised, a process overseen by the Plutonians, a radical sect of the [[Ordo Malleus]] that has lived on Banish for millennia. Were it known, many Puritan servants of the Imperium would denounce the Chapter as heretics.

History

Founding

The records of the Dark Founding are scant, and those concerning the Exorcists are not merely lost but have never been open even to the highest ranks of the Imperium; most archives on the Chapter are closed or missing. Officially its gene-seed descends from the Imperial Fists, but the [[Inquisition]] has sealed the question.The Founding can be dated only to the turn of M35 and M36.

Totem IV and the Plutonians

The Chapter's nature changed when it was sent to free the daemon-ravaged world of Totem IV from a host of [[Tzeentch]]ian Warp-spawn. The Exorcists advanced through the cities of the planet, resisting every assault the daemons made on their minds, and the greater daemon Keriath the Shrouded, angered by their resistance, cast its whole essence into the mind of Chapter Master Enoch Trismegistus. He was possessed, but he endured, and the ordeal taught him the weaknesses of his foe and awakened a latent psychic gift.

The Plutonians, who had planted an informant among the Chapter's servants, learned of the possession and used their Ordo Malleus authority to relieve Trismegistus of command and hold him on Banish. In their interrogations they explained to him their theory that controlled daemonic possession could be turned against [[Chaos]]. When his purity was proven, Trismegistus offered his brethren as partners in that cause. The first initiates then underwent what the Plutonians called "Daemonic Possession Therapy". Early failure rates were high, and one witness, Cardinal Rodrigo Nessun, went mad, turned to Chaos and fled with most of the Marines who could not be exorcised; they became the [[Chaos Space Marines|Chaos]] warband known as the Swords of Epiphany. The date of these events is sealed.

Campaigns

The Exorcists fought in the Aschen War between 300 and 307.M40, and in the [[Gothic War]] of 139–160.M41 against the twelfth [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. In 740.M41 they relieved Stonekraal, and between 908 and 912.M41 they took part in the [[Badab War]] on the Loyalist side. In 972.M41 they cleansed Opridia, in 998.M41 they fought in the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]], and in 999.M41 they fought the Black Legion's daemon hosts in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]].

Era Indomitus

With the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 the Chapter's incorruptibility became more valuable than ever. The Torchbearer fleet sent to bring [[Primaris Space Marines]] to Banish suffered such repeated daemonic attacks that the Rogue Traders travelling with it believed it cursed, and all of its Greyshields fell in battle. The fleet could give the Exorcists only the technology to make Primaris Marines. The Chapter raised its own Primaris initiates on Banish, where their training could be watched from the start, and found that they too showed the Chapter's resistance to daemons, which suggests that the trait lies in the gene-seed.

Initiation

An aspirant who has completed his training is possessed under tight security by a minor daemon and then exorcised in a ritual overseen by the Plutonians and the Chapter's Chaplains. Those who emerge carry physical and mental scars, but also an intimate knowledge of the enemy and an immunity to its influence. Those who cannot be cleansed are destroyed, or, the Chapter's own records concede, become the Broken Ones.
