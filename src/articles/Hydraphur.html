Hydraphur is a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] in the galactic west, and the [[Segmentum Fortress]] of that segmentum. It is the headquarters of Battlefleet Pacificus of the [[Imperial Navy]], a principal recruiting ground for naval officers, and a centre of commerce dominated by great merchant clans and [[Rogue Trader]] dynasties.

Hydraphur surrendered to the Apostate Cardinal [[Bucharis]] during the [[Plague of Unbelief]] in the [[36th millennium]], a disgrace that still shapes its politics: the Navy is barred from governing the planet, and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] holds a penitential festival there every year. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the world withstood three [[Chaos]] assaults, the most famous of which was broken by a small force of the [[Adeptus Custodes]].

History

Early history

The origins of Hydraphur are not recorded in open archives. It was a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Mechanicum]] by the time of the [[Great Crusade]] (M30), and after the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) became the fortress of the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Pacificus]] when the segmenta were created, c. 020.M31.The date of its appointment as a fortress is estimated from the creation of the segmentum commands in [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s reforms.

During the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] (early M35 to 975.M35) the Segmentum Pacificus was claimed by the Ur-Council of [[Nova Terra]]. Imperial records do not state clearly which court Hydraphur obeyed in those centuries, and the archives of the period on the planet were later purged.

The Plague of Unbelief

Main article: [[Plague of Unbelief]]

In the early 36th millennium, during the [[Age of Apostasy]], the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis of Gathalamor carved out a realm across the Segmentum Pacificus. When he moved on Hydraphur, c. 390.M36, the fleet of the segmentum accepted his claims and the world surrendered to him without a fight. After the heresy was broken and the world returned to the Imperium, the Ecclesiarch Chye Balronas instituted on Hydraphur an annual festival of fasting and public penance in memory of its shame.The date of the surrender is estimated from the dating of Bucharis's other conquests to about 390.M36.

The [[Administratum]] then set out to curb the power of the Navy in the system. The presence of the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Inquisition]] on the planet was increased, and the landholding naval families were stripped of their hereditary estates over the following centuries. The Navy answered by tightening its control over the rest of the system and the surrounding [[Sub-sector|sub-sector]], to the point that the Ecclesiarchy on Hydraphur came to rely on covert couriers to reach its own outposts on other worlds.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Hydraphur was attacked by the [[Black Legion]]. The Imperium sent many forces to its defence, including a detachment of the Adeptus Custodes. The traitors and their mortal followers attacked in such strength that the other defenders were forced back, but the Custodians held their line against every charge, and the Black Legion's advance broke against them. In the first years of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] Hydraphur was attacked by the forces of [[Chaos]] three times in all and held each time.The attacks are dated only to the early Indomitus Crusade, c. 000–015.M42.

Geography

Hydraphur has a gravity close to Terran standard and is heavily built over by forges, cities and the estates of the great families. The system around it is a naval base on the largest scale, with orbital docks, defence stations and the headquarters of the [[Imperial Navy|Battlefleet]].

Society

Hydraphur's politics are divided between institutions that distrust each other. The [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]]'s office is largely ceremonial, and real power on the surface lies with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], the [[Administratum]] and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]], whose writ ends at the upper atmosphere. The Navy, which controls everything beyond it, regards the Ministorum as meddlesome; the Ministorum regards the Navy as untrustworthy because of its part in the [[Age of Apostasy]]. [[Lex Imperialis|Imperial law]] is kept by the [[Adeptus Arbites]].

Commercial ties are as important as blood on Hydraphur. By local custom a person bears a clan name, from the commercial house they serve, and a family name; a noble of House Medell attached to the Kalfus clan is styled Kalfus-Medell. The great families supply many of the Navy's officers, and naval, commercial and [[Rogue Trader]] houses are so closely linked by contract that their alliances are spoken of as kinship.
