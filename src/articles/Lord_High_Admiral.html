The Lord High Admiral of the Imperial Navy is the supreme commander of the [[Imperial Navy]], the Navis Imperialis, and its representative among the [[High Lords of Terra]], where he oversees the political interests of the Navy on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. The title Lord High Admiral is also borne by the five commanders of the segmentum battlefleets, one for each of the [[Segmentum|Segmentae Majoris]]. The supreme admiral is usually chosen from among them, and most often from the Lord High Admiral of the [[Segmentum Solar]], whose command around [[Terra]] is the most prestigious of the five.

The office descends from that of the Lord High Admiral of the Armada Imperialis, the precursor of the Navis Imperialis. Its first recorded holder was the admiral [[Niora Su-Kassen]], who sat on the [[Grand Council of Reconstruction]] after the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31).

Several holders have been among the most powerful High Lords. [[Lansung]], Lord High Admiral during the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32), dominated the council and kept the fleets from the fight for most of the war. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the office was held by [[Merelda Pereth]], who joined the [[Hexarchy]] against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and was killed when its coup failed in early M42.

Powers and organisation

The fleets themselves are managed by the five segmentum Lords High Admiral from the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Fortresses]]: [[Hydraphur]] for Solar, [[Cypra Mundi]] for [[Segmentum Obscurus|Obscurus]], [[Kar Duniash]] for the [[Ultima Segmentum]] and the others. All five hold equal rank. Below them, Lord Admirals command the battlefleets of individual [[Sector|sectors]], and High Admirals command the fleets of crusades.

Because the Navy carries the [[Astra Militarum]] between the stars, a Lord High Admiral can hamper a war by withholding transport: in the [[War of the Beast]] the Lord Commander Militant Verreault could get his regiments into the fight only by agreeing to follow the political lead of the Lord High Admiral Lansung.

History

The Armada and the first Lord High Admiral (M31)

When the Heresy came to the [[Sol System]], [[Rogal Dorn]] drew the admiral [[Niora Su-Kassen]] of the [[Jupiter|Jovian]] Void Clans onto his Solar command staff. She fought through the [[Siege of Terra|Solar War]] and the defence of Terra, in the course of which she learned of the death of her daughter, a ship's captain who had refused to join [[Horus]]. After the Siege she sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction (014.M31) as Lord High Admiral of the Armada Imperialis, while the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Adreen]] commanded the army.

War of the Beast and the Beheading (M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]] and [[Beheading]]

A Lord High Admiral named [[Javor]] requested the construction of the Vanquisher-class battleship, the first of which was built at Hydraphur in the late 32nd millennium. [[Udin Macht Udo]], a decorated former Lord High Admiral, had become Lord Commander of the Imperium by 544.M32.

In 544.M32 the Lord High Admiral was Lansung, an officer of the Segmentum Solar who had built a personal following in the Navy and in the Imperial College of Fleet Strategy. Allied with Udo, the [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Tobris Ekharth]] and the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Erekart Mesring]], he was the most powerful High Lord when the Orks of the [[War of the Beast]] reached the Imperium. He kept the fleets on the frontier while the Orks advanced on Terra, hoping to preserve the Navy and to succeed Udo as Lord Commander. When he was finally forced to fight, he led the charge that destroyed the Ork Attack Moon over Port Sanctus from his flagship, the Autocephalax Eternal. He fell from favour when a second Attack Moon appeared above Terra, and was murdered in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32 by the [[Callidus Temple|Callidus]] Assassin who had taken Ekharth's place.

The Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]] gave the Navy's seat to [[Iryss Gelthor]], until then admiral of the Segmentum Obscurus.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

At the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 the Lord High Admiral was Merelda Pereth. She kept her seat when Guilliman restored the council, but she belonged to the faction that wished to return to the old order, and she joined the former Master of the Administratum [[Irthu Haemotalion]] in the Hexarchy. When the conspiracy moved to seize Terra in early M42, the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Fadix]], who had feigned membership, had her and the other conspirators killed.The Imperial records available to this article place the coup early in the Indomitus Crusade, while Guilliman was away from Terra.
