Stormclaw is a [[Knight world]] of the [[Sanctus Reach]]. Knight worlds are planets ruled by noble houses that pilot the [[Imperial Knight]] war engines, and Stormclaw is one of the knight worlds of the region, alongside [[Alaric Prime]].

Its sector is administered under the [[Administratum]], with the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] keeping relay stations in the region. The exact seat of its ruling house is not fixed in the sources consulted.

Status

As of M42 the world is contested. Imperial records describe its houses as holding their knights against rivals, and the contest has drawn in outside forces. The sources do not agree on the number of knights in service.
