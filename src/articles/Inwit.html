Inwit is an [[Ice world|ice world]] of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]] and the capital of the Inwit Cluster. It is the world on which the [[primarch]] [[Rogal Dorn]] grew to manhood, and on which the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] found him in 835.M30, during the [[Great Crusade]]. Dorn had made himself lord of the Inwit System and presented the Emperor with the vast star-fortress later called the [[Phalanx]].

Inwit was the first homeworld of the VII Legion, the [[Imperial Fists]], and many of its great warriors were born in its ice-hives. It was besieged by Traitor forces during the [[Horus Heresy]] (c. 007–012.M31). It remains a recruiting world of the Imperial Fists Chapter and is honoured by all the Chapters descended from Dorn. Its people are hard, collective and disciplined, and its orbital shipyards rank among the best in the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]].

History

Age of Strife

Further information: [[Age of Strife]]

Inwit was settled during the [[Dark Age of Technology]] (date unrecorded). Its orbital stations and shipyards are older than any record of the planet and are believed to date from that time. During the [[Age of Strife]] the people of Inwit survived in great hives cut into the ice and rock, and the clans that ruled them built warfleets and established a small interstellar realm over the neighbouring systems. They kept their nomadic customs while they did so.

The capsule of the infant Dorn came down on Inwit in the late Age of Strife (c. 800.M30, estimated from the scattering of the primarchs). He was taken in by the ice-clan whose name he later bore, and came to regard its old chieftain as his grandfather and teacher. He rose in the service of the clan-lords, and by the time of the Emperor's arrival he ruled the whole Inwit System as its warlord.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The Emperor arrived at Inwit in 835.M30. Dorn submitted at once and offered his father the Phalanx, a moon-sized starship that he claimed to have built or restored. He took command of the VII Legion, which recruited from Inwit's clans for the rest of the Great Crusade. [[Archamus]], master of Dorn's Huscarls, Captain [[Alexis Polux]] and [[Fafnir Rann]] were among the Inwit-born officers of the Legion.

Inwit became a recruiting world and a naval base. Its shipyards built and repaired vessels for the Imperial Fists' fleet, and the system was fortified in the manner Dorn taught.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

When the Heresy began in 005.M31, the Imperial Fists were recalled to [[Terra]] and Inwit was left with a small garrison. Traitor forces laid siege to the system during the war. The defenders held, but the siege cut the Legion off from its home recruiting grounds until after the [[Siege of Terra]]. The dates of the siege are not preserved in open records; Imperial Fists tradition places it in the middle years of the Heresy (c. 007–012.M31, estimated).The dates of the Siege of Inwit are estimated from the sequence of events of the Heresy.

Imperial era

After the [[Second Founding]] (c. 021.M31), the Imperial Fists Chapter kept Inwit as a recruiting world, alongside [[Necromunda]] and, unofficially, Terra itself. The Chapter's fortress-monastery remained the Phalanx, which has no fixed orbit, so Inwit is the Chapter's ancestral home rather than its seat. The [[Feast of Blades]], the great tournament of the [[Chapters]] descended from Dorn, has at times been held on Inwit (dates vary; last recorded in M41).

In the [[Era Indomitus]] Inwit serves as a base of the local sector fleet of the Imperial Navy and as a mustering ground for [[Astra Militarum]] regiments. Inwit has also given officials to the [[Adeptus Terra]]: the [[Master of the Administratum]] Violeta Roskavler, who held office in the early Era Indomitus (c. 000–010.M42), was born on Inwit, an unusual origin for an office normally held by a native of Terra.

Geography

Inwit orbits an old red star that is slowly dying. The planet is tidally locked: one face lies in permanent darkness and the other in dim, unending daylight. The dark side, the Splintered Land, is a wilderness of crevasse mazes, frozen ranges and frost dunes stalked by predatory beasts. The light side is a land of salt seas, crusted drifts and bare rock. Beneath the ice crust are slow, thick seas in which blind creatures hunt by vibration.

The inhabited regions are the ice-hives, cave-cities built under about three kilometres of ice and extending through the upper ten kilometres of the crust in a honeycomb that covers the world. Auroras shine perpetually above the planet, and the ancient orbital stations and shipyards look down on them.

Society

The people of Inwit are divided into clans and into a system of ice-castes. Much of the population is nomadic, moving between the hives to trade in weapons, fuel and technology. Feuds between clans are common, and boys learn to fight young. The clans value the group over the individual, endurance over display, and obedience to clan law above all. These are the virtues that the Imperial Fists look for in an aspirant.
