Laeran was a world of the Laer, a serpentine species that the [[28th Expedition]] brought into Imperial compliance in about 001.M31. The world is recorded in the [[Horus Heresy]] chiefly because the [[Emperor's Children]] primarch [[Fulgrim]] took a daemon blade from it. That blade was later used to kill [[Ferrus Manus]].

The Laer are described in Imperial records as a race of great cunning, and their world was among the first to fall to Traitor influence.

Compliance

The 28th Expedition brought Laeran into compliance in about 001.M31. It was the third world the Expedition subdued, and the Laer were recorded as having accepted Imperial rule under a treaty that was later broken.

Daemon blade

Fulgrim took the daemon blade of Laeran in 004.M31, before the battle of [[Tarsus]]. Eldrad Ulthran of the [[Eldar]] tried to warn him, but the blade was taken and the Emperor's Children began to fall. Later that year, at the battle of [[Deep Orbital DS191]], Fulgrim tried and failed to win Ferrus Manus to Horus.

Imperial accounts agree on the end of Ferrus Manus: Fulgrim cut off his head with a daemon-sword from Laeran. The Iron Hands tell this story more often than any other, and no one can say what became of the primarch's remains.
