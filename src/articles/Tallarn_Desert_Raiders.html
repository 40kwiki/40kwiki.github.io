Tallarn Desert Raiders

Homeworld[[Tallarn]]
RoleFast raiders and mobile infantry
Deployed[[Segmentum Tempestus]] and [[Segmentum Ultima]]

The Tallarn Desert Raiders are a [[Militarum Regimentum]] of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised from the desert world of [[Tallarn]]. Their soldiers are heirs to the survivors of a world scoured in the [[Horus Heresy]]. They are known for skill at desert and mobile armoured warfare.

The regiment is primarily deployed in the Segmentum Tempestus and the Segmentum Ultima. It is especially known for asymmetric warfare in arid climates and for rapid armoured assaults. Its soldiers are mobile guerrilla fighters, evasive and opportunistic, and their regimental icon is recognised across the Imperial Guard.

Doctrine

The Desert Raiders fight by movement. Their armoured columns strike quickly and withdraw before an enemy can concentrate, and their infantry is trained to ride with the vehicles. [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] value the regiment for operations in deserts and wastelands where supply is scarce.
