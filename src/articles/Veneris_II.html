Veneris II was an [[Ecclesiarch]] of the [[Imperial Cult]], the first holder of that office recorded as a member of the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. He died of old age.

The record is very thin. It names him by title and numeral only and gives no personal name, birthplace or events of his rule. Because the Ecclesiarch is entered under the numeral, scholars infer that at least one earlier Veneris held the throne, but no archive names him.This is an inference of later scholars; no record names a first Veneris.

Date

The Ministorum entry places Veneris II at c. 200.M31, a date followed here only as the recorded figure. This is hard to reconcile with the other records, which place the Cult's rise as the state church of the Imperium in the [[32nd millennium]], after the [[Horus Heresy]] and the [[Reformation of the Imperium]]. Imperial accounts therefore differ, and many scholars treat the figure as a scribal slip for 200.M32, the date used throughout this encyclopedia.Estimated; the date is disputed and may be an error in the archive.

Significance

His place in the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] marks the moment when the Cult's head first sat among the [[High Lords of Terra]], alongside the [[Master of the Administratum]] and the other heads of the great [[Adeptus Terra|Adepta]]. In later centuries the [[Ecclesiarch]] came to outweigh the Master of the Administratum in practice. The seat was lost for a time after the [[War of the Beast]] and then made permanent under [[Ostulus]] in 546.M32.
