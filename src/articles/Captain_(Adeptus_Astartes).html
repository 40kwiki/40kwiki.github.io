A captain is a senior officer of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] who commands one of the ten [[company|companies]] of a [[Space Marine Chapter]] that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. Primaris Space Marine Chapters have the same rank and duty. Captains lead the [[Firstborn]] and [[Primaris Space Marine]] warriors of their companies in battle.

Selection

A captain is chosen from the [[Veteran]]s of his Chapter who have proven themselves in battle. Most serve first in the 1st Company, the elite of the Chapter. Others rise through their own company, and take command when the previous captain dies or is promoted.

Role

A captain is expected to read the flow of a battle and adjust the company's formation to meet it. Imperial doctrine holds that he must lead from the front, so that his warriors learn what it means to be a [[Space Marine]] from his example. The teachings of [[Roboute Guilliman]] describe the captain as a master of many paths to victory.

A captain also keeps the wargear, heraldry and records of his company. He answers to the [[Chapter Master]] for its deployment, and his company may be detached for a crusade or campaign at the Chapter's command.

Heraldry

Each captain wears personal heraldry, which is worn on the armour alongside the badge of his company. Captains of many Chapters are known by the marks of their companies, and the badge of the 1st Company is held in particular honour.
