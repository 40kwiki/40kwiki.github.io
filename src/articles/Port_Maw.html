Port Maw is an artificial world built by an unknown [[Xenos|xenos]] people and later turned by Humanity into one of the great harbours of the [[Navis Imperialis]]. It lies in the [[Gothic Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. Its vast docks and its structure are far larger than anything the Imperium built itself.

In ancient times the world lay on the border between the Segmentum Obscurus and the [[Ultima Segmentum]], within what was then the Manachean Commonwealth. Imperial scholars record that its builders are unknown and that the world was reworked for shipping long before the Imperium reached it. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has surveyed its systems and keeps its machinery in service.

Harbour

Port Maw serves as a repair, resupply and muster point for Imperial warships passing through the Gothic Sector. Its docks can take capital ships that would not fit in most Imperial yards. The harbour is run by a [[Navis Nobilite]] governor under the sector's naval command, and its crews are drawn from the local [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[Mechanicus]] forges.

Legacy

The world is often cited in Imperial doctrine as proof that [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] holds older powers than the [[Age of Strife]] and the [[Dark Age of Technology]]. [[Administratum]] registers list the harbour as a fixed asset of the Gothic Sector, not a colony, so its population is counted only by the dockyard crews.
