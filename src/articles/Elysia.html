Elysia is a verdant [[civilised world]] of the Imperium of Mankind in the [[Segmentum Solar]], about 30 light years from the [[Armageddon]] system. It is the homeworld of the [[Elysian Drop Troops]], the airborne infantry regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]], and it gives its name to the [[Elysia Sector]].

The Elysia System lies on a major warp trade route, and its star system is full of asteroid fields and dense gas. For these reasons it was for long a haven for human and [[Aeldari]] pirates. Imperial patrols now keep the routes open, and the world's regiments are among the most sought after in the Imperium.

Drop troops

Elysia produces a large share of the airborne troops that are used by the Imperium's generals. Rare among the regiments of the Astra Militarum, its drop infantry are a strategic asset that many commanders seek to obtain.
