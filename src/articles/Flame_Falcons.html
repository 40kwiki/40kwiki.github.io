The Flame Falcons were a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] raised in the Twenty-first Founding, the "Cursed Founding". They are remembered for one thing: during battle their warriors burst into flame without harm, and fought on with fierce savagery. They took this as a gift of the Emperor. The [[Inquisition]] took it as possession, and the [[Grey Knights]] destroyed the Chapter on its homeworld Lethe.

Imperial records say little else. The Chapter was declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]], and its history was struck out.

History

The Cursed Founding

The Twenty-first Founding was the largest since the [[Second Founding]]. It came shortly before the [[Age of Apostasy]], and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] used it to perfect the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] by removing flaws and, under a project named Homo Sapiens Novus, to make warriors more like the [[Primarch|primarchs]]. The Genetors were far less skilled than the Emperor, and the gene-seed they made was seriously flawed. The Founding ended when one of its [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], already corrupted, sent a signal through the Warp that drew the notice of [[Fabius Bile]]. The Chapters of the Founding suffered such persistent misfortune that it was called the Cursed Founding. Most were lost to treachery, [[Mutant|mutation]] or the guns of the [[Inquisition]].

The Emperor's blessing, a Chapter's curse

The Flame Falcons began well. Their combat trials were a success, and they were put on active service on the Southern Rim from Lethe. Their first wars against Orks and Aeldari corsairs showed them hardy and courageous. At Raffenburg's World, in their largest battle to that date, the First Company was seen to burst into flame at the height of the fighting against rebel forces. Fearing witchcraft, the Chapter commander reinforced the line and sent for an Inquisitor who accompanied the Astra Militarum there. When the reinforcing Marines reported that the burning company was alive and fighting more savagely, the commander declared it a miracle.

The Inquisitor judged otherwise. He waited and watched the rest of the Chapter undergo the same change, and let the campaign end. The Flame Falcons returned to Lethe to celebrate. Soon after, the Grey Knights, acting for the [[Ordo Malleus]], assaulted Lethe and destroyed the Chapter, believing it daemon-possessed. A small number are thought to have escaped, but their fate is not recorded.Imperial sources differ on the date of the Scouring of Lethe. The Chapter's censure says it was destroyed within a century of its raising. Some Inquisitorial summaries place it in the 41st millennium. The two cannot both be true.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter's flame is thought to come from an unforeseen psychic [[Mutant|mutation]] in its [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], caused by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]]' alterations to the gene-seed of the Cursed Founding. The [[Inquisition]] remains uncompromising toward any such mutants it meets.

Notable members
