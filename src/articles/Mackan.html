Mackan is a world on which the [[Blood Angels]] fought the [[Black Legion]] during the [[Ghost War]] of the late [[37th millennium]]. The battle there is one of the major losses of the Blood Angels, which came close to extinction in the fighting.

Imperial accounts record the world as one of several sites of the Ghost War, a campaign of raids and false trails by the [[Traitor|Traitors]].

The Ghost War

Main article: [[Age of Redemption]]

During the Ghost War, [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] outmanoeuvred the commanders of [[Battlefleet Obscurus]] again and again. At Mackan and at [[Midian]] he inflicted grave losses on the [[Blood Angels]]. In the end the [[Traitor|Traitors]] returned to the [[Eye of Terror]] without having broken the Imperium's defences.

Legacy

The [[Blood Angels]] were brought close to extinction once before, at Mackan. The Chapter's later history, including the [[Kallius Insurrection]], is shaped in part by the memory of that loss.
