Ryza is a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]], counted among the most productive and most honoured in the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is renowned above all for plasma technology: its order of tech-priests, the Omnissiah Igvita, has made Ryza the leading source of plasma weapons and plasma reactors since the [[Great Crusade]]. Its Titan Legion, the [[Legio Crucius]], was once counted the equal of any outside [[Mars]].

Ryza withstood a great assault by the [[Dark Mechanicum]] during the [[Horus Heresy]], and in the 41st millennium has been under siege for more than seventy years by the [[Orks]] of WAAAGH! Grax and WAAAGH! Rarguts. Its output, once sent across the galaxy, now goes almost entirely to its own defence. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it nonetheless sent forces to [[Ultramar]] during the [[Plague Wars]].

History

Before the Imperium

Ryza's archivist-magi kept careful records of its early history. Settlers founded the city of Prosperity on a fertile world, and through the [[Age of Strife]] the colony grew into a technological power that travelled the stars for centuries, extending its reach to neighbouring systems and bringing back resources for its industry.

The Great Crusade

When the Great Crusade reached Ryza in the 30th millennium, some among the Mechanicum of Mars feared that so rich and capable a forge world might one day rival the Red Planet, and argued that it should be subdued. Others urged peace, and in the end Ryza accepted vassalage to both Mars and [[Terra]] without bloodshed.The date of Ryza's compliance is not recorded in open archives; it falls in the Crusade's eastward expansion, c. 850–900.M30 (estimated). Ryza's industry and position made it a mainstay of the Crusade's advance into the east, and its reverence among other forge worlds was resented by some of the Martian priesthood.

The Defence of Ryza

When civil war broke out on Mars at the start of the Horus Heresy (005.M31), Ryza became a refuge for loyalist forge worlds that had looked to Mars and drew many of them into its orbit. Late in the Heresy, while the main traitor host fought at [[Battle of Beta-Garmon|Beta-Garmon]], c. 010.M31, [[Horus]] ordered a force of the Dark Mechanicum to seize the world or cripple it. The army sent against Ryza contained no [[Space Marine Legions|Legionaries]], but consisted of cohorts from dozens of traitor forge worlds, with Titans of the Legio Vulturum and Legio Magna and their [[Imperial Knight]] allies, all eager for its plunder and directed by followers of the traitor Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]].

Ryza had prepared for the attack for years. The traitors had first to take the void station Ryza Secundus before they could land, and then to fight across fortified forge-cities defended by the Legio Crucius. The defence held, and Ryza survived the Heresy as one of the strongest loyal forge worlds.Accounts of the battle's end differ between Martian and Ryzan archives, and some details remain sealed.

WAAAGH! Grax

In 925.M41 two Ork invasions, WAAAGH! Grax and WAAAGH! Rarguts, struck the systems of Ryza. The war has continued ever since. Ryza turned every weapon it made to its own defence, and its plasma-armed armies and Titans held the forge-cities against hordes of greenskins. Imperial records credit the Warlord-class Titan Pugnus Vindictae with the death of Warlord Grax, but the Orks of his WAAAGH! fought on, and the war has drained the forge world's output for decades.

Many [[Astra Militarum]] regiments have been sent to Ryza's aid, among them the [[Catachan Jungle Fighters]], who fought on neighbouring worlds such as Dulma'lin in 926.M41.

Era Indomitus

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, every forge world was cut off from its Explorator fleets, and seven were destroyed. When [[Roboute Guilliman]] called for aid against the [[Death Guard]] in Ultramar during the Plague Wars, c. 111.M42, Ryza was among the more than a dozen forge worlds, with Mars, [[Metalica]] and Triplex Phall, that sent reinforcements, despite its own war with the Orks.

Geography

According to its first colonists' records, Ryza was once a world rich in life with two large continents. Millennia of industry have covered it with forge-cities linked by manufactoria canals that carry war materiel. The system also includes Ryza Secundus, a great orbital void station that guards the approaches to the planet.

Society

Ryza is ruled by its tech-priesthood. The Omnissiah Igvita, "the Life Blood of the Omnissiah", a sect founded on Ryza, teaches that plasma is the blood of the [[Omnissiah]] given to his servants, and its plasma fanes have produced more innovations in plasma technology than any other forge world. Ryza's [[Skitarii]] wear red and white and are known for heavy use of plasma weapons.

The forge world maintains the Legio Crucius and has long-standing ties with neighbouring [[Imperial Knight]] houses. Ryzan magi are noted for their stoicism, and since 925.M41 the whole society has been mobilised for war.
