Espandor is a star system of [[Ultramar]]. It was one of the systems attacked by the servants of [[Nurgle]] in the [[Plague Wars]], which were the invasion of Ultramar led by [[Mortarion]] and the [[Death Guard]]. The attack fell on Espandor, [[Iax]], Konor and [[Macragge]].

The campaign to retake the system is known as the Spear of Espandor. [[Roboute Guilliman]] led it, and it ended with the retaking of the system and the death of the Great Unclean One Qaramas at the hand of the Emperor's Sword.

Plague Wars

Main article: [[Plague Wars]]

The Death Guard and the daemonic Plague Legions of Ku'Gath operated from the corrupted [[Scourge Stars]]. Guilliman cleansed the hive world of [[Ardium]] of its Death Guard garrison, re-established the Tetrarch Council of Ultramar, and reorganised the defence before he marched on Espandor.The date of the Spear of Espandor is not recorded in the sources consulted. It falls within the Plague Wars, which Imperial accounts place in the late 41st millennium.
