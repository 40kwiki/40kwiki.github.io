The Orders Hospitaller is a non-militant order of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. It is dedicated to healing and to providing medical care for all the citizens of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Novices of the Adepta Sororitas who show a gift for healing and for caring for others are often moved into the Orders Hospitaller.

Sisters Hospitaller are among the most welcomed members of the Adepta Sororitas. They are sent to war zones and disaster areas, where they treat the people of the Imperium when care is most needed.

Work

The Sisterhood runs many hospitals and clinics across the Imperium, operated as charities. The convents of the Orders Hospitaller often take the form of hospitals and retreats. Large numbers of Sisters Hospitaller accompany the armies of the [[Astra Militarum]]. They are often found in battlefield hospitals aboard warships in orbit during an [[Imperial Crusade]].

Sisters Hospitaller rarely fight. When a platoon's medic is wounded during a battle, however, they will go into combat with Imperial Guard troops to support them. Members of the [[Inquisition]] look for these battle-hardened women when they recruit Acolytes for their retinues. Common soldiers regard the Sisters Hospitaller as saints. They much prefer their care to the crude work of the field surgeons of the [[Departmento Munitorum]].

Role with the Orders Militant

Sisters Hospitaller often serve alongside members of other Orders of the Adepta Sororitas. Their ability to heal wounded battle-sisters and return them to the field makes them valuable allies of the [[Orders Militant]]. Their knowledge of the human body also makes them useful to the [[Orders Famulous]] when investigations depend on genetics or anatomy.
