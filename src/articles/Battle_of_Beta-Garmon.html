The Battle of Beta-Garmon, also called Titandeath, was the last great battle of the [[Horus Heresy]] before the [[Siege of Terra]]. It was fought for the Beta-Garmon cluster in the [[Segmentum Solar]], which held the last stable route to the [[Sol System|Sol system]]. The traitor war host under the Warmaster [[Horus]] gathered an enormous force there, with Titans of several Legios. The loyalists, under [[Rogal Dorn]] and later [[Sanguinius]], gathered Titans from nearly thirty Legios and hundreds of thousands of Imperial Army troops to bar the way. It was the largest battle between god-machines in history.

The loyalists lost. The traitors overran Beta-Garmon II and III, and the way to [[Terra]] was opened. At the moment of victory, however, the Warmaster himself collapsed from an old wound and had to be carried from the field. Loyalist survivors, led by Sanguinius and Jaghatai Khan, withdrew to Terra. The cluster was left a ruin and was not reclaimed by the Imperium for some five thousand years.

Background

The cluster

The Beta-Garmon cluster survived the [[Age of Strife]] largely unharmed. Its worlds lay on stable [[Warp travel|Warp routes]] to Sol, and its forges and yards could repair a fleet. It became a muster ground and supply depot of the Great Crusade, and in 004.M31 the [[Space Wolves]] gathered there for the Censure of [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]]. By the Heresy it was the only direct way to Terra that the storms had not closed.

The first phase

Rebellion and invasion

After the [[Drop Site Massacre]], [[Alpha Legion]] agents set the cluster's worlds at odds and invaded the nearby Paramar system, where a civil war broke out. A Reaver maniple of [[Legio Mortis]] and the [[Emperor's Children]] seized Beta-Garmon II and its capital, Nyrcon City. The Titans stormed the city while the Emperor's Children took the orbital fort and the rest of the planet.

Between 007 and 010.M31 the cluster was a war zone. Loyalist forces cut off by the Ruinstorm gathered, a few ships at a time, but [[Traitor|traitor]] reinforcements kept arriving as well.

Dorn's counterattack

In 010.M31 [[Rogal Dorn]] sent the [[Imperial Fists]] and [[Salamanders]], with [[Imperialis Auxilia|Solar Auxilia]] and Legio Astorum, the Warp Runners, to retake Beta-Garmon II. They made early gains. The Emperor's Children and Alpha Legion counterattacked and nearly pushed them out again.

The Disciples of the Flame arrived aboard the Ebon Drake. They were a warband of [[Shattered Legions]] led by the Salamanders [[Dreadnought]] Cassian Dracos and a loyalist Iron Warrior, Nârik Dreygur. They struck Nyrcon and killed both the Alpha Legion and Emperor's Children commanders on the planet, though they took heavy losses. The Imperial Fists drove off the Emperor's Children, and Dorn began to fortify the cluster against an attack by [[Horus]].

The muster

By 012.M31 the Ruinstorm was receding. Reinforcements arrived on both sides, and some Imperial troops went over to the [[Warmaster]]. [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] determined that the cluster was to be held by troops that were not [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]]. He drew together hundreds of thousands of [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] troops and [[Titan Legions|Titan]] battlegroups from almost thirty loyalist Legios. By holding them at Beta-Garmon he hoped to avoid a war of Titans on Terra itself, and to delay the Warmaster until the Ultramarines and [[Dark Angels]], whose approach was expected, could strike him from behind.

[[Horus]] arrived with the main [[Traitor|traitor]] fleet soon after.

Forces

Loyalists

The loyalist force was a creature of necessity. It had no single Legio to anchor it, and its Astartes were few. The [[Titan Legions|Titan]] battlegroups included Legio Astorum, the Warp Runners, Legio Defensor, Legio Solaria and Legio Atarus. Knights of House Procon Vi and other houses fought beside them. The [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] supplied hundreds of thousands of troops, among them Solar Auxilia cohorts and regiments newly raised in the cluster. [[Lord General Militant|Lord General]] Bollivar commanded the army. The Titan princeps, among them Mohana Mankata VI, were the heads of proud and independent orders, and they had never been placed under a single hand.

Traitors

The [[Warmaster]] brought his own Legion, the [[Sons of Horus]], with detachments of the [[Iron Warriors]], [[Word Bearers]] and [[Emperor's Children]], and the [[Alpha Legion]] working in the cluster's shadow. The [[Dark Mechanicum]] furnished [[Legio Mortis]], Legio Fureans, Legio Vulpa and Legio Krytos, and the corrupted forge priests who tended them. [[Traitor]] Army regiments, some of them defectors from the loyalist muster, fought in the line. Imperial scholars name Dark Apostle Vorrjuk Kraal and Magos Ardim Protos among the leaders of the lesser contingents.

The battle

Beta-Garmon II

[[Horus]] bombarded the planet and then sent in the Titans of [[Legio Mortis]], Legio Fureans and Legio Vulpa. They broke through the defences and recaptured Nyrcon. The rest of the world fell quickly.

Beta-Garmon III and the outer worlds

Beta-Garmon III held. Legio Defensor stood against attacks by the [[Sons of Horus]] and [[Iron Warriors]]. Legio Krytos, the God-Breakers, attacked the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] citadels on Alpha-Garmon IX, where Defensor and Astorum held them. On Omega-Garmon, Legio Atarus and a detachment of Legio Mortis fought a battle of which no good account survives.

The Sea of Fire

The fleets fought between Beta-Garmon II and III in a battle that the survivors called the Sea of Fire. The wreckage of the ships rained on both worlds for months. Legio Solaria, Legio Fureans, and the Knights of House Procon Vi fought the [[Dark Mechanicum]] over Theta-Garmon V and Beta-Garmon III. The [[Traitor|traitors]] used a solar flare to blind the loyalist Titans, who were overrun. Iron Warriors siege battalions were later destroyed in turn.

The loyalist defence was poorly coordinated. The Warp was disturbed, and forces arrived piecemeal. The [[Titan Legions|Titan]] princeps refused to subordinate themselves to one another, and each Legio fought its own war. Imperial accounts describe this as the loyalists' greatest weakness.

Sanguinius takes command

The turning point was the arrival of [[Sanguinius]] with the [[Blood Angels]], and of [[Jaghatai Khan]] with the [[White Scars]]. Sanguinius took overall command. His authority, to the princeps and the generals alike, revived the defence. At Signus he had already been told what his brother intended, and Beta-Garmon was the first great field on which he stood against the [[Warmaster]]'s host. See [[Signus Campaign]].

The Titandeath

[[Sanguinius]] ordered a bombardment of Nyrcon. The [[Traitor|traitors]]' entrenchments, void shields and storms of debris blunted it. He then committed the Titans. More than a thousand fought on Beta-Garmon II in the largest engagement of god-machines in history. Sanguinius personally killed the Imperator [[Titan Legions|Titan]] Axis Mundi. Azkaellon, the commander of the Sanguinary Guard, led an assault on the star fort Anvil, which proved to be a trap. The [[Blood Angels]] withdrew before the fort destroyed itself, but its debris killed tens of thousands on both sides.

While the loyalists were committed on Beta-Garmon II, [[Horus]] struck Beta-Garmon III with his own Legion, with about a hundred Titans of [[Legio Mortis]], possessed engines of Legio Vulpa and Legio Krytos. They overran the defenders and destroyed the Carthega Telepathica, the [[Astropathy|astropathic]] station that coordinated the loyalist force. The defence lost its voice.

At the moment of victory the [[Warmaster]] fell. A wound that Imperial accounts attribute to [[Leman Russ]] re-opened, and Horus collapsed. Horus Aximand and Falkus Kibre carried him to the [[Vengeful Spirit]].

Aftermath

In 013.M31 [[Sanguinius]] and [[Jaghatai Khan]] withdrew to Terra with the surviving loyalists. The [[Traitor|traitors]] gathered at [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], but the incapacity of the [[Warmaster]] and the corruption within their ranks delayed them. The path to Terra was still secure.

The Beta-Garmon cluster was devastated and abandoned. The Imperium did not reclaim it for about five millennia, until about M36.Date estimated from the sequence of events. Millions of soldiers died, and billions of civilians were massacred, a toll that exceeded the dead of the last five years of the Great Crusade. Many loyalist Legios needed millennia to recover. Resentment at having been sacrificed for time led some survivors of the [[Titan Legions]] to refuse to fight at the [[Siege of Terra]]. The traitors salvaged Titans from Beta-Garmon II and reanimated them by sorcery as revenant engines, which they used as cannon fodder at the Siege.

Legacy

Beta-Garmon is regarded as the point at which the war became a matter of time. The loyalist [[Titan Legions]] never again took the field in such strength, and the cost of the battle is counted among the heaviest of the Heresy.
