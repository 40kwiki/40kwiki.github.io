The Battle of Gethsemane was a naval engagement in the [[Gothic War]], fought in mid-151.M41 in the [[Gethsemane]] System of the [[Gothic Sector]]. It was one of the first occasions on which the Imperial fleet went on the offensive during the war. Lord Admiral [[Cornelius von Ravensburg]] escaped a trap and destroyed the Chaos warfleet with the unexpected help of [[Aeldari]] warships.

Background

For the first seven standard years of the Gothic War, the Imperium fought mostly on the defensive across a long battlefront. As 150.M41 gave way to 151.M41, Lord Admiral [[Cornelius von Ravensburg]] decided to take the fight to the enemy and win back the worlds that had been lost. The Chaos warfleets outnumbered his, but they were divided into many smaller fleets. Ravensburg hoped to defeat each fleet separately by bringing the full weight of [[Battlefleet Gothic]] against it. The plan was risky, because it required him to concentrate his forces and leave other areas exposed.

The engagement

After several aborted attacks, Ravensburg saw his first real chance in mid-151.M41. Scout vessels reported a Chaos warfleet moving en masse towards the Gethsemane System. Ravensburg took personal command aboard his flagship, the Divine Right, and led a task force of seventeen capital ships, including two battleships and two battlecruisers, with twenty escorts. He pursued the enemy into the Gethsemane region.

The Chaos fleet tried to flee out of the system to get far enough from Gethsemane's star to attempt a Warp jump. Ravensburg detached the fastest vessels in his fleet to pursue, and a week-long stern chase followed. As the Imperial ships followed, signals warned of a second Chaos fleet on a closing course. The Imperial task force had been lured into a trap. Bolstered by a further twelve warships, the Chaos fleet turned on the Imperial vessels, which could do little more than evade the bulk of the enemy. They lost three destroyers and four frigates in a series of small skirmishes, while destroying at least five escorts of the enemy, and perhaps as many as ten.

For three days the two fleets circled and dodged each other through the system, since neither commander wished to commit the bulk of his ships against an enemy whose position was unknown. Three weeks after the Imperial fleet arrived in the system, six Firestorm-class frigates located the Chaos warfleet near Gethsemane II, hidden by dust clouds. Ravensburg moved his whole fleet in to attack. His Cobra-class destroyers launched torpedo salvoes at extreme range, which forced the Chaos ships to turn towards the Imperial capital ships and into a head-on clash. The Imperial escorts herded the enemy into a compact mass while the capital ships passed through their lines and poured fire into them at short range. In the first pass the Imperial fleet crippled four capital ships and destroyed eleven escorts.

The Chaos Warmaster then ordered his fleet to continue on its course in a bid to escape, and the faster ships began to draw away from the pursuit. Without warning, Aeldari warships appeared in front of the Chaos vessels, as if from nowhere, and attacked them. Caught between the Aeldari and Ravensburg's fleet, the Chaos warships were annihilated, though a dozen more of the Emperor's vessels were crippled or destroyed before the victory was won.

Outcome

Imperial accounts never learned why the Aeldari chose to join the Imperial cause. It is commonly believed that they had heard of Abaddon's capture of the Blackstone Fortresses and saw an alliance with Humanity as their only chance of survival. News of Ravensburg's victory spread through the fleet. In the late months of 151.M41 the Warp storms that had isolated the Gothic Sector began to abate, and warships from neighbouring battlefleets arrived, together with the battle barges and strike cruisers of several Space Marine Chapters. With their help the Imperium could go on the offensive.
