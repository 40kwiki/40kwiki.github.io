Urien Rakarth is a [[Haemonculus]] of [[Commorragh]]. He is the oldest of the [[Haemonculi]], and he is believed to have died and been rebuilt more often than any other being in the city.

The Haemonculi are masters of flesh. They rebuild the bodies of the dead for a price, which lets the wealthy of Commorragh live, in practice, for ever, and they shape monsters from living tissue. Rakarth is one of the most senior of them, and he is named among the leading figures of the city alongside [[Asdrubael Vect]] and [[Lelith Hesperax]].
