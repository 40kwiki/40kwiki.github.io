The psychic hood is a hooded helm worn by the [[Librarian]]s of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It is a psychic device that shields the wearer from the Warp and focuses his powers. Most Librarians carry the hood as part of their insignia, alongside a [[force weapon]] and blue armour.

Imperial doctrine treats the hood as a mark of office within the [[Librarius]]. It is worn by the Librarian in battle, and it is recorded as a sign of the [[Psyker|psyker]]'s control over his gift.

Function

The hood's shielding is intended to protect the wearer from the Warp's influence while he uses his powers. Its focusing function allows the [[Librarian]] to direct psychic energy more precisely than he could unaided. Imperial records describe it as the Librarian's primary defence against daemonic attack.

Use by Librarians

Most [[Librarian|Librarians]] of the Librarius wear a hood of their own, and its design varies with the Chapter. Notable holders include [[Mephiston]], [[Varro Tigurius]], [[Ahzek Ahriman]] and [[Njal Stormcaller]]. The hood is treated in doctrine as a symbol of the Librarian's oath, and it is often kept after the wearer's death.
