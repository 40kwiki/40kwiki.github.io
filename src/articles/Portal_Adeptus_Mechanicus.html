The Adeptus Mechanicus is the priesthood of the Omnissiah, the Machine God, and the keeper of the Imperium's technology. Its Forge Worlds build the weapons, vessels and war machines on which the Imperium relies. This portal gathers the articles on its forges, its tech-priests and its sacred rites.

Selected article

[[Adeptus Mechanicus]] is the priesthood of the Machine God and the technologists of the Imperium. It governs the [[Forge world]] of [[Mars]] and the Mechanicus worlds of the Imperium. Its members hold that all knowledge is sacred and that the machine is the highest form of worship.
