Solemnace is a world where the [[Inquisitor]] [[Katarinya Greyfax]] of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] was held captive for some centuries. Her captivity is believed to have lasted until shortly before 999.M41, although the start of it is not recorded.

Greyfax is a [[Psyker|psyker]] of some power, whose telepathy lets her detect any lie. The Ordo Hereticus describes her as utterly without mercy, and her peers have more than once questioned her methods.

Captivity

The Imperium's records do not state how [[Katarinya Greyfax|Greyfax]] was taken to Solemnace, or by whom. Her service in the [[Inquisition]] began in the [[41st millennium]], and the date of her capture is not recorded.The length of the captivity is estimated from the dates of her service and return. No record gives an exact span.
