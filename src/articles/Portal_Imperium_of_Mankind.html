The [[Imperium of Mankind]] is the human state that rules the [[Milky Way]] from the Throne of [[Terra]]. Its laws, faith and armies are administered by an immense bureaucracy, a priesthood and a web of secret orders. This portal gathers the articles that describe how the Imperium is governed, defended and remembered.

Selected article

[[Governance of the Imperium]] describes the chain of rule that runs from the [[High Lords of Terra]] to the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]] of every world. Authority is divided among the [[Administratum]], the [[Adeptus Terra]], the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Inquisition]], each checking the others by design. Imperial scholars record that this balance has survived the [[Horus Heresy]], the [[Great Rift]] and the long years of the [[Era Indomitus]].
