Chemos was a mining world of the Aquitane Sector, in the [[Segmentum Ultima]], and the homeworld of the [[primarch]] [[Fulgrim]] and the III Legion, the [[Emperor's Children]]. Cut off in the [[Age of Strife]], it was a starving world of fortress-factories when Fulgrim fell to it. He rose from labourer to ruler of the factory-fortress of Callax and then of the whole planet, and turned it from a dying colony into a world of art and wealth, before the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] found him in 830.M30.

After the Heresy, Loyalist forces bombarded Chemos from orbit, destroying the Emperor's Children's fortress-monastery near Callax and every city on the planet. Chemos has been a lifeless world under Inquisitorial quarantine ever since. Fulgrim, now a Daemon Primarch of [[Slaanesh]], rebuilt its likeness on a [[Daemon world]] that he named Callax.

Geography

Chemos was warmed by two small and distant suns and surrounded by a nebular dust cloud, so that it knew neither day nor night but only a constant grey twilight in which no stars were visible. It was harsh and dry. Its people drew water from the air with vapour mines and recycled their food in huge synthesisers. Before Fulgrim's reforms most of its cities, outposts and mines lay in ruins, and only a few fortress-factories, of which Callax was the largest, kept its people alive.

History

Isolation

Chemos was settled as a mining colony before the Age of Strife and depended on interstellar trade for food. When the [[Warp]] storms of the Age of Strife (c. M25–M30) ended that trade, thousands starved, cities were abandoned and the survivors withdrew into the fortress-factories. The ancient text called the Libram ex Dominar describes a society in which every citizen worked every waking hour, art and leisure were abandoned, and efficiency became the only virtue. Orphans and others who could not repay their keep were put to death.

Fulgrim

Fulgrim's capsule fell a kilometre outside the walls of Callax in about 792.M30. Its Executive Board sent scouts, hoping for news of survivors from other worlds. They found a child in the burning crater. Their captain refused to let the orphan be killed, appealed to the Board, and was allowed to adopt him. He named the boy after Fulgrim, a mythical god of creation the people of Chemos had long forgotten.

Fulgrim grew quickly, worked twice as hard as any labourer and soon understood the machines he worked with better than their engineers. By the fifteenth year after his fall he was one of the Executives. He persuaded the Board to fight the decline of their world: teams went out to reclaim and repopulate abandoned outposts and mines, the factory-fortress came to produce more than it consumed, and terraforming technology was rediscovered, spreading forests and seas across the planet. The other settlements allied with Callax, and fifty years after his arrival Fulgrim became sole ruler of Chemos. He fostered a revival of art and learning, and its new cities of glass, gold and steel became famous.Records of Fulgrim's rise conflict with the date of his discovery. If he arrived c. 792.M30, "fifty years" would place his sole rule c. 842.M30, after the Emperor's arrival in 830.M30. Imperial scholars suggest either an earlier arrival or a shorter period; the sources do not settle it.

The Great Crusade

In 830.M30 battle-scarred dropships bearing the [[Aquila (symbol)|Imperial aquila]] landed outside Callax. Fulgrim ordered the Caretakers, the world's police-soldiers, to let them in, and when the Emperor came before him he knelt and offered his sword. He took command of the III Legion, which had been reduced to a few hundred warriors by the loss of its gene-seed, and rebuilt it with recruits from Chemos and from [[Terra]]. Chemos became a world of the Imperium and the Legion's homeworld, and its wealth, art and manufactories supplied the Legion throughout the [[Great Crusade]].

Destruction

Fulgrim joined [[Horus]] at [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31 and fell to Slaanesh during the [[Horus Heresy]]. After the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31), Loyalist forces attacked Chemos from orbit. They destroyed the Legion's fortress-monastery outside Callax and bombarded the other factory-cities and palaces into nothing, to erase every trace of [[Chaos]]. The bombardment left the planet lifeless. The Inquisition then placed it under quarantine. In the ten thousand years since, no further record of Chemos has appeared in Imperial databases, not even a formal record of [[Exterminatus]].The date of the bombardment is not recorded; c. 015–020.M31 is estimated from the early campaigns of the Great Scouring.

Callax

Weary of his Legion's excesses after the Heresy, Fulgrim withdrew to a Daemon World of Slaanesh in the [[Eye of Terror]], renamed it Callax after his old capital, and spent centuries remaking it as a copy of Chemos, down to a re-enactment of his own fall from the sky. In the early [[Era Indomitus]] he left Callax to take personal command of the Emperor's Children again.
