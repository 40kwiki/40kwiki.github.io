The Megarachnids are a sentient race of [[Xenos]] resembling giant spiders. In the early [[31st millennium]] they lived on the planet [[Murder]], which was also known as Urisarach and One-Forty-Twenty. They were an extremely hostile species and the old enemy of the non-Imperial human [[Interex]].

Imperial records describe the Megarachnids as organised, territorial and fierce, and as able to outmatch the Interex in open ground. Their nests were built in the planet's deep forests and caves, where the Interex could not follow them in strength.

Contact with the Emperor's Children

The Megarachnids were later met by the [[Emperor's Children]] Legion, whose legionaries confronted the spiders on Murder. Accounts of the encounter are sparse, and the Legion's role on the world is disputed in Imperial records.
