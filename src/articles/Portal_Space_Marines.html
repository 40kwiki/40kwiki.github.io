The Adeptus Astartes are the transhuman warriors of the Emperor, bred and augmented to fight the wars of the Imperium. They were once organised into Legions, and since the [[Second Founding]] they have served as Chapters of a few thousand Battle-Brothers each. This portal gathers the articles on the Legions, Chapters and leaders of the Adeptus Astartes.

Selected article

[[Adeptus Astartes]] are the Emperor's Space Marines, genetically engineered warriors who form the core of the Imperial armed forces. Each Chapter follows the [[Codex Astartes]], written by [[Roboute Guilliman]] after the Horus Heresy. Their strength lies in discipline, their gene-seed and the sacrifice of their brothers.
