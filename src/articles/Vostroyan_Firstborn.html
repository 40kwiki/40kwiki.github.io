The Vostroyan Firstborn are the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] raised on the [[Industrial world]] of [[Vostroya]], near the [[Halo Stars]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. They have served the [[Emperor of Mankind]] for ten thousand years. Their service is also a penance, since their ancestors failed the Emperor in the [[Horus Heresy]].

Origins of the shame

During the [[Horus Heresy]], the Emperor called on loyal worlds to send troops against [[Horus]] and his [[Traitor Legions]]. Vostroya refused, arguing that it served the Imperium better by making weapons. The refusal is recorded as a betrayal of duty, and the Vostroyans have since sought to clear that record through their service.

Service

Vostroyan regiments are known for their discipline and for their grim devotion to the Emperor. They fight in toxic and poisoned environments with respirators, and they are armed with [[Meltagun]]s for the destruction of armour.
