A Crone world is one of the original homeworlds that the [[Aeldari]] colonised as they rose to power tens of thousands of standard years ago. Together they formed the heart of the Aeldari empire until the birth of [[Slaanesh]] led to the Fall and the growth of the [[Eye of Terror]]. The Crone worlds were consumed by the warp rift, and their realities became part of the [[Immaterium]].

The Aeldari name these worlds after [[Morai-Heg]], the goddess of fate. The Imperium designates the same worlds as [[Daemon world]]s. They are now domains of the [[Ruinous Powers]] and their servants, and many ancient Aeldari souls are trapped there. Those souls yearn for death and for the consumption by Slaanesh that ends their torment.

Spirit Stones and artefacts

The Crone worlds are the only known source of new [[Spirit Stone]]s, which the Aeldari need to house their souls after death. Reaching them means entering the most dangerous region of the galaxy. Many [[Craftworld Aeldari|craftworld]] Aeldari who travel there never return. Those who do are often so wounded in mind and spirit that they seek the solace of the [[Infinity Circuit]].

Besides Spirit Stones, the Crone worlds hold other artefacts of the old Aeldari civilisation that survived the Fall. They remain on the worlds for anyone willing to claim them.

Notable Crone worlds

Imperial and Aeldari records list the following Crone worlds, all within the Eye of Terror or the nearby Gehennabyss Reaches: [[Belial IV]], [[Iydris]], Aktosha, Arach-Cyn, Urum, the World of Immortal Sorrows and Yllen Satari. Many of their systems are unrecorded, and their positions are known only from the accounts of those who survived the journey.
