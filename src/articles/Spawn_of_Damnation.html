Spawn of Damnation is a [[space hulk]], a conglomeration of wrecked voidships some five kilometres across. It was first sighted by the Imperium c. 447.M36 and was captured by the [[Reclaimers]] Chapter in 928.M41.

Like other hulks in the [[Spinward Drift]], it drifts through [[Warp|the warp]] routes and carries the remains of many ships and crews. Its hulls are a mixture of Imperial, [[Xenos|xenos]] and unknown design, and the Imperium treats such hulks as a danger to navigation.

Capture

The Reclaimers took the hulk in 928.M41 after a long pursuit. The capture is recorded in the Chapter's history as one of its most important feats of salvage, and the hulk was taken for study and for the recovery of its technology.The capture date is recorded. The number of Reclaimers involved is not.
