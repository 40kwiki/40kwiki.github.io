Nadzybar was the archon, or overlord, of the [[forces of Chaos]] in the [[Sabbat Worlds]] [[Sector]]. He held that position from about 634.M41 until his death in 765.M41. He was killed during the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] by the Imperial [[Warmaster]] [[Slaydo]] on the world of [[Balhaut]]. He had organised the fractious [[Chaos Cult]]s and warbands of the Sabbat Worlds into a force able to extend the rule of the [[Dark Gods]] across the whole sector.

Rise to power

Nadzybar came to prominence as a powerful sect-leader, above a number of other [[magister]]s, the lesser warlords of the sector. He commanded a large coalition of tribes and clans, which Imperial forces in the Sabbat Worlds called the "[[Archenemy]]". The coalition was zealously devoted to the [[Ruinous Powers]]. Little is known of his origins, and the means by which he rose above the other warlords is not recorded.

Imperial intelligence gathered before the Sabbat Worlds Crusade began in earnest in 755.M41 shows that Nadzybar had been in overall command of the Chaos forces since at least 634.M41. His rule may have begun earlier. Some speculate that he was the charismatic [[Chaos Lord]] who first rallied the forces of Chaos to retake the Sabbat Worlds in the early centuries of the 41st millennium. If so, his long life would be a gift of the Dark Gods.The identification of Nadzybar with an earlier Chaos Lord is speculation. The sources give no firm record of his lifespan.

Command

Nadzybar controlled his scattered forces by giving authority to the magisters. These local leaders ruled their own regions and worlds in the archon's name. Many magisters were charismatic and deadly figures in their own right. They drew on private cults and tribal levies to build their own power. Under Nadzybar, the Archenemy resisted the Crusade on many worlds at once. This showed Slaydo that the Chaos forces were disciplined and capable, rather than a rabble of [[Chaos Cultist]]s.

As the Crusade advanced, each magister proved to command the complete loyalty of his own troops. This made the Archenemy hard to defeat as a whole.

Death

Nadzybar was a thorn in Slaydo's side for the first decade of the Crusade. Each time the Warmaster seemed close to winning enough ground to strike at the heart of the Archenemy on Balhaut, the archon launched a successful counter-stroke. The contest of wits culminated in the campaign that Imperial chronicles call the Fabian Ruse, which began in 764.M41.The account of Nadzybar's career dates the Ruse to 760.M41, while the record of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade dates it to 764–765.M41. The later dates agree with the rest of this encyclopedia. Nadzybar tried to draw the Crusade towards the world of Fabia, where he meant to destroy it in one blow. Slaydo saw through the plan, and ordered false fleet dispositions and troop movements to suggest that he had taken the bait.

As he gathered his forces for the assault on Fabia, the archon made a fatal error. In 765.M41 he brought a large part of the Archenemy forces in the sector to Balhaut, the central world, to be rearmed and resupplied. Slaydo struck with almost the whole Crusade at once. The Warmaster led the first wave against the Archon's Palace of the Oligarchy in the hope of meeting Nadzybar.

On the tenth day of the campaign, at the eighteenth hour, Slaydo's command force met the archon and his elite bodyguard on the Western Palisade outside the palace. The Warmaster and the archon fought in single combat. Witnesses estimated that the fight lasted between six and eleven solar minutes. Slaydo took two mortal wounds, but pushed through the pain and gave Nadzybar a lethal blow. The archon's cultists, not knowing he was dead, stormed the Palisade to carry him off, and Astra Militarum troops defended their dying commander. Slaydo was found near death on the marble floor of the West Atrium, and died later that day.

In their rage and grief the Warmaster's bodyguard slaughtered every Archenemy warrior they met. They cut down the fleeing cultists who carried Nadzybar's palanquin without knowing whom it held, and later found the archon's body among the dead. The loss of Nadzybar was a heavy blow to the Chaos forces of the Sabbat Worlds and a turning point in the Imperium's pacification of the sector, though the Dark Gods were not ready to give up the sector.
