The Black Consuls are a [[Loyalist]] [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. They are a [[Second Founding]] Successor or "Primogenitor" Chapter of the [[Ultramarines]]. An ancient tome of knowledge, the ''[[Mythos Angelica Mortis]]'', lists them among the twenty Chapters founded to make up the ''[[Astartes Praeses]]'', a standing task force charged with guarding the regions of the Imperium that surround the [[Eye of Terror]].

The Chapter is known as the least compromising of the Primogenitor Chapters. Its members keep strictly to the tenets of the [[Codex Astartes]]. Its history is rich with victories, and its warriors are stern and severe. Imperial scholars note a history of xenophobia and unusually brutal tactics for a Successor of the Ultramarines.

Founding

The Black Consuls were originally the 72nd Chapter of the [[Ultramarines]] [[Legion]]. Their first [[Chapter Master]], [[Arrias Cordos]], was a veteran of both the [[Great Crusade]] and the darkest days of the [[Horus Heresy]]. Cordos survived the [[Great Scouring]] and became the first Chapter Master of the new Black Consuls when the [[Second Founding]] raised them.

In the early [[31st Millennium]], during one of the Chapter's first campaigns, Cordos led his warriors to victory in the Orbstar System against the [[Word Bearers]]. The campaign is known as the Cleansing of the Orbstar. Cordos earned the name "Bane of [[Lorgar]]" from it. His [[Power Axe]], ''Lorgar's Bane'', became a treasured relic of the Chapter after his death.

Campaigns and fate

Imperial records state that the Black Consuls were annihilated at the [[Siege of Goddeth Hive]] in 455.M41. The attackers were elements of the [[Word Bearers]] and the [[Iron Warriors]]. Other records show a force of Black Consuls still active in the [[Jericho Reach]] during the [[Achilus Crusade]] at the same time.Imperial accounts differ on the fate of the Chapter in 455.M41. The Jericho Reach records are treated as the more reliable by the [[Adeptus Astartes]] archivists, but the two accounts are not reconciled.

Scholars note that it was rare for every battle-brother of a Chapter to fight at one time, even when the homeworld was under attack. Squads were often detached for special duties or attached to composite forces. Officers also served as consuls, meeting their counterparts in other Chapters to coordinate larger strategies. Some battle-brothers served in the [[Deathwatch]]. Others made pilgrimages to the Shrine of the Primarch on [[Macragge]].

Resurgence

When [[Roboute Guilliman]] was restored in c. 999.M41 and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] began, the Black Consuls were returned to full strength. [[Primaris Space Marine]] reinforcements were received on their homeworld of [[Cyclopia]]. The Chapter now serves as a guardian of the Imperium in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]].

Before Guilliman's return, the Chapter's battle-brothers made regular visits to the Shrine of the Primarch. Several past Chapter Masters were counted among the most valued counsellors of the [[Master of Macragge]].
