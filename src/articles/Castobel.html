Castobel is a [[hive world]] in the [[Orpheus Salient]] of the [[Jericho Reach]]. It remained loyal to the Imperium through the "Age of Shadow", when the Imperium lost control of the sector, and welcomed the [[Achilus Crusade]] when it arrived. Its industry supplied recruits and materiel for the re-conquest of the former Jericho Sector, until the [[Tyranids]] of [[Hive Fleet Dagon]] laid siege to it in 817.M41.

History

Castobel is ancient, and some evidence suggests that it was settled in the Age of Technology. It joined the Imperium in the Great Crusade after a brief, bloody war that destroyed its original ruling caste, and in the 30th millennium it was already a prosperous hive world. When the Jericho Sector collapsed in the Age of Shadow, the hive cities became almost independent city-states held together by their faith in the Emperor.

For several thousand years Castobel remained largely cut off, raided by aliens, Renegades and Heretics. In 789.M41, [[Battlegroup Santos]] reached the edge of its system and was greeted by the world's forces, and Castobel's leaders accepted the Imperium. Lord Commander Ortarna Lokk of Hive Tyralos was chosen to rule as Imperial Commander and Lord Castellan. Over two thousand regiments of the Castobel Reborn were raised for the crusade in the years that followed. The world was assigned the highest tithe grade, and the crusade's leader, [[Lord Militant]] [[Tiber Achilus]], saw it as a model of conquered worlds supporting the conquest of others.

In 812.M41 the world's defenders were joined by three hundred [[Astra Militarum]] regiments, but Warp travel became increasingly difficult and left them stranded. In late 816.M41 two companies of [[Blood Drinkers]] [[Space Marines]] arrived in orbit aboard the battle barge [[Crimson Sacrament]]. Castobel came under assault in the early months of 817.M41, when Hive Fleet Dagon arrived. The blockade and the ground invasion brought production to a near halt, and the population was trapped behind the hive walls.

The Tyranids overran Hive Trimalov within six weeks of making planetfall, and billions died. When they moved on Hive Tyralos, Lord Castellan Lokk ordered the defenders to withdraw into the city and lure the swarm in, and the reactors and generators of the hive were detonated, which destroyed the swarm and the city. A stalemate followed. Both sides were depleted, and the Tyranids, denied the biomass of the hive, could not easily replace their losses.
