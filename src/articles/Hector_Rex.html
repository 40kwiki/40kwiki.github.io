Hector Rex is an [[Inquisitor Lord]] of the [[Ordo Malleus]] of the [[Inquisition]], and Proctor-General of the Conclave of [[Scarus Sector|Scarus]]. He is known across the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] as the Master of the Thirty-Fourth Chamber-Practical, the Victor of Hellanus and the Saviour of Vraks. He is a hard puritan who accepts no compromise with those who treat with [[daemon]]s, and he has given his life to fighting the servants of [[Chaos]].

He also holds the honorific Auditorii Imperator. It records that he was admitted to the throne room on [[Terra]] and knelt before the [[Golden Throne]], a privilege that most of the [[High Lords of Terra]] never receive.

Early life and training

Rex was found to be a powerful [[psyker]] when he was screened by the [[Schola Psykana]] of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]]. He outshone his classmates, most of whom were bound for service as sanctioned psykers of the [[Astra Militarum]]. In adolescence he received genetic enhancement from the Imperium's chirurgeons, in a manner like that worked on the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and grew to eight feet in height with a heavy, muscled frame.

When the enhancement was complete he was handed to the Inquisition and entered the retinue of Inquisitor Thor Malkin as an [[Acolyte]]. The dates of his birth and recruitment are not recorded.

Career

Rex rose quickly in the Ordo Malleus and was admitted to the inner circle that holds the order's restricted knowledge of the nature of daemons. He became Proctor-General of the Scarus Conclave, which oversees Inquisitorial affairs in the Scarus Sector, with a staff of inquisitors and acolytes and almost unlimited powers against any Chaotic threat there.

He has led Imperial armies and called on the [[Grey Knights]] many times. He also tracked down and captured the renegade Radical inquisitor Galasek, and his agents still hunt Galasek's followers through the Segmentum.

The Judgement of Hellanus

His best-known earlier action was the closing of a warp portal on Hellanus III. He commanded a Grey Knights strike force there and fought a [[Greater Daemon]] of [[Khorne]] hand to hand, driving it back into the Warp. For this he was awarded the ancient [[Nemesis Force Weapon|nemesis force sword]] Arias, said to have been blessed by the Emperor and kept in stasis for the greatest heroes of the order. The year of the action is not recorded.

Siege of Vraks

See also: [[Siege of Vraks]]

In the [[Siege of Vraks]], the long Imperial campaign to retake the [[Armoury world]] of [[Vraks Prime]] from the rebel Cardinal-Astra [[Xaphan]], Rex read in the [[Emperor's Tarot]] that the planet might fall to the Warp and become a [[Daemon world]]. He lobbied the Scarus Conclave for direct control of the war and took the 88th Regiment of the [[Death Korps of Krieg]] into his own retinue. The [[Ordo Hereticus]] objected that Xaphan had belonged to the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and the matter was theirs. Rex was elected Proctor-General and became leader in practice of all Imperial forces in the sector.

At the climax in 830.M41 he and a Grey Knights force under Brother-Captain [[Arvann Stern]] landed on Vraks Prime. There Rex faced the summoned Bloodthirster [[An'ggrath the Unbound]]. He called the daemon by its true name, which drove it back, then cut into it with Arias and sent bolts of psychic force along the blade. A final thrust into its chest cast An'ggrath out of realspace, and it was destroyed.

Vraks Prime was retaken, but both Khorne and [[Nurgle]] had tainted it. Rex placed it under Ordo Malleus quarantine and declared it Perdita, over the protests of the Ordo Hereticus. After a long review he concluded that the whole Vraks System should be interdicted. Warning beacons were set to turn ships away, and [[Imperial Navy]] patrols sweep through to keep pirates and aliens out.

Retinue and wargear

Rex keeps acolytes, sages, [[Servitor]]s and scribes to run his affairs, though most stay out of the fighting. In battle he is escorted by [[Exorcists]], priests trained to banish daemons by faith, together with veteran guardsmen, mystics and familiars. He may also call on the [[Officio Assassinorum]] and the Grey Knights, the Chamber Militant of his order.

His armour is artificer-crafted to fit his enhanced frame. He carries a master-crafted bolt pistol that fires psychically reactive bolts, a storm shield, Arias, a Grimoire of True Names, the Emperor's Tarot and sacred incense.
