The Ullanor Crusade was the campaign by which the Imperium broke the greatest Ork empire it had yet met, fought at the close of the [[30th millennium]] and decided on the world of Ullanor Prime. It was directed by the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Horus]] of the [[Luna Wolves]], with the [[White Scars]] and [[Ultramarines]] in supporting roles, and it ended with the death of the Ork overlord [[Urlakk Urg]] in his tower. The victory was commemorated in the Triumph of Ullanor, the most celebrated spectacle of the [[Great Crusade]], at which the [[Emperor of Mankind]] named Horus Warmaster of the crusade, gave him authority over his brothers, and announced that he would return to [[Terra]] and lead the crusade no longer.

Ullanor is remembered as the high point of the age. It was also the place where the first seeds of the [[Horus Heresy]] were planted: the Emperor's departure, justified by a work he would not explain, left the primarchs hurt and resentful.

Background

The Ork empire of Ullanor

The Ullanor sector was ruled by the Ork overlord [[Urlakk Urg]] from the world of Ullanor Prime. The core of his empire was a dozen planets that had been inhabited by humans, and which the Orks had conquered and enslaved. It was the largest Ork empire the Imperium had met up to that time.

Imperial accounts of the Ork defenders are thin. Little is known of their makeup, beyond the fact that on Ullanor Prime they outnumbered the Imperial troops by about five to one.

The decision to strike

At the close of the 30th millenniumDate estimated from the sequence of events. the Imperium gathered a great host to destroy the empire at its root. The host included the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] of the 63rd Expedition, the [[White Scars]] and the Ultramarines, a force of about a hundred thousand [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] in all. It also included some eight million soldiers of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], six hundred ships of the [[Imperial Navy|Armada Imperialis]] and a hundred Titans of the [[Legio Mortis]], the Death's Head Legion.

The campaign

Plan of the Warmaster-to-be

The plan was [[Horus]]'s and is recorded as one of his best. Called the Speartip in the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]]' records, it rested on the idea that an empire without its head would collapse under its own weight. If the overlord could be killed quickly, the Imperium would not have to fight the greater part of the Orks. The strategy therefore called for a decoy and a strike at the head.

The outer worlds

The decoy was assigned to the [[White Scars]] and the Ultramarines, who with a large part of the Army and Titans of other Legios attacked the outer planets of the Ullanor system. The offensive was fought for its own sake and with great energy, so that the Ork fleets would take it for the main thrust. They did. The Ork war fleets rushed outward to defend their lesser worlds and left Ullanor Prime dangerously exposed to the waiting main body of the Imperial forces.

The assault on Ullanor Prime

The main thrust was made by the Luna Wolves, over two million Army soldiers and the hundred Titans of the [[Legio Mortis]]. The landings were made by drop-pod on the perimeter of the fortress-palace, with heavy gunships delivering armour to the fields before the walls. The [[Orks]] outnumbered the Imperial troops by five to one, but their fleets had been drawn away. The human slaves freed from the labour camps by the invaders joined the attack.

Imperial accounts do not say where the Emperor was or what he did. The records say only that the Emperor led the whole force, and give no detail of what he did.

The Tower of Ullanor

The decisive blow came in the assault on the overlord's keep. Horus teleported with the First Company of the Luna Wolves, the [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] of the Legion's elite, to the foot of the great central tower, which the Orks called by a name the Imperial scribes rendered as the Tower of Ullanor. While most of the Terminators held the base against the horde that came running back to defend its master, Horus fought his way up to the summit with only ten of them, the Justaerin squad of [[First Captain]] [[Ezekyle Abaddon]].

At the top was the throne chamber, where Urlakk waited with forty of his strongest champions. Horus went through them with the twin lightning claws of his armour. The Terminators, unwilling to fire into a melee that held their primarch, followed in with their close weapons. Horus fought Urlakk himself and crippled him, lifted the Ork above his head, carried him to the battlements and threw him down to the ground. When Horus returned to the chamber, he found every Terminator and every Ork dead save one: Abaddon, who stood among a great pile of broken Ork bodies. This is the account in the Luna Wolves' own records, and it is probably the best known battle story of the Great Crusade.

Collapse

With Urlakk dead, the Ork host dissolved into infighting. The Nobz who had held its commands declared themselves warlords and fought each other, which is the manner in which the Ork empires have always ended. The remaining Orks at the fortress-palace were trapped between the two forces of the Luna Wolves and slaughtered to the last. The other worlds of the empire were subdued within a Terran year of Urlakk's death, and the scattered bands that did not submit were hunted by the Legions for years to come, across hundreds of systems, as far as Chondax and the Kayvas Belt.

The Triumph of Ullanor

Preparations

With the greatest victory the Imperium had yet won, the Emperor called a triumph. Ullanor was made a trophy world, entered in the [[Administratum]]'s rolls as Mundus Tropaeum, and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]]'s geoformers were set to work on the battlefield. They brought world-engines and stone-burners and cut a vast swath across the broken land, burying the Ork dead by the millions beneath the heads of crushed mountains and paving them over with a boulevard as broad as the footprint of some Imperial cities. Along it they set marker posts topped with the skulls of Ork leaders and bowls of smokeless promethium that burned with a blue-white flame. They allowed a single structure to stand: a pavilion of black marble and granite built in sections on Terra and carried across the void for the purpose.

The assembly

The Army and the [[Titan Legions]] lined the way. Troops stood ranked in numbers that the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]] could not count, each of them chosen for valour and each to wear until death the onyx and gold Ullanor Triumph Bar, forged from [[Bolter|bolter]] shells recovered from the field. The Titans stood above them and the contrails of a thousand aircraft cut the sky, while above the clouds Imperial warships held their station in the upper atmosphere in a gesture of renewed fealty.

Fourteen of the Legions that were then active sent contingents, and nine of the [[Primarch|primarchs]] came in person. The Triumph rolls name them: [[Mortarion]], cowled, with his Deathshroud; [[Fulgrim]], resplendent in gold and platinum; [[Magnus the Red]], whose presence set the Army's front ranks to murmuring; [[Lorgar]], silent and watchful; [[Angron]], whose rage was visible from a distance; [[Rogal Dorn]]; [[Jaghatai Khan]]; [[Sanguinius]], with the Sanguinary Guard; and [[Horus]], hero of the day. They stood on the balcony of the pavilion above the assembled armies.

The Warmaster

Main article: [[Warmaster]]

At the Triumph the Emperor declared Ullanor the greatest victory of the crusade. He named Horus Warmaster, a rank created for the purpose, which gave him command over every Expeditionary Fleet and every primarch, and he declared that he himself would return to Terra to undertake a work of great importance for the future of Mankind. He would not say what it was. The announcement was received with a silence that the Remembrancers noted and then explained, in later years, as awe.

The announcement drew great concern among the primarchs, and not a little resentment. They never came to terms with the Emperor's absence, and their hurt at his leaving the crusade for a project whose purpose he would not reveal is held by Imperial accounts to have laid the seeds of the Heresy.

Aftermath

The new order

The Emperor left for Terra, and the crusade was placed in the [[Warmaster]]'s hands. In the Emperor's place a [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]] under [[Malcador the Sigillite]] managed the business of the Imperium at home. The Luna Wolves were renamed the [[Sons of Horus]] in 002.M31, a change the Emperor had offered at the Triumph and that Horus took up on the advice of [[Sanguinius]].

The [[Council of Nikaea]] was held in 001.M31, the year after the Triumph.

The Orks

Main article: [[Orks]]

The Imperium believed the Orks to be broken for good. Scattered bands that attacked the frontier were regarded as pests for more than a thousand years after the Heresy, and the complacency this produced proved costly. In 544.M32, in the [[Time of Rebirth]], a warlord called the Beast gathered a host greater than the one beaten at Ullanor and made Ullanor Prime the seat of his new empire. The [[War of the Beast]] that followed brought the Orks to Terra itself and crippled the Imperium's armed strength.

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] holds an additional tradition, which the High Lords have never confirmed. According to the records of the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kubik]], an order to destroy Ullanor Prime after the Beast's death was not carried out. The Mechanicum instead used captured Ork technology to move the entire world through the Warp, reported its destruction in a star, and settled it afterwards under another name. The tradition holds that the world is the [[Hive world|hive world]] of [[Armageddon]]. Imperial historians treat the account with reserve, and the [[Ordo Xenos]] has declined to comment.

Legacy

The Triumph of Ullanor fixed an image of the Great Crusade in the minds of the Legions that survived it. The image of the [[Primarch|primarchs]] on the balcony is among the best known of the age. For the loyalist Legions and for many [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the later age, it is the last moment at which the sons of the Emperor stood together.

For the [[Traitor|Traitors]] the memory is more complicated. Several of those who stood on the balcony turned against the Emperor within a decade, at [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31, and the Triumph rolls describe [[Lorgar]] as silent and watchful, already long a traitor to the Emperor's cause.
