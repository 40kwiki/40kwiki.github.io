The Ullanor Crusade was the campaign by which the Imperium broke the greatest Ork empire it had yet met, fought at the close of the [[30th millennium]] and decided on the world of Ullanor Prime. It was directed by the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Horus]] of the [[Luna Wolves]], with the [[White Scars]] and [[Ultramarines]] in supporting roles, and it ended with the death of the Ork overlord [[Urlakk Urg]] in his tower. The victory was commemorated in the Triumph of Ullanor, the most celebrated spectacle of the [[Great Crusade]], at which the [[Emperor of Mankind]] named Horus Warmaster of the crusade, gave him authority over his brothers, and announced that he would return to [[Terra]] and lead the crusade no longer.

Ullanor is remembered as the high point of the age. It was also the place where the first seeds of the [[Horus Heresy]] were planted: the Warmaster's elevation roused jealousy among his brothers, and the Emperor's departure, justified by a work he would not explain, left the primarchs to guess at his purposes.

Background

The Ork empire of Ullanor

The [[Orks]] had been a presence on the edge of human space since the start of the Great Crusade, and in the first century Expeditionary Fleets met them again and again, as raiders, as marauding warbands, and occasionally as states. None had proved so large as the empire of Ullanor. Its core was a dozen planets that had been settled by Mankind before the [[Age of Strife]], which the Orks had conquered and enslaved. Its capital, Ullanor Prime, was an Ork-built [[Fortress world|fortress world]] at the centre of the sector, and its overlord, [[Urlakk Urg]], ruled over many hundreds of warbands, whose obedience was held by his personal strength and by a ferocity that the Orks found persuasive.

Imperial accounts of the Ork empire are thin. The Orks left no records, and the scholars who followed the crusade learned about them chiefly from the testimony of the slaves liberated from their labour camps. Urlakk had apparently succeeded at what Orks seldom do, which is to hold a coalition together for longer than a single war. The Imperial Army's assessment, preserved in the Officio Militaris, set the Ork host at five times the strength of the force that Horus eventually brought to Ullanor Prime and judged it capable of breaking the Imperial Army's line if left to grow.

The decision to strike

The decision, taken in about 999.M30,Date estimated from the sequence of events. to assemble a great host and destroy the empire at its root was the Emperor's. [[Great Crusade|The Great Crusade]]'s usual practice was to defeat each enemy as its fleets found it, with the nearest Expeditionary Fleet and what reinforcements it could draw. At Ullanor the Emperor chose to gather a concentration of force greater than any since the Unification and to use the campaign to demonstrate the crusade's power to the worlds that had joined it. The host included the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] of the 63rd Expedition, the [[White Scars]] and the Ultramarines, a force of about a hundred thousand [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] in all. It also included some eight million soldiers of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], six hundred ships of the [[Imperial Navy|Armada Imperialis]] and a hundred Titans of the [[Legio Mortis]], the Death's Head Legion.

The campaign

Plan of the Warmaster-to-be

The plan was [[Horus]]'s and is recorded as one of his best. Called the Speartip in the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]]' records, it rested on the observation that an Ork empire has no more structure than the will of the Ork who rules it. If the overlord could be killed quickly and visibly, the whole empire would devour itself, and the Imperium would not have to fight the greater part of its hundred warbands. The strategy therefore called for a decoy and a strike at the head.

The outer worlds

The decoy was assigned to the [[White Scars]] and the Ultramarines, who with a large part of the Army and Titans of other Legios attacked the outer planets of the Ullanor system. The offensive was fought for its own sake and with great energy, so that the Ork fleets would take it for the main thrust. They did. The Ork war fleets rushed outward to defend their lesser worlds and left Ullanor Prime less guarded than it had been in many years. [[Jaghatai Khan]]'s Legion in particular was noted by the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]] for the speed with which it overran the first of the outer worlds, and [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s Legion for the method with which it dismantled the second.

The assault on Ullanor Prime

The main thrust was made by the Luna Wolves, over two million Army soldiers and the hundred Titans of the [[Legio Mortis]]. The landings were made by drop-pod on the perimeter of the fortress-palace, with heavy gunships delivering armour to the fields before the walls. The [[Orks]] outnumbered the force by five to one, and they came at it in the manner of their kind, but they had been drawn out of position and could not close the distance with the speed they needed. The Imperial line held, and the human slaves of the labour camps rose behind the Orks and joined the attack.

Imperial accounts do not say where the Emperor was or what he did. The Triumph rolls state that he led the host, and a few sources speak of the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] and of an unseen presence in the heaviest fighting. The rolls give no detail, and the Remembrancers present were not able to account for his actions.

The Tower of Ullanor

The decisive blow came in the assault on the overlord's keep. Horus teleported with the First Company of the Luna Wolves, the [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] of the Legion's elite, to the foot of the great central tower, which the Orks called by a name the Imperial scribes rendered as the Tower of Ullanor. While most of the Terminators held the base against the horde that came running back to defend its master, Horus fought his way up to the summit with only ten of them, the Justaerin squad of [[First Captain]] [[Ezekyle Abaddon]].

At the top was the throne chamber, where Urlakk waited with forty of his strongest champions. Horus went through them with the twin lightning claws of his armour. The Terminators, unwilling to fire into a melee that held their primarch, followed in with their close weapons. Horus fought Urlakk himself in single combat and crippled him, lifted the Ork above his head and carried him to the battlements, and cast him down before the army. When Horus returned to the chamber, he found every one of the Justaerin dead, and also every Ork, except for Abaddon, who was standing in a ring of bodies. This is the account in the Luna Wolves' own records, and it is probably the best known battle story of the Great Crusade.

Collapse

With Urlakk dead, the Ork host dissolved into infighting. The Nobz who had held its commands declared themselves warlords and fought each other, which is the manner in which the Ork empires have always ended. The remaining Orks at the fortress-palace were trapped between the two arms of the Luna Wolves and slaughtered to the last. The other worlds of the empire were subdued within a Terran year, and the scattered bands that did not submit were hunted by the Legions for years to come, across more than a hundred systems, as far as Chondax and the Kayvas Belt. In 002.M31 the [[Alpha Legion]] followed a body of the survivors into the Kayvas Belt and destroyed it.

The Triumph of Ullanor

Preparations

With the greatest victory the Imperium had yet won, the Emperor called a triumph. Ullanor was made a trophy world, entered in the [[Administratum]]'s rolls as Mundus Tropaeum, and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]]'s geoformers were set to work on the battlefield. They brought world-engines and stone-burners and cut a vast avenue across the broken land, burying the Ork dead beneath the heads of crushed mountains and paving them over with a boulevard as broad as a city. Along it they set marker posts topped with the skulls of Ork leaders and bowls of smokeless promethium that burned with a blue-white flame. They allowed a single structure to stand: a pavilion of black marble and granite built in sections on Terra and carried across the void for the purpose.

The assembly

The Army and the [[Titan Legions]] lined the way. Troops stood ranked in numbers that the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]] could not count, each of them chosen for valour and each to wear until death the onyx and gold Ullanor Triumph Bar, forged from [[Bolter|bolter]] shells recovered from the field. The Titans stood above them and the contrails of a thousand aircraft cut the sky, while above the clouds Imperial warships held their station in the upper atmosphere in a gesture of renewed fealty.

Fourteen of the Legions that were then active sent contingents, and nine of the [[Primarch|primarchs]] came in person. The Triumph rolls name them: [[Mortarion]], cowled, with his Deathshroud; [[Fulgrim]], resplendent in gold and platinum; [[Magnus the Red]], whose presence set the Army's front ranks to murmuring; [[Lorgar]], silent and watchful; [[Angron]], whose rage was visible from a distance; [[Rogal Dorn]]; [[Jaghatai Khan]]; [[Sanguinius]], with the Sanguinary Guard; and [[Horus]], hero of the day. They stood on the balcony of the pavilion, under an inscription naming the Emperor lord of Terra and Ullanor, and the armies shouted for them for the better part of a day.

The Warmaster

Main article: [[Warmaster]]

At the Triumph the Emperor declared Ullanor the greatest victory of the crusade. He named Horus Warmaster, a rank created for the purpose, which gave him command over every Expeditionary Fleet and every primarch, and he declared that he himself would return to Terra to undertake a work of great importance for the future of Mankind. He would not say what it was. The announcement was received with a silence that the Remembrancers noted and then explained, in later years, as awe.

The reaction of the primarchs was not unanimous. Most welcomed the appointment as a fitting honour for the first of the Emperor's sons, and the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] celebrated for days. A few did not. [[Angron]], [[Konrad Curze]] and [[Perturabo]] are named in the sources as resentful. Several primarchs resented the loss of the Emperor's presence rather than Horus's elevation, and the confusion over the Emperor's purpose spread through the Legions as a rumour that the Imperium would pass into the hands of Terran officials and that the Legions would be disbanded when the crusade was over.

Aftermath

The new order

The Emperor left for Terra, and the crusade was placed in the [[Warmaster]]'s hands. In the Emperor's place a [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]] under [[Malcador the Sigillite]] managed the business of the Imperium at home. The Luna Wolves were renamed the [[Sons of Horus]] in 003.M31 at the Warmaster's own request. The change, apparently trivial, was taken by some as the sign of an allegiance to the primarch that did not run through the Emperor.

The shock of the Emperor's departure was compounded by the [[Council of Nikaea]], held in 001.M31, the first year after the Triumph, where the [[Primarch|primarchs]] found their father's attention turned in another direction and heard him pass a severe judgment on the [[Thousand Sons]]. The relationship between the primarchs and their creator, so intimate at Ullanor, had begun to fray.

The Orks

Main article: [[Orks]]

The Imperium considered the Ork threat to be over. The scattered bands that survived on the fringe were regarded as an annoyance for a thousand years after the Heresy, and the complacency that this produced proved costly. In 544.M32, in the [[Time of Rebirth]], a warlord called the Beast rose among the Orks, gathered a host that dwarfed the one at Ullanor, and made the ruins of the Ullanor empire his capital. The war that followed took the Orks to the edge of Terra and cost the Imperium a large part of its armed strength before the Beast was killed.

The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] holds an additional tradition, which the High Lords have never confirmed. According to the records of the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kubik]], an order to destroy Ullanor Prime after the Beast's death was not carried out. The Mechanicum instead used captured Ork technology to move the entire world through the Warp, reported its destruction in a star, and settled it afterwards under another name. The tradition holds that the world is the [[Hive world|hive world]] of [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]]. Imperial historians treat the account with reserve, and the [[Ordo Xenos]] has declined to comment.

Legacy

The Triumph of Ullanor fixed an image of the Great Crusade in the minds of the Legions that survived it. The mural of the [[Primarch|primarchs]] on the balcony, from which the [[Imperial Palace]]'s picture of the Great Crusade is derived, is the most reproduced image of the age. For the loyalist Legions and for many [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the later age, it is the last moment at which the sons of the Emperor stood together. The [[Imperial Truth]], the unity of the primarchs and the primacy of reason were all at their height at Ullanor, and none lasted more than a few years after it.

For the [[Traitor|Traitors]] the memory is more complicated. Several of those who stood on the balcony turned against the Emperor within a decade, at [[Isstvan III]] in 005.M31, and the [[Word Bearers]]' own accounts treat the Triumph as the moment when [[Lorgar]]'s disappointment with his father became final. It is known that Lorgar stood on the balcony and said little. Imperial accounts of his bearing agree on that point and on no other.
