Morst Bolifax was a [[cardinal]] of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]]. He served as [[planetary governor]] of the [[Cardinal World]] of [[Benediction]] in the [[Talledus System]] of the [[Veritus Sub-sector]]. He was also the [[sector lord]] of the sub-sector. He was a brutal traditionalist of the [[Imperial Creed]], and he ruled the system with an iron hand.

Purges

Suspicious of the deviations that had grown in the Imperial faith of his domain, Bolifax began a series of purges to remove subversive figures. He sent his most fervent [[missionaries]] to cleanse the impurities. Shrines and shantytowns were burned and forbidden texts seized. More than ten thousand apostates were crucified or stoned. A dozen sub-cults of the Imperial Creed were condemned as [[heretical]] by his decree, and their members faced torture and execution.

The Word Bearers' answer

The surviving sects were infiltrated by charismatic orators and zealous iconoclasts in the service of the [[Dark Gods]], and they were directed by the masters of the [[Word Bearers]]. At first these figureheads preached the supremacy of the [[God-Emperor]]. They railed against the tyranny of the [[Ministorum priest]]s and the executions of those who differed only in belief. They stirred popular anger against the cruel Cardinal Bolifax and compared his excesses to those of [[Goge Vandire]] during the [[Reign of Blood]].

The brutality of the crackdown made it easy for the cultist demagogues of the Veritus Sub-sector. Fear and hatred made new followers easy to find, and many disillusioned pilgrims were drawn into the worship of the [[Chaos God]]s. The cultists sowed the seeds of heresy, hiding their beliefs behind metaphor and rhetoric.

The Talledus War

When the Word Bearers led the [[forces of Chaos]] in an assault on the Talledus System, in what became known as the [[Talledus War]], Bolifax was formally given command of the Imperial defence as the [[Imperial Commander]]. He had no real role in the day-to-day strategic decisions of the Imperial forces.
