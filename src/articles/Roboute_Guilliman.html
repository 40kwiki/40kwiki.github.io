Roboute Guilliman, called the Avenging Son and in the early [[42nd millennium]] the Lord Commander of the Imperium, is the primarch of the [[Ultramarines]] and the Imperial Regent. He was the thirteenth of the twenty [[Primarch|primarchs]] created by the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. He was raised on [[Macragge]] in the east of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], where he became the ruler of a flourishing republic before the Emperor found him in 837.M30. Under his command the Ultramarines became the largest of the [[Space Marine Legions]], and the realm of [[Ultramar]], which grew from Macragge, covered some five hundred worlds at the height of the [[Great Crusade]].

During the [[Horus Heresy]] Guilliman was caught on the far side of the Ruinstorm. His Legion was ambushed on [[Calth]] by the [[Word Bearers]] in the opening move of the Warp storm that cut Ultramar from Terra. Guilliman could not reach the [[Siege of Terra]] in time, and arrived after the Emperor had been entombed on the [[Golden Throne]]. He took the mantle of Lord Commander, wrote the [[Codex Astartes]] and oversaw the [[Second Founding]], which broke the Legions into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of about a thousand warriors. About a century later he was mortally wounded by his brother [[Fulgrim]], and was placed in stasis in the Temple of Correction on Macragge.

Guilliman was revived in 999.M41, soon after the [[Fall of Cadia]], by the [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] and the power of the [[Aeldari|aeldari]] god [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnead]]. He crossed the galaxy to [[Terra]] in the [[Terran Crusade]], spoke with the Emperor, and proclaimed the [[Indomitus Crusade]], the largest military effort of the Imperium since the Great Crusade. His forces include the [[Primaris Space Marines]] raised by Cawl, with which the Lord Commander is rebuilding the Imperium's defences against [[Chaos]] and the powers that menace it.

Early life

Macragge

Main article: [[Macragge]]

The capsule that held the infant Guilliman fell on Macragge in about 792.M30, a cold world on the eastern edge of the galaxy that belonged to a decayed star empire dating from before the [[Age of Strife]]. Macragge had kept a few short-range warp-capable ships, a functioning industry, and an administrative culture that treated war as a science. The capsule was found in a forest by a party of Macraggian magnates, who recognised it as advanced technology and opened it to find a healthy child. The boy was adopted by the consul Konor Guilliman, who gave him the name Roboute.

The boy grew with unnatural speed and exhausted the teaching of the wisest scholars of the republic by his tenth year. His foster father granted him command of an expedition to subdue the barbarous northern lands of Illyrium as soon as he reached legal adulthood. Roboute pacified the region by a campaign of manoeuvre and negotiation, earning the respect of its warlords as well as their submission.

Death of Konor Guilliman

While Roboute was in the north, Konor's co-consul Gallan staged a coup with the support of the old aristocracy, whose estates were threatened by Konor's reforms. Roboute returned to find Macragge Civitas in flames and the Senate House under siege. Konor had defended it for three days against the rebels, but died before his son reached him. Roboute crushed the conspirators, executed their ringleaders, put the others to hard labour rebuilding the city, and took over the consulship himself.

The new consul broke the power of the old nobility, spread pre-Strife technology beyond the elite, and recast Macragge as a strict meritocracy in which office was earned by service. Within a few years the republic had one of the best-equipped planetary armies in the region. Macragge, the Imperial chroniclers recall, "flourished as never before".

Discovery and the realm of Ultramar

See also: [[Ultramar]]

The Emperor arrived at [[Macragge]] in 837.M30 and found his son at the head of a prosperous state. According to [[Ultramarines]] tradition the Emperor met Guilliman without the dissembling that had been needed with more savage sons such as [[Leman Russ]], [[Ferrus Manus]], [[Mortarion]] and [[Vulkan]]. Guilliman had already surmised the purpose for which he had been created, and swore fealty at once. The XIII Legion, previously known as the "War-born", was placed under his command.

Guilliman was given an independent command in the east, with Macragge as his base and the 12th Expeditionary Fleet at his disposal. He rebuilt the Legion on a new plan, drawing on the military doctrines and political philosophies of Macragge and on a study of every Legion and army in the Emperor's service. The reform allowed the Ultramarines to absorb recruits faster than any other Legion. Recruitment was meritocratic, and the intake dwarfed that of every other Legion by the middle years of the crusade.

Under Guilliman, Macragge grew into Ultramar, a realm of some [[Ultramar|five hundred worlds]] that he administered as a model Imperial province. The worlds he brought into compliance were given self-supporting defences and an efficient government. By some Imperial records the Ultramarines liberated more human worlds than any other Legion. By the time [[Horus]] was named [[Warmaster]] at [[Ullanor]], the Ultramarines were by any reckoning the largest Legion, and before the atrocity at [[Isstvan III]] they numbered more than a quarter of a million [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]].

The Ultramarines' [[Great Crusade]] campaigns included the war against the [[Osirian Psybrids]] at the Eurydice Terminal, where Guilliman himself killed the psybrid king in its innermost chamber, at the cost of his chief battle [[Psyker|psyker]], and against Ork empires and the [[Xenos|xenos]] of [[Eastern Fringe|the eastern fringe]].

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Calth and the Ruinstorm

See also: [[Battle of Calth]]

When Horus rebelled in 005.M31, the Ultramarines had been ordered to muster on [[Battle of Calth|Calth]], in the Veridian system. In 007.M31 the [[Word Bearers]], who had long resented Guilliman's Legion, struck at the Ultramarines on Calth and turned the world's own defence platforms against its sun. The star's outer layers were stripped away and the surface became uninhabitable. At the same time the Word Bearers' sorcerers conjured the Ruinstorm, a [[Warp storm]] that cut [[Ultramar]] off from the rest of the Imperium. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] on Calth, many of whom were native to the world, held fortified positions, and Guilliman coordinated their defence by clear orders broadcast to each pocket of resistance while his fleet struck at the Word Bearers' ships.

The Shadow Crusade

Main article: [[Shadow Crusade]]

After Calth the Word Bearers and the [[World Eaters]] launched the Shadow Crusade in the south and east, laying waste to twenty-six worlds. Guilliman's retribution fleet caught up with them at Nuceria in 008.M31, the home of [[Angron]], whose Legion had scoured the world of life in vengeance for his old slavery.

Imperium Secundus

Main article: [[Imperium Secundus]]

Beyond the Ruinstorm, Guilliman met his brothers [[Sanguinius]] and [[Lion El'Jonson]], whose Legions had been guided to Macragge by a strange alien beacon on the world of Sotha. In 009.M31 the three primarchs founded Imperium Secundus, a stopgap realm intended to continue the Emperor's work if Terra fell. Guilliman refused the title of Emperor and persuaded a reluctant Sanguinius to rule as Imperator Regis. Guilliman himself became Lord Warden and the Lion Lord Protector. The arrangement soured after [[Konrad Curze]] was loosed on Macragge. The Lion, hunting him, pressed for martial law and, in the rebellion in Illyrium, for weapons of mass destruction, and Guilliman resisted. Curze was captured and tried, and he revealed that the Lion had ordered secret orbital strikes against Illyrium in defiance of Guilliman's wishes. Guilliman and Sanguinius ordered the Lion and his Legion to leave, though the Lion later asked to be Curze's jailer instead.Imperial accounts of the quarrel differ in tone, since the Ultramarines and the Dark Angels each record the other as the party at fault.

Davin and the Siege of Terra

See also: [[Davin]] and [[Siege of Terra]]

The three brothers learned that the Emperor lived beyond the storm, abolished Imperium Secundus and fought through the Ruinstorm toward [[Terra]]. At the [[Davin|Second Battle of Davin]], on the world where [[Horus]] had first been corrupted, Guilliman and the Lion at last fought together to save Sanguinius, who was trapped in a portal to the Warp. Davin was destroyed, and the breach that its destruction opened allowed the [[Blood Angels]] to race to Terra while the Ultramarines and [[Dark Angels]] fought a diversion.

Guilliman was too far from Terra to take part in the [[Siege of Terra]]. His fleet of more than three thousand capital ships emerged from the Warp within hours of the Emperor's duel with Horus, but the Astronomican was dark and he could not find Terra. It was relit by the faith of [[Euphrati Keeler]] and her followers, and Guilliman arrived to find Terra in ruins and the Emperor on the Throne.

Lord Commander of the Imperium

Main article: [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]

The Imperial reform

Guilliman became the leading figure of the new regime. He took over the Council of Terra, held the office of Lord Commander of the Imperium and took the role of [[Imperial Regent]] that [[Malcador the Sigillite]] had held. He worked to restore order for more than a century while the loyal Legions fought the [[Great Scouring]] against the [[Traitor|Traitors]] who remained.

He had meanwhile written a new doctrine of war, the Codex Astartes, whose key provision was the division of the Legions into Chapters of about a thousand warriors each. The [[Second Founding]], decreed in 021.M31, seven years after the death of Horus, put it into effect. The Ultramarines were divided more often than any other Legion, and the new Chapters drawn from their stock, the Primogenitors, honour Guilliman as their founding father and patron.

Cawl and the Primaris project

At some point in this period Guilliman charged the Archmagos [[Belisarius Cawl]], who had worked under the Emperor in the Biotechnical Division, with two tasks. The first was to find a way to restore Guilliman to life should he fall. The second was to create a new breed of Space Marine, stronger and more resilient than the Emperor's originals. Cawl accepted both, and spent ten thousand years on them on [[Mars]].

Eskrador and Thessala

See also: [[Battle of Thessala]]

Guilliman continued to lead the Imperium and the Ultramarines for about a century. At Eskrador, in one of the campaigns against the remaining Traitors, he met the [[Alpha Legion]] and killed a warrior whom he took for [[Alpharius Omegon|Alpharius]]. The Alpha Legion did not break, and the Imperial accounts of the death are disputed, since another record has Alpharius killed by Rogal Dorn at [[Pluto]]. In 121.M31, at [[Battle of Thessala|Thessala]], Guilliman was struck down by [[Fulgrim]], by now a [[Daemon|daemon prince]] of [[Slaanesh]], whose blade cut his throat and poisoned him. He was carried to Macragge and placed in a stasis field in the Temple of Correction, in the [[Fortress of Hera]].

The temple became one of the holiest places of the [[Imperial Cult]]. Pilgrims flocked there in their millions each year. Some claimed that the wound on his throat was slowly healing, though savants insisted that nothing can change in a stasis field.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Awakening

In 999.M41 the [[13th Black Crusade]] under [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] broke through the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]]. At the Fall of Cadia the planet was destroyed, and the Warp storms that followed joined to form the [[Great Rift]]. [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]], who had been on [[Cadia]], escaped with an armoured reliquary and a suit of [[Artificer armour|artificer armour]], the Armour of Fate. Guided by the [[Harlequins]] and aided by the [[Aeldari|aeldari]] prophetess [[Yvraine]] of the [[Rise of the Ynnari|Ynnari]], the [[Celestinian Crusade]], named for the saint [[Celestine]], made for [[Macragge]].

Macragge was under attack from the Black Legion and its allies. In the Temple of Correction, Cawl set about reviving the body in the stasis field, and the Chaos forces assaulted the temple to prevent it. When the defenders were close to being overwhelmed, Yvraine called on Ynnead, the aeldari god of the dead. The power of the god, working with Cawl's technology, healed the wound on Guilliman's neck, and the primarch rose from his throne after ten thousand years. He shattered the Chaos assault and, in the days that followed, drove the Archenemy from Macragge and most of Ultramar within seven months.

Guilliman was crowned as the primarch of the Ultramarines and of their Successor Chapters, and resumed his place as Lord of Macragge. Marneus Calgar ceded the leadership of the Chapter to him, and Guilliman revoked the autonomy of the worlds that had once belonged to Ultramar, so that the Five Hundred Worlds would be restored.

The Terran Crusade

Main article: [[Terran Crusade]]

Guilliman was shaken by what he found. The Imperium of his memory had been a state of reason, and the Imperium of the 41st millennium was a theocracy that burned its psykers and worshipped its dead Emperor. He resolved to go to Terra and consult the Emperor. He assembled a fleet and left Ultramar in the hands of Marneus Calgar.

The Terran Crusade fleet was intercepted by the Daemon Primarch [[Magnus the Red]] near the Maelstrom and flung into the Warp rift. It wandered from daemon world to daemon world for months, losing ships. The Lord Commander was finally bound by the greater daemon Kairos Fateweaver in chains made of his own guilt. The fleet was saved by the Harlequin Shadowseer Sylandri Veilwalker and by the Fallen Angel Cypher, who freed Guilliman in return for a promise that the Fallen would be brought before the Golden Throne. The Imperials reached Terra through the Webway by way of a Blackstone Fortress, where Guilliman fought the Bloodthirster Skarbrand the Exiled One and defeated him. At the Lunar gate of the Webway they were met by Magnus and his Thousand Sons, whom Guilliman beat back with the help of the Harlequins, the Sisters of Silence and the Imperial Fists. The gate was sealed.

On Terra, Guilliman spent a solar day before the Golden Throne. What passed between father and son is not recorded, though Guilliman's later reflections suggest that the Emperor's attitude to him was colder than he had expected, and that the primarch left the Inner Palace convinced that he had been regarded as a weapon. He emerged to proclaim himself Lord Commander and to promise the largest fleet and armies since the Great Crusade.The vision that Guilliman later received on Iax suggests that the Emperor was conscious for a time during the meeting, a point that the Lord Commander has not discussed in public.

The Indomitus Crusade

Main article: [[Indomitus Crusade]]

The [[Indomitus Crusade]] followed the Battle of Lion's Gate, in which the Blood Legions of [[Khorne]], the Red Host, assaulted the [[Imperial Palace]] as the Great Rift opened and the light of the Astronomican failed. Guilliman led the [[Primaris Space Marines]], the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]] in the defence, and the daemonic assault was beaten back. He then assembled Fleet Primus, with Primaris Marines of the new Chapters and the nine Legions of the "Unnumbered Sons", elements of the Adeptus Custodes and strike forces from many First Founding Chapters led by the Imperial Fists.

The crusade fought across the Imperium Sanctus, breaking sieges and relieving worlds. The relief of [[Baal]], which was assaulted by Hive Fleet Leviathan, brought the Blood Angels' Chapter Master Dante and the Sanguinary Brotherhood into the crusade. Its first phase ended some twelve years later at the Battle of Raukos, where Guilliman declared the [[Imperium Sanctus]] stabilised. He then turned to the defence of Ultramar.The earliest Imperial reports described the crusade as lasting about a century and ending with the Plague Wars. Later records place the Plague Wars in c. 012.M42, early in the crusade, and set no end date. The later chronology is the accepted one.

The Plague Wars

Main article: [[Plague Wars]]

The [[Plague Wars]] were the invasion of [[Ultramar]] by the servants of [[Nurgle]], led by [[Mortarion]], the [[Death Guard]] and the daemonic Plague Legions of the [[Great Unclean One]] Ku'Gath, who operated from the corrupted [[Scourge Stars]]. The attack fell on [[Espandor]], [[Iax]], Konor and Macragge. Guilliman cleansed the [[Hive world|hive world]] of Ardium of its Death Guard garrison, then re-established the Tetrarch Council of Ultramar and reorganised the defence. In his Spear of Espandor campaign he retook that system and killed the Great Unclean One Qaramas with the Emperor's Sword.

The war ended on Iax, a hospital world that had been taken by the enemy. Guilliman confronted Mortarion in person and, like his experience of [[Fulgrim]] and Magnus, was beaten and infected with the daemon's Godblight. In the moment between life and death he experienced a vision of his meeting with the Emperor, and understood that the Emperor had awoken for a moment during their conversation. The Emperor's power burned the plague from his body and, as a conduit of that power, Guilliman struck through the Warp at the Garden of Nurgle itself. Mortarion fled and the Death Guard were recalled by their god. Ultramar was freed, though it remains under threat from the Scourge Stars.

Reform of the Imperium

As [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], Guilliman has attempted to reform the Imperium as well as defend it. He has replaced corrupt [[High Lords of Terra]], ordered a purge of the bureaucracy and restored the Tetrarch Council of Ultramar. He has resisted the veneration of himself as a divinity by the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]], though the Plague Wars led him to question the purely material foundation of the Imperial Truth, and he has treated with the aeldari when the Imperium's need is great. His reforms have met stiff resistance from the [[Administratum]].

Return of the Lion

In the Era Indomitus, during the Arks of Omen campaign, Guilliman's brother [[Lion El'Jonson]] woke from his long sleep. The two brothers have old differences, dating from [[Imperium Secundus]], and Imperial records say little about how they have dealt with each other since. The [[Dark Angels]] hold that their primarch answers to the Emperor alone, and the Lord Commander has not publicly contested the point.

Character and abilities

Guilliman is the most methodical of the [[Primarch|primarchs]]: a statesman, administrator and strategist before he is a warrior. Imperial chroniclers describe him as cold, exacting and rigorous in his planning, and as capable of fierce rage when provoked. He is a stern judge of other men and of himself. After his return he has been described as burdened by a spiritual malaise, grieving that he reached Terra too late to save his father and at the discovery of what the Imperium has become. His tactical gifts are unmatched among his surviving brothers; he has defeated armies larger than his own by coordination and logistics, and by an ability to broadcast clear orders to every part of a battle at once.

His strength as a warrior, though never his chief gift, is well attested. In the Great Crusade he killed the Osirian Psybrid king in single combat, and in the [[Era Indomitus]] he has slain [[Daemon|greater daemons]], including [[Skarbrand]] and Qaramas. Against the daemon primarchs he has been less successful: [[Fulgrim]] wounded him, [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] defeated him on the road to Terra, and [[Mortarion]] beat him on [[Iax]]. He is not infallible, and he admits it.

Wargear

In the Great Crusade Guilliman wore the Armour of Reason, an artificer suit that was remade many times to eliminate any flaw revealed in battle. His arms included the power fist called the Hand of Dominion, a master-crafted power sword, the Gladius Incandor, and a custom combi-bolter, the Arbitrator. His armour held a battlefield sensorium, the Cognis Signum, in the manner of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]]' own Thallax troops.

On his return Guilliman took up:

  • The Armour of Fate, a unique suit of [[Artificer armour|artificer armour]] made on [[Mars]], whose workings include life-support technology. It was kept by [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]] for ten thousand years.
  • The Emperor's Sword, the blade that the Emperor himself bore in the Great Crusade, given to Guilliman on his assuming the mantle of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]. It is wreathed in flame from hilt to tip.
  • The Hand of Dominion, a newer version of his old power fist, now with a built-in [[Bolter|bolter]].
  • The Iron Halo, a halo-shaped protective ring worn above the head, an honour granted to the most exceptional Space Marines.

Legacy

Guilliman is the most celebrated of the surviving [[Primarch|primarchs]]. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] and the Primogenitor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] honour him as their founding father, and the [[Imperial Cult]], in defiance of his own wishes, has begun to venerate him as a [[Saint Celestine|living saint]]. His [[Codex Astartes]] remains the foundation of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]' doctrine, though some Chapters, such as the [[Space Wolves]], the [[Dark Angels]] and the [[Blood Angels]], follow their own traditions. His return has revived the Imperium's morale and brought an influx of [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris Marines]] to the front lines, and it has divided the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] between those who regard the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] as the Imperium's best hope and those who fear that he threatens the entrenched powers of Terra.
