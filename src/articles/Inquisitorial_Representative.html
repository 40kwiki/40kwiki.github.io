The Inquisitorial Representative is the seat of the [[Inquisition]] among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. It is not a fixed person: it is held by whichever Inquisitor the Inquisition sends to a session, and the Inquisition answers to no High Lord for the choice.

In 544.M32 the seat was held by [[Wienand|Marguerethe Wienand]] and [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus]], who shared it. The seat had been granted to the Inquisition in place of the Grand Master of Assassins, [[Drakan Vangorich]], who lost his formal place but kept attending. In the early [[42nd millennium]] the Representative named among the restored High Lords is [[Kleopatra Arx]]. The Representative of 999.M41 is not named.

Standing

The [[Inquisition]] holds that it may stand above the council, and its Representative is treated with care by the other [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]. Imperial records suggest that some High Lords have disappeared because of the Inquisition, though the Representative has also been asked to investigate such disappearances.

History

The seat first appears in the roll of 544.M32 at the outset of the [[War of the Beast]]. [[Wienand]] and [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Veritus]] sat together, and with the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Heth]] they were among the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] who opposed the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] [[Kleopatra Arx]] sits for the [[Inquisition]].
