Helbrecht is the High Marshal, the [[Chapter Master]], of the [[Black Templars]], elected in 990.M41. He is accounted the greatest naval commander in the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and in 998.M41 he commanded every Space Marine warship at the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]], helping the [[Imperial Navy]] to break the Ork fleets. He then swore to pursue the warlord [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], with [[Commissar Yarrick]] at his side.

Stubborn, zealous and fiercely honourable, Helbrecht embodies the character of his Chapter. In the [[Era Indomitus]], at the urging of [[Roboute Guilliman]], he sent the Black Templars' crusades to defend every world of the [[Adeptus Ministorum|Ecclesiarchy]] within reach, and he has himself crossed the [[Primaris Space Marines|Rubicon Primaris]].

Early career

Helbrecht's rise was swift. As an Initiate he held the line against a [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] swarm at Deathcore Ridge. As a Neophyte he witnessed the Vinculus Crusade of 833.M41, when High Marshal Ludoldus fought beside the [[Inquisitor]] Vinculus and the [[Adepta Sororitas|Order of the Bloody Rose]] against [[Chaos]] death cults on Peleregon IV. A few decades later he joined the Sword Brethren for killing a [[Daemon|daemon prince]] with only his combat blade. He fought in Marshal Daidin's Crusade for many years and took command of it when Daidin died.His birth is estimated from his rank in 833.M41; Black Templars Neophytes are typically youths.

Schrödinger VII

In 930.M41 the [[Necrons]] of [[Imotekh the Stormlord]], [[Necron dynasty|phaeron]] of the Sautekh Dynasty, attacked the ice world of Schrödinger VII, and Marshal Helbrecht answered its distress call. His drop zones were struck by carefully planned ambushes, and he met Imotekh in single combat on an ice bridge above the cryonite mines. Helbrecht was the better swordsman, but the phaeron's living metal repaired every blow, and Helbrecht at last collapsed from his wounds. Imotekh cut off his right hand to mark the defeat and threw him from the bridge. The Templars carried their Marshal out and abandoned the world. Helbrecht swore an Oath of Vengeance against Imotekh. Later his crusade caught the Stormlord's flagship, the tombship Inevitable Conqueror, above Davatas; the battle barge [[Sigismund]] crippled it and the Templars boarded, but Imotekh teleported away, and Helbrecht could only send the empty ship into the nearest star.

High Marshal

In 990.M41 High Marshal Kordhel was killed by a [[World Eaters|Khornate Berzerker]], and the Marshals unanimously elected Helbrecht in his place. He received the Sword of the High Marshals, which by the decree of the first High Marshal, [[Sigismund]], contains fragments of the sword that [[Rogal Dorn]] broke in grief after finding the Emperor's body aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]]. He at once declared the Ghoul Stars Crusade against the Cythor Fiends, joined by the [[Death Spectres]]. In eight years the outlying systems were cleansed, but the aliens' core systems were found eerily empty. Before the mystery could be explored, word came that [[Ghazghkull Thraka|Ghazghkull]] had returned to [[Armageddon]].

Armageddon and the pursuit of Ghazghkull

See also: [[Wars for Armageddon]] and [[Ghazghkull Thraka]]

Three crusades mustered at the Chapter keep on Fergax and went to [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]] in 998.M41. Marshals Ricard and Amalrich took theirs to the surface, while Helbrecht, as the most experienced naval officer among the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] present, was given command of the combined Space Marine fleet. He led many boarding actions against Ork [[Space hulk|space hulks]] and, with Admiral Parol's [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]], crippled the Ork invasion in orbit. When the hulk believed to carry [[Ghazghkull Thraka|Ghazghkull]] withdrew, Helbrecht swore to follow it, and [[Commissar]] [[Sebastian Yarrick]] asked to come with him. They cornered the flagship Kill Wrecka, but Ghazghkull escaped into the Warp.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41 the [[Black Templars]] fleet was scattered. As it regathered to resume the hunt for [[Ghazghkull Thraka|Ghazghkull]], [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] arrived with news that many Ecclesiarchal worlds were under attack by [[Chaos]]. Helbrecht welcomed the [[Primarch|primarch]] and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] he brought, but was set on his oath until Guilliman reminded him of the sword he carried and the duty it stood for. Chastened, Helbrecht sent crusades to every Ministorum world within reach. At some point after the opening of the Rift he crossed the Rubicon Primaris himself.The date of Helbrecht's ascension is not recorded; it falls in the first years of M42.
