The Elysion Fields are a region of [[Cadia]]. Beneath them lie catacombs that were fortified by [[Ursarkar E. Creed|Lord Castellan Creed]] in the lead-up to the [[Fall of Cadia]]. The catacombs gave access to the systems that controlled the Cadian pylons.

During the respite before the fall, the [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] revealed that [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]'s purpose was to destroy the ancient pylons, which held the [[Eye of Terror]] in check. Cawl, secretly advised by the [[Necrons|Necron]] [[Trazyn the Infinite]], learned to turn the pylons into a weapon.

Fall of Cadia

Main article: [[Fall of Cadia]]

When the pylons were activated they severed the region from the Warp and banished the daemons. The same effect weakened [[Saint Celestine|Celestine]], whose power flows from the faith of the Emperor's servants through the Immaterium. She was beaten by Abaddon himself and left at his feet.The sources describe the sequence of the battle but do not give the exact date of each action. The Fall is dated to 999.M41.

Several hundred Cadian regiments were positioned on the Elysion Fields during the defence, among them the 99 regiments at the fields recorded in the sources.
