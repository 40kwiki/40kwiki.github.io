Legion of the Damned
Legendary band of [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]]
OriginUnknown; said to dwell in a hidden realm of the [[Immaterium]]
AppearsWhen a world or a campaign is in its last hope
StatusUnverified; reported by many sources across the Imperium

The Legion of the Damned is a mysterious band of [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] that is said to appear unbidden when all hope seems lost. Its legionaries come forth from a hidden realm of the [[Immaterium]], strike down the enemies of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], and vanish once their work is done. Imperial records treat the band as a legend, not a recorded unit.

Accounts from many worlds describe the same pattern. Soldiers under siege see armoured warriors in dark or ancient livery, who fight with great force and then leave no trace. Some commanders have reported that they carried the mark of Space Marine Chapters long dead, though no such Chapter has been identified.

Imperial view

The [[Ecclesiarchy]] regards the band as a sign that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] has not abandoned his servants. The [[Inquisition]] is more cautious. Its records list the Legion as a possible [[Warp]] phenomenon, and the [[Ordo Malleus]] has never confirmed that any of its appearances were genuine.
