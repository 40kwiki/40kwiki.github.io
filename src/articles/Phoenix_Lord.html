A Phoenix Lord is one of the greatest warriors of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]], and the leader of one of the [[Aspect Warriors|Aspect Shrines]] of the [[Asuryani]]. Each Phoenix Lord founded one of the shrines, and is taken to be the embodiment of that shrine's aspect of the war god [[Kaela Mensha Khaine]]. The title is given to the warrior who founded an Aspect, and it passes to the strongest of that Aspect's [[Exarch]]s in each generation.

Phoenix Lords are, in the Asuryani view, demigods of battle. Their legends are told across the craftworlds and are used to teach the [[Path of the Warrior]]. Their powers allow them to cheat death, and the Asuryani regard them as the living proof that the Path can outlast a single life.

Souls and armour

The soul of an original Phoenix Lord was placed in a [[Spirit Stone]] set inside its [[Phoenix armour]]. The soul merges with the personality of whichever Exarch wears the armour, and the chosen wearer is always among the mightiest of the Exarchs of that shrine. When the Phoenix Lord falls in battle, the next Exarch takes up the armour and assumes the memories and abilities of the Phoenix Lord and of every Exarch who has worn it before.

Imperial scholars disagree on what this means. Some believe that the Phoenix Lords are animated suits of armour possessed by the spirits of dead Exarchs. The Asuryani reject this view. Imperial records say that it is contradicted by the existence of Soul Stone technology, which every Asuryani uses to keep its soul from being consumed by [[Slaanesh]] when it dies.

Known Phoenix Lords

The best-recorded Phoenix Lords are the founders of the main Aspects. [[Asurmen]], "The Hand of Asuryan", leads the [[Dire Avengers]]. [[Jain Zar]], "The Storm of Silence", leads the [[Howling Banshees]]. [[Karandras]], "The Shadow Hunter", leads the [[Striking Scorpions]]. [[Maugan Ra]], "The Harvester of Souls", leads the [[Dark Reapers]]. [[Fuegan]], "The Burning Lance", leads the [[Fire Dragons]]. [[Baharroth]], "The Cry of the Wind", leads the [[Swooping Hawks]]. [[Drastanta]], "The Tempest of Starlight", leads the [[Shining Spears]]. [[Lhykis]], "The Whispering Web", leads the [[Warp Spiders]]. [[Irillyth]], "The Shade of Twilight", leads the [[Shadow Spectres]].

Imperial records also name Amon Harakht as the Phoenix Lord of the Eagle Pilots. No Phoenix Lord is recorded for the Crimson Hunters.
