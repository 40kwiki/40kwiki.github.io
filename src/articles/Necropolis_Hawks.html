The Necropolis Hawks are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] made up entirely of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. They were raised in the [[Ultima Founding]] of c. 999.M41 from a rare line of the [[Raven Guard]] [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], and carry the heritage of [[Corvus Corax]] into a new kind of war.

The Chapter trains for close-quarters war and has fought a dozen city battles in the [[Era Indomitus]]. Its warriors are stoic and efficient, and have gained a reputation for pragmatic brutality and relentless, carefully measured aggression. Their tactical role insignia is worn on the backpack as well as the shoulder, so that comrades can find them in the smoke and dust of urban war.

History

Founding

The Chapter was created in the first years of the [[Era Indomitus]], when new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] were raised from the existing [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of the Legions under [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The Necropolis Hawks are the only known Chapter of this line, and have no recorded successors. Their homeworld is not named in Imperial records.

War of Beasts

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

On the hive world [[Vigilus]], which straddles the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]] between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]], seven companies of the Necropolis Hawks turned the tide against a xenos insurrection and an outbreak of the Gellerpox in the Dontoria hive. They took the hive sprawl building by building, built several heavily defended safe zones and, without sentiment, demolished those they judged lost. Several battle-brothers were taken by Drukhari raiders escaping the world, to be used as pain slaves. Chapter Master Raquilon Zandtus led the purge of Dontoria in person.

Nachmund Rift War

Main article: [[Nachmund Gauntlet]]

In the Nachmund Rift War of M42 the Chapter sent more than 130 battle-brothers from its 2nd, 4th and 6th Companies. They joined the Imperial invasion of Dharrovar, where Captain Miloni Takta volunteered to stay behind during the evacuation and delay the enemy.Dates for the Nachmund Rift War and the Fourth Tyrannic War are given only as M42; the order follows the sequence of events in the Imperial record.

Fourth Tyrannic War

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]

The Chapter was among those sent to guard the Western Reaches of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] from [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]. [[Librarian|Epistolary]] Drakht, a battle-psyker and xenos-hunter, joined a Solblade task force against the splinters called Hive Fleet Nautilon and Hive Fleet Promethor. He followed a line of bioships to the [[Hive world|hive world]] Shakar, which the [[Tyranids]] had left alive, covered in a membrane that held vast numbers of other life forms in stasis beneath. Before the task force could learn why, the Tyranids noticed it.

Organisation and heraldry

The Necropolis Hawks wear blue-grey armour with white helmets, backpacks and gauntlets and a gold [[Aquila]]. The colours were chosen to blend with ruined cities and the smog of urban war. Company is shown by the colour of the pauldron trim and helmet crest following the [[Codex Astartes]]. Sergeants wear red helmets, veteran sergeants a red helmet with a white stripe, and lieutenants a white helmet with three vertical stripes. The badge is a black raptor's head on a white field, reminiscent of the ancient Raptor Imperialis of Terran Unity, and stands for the sharp wits and sight of the urban fighter.

Notable members

  • Raquilon Zandtus, [[Chapter Master]], who led the purge of the Dontoria hive.
  • Miloni Takta, Captain, who held the rearguard at [[Nachmund Gauntlet|Dharrovar]].
  • Lannator, [[Chaplain]] in the [[War of Nightmares|War of Beasts]], and Barbateion Zer, Chaplain at Dharrovar.
  • Hozidik Opolio and Drakht, Epistolaries; Drakht is a renowned xenos-hunter.
  • Caelun Durus, Lieutenant at Dharrovar.
