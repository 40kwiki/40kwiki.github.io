A Warp rift is a place where the [[Warp]], also called the [[Immaterium]], breaks through into the physical universe. The barrier between realspace and the Warp weakens, and creatures of the Warp, including [[Daemon]]s, enter the material universe. Some rifts are a seething whirlpool of raw psychic power, while others show glimpses of the [[Realm of Chaos]]. Imperial records also describe rifts that take the form of gates of green fire, clouds of impenetrable shadow and boiling lakes, all of which are said to drive mortals mad.

Rifts can open at random, or they can be made. Imperial scholars list several causes: an unpredictable movement of the Warp that breaks into realspace, the possession of a psychic mortal by a Daemon, a ritual of Daemon-summoning carried out by [[Chaos Cult]]ists, a breach caused by the psychic force of suffering after a great war or atrocity, a [[Warp drive]] implosion, and the unified will of the major [[Chaos Gods]]. A rift may last for moments or for centuries, and it is often settled by the once-mortal followers of the [[Ruinous Powers]].

Warp storms and rifts

A [[Warp storm]] is a related disturbance. It is a region of the Warp in which turbulence becomes so violent that it isolates star systems and sectors from one another. Starships caught in a storm are driven far off course, or are trapped in the Warp. Storms can occasionally break out into realspace and create a temporary rift, which allows a Chaos incursion into Imperial space.

Notable rifts

The best known rift in the Milky Way is the [[Eye of Terror]]. Imperial records say it was created when the [[Aeldari]] caused the birth of [[Slaanesh]] in the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], early in the 30th millennium. It is now the home of the [[Traitor Legions]] and their human followers, including [[Chaos Cultist]]s, [[Heretek]]s and the Traitor Titan Legions of the [[Dark Mechanicum]].

The [[Great Rift]], known in High Gothic as the Cicatrix Maledictum, is a tear in reality and a galaxy-wide [[Warp storm]] that divides the territory of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It was formed in 999.M41 as the events of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]'s 13th Black Crusade unfolded. The rift spread from the Eye of Terror towards the [[Hadex Anomaly]] on the [[Eastern Fringe]]. Imperial accounts record that travel and communication had grown more disturbed for some time before the rift formed.

The [[Hadex Anomaly]] lies at the core of the [[Jericho Reach]]. It bathes many worlds in a red glow and is a major obstacle to navigation and to astropathic communication. Entire star systems have been lost to it.
