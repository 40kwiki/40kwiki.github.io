Veiled Region

The Veiled Region is a vast, unmapped area of [[Wilderness space]] in the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], in the galactic south of the [[Imperium Sanctus]]. It is filled with dense nebulae and newborn stars, which hide its systems from long-range survey. Imperial navigators do not treat the region as a route, and few ships have entered it since the [[Great Rift]] opened.

The nebulae block most sensor readings and interfere with the [[Astronomican]] in parts of the region. Survey fleets sent in the [[Era Indomitus]] have returned with only partial data, and the Administratum lists the region as a zone of unknown extent rather than a sector.

Exploration

The [[Indomitus Crusade]] has marked the Veiled Region as a target for later survey. Its planners regard the region as a possible source of worlds for resettlement, but no formal expedition is recorded as complete at the time of writing.
