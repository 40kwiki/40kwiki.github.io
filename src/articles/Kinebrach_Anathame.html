The Kinebrach Anathame, often simply the Anathame, is a blade made by the Kinebrach, a [[Xenos|xenos]] people of the [[Interex]]. Bound to a power of the [[Warp]], it could be named against one being and would then be lethal to that being alone, even to a [[Primarch|primarch]]. The Word Bearer [[Erebus]] stole it from the Interex in 002.M31, starting the war that destroyed the Interex's alliance with the Imperium. On [[Luna|the moon]] of [[Davin]] in 005.M31 the blade wounded the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], and the healing of that wound in a Davinite lodge temple was the moment of his fall to [[Chaos]]. Erebus later broke it into the athames carried by the [[Word Bearers]] in the [[Horus Heresy]].

Origin

The [[Interex|Kinebrach]] were a long-limbed simian race who had once had a great empire of their own, and who lived within the Interex as its makers of devices and weapons. Long before, they had made weapons with the aid of powers of the Warp, and the Anathame was one. The Interex, which had fought the Warp's creatures and knew their danger, kept it under guard in the Hall of Devices on Xenobia Principis, among other forbidden things of its client races.

Nature

The Anathame was a long, curved blade of dark metal, cold to the touch, and those who handled it spoke of a presence within it. Its power lay in naming: a wielder who gave it the name of a victim made it a poison to that one being, against which neither armour nor a [[Primarch|primarch]]'s flesh was proof. To any other target it was only a knife. Imperial scholars who later studied the record of [[Davin]] concluded that the blade was a vessel for a Warp entity rather than a work of [[Xenos|xenos]] science alone. The [[Inquisition]] has since listed such named-death weapons among the most dangerous relics of the Heresy.

Theft

In 002.M31 the 63rd Expedition under [[Horus]] made contact with the [[Interex]], and its embassy visited Xenobia Principis. [[Erebus]], First [[Chaplain]] of the [[Word Bearers]], was among the visitors. In secret he stole the Anathame from the Hall of Devices, and he made it seem that the [[Luna Wolves]] were to blame. The Interex took the theft, and the slaughter of its guards, as an act of war. The talks collapsed, and the Luna Wolves and the Interex fought.

Davin

Main article: [[Davin]]

[[Erebus]] carried the blade to Davin, a world brought to Compliance by the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]]. There he gave it to Eugen Temba, the [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commander]] of Davin, whom the powers of Nurgle had already corrupted. When rebellion broke out on Davin's moon in 005.M31, [[Horus]] went down to it himself. Temba, by then a creature of [[Nurgle]], struck him with the Anathame. The wound would not heal, and no [[Apothecary]] could treat it.

The Luna Wolves' captains, the Mournival, took their dying primarch to the temple of the Serpent Lodge on Davin, as Erebus had arranged. In the temple Horus was healed in body, and in the Warp he was shown visions by the powers of [[Chaos]]. He woke turned against the Emperor. Imperial historians mark the wound on Davin as the true start of the [[Horus Heresy]].

The athames

The Anathame did not stay whole. Before the [[Word Bearers]]' attack on [[Calth]] in 007.M31, [[Erebus]] broke it into eight shards and from them made athames, ritual daggers carried by his chosen. The athames were used in the blood rites at Calth that raised the Ruinstorm. With one he stabbed [[Argel Tal]] of the Gal Vorbak c. 008.M31. The fate of the other shards is unrecorded.The date of the stabbing of Argel Tal is estimated from the sequence of the Shadow Crusade.
