The Zeist Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It was the site of the [[Zeist Campaign]], fought against the [[Third Sphere Expansion]] of the [[T'au Empire]] in 303.999.M41.

Notable worlds

[[Augura]] was a staging post for the T'au expansion into Imperial space during the Third Sphere Expansion. By the end of the Zeist Campaign, forces from more than a dozen [[Chapter]]s of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] had fought the T'au there, and the T'au were forced to evacuate. By then the planet's military and defensive infrastructure had been reduced to ruins.

[[Praetonis]] is a [[Forge world]] in the sector.
