The ''Talon of Horus'', first known as the ''Warmaster's Talon'', is a large lightning claw fitted with an early custom-built combi-bolter. It is carried by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], master of the [[Black Legion]] and [[Warmaster of Chaos]], who took it from the body of [[Horus]] at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]]. Abaddon wears it on his right hand as a chief emblem of his authority over the [[forces of Chaos]].

To the Imperium the ''Talon'' is a symbol of the Traitors' cause. The sight of it unnerves Imperial troops, and it provokes an unyielding hatred in the [[Blood Angels]], whose primarch it killed.

Description

The weapon is a heavy lightning claw with a thumb blade. A combi-bolter runs from the wrist to the knuckles of the gauntlet. It was designed for the first [[Terminator armour]] project as a flexible weapon, and is in effect two [[bolter]]s slaved to fire together. Two ammunition belts feed it from the right side, and the ejector casing is formed as the faces of howling [[daemon]]s. It is bulky and crude by later standards, but it saw wide use in the years of the Heresy.

Imperial analysts assume that Abaddon's ranged attacks combine the combi-bolter with the daemon sword ''[[Drach'nyen]]'', and that in close combat the claw is at least equal to a standard lightning claw.

History

Forging

The ''Warmaster's Talon'' was made by Master Adept [[Urtzi Malevolus]], a follower of the traitor [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kelbor-Hal]] and of the [[Dark Mechanicum]] that sided with Horus. In the same forge Malevolus built a unique suit of Artificer Terminator armour, one of the earliest of its kind, and the claw was made to match it. It soon became Horus's favoured weapon and a symbol of his office as [[Warmaster]] of the [[Great Crusade]]. Some apocryphal accounts say instead that it is a relic of the [[Age of Technology]] found on the world of [[Clthon]]. No record settles the question.

The Siege of Terra

During the [[Siege of Terra]], on the flagship ''[[Vengeful Spirit]]'', Horus used the claw to kill his brother [[Sanguinius]] and to mortally wound the Emperor. [[Ezekyle Abaddon]], First Captain of the [[Sons of Horus]], and his [[Justaerin]] Terminators fought through the [[Imperial Fists]] to the command deck but arrived too late. He took up the body of his gene-father and detached the claw. He is recorded as swearing to use it to strangle the Emperor himself, but he abandoned the assault on [[Terra]] and led the Traitors to the [[Eye of Terror]].

The Long War

In the ten thousand years since, Abaddon has led thirteen [[Black Crusade]]s, and in each of them the ''Talon'' has been on his right hand. The [[13th Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 was the last of these. In the Despoiler's own words, recorded by a dying [[Sigismund]], the claw has killed two primarchs.
