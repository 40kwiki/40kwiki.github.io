The Webway, also called the Labyrinth Dimension, is a network of tunnels and passages that lies between [[Realspace|realspace]] and the [[Warp]] and connects distant parts of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. It was made, according to aeldari tradition, by the [[Old Ones]] in the remote past, and inherited by the [[Aeldari]], who used it to bind together their empire. Within its passages a traveller can cross the galaxy without exposure to the Warp and without the need for a [[Navigator]] or the light of the [[Astronomican]].

The Webway was badly damaged by the [[Eye of Terror|Fall of the Aeldari]], and many of its passages are broken, sealed or open to the Warp. It nonetheless links the [[Craftworld|craftworlds]] of the Asuryani, the hidden [[Black Library]] of the [[Harlequins]], and [[Commorragh]], the vast city of the [[Drukhari]]. It is the chief reason that the aeldari, though few, can strike anywhere in the galaxy without warning.

The Webway is of the greatest importance in the history of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] attempted to open it to humanity in a secret project beneath the [[Imperial Palace]], and the ruin of that project at the start of the [[Horus Heresy]] bound him to the [[Golden Throne]]. The opening of the [[Great Rift]] at the end of the [[41st millennium]] tore further through the network.

Nature

The Webway is both a physical and a psychic structure. Its walls are said to be made of a psychoplastic substance akin to the [[Wraithbone|wraithbone]] of the [[Aeldari|aeldari]], and they generate a shield that keeps out the energies and the inhabitants of the Warp. A traveller within it moves through something like a tunnel, whose appearance may be of glowing crystal, of an endless corridor, or of shifting light, depending on the region and on the observer. Distances within the Webway do not correspond to distances in [[Realspace|realspace]], and a short walk may cross many light years.

The network varies greatly in size. Its main arterial passages are wide enough to carry starships and even, in the time of the aeldari empire, whole fleets. Most passages admit only travellers on foot or in small vehicles. It is joined to realspace by gates of many kinds. Some are built into the infinity circuits of [[Craftworld|craftworlds]], some stand on [[Maiden world|maiden worlds]] and the ruins of the old aeldari empire, some hang in orbit and a few, long forgotten, lie beneath the surface of human worlds. Every gate is sealed by runes of psychic power that only the aeldari know how to open, and many have not been opened for millennia.

The Webway's speed is its great advantage, but it is also a limitation. The aeldari can move swiftly between places joined by the network, but find it hard to reach worlds that have no gate. Because their craftworlds travel only with difficulty through the Warp, the Webway is the main means of contact between them.

History

The Old Ones

Main article: [[Old Ones]]

[[Aeldari]] legend holds that the Webway was built by the Old Ones, an ancient race who seeded the galaxy with life, during their [[War in Heaven]] against the [[Necrontyr]] and the [[C'tan]]. It allowed them to move their forces between the stars without risking the Warp, and to reach worlds that the Necrons could not. The Necrons, for their part, developed dolmen gates that can tear their way into the network, and Imperial records of the [[Era Indomitus]] describe Necron forces entering it through such breaches.

The aeldari empire

After the War in Heaven the young aeldari race took the Webway as its own and extended it. At the height of their empire, every aeldari world was joined to every other through its passages, and the aeldari used it so freely that they no longer had any need of the Warp for travel. This unity, which no human empire has ever possessed, was one of the foundations of their supremacy in the galaxy.

The Fall

The [[Fall of the Aeldari]], which gave birth to the [[Chaos]] god [[Slaanesh]] and created the [[Eye of Terror]], ruptured the Webway in countless places. Whole regions were swallowed by the Warp, and the passages that led to them became doorways into the realm of [[Daemon|daemons]]. The surviving aeldari sealed these doors where they could, but many paths now lead to dead ends, to abandoned worlds or to places inhabited by Warp entities. Even the aeldari no longer know the network's full extent. Their seers guard the knowledge of each [[Craftworld|craftworld]]'s place in it, and the [[Harlequins]], servants of the Laughing God [[Cegorach]], are said to know secret paths that no one else may walk.

The Imperial Webway Project

Main article: [[Webway War]]

During the [[Unification Wars]] the Emperor discovered, buried beneath a desert of [[Terra]], an ancient alien device that gave access to the Webway. He set the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] to building around it the machine that would become the [[Golden Throne]], and for centuries he directed in secret what he called his Great Work: an Imperial passage into the Webway, through which humanity could travel between its worlds without [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] and communicate without [[Astropath|astropaths]]. He believed that the Imperium could only survive if it possessed the same unity as the aeldari had once enjoyed. The project was known only to a few, among them [[Malcador the Sigillite]] and the [[Adeptus Custodes|Legio Custodes]].

The Emperor had cleared the human-built passages of their alien inhabitants and maintained, by his own power, the psychic shield that protected them from the Warp. In 005.M31 [[Magnus the Red]], seeking to warn his father of [[Horus|Horus's]] treachery, sent a sorcerous message to Terra that broke through the Palace's wards and through the shield of the Imperial Webway. The daemons of the Warp poured into the passages. The Legio Custodes, the [[Sisters of Silence]] and the [[Mechanicum]]'s forces fought a [[Long War|long war]] to hold them back, which is described in the article [[Webway War]].

The breach could not be repaired. To keep the gate beneath the Palace closed, the Emperor had to sit upon the Golden Throne and hold it shut with his own mind. He was relieved for a time by Malcador, who gave his life to the task, and after the [[Siege of Terra]] the Emperor's broken body was entombed in the Throne, where it remains. The Webway Project was abandoned, and its existence was concealed from all but a few. Imperial scholars still debate whether the Emperor's protection of humanity is connected to the sealed gate beneath him.

Places within the Webway

Commorragh

Main article: [[Drukhari]]

[[Commorragh]], the Dark City, is the largest settlement in the Webway and the home of the [[Drukhari]]. It began as a port of the [[Aeldari|aeldari]] empire and grew, in the millennia before the Fall, into a refuge for those who wished to indulge their appetites beyond the reach of aeldari law. Its isolation in the Webway allowed it to survive the Fall. It is now a city of countless spires and sub-realms, lit by stolen suns, from which the Drukhari raid [[Realspace|realspace]] for slaves and souls.

The Black Library

Main article: [[Harlequins]]

The [[Black Library]] is the best-hidden place in the Webway, a repository of the aeldari's knowledge of Chaos guarded by the Harlequins. It is said to hold a map of the whole Webway, made thousands of years ago. Few outsiders have ever entered it, and the [[Inquisition]] regards every account of it with suspicion.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The opening of the [[Great Rift]], which the [[Aeldari|aeldari]] call the Dathedian, shattered many of the Webway's arterial passages. Sections were cut off, gates were ruptured, and some passages now open directly into the Warp. The aeldari who survived abandoned large parts of the network. The [[Drukhari]], however, have used the confusion of the Rift to raid human worlds in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] on an unprecedented scale. Imperial forces have recorded sudden breaches of the Webway on human worlds, from which [[Xenos|xenos]] creatures and raiders have emerged without warning.

The [[Ynnari]], followers of the aeldari god of the dead, have used the network to move between the [[Craftworld|craftworlds]], and occasionally to fight alongside Imperial forces.

Imperial knowledge

The Imperium's knowledge of the Webway is fragmentary. The existence of the network is known to the [[Ordo Xenos]] and to some senior officers, but the details are sealed, and the history of the Emperor's project is known only to the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] and the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]]. Imperial forces have occasionally entered the Webway, usually with [[Aeldari|aeldari]] guides, and the few who have returned describe it as a place of unsettling beauty and danger.
