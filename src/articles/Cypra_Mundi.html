Cypra Mundi is a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Segmentum Fortress]] of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], the northern of the five segmenta of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is the headquarters of Battlefleet Obscurus, long counted the largest and most battle-hardened of the segmentum fleets of the [[Imperial Navy]], whose task for ten thousand years was to contain the forces of [[Chaos]] in and around the [[Eye of Terror]].

The world's orbital yards are among the greatest outside [[Mars]], and they are known above all for the Overlord-class battlecruiser. Cypra Mundi is also the seat of the Collegium Analytica, the Navy's institute of strategic study, and the home of many naval officer families. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 it has stood on the far side of the Rift from [[Terra]], in the [[Imperium Nihilus]].

History

Origins and the Great Crusade

Little is known of Cypra Mundi before the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]]. [[Mechanicum]] scholars believe that it was settled by Explorator fleets during the [[Dark Age of Technology]], before the 25th millennium.The date of settlement is inferred from the character of the world's oldest forges and is not recorded. Early in the [[Great Crusade]], probably about 820.M30, armoured formations of the XIII Legion, the future [[Ultramarines]], stopped an [[Orks|Ork]] advance on the planet's ash plains and saved one of its cities, bringing the world into compliance.The date is estimated from the fact that the XIII Legion is described as fighting before it took the name Ultramarines, which places the action before the discovery of [[Roboute Guilliman]] in 837.M30.

In the last decades of the Crusade, Cypra Mundi's yards were enlarged alongside those of Voss and Lorin to supply the expanding fleets, and the world grew into one of the main naval foundries of the northern galaxy.

Segmentum Fortress

After the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s reforms divided the Imperium into segmenta and gave each a fortress world to command its fleet. Cypra Mundi became the fortress of the Segmentum Obscurus at the time of these reforms, c. 020.M31, and has been overseen ever since by an official of the [[Administratum]] of the grade of Master, under the Lord High Admiral of the segmentum.

Its fleet bore the brunt of the [[Black Crusade|Black Crusades]] that issued from the Eye of Terror, from the First in 781.M31 to the Thirteenth in 999.M41, and in the [[Gothic War]] (139–151.M41) it supplied ships to Battlefleet Gothic.

The Curse Entropic

In 865.M41 a fleet of the [[Iron Warriors]] and their daemon engines laid a machine-curse on the planet. Its forges ran with molten metal and a million machines burned out on the surface. The [[Cult Mechanicus]] answered with a psalmic banishment, a coordinated rite performed across the world, which broke the curse and stripped the traitors of their daemonic allies. The planet was cleansed within a week.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade and after

When [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] launched the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, a great fleet left Cypra Mundi to reinforce the Navy at the [[Cadian Gate]] and staged at Belis Corona for a counter-offensive. The [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the Great Rift followed. The Rift left Cypra Mundi in the Imperium Nihilus, cut off for a time from the light of the [[Astronomican]]. Accounts of its condition in the [[Era Indomitus]] are fragmentary, but Navy records state that the fortress still stands and that its fleet has been reorganised to hold the northern rim of the Rift.Some Imperial records describe Cypra Mundi as lying on the edge of the Rift rather than beyond it. The Office of the Lord Commander lists it among the worlds of the Imperium Nihilus.

Geography

Cypra Mundi lies on the northern fringe of the galaxy, within striking distance of the Eye of Terror and the Cadian Gate. Its surface is largely given over to the forges of the Mechanicus and to ash plains left by millennia of industry. The orbital shipyards, ringed by defence platforms and dry docks, house the Battlefleet's headquarters and its dockyards.

Society

The planet is shared between three authorities: the Adeptus Mechanicus, which governs the forges; the [[Imperial Navy]], which commands the system and its approaches; and the Administratum's Master of the Fortress. Many officers of Battlefleet Obscurus are drawn from the aristocracy of Cypra Mundi itself, and naval service is the chief ambition of its noble families.

The yards build the Overlord-class battlecruiser, an ancient and difficult design of which new hulls are still laid down about once a decade. On rare occasions they also build a capital ship to the order of a wealthy [[Rogue Trader]] dynasty. The world also produces patterns of aircraft for the Navy, including Cypra Mundi pattern Lightning fighters and Marauder bombers.

The Collegium Analytica trains naval strategists and publishes studies of current campaigns for the fleets of the Imperium.
