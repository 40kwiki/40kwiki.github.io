Ursarkar E. Creed is a [[Cadia]]n officer of the [[Astra Militarum]] who was Lord Castellan of Cadia during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41. He was raised to command after [[Traitor|traitor]] regiments murdered the Cadian high command at the [[Battle of Tyrok Fields]], and he led the defence of Cadia against [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] until the planet was destroyed. He is recorded as missing, and his fate is not known.

Creed was considered the most able Cadian commander of his generation, a strategist compared by his admirers to [[Macharius]]. He was rarely seen without his friend and bodyguard, Colour Sergeant Jarran Kell, who died to save him on the [[Elysion Fields]].

Early life

Creed was found as a boy by soldiers of the [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian 8th]] Regiment in the burning ruins of [[Cadia|Kasr]] Gallan after a [[Chaos]] raid. He was holding a service pistol and a torn copy of De Gloria [[Macharius]], and refused to speak of what he had seen. He joined the Whiteshields, Cadia's youth corps, where he met Jarran Kell, and the two became lifelong friends. By the end of the five-year Drussite Crusade he was a captain and Kell his colour sergeant. He rose quickly, and by 992.M41 he was the most successful living Cadian commander, held back from higher office only by his unknown birth.Creed's birth year is unrecorded. It is estimated from his rank in 992.M41 and the length of his service.

Lord Castellan

Early in 999.M41, as the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] gathered, the [[Traitor|traitor]] Volscani Cataphracts struck at a review on the Tyrok Fields and in the Excubitoi Castellum, killing Governor Primus Marius Porelska and much of the Cadian high command. Creed, then a colonel commanding the 8th, took control of the surviving troops and put down the revolt. His soldiers demanded that he lead them; after refusing three times he accepted the office of Lord Castellan. He promoted officers he trusted and dismissed those he did not, making many enemies in the Cadian officer class, and reorganised the defences of the [[Cadian Gate]].

Defence of Cadia

When [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s fleets came out of the [[Eye of Terror]], Creed commanded the defence of [[Cadia]] with forces that included [[Logan Grimnar]]'s [[Space Wolves]], [[Saint Celestine]] and the [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]]. He held [[Thirteenth Black Crusade|Kasr Kraf]] and then moved the defence to the [[Elysion Fields]], the largest cluster of Cadia's ancient pylons, which Cawl reactivated to shrink the Eye.

Abaddon's [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] then struck at Creed's command post on the Elysion Fields. Kell pushed the Lord Castellan into a Valkyrie and stayed behind. Abaddon lifted him by the throat; Kell spat in his face and said "Cadia stands" before the [[Warmaster]] killed him. When Abaddon dropped the wreck of a [[Blackstone Fortress]] onto the planet, Creed organised an evacuation that carried about three million people off Cadia before it broke apart. He was not among those who escaped.

Fate

The Officio Militaris lists Creed as missing. No body was recovered, and the circumstances of his disappearance are not known. In the [[Era Indomitus]], [[Cadian Shock Troops|Cadian regiments]] of the diaspora have reported seeing him in command at the front, and some of their officers claim to have received his orders, but the Imperium has not confirmed that he is alive. Among the scattered Cadians he is treated as their commander in exile whatever the truth.The reports of Creed's survival are recorded here as claims. No Administratum or Officio Militaris record confirms them.

Character

Creed was silent and calculating where Kell was loud, and he rarely spoke of himself. He was famous for his trust in the common soldier and his disdain for officers who owed their rank to birth, and for the mantra that became the motto of the Cadian defence: "[[Cadia|Cadia stands]]".
