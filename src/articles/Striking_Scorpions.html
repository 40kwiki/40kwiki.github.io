Striking Scorpions
[[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Asuryani]]

Patron[[Kaela Mensha Khaine]]
Founder[[Asurmen]], first [[Phoenix Lord]]

The Striking Scorpions are the [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]] who are most skilled in close assault. They are the deadliest of the [[Asuryani]] warrior Aspects. They use stealth and shadow to close with their enemies, and they strike without warning. They are said to carry the wrath of the war god [[Kaela Mensha Khaine]].

History

After the [[Fall of the Aeldari]], [[Asurmen]], the first [[Phoenix Lord]], led the [[craftworlds]] away from the homeworlds of the [[Aeldari Empire]]. He founded the first [[Aspect Shrine]]s, including the Striking Scorpions' shrine. The Aspect warriors of each shrine trace their path back to his teaching.

Role

The Striking Scorpions are merciless and do not show mercy in battle. Their skill and their stealth make them useful for ambushes and for the removal of enemy leaders. They are a close-assault Aspect, as are the [[Howling Banshees]].
