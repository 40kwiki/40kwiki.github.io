The Triplex System is an Imperial star system of the [[Triplex Sector]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. At the close of the [[Great Crusade]] it was the centre of the [[Eastern Fringe]]. It was once counted among the greatest industrial hubs of the Imperium, rivalling [[Mars]] and [[Anvilus]] in the capacity of its forges. Its factories sustained the Imperial conquests on the far edge of the galaxy.

The system was home to the three-fold [[Legio Victorum]] and to several hundred militant forces of the [[Mechanicum]] Taghmata. It was one of the largest concentrations of military force in the east. The few inhabited worlds and mining colonies of the sector were held in thrall to Triplex by the threat of its Forge Worlds' anger.

Worlds

[[Triplex Galatia]] was the system's second-largest planet, and the first to be colonised. It was the most prominent world of the system before the [[Horus Heresy]]. During the Heresy the [[Dark Angels]] Legion devastated it for siding with the traitors, and in the 41st millennium it is a dead world.

[[Triplex Phall]] is the system's largest world. It remains a major Forge World in the 41st millennium, and the homeworld of the [[Legio Victorum II]], the Foe Hammers.

[[Triplex Thule]] is the system's smallest planet, and the homeworld of the [[Legio Victorum III]], the Foe Breakers.

Relations with Mars

Imperial records describe the Triplex System as a nascent power that strained against the Emperor's law and against the orders of distant Mars. Its worlds professed loyalty to the newly founded Imperium, but they saw in Horus's rebellion a chance to rise on their own terms.
