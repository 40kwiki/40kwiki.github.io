The Celestinian Crusade is an Imperial host named after [[Saint Celestine]], the Living Saint of the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. It is recorded in Imperial accounts as a crusading force of the [[Order of Our Martyred Lady]], and it fought on the far side of the [[Great Rift]] during the late 41st millennium.

The host takes its name from the Saint, whose cult is one of the most important in the Ecclesiarchy. Its members are sworn to her example of faith, and its banners carry her image and the symbols of the Order.

Background

In 999.M41 the [[13th Black Crusade]] under [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] broke through the [[Cadian Gate]]. Cadia was destroyed at its fall, and the warp storms that followed joined to form the [[Great Rift]]. The Celestinian Crusade was raised in the years of the Rift to hold the worlds that were still in the Emperor's keeping.The formation date of the host is not recorded. The period is estimated from the sequence of the Rift's wars.

Organisation

The host is led by Sisters of Battle of the Order and supported by Imperial Guard regiments. Its commanders answer to the Ecclesiarchy rather than to the sector governors, which is one reason for the host's independence.
