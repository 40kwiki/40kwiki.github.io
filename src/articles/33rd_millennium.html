The 33rd millennium (M33) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M33 to 999.M33. It was the high point of [[The Forging]], the age that the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] calls the golden age of the Imperium. The [[Imperial Cult]] spread to almost every human world, the Ministorum fought its first [[War of Faith|Wars of Faith]] against rival creeds, and new [[Foundings]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] raised [[Chapters]] such as the [[Howling Griffons]] and the [[Sons of Guilliman]].

The age was not peaceful. The [[Dark Angels]] fought a long series of campaigns against [[Xenos|xenos]] and in pursuit of the [[Fallen]], and the [[War of the False Primarch]] threw the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] into anarchy for eighty years. By the close of the millennium the [[Administratum]] had won back much of the influence it had lost to the Ecclesiarchy, at a cost to the efficiency of [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial government]].
