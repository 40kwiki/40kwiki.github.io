The Adeptus Mechanicus is the priesthood of the Machine God and the keeper of the Imperium's technology. It is the Imperial name for the Cult Mechanicus, the religion of the Machine God that arose on [[Mars]] in the Age of Strife, and its members, the Tech-priests, are the scientists, engineers and technicians on whom the Imperium depends for its weapons, its ships and the life-support machine of the [[Golden Throne]]. The order rules Mars and its [[Forge world|Forge Worlds]], fields armies of its own and enjoys more political and religious independence than any other adeptus.

The Mechanicus is older than the Imperium. Before the Horus Heresy it was called the Mechanicum, and its alliance with the [[Emperor of Mankind]] under the [[Treaty of Olympus]] made the [[Great Crusade]] possible. Its priests believe that knowledge is the manifestation of divinity, that machines carry a spirit of the Machine God, and that the Emperor is the Machine God's living avatar, the Omnissiah. They therefore reject the [[Imperial Cult]]'s doctrine of the God-Emperor while revering the Emperor above all.

The Mechanicus is the one institution that understands the machines of the Imperium, and it understands them less than it believes. Most of its priests can no longer create, only preserve; the work of a technician is a rite of oils, resins and hymns, and innovation is regarded with suspicion. In the [[Era Indomitus]], a faction around the [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] has challenged this orthodoxy, with the encouragement of [[Roboute Guilliman]].

History

Mechanicum and the Age of Strife

[[Mars]] was colonised early in human history and terraformed, and during the [[Age of Technology|Dark Age of Technology]] it shared with [[Terra]] the role of mother world to the galactic colonies. In the [[Age of Strife]] its infrastructure failed, the atmosphere collapsed and plagues killed most of its people. The survivors made a religion of preserving the technology that kept them alive. This was the Cult Mechanicus, whose priests built shelters beneath the dunes, rebuilt the machines they found and raised the first forge cities, and whose governing parliament of magi became the Mechanicum. Its [[Explorator]] fleets founded new [[Forge world|Forge Worlds]] in the image of Mars.

Great Crusade and Treaty

The Emperor travelled to Mars in peace (712.M30) and was received on [[Olympus Mons]], where many magi acknowledged him as the Omnissiah. The [[Treaty of Olympus]] gave the Mechanicum political autonomy and exemption from the Imperial Truth, six [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] houses and new [[Astropathy|astropath]] choirs, and in return it undertook to supply the armaments and ships of the crusade. The Emperor's own condition was a ban on the Silica Animus, thinking machines, and the sealing of dangerous machines in the Vaults of Moravec. The magi supplied the Legions, the [[Skitarii]], the [[Legio Cybernetica]], the [[Titan Legions]] and the Knights that marched with them.

Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

The ban on forbidden knowledge festered. When [[Horus]] rebelled, the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kelbor-Hal]] declared for him in 005.M31 and the Mechanicum split in the [[Schism of Mars]]. Skitarii fought Skitarii and Titan fought Titan. Those who turned to [[Chaos]] were henceforth called the Dark Mechanicum, who fused [[Daemon|daemons]] with the [[Omnissiah|machine spirits]] of their engines. In 012.M31 the loyalists, led by [[Zagreus Kane]], were formally recognised as the Adeptus Mechanicus, separate from the corrupted Mechanicum. Kane became its first Fabricator-General and the first High Lord to represent it. Thousands of Kelbor-Hal's captive followers were executed, and the loyal Titan Legions formed the Collegia Titanica. The [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]]'s decision to recognise the order is recorded as having ended a long political deadlock.

After the Heresy, [[Roboute Guilliman]] gave a secret task to the [[Archmagos|Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] that would bind the order and the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] for ten thousand years. The new Fabricator-General held a permanent seat among the High Lords. The Emperor, crippled in the last battle, was placed on the Golden Throne, a device so complex that only the Mechanicus can hope to maintain it.

Age of Rebirth to Age of Redemption

The following millennia are recorded in the order's own annals in bursts of crisis. The Mechanicus grew more secretive. Its conflicts include the so-called Shadow War with the [[Inquisition]] over the control of records, fought in secret by deletion programs and purge protocols. In the [[35th millennium]], in the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], the [[Moirae Schism]] split the order after the rebel Forge World of Moirae was destroyed by [[Exterminatus]], and it took more than two thousand years of warfare to purge its doctrines. In the [[36th millennium]] a growing distrust between the Mechanicus and the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] led to secret wars between the two. In the [[37th millennium]] the Apostles of the Blind King, rogue tech-priests, devastated the north-western fringe of [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] before they were destroyed.

Time of Ending and Era Indomitus

In the [[41st millennium]] the order was assailed on every front. Its research outpost on [[Tyran Primus|Tyran]] was the first to meet the [[Tyranids]], and the [[Tyrannic Wars]] destroyed several Forge Worlds, including [[Gryphonne IV]]. The [[Fall of Cadia]] drove refugees to the Forge Worlds of [[Agripinaa]] and [[Stygies VIII]], and the loss of Titans and fleets was irreplaceable. The High Lords ordered every Forge World to increase production.

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] cut the Forge Worlds off from their Explorator fleets and from each other. Seven Forge Worlds were destroyed outright, and several more were buried beneath the storms. The [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb worlds awoke, and with them the long-dormant Technomandrites of Magistrakh, which struck at Mechanicus holdings from within. Meanwhile the Mechanicus claimed credit for the awakening of the [[Primarch|primarch]] Guilliman and, under his orders, Cawl unlocked the vaults of the Primaris Project and awoke from stasis the armies of the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Ultima Founding]].

In the [[Indomitus Crusade]], the Mechanicus sent Skitarii Legions, Legio Cybernetica maniples and Knights of [[Imperial Knight|House Taranis]], together with fleets from more than a dozen Forge Worlds. When Guilliman returned to aid [[Ultramar]] in the [[Plague Wars]], Mars, [[Ryza]], [[Metalica]] and [[Thramas Crusade|Triplex]] [[Battle of Phall|Phall]] sent reinforcements. Imperial records of the early [[42nd millennium]] also record the Mechanicus's campaigns in the Rust Fields, where Forge Worlds led by Mars and [[Graia]] sent mining fleets into an asteroid belt held by [[Orks|Greenskins]], and the war with the Necrons for blackstone deposits, in which Cawl's agents fought beside the Knights of House Taranis.

Cawl has repeatedly asked the primarch to name him Fabricator-General of Mars. Guilliman has refused, explaining that he lacks the power to grant it, and that to try would provoke a second Martian civil war among the orthodox who regard Cawl and his innovators as [[Heretic|heretics]].

Beliefs

Knowledge is divine

The Cult Mechanicus holds that knowledge is the manifestation of divinity and that all creatures and artefacts that embody it are holy. Its object of worship is the Machine God, an immanent spirit that governs all machines and knowledge and is friendly to humanity. In order to interact with the world the Machine God is held to take a physical avatar, the Omnissiah, whom the Mechanicum recognised in the Emperor. The Cult also reveres the Motive Force, the divine energy that moves all things, which is especially important to the Electro-priests. A person's worth is the sum of his knowledge, and the flesh is an imperfect machine, so Tech-priests replace organs and limbs with cybernetic parts, and the oldest retain few biological parts at all.

Machine spirits

Every machine contains a minute fragment of the Machine God, a [[Omnissiah|machine spirit]], which controls its operation and must be addressed with respect. Tech-priests, and the [[Techmarine|Techmarines]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] whom they train, approach their machines with prayers, oils and incense, and the ritual may include the useful (lubricating an axle) and the apparently pointless (chant and libation).

Quest for Knowledge

The order's chief duty is the Quest for Knowledge, the search for lost technology across the galaxy, above all the [[Standard Template Construct|Standard Template Constructs]] of the [[Age of Technology|Dark Age of Technology]]. It is governed by two codes, the Mysteries, which the Cult holds sacred, and the Warnings, which forbid, above all, the creation of thinking machines.

Factions

The Cult contains several sects. The Khamrians pursue the forbidden science of artificial minds; the Omnissiads seek to summon the Machine God into an avatar other than the Emperor; the Organicists regard biological enhancement as the equal of cybernetics. Some are tolerated, while others are persecuted by the orthodox and the [[Inquisition]] as [[Heretic|heretics]].

Organisation

Leadership

The head of the order is the Fabricator-General of Mars, who is at once the secular leader of the Adeptus Mechanicus, the high priest of the Cult Mechanicus as [[Fabricator-General|Magos Mechanicus]], and the [[Planetary Governor]] of Mars. He holds a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The rulers of other Forge Worlds are also styled fabricator-general, but only the Martian one sits on the council. Below him is the Fabricator Locum, his deputy, and a hierarchy of priests, in which ranks are determined by knowledge and by the proportion of the body that has been replaced by machine.

Holy orders

The term Tech-priest covers thousands of roles. The principal holy orders are:

Military forces

[[Skitarii|Skitarii Legions]]
The cybernetic infantry of the order, raised as menials or vat-grown and psychosurgically conditioned. Skitarii are organised into legions, macroclades, war cohorts and battle maniples, and fight beside Dunecrawlers, Ironstriders and the covert Sicarian infiltrators. Tech-priests commonly direct them from orbit by data-tether. Their elite Secutarii guard forge cities.
[[Titan Legions|Collegia Titanica]]
The Titan Legions, whose god-machines are the heaviest weapons the Imperium can field. Each Titan is commanded by a princeps through a link to its machine spirit. The Collegia split in the [[Schism of Mars]], and the loyal Legions were united in the Collegia in 012.M31.
[[Legio Cybernetica]]
Maniples of battle robots, who also split in the Heresy.
[[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]]
The noble houses of the [[Knight world|Knight Worlds]], descended from the original colonists, who pilot the Imperial Knights. They answer to the Mechanicus through the Questor Mechanicus.
Centurio Ordinatus
Giants of war: the great Ordinatus engines, each built to deliver a single weapon.
Ordo Reductor
The Bringers of Blessed Ruin, the order's corps of demolition.

Forge Worlds

Main article: [[Forge world]]

The Mechanicus rules its Forge Worlds as a theocracy, and each is a planet-wide manufactory. Mars is the first. Others include [[Graia]], [[Metalica]], [[Ryza]], [[Lucius the Eternal|Lucius]], [[Stygies VIII]], [[Agripinaa]] and [[Thramas Crusade|Triplex]] [[Battle of Phall|Phall]]. Each has its own variant of the Cult's customs, and some have fought one another in great schisms.

Duties within the Imperium

The Mechanicus supplies the Imperium with its technology, and also with many tasks that keep it running. It builds and runs the fusion plants of the Generatoriums; the Genetoriums that govern every planet's biosphere, which also serve the [[Inquisition]] in searching for impurity and [[Mutant|mutation]]; the Librariums of Terra and Mars, where knowledge is stored; and the manufactoriums of the [[Forge world|Forge Worlds]]. Every [[Space Marine Chapter]] pays a tithe of a twentieth of its [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] each year to the Mechanicus, which monitors the health of the Chapters and prepares new [[Third Founding|Foundings]] when the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] decree.

Relations

[[Imperial Cult]]
The Mechanicus holds that the Emperor is the Omnissiah, but rejects the Ecclesiarchy's doctrine. The treaty protects it from missionary attention, and relations with the Ministorum have long been marked by suspicion.
[[Inquisition]]
The [[Ordo Machinum]] watches the Mechanicus, and the two have fought a long shadow war over records. The Inquisition persecutes the order's [[Heresy|heretical]] sects.
[[Adeptus Astartes]]
The [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Iron Hands]] and others have close ties to Mars, and [[Techmarine|Techmarines]] are trained there.
