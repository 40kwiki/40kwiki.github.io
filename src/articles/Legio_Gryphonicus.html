The Legio Gryphonicus, the "War Griffons", is a Loyalist [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Collegia Titanica]]. Many regard it as one of the greatest of all Titan Legions.

History

The Legio suffered a trial by fire during the [[Horus Heresy]]. It kept its oaths to the Imperium and fought the [[Traitor Legion]]s without wavering.

In 997.M41 the homeworld of the Legio, [[Gryphonne IV]], was devastated by the [[Tyranid]]s of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]. Gryphonne IV was known in myth before the [[Age of Strife]] as a seat of great technology, and it was the site of the Legio's founding.The founding date is not given in the sources.
