The Corinth Crusade, also called the Corinthian Crusade, was a campaign fought in 698–705.M41 by a combined force of Space Marines against the Ork WAAAGH! Skargor. It was among the best-known Space Marine campaigns of the mid-41st millennium. [[Marneus Calgar]], Chapter Master of the [[Ultramarines]], devised it and led it.

The Orks of the Corinth System, deep inside the Ork Empire of Charadon, threatened the eastern worlds of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Calgar called on five allied Chapters, the [[Angels of Absolution]], the [[Lamenters]], the [[Silver Skulls]], the [[Scythes of the Emperor]] and the [[Marines Errant]], and on over fifty [[Astra Militarum]] regiments.

Course of the crusade

The call to arms

The Ultramarines began the crusade to scour the Ork empires of the Charadon region on the Eastern Fringe. The Lamenters answered at once. They remembered the aid the Ultramarines had given them at the Siege of Corillia in 537.M38, during the [[Ninth Black Crusade]], and counted it a debt. They sent 300 Astartes with the battle barge Daughter of Tempests and her escorts.

Lhak

When the Ultramarines' 3rd Company lost its strike cruisers above the dust moon of Lhak, it was trapped on the surface by an Ork horde. The Marines Errant fought through a ring of freebooter ships and ramships to take their progenitor-kin off the planet. The Ultramarines gave the Marines Errant one of their own relic blades in thanks for the shed blood.The record gives no date for the rescue; it fell within the campaign of 698–705.M41. The Marines Errant were also singled out for honour for a series of boarding actions against Skargor's Terror Ships.

Slaughterhouse III

In 701.M41 the Lamenters asked for the task of attacking Slaughterhouse III, an Ork mining world where hundreds of thousands of human slaves were worked to death. Calgar agreed but promised no support. The Lamenters destroyed the orbital defences and swept away the Ork garrison, and the slaves rose to join them. The Lamenters then found themselves holding the world with 3,000,000 liberated prisoners, who could not be carried away.

The Techmarines tried to restore captured Ork ships. One battle barge and fewer than 200 Lamenters faced wave after wave of Ork ships and fighter-bommers, and many of the Chapter fell to the Black Rage. In the end the freed slaves asked for a merciful death rather than recapture. With a new Ork fleet gathering, the Lamenters set off hundreds of seismic charges laid through the mines, which collapsed the tunnels and made the world a vast tomb. Fewer than 100 Lamenters survived, and they carried away less than a tenth of the prisoners.

Militarily the raid was a success, because the mines were lost to the Orks and their clans fell to fighting over the system's minerals. Calgar offered the Lamenters the honour of an [[Iron Halo]]. They took the nearly unheard-of step of refusing it. Some allies called this an insult to the crusade's commander, which fed the suspicion that already surrounded the Chapter.

Aftermath

The crusade killed or scattered the leaders of WAAAGH! Skargor and removed the Orks of Charadon as a danger to the Imperium for more than thirty standard years. The Marines Errant and the Lamenters both came out of it with their reputations changed: the first with honours, the second with a refusal that was held against them later in the [[Badab War]].
