The Rock is the fortress-monastery of the [[Dark Angels]] and all that remains of their home world, [[Caliban]]. When Caliban was destroyed at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]], c. 014.M31, one great fragment survived that carried the fortress of Aldurukh, seat of the Order and of the Legion's Calibanite masters. The Dark Angels fitted it with [[Warp]] drives and void shields and made it the heart of their Chapter. Its official name is the Angelicasta, the Tower of Angels, but the Chapter and the Imperium call it the Rock. Deep within it the Dark Angels held their Legion's greatest secret, [[Luther]], for ten thousand years.The date of Caliban's fall is estimated from the close of the Heresy; Dark Angels records give no year.

History

Aldurukh

Before the [[Great Crusade]] Aldurukh was the fortress-monastery of the Order, the knightly brotherhood of Caliban that [[Lion El'Jonson]] and Luther led against the Great Beasts of the world's forests. When the Emperor found the Lion, Aldurukh became the Legion's home on Caliban, where Luther later trained new recruits after he was sent back from the Crusade.

The fall of Caliban

At the end of the Heresy the Lion returned to Caliban to find Luther and many of the Legion's Calibanite warriors in revolt. The planet's defence batteries fired on his fleet, and the battle that followed tore Caliban apart. Luther was found raving in the ruins of Aldurukh, and a Warp storm swept the rebels who would become the [[Fallen Angels|Fallen]] across time and space. The Lion vanished. What was left of Aldurukh, sitting on a vast shard of the planet's crust, was taken in hand by the surviving Dark Angels.

Ten thousand years

From the [[Second Founding]] (c. 021.M31) the Rock has carried the Dark Angels Chapter across the galaxy. Its upper works are a fortress city of towers and weapon-batteries; its lower levels are given to the cells where captured Fallen are brought to repent under the Interrogator-Chaplains, and to sealed chambers known only to the Inner Circle of the Chapter. Luther was held in the deepest of them, in a stasis cell, and only the Supreme Grand Master and a very few others knew he was there. It was also said within the Inner Circle that the Lion slept beneath the Rock, waiting to return.

In the late 41st millennium the Chaplain Boreas brought the Fallen Chapter Master [[Merir Astelan]] to the Tower of Angels, where he was questioned for fifteen days. Astelan later escaped. In 998.M41 the Fallen learned that one part of the ancient Tuchulcha engine lay inside the Rock, where it powered one of its systems, and they tried to take it; [[Azrael]] defeated them in the Caliban system.

The Era Indomitus

When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, a daemonic host led by the Fallen daemon prince Marbas struck the Rock to free Luther. Luther broke free of his cell and his long captivity ended. The Rock now serves in the [[Imperium Nihilus]], on the dark side of the Rift, where the Dark Angels fight to hold the worlds cut off from [[Terra]]. The return of the Lion to the Imperium in the Era Indomitus has not yet brought him back to the Rock.

Description

The Rock is far larger than any ship of the Chapter's fleet. Its surface bristles with defence lasers, torpedo tubes and docks for the strike cruisers and battle-barges of the Dark Angels, and its void shields can hold off a fleet. Inside, the halls of Aldurukh have been extended deep into the stone. They hold the Chapter's armouries, its vaults of relics from the Great Crusade, and the Tower of Angels, where the Inner Circle meets. Visitors from outside the Chapter are rarely allowed past its outer docks.
