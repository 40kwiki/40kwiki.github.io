The Order of Our Martyred Lady is an [[Order Militant]] of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], the militant branch of the [[Ecclesiarchy]]. Its patron is [[Saint Celestine]], the Living Saint whose martyrdom is the centre of the Order's rites. Its preceptories are on [[Ophelia VII]], the Cardinal world, where the Order's officers sit beside those of the [[Convent Sanctorum]].

Members of the Order are known for their devotion and their discipline. Imperial records describe its Battle Sisters as among the most fervent in the Sororitas.

Indomitus Crusade

Main article: [[Indomitus Crusade]]

During the [[Indomitus Crusade]], the Order served in Fleet Secundus, led by Battle Sisters of the [[Ebon Chalice]] and the Order of Our Martyred Lady. The fleet was sent toward the [[Eye of Terror]] in a deliberate thrust into the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. Its assignment was nicknamed the Road of Martyrs.
