Malfi is an Imperial [[hive world]] in the [[Malfian System]] of the [[Calixis Sector]]. It lies eight solar days through the [[Warp]] from the sector capital, [[Scintilla]], and is a semitropical, gloomy world of overbuilt hive cities and habitation ledges. It is the main population and manufacturing centre of the rimward territories of the sector, and its population of 23 billion is close to that of Scintilla. Its people believe it should be the sector capital, and they resent Scintilla's political supremacy. Scintilla is better placed in the stars to govern the whole sector.

Malfi is the planetary capital of the Malfian Sub-sector, whose governor answers only to the Lord Sector, and it is a seat of the [[Adeptus Administratum]] and of several powerful Calixian banking houses. Its central super-continent covers about seventy per cent of the surface and is divided into 1,243 administrative dominions held by the noble houses. The world can feed only about a fifth of its people without imports from agri-worlds such as [[Gallowglass]].

Government and society

Malfi is ruled by an indigenous hive oligarchy, the League of Patented Nobility, which lists three to four hundred noble houses at any one time. Under the Articles of Compliance of the Warmaster [[Golgenna Angevin]], the Matriarch or Patriarch is elected for life from the heads of the houses and serves as planetary governor and Imperial Commander of the Malfian System, with secondary authority over [[Gallowglass]] and [[Borusa]]. The houses fight their feuds by proxy through intermediaries and sponsored scav-gangs, and a true blood feud begins only with the killing of a noble or a close agent. Malfian aristocrats pay obedience only to the [[Adeptus Terra]], the Lords of [[Mars]] and the Ordos of the [[Inquisition]].

The [[Calixian Conclave]] of the Inquisition keeps a district office on Malfi and regards the world as a hotbed of heresy. Three times in two standard centuries Malfian dissenters have almost provoked civil war with Scintilla. Three heretical sects are known to be active: the [[Pilgrims of Hayte]], the Masqued and the [[Menagerie]]. The Masqued, whose members wear masks derived from the courts' masques, include many nobles and hold that nothing is what it seems and that Chaos alone is true. The Conclave has not treated it as a priority.

History

The early sector governors of the Calixis Sector set up their headquarters on Malfi after the [[Angevin Crusade]] conquered the sector in the 39th millennium. The sector's population later spread out, and Scintilla became the seat of government.

In 428.M41 House [[Koba]] established undisputed rule over the world. During this "Reign of Terror" the Malfian Ecclesiarchy became little more than a puppet of the house, and the nobility has never forgiven it. What moral authority the Church kept was shattered in 499.M41, during the "Bloody Solstice", when the birth of the [[Pilgrims of Hayte]] [[Chaos Cult]] tore Malfi apart and the high clergy shut themselves in their temples. The Church has since regained the trust of the people, though not of the nobility.
