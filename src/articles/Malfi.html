Malfi is an Imperial [[hive world]] in the [[Malfian System]] of the [[Calixis Sector]]. It lies eight solar days through the [[Warp]] from the sector capital, [[Scintilla]]. It is the main population and manufacturing centre of the rimward territories of the sector, and its population of 23 billion is close to that of Scintilla. Its people believe it should be the sector capital, and they resent Scintilla's political supremacy.

History

The early sector governors of the Calixis Sector set up their headquarters on Malfi after the [[Angevin Crusade]] conquered the sector in the 39th millennium. The sector's population later spread out, and Scintilla became the seat of government.

In 428.M41 House [[Koba]] established undisputed rule over the world. The Malfian Church was broken in 499.M41, during the "Bloody Solstice", when the [[Pilgrims of Hayte]] [[Chaos Cult]] tore Malfi apart.
