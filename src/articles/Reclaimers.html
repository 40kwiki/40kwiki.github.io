The Reclaimers are a Loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. Their Founding and genetic origin are unrecorded. Like the [[Iron Hands]], they keep unusually close ties to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] for a Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes.

Their equipment is more advanced than the Imperial average, even among the well-equipped Space Marines. Outside observers therefore assume that the Chapter's [[Armoury]] is large and varied. Their power armour is noted for its fine craftsmanship and decoration.

Chapter history

The Chapter's early history is not recorded. The surviving records begin in the late 41st millennium, and they describe campaigns against the [[Tyranids]], the [[Necrons]] and other xenos.

Notable campaigns

Defence of Corania. The Tyranids attacked the Imperial world of Corania without warning. Every Astra Militarum regiment on the planet held out for nearly a year. The fleet of the [[Imperial Navy]] and several companies drawn from the Reclaimers and the Swords of the Emperor Chapters then turned the tide. The date is not recorded.

Interitus Prime Incident (928.M41). A Reclaimers company under Captain Gries was sent to help the Adeptus Mechanicus investigate an archeotech structure on the desolate world of Interitus Prime. On arrival, the company found that a Mechanicus transport had been destroyed by a [[Night Scythe]]. The company boarded the xenos craft to plant demolition charges. A [[Commissar]], Ciaphas Cain, escaped from a [[Warp]] portal, the sole survivor of an Explorator team that had been wiped out by waking [[Necrons]]. The tomb complex was probably destroyed by the Reclaimers, though this was never confirmed.

Cleansing of Viridia (928–938.M41). The Reclaimers made their first landing on Viridia, at the palace of the Imperial governor. They broke the heretical siege lines around the palace and then conducted a long campaign to purge the world.
