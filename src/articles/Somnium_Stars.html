The Somnium Stars are a stellar region on the northern rim of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], in the far galactic north of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. They lie between the dying suns of the [[Ghoul Stars]] to the north-east and the frontier worlds of the [[Eastern Fringe]] to the south. Their name is recorded on the Imperial chart of galactic corruption, which marks the region as afflicted by [[Warp storm|warp storms]] and by strongholds of [[Chaos]].

Imperial records of the Somnium Stars are few and mostly incomplete. The region has been treated as a dangerous frontier since the [[Great Crusade]], and its worlds are rarely visited by the [[Imperial Navy]]. Most of what is known comes from the survey reports of [[Ordo Xenos]] agents and from the star-charts of the [[Astra Cartographica]].The Somnium Stars have no dedicated Imperial survey, and their boundaries on the galactic chart are estimated. The account here rests on chart labels and on sealed Inquisitorial reports.

Geography

The Somnium Stars lie beyond the most northerly [[Sector|sectors]] of the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]], and their stars are separated by wide stretches of [[Wilderness space]]. [[Warp|The warp]] storms that mark the region on the galactic chart lie across its [[Warp travel|warp routes]], and [[Navigators]] have found them hard to chart. Imperial charts show the storms growing and shrinking over the course of the [[41st millennium]], which makes the region's routes unreliable.

The Somnium Stars are close enough to the [[Ghoul Stars]] that some Imperial scholars treat the two as one frontier, and some of their warp routes are shared. The [[Astronomican]] reaches only the nearer part of the region, so passage into its interior depends on the navigational skill of the ship's crew.

History

Early Imperial presence

The first human colonies in the Somnium Stars were founded during the [[Great Crusade]] of the late [[30th millennium]], or soon after. Few of them survived the [[Horus Heresy]], when the region was cut off from the Imperial core. Later [[Administratum]] records mention worlds of the Somnium frontier that were lost without trace.The dates of the first colonies are estimated from the order of the Crusade's frontier expeditions.

Chaos incursions

The warp storms that cover the Somnium Stars on the galactic chart are the mark of [[Chaos]] activity in the region. Their strongholds are listed in the chart's key. The Imperium's accounts of the early [[41st millennium]] describe raids by [[Chaos Space Marines]] and cult fleets against the frontier worlds, and the loss of several settled systems to the warp.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 closed the Somnium Stars off from the rest of the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]]. The region's [[Warp storm|warp storms]] grew stronger as the Rift widened, and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has since made only limited attempts to reach it. The sector of the Ultima Segmentum that borders the region was left to hold its own defences, as the Crusade's fleets concentrated on the core.

Geography and warp routes

The region is a loose group of star-systems, linked by a few [[Warp travel|warp routes]] that [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] use with caution. The storms that cover the region are said to shift with the wider storms of the Rift. Ships that cross them must usually be guided by a Navigator who has been trained in the Imperial routes, and most charted routes end at the frontier worlds of the [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]].
