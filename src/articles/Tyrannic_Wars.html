Tyrannic Wars
Wars against the [[Tyranids]]
Date745.M41 – late 41st millennium
BelligerentsThe [[Imperium of Mankind]] and allies; the [[Tyranids]]
Fronts[[Ultramar]], the Eastern Fringe, southern [[Ultima Segmentum]]

The Tyrannic Wars were the wars fought against the [[Tyranids]], a biomorphic race that reached [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] from the intergalactic void beyond the Eastern Fringe. The wars took place over many fronts and across many centuries of the [[41st millennium]].

Each Tyranid [[Hive Fleet|hive fleet]] is recorded by its own name, and each struck different parts of the Imperium. The Fringe has borne the heaviest losses of all the Tyrannic Wars.

Hive fleets

Main article: [[Hive Fleet]]

Hive Fleet Behemoth destroyed [[Tyran Primus]] and fell on [[Ultramar]] in 745.M41, where it was destroyed at the [[Battle of Macragge]]. Hive Fleet Kraken struck in 992.M41, and was defeated at [[Ichar IV]] and by the craftworld [[Iyanden]]. [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] came from beneath the galactic plane in 997.M41 and spread into the southern [[Segmentum|Ultima Segmentum]].
