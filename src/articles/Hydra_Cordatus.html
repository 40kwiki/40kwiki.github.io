Hydra Cordatus is an [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperial]] [[Forge world]] and [[Desert world]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], near the [[Eye of Terror]]. It is one of the few places in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] where the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] stored its tithes of [[Gene-seed]] from the [[Space Marine]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. In this case the stored material was drawn from the [[Imperial Fists]].

The world has almost no population on its surface. Its settlements consist of a spaceport and a single large laboratory complex, and most of its work is carried out by [[Servitor|servitors]] and Tech-priests. Its position near the Eye made it a strategic point for storing material that could not safely be kept on a Chapter homeworld.

Gene-seed storage

The [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] held on Hydra Cordatus was kept against the loss of a Chapter's own stores. Imperial records state that the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] guarded the vaults with a force of [[Skitarii]], and that access was restricted to the Chapter's own envoys. The arrangement is described as one of the most secret in the Mechanicus archives.
