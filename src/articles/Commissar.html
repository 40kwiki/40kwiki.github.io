A Commissar is an officer of the Officio Prefectus, also called the Commissariat, who serves with the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] and the ships of the [[Imperial Navy]] to keep their morale, discipline and loyalty to the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. A Commissar stands outside the regiment's chain of command. He may take command of a unit whose officers have failed, and he has the power to execute summarily any soldier or officer, of any rank, for cowardice, desertion or heresy. Every Imperial citizen knows his peaked cap and greatcoat from the frescoes of the hab-blocks and the recruitment picts of the [[Departmento Munitorum]].

Commissars are drawn from the pupils of the [[Schola Progenium]], the institutions that raise the orphans of Imperial servants, and only the most zealous and capable are recommended for the Officio Prefectus. They serve first as Cadet Commissars in training squads, are then assigned to regiments, and the most distinguished rise to the rank of Lord Commissar, who advises a general or leads troops directly. Because a Commissar comes from a different world than the regiment he serves, he is untouched by its traditions and judges it purely from the view of the Imperium as a whole.

The Commissariat traces its foundation to the reform of the [[Imperial Army]] after the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31), when the whole body of the Army was divided into the Astra Militarum, also called the Imperial Guard, and the Imperial Navy, and when it was resolved to place in every regiment an officer whose only loyalty was to the Imperium. Its forerunners were the discipline masters of the Great Crusade, who enforced the [[Imperial Truth]] in the Army's regiments. In the centuries since, the Commissariat has become a permanent part of the command staff of the Astra Militarum, and its officers have served in every great war of the Imperium.

The Commissar is one of the most familiar figures of the Imperium, feared by the soldiers he serves with and often hated, though many are respected and some loved. [[Sebastian Yarrick]] of [[Armageddon]], [[Ciaphas Cain]], called the Hero of the Imperium, and Colonel-Commissar [[Ibram Gaunt]] of the [[Tanith First and Only]] are among the most celebrated soldiers of the [[41st millennium]].

History

Discipline masters of the Great Crusade

Main article: [[Great Crusade]]

The [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30 – 005.M31) was raised from the fighting men and women of the worlds liberated by the [[Space Marine Legions]]. In the early crusade it served almost entirely in garrison and pacification roles, but by the latter half it fought on the front line and supplemented the [[Legiones Astartes|Legions]] in the first assaults on planets, as the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] were stretched too thin across the growing Imperium. The Army had no standard organisation. Its regiments ranged from barbarians with power axes to mechanised infantry with directed-energy weapons whose designs are now lost, and they were left largely to their own devices as long as they served faithfully.

Some regiments carried specialists known as discipline masters. They were veterans of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|auxilia]] regiments of the civilised worlds of the [[Segmentum Solar]], and their task was to enforce order according to the writ of law and to impose the Imperial Truth, and belief in the destiny of Mankind to rule the stars, on native soldiers to whom the Imperium was a new master. They embodied the rule of distant [[Terra]] and, as the militia saw it, had the ear of the Emperor himself. To falter in the sight of a discipline master was to fail the Emperor in person. They kept hunting eagles as familiars to track down shirkers, and carried a ceremonial electro-scythe for summary executions. The Commissariat later took them as its model.

The Heresy and the founding of the Officio Prefectus

Main article: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) an estimated forty to fifty per cent of the Imperial Army turned on the Emperor and followed the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]]. Most of those regiments later broke down into fragmented [[Chaos Cults]] that worshipped the [[Chaos Gods]] and ravaged the civilian populations of thousands of worlds, and with the cruisers lent by the Armada Imperialis they spread quickly and left dead worlds behind them, some of which had been their own homes. It is suspected that some Traitor Army regiments took part in the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31), though the extent of the Traitor forces has never been calculated.

When Horus was defeated and the forces of Chaos had fallen back to the [[Eye of Terror]], the loyal regiments were reformed under the logistical authority of the [[Departmento Munitorum]]. The Imperial Navy was separated from the Armada Imperialis and made a distinct service, and the ground forces were renamed the Astra Militarum. In the same reforms, in about 021.M31, the Officio Prefectus was formed to be the Imperium's bulwark among the Guard and the Navy against the influence of Chaos. A Commissar was given the power to ensure his regiment's continued loyalty and was charged to prevent the spread of corruption by any means. Commissars were attached to every regiment and were used heavily in the wake of the Traitors' retreat to ensure that worlds ravaged by Chaos were not subverted by sleeper cults.

Rebirth and the transfer of the purge

Before the Imperium was fully reforged under the primarch [[Roboute Guilliman]] in the [[Time of Rebirth]], millions were executed by the Commissariat as traitors and heretics. Eventually the task of rooting out internal threats passed to the [[Inquisition]], first to the [[Ordo Malleus]] and, after its foundation in the [[Age of Apostasy]] in the early 36th millennium, to the [[Ordo Hereticus]], whose investigators were much better suited to the work than the military political officers of the Commissariat. The Commissars were left with the discipline and morale of the troops, which they have kept since.

The late 41st millennium and the Era Indomitus

Over the last ten thousand years the Commissariat has remained a part of the Guard's command structure, and it is rare for a regimental commander or [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Commander]] to lack a Commissar on his staff. A few have risen to commands of their own. [[Sebastian Yarrick]] took command of the [[Armageddon Steel Legion|Steel Legion]] garrison of Hades Hive in the Second War for Armageddon (941.M41) and became overall commander of the Imperial forces in the Third War (998.M41). Ibram Gaunt, who received command of the Tanith regiments from Warmaster Slaydo in the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] (from 755.M41), became the only known Colonel-Commissar to keep command of a full regiment.

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 and the launch of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], Commissars have served in the armies of the crusade and in the isolated regiments of the [[Imperium Nihilus]], where their duty to hold troops to their posts without hope of relief has become harder. The Commissariat remains attached to every regiment that the Departmento Munitorum raises in the [[Era Indomitus]].

Training

Main article: [[Schola Progenium]]

Commissars are trained from a young age to uphold the [[Imperial Creed]] and its strictures by the Schola Progenium, which also provides training for cadets, [[Grenadiers]] and [[Militarum Tempestus|Storm Troopers]]. The course is demanding and meant to weed out the weak as early as possible. It includes military exercise, weapons proficiency, hand-to-hand combat and religious study, and it aims at a total dedication that lets the pupil deliver the Emperor's justice without hesitation or remorse. A candidate must have unreserved faith in the Emperor, and must fight as well as any [[Hive city|hive-spire]] duellist while acting as both merciless taskmaster and inspiring hero.

On leaving the Schola a pupil is assigned to a regiment as a Cadet Commissar, with the standing of a private soldier, under the tutelage of the regiment's commander or of a senior Commissar. Cadets form their own units of ten to twenty, led by a veteran Commissar, and are armed with the ordinary [[lasgun]]. Commanders think themselves fortunate when such a unit joins a campaign, for the cadets fight with a ferocity uncommon even in the Guard's elite, and they know that they will be shot if they falter. Promotion to full Commissar comes only when the unit's observer judges a cadet ready, and it may take any length of time. Cadets who fail their training, but not through cowardice, are often given a commission in a [[Penal Legion|penal battalion]] or volunteer for a [[Rogue Trader]]'s retinue.

Duties and powers

A Commissar is empowered to preserve the moral purity and devotion of the men of his platoon, company or regiment by any means. He may execute any trooper, non-commissioned officer or officer who shows signs of breaking, a punishment which reminds the Guard that they must fear the Commissar's bolt pistol more than the enemy and which spares adjacent units the same fate. In principle his authority extends to regimental commanders, to [[Lord General|Lord Generals]] and to governors, if he has evidence of incompetence or treachery. Most units rarely break while a Commissar is with them. Commissars who cannot command the fear and respect of a broken unit have sometimes been killed by the deserters.

He may take command of a unit for a short time, usually a single battle, after shooting its leader. He fights in the front line and is trained as an orator, delivering speeches before battle and roaring prayers to the Emperor during it, which leads to friendly rivalries with any priest of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] present. In long campaigns, particularly sieges, he may requisition [[Administratum]] scribes to produce propaganda for the troops if the commander approves. After the reconquest of a world held by aliens he may open information stations where the locals can name human collaborators, a practice most common on worlds retaken from the [[T'au Empire]].

A Commissar must also watch any [[psyker]] under his care. [[Astropath|Astropaths]] and [[Sanctioned Psyker|Sanctioned Psykers]] attached to the Guard are in theory able to control their powers, but the Commissar looks for the signs of warp taint and [[Daemonic possession|possession]]. When he finds them he executes the psyker with a point-blank shot to the head, since it is held better to kill an innocent than to face a newborn [[Daemonhost]]. Where the hand of [[Chaos]] is suspected he refers the case to the Inquisition, though he may act alone if no Inquisitor is at hand. The Officio Prefectus has authority over all branches of the Imperial military except the [[Adeptus Astartes]], the [[Adepta Sororitas]] and the [[Skitarii]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]].

Ranks

The Commissariat has a ladder of ranks, of which the table gives the main grades from the highest to the lowest. A Commissar is attached to every regiment of the Astra Militarum or ship of the Imperial Navy, and regiments of great size have several.

Insignia and wargear

The Commissar's uniform is a black greatcoat with gold and red epaulets and red lining on collar and cuffs, a peaked cap with an [[aquila]] or a skull that marks a member of the Officio Prefectus, and often a red sash. Many wear a breastplate with the same devices as the cap and carry devotional symbols, such as an aquila pendant or a volume of the gospels of the [[Imperial Cult]]. His badge is the skull between spread wings. The uniform is the same in every regiment, which marks him as an outsider, though many adopt a regimental detail such as a gas mask or a desert scarf to seem more at home.

The standard weapons are a [[bolt pistol]] and a [[chainsword]], first presented at the commissioning ceremony in the high chapel of the Schola. A Commissar has access to an officer's armoury and may carry a [[power sword]], a [[power axe]] or, rarely, a [[power fist]], and some prefer a full [[bolter]]. A [[plasma pistol]] is reserved for high-ranking officers and Commissars. Many keep to their first weapons for sentiment or for the reliability of laser arms learned as cadets. A Lord Commissar wears a flak breastplate and usually carries a power sword and a bolt pistol.

Reputation

Propaganda of the Departmento Munitorum presents the Commissar as the soldier's guardian and the embodiment of the Emperor's will, and regimental songs and votive pictures honour famous Commissars as heroes. Soldiers often see him differently, as the officer who stands behind the line with a drawn pistol. Both images are true. The Officio Prefectus teaches that a Commissar must be feared before he is loved, but its most successful officers have been those who won their regiments' trust by sharing their dangers. The Commissar must learn the customs of his regiment and command equal respect from the dutiful [[Vostroyan Firstborn|Vostroyans]] and the hard-bitten [[Catachan Jungle Fighters|Catachans]]. Some regiments, such as those of [[Cadia]] and the [[Death Korps of Krieg]], hold their Commissars in high regard, and the Death Korps has its own Commissar-Generals. Others have murdered them.

The relation between a Commissar and the regiment's own colonel is delicate. The Commissar may overrule or execute the colonel, but in most regiments the two work together, the colonel commanding and the Commissar enforcing.

Notable Commissars

Several Commissars have become famous beyond their regiments. [[Sebastian Yarrick]] is held to be the greatest in the history of the Officio Prefectus, and was central to the defeat of the greenskin invasions of Armageddon. [[Ciaphas Cain]] of the 597th Valhallan Regiment, who is celebrated as a hero but recorded in his private memoirs that he thought himself a coward and a fraud, belongs to the same generation. The [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] produced many: [[Ibram Gaunt]], who commanded the [[Tanith First and Only]] and who learned his trade from Commissar-General [[Delane Oktar]] of the Hyrkan 8th; the severe [[Pius Kowle]], who was arrested by Gaunt for the flogging of a guardsman and died in the defence of Vervunhive; and Lady Commissar-General Viktoria Balshin, who was sent to purge the Second Front of the taint of Chaos.

Others are recorded for the manner of their falls. Lord Commissar Dominic Seroff, once the comrade of Yarrick, was banished by the Officio Prefectus to the backwater world of Eremus after his association with the governor of Armageddon, and the world died of a warp-plague soon after. Rumour places the [[Death Guard]] ship [[Terminus Est]] over it. [[Vance Theren]], the senior Commissar of the Orpheus Salient of the [[Achilus Crusade]], oversees thousands of lesser Commissars aboard ships and in regiments. Commissar [[Tomas Beije]] of the Tallarn 229th Regiment served at Adumbria in 937.M41, and Lord Commissar Auys Salieri is remembered as a man who could turn a battle with a single shot at a cowardly officer. The world of [[Terrax]] holds one of the largest Scholae, and the regiments raised there are trained by the Commissariat's course, so that a Terrax Guard regiment is held equal to a regiment of Commissars.
