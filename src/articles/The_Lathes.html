The Lathes, also called the Lathe Worlds, are three terraformed planetoids at the edge of the [[Malfian Sub-sector]] in the [[Calixis Sector]]. They are the foremost [[Forge world]]s of the sector, rivalling [[Scintilla]]'s Gunmetal City for weapons manufacture. The planetoids, Het, Hesh and Hadd, follow an irregular orbit that brings them together and produces periods of hyper-gravity.

Manufacture

At times of hyper-gravity, industry goes into frantic production. The rare metals and alloys can be smelted with special properties. Lathe-world blades are famed throughout the sector for their unbreakable character, and the gravitic forces that shape the blades also make Lathe-world ammunition dense and armour-piercing. These rounds are expensive and rare, and are often bought singly. Blades forged under gravitic duress are considered holy.

[[Lord Marius Hax]] owns a rapier of Lathe origin, given to him by [[Magos]] Luol Rho, the Lathe-world emissary to the court of Scintilla.

History

The Lathe Worlds began with the Calixis Sector itself, during the [[Angevin Crusade]] in 322.M39. The first actions of the crusade went as planned, and the Adrantis Region was subdued in 380.M39, the year of the "Dawn of the Lathe Worlds". Two centuries later the system was still only partly tamed.

During the [[War of Brass]] of 738-740.M40, the Hive Worlds of the [[Gelmiro Cluster]] fell into apostasy. The [[Calixian Mechanicus]] suffered heavy losses, and its forces then sealed the [[Iron Wall of Silence]]. The [[Meritech Wars]] of 211-226.M41 were a later threat from the [[Merates Clans]]. In 742-770.M41 the insular Calixian Mechanicus was again shaken by the [[Malygrisian Tech-Heresy]], when one of its own turned viciously against the Imperium.
