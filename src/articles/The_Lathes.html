The Lathes, also called the Lathe Worlds, are three terraformed planetoids at the edge of the [[Malfian Sub-sector]] in the [[Calixis Sector]]. They are the foremost [[Forge world]]s of the sector, rivalling [[Scintilla]]'s Gunmetal City for weapons manufacture. The planetoids, Het, Hesh and Hadd, follow an irregular orbit that brings them together and produces periods of hyper-gravity. Their workforce is small, and those born there are squat, heavy-boned and powerful. As on all forge worlds of the Mechanicus of Mars, visits require special permits, and a fraternity of warrior tech-priests guards the worlds and can call on Titans.

Manufacture

At times of hyper-gravity, industry goes into frantic production. The rare metals and alloys can be smelted with special properties. Lathe-world blades are famed throughout the sector for their unbreakable character, and the gravitic forces that shape the blades also make Lathe-world ammunition dense and armour-piercing. These rounds are expensive and rare, and are often bought singly. Blades forged under gravitic duress are considered holy.

[[Lord Marius Hax]] owns a rapier of Lathe origin, given to him by [[Magos]] Luol Rho, the Lathe-world emissary to the court of Scintilla.

History

The Lathe Worlds began with the Calixis Sector itself. The [[Angevin Crusade]] set out in 322.M39 under Praetor [[Golgenna Angevin]] to conquer the Calyx Expanse, accompanied by a sizeable contingent of the Adeptus Mechanicus. Its first actions went as planned, but the final push into the Adrantis Region (359-369.M39) failed and the Lord Militant withdrew into seclusion. Lord General [[Drusus]] took command, and a Mechanicus force under Archmagos Mat Aleph Xenon helped turn the tide. Adrantis was finally subdued in 380.M39, the year of the "Dawn of the Lathe Worlds". In gratitude Drusus signed a pact ceding several worlds to the Mechanicus, among them Hadd, Het and Hesh, which other agencies had judged unsuitable because of the gravity of the system. Fabricator ships landed on the three planets and in less than two decades laid the foundations of three forge worlds. The huge canyons cut into the surface by the autofactories were said to look as if they had been turned on a lathe, and the name stuck.

Two centuries later, in 580.M39, the system was still only partly tamed when a conspiracy of agents called the Praecursators shook the sector. The Lathes concluded that a rogue Inquisitor Lord was at its heart. In its aftermath the Fabricators worked to weaken the Inquisition's hold over Mechanicus holdings, and the sector governor ceded the planet Synford, now the sector's main producer of Baneblades. The Inquisition later reported that the Praecursators evolved into the tech-heretic cult of the [[Logicians]].

During the [[War of Brass]] of 738-740.M40, the hive worlds of the [[Gelmiro Cluster]] fell into apostasy under a ruler who called himself the Emperor of Brass. The [[Calixian Mechanicus]] suffered heavy losses, and in 740.M40 High Fabricator Kovikal Quietus declared the Iron Wall of Silence, a rule of segregation from all other groups of the sector. The [[Meritech Wars]] of 211-226.M41 were a later threat from the Meritech Clans. In 507.M41 the Lathes sent two Explorator fleets to help [[Battlefleet Calixis]] stop the Ork space hulk Pinnacle of Savagery at the Second Siege of [[Vaxanide]]. In 742-770.M41 the insular Calixian Mechanicus was shaken by the [[Malygrisian Tech-Heresy]], when Explorator Archmagos Umbra Malygris turned Renegade and carried a path of carnage across the sector.
