Simion Pentasian was the [[Master of the Administratum]] at the time of the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31). He led the [[Administratum]] through the war, keeping the records of the loyal worlds and passing them to the Council of Terra.

Pentasian is one of the few Administratum officials of the Heresy whose name is preserved in the civil records. He sat on the council of the High Lords through the war, alongside the [[Regent]] [[Malcador the Sigillite]] and the [[Adeptus Custodes]] Captain-General [[Constantin Valdor]].

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

During the Heresy the Imperial Administration kept the registers of the loyal worlds, measured the tithes and passed the records to the council. Pentasian's office was the working arm of that effort. The Administratum's records of the period are largely sealed, and his conduct is recorded mostly in the summaries of the council.
