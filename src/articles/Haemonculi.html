The Haemonculi, singular Haemonculus, also called the Lords of Pain, are flesh-sculptors of the [[Drukhari]]. They have lived in the depths of [[Commorragh]] for many [[Terran]] centuries. They are the greatest torturers of the Drukhari, and they compose their agony from captives in their dungeons.

Covens

The Haemonculi form units called covens. Covens are part of [[Commorrite]] society. They manage the regenerative processes the Drukhari need, and they draw the [[psychic]] essences of their victims to sustain their kin.

Fear

Other Drukhari secretly fear the Haemonculi, since they can reshape the soul as well as the body. Even among their [[Aeldari]] kin, they are feared for their terror and paranoia.
