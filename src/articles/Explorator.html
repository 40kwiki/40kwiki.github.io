An Explorator is an [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[Tech-priest]] dedicated to the exploration and exploitation of new worlds and new regions of the galaxy for the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Explorators act as adventurers, warriors and emissaries of the [[Machine God|Machine Cult of Mars]]. Their task is to travel into the depths of unknown space and to bring the technology and resources of newly found worlds back into Imperial service.

An Explorator usually leads a small expedition, accompanied by [[Servitor|servitors]] to carry equipment and [[Skitarii]] to provide protection. Each expedition is sent with a mandate from its forge, and its findings are recorded in the forge's archive. Imperial records describe the role as part scholar, part soldier and part diplomat.

Duties

Explorators survey worlds for mineral wealth, ancient machines and relics of the [[Dark Age of Technology]]. They also attempt to recover technology lost in the [[Age of Strife]]. When a world is found to hold useful resources, the Explorator may claim it for the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and petition the [[Administratum]] for its registration.
