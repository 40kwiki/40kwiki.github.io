Orask is an Imperial sentinel world at the edge of the Iquathan Deeps in the northern [[Segmentum Ultima]], close to the border of the [[Ghoul Stars]]. Its people are a feudal and warlike folk who, like the peoples of [[Cadia]] or [[Mordian]], are used to war. Orask has been a battlefront and bastion for millennia, and it guards the Imperium from the dangers beyond.

The world sits at the nexus of one of the few viable Warp routes through the Iquathan Deeps. That makes it a key point for the defence of the Imperium. Its garrison is therefore larger than that of most feudal worlds.

Defences

Orask has a planet-wide network of defences. Its garrison is made up of its own people and many regiments of the Astra Militarum. Detachments of the Adeptus Astartes and the Titan Legions are rotated through the world on a regular basis. Forces from the [[Imperial Navy]] also operate from Orask, and they can sally out to nearby systems when needed.

Recent history

In the 41st millennium, the defenders of Orask have held out against Ork marauders, Chaos Space Marine warbands, a previously unknown xenos species, and the forces of an Arch-Heretek of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]].

The most terrible attack came in 977.M41. A splinter of the eastern [[Hive Fleet]]s of the [[Tyranids]] reached the Orask System, twisted by its passage through the Ghoul Stars. The defenders fought a short but bloody battle. They were backed by a full company of the [[Red Talons]] and a Titan maniple from the Legio Magna. Tens of thousands of defenders were lost within a few solar days. The Tyranids were pushed back and purged from the planet's surface. Imperial records note that some harbinger organisms escaped to the outer systems.

Imperial accounts

Some Imperial records list the Legio Magna as responsible for the defence of Orask in 977.M41. The Legio Magna became a Traitor Titan Legion in the Horus Heresy, so this entry is disputed. Scholars disagree on whether a loyal part of the Legio survived the Heresy under the same name, or whether the record is simply in error.The disputed entry is recorded in a single Imperial source. Other records do not mention it.
