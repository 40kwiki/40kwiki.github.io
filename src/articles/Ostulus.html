Ostulus was the [[Ecclesiarch]] of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] who followed [[Erekart Mesring]] in 546.M32. He held office in the years after [[the Beheading]], when [[Drakan Vangorich]] was the real master of the Imperium, and he is recorded as the first Ecclesiarch whose seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] was made permanent and handed to every successor.

Little else is preserved of him. The archives give neither his birth, his path to the Ecclesiarchal Throne nor the manner of his death.Estimated; no record gives the end of his tenure or his death.

Background

Mesring had been shot by [[Koorland]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] for heresy at the height of the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32), and Koorland had stripped the Ecclesiarchy of its seat in the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] and of the state privileges of the Ministorum. The Ecclesiarchy thus stood at its lowest political point in centuries when Ostulus took the throne.

Tenure

In the aftermath of the Beheading, Vangorich reappointed Ostulus to the High Lords. The seat was restored, and from then on the Ecclesiarch has been a standing member of the council rather than one admitted at the pleasure of other lords. Ostulus ruled during Vangorich's regency, when the Ecclesiarch counted among the most powerful figures on Terra.

Legacy

The permanence of the seat shaped the Senatorum Imperialis for the remaining millennia. The Ecclesiarchy, which had lost its place for a time, never lost it again, and Ostulus's appointment is the usual point from which scholars date that arrangement.
