The Saruthi are an intelligent [[alien]] species native to a small interstellar empire bordering the [[Helican Sub-sector]]. Their empire is centred on the world of 56-Izar. Their worlds show a lack of symmetry, the result of [[Chaos]] mutation, which unsettles any [[human]] who sees it.

Society

The Saruthi seem to have no space travel of their own. They rely on interplanetary gates, called Tetragates, which work much like the [[Webway]] of the [[Aeldari]].

History

In the [[Age of Strife]], the Saruthi held more than forty star systems around 56-Izar, including the human colony of Damask. Humans who fled [[Terra]] carried a copy of the [[Necroteuch]]. When the tome was translated into a language the Saruthi could read, its exposure corrupted their minds beyond recovery.
