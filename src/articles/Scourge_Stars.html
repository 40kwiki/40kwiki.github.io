The Scourge Stars are a small, pestilential stellar realm made up of three former [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperial]] systems in the south of the [[Segmentum Ultima]], to the galactic north of [[Ultramar]]. They fell to the control of the [[Plague God]] [[Nurgle]] after the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41. The realm was the base from which the [[Daemon Primarch]] [[Mortarion]] and the [[Death Guard]] launched the [[Plague Wars]] against Ultramar.

The three systems are Noxias, Verminox and Rottgrave, which all lie north of Ultramar. The realm lies in the path of the Great Rift's [[Warp|warp]] rift, which runs from the [[Eye of Terror]] towards the [[Hadex Anomaly]]. Imperial scouts gave the three systems their collective name as a "trio of sickly systems", ruled by the servants of the Plague God.

History

Fall of Cadia and the Blackness

The Scourge Stars were born out of the fall of the [[Fortress World]] of [[Cadia]] to [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] in 999.M41. The destruction of Cadia cracked open the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]], and unnatural [[aetheric]] energies spilled into [[realspace]]. Long-range [[astrotelepathic]] communication and interstellar travel became almost impossible across the southern [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Imperial accounts call this period the [[Noctis Aeterna]], or "the Blackness".

In the Blackness, [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]] rose in several prosperous systems north of Ultramar. The [[Hive World]]s of the region were toppled, and the cults summoned daemonic aid from the [[Realm of Chaos]]. The worlds fell under the influence of [[Mortarion]], and so became the Scourge Stars, a foothold for the servants of the Plague Father in realspace.

Early campaigns

When the Scourge Stars were first established, [[Death Guard]] warbands, called vectoriums, struck nearby worlds. Their purpose was to keep any enemy from disrupting Mortarion's muster for the invasion of Ultramar. On [[Daxar]], the [[Apostles of Contagion]] met the [[Cadian Shock Troops]] of the 56th Cadian Heavy Infantry. The Cadians were driven back across the sulphur deserts, and Daxar fell to the [[Nurgle|Plague God]] after the Death Guard summoned hundreds of [[Beasts of Nurgle]] to break the Cadian line at Tarran's Pass.

During the siege of Godorian, the Death Guard's [[Plagueburst Crawler]]s fired diseased munitions into the city's manufactorum vents. The smog drove the defenders into the open, where they could be killed. In a later battle at [[Krug's World]], the Death Guard drew the [[WAAAGH!]] of [[Warlord]] [[Badsmak]] into a killing ground of [[Plaguespitter]]s and [[Noxious Blightbringer]]s, and destroyed the Ork host.

Plague Wars

Main article: [[Plague Wars]]

Mortarion's campaign against Ultramar, the [[Plague Wars]] of c. 000–012.M42, began with the "War of Flies". Virus bombardments and the Creeping Doom offensive against [[Espandor]] and [[Drohl]] wore down the [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Ultramar Auxilia]]. When [[Roboute Guilliman]] returned from the [[Indomitus Crusade]], his counterattacks stopped the Death Guard across several systems, and the largest battles of the war followed.

Two other commanders led armies out of the Scourge Stars. [[Ku'gath]] Plaguefather led the [[Daemon]] legions into the [[Tartella System]] and on to the [[Garden World]] of [[Iax]]. [[Typhus]], the Herald of Nurgle, led a rot-armada of Renegades, cultists and loyal Death Guard [[Heretic Astartes]] that destroyed three of the six star fortresses guarding Ultramar's shipping lanes. Guilliman answered with the Spear of Espandor counterattack.

War in the Rift

The war ended when the [[Chaos Gods]] [[Tzeentch]], [[Khorne]] and [[Slaanesh]] invaded the Scourge Stars to contest Nurgle's new empire. Nurgle recalled his forces from Ultramar to defend his realm, and Mortarion withdrew the Death Guard from Iax under the cover of a [[Virus Bomb]] assault. The Dark Gods then fought one another in realspace, in what Imperial scholars call the War in the Rift. The greatest Daemons met at the [[Battle of Vigrid]]. The fighting ended only when the Chaos Gods agreed to Tzeentch's proposal to settle the war by a contest of champions.

Governance

The realm is governed as a fief of [[Nurgle]], through the [[Death Guard]] and the [[Daemon|Daemon Primarch]] [[Mortarion]]. Its worlds are ruled by cults of corruption and by the Death Guard's vectoriums. No Imperial authority exists in the Scourge Stars, and the Imperium has never recognised the realm.
