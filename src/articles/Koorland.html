Koorland, known by his wall-name Slaughter and remembered as the Last Son of Dorn, was the Second Captain of the [[Imperial Fists]] and, after the destruction of his Chapter in 544.M32, believed to be its only survivor. In the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) he called the successor [[Chapters]] of [[Rogal Dorn]] together as the Last Wall, overthrew the failing council of Terra, and became [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. He brought [[Vulkan]] back into the Imperium's wars, founded the kill-teams that became the [[Deathwatch]], and died fighting the [[Orks]] at [[Ullanor]].

Ardamantua

Main article: [[Ardamantua]]

At the opening of the war in 544.M32 Koorland served on the world of Ardamantua with [[Chapter Master]] Cassus Mirhen, fighting the [[Xenos|xenos]] called the Chromes. When the [[Orks]] arrived with an attack moon, the [[Imperial Fists]] were overrun. Koorland was saved by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|tech-priest]] Phaeton Laurentis and healed by the Magos Biologis Eldon Urquidex. As far as the Imperium knew, he was the last of his Chapter.

The Last Wall

Rather than return to [[Terra]], Koorland invoked the Last Wall Protocol, a contingency of [[Rogal Dorn]]'s by which the successors of the [[Imperial Fists|VII Legion]] would reunite in a threat to Terra. He went to [[Battle of Phall|Phall]] without leave and met the [[Black Templars]], [[Crimson Fists]], Fists Exemplar and Excoriators. He led their combined fleets against the Ork moon over Terra, and was scolded by the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Udin Macht Udo]]. Though [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]] such as [[Maximus Thane]] and the Black Templars' Bohemond urged him to defy the council, he held at first that the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] must obey the [[High Lords of Terra]].

When Udo moved to expel the [[Inquisition]] from the [[Senatorum Imperialis|Senatorum]], Koorland acted. With [[Drakan Vangorich]], [[Wienand]], [[Lastan Neemagiun Veritus|Veritus]] and the [[Grand Provost Marshal]] [[Vernor Zeck]] he deposed Udo, and the council named him Lord Commander. His first act was to call the Imperium's forces to Terra for an attack on [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], now revealed as the Beast's home.

Lord Commander

Main article: [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]

Learning that [[Vulkan]] had been found fighting [[Orks]] on the world of Caldera, Koorland went to him, helped save the world, and won the [[Primarch|primarch]] to lead the attack on [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]]. Vulkan said that Koorland was worthy of [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]. On Ullanor, when Bohemond challenged him for command, Vulkan set a test: whoever could lift his hammer and break the gate before them would lead. Bohemond and the [[Crimson Fists]]' Quesadra failed; Koorland and [[Maximus Thane|Thane]]'s squad lifted it together and hurled it through the gate. Vulkan said that this willingness to work with others, not any single prowess, was why Koorland should lead.

Koorland went with Vulkan into the Beast's palace at Gorkogrod, but withdrew when he saw the primarch meant to fight alone. When Vulkan appeared to fail, Koorland created the kill-teams of picked Space Marines from many [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], despite the council's reluctance. They destroyed the Ork moon over Terra, and Koorland led an expedition to Nadiries that recovered the last of the [[Sisters of Silence]].

Back on Terra he treated the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] ever more harshly. When the [[Ecclesiarch]] Mesring, now mad, declared for the Beast, Koorland executed him, proclaimed the return of the [[Imperial Truth]] and stripped the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] of its seat on the council.

Death

Koorland planned to use a captured Ork [[Psyker|psyker]] together with the null-field of the [[Sisters of Silence]] to create a reversed [[Waaagh!]] that would kill the [[Orks]] around it. In the second assault on [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]] the plan worked. Koorland led an assassination team into the palace and killed the Ork on the throne, only to find that it was one of six Prime-Orks, each a Beast in its own right. The next of them crushed Koorland under its foot. His comrades recovered his body and withdrew. He was buried on [[Inwit]], and [[Maximus Thane]] rebuilt the [[Imperial Fists]] in his honour.
