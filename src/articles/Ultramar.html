Ultramar, also called the Realm of Ultramar, is a stellar realm in the [[Eastern Fringe]] of the [[Segmentum Ultima]], ruled by the [[Ultramarines]] from their homeworld of [[Macragge]]. It was created by the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] during the [[Great Crusade]], when it was a domain of some five hundred worlds, and is now a smaller realm of about a dozen star systems that is being expanded again. Its worlds are renowned in the Imperium for order, prosperity and civic learning, and Imperial publicists call it the Jewel of the Imperium.

Ultramar was the target of the [[Shadow Crusade]] in the [[Horus Heresy]], was the capital of the short-lived [[Imperium Secundus]], and was reduced in size by the [[Second Founding]]. In the [[41st millennium]] it repelled [[Battle of Macragge|Hive Fleet Behemoth]] and several [[Chaos]] invasions. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it was the main theatre of the [[Plague Wars]], and since the return of Guilliman it has been restored and has become one of the strongholds of the [[Imperium Sanctus]].

History

Foundation

Ultramar began as the alliance of worlds around [[Macragge]] that [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] built before the Emperor arrived. Its first members were neighbouring systems that came under Macragge's influence through trade, treaties and the suppression of piracy. When Guilliman took command of the [[Ultramarines|XIII Legion]], the realm grew rapidly: the Ultramarines' expeditions brought world after world into compliance and, rather than moving on, Guilliman bound many of them into a common state, sharing laws, coinage and defence. By the end of the Great Crusade, Ultramar was a domain of some five hundred worlds, the largest single realm of the Imperium under a primarch.

Guilliman governed Ultramar as a model for the Imperium. Each world kept its own institutions but sent tithes and soldiers to the realm, and the realm's armies, the Ultramar Auxilia, fought beside the Legion. The realm was administered from Macragge by the primarch's officials, and some worlds were raised as recruiting grounds, shipyards or arsenals for the Legion.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The Heresy came to Ultramar in 005.M31, when the [[Word Bearers]] attacked the Ultramarines assembled at [[Calth]]. The [[Battle of Calth]] began the [[Shadow Crusade]], in which the Word Bearers and [[World Eaters]] devastated many worlds of the realm, among them Calth itself, whose star was poisoned and whose people retreated underground. The realm was then cut off by [[Warp|the warp]] storm called the Ruinstorm.

In the belief that [[Terra]] might have fallen, Guilliman made Ultramar the core of [[Imperium Secundus]], a provisional successor state, and proclaimed the primarch [[Sanguinius]] as its emperor. The state was folded back into the Imperium before the end of the Heresy, in 014.M31. The episode was afterwards counted against him by his critics, and many records of it were sealed.

The Second Founding

Main article: [[Second Founding]]

After the Heresy, the Ultramarines were the largest surviving Legion, and the realm of Ultramar the largest surviving realm under a primarch. Guilliman's [[Codex Astartes]] required the Legions to be divided into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], and he applied the rule to his own Legion first. The Ultramarines were split into the Ultramarines Chapter and more than twenty Successor Chapters, and the Five Hundred Worlds were divided among them or allowed to become autonomous, since Guilliman did not wish Space Marines to rule mortals as a warrior caste. Guilliman let hundreds of worlds declare their autonomy, until only a core of eleven systems remained under the Ultramarines.

From M31 to M41

For ten thousand years after Guilliman was struck down, Ultramar was governed by the [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]] of the Ultramarines and remained one of the most stable regions of the Imperium. In 745.M41 it was attacked by Hive Fleet Behemoth, which consumed [[Prandium]] and was destroyed at the [[Battle of Macragge]]. A second hive fleet, Kraken, later destroyed the world of Sotha on the realm's eastern edge.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Guilliman was restored to life on Macragge in 999.M41 and left for Terra. Soon afterwards the [[Great Rift]] and the [[Noctis Aeterna]] cut Ultramar off. The forces of [[Nurgle]] under the daemon primarch [[Mortarion]] launched the [[Plague Wars]] against it, attacking [[Iax]], Parmenio, [[Espandor]] and other worlds. Ultramarines and Successor Chapters fought to hold them.

After more than a decade, when the first phase of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] ended at the Battle of Raukos in c. 012.M42, Guilliman returned to save his realm with thousands of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. His campaign, called the Spear of Espandor, recovered most of the ground lost, and the war ended in a final confrontation on Iax, where the [[Death Guard]] escaped under cover of a [[Exterminatus|virus bomb]]. Mortarion withdrew, and Guilliman began the restoration of Ultramar.

The restoration of Ultramar has gone hand in hand with the reforms of the [[Imperium Sanctus]]. Guilliman admitted that he had been wrong to let the realm shrink, re-established the Tetrarch Council and ordered the Ultramarines and nine Shield Chapters to expand Ultramar back to its old borders. Eight Primaris Chapters raised from Ultramarines [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] in the [[Ultima Founding]] were stationed in the region at the end of the Plague Wars, each with a new homeworld.

Government

Ultramar is governed by the Lord of Ultramar, a title held by [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] and, in his absence, by the [[Chapter Master]] of the Ultramarines as his regent. Beneath the lord are four Tetrarchs, each ruling a region of the realm (north, south, west and east), while the central region is ruled directly from Macragge, and the civil governments of the individual worlds. Each world keeps its own institutions, many of them modelled on the consular government of ancient [[Macragge]]. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] are both the realm's protectors and its ruling order, and their captains and veterans often hold civil offices on its worlds.

Society

The people of Ultramar are educated, prosperous and civic-minded by the standards of the Imperium. Each citizen is trained to bear arms and serves in the defence forces, and the realm can raise very large armies in a short time. Schools, libraries and public buildings are maintained on every world, and Ultramar's public life gives a place to debate, law and the arts. Pilgrims came to [[Macragge]] in large numbers to see [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s body before his revival, and many still visit the realm's shrines.

Ultramarian architecture is classical, with columns, domes and marble halls, and the Ultramarines' iconography of the omega and the colours blue and gold is used across the realm. The realm's culture places the primarch at its centre, and the Ultramarines' own teachings of order, duty and practical wisdom are widely studied among its people.
