Office[[Lord Militant]]
Period777.M41 – 786.M41
Died786.M41, on a voyage from Alphos to Karlack

Tiber Achilus was a [[Lord Militant]] who gained command of the [[Achilus Crusade]] into the [[Jericho Reach]]. The [[High Lords of Terra]] ordered the crusade in 777.M41, and it was drawn from the forces of the [[Calixis Sector]] and the [[Astra Militarum]].

The crusade was made possible by the discovery of the stable [[Jericho-Maw]] [[Warp]] Gate, which links the Calixis Sector to the Reach. Achilus did not live to see its first decade out.
