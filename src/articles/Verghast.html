Verghast is a world of the Newfound Trailing Group. It is known for the regiments raised there, including the Verghastite regiments that fought in the Balhaut campaign alongside the [[Tanith First and Only]].

The enemy fleeing [[Balhaut]] seeded many worlds with garrisons and cults. Verghast was among those caught up in the campaign that followed.
