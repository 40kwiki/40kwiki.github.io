A Sentinel World, also called a Bastion World, is an [[Imperial]] planet designated as an inviolable bulwark of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]'s order in the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. Sentinel Worlds must defend themselves against the horrors that assault the [[Dark Imperium]] beyond their aegis.

Designation

The designation is new. It was created on the order of [[Roboute Guilliman]], the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], after his resurrection. It applies to worlds that lie beyond the [[Great Rift]], which cut the Imperium Nihilus off from the rest of the Emperor's realm.

Notable worlds

[[Vigilus]] is the most notable Sentinel World. It lies at the mouth of the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], the only known stable route through the [[Cicatrix Maledictum]].
