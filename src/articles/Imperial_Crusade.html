Imperial Crusade
Military campaign of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]
PurposeTo conquer or retake multiple worlds, a sector or several sectors
ScaleMobilisation of Imperial military resources across many planets
DurationOften many years

An Imperial Crusade is the term used by the [[Imperium of Mankind]] for a large-scale military campaign organised to conquer or retake multiple worlds, or an entire sector or sectors of the galaxy. Crusades are very large offensives that require a major mobilisation of Imperial military resources across many planets, and they often last for years.

The Crusade is led by a commander appointed by the [[High Lords of Terra]] or by a [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], and its forces are drawn from the [[Imperial Armed Forces]], including the [[Imperial Navy]], the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Space Marine]] Chapters. Administratum registers record each Crusade's conquests and its tithes, and these are used to plan the next campaign.

Notable Crusades

The earliest large campaigns of the Imperium were the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Macharian Crusade]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[Indomitus Crusade]] is the largest such campaign in recorded history. Imperial records also list the [[Ullanor Crusade]] and the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] as major examples of the form.
