Tyran Primus, or simply Tyran, was a former ocean world on the outer edge of the [[Milky Way]], in the [[Eastern Fringe]]. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] maintained a small [[Planetary classification|research station]] there. It was on Tyran that Humanity first made contact with the [[Tyranids]] of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]], which destroyed all life on the planet in early 745.M41.

The loss of Tyran is the event that started the [[First Tyrannic War]]. Imperial records describe the speed of the attack as unprecedented, and the world's research station was lost before any warning could be sent to the [[Sector|sectors]] beyond.
