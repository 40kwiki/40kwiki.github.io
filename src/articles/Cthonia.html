Cthonia was a mining world of the [[Segmentum Solar]], in one of the star systems nearest to [[Terra]]. Mined out before the [[Age of Strife]], it had become a world of crumbling industry, catacombs and violent gangs. It was the world on which the [[primarch]] [[Horus]] was raised, and the first on which one of the Emperor's lost sons was found, early in the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 800.M30, estimated). Its gangs gave the XVI Legion, the [[Luna Wolves]], later the [[Sons of Horus]], most of its recruits, among them [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]].

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the loyalists blockaded and invaded Cthonia from 006.M31, and fought a long war in its tunnels, the Siege of Cthonia. The planet was destroyed by the [[Dark Angels]] at the end of the Heresy in 014.M31. The Sons of Horus and the [[Black Legion]] have kept many Cthonian customs in the [[Eye of Terror]].

History

Before the Crusade

Cthonia lay close enough to Terra to be reached by ships without warp drives, and it was colonised, tunnelled and mined from the earliest days of human space travel. By the [[Dark Age of Technology]] its natural resources were exhausted, and its old mining technology was later recovered and removed by the [[Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]]. What remained was a rocky, volcanic world riddled with exhausted mine-workings and abandoned plants. Most of its people lived and fought in the tunnels below the Mechanicum-held surface, in gangs that warred over the little that was left.

Horus

The capsule carrying the infant Horus came down on Cthonia not long after the scattering of the primarchs (late M30; date unrecorded). He was taken in by the gang-lords of the hive and grew up among them, and he was found by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] at a younger age than any of his brothers. The first Imperial contact with Cthonia was made by a pioneer company of the [[Star Hunters]] under Captain Kornelius Dure, who called the world a nest of serpents best destroyed. The Emperor found his son there instead, and Horus, the first primarch to be recovered, became the Emperor's companion through the early Crusade.

When the Luna Wolves were placed under Horus, the Legion began to recruit from Cthonia's gangs. The Cthonians brought with them a gangland code of loyalty, honour and revenge, and customs such as the topknot and the oath of the blade-cut eye. Over the Crusade the Cthonian intake came to outnumber the Legion's Terran veterans. Famous Cthonians included Ezekyle Abaddon, [[Falkus Kibre]] and many of the [[Justaerin]], whose name was taken from a Cthonian gang.

Siege of Cthonia

When Horus rebelled in 005.M31, Cthonia was a Traitor recruiting world in the heart of the [[Segmentum Solar]]. In 006.M31 [[Rogal Dorn]] sent a force of [[Imperial Fists]], [[Space Wolves]] and [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodians]] under Lord Castellan Evander Garrius to take it. The Sons of Horus garrison was driven into the underworld, where it fought a guerrilla war for years. In the course of the siege parts of the population sided with the loyalists, and the Imperial Fists began to recruit from the gangs.

After the [[Battle of Beta-Garmon]], a Traitor fleet under [[Vheren Ashurhaddon]], with [[Word Bearers]], [[Alpha Legion]] and [[Dark Mechanicum]] forces, invaded in 013.M31 to relieve the world. The relief failed, and the loyalists held their ground until the end of the war. Lupercal's Gate, the largest hive, was renamed Traitor's Gate by the besiegers.

Destruction

In 014.M31, at the end of the Heresy, the Dark Angels under [[Lion El'Jonson]] destroyed Cthonia, so that it could never again serve as a source of warriors for the Traitors. The world's surviving gangs and its people died with it. Earlier Imperial records stated only that Cthonia had been abandoned and its population scattered; the newer account of the siege is now accepted.

Legacy

Cthonia remains a word of horror in the Imperium and of pride among the Traitors. The Sons of Horus, and the Black Legion after Abaddon renamed it (date disputed; c. M31–M32), have kept Cthonian words, gang oaths and the honour of the blade-cut eye. Open Imperial records note no later settlement of the system. In the [[Era Indomitus]] Abaddon, who calls himself [[Warmaster]] of the [[Imperium Nihilus]] since the [[Fall of Cadia]] (999.M41), remains the most famous Cthonian alive.

Geography

Cthonia was a rocky, volcanic world with a thin, poisoned atmosphere. At its heart lay a great volcanic cavern in which the gangs burned their dead. Notable places included:

  • Lupercal's Gate, an immense hive city, more crowded before the Age of Strife than after it; called Traitor's Gate during the siege.
  • Terminal Atlas, a space elevator of the Dark Age of Technology linking the surface to an orbital plate.
  • The Titan Yards, which despite their name mainly built [[Leman Russ battle tank|Leman Russ tanks]].
  • Tyrant's Den, the underground fortress of the Sons of Horus garrison, and the Aanlok Chasm and Serpent's Jaws, sinkholes leading into the underworld.

Society

Cthonian society was built around the gangs, of which the Justaerin, the Black Grin, the Reivers and the Iron Teeth are among those recorded. Gang members lived in the old tunnels and paid a blood price to the dark when they entered unknown passages. Cthonians were pale, often freckled and craggy-faced, and were known for loyalty to their crews and cruelty to rivals.
