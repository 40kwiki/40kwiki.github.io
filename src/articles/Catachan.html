Catachan is a [[Death world|death world]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], covered by a single tropical rainforest whose plants and animals are hostile to all outside life. It is held to be the most dangerous world inhabited by humans in the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Its only export of value is its people, who are recruited in great numbers into the [[Astra Militarum]] as the [[Catachan Jungle Fighters]], the Imperium's foremost light infantry for jungle, ambush and survival warfare.

The world was settled by accident during the [[Dark Age of Technology]], when colonists crash-landed and could not leave. In the 41st millennium it repulsed a [[Night Lords]] invasion in 972.M41, and in the first years of the [[Era Indomitus]] its own regiments defeated a daemonic incursion that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]] before the [[Indomitus Crusade]] arrived.

History

Settlement

Survey probes of the Dark Age showed Catachan from orbit as a calm green world apparently ideal for human life. The first colony ships were forced by an emergency to crash-land in the jungle, probably in the 20th or 21st millennium, and the colonists, unable to leave, held out in their wrecked ships against a jungle that seemed to besiege them.The date of settlement is not recorded and is estimated from the world's isolation through the [[Age of Strife]]. Many died before the survivors learned enough of the planet to live beneath its canopy. Over the following millennia their descendants became a people of great size, strength and endurance.

Recruitment into the Imperium

Catachan was rediscovered during the [[Great Crusade]] (c. M30). The [[Departmento Munitorum]], founded after the [[Horus Heresy]] in the reforms of c. 020.M31, recognised that men and women who could survive on Catachan would make soldiers for the Imperium's most hostile battlefields, and raised the first regiments of Jungle Fighters.The first founding of Catachan regiments is not dated in open records. It is placed after the creation of the Departmento Munitorum and before the 34th millennium, from which the first regimental honours survive. Catachan regiments have since served in nearly every major campaign of the Imperium, among them the [[Macharian Crusade]] (392–399.M41) and the [[Tyrannic Wars]].

The Defence of Catachan

In 972.M41 the Chaos Lord Ratathrax of the Night Lords invaded Catachan. The invaders found that the jungle and its people fought them together; the defenders used the planet's predators and poisons as weapons, and the Night Lords were driven off the world.

The Great Rift

Late in 999.M41, during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], Catachan lay in the path of a wounded void whale and of the [[Orks|Ork]] freebooter Kaptain Badrukk, and the warp storm Bas Infernia prevented the Imperium from warning the planet by [[Astropath|astropathic]] message. When the Great Rift opened soon after, Catachan was struck by a daemonic invasion. When the fleets of [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s Indomitus Crusade reached the system, c. 000–001.M42, they found that the Catachan regiments had already destroyed the daemons and their cultists. The fleet halted to take on new regiments and left with more troops than it had brought.

Geography

Catachan is a hot, humid world, with temperatures above 30 °C, covered from pole to pole by rainforest. Its ecosystem is uniformly predatory: carnivorous plants, poisonous fungi, swarming insects and great beasts all attack humans. The most famous of its animals is the Catachan Devil, a giant many-legged arthropod with a venomous sting, which gives its name to the 2nd Catachan regiment. Other dangers include the Venus mantrap, the barking toad and the Catachan face-eater.

The few settlements are great fortresses surrounded by cleared ground to give fields of fire. Vines and lichen take root in every structure, acidic sap dissolves mortar, and roots crush bunkers, so the buildings must be constantly repaired or rebuilt.

Society

Every Catachan must survive the jungle to reach adulthood, and the people are, as a result, unusually tall, strong and resourceful, so much so that soldiers of other regiments call them "baby Ogryns", a name used at some risk. They value self-reliance and contempt for danger, and treat rank and ceremony lightly. The Catachan knife, carried by every adult, is a symbol of their culture as much as a tool.

Almost all able adults leave the planet in the regiments, and the world's tithe is paid in soldiers. The [[Imperial Cult]] is observed plainly, and Catachan veterans are known for honouring the memory of fallen comrades over formal religious practice.
