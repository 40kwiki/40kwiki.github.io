The Astral Claws were a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created according to most records in the [[Tenth Founding]] of the mid-35th millennium, during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], from gene-seed of unknown origin. For most of their history they had an exemplary record, and in 587.M41 they were given overall command of the Maelstrom Warders, the Chapters set to guard the [[Maelstrom]], with the world of Badab Primaris as their homeworld.

Under their Chapter Master [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]], who took office in 697.M41, the Chapter grew in strength and pride until, in 901.M41, Huron declared the Badab Sector independent of the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The resulting [[Badab War]] drew in some two dozen Chapters and ended in 912.M41 with the fall of Badab Primaris. The Astral Claws were destroyed as a Chapter, and Huron fled into the Maelstrom with the survivors, who became the [[Red Corsairs]].

History

Founding and early service

The Tenth Founding was made to counter the erosion of the Imperium's borders during the Interregnum, and few records of it remain. In 723.M36 the Astral Claws helped defend [[Cadia]] in the fifth [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], and between 678 and 714.M37 they fought in the Taninim Crusade to retake the north-western fringe of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] after the Occlusiad War. In 288.M38 the Chapter was recorded as destroyed in the Altid Crusade, fighting beside the [[Dark Angels]]; it reappeared later in full strength, and Imperial scholars still dispute what happened. In the 39th millennium it broke the siege of the [[Executioners]]' base of Darkenvault at the cost of its Chapter Master, Acas Seneca, and won the Executioners' lasting loyalty.The Siege of Darkenvault is dated only to M39.

Wardens of the Maelstrom

Installed on Badab Primaris in 587.M41, the Chapter ruled the [[Badab Sector]] with skill, using the world's industry and its link to the isolated forge world of Angstrom to grow its strength. Between 640 and 651.M41 it led the Scourge Campaign, the greatest operation of its time in the Maelstrom Zone. In 680.M41 the Tiger Claws, an Astral Claws successor declared destroyed some fourteen centuries before, reappeared on the edge of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]]; Inquisitorial rumour held that they were tainted, and they vanished again. In 697.M41 Lufgt Huron became Chapter Master. In 780.M41 he led a large force to the Lycanthos Drift after the Fourth Quadrant Rebellion, to the lasting resentment of the [[Fire Hawks]]' master Stibor Lazaerek, and in 869.M41 he persuaded the [[Black Templars]] to launch a Crusade of Wrath into the Maelstrom.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

In the decades before the war the Chapter changed in secret. Huron kept the gene-seed tithe owed to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and used it to raise his Chapter's numbers far beyond the limit of the [[Codex Astartes]], and he presented himself to the [[Mantis Warriors]], the [[Lamenters]] and the Executioners as the defender of Astartes autonomy against the High Lords. In 901.M41 he proclaimed the secession of the Badab Sector and was called the "Tyrant of Badab". Loyalist Chapters under the [[Red Scorpions]] fought the Secessionists for a decade. The war ended in 912.M41 with the storming of Badab Primaris, where Huron was mortally wounded. His followers carried him into the Maelstrom, and he survived to found the Red Corsairs. The Inquisition declared the Astral Claws destroyed, and the survivors are counted among the [[Chaos Space Marines]].

Organisation

Before the war the Astral Claws followed the Codex. By its end they had grown far above the usual thousand battle-brothers and fielded large numbers of Terminators and heavy war machines from Angstrom. Their gene-seed origin was never established, and they were suspected of having been engineered from more than one line.
