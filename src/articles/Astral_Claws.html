The Astral Claws were a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created according to most records in the [[Tenth Founding]] of the mid-35th millennium, during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], from [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of unknown origin. They were raised to guard the eastern marches of the Imperium against the threats of the [[Maelstrom]], and for more than five thousand Terran years they stood beside the finest warriors of the Imperium, their honour unquestioned. In 587.M41 the [[High Lords of Terra]] gave them overall command of the Maelstrom Warders, the Chapters set to guard the Maelstrom Zone, with the world of Badab Primaris as their homeworld.

Under their Chapter Master [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]], who took office in 715.M41, the Chapter grew in power and pride, and in secret broke the limits of the [[Codex Astartes]]. In 901.M41 Huron declared the [[Badab Sector]] independent of the High Lords. The resulting [[Badab War]] drew in some two dozen Chapters and ended in 913.M41 with the fall of Badab Primaris. About two hundred Astral Claws survived the final assault. They fled with their wounded Chapter Master into the Maelstrom and became the [[Red Corsairs]]. The Astral Claws still in Imperial hands were tried by a court of their peers and put to the sword.

The name is now vilified within the Imperium. The High Lords passed an Edict of Obliteration against the Chapter and declared it [[Excommunicate Traitoris]], and its own archives were burned in the last battles at Badab Primaris, so that no records survive that give the full account of its origins.

History

Founding and early service

The Tenth Founding was made to counter the erosion of the Imperium's borders during the [[Nova Terra Interregnum|Interregnum]], and few records of it remain. The few that deal with the Astral Claws show a crusading Chapter sent from war zone to war zone. Their traces appear in the records of the Departmento Munitorum for the Perfidian Wars and the anti-Ork purges of the Lord Solar Jurastis Macro, and on battle shrines to the fighting against the forces of [[Nova Terra]] in the Cataclysm of Souls that ended the Interregnum.

In 723.M36 the Astral Claws helped to defend [[Cadia]] in the fifth [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], and their standard was taken to hang in the Hallway of Heroes before the Eternity Gate of the [[Imperial Palace]]. In the 37th and early 38th millennia they kept to the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], and are named repeatedly in the Apocrypha Kaali, kept in the Scholastica Corpus on [[Cypra Mundi]], which records the Occlusiad War and its aftermath. It speaks of them as having "three sons", which implies that three Successor Chapters had been drawn from their ranks.

In 288.M38 the Chapter is recorded as destroyed in the Altid Crusade, while fighting beside the [[Dark Angels]]. It is next reported at full strength nearly eighteen centuries later, with the fall of the Gorgon Pentarchy, and mention of a penitent crusade in the years between. In the 39th and 40th millennia they were a fleet-based Chapter in the Segmentum Solar and Segmentum Tempestus, responding to emergencies. Imperial scholars still dispute what became of the Chapter between these dates.The Altid Crusade date of 288.M38 and the reappearance with the fall of the Gorgon Pentarchy are as recorded; the interval is not explained in the sources consulted.

Notable campaigns before the war

In the Taninim Crusade of 678–714.M37, eleven Chapters were sent into the Taninim Expanse at the north-western edge of the Segmentum Obscurus after the Occlusiad War. Many never returned, and one, the Storm Callers, fell to Chaos. The Astral Claws crusaded for more than four solar decades and came back relatively unscathed. They were given a Sceptre of Victory at Cypra Mundi, and a temple was raised to them on the shrine world of Pycentos.

At a date known only as the 39th millennium the Chapter broke the siege of Darkenvault, the fortress-monastery of the [[Executioners]] on the twin worlds of Stygia and Aquilon. The Executioners had been reduced to under three hundred battle-brothers by Ork fleets and other attacks, and a new xenos species of ghostly, disc-shaped ships was destroying the fortress from within. The Astral Claws, supported by the cruisers of Battlefleet Tempestus, arrived as the last Executioners prepared to die. The Chapter Master Acas Seneca fell in the fighting. The survivors swore a blood-debt to the Astral Claws, which Huron called in two thousand years later.The Siege of Darkenvault is dated only to the 39th millennium.

Wardens of the Maelstrom

Installed on Badab Primaris in 587.M41, the Chapter ruled the [[Badab Sector]] with diligence, using the world's industry and its link to the isolated [[Forge world|forge world]] of Angstrom to increase its strength. Between 640 and 651.M41 it led the [[Scourge Stars|Scourge]] Campaign, the greatest operation of its time in the Maelstrom Zone, and the first by Imperial forces in centuries to strike into the Maelstrom itself. Dozens of enemy strongholds were destroyed before the Charnel Guard were withdrawn and the Warders lost a quarter of their strength.

In 680.M41 the strike cruiser Bakasurra of the Tiger Claws, a Chapter declared destroyed fourteen centuries earlier, reappeared on the edge of the Segmentum Pacificus after a long time distortion. Its crew found their homeworld Krodha dead beneath an expanded sun, and Captain Vetala vanished on Terra while waiting for his petition to refound the Chapter to be heard. Inquisitorial rumour held that the Tiger Claws belonged to a "Cursed Founding". Centuries later, the Astral Claws secretly absorbed what remained of them. Some scholars hold that the Chapter thereby took a poisonous viper to its heart.

In 715.M41 Huron became Chapter Master. In 780.M41 he led a force to the Lycanthos Drift, one of the last strongholds of the Fourth Quadrant Rebellion, and was elected battle leader by common consent of the Astartes contingents, to the lasting resentment of the Fire Hawks' Chapter Master Stibor Lazaerek. In 869.M41 he persuaded the [[Black Templars]] to launch a Crusade of Wrath into the Maelstrom, while the Astral Claws, [[Lamenters]] and [[Mantis Warriors]] attacked from the south and north-east. Twenty-three strongholds were purged, and the Astral Claws destroyed the flesh-haunters of the Howling Gyre with the Life-Eater virus, before the Black Templars were called away to Ultramar after the First Tyrannic War, to Huron's fury.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

In the decades before the war the Chapter changed in secret. Huron raised its numbers far above the thousand battle-brothers allowed by the Codex Astartes and used the wealth of the sector to bind the other Maelstrom Warders to him, giving them vehicles and weapons that Badab's factories made almost without cost. He presented himself to the Mantis Warriors, the Lamenters and the Executioners as the defender of Astartes autonomy against the interference of the High Lords, the economic interests of Terra and the [[Inquisition]]. He was a gifted spymaster, and he kept agents among the outlaws he had sworn to hunt.

In 901.M41 Huron proclaimed the secession of the Badab Sector and was called the Tyrant of Badab. Loyalist Chapters under the [[Red Scorpions]] fought the Secessionists for a decade. The war ended in 913.M41 with the storming of Badab Primaris. Huron was mortally wounded by a meltagun blast on his right side, and his chief Techmarine, Armenneus Valthex, is believed to have carried his body into the Maelstrom. The captured Astral Claws were tried before a Consistorial Court of the Chapters, found guilty of breaking the Codex Astartes, and blindfolded, shackled and put to the sword. The other Secessionist Chapters were later forgiven, and given penitent crusades to fight.

Huron survived. He swore himself and his remaining battle-brothers to the Ruinous Powers in return for health and power, replaced his ruined body with augmetics, and called himself Huron Blackheart. His followers renamed themselves the [[Red Corsairs]], and returned to the Maelstrom as raiders of the kind that the Chapter had once existed to hunt.

Organisation

For its first centuries the Chapter followed the Codex Astartes, with ten companies, a Chapter command, an Apothecarion and the forge armouries. It fought as a crusading, fleet-based force that favoured speed and boarding actions, and kept strong contingents of assault bikers and Dreadnoughts. Its pride sometimes made it assault superior forces, or fail to retreat when a more cautious Chapter would have withdrawn.

Huron changed this. Over the years of the secession the Chapter grew into something more like a Space Marine Legion of old, with an additional force of battle companies, heavy in firepower and boarding troops, seeded through the fleet. It also created the Tyrant's Legion, a series of mixed armies of human defence troops built around a core of Astral Claws, which numbered well over a thousand additional Marines and was the greatest offence against the Codex. Each large detachment had an Astral Claws officer called a Centurion, who held tactical command and made bloody examples of the faint-hearted. The human soldiers saw them as angels of death.

Specialist units appeared only in this Chapter. The Corpse-Takers were Apothecaries who harvested gene-seed from fallen Marines, friend and enemy alike, and their practice guaranteed the hatred of every loyal Chapter they met. The Iron Hunters were bike squads, and the Retaliators were assault troops chosen for brutality and obedience, who enforced loyalty with terror among the mortal auxiliaries. The Chapter kept a gene-seed whose origin was never established. Rumour points to the Ultramarines or the Dark Angels, and wilder tales to forbidden or xenos sources.

Beliefs

The Astral Claws were proud and imperious, fond of martial glory and the heroes of the past. They believed themselves the heirs of the Emperor's task of uniting Mankind under the protection of the Space Marines, and held that the transhuman Marines stood above those they ruled and defended. Under Huron this conviction hardened, together with a belief in their superiority over the other agencies of the Imperium and resentment at outside interference in the Chapter's holy mission. Humanity had to be preserved at all costs, in their view, but individual lives mattered little. The authority to sacrifice millions rested with the High Lords, and Huron came to resent that.

In the Deathwatch the Astral Claws showed a disdainful air towards the other Chapters. They seldom sent battle-brothers to stand the Long Vigil, perhaps because the service brought them close to senior Inquisitors, and so to questions that the Chapter had no wish to answer.

Notable members

[[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]], the Tyrant of Badab, was the brilliant and ambitious Chapter Master whose pride led to the war. Acas Seneca, his predecessor of the 39th millennium, died at Darkenvault. Captain Corien Sumatris of the 2nd Company, the Tyrant's Champion and warden of Piraeus, may have been a hidden scion of the Tiger Claws, and was believed killed defending Sentinel-Sigma before the final siege. Armenneus Valthex, called the Alchemancer, was Master of the Forge, famed for his poisons and as a siege engineer. Chief Apothecary Garreon, the Corpsemaster of the Red Corsairs, dissected living Marines. Ancient Kleitor, a Dreadnought of the Tabernacle of the Old Ones, was destroyed on Piraeus by Silas Alberec of the Exorcists, and Arch-centurion Carnac Commodus commanded the strike cruiser Hyrcania.
