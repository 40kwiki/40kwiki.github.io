The Excoriators are a [[Codex Astartes]]-compliant Loyalist [[Chapter]] of [[Space Marines]] and a [[Second Founding]] Successor of the [[Imperial Fists]]. The [[Mythos Angelica Mortis]] lists them among the twenty Chapters of the [[Astartes Praeses]], which guard the regions around the [[Eye of Terror]]. Their homeworld is [[Eschara]].

Origin

The Excoriators were created during the Second Founding from the genestock of the Imperial Fists. The Imperium recognised a group of Imperial Fists who had held the [[Imperial Palace]] during the [[Siege of Terra]]. For that stand they were granted the right to become a separate Chapter.
