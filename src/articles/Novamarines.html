The Novamarines are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and one of the [[Primogenitor]] Chapters, the successors created directly from the [[Ultramarines]] Legion in the [[Second Founding]] of 021.M31. Their homeworld is the [[Feral world|feral world]] of Honourum in the galactic north of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], far from [[Ultramar]], and their fortress-monastery is Fortress Novum. At their founding they swore to defend the Ultima Segmentum against the enemies of mankind, and they are held to have kept that oath for ten thousand years.

In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Novamarines are one of the nine Shield Chapters chosen by [[Roboute Guilliman]] to defend and extend Ultramar under the direction of the Ultramarines. Their Chapter Master Bardan Dovaro was killed in the [[Plague Wars]], at about the time the [[Indomitus Crusade]] ended in c. 012.M42.

History

Founding

See also: [[Second Founding]]

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] created the Novamarines himself during the [[Second Founding]] and appointed as their first [[Chapter Master]] Lucretius Corvo, captain of the 90th Company, "Nova" Company, of the Ninth Chapter of the [[Ultramarines|XIII Legion]]. Corvo was devoted to his [[Primarch|primarch]] and to the [[Codex Astartes]], and the Chapter has held to it strictly ever since. It is believed that Novamarines [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] was later used to found other Ultramarines successors, among them the Dark Sons and the Angels of Fury, though no official record lists them as Novamarines successors.

The Great Scouring and after

In 121.M31 the Novamarines fought beside the Ultramarines and five other successor Chapters at the Battle of Thessala against the [[Emperor's Children]], the battle in which Guilliman was struck down by [[Fulgrim]]. A stele in the [[Imperial Palace]] on Terra records their service against the Pale Wasting in M34. Between 119 and 164.M37 the Chapter waged the Lok'Kroll Xenocide, a crusade of extermination against a xenos race of that name, and in 887.M39 it cleansed the [[space hulk]] Death of Integrity when it appeared near Vol Secundus.

Wars of the 41st millennium

In the 740s.M41 the Novamarines fought in the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] and the Lithesh Sector Crusade against the expanding [[T'au Empire]]. In 812.M41 they put down the Luxor Uprising on the industrial hive world of Luxor. Delayed by other wars, a Novamarines contingent under Captain Mordacai Blaylock reached the [[Badab War]] in 906.M41, moved by the Chapter's revulsion at the arrogance of [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]]. It withdrew in 908.M41 to defend Honourum against a growing Ork [[Waaagh!]], having won much renown. In 999.M41 the Chapter sent six full companies to the defence of the [[Cadia|Cadian Sector]] in the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

During the [[Indomitus Crusade]] the Third Company, under Captain Tyvo Gallemnus, served in Battle Group Erastus of Fleet Primus in the Ispolin Sub-sector Offensive, with the strike cruiser Spear of the Void as the battle group's command ship. The Chapter received [[Primaris Space Marines]], and Brother Lyone, a Novamarines Primaris seconded to the [[Deathwatch]], became one of the first Primaris Marines to reach the rank of Watch Captain.

Named one of the Shield Chapters of Ultramar, the Novamarines contributed six companies to the defence of the realm in the [[Plague Wars]]. Chapter Master Bardan Dovaro fell defending the starfort Galatan against a boarding attack by the plague fleets of [[Nurgle]], at about the time of the Battle of Raukos that closed the Indomitus Crusade in c. 012.M42.The date follows the Lord Commander's records; the Novamarines chronicles give a later year. See [[Raukos|the Battle of Raukos]].

Organisation and beliefs

The Novamarines are a [[Space Marine Chapter|Codex Chapter]] in every respect. Officers are said to oversee the administration of the worlds under the Chapter's protection before they rise to higher rank, in imitation of the Ultramarines' rule of [[Ultramar]]. The Chapter recruits from the stone-age hunter tribes of Honourum. Like many of the Ultramarines' line, it is known for a deep hatred of [[Xenos|xenos]].
