Abel Verreault was a politician who held a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] from 544.M32. He was a successor in the office of [[Lord Commander Militant]], and his term fell in the [[War of the Beast]]. He was murdered in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32.

Verreault found that his troops had no ships, and he bought transport from [[Lansung]] with his vote. He supported the disastrous [[Proletarian Crusade]] and the Lord Commander [[Udin Macht Udo]], and he abandoned Udo when the [[Inquisition]] moved against him.

Death

Verreault was murdered in the Beheading of 546.M32 by the Callidus assassin who had replaced the [[Master of the Administratum]]. The Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]] gave the seat to [[Oskar Lowis]], who served as his instrument until Vangorich's fall, c. 626.M32.The end of Oskar Lowis's term is estimated from Vangorich's fall. No record gives an exact year.
