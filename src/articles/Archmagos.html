An archmagos (plural archmagi) is a tech-priest of the highest rank in the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] below the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]]. Archmagi rule [[Forge world|forge worlds]], command the Mechanicus's armies and fleets, lead its great research orders and sit on the councils that govern Mars. They are among the most powerful individuals in the [[Imperium of Mankind]], and among the strangest: centuries of augmentation leave many of them more machine than human.

The rank has several forms. An Archmagos Dominus commands the forces of the Mechanicus in war; an Archmagos Veneratus is an ancient tech-priest of great learning, often kept alive by elaborate machinery; and an Archmagos Explorator leads an Explorator fleet into the unknown. The best-known archmagos of the present age is [[Belisarius Cawl]], Archmagos Dominus, creator of the [[Primaris Space Marines]].

History

The rank of archmagos is older than the Imperium. The Mechanicum of Mars was ruled before the [[Great Crusade]] by its Fabricator-General and a council of archmagi, and it was this body that agreed the [[Treaty of Olympus]] with the [[Emperor of Mankind]] in 739.M30. During the crusade, archmagi led the Mechanicum's [[Mechanicum Taghmata|Taghmata]] and Explorator fleets alongside the expedition fleets of the Imperium.

In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the archmagi of Mars were divided. The Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] and many archmagi joined [[Horus]] and became the [[Dark Mechanicum]], and the [[Schism of Mars]] of 005–006.M31 set archmagos against archmagos. The Fabricator Locum [[Zagreus Kane]] led the loyal tech-priests and became the first Fabricator-General of the reformed Adeptus Mechanicus.

Since then, archmagi have led the Mechanicus in every age of the Imperium. In the [[Era Indomitus]], the Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl, who had worked in secret for ten thousand years on the Primaris project, revealed his work at the end of the 41st millennium, and his Primaris Space Marines were distributed to the Chapters in the [[Ultima Founding]] from 999.M41. Cawl also commanded Mechanicus forces in the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and in the [[Pariah Nexus]].

Forms of the rank

Archmagos Dominus

The Archmagos Dominus is a war leader. He commands the [[Skitarii]] Legions, the battle servitors and the war engines of a forge world or a Mechanicus expedition, directing them through the noosphere and the doctrina imperatives that govern the Skitarii. Lesser tech-priests who command in battle are called Tech-Priests Dominus or Magi Dominus.

Archmagos Veneratus

An Archmagos Veneratus is a tech-priest of exceptional age and learning, whose body has often failed and been replaced by machinery entirely. Some exist as little more than a brain sustained in a mechanical shell. They are revered as repositories of ancient knowledge and serve as counsellors to the Fabricator-General and the rulers of forge worlds.

Archmagos Explorator

An Archmagos Explorator leads an Explorator fleet, the arm of the Mechanicus that searches the galaxy for lost technology and, above all, for fragments of the [[Standard Template Construct]]. Explorator fleets have charted much of the [[Milky Way|galaxy]], and their records form a large part of the Imperial charts.

Archmagi of the orders

Each of the great specialist orders of the Mechanicus, such as the Genetors, the Lexmechanics and the Artisans, has archmagi at its head. The [[Legio Cybernetica]] and the [[Titan Legions]] also have their own senior magi.

Powers and duties

An archmagos may rule a forge world in his own right, under the Fabricator-General's distant authority, or sit on the council that advises the Fabricator-General on Mars. The council chooses the Fabricator-General from among the archmagi when the office falls vacant. The Fabricator-General in turn holds a seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]].

Archmagi are bound by the doctrines of the [[Omnissiah]] and by the Mechanicus's horror of innovation. Those who stray too far are condemned as [[Heretek|hereteks]], and several archmagi in Imperial history, such as those of the Dark Mechanicum, have been declared traitors.

Notable archmagi

  • [[Belisarius Cawl]], Archmagos Dominus, born in the early 31st millennium; creator of the Primaris Space Marines.
  • [[Kelbor-Hal]], archmagos and then Fabricator-General of Mars until his treachery in 005.M31.
  • [[Zagreus Kane]], Fabricator Locum, who led the loyalists of Mars in the Heresy and became Fabricator-General.
