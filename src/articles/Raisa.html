Raisa is a forested [[Knight world]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], far to the galactic south, and the homeworld of [[House Cadmus]]. Its nobles pilot [[Imperial Knight]]s under the [[Questor Imperialis]] and were once sworn to the Forge World of [[Gryphonne IV]].

House Cadmus

House Cadmus holds Raisa from the stronghold of Golem Keep, on the largest of its highland plains. Its High King is known on the world as Baron Roland of Swinford Hall. The Knights hunt the beasts and the abhuman tribes of the woodlands, and the yearly Cull, in which the nobles compete to wipe them out, has shaped Raisan society for millennia.

Knights and the Questor Imperialis

The Knights of Raisa swore to the Emperor's cause as the Questor Imperialis, and since the fall of Gryphonne IV they answer to the Imperium directly. For the wider story of the house, see [[House Cadmus]].
