Drach'nyen is a powerful [[Daemon]] that has been known by many names in the history of Mankind, among them the "Echo of the First Murder" and the "End of Empires". It takes the form of a [[daemon weapon|Daemon Sword]] of the same name, wielded by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]], the [[Warmaster of Chaos]].

Although its existence is linked to the shedding of mortal blood, Drach'nyen has no ties to the Blood God. It is powerful enough to be an independent Chaos entity.

Saint's Haven

In early M42 Abaddon arrived at Vigilus aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]]. The defence of the world was led by [[Marneus Calgar]], Chapter Master of the [[Ultramarines]]. Calgar challenged Abaddon to single combat in the palace of Saint's Haven, and the Chapter Master was cut down by Drach'nyen there. Witnesses record that Calgar had first struck the Warmaster a blow to the chest that staggered him.The date of the combat at Saint's Haven is recorded as early M42. Imperial accounts of the duel differ on its details.
