Mordax was a [[Forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Scarus Sector]]. It was captured during the [[13th Black Crusade]] by massed [[Ork]] warbands, and it is now known as Moredakka, an [[Ork world]] controlled mainly by the Deathskulls klan.

Capture

During the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41, [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] recruited massed Ork warbands as mercenary auxiliaries for his [[Traitor Legions]]. Terror raids by the [[Night Lords]] and the [[Alpha Legion]] helped the Orks to band together. Many tribes declared themselves part of the "Green Krusade", which overran the Scarus Sector.
