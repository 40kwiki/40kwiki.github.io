Anakwanar Sek was a Magister of Chaos who fought in the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] in the late 41st millennium. He was among the most capable Chaos commanders of the [[Sabbat Worlds Sector]]. Imperial accounts describe him as a planner who preferred to break his enemies' will before he broke their armies.

Sek served under Archon Nadzybar at the start of the Crusade. After Nadzybar died on [[Balhaut]] in 764.M41, Sek kept attacking the Imperial advance into the Cabal System. He grew resentful when [[Urlock Gaur]] was chosen as Archon in 771.M41. Gaur led a disciplined host of his own, and Sek had none of that strength. Sek intended to challenge Gaur for control of the Chaos forces in the sector, and he began to build a force of his own to do it.

Methods

Sek was known for his use of words as weapons. He did not rely on long bombardments. He sent broadcasts to target worlds to stir terror among their civilians. Once a population was afraid, he moved in with a small force and took the world with little resistance. Imperial records describe this as the most economical method used by any Magister of the sector.

Flank attack

In the latter months of 773.M41, Sek and the Magister [[Enok Innokenti]] attacked the Imperial flank in a double offensive into the Khan Group. Their aim was to split the Crusade force and cut off its command. By the end of 773.M41 the two Magisters had halted the Imperial thrust into the Cabal Systems and divided the Khan Group. Three worlds were badly damaged and three more were altered so badly that their climates changed.

By the turn of 775.M41, Sek's forces posed the greatest threat to the Imperial flank. The Crusade forces were pushed close to the limit of their endurance.

The Sons of Sek

To challenge Gaur, Sek raised an elite Chaos army called the Sons of Sek. He began on the [[Agri-world]] of Gereon. The force was trained under the supervision of Noches Sturm, a former Imperial Guard officer who had been disgraced and turned to Chaos. The Sons of Sek used the tactical structure of the Imperial Guard and became a disciplined and dangerous army. Some Imperial tacticians hoped that the Crusade could profit from a civil war between Gaur and Sek. The Imperial command did not want Sek to win.
