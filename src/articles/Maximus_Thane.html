Maximus Thane was a [[Space Marine]] of the VII Legion who fought in the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31) and lived for more than six centuries after it. He became [[Chapter Master]] of the Fists Exemplar, a successor Chapter of the [[Imperial Fists]], and in the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) the closest ally of [[Koorland]]. After Koorland's death he restored the Imperial Fists, led the assault that killed the Beast, and became [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. He ordered the Fourth Founding, the largest since the First, and named [[Drakan Vangorich]] Lord Protector; a century later he came back to Terra to kill him.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the last months of the [[Horus Heresy]] Thane was captain of the Imperial Fists' 22nd Company. In the Siege of Terra in 014.M31 he fought at the Helios Gate under the Blood Angels' First Captain Raldoron, and spoke of the mortal soldiers beside him as true warriors of the Emperor. He defended the Saturnine Gate with [[Rogal Dorn]] and [[Sigismund]] against [[Fulgrim]] and his guard, and after the fall of the Bhab Bastion held the Inner Palace under the Imperial Fists' Archamus. When [[Horus]] fell and the traitor host broke, Thane led several thousand surviving Imperial Fists in the counter-attack that drove them from Terra.

The War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

At the [[Second Founding]] (c. 021.M31) Thane joined the Fists Exemplar. At the opening of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 he was its Second Captain, and he became its master when Chapter Master Alameda and the First Captain were killed. When Koorland, the last Imperial Fist, called the successors of Dorn to [[Battle of Phall|Phall]] under the Last Wall Protocol, Thane was the first Chapter Master to accept Koorland's command. He led a Terminator assault on the Ork attack moon over Terra and openly defied the High Lords afterwards, calling the [[Imperial Cult]] a fallacy. He fought Mechanicus troops at Pavonis Mons on [[Mars]], went with Koorland to Caldera to find [[Vulkan]], and commanded the fleet in the first assault on [[Ullanor]]. At Ullanor he and his squad helped Koorland lift Vulkan's hammer Doomtremor, the test Vulkan had set for the office of Lord Commander.

He fought beside Koorland as the Fists Exemplar fell to fewer than seventy warriors. In the second attack on Ullanor Koorland was killed, and Thane felt he should have died in his place.

Lord Commander

Main article: [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]

After Koorland's funeral on [[Inwit]], Thane resolved to act without the High Lords. He restored the Imperial Fists by drawing warriors from every Chapter of the Last Wall, a thousand of whom paraded through the [[Imperial Palace]] in the colours of the VII Legion, and became their Chapter Master. With Vangorich, the Knight-Abyssal Kavalanera Brassanas of the [[Sisters of Silence]] and the [[Fabricator-General]] Kubik, he mounted the third assault on Ullanor in 546.M32. The Sisters, using a captured Ork psyker, killed the Beast of Beasts moments before it could kill Thane.

The council named him Lord Commander, on the nomination of Wienand and Vangorich. He ordered that every gene-seed in the vaults of Terra be used in a Fourth Founding, declared that the Imperial Fists would no longer garrison Terra but would crusade without end, named Vangorich Lord Protector, and left aboard the [[Phalanx]].

The Beheading

Main article: [[Beheading]]

Soon after, Vangorich murdered the High Lords in the [[Beheading]]. Thane, far away, did not move against him, judging that a ruthless ruler might spare the Imperium the indecision that had nearly lost the war. When reports of the Lord Protector's madness reached him, some eighty years later, Thane returned c. 646.M32 with four hundred Imperial Fists, Halo Brethren and Sable Swords. In the Eversor Temple at Terra's north pole his force was attacked by a hundred Eversors; only Thane, Vangorich and one assassin survived. The assassin, Esad Wire, refused to kill Thane, and Thane shot Vangorich.

Later life

Thane remained Chapter Master of the Imperial Fists after the Beheading and put down many of the revolts that followed the Lord Protector's death. He rebuked the former Black Templar Helbrant Alderic for bringing his old Chapter's ways into the Imperial Fists' 8th Company. His name is carved on the first bone of Dorn's hand in the Chapter's reliquary. The date and manner of his death are not recorded.
