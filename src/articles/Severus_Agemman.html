Severus Agemman is the First Captain of the [[Ultramarines]], commander of the Chapter's elite [[Ultramarines 1st Company|1st Company]] of [[Veteran Marine|Veterans]]. As Master of the Keep he governs [[Macragge]] and acts as Regent of [[Ultramar]] whenever the [[Chapter Master]] is away from the Chapter's homeworld. Around 012.M42 the resurrected [[Roboute Guilliman]] named him one of the restored [[Tetrarchy of Ultramar|Tetrarchs]] of Ultramar, with the lordship of [[Konor]].

He has held command of the 1st Company since the end of the [[First Tyrannic War]], and fought through both the [[Invasion of Ultramar]] and the [[Ultramar Campaign]] in 999.M41. He is known as a stoic, cautious officer who values prudence over boldness.

Career

Rise to First Captain

Imperial records say little of Agemman's youth or his induction into the Chapter. He first appears as captain of the [[Ultramarines 2nd Company|2nd Company]]. When [[Saul Invictus]], captain of the 1st Company, was killed in the defence of Silo 8 of the northern polar fortress during the [[Battle of Macragge]] (745.M41), Agemman was promoted to lead the Veterans, and [[Cato Sicarius]] took the 2nd Company in his place.The date of the Battle of Macragge is that of Hive Fleet Behemoth's assault on Macragge. Agemman's promotion is placed at the end of that war in the sources; the exact year is estimated.

As First Captain he was the formal heir to the Chapter Master, [[Marneus Calgar]]. Many in the Chapter believed that Calgar favoured Sicarius, and a rivalry between the two captains was widely spoken of on Macragge. The assignment of Sicarius to the primarch's [[Victrix Guard]] on [[Terra]] later put the question to rest.

Invasion of Ultramar

In 854.999.M41 the [[Bloodborn]] under the [[Daemon Prince]] [[M'kar]] invaded Ultramar. Agemman led the 1st Company against the host and was struck down by M'kar in single combat, though he recovered. [[Varro Tigurius]], the [[Chief Librarian]], afterwards told Calgar privately that he doubted Agemman had fully healed from the duel. Calgar took no action and allowed his First Captain more time.

Ultramar Campaign

Soon after the Bloodborn were beaten, a force of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]'s [[13th Black Crusade]] attacked Ultramar. The survivors of [[Cadia]] arrived on Macragge with [[Saint Celestine]], [[Katarinya Greyfax]], [[Marius Amalrich]], the [[Ynnari]] and the [[Archmagos Dominus]] [[Belisarius Cawl]]. Agemman argued before Calgar's war council that the pilgrims should be confined, their sealed relic locked in a stasis vault and the xenos destroyed. Calgar overruled him on the counsel of Tigurius, and Agemman accepted the verdict without protest.

The device Cawl carried healed Roboute Guilliman, who rose from stasis in the Temple of Correction. Agemman stood with his battle-brothers as the primarch retook his throne. Over the following seven months he led strikes with Celestine and Greyfax to seize the orbital batteries of Magna Civitas and drive the Chaos fleet from the skies. When Guilliman left for Terra on the [[Terran Crusade]], Agemman remained with Calgar and Tigurius to finish the reconquest of Ultramar.

Tetrarch of Konor

After the [[Plague Wars]] in Ultramar, Guilliman restored the ancient rule of the [[Tetrarchy of Ultramar|Tetrarchs]] and made Agemman Tetrarch of Konor. His task is to extend the borders of the realm toward the northern galaxy.The date c. 012.M42 follows the Ultramarines supplement. It is an approximation.

Wargear

Agemman fights in a suit of [[Terminator armour]] of the Indomitus Pattern, armed with a [[Power sword]] and a [[Storm bolter]]. His personal heraldry is carried on his armour as First Captain.
