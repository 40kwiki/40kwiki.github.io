Vostroya is an Imperial world, home of the [[Vostroyan Firstborn]]. Its regiments are armed with heirloom weapons made by the world's gunsmiths, and they are raised only from first sons. The Vostroyan Firstborn serve in the [[Imperial Guard]] alongside the regiments of other worlds, including the [[Cadian Shock Troops]] and the [[Catachan Jungle Fighters]].

In the late 41st millennium the world's caverns were the site of a battle with the [[Black Legion]]. [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] led the Black Legion into the caverns, and [[Trazyn the Infinite]] opened a Tesseract Labyrinth there, releasing part of his collection of Imperial figures from the past.

The caverns

When Abaddon led the Black Legion into the caverns, Trazyn released a detachment of [[Ultramarines]] from the [[Horus Heresy]], Vostroyan Firstborn of the XXI Regiment, and snipers of [[Tanith]]. These were taken from his collection, and they fought on both sides of the battle as the Necron wished.The date of the battle is given only as the late 41st millennium. The sources describe the Tesseract Labyrinth but do not record its exact date.
