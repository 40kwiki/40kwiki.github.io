Vostroya is an Imperial world, home of the [[Vostroyan Firstborn]]. Its regiments are armed with heirloom weapons made by the world's gunsmiths, and they are raised only from first sons. The Vostroyan [[Primaris Space Marines|Firstborn]] serve in the [[Imperial Guard]] alongside the regiments of other worlds, including the [[Cadian Shock Troops]] and the [[Catachan Jungle Fighters]].

In the late [[41st millennium]] the world's caverns were the site of a battle with the [[Black Legion]]. [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] led the Black Legion into the caverns, and [[Trazyn the Infinite]] opened a Tesseract Labyrinth there, releasing part of his collection of Imperial figures from the past.

The caverns

When [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] led the [[Black Legion]] into the caverns, [[Trazyn the Infinite|Trazyn]] released a detachment of [[Ultramarines]] from the [[Horus Heresy]], Vostroyan [[Primaris Space Marines|Firstborn]] of the XXI Regiment, and snipers of [[Tanith]]. These were taken from his collection, and they fought on both sides of the battle as the [[Necrons|Necron]] wished.The date of the battle is given only as the late 41st millennium. The sources describe the Tesseract Labyrinth but do not record its exact date.
