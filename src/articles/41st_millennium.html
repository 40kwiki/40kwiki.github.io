The 41st millennium, abbreviated M41, is the thousand-year period of the [[Imperial Dating System]] running from 001.M41 to 999.M41. For most of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]'s history it was the "present age", and many Imperial records written before the opening of the [[Great Rift]] describe the whole Imperium simply as it stood in the 41st millennium.

The millennium opened in the long decline that Imperial historians call [[The Waning]] and closed in the [[Time of Ending]], a name taken from the prophecy of [[Taggarath]], the Seer of Corrinto, in 744.M41. Its first centuries saw the [[Gothic War]] (139–160.M41) and the conquests of [[Macharius|Lord Solar Macharius]] (392–399.M41). Its last three centuries brought the first contact with the [[T'au Empire]], the arrival of the [[Tyranids]] in 745.M41, the reawakening of the [[Necrons]], the [[Badab War]] and the Ork wars for [[Wars for Armageddon|Armageddon]].

It ended in catastrophe. The [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] destroyed [[Cadia]] in 999.M41, and the [[Great Rift]] tore [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] in two. In the same year the [[primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] was restored to life, returned to [[Terra]] and began the [[Indomitus Crusade]], events that Imperial records treat as the threshold of the [[Era Indomitus]] and the [[42nd millennium]].

Overview

The Imperium at the start of the millennium

By 001.M41 the Imperium had stood for some ten thousand years since the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31). It had survived the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] (early M35 to 975.M35) and the [[Age of Apostasy]] (early M36 to c. 310.M36), and its institutions had hardened into the forms recorded in the [[Codex Imperialis]]: a bureaucracy of the [[Adeptus Terra]] governed by the [[High Lords of Terra]], the state religion of the [[Imperial Cult]], about a thousand [[Space Marine Chapter]]s and the vast [[Astra Militarum]]. [[Warp storm|Warp storms]] and losses during [[The Waning]] (M38–M41) had left many regions isolated and much of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] and the [[Ultima Segmentum]] thinly held.

The early and middle centuries

The first great conflict of the millennium was the [[Gothic War]] (139–160.M41), [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s Twelfth [[Black Crusade]], fought for the Gothic Sector of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] around the ancient [[Blackstone Fortress]]es. In 392.M41 [[Lord Solar]] [[Macharius]] began the [[Macharian Crusade]], which conquered about a thousand worlds in seven years; after his death in 400.M41 his generals fought each other in the Macharian Heresy until c. 470.M41. In 444.M41 the [[World Eaters|daemon primarch]] [[Angron]] invaded [[Armageddon]] in the First War for Armageddon.

The Time of Ending

Further information: [[Time of Ending]]

Imperial chronology marks a turn in 744.M41, when the seer Taggarath of Corrinto prophesied the Time of Ending. Over the next two and a half centuries the Imperium met three new kinds of enemy. The [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] (742–745.M41) made first open contact with the [[T'au Empire]] on the [[Eastern Fringe]]. In 745.M41 [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] reached [[Ultramar]] and was destroyed at the [[Battle of Macragge]], beginning the [[Tyranids|Tyrannic Wars]]. [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb worlds began to wake across the galaxy.

The same centuries saw the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] (from 755.M41), the [[Siege of Vraks]] (813–830.M41), the [[Badab War]] (901–913.M41), the Second War for Armageddon (941–943.M41) and the Third (from 998.M41), and the arrival of [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] in the 990s.

The end of the millennium

The last years of the millennium were marked by spreading warp storms, rising numbers of psychic births and a series of disasters that later historians grouped together as the [[Gathering Storm]]. In 999.M41 Abaddon launched the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]], broke the [[Cadian Gate]] and brought about the [[Fall of Cadia]]. The [[Craftworld]] [[Aeldari]] and the [[Ynnari]] engineered the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]], who crossed to [[Terra]] in the [[Terran Crusade]] and resumed the office of [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]]. The [[Great Rift]] opened across the galaxy and the [[Noctis Aeterna]] cut most of the Imperium off from the [[Astronomican]].

Historiography

The 41st millennium is the best-documented period of Imperial history after the Great Crusade, because so many of the archives that survive were written in it. It is also one of the most disputed. Many of its dates are check 7 or 8 records, compiled long after the events, and the [[Chronostrife]] has shown that different Imperial calendars do not agree on when the millennium ended. The [[Logos Historica Verita]] has noted that the final decades of M41 and the first years of M42 overlap in the records of different regions, because time passed at different rates across the galaxy after the [[Great Rift]] opened.For this reason some events dated "999.M41" in Terran records are dated to the first years of M42 elsewhere, and vice versa.
