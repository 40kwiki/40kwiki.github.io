Lansung was the [[Lord High Admiral]] of the [[High Lords of Terra]] during the [[War of the Beast]] in 544–546.M32. He was the most powerful member of the council in the early part of the war, and he was an ally of the [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Erekart Mesring]].

Lansung's power rested on the Imperial Navy and on the shipyards held by the merchant family of Mesring. Together they controlled the movement of fleets during the war. His role in the conflict is recorded mainly through the actions of Mesring and the other High Lords.

War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

At the outset of the war in 544.M32, the council sat under [[Udin Macht Udo]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]. Lansung backed the [[Ecclesiarch]] in proclaiming a [[War of Faith]] against the Beast. The war ended with the deposition of several [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] and the death of Mesring, and Lansung's later fate is not recorded in full.Lansung's death is not recorded, and his later fate is estimated from the council's records of 546.M32.
