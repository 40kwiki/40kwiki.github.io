Daemon Weapon
Artefact of [[Chaos]]
NatureA weapon bound to the essence of a [[Chaos Daemon]]
Given byThe [[Ruinous Powers]] to favoured mortal servants
Typical formClose-quarters weapon; ranged examples are known
EffectGreat power and daemonic senses for the bearer, at the cost of constant slaughter

A Daemon Weapon is a powerful artefact of [[Chaos]], a blasphemous union of the matter of the [[Materium]] with the spirit of the [[Immaterium]]. It is usually a gift of the [[Ruinous Powers]] to their favoured mortal servants. It is a weapon in which the essence of a [[Chaos Daemon]] has been bound. Most are close-quarters weapons, although ranged examples have been recorded.

How the Daemon is bound

Often the Daemon is bound into the weapon as punishment by its patron [[Chaos God]]. Sometimes an exceptional mortal servant of Chaos persuades a Daemon to help in its slaughter, and the Daemon binds itself to the bearer of its own will.

Powers and costs

A Daemon Weapon is a sentient item that grants its bearer great power. The weapon is usually destructive in its own right, and nearly all Daemon Weapons give the bearer daemonic senses, which sharpen perception of the surroundings. A bound Daemon that has not been driven mad by its imprisonment can counsel its bearer, or manipulate them. Bound [[Tzeentchian]] Daemons are especially skilled at this.

Bearing such a weapon is a mark of the favour of the Dark Gods, and it draws eager followers to the bearer. The price is high. The bound Daemon cares nothing for mortal frailty and will rebel if its bearer does not keep it fed with slaughter or serve its own aims. If the bearer cannot control the weapon, the weapon will withdraw its support at a critical moment, or will pass to a better bearer, and the old bearer will be killed.

Types

Every Daemon is a unique individual, so every Daemon Weapon is unique. Weapons bound to similar kinds of Daemon share similar properties, and their forms vary greatly. The power granted to the bearer matches the strength of the Daemon within. A blade holding a [[Lesser Daemon]] is a child's toy beside one holding a [[Greater Daemon]], but it is far easier to placate. Even the weakest bound entity makes a blasphemously powerful weapon, able to channel raw energy of the [[Immaterium]] and sunder whatever it strikes.

The [[Ordo Malleus]] has catalogued many kinds in order to oppose them, grouped by the patron of the bound Daemon: [[Chaos Undivided]], [[Khorne]], [[Nurgle]], [[Slaanesh]] and [[Tzeentch]].

Among the weapons bound to Daemons of Chaos Undivided is the Accursed Crozius, the mark of office of a [[Word Bearers]] [[Dark Apostle]]. The [[Chaplain]]s of the Legion ritually desecrated their once sacred weapons when they embraced Chaos before the [[Horus Heresy]]. Another is the Black Mace, said to have been cursed by each of the [[Daemon Primarch]]s: a foe it strikes collapses into a heap of mouldering bone.
