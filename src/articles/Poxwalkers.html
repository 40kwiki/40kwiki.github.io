Poxwalkers
Chaos-infected undead
Patron[[Nurgle]]
Used byThe [[Death Guard]] and other mortal servants of the Plague Lord
AfflictionThe Walking Pox

Poxwalkers are mutant undead infected with a [[Warp]]-born malady of [[Nurgle]], the so-called Walking Pox. They are cursed so that they can serve as unliving weapons for the [[Death Guard]] and the other mortal followers of the Plague Lord. They are similar to [[Plague Zombies]].

Nature

Some [[Inquisitor]]s of the [[Ordo Sepulturum]] claim that the Walking Pox is a form of [[Nurgle's Rot]]. Unlike zombies, a poxwalker's flesh often mutates after death, growing horns, tentacles and spikes. The change is like that seen in the victims who become [[Plaguebearer]]s.

After the Great Rift

Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] during the [[13th Black Crusade]], the energies of Chaos have mutated many strains of the original plague. Strains that once affected the dead now spread in new forms.
