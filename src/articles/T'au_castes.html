The T'au castes are the five divisions of T'au society, which together make up the working body of the [[T'au Empire]]. Four are the descendants of the tribes that once fought one another, the Fire, Earth, Water and Air castes, and the fifth is the ruling caste of [[Ethereal]]s. The castes are held in balance by the doctrine of the Greater Good (Tau'va), which states that the individual finds meaning in service to the whole.

Imperial scholars regard the caste system as the reason the T'au Empire has grown so quickly. A T'au does not choose a vocation and does not compete for one. He is born into a caste, is taught its skills and is expected to be content. The Ordo Xenos records that the empire has no analogue of the heresies and rebellions that trouble the Imperium, and that its soldiers are not offended by the sight of aliens who fight beside them.

The Greater Good

The Greater Good is the empire's philosophy, a creed with some of the features of a religion but without gods. It was taught first, by the T'au account, by the Ethereals of Fio'taun in the late 37th millennium, when the castes made peace. Its main teachings are that all castes are necessary to one another, that conflict is a waste, that the empire must grow so that its benefits can be shared, and that those outside it should be invited into it before they are conquered. It is offered to alien species under the terms of the Greater Good, and the aliens who accept it, among them the [[Kroot]], the Vespid and the human [[Gue'vesa]], are treated as members of the empire though not of its castes.

The Imperium regards the Greater Good as heresy, and the Ecclesiarchy preaches against it. The Ordo Xenos regards it as the empire's most effective weapon, for it can persuade an Imperial citizen who is dissatisfied with the Imperium that a better life is possible.

The castes

Fire caste

The Fire caste (Shas) is the warrior caste. It is organised into cadres, in which Fire Warriors serve under a Shas'ui and a Shas'vre, and above these are the commanders, the Shas'O, who are chosen from the best. The Fire caste is bound by the Code of Fire, which sets out how a Fire Warrior is to fight and what he is to protect. Its soldiers carry pulse rifles, and its best are given battle-suits. Famous commanders include [[Commander Shadowsun]], who commands the Third Sphere, and [[Commander Farsight]], who left the empire and founded the [[Farsight Enclaves]]. The Fire caste's weakness, in the view of its own Ethereals, is a tendency to zeal.

Earth caste

The Earth caste (Fio) are the empire's engineers, craftsmen and builders. They design and build its weapons, vehicles and battle-suits, and the drones that fight with the cadres. Their guild-halls are among the empire's most important institutions. The Earth caste is the least interested in rank. Imperial scholars note that it is also the caste that has lost the fewest members in war.

Water caste

The Water caste (Por) are the diplomats and traders. Their emissaries are the first T'au to meet a new species, and the offer of trade and of membership in the empire is made by them. They are skilled in the language and customs of other peoples. When the Water caste fails, the Fire caste follows, and the Damocles Gulf Crusade (742–745.M41) began after Water caste missions had opened trade with frontier worlds of the Imperium. Water caste missions among the Imperium's worlds are a main source of the Gue'vesa.

Air caste

The Air caste (Kor) pilot the empire's ships and fly its aircraft, and carry its messages. Their light bodies suit them to the work, and they are the empire's link with its widely scattered colonies. They have the most contact with other species outside the empire after the Water caste, and are known to be fond of freedom of movement. The Air caste's fleets are small by Imperial standards and are well armed.

Ethereals

Main article: [[Ethereal]]

The [[Ethereal]]s are the fifth caste and rule the others. They are fewer in number than the rest and are headed by the Ethereal Supreme, [[Aun'Va]] until his death in 999.M41.

Outside the castes

Members of other species, among them the [[Kroot]] and the Imperial humans called [[Gue'vesa]], live in the empire but have no caste. They are not subject to the Code of Fire. Some serve the empire as auxiliaries and others as merchants. The empire has also taken in the Vespid of Vespid Prime and the Nicassar, whose psychic abilities it values.
