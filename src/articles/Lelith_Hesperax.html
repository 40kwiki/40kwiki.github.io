Lelith Hesperax is a [[Drukhari]] of [[Commorragh]], the Dark City of the Aeldari. She is one of the Drukhari figures named in Imperial records of the [[Era Indomitus]], and she is recorded in the Ynnari war as a rival of [[Yvraine]].

Hesperax is noted in the records for her skill in the arena and her ties to the cults of [[Slaanesh]].

The Crucibael

In the arena of the Crucibael, Yvraine killed a captive [[Tyranid]] Hive Tyrant before a crowd that included Hesperax. Hesperax mortally wounded Yvraine in the fight. Yvraine was then dying in a last duel with a priestess of Morai-Heg when the mote of the [[Ynnari]] reached her. The mote struck her at the threshold of death.

Council on Iathglas

Hesperax was later among the Aeldari who fought together against [[Shalaxi Helbane]] at the council on the maiden world of [[Iathglas]]. The Aeldari were forced to fight together when Helbane's daemons attacked.
