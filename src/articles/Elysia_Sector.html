The Elysia Sector is the Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Solar]] that takes its name from the verdant world of [[Elysia]]. Elysia is a [[Civilised World]] some 30 light years from [[Armageddon]], and it is best known as the homeworld of the [[Elysian Drop Troops]], the elite regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The sector lies on a major [[Warp]] trade route, and its pirate-infested star systems have given the Elysian forces much of their experience in ship-to-ship combat.

Imperial records give the sector's boundaries only in outline. The Administratum's charts name Elysia as its principal world, and most of the sector's surviving records concern the Elysia System. The sector is not well documented in the Imperial archives, and its neighbouring worlds are listed only as "unknown" in the registers.The sector's formal designation and its neighbouring sectors are not fixed in the sources consulted. The name is taken from the world, and the page is limited to what the records for Elysia itself show.

History

Early history

[[Elysia]] was settled as an Imperial world at an uncertain date, and its population was tithed into the [[Astra Militarum]] through the [[Imperial Tithe]]. The Elysia System was known for its asteroid fields and dense gas clouds. These gave cover to human and [[Aeldari]] pirates, who waited there to raid passing Imperial commerce.

Pirate wars

The pirate threat gave the Elysian [[Planetary Defence Force]] its character. Its troops gained intensive experience in ship-to-ship combat and in assaults on isolated, space-based pirate outposts, working alongside the Navy's naval support. Those campaigns are the origin of the Elysian ability to fight in space and from orbit.

Formation of the Elysian Drop Troops

The Elysian Drop Troop regiments were raised from the Elysian populace for service in the Astra Militarum. Every recruit must first serve a tour of combat duty in the Elysian PDF before joining. The regiments then train at bases across Elysia and the Elysia System, learning [[grav-chute]] drops, high-orbit insertions, sharpshooting and survival in extreme environments. No trooper may enter combat before he has been rated an expert marksman, because resupply is often weeks away.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Sometime after the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Elysia was invaded by a large force of [[Heretic Astartes]]. To keep the invaders from threatening [[Terra]], the [[Adeptus Custodes]] [[Captain-General]] [[Trajann Valoris]] led the Fury of Terra [[Shield Host]] of Custodians against the traitors. The date of the invasion is not given in the sources, and it is estimated to fall in the early 42nd millennium.The exact year of the invasion is not recorded. The estimate is taken from the sequence of Trajann Valoris's campaigns after the Great Rift.

Geography

[[Elysia]] is a temperate, verdant world with a gravity of one standard gee. The Elysia System contains several asteroid fields, dense gas clouds and the training grounds of the drop regiments. Its climate and terrain are chosen for the sharpshooting and survival training that its troops receive.

Notable worlds

  • '''[[Elysia]]''': the homeworld and sector capital; the source of the [[Elysian Drop Troops]] and the Elysian PDF.
  • '''Elysia System''': the surrounding star-system, with its asteroid fields and pirate-haunted lanes.

Governance

[[Elysia]] is governed by its planetary authorities under the [[Administratum]], and its defence is in the hands of the Elysian PDF. The [[Astra Militarum]]'s Elysian regiments answer to the Departmento Munitorum, which draws their recruits from the world. In time of war, the Imperial command may place Elysian forces under a [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]].
