Goge Vandire was the 361st Master of the [[Administratum]] and, for a part of the early [[36th millennium]], the most powerful man in the Imperium. By killing the Ecclesiarch and taking his office, he united the two greatest civil bureaucracies of the Imperium in his own person and ruled by terror for about seventy standard years. His rule is called the Reign of Blood, and it began the [[Age of Apostasy]].

He was the most reviled man in the history of the Imperium after the [[Traitor|traitor]] [[Primarch|primarchs]]. He is called the Renegade High Lord and the Apostate. He ordered the deaths of thousands of the Imperium's best servants, the burning of whole populations by the [[Frateris Templar]] and the building of monuments to himself that the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] was later forced to tear down. He died, alone and unrepentant, at the hands of his own bodyguard. In the Administratum's practice a "Vandire" is any official who confuses his own will with the Emperor's.

Rise

The Master of the Administratum

Main article: [[Master of the Administratum]]

Nothing of Vandire's family or birth is recorded. The archives of the [[Administratum]], which are meticulous in other matters, have nothing before his first appointments, and it has been argued that they were edited after his fall. He rose in the Administratum through the means which that organisation rewards: patronage, blackmail and, when needed, murder. By the early [[36th millennium]] he was Master of the Administratum, and as such one of the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], with a deep and well-founded resentment of the [[Imperial Cult|Adeptus Ministorum]], which had for centuries preyed on the Administratum's revenues.

The Administratum's quarrel with the church was by then old. Vandire had one advantage over his predecessors: he had contacts in the [[Officio Assassinorum]], chief among them the traitorous Assassin Tziz Jarek. With such tools he made himself a High Lord others feared. He used them first on the Ecclesiarchy.

Paulis III

In the early years of his mastership an election for [[Ecclesiarch]] was due. Vandire contrived the choice of Paulis III, a man whom the Ministorum itself later thought the weakest and most repugnant ever to hold the office. Within a short time rumours of Paulis's degeneracies circulated among the upper ranks of the Imperium, and confidence in the Ecclesiarch collapsed. Vandire had expected this. He had set up the puppet to fail.

He moved at the moment of greatest weakness. Leading his most loyal retainers, he forced entry into the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] and shot down those who tried to stop him. Paulis, it is said, was found at some vile pleasure that would have shamed the Imperium had it been known. He was killed without trial. Vandire then declared himself both Ecclesiarch and Master of the Administratum, and for the next days he carried out a purge of the Holy Synod. Cardinals who did not flee, and those who protested, were killed. Their replacements were chosen for weakness or for loyalty to him.

The Reign of Blood

Main article: [[Reign of Blood]]

Government

Whether Vandire was mad before he took power is a question that the [[Inquisition]] has never settled. Once he had it he plainly was. He said that he spoke with the authority of the Emperor and held communion with Him in trances, from which he returned in rage and demanded that a staff of scribes write down all he said. His decrees were often illogical and often savage. They went out as the word of the [[Ecclesiarch]] to every world, and few officials dared to question them.

The wealth of the Ministorum went into pogroms of imagined [[Heretic|heretics]] and into memorials. Distant worlds were given spires and cathedrals of a kilometre's height, dedicated to the Emperor and, in practice, to Vandire. The [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] on [[Terra]], by contrast, fell into decay, its great wings empty and silent. The High Lord did not like to be seen. Those who came to his audience chamber came at their peril, and for the last decades of his rule only his bodyguard dared enter.

The instruments of power

Vandire's chief means of control were three. The first was the [[Frateris Templar]], the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]'s armies, which carried out his purges on the planets. The second was the [[Officio Assassinorum]], whose Callidus and Culexus temples he used on his enemies in the Imperium. When the [[Master of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica]], Lord Phaedrus, proved immune to his authority, Vandire had a Culexus Assassin cut Phaedrus's connection with the Warp, which should have killed him, and then kept him alive with a bribed Magos Biologis on a life-support machine. He promised not to tell Phaedrus's subordinates of his condition, and in return the broken man did what he was told.

The third was the Brides of the Emperor. Vandire learned of a convent of warrior-women on the [[Agri-world|agri-world]] of [[Daughters of the Emperor|San Leor]], the Daughters of the Emperor, who trained in arms as an expression of devotion. He went in person with a large ecclesiarchal retinue, and was refused entry because the sisters did not believe that he served the Emperor's will. He asked their leader, [[Alicia Dominica]], to shoot him. Her shot was turned aside by a conversion field generator that he wore under his robes, and he persuaded the sisters that the Emperor had protected him. They took him for a saint, and followed him to Terra as his bodyguard, which he renamed the Brides of the Emperor. They tasted his food, nursed him in sickness, sang to him and killed on his word. When the Holy Synod tried to have him assassinated a few years later, they locked its members in their meeting room and returned an hour later with all the heads.

The rebellion

By the end of his reign Vandire had cut Terra off from the greater part of the Imperium. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] and the [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]], which owed him nothing and had been left to their own devices, reported to no one. The Ecclesiarchy's tithes and the Frateris Templar maintained the High Lord's authority over the worlds nearest Terra, and the rest was anarchy.

Vandire's response to the news of [[Sebastian Thor]] on [[Dimmamar]] was to send the Frateris Templar against him. The fleet carrying them was destroyed by a [[Warp storm]] in the Clax system. When the [[Fabricator-General|Fabricator-General of Mars]], [[Gastaph Hediatrix]], demanded that the Holy Synod put Vandire on trial, he dissolved the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] and declared the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the Mechanicus [[Traitor|traitors]]. His commanders refused to attack them. The [[Terran Crusade]], led by four Space Marine Chapters and a Mechanicus [[Skitarii|Tech-Guard]] force, besieged the Ecclesiarchal Palace.

Character

The Annals describe Vandire as a small man, quick in his movements, with a pleasing voice that he used to great effect in the early years of his rule, and a habit of listening to a subordinate's plea with great attention before ordering him killed. Those who knew him say that he was never cruel without a reason. He believed that he was surrounded by enemies, and the [[Reign of Blood]] was his answer to what he saw. His fear grew with his power. In the last decades he ate only what his Brides had tasted, slept in a different room each night and, it was said, no longer spoke to any man.

Death

In the final days of the siege a Custodian Centurion of the Companions appeared among the [[Reign of Blood|Brides of the Emperor]] and, after an hour's plea, led [[Alicia Dominica]] and her five closest sisters to the Golden Throne. When they returned they renounced the name Brides and denounced the High Lord for having perverted their order and the Emperor's work. Dominica sentenced him in the words that are engraved on her sarcophagus and drew her power sword. Vandire looked about at the soldiers with a puzzled expression, and said, it is recorded, that he had no time to die, since he was too busy. She beheaded him with a single stroke. The [[Rosarius|rosarius]] that had once convinced the Daughters of his sanctity broke in two with him.

His corpse was burned and the ashes scattered. His decrees were struck from the registers and his monuments pulled down. The [[Administratum]]'s own history of the period, compiled by his successors, says little of him, and that little is written in the third person and in the passive voice.

Legacy

[[Reign of Blood|Vandire's reign]] persuaded the Imperium that the power of the state and the power of the church must not rest in one hand. The [[Sebastian Thor|Decree Passive]], the creation of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] and the [[Ordo Sicarius]] and the founding of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], a force that is both the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]'s own and the [[Inquisition]]'s, were all consequences. The [[Master of the Administratum]] and the [[Ecclesiarch]] have never since been the same person.

In the Adepta Sororitas his memory is a lesson in the danger of false saints. In the [[Administratum]] it is used to warn clerks against vanity. The Imperial Cult mentions him rarely and in the same breath as the Archenemy. Among the common people of the worlds that lived through the Reign of Blood, his name survives in a few proverbs, among them the saying that a man who has no time to die has already done so.
