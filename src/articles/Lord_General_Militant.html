The Lord General Militant, also known as the Lord Militant, is a general officer rank of the [[Astra Militarum]]. The name and its variations differ by era and by the cultural origin of the officer who holds the rank. It is the highest operational rank within the Astra Militarum, and its holder serves as the supreme military commander of a theatre of war or of a crusade.

A Lord General Militant answers to the [[Lord Commander Militant]] and to the [[High Lords of Terra]] for the conduct of their campaign. Imperial records describe the rank as one reserved for those who have led large forces to victory over several years, and few officers reach it in a single career.

Notable holders

[[Ibram Gaunt]] later became a [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]] and was appointed First Lord Executor Militant of the [[Sabbat Worlds]]. Other holders are recorded in the regimental histories of the [[Astra Militarum]].
