The Drop Site Massacre, also called the Battle of Isstvan V, was the second great atrocity of the [[Horus Heresy]]. In about 006.M31 three Legions of the Emperor's loyal [[Space Marine Legions]], the [[Iron Hands]], [[Salamanders]] and [[Raven Guard]], were sent to the [[Planetary classification|dead world]] of Isstvan V to bring the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]] to account. At the moment of their landing, four further Legions that had been sent as their reserve revealed that they had sided with Horus, and the loyalists were caught between two armies. The massacre cost the Imperium three of its Legions as fighting forces, killed the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Ferrus Manus]] and left [[Corvus Corax]] gravely wounded. It also announced to the Emperor that the Heresy was a war on a galactic scale.

Background

After the destruction of [[Isstvan III]], the frigate [[Nathaniel Garro|Eisenstein]] carried news of the [[Warmaster]]'s treachery to [[Terra]]. [[Rogal Dorn]], the Praetorian of Terra, and the Emperor's regent [[Malcador the Sigillite]] ordered seven Legions to the Isstvan system under the Emperor's authority to bring [[Horus]] to heel. The first wave was composed of the [[Iron Hands]], [[Salamanders]] and [[Raven Guard]]. The reserve was composed of the [[Iron Warriors]], [[Alpha Legion]], [[Word Bearers]] and [[Night Lords]]. [[Ferrus Manus]] was given overall command of the first wave, and its [[Primarch|primarchs]] were [[Vulkan]] and [[Corvus Corax|Corax]].

The Emperor had not known that four of the reserve Legions had already given their oaths to the Warmaster. Horus had also had time to prepare. On Isstvan V, a world that had long been dead and whose basalt plains were crossed with the ruins of an ancient civilisation, his four Legions, the [[Sons of Horus]], [[Emperor's Children]], [[World Eaters]] and [[Death Guard]], had dug earthworks and trenches intended to withstand orbital bombardment.

The battle

The landing

The loyalist fleet entered orbit over Isstvan V without difficulty, and the first wave landed by drop-pod and gunship in the Urgall Depression, a vast basin of black sands. The loyalists were convinced they faced only the four Legions that [[Horus]] had taken to [[Isstvan III]], whose numbers had been reduced by the purge. Ferrus Manus placed himself with ten companies of his elite Morlock [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] at the centre. The [[Salamanders]] under [[Vulkan]] attacked the left flank, and the [[Raven Guard]] under Corax the right. The first accounts reckoned roughly forty thousand loyalists against thirty thousand [[Traitor|traitors]], though later records give far larger Legions and losses in the hundreds of thousands. For almost three hours the fighting favoured neither side.

The betrayal

The traitors were ready. Horus had chosen the position of the drop site, and his artillery and Titans, among them the war machines of the [[Legio Mortis]], fell on the first wave. At the signal the reserve Legions, which had arrived in orbit and been welcomed as allies, opened fire on the loyalist fleets and then landed on the surface behind them. The [[Iron Warriors]], [[Night Lords]], [[Word Bearers]] and [[Alpha Legion]] fell on the loyalist rear and flanks.

Ferrus Manus fought through the traitor lines to reach Fulgrim, and the two [[Primarch|primarchs]] duelled on the Urgall Depression. Ferrus was beheaded by Fulgrim, who had been possessed by the daemon of his sword. His Legion's Morlocks fought to the last at the centre of the field. Vulkan's Salamanders were encircled and, at the height of the slaughter, struck by a nuclear missile of the Iron Warriors; the primarch was lost to sight and presumed dead. On the other flank the Raven Guard were assaulted by the Word Bearers, and Corax fought [[Lorgar]] in single combat until [[Konrad Curze]] intervened to save his brother. Corax later fell to an artillery strike and was gravely wounded.

Escape

A number of Salamanders, [[Iron Hands]] and Raven Guard escaped from the Urgall Depression, many with the help of Corax's tactics, and fled the system. Perhaps twenty-two thousand loyalist Astartes survived in all. The Raven Guard, carrying their wounded primarch, hid in the ravines beyond the Urgall Hills and fought a guerrilla war for ninety-eight days while the Dark Mechanicum salvaged the battlefield, until about three thousand of them were taken off the world at the last moment.

Aftermath

By the end of the battle the three loyalist Legions had been reduced to a fraction of their strength, and have since been known as the "[[Shattered Legions]]". The [[Iron Hands]] lost their primarch, the [[Salamanders]] theirs to an unknown fate, and the [[Raven Guard]] theirs to injury. The three Legions were too weakened to play a further large part in the war. [[Horus]] had eight Legions in open revolt, nine when the [[Thousand Sons]] joined him, and he could advance on Terra with no loyalist force strong enough to stop him.

Corax made the dangerous passage through the warp to Terra, where he arrived 133 days after leaving the Isstvan system, while Vulkan was missing for many months before he returned to lead his Legion again. The Dark Mechanicum stripped the field of wrecked war machines for the Traitor Legions, and over three thousand ships of eight Legions then gathered above the planet before the advance toward Terra.

Horus called the Primarchs of his Legions to a council aboard the Vengeful Spirit, and each Legion left for an assigned task: the [[Iron Warriors]] to the Phall system, the [[Night Lords]] toward Tsagualsa, and the [[Alpha Legion]] against the [[Space Wolves]] and [[White Scars]].
