Uriel Ventris is a captain of the [[Ultramarines]], commanding the Chapter's Fourth Company. Born on Calth, he rose quickly through the ranks and took part in many of the Chapter's greatest wars in the closing years of the [[41st millennium]]: the defence of Pavonis against [[Heretic|heretics]] and the [[T'au Empire]], the destruction of a [[Tyranid]] splinter fleet at Tarsis Ultra, and the Invasion of Ultramar in 999.M41. In between he endured exile on a Death Oath to the [[Iron Warriors]]' [[Daemon world|daemon world]] of Medrengard and returned, one of very few to do so.

In the [[Era Indomitus]] Ventris carried word of [[Mortarion]]'s assault on Ultramar to [[Roboute Guilliman]] at the end of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], c. 111.M42, and fought beside the [[Primarch|primarch]] in the [[Plague Wars]]. He has crossed the Rubicon Primaris.

Early life

Ventris was born in the subterranean cities of Calth, the world of [[Ultramar]] that never recovered its surface after the [[Battle of Calth|Calth atrocity]] of the [[Horus Heresy]]. He passed the Chapter's trials, rose from Neophyte to the Fourth Company, and served under Captain Idaeus, whose unorthodox methods shaped him. He succeeded Idaeus as captain on Idaeus's death and inherited his sword.Imperial chronologies disagree on the order of Ventris's early campaigns and the date of his captaincy; one places his promotion in 999.M41, two years after the Tarsis Ultra campaign of 997.M41, while others make him captain before Tarsis Ultra.

Pavonis and Tarsis Ultra

Ventris's first command took him to Pavonis, a troubled [[Planetary classification|industrial world]] of the [[Eastern Fringe]], to escort the [[Administratum]] adept Ario Barzano, later revealed as an [[Inquisition|Inquisitor]] of the [[Ordo Xenos]]. The world was beset by the bombings of the [[Heresy|heretical]] Church of Ancient Ways, by six years of [[Drukhari]] raids and by an insurrection of its nobles, and beneath it slept a [[C'tan]], the Nightbringer. Ventris dealt with all of them. His friend and sergeant Pasanius lost an arm to the Nightbringer.

At Tarsis Ultra in about 997.M41 the Ultramarines, the Mortifactors and [[Inquisitor]] Kryptman fought a splinter of [[Hive Fleet Behemoth|Hive Fleet Behemoth]]. The outlying worlds were lost and the planet itself nearly overrun, until Ventris and a [[Deathwatch]] kill-team destroyed the fleet's Norn-Queen.

Medrengard

Main article: [[Medrengard]]

For breaches of the [[Codex Astartes]], Ventris and Pasanius were sent on a Death Oath to destroy the daemonculaba of Medrengard, the [[Daemon world|daemon world]] of the [[Iron Warriors]] in the [[Eye of Terror]]. Joined by renegades and escaped slaves, they destroyed it, but earned the undying hatred of the Warsmith Honsou. They escaped to Salinas, where the [[Grey Knights]] intercepted their call for help, arrested them, and declared them untainted after long trials. On [[Macragge]] the [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]] [[Varro Tigurius]] tested them again before they were readmitted.The Death Oath and Ventris's return are dated only to the last years of M41.

Return to Pavonis

Back on Pavonis, the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] had used the world's economic turmoil to court its rulers and then invaded when rebuffed. Ventris gave them an ultimatum: withdraw, or the strike cruiser Vae Victus would destroy the planet by [[Exterminatus]]. They withdrew. Pasanius was demoted and isolated for a hundred days for hiding the strange regenerating power of the bionic arm he had received after Pavonis.

Invasion of Ultramar

See also: [[Ultramar]]

In 999.M41 Honsou joined the [[Daemon|daemon prince]] [[Cato Sicarius|M'kar the Reborn]] in an invasion of [[Ultramar]]. They used a stolen virus as bait, turned a star fort into a gateway to the Warp, and fielded the Newborn, a psychic and physical clone of Ventris grown from his tissue in the daemonculaba. Ventris confronted Honsou and the Newborn at the Tomb of Ventanus on [[Battle of Calth|Calth]], the shrine of the Fourth Company's hero of the Heresy, and killed the Newborn. Honsou detonated the tomb rather than be taken. Ventris survived and went on to Talassar to help defeat M'kar.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Ventris and the Fourth Company were fighting elsewhere in [[Ultramar]] during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] when [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] was restored at [[Macragge]] in 999.M41. Long afterwards a visibly aged and scarred Ventris reached the primarch aboard Macragge's Honour after the Battle of [[Raukos]], the last battle of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], in about 111.M42, and told him of [[Mortarion]]'s assault on Ultramar. Guilliman declared the crusade at an end and led reinforcements home, and Ventris commanded the Fourth Company in the [[Plague Wars|Spear of Espandor]] counter-attack. He has since crossed the [[Primaris Space Marines|Rubicon Primaris]] and led the company against the [[Necrons]] on Sycorax.
