Excommunicate Traitoris is a [[High Gothic]] designation used by the [[Administratum]] and the [[Inquisition]] to declare an individual or organisation a [[Traitor]] to the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is the highest form of Imperial reproach. It is given to those who have committed extreme acts of heresy against the [[Emperor of Mankind]], including mutation, apostasy, blasphemy, rebellion and corruption by Chaos.

The designation banishes the accused from the Imperium on pain of death, and casts them out of the light of the Emperor's protection. Imperial records describe it as the ultimate form of censure, and no fate is held to be graver for those who serve the Imperium.

Declaration

An accused is declared Excommunicate Traitoris by one of two authorities: the [[High Lords of Terra]], or an [[Inquisitor]] of the Holy Ordos of the Inquisition. The declaration does not require a trial in the usual sense. The designation is made when the authority is satisfied that the accused has committed treason, and the record is then sealed.

Once the declaration is made, the accused is struck from the annals of Imperial history. The Imperial record gives the designation a formal character that other punishments lack, so that it can be applied to a whole Chapter as easily as to a single servant of the Imperium.

Consequences

Erasure of records

An Imperial servant who is declared Excommunicate Traitoris has their name removed from the scrolls of honour in the Hall of Heroes in the [[Imperial Palace]]. Their name is removed from the databases of the [[Adeptus Terra]] and other archives by the deletion teams of the Administratum's Historical Revision Unit. The [[Logos Historica Verita]] is responsible for keeping the sealed record of the declaration.

Chapters and individual Astartes

When an individual Astartes, or a whole [[Space Marine Chapter]], is declared Excommunicate Traitoris, the gene-seed stores held by the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] for that Chapter are destroyed. If any of the traitor Space Marines are killed, their bodies are incinerated so that their blood cannot taint Mankind. The destruction of gene-seed is one of the most serious consequences of the designation, because it ends the Chapter's ability to recruit.

Fate of the accused

The Imperial record holds that a heretic who confesses and repents may receive a quick and clean death. Most accused traitors do not do so. They flee, usually towards the Warp rift known as the [[Eye of Terror]], where the Imperium cannot follow. They are then hunted for the rest of their lives, or executed on sight by any loyal servant of the Imperium.

Use in the Imperium

The designation is used sparingly, because a declaration is final and erases the accused from the record. The [[Edict of Obliteration]] is a related measure, which authorises the destruction of a world. Both are held to apply to the same class of offence, the wholesale betrayal of the Imperium.

Historical cases

The [[Horus Heresy]], which began in 005.M31, is the largest body of traitors in Imperial history. The [[Traitor Legions]] that followed the [[Warmaster Horus]] are the principal subjects of the designation, and the [[Great Scouring]] that followed the Heresy hunted their remnants across the galaxy.

Dates

The date on which the designation was first formalised is not recorded in the sources consulted. The sources consulted do not date the earliest declaration, and the individual declarations are not dated in them.
