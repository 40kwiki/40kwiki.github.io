The 42nd millennium, abbreviated M42, is the millennium of the [[Imperial Dating System]] that began after 999.M41 and is now in progress. By the Terran reckoning followed in this encyclopedia it is the present age. Its history so far is the history of the [[Era Indomitus]], the period that opened with the [[Fall of Cadia]], the birth of the [[Great Rift]] and the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]] at the end of the [[41st millennium]].

The millennium began in the darkness of the [[Noctis Aeterna]], when the [[Astronomican]] could not be seen across most of the galaxy. Guilliman's [[Indomitus Crusade]] reconnected much of the southern half of the Imperium, the [[Imperium Sanctus]], in a first phase that ended at the Battle of [[Raukos]] in about 012.M42, and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of the [[Ultima Founding]] reinforced almost every Chapter. North of the Rift, the [[Imperium Nihilus]] is ruled by [[Commander Dante]] as Regent.

The age also saw the return of a second loyal [[Primarch|primarch]], [[Lion El'Jonson]]; the reappearance of the [[Daemon|daemon primarchs]] [[Mortarion]] and [[Angron]]; the [[Psychic Awakening]]; the [[Arks of Omen]] crisis caused by [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]]; the Fourth Tyrannic War against [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]; and the war for the [[Pariah Nexus]]. The dates of most of these events are uncertain, because time has passed unevenly across the galaxy since the Rift opened.

Dating

The Warp distortion that followed 999.M41 left worlds and fleets years apart from Terran time, and the old count of years had grown inaccurate even before the [[Fall of Cadia]]. [[Roboute Guilliman]] therefore ordered the [[Imperial Dating System|Imperial Calendar]] revised, so that the opening of the [[Great Rift]] is the point from which later years are reckoned. Most events of the millennium are still known only roughly, and the dates below are provisional: an event given here as "early M42" may in truth have fallen at some other time in the Era Indomitus.

Overview

The Indomitus Crusade

Main article: [[Indomitus Crusade]]

The [[Indomitus Crusade]] was proclaimed on [[Terra]] in 999.M41 and launched across the [[Imperium Sanctus]] in its first years. Its fleets broke the sieges of thousands of worlds, and its first phase ended in about 012.M42 when the crusade defeated a [[Traitor|traitor]] host at the Pit of [[Raukos]]. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] then returned to defend [[Ultramar]], where [[Mortarion]] had been waging the [[Plague Wars]] since the opening of the Rift, and fought the Death Lord to a standstill at [[Iax]].

The Rift and the Gauntlet

See also: [[Nachmund Gauntlet]]

Crossings of the [[Great Rift]] are few. The most important, the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], was the scene of the [[War of Nightmares]] on [[Vigilus]] in which [[Marneus Calgar]] of the [[Ultramarines]] led the Imperial forces that defeated the invasion of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]].

Primarchs returned

[[Lion El'Jonson]], first primarch of the [[Dark Angels]], woke again after ten thousand years of absence and has campaigned through the worlds of the [[Imperium Nihilus]], gathering the Dark Angels and their successors to him. The [[Daemon|daemon primarchs]] [[Mortarion]] and [[Angron]] have also reappeared, the latter in the Imperium Nihilus.

New and renewed threats

The [[Psychic Awakening]] saw psychic births multiply across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. The daemon engineer [[Vashtorr the Arkifane]] drove the [[Arks of Omen]] crisis, assembling the pieces of a key in the battle of [[Wyrmwood]]. [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] began the Fourth Tyrannic War in the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] in three new tendrils. The [[Necrons]] expanded the [[Pariah Nexus]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]], and the daemons of Khorne and Tzeentch overran [[Armageddon]].
