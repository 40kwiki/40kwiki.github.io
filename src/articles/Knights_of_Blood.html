The Knights of Blood were a Renegade [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a successor of the [[Blood Angels]] of unknown Founding. At the start of the [[41st millennium]] the [[High Lords of Terra]], on the advice of the [[Inquisition]], declared them Renegades and [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] for the destruction they had done to friends and foes alike. They held themselves loyal to the Emperor even if not to His Imperium.

They died to the last battle-brother at the [[Devastation of Baal]], defending [[Luna|the moon]] [[Baal|Baal Prime]] against the [[Tyranids]] of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] and the [[Daemon|daemons]] of [[Khorne]]. Their sacrifice let the [[Flesh Tearers]] and other sons of [[Sanguinius]] escape.

History

Origins

The Founding that made the Knights of Blood is not recorded. Their first [[Chapter Master]], Ousten Galael, is remembered as a Baal-born captain of the [[Blood Angels]] Legion, a scholar and a lover of the [[Librarian|Librarius]], which would suggest an early Founding. The fatal flaws in their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] suggest a late one, as with so many [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the later [[Third Founding|Foundings]]. Imperial scholars do not agree, and the question is open. Soon after its creation the Chapter set out on a crusade across the Imperium. Over several centuries it committed so many excesses in the Emperor's name, striking his foes and his allies without distinction, that the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] declared it renegade.

Renegades

After the declaration the Chapter largely disappeared from the records. It did not stop. It continued its crusade and was seen to come to the aid of beleaguered Imperial forces, unannounced and often unwanted. Few worlds will accept its help, for accepting it invites investigation by the [[Inquisition]]. [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] dislike fighting alongside warriors who are so near to madness. The Chapter kept its distance from those it fought beside, which protected them from censure and from the harm done by the more extreme Blood Angels successors. In the Jericho Reach, rumours persisted from 777.M41 into the 800s that [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] in Knights of Blood colours appeared unheralded in the [[Achilus Crusade]], struck to great effect and left. The Crusade's high command had no dealings with them.

Devastation of Baal

In 999.M41 the Knights of Blood came to [[Baal]] with other Blood Angels successors, to help against the [[Daemon|Bloodthirster]] [[Ka'Bandha]] and the [[Tyranids]] of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]]. Their Chapter Master Sentor Jool, mindful that his Chapter was renegade, ordered his warriors to keep clear of the other Chapters. When the Tyranid tide turned against the defenders, he and the remains of the Chapter chose to fight to the end on Baal Prime, so that the [[Flesh Tearers]] could retreat.

Organisation and doctrine

The Knights of Blood were once Codex compliant, but the flaws in their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] so reduced their numbers that their battle companies had to be rearranged. They preferred close combat and deployed most of their warriors as assault troops.

Notable members

  • Ousten Galael: first [[Chapter Master]], a scholar whose chronicle was the core of the Chapter's library.
  • Sentor Jool: last Chapter Master, Firstblade of the Chapter.
  • Ser Koth: [[Librarian]] who led a delegation to return a fallen Blood Angel's remains to his parent Chapter.
