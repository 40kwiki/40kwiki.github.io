A sector is the principal territorial division of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] below the [[Segmentum|segmentum]]. Each of the five segmenta is divided into sectors, each sector into sub-sectors, and each sub-sector into star systems and their worlds. The sector is the level at which most of the Imperium's government is actually carried out: tithes are assessed, battlefleets are based, the [[Inquisition]] gathers in conclaves and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] appoints its cardinals.

Sectors vary greatly in size and wealth. A typical sector holds several million cubic light years of space, but its worlds lie clustered along the warp routes, and much of its volume is uncharted. The space between sub-sectors, called inter-sector or [[Wilderness space|wilderness space]], may contain unknown worlds, nebulae, xenos or nothing at all. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, many sectors have been divided between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]].

History

The [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31) had no fixed provinces. Conquered worlds were placed under the expedition fleets that had taken them, and the [[Administratum|Imperial Administration]] grouped them for tithe by their distance and direction from [[Terra]]. Records of the late 30th millennium already use the word "sector" for such groupings.

The present system took shape after the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31). The reforms of [[Roboute Guilliman]], in roughly 015–021.M31, divided the old Imperial Army into the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Imperial Navy]] and fixed the five segmenta and the sectors within them. Each sector was given its own battlefleet and was made responsible for its own defence. No sector governor was to command both a fleet and an army.The dates of the reform are estimated from the sequence of events. No Administratum record of the first division into sectors survives.

In later millennia sectors were redrawn many times as worlds were lost or gained. Warp storms during the [[Age of Apostasy]] in M36 cut off whole sectors, among them the Jericho Sector of the [[Eastern Fringe]], which was not re-entered until the [[Achilus Crusade]] of 777.M41. New sectors were created by conquest, such as the [[Calixis Sector]], founded after the Angevin Crusade of the [[39th millennium]].

The [[Great Rift]] split many sectors along its length. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Administratum has had to recognise sectors that it can no longer reach, and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has redrawn others as it reconquered them.

Organisation

Sub-sectors

Main article: [[Sub-sector]]

A sub-sector is a cluster of between two and eight star systems close enough to be patrolled by a single squadron of Navy ships. Its systems share warp routes, trade and defence. A sub-sector is usually named after its principal world, and often has its own capital and naval base. In the [[Gothic Sector]], for example, each sub-sector was based on one of the [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] until the [[Gothic War]] of 139–160.M41.

Civil government

Each sector is ruled in name by a Lord Sector, also called a sector governor, chosen from among the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]] of its worlds or appointed by the [[High Lords of Terra]]. He governs from a sector capital, collects the tithes owed to Terra and answers to the Administratum. In practice his power varies: in a wealthy, well-connected sector such as Calixis the Lord Sector is a great lord, while in the frontier sectors he may control little beyond his capital.

Military

Every sector, or group of sectors, has its own battlefleet of the [[Imperial Navy]], commanded by a Lord Admiral who answers to the Lord High Admiral of the segmentum. The [[Departmento Munitorum]] raises regiments sector by sector, and a sector's regiments often serve together on campaign. In time of war, the High Lords may appoint a [[Lord Militant]] or Lord Commander to direct every Imperial force within one or more sectors.

Other Adepta

The other great Adepta also follow sector divisions. The [[Adeptus Arbites]] keeps a fortress precinct in each sector capital. The [[Inquisition]] organises its members in sector conclaves, such as the Calixian Conclave. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] appoints a cardinal for each sector or group of sectors, and the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] maintains an astropathic relay station at each sector capital.
