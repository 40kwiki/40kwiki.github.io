A sector is the principal territorial division of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] below the [[Segmentum|segmentum]]. Each of the five segmenta is divided into sectors, each sector into sub-sectors, and each sub-sector into star systems and their worlds. Each sector is the station of a battlefleet of the [[Imperial Navy]], and each is ruled by a Lord Sector.

No two sectors are alike. The size of each is set by local demands and by the density of the stars within it. A "typical" sector, if there can be such a thing, spans some seven million cubic light years, equal to a cube almost two hundred light years on each side. Within it the sub-sectors are groups of worlds, and the systems that belong to no sub-sector, called inter-sectors or [[Wilderness space|wilderness space]], may hold nebulae, xenos-held systems or unexplored space.

The Imperium assigns each sector a battlefleet of the [[Imperial Navy]], usually named for the sector it guards: Battlefleet Gothic is based in the [[Gothic Sector]] and Battlefleet Cadia in the [[Cadian Sector]]. Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 many sectors have been divided between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[Administratum]] has had to recognise sectors it can no longer reach.

Structure

Size and shape

The size of a sector varies with local demands and the density of the stars within it. The size of its sub-sectors is governed by the practical patrol ranges of Imperial starships through the [[Warp travel|warp]].

Sub-sectors

Main article: [[Sub-sector]]

Each sector is divided into a variable number of sub-sectors. A sub-sector usually comprises two to eight star systems within a radius of about ten light years, although some hold more and others only one. A sub-sector is a division of worlds and not of volume, so that there are vast numbers of star systems within each sector that fall within no sub-sector.

Inter-sectors and wilderness space

Main article: [[Wilderness space]]

The systems that belong to no sub-sector are known as inter-sectors, and are more commonly called wilderness space, forbidden zones, empty space and frontier space. They may hold gas or dust nebulae, inaccessible regions, [[Xenos|xenos]]-held systems, unexplored or uninhabited systems and worlds that cannot support life.

Government and institutions

Civil government

Main article: [[Sector Governor]]

A sector is governed by a Lord Sector, also called a [[Sector Governor|sector governor]] or sector lord, an Imperial noble who is usually also the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]] of the sector's capital or most important world. His duties resemble those of a planetary governor but reach across many systems. Lords Sector come from the nobility of their region of space and sometimes from the high prelates of the [[Ecclesiarchy]], such as a [[Cardinal]]. Marius Hax, the Lord Calixis, ruled the [[Calixis Sector]] from [[Scintilla]].

Military

Each sector has a battlefleet of the [[Imperial Navy]] assigned to safeguard it, generally named after the sector. In the Gothic Sector Battlefleet Gothic was commanded by Lord Admiral Cornelius von Ravensburg during the [[Gothic War]].

Other Adepta

The other Adepta may follow sector divisions. In the Calixis Sector, for example, the Inquisitors of the Calixian Conclave, the Ordos Calixis, are based in the Tricorn Palace on Scintilla, and the Lord Sector shares the capital with a Cardinal of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], a Lord Marshal of the [[Adeptus Arbites]], a Senior Astropath of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] and a Canoness of the [[Adepta Sororitas]].

History

The Great Crusade

The records do not say when the Imperium first divided its territory into sectors. The five segmenta, of which sectors are the subdivisions, were created by Imperial decree in M31, with no year recorded; see [[Segmentum]].

The post-Heresy reforms

The [[Imperial Navy]] was re-established as a separate service after the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) under the Imperial Reformation overseen by [[Roboute Guilliman]], and divided among the five segmentum fleets (see [[Segmentum]]). Each sector has a battlefleet of the Navy assigned to it.

The middle millennia

In later millennia sectors were lost and won. In the early 36th millennium, during the [[Age of Apostasy]], communication between the Jericho Sector of the [[Eastern Fringe]] and the Imperial core broke down, and the sector was lost for more than four thousand years until the [[Achilus Crusade]] of 777.M41 began the reconquest of the [[Jericho Reach]]. The [[Calixis Sector]] was won by the [[Angevin Crusade]] of 322–384.M39. The [[Sabbat Worlds]] were lost to [[Chaos]] in the 41st millennium and have been the object of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] since 755.M41.

The Great Rift and the Era Indomitus

The [[Great Rift]], opened in 999.M41, split many sectors along its length. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Administratum has had to recognise sectors that it can no longer reach, and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has set out to reclaim others.

Many more sectors are known by name. The registers list dozens, among them Askellon, Caligari, Octarius, Korianis, Narasima, Zeist and Ullanor, and each has its own record of its sub-sectors, its fleets and its wars.

Navigation and astrography

The Imperium does not map its sectors as a traveller would map a coast. Every journey between stars passes through the [[Warp]], and the length of a voyage depends on the state of the warp more than on the distance in realspace. Sub-sector size is set by the patrol ranges of Imperial ships through the warp, not by distance in realspace alone.

Sectors can be lost to the Imperium. The Jericho Reach fell out of contact in the 36th millennium and was found again only in the 41st. In 741.M41 the Administratum suspended the Civitas Imperialis in the Sabbat Worlds, and the sector's lord governor was moved to a neighbouring sector for his own safety; the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] began to retake it in 755.M41.

Defence of a sector

The battlefleet assigned to a sector is its main shield. In the Gothic War of 139–160.M41, the Black Legion's 12th Black Crusade fell upon the Gothic Sector and Battlefleet Gothic bore the brunt of the fighting. The [[Cadian Sector]] was guarded by the fortress world of [[Cadia]] until its fall in 999.M41.
