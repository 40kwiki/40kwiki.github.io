The Laanah Rifts is a region of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] on the northwestern fringe of the galaxy. Much of it had remained untouched by Humanity since before the [[Age of Strife]], and for a long time it was held by aliens and Heretics. The [[Imperium of Mankind]] launched the [[Belrath Crusade]] to retake it in 182.M38, and within about a century and a half a great part of the region had come under Imperial rule, which led to the founding of the [[Laanah Sector]].

History

In 182.M38, under [[Praetor]] Slayban Bellrath, a noble of the [[Terran]] court raised to the rank of [[Lord Militant]], the crusade was launched. It met fierce resistance from the [[Hellgrammite]] xenos, frail anthropoids who rode to war on howling bio-mechanical monstrosities and whose occult weapons flooded the void around their worlds with aetheric fire. Within fifty years the crusade had begun to falter.

The [[Sons of Medusa]] Chapter intervened and won one of its greatest victories of the crusade. Its full strength circled the Hellgrammite attack fleets and struck the heart of their domains at the star system codenamed "Wormgyre". With the death of Wormgyre, the Hellgrammites' will to resist collapsed. Within a century a great swathe of the Rifts was under Imperial dominion, and the Sons of Medusa are remembered in the sector's legends as "the Emerald Death". The Chapter keeps infrequent contact with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] factions that founded bases on the frontier, and holds seldom-used recruiting rights on several worlds there.
