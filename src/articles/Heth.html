Heth was the [[Lord Commander Militant]] of the [[Astra Militarum]] and a member of the [[High Lords of Terra]] in the middle of the 32nd millennium. He was a soldier's general, unusually warm in his dealings with the ordinary troopers of the Guard for a man of his rank, and he was the chief political rival on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] of the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]]. He was killed at the start of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32, leading a relief expedition to [[Ardamantua]].

Before his fleet was destroyed he sent an astropathic message to Terra warning that the Imperium faced a threat unlike any it had known. It was the first report of the Beast and its Attack Moons to reach the Throneworld.

On the council

By 544.M32 Heth sat among a council of twelve presided over by the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] [[Udin Macht Udo]]. The council was divided between those who looked to Lansung, whose faction controlled the fleets and with them the transport of every army, and those who opposed him. Heth differed from Lansung in his wish to lead his troops in the field himself. He was joined in opposition to Lansung by the Inquisitorial Representative [[Wienand]] and the [[Grand Master of Assassins]], [[Drakan Vangorich]], who regarded him as one of the few High Lords with whom he was on good terms.

Nothing is recorded of Heth's origins or career before he took the office, nor who held it before him.

Ardamantua

In 544.M32 the Imperial Fists sent their Shield Company to Ardamantua in the [[Segmentum Solar]], six solar weeks of warp travel from [[Terra]], to exterminate an insectoid species, the Chromes. The Chromes were fleeing something. When Ardamantua's gravity began to fail they made a last assault on the Imperial lines, and Chapter Master Cassus Mirhen was killed.

The High Lords realised that disaster would reach Terra itself if the insectoids continued their flight. They ordered a rescue mission to Ardamantua, led by Heth, with the entire Imperial Fists Chapter, an expedition that Vangorich particularly urged.

The cause of the gravitational disturbances became clear when a massive planetoid, an Ork Attack Moon, materialised above the world. It attacked the Imperial fleet. After he had transmitted his final warning to Terra, Heth's fleet was destroyed by the Attack Moon, and he was killed with it. On the surface the Imperial Fists were overwhelmed, and Second Captain [[Koorland]] was left the last of his Chapter.

Legacy

Imperial strategos concluded from Heth's message that the Attack Moon's next likely target was Terra. His warning was later used by the Navy in planning the defence of the Throneworld. His seat passed to [[Abel Verreault]], a politician who bought transport from Lansung with his vote, and whom the Lord Protector Vangorich's assassins killed in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32.
