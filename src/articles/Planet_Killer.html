The Planet Killer, called the Krukal'Righ in the Cthonic tongue of the [[Black Legion]], is a vast stellar vessel built to destroy worlds. It was the flagship of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] during his twelfth [[Black Crusade]], known to the Imperium as the [[Gothic War]], from 139.M41 to 160.M41.

The ship is built around a single directed energy cannon of enormous power. Imperial accounts describe the weapon as able to strip a world's crust in a single discharge, and the vessel as able to take on the defences of a sector alone. Its construction and origin are not recorded in any surviving Imperial register.

Gothic War

Main article: [[Gothic War]]

During the [[Gothic War]] the Planet Killer led the [[Black Legion]] fleets through the [[Gothic Sector]] and against the Imperial worlds of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It was used to threaten the populations of worlds that refused to yield, and its presence was enough to break the resistance of some systems before its gun was fired.

Records

Imperial scholars do not agree on the number of worlds the ship destroyed. The [[Officio Militaris]] counts a small number of confirmed worlds. [[Administratum]] registers list more, but they are not checked against survey data.
