The Estate Imperium is a division of the [[Adeptus Administratum]]. Imperial scholars describe it as "the million-strong records office of the Administratum". Its purpose is to keep and supply the records of [[Adepta|Imperial adepta]] whenever the arms of the [[Adeptus Terra]] call for them. The Estate Imperium also oversees the vast [[Departmento Munitorum]]. It is one of the oldest bodies of the Administratum and dates back to the [[Unification Wars]].

The head of the division holds the title of Chancellor of the Estate Imperium. Because the Estate's importance is so great, its Chancellor sometimes holds one of the temporary, rotating seats on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] of the [[High Lords of Terra]].

Records and duties

The Estate keeps the personal files of adepta across the Imperium. When an arm of the Adeptus Terra needs to confirm a servant's service, rank or posting, it sends a request to the Estate. The Estate answers from its archives, which are kept in the [[Administratum Archive, Terra|Administratum Archive]] on [[Terra]]. Imperial scholars note that the Estate's clerks are rarely seen in the field, yet their work decides who may hold office and who may be promoted.

As an overseer of the [[Departmento Munitorum]], the Estate also checks the supply and logistics records of the Imperial Army. This gives it a role in the Imperial tithe, since the Munitorum uses those records to measure what each world has paid.

Chancellors

In 945.M41 the Chancellor was Seward Rosencranz. By 999.M41 the post was held by an official named Brach. Brach died during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]. His seat on the Senatorum Imperialis stayed empty for a time, until the [[Adeptus Custodes]] Captain-General [[Trajann Valoris]] claimed it during the fifth standard year of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], c. 004.M42 (estimated).
