The Shattered Legions is the collective term for the survivors of the three Loyalist [[Legiones Astartes]] that were betrayed at the [[Drop Site Massacre]] on [[Isstvan V]] in 006.M31, in the opening stage of the [[Horus Heresy]]. They were the Iron Hands, the [[Raven Guard]] and the [[Salamanders]].

The survivors of each Legion were scattered across the northern sectors of the Imperium. In the solar weeks that followed, small groups of warriors gathered and fought on as bands, and they took the name Shattered Legions from the Heresy's accounts. Their history after Isstvan is one of survival, flight and hard fighting.

History

[[Ferrus Manus]] of the Iron Hands was slain by [[Fulgrim]] at Isstvan. [[Vulkan]] of the Salamanders was taken prisoner. [[Corvus Corax]] escaped with a few thousand Raven Guard. The Shattered Legions formed from these survivors.Casualty figures at Isstvan V are recorded only in approximate terms.

The refuge at [[Baal]] was besieged by traitor forces in 008–013.M31. Later in 009.M31 the Shattered Legions fought at Arissak, where a captain of the Imperial forces destroyed one of their cells.
