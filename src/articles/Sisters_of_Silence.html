The Sisters of Silence, also called the Silent Sisterhood and in the Era Indomitus part of the Anathema Psykana, are a militant order of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] made up entirely of women who are psychic nulls, or "untouchables": rare humans who have no presence in the [[Warp|warp]] and whose very nearness weakens psychic powers and repels daemons. Every sister takes a lifelong vow of silence and speaks only through the order's sign languages.

The order was founded early in the [[Great Crusade]] as the armed branch of the Divisio Investigates, which hunted rogue psykers and gathered the psyker tithe for [[Terra]]. Its sisters crewed and guarded the [[Black Ships]] and, with the [[Adeptus Custodes]], held the right to walk the Emperor's private vaults beneath the [[Imperial Palace]]. They fought at the [[Burning of Prospero]] and in the war beneath the Palace during the [[Horus Heresy]].

After the Heresy a fearful Imperium turned against the Sisterhood. Its orders were scattered and reduced, and for most of ten thousand years it was believed to have been disbanded, though the sisters of the Black Ships never ceased their duty. In 999.M41 the Lord Commander [[Roboute Guilliman]] ordered the surviving sisters found and restored, and the order now serves openly alongside the Custodes and the fleets of the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

History

Origins

The origins of the order are uncertain. The only account the Imperium keeps concerns the Cataclysm of Pentacanaes, a hive world where a psychic cult broke loose early in the Great Crusade, in about the first decades after 798.M30.Date estimated from the sequence of events. A Black Ship that had triggered the disaster carried away a group of null women called the Daughters of the Crow, who fought the horrors of the hive before the VI Legion, later the [[Space Wolves]], arrived to restore order at the cost of a billion lives. The Daughters came from a world listed only as "9-13", which is missing from the Imperial cartographic records.

Some scholars doubt this account, suggesting that the events at Pentacanaes were arranged as a test, or that the order's roots lie further back, in the Emperor's own plans or in some lost agency of the [[Age of Strife|Dark Age of Technology]]. What is agreed is that within four decades of the start of the Crusade the Divisio Investigates had become a militant order of female nulls, thousands strong, beholden to the Emperor alone. Its recruitment was folded into the psyker tithe: the Black Ships took female null children along with the psykers they gathered.

Great Crusade

The Emperor kept a standing force of the Sisterhood in his own armies. The sisters hunted psykers who turned their gifts against the Imperium, gathered the tithe and kept order aboard the Black Ships, and were sent where the Crusade met psychic enemies. Remembrancers called them the Null Maidens. Their headquarters was the Somnus Citadel on [[Luna]], maintained by the gene-engineering cults of the Selenites.

At the time of the Heresy the order was led by three offices known outside it. The Knight-Commander, its senior field officer, was Jenetia Krole, known in popular myth as the Soulless Queen and commander of the Emperor's Raptor Guard. The Mistress of the Black Ships, admiral of the order's fleet, was Varonika Sulath. The Nemesis Praxia, keeper of the order's lore and estates and responsible for training, was Ebon Naroda.

Horus Heresy

In 004.M31 the Sisterhood formed part of the censure host sent with [[Leman Russ]] and the Custodes of [[Constantin Valdor]] to bring [[Magnus the Red]] to Terra. Their presence stifled the powers of the [[Thousand Sons]] in the battle that destroyed [[Prospero]].

On Terra the sisters fought beside the Custodes in the war beneath the Palace (004–014.M31), when Magnus's breach let daemons into the Emperor's unfinished webway. An Oblivion Knight of the order sat on the Terran war council. Under the decree called the Unspoken Sanction, Varonika Sulath oversaw the gathering of tens of thousands of psykers whose sacrifice powered the Golden Throne while the Emperor fought beyond it.

During the [[Siege of Terra]] in 014.M31, the Witchseeker Pursuivant Celia Harroda is recorded as holding a webway gate against a [[Bloodthirster]] of [[Khorne]]. Knight-Commander Krole fell in the Siege, in the defence of the Eternity Wall space port.

Decline

After the Siege the Sisterhood lost direction. Many sisters were dead or stranded across the galaxy. The Imperium that emerged was more superstitious than the one they had served, and the order was cast out of the Palace; its dead were defiled and its tombs plundered. Some sisters joined the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]]; others went among the wider population and hunted alone, and some founded families, spreading the null gene further through humanity.

The order vanished from Imperial history for some fifteen centuries. During the [[War of the Beast]] of 544–546.M32, a remnant of fifty sisters living in exile on Nadiries, led by the Knight-Abyssal Kavalanera Brassanas, was persuaded to fight by the last Imperial Fist, Koorland, and took part in the battles at [[Ullanor]].

The Sisterhood was formally disbanded after the [[Age of Apostasy]] in M36. Many enclaves fought on in secret. The sisters of the Chamber Astra, who crewed the Black Ships, were never disbanded and kept their duty throughout.

Restoration

On his return in 999.M41 Guilliman remembered the value the Emperor had placed on the Sisterhood. He issued the decree known as the Dispensatus Anathema, sending as messengers the sisters who had fought with him at the Battle of Luna, and called the order's survivors to Terra. Some three thousand gathered in about 000.M42Date estimated from the sequence of events. and were named the Vigil Indomitus, divided into cadres and sent with the battle groups of the Indomitus Crusade. Guilliman was surprised to find that the sisters, unlike their predecessors, worshipped his father as a god.

The order has since founded vigils across every segmentum. It is mainly a fleet-based force, with pacts with veteran astropaths and Navigator houses able to bear its presence. Gathered in numbers, the sisters can project a null-field strong enough to calm warp storms, and they have helped Imperial fleets cross the turbulence of the [[Great Rift]]. With the Custodes they make up the Talons of the Emperor. Sister-Commanders Aphone and Bellas fought with Guilliman in the [[Plague Wars]] in Ultramar (c. 000–012.M42).

Organisation

The Knight-Commander leads the order. Under her the order is divided into chambers:

  • The Chamber of Vigilance, the largest, which garrisons the tithe and gathers it.
  • The Chamber Astra, which captains and guards the Black Ships.
  • The Chamber of Oblivion, the martial elite, made up of the strongest nulls. Its Oblivion Knights and Knights-Centura command in battle.
  • The Chamber of Judgement, whose Silent Judges of the Excruciatus investigate those who hide psykers from the tithe and decide the fate of the accused.

Sisters-in-waiting train for years before taking their vows. Full sisters serve in squads by role: Prosecutors armed with boltguns, Witchseekers with flamers, and Vigilators with two-handed blades, together called a cadre. Many recruits fail the order's trials; some of those judged suitable are given instead to the Culexus Temple of the [[Officio Assassinorum]].

Vow of silence

Every sister takes a vow of silence on joining the order and communicates through sign. Thoughtmark is a subtle language of small hand gestures for complex ideas; Battlemark is a cruder set of signs for the field. Transmissions beyond sight are sent in Orsköde, a machine-readable form of Thoughtmark that sounds like turning cogwheels. Sisters deal with outsiders through interlocutors.

Abilities and equipment

A sister's null aura disrupts psychic power around her and causes unease and fear in other people, psykers and non-psykers alike. Sisters are trained to project or suppress it. In battle they are almost immune to psychic attack, possession and mental control, and daemons near them lose their grip on reality.

They wear light armour of gold plate over mesh and carry execution blades, boltguns and flamers. The order fields its own transports, of the Kharon pattern among others.

Relations

The Sisterhood's oldest bond is with the [[Adeptus Custodes]], with whom they guarded the Emperor's household. They also work with the [[Inquisition]], above all the Ordo Hereticus, and with the Adeptus Astra Telepathica, for which they still gather the tithe.
