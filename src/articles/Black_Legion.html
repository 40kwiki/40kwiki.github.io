The Black Legion is the name that [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] gave to the survivors of the Sons of Horus, and to all the [[Traitor]] [[Space Marines]] who have since bowed to him. It is the largest and the most dangerous single force of [[Chaos Space Marines]] in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]], and the only one of the nine [[Traitor Legions]] that can call itself a Legion and not a coalition of warbands. It is not an army in the sense that the Imperium understands. It is a host of hundreds of independent warbands, each led by a Chaos Champion and each free to pursue its own god, that gather only when Abaddon calls a [[Black Crusade]], and that wear black and gold, in honour of the dead [[Warmaster]], when they do. In that gathering lies its strength, since there is no other leader in the Eye of Terror whom so many will follow.

Its purpose is to finish what [[Horus]] began. The Black Legion has launched thirteen Black Crusades from the [[Eye of Terror]] since 781.M31. In the last of them, in 999.M41, it destroyed the [[Fortress world|fortress world]] of [[Cadia]] and broke the Imperium in half by the opening of the [[Great Rift]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the Legion has carried the war across the galaxy. Its fleets have blockaded [[Forge world|forge worlds]], its warbands have taken [[Sector|sectors]] from the Imperium's isolated frontiers, and its Warmaster has joined with the [[Daemon|daemon]] [[Vashtorr the Arkifane|Vashtorr]] in a campaign, the Arks of Omen, whose purpose is the recovery of an ancient weapon. The Imperium has not defeated it. It rates the Black Legion the greatest danger to [[Terra]] after the daemon hosts themselves.

History

From the Sons of Horus

See also: [[Sons of Horus]]

The Black Legion has no separate history before the Heresy. It descends from the [[Sons of Horus|XVI Legion]], which was raised from Terran volunteers late in the [[Unification Wars]], took the name Luna Wolves after the [[Luna|First Pacification of Luna]] in 703.M30, and was given to the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Horus]] when he was found on [[Cthonia]]. As the Sons of Horus it was the [[Warmaster]]'s chief instrument in the Heresy. It was one of the Legions at [[Isstvan III]] and the [[Drop Site Massacre]], defended Cthonia against the [[Imperial Fists]] and fought at the walls of the [[Imperial Palace]]. The details of those years are told in the article on that Legion.

The Legion came out of the [[Siege of Terra]] broken. It had lost the primarch it had been raised to follow and a great part of its warriors, and it lost the Mournival, its council of captains. Torgaddon was dead, and Loken, who had fought his way into the Palace, had sided with the Emperor. Under [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]], its [[First Captain]], the survivors broke orbit in the confusion after Horus's death and fled in the [[Great Scouring]] to the Eye of Terror.

Maeleum and the Slave Wars

The Sons of Horus carried the body of their primarch in stasis into the Eye of Terror. On the [[Daemon world|daemon world]] of Maeleum, a graveyard of steel and rust, they built a fortress from the wrecks of lost ships and laid the body in a tomb in its centre. The fortress was called Lupercalios. In the following decades the Legion fell to quarrelling over the question of what it was now. Some captains held that it must replace its losses from new stocks, since no fortress could hold off a determined assault. Others held that the Warp would give it all the power it needed, and sought it in the merging of warrior and [[Daemon|daemon]]. The latter prevailed, and the Legion's first Possessed warriors, the Tormented, were formed in the defence of the tomb.

Abaddon, sick of the war, walked alone into the Eye. He was gone for a long time, and his Legion in his absence was attacked by its brothers. The [[Legion Wars|Slave Wars]], in which the [[Traitor Legions]] turned on each other, found the Sons of Horus on the edge of ruin. The [[Emperor's Children]], who had grown strong in the service of [[Slaanesh]], stormed Lupercalios and took the body of Horus, to give it to [[Fabius Bile]] for cloning. The fortress was destroyed. Falkus Kibre, once commander of the Justaerin, called an assembly of the remaining captains and decided that their only hope was to find the one warrior who could save them, the First Captain, and to bring him back.

The reforging

Abaddon was found aboard the [[Vengeful Spirit]], the flagship of the Warmaster, which he had taken into the furthest reaches of the Eye. He returned to his Legion and destroyed the body of Horus, and with it the clone that the Emperor's Children had been raising from it. He subdued those captains who would not follow him. The most prominent of them, [[Sons of the Eye|Drecarth the Sightless]], left and took his warband with him, and those who remained loyal to Horus's memory or to no master became known as the Thrice-Cursed [[Traitor|Traitors]]. They include the Sons of the Eye, the Wolves of Horus and the True Sons. The rest he took in hand. Under his leadership the Sons of Horus gave up their name and their colour and became the Black Legion, in black and gold, "from shame and shadow recast". Any Traitor [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] who would bow to the Despoiler was welcome to wear the colours.

He built the Legion not as a single order but as a host of warbands, sworn to him through an [[Dark Angels|inner circle]] of lieutenants, the Chosen of Abaddon. It was a deliberate choice. The Legion of Horus had been bound to a man, and he had died. The Legion of Abaddon was bound to a purpose, and the purpose was to kill the Emperor, which is a purpose that outlasts any warrior's life.

The Black Crusades

Main article: [[Black Crusade]]

In 781.M31 Abaddon led the Black Legion out of the Eye of Terror at the head of the greatest Traitor host since the Heresy. This was the 1st Black Crusade. The Imperium was taken by surprise. It was forced to withdraw many of the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Second Founding]] from other fronts, and only [[Cadia]], with its fortifications, held. In the course of it Abaddon, with a cadre of his elite, entered the Tower of Silence on [[Uralan]], where the [[Chaos|Dark Gods]] had locked away their secrets, and took from its heart the daemon sword [[Drach'nyen]]. He fought the High Marshal of the [[Black Templars]], [[Sigismund]], on the deck of the Eternal Crusader and was left with a scar in the chest that has never healed, and he took the title of Warmaster of Chaos.

Twelve further crusades followed, in no regular order. Between them the Legion raided without pause. Abaddon gave his warlords licence to range as they would, on the one condition that the atrocities they committed were done in the name of the Black Legion and at the Emperor's cost. Most of the Crusades are known to the Imperium only under other names. The 12th, in 139.M41, was the [[Gothic War]], in which Abaddon's fleet seized two of the six [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone Fortresses]] that lay abandoned in the Gothic Sector and used a vast vessel, the [[Planet Killer]], as a decoy. Only the intervention of the [[Aeldari]], who had foreseen his purpose, prevented the seizure of all six. In 777.M41 a Chaos fleet led by the Legion was beaten back at the Iron Labyrinth in the Cadian System. In 959–961.M41, in the Pandorax Campaign, Abaddon opened a gateway to the Warp on the [[Death world|death world]] of Pythos, which the Dark Angels and the [[Grey Knights]] closed.

The Imperium believed each crusade a separate act of aggression and defeated each in turn. It did not realise that each was keyed to a specific purpose, and that they were a path. In the Black Legion this path is called the Crimson Path: a line, laid across the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Obscurus]] by thirteen wars, that ends at Terra.

The 13th Black Crusade and the Fall of Cadia

See also: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] and [[Fall of Cadia]]

The [[Thirteenth Black Crusade|13th Black Crusade]] was the culmination. For solar decades Abaddon prepared it. He bargained with the daemon princes of the Eye of Terror: with [[Mortarion]] of the [[Death Guard]], to whom he gave the Hand of Darkness; with [[Angron]] of the [[World Eaters]], to whom he gave the Hellfire Stone; with [[Fulgrim]], who was promised an innocent of rare gift and a third of the civilians in the Crusade's path; and with [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]], who refused to take the field himself but whose Rubricae were lent through his sorcerer [[Ahzek Ahriman|Ahriman]]. The Crusade fell upon the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]], the only stable passage out of the Eye of Terror, in 999.M41. Several hundred thousand [[Chaos Space Marines]] took part. Cadia's ninth world, [[St. Josmane's Hope]], was destroyed. The defenders, led by Lord Castellan [[Ursarkar Creed]], held for months.

Cadia fell when Abaddon sent the wreckage of the Blackstone fortress Will of Eternity, damaged in the fighting, hurtling onto its surface. The impact destroyed most of the defenders and broke the Cadian Pylons, ancient machines of [[Xenos|xenos]] making that had held back the Eye of Terror for ten thousand years. The warp storm overran the [[Fortress world|fortress world]]. The blood spilled on Cadia, and the fury loosed across the Segmentum Obscurus, thinned the veil between [[Realspace|realspace]] and the Warp to the point of collapse. The [[Great Rift]] opened and cut the galaxy in two, and the Astronomican failed for a season. The Imperium calls the period of darkness that followed the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]].

The Legion struck another blow in the same hour. When the [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] awakened [[Roboute Guilliman]] on [[Macragge]], a Black Legion force fell on [[Ultramar]] in an attempt to prevent the return of the Regent, and was beaten back with great loss.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Since the opening of the Rift, the Black Legion has been at war across the whole galaxy. Its fleets, no longer pinned to the Eye of Terror, have appeared from the Warp in many places. They have blockaded [[Forge world|forge worlds]], such as Raeddon, to take their relics, and have destroyed the fleets of [[Sector Governor|sector governors]]. In the Bellicose Stars, the Hounds of Abaddon were halted on Gandor's Providence by the Imperial Fists and their [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] Intercessors, and went on to burn the worlds around it. In the Aralest System, a Black Legion armada destroyed the Navis Imperialis in a battle of solar months. Its warlords have continued to launch independent raids, and the Imperium can no longer tell whether a given attack is the act of the Warmaster or of one of his captains.

The Warmaster's own campaign is the [[Arks of Omen]]. After the war on [[War of Nightmares|Vigilus]], he met aboard the Vengeful Spirit the daemon [[Vashtorr the Arkifane|Vashtorr]], called the Arkifane, who rules the Forge of Souls and who offered an alliance. Vashtorr said that a Key to a Lock lay in pieces across the galaxy, and that when it was whole it would unlock a weapon with which the Imperium could be destroyed. Abaddon, who distrusts every daemon, agreed. With the help of the [[Dark Mechanicum]], Vashtorr made a fleet of Arks of Omen from captured [[Space hulk|space hulks]], and Abaddon gave them to his own officers and to other Chaos lords who swore to him. Their task was to find the fragments of the Key and to destroy whoever stood in the way. The campaign took the Black Legion to many worlds, including the ancient monastery of the Dark Angels, [[The Rock]], which Vashtorr invaded with the Legion's help. In the last battle, over a false [[Caliban]] that Vashtorr had made out of fragments and called [[Wyrmwood]], the loyalist fleet of the Unforgiven and the [[Blood Angels]] fought the Arks, the Black Legion and the daemon Angron. Vashtorr completed the Key with his Dissonance Engine and the false world, with the Arkifane and his fleet, vanished into a storm. The Imperium reckoned the battle a victory for Abaddon, and the Vengeful Spirit, in the most recent accounts, entered the Webway with Vashtorr in search of the Lock. Its present whereabouts are not known.

The Black Legion's other work in the Era Indomitus is quieter. Its agents, called by the Imperium the Hand of Abaddon, have been recorded in the [[Indomitus Crusade]]'s rear areas, where they have subverted garrisons, and in the Imperium Nihilus, the half of the Imperium that the Rift has cut from Terra, where the Warmaster has declared himself the lord. It has taken the title Warmaster of the Imperium Nihilus for itself, and in the name of that realm demands the submission of every Traitor, and every world, that the Rift has cut off.

Organisation

The warbands

After [[Horus]]'s death the structure of the [[Sons of Horus|XVI Legion]] broke down into warbands, each led by a [[Chaos]] Champion, who might be an old officer of the Legion or a new warlord who had earned the favour of the Powers. That is the shape of the Black Legion today. A warband may number a few dozen or several thousand, and it may be composed of the Legion's own, of renegades from other [[Traitor Legions]], of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] that have fallen from the Imperium, or of all three. Many are devoted to one of the four Chaos Gods, and carry the icons and colours of their patron on their black armour in the hope of divine favour. All of them wear black and gold in the Despoiler's honour, and all bend the knee to him as [[Warmaster]], though some claim autonomy. The Legion gathers in full only when [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] calls a Crusade.

The Chosen of Abaddon

The [[Dark Angels|inner circle]] is the Chosen of Abaddon, a shadow of the Mournival in which he once served. There are four of them, each holding a title that names an office in the Crusades. The Lord Ravager commands the Black Fleet and the Legion's ground forces, and is always a devotee of [[Khorne]]. The Lord [[C'tan|Deceiver]] is a Tzeentchian sorcerer whose visions lead the Crusade from system to system. The Lord Corruptor is a servant of [[Nurgle]] who spreads fear and disease ahead of the host and keeps the lesser warlords in line. The Lord Purgator, who serves [[Slaanesh]], sees that every living soul on a conquered world is taken in chains into the holds of the fleet, and that no building stands that is not dedicated to the Powers. The Chosen are replaced as they fall, and Abaddon has little patience with failure. The last [[Officio Assassinorum]] [[Execution Force|execution force]] to be fielded in full was sent against them, and was destroyed by a trap Abaddon laid.

Urkrathos, the Lord Ravager, became a [[Daemon|Daemon Prince]] and fell on [[Cadia]]. He was succeeded by Threxos Hellbreed, a master of ship-to-ship ambush. Ygethmor the Deceiver, called the Twice Damned, has the sorcerer's office. Skyrak Slaughterborn leads the Nurglite warbands and led the Slaughterkin against Cadia. Devram Korda, once a veteran sergeant of the Sons of Horus, rose to be the Tyrant of Sarora and the Lord Purgator, having distilled the life of a [[Hive world|hive city]] into a single vial.

Formations

Abaddon's personal guard is the Aphotic Blade, drawn from the strongest of his [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] and known to its enemies as the Bringers of Despair. Their arrival marks the presence of the Warmaster. Each god has its own formation. The Hounds of Abaddon are berserker devotees of Khorne, who lead the assaults. The Bringers of Decay serve Nurgle and carry the plagues. The Children of Torment follow Slaanesh. The Sons of the Cyclops, the smallest formation, are the sorcerers of [[Tzeentch]], among them Rubricae and renegades from the [[Thousand Sons]], such as Iskandar Khayon, and their small number is offset by the favours Abaddon grants them. The Tormented are the Legion's Possessed warriors, who gave their bodies to daemons and left their old warbands for their own. The Oath-Broken are those who failed in their tasks or were crippled, and who suffer for it. The Sarissan Iron Pact, which traces its origin to the 28th Armoured Assault Company of the Luna Wolves, is a sub-faction that serves under Abaddon directly.

Combat doctrine

The Black Legion fights the way its ancestors did: it finds the weakest point of an enemy and strikes it with overwhelming force. Under [[Horus]], the target was the enemy's command. Under [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]], it is the enemy's will. The Legion uses its [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] and its armoured vehicles in the vanguard, the Khornate berserkers in the first assault, and the [[Daemon|daemons]] and sorcery of its Possessed and sorcerers in the support. It deploys [[Blackstone Fortress|Blackstone fortresses]] and daemon engines, and in the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[Arks of Omen]]. Its warlords have the freedom to raid when and where they wish, and the Legion's strategy is therefore the sum of many decisions, tied together by Abaddon's long design.

Beliefs

The Black Legion worships [[Chaos|Chaos Undivided]], which is to say all four powers, and permits its warbands to favour one. Its warlords believe that victory is the only measure of worth, that power is the proper reward of the strong and that the Space Marines, as the strongest of humanity, were made to rule. They despise the Emperor not as a tyrant but as a weakling who ruled badly what he could have ruled well, and they hold their primarch in a double regard: as the [[Warmaster]] who nearly won, and as the man who did not. The Legion has never forgotten the failure at Terra, and the lesson it has drawn is that it must not be repeated. Where [[Horus]] believed himself a servant of the gods, [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]] is held to believe that he uses them, and that the Legion should do the same.

Gene-seed

Main article: [[Gene-seed]]

The gene-seed of the Black Legion was pure and potent before [[Davin]], being drawn from [[Horus]]. After his corruption its warriors began to suffer random [[Mutant|mutation]], and the practice of seeking daemonic possession has quickened it. Mutation is worn with pride as a sign of favour. The organs no longer grow reliably, or grow so warped that the human subject dies, and so the Legion cannot make many new Space Marines. It takes recruits from the gene-stocks of captured Imperial [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and from other [[Traitor Legions]], and its numbers are a small fraction of what the [[Sons of Horus]] mustered at [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]].

Heraldry

The [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] wore white, with the emblem of a wolf's head. The Sons of Horus wore sea green and took the Eye of [[Horus]], an eye with a downward stylus. The Black Legion wears black and gold, and its badge is the Eye of Horus set in the eight-pointed star of Chaos. Its warbands add the colours and icons of their gods, and its warriors wear trophies, daemonic marks and the symbols of their conquests. The [[Warmaster]]'s banners bear the black eye on a black ground, and the flagship of the Legion, the [[Vengeful Spirit]], once the flagship of Horus himself, is black.
