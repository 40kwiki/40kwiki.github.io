An abhuman is a member of one of the stable variant strains of humanity recognised by the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Abhumans are descended from human colonists of the [[Dark Age of Technology]] whose descendants adapted over thousands of years to the extreme conditions of the worlds they settled, or, in some cases, from humans deliberately altered for a purpose. Unlike [[mutant]]s, abhumans share a common and consistent physical form, breed true, and are no more prone to further mutation than other humans. For that reason the [[Administratum]] records them as strains of mankind rather than as deviants.

By the [[41st millennium]] the [[Adeptus Terra]] had recognised seventy-three abhuman strains, of which forty-six had been declared extinct and a further twelve were close to being so declared for lack of contact. The two best known are the Ogryns, huge and immensely strong, and the Ratlings, small and quick, both of which serve in large numbers in the [[Astra Militarum]]. The Squats of the galactic core were long counted among the abhumans before they were believed destroyed; they have since returned as the [[Leagues of Votann]]. The horned Beastmen served the Imperium during the [[Great Crusade]] but are now proscribed.

Abhumans occupy an uneasy place in Imperial society. Those that hold citizenship are indoctrinated in the [[Imperial Cult]], watched by [[Commissar]]s and priests, and widely distrusted, especially by puritan members of the [[Inquisition]] and by the devout, who see little difference between abhuman and mutant.

Origins and status

During the [[Dark Age of Technology]] (c. M15–M25) humanity colonised worlds of every kind, many of them hostile. When the [[Age of Strife]] (c. M25–M30) cut those colonies off from [[Terra]], some populations adapted physically to their worlds over many generations: to high gravity, to extreme cold or heat, to darkness or to scarce resources. When the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30–005.M31) rediscovered them, the Imperium had to decide whether they were human.

Under the secular [[Imperial Truth]] of the crusade, the answer was pragmatic. Stable strains that could serve were enrolled in the [[Imperial Army]], even ones as divergent as the Beastmen. After the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) and the rise of the Imperial Cult, attitudes hardened. Only the less divergent strains, chiefly Ogryns and Ratlings, retained full citizenship; others were outlawed or left to themselves.

Strains

Ogryns

Ogryns (Homo sapiens gigantus) are abhumans of great size and strength, often standing twice the height of an ordinary man and weighing several times as much. They evolved on high-gravity, low-temperature worlds. Ogryns are unusually stupid for a human strain, but they are unquestionably loyal once they are introduced to the faith of the Imperial Cult. They are claustrophobic and therefore hard to transport. In the [[Astra Militarum]] they serve as shock troops and bodyguards, commonly armed with the Ripper Gun, a weapon simple enough for them to use, or with a club when there is no time to teach them. Ogryns led by "Bone 'eads" have undergone the Biochemical Ogryn Neural Enhancement, or BONE treatment, which raises their intellect to nearly human standards. Others serve as Bullgryns, heavily armoured with shields and maces.

Ratlings

Ratlings (Homo sapiens minimus) are abhumans of small stature, who are said to have adapted to life in the tunnel systems of worlds with hostile surfaces. Imperial records describe them as loud, hungry and light-fingered, and as superb marksmen. In the Astra Militarum they serve chiefly as snipers and as cooks, and commissars report that petty theft and gambling rise wherever Ratlings are attached to a regiment. One named Ratling homeworld, Ornsworld, was attacked by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] in the [[Gothic War]].

Squats

Main article: [[Leagues of Votann]]

The Squats (Homo sapiens rotundus) of older Imperial records were short, stocky and immensely tough abhumans from high-gravity mining worlds near the galactic core. They were talented engineers, and held long grudges against the Orks. Imperial histories record that their worlds briefly seceded from the Imperium during the [[Age of Apostasy]] and later returned as autonomous but loyal worlds, and that the [[Tyranids]] attacked their homeworlds in the [[41st millennium]] and nearly drove them extinct. In the [[Era Indomitus]], a starfaring civilisation of the galactic core, the [[Leagues of Votann]], whose people call themselves the Kin, made itself known to the Imperium. Its records trace the Kin to mining expeditions sent out from ancient Terra, and Imperial scholars now regard the old Squat records as fragmentary accounts of the same people. Whether the Kin are abhumans or xenos remains disputed in Imperial law.

Beastmen

Beastmen (Homo sapiens variatus) are horned, hoofed and heavily furred abhumans, resembling humanoid goats or rams. During the Great Crusade they were recruited into the Imperial Army, where they fought under packmasters of their own kind. After the [[Horus Heresy]], puritan elements of the [[Inquisition]] convinced the Administratum that Beastmen could not be so far removed from the human form without being tainted by [[Chaos]], and "pureblood" Beastmen no longer serve the Imperium; overt mutants now serve only in the forces of Chaos. Some lesser variants of the strain, less greatly changed, are still allowed in the Imperial armed forces as cannon fodder and expendable assault troops.

Other strains

The [[Administratum]] register lists many lesser strains, among them the Nightsiders of sunless worlds, who live in near or complete darkness and may have enlarged or vestigial eyes; the Felinids; the Troths; the Longshanks; and the Pelagers of ocean worlds. Some scholars also count the giant furnace-workers of House Goliath on [[Necromunda]] as a strain. The [[Navigator]]s of the [[Navis Nobilite]] are a sanctioned human mutation: because the Navigator gene is hereditary, their existence is tolerated by an edict issued by the Emperor Himself at the time of the Unification Wars.

Military service

Abhumans who are Imperial citizens serve in the [[Militarum Auxilla]], the auxiliary branch of the [[Astra Militarum]] for specialist and abhuman troops. Ogryn and Ratling units are attached to human regiments as required. Abhuman soldiers are indoctrinated in the [[Imperial Cult|Imperial Creed]], often taught to regard themselves as lesser, and watched closely by [[Commissar|Commissars]] and regimental priests. Many [[Planetary Governor|Imperial commanders]] nonetheless value them highly, and the loyalty of Ogryns in particular is proverbial.
