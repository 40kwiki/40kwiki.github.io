A Supreme Grand Master, also called the Chapter Lord, is the senior-most battle-brother of the [[Grey Knights]] [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapter]] and holds a rank equal to that of a [[Chapter Master]] in other Chapters. Within the Chapter his authority is complete. The same title is borne by [[Azrael]], master of the [[Dark Angels]].

Grey Knights

The Chapter is governed by a Chapter Council of its eight Grand Masters and the Supreme Grand Master, a custom laid down by [[Malcador the Sigillite]] when he founded the Inquisition and its eight Astartes recruits. The Supreme Grand Master can be appointed only with the unanimous consent of every Grand Master, which makes it hard for a reckless or unsuitable candidate to take the Chapter. Each Grand Master holds sway over one of the Chapter's secondary bodies, such as its fleet or Armoury, but his chief duty lies on the battlefield, where he takes charge of the campaigns too grave for a captain, most often when one of the greater daemons of the Conclave Diabolus is sighted.

The first Supreme Grand Master was [[Janus]], one of the eight Astartes Malcador brought before the Emperor at the end of the [[Horus Heresy]]. Malcador named him to lead the new Chapter on Titan before returning to [[Terra]].

Dark Angels

In the [[Dark Angels]] the title belongs to the Chapter's master. [[Azrael]] has held it since 939.M41, after serving as Grand Master of the [[Deathwing]] from 917.M41.
