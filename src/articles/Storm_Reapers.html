The Storm Reapers are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] composed wholly of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. They were raised in the [[Ultima Founding]] of c. 999.M41 from the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of the [[White Scars]], and are the first Primaris Chapter recorded as descended from [[Jaghatai Khan]]. Their home is the feral world of [[Jagun]], whose tribes are famous for their savagery.Imperial records give the Ultima Founding only as c. 999.M41; the Chapter's first campaigns are dated no more closely than the early 42nd millennium.

Imperial commentators say that the Storm Reapers fight as their forebears did, in sudden lightning strikes on an enemy's flank, but with a harder edge than the White Scars of [[Chogoris]]. The Chapter was commended for spearheading the Absolutis Crusade.

History

Founding

The Storm Reapers were created in the first years of the [[Era Indomitus]], when [[Roboute Guilliman]] ordered the [[Ultima Founding]] and the [[Primaris Space Marines]] of [[Belisarius Cawl]] filled the ranks of new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. They draw on a purer line of the Khan's blood than the older White Scars [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], and their recruits are taken from Jagun's tribes, who mark their bodies with ritual scars. The Chapter claimed Jagun as its home soon after its raising.

Brimstone Stampede

Soon after the [[Great Rift]] opened, the [[Daemon|Bloodthirster]] Xakros'Ka led a host later called the Brimstone Stampede out of the new Rift and killed trillions across the Carricus Sector. The Storm Reapers fought beside the 2nd Brotherhood of the [[Grey Knights]]: the Chapter drove lightning assaults into the flank of the daemonic horde while the Grey Knights struck at its heart and destroyed Xakros'Ka's physical form with its true name. The victory was bought at terrible cost.

War of Beasts

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

Two demi-companies of the Chapter joined a [[White Scars]] strike force sent to [[Vigilus]], the [[Hive world|hive world]] that guards the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], the vital link between the [[Imperium Sanctus]] and the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. They fought there against [[Xenos|xenos]] and [[Chaos]] in the War of Beasts, c. 001–025.M42.

Organisation and heraldry

The Chapter wears white armour with black backpacks and a black [[Aquila]] on the breastplate. Like the White Scars, its iconography is taken from the tribal art of its recruiting world: jagged lightning bolts are painted on armour and helms, echoing the scars cut into the flesh beneath. Company markings are borne on the left knee plate, in black with white lightning, rather than on the shoulder, and squad specialty is marked in black on the right pauldron. The Chapter badge is a black double-bladed battleaxe struck by two red lightning bolts, on a white field.

As an all-Primaris Chapter, the Storm Reapers have no [[Firstborn]] veterans in their ranks. Their doctrine is little recorded beyond the Chapter's habit of striking an enemy's flank at speed, as the [[White Scars]] have always done.

Notable members

No [[Chapter Master]] is named in Imperial records. The only battle-brother recorded by name is Brother Khol of the 4th Company, 5th Squad, a battleline Marine known from a pict-capture of the Chapter's livery.

Notable campaigns

  • The Absolutis Crusade, spearheaded by the Storm Reapers (M42; date not recorded).
  • The Brimstone Stampede, beside the [[Grey Knights]] (M42).
  • The [[War of Nightmares|War of Beasts]] at Vigilus (c. 001–025.M42).
