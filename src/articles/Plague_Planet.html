The Plague Planet is the [[Daemon world]] of the [[Mortarion|Daemon Prince Mortarion]] and the homeworld of the [[Death Guard]] Traitor Legion. It lies within the [[Eye of Terror]], where the warp is most closely bound to the [[Ruinous Powers]]. It is a world where disease is the normal condition of life, and its skies are shrouded in viridian clouds that carry contagion to any living thing not blessed by [[Nurgle]], the Plague Lord.

Imperial accounts describe the surface as a foetid globe of rot, pools, and endless decay. Those who land there are said to be changed by its air, and the Death Guard regard the planet as the source of their gifts. The Imperium has never recovered a survey of its surface.

Role in the Heresy

The world is tied to the [[Horus Heresy]], when the [[Death Guard]] under Mortarion turned to [[Chaos]] and withdrew into the Eye. Their fortress-world became the centre of a warband that fought the Imperium across the galaxy for ten thousand years of Imperial reckoning.
