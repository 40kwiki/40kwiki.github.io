Gudrun is the former capital world of the [[Helican Sub-sector]] of the [[Scarus Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It had been home to Humanity for about 3,500 standard years by the [[41st millennium]]. Its government is a feudal system of noble patronage, in which the great houses hold authority over their clients and the lesser worlds around them.

The most powerful houses of Gudrun, among them the Glaws, the Pariti and the Froigre, extend their influence beyond the planet into neighbouring star systems. Their rivalries have shaped the politics of the Helican Sub-sector for many generations. Administratum records list the planet's capital status as a matter of tradition rather than present power.

Society

Gudrun's nobility is supported by a large body of clients, retainers and tithe-paying farmers. Imperial census records describe its cities as walled and its roads as controlled by the houses that hold them. Offices of the [[Administratum]] exist on the world, but their authority is limited by the houses.
