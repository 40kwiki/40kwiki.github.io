The Legio Ignatum, the "Fire Wasps", is a Loyalist [[Titan Legion]] of the [[Collegia Titanica]], one of the military arms of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. It is based on [[Mars]] in the [[Segmentum Solar]], the centre of the [[Cult Mechanicus]].

History

The Legio is believed to have been founded during the [[Age of Strife]], before the [[Age of the Imperium]]. It is one of the three original Titan Legions of Mars, called the [[Triad Ferrum Morgulus]].

During the [[Horus Heresy]] the Legio broke away from the traitor [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kelbor-Hal]] and chose to fight for the [[Emperor]]. It was known for its loyalty and was often given the defence of places of great importance, above all the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Terra]].
