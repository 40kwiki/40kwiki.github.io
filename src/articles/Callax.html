Callax is a [[Daemon world]] of [[Slaanesh]] named after the city of Callax on the lost homeworld of [[Chemos]], which [[Fulgrim]] ruled in his youth. Slaanesh granted Callax to Fulgrim as his personal playground. The gift was a reward for Fulgrim's mortal wounding of the [[Ultramarines]] [[Primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] at the [[Battle of Thessala]] in 121.M31.

The world's position is unknown. It seems to lie within the [[Warp]], perhaps in the [[Realm of Chaos]] itself or in a [[Warp rift]] like the [[Eye of Terror]]. The [[Daemon]]s that dwell there do not need a [[Daemonhost|mortal host]] to act in the material realm. Many within the [[Emperor's Children]] and other [[Slaaneshi]] [[Chaos Cultists]] seek the world as the ultimate expression of their faith.

Nature of the world

Callax is a world of endless pleasure, which its visitors call the "Gardens of Hell". Its oceans are human blood one day and pomegranate juice the next, and its forests rise and fall like waves. [[Slaaneshi]] pilgrims flock to the world in search of Fulgrim, but those who make planetfall are forced to dance without rest. If they stop, they are torn apart by waiting [[Daemonette]]s. Callax is described as not a true planet, but a shadow of one cast upon the light of a dying sun, in what might be called the Callax System. Its oceans, forests and climate change without regard for physical law.

Fulgrim's rule

After the [[Horus Heresy]] the [[Emperor's Children]] lost their [[primarch]], Fulgrim. He was granted apotheosis by Slaanesh and took the form of a [[Daemon Primarch]], a serpentine shape with four arms and wings. His new form resembled that of the [[Laer]] [[xenos]] whom Fulgrim and his Legion exterminated as he fell to Chaos.

Bored by the pedestrian excesses of his Legion during the [[Legion Wars]], Fulgrim withdrew to a [[Slaaneshi]] [[Daemon world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]]. He renamed it Callax after the capital of his lost homeworld. He spent Terran centuries shaping it into a reflection of Chemos. He then re-created the circumstances of his arrival on Chemos and his drive to rule it. He now spends most of his time fighting the old battles that unified his homeworld under his leadership, trying different strategies in search of a perfect victory that would rewrite his history. When truly bored he sometimes lets the enemy win.

The [[Inquisition]]'s [[Ordo Malleus]] and the scattered warbands of the Emperor's Children seek the world still. Only a handful have found it, some invited by its master and some not. Devotees of Slaanesh claim that Fulgrim oversees extremes of sensation on Callax that no mortal can imagine. [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] reached Fulgrim by trading an unblemished [[psyker]] from [[Pythos]], taken during the [[Pandorax Campaign]], for the favour of Slaanesh.
