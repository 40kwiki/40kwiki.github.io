Callax is a [[Daemon world]] of [[Slaanesh]] named after the city of Callax on the lost homeworld of [[Chemos]], which [[Fulgrim]] ruled in his youth. Slaanesh granted Callax to Fulgrim as his personal playground. The gift was a reward for Fulgrim's mortal wounding of the [[Ultramarines]] [[Primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] at the [[Battle of Thessala]] in 121.M31.

The world's position is unknown. It seems to lie within the [[Warp]], perhaps in the [[Realm of Chaos]] itself or in a [[Warp rift]] like the [[Eye of Terror]]. The [[Daemon]]s that dwell there do not need a [[Daemonhost|mortal host]] to act in the material realm. Many within the [[Emperor's Children]] and other [[Slaaneshi]] [[Chaos Cultists]] seek the world as the ultimate expression of their faith.

Nature of the world

Callax is a world of endless pleasure, which its visitors call the "Gardens of Hell". Its oceans shift between human blood and pomegranate juice, and its forests rise and fall like waves. Pilgrims who reach its surface are compelled to dance without rest. If they stop, they are torn apart by waiting [[Daemonette]]s. Some scholars note that Callax is less a planet than a shadow cast upon a dying sun, and so the term "Callax System" is used with care.

Fulgrim's rule

After the [[Horus Heresy]] the [[Emperor's Children]] lost their [[primarch]], Fulgrim. He was granted apotheosis by the Ruinous Powers and took the form of a [[Daemon Primarch]], with four arms and wings. His new form resembled that of the [[Laer]] [[xenos]] whom Fulgrim and his Legion exterminated as he fell to Chaos.

Bored by the failings of his Legion during the [[Legion Wars]], Fulgrim withdrew to a [[Slaaneshi]] [[Daemon world]] in the [[Eye of Terror]]. He named it Callax. He spent centuries shaping it into a reflection of Chemos. He then re-enacted the events that brought him to power, and he now fights the old battles that unified his homeworld, searching for a perfect victory. At times he lets his enemies win.

The [[Inquisition]]'s [[Ordo Malleus]] and the scattered warbands of the Emperor's Children have sought the world for millennia. Few have found it, and some were invited by its master. [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] reached Fulgrim by trading an unblemished [[psyker]] from [[Pythos]], taken during the [[Pandorax Campaign]], for the favour of Slaanesh.
