Vashtorr the Arkifane, called the Master of the Soul Forges and the God in the Machine, is a [[Daemon|greater daemon]] of the [[Warp]] who serves none of the four Chaos Gods. He is the maker of daemon engines and the forger of the pacts that bind daemons into them. In the [[Era Indomitus]] he allied with the Warmaster [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and built the fleet of daemon-wrought voidships that the Imperium named the [[Arks of Omen]]. With them the two hunted the scattered fragments of an ancient artefact called the Key. At the end of that campaign Vashtorr had the Key in his hands.

The [[Ordo Malleus]] counts him among the most dangerous powers of the age. He is not a servant of a god but, by his own account, a god in the making. He does not lie, and he has said plainly what he means to do.

Nature and origins

Nothing certain is known of Vashtorr's beginning. He is older than the Imperium, and possibly older than humanity's spread among the stars. The records of the [[Inquisition]] hold several theories, none proven.

  • That he was born of the arms races of the ancient [[Milky Way|galaxy]], from the hatred and ingenuity poured into weapons of war over countless millennia.
  • That he grew strong in the [[Dark Age of Technology]], when humanity's craft was at its height and its machines were turned against it.
  • That he fed on [[War in Heaven|the war in heaven]] and on the [[Necrons]]' biotransference, the moment when a whole species gave up its flesh for bodies of metal.
  • That he was present, in some form, at the making of the [[Primarch|primarchs]], at the cursed Twenty-First Founding, or at the creation of the [[Primaris Space Marines]]. Imperial scholars hold this last theory to be heresy, and the Office of the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] has forbidden it.

What these theories share is the belief that Vashtorr is a power of invention without conscience: the joy of making a thing, cut loose from any question of what it is for. Every mind that has made a weapon without caring whom it would kill is said to have fed him. This is why the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] regards him with a particular dread. Its own hunger for knowledge is the hunger he answers.

The Soul Forges

Vashtorr's domain in the Warp is the Soul Forges, a realm of furnaces and anvils that no mortal has described and lived. There he makes the bodies of [[Daemon|daemon]] engines and binds daemons into them. The Soul Grinders that serve the [[Chaos|Chaos Gods]], the Calamity Engines and the Infernal Bombards are said to come from his forges, and many of the daemon engines of the [[Traitor Legions]] carry spirits he has bound.

He works by pacts. A daemon that wants a body of iron, or a mortal who wants a weapon, comes to him and makes a bargain, and he keeps it to the letter. Daemons that he has bound bear a collar or a brand that marks their indenture to him, and some Imperial witnesses claim to have seen the mark on engines fighting for other powers. The gods of Chaos tolerate him because all four of them buy from him. For this the [[Ordo Malleus]] calls him the arms dealer of the Great Game.

Unlike most of his kind, Vashtorr does not lie. He sees no need to. He states the terms of a bargain and his own aims openly, and he trusts that the greed of those he deals with will do the rest.

The Cult of the Arkifane

Among mortals, Vashtorr is worshipped by the Cult of the Arkifane, whose members call themselves the Cogs of Vashtorr. They are drawn from the [[Dark Mechanicum]], from renegade forge crews and from [[Chaos Space Marines|Traitor Astartes]] whose [[Techmarine|Techmarines]] and [[Warpsmith|Warpsmiths]] have gone over to him. Many of them have replaced their flesh with daemon-touched machinery. The cult fought beside him in person during the [[Arks of Omen]].

The hereteks of the Dark Mechanicum treat him as a patron of the forge, and his rise has drawn [[Traitor|traitor]] magi together under him who were rivals before. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] regards this as the gravest threat from the traitor priesthood since the [[Schism of Mars]].

History

Alliance with the Warmaster

Vashtorr learned, over the long course of mortal history, that an artefact of great power had been broken and its pieces scattered across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. This was the Key. According to a prophecy he believed, whoever reassembled it could open a Lock hidden somewhere in the galaxy and take up what lay behind it, a power that could change the fate of all things.

After the [[War of Nightmares]] on [[Vigilus]], Vashtorr came to Abaddon with an offer. He would build the Warmaster a fleet, and the fleet would find the fragments of the Key. Abaddon distrusted him, as he distrusts all daemons, but accepted when Vashtorr told him that the Key could be used to break the Imperium and win the Long War.

Vashtorr's own aim was different, and he did not hide it. He meant to use the Key to raise himself to the rank of the four Chaos Gods, and to become a fifth power of the Warp. The Ordo Malleus records that Abaddon knew this and accepted the risk.

The Arks of Omen

Main article: [[Arks of Omen]]

The Arks were built by Vashtorr and the hereteks of the Dark Mechanicum from captured and modified space hulks, filled with daemon engines and Warp-forged weapons. Vashtorr gave them to Abaddon, who handed them out to officers of the [[Black Legion]] and to the Chaos lords who swore to aid him. The Arks struck across the Imperium Nihilus in search of the fragments, and wherever one appeared a world fell or burned.

One fragment was held aboard the Rock, the fortress-monastery of the [[Dark Angels]]. Abaddon and Vashtorr attacked it, and the Arkifane came in person at the head of the Cogs of Vashtorr. The attack failed. The daemon prince [[Be'lakor]], who hates Vashtorr, interfered with it, and the Dark Angels drove the invaders off. Under Supreme Grand Master [[Azrael]] the Chapter and its successors pursued the Arkifane into the Somnium Stars.

Wyrmwood

Main article: [[Wyrmwood]]

In the Somnium Stars the Dark Angels found Vashtorr's base, the rogue world called Wyrmwood, a planet made into a machine. Some Dark Angels sources claim that it was built from the ruins of [[Caliban]], the Chapter's lost homeworld; the Chapter has neither confirmed nor denied this. Vashtorr had gathered the other fragments of the Key there.

The pursuit was a trap. Abaddon and Vashtorr had drawn the Dark Angels to Wyrmwood, and when the Chapter landed it met Chaos Space Marines and daemons in strength. World Eaters allied to Vashtorr, and cultists who gave their lives in the rite, summoned the Daemon Primarch [[Angron]] to the world, and his coming broke the Imperial assault. While the Imperials fought for their lives, Vashtorr went aboard the Rock and took the fragment he had failed to seize before. Then the fleets of Abaddon and Vashtorr were taken into the Warp, the Key complete.

The fate of the Key since then is uncertain. Imperial reports name it in some accounts as a weapon of the Arkifane's making, the Dissonance Engine; others treat the two as different things. The Ordo Malleus believes that Abaddon's flagship, the Vengeful Spirit, has since entered the [[Webway]] in search of the Lock.The relationship between the Key and the device some records call the Dissonance Engine is not settled in the surviving reports. This article follows the Ordo Malleus in treating the Key as the object Vashtorr holds.

The Pariah Nexus

Main article: [[Pariah Nexus]]

Vashtorr next appeared in the war for the [[Nephilim Sector]], the region of the [[Pariah Nexus]] that the [[Necrons]] had cut off from the Warp. From Wyrmwood he worked on the mind of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] magos Ghelf, who had studied the Necron pylons of the region. Under his influence Ghelf activated a device known as Shivarik's Constellation. It destroyed a great Necron fleet, and the feedback killed Ghelf. The pylon at Skahren was turned into an empyric amplifier, and Vashtorr emerged from it with Wyrmwood and tore a rift into [[Realspace|realspace]], letting his forces into a region where no [[Daemon|daemon]] should have been able to walk.

Why he sought the Nephilim Sector is not known. The [[Ordo Malleus]] suspects that a place where the Warp has been silenced is, to a maker, a thing to be taken apart and understood.

Character and abilities

Witnesses describe Vashtorr as a giant of iron and flame, horned, with one arm ending in a great mechanised claw and a body worked through with machinery that moves of its own will. He is a fighter of great power, but his true strength is his craft. It is said that he can unmake a machine with a touch, or wake the spirit in a weapon and turn it on its bearer. Tech-priests who have stood near him report that their own augmetics answered to him rather than to them.

He is patient and does not hurry. Those who have dealt with him describe him as courteous and precise, and as caring for nothing but the work. He does not seem to hate the Imperium as the [[Chaos|Chaos Gods]] do. He sees it, as he sees everything, as material.

He has many enemies among his own kind. [[Be'lakor]], the first [[Daemon|daemon prince]], resents his independence and his ambition, and the two have worked against each other for millennia. The [[Ordo Malleus]] believes that Be'lakor's intervention at the Rock was meant to keep the Key from Vashtorr, not to help the Imperium.

Wargear

  • The Hammer of the Soul Forges, the great hammer with which he forges [[Daemon|daemon]] engines and breaks the machines of his enemies.
  • His mechanised claw, with which he is said to tear apart armour and pull the bound spirits out of machines.
  • The Key, the reassembled artefact taken at the end of the [[Arks of Omen]].

Legacy

For the Imperium, the Arkifane is a new kind of danger: a [[Daemon|daemon]] who does not want worship for its own sake, who trades with all sides and who holds an artefact even the [[Warmaster]] wanted. The [[Inquisition]]'s records of him are sealed. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] has declared his name anathema, and any tech-priest found to have studied his works is put to death.

His story is far from over. The Key is still in his hands, the Lock has not been found, and his [[Forge world|forge-world]] still moves through the dark.
