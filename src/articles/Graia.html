Graia is a major [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[forge world]] in the Graia System of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]]. It is known as the "Crown of Miracles" for its ring of void stations, which circles the planet in geosynchronous orbits and is said to be able to cross interstellar distances under its own power.

The world is the home of [[Legio Astraman]], a Loyalist [[Titan Legion]]. Its warriors live on the void stations, and the forges of the planet supply Titans and war machines to the Imperium. Graia is one of the few forge worlds of the Segmentum Tempestus that is still fully in Mechanicus hands.

History

The [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) split the [[Forge world|forge worlds]]. Graia remained loyal, as did most of the forge worlds in the outer [[Segmentum|segmenta]]. Its records of the Heresy are held in the [[Mechanicus]] archives on [[Mars]], and they describe the world's loyalty as unbroken.

The opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 cut Graia off from many of its daughter worlds. The forge's supply lines were reduced, but the void stations kept it in contact with the Imperium. Its exports to the Cadian front were disrupted in the same years.The effects of the Great Rift on Graia are reconstructed from Mechanicus records and are not dated precisely.
