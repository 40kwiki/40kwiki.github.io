A Chaplain is a priest-warrior of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He keeps his Chapter's rites, guards its spiritual purity, teaches its warriors their traditions and leads them in battle with prayer and example. A Chaplain is attached to each company of a Chapter that follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. The Chapter's [[Reclusiam]], its central shrine, is kept by a senior Chaplain called the Reclusiarch, and the Chapter's spiritual head is the Master of Sanctity, though the two offices are sometimes held by one warrior.

The office was created at the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31, in the early years of the [[Great Crusade]]. When the Emperor's Decree Absolute ordered the Legions to abolish their [[Librarian|Librarius]], the "Chaplain Edict" required every Legion to appoint chaplains, on the model of the [[Word Bearers]], to keep its warriors loyal to the [[Imperial Truth]]. Imperial accounts attribute the edict to the Emperor himself, while others credit [[Malcador the Sigillite]], who issued it through the [[Council of Terra]] in his name. In the [[Horus Heresy]] the Word Bearers' own chaplains proved to be chief agents of [[Chaos]], but the office endured in the loyal Chapters.

Chaplains are recognised by their black armour, skull-faced helms, the [[Crozius Arcanum]] they carry as both mace and badge of office, and the [[Rosarius]], a protective amulet that marks their rank. Chapters differ on the Emperor's divinity: many revere him as a god, while others hold him to be the supreme example of what a man can become. Most keep their own ancient traditions and pay little heed to the priests of the [[Imperial Cult]].

History

Origins in the Word Bearers

See also: [[Word Bearers]]

The first chaplains of the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] served in the XVII Legion, the [[Word Bearers]], during the [[Great Crusade]]. [[Lorgar]] raised them to preach his Legion's worship of the Emperor and to lead the conversion of conquered worlds; the most prominent of them was the First Chaplain [[Erebus]]. After the Emperor punished the Legion's religious excesses by razing [[Monarchia]] on [[Khur]], the Word Bearers turned in secret to the [[Chaos|Ruinous Powers]], and their chaplains became the first preachers of the heresy that would later corrupt [[Horus]].Khur was brought into Compliance in 903.M30. Monarchia was destroyed some sixty years later, in 964.M30 by the wiki's reckoning (see [[Word Bearers]]).

The Council of Nikaea

Main article: [[Council of Nikaea]]

At the Council of Nikaea in 001.M31 the Emperor ruled on the place of psykers and ordered that every Legion abolish its [[Librarian|Librarius]]. The Chaplain Edict that followed required each Legion to appoint officers who would see to the spiritual well-being of its warriors, enforce the ban on psychic practice and keep them faithful to the Imperial Truth. They were to be chosen from the warriors most steadfast in duty and loyalty to their primarch and the Emperor. Most primarchs complied, but some appointed no chaplains at all and only claimed they had, a deceit that became plain once the Heresy broke out.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

During the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the Word Bearers' chaplains, Erebus chief among them, served the cause of Chaos, and Erebus is held by most Imperial records to have arranged the wounding of Horus on [[Davin]]. In the traitor Legion the Chaplain Edict had given the Word Bearers a place in the councils of every Legion.

The Codex Astartes

Main article: [[Codex Astartes]]

In a Chapter that follows the [[Codex Astartes]], every one of the ten companies has its own Chaplain, who is second to the company captain in rank, and its own chapel. The Master of Sanctity, the Chapter's chief Chaplain, usually ranks below only the [[Chapter Master]]. The Reclusiarch trains new Chaplains, while the Master of Sanctity assigns the Chaplains to their companies. The Codex lists the Chaplain's standard wargear, and some Chapters add duties of their own.

Since the [[Ultima Founding]] of 999.M41, [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] Chaplains have served alongside their Firstborn brothers.

Duties

A Chaplain is the spiritual leader of his company or Chapter. His duties are religious and martial:

  • Rites. He administers the Chapter's rituals and ceremonies of initiation, vindication and redemption.
  • Instruction. He instils the values and beliefs of the Chapter in its battle-brothers and guards them against heresy and false pride.
  • Chapter duties. In the [[Blood Angels]] and their successors, the Chaplains seek out signs of the Black Rage and lead the [[Death Company]] into battle.
  • Battle. He fights in the front rank, chanting the liturgies of battle and striking with his crozius, and urges his brothers to greater devotion.

Insignia and wargear

A Chaplain's armour is black by tradition and by the Codex Astartes, though many Chapters add their own colours and insignia. His helm is shaped as a skull, a death-mask that evokes the visage of the Emperor and reminds all who see it of mortality. His two marks of office are:

  • the [[Crozius Arcanum]], a power weapon in the form of a mace, the symbol of his office and his chosen weapon of war;
  • the [[Rosarius]], an amulet that conceals a personal shield generator, often given to Chaplains by cardinals and other high priests of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] as a sign of the shared faith of Astartes and Ecclesiarchy.

Senior ranks and variants

Reclusiarch and Master of Sanctity

The Reclusiarch cares for the Chapter's Reclusiam and trains new Chaplains. Above him stands the Master of Sanctity, the chief Chaplain and spiritual head of the Chapter, whose rank is usually subordinate only to that of the [[Chapter Master]]. The Master of Sanctity of the [[Ultramarines]], [[Ortan Cassius]], is close to four hundred years old and fought beside [[Marneus Calgar]] against [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] in the [[Tyrannic Wars|First Tyrannic War]].

Chapter variants

  • The [[Dark Angels]] keep Interrogator-Chaplains, members of the secret Inner Circle whose rank stands above the company level. They hunt the [[Fallen Angels|Fallen]] and force them to repent before they are executed.
  • The [[Space Wolves]] have no Chaplains but Wolf Priests, who combine the duties of Chaplain and Apothecary; the senior of them is [[Ulrik the Slayer]], the Wolf High Priest.
  • The [[Iron Hands]] have no Chaplains but Iron Fathers, who combine the duties of Chaplain, Apothecary and Techmarine.
  • The [[Black Templars]] are among the few Chapters that acknowledge the doctrines of the Ecclesiarchy. Their Reclusiarch Grimaldus is honoured as the Hero of Helsreach for the defence of the Temple of the Emperor Ascendant in 998.M41 during the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]].
  • The [[Blood Angels]] Chaplain Lemartes, himself afflicted by the Black Rage but able to master it, leads the Death Company in battle.
