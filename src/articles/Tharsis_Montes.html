The Tharsis Montes are the three great volcanoes, Arsia Mons, Pavonis Mons and Ascraeus Mons, that stand in a line across the Tharsis uplands of [[Mars]]. The name is often used for the whole volcanic region around them, which also contains [[Olympus Mons]], the capital of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Tharsis holds many of the oldest and most honoured forge temples on the planet, and mastery of a Tharsis forge is still among the highest honours of the priesthood.

During the [[Great Crusade]] the forges of Tharsis made much of the [[Power armour|power armour]] and the bolters of the [[Space Marine Legions]], and the fortresses of several [[Titan Legions]] stood among its peaks. In the [[Schism of Mars]] of 005–006.M31 the region became the main battlefield of the Martian civil war.

Geography

Tharsis is a vast volcanic plateau raised high above the Martian datum. The three Tharsis Montes rise from it in a line running from south-west to north-east, with Olympus Mons further to the north-west. To the east the land falls away into the canyons of the [[Noctis Labyrinthus]] and the Valles Marineris. The plains between the volcanoes are crossed by fault valleys, called fossae, many of which have been built over by assembly yards and refineries.

Forges and fortresses

A Mechanicum chart of the early 31st millennium names the following domains in Tharsis:

  • Olympus Mons, seat of the [[Fabricator-General]], then [[Kelbor-Hal]].
  • Ascraeus Chasmata, on Ascraeus Mons, fortress of the [[Legio Tempestus]].
  • Pavonis Mons, fortress of the [[Legio Mortis]], which turned traitor in 005.M31.
  • Arsia Chasmata, near Arsia Mons, fortress of the Knights of [[House Taranis]].
  • The Magma City, on the slopes of Arsia Mons, domain of the forge master [[Koriel Zeth]].
  • Mondus Occulam, in the north-east, domain of [[Zagreus Kane]], then Fabricator Locum.
  • Mondus Gamma, on the edge of the Noctis Labyrinthus, domain of the adept Lukas Chrom.
  • The Olympica Fossae assembly yards, north of the volcanoes.

History

Before the Imperium

The forges of Tharsis are among the oldest on Mars. Mechanicus records trace several of them to the [[Dark Age of Technology]], when the region's mineral wealth and stable rock made it the centre of Martian industry. During the [[Age of Strife]] it was the heartland of the Mechanicum, and the Fabricator-General ruled from Olympus Mons on its edge.The founding dates of the oldest forges are not recorded and are estimated from the Mechanicum's chronology.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

After the [[Treaty of Olympus]] of 739.M30, the forges of Tharsis armed the Great Crusade. Forge masters such as Koriel Zeth and Zagreus Kane became powers in their own right, and the Titan Legions based in the region marched with the expedition fleets.

Schism of Mars

Main article: [[Schism of Mars]]

When Kelbor-Hal declared for [[Horus]] in 005.M31, a corrupting scrapcode spread across Tharsis and the forges turned on one another. The Legio Mortis marched from Pavonis Mons against the loyalist Legio Tempestus and Legio Ignatum, and the [[Titan Legions|Titans]] fought across the Tharsis plains. The Magma City of Koriel Zeth was destroyed and Zeth was killed. A force of [[Imperial Fists]] under [[Sigismund]] landed at Mondus Occulam to recover what it could before the forge was overrun. Zagreus Kane escaped and fought on in the deserts and catacombs.

Recovery

Loyalist forces retook Tharsis with the rest of Mars during the [[Great Scouring]], about 014–020.M31. Kane became Fabricator-General, and the forges of the region were purged, rebuilt and rededicated over the following century. Tharsis has remained the industrial centre of the planet ever since, and in the [[Era Indomitus]] its forges are among those working at full output for the [[Indomitus Crusade]].Dates of recovery estimated from the sequence of the Great Scouring.
