The Phalanx is the mobile fortress-monastery of the [[Imperial Fists]] and the largest starship ever built by human hands. Made in the [[Dark Age of Technology]] by unknown hands, it came to the Imperium with [[Rogal Dorn]] in 835.M30 and was the flagship of the Imperial Fists in the [[Great Crusade]]. Officially the Imperial Fists' home is [[Terra]], but the Chapter has lived aboard the Phalanx for its whole history, and the ship has spent most of the last ten thousand years in orbit above the Throneworld.

The ship was badly damaged in the [[Solar War]] and again in the daemonic assault of 999.M41, and by the end of the [[41st millennium]] its systems were failing. In the Era Indomitus it has been restored with technology given by the [[Adeptus Custodes]].

History

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

When the Emperor found [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] on [[Inwit]] in 835.M30, the [[Primarch|primarch]] had already discovered the ancient vessel adrift in space. He gave it to the Emperor, who returned it to him when he was appointed to command the [[Imperial Fists|VII Legion]]. The Emperor is recorded as travelling often aboard it in the Crusade. It was built to sustain a whole Legion.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

For much of the [[Horus Heresy]] the Phalanx stayed above [[Terra]] as Dorn's headquarters. When the [[Traitor|traitor]] armada advanced into the Sol System, Dorn moved his headquarters to the Bhab Bastion of the Imperial Palace. In the last stages of the [[Solar War]] he took personal command of the Phalanx to shatter the traitor armada emerging from the Warp near [[Luna]] and [[Mars]]. The [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancer]] Mersadie Oliton unwittingly let in the [[Daemon|daemon]] Samus, and its host nearly took the ship. Dorn was about to order the ship destroyed when Oliton took her own life, closing the gate by which the daemons had come. Dorn then gave the damaged ship to the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] admiral [[Niora Su-Kassen]], ordering her to take it to the outer reaches of the system, to save it and to wage war on the rear of the traitor armada.

Decay

By the [[32nd millennium]] the Phalanx was too large for a Chapter of a thousand warriors. Most of its docks stood empty, and because the Imperium could no longer make its ancient systems, even small damage was hard to repair. In the [[War of the Beast]] it was moved between Terra and [[Venus]] to keep it from the Ork moons.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In 999.M41, as [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Abaddon]]'s [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] began, the [[Iron Warriors]] Warsmith Shon'tu and the daemon prince [[Be'lakor]] tried to take the Phalanx and turn its guns on the [[Imperial Palace]]. The Third Company under Captain Tor Garadon defended it. The attackers' daemonic code took over much of the ship, and Garadon turned its guns on its own infected sections and drove it into the [[Warp]] to keep it from Terra. When Be'lakor opened a rift within it, the [[Legion of the Damned]] arrived and turned the battle. About a tenth of the ship was rendered unusable.

The Phalanx then sailed to [[Cadia]], where it destroyed the [[Blackstone Fortress]] Will of Eternity after the [[Space Wolves]] had brought down its shields. It carried many of the faithful who escaped the [[Fall of Cadia|fall of Cadia]] back to the [[Sol System]] and took up its old position above Terra.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After his return Roboute Guilliman ordered the Phalanx to stand guard over Terra. By the time the [[Indomitus Crusade]] began it was badly damaged, and the Shadowkeepers of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] opened one of their Dark Cells and gave the Imperial Fists secret technology to restore it. It now works at a level not seen in centuries.

Description

The Phalanx is a city in the void, its upper hull crowded with towers, bastions and weapon-batteries. In its strategium the Chapter keeps the Hand of Dorn, held to be the last remaining part of the body of [[Rogal Dorn]], before which members of the Chapter renew their vows. The ship is defended by the [[Imperial Fists]] and by the Auric Auxilia, a security force, and aspirants for the Chapter are gathered and trained in its Hall Martalis.

Much of the ship is old and failing. In the [[Great Crusade]] its walls displayed the battle honours of the Imperial Fists in a gallery that ran for kilometres, and its foredeck is large enough to dock a dozen cruisers.
