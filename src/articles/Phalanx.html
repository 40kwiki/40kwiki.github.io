The Phalanx is the mobile fortress-monastery of the [[Imperial Fists]] and the largest starship ever built by human hands. Made in the [[Dark Age of Technology]], it came to the Imperium with [[Rogal Dorn]] in 835.M30 and was Dorn's command throughout the [[Great Crusade]]. Officially the Imperial Fists' home is [[Terra]], but the Chapter has lived aboard the Phalanx for its whole history, and the ship has spent most of the last ten thousand years in orbit above the Throneworld.

The ship was badly damaged in the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31) and again in the daemonic assault of 999.M41, and by the end of the 41st millennium it was in deep decay. In the Era Indomitus it has been restored with technology given by the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and has left Terra to serve as the headquarters of a crusade fleet.

History

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

When the Emperor found Dorn on [[Inwit]] in 835.M30, the primarch had already restored the ancient vessel. He brought it to the Emperor, who returned it to him with command of the VII Legion. From the Phalanx Dorn, and at times the Emperor himself, directed many campaigns of the Crusade. Built to carry a whole Legion, it held fleets in its docks and the Legion's companies, armouries and archives in its spires.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

For most of the [[Horus Heresy]] the Phalanx stayed above Terra as Dorn's headquarters. When the traitor armada entered the Sol System in 013.M31, Dorn took personal command of it to strike the traitor fleets at [[Luna]] and [[Mars]]. The remembrancer Mersadie Oliton unwittingly let in the daemon Samus, and its host nearly took the ship. Dorn was about to order the ship destroyed when Oliton took her own life, closing the gate by which the daemons had come. Dorn then gave the damaged ship to the Imperial Army admiral Niora Su-Kassen, ordering her to hide it at the edge of the system and strike the enemy's rear.

Through the Siege it lay hidden in the rings of [[Saturn]] with other loyal ships. When the [[Astronomican]] was rekindled in the last hours of 014.M31, Su-Kassen and the Imperial Fists' Halbrecht took it as a sign and attacked the traitor fleet, hours before [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s fleet arrived. The ship ended the Siege a half-ruin.

Decay

By the 32nd millennium the Phalanx was too large for a Chapter of a thousand warriors. Most of its docks stood empty, and because the Imperium could no longer make its ancient systems, even small damage was hard to repair. In the [[War of the Beast]] it was moved between Terra and [[Venus]] to keep it from the Ork moons. It sailed again in 546.M32, when [[Maximus Thane]] took the restored Imperial Fists on their crusade from it.

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

Main article: [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]]

In 999.M41, as Abaddon's [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] began, the Iron Warriors Warsmith Shon'tu and the daemon prince [[Be'lakor]] tried to take the Phalanx and turn its guns on the [[Imperial Palace]]. The Third Company under Captain Tor Garadon defended it. The attackers' daemonic code took over much of the ship, and Garadon turned its guns on its own infected sections and drove it into the [[Warp]] to keep it from Terra. When Be'lakor opened a rift within it, the Legion of the Damned arrived and turned the battle. About a tenth of the ship was destroyed.

The Phalanx then sailed to [[Cadia]], where it destroyed the [[Blackstone Fortress]] Will of Eternity after the [[Space Wolves]] had brought down its shields. When [[Fall of Cadia|Cadia fell]] in 999.M41, it led the evacuation fleet out of the system.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After his return Roboute Guilliman ordered the Phalanx to stand guard over Terra, wrapped in repair-cradles. In a rare gesture, the Shadowkeepers of the Adeptus Custodes opened one of their Dark Cells and gave the Imperial Fists sealed technology to restore it, and it now works at a level not seen for millennia. In the [[Fourth Tyrannic War]] of the Era Indomitus it became the headquarters of the [[Lord Commander Solar]] Arcadian Leontus and left Terra for the Bastior sector.

Description

The Phalanx is a city in the void, its upper hull crowded with towers, bastions and weapon-batteries. In its reliquary the Chapter keeps the bones of Rogal Dorn's hand, on which the name of every [[Chapter Master]] is carved. The ship is defended by the Imperial Fists and by the Auric Auxilia, a mortal guard, and by the Phalanx Warders, a company trained to fight behind boarding shields. Aspirants spend six months aboard learning [[Low Gothic]] before their trials.
