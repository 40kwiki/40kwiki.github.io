Hetrax is a [[Knight world]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], ruled by [[House Cadmus]]. The house pilots [[Imperial Knight]]s and is allied with the [[Questor Imperialis]], one of the Knight-house allegiances recorded in the Imperial lists.

Hetrax is among the knight worlds listed beside [[Alaric Prime]], [[Voltoris]], [[Kimdaria]] and [[Chrysis]] in the Imperial Knight lists. Its knights serve the Imperium in the Questor Imperialis's campaigns.

House Cadmus

House Cadmus is one of the noble houses of Hetrax. Its knights are recorded in the lists of the Questor Imperialis, and the house is named as a Hetrax house in the Knight records. The sources do not describe its internal structure in any detail.

Knights and the Questor Imperialis

The Questor Imperialis is one of the Knight allegiances that hold the knight worlds. Its houses swear to the Imperium's cause and keep their own traditions of arms. Hetrax's knights are counted among the house forces that the Questor Imperialis can call on in a campaign, though the sources give no fixed number.
