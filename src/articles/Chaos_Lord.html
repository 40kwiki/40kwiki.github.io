A Chaos Lord is one of the most powerful of the [[Champions of Chaos]]. He usually commands a [[Chaos Space Marine]] warband and the other [[forces of Chaos]] that serve it. A Chaos Lord is usually, though not always, a [[Heretic Astartes]].

Chaos Lords are not necessarily those who rose properly through a hierarchy. They are often those most blessed by the [[Chaos Gods]], or the most powerful or charismatic among the forces of Chaos. The greatest Champions become Chaos Lords, and the [[Ruinous Powers]] often reward them with grotesque [[mutations]] and physical alterations to further their cause.

Authority

A Chaos Lord can bind the other forces of Chaos to his will. This position is dangerous. Advancement among the forces of Chaos is commonly won by killing another Chaos Lord or Champion. Some Chaos Lords lead through brute strength, some through cunning, and some are maniacs bent only on self-glorification. Others are true believers who serve the [[Dark Gods]] with zeal.

Many were once noble [[Chapter Master]]s or [[captain]]s of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. Long years of unremitting war have twisted their souls beyond recovery. A Chaos Lord is ultimately a tyrannical warrior-king who seeks to bring whole star systems low in the name of his patron deities. His ambition is merciless and his pride fierce.

It takes exceptional will and personal magnetism to unite the warbands of Chaos Space Marines who lurk in the [[Warp]]. Even then a would-be warlord cannot be everywhere at once. Only those marked for greatness by the gods can count on the support of Heretic Astartes, mortals and [[Daemon]]s alike.

Appearance and wargear

All Chaos Lords are imposing. Chaos Space Marines have genetically enhanced physiques that the Warp has made more impressive still. Their wargear is often as outlandish as their bodies. A Chaos Lord may fight with a massive [[chainaxe]], blast foes with an ancient [[combi-weapon]], or split enemy vehicles with a screaming [[Daemon Weapon]].

Chaos Lords usually lead from the front, whatever their patron. Those of [[Khorne]] lead by example, in gore-stained armour, and their followers are lost to reason in their thirst for blood. Those of [[Tzeentch]] command almost limitless sorcerous power and outguess their foes. Their bodies often display arcane mutations, and their plans are so labyrinthine that only the insane may begin to understand them. Those of [[Nurgle]] are bloated, filth-encrusted hulks who devote their lives to spreading disease across the stars. Those of [[Slaanesh]] bear strange sensory organs that let them savour the stimuli of battle, and their speed of mind lends them blurring swiftness in the fight.

Sorcerer Lords

A Sorcerer Lord is a Chaos Lord who also wields [[psychic]] power as a [[Chaos Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. Most are devotees of [[Tzeentch]], the Changer of Ways and god of change and sorcery.
