Vergoyz Alphic is a world where the Sautekh dynasty of the [[Necrons]] was held by Imperial forces in late M42. [[Magos-Cantic]] Lundkest and her [[Skitarii]] held the Sautekh there, while Inquisitor Draxus destroyed a pylon on the world.

After the battle [[Imotekh the Stormlord]] withdrew from the field to fortify his territory. Draxus later told [[Belisarius Cawl]] that the Necron Szeras had sent [[Deathmark]] assassins after him.
