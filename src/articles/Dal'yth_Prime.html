Dal'yth Prime is the principal world of the Dal'yth Sept of the [[T'au Empire]]. It was settled during the [[First Sphere Expansion]] and became the centre of trade and commerce for the T'au. Its culture is dominated by merchants of the [[Water Caste]], and it is counted among the Nineteen Wonders of the T'au Empire.

The world has been invaded several times, most notably at the climax of the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]]. In the present age it is threatened by Ork forces fighting in the [[War of Dakka]].

Settlement and landscape

Before the T'au came, Dal'yth Prime was a wild world of deep blue foliage and slithering, segmented beasts. The first expeditions found the native life hostile. The T'au tamed it over the course of the First Sphere Expansion and brought the world up to prime colony standards.

Most of the surface is covered by a net of hexagonal cities and sub-cities. Each is linked to its neighbours by perfectly regular transit tubeways. Clean white magnorail trains carry the population between them without stopping. Landscaped hills and enormous hexagonal reservoirs are scattered across the land. From orbit the world looks as though it was laid out by a scientist of the [[Earth Caste]].

Trade and technology

The world's Water Caste merchants set up embassies and merchant guilds in its great markets. These attracted prosperous trade agreements with other septs and with alien species. The most notable was the agreement with the [[Demiurg]]'s Syrr'Tok Brotherhood. The T'au bartered for Ion Technology, which later appeared in starships, battlesuits, vehicle weapons and infantry weapons.

Notable residents and wars

Dal'yth Prime was the birthplace of [[Commander Puretide]], regarded by much of the T'au Empire as the greatest military mind of the [[Fire Caste]]. After being badly wounded by raiders, he retired to Mount Kan'ji on his homeworld. He is credited with teaching several of the Fire Caste's greatest commanders, among them [[Commander Farsight]], [[Commander Shadowsun]] and [[Shas'O Kais]].

The world was endangered at the climax of the Damocles Gulf Crusade, in 742–745.M41. It is now at risk from Orks fighting in the War of Dakka.
