The Master of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica, in the earliest records the Choirmaster, is the head of the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]], which trains the [[Astropath]]s who carry messages across the Imperium and sends the [[Black Ships]] to gather [[Psyker]]s from every world. The Master holds one of the permanent seats among the [[High Lords of Terra]].

The earliest recorded holder, [[Nemo Zhi-Meng]], sat on the [[Council of Terra]] and the Grand Council of Reconstruction. [[Abdulias Anwar]] held the office in 544.M32, and in the early [[42nd millennium]] it is held by [[Zlatad Aph Kerapliades]]. No Master is named for 999.M41, and the Master's name at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] is withheld in Imperial records.

Duties

The Master speaks for the [[Adeptus Terra|adepta]] in the council: the tithe of [[Psyker|psykers]], the upkeep of the [[Astropathy|astropathic]] choirs and the safety of the [[Black Ships]]. The communication on which the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] depend passes through the Master's hands, which gives the office a reach that its rank does not suggest.

History

Zhi-Meng served through the [[Horus Heresy]] and sat in the Grand Council of Reconstruction of 014.M31. Anwar sat at the outset of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32. Kerapliades is named among the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] restored by [[Roboute Guilliman]] in the [[Indomitus Crusade]].
