Chaos is the Ruinous Powers of the Warp, their daemons and the mortal servants who worship them. Its champions have launched Black Crusades against the Imperium from the [[Eye of Terror]] for ten thousand years. This portal gathers the articles on the Chaos Gods, the Traitor Legions and the daemons.

Selected article

[[Chaos]] is the force of the Warp that corrupts mortal minds and fuels the Imperium's enemies. Four Chaos Powers, [[Khorne]], [[Tzeentch]], [[Nurgle]] and [[Slaanesh]], rule its daemonic realms and its worshippers. Imperial scholars record that Chaos cannot be destroyed, only held back, and that every age must fight it again.
