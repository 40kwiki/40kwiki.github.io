The Logos Historica Verita, often called simply the Logos, is a small office of the [[Adeptus Terra]] founded by [[Roboute Guilliman]] after his return at the end of the [[41st millennium]]. Its task is to recover a full and accurate history of mankind, unmarred by religious superstition, political interest or the deliberate falsification of the past. Its scholars, the historitors, were trained by Guilliman himself, or by those he had trained, in objective methods of gathering evidence.

The Logos was born of Guilliman's dismay at the state of the Imperium's records. He found that the history of the Imperium had fallen afoul of superstition, fanaticism and hearsay, that the knowledge painstakingly pieced together by the [[Remembrancer Order|Remembrancers]] of his own era had been lost, and that much of what remained was woefully inaccurate. Its historitors travel with the Lord Commander's seal, open vaults that had been forbidden for millennia and send what they find to Guilliman wherever his crusade is fighting.

Its work has been dangerous. Historitor teams have disappeared without trace in the war zones of the Era Indomitus, and they have met opposition from the more conservative agents of the [[Inquisition]] and from the existing powers of the Imperium.

History

Background

Imperial history had long been obscured. Knowledge of the [[Immaterium]]'s true nature was suppressed, though only intermittently, through [[Edict of Obliteration|Edicts of Obliteration]] and through the [[Administratum]]'s [[Historical Revision Unit]]. The [[Inquisition]] forbade knowledge of [[Daemon|daemons]] and the [[Ruinous Powers]] to Imperial citizens, and many innocents paid the ultimate price for learning the truth by accident. Within the Inquisition itself, the [[Ordo Originatus]] was dedicated to unravelling ten millennia of myth, exaggeration and lies, and was opposed by the [[Ordo Redactus]], which deliberately obscured the past.

The result, by the end of the [[41st millennium]], was a history of fragments and contradictions that few in the Imperium could separate from legend. Much of the ancient history of [[Terra]], pieced together by the Remembrancers, had been lost again. Even the [[Imperial calendar]] had decayed.

Founding

[[Roboute Guilliman]] was restored to life in 999.M41, after ten thousand years in stasis, and almost at once launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. Between campaigns he worked in his scriptorium aboard his flagship, [[Macragge]]'s Honour, trying to understand the ten millennia he had missed. He was dismayed by what he found. The objective learning of the Great Crusade had given way to superstition and hearsay, and the state of mankind's knowledge of itself seemed to him to be worse than it had been after the [[Unification Wars]].

Guilliman resolved to recover the truth, against the opposition of the Inquisition if necessary. He sought out inquisitive minds, of exactly the kind that the Imperium had learned to distrust, and in several cases rescued them from [[Servitor|penal servitude]] or from the mind-wipe that awaited them. The first four he tutored himself. They taught others, and those taught more. Each candidate was examined by the primarch in person. Those who met his standards were given the rank of historitor-investigatus. Those who did not were given less demanding roles in the new organisation as [[Librarian|librarians]], servants and assistants, because Guilliman, saddened by the waste of the Imperium's machinery of government, had determined that no life in his service would be thrown away for failure.

The Logos grew from four historitors to eight, then to sixteen, and within its first decade numbered some five hundred operatives and a thousand support staff.

The Chronostrife

One of the first problems the Logos encountered was the date. Guilliman learned, from the records he read, of the Chronostrife of Terra, a long and bitter dispute within the Inquisition's [[Ordo Chronos]] over the reckoning of the Imperial calendar. The standard dating system of the Great Crusade had given at least a reliable order of events, but in the ten thousand years since it had been corrupted by dogma and revision, and several rival calendars had grown from it. When the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade drew to a close, about twelve years after his return, Guilliman calculated the current year by the five principal variants of the calendar, and found that they placed it anywhere from the early 41st millennium to a full millennium later, without counting the lesser and more heretical reckonings.

Guilliman had hoped to find a solution; he found instead that there was none. The repair of the calendar, he concluded, would ultimately require his personal attention.

Methods

The historitors work in small cells that search out ancient records. On presentation of the Lord Commander's seal, forbidden vaults are opened and emptied, and their contents are copied and dispatched to [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] wherever his crusade is. Using long-dead academic arts, the Logos attempts what had seemed impossible, the construction of a reliable history of the Imperium. It must work from thousands of years of history recorded on every device that mankind has employed.

Opposition

The Logos has been opposed by the existing powers of the Imperium and by the more conservative agents of the Inquisition, and its historitor teams sometimes disappear into the war zones that now engulf half the galaxy without trace. Within the Inquisition the [[Ordo Redactus]], which deliberately obscures the past, works against the [[Ordo Originatus]], whose labour has been eased by the establishment of the Logos.
