The Logos Historica Verita, often called simply the Logos, is a small office of the [[Adeptus Terra]] founded by [[Roboute Guilliman]] after his return at the end of the [[41st millennium]]. Its task is to recover a full and accurate history of mankind, unmarred by religious superstition, political interest or the deliberate falsification of the past. Its scholars, the historitors, were trained by Guilliman himself in methods of evidence and inquiry that had been forgotten in the Imperium since the [[Great Crusade]].

The Logos was born of Guilliman's dismay at the state of the Imperium's records. He found that the history of his own lifetime, including the [[Horus Heresy]], had been reduced to legend, that the knowledge painstakingly recovered by the [[Remembrancer Order]] had been lost, and that much of what remained had been altered or suppressed. Its historitors travel with warrants under the Lord Commander's seal, open vaults that had been closed for millennia and send what they find to the Lord Commander wherever he is fighting.

Its work has been dangerous. Historitors have vanished into the war zones of the Era Indomitus, and they have met opposition from the more conservative members of the [[Inquisition]] and the adepta. Many of the accounts of the Heresy now accepted as authoritative, including the sealed Chronicles of the Heresy, were assembled or verified by the Logos.

History

Background

Imperial history had been subject to deliberate alteration since the end of the Heresy. Knowledge of the [[Warp]] and of [[Chaos]] was suppressed by the Inquisition, which held that the truth was more dangerous to ordinary citizens than any lie. The [[Administratum]]'s Historical Revision Unit amended records on the instructions of the [[High Lords of Terra]], and Edicts of Obliteration erased people, organisations and even worlds from the record. The [[Adeptus Ministorum|Ecclesiarchy]] replaced much of what was known of the Emperor and his sons with doctrine. Within the Inquisition itself, the Ordo Originatus, which sought the truth of the past, was opposed by the Ordo Redactus, which existed to obscure it.

The result, by the end of the 41st millennium, was a history that few in the Imperium could separate from legend. The primarchs were remembered as saints and devils. The cause and course of the Heresy were known in fragments. Even the [[Imperial calendar]] had decayed.

Founding

Roboute Guilliman was restored to life in 999.M41, after ten thousand years in stasis, and almost at once launched the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. Between campaigns he worked in his scriptorium aboard his flagship, Macragge's Honour, trying to understand the ten millennia he had missed. He was dismayed by what he found. The objective learning of the Great Crusade had given way to superstition and hearsay, and the state of mankind's knowledge of itself seemed to him to be worse than it had been after the [[Unification Wars]].

Guilliman resolved to recover the truth, against the opposition of the Inquisition if necessary. He sought out inquisitive minds, of exactly the kind that the Imperium had learned to distrust, and in several cases rescued them from penal servitude or from the mind-wipe that awaited them. The first four he tutored himself. They taught others, and those taught more. Each candidate was examined by the primarch in person. Those who met his standards were given the rank of historitor-investigatus. Those who did not were given less demanding roles in the new organisation as librarians, servants and assistants, because Guilliman, saddened by the waste of the Imperium's machinery of government, had determined that no life in his service would be thrown away for failure.

The Logos grew from four historitors to eight, then to sixteen, and within its first decade numbered some five hundred operatives and a thousand support staff.

The Chronostrife

One of the first problems the Logos encountered was the date. Guilliman learned, from records submitted to him, of the Chronostrife of Terra, a long and bitter dispute within the Inquisition's [[Ordo Chronos]] over the reckoning of the Imperial calendar. The standard dating system of the Great Crusade had given at least a reliable order of events, but in the ten thousand years since it had been corrupted by dogma and revision, and several rival calendars had grown from it. When the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade drew to a close, about twelve years after his return, Guilliman calculated the current year by the five principal variants of the calendar, and found that they placed it anywhere from the early 41st millennium to a full millennium later, without counting the lesser and more heretical reckonings.

Guilliman had hoped to find a solution; he concluded that there was none to be found in the records. The dates of the Era Indomitus are therefore provisional, and the Logos marks them as such. The reform of the calendar is a task that the Lord Commander has reserved for himself.

Methods

The historitors work in small cells. Each cell is sent to a region, an archive or a war zone with a warrant from the Lord Commander, and has authority to open sealed vaults and to copy whatever it finds. The copies are sent to Guilliman, who reads much of them himself. The historitors are taught to compare sources, to prefer the earliest record to the later copy, and to record disagreements between sources in place of resolving them by doctrine. These methods, which were once commonplace among the [[Remembrancer Order|remembrancers]] of the Great Crusade, had been dead in the Imperium for ten thousand years.

The Logos does not publish its findings to the Imperium at large. Its reports go to the Lord Commander, and many of its compilations are held under seal on Terra. Guilliman has explained that the truth must be restored before it can be shared, and that a population raised on doctrine cannot be given the whole history of the Heresy at once.

Opposition

The Logos has many opponents. Puritan Inquisitors regard its work as a threat to the Imperium's safety, because it brings to light knowledge of daemons and of the nature of the Warp. Members of the Ecclesiarchy fear that its findings on the Emperor's own words, which were hostile to religion, may undermine the [[Imperial Cult]]. Officials of the Adeptus Terra resent its access to their archives. Historitor teams have been obstructed, imprisoned and, in some cases, have disappeared. The Ordo Redactus is said to regard the Logos with particular alarm, while the Ordo Originatus has found it a useful ally.

The Logos has also been accused, by its critics, of serving Guilliman's own interests: of recovering a history in which the primarch's reforms are justified. Its supporters reply that its records are kept with their sources, so that later scholars may check its conclusions.This article, like the others in this encyclopedia that cite the Chronicles of the Heresy, relies in part on Logos compilations. Where the Logos's account differs from older Imperial records, both are given.

Influence

Many of the accepted accounts of the Imperium's early history have been revised in light of the Logos's work. Among its contributions are the reconstruction of the founding of the [[Inquisition]] from the recovered records of [[Malcador the Sigillite]], the correction of the history of the [[Siege of Terra]], and the recovery of records of the [[War of the Beast]]. It has also supported the Lord Commander's reforms of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and the [[Administratum]] with evidence of how those bodies came to their present form.
