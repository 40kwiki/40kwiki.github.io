The Senatorum Imperialis is the council chamber in the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Terra]] in which the [[High Lords of Terra]] meet, and, in [[High Gothic]] usage, the name of the council itself. The High Twelve govern the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] in the name of the [[Emperor of Mankind]], and the decisions taken in the chamber bind every Adeptus and world under Imperial rule.

The Senatorum took shape during the first regency of [[Roboute Guilliman]], after the [[Siege of Terra]] of 014.M31, as the successor to the Emperor's [[Council of Terra]]. Its chamber has seen some of the gravest events in Imperial history: the murder of the High Lords in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32, the dissolution of the council by [[Goge Vandire]] in the [[Age of Apostasy]], and the return of Guilliman as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] in 999.M41.

The chamber

The chamber stands among the government halls of the outer Palace, away from the [[Sanctum Imperialis]]. Imperial accounts describe a vaulted hall in which the twelve seats of the High Lords are set around a central floor, with galleries above for the lesser lords, the scribes of the [[Administratum]] and the petitioners admitted to a session. Its day-to-day business, from the order of debate to the keeping of the rolls, is run by the [[Chancellor of the Imperial Council]], who is not one of the Twelve.

Sessions are closed. Records of the debates are kept in the Rolls of the Senatorum Imperialis and the sealed Proceedings, and only summaries reach the wider Adeptus Terra.

History

Origins

During the [[Great Crusade]] the Emperor ruled through the Council of Terra, led by the regent [[Malcador the Sigillite]], which sat in the Palace and included no [[Space Marine Legions|Astartes]]. In 014.M31, at the end of the Siege, a Grand Council of Reconstruction took charge. During his regency Guilliman reshaped the council into the Senatorum, set its members at twelve and took a seat himself as Lord Commander of the Imperium. The first full roll of the council in its known form is the one that sat in 544.M32.No single date is recorded for the change of name. The Rolls use "Senatorum Imperialis" for the council from the early years of the Time of Rebirth.

Beheading

Main article: [[Beheading]]

In 546.M32, during the [[War of the Beast]], the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Drakan Vangorich]] had the High Lords killed in one solar day, across the Palace and in the Senatorum itself. Only [[Maximus Thane]], [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Imperial Fists]], survived, and went on alone to the chamber where Vangorich waited. A new council was chosen soon after, and Vangorich ruled behind it as Lord Protector until his fall in about 646.M32.

Age of Apostasy

Further information: [[Age of Apostasy]]

In the 36th millennium the High Lord [[Goge Vandire]] held both the Administratum and the office of [[Ecclesiarch]]. When the Fabricator-General Gastaph Hediatrix demanded in the chamber that Vandire be tried, Vandire dissolved the Senatorum and declared the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] traitors. After Vandire's death in about 288.M36, [[Sebastian Thor]] restored independent High Lords to the council, and it met again in its chamber.The date of the dissolution is not recorded separately; it fell shortly before the end of the Reign of Blood.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In 999.M41 Roboute Guilliman, restored to life, reached Terra at the end of the [[Terran Crusade]]. He entered the Senatorum, took the offices of Lord Commander and [[Imperial Regent]], and replaced five of the High Twelve along with hundreds of the lesser lords. The chamber has since been the seat from which the [[Indomitus Crusade]] and the government of the [[Imperium Sanctus]] are directed.
