A hive world is a class of planet of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] whose population lives almost entirely within enormous city-structures called hives or hive cities. A single hive may rise kilometres into the sky and descend as far beneath the surface, and may house billions of people. Hive worlds are the most populous and productive planets of the Imperium after the [[forge world]]s, and they provide the greater part of its manufactured goods and of the soldiers of the [[Astra Militarum]].

Most hive worlds were heavily industrialised long before the Imperium. Their surfaces outside the hives are usually polluted wastes, and they depend on [[Agri-world]]s for food. They are ruled by hereditary governors and noble houses whose wealth rests on the labour of billions. Because they are so valuable, the Imperium will fight for them at almost any cost, as in the three [[Wars for Armageddon]] (444.M41, 941.M41 and 998.M41).

Structure of a hive

A hive is built in layers, and its society follows the same order. At the summit is the spire, the home of the governor and the noble houses, where the air is filtered and natural light still falls. Below it lies the hive city proper, a vast warren of habitation blocks, manufactoria and transit tunnels in which the working population lives and labours, often never seeing the sky. Beneath that lies the underhive, older levels abandoned or collapsed under the weight of the hive above, where outcasts, mutants, gangs and heretics live beyond the reach of the law. At the very bottom is the sump or hive bottom, a lake of toxic waste.

Between the hives the surface is commonly an ash waste: a desert of industrial dust, slag and acid rain, crossed by armoured convoys and roamed by nomads and raiders. Some hive worlds have only one hive; others have dozens linked by highways, rail and orbital shuttles.

Society and government

A hive world is usually ruled by a hereditary planetary governor drawn from the most powerful noble house. The other houses control particular industries or districts and compete for the governor's favour, sometimes with violence. The workers of the hive city belong to guilds or industrial clans whose loyalties are bound to their employers. Crime, gang war and cult activity are endemic in the lower levels, and the planetary enforcers, assisted by the [[Adeptus Arbites]], keep order only in part.

The overcrowded lower hives are fertile ground for heresy. [[Genestealer Cults]] in particular spread unnoticed through the underhive for generations, and many hive worlds have been lost to cult uprisings. The [[Inquisition]] keeps watch on hive worlds for this reason.

Tithe and military role

Hive worlds pay some of the highest [[Imperial tithe|tithe grades]] in the Imperium, usually in the Exactis band. They supply manufactured goods, labour for other worlds, ratings for the [[Imperial Navy]] and, above all, regiments for the Astra Militarum. The soldiers of hive worlds are used to cramped and confused fighting, and hive regiments such as the [[Necromundan Spiders]] and the [[Armageddon Steel Legion]] are noted for city fighting and mechanised war.

Fighting for a hive is among the most costly forms of war. Defenders can hold every level, and attackers must clear the city floor by floor. The defence of [[Hades Hive]] on Armageddon under Commissar [[Sebastian Yarrick]] in 941.M41 is the most famous example.

History

Many hive worlds date from the [[Dark Age of Technology]], when human worlds were rich and populous. In the [[Age of Strife]] some survived by sealing themselves inside their cities, and the hives grew in height and depth as populations rose and surface environments failed. During the [[Great Crusade]] (c. 798.M30–005.M31) the hive worlds were prized for their industry and manpower, and their governors were confirmed in power in exchange for the tithe.

Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41 hive worlds have been among the main targets of the Imperium's enemies. Many in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] have been isolated, some have been lost to uprisings or invasions, and the [[Departmento Munitorum]] has demanded foundings far above their usual grade to supply the [[Indomitus Crusade]].
