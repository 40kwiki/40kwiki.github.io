The Red Hunters is a loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] whose origins and Founding are unknown. It has strong ties to the [[Inquisition]], and it has a long record of placing itself at the service of the Inquisition. Its warriors have served under the command of an [[Inquisitor]] Lord on several recorded occasions.

Because of this service, the Red Hunters are seldom seen in the field with other Chapters. Imperial records note that their campaigns are often recorded by the Inquisition rather than by the Chapter, and that their reports are sealed for many years after each mission.
