The Ixaniad Sector is an Imperial sector in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], located in a ragged stellar cluster with the [[Calixis Sector]], the [[Scarus Sector]] and the [[Askellon Sector]]. Its planetary systems are ruled by several minor [[Highborn]] dynasties, which have fought each other for control of its politics for millennia. Little else is recorded of the sector in Imperial archives.

History

During the [[Age of Plunder]] of the Calyx Expanse, from the 37th to the 39th millennium, [[Rogue Trader]]s frequented the region that later became the Ixaniad Sector and returned with tales of xenos empires, lost human and Imperial worlds, and Warp-worshipping once-men. The sector has also known conflicts that spilled over from the Calixis Sector, among them the [[Meritech Wars]], which threatened the stability of the whole sector. Refugees from the dynastic conflicts of the Highborn formed the original [[Meritech Clans]] in the border regions with the Calixis Sector.

In the early years of the [[Angevin Crusade]] in the 39th millennium, after millennia of lawlessness the sector was brought back into [[Imperial Compliance]]. It was easily pacified before the Crusade moved on to the harder conquest of the Calixis Sector.

The [[Inquisition]]'s Phaenonite faction, declared Excommunicate Traitoris, is recorded as growing in influence on the region's Imperial planets and the few forge worlds in the sector. The Inquisition's Conclave Ixaniad was purged of Phaenonite influence in 427.M39. The Black Templars are known to have fought the Aeldari on the sector's world of Scoth.
