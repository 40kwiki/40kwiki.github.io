Iathglas is a [[maiden world]] of the [[Aeldari]]. It is recorded chiefly as the place where [[Yvraine]] convened a council of the Aeldari kindreds, and where the daemon [[Shalaxi Helbane]] attacked the gathering and was driven off.

The maiden worlds are the Aeldari's ancestral settlements, which were largely abandoned as the species' population fell. Iathglas is one of the few that Imperial records describe in any detail.

Council of the kindreds

[[Yvraine]] convened the council on Iathglas to bring the [[Aeldari]] kindreds together. The Aeldari were quarrelling, and the council's purpose was to unite them against the threats to their people. The gathering was attacked when Helbane's [[Daemon|daemons]] appeared, and the Aeldari were forced to fight together.

Yvraine, [[Jain Zar]], [[Lelith Hesperax]], the [[Visarch]] and the [[Yncarne]] fought the daemon together. Helbane was defeated, but its presence on Iathglas showed that [[Slaanesh]] had been tracking the council.
