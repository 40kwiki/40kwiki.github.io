The [[Inquisition]] is the secret order with authority over every institution of the Imperium. Its agents hunt heresy, [[Xenos|xenos]] and [[Daemon|daemons]], often without the knowledge of the institutions they police. This portal gathers the articles on its Ordos, its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] and its records.

Selected article

[[Inquisition]] is the secret order that holds authority over every Imperial institution, answerable only to the Emperor's will. It is divided into Ordos, each with its own duty, among them the [[Ordo Malleus]], the [[Ordo Xenos]] and the [[Ordo Hereticus]]. Its records are often sealed, and Imperial accounts of its deeds disagree.
