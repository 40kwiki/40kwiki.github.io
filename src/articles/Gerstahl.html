Gerstahl is a [[Shrine world]] of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], close to the Cadian Gate. For centuries its pilgrims offered daily prayers to the God-Emperor in peace. The world takes its name from Saint Gerstahl, an officer of the [[Astra Militarum]] who fell defending the Cadian Gate in the first centuries after the [[Horus Heresy]].

In 909.M32, in the course of the Third Black Crusade, the tomb of the saint was desecrated by [[Abaddon the Despoiler]]. Imperial records treat the attack on Gerstahl as the real aim of the campaign, with the assault on [[Cadia]] as a diversion.

Saint Gerstahl

Gerstahl died in the early centuries of the Imperium holding the Cadian Gate against the [[Forces of Chaos]]. The date of his death is not recorded, but the sources place it in the first centuries after the Heresy.Estimated as M32 from the sources' wording. No year is given.

He was laid in a tomb beneath the alabaster capital of the world. The [[Imperial Cult]] holds that he will rise again as a Living Saint when "the Eye of Darkness opens for the last time", and that he will then hold back the tide of the traitors. Pilgrims came to his tomb for this promise.

Desecration of Gerstahl

See also: [[Black Crusade]]

Abaddon learned of the prophecy and wished to prevent its fulfilment. He launched the Third Black Crusade against Cadia, the fortress world that guards the Cadian Gate, so that the attention of the Imperium would turn away from Gerstahl.

To keep the defenders occupied he freed the Daemon Prince [[Tallomin]], the "Prince of Princes", against the Gate. Abaddon had promised the creature that the blood of Loyalist [[Adeptus Astartes]] would give it the power to crush its rivals in the [[Eye of Terror]]. Tallomin and his followers killed millions of Imperial soldiers and drew a dozen Loyalist Space Marine Chapters to the fight.

The [[Space Wolves]] finally drove Tallomin back into the Warp. By then Abaddon had done his work. The remains of Saint Gerstahl were destroyed, and the prophecy could no longer be fulfilled.

Legacy

Gerstahl remains a shrine world of the Adeptus Ministorum. The destruction of the saint's remains is recorded as the achievement for which Abaddon planned the campaign, and the prophecy of the Living Saint is counted lost.
