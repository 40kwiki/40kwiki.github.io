The Wolf Guard are the most senior veteran warriors of the [[Space Wolves]] Chapter of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. Each Great Company has its own band, hand-picked by the [[Wolf Lord]] who commands it to serve as his guard, his champions and the vanguard of his army. They fill the role that [[Sternguard Veterans]] hold in a Chapter that follows the [[Codex Astartes]].

A [[Space Wolves]] warrior joins the Wolf Guard for what he has done, never for how long he has served. A newly raised Blood Claw has the same chance of being chosen as a veteran Long Fang, so the ranks hold hot-blooded young warriors beside grizzled veterans.

Selection and standing

No fixed test governs promotion. Battlefield elevation is common in the Chapter, because Wolf Lords judge by instinct. The surest road is to save a Wolf Lord's life in the heat of battle, since the first duty of the Wolf Guard is to be the sword and shield of their lord. A warrior raised to the Wolf Guard leaves his old [[Space Wolves]] pack and takes a new pack mark, engraved on the right shoulder plate in the yellow and black of a Fenrisian noble. Terminator-armoured members carry the Great Company's icon there instead and wear their pack mark on the knee guards and weapon casings.

Wolf Lords reward their chosen with the best arms in the company armoury: ancient weapons, ornate [[Artificer armour]] and, most prized, rare suits of [[Terminator armour]]. Each Wolf Guard fights in whatever style he excels at, whatever his old pack role was.

The Wolf Guard also teach. They lead packs of younger brothers in the field and are held up as models to them. Every Space Wolf hopes to join them, and fights harder when a Wolf Lord is near in the hope of being noticed.

Organisation

Battle Leader

The most capable Wolf Guard of a Great Company is named its Battle Leader, the Champion of the company and its lieutenant in rank. He either guards the Wolf Lord in person or acts as his herald and leads part of the Great Company where the lord is not present. A Battle Leader who earns the trust of the Wolf Guard and the company may be named the next Wolf Lord. Battle Leaders wear runic armour and are sometimes accompanied by packs of semi-tame Fenrisian wolves.

Packs and Terminators

The ordinary Wolf Guard fight as a pack that forms the sharpest blade of the Great Company. They lead the battle line or take vital objectives in strength. Many choose [[Terminator armour]] and fight as the spearhead of an assault, scorning the enemy's fire as they advance. On rare occasions a Great Company arms all its Wolf Guard so and commits them to one war zone as a hammer blow.

Thunderwolf Cavalry

The records of the Imperium deny that the Thunderwolf Cavalry exist, and the Space Wolves themselves deny that they ride beasts to war. Rumour in the [[Fenris]] sector says otherwise. The mounts are Thunderwolves, giant wolves of Fenris as tall at the shoulder as a Terran rhinoceros, which Wolf Guard capture on the Lone Hunt and break to the saddle. This is thought to be a rite of entry into the upper ranks. The Chapter's Iron Priests fit the beasts with adamantium jaws and bionic limbs.

Bladeguard Veterans

In the Era Indomitus the Wolf Guard began to take Primaris Bladeguard Veterans into their ranks.

Notable members

  • [[Ragnar Blackmane]], raised directly from the Blood Claws after slaying the Ork warlord Borzag Khan in single combat, and later the youngest Wolf Lord in the Chapter's records.
  • Arjac Rockfist, called the Anvil of Fenris, once a blacksmith of the Bear Claw tribe, named Champion of [[Logan Grimnar]], the [[Great Wolf]], before he joined the Chapter.
  • Canis Wolfborn, a loner raised by wolves according to the [[Wolf Priest]]s, who serves the Wolf Lord Harald Deathwolf and rides a huge Thunderwolf.
  • Mikal Sternmark, champion of the Wolf Lord Berek Thunderfist. He fell to the Wulfen curse during the Charys Campaign and killed an Imperial general, yet returned to fight at Berek's last stand.
  • Brynngar Sturmdreng, a senior officer of the VI Legion at the end of the [[Great Crusade]], who lost an eye saving an Ultramarines captain on Carthis.The Carthis event belongs to the closing years of the Great Crusade; no source gives a year.
