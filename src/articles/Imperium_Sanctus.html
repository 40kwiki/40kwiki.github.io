The Imperium Sanctus ("Holy Imperium") is the name given in the [[Era Indomitus]] to the part of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] that lies south and west of the [[Great Rift]] and can still see the light of the [[Astronomican]]. It includes the [[Segmentum Solar]], [[Segmentum Pacificus]] and [[Segmentum Tempestus]] and the southern parts of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] and [[Ultima Segmentum]], and has [[Terra]] at its heart. Its counterpart beyond the Rift is the [[Imperium Nihilus]], the "Dark Imperium".

The Imperium Sanctus is governed directly from Terra by the [[High Lords of Terra]] and the Lord Commander, [[Roboute Guilliman]]. It holds most of the Imperium's surviving [[Forge world|forge worlds]], recruiting worlds and naval bases, and supplies most of the armies of the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. The name is often said to be a hollow one: Terra itself has been attacked by [[Daemon|daemons]] since the Rift opened, and no world of the Sanctus is free of war.

Name and extent

The terms Sanctus and [[Imperium Nihilus|Nihilus]] came into use in the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]'s dispatches after the [[Terran Crusade]], to distinguish the regions that [[Terra]] could still reach from those it could not. The boundary is not a line on a map but a matter of navigation: a world belongs to the Sanctus if its [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] can see the Astronomican. Some systems close to the Rift have passed from one half to the other as the storms moved, and [[Administratum]] charts mark a band of "uncertain" worlds along its edge.

The Rift runs from the [[Eye of Terror]] in the galactic north-west, across the core, to the Hadex Anomaly in the east. The Sanctus therefore holds the whole south and west of the Imperium, the Solar core around Terra and the [[Ultramar]] region in the east. The worlds of Ultramar lie on the very edge of the Rift and are counted in the Sanctus only because the light can still be seen there.

History

Formation

When the Rift opened in 999.M41, after the [[Fall of Cadia]], the Warp storms that followed blinded the whole Imperium for a time. This was the [[Imperium Nihilus|Noctis Aeterna]]. On Terra it lasted thirty-three days; in other regions it lasted decades. As the storms in the south and west eased, the Astronomican became visible again, [[Warp travel]] became possible once more, and [[Astropathy|astropathic]] contact was restored. The worlds that regained the light became the Imperium Sanctus. Those beyond the Rift did not, and became the Nihilus.

Under the Lord Commander

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s reforms took hold first in the Sanctus, where his decrees could be delivered. The first fleets of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] fought to clear its sieges and restore its lines of supply before pressing into the north. By the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]'s records the crusade's first phase, which ended at [[Raukos]] in c. 012.M42, stabilised the Imperium Sanctus.

The Sanctus has nonetheless suffered many of the era's greatest wars. The [[Daemon|daemon]] host of the [[Battle of Lion's Gate]] reached the [[Imperial Palace]] itself. The [[Plague Wars]] were fought across [[Ultramar]]. The [[Black Templars]] have fought crusade after crusade to protect the [[Shrine world|shrine worlds]] of the [[Segmentum|Segmenta]] Solar and Pacificus from the [[Word Bearers]]. [[Armageddon]] has been fought over by [[Orks]] and daemons, and in the most recent years by the returned [[Ghazghkull Thraka]]. In the [[42nd millennium]] the Segmentum Pacificus, long the quietest of the segmenta, became the front of the Fourth Tyrannic War when the largest mass of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] yet recorded rose from below the galactic plane on its western edge.

Life in the Imperium Sanctus

For most of the subjects of the Sanctus, the age has meant heavier tithes. The loss of the resources of the [[Imperium Nihilus|Nihilus]] fell on the worlds that remained, and every world has been asked to raise more regiments, more ships and more war materiel. The [[Administratum]]'s own reports state that halving the reach of the Imperium has not made it easier to supply, but has doubled the danger to every world. Response times to crises are still measured in years.

[[Astropathy|Astropathic communication]], though restored, is less reliable than before the Rift. Messages are distorted, delayed or lost, and the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] has suffered heavy losses among its choirs. [[Navis Nobilite|Navigators]] report that the Astronomican, though visible, is dimmer and harder to follow.

The [[Psychic Awakening]] has been felt in the Sanctus as in the Nihilus. The number of [[Psyker|psykers]] among the population has risen sharply, and the [[Black Ships]] of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica cannot gather them all. At the same time the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] reports miracles on a scale not seen for millennia, and pilgrims to the [[Shrine world|shrine worlds]] have grown in number.

Relations with the Imperium Nihilus

The two halves are connected by the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], the only known stable passage across the Rift, which runs between Sangua Terra on the Sanctus side and [[Vigilus]] on the [[Imperium Nihilus|Nihilus]] side. Reinforcements, supplies and refugees pass through it. Its defence was the object of the [[War of Nightmares]] on Vigilus and of the later wars for the Gauntlet. Smaller and less reliable crossings are known, and the [[Webway]] has been used by the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]'s allies.

The Nihilus is administered in the Lord Commander's name by [[Commander Dante]] of the [[Blood Angels]], Regent of the Imperium Nihilus, who was given the title after the [[Devastation of Baal]]. Communication between the regent and Terra is slow and irregular. Some officials of the Sanctus regard the worlds beyond the Rift as already lost; the Lord Commander has refused to accept this.
