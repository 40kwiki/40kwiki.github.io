The II Legion, the Second Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]], is one of two Legions of the [[Great Crusade]] that no surviving Imperial record names in full. It was raised in the [[First Founding]] in the 30th millennium and fought in the early campaigns of the Crusade. Its primarch was found in the years between the recovery of [[Leman Russ]] in 819.M30 and that of [[Ferrus Manus]] in 824.M30, and by the time of the [[Horus Heresy]] it had been removed from the archives together with its commander. Nothing in the Imperial record shows that it turned against the Emperor. It simply stops appearing.

[[The Imperium]] calls this removal an Edict of Obliteration, also called a Damnatio Memoriae, the policy by which every record, icon and monument of a person or body declared Excommunicate Traitoris is destroyed. The Legion's name, number and primarch were struck from the rolls. Imperial archivists agree on that much. They disagree on everything else, including whether the Legion was destroyed, dishonoured, absorbed into another Legion or sent somewhere that no record can reach.

The reasons are sealed. The [[Inquisition]]'s policy on the II Legion is that it is not to be discussed, and the few references that survive are either damaged or annotated with a single word, expunged. Scholars who write on the subject do so from fragments, rumours and the absence of the Legion from documents where it ought to appear. This article is written in the same spirit. Where a claim rests on a rumour, it is marked as one.

History

Raising and early service

The II Legion was among the twenty Legions raised on [[Terra]] in the First Founding. Like the others, it went to war under a Legion Master before its [[Primarch|primarch]] was found.

The finding of the primarch

The records of the Great Crusade list the primarch of the II Legion only as redacted. In the rolls of recovered primarchs the entry stands between those of Leman Russ in 819.M30 and Ferrus Manus in 824.M30, but the date, the name and the world on which he was found are all blanked.

The Excision, by contrast, is never recorded as an event at all. It is inferred from the absence of later entries, from the empty second plinth in the ring of primarch statues in the Imperial Palace, and from the gap in the Legion numbering.

The Rangdan Xenocides

Main article: [[Rangdan Xenocides]]

The only unambiguous evidence that the II Legion fought in the Great Crusade is a passage that came by accident into copies of the [[Imperial Infantryman's Uplifting Primer]] in the 41st millennium. It states that the II and XI Legions took part in the [[Rangdan Xenocides]] in the 860s.M30, and it says nothing about the nature or scope of that participation. The passage is treated as the last clear mention of the Legion's active service.

The Excision

The date of the Excision is not recorded. Imperial scholars infer it from stray remarks in later accounts. A Raven Guard record has the Emperor tell [[Corvus Corax]] in 922.M30 that he has seventeen brothers, not nineteen, which shows that the two were already lost by then, while a Dark Angels account of the 840s.M30 still speaks of nineteen brothers. The Word Bearers' account of the rebuke at Monarchia on Khur names only eighteen Legions, so the Excision stands before 963.M30 at the latest.

Imperial accounts agree on the form of the removal. The Legion's name and number were stricken from the rolls, and every record that named its officers was sealed or destroyed. The Edict of Obliteration is held by Imperial historians to be the most complete ever carried out. Other primarchs speak of the two lost brothers only in whispers, and the places set for them in the stone hall of Macragge are marked with blank pennants.

Rumours of the Legion's fate

The II Legion's fate is the subject of many rumours, and they are recorded here only so that the reader can see what has been said. Imperial accounts differ on the following points, and none has been confirmed.

  • Destruction. The earliest and simplest rumour holds that the Legion was destroyed by the Emperor's own forces, with the [[Space Wolves]] given the task that the Imperium has always reserved for its [[Executioners|executioners]]. Leman Russ is recorded as saying that one Legion fighting another was not unprecedented, and a Cryptaesthesian of Terra spoke of the Wolves being "loosed again", but neither remark names the II Legion.
  • Absorption. A long-standing rumour among the Legiones Astartes holds that the Legion's warriors were drafted into another Legion, and that the Ultramarines grew to outnumber every other Legion in the years of the Excision. [[Word Bearers]] of the [[Argel Tal|Gal Vorbak]] are recorded repeating the claim.
  • Disease. When [[Sanguinius]] confessed the flaw in his Legion's blood to [[Horus]], his angry reply implied that one of the lost Legions might have been purged for a similar defect. No Legion's genetic records support the claim.
  • A founding of the Blood Ravens. Some scholars have proposed that the II Legion's primarch was the founder of the [[Blood Ravens]] Chapter. The theory has little support. It does not explain why the Blood Ravens would survive into the [[41st millennium]] when the Legion they might descend from was erased so thoroughly.

The Horus Heresy and after

The II Legion took no part in the Heresy as recorded by any Legion. It is not named at the [[Isstvan III]] massacre, the [[Drop Site Massacre]] or the [[Battle of Calth]], and no loyalist or traitor report from the early war mentions its colours. Its warriors, if any survived, are not known to have fought under their old name. The Legion's absence from the Heresy is one of the strongest reasons for the belief that it had already been removed before the war began.

Organisation

Nothing is known of the II Legion's internal structure. [[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]] of the early Crusade were generally organised along the notional pattern set out by the Imperial staff, with [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], battalions and companies beneath a command structure headed by the primarch. Whether the II Legion followed that pattern, or organised itself differently, cannot be established from the record.

Heraldry

No Legion badge, livery or battle cry of the II Legion survives in an Imperial archive. The emblem of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] is used in this article only because a Legion infobox requires an image. Any surviving Legionary heraldry would have been removed in the Excision, and no Chapter that claims descent from the II Legion is recognised by the Adeptus Astartes.
