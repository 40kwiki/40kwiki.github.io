The 35th millennium (M35) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M35 to 999.M35. For most of it the Imperium was divided by the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]], the "Time of Twin Empires", in which the Ur-Council of [[Nova Terra]] ruled the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] apart from [[Terra]]. In the same years the [[Moirae Schism]] split the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and exiled a third of the [[Iron Hands]]. The Ecclesiarchy itself left Terra for a time and moved its [[Holy Synod]] to [[Ophelia VII]].

The Interregnum ended in 975.M35 with the Cataclysm of Souls, the religious wars in which the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] reunited the Imperium and emerged as its strongest institution. The millennium closed with the [[Hrud]] Rising and the [[Cursed Founding]] of 991.M35, the largest Founding since the Second. It also saw the first Imperial contact with the tombs of the [[Necrons]] and the discovery of the [[T'au]].
