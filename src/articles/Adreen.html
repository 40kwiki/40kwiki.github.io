Adreen was the commander of the Terran fleet during the [[Siege of Terra]]. He held the post alone after it had been shared by two officers, [[Tabor Ludovicia]] and [[Haldane Ma'lon]], at the time of the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31.

Adreen sat on the council during the siege and supported [[Rogal Dorn]]'s decision to keep the Legions within the [[Imperial Palace]] while the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] defended the rest of [[Terra]]. He survived the [[Horus Heresy]].

After the Heresy

Adreen sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. The fleets then passed to the first [[Lord High Admiral]], [[Niora Su-Kassen]]. The sources do not record Adreen's later service.The date of Adreen's entry into the post is not recorded. It is placed in the early 13th year of the siege.
