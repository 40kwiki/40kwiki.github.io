Tjemen was a [[Master of the Administratum]] in the 41st millennium. The sources name him only in the list of masters, and no record gives the year of his tenure.

Masters of the later Imperium left few marks in the record. [[Skito Gavalles]] held the office in the 500s of the 41st millennium. By the close of the millennium the office was held by [[Irthu Haemotalion]], who sat on the council during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41.The place of Tjemen in the sequence of masters is not fixed in the sources. He is placed between Gavalles and Haemotalion only in the order of the list.
