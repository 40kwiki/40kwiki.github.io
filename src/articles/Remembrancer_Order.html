The Remembrancer Order was the corps of civilian artists, writers and chroniclers that accompanied the Expeditionary Fleets of the [[Great Crusade]] in the early 31st millennium. A Remembrancer was a human poet, historian, painter, sculptor, composer, actor or imagist, whose duty was to chronicle the deeds of the [[Space Marine Legions|Legions]] and the Army so that the future of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] would know how Mankind had come to dominate the galaxy. It is not known what became of the records that the Order made of the Great Crusade and the early Heresy.

The Remembrancers were rarely welcome. They were overwhelmingly natives of Terra who had competed with the foremost artists of the age for the honour of sailing with the fleets, and the [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]] accepted them very grudgingly. The [[Primarch|primarchs]], the Astartes and the line troops of the Army disliked them with the disdain that fighting men of every age have for those who watch war from a distance. In the Heresy most of the Remembrancers of the fleets that followed [[Horus]] were murdered by the Traitors, and the Council of Terra dissolved the Order.

Purpose and foundation

The record

The Order's purpose was to witness, record and reflect the light of the [[Imperial Truth]] that the Great Crusade spread, so that an accurate record of the founding of the Imperium would always be available to later generations. The idea came from Imperial intellectuals before the Triumph of [[Ullanor Crusade|Ullanor]], and the wordsmith [[Solomon Voss]], whose works included The Edge of Illumination, was instrumental in petitioning the Emperor to create a corps of a thousand artists to travel with the expeditionary fleets. The [[Council of Terra]] took up the proposal and later ratified the Order of Remembrancers.

Organisation

Branches

The Order included many kinds of artist and chronicler.

  • Poets, who composed verse.
  • Imagists, who made pict-records, the Order's equivalent of photographers.
  • Historiographers, who were its historians.
  • Documentarists, who were its journalists.
  • Painters and sculptors.
  • Actors, singers and composers.

Relations with the Legions

See also: [[Space Marine Legions]]

[[Leman Russ]], primarch of the [[Space Wolves]], once suggested that every Remembrancer should be armed and ready for battle if he wished to see the front lines of a war. [[Fulgrim]], primarch of the [[Emperor's Children]], took a fundamentally different view. He welcomed the Remembrancers among his Astartes and mortal troops, believing that the promotion of an artistic sense was as important to the life of a warrior as training in the arts of war. The Remembrancers of Fulgrim's 28th Expeditionary Fleet included the painter Serena d'Angelus, the sculptor Ostian Delafour and the composer Bequa Kynska.

Notable Remembrancers

  • Ignace Karkasy, poet of the Luna Wolves' 63rd Expeditionary Fleet, a drinker and satirist who was known for telling the truth as he saw it and who was protected by Captain [[Garviel Loken]]. After the wounding of [[Horus]] on [[Davin]], the [[Mournival]] trampled twenty-seven civilians to death in their haste to reach the Apothecarion. Karkasy wrote of the incident in his work The Truth Is All We Have and in newsletters, which earned him the anger of many of the Legion. He was later shot in the head by Maggard, the bodyguard of [[Petronella Vivar]], on Horus's behalf, and his death was disguised as suicide.
  • Mersadie Oliton, a documentarist who was Loken's personal Remembrancer and a friend of Karkasy and Euphrati Keeler. She escaped the killing of the Remembrancers on Horus's flagship aboard the frigate [[Eisenstein]]. On reaching Terra she was imprisoned on an orbital prison near Titan on the order of [[Rogal Dorn]], and her memory coils were removed. Her fate after the Heresy is not known.
  • Euphrati Keeler, an imagist of the 63rd Expeditionary Fleet, who escaped the same slaughter aboard the Eisenstein and became an avid proponent of the Lectitio Divinitatus, the forerunner of the [[Imperial Cult]]. One of the earliest saints later recognised by the Imperial Creed, she was held to have a psychic protection against daemons through her faith.
  • Petronella Vivar, a documentarist from a Terran noble family that had chronicled the Unification Wars. She sought to be Horus's personal Remembrancer and biographer, and was killed by Horus himself aboard the Vengeful Spirit to protect the secrets he had told her.
  • Serena d'Angelus and Ostian Delafour, painter and sculptor of the 28th Expeditionary Fleet, attached to the [[Emperor's Children]]. Delafour, who had been patronised by the Emperor, fell from [[Fulgrim]]'s favour by criticising the primarch's own sculpture as too flawless. After the Legion fell to [[Slaanesh]] on [[Laeran]], he made a statue of the Emperor, and Fulgrim killed him by driving his sword through both the statue and the sculptor. D'Angelus, corrupted by the touch of Slaanesh on Laeran, painted a likeness of Fulgrim that later housed the daemon of his blade and, for a time, the primarch's own soul. She killed herself on the sword that had killed Delafour.
  • Bequa Kynska, a composer of the same fleet, who was corrupted by Slaanesh and composed the symphony Maraviglia. When it was performed, daemonettes possessed several of the performers and the corrupted Astartes of the fleet began a massacre in the Remembrancers' space. Kynska was killed by a daemonette that had possessed her lead singer.
  • Kallista Eris, a historiographer and a [[Psyker|psyker]], assigned to record the deeds of the [[Thousand Sons]] on [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]]. She was consumed by the Warp when the Thousand Sons used her gift to foresee the assault of the [[Space Wolves]].
  • Mahavastu Kallimakus, called the Scribe of [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]], who served the primarch of the Thousand Sons personally and was present at the [[Council of Nikaea]]. He wrote the Book of Magnus. He fled Prospero aboard the Cypria Selene, which was boarded by the Space Wolves, but escaped the scrutiny of the Rune Priests and was released. [[Ahzek Ahriman|Ahriman]] found his writings in the Athenaeum of Kallimakus on the world of Appollonia nearly a millennium and a half later, and burned the library after he had taken them.
  • Solomon Voss, wordsmith, author of The Edge of Illumination and a petitioner for the Order's creation. When the Council of Terra dissolved the Order, he set out for [[Isstvan III|Isstvan V]] against its orders with the illuminator Askarid Sha and twenty other Remembrancers. Horus's Justaerin killed the others and spared Voss to be Horus's own witness. He was later captured by the Imperials and was executed by Rogal Dorn himself on Titan, his work was burned and he was declared a Traitor.

The Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Once the Heresy began, most of the civilian Remembrancers in the Expeditionary Fleets that served Horus and turned to [[Chaos]] were slaughtered by the Traitors to keep them from documenting the betrayal. The exceptions were the Remembrancers of the 28th Expeditionary Fleet, many of whom were exposed to the corruption of [[Laeran]] and turned, like the Emperor's Children, to the service of Slaanesh.

The Council of Terra then issued the Edict of Dissolution, on the grounds that the Remembrancers could not be allowed to wander through a civil war recording the shame of the Legions and of Mankind in literature, painting and song. The Corps was dissolved back into Imperial society, and those with the fleets were ordered to stop recording events.

Legacy

It is not known what happened to the records of the Great Crusade and the early Heresy that the Order created. They are not among the thin chronicles that survive from that time in the late 41st millennium.
