Gregor Eisenhorn is an [[Inquisitor]] of the [[Ordo Xenos]]. He began as a Puritan of the Amalathian faction, but his ideology changed over the course of his career so far that other members of the [[Inquisition]] regarded him as a possible [[Heretic|heretic]]. The Inquisition officially named him a [[Traitor|traitor]] at least twice in his career, and each time he was later proved a loyal servant of the [[God-Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]].

Eisenhorn's memoirs are among the most cited Inquisitorial accounts of the late [[41st millennium]]. The Ordo Xenos keeps them under seal, and the Imperium's copies are heavily restricted.

Career

Eisenhorn's campaigns took him across the Imperium and into the [[Ordo Xenos]]' cases against [[Xenos|xenos]], heresy and daemonic corruption. Imperial [[Inquisitor|inquisitors]] of his period describe the draw of [[Psyker|psykers]] from the holds of the [[Black Ships]] as a common practice, and his memoirs record its use.The date of the memoirs is recorded as c. 300.M41. The events they describe are not dated in full.

The Ordo's records name Eisenhorn and [[Solomon Lok]] among its Inquisitors. Lok was reported slain on [[Beta Anphelion IV]] in 850.M41, though later accounts dispute his fate.
