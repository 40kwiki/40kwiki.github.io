The 39th millennium (M39) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M39 to 999.M39. It falls within [[The Waning]], but it was not only an age of loss. The Redemption Crusades of the 130s pushed the borders outward again, and the [[Angevin Crusade]] of 322–384.M39 conquered the [[Calyx Expanse]] and created the [[Calixis Sector]], whose first [[Lord Sector]], Drusus, was made a saint in 502.M39.

The Tenth [[Black Crusade]] opened the millennium with the [[Iron Warriors]] of [[Perturabo]] striking the [[Iron Hands]] at [[Medusa]], and the Eleventh, the Doom of Relorria, followed in 301.M39. Beyond the Eastern Fringe the [[T'au Empire]] began its Second Sphere Expansion and took the [[Vespid]] into its service.
