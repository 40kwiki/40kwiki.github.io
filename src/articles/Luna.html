Luna is the moon of [[Terra]], in the [[Sol System]]. It was the first world beyond Terra that humanity reached, in the second millennium, and the first world conquered in the [[Great Crusade]]: its Selenar gene-cults were subdued by three of [[Uriel Ventris|the newborn]] [[Space Marine Legions]], and their gene-craft helped to build the rest. The XVI Legion took its first name, the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]], from the conquest.

Luna has served ever since as the outer bastion of the Throneworld. Its surface carries the defence lasers of the Ring, Port Luna was the great naval yard of the early Imperium, and the Somnus Citadel was the seat of the [[Sisters of Silence]]. It was taken by the [[Traitor|Traitors]] in the [[Solar War]] before the [[Siege of Terra]], and in the first years of the [[Era Indomitus]] it was the place where [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s [[Terran Crusade]] emerged from the [[Webway]] and fought the [[Thousand Sons]] on the threshold of Terra.

Geography

Luna's surface is airless rock, scarred by ancient maria and impact craters, and dotted with manufactoria and defensive works. Most of its people live underground in [[Hive world|hive cities]] with artificial gravity and atmosphere. A man-made canyon called the Circuit, kilometres wide, runs around the equator, with gateways into the moon's honeycombed interior. Around the moon lies the Ring, an artificial hoop built from [[The Rock|the rock]] of the Circuit and the metals of Luna's core, carrying lance batteries, defence lasers and docking bays.

People and government

Luna is governed as part of the [[Sol System]] under the authority of the [[Adeptus Terra]], and its defences are under the command of Battlefleet Solar. Its billions live in sealed hives far below the regolith. Imperial observers on [[Terra]] have long regarded the Lunar folk as a separate people with grievances of their own. Their [[Hive world|hive cities]] depend on air and water plants that date in places from before the Imperium, and much of the moon's old machinery is no longer understood.

Under the Selenar the moon's most important settlements were domed cities such as Herodotus Omega, beneath which the gene-cults kept their laboratories. The vaults held the cults' failed experiments, among them the first attempts at warriors of the Legion pattern that the Selenar called Ur-legionaries, and their greatest relic, the gene-archive known as the Magna Mater. During the [[Siege of Terra|Solar War]] the gene-witch Ta'lab Vita-37 enlisted the crew of the [[Iron Hands]] strike cruiser Sisypheum to keep the Magna Mater from the [[Sons of Horus]]; Herodotus Omega was destroyed in the fighting. The Selenar also kept the Esoterikon Livris, an archive said to hold records of the first founding of the Legions.

History

Early settlement

Humans first landed on Luna in 969.M2 and soon built domed cities on and below its surface. During the [[Dark Age of Technology]] it became a centre of biomedical research and zero-gravity manufacture. In the [[Age of Strife]] it fell almost empty, and by its end it was ruled by the Selenar: a conglomeration of gene-cults who believed that human nature was fractured into archetypes and sought to perfect each one through generations of engineered bodies. Their gene-forging was surpassed only by the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]]'s own laboratories.

The First Pacification

After the [[Unification Wars]] the Emperor needed the Selenar's craft to make [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] in the numbers the Great Crusade would demand. The cults rebuffed his ambassadors, and returned the last delegation as fused, living flesh in the shell of their shuttle. The Emperor ordered Luna taken by force, the first off-world compliance of the Imperium. The VII, XIII and XVI Legions, later the [[Imperial Fists]], [[Ultramarines]] and Luna Wolves, attacked together; the XVI drifted silently to the surface with their assault craft unpowered and struck the Selenar defences before they were detected. Luna fell in six hours. The Selenar surrender is said to have asked the Emperor to "call off his wolves", and the XVI took the name Luna Wolves.

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

Under the Imperium Luna became the largest military complex humans had built: Port Luna, the home of the Sol [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]], built and maintained thousands of starships, and the Selenar gene-wrights served the Legions. The Somnus Citadel, a brass fortress, was the seat of the [[Sisters of Silence]] and the landing ground of the [[Black Ships]]. The near-destruction of the [[Emperor's Children|III Legion]]'s [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] stores, when a ship carrying them to Luna was lost, is recorded as a disaster of this period; whether it was accident or sabotage by Selenar holdouts is still disputed.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

In the early years of the [[Horus Heresy]], [[Rogal Dorn]] confined the loyalist survivors of the frigate [[Nathaniel Garro|Eisenstein]] in the Somnus Citadel, and after a daemonic outbreak among them the citadel became the base of [[Malcador the Sigillite]]'s Knights-Errant. The Sisters abandoned it before the Siege to reinforce the [[Imperial Palace]].

In the [[Solar War]] of 014.M31, the opening campaign of the Siege, Luna was assaulted by the [[Sons of Horus]] under their [[First Captain]] [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]]. The Ring was struck by the battle-barge War Oath in a deliberate ramming, and Abaddon's warriors took the surface and the Selenar gene-labs. The Selenar, who had never forgiven their conquest, sided with the [[Warmaster]] and gave him their gene-craft. The loyalists retook Luna early in the [[Great Scouring]].

The long decline

Main article: [[The Waning]]

Over ten thousand years Luna's mines were exhausted by Terra's industries. By the [[41st millennium]] much of its infrastructure outside the port facilities had been abandoned, the Somnus Citadel among it, and its people were known for their resentment of a [[Terra|Throneworld]] that had used them and then forgotten them. The Ring's defence lasers remained in service.

The Battle of Luna

Main article: [[Terran Crusade]]

At the end of the [[Terran Crusade]], in the first years of the Era Indomitus, [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s surviving fleet, freed from captivity in the [[Maelstrom]], passed through the Webway and emerged on Luna, pursued by [[Magnus the Red]] and his [[Thousand Sons]]. They were saved by reinforcements from Terra, among them the [[Imperial Fists]], the [[Adeptus Custodes]], the Sol battlefleet and the Sisters of Silence, and by the [[Harlequins]] of the Shadowseer [[Sylandri Veilwalker]], who sealed the Webway gate behind them.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

As [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]], Guilliman restored a measure of Luna's former strength. The Somnus Citadel was regarrisoned by the revived Sisters of Silence, and resources were sent to revive the moon's industry. The [[Inquisition]] maintains its fortress of Sylem on Luna.
