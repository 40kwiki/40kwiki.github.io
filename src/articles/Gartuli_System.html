The Gartuli System is an Imperial [[star system]] in the [[Yasan Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]], near the [[Maelstrom]] [[Warp rift]]. It is held by the forces of [[Chaos]] led by the [[Red Corsairs]] [[warband]] of [[Heretic Astartes]], and it is named after its [[fortress world]], Gartuli.

History

In 999.M41, the [[Red Corsairs]] Chaos Lord [[Huron Blackheart]] launched a massive invasion of the Yasan Sector out of the Maelstrom. Most of the [[White Scars]] Chapter, which had kept the sector, were deployed elsewhere in the galaxy. The Chaos forces occupied several systems and nearly overwhelmed the White Scars garrison on [[Chogoris]].

The White Scars held their fortress-monastery of [[Quan Zhou]]. The Red Corsairs withdrew from Chogoris only when the [[Noctis Aeterna]] ebbed and their daemonic allies were lost. They kept a presence on the Chogoris world of [[Shovith]] and took the neighbouring [[Gartuli System]] and [[Thaxis System]] outright, from where they continue to attack the White Scars' homeworld.
