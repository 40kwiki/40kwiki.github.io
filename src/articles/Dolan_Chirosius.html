Dolan Chirosius, known as the Great Confessor, is a Saint of the [[Imperial Cult]]. He was a confessor of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] from Chiros, and he was martyred by the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis during the [[Plague of Unbelief]] of the [[Age of Apostasy]] in the [[36th millennium]].

His actions brought an end to the Apostate Cardinal's renegade pocket empire. The Ecclesiarchy holds him as an example of faith under torture, and his martyrdom is taught to every novice of the Ministorum. The Imperial Cult regards his death as the proof that the [[Emperor of Mankind]] rewards those who die for the faith.
