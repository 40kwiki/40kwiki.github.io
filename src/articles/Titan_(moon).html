Titan is the largest moon of the gas giant [[Saturn]] in the [[Sol System]]. It is wholly controlled by the secret [[Grey Knights]] Chapter of psychically active [[Space Marine]]s, who serve as the Chamber Militant of the [[Ordo Malleus]]. Its fortress-monastery is the Chapter's principal base and the site where its warriors are trained.

The Imperium keeps [[Luna|the moon]]'s location secret. Few outside the Ordo Malleus and the Grey Knights have been permitted to land there, and Imperial records describe it only in general terms. The fortress-monastery is said to be warded against the [[Warp]] by a dense array of psychic defences.
