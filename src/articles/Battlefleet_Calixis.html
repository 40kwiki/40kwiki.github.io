Battlefleet Calixis is the sector battlefleet of the [[Imperial Navy]] in the [[Calixis Sector]]. It guards the sector's [[Warp travel|warp routes]] from its base at [[Scintilla]]. It is one of the most important battlefleets on the spinward edge of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]].

The fleet is the naval arm of the sector's defence. Its regiments are raised by the [[Departmento Munitorum]], among them the [[Scintillan Protectorate]], which was shaped by the wars against the [[Orks]] and the [[Tyrant Star]].

The Expanse

There is no sector [[Imperial Navy|battlefleet]] in the [[Expanse]]. Warfare there is conducted by [[Rogue Trader]] fleets and their private armsmen. Battlefleet Calixis gives occasional support when a threat reaches the Maw, the boundary between the Expanse and the sector.The Expanse's arrangements are described in Imperial records of the sector, which do not give the dates of individual deployments.
