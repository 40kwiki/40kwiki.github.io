Alpharius Omegon, called the Hydra and the Aleph Null, was the [[primarch]] of the XX Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]], the [[Alpha Legion]]. Of all the twenty primarchs he is the least certainly known. Imperial scholars now accept that the primarch was not one man but a pair of identical twin brothers, Alpharius and Omegon, who shared a single identity and a single command. For the whole of the [[Great Crusade]] and most of the Heresy their existence was a secret held only by the Legion itself, and neither the Emperor's court nor the other primarchs knew it.

The Alpha Legion's reputation was built on subversion, infiltration and misdirection. It took its symbol from the hydra of Terran myth and fought, like [[War of the Beast|the beast]], with attacks from several quarters at once. Its primarchs, who might fight as one of their own legionaries, in the semblance of another primarch's warrior or as a primarch in baroque scale-armour, ensured that an enemy often did not know it was fighting the Alpha Legion until the battle was lost.

In 005.M31 the Legion joined [[Horus]] in rebellion. The reasons for that choice are among the most disputed questions of the Heresy; the sealed account of the Cabal, discussed below, suggests that the Legion's rebellion was a loyal act. In the [[Siege of Terra]], Alpharius was killed in a duel with Rogal Dorn at the Battle of Pluto, and Omegon took his brother's name for the rest of the war. The Alpha Legion did not retreat into the [[Eye of Terror]] after the Heresy; it spread through [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] in small, autonomous warbands, and the [[High Lords of Terra]] have declared it destroyed at least three times, in the 31st, the 32nd and the 39th millennia.

Origins

The question of the twins

The Imperium's archives preserve several accounts of how the twentieth [[Primarch|primarch]] was found. None is verified, and several are plainly manufactured by the Legion itself. The most widely repeated holds that Alpharius was the leader of a confederation of human systems whose fleet inflicted a defeat on a battleship of the [[Sons of Horus|Luna Wolves]] by trickery and ambush. [[Horus]] led a pursuit and, on the flagship [[Vengeful Spirit]], a lone intruder cut his way through the Justaerin and reached the [[Warmaster]]'s command chamber, where Horus recognised him as a brother.

A second account, extracted from a captured [[Alpha Legion]] centurion, tells of an unnamed [[Planetary classification|dead world]] at the edge of the Mandragoran Stars, where the infant primarch survived alone in the ruins of a murdered alien city until a corsair ship landed; he killed its crew and took its vessel to search for his father. A third, from the suppressed text called the Codex Hydra, places him on the world of Bar'Savor, where he was taken by the Slaugth, enslaved and taught to sow discord on the worlds they meant to feed on, and was freed by the Emperor himself. The same text offers a still stranger version, in which Alpharius was never scattered at all but raised secretly on [[Terra]] by the Emperor as his own agent. Archivists of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] conclude that none of the accounts is reliable, and that all are, in the manner of the Legion, a screen.

The Emperor and the Legion

The records of Terra suggest that Alpharius, found in 981.M30, spent little time with the Emperor after his discovery. He was sent to take command of his Legion, and the Emperor, at that point deep in the affairs of the Great Crusade, is not known to have spoken of him afterwards. The Emperor is held to have known the secret of the twin primarchs, though whether the twin birth was intended or an accident of the Primarch Project is not recorded. The two were said to share a single soul, Omegon acting as second to Alpharius in the Legion's command.

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The [[Alpha Legion|XX Legion]]'s service in the Great Crusade was short compared with that of the First Founding Legions and was conducted in conditions of the greatest secrecy. The warriors were taller than most, recalled the primarch's appearance, and fought as a disciplined, closely co-ordinated force. They kept apart from the other Legions, even when fighting alongside them, and cultivated a reputation for obliquity.

In war the Alpha Legion combined heavy force with sabotage, ambush, stealth and assassination. It studied the strengths and weaknesses of its fellow Legions and borrowed their tactics. It also gained notoriety for the needless intricacy of its methods. On Tesstra Prime, a world whose capital could have been taken at once, the Legion allowed the defenders to dig in so that they could be dismantled by sabotage, ambush and random misfortune, and the garrison surrendered after suffering ninety per cent casualties. Asked why, Alpharius is reported to have said that the simple course "would have been too easy". [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] called the operation a waste of the Emperor's ordnance. Only [[Horus]] admired it.

The Legion's relations with its brothers deteriorated. The Alpha Legion's refusal to take orders from other Legions' commanders led to quarrels with Guilliman and [[Mortarion]] after the [[Ullanor Crusade]], and with [[Leman Russ]], whose distaste for trickery was well known. The rest of the Legions regarded it with distrust.

The Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Why the Alpha Legion rebelled

The common explanation, that the [[Alpha Legion]] joined its only friend among the [[Primarch|primarchs]], [[Horus]], is not the only one. The scheme at [[Drop Site Massacre|Isstvan V]], in which Horus destroyed three Legions in a single ambush, resembled operations that Alpharius had planned in the past. An older Inquisitorial account suggests that a second motive may lie in the Legion's private history. About two years before the Heresy, during the compliance of Nurth, Alpharius Omegon is said to have been approached by an alliance of [[Xenos|xenos]] led by the [[Aeldari]], called the Cabal, and by a human agent, the Perpetual John Grammaticus. They showed him two visions of the future. In the first the Alpha Legion remained loyal, the Emperor won and was entombed in the Golden Throne, and humanity declined for ten thousand years until [[Chaos]] returned to destroy it. In the second the Legion joined Horus, and Horus, once he had slain the Emperor, would repent and destroy a corrupted humanity in a single century of slaughter, and the Powers of Chaos would perish with the race that fed them.

The Legion, according to this account, chose the second, and so joined the [[Traitor|Traitors]] in the belief that it was acting as the Emperor would have chosen. The account is an Inquisitorial reconstruction and has never been confirmed. Its authors concede that the Cabal may have lied, or been mistaken, or been in the service of Chaos, and that the outcome did not match either vision. The Alpha Legion itself has never explained its choice.

The Deliverance Uprising

After the Drop Site Massacre the Cabal reportedly directed the Alpha Legion to help Horus to a swift victory. The remnant of the [[Raven Guard]] returned to Deliverance, where [[Corvus Corax]] was trying to rebuild his Legion with a sample of undifferentiated primarch gene-material given him by the Emperor. Alpha Legion operatives, surgically disguised as Raven Guard dead of Isstvan V, entered the Legion and waited until a breakthrough was made. They then contaminated the pure material, so that newly inducted aspirants mutated into monsters, and fomented a rebellion among the tech-guilds of the [[Forge world|forge world]] of Kiavahr. The Raven Guard crushed the rebellion and killed every Alpha Legion agent, but the damage was done. Alpharius gave the corrupted research to [[Fabius Bile|Fabius]] of the [[Emperor's Children]], who found it useless; the Alpha Legion kept the integral data and a copy of the contaminated genome for its own use. Omegon, who met the Cabal's representative afterwards, informed him that the Legion would henceforth act without direction.

Chondax and the Alaxxes Nebula

Horus sent the Alpha Legion to blockade the [[White Scars]] in the Chondax System, where they fought until 007.M31, so that they would not learn of the rebellion or join the loyalists on Terra. The Legion's actual conduct there was ambiguous. Omegon authorised the destruction of an Alpha Legion outpost that was using alien pylons to jam [[Astropathy|astropathic]] messages, an act that allowed word of the Heresy and [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]'s order to reach [[Jaghatai Khan]]. The Alpha Legion fleet then appeared to menace the White Scars, but allowed them to break out. [[Mortarion]] raised with Horus the suspicion that the Legion was undermining the Traitors' plans, and Horus did not respond.

In 007.M31 Alpharius attacked the [[Space Wolves]] in the Alaxxes Nebula, where [[Leman Russ]] was recovering from the war on [[Burning of Prospero|Prospero]]. The Legion had old grudges against the Wolves, and relished the chance to prove its method superior to Russ's open warfare. Teleporting Lernaean [[Terminator armour|Terminators]] onto Russ's flagship, the Hrafnkel, it came near to victory, but a [[Dark Angels]] fleet arrived and the Alpha Legion fell back.

The Shattered Legions

Main article: [[Shattered Legions]]

In the Heresy's years of attrition the Alpha Legion also hunted its own loyalists. Testimony from the survivors of a Shattered Legions vessel, the Sisypheum, places one of the primarchs, presumed to be Alpharius, in a secret facility designated Lerna 2-12, disguised as the [[Iron Hands]] commander Shadrak Meduson. His disguise was discovered only at the end of the affair, after the survivors had escaped.

Terra and Pluto

As the Solar defences took shape under Rogal Dorn, the Alpha Legion activated agents that had been placed in the [[Sol System]] decades earlier. They penetrated the [[Imperial Palace]], entered the Investiary, where statues of the twenty primarchs stood, and destroyed all except two, those of Alpharius and Dorn, and left a message for the [[Imperial Fists]]. Dorn kept the affair secret, even from the [[Adeptus Custodes|Custodes]] and [[Malcador the Sigillite|Malcador]]. A fleet under the Harrowmaster Kel Silonius then took several of the moons of Pluto.

At the Battle of Pluto, in about 010.M31, Alpharius himself brought a fleet of over two hundred ships into the Sol System unobserved by placing it and its crews into stasis for a year of drifting, while his agents carried out terrorist attacks across the system to draw the defenders' attention. His objective was Hydra, a fortress moon that served as the Solar System's main astropathic monitoring station. [[Sigismund]] of the Imperial Fists defended with thirty ships. When Dorn's flagship, the [[Phalanx]], arrived with a fleet, the Alpha Legion's attack stalled, and Dorn and his Huscarls teleported into the astropathic chamber, where Alpharius and his Lernaean Terminators were waiting. The two primarchs fought. When Alpharius was about to drive the Pale Spear through Dorn, Dorn took the blow in his shoulder to pin Alpharius, severed his wrists, and killed him with his chainsword.

The Alpha Legion fleet then withdrew. At the moment of his twin's death Omegon is said to have felt it. When Horus later demanded to speak to Alpharius, Omegon took his brother's name, as he had done many times before, and was from that moment the sole primarch of the Legion. The Alpha Legion did not go to Terra, but spent the rest of the war in delaying actions, defeating a White Scars force on [[Battle of Tallarn|Tallarn]] and a Space Wolves force on Yarant.

After the Heresy

Eskrador

An older tradition, preserved in the records of the Ultramarines, places the death of Alpharius after the Heresy, on the world of Eskrador, where [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] led a strike force against the [[Alpha Legion]]'s headquarters. According to this account the two [[Primarch|primarchs]] fought, and Alpharius was killed, but the Alpha Legion counterattacked a day later and nearly destroyed the Ultramarines' strike force. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]] themselves have long suspected that the death was staged, and the account of [[Sol System|Pluto]], supported by the records of the [[Imperial Fists]], is now accepted. The Eskrador tradition is held by Imperial historians to be either a record of a later engagement in which Omegon, in his brother's name, was the one who fell or was feigned to fall, or a fabrication by the Alpha Legion. Which of these is correct is not recorded.

The Legion at large

The Alpha Legion divided into autonomous warbands and spread across the galaxy, hidden in asteroid fields, [[Space hulk|space hulks]] and uninhabited worlds. It raises [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]] on Imperial worlds, uses uprisings to draw Imperial forces away from its real targets, and has, to the knowledge of the [[Inquisition]], largely escaped the [[Mutant|mutation]] that afflicts other [[Traitor Legions]]. This has led some [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] to suspect a continued, hidden loyalty to the Emperor. The [[Ordo Malleus]] and [[Ordo Hereticus]] have devoted heavy resources to finding its bases. In the [[Era Indomitus]], the Imperium cannot say whether a primarch of the Legion yet lives.

Character and appearance

Contemporary accounts describe Alpharius as a masterful tactician whom the Imperial Stratagos rated alongside [[Perturabo]] and [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]. His intellect was said to be cool and watchful, like a viper's gaze. He sought the company of none of his brothers save [[Horus]], though there are reliable reports that place a primarch of the Legion on [[Caliban]] and on Terra at various times. He encouraged his commanders to act with extreme independence, and yet the Legion took pride in its unity of purpose. The contradiction was part of the design.

Records of his appearance contradict one another. In some he is of the build and face of a common legionary and passes unnoticed in their ranks; in others he is a towering figure in ornate armour with a double-bladed spear, with a likeness, in the cast of his features, to both Horus and the Emperor. The accepted explanation is that the two brothers, who were identical, used the difference deliberately.

Wargear

  • The Pythian Scales: baroque, reptilian [[Artificer armour]], faceless and fashioned after the manner of an ancient Terran myth. It turned aside blade, energy and alchemical attack.
  • The Pale Spear (Sarrisanata): a double-bladed spear that flickers out of phase with the material world and emits a howling sound when wielded. It pierces any physical defence, and leaves in living matter a bloodless wound, the flesh turning to oily smoke. It is held to be a [[Xenos|xenos artefact]] older than the rise of the [[Aeldari]].
  • A master-crafted plasma blaster.
  • Venom spheres: grenades employed only by the [[Alpha Legion]], loaded with toxin-impregnated crystalline splinters.

Legacy

Alpharius and Omegon are the only [[Primarch|primarchs]] about whom the Imperium cannot be sure of the basic facts, and the [[Alpha Legion]]'s custom of answering "I am Alpharius" in answer to an enemy's demand for its commander's name has made that uncertainty a weapon. The [[Inquisition]] regards any suspected sighting of the primarch as an event of the first order. The [[Imperial Fists]] keep the record of the Battle of [[Sol System|Pluto]], and the Ultramarines keep that of Eskrador, and the two remain unreconciled.
