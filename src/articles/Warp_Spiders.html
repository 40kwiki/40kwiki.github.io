The Warp Spiders are [[Craftworld Aeldari]] [[Aspect Warriors]] who use a personal [[teleportation]] device built into their [[Aspect Armour]]. It lets them make rapid jumps through the [[Immaterium]]. These jumps make them very hard to target. They strike suddenly and vanish before the enemy can respond. They take their name from the arachnoid creatures that guard the [[Infinity Circuit]] of their craftworlds, and they embody the idea of aggressive defence.

Origins

Like the other Warrior Aspects, the Warp Spiders trace their history to [[Asurmen]], the first [[Phoenix Lord]]. He established the first [[Aspect Shrine]], the Shrine of Asur, on the world of the same name. He trained the first [[Exarch]]s, several of whom became the Asurya, the founders of the Warrior Aspects.

The Warp Spider shrines have no known founder. The lore of the [[Kaelor]] [[craftworld]] speaks of the [[Lhykosidae]], the Wraith Spider, who may have been the first Phoenix Lord of the Aspect. The truth may not be known even to the Aeldari, which have lost it in the mists of the past.

The spiders of the Infinity Circuit

The name comes from the tiny but aggressive creatures found among the slender [[wraithbone]] trees of the [[Dome of Crystal Seers]], at the heart of every Aeldari craftworld. These sparkling creatures can move anywhere within the craftworld. They melt into the [[Infinity Circuit]] as pure [[psychic]] energy and recrystallise elsewhere in the [[Materium]]. They gather in great numbers around invasive psychic entities.

The wraithbone core of a craftworld supports psychic consciousness much as the [[Immaterium]] does. It can be corrupted by the fragments of lost [[Human]] or other non-Aeldari souls. In theory a [[Daemon]] could enter the core, although such an event would be obvious at once. The Warp Spiders' namesakes hunt and destroy alien psychic fragments, much as white blood cells attack infection.

Role in battle

Warp Spiders embody the doctrine of aggressive defence. A compact [[Warp Spider Jump Generator]] in the backpack of their armour lets squads of five to ten warriors, sometimes led by an [[Exarch]], make short jumps through the Immaterium and reappear in realspace in a blink. This makes their attacks wholly unexpected. It also leaves the warrior briefly unshielded in the [[Empyrean]], where [[Slaanesh]] hungers for Aeldari souls, so the Warp Spiders are counted the bravest of the Aspects, since they risk their souls as well as their lives.

Despite this honour they are regarded as strange and even terrifying, the furthest from the common Asuryani mindset. They have a bleak outlook and rarely mix with the warriors of other shrines.

Their ritual weapon is the [[Death Spinner]], which extrudes a cloud of razor-sharp monofilament wire. A magnetic containment field spools the wire together and hurls it at the enemy, where its own tension makes it writhe and slice through flesh. They also carry standard Craftworld weapons, including [[Power Blades]], [[Shuriken Weapons]] and [[Fusion Gun]]s.

Shrines

  • Shade Gate Shrine: its warriors wear only token flourishes of red and white and favour all-black bodysuits. They drape foes in cascades of wire and often withdraw to hidden positions to watch the struggle.
  • Slicing Skein Shrine: crimson-clad warriors who see themselves as the thread of fate that severs all others. They specialise in ambush from several directions.

Both shrines are recorded in the Aspect's shrine lists.

Exarchs

Exarchs of the Warp Spiders have honed ambush to a high degree, and teleport in on a storm of crackling psychic lightning. They also know when to withdraw and regroup for further guerrilla strikes. Some carry two Death Spinners, or a [[Spinneret Rifle]], which fires a single rigid strand of monomolecular wire that punches through armour. Many also wear a pair of forearm-mounted powerblades, which leave the hands free.

Phoenix Lord

The Phoenix Lord of the Warp Spiders is [[Lhykhis]], the "Whispering Web". She is held to embody [[Kaela Mensha Khaine]]'s willingness to risk everything for victory, and is a daredevil even among Phoenix Lords. She plunges into the Warp without fear and navigates further and more accurately through it than the oldest Exarchs. Her unique Phoenix Armour has four extra arms, two bearing Death Spinners and two ending in monofilament blades. Her most dangerous weapon is the power sword ''Vhattarkan'', or ''Weaverender'', said to be sharp enough to cut the fabric of reality.

Because Lhykhis often vanishes into the Warp for long periods, many Asuryani long believed the Aspect had no Phoenix Lord. On Kaelor the Wraith Spider, [[Lhykosidae]], was named in his place, a legendary rogue Exarch who returns every few millennia to bring justice and peace to the craftworld. Kaelorian opinion is divided as to whether he is a lost Phoenix Lord or a spiritual awakening of the Aspect itself.

Wargear

Warp Spider Aspect Armour is bulkier than that of other Aspects, which makes the wearer better protected but slower afoot, a handicap that teleportation removes. It is warded for safer travel through the Warp, which gives some protection against psychic powers in realspace. The helmet is swept back to a slight point, with a targeter that tracks several foes and an external respirator for the void and toxic air.
