Snagrod, the Arch-Arsonist of Charadon, is an [[Ork]] warlord who united the warring Ork factions bordering the [[Loki Sector]]. In 989.M41 he launched the largest [[WAAAGH!]] that the [[Peryton 163 Cluster]] had seen since the [[Great Crusade]], attacking the isolated Imperial agri-world of [[Badlanding]].

Imperial observers noted that Snagrod, although an Ork, showed unusual tactical skill. He used Imperial tactics captured or copied from his enemies, and his warbosses answer to him in the empire of Charadon.

WAAAGH! of 989.M41

Snagrod's campaign began with the conquest of the Ork worlds around Charadon and then struck into the Loki Sector. The attack on Badlanding in mid-989.M41 was the first major blow of the campaign. The Ork empire grew by conquest in the years that followed.The date of the attack on Badlanding is recorded as mid-989.M41. The course of the wider WAAAGH! is estimated from the Imperial records of the sector.
