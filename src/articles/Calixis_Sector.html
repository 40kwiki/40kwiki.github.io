The Calixis Sector, also called the Calixis Sector of the Imperium, is an Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] on the northern edge of the known [[Milky Way|galaxy]], close to the [[Eye of Terror]]. It was conquered by the [[Lord Militant]] [[Golgenna Angevin]] during the [[Angevin Crusade]] of the [[39th millennium]] and was formally constituted as a sector in 384.M39. Its worlds are among the most populous and productive of the Segmentum, although the sector lies far from the Imperium's core, and it must largely defend itself.

The sector is bounded by the [[Fydae Great Cloud]] on its trailing edge, by the [[Scarus Sector]] to spinward and the [[Ixaniad Sector]] to coreward. To rimward lie the frontier stars of the [[Halo Stars]], where the [[Koronus Expanse]] begins beyond the [[Koronus Passage]]. Calixian records describe it as a sector that must "ultimately fend for itself", since the great fleets of [[Terra]] rarely reach it.

History

Angevin Crusade

Main article: [[Angevin Crusade]]

The sector was won by the [[Angevin Crusade]], led from 322.M39 by [[Golgenna Angevin]], a praetor of the Terran Court raised to [[Lord Commander Militant|Lord Militant]]. The crusade fought in several phases, the last of which opened a second front in 359.M39. After a stalled advance and sustained Ork attacks during the [[Bleak Years]] (363–369.M39), the campaign was completed in 384.M39 with the defeat of the [[Yu'vath]] and the [[Exterminatus]] of their homeworlds.The dates of the crusade's phases are taken from Calixian chronicles, which record them in Imperial years. Dates for the early phases are estimated.

The first [[Lord Sector]] was [[Drusus]], one of Angevin's most capable generals. Drusus governed until his death in 417.M39, and the [[Imperial Cult|Adeptus Ministorum]] declared him a saint in 502.M39. Calixian Imperial accounts give his death in 367.M39 as an assassination by rivals among Angevin's generals, a claim that is not accepted by all scholars.

Early centuries

The sector's first major trials came in the [[40th millennium]]. The [[Macharian Crusade]] (387–401.M40) passed beside the sector and drew off many of its forces. In 709.M40 the [[Hive world|Hive World]] of [[Tanis]] was visited by an unknown [[Xenos|xenos]] force, and in 738–741.M40 the Hive Worlds of the [[Gelmiro Cluster]] fell into sedition during the [[War of Brass]].

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

In the 41st millennium the sector suffered many crises. [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]] flourished on [[Malfi]] under the House of Koba, whose Reign of Terror (428–479.M41) is recorded as the most brutal tyranny in the sector's history. The Khornate cult of the Pilgrims of Hayte was broken in 499.M41 at great cost. A [[Planetary classification|mining world]], [[Mara]], was abandoned in 768.M41 after it was overrun by a plague, and was quarantined by Inquisitorial edict.

The [[Tyrant Star]] first became a recorded presence in the sector in 389.M41, when the Asteroth Mining Colony in the Drusus Marches fell to rebellion after a visitation. Further sightings followed at [[Snowden's World]] in 503.M41 and at [[Zillman's Domain]] in 807.M41. The [[Inquisition]] began to describe a single prophesied threat across them.

The [[Fydae Strain]] was first confirmed on the mining colony of [[Sutters Rock]] in 724.M41, and the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] (742–745.M41) brought the sector's warfare into contact with the [[T'au Empire]]. In 814.M41 the [[Waaagh!|Ork WAAAGH!]] of Ghenghiz Grimtoof struck the Periphery, opening a spinward front that the sector's regiments fought for many years.

Geography

The sector is a loose collection of star systems clustered around old [[Warp travel|warp routes]]. Its interior is divided into named regions that appear on the Imperial charts. The Hazeroth Abyss holds the cold worlds of [[Mara]] and [[Ice Station Mara]]. The Drusus Marches is a region of frontier mining and the scene of many early Imperial conquests. The Golgenna Reach and the Josian Reach hold the sector's richest industrial worlds, while the Adrantis region is defended by the [[Scintilla]] fleets.

The sector is also crossed by the Meratis Cluster, where isolationist voider families settled in the middle of the [[38th millennium]]. Their clans seceded from the Imperium in the [[Meritech Wars]] (211–226.M41), and the cluster has remained a difficult border.

Notable worlds

The sector capital, [[Scintilla]], is a [[Hive world|Hive World]] on which the Lucid Court and the Tricorn Palace stand. Its civic life is set out in the Calixian codes, and it is the seat of the sector's [[Adeptus Arbites]] precinct.

[[Hive Tarsus]] and [[Hive Sibellus]] are the two other great Hive Worlds of the sector. Tarsus is known for its commercial houses and its trade within the hive, and Sibellus for its ship society and its castes. [[Ambulon]] is a Hive World of some note, and [[Sepheris Secundus]] is a [[Planetary classification|mining world]] whose serfs have fought several uprisings.

The sector also holds the [[Shrine world|Shrine World]] of [[Drusus]], which is a place of pilgrimage for the [[Imperial Cult]]. Many of the worlds in the sector are [[Agri-world|Agri-worlds]] or Mining Worlds that supply the Hive Worlds.

Governance

The sector is ruled in name by a [[Sector Governor|Lord Sector]] Calixis. Under the Lucid Court, the Lord Sector commands the sector's civil administration, collects the tithe for Terra and appoints the governors of the sector's worlds. The title has passed through many hands, and several Lord [[Sector|Sectors]] have been chosen from the sector's Great Houses.

The [[Calixian Conclave]] of the [[Inquisition]] meets in [[Scintilla]]. It holds the sector's [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] in a conclave shared with the [[Ordo Xenos]] and the [[Ordo Hereticus]], and it is responsible for the sector's wardens against the [[Tyrant Star]]. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] maintains a cardinal's seat in the sector capital, and the [[Adeptus Astra Telepathica]] keeps a relay station at Scintilla.

Military

The [[Battlefleet Calixis]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] guards the sector's [[Warp travel|warp routes]] and is based at [[Scintilla]]. It is one of the most important battlefleets on the [[Segmentum]]'s spinward edge. The sector's regiments, raised by the [[Departmento Munitorum]], include the [[Scintillan Protectorate]], which was shaped by the wars against the [[Orks]] and the [[Tyrant Star]].

The [[Inquisition]] has used the sector's regiments in several shadow wars, and the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of the [[Deathwatch]] have made the sector one of their watch areas. In 742.M41, the sector's fleets joined the [[Damocles Gulf Crusade]] against the [[T'au Empire|T'au]], and the sector's losses there are still recorded in its memorials.
