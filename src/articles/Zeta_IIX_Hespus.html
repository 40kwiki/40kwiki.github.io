Zeta IIX Hespus is a world in the Necron wars. It is recorded as the place where the Nihilakh [[Crypteks]] released shards of the [[C'tan]], in the middle of the [[42nd millennium]].

The release was part of a series of superweapon attacks that the Necron dynasties made against one another and against the enemies of the [[Necrons]]. Other attacks of the same war include the Ark of Oblivion at [[Torantis]] and the bound dwarf star at [[Zann]].

Release

The [[Necron dynasty|Nihilakh]] released the shards at Zeta IIX Hespus after the other superweapon attacks of the war had been made lawful by the decree of [[Szarekh]]. The sources give the order of these events but not their exact dates.The date of the release is estimated from the order of events in the sources. No record gives an exact year.
