Tsagualsa is a world that is recorded chiefly for the meeting of [[Lion El'Jonson]] and [[Konrad Curze]] in 009.M31. The encounter, known as the Tsagualsa incident, was the moment when the two primarchs came to blows. It came during the Dark Angels' campaign to delay the [[Night Lords]], which lasted almost three years, from 007 to 009.M31.

Imperial records give little detail about the world itself. Its importance comes from the primarchs' fight there.

The Tsagualsa incident

In 009.M31 Curze lured the Lion to a parley on Tsagualsa, and the two primarchs came to blows. The Lion was nearly strangled before a guardsman of the [[Dark Angels]] drove a sword through the Night Haunter's back. Imperial accounts record the fight as one of the most violent meetings between two primarchs of the Heresy.

Great Scouring

The [[Great Scouring]] was the Imperial counter-offensive that followed the death of [[Horus]] at the [[Siege of Terra]]. Its records list Curze's execution on Tsagualsa among its events. The date of that execution is not recorded.The Great Scouring records give no exact date for Curze's execution on Tsagualsa. Some accounts place it at the time of the 009.M31 fight, and others after the Siege of Terra.
