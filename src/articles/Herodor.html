Herodor is a desolate world in the [[Sabbat Worlds Sector]] that is sacred to the [[Imperial Cult]]. It is recorded as the place where the [[Imperial saint|Imperial Saint]] [[Saint Sabbat]] was believed to have been reincarnated in 773.M41. Her appearance gave a major morale boost to the forces of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]] fighting against the forces of [[Chaos]] that had overrun much of the sector.

The Ecclesiarchy holds the world sacred because Saint Sabbat, in her original crusade to conquer the [[Sabbat Worlds]] in the Emperor's name, paused there to cleanse her wounds in the River Civi.

Pilgrimage

Herodor is visited by pilgrims of the [[Imperial Cult]]. Its desolate surface and the river are kept as they were in the Saint's time, and the Ecclesiarchy records the world among its [[Shrine world|shrine worlds]] with [[Hagia]], where the Saint was born.The date of the Saint's first crusade is recorded as 500–605.M35. The reincarnation of 773.M41 is recorded in the Ecclesiarchy's registers.
