The Frateris Templar were the armies and fleets of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], the Ecclesiarchy of the [[Imperial Cult]], before the [[Age of Apostasy]] ended. An all-male order, they served as the private army of the Ecclesiarchy, putting down revolts against its authority and fighting Wars of Faith on its behalf. Under the High Lord [[Goge Vandire]] they carried out the purges of the [[Reign of Blood]]. After Vandire's fall, the [[Decree Passive]] of 378.M36 forbade the Ecclesiarchy to keep "men under arms", the new [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Sebastian Thor]] disbanded the Templar, and the [[Adepta Sororitas]] took their place.

History

Origins

The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] kept armed bands from early in its history. During the [[War of the Beast]] of 544.M32 a Templar force under Confessor-Militant Rawketh fought in the second invasion of [[Ullanor]]. These were small forces raised for particular wars.

The Templar grew into a true army after the Ecclesiarch Benedin IV moved the Holy Synod from Terra to [[Ophelia VII]], a rich world of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], to escape the control of the [[Administratum]]. Freed from the other High Lords, the Ministorum raised armies and fleets of its own, with merchant and military starships and dozens of armies each as strong as a regiment of the [[Imperial Guard]]. They rivalled the Imperium's regular forces and were hated wherever they went.The date of Benedin IV's move to Ophelia VII is not recorded. Ministorum histories place the formal founding of the Templar armies about three centuries before the Reign of Blood, which ended in 378.M36; the founding is therefore estimated at the late M35.

Age of Apostasy

Further information: [[Age of Apostasy]]

[[Goge Vandire]], [[Master of the Administratum]], took the office of [[Ecclesiarch]] in the early [[36th millennium]] and moved the Ecclesiarchy back to [[Terra]]. The Templar became the instrument of his rule. They fought Wars of Faith on his orders, burned whole populations he named as [[Heretic|heretics]] and enforced the Ecclesiarchy's tithes on the worlds nearest Terra.

When the preacher Sebastian Thor led the [[Confederation of Light]] against Vandire, Vandire gathered a great Templar army in the Clax System and sent it, with its fleet, to destroy Thor's homeworld of [[Dimmamar]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. On the way the fleet was caught in a [[Warp]] storm and lost. Thor's followers called it the [[Storm of the Emperor's Wrath]] and took it as a sign of the Emperor's judgement on Vandire.

Disbandment

After [[Alicia Dominica]] beheaded Vandire, the [[High Lords of Terra]] issued the Decree Passive in 378.M36. It forbade the Ecclesiarchy to keep men under arms, and Thor, as Ecclesiarch, disbanded the Frateris Templar. [[Daughters of the Emperor|The Daughters of the Emperor]], as women, were not covered by the decree, and from them Dominica founded the Orders Militant of the [[Adepta Sororitas]], which now defend the Ecclesiarchy and serve the [[Ordo Hereticus]].

Arms and appearance

Early Templar troops were equipped like the [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]], with carapace armour and gold-plated [[Lasgun|lasguns]] fitted with power bayonets. By the [[Age of Apostasy]] their warriors had become zealots, their personalities rebuilt around the [[Imperial Cult|Imperial Creed]]. They fought bare-chested, covered in ritual scars and tattoos, in white robes laced with gold, protected by energy shields from spinal implants, and armed with lasblasters and gold-hilted swords of haematite.

Later legacy: the Frateris militia

The [[Sebastian Thor|Decree Passive]] forbids standing armies, not the faithful. In the centuries since, the Ecclesiarchy has raised Frateris militia: bands of zealots and pilgrims who take up arms for a single crusade under a preacher and disperse afterwards. They are not permanent and so do not break the Decree.

Temple of the Saviour Emperor

The Temple of the Saviour Emperor was the cult from which the Ecclesiarchy grew, and the body the first Templar served. It was founded on Terra after the [[Horus Heresy]] by Fatidicus, a veteran officer of the [[Imperial Army]] who had fought in the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31), and it taught that the Emperor had sacrificed himself to save mankind. When Fatidicus died at the age of 120, the Temple had more than a billion followers on Terra and many more across the [[Segmentum Solar]].

By the early 32nd millennium about two-thirds of the Imperium's people followed the Temple's teaching, and it absorbed many smaller cults of the [[Lectitio Divinitatus]]. It was recognised in M32 as the state church under the title of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]]; the Ecclesiarch Veneris II gained a seat among the High Lords in the middle of the millennium. Cults that would not join it were destroyed in the first Wars of Faith. In the [[War of the Beast]] of 544.M32 the Temple's armed followers, the first Frateris Templar, fought under its banner.

After the Age of Apostasy, Sebastian Thor's reforms replaced the Temple's doctrine that the Ecclesiarchy should rule every part of Imperial life. Those who held to the old ways were condemned as heretics of the Temple Tendency, and the name survives in Imperial law only as a mark of heresy.The year of the Temple's founding is not recorded. It is placed in the decades after the Siege of Terra; Fatidicus' long life is the only measure of its early growth.
