A Pariah, also called a Blank, Untouchable, psychic Null or Blacksoul, is a [[Humanity|human]] born with a mutation that leaves them with no presence in the [[Immaterium]]. Their mind does not show in the Warp, so they appear to have no [[soul]]. Pariahs are the opposite of [[psyker]]s, whose souls burn like beacons in the Warp.

A Pariah's presence cuts psychic power in their vicinity. Psykers nearby find their powers weakened or silenced. Pariahs are immune to direct psychic attacks, though a psyker can still harm them with telekinesis or other indirect means. Pariahs are also immune to [[Daemonic possession]] and to corruption by [[Chaos]]. Daemons find them painful to be near, and some are driven back into the Warp just to escape.

Blanks and true Pariahs

The term Blank covers two kinds of person. A true Blank carries the gene in its recessive form. Their aura is weak, and most people pass it off as strangeness, so Blanks often live quiet lives. A Pariah carries the dominant form. Their aura is strong enough to make ordinary people uneasy and hostile, and it can be fatal to psykers close by. Pariahs are often killed at birth, but they have always been sought after by the Imperium.

Imperial use

The [[Sisters of Silence]] were the best known Imperial users of Pariahs. Their order hunted rogue psykers during the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. After the Heresy the Imperium turned against them, and they were lost to history for nearly 1,500 years. They were reinstated in 999.M41, when [[Roboute Guilliman]] was resurrected during the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]].

The [[Culexus Temple]] of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] trains Pariahs as assassins who are a terror to every psyker. The [[Ordo Malleus]] uses Blanks as living weapons against Daemons. Inquisitors of the [[Ordo Hereticus]] keep them as [[Acolyte]]s, who protect their master from witchcraft. The Ordo Sinister used Blank commanders to direct the psykers bound into its [[Psi-Titan]]s during the Great Crusade and the Heresy.

No Space Marine has ever been recorded as a Pariah. Some scholars link this to the psychic nature of [[gene-seed]], which might kill a Pariah if implanted. A Space Marine can still become a Pariah after implantation, as the [[Ferrymen]] of the [[Grey Knights]] show.

The Pariah Gene

The biology behind the Pariah Gene has proved elusive. Testing has found no single gene. During the early Great Crusade, the Imperial Archeotechnologist Corps and the [[Mechanicum]] tried to isolate and exploit the trait, and the attempts ended in disaster. The Emperor then decreed a moratorium on the study of the psychic Null, except for His own experiments. Most attempts to synthesise or weaponise it failed.

Notable Pariahs

[[Iota]] was a replica of the Culexus Clade, created in a laboratory. She was sent with the first Imperial [[Execution Force]] to assassinate the Traitor Primarch [[Horus]] on Dagonet, and she died there. "Spear" was a former Imperial Assassin and a Human Untouchable who was augmented by the Culexus Clade. He was later bonded to a Daemon by the [[Dark Apostle]] [[Erebus]] and sent to kill the Emperor. He was destroyed on Dagonet. [[Ferik Jurgen]] was an Imperial Guardsman who served as aide to [[Ciaphas Cain]] and saved his commissar more than once by dampening psychic powers.
