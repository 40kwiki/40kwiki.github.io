Mordian is an Imperial [[Hive World]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] portion of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It is the homeworld of the [[Mordian Iron Guard]], the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] that are known for their discipline. Many of the Imperium's enemies have underestimated these colourfully uniformed troops, and they have found them to be among the toughest and most uncompromising of the [[Emperor]]'s soldiers.

Mordian is tidally locked with its star. Its day is the same length as its year. Like [[Luna]], one half of the planet lies in perpetual darkness, while the other is scorched by the sun and is too hot to settle. All of the [[Human]] settlements are on the dark side. This is why the world is called the "World of Eternal Night".

Society

The inhabitants of Mordian are often dour and grim, shaped by the perpetual dark. Imperial reports say that the suicide rate is the highest in the Imperium since the destruction of [[Nostramo]]. Its billions live in vast [[hive cities]] that rise as pyramidal, multi-levelled towers of steel and ceramic. All resources are controlled and rationed by the Mordian state. The land surface is barely a tenth of the size of [[Terra]]. The air of the cities is often hot and stagnant.

The planet is ruled by an oligarchy known as the "Tetrarchy". It strictly controls and rations the scarce resources of the world. Scarcity and repression breed discontent, and the Mordian Iron Guard is often the only force that keeps order from giving way to anarchy. When not sent to war zones, the regiments spend much of their time keeping the populace in line.

Battle of Mordian

Mordian is one of the few worlds that the [[Inquisition]] records as having been won back from the armies of Chaos at the moment of their success. The date is not recorded.

One hot summer, hotter than usual even for Mordian, a conclave of men and women met in secret beneath the streets of the capital, [[Vanandra]]. They knew the wealth of the world and wanted it for themselves. They called on the [[Dark Gods]] with a great sorcerous rite, and the planet seethed with unrest as it neared completion. Then the sky erupted into flame. Warships came from the [[Eye of Terror]], [[Chaos Space Marines]] poured into Vanandra, and [[Daemon]]s stalked the burning towers. The Tetrarchs ordered their [[astropath]]s to call for help, and the strength of Chaos was such that the astropaths' minds melted under the strain.

The Iron Guard fought a stubborn resistance. They withdrew from the rest of the planet and fortified the capital, turning every building into a fortress and every street into a killing zone. They threw back the Chaos Space Marines, and then the cultists who rose out of the sewers. Daemons of [[Khorne]], [[Slaanesh]] and [[Tzeentch]] followed, and then [[Greater Daemon]]s of [[Nurgle]] with [[Plague Marines]] of the [[Death Guard]]. Street by street the Iron Guard fell back to the Tetrarchal Palace, the last strongpoint on the world.

At the last moment the sorcery that held the invaders in the material world failed. The sky darkened and the daemons dissolved. The Iron Guard had bought the time that Imperial [[psyker]]s needed to weave a counter-spell from beyond the orbit of the world. The remaining Chaos forces lost heart, and the Iron Guard drove them from the planet. The regiments came to be reckoned among the finest in the [[Astra Militarum]].

Stygius Crusade

After the birth of the [[Great Rift]], following the [[13th Black Crusade]] of 999.M41, [[Tzeentch]] set out to claim star systems of its own and chose the [[Stygius Sector]]. The [[Stygius Crusade]] began with cult uprisings on a score of worlds. Invasion followed once each planet was in revolt, led by [[Magnus the Red]] and his [[Thousand Sons]], the Greater Daemon [[M'Kachen]] and the [[Coven of Nine]]. Three systems were swiftly conquered. Of the worlds attacked, only Mordian held out.

Seven sub-sectors fell before Imperial Chapters and [[Skitarii]] arrived. The siege of Mordian was relieved in a long campaign, and it was followed by six major clashes in the [[Dhobash System]], where the Thousand Sons met the [[Space Wolves]]. Initial Imperial gains were lost, and only the arrival of an [[Asuryani]] warhost from [[Craftworld]] [[Ulthwe]] at [[Rimenok]] allowed an orderly withdrawal.

Military role

The Iron Guard are drawn from the world's populations. The regiments are fiercely loyal to the prosecution of the Emperor's enemies. They are deployed chiefly in the [[Segmentum Solar]] and the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], and their chief skills are in suppressing rebellion and in urban warfare. Newly raised regiments often have several standard years of experience in riot control and insurrection before they leave the world.
