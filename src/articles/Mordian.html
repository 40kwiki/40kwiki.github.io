Mordian is an Imperial [[Hive World]] in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] portion of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. It is the homeworld of the [[Mordian Iron Guard]], the regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] that are known for their discipline. Many of the Imperium's enemies have underestimated these colourfully uniformed troops, and they have found them to be among the toughest and most uncompromising of the [[Emperor]]'s soldiers.

Mordian is tidally locked with its star. Its day is the same length as its year. Like [[Luna]], one half of the planet lies in perpetual darkness, while the other is scorched by the sun. All of the [[Human]] settlements are on the dark side. This is why the world is called the "World of Eternal Night".

Society

The inhabitants of Mordian are often dour and grim, shaped by the perpetual dark. Its billions live in vast [[hive cities]] that rise as pyramidal, multi-levelled towers of steel and ceramic. All resources are controlled and rationed by the Mordian state. The land surface is barely a tenth of the size of [[Terra]]. The air of the cities is often hot and stagnant.

The planet is ruled by an oligarchy known as the "Tetrarchy". It strictly controls the scarce resources of the world. Scarcity and repression breed discontent, and the Mordian Iron Guard is often the only force that keeps order from giving way to anarchy.

Military role

The Iron Guard are drawn from the world's populations. The regiments are fiercely loyal to the prosecution of the Emperor's enemies. Their chief skills are in suppressing rebellion and in urban warfare. Newly raised regiments often have several standard years of experience in riot control and insurrection before they leave the world.
