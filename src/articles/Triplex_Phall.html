Triplex Phall is a major [[Forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Triplex System]] of the [[Triplex Sector]], in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It is home to the [[Legio Victorum]] [[Titan Legions|Titan Legion]], the "Foe Slayers". The world lies in the far east of the Imperium, in the [[Eastern Fringe]], and has grown largely self-sufficient through long periods of isolation.

During the [[Second Tyrannic War]] in 992.M41, the world was among the first in the Segmentum to come under the shadow of the [[Hive Fleet Kraken]]. Its defenders held, but the cost was high.

History

For much of the [[Great Crusade]], Triplex Phall helped mass-produce arms and equipment for the armies of the expanding [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Its forges were among the most productive in the Eastern Fringe.

The Horus Heresy

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

When the [[Horus Heresy]] broke out, the Triplex Sector became a battleground between the [[Night Lords]] and the [[Dark Angels]]. The [[Night Lords]] had been sent by [[Horus]] to destroy the region's industry, which he could not control and which could supply Loyalist forces against his advance on [[Terra]]. The campaign that followed is known as the [[Thramas Crusade]].The Thramas Crusade is dated 007 to 009.M31, as on the Triplex System page.

During the campaign the Night Lords destroyed the Forge Worlds of Triplex Galatia and Triplex Thule and badly damaged Triplex Phall. The three worlds had supplied most of the Loyalist warship capacity in the Ultima Segmentum. Triplex Phall went on to supply the advanced las weapon designs used by the [[Solar Auxilia]] during the rest of the war.

Recent history

The Tech-priests of Triplex Phall have recovered many lost colonies settled in the [[Age of Technology]]. Some were [[Knight world|Knight Worlds]] whose noble houses left caches of old designs and [[archeotech]]. Few of these secrets have been shared with [[Mars]], and the Red Planet has responded by stationing [[Skitarii]] legions with the world's [[Explorator]] fleets.

During the [[Plague Wars]] around 012.M42, the Forge World was cut off by the warp storms that split the galaxy. It was attacked by a splinter of the [[Hive Fleet Kraken]], by a [[Plague Fleet]] led by [[Typhus]], and by a [[Daemon]] incursion. All three landings were driven back before the enemy could establish a foothold. The Forge World then sent forces to [[Ultramar]] at the call of [[Roboute Guilliman]].

Forces and industry

The world keeps its own Skitarii forces to protect its interests and to fight at the front of the Quest for Knowledge. Its Tech-priests and Skitarii wear dark grey robes lined with the deep crimson of [[Mars]], with pale yellow cog-teeth trim at the hoods, sleeves and hems.

The forges are best known for the [[Sentinel]] walkers issued to the [[Astra Militarum]]. These include a utility powerlifter pattern and a rocket-armed pattern. Many weapons and vehicles in Imperial service also carry Triplex Phall designs, and the fabrication secrets behind them have never been released to Mars.
