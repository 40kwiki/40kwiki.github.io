The Hrud, also known as the Nocturnal Warriors of Hrud, are an intelligent alien species of the [[Milky Way]]. They prefer to live under the surface of their worlds. The Imperium records them as a xenos people of the [[Xenos|xenos]] classes that are dangerous to all other life.

The Hrud are feared for an ability that Imperial scholars have not fully explained. It appears to accelerate the ageing of any living being, or to push the body further along the decline of its own life. The ability is thought to be Warp-based, although no [[Psyker]] has been studied in full.

Imperial encounters

Imperial contact with the Hrud is recorded in a small number of reports. These describe ambushes in tunnels and caverns, where Imperial troops aged rapidly and died within days. The [[Officio Assassinorum]] has been ordered to avoid engagement and report all sightings to the [[Ordo Xenos]].
