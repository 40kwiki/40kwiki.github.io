The Crozius Arcanum is the power weapon carried by a [[Chaplain]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It is both a weapon and the badge of his office. It takes the form of a mace or staff topped by a winged skull or [[Aquila (symbol)|aquila]], and it is given to the Chaplain at his ordination.

Chaplains are recognised by their black armour, their skull-faced helms, the Crozius Arcanum and the [[Rosarius]], a protective amulet that marks their rank. Unlike priests of the [[Imperial Cult]], they do not usually worship the Emperor as a god, but venerate him as the father of their [[Primarch]] and the [[Emperor of Mankind|master of mankind]].

Use

In combat the Crozius is used as a power mace, and it is held to be a sacred object in its own right. Imperial accounts describe [[Chaplain|Chaplains]] who fought with it in close combat against the [[Chaos Space Marines]] and the [[Tyranids]]. The weapon is passed to a successor when its bearer dies, and the Chapter keeps the earlier weapons in its reliquary.The custom of passing the weapon is recorded in Chapter doctrine. The sources do not give dates for individual Crozius weapons.
