The Knights of Byzantium are a loyalist [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a successor of the [[Iron Hands]], raised in the [[Ultima Founding]] of c. 999.M41. They are composed entirely of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. In battle they bring as much firepower to bear on the enemy as they can and employ many [[Dreadnought]]s, which they venerate deeply, as the Iron Hands do.The Founding date is given as c. 999.M41 in the Imperial record, the start of the Ultima Founding. The Chapter's first actions are not dated, and no campaign has yet been recorded under its name.

History

The Chapter was raised in the opening years of the [[Era Indomitus]], when the [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris Marines]] made by [[Belisarius Cawl]] were formed into new [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] under [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The Knights of Byzantium are among the Chapters drawn from the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of [[Ferrus Manus]]. Their badge, a hippocampus, is not that of any other Chapter of the line.

Imperial records give no campaign or [[Chapter Master]] for the Knights of Byzantium. The Chapter is known only through the appearance of its battle-brothers and its stated doctrine of overwhelming fire, a doctrine it shares with the [[Iron Hands]].

The Iron Hands were nearly destroyed at [[Isstvan V]] in 005.M31, when [[Ferrus Manus]] was killed, and the [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] of the surviving Legion are among those most revered by its Iron Council. The Knights of Byzantium keep the same respect for the Dreadnoughts of their own ranks. The [[Ultima Founding]] gave them the Primaris armour and weapons that the Iron Hands' successors had never before been able to field in such numbers, and their battle-brothers carry the heavy firepower of the new patterns into the field.

Doctrine

As an all-Primaris Chapter, the Knights of Byzantium are fielded in the roles of the modern Codex: battleline, close support, fire support, veteran and command. Their commanders favour fire support, in which many guns concentrate their shells on one point, and keep their [[Dreadnought|Dreadnoughts]] in the line as long as the machines can be maintained. A Dreadnought is treated as a living brother and is accorded the honours of his rank.

Organisation and heraldry

The Chapter wears purple armour with black insets and bronze shoulder trim. The chest [[Aquila]] is bronze, and a white marking on the right shoulder shows a battle-brother's role: battleline, fire support, close support, veteran or command. A small white [[High Gothic]] numeral on the right knee guard gives the company. It is not known how squads are numbered. Sergeants wear bronze helmets, veteran sergeants have white insets and black specialty marks, and officers have golden helmets. Veterancy is shown by a bronze left gauntlet. The badge is a white hippocampus, a creature with the upper body of a horse and the lower body of a fish.

Notable members

No individual of the Chapter is named in Imperial records.
