The Scarus Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], close to the [[Eye of Terror]] and adjacent to the [[Calixis Sector]] to spinward. It takes its name from Captain Scarus, a [[Word Bearers]] officer of the [[Serrated Suns]] who was lost with his ship during a [[Warp storm|warp storm]] in the [[Pilgrimage of Lorgar]]. The sector has been the theatre of some of the Imperium's most notable Inquisitorial campaigns and of several long wars against the [[Orks]].

The sector's worlds are linked by a web of [[Warp travel|warp routes]], and most are held by Imperial governors under the [[Administratum]]. Its capital is the [[Hive world|Hive World]] of [[Eustis Majorus]], and its [[Sub-sector|sub-sectors]] are named for their principal worlds. The sector is important to the Imperium because it lies on the approach to the Calixis Sector and to [[Warp|the warp]] storms of the Eye.

History

Great Crusade and Heresy

The region that became the Scarus Sector was discovered during the middle years of the [[Great Crusade]], in the late [[30th millennium]]. The [[Primarch]] [[Lorgar]] named it for Captain Scarus, whose strike cruiser Shield of Scarus and two other vessels were lost in a savage [[Warp storm|warp storm]] during the Pilgrimage of Lorgar, about forty standard years before the [[Horus Heresy]].Accounts of the Pilgrimage differ in their dates. The date given here follows the sector's own chronicles.

The Helican Schism

The [[Helican Sub-sector]] was the site of the Helican Schism, a wave of rebellion, terrorism and sabotage that swept the region in the early [[41st millennium]]. Its causes were disputed at the time, and Imperial records of the period were later sealed by Inquisitorial edict.

Siege of Vraks

Main article: [[Siege of Vraks]]

The Scarus Sector was the scene of the protracted [[Siege of Vraks]] from 813.M41 until 830.M41, when Chaos forces laid siege to the Imperial world of Vraks. The siege drew in Inquisitorial agents, regiments of the [[Astra Militarum]] and a number of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and it is still remembered among the sector's garrisons.

Eisenhorn and Ravenor

The sector has long been the principal theatre of operations for the [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] [[Gregor Eisenhorn]] and [[Gideon Ravenor]]. Their actions against the [[Chaos cult|Chaos cults]] and [[Xenos|xenos]] conspiracies of the sector are recorded in the sealed archives of the [[Ordo Xenos]], and Eisenhorn's memoirs make the sector one of the best-known in Imperial literature.

The Ork wars of the Era Indomitus

In the [[Era Indomitus]], Ork WAAAGHs have overrun much of the sector's south and east. The Green Kroosade, which Imperial strategists regard as a full-scale [[Waaagh!|WAAAGH!]], has taken the weapon forges of Mordax and turned them to the building of Gargants, while Ork Mekaniaks race to complete their machines on the plains of Mordax daily. [[Chaos Space Marines]] of the [[Night Lords]] and the [[Black Legion]] are reported to have aided the [[Orks]] in their advance.

Sub-sectors

Main article: [[Sub-sector]]

The Helican Sub-sector is the sector's most disturbed region, and it holds the Imbrium, Quinx and [[Thracian Primaris]] systems. The Angelus Sub-sector is a frontier region along the Ulant approach. The Antimar, Kerak and Ophidian Sub-sectors form the sector's rimward ring of worlds. The Vincies Sub-sector is lost, and its worlds are no longer in contact with the Imperium.

Notable worlds

[[Eustis Majorus]] is a [[Hive world|Hive World]] and the sector capital. [[Thracian Primaris]] is one of the few worlds of the [[Helican Sub-sector]] that has held firm against the [[Orks]], and [[Elnaur Delta]] has also held out. [[Lethe Eleven]] and [[Mordax]] are now Ork-held, and Mordax's weapon forges are reported to be building Gargants.

[[Gudrun]] and [[Nysa Stromolo]] stand on the brink of anarchy. [[Vagera]] is a [[Planetary classification|frontier world]] of the Ulant approach, and [[Ulant]] is a Hive World in the sector's north-east.

Governance

The sector is governed by a [[Sector Governor]] from Eustis Majorus, who collects the tithe and answers to the [[Administratum]]. The [[Imperial Navy|Battlefleet]] Scarus of the Imperial Navy guards its [[Warp travel|warp routes]], and it has frequently been stretched by the Ork campaigns.

The [[Ordo Xenos]] and [[Ordo Hereticus]] keep their [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] in the sector, and the [[Inquisition]]'s campaigns there are often coordinated with those of the [[Calixian Conclave]].

Military

The sector's regiments are raised by the [[Departmento Munitorum]] and fight largely in the Ork wars. Their losses at Mordax and [[Gudrun]] are recorded in the Officio Militaris. The sector's [[Adeptus Astartes]] are drawn from a number of [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]], several of whose homeworlds lie beyond the Imperium's borders.
