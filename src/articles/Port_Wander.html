Port Wander is a massive [[void station]] on the outer edge of the [[Drusus Marches]] in the [[Calixis Sector]]. It sits in the Rubycon II System and is the last major Imperial stop before the [[Koronus Expanse]], the region of the [[Halo Stars]] that lies beyond the sector.

The station is administered from the sector's way stations, and it is one of the few places where the Imperium's writ runs without a planetary government. Its population is transitory: traders, spies, merchant factors, pilgrims and missionaries pass through it on the way to the Expanse.

Role

Port Wander is the principal supply point for ships entering the Koronus Expanse. [[Rogue Trader]] houses keep factors at the station and register their charters there. Its records describe the Expanse as untamed, and the station is the place where expeditions are fitted out and where their losses are recorded.The number of houses and their charters at Port Wander varies by year. Charter records from the late M41 period are incomplete.
