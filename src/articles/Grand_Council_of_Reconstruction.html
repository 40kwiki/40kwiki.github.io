The Grand Council of Reconstruction was the temporary ruling body that governed the [[Imperium of Mankind]] after the [[Siege of Terra]]. It was established in 014.M31 after the Loyalist victory. The Council was the precursor to the present [[Senatorum Imperialis]] and to the emergence of the [[High Lords of Terra]], which took place before the mid-32nd millennium (estimated).

Background

After the Loyalist victory at the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, the Emperor lay unresponsive. He had been placed in the life-support mechanisms of the modified [[Golden Throne]]. [[Malcador the Sigillite]], the [[Imperial Regent]], had been reduced to ash while taking the Emperor's place on that device. The Imperium was leaderless, politically broken and largely in ruin. Many of its worlds were still under the control of the Traitors.

Formation

The old War Council and the civilian Council of Terra had ruled the Imperium in the Emperor's name. The surviving Loyalist primarchs reorganised them into a single council. It was to be a temporary government for the restoration of political stability and the rebuilding of Imperial worlds. It was also to act as a war council, directing the Great Scouring of the Traitor forces still in Imperial space.

The Council was called together on the orders of [[Rogal Dorn]], the Praetorian of Terra, and [[Roboute Guilliman]]. It met soon after the Traitor forces had fled Terra following the death of [[Horus]] at the hands of the Emperor. Every surviving Loyalist primarch sat on it. [[Corvus Corax]] of the [[Raven Guard]] chose to remain outside the emerging Imperial command structure.

Controversy

The Council's membership was controversial at the time. Many unenhanced humans blamed the primarchs and their [[Space Marine Legions]] for the bloodshed of the Heresy. They wanted to reduce the political role of the Legions in the new Imperium. During the Great Crusade, the primarchs had ruled the military with the Emperor's sanction. On the Grand Council, baseline humans held far more say over decisions than they had before.

Membership

The Council included [[Vulkan]], [[Leman Russ]], [[Lion El'Jonson]] and [[Jaghatai Khan]], together with the Captain-General of the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and other Loyalist leaders. Its debates were often long and divided. Imperial records describe a council under pressure from the wartime emergency, from the Great Scouring, and from the need to rebuild.
