Kadak Mir is the [[Paternoval Envoy]] of the [[Navis Nobilite]], the Navigator houses' representative on the [[High Lords of Terra]]. He has held the envoy's seat since c. 000.M42, and he reports to the [[Paternova]], the head of the Navigator houses.

The envoy's seat is permanent, and it gives the Navigators a voice on the High Lords in matters of [[Warp travel|warp travel]] and the Astronomican. Mir is one of the few High Lords whose office has a fixed link to the Navigator houses rather than to the Imperial bureaucracy.

Office

The [[Paternoval Envoy]] speaks for the [[Navis Nobilite|Navigator]] houses at the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]' council, and reports to the Paternova on the council's decisions. The office is recorded in the High Lords' lists alongside the other permanent seats, including the [[Lord Commander Militant]] and the [[Lord High Admiral]].
