Chaos Spawn
[[Mutant]] creature of [[Chaos]]
OriginA mortal of any intelligent race, corrupted by the [[Ruinous Powers]]
MindMindless
PatronUsually one of the [[Chaos Gods]]

A Chaos Spawn is a mindless [[Mutant|mutant]] creature that was once a member of an intelligent mortal race. Through long servitude to [[Chaos]], it has become so changed that it has lost its reason and devolved into a twisted and insane abomination. Those who seek immortality and power through daemonhood in service to the [[Ruinous Powers]] are the source of most spawn.

Spawn are unpredictable and violent. They attack whatever they meet, including their own former followers, and they cannot be commanded. Imperial records describe them as the failed form of a cult member who was not favoured enough to become a [[Daemon]], and they are often found on the battlefields where [[Chaos Space Marines]] have fought.
