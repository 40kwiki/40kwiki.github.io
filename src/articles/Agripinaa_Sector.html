Agripinaa Sector is an Imperial sector of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]], lying to the galactic north of [[Terra]]. It borders the [[Eye of Terror]] and lies close to the [[Cadia|fallen Fortress World of Cadia]]. Its worlds supplied the Imperial front at the [[Cadian Gate]] and were among the first to suffer when the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] broke out in 999.M41.

Geography

The sector is one of the Imperial regions closest to the [[Cadian Gate]], the only predictably stable [[Warp route]] that leads out of the [[Eye of Terror]] into Imperial space. The Imperial Navy orbital shipyards for the sector are located on the [[Forge World]] of [[Agripinaa]], whose industry was devoted almost wholly to the making of ammunition for the Cadian front.

History

The Thirteenth Black Crusade

In 999.M41, the forces of Chaos opened their attack without warning. Plague-ridden [[Space hulk|space hulks]] entered Imperial space in large numbers. Many were destroyed by the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]], but some escaped and spread disease through the sector. The worlds of the sector were among the first to see hordes of infected dead, which the Imperial populace came to call the "Plague of Unbelief". Its popular name reflects a belief that it struck only those who lacked faith in the [[God-Emperor]].

The Imperial fleet was pulled thin. A small force of Imperial Navy ships held back a larger Chaos fleet at the [[Ilthirium Belt]], which bought time for [[Battlefleet Agripinaa]] to arrive, but it also left the sector open to invasion. The defence was carried by the Space Marine Chapters that broke through the [[Warp storm]]s to join the battle. Imperial sources record that [[Regu Hane]] led a counter-attack that drove back a host of [[Khorne]]'s daemons, and was later canonised as a saint of the Imperial Cult.

Imperial accounts differ on the role of the [[Aeldari]] [[Harlequins]], who intervened in the fighting. The [[Word Bearers]] Dark Apostle [[Erebus]] is said to have caused the [[Warp storm]] known as [[Baphomael]] to surge over the outer systems.

By the end of the war, the worlds of [[Albitern]], [[Amistel]], [[Lelithar]], [[Malm's Reach]], [[Ulthor]] and [[Yayor]] lay in ruins. [[Typhus]] of the [[Death Guard]] took the blasted world of Ulthor as the capital of a plague-ridden realm of his own. Imperial sources warn that the loss of the Agri-worlds Yayor, Ulthor and Dentor leaves the sector's populace at risk of famine.
