Elara's Veil is a large crimson [[nebula]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]], and an isolated sector of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is named after the legendary hero Elara, a figure in the myths of the [[Nemeton]] peoples. It contains twenty human-settled worlds, which are protected by the [[Adeptus Vaelarii]], the "Sentinels of the Veil".

Geography

The nebula gives its name to the sector and gives the stars a red cast. The local peoples believe it is the funeral veil of Elara, the Emperor's bride, and that the Emperor weeps for her, which causes the constant rains of [[Nemeton]].

History

Elara's historical origin is unclear. She may have been an Imperial commander or a saint of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] who first brought the region into Imperial compliance during the [[Great Crusade]] in the late 30th millennium. The Adeptus Vaelarii were once three Chapters, the Emperor's Spears, the Celestial Lions and the Star Scorpions, but the Star Scorpions were destroyed and corrupted by Chaos.

At the formation of the [[Great Rift]] at the dawn of the [[Era Indomitus]], Elara's Veil was cut off from the light of the [[Astronomican]] and became part of the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. The sector was beset by the Chaos cult called the [[Exilarchy]], which included Heretic Astartes who had once been Star Scorpions and had formed a warband called [[The Pure]] to claim their former home sector for the Dark Gods. After the Star Scorpions returned from the Warp as The Pure, the Emperor's Spears and Celestial Lions carried out an Exterminatus on their former homeworld [[Khamun-Sen]], which killed all three billion of its people. [[Elysium IX]], the homeworld of the Celestial Lions, later fell to the Exilarchy, and Chapter Master Ekene Dubaku evacuated as many of its people as he could.

Because of the temporal instability of the Great Rift, nearly a century of fighting passed in the Veil by local reckoning with no contact with the wider Imperium. During the [[Indomitus Crusade]], [[Roboute Guilliman]] sent Lieutenant Commander Amadeus Kaias Incarius of the [[Mentors]] across the Rift to learn the state of the worlds of the Veil.
