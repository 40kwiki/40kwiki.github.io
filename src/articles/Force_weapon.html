Force weapons are psychically attuned close combat weapons. Their power comes from the mind of the psyker who wields them, not from a machine. A Force weapon channels the wielder's energy from the [[Warp]] into the target, so a single blow can destroy large [[alien]] beasts and [[Daemon]]s that shrug off conventional weapons.

Force weapons are made as swords, spears, warhammers, axes and staves. Each one contains a psi-convector, a serpentine device that focuses psychic energy. The device sometimes shows as a decorative pattern on the blade.

Making and attunement

A Force weapon is hand-crafted to its user's specifications, often by the user. Once the weapon is complete, the psyker must attune it, learning to channel power through it without harming allies or their surroundings. Even Librarians of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] find this difficult. A mastered weapon is bound so closely to its wielder's mind that another psyker can barely use it. Many are buried or cremated with their owner.

A non-psyker can wield a Force weapon, but it then has none of its powers. A [[Power Weapon|power weapon]] draws its strength from machinery. A Force weapon draws its strength from the mind. The most skilled psykers can lend their weapon to another person and still channel power through it without touching it.

Forms and variants

The basic Force weapon is a sword, axe or staff whose design reflects the culture of its user. The Force axe is carried by Librarians who serve as [[Terminator]]s in psychically attuned armour. The Force hammer, or [[Daemonhammer]], is the signature weapon of the [[Ordo Malleus]]. A Force rod stores psychic energy that its wielder can draw on in combat. The Force staff is a favourite of Librarians, [[Sanctioned Psykers]] and [[Chaos Sorcerer]]s.

The [[Grey Knights]] use the [[Nemesis Force Weapon]], which is tuned to the psychic strength of its wielder. The [[Aeldari]] carry [[Witchblade]]s and [[Singing Spear]]s, grown from wraithbone. Aeldari Force weapons are often finer in craft than Imperial ones, although their principle is the same.

Notable weapons

Several Force weapons are famous. Inquisitor [[Gregor Eisenhorn]] carried the Runestaff, a steel staff topped with an electrum sunburst that holds a psy-reactive crystal skull. The [[Black Staff of Ahriman]] focuses the power of [[Ahriman]] of the [[Thousand Sons]]. [[Varro Tigurius]], Chief Librarian of the [[Ultramarines]], carried the Rod of Tigurius, which let him strike almost without missing in close combat. [[Mephiston]] of the [[Blood Angels]] wields the Force sword Vitarus, which bursts into crimson flame in battle. The Traitor's Bane of the [[Dark Angels]] is said to trap the souls of the Fallen Angels it slays.
