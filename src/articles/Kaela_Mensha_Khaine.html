Kaela Mensha Khaine, which means "Khaine the Bloody-handed" in the Aeldari tongue and is usually called simply Khaine, is the [[Aeldari]] god of war, wrath, destruction and murder. He is one of only three gods who survived the [[Fall of the Aeldari]]. In the old Aeldari pantheon he was second in power only to his brother [[Asuryan]], the Phoenix King who led the pantheon, and he was usually depicted as the enemy of [[Vaul]], the smith god.

Khaine is the most violent and reckless of the Aeldari gods. Imperial accounts say that Asuryan was so appalled by Khaine's murder of [[Eldanesh]], the first and greatest of the mortal Aeldari, that he cursed his brother. Khaine's hands were made to drip eternally with Eldanesh's blood, so that every Aeldari would remember the crime. From this curse the title "Bloody-handed" was taken.

War in Heaven

Aeldari tradition holds that Isha, goddess of the harvest and healing, asked for a barrier between mortals and the gods because she grieved for the destruction Khaine caused among her children. Asuryan built that barrier, but some Aeldari gods then sought a way through it. The smith god Vaul was ordered to forge a set of blades for Khaine. Vaul substituted a mortal-made blade for the last one, but Khaine discovered the trick and cried out in rage, and the conflict known as the [[War in Heaven]] followed.

The War in Heaven divided the gods into the faction of Khaine and the faction of Vaul. Gods changed sides, and the war in the Immaterium was mirrored in a struggle between the mortal Aeldari. Asuryan himself took no side and watched the carnage. Vaul later reforged the final blade.

The Avatar of Khaine

Khaine manifests in battle as the [[Avatar of Khaine]], a huge armoured form that strikes down the foes of the Aeldari. The Avatar is seen as the living incarnation of the Bloody-handed God, and it appears where the Aeldari are at their most desperate. Imperial records describe the Avatar in the [[Eye of Terror]] during the pilgrimage of [[Lorgar]], who faced one in the wake of the Fall.

Many Imperial scholars believe Khaine embodies the Aeldari's passion for battle and war. Whether he was once a mortal hero who ascended to divinity is not recorded. His nature within the cosmology of the Warhammer 40,000 setting is described as undefined and contradictory. Because he interacts with the [[Chaos Gods]], he appears to be an entity of the [[Warp]], although not one like the Ruinous Powers.

Aspects and cults

The [[Aspect Warriors]] of the Craftworlds each embody a single aspect of Khaine, and the [[Phoenix Lord]]s are the founders of those aspects. The [[Drukhari]] of the Dark City also honour Khaine in their own dark rites. The [[Howling Banshees]] are said by Aeldari legend to owe their Aspect to a bargain between Khaine and the goddess [[Morai-Heg]].
