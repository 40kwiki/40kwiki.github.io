Kaela Mensha Khaine, which means "Khaine the Bloody-handed" in the Aeldari tongue and is usually called simply Khaine, is the [[Aeldari]] god of war, wrath, destruction and murder. He is one of only three gods who survived the [[Fall of the Aeldari]]. In the old Aeldari pantheon he was second in power only to his brother [[Asuryan]], the Phoenix King who led the pantheon, and he was usually depicted as the enemy of [[Vaul]], the smith god.

Khaine is the most violent and reckless of the Aeldari gods. Imperial accounts say that Asuryan was so appalled by Khaine's murder of [[Eldanesh]], the first and greatest of the mortal Aeldari, that he cursed his brother. Khaine's hands were made to drip eternally with Eldanesh's blood, so that every Aeldari would remember the crime. From this curse the title "Bloody-handed" was taken.

War in Heaven

Aeldari tradition holds that Isha, goddess of the harvest and healing, asked for a barrier between mortals and the gods because she grieved for the destruction Khaine caused among her children. Asuryan built that barrier, but some Aeldari gods then sought a way through it. When [[Asuryan]] learned that the gods were again in contact with mortals, he gave Isha and her husband [[Kurnous]] to Khaine to do with as he wished. Khaine held them captive until [[Vaul]] bargained with him: Vaul would forge 100 enchanted swords for Khaine within a year, and Khaine would free the two gods. Vaul finished all but the last blade and substituted a mortal-made one for it. Khaine discovered the trick and cried out in rage, and the conflict known as the [[War in Heaven (Aeldari)|War in Heaven]] followed.

The War in Heaven divided the gods into the faction of Khaine and the faction of Vaul. Gods changed sides, and the war in the Immaterium was mirrored in a struggle between the mortal Aeldari. Asuryan himself took no side and watched the carnage. Vaul later reforged the final blade as Anaris and fought Khaine with it, but Khaine cast him down. The sword passed to Eldanesh, who was crushed by Khaine in single combat. As Khaine slew him, his hands began to drip with blood. Asuryan then proclaimed the war over.

The Avatar of Khaine

Khaine manifests in battle as the [[Avatar of Khaine]], a fiery armoured form that leads the host of a craftworld into battle. Each craftworld keeps a lifeless Avatar in a sacred chamber, and it is awakened by the sacrifice of an [[Exarch]] on the eve of war. Imperial records describe an Avatar deep within the [[Eye of Terror]], on a Crone World of the lost Aeldari empire, standing against [[Lorgar]] during his pilgrimage.

Many Imperial scholars believe Khaine embodies the Aeldari's passion for battle and war. Whether he was once a mortal hero who ascended to divinity, an aspect of the Aeldari psyche shaped in the [[Immaterium]], or a creation of the [[Old Ones]] is unknown. His precise nature is not well understood: because he interacts with the [[Chaos Gods]], he appears to be an entity of the [[Warp]], and some scholars hold him to be an incarnation of Order rather than Chaos.

Aspects and cults

The [[Aspect Warriors]] of the Craftworlds each embody a single aspect of Khaine, and the [[Phoenix Lord]]s are the founders of those aspects. After the Fall, [[Slaanesh]] struck Khaine down, but [[Khorne]] fought the new god for his power. Khaine was driven into the material world, where his psychic core shattered into a thousand pieces. Each piece came to rest in the [[Infinity Circuit]] of a craftworld, and from it an Avatar can be woken.
