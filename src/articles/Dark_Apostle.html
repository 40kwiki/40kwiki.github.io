A Dark Apostle is a [[Heretic Astartes]] [[Chaplain]] and one of the leaders of the [[Word Bearers]] [[Traitor Legion]]. He is second in power only to the Legion's [[primarch]], [[Lorgar]]. Dark Apostles of other Traitor Legions have also been recorded as dark missionaries.

Power

The Dark Apostles together rule the XVII Legion through the [[Dark Council]], which is made up of the most senior among them. Like all Word Bearers, they worship the [[Dark Gods]] as [[Chaos Undivided]] rather than as one of the [[Chaos Gods]].

History

Even before the [[Horus Heresy]], the Dark Apostles had turned the zeal of the XVII Legion away from the teachings of the [[Imperial Truth]] and towards the worship of Chaos.
