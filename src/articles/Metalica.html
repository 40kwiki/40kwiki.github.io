Metalica, called the Gleaming Giant of the Ultima Segmentum, is a [[Forge world|forge world]] of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] in the [[Charadon Sector]] of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Its surface is almost entirely metal, stripped of all native life by the [[Cult Mechanicus]] so that its work may proceed in sterility. It is the home of the Legio Metalica, the Iron Skulls, one of the oldest and most honoured [[Titan Legions]].

Lying close to the [[Orks|Ork]] empire of Charadon, Metalica lives in permanent readiness for war. Its forces fought in all three [[Wars for Armageddon]] (444.M41, 941.M41 and 998.M41) and garrisoned the [[Cadian Gate]] before the [[Fall of Cadia]]. In the [[Era Indomitus]] it has twice been besieged by the [[Death Guard]] and has held both times.

History

Origins

Metalica was once a living world, but its tech-priests, jealous of its resources, had their [[Skitarii]] drive every other species on it to extinction and stripped away its rocky crust. The date of its settlement and of its compliance with the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] are not recorded in open archives; its Titan Legion was already among the senior Legions of the [[Collegia Titanica]] in the [[Great Crusade]] (M30).The Legio Metalica's founding is not dated in available records. It is placed before the Horus Heresy from its seniority among the Legions.

The Elucidan Schism

In 923.M39 the Elucidan Schism divided the priesthood of Metalica. Hard-line Fulgurites shot down a congregation of Corpuscarii electro-priests on pilgrimage to [[Mars]], claiming that their wasteful rites would upset the balance of the priesthood on the Red Planet. The schism was eventually healed, and Metalica's cult has since been united against common enemies.

The Wars for Armageddon

Main article: [[Wars for Armageddon]]

When a renegade host of [[Chaos Space Marines]] and daemons, led by the Daemon Primarch [[Angron]], invaded the [[Hive world|hive world]] [[Armageddon]] in 444.M41, Metalica's priesthood answered the call at once, out of a loathing of anarchy and also, records admit, out of curiosity. Its Titans and Skitarii fought the Blood God's hosts, among other places at the Battle of Blood Glade, and gathered great quantities of data while doing so. They bought time for the defence of the planet until the [[Space Wolves]] and [[Grey Knights]] could defeat Angron.

With [[Imperial Knight|Knights]] of the neighbouring Knight world of Kolossi, Metalica's forces fought again in the Second (941.M41) and Third (998.M41) wars against the Orks of [[Ghazghkull Thraka]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, Metalica was among the forge worlds that sent reinforcements to [[Ultramar]] during the [[Plague Wars]], c. 012.M42. In the War of Slime and Metal, in the early 42nd millennium, Metalica was itself besieged by the plague-hosts of [[Nurgle]]: the 3rd and 7th Plague Companies of the Death Guard, other renegades, the corrupted Titans of the Legio Morbus and seven fallen Knight houses. Defeat was averted only by the arrival of the whole host of House Raven, and the attack was broken by a war fleet from Deimos. Later, in the Charadon campaign, [[Typhus]], the Herald of Nurgle, led a still larger Death Guard force against Metalica in an attempt to claim it for the Plague God.Neither Metalican siege is precisely dated in open records. Both are placed after the Plague Wars, c. 111–130.M42 (estimated).

Geography

Metalica's surface is dull silver metal, without soil, plants or animals, and its atmosphere cannot support life. It is covered with forge complexes, furnace-mountains and strata of industrial waste, and is constantly loud with machinery. The planet is heavily polluted and stands near the borders of the [[Charadon Sector|Ork empire of Charadon]].

Society

Metalica is governed by its tech-priests, who have a particular revulsion for disorder and seek to impose their logic on neighbouring worlds. Its weapons and machines are built to emit a deliberately loud blaring noise, so that the enemy may hear the glory of the [[Omnissiah]] before death. Its [[Skitarii]], inured from induction to the din of the forges, wear white and red.

Among its notable servants is the [[Archmagos|Magos Dominus]] Ozmerhadus Phal, who argued that the worlds around Metalica should be remade on its sterile model.
