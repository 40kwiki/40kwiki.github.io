Oskar Lowis was the [[Lord Commander Militant]] of the [[Astra Militarum]] who took his seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] after the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32. The Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]] gave him the office left empty by the murder of [[Abel Verreault]]. Lowis served as the Lord Protector's instrument and carried out his plans for the Imperium until Vangorich was killed.

Almost nothing else about Lowis is recorded. His origins, earlier career and the manner of his death are not in the archives open to Imperial scholars, and the date on which his service ended is known only by inference.

Appointment

In 546.M32 the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] [[Maximus Thane]] left [[Terra]] on crusade and named [[Drakan Vangorich|Vangorich]] Lord Protector of the Imperium. Soon after, Vangorich learned that the [[Fabricator-General]] [[Kubik]] had defied Thane's order to destroy [[Ullanor]]. He concluded that the council could not be reformed, and in a single solar day his assassins killed eleven of the twelve [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]]. Verreault, then [[Lord Commander Militant]], was killed by the Callidus assassin who had taken the place of the [[Master of the Administratum]].

Vangorich installed new High Lords of his own choosing at once. Lowis was among them. The council that resulted sat in name while the Lord Protector governed.

Under the Lord Protector

For nearly eighty years [[Drakan Vangorich|Vangorich]] ruled through his appointees. The Imperium was reconstructed after the [[War of the Beast]]: the [[Astra Militarum]] and the Navy were rebuilt, [[Terra]] was refortified and the [[Fourth Founding]] of new [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] was carried out. As head of the Guard, Lowis would have shared in the first of these tasks, though no regiment or campaign is credited to him by name.

The Lord Protector's rule decayed into paranoia and purge. When [[Maximus Thane]] returned to Terra with the [[Imperial Fists]] and killed Vangorich in the [[Officio Assassinorum|Eversor Temple]], in c. 626.M32, the council of appointees fell with him. Almost two solar decades of anarchy followed until the [[Chapter Master]] [[Agnathio]] of the [[Ultramarines]] brought fifty Chapters to Terra and compelled the factions to settle. A new council was then seated. Lowis is not recorded among its members.

Legacy

Lowis is remembered as a creature of the Lord Protector. His predecessor [[Heth]] had been a soldier's general and Verreault a politician who traded his vote for transport, while Lowis is described in the annals simply as a puppet of the [[Grand Master of Assassins]].
