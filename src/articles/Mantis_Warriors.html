The Mantis Warriors are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of the [[White Scars]] line, raised to help guard the [[Maelstrom]] in the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. Fragments of the record suggest they were created in the [[Eighth Founding]] of the mid-34th millennium. In the [[Badab War]] of 904–913.M41 they joined the secession of [[Lufgt Huron]] and the [[Astral Claws]], and at its end they were stripped of their homeworld of Ootheca and sent on a [[Penitent|penitent]] crusade, from which they have not returned.

The Chapter came out of the war with about a hundred battle-brothers. It received no [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] reinforcements of its own when the [[Indomitus Crusade]] reached it, only the technology to make them, and its numbers remain dangerously low in the [[Era Indomitus]].

History

Origins

The earliest record likely to refer to the Chapter is in the Libris Gloriana, a 777-volume treatise on Imperial power written for the accession of High Lord Gogol after the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] in 093.M36. It mentions a "warrior legion of the Mantis of the 8th Founding", but names no homeworld. The Chapter's [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] carries the markers of the [[White Scars]] through the [[Marauders (Chapter)|Marauders]], a [[Second Founding]] successor of that Legion. The first certain record dates from 455.M38, when the Magos Inviglia of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] noted a gene-seed tithe from the Mantis Warriors.

The Chapter long held the Endymion Cluster in isolation, recruiting from Ootheca and the world of Tranquility III, where it kept a training base in the Valley of the Nine Winds. In 330.M39 it hunted the renegade psyker Kharfra the Antipath. In 587.M41 an edict of the [[High Lords of Terra]] made it one of the Maelstrom Warders, the alliance of Chapters charged with containing the Maelstrom, alongside the Astral Claws.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

When Lufgt Huron declared the secession of the Badab Sector, the Mantis Warriors joined him in the belief that they were defending the ancient autonomy of the Adeptus Astartes against the High Lords. The first recorded clash with Loyalist Space Marines came in 904.M41, when the Mantis Warriors attacked and captured the [[Fire Hawks]]' ship Red Harbinger. In 907.M41, while Badab itself was besieged, two Loyalist Chapters were sent to investigate the worlds held by the Mantis Warriors and the [[Executioners]], and a long campaign followed in the Endymion Cluster.

The Mantis Warriors came to understand that Huron had been corrupted by [[Chaos]] and had used them. At the end of the war in 913.M41 they surrendered and were granted the Emperor's forgiveness, on terms. Chapter Master Khoisan Neotera was stripped of his honour and his armour and imprisoned for life in isolation in the Penitentiacon. Ootheca and Tranquility III were handed to the Space Sharks, and the Chapter was ordered on a hundred-year penitent crusade, during which it was forbidden to recruit.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

The ban on recruitment left the Chapter close to extinction at the end of the 41st millennium. When a Torchbearer fleet of the Indomitus Crusade reached it after 999.M41, it gave the Mantis Warriors the gene-technology and Mechanicus personnel to create [[Primaris Space Marines]], but no Greyshield reinforcements, because of the Chapter's crimes. In a campaign against a splinter Tyranid fleet on the world of Herodian IV in the early 42nd millennium, the Mantis Warriors earned the [[Inquisition]]'s grudging leave to recruit again, but they remain far below strength.The Herodian IV campaign is dated only to the early Era Indomitus.

Combat doctrine

True to their [[White Scars]] descent, the Mantis Warriors favour speed, ambush and close assault. They were known before the [[Badab War]] for the subtlety of their tactics and for the camouflage they used in the jungles of the Endymion Cluster.
