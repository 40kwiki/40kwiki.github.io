Aurum, also called the "Golden Planet", is a [[feral world]] in the Orpheus Salient of the [[Jericho Reach]] in the [[Segmentum Ultima]]. It is being brought into the Imperium through contact with the [[Achilus Crusade]]. Its people are ruled by a native monarch called the [[Caele]], and its rare [[Decavane Crystal|Decavane Crystals]] make it strategically valuable to the Imperium.

Geography

Aurum has two main continents. Each is dominated by arid plains and rainforest, surrounded by mountain ranges. The equator is bordered by deserts, and the polar oceans are small. The planet is rich in [[promethium]] and in Decavane Crystals, which hold a far larger power charge than standard crystal batteries and are used in alternative Imperial weapon patterns. Its amber sky comes from these crystals.

History

The native Aurans are a warrior culture that the [[Astra Militarum]] regards as well suited to service. They worship the "Soul of Aurum", a personification of the planet's will, whose story resembles that of the [[God-Emperor]]. Their language and artefacts show an Imperial origin, but the date of their first settlement is unknown.

The Imperium has held a presence in the capital, Haistand, since 783.M41, but the Auran tribes have admitted Imperial envoys only to the city. Between 789.M41 and 809.M41, a series of envoys, a Navy captain and a [[Rogue Trader]], Diaz Lan, tried to extend the Imperial presence. The [[Ecclesiarchy]] sister who accompanied the envoys was found dead, and [[Inquisitor]] Adrielle Quist reported a series of similar deaths among Imperial diplomats. She warned that the world must be brought into compliance.
