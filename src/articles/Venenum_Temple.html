The Venenum Temple, originally the Venenum Clade, is one of the temples of the [[Officio Assassinorum]]. Its operatives train in the making and use of exotic poisons and in the discipline of subtle approach. In the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]] the clade's members were the galaxy's finest toxin-artisans, able to build killing philtres from common materials and to leave no metabolic trace in the body of the target.

The temple was one of the six prime clades whose assassins were sent against the Warmaster [[Horus]]. It is also remembered for the Morisha Incident of 563.M37, in which an assassin arrived at her target seven centuries late.

History

Foundation

The clade was founded in the late thirtieth millennium, during the [[Great Crusade]], at the wish of [[Malcador the Sigillite]], Regent of [[Terra]], who was secretly the [[Grand Master of Assassins]]. Its first Director Primus was Siress Venenum, known by no other name. Like the other clades it was tasked with removing those who opposed the Emperor's will and the unification of Mankind.

The clade was housed in a hidden place called the Orchard: an ancient house, thousands of years old, set in a tropical jungle. The grounds held thousands of plants and animals kept as the raw stock of the clade's poisons.

The Horus Heresy

See also: [[Horus Heresy]]

When [[Horus]] turned traitor the clades were ordered to kill him. Each sent its best pupils singly, half prepared, and each strike failed. In all, eight operatives from the six prime clades were lost. Tobeld, the premier assassin of the Venenum Clade, was the last known to attempt the task alone, and he came closer to the target than any other before failing.

The failures led the Council of Assassins to a plan that had never been tried. With the approval of the Grand Master, the founders of the clades assembled an [[Execution Force|Imperial Execution Force]] with one assassin from each prime clade, to strike at Horus on [[Dagonet]]. Malcador reasoned that Horus had broken every rule of war already, having bombed his own warriors on [[Isstvan III]]. The Venenum Clade sent Jenniker Soalm, born Jenniker Kell, a secluse of the senior Epsilon-dan rank. The Execution Force failed and all its members were killed by the Traitors.

The Morisha Incident

In 563.M37 the assassin Urhua Theraux was sent to kill the renegade governor Yawell of Morisha, a world on the [[Eastern Fringe]]. Her ship was caught in a warp rift and held in stasis for 698 standard years. Unaware, she reached Morisha in c. 261.M38 and found Yawell long dead and the world run by an anti-Imperial committee of some 1,000 members. She spent three days poisoning every chair in the council auditorium, and killed the whole committee when it next met in full session.

Methods and notable members

Venenum assassins learn their art through a long apprenticeship. They study the making of toxins from base components, and the timing of a death to the hour or the minute. The temple's lord assassin Echo Malensis is said to be so skilled with psychopoisons that she could time a target's death to the very second of her return to the debriefing chambers of the temple.
