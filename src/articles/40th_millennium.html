The 40th millennium (M40) is the span of the [[Imperial Dating System]] from 000.M40 to 999.M40, the last full millennium of [[The Waning]]. No [[Black Crusade]] is recorded in it, and its history survives mostly as scattered entries from the frontier [[Sector|sectors]]: the [[Calixis Sector]] and its [[Koronus Expanse]], and the [[Jericho Reach]], where the [[Hadex Anomaly]] swallowed the old sector capital of Verronus in 656.M40.

The best-known event of the age is the rebellion of [[Krieg]] in 433.M40 and the five centuries of atomic war by which its loyal regiments retook their world. The [[Necrons]] began to wake on scattered tomb worlds, the [[T'au Empire]] grew by diplomacy under the Golden Ambassador, and the future [[Commander Dante]] was born on [[Baal]] Secundus in 447.M40. In 997.M40 the [[Rogue Trader]] Purity Lathimon found the safe passage through the Maw into the Koronus Expanse.
