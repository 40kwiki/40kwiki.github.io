The Devastation of Baal, also called the Siege of Baal or the Battle of Baal, was the assault by the greatest concentration of [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] ever met by mankind on the homeworld of the [[Blood Angels]]. It was fought in the closing days of the [[41st millennium]], when the [[Great Rift]] opened across the sky. It was the bloodiest and most costly battle in the history of the Blood Angels, and it ended not with their victory in arms, but with an intervention: the opening of the Rift, the appearance of the Khornate [[Daemon|daemon]] [[Ka'Bandha]], and the arrival of the Indomitus Crusade of [[Roboute Guilliman]].

The Blood Angels and their Successors survived. Baal was cleansed, and Guilliman gave the survivors Primaris Space Marines of the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of [[Sanguinius]]. The victory was won at heavy cost, and it left a mystery and a menace: it is unknown why Ka'Bandha, the oldest enemy of the Chapter, struck at the Tyranids and spared the Angels. The battle was the end of the Third Tyrannic War. It also led to the appointment of Dante as [[Imperial Regent|Lord Regent]] of the [[Imperium Nihilus]].

Background

The Third Tyrannic War

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]

Hive Fleet Leviathan came to [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] from beneath the galactic plane in 997.M41, the third and largest of the [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] [[Hive Fleet|hive fleets]] to reach the Imperium and the one that began the Third Tyrannic War. Its coming was foreseen by [[Inquisitor|Inquisitor Lord]] Fidus Kryptman of the [[Ordo Xenos]], who tracked it by the census that bears his name and defeated half of it with the aid of the Ultramarines and Mortifactors at [[Uriel Ventris|Tarsis Ultra]]. When the fleet carved on, he diverted much of it into the Ork Empire of [[Octarius]] by means of a stolen [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer]] brood, so that the two species fought each other. The tendril that fell on [[Baal]] was another part of the fleet, the largest tendril it ever sent.

The Cryptan Shield

Baal, with its moons Baal Prime and Baal Secundus, was protected by a line of fortified worlds in the Cryptus System, called the Cryptan Shield, which was intended to hold Leviathan back. In 998.M41 word came that its defences had fallen. [[Commander Dante|Dante]], the [[Chapter Master]], had already put plans in motion for the defence of Baal, with strike forces fighting hit-and-run battles against the Leviathan's splinter fleets across the Red Scar. When the Shield broke, he took the 1st and 2nd Companies, [[Librarian|Chief Librarian]] [[Mephiston]], the [[Apothecary|Chief Apothecary]] Corbulo, Captain Karlaen and the greater part of [[Flesh Tearers|Gabriel Seth]]'s Flesh Tearers to the Cryptus System, in the campaign that is now called by that name. They arrived in time to aid the last defenders and avert complete collapse, but the fighting was savage. In the end the [[Blood Angels]] were forced to unleash an ancient weapon of [[Necrons|Necrontyr]] origin, the Magnovitrium, to destroy an entire world of the system and use the debris to gut the oncoming fleet. Dante accepted that the Tyranids had been slowed but not stopped, and ordered the Chapter to return to Baal.

Preparation

By the first days of 999.M41, [[Baal]] had been reinforced as never before. Fortresses were raised on the sands of Baal and its moons. Fleets of cruisers and battle barges gathered in the void. One by one the Successor [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the Sanguinary Brotherhood answered [[Commander Dante|Dante]]'s call, from the [[Flesh Tearers]] and the Blood Drinkers to the insular Carmine Blades, the Angels Encarmine and the [[Knights of Blood]]. The Knights of Blood, who had been declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] by the [[High Lords of Terra]], were accepted by Dante as allies. Only the [[Lamenters]] did not come.

Dante also did what no [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Blood Angels]] had done before. He authorised the removal of the Chapter's [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] reserves from their crypts, and sent them away in a swift ship under the guard of Sanguinary Priests. He ordered his gunships to collect every viable aspirant from the nomadic tribes of Baal Prime and Baal Secundus and take them to the Arx Angelicum, where at least forty thousand young men were armed and posted to the defence, and told that any who survived the battle would have proved themselves worthy of the Chapter. Of the tribes that remained, little is said, for they were left to hide or to fight, and most did not survive.

The [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] fleet entered the Baal system in clusters so thick that it looked like a single many-limbed organism. Dante sent out his fleet against it, with the aid of the Successors' spacefaring Chapters. The battle was valiant and hopeless. One by one the battle barges and cruisers were isolated and overwhelmed. Where they could, the ships fell back to harass the flanks of the swarm. In low orbit the [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]]'s ships began to release clouds of spores and organic transports, and the [[Shadow in the Warp]] closed over the system, so that no further hope of reinforcement could be given. The Blood Angels were alone.

Course of the battle

Against the swarm

The defenders awaited the first waves of the [[Tyranids]] in [[Horus]] Heresy-era fortifications which the serfs of the Chapter had dug up and restored. The first swarms to land were destroyed by artillery before they reached the lines. By the tenth wave the ground was barely visible under the bodies of the swarm. Hive Tyrants and Tervigons directed their broods and tore holes in the fortifications, into which Genestealers came to fight at close quarters. At the rear of the swarm stood the Swarmlord, spawned again by the Hive Mind to destroy the [[Blood Angels]], who watched their tactics and directed the swarm to counter each of them.

The aspirants' settlement of Angel's Fall, at which the aspirants of the Chapter gathered for their trials, was overrun by an avalanche of chitin. [[Flesh Tearers|Gabriel Seth]] and his Flesh Tearers led the counter-attack, cutting the heart out of one swarm after another, but at a price. On Baal Secundus the Carmine Blades defended the [[Astropathy|astropathic relay]] in the hope that someone would hear the Chapter's call. Baal Prime, [[Luna|the moon]], was in a worse state, for the Tyranids committed overwhelming force there.

In solar weeks of fighting, the Blood Angels and their Successors threw back nineteen waves of assault. Blood Drinkers fought back to back with Angels Encarmine. Assault Marines leapt into the swarm to kill the greatest of the beasts. On the radiation-scorched valleys of Baal, massed [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]] tanks and artillery met stampedes of Carnifexes. [[Commander Dante|Dante]]'s warriors felled hundreds of Tyranids for every battle-brother lost. But every line broke in turn. Five [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]] of the Sanguinary Brotherhood died in the defence. Three fell in the battle for the Dome of Angels alone.

The Great Rift opens

It was in this hour that the [[Great Rift]] opened. The sky of Baal burned with [[Warp]] fire, and, as the fighting on Baal Prime was at its fiercest, reality was torn and the air filled with the smell of brimstone and burning blood. [[Space Marine Legions|The legions]] of the [[Khorne|Blood God]] poured through. At their head came the [[Daemon|Bloodthirster]] [[Ka'Bandha]], a bat-winged monster with an axe in one hand and a barbed whip in the other.

No enemy was more hated by the sons of Sanguinius. In the [[Signus Campaign]], thousands of years earlier, Ka'Bandha had stoked in them the Red Thirst, the curse that has ravaged the Chapter since. He wanted the honour of destroying the Blood Angels for himself, and he would not let the Tyranids take it from him. His legions fell on both the Tyranids and the Space Marines, and blood of both species ran in streams. Bloodletters tore at Tyranid Warriors, Bloodcrushers trampled Termagants and Ka'Bandha himself walked through the madness, killing all in his path.

The defenders on Baal Prime, under Gabriel Seth, fell back. The survivors were able to board their ships only because of the sacrifice of the [[Knights of Blood]], who threw themselves into the fighting in a state of the Black Rage and held the Warp-spawned monsters long enough for them to go. Baal Prime was overwhelmed. In orbit, thousands of the [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]]'s ships were snatched into the Warp by coils of empyric energy, or swallowed by rifts in space. What remained was drawn down by the gravity of Baal and burned in its air.

The Arx Angelicum

Main article: [[Baal]]

Dante led his few remaining warriors in a fighting retreat to the walls of the Arx Angelicum, the fortress-monastery. Time was a victim of the empyric storm. It seemed to the defenders that the fighting lasted for years. Perhaps it lasted for hours or days, and perhaps for years by the standards of the rest of the galaxy. The greatest wave of Tyranids broke the defences of the fortress. Three [[Space Marine Chapter|Successor Chapter]] Masters fell in the battle of the Dome of Angels. The defenders opened the Tower of Amareo and released the captive members of the Death Company, because if the Blood Angels were to die, they intended to die in battle, on their own terms.

At this critical point the Swarmlord came forward and carved the Space Marines apart with its jagged bone swords. Dante, wounded in a dozen places, fought his way through the press to the monster with his axe, the Axe Mortalis, and the two duelled under the burning sky. Dante killed it, and fell. As his warriors strained to reach him, the sky cleared. The storm had passed. Where the Tyranid fleet had been, there was not a single ship of the [[Xenos|xenos]]. In their place stood the ships of the Imperium. [[Roboute Guilliman]], the resurrected [[Primarch|primarch]], had arrived with the [[Indomitus Crusade]].

Relief

[[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] and his warriors came down in drop pods and Stormravens into the midst of the swarm. The [[Tyranids]] on the ground still outnumbered the Space Marines, but the death of the Swarmlord and the loss of their fleet left them reeling, and the Hormagaunts and Termagants reverted to the instincts of beasts. The [[Primaris Space Marines]] of [[Belisarius Cawl|Cawl]], in the colours of the [[Blood Angels]] and their Successors, fought at the primarch's side with their new bolt rifles. The salvation of [[Baal]] was neither quick nor cheap, and more Imperial lives were lost before the world was clean. Baal and Baal Secundus were cleared of Tyranids in less than a solar month. On Baal Prime no fighting was needed, for [[Luna|the moon]] had been scoured of life, both Imperial and Tyranid.

The Chapter holds that Guilliman learned, in these days, secrets of its gene-line. He has not spoken of them.

[[Commander Dante|Dante]], gravely wounded, knelt before the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]]. In him and in the survivors of the Sanguinary Brotherhood, Guilliman saw a flame kept alive from the Imperium's past, and he gave them an unusual gift: formations of Primaris Space Marines, raised from the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] of [[Sanguinius]], to fill their ranks.

Aftermath

The [[Blood Angels]] began to rebuild. Their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] stocks were recalled to the restored [[Baal|Arx Angelicum]]. The thousands of aspirants who had survived the siege were all taken into the Chapter, and those who endured the transformation were placed in Scout Companies that [[Commander Dante|Dante]] enlarged for his Chapter and the Successors alike. The [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris Marines]] of the Ark [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] Zar-Quaesitor were joined by others raised on Baal itself with the apparatus that [[Belisarius Cawl]] brought. In the Chapter's reconstruction a Sergeant Carleon of the 8th Company, charged with the purge of Baal Secundus and the evaluation of the new Primaris warriors, was promoted to lieutenant.

On Baal Prime, the [[Daemon|Daemons]] had left behind a mountain of [[Tyranids|Tyranid]] skulls, piled in the eight-pillared sigil of [[Khorne]] across the south of [[Luna|the moon]]. It was a message to the Chapter. Dante ordered it destroyed by orbital strikes. It remains unknown why [[Ka'Bandha]] acted as he did, and it troubles the Blood Angels more than the battle itself. It may be that the Bloodthirster has determined that he alone will be the architect of the ruin of the sons of Sanguinius.

The [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Third Tyrannic War]] ended at Baal. The Hive Mind, having failed against the Blood Angels, turned its strength to the [[Segmentum|Segmentum Pacificus]] in the galactic west, in what the Imperial [[Logos Historica Verita|historitors]] call the Fourth Tyrannic War. [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] named Dante as [[Imperial Regent|Lord Regent]] of the [[Imperium Nihilus]], the dark half of the Imperium beyond the Rift. The Blood Angels and their Successors, after a period of rebuilding, went out into the Dark Imperium to bring the Emperor's light to those who were oppressed by [[Xenos|xenos]] and [[Chaos]].

Records and disputes

The accounts of the battle differ in several points, as is to be expected given what the Rift did to the passage of time in the system. The Chapter's own record has [[Ka'Bandha]]'s host appear at [[Baal|Baal Prime]] at the moment of the Rift's opening. A second report, kept in the archives of the [[Ordo Malleus]], says that the [[Daemon|daemon]] host struck first at Ammonai, the outermost world of the Baal system, and that it was a separate attack. The Chapter's late records say that the [[Tyranids]] on Baal lost contact with their fleet when the storms came, and that the Imperial fleet was already in orbit when the defenders broke out of their last perimeter, rather than arriving at the moment of the Swarmlord's death. What became of the [[Hive Fleet Leviathan|Leviathan]] fragment that was drawn into the Warp is not known.
