Kli-San Weia was the Lord Commander Militant of the Imperial Armies at the time of the late [[Unification Wars]], and is the earliest holder of that office whose name survives. He was one of the four High Lords Civilian, the officials who answered directly to the [[Emperor of Mankind]] in the first [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial government]] on [[Terra]].

Nothing else about him is preserved in the archives open to Imperial scholars. His origins, his campaigns and his death are not recorded, and the name appears in the rolls of the High Lords only once.

The High Lords Civilian

The earliest form of the body that became the [[High Lords of Terra]] was a body of administrators, the Magisters Temporal, formed by the Emperor during the [[Unification Wars]] to govern his growing realm. They were replaced by the Lords Civilian, who were in turn overseen by four High Lords Civilian.

At the time the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]], the first of the [[Space Marine Legions]], was brought into the field late in the Unification Wars, the four were Pelops Dravagor, Chancellor of the Estate Imperium; [[Uwoma Kandawire]], [[Grand Provost Marshal]]; [[Noum Retraiva]], Master of the [[Administratum]]; and Weia, [[Lord Commander Militant]] of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armies]]. Their offices are the ancestors of four of the permanent seats of the later council.

The Imperial armies

The armies Weia commanded were those of the first Imperial state: the early geno-infantry of the Old Hundred, the regiments that held the lands the [[Thunder Warriors]] conquered, and the soldiers of the Terran warlord armies that had submitted to the Emperor. They fought beside the Thunder Warriors and the [[Adeptus Custodes]] in the last campaigns of the [[Unification Wars]], which ended in c. 712.M30. The post of [[Lord Commander Militant]] outlived the wars. When the [[Great Crusade]] began in c. 798.M30, the commanders of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] held the title through the Crusade and the [[Horus Heresy]], and their successors are the Lords Commander Militant of the [[Astra Militarum]].

The record does not say whether Weia was still in office when the Great Crusade began, or whether he played any part in its first campaigns. The first holders named after him are [[Tabor Ludovicia]] and [[Haldane Ma'lon]], who shared the office at the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31.
