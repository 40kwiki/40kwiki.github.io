A High Marshal is the senior-ranking [[Astartes]] of the [[Black Templars]] [[Chapter]]. The title is the equal of the [[Chapter Master]] in other Chapters that follow the [[Codex Astartes]]. The High Marshal is the greatest warrior of the Chapter and is steeped in its secrets.

Role

The High Marshal commands the Chapter's ongoing Imperial Crusades. He keeps track of their progress and decides where the Chapter's forces will fight. Occasionally he joins a crusade of special importance in person to inspire its warriors. He is one of the most autonomous military commanders in the Imperium, and he decides where his warriors fight without waiting for the orders of the [[High Lords of Terra]].

Qualifications

A High Marshal is unmatched in combat with the bolter, the blade and bare hands. He must also be a skilled tactician and strategist, and he is expected to make his decisions in the heat of battle. Every candidate must have a long record of service in the crucible of war.

History

The office dates from the [[Second Founding]], after the [[Horus Heresy]]. The Chapter's founder, [[Sigismund]], was then the [[Praetor (Space Marine)|First Captain]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] Legion. He was chosen by his [[primarch]], [[Rogal Dorn]], to lead the newly created Black Templars as their first High Marshal. Imperial records place the founding in the early 31st millennium, and the office has been held continuously since.The date of the founding is estimated from the sequence of the Second Founding. No record gives an exact year.

Imperial records also preserve a prayer, the Prayer of Repugnance, written by the High Marshal Gerwald. It calls on the Chapter to destroy aliens and [[Warp]]-spawned creatures wherever they are found.
