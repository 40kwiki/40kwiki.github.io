The Astartes Praeses are a group of twenty loyalist Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] whose sole task is to guard the [[Eye of Terror]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. The only record that names them is the ancient tome known as the [[Mythos Angelica Mortis]], which mentions twenty Chapters created from the gene-seed of the primarchs who did not fall to [[Chaos]] in the [[Horus Heresy]]. They were charged with watching the Imperial sectors that border the Eye and with supporting [[Cadia]] and the Inquisition when called on.

The Imperium keeps no complete list. One Chapter has been destroyed by the forces of Chaos, and two others, the [[Relictors]] and the Sons of Malice, have been declared [[Excommunicate Traitoris]] by the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The Sons of Malice are only suspected of belonging to the original twenty.

Founding and purpose

The Praeses were founded anew rather than assembled from the remnants of the Legions, as many other [[Second Founding]] Chapters were. This is taken to be a precaution. It was meant to reduce the risk that veterans who had been exposed to Chaos in the fighting of the Heresy might bring corruption into the new force. They were made from the unbroken lines of the loyal primarchs and placed to hold the edge of the Eye.

No complete list of the twenty survives. The Chapters are known only from scattered references in the records of the Eye of Terror and in a few other accounts.

Known members

Imperial scholars name fourteen Chapters, though one of them, the Subjugators, is a much later foundation. The identity of seven more has been lost to data corruption. Because some Chapters keep their affairs secret and communications are poor, the list is held to be conjecture.
