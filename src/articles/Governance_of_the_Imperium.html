Governance of the Imperium is the chain of rule by which the [[Imperium of Mankind]] is administered in the name of the [[Emperor of Mankind]]. Authority runs from the High Lords of Terra, through the segmenta and sectors, to the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governors]] who rule each world. The chain is the same in every region of Imperial space, although each level has different powers in different places.

The system is not a single hierarchy. It combines civil, naval, military, ecclesiastical and Inquisitorial lines of authority, and most of them meet at Terra. The Administratum keeps the records and collects the tithe. The Navy and the Astra Militarum defend the space that the Administratum governs. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] and the [[Inquisition]] act as checks on the civil line, and the [[Adeptus Arbites]] enforce the law at every level.

History

The Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

During the [[Great Crusade]] (798.M30 – 000.M31) the Imperium had no fixed chain of rule. Conquered worlds were governed by the expedition fleets that took them, and the [[High Lords of Terra|Council of Terra]] met on [[Terra]] to direct the crusade from the [[Imperial Palace]].

The reforms

The [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) ended the Council of Terra's authority. The reforms of [[Roboute Guilliman]], in roughly 015–021.M31, fixed the five segmenta, the sectors and sub-sectors, and the offices that govern each. Civil and military authority were separated, so that no governor commanded both a fleet and an army.The dates of the reforms are estimated from the sequence of events. The Administratum's first charter of the reforms is lost.

The High Lords were formally established before the middle of M32, as recorded on their own page. Their number and their seats have changed since, and some seats have stood empty for long periods.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In the [[Era Indomitus]] the chain of rule has been broken in many places. The [[Great Rift]] cut off whole [[Segmentum|segmenta]] from Terra, and the [[Indomitus Crusade]] has restored some levels in regions that were lost. Many [[Sector|sectors]] now answer to a governor who has never been seen by the [[Administratum]], and the Imperial registers record them as held in trust.

Principles

Tithe and returns

Every world pays the [[Imperial tithe]] to its [[Sub-sector|sub-sector]], which passes it to the sector and the [[Segmentum|segmentum]] and on to Terra. Each level keeps part of the return for its own use. The [[Administratum]] records every payment, and the chain is judged by the completeness of its returns.

Oversight

The [[Adeptus Arbites]] and the [[Inquisition]] hold powers that cut across the chain of rule. The Arbites answer to their own command on Terra, and the Inquisition may act in any level without the consent of the local governor. A governor who resists these powers is liable to be removed.

Religious authority

The [[Ecclesiarchy]] has its own hierarchy, with a cardinal for each sector or group of [[Sector|sectors]]. Cardinals are not subordinate to the civil governors, although they often advise them and in some sectors hold the office of governor themselves.
