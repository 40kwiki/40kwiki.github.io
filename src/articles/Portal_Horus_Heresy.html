The [[Horus Heresy]] was the galaxy-wide civil war of 005–014.M31 that ended the [[Great Crusade]]. Led by the [[Warmaster]] [[Horus]], a host of [[Space Marine Legions]] turned against the Emperor and attacked [[Terra]]. This portal gathers the articles on its battles, its [[Traitor|traitors]] and its loyalists.

Selected article

[[Horus Heresy]] was the civil war that split the [[Space Marine Legions]] and the Imperium in the early [[31st millennium]]. It began with the atrocity at [[Isstvan III]] and ended at the [[Siege of Terra]]. Imperial accounts still dispute the exact causes, but all agree that the war cost the Emperor his greatest servants.
