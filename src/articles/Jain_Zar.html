Jain Zar, called the "Storm of Silence", is the [[Phoenix Lord]] who founded the [[Howling Banshees]], the [[Aspect Warriors]] of the [[Craftworld Aeldari]]. He is one of the Phoenix Lords of the Asuryani.

Origins

[[Asurmen]], the "Hand of [[Asuryan]]", was the first Phoenix Lord. He led the surviving craftworlds away from the homeworlds of the lost [[Aeldari Empire]] after the [[Fall of the Aeldari]]. He founded the first Aspect Shrine, the Shrine of Asur, on the moon of the same name in the [[Webway]]. Other Aspect Shrines later produced their own Phoenix Lords, trained by [[Exarch]]s from that teaching.
