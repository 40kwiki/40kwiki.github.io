Solomon Lok was an [[Inquisitor]] Lord of the [[Ordo Xenos]]. He was a veteran of the [[Hrud]] infestation on [[Chincare]]. In 850.M41 his brethren sent him to investigate the sudden silence of an [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] biological research facility on [[Beta Anphelion IV]].

Beta Anphelion IV

Lok's retinue included a [[Servo-skull]], Major Durra of the 99th Detachment D of the [[Elysian Drop Troops]], and the [[Tech-priest]] Magos Biologis Arthon. An autosavant named Wassily kept a log of events on the world, and the [[Astropath]] Zarneck sent that log to Inquisitor Lord Varius, Lok's superior.

The force also included [[Cadian Shock Troops]] and an [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] Biologis Exploritas detachment. The account of the mission ends with the facility lost, and Lok is recorded as believed slain.Imperial accounts differ on whether Lok died. The Ordo Xenos treats him as slain.

Legacy

The log of Wassily and the transmissions of Zarneck are held in the archives of the [[Ordo Xenos]], as the record of the Beta Anphelion IV disaster.
