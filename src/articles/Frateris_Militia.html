The Frateris Militia are the masses of the [[Imperial Cult]]'s most zealous faithful who have gathered to fight Wars of Faith against the enemies of the [[Emperor of Mankind]] and of Humanity. They are the unofficial and generally ill-trained troops of the [[Adeptus Ministorum]], who fight in the interests of the [[Ecclesiarchy]].

Most members of the Militia have no formal military training. They are drawn from the faithful of hive and feudal worlds, and they fight with whatever weapons they can find. Imperial records describe their courage as considerable and their discipline as poor. The Ecclesiarchy tolerates their service because it fills gaps that the [[Astra Militarum]] cannot cover.

Role in war

Frateris Militia units are often raised to hold a city or a shrine while regular forces are elsewhere. They are sent into the fiercest fighting against heretics and xenos, and their losses are recorded in Ecclesiarchy registers as a sacrifice to the Throne. Some commanders have sent them forward to absorb enemy fire before the regiments advance.
