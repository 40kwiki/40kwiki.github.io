Thrax was an [[Inquisitor]] who held a long grudge against the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He arrived with a retinue of [[Black Templars]] and took [[Demetrian Titus]] into custody after his captain's resistance and his deviations from the [[Codex Astartes]] were reported to him.

The grudge dated from the [[Badab War]]. Thrax was killed in the 990s.M41 during an operation against the corrupted Grey Slayers, after he had been possessed by a [[Daemon|daemon]].
