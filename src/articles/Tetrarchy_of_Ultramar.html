The Tetrarchy of Ultramar, also called the Tetrarch Council, was the governing body of the [[Realm of Ultramar]], the autonomous domain of the [[Ultramarines]]. Its members were the [[Tetrarch]]s, also given as Lords Martial, together with the [[primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]]. The title was used only by the Ultramarines in the late 30th and early 31st millennia, and was re-established in the early [[Era Indomitus]]. The Council administered the institutions of the Five Hundred Worlds and was entrusted with their defence. Each Tetrarch was a military dictator of one of the five "king worlds" of Ultramar and ruled over an entire sub-sector of the realm.

History

Great Crusade

In the latter years of the [[Great Crusade]] Guilliman divided the Five Hundred Worlds into four major fiefdoms under the overall dominion of [[Macragge]], to improve the governance of his domain. A Tetrarch ruled each fiefdom from a major world, and the four were chosen from the veterans of the Legion. Each served as a military governor and gave the Legion a Champion and an honour guard drawn from the Invictarus Suzerain. Because a Tetrarch answered only to Guilliman and controlled several worlds, the rank stood above that of any planetary governor of the Imperium or chapter master of the Legion. It was comparable to that of a sector governor in the 41st millennium. Guilliman himself was the ruling lord of Macragge, but his duties as political governor often fell to his seneschal while he led the Legion in the field.

The Invictarus Suzerain served as the retinue of a Tetrarch, as a military force, arbiters of law and an honour guard. Their numbers varied. Stolos Amyntas kept several thousand as peacekeepers in the troubled worlds around Iax, while Eikos Lamiad had only a hundred, partly because the Mechanicum warriors of Konor needed no aid in its defence. Before the [[Battle of Calth]] it was rumoured that Guilliman meant to make Calth and its surrounding space a new Tetrarchy, and that Chapter Master Aetheon of the 19th Chapter would be its Prince. The betrayal of the [[Word Bearers]] ended that plan.

After the Heresy

Imperial records do not say whether any form of the structure survived into the 41st millennium. After the Heresy and Guilliman's own grants of sovereignty, Ultramar broke apart into autonomous domains and the [[Second Founding]] Ultramarines Chapter held only the rump around Macragge, some eleven of the five hundred worlds.

Era Indomitus

After the first phase of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] ended with the stabilisation of the [[Imperium Sanctus]], Guilliman returned to Ultramar to aid its defence against [[Nurgle]]'s servants in the [[Plague Wars]]. Having declared in the [[Ultramar Campaign]] of 999.M41 that all five hundred worlds were again part of his dominion, he revived the Tetrarch Council as Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent. Each Tetrarch was ordered to expand the realm back to its pre-Heresy size of five hundred worlds. At about 012.M42 the four known Tetrarchs were Severus Agemman of Konor, assigned to expand northward; Portan of Andermung, a captain of the 2nd Company of the Genesis Chapter, to the south; Balthus of Protos, a captain of the Doom Eagles, to the west; and Decimus Felix of Vespator, Eleventh Captain of the Ultramarines, to the east.
