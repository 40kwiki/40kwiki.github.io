The Tetrarchy of Ultramar, also called the Tetrarch Council, was the governing body of the [[Realm of Ultramar]], the autonomous domain of the [[Ultramarines]]. Its members were the [[Tetrarch]]s together with the [[primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]]. Each Tetrarch was a military dictator of one of the five "king worlds" of Ultramar and ruled over an entire sub-sector of the realm.

History

The Tetrarchs were four distinguished veterans of the Ultramarines Legion, appointed by Guilliman. The title was used only by the Ultramarines during the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]], in the late 30th and early 31st millennia. Guilliman was the ruling lord of [[Macragge]], but his duties as political governor often fell to his [[seneschal]] while he led the Legion's armies.

The Council was revived by Guilliman after the [[Ultramar Campaign]] of 999.M41, when he declared all five hundred worlds of Ultramar part of his dominion again. In 012.M42 the Tetrarchy was re-established after the [[Plague Wars]], and the Tetrarchs were given the title of Lord Martial of the realm.
