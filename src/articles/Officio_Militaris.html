The Officio Militaris, also called the Divisio Militaris in [[High Gothic]], was a division of the government of the early [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It ran the bureaucracy and administration of the Imperium's military forces during the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]].

Duties

Its most important task was to meet the logistical needs of the Great Crusade. It oversaw the supply, manufacture and transport of materiel for crusade forces. It also gathered intelligence and coordinated the deployment of the [[expeditionary fleet]]s to their next targets. Its top officials were called strategos.

Reorganisation

The Imperium reorganised its government and military after the Horus Heresy. The reorganisation came early in the [[Time of Rebirth]], and the Officio Militaris was changed in that process.The sources do not record the exact successor offices. The [[Administratum]] is the body most often linked to its work.
