Asger Warfist was a [[Wolf Lord]] of the [[Space Wolves]] [[Chapter]] in the mid-32nd millennium (c. 544–546.M32). He was appointed the first [[watch commander]] of the [[Deathwatch]] during the [[War of the Beast]] by the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], [[Koorland]].

Early service

Warfist fought with his Chapter against the [[Ork]] forces of [[War of the Beast|the Beast]] that invaded an Imperial system near the [[Eye of Terror]]. Later he led his Great Company in the defence of the Industrial Moon of Fabrikk.

When the Space Wolves received word that their aid was needed, their [[Great Wolf]] sent Warfist to [[Terra]]. A strike force of [[Adeptus Astartes]] was to attack the world of [[Ullanor]], which was found to be the origin of the greenskin invasion. Warfist appeared before Koorland as part of the Chapter's taskforce. Reluctantly, he accepted the presence of the [[Dark Angels]] within the strike force. He then fought in the invasion of The Beast's homeworld.

The Deathwatch

Warfist became one of the first members of the Deathwatch and helped destroy the [[Attack Moon]] over Terra. Shortly before the second invasion of Ullanor, Koorland named him the first [[Chapter Master]] of the Deathwatch, with the title of "watch commander". The title then denoted the overall master of the force, like the rank of a Chapter Master.

Warfist fought alongside Koorland when the Lord Commander was killed by a second great [[greenskin]] [[warlord]], who was revealed to be another form of The Beast. In the third Imperial offensive on Ullanor, Warfist commanded one of the five attack groups.

In the millennia after the War of the Beast, the Deathwatch became far more decentralised. Its members were deployed across the [[galaxy]] from [[watch fortress]]es that were largely independent of each other. The title of watch commander no longer named a single overall leader. In the 41st millennium it refers to the commander of a particular watch fortress, usually a [[watch master]] and sometimes a senior [[watch captain]].
