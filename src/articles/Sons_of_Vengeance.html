The Sons of Vengeance are a Renegade [[Space Marine Chapter]] who turned to the service of the Ruinous Powers during the Obscuran Uprisings that ravaged the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] in the 34th millennium. Little is known of their deeds before they fell, or of their parent Chapter.

History

The Obscuran Uprisings

In the late 34th millennium the Sons of Vengeance were sent to put down anarchist separatist rebellions that had plagued much of the Segmentum Obscurus for nearly four centuries. With a second Chapter, the Silver Guards, they began as faithful servants of the Imperium. That ended with the fall of the rebel Free Council of Hannedra II. The two Chapters crushed the rebellion that had overthrown the planetary governor of that world. They then ran wild, slaughtering and looting in the name of the Emperor.

Treachery

Soon they dropped even the pretence of punishing rebels. The Sons of Vengeance took the campaign to the neighbouring system of Laskaria, which was loyal. By the time word of their treachery reached the [[Adeptus Terra]], the two Chapters had split into dozens of quarrelling Chaos Space Marine warbands. Fourteen star systems became a battleground as they fought each other for supremacy.

Background

The Segmentum Obscurus is the segmentum that holds the [[Eye of Terror]], and it has known rebellion and anarchy for much of the Imperium's history. The Obscuran Uprisings were separatist risings against the Imperial order that lasted almost four centuries in the late 34th millennium, and the Imperium met them with Space Marines because the planetary forces could not contain them. The Sons of Vengeance and the Silver Guards were sent for that purpose.

Fall to Chaos

What separates a punitive war from massacre is restraint, and the Imperial record says the two Chapters showed none at Hannedra II. Brutality in the name of the Emperor is the first step of a well-known road. Once the Chapters had begun to kill and loot without orders, the further step to open treachery at Laskaria was short, and the Chaos powers had already begun to win them. Who gave the Chapters their orders and who among their officers first broke discipline is not recorded. Nor is it known which of the many warbands that arose from the split still carries the Sons' name. The warband that does appears in the later records as an independent force of Chaos Undivided.

Organisation and beliefs

The warband paints its armour in a non-standard red and blue camouflage pattern with silver on the shoulder plates. Its badge is a white morning star on the same pattern. It serves Chaos Undivided and does not belong to a single god.The Chapter's loyalist heraldry has not survived. The records show only the badge it took after its fall.

Notable campaigns

  • Hannedra II (late M34): destruction of the Free Council.
  • Laskaria (late M34): an attack on a loyal system that ended the pretence of Imperial service.

Notable members

No member of the Sons of Vengeance is named in Imperial records.
