Octarius is an [[Orks|Ork]] empire in the [[Ultima Segmentum]], a dense cluster of Ork-held systems in the Octarius Sector that has existed for many thousands of years. It is known to the [[Imperium of Mankind|Imperium]] chiefly for the Octarius War, also called the War in Octarius, a war between the Orks and [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] that the Inquisitor Lord [[Fidus Kryptman]] started deliberately in the last years of the 41st millennium to turn the hive fleet away from the Imperium. That war has continued into the [[Era Indomitus]].

The Kryptman Gambit is the most famous example of the Imperium setting its enemies on each other. It bought the Imperium time, but the [[Ordo Xenos]] warns that the war will produce either Tyranids strengthened by the Orks' hardy genetic stock or a Waaagh! larger than any the Imperium has faced. In 999.M41 the Ork warlord [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] came to Octarius, broke the Tyranid blockade of its main world and took the Orks of the empire into his Great Waaagh!.

Background

The Ork empire of Octarius grew over many centuries from a few systems into a realm of crowded worlds, ruled at the end of the 41st millennium, according to earlier Imperial records, by the Grand Warlord Urlakk Urg. Its chief world, Octaria, held vast shanty cities, among them the capital Gargates. The Imperium regarded it as one of the largest greenskin realms on its borders, alongside [[Charadon]].

The Kryptman Gambit

In 997.M41 Kryptman detected the coming of a new hive fleet, Leviathan, which rose from below the galactic plane in two arms. After the victory at Tarsis Ultra, he ordered a cordon of [[Exterminatus]] across the fleet's path to deny it biomass. When migrating Orks occupied the emptied worlds, Kryptman was denounced as a radical and expelled from the [[Inquisition]].

Kryptman then turned to a new plan. With officers of the [[Deathwatch]] who remained loyal to him, he took a brood of genestealers in stasis from a drifting space hulk and released them among the Orks of Octarius, close to Leviathan's line of advance. The genestealers drew the hive fleet towards the crowded Ork worlds. Almost the whole of Leviathan turned into Octarius, and the Orks, delighted by an enemy that fought back without end, met it in battle.

Course of the war

The war is the largest recorded struggle between the two species. The Orks fight it gladly; the Hive Mind is drawn by the endless biomass. Neither side has been able to win, since every Ork killed feeds the Tyranids, and every battle makes the Orks more numerous and warlike. Imperial observers report that Tyranid bioforms have begun to show Ork traits, and that Ork war machines have grown larger and more numerous.

Ghazghkull at Octarius

In 999.M41, after the [[Third War for Armageddon]], Ghazghkull Thraka led his fleet to Octarius. His ships broke the Tyranid blockade, dropped asteroid fortresses across the main continent of Octaria and teleported his mobs into battle. At Gargates Ghazghkull led the charge himself; a Mawloc of unusual size swallowed him whole, and moments later he cut his way out with his power klaw. The Tyranids were cleared from Octaria, and the Overfiend Zog Steeltoof swore allegiance to him. The flood of Orks that joined him released a psychic surge felt by psykers across the Imperium.

Era Indomitus

When the [[Great Rift]] opened in 999.M41, Ghazghkull took it for the signal to begin his Great Waaagh! and left Octarius at the head of a fleet that the Imperium estimates at more than five million vessels. The war in Octarius did not end. Leviathan's tendrils remain locked in the sector, and Imperial forces sent in the Era Indomitus have tried to keep both sides contained and to stop either from breaking out. Some Ordo Xenos reports claim that Tyranid strains that fought in Octarius have since appeared in Imperial space.

Assessment

Kryptman's methods have been condemned and praised in equal measure. He bought the Imperium years to prepare against Leviathan, at the price of feeding Orkoid genes to the Great Devourer and creating a war that may one day produce a greater threat than either species alone. The Inquisition has never formally restored him to the Ordo Xenos, though several of its members have since followed his example.
