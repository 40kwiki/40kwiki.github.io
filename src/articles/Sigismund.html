Sigismund was the First Captain of the [[Imperial Fists]] Legion, the first Emperor's Champion, and the founder and first High Marshal of the [[Black Templars]]. He was held to be the finest swordsman of the [[Space Marine Legions]] below the [[Primarch|primarchs]]. In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) he led the mission to [[Mars]] that saved the arms of Mondus Occulum, and in the [[Siege of Terra]] (013–014.M31) he fought as the Emperor's Champion on the walls of the [[Imperial Palace]], killing a long roll of traitor champions.

After the Heresy Sigismund led the most zealous of Dorn's sons as the Black Templars and swore an oath never to cease the crusade against the Emperor's enemies, an oath each of his successors has renewed. He hunted [[Abaddon the Despoiler|Ezekyle Abaddon]] for more than seven centuries and met him at last in the first [[Black Crusade]] of 781.M31, where he was killed.

Early life

Sigismund was born on [[Terra]] in the last years of the [[Unification Wars]], which ended c. 712.M30, in a city later burned in the fighting. He grew up an orphan in a refugee camp on the Ionus plateau, among children protected by an older girl named Thera. Thera knelt and rested her head on her iron bar before every fight. When the gangs of the camp killed her, the boy Sigismund took up the bar, made the same gesture and drove them off. He kept that ritual for the rest of his life.

He was recruited into the Imperial Fists, where he met his lifelong comrade Fafnir Rann, and twelve years after the fight in the camp he was chosen for the Templar Brethren, among the youngest ever to be so honoured.

The Great Crusade

To become First Captain, Sigismund undertook the Templars' trial of succession: two hundred single combats, one after another, with no rest. He won all of them, the last against his old weapons master Appius, now entombed in a Dreadnought. [[Rogal Dorn]] watched the trial and gave him the relic sword Imperator Rex. Late in the [[Great Crusade]] the Imperial Fists served beside the [[World Eaters]], and in the fighting pits of their flagship Sigismund earned the name "the Black Knight" and swore brotherhood with [[Khârn the Betrayer|Khârn]], [[Angron]]'s equerry. From the World Eaters he took the custom of chaining his sword to his wrist.

The Horus Heresy

The Eisenstein and the vision

In 005.M31 Sigismund was with Dorn when the frigate Eisenstein brought [[Nathaniel Garro]] and the news of Horus's treachery. Dorn gave him command of the Retribution Fleet to [[Isstvan III|Isstvan]]. Before it sailed the remembrancer [[Euphrati Keeler]] showed him a vision of two paths: one led to a lonely death far away, the other to war without end, and on the second his father would need him. Sigismund asked to stay at Dorn's side and gave the fleet to Captain Yonnad. The Retribution Fleet was destroyed at the [[Battle of Phall]] in 007.M31. When Sigismund later confessed his true reason, Dorn kept him as First Captain but disowned him as a son, a shame known to no one else.

Mars

In 005.M31 Dorn sent Sigismund with four companies to secure the forges of Mars in the [[Schism of Mars]]. At Mondus Occulum he found the Fabricator-Locum [[Zagreus Kane]] loyal, and against the urge to stand and fight he carried out his orders, loading nearly twelve thousand suits of Mark IV armour onto transports before traitor Titans overran the forge.

The Siege of Terra

Dorn named Sigismund the first Emperor's Champion before the Siege. His armour was painted black, the colour of those who serve the Emperor directly, and he bore the Black Sword, a blade of unknown origin given at the Emperor's command. Keeler told him that the Emperor meant him to kill Abaddon.

In the battle for the [[Battle of Lion's Gate|Lion's Gate Spaceport]] in 014.M31 he saw Abaddon's banner and chose instead to cover the withdrawal of his men. In the last fight for the spaceport he met Khârn, who was swollen with the power of [[Khorne]], and would have died had Dorn not intervened. At the Saturnine Gate he held the wall against the [[Emperor's Children]], was saved again by Dorn from [[Fulgrim]], and threw the Lord Commander Eidolon from the battlements. Later in the Siege, now silent and merciless, he killed Indras Archeta of the [[Sons of Horus]] and, by the Palace accounts, Khârn himself, though Khârn is reported alive in later centuries.Imperial Fists records list Khârn among the champions Sigismund killed. Accounts of the [[Legion Wars]] and of later millennia place Khârn in battle again; Imperial scholars disagree whether he survived, was restored by his god, or is more than one warrior.

When Dorn went with the Emperor to board the [[Vengeful Spirit]], he left Sigismund in command of the ground forces. Sigismund held the Bhab Bastion until it fell, rescued Keeler and her refugees from the Sons of Horus, and led them to the Hollow Mountain. There he stood with [[Corswain]] and the [[Dark Angels]] against the [[Death Guard]] of [[Typhus]], until Keeler's prayer threw back the enemy and relit the [[Astronomican]]. After the battle he knelt with her to pray to the enthroned Emperor.

Black Templars

In the [[Great Scouring]] (014–021.M31) Sigismund hid Keeler away and led his Templars in a merciless purge of the traitors left in the [[Sol System]], urging the Legion to pursue the enemy rather than consolidate. At the [[Second Founding]] (c. 021.M31) he took the most zealous of the Imperial Fists as a new Chapter in the black and white of his heraldry. To answer those who suspected the Fists of resisting the [[Codex Astartes]], he swore that the Chapter would never rest from crusade. So began the Black Templars' crusade, unbroken since.

Death

Sigismund never stopped looking for Abaddon and was certain, against the doubts of the High Lords, that the traitors still lived in the [[Eye of Terror]]. In 781.M31, more than seven centuries after the Siege, Abaddon led the first Black Crusade out of the Eye, and the aged High Marshal found him. Sigismund drove the Black Sword through Abaddon's chest, but Abaddon killed him with the Talon of Horus. The traitor sent Sigismund's body to Terra aboard a captured Black Templars ship with a two-word message: "We are returned."

Legacy

Every Black Templars crusade still names an Emperor's Champion in black armour, and every High Marshal renews Sigismund's vow. Many in the [[Imperial Cult]] count him among the saints of the Siege.
