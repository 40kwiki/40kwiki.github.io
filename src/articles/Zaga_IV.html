Zaga IV is a world of the [[Macharian Crusade]]. It is known for the fighting against renegade [[Chaos Space Marines]] there in 393.M41, during which a bolt round lodged in the chest of the [[Lord Solar]] and failed to detonate.

The crusade's army groups fought across the worlds of the region in the years that followed. Zaga IV is one of the places where the Confessors of the crusade proclaimed a miracle.

Campaign

In 392.M41 the [[Hive world|hive world]] of [[Persepolis]] was brought back into contact with the Imperium for the first time in some five thousand years. The 1st and 2nd Army Groups then fought together across the deserts of [[Gedrosia]]. In 393.M41, during fighting against renegade [[Chaos Space Marines]] on Zaga IV, the bolt round struck the [[Lord Solar]]'s chest and did not detonate. The Confessors proclaimed it a miracle.
