The Rampagers are a loyal [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]] and a [[Second Founding]] Successor of the [[White Scars]]. Like their progenitors, they use ritual facial scarring to mark the rank and achievements of their [[Astartes]]. The scars are cut during a long and sacred Chapter ceremony known as the Blooding. The blood drawn during the rite is mixed into the drink served at the feast that always follows.

After a conquest, the Rampagers claim [[Feral world]]s as fresh recruiting grounds. They test the people of the world for spiritual and physical corruption before they recruit from them.

Campaigns

Battle of Corusil V (084.M31)

The Rampagers accompanied the White Scars force led by their primarch, [[Jaghatai Khan]], to the world of [[Corusil V]]. They sought vengeance on the [[Drukhari]] who had ravaged the White Scars homeworld of [[Chogoris]] and the wider [[Yasan Sector]] after the [[Horus Heresy]]. The White Scars and their successors defeated the Drukhari, but the [[Archon]] of the raiders escaped into the [[Webway]] through a portal. Jaghatai Khan and much of the White Scars' 1st Brotherhood followed him. The portal closed behind them. The fate of the Great Khan remains unknown.

Dabenlar III campaign (490.M34)

The 6th Company of the Rampagers advanced 3,000 kilometres in an armoured operation across the Burning Plains of the equatorial deserts of Dabenlar III. In a single night, they toppled a rebel theocracy that was outflanked and ill-prepared. After the battle the Chapter carried out a brutal purge of the population, which the Chapter then declared free of heresy. Before it left, the Chapter claimed recruitment rights over the population, from which it still draws its Aspirants.

7th Black Crusade (811.M37)

The [[7th Black Crusade]] was launched out of the [[Eye of Terror]] in 811.M37 by the [[Chaos Champion]] [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] and his [[Black Legion]]. The [[forces of Chaos]] poured through a great [[Warp rift]] past [[Cadia]] and then vanished. The conflict is known informally as the Ghost War.
