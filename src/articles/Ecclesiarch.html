The Ecclesiarch, also styled the Arch-Prelate of Terra, is the head of the [[Imperial Cult|Adeptus Ministorum]], the state church of the [[Imperium of Mankind]], and the high priest of the [[Imperial Cult]]. The Ecclesiarch is elected for life by the [[Holy Synod]], the assembly of the senior [[Cardinal]]s, and rules the Ministorum's hierarchy of priests, missionaries and confessors from the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] on [[Terra]], a fortified complex the size of a continent.

The office was created in the early [[32nd millennium]], when the [[High Lords of Terra]] recognised the Temple of the Saviour Emperor as the state religion. An Ecclesiarch was admitted to the council by the middle of that millennium, and the seat has been held without interruption since 546.M32. The Ecclesiarch declares [[War of Faith|Wars of Faith]], directs the [[Missionarius Galaxia]] and the [[Adepta Sororitas]], canonises [[Imperial Saint|saints]], and watches over the orthodoxy of the faithful across a million worlds.

Some six hundred men and women have held the office. Its history is marked by rivalry with the [[Master of the Administratum]], which ended in the usurpation of both offices by [[Goge Vandire]] in the [[36th millennium]] and the [[Reign of Blood]]. The reformer [[Sebastian Thor]], who succeeded him, is honoured as the greatest Ecclesiarch. Since the return of [[Roboute Guilliman]] in 999.M41 the office has been held by [[Eos Ritira]], who succeeded the deposed [[Baldo Slyst]].

Election and powers

On the death of an Ecclesiarch the Holy Synod gathers in the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] and elects a successor from among its own number. Since the reign of [[Equitus XI]] in the mid-36th millennium, the new Ecclesiarch takes a regnal name at coronation; Sebastian Thor, who refused one, was the last to rule under his own. Elections are frequently contested, and the records of the Ministorum name several Ecclesiarchs whose deaths were neither natural nor investigated.

The Ecclesiarch's principal powers are:

  • Representation. The Ecclesiarch sits among the [[High Lords of Terra]] and speaks for the Ministorum in the [[Senatorum Imperialis]].
  • Wars of Faith. Only the Ecclesiarch may declare a [[War of Faith]], calling the faithful of whole [[Sector|sectors]] to arms against a [[Heretic|heretic]] or [[Xenos|xenos]] foe.
  • Doctrine. The Ecclesiarch defines the orthodox worship of the [[Emperor of Mankind|God-Emperor]], recognises saints and issues the decrees of the Ministorum.
  • The Orders Militant. Under the [[Decree Passive]] of the [[36th millennium]] the Ministorum may keep no "men under arms". The [[Adepta Sororitas]], which is answerable to the Ecclesiarch, is the means by which it keeps a fighting force within that law.

History

Foundation (early M32)

The worship of the Emperor as a god was forbidden under the [[Imperial Truth]] during the [[Great Crusade]], and spread in secret through the ranks of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armies]] as the [[Lectitio Divinitatus]]. After the [[Horus Heresy]] many sects grew up across the Imperium, and the most powerful, the [[Frateris Templar|Temple of the Saviour Emperor]], spread from [[Terra]]. In the early [[32nd millennium]] the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]], seeing the value of a single faith for the unity of the realm, declared it the state religion, and its leader took the title of Ecclesiarch.

By the middle of the millennium the Ecclesiarch [[Veneris II]] had been admitted to the council. Imperial accounts differ on when the seat became permanent. The Ministorum's own chronicles hold that it became so three centuries after Veneris; the records of the Senatorum date it to 546.M32, when the seat was restored to Ostulus after a brief removal.

War of the Beast (544–546.M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]]

The Ecclesiarch at the outbreak of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 was [[Erekart Mesring]], a scion of a merchant family who used his seat to protect his house's shipyards. He was an ally of the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]]. Poisoned by the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Drakan Vangorich]], he was forced to declare a War of Faith against the [[Orks]]. The poison broke his mind as well as his faith. In 546.M32 he denounced the Lord Commander [[Koorland]] before the council and proclaimed the Ork warlord a god. Koorland shot him dead, and the Holy Synod did not protest. Koorland removed the Ecclesiarch's seat, and it was restored to Mesring's successor Ostulus after the [[Beheading]] later that year.

Ophelia VII and the rise of the Ministorum (M34–M35)

See also: [[Ophelia VII]]

The Ministorum grew in wealth and power over the following millennia. In c. 200.M34 the Ecclesiarch [[Benedin IV]] moved the seat of the church from Terra to the shrine world of [[Ophelia VII]], putting distance between himself and the Senatorum. A century later, in c. 300.M34, [[Greigor XI]] announced its return to Terra. He died of poison before the move was complete, but it went ahead, and the Ecclesiarchy's control over the council grew.The Ministorum's chronicles place both moves in M34; Administratum histories date them to the 35th millennium. This article follows the Ministorum. In the [[35th millennium]] [[Alexis XXI]] corrupted members of the [[Officio Assassinorum]] and used them to remove governors who opposed him.

Age of Apostasy (M36)

At the beginning of the 36th millennium the Ecclesiarch was [[Paulis III]], a weak man controlled by the Master of the Administratum, [[Goge Vandire]]. Vandire had him executed for heresy and took the Ecclesiarchy himself, c. 218.M36, becoming the only person in Imperial history to hold both offices. The [[Reign of Blood]] that followed lasted some seventy years. It ended in c. 288.M36, when [[Sebastian Thor]] of [[Dimmamar]] led a great army against Terra, Vandire's bodyguard, the Brides of the Emperor under [[Alicia Dominica]], learned the truth of his crimes, and Dominica beheaded him in the Ecclesiarchal Palace.

Thor was tried by the High Lords and acquitted. He refused the office of Ecclesiarch until the Captain-General of the [[Adeptus Custodes]], [[Excelsor]], persuaded him to accept it. As Ecclesiarch (c. 288–350.M36) he issued the Decree Passive, which forbade the church to keep armies, and turned the Brides into the [[Adepta Sororitas]]. He died in office in 350.M36, aged 112, and was declared a saint.

His successor Equitus XI (from 350.M36) completed the organisation of the Sororitas, created the convents and appointed the first [[Abbess Sanctorum]]. [[Alexis XXII]] (late M36) divided the two convents into four Orders Militant.

Later millennia (M37–M41)

[[Deacis VI]] (mid-M38) spread the Sisterhood further and founded the Orders of the Bloody Rose and the Sacred Rose. In the 39th millennium [[Inovian III]] canonised the heroes of the Redemption Crusades, and [[Umberto II]] reasserted the authority of the Ministorum over the Imperium for the first time since Vandire. [[Decius XXIII]], of the late 41st millennium, quarrelled repeatedly with the [[Inquisition]]; Ministorum records say his corruption brought him to his death at the hands of the [[Saint Celestine|Living Saint]].The manner of Decius XXIII's death is recorded only in a devotional account and is not confirmed by the Synod's records.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

At the time of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 the Ecclesiarch was [[Baldo Slyst]], who opposed the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]]'s attempt to release the Adeptus Custodes from their oath to remain on Terra. Slyst was among the High Lords removed by [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] after his return. He joined the former [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Irthu Haemotalion]] in the [[Hexarchy]], and was killed by the agents of the Grand Master of Assassins [[Fadix]] when its coup failed in early M42. [[Eos Ritira]], appointed when Guilliman restored the council (c. 000.M42), is the incumbent.The year of the Hexarchy's coup is not recorded in the available sources. It is estimated as c. 000–012.M42.
