The Angels Numinous are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a successor of the Blood Eagles, a [[Blood Angels]] successor in turn. They carry the flaws in the blood of [[Sanguinius]]: the [[Red Thirst]] and the [[Black Rage]]. The Chapter's badge is a skull in a spiked halo, and its armour is dark bronze.

The Angels Numinous differ from the Blood Angels in their treatment of the afflicted. When a battle-brother falls to the Black Rage he is placed in the Chapter's Death Company, but the Chapter holds that only the weak fall to it. It does not honour the Death Company as the Blood Angels do, and regards its lost brothers as an embarrassment. Its Chaplains hurl them into battle as a blunt instrument to break an enemy's front, with no concern for their lives, and have done so across thousands of years.

History

The Triarchy

The Blood Eagles tore themselves in two in a conflict whose cause is unrecorded, and their halves became the Angels Numinous and the Red Seraphs. The two joined a third Blood Angels successor, the Charnel Guard, in an alliance called the Triarchy. The Angels Numinous and Red Seraphs agreed on nothing, but all three Chapters used the formula "We are Three, drawn from Two, born of One" at their meetings, held at the abandoned homeworld of the Blood Eagles. Representatives would speak the words before any council could begin.

The Kallius Insurrection

In the 40th millennium (the date is not recorded) the worlds of the Kallius Sub-sector rose in rebellion, and the Angels Numinous, under Chapter Master Deren, were sent to put it down. They were overwhelmed, and the Blood Angels sent reinforcements. In the war that followed the Blood Angels lost nine in ten of their Marines and almost their whole Chapter council. Three companies, among them the 8th under the young Brother-Captain [[Dante]], attacked Kallius' Landing and its dependent worlds. Entire populations and scratch fleets were led by chanting Traitor Marines from many warbands, and the war became one of trenches and sieges. The Blood Angels withdrew with their heads high when relief Chapters and Astra Militarum regiments arrived. As their fleet mustered above Kallius' Landing, a Traitor armada came out of the Warp and fought them for three solar days.The Kallius Insurrection is dated only to the 40th millennium in Imperial records.

Devastation of Baal and the Great Rift

The Angels Numinous were among the Blood Angels successors who rushed to defend Baal from [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] in the Devastation of Baal (c. 999.M41). After the [[Great Rift]] opened, the Triarchy was cut off in the [[Imperium Nihilus]]. At a conclave held at the Blood Eagles' homeworld, Jagudir of the Angels Numinous, Ynoch of the Red Seraphs and Mordath of the Charnel Guard decided to reject tradition. Rather than defend their shrinking domains, they would hunt the Emperor's enemies in the Dark Imperium even at the cost of their Chapters, and join [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s [[Indomitus Crusade]] if they could find it.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter is bound by tradition, as are all of the Triarchy. It calls the [[Emperor of Mankind]] the Padizhah, and cremates its dead with careful rites to free the spirit, that the shades of the fallen may come to the Padizhah's golden throne. The Chapter's attitude to the Black Rage is a point of tension with its parent, and its treatment of the Death Company is one of the features that set it apart from the sons of Baal.

Notable members

  • Geron, Chapter Master in the Era Indomitus.
  • Deren, Chapter Master during the Kallius Insurrection.
  • Toranis, Captain at Kallius.
  • Jagudir, the Chapter's delegate to the post-Rift conclave of the Triarchy.
