The Executioners are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a [[Third Founding]] successor of the [[Imperial Fists]] created in the [[31st millennium]] after the [[Great Scouring]]. Their homeworld is the binary world of Stygia-Aquilon on the southern fringe of the [[Maelstrom]], and their fortress-monastery the asteroid base of Darkenvault. They are regarded by some as little better than headhunters, and were one of the four Chapters that joined the Secession of [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]] in the [[Badab War]].

After the war the Executioners were sent on a hundred-year [[Penitent|penitent]] crusade. By the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[High Lords of Terra]] judged it complete, and the Chapter returned to its homeworld, received [[Primaris Space Marines]] from a Torchbearer fleet of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and became a Chapter made entirely of Primaris warriors when every surviving Firstborn crossed the Rubicon Primaris.

History

Early campaigns

In 302.M36, at the start of the [[Plague of Unbelief]], the Executioners were among the first [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] to take up arms against the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis, and fought at [[Gathalamor]] alongside the [[Space Wolves]], the Angels of Absolution and the Raven Guard. In recognition, a new Chapter, the Iron Champions, was later raised from their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. A second successor, the Crimson Axes, is recorded as lost in about 092.M39.

In the 39th millennium the southern Segmentum Tempestus was assailed by Ork nomad fleets, xenos migrations and psychic phenomena, and Darkenvault was besieged. The [[Astral Claws]] broke the siege at great cost, among their dead their own Chapter Master, Acas Seneca, and the Executioners owed them a debt of honour from then on. In 137.M40 the Chapter hunted down the last of the Revvokan line of Rogue Traders, Hektor Revvokan, who had turned to the Dark Gods. In 899.M41 the Second Company under High Chaplain Thulsa Kane fought an awakened threat of metallic foes at Carrion Deep, after distress calls from Imperial deep-range outposts.The Siege of Darkenvault is dated only to M39.

The Badab War

Main article: [[Badab War]]

Bound by their debt, the Executioners had fully joined the Secession by 904.M41. An Inquisitorial mission reached Stygia-Aquilon after a five-month journey and was given full access, and found their gene-seed and faith uncorrupted. The Executioners refused to serve under the Tyrant of Badab, and fought only as uneasy allies. Their forces won a fearsome name in the war. In 907.M41 they inflicted catastrophic losses on the [[Howling Griffons]] at the lunar outposts of Khymara, and they hounded Loyalist supply convoys with their cruiser Night Hag. When Astral Claws forces attacked the Salamanders battle barge Pyre of Glory, which had peacefully surrendered to the Executioners, the Executioners turned on their former allies in the battle called the Red Hour, and afterwards fought a private war until they negotiated their surrender and withdrawal in 911.M41.

The Executioner force under High Chaplain Thulsa Kane was escorted to the [[Salamanders]]' homeworld of [[Nocturne]], and the Salamanders took custody of Stygia-Aquilon. In 913.M41 the Chapter was tried with the other Secessionists and ordered on a hundred-year penitent crusade.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Released by the Salamanders, the Executioners began their hundred-year penitent crusade in 919.M41. When it was judged successfully completed in the Era Indomitus, the Executioners returned to Stygia-Aquilon, which the Salamanders gave back to them, and met a Torchbearer fleet. It brought them Primaris Marines raised from their own gene-seed and the technology to make more. All the Chapter's surviving Firstborn chose to cross the Rubicon Primaris, and the Executioners now consist entirely of Primaris Space Marines.

Organisation and culture

The [[Chapter Master]] bears the title High Executioner. The Executioners keep a warrior culture that values the taking of trophies, notably the heads of fallen enemies, and the Chapter has a fearsome reputation for undaunted endurance and destructive wrath, and its Astartes carry trophies from the battlefield, such as skulls, heads and enemy weapons.
