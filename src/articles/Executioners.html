The Executioners are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], a [[Third Founding]] successor of the [[Imperial Fists]] created in the 31st millennium after the [[Great Scouring]]. Their homeworld is the binary world of Stygia-Aquilon on the southern fringe of the [[Maelstrom]], and their fortress-monastery the asteroid base of Darkenvault. They are a savage, head-taking Chapter, and were one of the four Chapters that joined the Secession of [[Huron Blackheart|Lufgt Huron]] in the [[Badab War]].

After the war the Executioners were sent on a hundred-year penitent crusade. By the [[Era Indomitus]] the [[High Lords of Terra]] judged it complete, and the Chapter returned to its homeworld, received [[Primaris Space Marines]] from a Torchbearer fleet of the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and became a Chapter made entirely of Primaris warriors when every surviving Firstborn crossed the Rubicon Primaris.

History

Early campaigns

In 302.M36, at the start of the [[Plague of Unbelief]], the Executioners were among the first Chapters to take up arms against the Apostate Cardinal Bucharis, and fought at Galthamor alongside the [[Space Wolves]]. In recognition, a new Chapter, the Iron Champions, was later raised from their gene-seed. A second successor, the Crimson Axes, is recorded as lost in about 092.M39.

In the 39th millennium the southern Segmentum Tempestus was assailed by Ork nomad fleets, xenos migrations and psychic phenomena, and Darkenvault was besieged. The [[Astral Claws]] broke the siege at great cost, among their dead their own Chapter Master, Acas Seneca, and the Executioners owed them a debt of honour from then on. In 137.M40 the Chapter hunted down the last of the Revvokan line of Rogue Traders, Hektor Revvokan, who had turned to the Dark Gods. In 899.M41 the Second Company under High Chaplain Thulsa Kane fought an awakened threat at Carrion Deep in defence of Imperial deep-range outposts.The Siege of Darkenvault is dated only to M39.

The Badab War

Bound by their debt, the Executioners had fully joined the Secession by 904.M41. An Inquisitorial mission reached Stygia-Aquilon after a five-month journey and was given full access, but the Chapter's forces won a fearsome name in the war. In 907.M41 they inflicted catastrophic losses on the [[Howling Griffons]] at the lunar outposts of Khymara, and they hounded Loyalist supply convoys; in 911.M41 they destroyed the [[Sons of Medusa]] strike cruiser Warspite near the Grief System. As Huron's corruption grew clear, the Executioners turned on their former allies and fought a private war against the Astral Claws until they negotiated their surrender and withdrawal in 911.M41.

The Executioner force under High Chaplain Thulsa Kane was escorted to the [[Salamanders]]' homeworld of [[Nocturne]], and the Salamanders took custody of Stygia-Aquilon. In 913.M41 the Chapter was tried with the other Secessionists and ordered on a hundred-year penitent crusade.

Era Indomitus

When their century of penance was judged successfully completed, the Executioners returned to Stygia-Aquilon, which the Salamanders gave back to them, and met a Torchbearer fleet. It brought them Primaris Marines raised from their own gene-seed and the technology to make more. All the Chapter's surviving Firstborn chose to cross the Rubicon Primaris, and the Executioners now consist entirely of Primaris Space Marines.The end of the penitent crusade is dated c. 011.M42, a hundred years after the surrender of 911.M41.

Organisation and culture

The Chapter Master bears the title High Executioner. The Executioners keep a warrior culture that values the taking of trophies, notably the heads of fallen enemies, and their warriors are known for their ferocity in close assault and their skill in void war.
