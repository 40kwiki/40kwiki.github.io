This is a timeline of the Tyranid invasions, the attacks of the [[Tyranids]] on the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and its neighbours. The swarms came into the galaxy from beyond the intergalactic void in successive hive fleets, each directed by its own [[Hive Mind]]. Each major fleet produced a Tyrannic War, which the Imperium numbered in the order of the fleets it had defeated.

The Imperium first met the Tyranids in early 745.M41, and the years since have been counted from that first defeat. Because the fleets arrived in splinters and the Imperium's own reports were delayed by the warp, some dates are given as periods. Such dates are marked "c.".Estimated; no record gives an exact year.

First Tyrannic War

Main article: [[Battle of Macragge]]
DateEvent
Early 745.M41First recorded contact. [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]] strips the research station of [[Tyran Primus]] in a short time.
745.M41The [[Battle of Macragge]]. The [[Ultramarines]] and the [[Navis Imperialis]] destroy Hive Fleet Behemoth, and splinter fleets flee into the galaxy.

Gorgon and the T'au

DateEvent
899.M41[[Hive Fleet Gorgon]], a splinter of Behemoth, consumes the trading partners of the [[T'au Empire]]. Late in the year the Sha'draig orbital outpost is destroyed.
903.M41The T'au and the [[Astra Militarum]] destroy Gorgon at [[Kel'shan]] in a rare joint operation.

Second Tyrannic War

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Kraken]]
DateEvent
990.M41By early 990.M41 [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] has already sunk its tendrils into the galaxy, isolating Imperial worlds before any astropathic warning can be sent.
991.M41An outer wing of Kraken strikes the T'au near the [[Damocles Gulf]]. The T'au suffer heavy losses before they drive it off.
992.M41The Second Tyrannic War is declared, beginning with the Ichar IV rebellion, the defence of [[Hollonan]] by [[Mephiston]] of the [[Blood Angels]], and the defence of [[Iyanden]] by the [[Aeldari]].
992.M41Kraken destroys the homeworld of the [[Scythes of the Emperor]], and nearly the whole Chapter with it.
993.M41The main fleet of Kraken is broken, and its splinter fleets flee towards the core.

Third Tyrannic War

Main article: [[Hive Fleet Leviathan]]
DateEvent
997.M41[[Hive Fleet Leviathan]] arrives, the largest fleet yet recognised by the Imperium. [[Fidus Kryptman]] identifies its signs and the Third Tyrannic War begins less than five years after Kraken's defeat.
998.M41News reaches Baal that the Cryptan Shield, the fortified worlds of the Cryptus System, is threatened by Leviathan.
c. 999.M41The Third Tyrannic War ends with the [[Devastation of Baal]], in which the [[Blood Angels]] and their allies, aided by [[Khorne]]'s daemons, defeat Leviathan.Estimated; the Devastation of Baal is dated to the Rift year.
c. 999.M41[[Hive Fleet Hydra]] enters the galaxy from the south in the wake of the [[Great Rift]].

Fourth Tyrannic War and the Era Indomitus

DateEvent
058.M42After three decades with no confirmed Tyranid sighting, the [[Mechanicus]] world of [[Fecundia]] is declared cleansed.
Early M42The [[Fourth Tyrannic War]] is fought, with the fall of Oghram and the siege of Sanctum.Estimated; the Fourth Tyrannic War is dated only to the early 42nd millennium.
