The Lord Commander Militant is the highest officer of the [[Astra Militarum]], the Imperial Guard, and its representative among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. In law the office commands every regiment of the Imperium's ground armies and shares the direction of the [[Departmento Munitorum]] with the [[Master of the Administratum]]. In practice the holder is a political figure who seldom sees a battlefield. The conduct of war is left to the five Lords Commander of the [[Segmentum|segmenta]] and, below them, to the [[Lord General Militant|Lords Militant]] who command individual theatres and crusades.

The office is one of the oldest of the Imperium. A Lord Commander Militant of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armies]] sat among the High Lords Civilian during the [[Unification Wars]] of the late [[30th millennium]], and the commanders of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] held the title through the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]]. When the [[Primarch|primarch]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] separated the army from the fleets after the Heresy (c. 014.M31), the Lord Commander Militant became the head of the ground forces alone, a rival to the new [[Lord High Admiral]].

The seat on the council is formally a rotating one, but a Lord Commander Militant has sat there in nearly every recorded age. The holder at the dawn of the [[Era Indomitus]], [[Mar Av Ashariel]], joined the [[Hexarchy]] against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and was killed when its coup failed in early M42.

Powers and duties

The Lord Commander Militant is the commander-in-chief of the [[Astra Militarum]] and the [[Militarum Tempestus]], and the chief military adviser of the council on matters of ground war. He issues the general orders under which regiments are raised, tithed and assigned, and he can name a [[Lord General Militant]] to command a theatre or confirm one named by a [[Segmentum|segmentum]] commander. Together with the [[Master of the Administratum]] he oversees the [[Administratum|Departmento Munitorum]], the vast department that raises, trains, equips and transports the Guard; the balance between the two offices has changed many times.

The office is limited by the distances of the Imperium. A Lord Commander Militant on [[Terra]] may learn of a war years after it began, and his orders reach the front later still. He has no command over the [[Imperial Navy]], which carries his armies, nor over the [[Adeptus Astartes]], and he must negotiate with the [[Lord High Admiral]] for every transport.

Since 999.M41 the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], [[Roboute Guilliman]], has also styled himself Lord Militant of the council, taking direct command of the Imperium's armies for the [[Indomitus Crusade]]. The Lord Commander Militant remains the head of the Astra Militarum under him.

History

Unification and the Great Crusade (late M30 – 014.M31)

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

The earliest recorded holder is [[Kli-San Weia]], Lord Commander Militant of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial Armies]] and one of the four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian at the time the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]] took the field, late in the [[Unification Wars]] and before the Great Crusade began in c. 798.M30. Through the Crusade the Lords Commander Militant led the mortal [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], while the [[Space Marine Legions]] answered to their [[Primarch|primarchs]] and to the Emperor.

At the time of the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 the office was shared by two officers, [[Tabor Ludovicia]] and [[Haldane Ma'lon]]. By the [[Siege of Terra]] (c. 013–014.M31) it was held by a single commander, [[Adreen]]. Adreen sat on the council during the siege and supported [[Rogal Dorn]]'s decision to keep the Legions within the [[Imperial Palace]] while the Imperial Army defended the rest of Terra. He survived the Heresy and sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31, while the fleets passed to the first Lord High Admiral, [[Niora Su-Kassen]].

The separation of the services (014–121.M31)

In his reforms as Lord Commander of the Imperium, Guilliman divided the Imperial Army into the Imperial Guard and the Imperial Navy, so that no commander could hold both soldiers and the ships that carried them. Regiments were limited in size and drawn from many worlds, and the Lord Commander Militant, now head of the Guard alone, lost the fleets that had given his predecessors their reach.

War of the Beast and the Beheading (544–646.M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]] and [[Beheading]]

At the start of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 the Lord Commander Militant was [[Heth]], a soldier's general who opposed the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]] on the council. Heth led an expedition in person to relieve the [[Imperial Fists]] on [[Ardamantua]] and was killed when his fleet was destroyed by the Orks, but not before he sent Terra the first warning of the Beast and its Attack Moons.

His successor, [[Abel Verreault]] (544–546.M32), was a politician who found that his troops had no ships, and bought transport from Lansung with his vote. He supported the disastrous Proletarian Crusade and the Lord Commander [[Udin Macht Udo]], and abandoned Udo when the [[Inquisition]] moved against him. He was murdered in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32 by the [[Callidus Temple|Callidus]] Assassin who had replaced the Master of the Administratum. The Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]] gave the seat to [[Oskar Lowis]], who served as his instrument until Vangorich's fall, c. 626.M32.

Later millennia (M33–M41)

Few holders of later ages are named in public records. [[Alberan Varn]], at an unrecorded date, commanded a battle group sent to conquer the world of Vengeance; it was lost in the [[Warp]] for 107 years and arrived to find the world a ruin overrun by [[Tyranids]], which Varn conquered regardless.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

Mar Av Ashariel held the office at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 and kept his seat when Guilliman restored the council. He was one of the serving High Lords who joined the Hexarchy of the former Master of the Administratum [[Irthu Haemotalion]], and he was killed by the agents of the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Fadix]] when the conspirators moved to seize Terra in early M42. The Lord Commander Solar [[Arcadian Leontus]] is also recorded among the High Lords of the later Era Indomitus.The year of the Hexarchy's coup is not given in the available records. It is estimated as c. 000–012.M42.
