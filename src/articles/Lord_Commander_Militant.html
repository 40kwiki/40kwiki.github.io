The Lord Commander Militant is the highest officer of the [[Astra Militarum]], the Imperial Guard, and its representative among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The office is primarily a political one: its holder spends most of his time overseeing the bureaucracy of the [[Departmento Munitorum]] and in political in-fighting with the other High Lords. The administration of military matters and the strategy of the Guard are left to the five Lords Commander of the [[Segmentum|segmenta]] and, below them, to the [[Lord General Militant|Lords Militant]] who command individual theatres and crusades.

The office is one of the oldest of the Imperium. A Lord Commander Militant of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial armies]] sat among the High Lords Civilian during the [[Unification Wars]] of the late [[30th millennium]], and the commanders of the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] held the title through the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]].

The holder at the dawn of the [[Era Indomitus]], [[Mar Av Ashariel]], joined the [[Hexarchy]] against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and was killed when its coup failed in early M42.

Powers and duties

The Lord Commander Militant is the supreme commander of the [[Astra Militarum]] and its representative on the [[Senatorum Imperialis]]. His immediate subordinates are the five Lords Commander of the segmenta, who keep the real direction of the Guard. A Lord Commander Militant on [[Terra]] may learn of a war years after it began, and he has no ships of his own: in the [[War of the Beast]] the Lord Commander Militant Verreault could not move his regiments until he agreed to follow the political lead of the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]], who controlled the transports.

History

Unification and the Great Crusade (M30 – M31)

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

The earliest recorded holder is [[Kli-San Weia]], Lord Commander Militant of the [[Imperial Armed Forces|Imperial Armies]] and one of the four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian at the time the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]] took the field, late in the [[Unification Wars]] and before the Great Crusade began in c. 798.M30. Through the Crusade the Lords Commander Militant led the mortal [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], while the [[Space Marine Legions]] answered to their [[Primarch|primarchs]] and to the Emperor.

At the time of the [[Council of Nikaea]] in 001.M31 the office was shared by two officers, [[Tabor Ludovicia]] and [[Haldane Ma'lon]]. By the [[Siege of Terra]] (014.M31) it was held by a single commander, [[Adreen]]. Adreen sat on the council during the siege and supported [[Rogal Dorn]]'s decision to keep the Legions within the [[Imperial Palace]] while the Imperial Army defended the rest of Terra. He survived the Heresy and sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31, while the fleets passed to the first Lord High Admiral, [[Niora Su-Kassen]].

War of the Beast and the Beheading (544–546.M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]] and [[Beheading]]

At the start of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32 the Lord Commander Militant was [[Heth]], a soldier's general who opposed the Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]] on the council. Heth led an expedition in person to relieve the [[Imperial Fists]] on [[Ardamantua]] and was killed when his fleet was destroyed by the Orks, but not before he sent Terra the first warning of the Beast and its Attack Moons.

His successor, [[Abel Verreault]] (544–546.M32), was a politician who found that his troops had no ships, and bought transport from Lansung with his vote. He supported the Proletarian Crusade and the political schemes of the Lord Commander [[Udin Macht Udo]]. He was assassinated in the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32. His successor [[Oskar Lowis]] was a political puppet of the Grand Master of Assassins [[Drakan Vangorich]], and carried out Vangorich's plans until the Grand Master was slain by the intervention of the [[Adeptus Astartes]].

Later millennia (M33–M41)

Few holders of later ages are named in public records. [[Alberan Varn]], at an unrecorded date, commanded a battle group sent to conquer the world of Vengeance; it was lost in the [[Warp]] for 107 years and arrived to find the world a ruin overrun by [[Tyranids]], which Varn conquered regardless.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

Mar Av Ashariel held the office at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 and kept his seat when Guilliman restored the council. He was one of the serving High Lords who joined the Hexarchy of the former Master of the Administratum [[Irthu Haemotalion]], and he was killed by the agents of the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Fadix]] when the conspirators moved to seize Terra in early M42. The Imperial records available to this article place the coup early in the Indomitus Crusade, while Guilliman was away from Terra.
