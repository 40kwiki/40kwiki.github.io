Mar Av Ashariel was the last recorded [[Lord Commander Militant]], the supreme commander of the [[Astra Militarum]], before the seat was lost in the [[Hexarchy]]'s coup in early M42. He was one of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and was killed in the fighting.

His successor is not named in the public record.

The Hexarchy's coup

The coup was carried out by the [[Hexarchy]], a group of conspirators among the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] and their allies. Three conspirators who still held seats, the [[Grand Provost Marshal]] [[Aveliza Drachmar]], Ashariel and the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Merelda Pereth]], were killed in the fighting.

Legacy

Ashariel's name is recorded in the [[Astra Militarum]]'s lists of its supreme commanders. The [[Lord Commander Militant]]'s seat on the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] has been unfilled in the public record since the coup.
