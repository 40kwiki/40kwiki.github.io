The Angels of Vigilance are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of mysterious origin that has stood vigil over the [[Eye of Terror]] for nearly ten thousand years. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Administratum]] cannot name its [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] with certainty, though its monastic discipline and unflinching sense of duty have led scholars to suspect a descent from the [[Dark Angels]]. The Chapter does not appear in the Apocrypha of Davio as a Dark Angels successor, so the claim is unconfirmed.

The Chapter's fortress-monastery is a vast orbital dock circling the world of Pervigilium, in the system just beyond the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]], the stable corridor from the Eye of Terror to [[Terra]]. Each time fleets of [[Chaos]] pour out of the Eye, the Angels muster to meet them; each time the Warp calms enough, they take the war to the Eye.

History

Origins

Imperial records disagree about when the Chapter was raised. [[Administratum]] registers list it among [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of "later [[Third Founding|Foundings]]", and one Imperial source dates it to the [[40th millennium]]. The records of the Third War for Armageddon place its beginning in or soon after the [[Second Founding]], and that account, the more detailed, is followed here.

The tradition of the oath of the Chapter is tied to a story told among the [[Dark Angels]] as the "Fall of the House of Pervigilium". In the years after the [[Horus Heresy]] the Legion disobeyed orders from the [[High Lords of Terra]] and campaigned against the ruling castes of Pervigilium. The Dark Angels decreed that a detachment should stay on the world to watch the ruling caste and make sure it never again strayed. Many years after the Second Founding, the Angels of Vigilance took up station about the planet, and have kept watch over it and the Eye ever since.

The blood oath

Unlike most Chapters the Angels of Vigilance have never committed their full strength to a single war. Every battle-brother has sworn a blood oath that the fortress-monastery shall never be left undefended, and at least one company is always on garrison. The Masters of the Order have several times had their loyalty questioned for ignoring requests from the High Lords of Terra for the entire Chapter's aid.

Wars of the late 41st millennium

In 998.M41 the Chapter sent six companies to the Third War for Armageddon ([[Wars for Armageddon]]). In 999.M41 it sent five companies to fight the 13th [[Black Crusade]] of [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] on the surface of [[Cadia]], and held there until the world was destroyed. It is one of only four Chapters known to have fought in both wars.

Organisation and heraldry

It is not clear that the Chapter follows the [[Codex Astartes]] entirely, as the [[Dark Angels]], its suspected progenitors, follow it only in part. The [[Chapter Master]] goes by the title Master of the Order. The Marines wear yellow armour with a black [[Aquila]] and black vent ports on the backpack. The right shoulder carries the black squad specialty symbol with a white numeral, and company is shown on the left knee, in white for the 1st Company, yellow for the 2nd, red for the 3rd and so on. The badge is a black crucible with black flames and two stylised [[Black Wings|black wings]], on a yellow field.

Notable members

No individual of the Chapter is named in Imperial records. Its Masters of the Order are not recorded.
