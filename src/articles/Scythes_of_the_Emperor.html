The Scythes of the Emperor, also called the Emperor's Scythes, are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a successor of the [[Ultramarines]]. They follow the [[Codex Astartes]] and are counted among the nine Shield Chapters that help the Ultramarines defend and extend the Realm of [[Ultramar]]. The Chapter grew from the garrison that [[Roboute Guilliman]] left on the restricted world of Sotha to guard a [[Xenos|xenos]] beacon known as the [[Pharos]].

In 992.M41 the Chapter lost Sotha to [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] and was reduced to roughly two companies, many of whom carried the taint of the [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer]]'s Kiss. It was restored during the [[Indomitus Crusade]] with [[Primaris Space Marines]] and now keeps its old watch from the rebuilt star fort Aegida.

History

The Aegida Company

Sotha was classed a restricted world because of the [[Pharos]], a pre-Imperial biotechnological device buried in Mount Pharos, which used the psychic energy of empathy to aid navigation through the [[Warp]] in the manner of a much smaller [[Astronomican]]. During the [[Imperium Secundus]] [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]] used it as a beacon to call scattered loyalists to [[Macragge]]. [[Ultramarines|The Ultramarines]]' 199th "Aegida" Company was left to hold the mountain, and its warriors took crossed scythes as their badge, after the farmers of the world and the ritual clearing of the ground about the site.

At the Battle of Sotha, c. 010.M31,Dated from the sequence of events in the Imperium Secundus; no record gives the year. a fleet of the [[Night Lords]] under Captain Krukesh the Pale tried to seize the Pharos. The [[Iron Warriors]] Warsmith Barabas Dantioch overloaded the device and died doing so, which disabled the Night Lords above the world. The Scout Oberdeii led his squad in the defence and was promoted sergeant by Guilliman himself.

Founding

See also: [[Second Founding]] and [[Third Founding]]

After the [[Second Founding]] in 021.M31 the Aegida was kept as a phantom eleventh company against the Codex. When the [[Third Founding]] began, Oberdeii was its last survivor, now Warden of the Pharos under an Aegidan Oath sworn on the [[Primarch|primarch]]'s blade. To remove an embarrassment to the Chapter of Macragge, the Ultramarines [[Chapter Master]] Tigris Decon sent the [[Chaplain]] Segas to offer him a choice: serve in the Fifth Company, or command a new Chapter. He chose the Chapter and named it for the scythes. Segas, Brother Wenlocke and seventy-two veterans of the Orlan Conquest were assigned to him. A fortress-monastery rose on Sotha in 001.M32.

Crusades and the fall of Sotha

From Sotha, near the [[Damocles Gulf]], the Scythes policed nearby mines and manufactories and later turned on Ork migrations. In the Damocles Gulf Crusade they led the first landing on the [[T'au Empire|T'au]] world of Sy'l'kell in 742.M41 and fought at Dal'yth Prime with the Ultramarines and [[Iron Hands]]. They also served in the Corinth Crusade under [[Marneus Calgar]] and, in 913.M41, were set by the [[Inquisition]] to watch the [[Penitent|penitent]] [[Lamenters]], with whom they formed a lasting alliance.

In 992.M41 a [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer cult]] on Sotha drew [[Hive Fleet Kraken]] to the world. Chapter Master Thorcyra held the fortress long enough for the population to flee, then broke out with some two hundred battle-brothers and regrouped in the Miral System. Sotha was stripped to an asteroid. Many survivors bore the Kiss and wore psychic dampening hoods against the Broodmind.

Era Indomitus

Guilliman named the Scythes a Shield Chapter of [[Ultramar]] and ordered [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] reinforcement. Within a few years the surviving Firstborn were reduced to a few veterans and officers. They went with [[Belisarius Cawl]] to Sotha to study the Pharos, where a cult that had infiltrated the Chapter's serfs attacked them. Chapter Master Thrasius died fighting the Patriarch that had first brought Kraken, and every remaining Firstborn fell with him. Aegida was rebuilt as the fortress-monastery. In M42 a Scythes force fought in the Aberrus System beside the Iron Lords, the [[Knights of the Raven]] and the Lamenters.

Organisation and beliefs

The Chapter keeps the standard ten companies of the Codex. Its war cries include "For the ghosts of Sotha!" and, against [[Tyranids]] only, "For the Emperor and for Sotha!". Since [[Hive Fleet Kraken|Kraken]] its members have been secretive and watchful for genetic corruption.

Notable campaigns

  • Verdeworlds (865.M41): with the [[Black Templars]] and [[Blood Angels]], cleansed the Verdeworlds of [[Aeldari]] settlers.
  • Sy'l'kell and Gel'Bryn (742–745.M41): broke a [[T'au Empire|T'au]] counter-assault and fought for the city of Gel'Bryn until the crusade withdrew before [[Hive Fleet Behemoth]].
  • Corinth Crusade (698–705.M41): drove the Ork warlord Skargor back to Corinth.

Notable members

  • Oberdeii: first [[Chapter Master]], Warden of the [[Pharos]].
  • Thorcyra: Chapter Master who led the defence of Sotha in 992.M41.
  • Thrasius: Chapter Master killed on Sotha in the [[Era Indomitus]].
