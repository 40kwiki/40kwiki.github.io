Thessalon is a world of the [[Helican Sub-sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. Its [[Sub-sector|sub-sector]]'s capital is the [[Hive world|hive world]] [[Thracian Primaris]], and the other notable worlds of the sub-sector include [[Gudrun]], [[Bonaventure]], [[Hubris]] and [[Lethe]].

Imperial records describe Thessalon as a settled world of modest standing. Its governance is recorded through the sub-sector's governor rather than through a separate planetary office.

Governance

The [[Sub-sector|sub-sector governor]], Jeromya Faurlitz IV, also commands the [[Astra Militarum]] forces in the [[Helican Sub-sector]]. Thessalon's regiments are raised and kept under that command. The Imperial records give no independent governor for the world.

Position in the sub-sector

Thessalon lies among the [[Sub-sector|sub-sector]]'s inner worlds and is linked to [[Thracian Primaris]] by the sub-sector's regular [[Warp travel|warp routes]]. The Imperial registers record its population as settled and its industry as modest, and the sub-sector's regiments draw on the world for recruits.
