The battle-barge is the largest warship used by the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. Most [[Space Marine Chapter]]s control two or three of them. The name is not a single class. It applies to any variant of a [[battleship]] hull that has been adapted for the [[Space Marines]] and their assault doctrine.

Design

Battle-barges were designed during the [[Great Crusade]] for one purpose, which is to assault planets. They carry many bombardment turrets and torpedo tubes for close support of landings. Much of the hull is given to launch bays for [[Thunderhawk]]s and [[Drop Pod]]s, and up to three companies can be deployed at once.

The hull is heavily armoured and protected by strong [[void shield]]s. A battle-barge can breach a planet's defences without harm to its cargo of warriors. Boarding an enemy battle-barge is extremely hard, because its decks are built as a fortress.

Power

A battle-barge is one of the most powerful starships of the Imperium. Its strength is comparable to a [[battlecruiser]] or a battleship, and its Space Marine contingent adds to that power. Many Chapters use the vessel as a [[fleet-based]] [[fortress-monastery]] and as the flagship of their fleet.

Examples

The [[Space Wolves]] battle-barge Allfather's Honour serves as the Chapter flagship. The [[Black Templars]] keep the battle-barge Eternal Crusader as the monastery of their Chapter.
