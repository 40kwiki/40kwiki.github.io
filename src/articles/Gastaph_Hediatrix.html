Gastaph Hediatrix was the [[Fabricator-General]] of [[Mars]] during the [[Age of Apostasy]], the period in the early [[36th millennium]] when the High Lord [[Goge Vandire]] held the Imperium's government in his own hands. Hediatrix was the first of the High Lords to demand that Vandire answer for his rule, and when Vandire answered by declaring the Mechanicus a [[Traitor|traitor]], he sent the Martian [[Skitarii|Tech-Guard]] to [[Terra]].

The Tech-Guard landed beside several [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and took part in the siege of the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]], which ended the [[Reign of Blood]] in c. 288.M36. Afterwards Hediatrix helped to restore the council of the High Lords and to see that those who had profited from the Reign were brought to trial.

Background

The records open to Imperial scholars give nothing of Hediatrix's origins, forge or early career, and no predecessor or successor is named. The office he held was the ruling office of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], bound by the [[Treaty of Olympus]] to supply Terra's armies but not to obey its council, and the [[Fabricator-General]] sat among the [[High Lords of Terra]] as an ally rather than a subject.

The [[Reign of Blood]] began when [[Goge Vandire|Vandire]], Master of the [[Administratum]], seized the office of [[Ecclesiarch]] as well and for some seventy years held the two greatest bureaucracies of the Imperium. The council could not resist him. Many worlds and [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] held aloof and fortified their own domains.

Opposition to Vandire

As word spread of the reformer [[Sebastian Thor]], [[Chapter Master|Chapter Masters]] of the [[Segmentum Solar]] began to speak in his favour. At about the same time Hediatrix demanded in the council that the [[Ecclesiarch|Holy Synod]] indict [[Goge Vandire|Vandire]] and put him on trial. Vandire's answer was to dissolve the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] and to declare both the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] [[Traitor|traitors]] to the Imperium. He ordered his remaining forces against them, but most of his commanders refused.

Hediatrix concluded that the High Lord could only be removed by force. By the account of the Mechanicus he was enraged by what he saw, and he ordered regiments of the Martian [[Skitarii|Tech-Guard]] to Terra.

The Terran Crusade

Main article: [[Terran Crusade]]

The [[Skitarii|Tech-Guard]] sailed with four [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]: the [[Imperial Fists]], the [[Fire Hawks]], the [[Soul Drinkers]] and the [[Black Templars]]. It was the first time since the [[Horus Heresy]] that armed [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]] had landed on Terra. For some months the combined force besieged the [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]], where [[Goge Vandire|Vandire]] had taken refuge. The siege was broken, and the Palace stormed. Vandire himself was killed not by the Space Marines but by his bodyguard, the [[Reign of Blood|Brides of the Emperor]] under [[Alicia Dominica]], in c. 288.M36.

Aftermath

With the tyrant dead, Thor restored independent [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] to the council. Hediatrix helped to bring back what remained of it. He took an active part in ensuring that those who had profited from the [[Reign of Blood]] faced judgment for their conduct. How long he held office afterwards is not recorded. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] lists name no further [[Fabricator-General]] until [[Uixot]] in the late [[37th millennium]], and the holders between are not published.The Mechanicus keeps its rolls of Fabricators-General private. The absence of a name from the open record is not evidence that the office was vacant.
