A fortress-monastery is the great citadel where a [[Space Marine Chapter]] keeps its headquarters. Chapters of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] are monastic military orders. Their fortress-monasteries are both battle-bases and places of devotion to the [[Emperor of Mankind]].

Location

Most fortress-monasteries stand on a Chapter's [[Space Marine Chapter Homeworld|homeworld]] or on one of its seed worlds. Others are built on deserted moons or asteroids. Fleet-based Chapters use their flagship, usually a [[battle barge]], as a mobile fortress-monastery. Where the Chapter Master also serves as [[planetary governor]], the monastery may stand on an Imperial world by ancient accord.

Many monasteries are kept in wild country, far from the towns of their worlds. Local people often leave the lands around them alone, and some worlds have forbidden zones that stretch for hundreds of kilometres.

Design

The monastery is built for training and prayer. Its halls hold shrines and alcoves, and its vaults hold combat arenas and firing ranges. It also keeps the Chapter's relics, trophies and archives. Few Chapters have more than a fraction of their warriors at home at one time, because most companies serve on campaign for years.

The menial work is done by [[Chapter serf]]s. They clean the halls, prepare meals and tend to tasks that servitors cannot manage. Their service lets the battle-brothers give their time to training and devotion.

Forms

Monasteries take many forms. Some are towering keeps or spires, others are underground vaults or domes on the seabed. A few are built into void craft. The [[Black Templars]] keep their monastery in the battle barge Eternal Crusader, and the [[Imperial Fists]] keep theirs in the star fort Phalanx.
