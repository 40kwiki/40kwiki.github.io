The Soul Drinkers are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] whose name is borne by two bodies. The first Soul Drinkers believed themselves a [[Second Founding]] successor of the [[Imperial Fists]], raised in 021.M31 from the personal guard of [[Sigismund]] and the fleet-based elements of the VII Legion. After centuries of loyal service they broke with the [[Inquisition]] and the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] in the late [[41st millennium]], were declared renegade, and fought their own former allies until their destruction. The [[High Lords of Terra]] then struck them from the records by an Edict of Obliteration.

In the [[Era Indomitus]] [[Roboute Guilliman]] ordered the name, colours and heraldry restored for a new Chapter of [[Primaris Space Marines]] created in the [[Ultima Founding]]. The new Soul Drinkers knew nothing of their predecessors' fate until the Inquisition revealed it to them after the Keprian Reclamation, and the knowledge has shaken the Chapter.

History of the first Chapter

Origins

Chapter tradition held that the Soul Drinkers were founded in 021.M31 and given the relic weapon called the Soulspear. Later analysis of their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] by the [[Apothecary|Apothecaries]] of the Angels Sanguine showed it to be of unknown origin and not of [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]'s line, and Imperial scholars now date the Chapter's creation only to some time before the [[Fourth Founding]] of M32.

Between 544 and 546.M32 the Soul Drinkers joined the other Imperial Fists successors under the Last Wall Protocol to defend Terra during the [[War of the Beast]]; Chapter Master Euclydeas commanded a third of the combined fleet, and after the war the Chapter gave warriors to rebuild the Imperial Fists. In 378.M36 they fought beside the [[Black Templars]] and the [[Fire Hawks]] in the crusade against [[Goge Vandire]] that ended the [[Reign of Blood]].

Fall

The Chapter's fall began with the Lakonia Star Fort Incident, when a Soul Drinkers strike force was sent with Inquisitorial sanction to recover the Soulspear and refused to hand it over to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. Rather than accept the Inquisition's terms, which required the deaths of the battle-brothers involved, the Chapter fought. In the First Chapter War that followed, Captain Sarpedon, a [[Librarian]], killed Chapter Master Gorgoleon in an honour duel, and in the course of it was struck by a mutation that gave him eight arachnid legs. Sarpedon led the renegade Chapter for years afterwards in a war against the Imperium's institutions, and later killed Eumenes, a captain who rose against him, in the Second Chapter War.The Lakonia incident and the Chapter Wars are not dated in surviving records; they are placed in the last decades of M41 from the sequence of events.

The renegade Soul Drinkers were eventually captured and tried aboard the [[Imperial Fists]]' fortress [[Phalanx]]. During the trial the Chapter's revered ancient, the philosopher-soldier Daenyathos, was shown to have planned the whole chain of events, and the [[Daemon|daemon]] Abraxes broke loose on the Phalanx. Sarpedon and the last Soul Drinkers fought beside the Imperial Fists under [[Chapter Master]] Vladimir Pugh against it, and Sarpedon held up Daenyathos's remains to expose him before he was taken. The Chapter did not survive the affair, and the Edict of Obliteration followed.

The refounded Chapter

After the [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the [[Great Rift]] in 999.M41, the Soul Drinkers were among the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] refounded by [[Roboute Guilliman|Guilliman]]'s order, made entirely of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. One of their first campaigns was the Keprian Reclamation, an operation of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] to retake Kepris, a world holding relics of the [[Imperial Cult]], from the cult of the arch-heretic Yeceqath, "the Voice of All". Captain Quhya's Third Company won it, but the Chapter's unorthodox methods drew the [[Inquisition]]'s attention, and the investigation revealed the fate of the first Soul Drinkers. [[Rise of the Ynnari|The reborn]] Chapter continues to fight in the Indomitus Crusade.The Keprian Reclamation is dated only to the early Era Indomitus.

Beliefs

The first Soul Drinkers held honour duels in the [[Imperial Fists]]' manner, and their battle-brothers ritually consumed the brains of worthy foes to take their memories through the omophagea. Aspirants to the [[Reclusiam]] faced the trial of the Scintillating Death, a ship that had vanished and returned in the [[34th millennium]].
