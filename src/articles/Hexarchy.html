The Hexarchy was a conspiracy of serving and deposed [[High Lords of Terra]] that tried to seize control of Terra in early M42. It was led by the former [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Irthu Haemotalion]] and the deposed [[Ecclesiarch]] [[Baldo Slyst]]. Its members had been removed from the council by [[Roboute Guilliman]] after his return.

The conspiracy grew from the resistance to Guilliman's reforms. Many High Lords supported the old order that had stood before the primarch's return, and the Terran nobility called their cause the Imperium Eterna. The conspiracy drew these supporters together under the Hexarchy.

Background

At the time of the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41, Slyst was Ecclesiarch and opposed the [[Chancellor of the Imperium|Chancellor]] [[Lev Tieron]]'s attempt to release the [[Adeptus Custodes]] from their oath to remain on Terra. Slyst was among the High Lords removed by Guilliman. Haemotalion had been Master of the Administratum and was also removed.

Coup

The Hexarchy moved to seize Terra while Guilliman was away from the capital. Its agents were met by the [[Grand Master of Assassins]], [[Fadix]], who acted for the council. [[Aveliza Drachmar]], a serving High Lord who had joined the conspiracy, was killed by the Grand Master's agents. The coup was broken and the conspiracy's members were killed or removed from Terra.The date of the coup is recorded as early M42. The sequence is reconstructed from later Administratum records.
