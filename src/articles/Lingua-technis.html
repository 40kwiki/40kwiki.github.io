Lingua-technis, also called Techna-Lingua, is the official language of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and its [[Tech-priest]]s. It is a form of binary machine code, carried as bursts of non-random static emitted through the cybernetic implants present in most Tech-priests. Outsiders cannot understand it without the right equipment.

Lingua-technis is used to speak to machines, to record the rites of the [[Omnissiah]], and to exchange information between forges. Imperial scholars describe it as far more precise than spoken language, but also as unable to express the ambiguity that human thought depends on. The Mechanicus regards it as the true language of the Machine God.
