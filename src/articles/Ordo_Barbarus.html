The Ordo Barbarus is a minor order of the [[Inquisition]] whose area of vigilance is the pre-industrial worlds of the Imperium. The roll of the [[Ordos Minoris]] gives it an established strength of ten [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] and no date of foundation.

Remit

The record does not say what the Ordo watches on such worlds. They are the [[Feral world]]s and [[Feudal world]]s of the [[Planetary classification|planetary classification]], populated by humans who live in the manner of ages long gone, and cut off from the Imperium's technology. The Ordo takes its name from the word for the barbarian.

It would be reasonable to infer what the Ordo is for. Primitive worlds are fertile ground for cults, for the worship of a [[Daemon|daemon]] as a local god, and for [[Xenos|xenos]] who pass as spirits. Their people are unprepared for any of these, and the Imperium does not often visit. A single world such as this may be controlled for a thousand years by a creature no Imperial official has ever seen. Ten [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], however, could not watch more than a small portion of the worlds of this kind, and they are of necessity a watch over a chosen few.

Cases and rumour

The Ordo has no recorded case. Imperial rumour says that its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] sometimes pass as holy men on the worlds they watch, taking the place of a local shaman. The report is plausible and unsupported.

A second rumour is that the Ordo is consulted whenever the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] or the [[Astra Militarum]] proposes to bring a primitive world into the Imperium's industry or armies, in case the world has something worse than primitive people in it. No record supports this either.

Place among the Ordos

The Ordo is one of the many scores of lesser branches that the [[Inquisition]]'s records describe as dedicated to more transient dangers, and the roll shows its size at ten [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]]. By the standard of the [[Ordos Minoris]] this is small. The [[Ordo Excorium]] has a hundred and the [[Ordo Astartes]] fifty. It is the work, not the size, that is unusual: an Ordo whose area of vigilance is defined by how backward a world is, not by what threatens it.

Relations

An Ordo of this kind would have to rely on the [[Ordo Xenos]] when the threat was alien and on the [[Ordo Hereticus]] when the threat was a heresy. It could call on the [[Planetary Defence Force]]s only with care, since the force is a local one, and a [[Feudal world|feudal world]] rarely has any. The record does not give its relations with any other Ordo.

Method

The record gives no method. An [[Inquisitor]] sent to a [[Feral world|feral world]] would need to learn the local tongue and customs, and to pass for a pilgrim or a trader, since an Imperial official is rarely welcome on such a world. Such a mission may last years. With ten Inquisitors the Ordo can mount very few of them at one time, which may be why so little of its work has been recorded.

Members

No [[Inquisitor]] of the Ordo is named in the record.

Legacy

The Ordo's focus is on the least visible worlds of the Imperium, and few records are kept of what happens there. That is the reason it exists, and the reason almost nothing is known about its work.
