Konrad Curze, called the Night Haunter, was the [[Primarch]] of the VIII Legion of the [[Space Marine Legions]], the [[Night Lords]]. He was raised in the unending darkness of the [[Hive world|hive world]] [[Nostramo]], where he ended its crime by killing the guilty and so became its king. He held that fear was the one effective restraint on human wrongdoing. He was tormented from birth by visions of the future, in which he saw only the failure of his work, his own death at his father's hands and the ruin of his Legion.

The Night Haunter destroyed Nostramo in 984.M30, tearing it apart with precision lance strikes from his fleet. He attacked Rogal Dorn, was imprisoned on Cheraut, and escaped. In the [[Horus Heresy]] he joined the [[Warmaster]] and fought at [[Isstvan V]], captured [[Vulkan]] and tried for months to kill him, and was taken in turn on [[Macragge]] by his brothers. His Legion recovered him, and he spent his last years on the world of [[Tsagualsa]] in the Eastern Fringe.

The accepted account of his death, drawn from the vid-log of the assassin sent against him, is that he was killed on Tsagualsa after the Heresy by the Callidus assassin M'Shen, whom he allowed to reach him. He had foreseen it and took it as proof that he had been right. The Night Lords broke into warbands and went into the [[Eye of Terror]].

Early life

Nostramo

Main article: [[Nostramo]]

The Night Haunter's own journals, a [[Heresy|heretical]] chronicle named The Dark, record his earliest memory: a descent through the sky in a crackling ball of light onto a night-shrouded planet. His capsule struck Nostramo Quintus, the largest of the planet's [[Hive world|hive cities]], tore through the layers of the city and the crust, and came to rest near the unstable liquid core. It left a scar in the planet's adamantium strata. How the infant reached the surface is unknown, and the people of the city later spoke of a "glowing child-form" that crawled out of a pit onto a broken street.

Nostramo circled a dying star whose light barely reached it, and its crust was rich in adamantium, the basis of a great mining and refining industry. It was a world of corrupt guilds and syndicates. The infant lived as a scavenger. He killed those who preyed on him, and learned early that he was unlike them. They were weak and slow, and he was not. His dreams were of wars in the stars and of the dead on worlds he had never seen. He woke with screams in his ears and found that they were real.

The Dark King

As a youth he began to hunt the guilty. When news of an injustice reached him, he alone tracked the offender through the empty streets until the quarry collapsed, and then mutilated him, though not beyond recognition. The pattern, temperate judgement punctuated by terrible reprisal, produced in Nostramo Quintus a society of efficiency and honesty. Adamantium exports tripled. No one dared to have more than his neighbour, and the city grew prosperous and well lit. In time Nostramo's other cities followed, anxious to keep the Night Haunter from their doors. After a few decades he no longer needed to hunt.

He took the title of king, ruled by terror, and studied with a greed for knowledge. His transhuman senses sharpened, and he foresaw the coming of the Emperor.

The Emperor

Nostramo's history had prophesied the arrival of the Emperor as the event that would bring the planet down. In 896.M30 the Emperor landed and walked with His delegation into the heart of Nostramo Quintus on foot, in the event the city remembered as the "Delegation of Light". The citizens, adapted to darkness, wept at the radiance, and those who looked at Him directly were blinded. The Emperor had studied how Nostramo worked. He found it clean, silent and obedient. Curze submitted, and took command of the [[Night Lords|VIII Legion]].

Great Crusade

Further information: [[Great Crusade]]

The Night Lords

Main article: [[Night Lords]]

The Night Lords were a Legion of terror. Curze taught his Legionaries that fear is the surest weapon, and that the cruelty needed to impose peace is a kindness compared with endless war. "It is better by far to be an object of fear than of respect, for one is a truth of the soul and the other an illusion of the mind." He was the only [[Primarch|primarch]] who showed little affection for his sons.

Replacements for the Legion's losses were taken from Nostramo. After the primarch's departure, the world had slid back into its old corruption, and only the strongest and most ruthless criminals were now fit to serve. They poisoned the Legion, drove it to greater cruelty and eroded the discipline that Curze's own example had imposed. Other Legions' dislike of its methods turned to scorn. At the pacification of Listrantia IV the Night Lords refused a surrender on the grounds that they had come to enact judgement, not to receive supplication.

Cheraut

The first blow between brothers fell not on Isstvan but on Cheraut. The compliance of Cheraut, a stubborn lost clade of humanity, was a joint campaign of 984.M30 of the [[Emperor's Children]], the [[Imperial Fists]] and the Night Lords. The indiscriminate slaughter by which Curze pacified the defeated had brought him to a bitter quarrel with [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]. Curze learned that his homeworld had fallen back into corruption, and tried to confide the visions that tormented him to his brothers, who received them coldly. He told [[Fulgrim]], who repeated what he had learned to Dorn. Dorn confronted Curze. What passed between them is not recorded in detail, but Dorn was found severely wounded and Curze's personal bodyguard slaughtered. The primarch was placed under guard to await the sentence of his brothers. He broke out and left corpses in his wake. The incident was sealed in the archives of the Palace.

Destruction of Nostramo

Main article: [[Nostramo]]

Curze returned to Nostramo, and in 984.M30 destroyed it. His fleet fired precise lance strikes into the planet's volatile core, and Nostramo tore itself apart. In his journals he wrote that he had seen the world split by a lance of pure light, and he believed that he had fulfilled a vision to prevent the poison of its corruption from spreading further. The Night Lords had by now lost the last of their honour in the eyes of the Imperium, and the Legion was left without a home. Few outside the [[Imperial Palace]] knew of the destruction or the attack on Dorn, and the Night Haunter was permitted to remain in the Crusade, although [[Horus]], who then had the Night Lords' trust, placed him in [[Vulkan]]'s care.

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

Betrayal

The Night Haunter is often seen as the plainest example of evil among the [[Traitor]] [[Primarch|primarchs]]. His methods, his tortures and the death of [[Nostramo]] were known to his brothers before the Heresy. Imperial scholars add that his tragedy was to have been too much the son of his father. Like several of his brothers, he had inherited a fragment of the Emperor's foresight. The Emperor could see the many futures and chart a course through them. Curze saw one: a dark path of failure and death that soured his mind and clouded his purpose. Whatever he achieved, he knew how it would end. He called it a [[Blackstone Fortress|precipice]], and said that he would step off it and welcome the dark.

He took no devotion to any of the [[Chaos|Chaos Gods]]. He used the powers of Chaos as tools for his terror. In the first Traitor council before [[Drop Site Massacre|Isstvan V]], he met the [[Word Bearers]]' commanders aboard the flagship Fidelitas Lex with [[Perturabo]] and [[Alpharius Omegon|Alpharius]]. At Isstvan V the [[Night Lords]] formed part of the second wave that turned on the loyalists, and afterwards ran through the survivors in hunting parties.

Vulkan

Main article: [[Vulkan]]

The [[Iron Warriors]]' atomic strike left [[Vulkan]] alive. Surrounded by Night Lords and Iron Warriors, he fought until he was overwhelmed. Curze took him aboard a gaol-hulk, the Nightfall, and spent months trying to break him and then to kill him. Every death was undone. When Vulkan's body renewed itself, Curze understood that he held a Perpetual, a being whose body renews itself without limit, like the Emperor's. He beheaded him, stabbed him, tore him apart, shot him with hundreds of bolters and threw him into the void. When killing failed, he turned to trials designed to prove that Vulkan was no better than he was. Vulkan did not give way.

Curze then made a final offer. If Vulkan could cross a maze built for him by Perturabo, he could have his freedom and his hammer. Curze ambushed him repeatedly on the way. Vulkan was recovered by the Ultramarines on Macragge, and the bond formed between the two primarchs by their months together is blamed for the instability that Vulkan showed on waking.

Outriders of rebellion

[[Horus]] used the Night Lords as outriders, to run in front of the Traitor host and to terrorise worlds into submission. The Nostramo Sector, a hundred inhabited systems, was a recruiting and manufacturing hub for the [[Warmaster]]. The Night Haunter, seen as a monster, was a perfect tool for its conquest. In his own mind, however, every shortfall in production and every misfortune among the conquered worlds was treachery, and he punished it with death. He tried to cut away the rot in the sector and in his Legion by sending new, harsher recruits, and found that they were drawn from the same corrupted stock.

In 007.M31 the Night Lords' fleet made for [[Tsagualsa]] in the Eastern Fringe, where an asteroid belt shaded their base, and from there began the [[Thramas Crusade]] against the Imperial strongholds of Heroldar and Thramas. The campaign was meant to leave the Warmaster's flank secure and to delay the [[Dark Angels]]. It lasted nearly three years. At a parley on Tsagualsa, Curze tried to sway [[Lion El'Jonson]] to the Warmaster. It became a brawl. Curze had his brother by the throat when a Dark Angel guardsman ran his sword through the Night Haunter's back. In the ensuing campaign the Lion ambushed the Night Lords' fleet and destroyed about a quarter of it, and the Night Haunter, mortally wounded, led a boarding action on the Lion's flagship Invincible Reason and fled through its depths for months before the survivors escaped.

Macragge

Main article: [[Macragge]]

The Night Haunter was later captured on Macragge, in about 009.M31, where [[Roboute Guilliman]] had founded the realm of [[Imperium Secundus]]. The loyalist primarchs there took from him his Nightmare Mantle and his claws. His prison sarcophagus was ejected into space by his brothers [[Sanguinius]] and the Lion, and was recovered by the Night Lords. At Tsagualsa they made him new arms and armour, crude replicas of those he had lost, and he wore them to the end.

Death

The accepted account of the Night Haunter's end is that he did not go to [[Terra]]. After the [[Warmaster]] fell, the [[Night Lords]] did not break and flee into the Eye of Terror like the other [[Traitor Legions]]. They continued to attack the Eastern Fringe with a self-destructive desperation. At a last gathering of the Legion, he told his sons that their way was not the way of the Imperium, and that they would never wear the [[Aquila (symbol)|Imperial eagle]]: "You shall stand in midnight clad, your claws forever red with the lifeblood of my father's failed empire, warring through the centuries as the talons of a murdered god."

The Emperor sent half the [[Officio Assassinorum|Callidus Temple]] of Assassins to end him. One agent, M'Shen, was permitted to enter the Legion's lair on [[Tsagualsa]] and reach the primarch, who was by then a hunched, naked figure. M'Shen's vid-log records his last words: "Your presence does not surprise me, Assassin. I have known of you ever since your craft entered the Eastern Fringes. Why did I not have you killed? Because your mission and the act you are about to commit proves the truth of all I have ever said or done. I merely punished those who had wronged, just as your False Emperor now seeks to punish me. Death is nothing compared to vindication." The log shows the assassin springing, and then it cuts out. The kill was never confirmed on the record. The Night Haunter is believed to have allowed it, seeing himself as the corrupt and murderous thing he had set out to destroy.

Earlier Imperial summaries recorded the death only briefly, as the work of an "Imperial assassin", and some placed it at the end of the Heresy without naming the world. The detailed account is now accepted, on the evidence of the vid-log and the testimony of surviving Night Lords.

Legacy

Without their primarch the [[Night Lords]] divided into warbands. They fled into the Eye of Terror, where their descendants raid the Imperium, and they remain a force that serves no god. The Legion that Curze despised for its corruption has continued his method of terror and carries his name as a curse. The warbands still strike from the dark, and his death remains among the most debated entries in the Imperium's archives.

Character and abilities

Curze was a man of two aspects. One was a strategist, ruthless and efficient, a believer in his father's order. The other, the Night Haunter, was a creature of fear. His mind was fractured by early life and by his visions. He was convinced of the fallibility of humanity and of fear as the sole means of controlling it. He did not enjoy command, was the only primarch who showed no warmth to his sons, and never joined in the fellowship of his brothers.

His precognition was a curse more than a gift. It allowed him to anticipate attacks and to appear where he was least expected, and it persuaded him that nothing he did would alter his death. He was a superb duellist and hunter, who took pleasure in stalking his prey through the dark.

Wargear

His raiment of war, the Nightmare Mantle, was a customised suit of [[Artificer armour|artificer armour]] hung with trophies of judgement and the skins of those he deemed to have sinned. He fought with a pair of lightning claws of unknown origin, Mercy and Forgiveness, names given to them by his Legionaries. He also favoured the Widowmakers, serrated throwing blades based on the signature weapons of the assassin cults of [[Nostramo]], which he used to cripple and maim. The originals were taken from him on [[Macragge]]. He wore replicas until his death.
