Nova Terra is a populous and wealthy world of the [[Segmentum Pacificus]] in the western galaxy. In the [[35th millennium]] its ruling body, the Ur-Council, repudiated the authority of the [[High Lords of Terra]] and claimed to govern the worlds of the Segmentum Pacificus in their place. For nearly nine centuries, a period Imperial historians call the [[Nova Terra Interregnum]] and the schismatic worlds called the Time of Twin Empires, the [[Imperium of Mankind]] had two governments and two [[Administratum|Administratums]].

The Ur-Council fell in the [[Cataclysm of Souls]] of 975.M35, when the population of the Pacificus, roused by the [[Ecclesiarchy]], rose against it. Nova Terra has been ruled since then as an ordinary world of the Imperium. Its people remember the Interregnum with a mixture of pride and shame.

History

Before the Interregnum

The settlement and compliance of Nova Terra are not recorded in open archives. Its name, "New Terra", suggests a colony of the [[Dark Age of Technology]] founded from or in honour of Terra, but no record confirms it.The origin of the name is not documented; this is the opinion of Administratum historitors. By the [[34th millennium]] the world had grown into the effective capital of the western galaxy. The Imperium of that age relied on its sector governors to act on their own judgement, since an answer from Terra might take years to reach the western rim, and Nova Terra's sector lords, merchant houses and Administratum heads met as a council that ruled much of the region in practice.

The Ur-Council

Main article: [[Nova Terra Interregnum]]

In the early 35th millennium the council, by then calling itself the Ur-Council, declared that the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] had no right to command the west. The exact date of the declaration is not recorded; the earliest datable act of the council as a government is from 257.M35, when the [[Dark Angels]], believing that one of its members was the Fallen Angel Cypher, sent a [[Ravenwing]] strike against Nova Terra that took no prisoners.

The Ur-Council did not call itself a rebel. It kept the forms of the Imperium, collected tithes, raised [[Planetary Defence Force|Planetary Defence Forces]], upheld the [[Imperial Creed]] and prayed for the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]], while denying the authority of the High Lords. Tithes from the west were paid to Nova Terra, and the astropathic choirs of the segmentum were reorganised under its own officials.

For most of the following centuries the border between the two courts saw raids, sieges and local truces but no decisive battle. The same period saw the [[Moirae Schism]] in the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], the Pale Wasting, and lesser revolts such as the Tellarite Rebellion. In the Hrakon campaign the Dark Angels hunted the despot Obidiah Hrakon, one of the Ur-Council's chief supporters.

The Cataclysm of Souls

Main article: [[Nova Terra Interregnum]]

Late in the 35th millennium the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] began to bind the divided Imperium together as a single theocratic state that transcended the quarrel of the courts. The Ur-Council rejected the Ecclesiarchy's teachings, and the population of the Segmentum Pacificus rose against it. Billions died in the religious wars that followed. In 975.M35 the Ur-Council was overthrown and the authority of the High Lords restored. The Ecclesiarchy emerged with greatly increased power, which led directly to the [[Age of Apostasy]].

Later history

After the Cataclysm Nova Terra's records of the Interregnum were largely destroyed or rewritten by Administratum officials who had reason to portray the Ur-Council as simple rebels. Little is recorded of the world in later millennia. It lies in the [[Imperium Sanctus]], south of the [[Great Rift]], and is not recorded among the worlds lost after 999.M41.

Geography

Nova Terra's position within the Segmentum Pacificus is not given in open archives. Imperial accounts describe it as a central world of the western reaches, reachable from much of the segmentum, which allowed it to serve as a capital.

Society

During the Interregnum Nova Terra was ruled by a council of sector lords, merchant houses and the heads of the local Administratum rather than by a single governor. Its officials maintained a full parallel administration, with its own tithe-rolls and offices. Since 975.M35 it has been governed by a planetary governor under the High Lords.
