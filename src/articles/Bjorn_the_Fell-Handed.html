Bjorn the Fell-Handed is a Venerable [[Dreadnought]] of the [[Space Wolves]] and the oldest [[Space Marine]] Dreadnought in the service of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. He fought in the [[Great Crusade]] and the [[Horus Heresy]] as a warrior of the VI Legion, lost his hand at the [[Burning of Prospero]] in 004.M31, and was raised by [[Leman Russ]] to his Wolf Guard for slaying the [[Daemon|daemon]] Arvax the Arch-slaughterer. When Russ vanished in 211.M31 Bjorn was the one member of the Wolf Guard left behind, and he is now the last of the Company of Russ.

Mortally wounded in the Proxima Rebellion in 934.M31, Bjorn was interred in a Dreadnought. He sleeps in the Fang and is woken only in the Chapter's greatest need, as in the Battle of the Fang in M32, when he badly wounded [[Magnus the Red]], and again in the [[Era Indomitus]], when the Space Wolves have needed his counsel more than ever.

Great Crusade and Horus Heresy

Bjorn served in Tra, the Third Great Company of the [[Space Wolves|VI Legion]], under Jarl Ogvai Ogvai Helmschrot. He fought in the Olamic Quietude and other [[Great Crusade]] campaigns and saw the Emperor before His entombment.

His saga begins with the Burning of Prospero in 004.M31. A Warp entity that had marked the remembrancer Kasper Hawser as a spy revealed itself in the ruins of Tizca. Bjorn cut off its arm and drove it back, and it could not use the stolen true names of the Space Wolves against him, because Hawser had only ever known him by the word for "bear". The creature seized his arm and poured Warp energy through it, and the corruption began to climb toward his shoulder. A Rune Priest arrived with [[Sisters of Silence]] and two Dreadnoughts, and by some accounts [[Constantin Valdor]], and the entity was destroyed. Hawser or Valdor saved Bjorn's life by cutting off his arm at the elbow. The Iron Priests refused him an augmetic hand, and he took instead a great Wolf Claw.

On the volcanic world of Gryth, during the Heresy, his pack was slaughtered by Arvax the Arch-slaughterer, a Greater Daemon of [[Khorne]], and he alone survived. He swore vengeance. Five years later, when Russ led the Legion against Arvax, Bjorn reached the daemon first, climbed its body and tore out its throat with his Wolf Claw. Russ exonerated him before the whole Legion, made him one of his Wolf Guard and named him the "Fell-Handed".The battle on Gryth and the slaying of Arvax are dated only to the Heresy years, 005–014.M31.

The disappearance of Russ

In 211.M31, nearly two centuries after the Emperor was placed on the [[Golden Throne]], the [[Space Wolves]] were gathered for the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension when [[Leman Russ|Russ]] fell silent, saw some vision and called his Wolf Guard to him: all save Bjorn, the youngest. He gave Bjorn charge of the Chapter, then left with his companions and was never seen again. Some believe he went into the [[Eye of Terror]] to seek [[Lion El'Jonson]], or the Tree of Life whose fruit might heal the Emperor. Every thousand years Bjorn retells the tale to the assembled Chapter.

Internment

Several sagas name Bjorn the first Great Wolf, who led the Chapter after [[Leman Russ|Russ]]'s disappearance; others say only that he led his own company. His career as a warrior ended in 934.M31 in the Proxima Rebellion, where he was mortally wounded, and he was interred in the sarcophagus of a [[Dreadnought]]. In the Battle of the Fang in M32 he faced and severely wounded [[Magnus the Red]] when the [[Thousand Sons]] attacked [[Fenris]].

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Bjorn was woken to fight in the defence of the [[Fenris|Fenris System]] against [[Magnus the Red|Magnus]] and the [[Thousand Sons]] in 999.M41, and in the wars that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]] he has spent more time awake than at any point in ten thousand years. He is the Chapter's living memory of its [[Primarch|primarch]], and the Wolf Lords and [[Logan Grimnar]] look to his counsel as [[Milky Way|the galaxy]] turns toward the prophesied [[Wulfen|Wolftime]], when [[Leman Russ|Russ]] is said to return.
