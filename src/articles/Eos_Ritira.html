Eos Ritira has been [[Ecclesiarch]] of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] since c. 000.M42. Some six hundred men and women have held the office.

The Ecclesiarch heads the [[Adeptus Ministorum]] and the [[Imperial Cult]] on Terra and in the sectors. Ritira's term follows the restoration of the council after the [[Hexarchy]] and the reforms that Guilliman made to the High Lords.

Office

The office's history is marked by its rivalry with the [[Master of the Administratum]], which ended in the usurpation of both offices by [[Goge Vandire]] in the 36th millennium. The [[Reign of Blood]] followed in that period. Since then the two offices have been kept separate by the council's rules.
