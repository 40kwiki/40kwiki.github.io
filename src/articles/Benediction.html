Benediction, originally called Talledus, is a [[Cardinal world]] of the Imperium of Mankind. It is the capital of the [[Talledus System]] and of the wider [[Veritus Sub-sector]]. Its religious significance to the [[Ecclesiarchy]] made it one of the most important worlds of the sub-sector.

The world was ruled by the Astra Militarum in the person of its planetary governor, Cardinal Morst Bolifax. It was the Ecclesiarchy's most important world in the Veritus Sub-sector, and that importance made it a target for the forces of Chaos.

Attack

In the early 42nd millennium the [[Word Bearers]] attacked Benediction under the senior [[Dark Apostle]] [[Kor Phaeron]]. The attack came in the days after the Great Rift had opened the warp routes into the sub-sector. Imperial accounts record the assault as a deliberate act against the church's heart in the region.The date of the attack is estimated from the early 42nd millennium records of the Veritus Sub-sector.
