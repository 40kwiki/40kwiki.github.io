A void shield is a form of energy field used by the military forces of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] to protect voidships and super-heavy vehicles such as [[Titan]]s. Void shields use Warp-based technology to power the field, which then displaces incoming attacks by distorting the space-time around the point of impact. The field is one of the defining technologies of Imperial warfare in both void and ground combat.

Imperial records do not agree on how the shield works. One account holds that it neutralises a projectile or beam in realspace by manipulating gravitic or other physical forces. Another holds that it sends the attack directly into the [[Warp]]. A third holds that some other method displaces the damage. Imperial scholars treat all three as possible, and none is confirmed.

Properties

A void shield blocks most physical and energy effects, including weather. Rain that strikes a shield makes a crackling sound. The shield can be penetrated if an incoming projectile is moving below a certain minimum speed. A shield of high enough intensity can halt all movement, interfere with vox communications and stop interdimensional travel. Imperial shields can be reactivated after they have collapsed, even during battle.

In close combat a void shield generally does not protect the vehicle from a melee assault, and a vehicle that moves through the shield can attack the protected machine. Titans can also be bypassed by teleportation, as Terminator Titanhammer squads have shown, or by a Warp missile that travels through the Warp.

Titans and banks of shields

On ground forces, void shields are most commonly mounted on [[Imperator-class Titan]]s to protect them from ranged fire. Other super-heavy battle tanks have also carried them. A vehicle with several shields, or a shield bank, must have its shields calibrated to overlap exactly. Imperial records describe small gaps between overlapping fields that are almost invisible to the eye. A Titan-grade weapon fired into such a gap can cause a catastrophic shield failure. Shields are said to whine when active, and they fizzle when two fields touch.

Traitor Titans of the [[Legio Mortis]] used their shields during the [[Siege of Terra]], and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] maintains the generators that power them.

Orks and other fields

The [[Orks]] have a similar technology, the Kustom Force Field, which works in the same way as the Imperial void shield. Ork energy fields are far less reliable, and they tend to be inoperable by other species once deactivated, since they depend on the Orks' gestalt [[WAAAGH!]] energy.
