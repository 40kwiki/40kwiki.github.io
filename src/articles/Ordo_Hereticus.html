The Ordo Hereticus, known to the Imperium as the "Witch Hunters", is one of the three [[Ordos Majoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. It protects Mankind from itself. Its agents hunt the internal threats to the [[Imperium of Mankind]], including treason, [[Mutant|mutation]], heresy, apostate members of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and [[Unsanctioned Psykers]], who are the "[[Psyker|witches]]" of common speech. Its [[Inquisitors]] are among the most feared figures in the Imperium.

The Ordo is one of three primary divisions of the Holy Ordos alongside the [[Ordo Malleus]] and the [[Ordo Xenos]]. Its Inquisitors have ultimate authority over other Imperial organisations within the [[Adeptus Terra]], and they may call on any other source of Imperial authority for help.

History

The Ordo Hereticus was founded long after the other two Ordos Majoris. Its origin lies in the [[Age of Apostasy]], which fell in the second century of the [[36th millennium]]. During that age, the many [[Heresy|heresies]] that took root in the anarchy of the [[Reign of Blood]], including the [[Plague of Unbelief]], destabilised the Imperium's political structure in a way not seen since the [[Horus Heresy]].

For its first standard millennia the Ordo was almost a rumour, even among other [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]], because its secrecy was guarded so closely. After the Age of Apostasy, the Ordo was made deliberately visible, on the reasoning that an intimidating presence would do more good than a myth that could be ignored. It grew with the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]], because a holy man claiming to speak with the Emperor's voice is the kind of claim that draws an Inquisitor's suspicion most readily.

Methods

The Ordo's [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] often pose as servants of the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]. They serve a cardinal or a priest until they have enough evidence to act against him, and it is said to be rare for a member of the Ordo to be unable to find a fault in a priest. The Ordo also hunts the Unsanctioned [[Psyker|Psykers]] and [[Mutant|mutants]] of the Imperium, and it uses the [[Inquisitorial Black Ships]] to round them up across [[Milky Way|the galaxy]].

Because the Ordo deals with heresy, it treats evidence of belief as a matter of degree. Its records describe three kinds of clue: physical clues, such as the form of an object or the deeds of an accused; informational clues, such as what a person is recorded as saying; and correlational clues, such as patterns that link the accused to other [[Heretic|heretics]].

Chamber Militant

The Ordo's Chamber Militant is the [[Adepta Sororitas|Sisters of Battle]], the Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas. The Ordo can also call on the military forces of the [[Ecclesiarchy]], and on the Imperial [[Black Ships]] for the removal of [[Psyker|Psykers]] and [[Mutant|mutants]].

Notable members

Imperial records give the Ordo's most notable members as among the most feared [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] in the Imperium. The Ordo has executed a number of Renegade [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] cardinals and corrupt Tech-priests, and many of its cases are sealed.
