An Apothecary is a battlefield surgeon of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. He heals wounded Space Marines, gives the [[Emperor's Peace]] to those who cannot be saved, and recovers from the dead their progenoid glands, the organs that carry the Chapter's [[gene-seed]] to the next generation. Apothecaries serve in the Apothecarion of their Chapter, which also oversees the implantation of gene-seed in new recruits. The Apothecarion is led by the Chief Apothecary, also called the Master of the Apothecarion.

The office is as old as the [[Space Marine Legions]], whose Apothecaries oversaw the creation of new legionaries during the [[Great Crusade]]. In the [[Codex Astartes]] of c. 021.M31, [[Roboute Guilliman]] gave the Apothecarion its present form and made it the guardian of each Chapter's gene-seed, which he called the Chapter's most precious possession. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the [[Administratum]] rely on Apothecaries for the tithes of gene-seed that the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] owe to [[Mars]].

Apothecaries wear white armour, or white markings on their Chapter's colours, and carry the [[narthecium]], a medical gauntlet, and the [[reductor]], which extracts the progenoid glands. Since the [[Ultima Founding]] the Apothecarion has also been charged with the surgery that turns veteran warriors into [[Primaris Space Marines]].

History

In the Legions

Each Legion of the Great Crusade, which began in c. 798.M30, kept an Apothecarion to tend its warriors and to make new ones. Its Apothecaries performed the implantation of the organs of the [[Space Marine]] on aspirants and watched for flaws in the [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]], which differed from Legion to Legion. The most famous Legion Apothecary was [[Fabius Bile]] of the [[Emperor's Children]], who as Chief Apothecary of his Legion began, before the [[Horus Heresy]], experiments on the gene-seed that led him into heresy. After the Heresy he became the most notorious renegade gene-wright in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]].

In the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the loyalist Apothecaries were overwhelmed by the scale of the slaughter. At the [[Drop Site Massacre]] in 006.M31 entire Legions' gene-seed was lost, and the [[Iron Hands]], [[Salamanders]] and [[Raven Guard]] could only rebuild by the work of their surviving Apothecaries over many years.

The Codex Astartes

Main article: [[Codex Astartes]]

The [[Codex Astartes]] made the Apothecarion a permanent order of each Chapter and fixed its duties: the healing of the wounded, the recovery of gene-seed, the testing of aspirants and the keeping of the gene-seed vaults. It also set the tithes of gene-seed that every Chapter must send to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] for testing and storage, with the Apothecaries of each Chapter responsible for their purity. [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] whose gene-seed is found to be mutated may be judged unfit, and the [[Inquisition]] has destroyed Chapters for corrupting it.

The Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

In 999.M41 [[Belisarius Cawl]] revealed the [[Primaris Space Marines]], with their twenty-two gene-seed organs. The Apothecaries of the Chapters received both the new warriors and the technique of the Rubicon Primaris, the surgery that implants the three new organs in a veteran Space Marine. Many Apothecaries were among the first to cross it. A new specialist, the Apothecary Biologis, studies the enemy's own biology on the battlefield to devise new weapons and remedies against it.

Duties

  • Healing. The Apothecary treats wounded battle-brothers in the field. A [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marine]]'s own organs heal most injuries; the Apothecary sees to the rest, and may graft bionics or reattach limbs.
  • The Emperor's Peace. If a warrior is too badly hurt to recover, the Apothecary ends his life with a bolt or blade of the narthecium, and takes his [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]].
  • Gene-seed recovery. With the reductor, the Apothecary removes the two progenoid glands of [[Dark Angels|the fallen]], one in the neck and one in the chest. These glands carry the gene-seed from which new warriors are made, and are kept in the Chapter's vaults.
  • Recruitment. The Apothecarion tests aspirants for compatibility and performs the implantations that make a Space Marine.

Insignia and wargear

In the Codex Apothecaries wear white armour, or white helms and pauldrons over their Chapter's colours. The Apothecarion's badge, the double helix between wings, marks their shoulder plates. Their wargear is the narthecium, a gauntlet holding drugs, sutures, diagnostic instruments and a chainsaw, and the reductor, a drill that cuts into the body and extracts the [[Gene-seed|progenoid glands]]. [[Primaris Space Marines|Primaris]] Apothecaries carry an absolvor [[Bolter|bolt pistol]], which delivers the Emperor's Peace.

Chapter variants

  • The [[Blood Angels]] and their successors keep Sanguinary Priests, who combine the Apothecary's work with the care of the Chapter's flaw, the Red Thirst. They guard the Red Grail, a chalice said to hold the blood of [[Sanguinius]]. The greatest of them in the [[41st millennium]] is the Sanguinary High Priest Corbulo.
  • The [[Space Wolves]] combine the offices of Apothecary and [[Chaplain]] in their Wolf Priests.
  • The Red Scorpions, who hold the purity of their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] above all things, give their Apothecaries the highest place in the Chapter after its master. Their Master of the Apothecarion is second in command of the whole Chapter, and Apothecaries often lead battleline squads in place of a sergeant. Master Apothecary [[Kregor Thann]], seconded to the [[Deathwatch]], has served at Watch Fortress Erioch in the [[Jericho Reach]] since 815.M41.

Standing in the Chapter

Apothecaries hold a unique place in their [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]]. They are the only warriors permitted to end the life of a battle-brother, and the only ones who see each brother as flesh to be preserved. Many Chapters regard them with a reverence close to that given to [[Chaplain|Chaplains]], since the survival of the Chapter's line depends on their work. [[Gene-seed]] recovered from the dead is honoured in rites that the Apothecarion and the Reclusiam conduct together, and in some Chapters the names of [[Dark Angels|the fallen]] are recorded with the progenoid glands taken from them.

The Apothecarion is also the Chapter's link with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] on matters of gene-seed. Its masters must account for every gland sent to the vaults of [[Mars]], and the [[Badab War]] (901–913.M41) grew in part from the [[Astral Claws]]' gene-seed tithes to the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], which became infrequent and incomplete in the mid-700s.M41.
