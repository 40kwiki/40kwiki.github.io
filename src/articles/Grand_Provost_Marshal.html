The Grand Provost Marshal is the head of the [[Adeptus Arbites]], the enforcers of [[Imperial law]], and holds a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. From the Hall of Judgement on [[Terra]], where the Book of Judgement containing the Lex Imperialis is kept, the Grand Provost Marshal represents the Emperor's law to the council, advises it on legal matters and directs the Arbites of a million worlds through their judges and marshals.

The office is one of the four oldest in the Imperium. A Grand Provost Marshal sat among the High Lords Civilian of the [[Unification Wars]] in the late [[30th millennium]], and another, [[Harr Rantal]], on the Council of Terra during the [[Horus Heresy]]. After the Heresy the scattered offices of the Marshals were united into the Adeptus Arbites under the Grand Provost Marshal. The most recent holder named in the record, [[Aveliza Drachmar]], joined the [[Hexarchy]] against the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]] and was killed in its failed coup in early M42.

Powers and duties

The Grand Provost Marshal is concerned mainly with the politics of [[Terra]] and leaves the daily work of the [[Adeptus Arbites|Arbites]] to subordinates: the judges of [[Sector|sectors]] and systems and the marshals who command the precinct-fortresses. The Arbites enforce only the laws that touch the Imperium itself, such as the tithe, loyalty and the security of Imperial worlds, and leave common crime to local enforcers. The Grand Provost Marshal is the final authority on the interpretation of that law within the Arbites and the council's chief source of legal counsel.

The office carries great weight in a crisis. When a planetary government fails or rebels, the local precinct is often the last Imperial authority left, and the Grand Provost Marshal's word decides whether the Arbites hold, withdraw or call for the [[Astra Militarum]] or the [[Inquisition]].

History

Unification and the Heresy (late M30 – 014.M31)

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

The earliest recorded holder is [[Uwoma Kandawire]], one of the four [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] Civilian who governed for the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] at the time the [[Dark Angels|First Legion]] took the field, late in the [[Unification Wars]] and before the [[Great Crusade]] began in c. 798.M30.Kandawire's dates are not recorded. The dating is inferred from the beginning of the Great Crusade. During the Heresy (005–014.M31) the office was held by Harr Rantal, who sat on the Council of Terra and, after the [[Siege of Terra]], on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. In the reforms that followed, the laws and edicts of the Emperor and the Council were gathered into the [[Lex Imperialis]], and their enforcement given to a single [[Adeptus Terra|adepta]] under the Grand Provost Marshal.

War of the Beast and the Beheading (544–546.M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]] and [[Beheading]]

In the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32) the Grand Provost Marshal was [[Vernor Zeck]]. He was one of the few High Lords who openly supported the [[Imperial Fists]] Chapter Master [[Koorland]] when he deposed the Lord Commander [[Udin Macht Udo]] in c. 545.M32. After the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32 the seat in the Lord Protector [[Drakan Vangorich]]'s council was held by [[Galatea Haas]], nominally as Lady Speaker for Zeck.The record does not say whether Zeck survived the Beheading. Haas's title suggests the seat was kept in his name.

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

[[Aveliza Drachmar]] held the office at the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 and kept it when [[Roboute Guilliman]] restored the council. She was among the serving High Lords who joined the [[Hexarchy]] of the former [[Master of the Administratum]] [[Irthu Haemotalion]], and she was killed by the agents of the [[Grand Master of Assassins]] [[Fadix]] when the conspiracy moved to seize Terra in early M42.The year of the coup is not given in the available records. It is estimated as c. 000–012.M42.
