Slaydo was the chief architect and first Imperial [[Warmaster]] of the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade]]. The High Lords granted him the rank in 755.M41. He was killed at [[Balhaut]] in 765.M41 during the campaign to reclaim that world from the forces of Chaos.

Slaydo was killed in single combat with the Chaos commander of the sector, the [[Archon]] Nadzybar. The Crusade won the battle on Balhaut, and Slaydo was replaced by his chosen heir, [[Macaroth]].
