The Iron Fists are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] and a successor of the [[Iron Hands]], raised in a Founding that is not recorded. They are loyal to the Imperium and revere [[Ferrus Manus]] as their [[Primarch|primarch]]. They keep the traditions of the Iron Hands, and like them they cross the surface of their homeworld in the Land-Behemoths, vast mobile fortresses that serve as their strongholds.

The Chapter's name, like its badge, has confused Imperial scribes. Some earlier registers listed the Iron Fists as a successor of the [[Imperial Fists]], whose gauntlet badge is similar. A later review of the Chapters' records settled the matter in favour of the Iron Hands, and that finding is the accepted one.The Imperial Fists listing appears in earlier Administratum registers. The later review is undated in the record.

History

Almost nothing of the Chapter's early history survives in Imperial records. It is likely that the Iron Fists were raised at the [[Second Founding]] or in a later Founding from the reformed [[Iron Hands]] [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]]. They share a past with their parent Legion, which was nearly destroyed at [[Isstvan V]] in 005.M31 when [[Ferrus Manus]] was killed.

Assault on Hive Vortis

The Chapter's one recorded campaign is the Assault on Hive Vortis, whose date is lost. The [[Techmarine|Techmarines]] of the Iron Fists took the Whirlwind, a missile launcher of short range, and developed an extended-range missile that turned it into a long-range artillery piece. The missile needed more fuel and a larger rocket motor, which left less room for explosive and a smaller blast. The loss of destructive power made it ineffective for bombardment, and although several other [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] have tried to copy the design, none has succeeded in making it a success.

Organisation and traditions

The Iron Fists follow many of the customs of the [[Iron Hands]]. The Chapter's Marines also carry storm shields moulded in the shape of a medieval gauntlet. Their armour is a light blue-grey silver with gold [[Aquila]], arms and gauntlets, and black shoulder insets. Squad specialty is shown by a golden open symbol on the right shoulder, and squad number by a black numeral on the right knee. The Chapter badge is a clenched golden gauntlet on a field of pale bluish silver.

The Iron Hands are governed by an Iron Council of clan-company officers and keep ten Land-Behemoths, one for each clan company, as mobile fortress-monasteries. The Iron Fists differ from their parent in having a single [[Chapter Master]], Kastal Verchen. The number of Land-Behemoths they keep is not recorded.

Notable members

  • Kastal Verchen, [[Chapter Master]].
  • Wotan, a [[Dreadnought]] of the Chapter, now fallen.
