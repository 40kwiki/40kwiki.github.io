The Ordo Malleus, whose Inquisitors are often called "Daemonhunters", is one of the three [[Ordos Majoris]] of the [[Inquisition]]. Its task is to protect the [[Imperium of Mankind]] from the daemonic dangers of [[Chaos]]. Along with the [[Ordo Hereticus]] and the [[Ordo Xenos]], it forms the greater part of the Holy Ordos of the Emperor's Inquisition, the secret police and galaxy-wide intelligence service of the Imperium.

The Ordo is aided by the [[Grey Knights]], the psychically trained [[Space Marine Chapter]] that serves as its Chamber Militant. Its Inquisitors and Acolytes destroy the physical manifestations of Chaos, the [[Daemon]]s, wherever they break into [[Realspace|realspace]], and they hunt the mortal agents of Chaos who threaten the peace and the souls of the Imperium's people.

History

The Ordo Malleus is held to be the original heart of the [[Inquisition]]. It traces its origin to the period after [[Horus]] launched his rebellion against the Emperor. When the frigate ''[[Nathaniel Garro|Eisenstein]]'' brought news of the betrayal to [[Terra]], the Emperor asked his regent, [[Malcador the Sigillite]], to find loyal men and women to investigate threats from within and without the Imperium.

Malcador brought twelve individuals before the Emperor in the days before the [[Siege of Terra]], which began in 013.M31. Four were Imperial lords and administrators of an inquisitive bent. Eight were psychically gifted [[Adeptus Astartes|Astartes]], loyal to the Emperor, who served Malcador as Knights Errant. The eight Space Marines became the first Grand Masters of the [[Grey Knights]], and the four mortals founded what became the Inquisition proper.

The division that became the Ordo Malleus was not formally constituted until the mid-32nd millennium, after the [[War of the Beast]]. It was one of the two original Ordos Majoris, the other being the [[Ordo Xenos]], which was created in the same period.

Organisation

The Ordo Malleus is the secretive inner and first order of the [[Inquisition]], and it has a more rigid hierarchy than the other Ordos. It is governed by a council of 169 [[Inquisitor|Inquisitor Lords]], who are known as the "masters". Each master may request an audience with the [[Emperor of Mankind]] in the [[Sanctum Imperialis|inner sanctum]] of the [[Imperial Palace]].

Below the masters are the proctors and the proctors minor, each of whom leads a Chamber Practical. The Chambers Practical are named after their founders, and each one may hold from a few score to several hundred Inquisitors. The masters' authority extends to the [[Inquisitorial Representative]] to the [[Senatorum Imperialis]], and Imperial records state that some representatives have been tried and executed by the masters.

An Ordo Malleus Inquisitor may call on the aid of any Imperial organisation. Only the [[Grey Knights]], the Ordo's own Chamber Militant, are held to be able to meet a daemonic invasion of a world.

Role

The Ordo Malleus is concerned with the Warp, and with the [[Daemon|daemons]] that enter the Imperium through it. Its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] study the Warp's influence on the Imperium and on individual people, and they seek out corrupted worlds, cults and [[Psyker|psykers]] before a daemonic incursion can take hold. Its work is often carried out in secret, and the Ordo has a long tradition of keeping its methods from the rest of the [[Inquisition]].

The Ordo's Inquisitors are supported by the [[Grey Knights]] and by Inquisitorial storm troopers under their command.

Notable members

The Ordo's records name [[Hector Rex]] among its [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]]. Other Inquisitors of the Ordo appear throughout the Imperial rolls, but many of their names are sealed.
