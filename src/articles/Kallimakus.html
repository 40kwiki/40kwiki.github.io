Kallimakus, called Mahavastu Kallimakus and known as the Scribe of [[Magnus the Red]], was the servant who kept the records of the [[primarch]] of the [[Thousand Sons]]. He was present at the [[Council of Nikaea]] and wrote the Book of Magnus, one of the best-known records of the Thousand Sons' history.

Kallimakus served Magnus personally, and his account is among the few records of the Thousand Sons written from inside the Legion. Imperial scholars treat his text with caution, as it was written by a loyal servant of a primarch who was later declared a Traitor.

Council of Nikaea

Main article: [[Council of Nikaea]]

Kallimakus attended the [[Council of Nikaea]], where the Legions discussed the crisis of the Heresy. He recorded the debates as Magnus understood them, and his account of the council is the main source for the Thousand Sons' position there.

Book of Magnus

The Book of Magnus is recorded as the work Kallimakus wrote on the Legion's history and on the primarch's teachings. The text was sealed by Imperial order after the Heresy, and the surviving copies are held by the Inquisition.
