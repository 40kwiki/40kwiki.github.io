The Vorpal Swords are a loyal [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]] of unknown Founding and lineage. Imperial records keep almost nothing about them except two events: their part in the [[Abyssal Crusade]], and the Gheistos Cataclysm.

The Chapter returned from the [[Eye of Terror]] in 112.M38 with its Chapter Master Konvak Lann, who denounced the [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]]'s saint, [[Basillius|Basillius the Elder]], as a false idol. The testimony of the Vorpal Swords led to his unmasking and the destruction of his cult.

History

The Judged

After the Ecclesiarchal Purges of 321.M37 the Warp storm Dionys engulfed a dozen star systems, and cults and [[Mutant|mutations]] multiplied overnight. Many [[Space Marine Chapter|Space Marine Chapters]] whose worlds the storm touched found the flaws in their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] worsened in new recruits. The Vorpal Swords were one of them. [[Abyssal Crusade|Basillius]] the Elder, then very old and highly influential, demanded that all such Chapters be brought before him, and within a year the [[High Lords of Terra]] agreed. After tests and prognostications, hundreds of Chapters were passed as unaffected. Thirty were found wanting. These became The Judged.

The Judged volunteered for a [[Penitent|penitent]] crusade, and the most militant demanded to burn out their stain in battle. Basillius surprised his advisers by agreeing and sending them into the Eye of Terror. A Council of Dismay of one delegate from each Chapter accepted, preferring martyrdom to lifelong suspicion. In the last days of 321.M37 their ships passed through the [[Fall of Cadia|Cadian Gate]] with their relays silent and their colours hidden under black plates. A [[Chaos]] fleet fell on them as they entered and scattered them, and most of the Chapters were lost to the Eye. The Vorpal Swords were not.

Judgement of Basillius

The Vorpal Swords purged more than four hundred worlds in the Eye and came out with the other survivors in 112.M38, almost eight centuries after they went in. Held and tested by the [[Inquisition]] for the taint of Chaos, they passed every test. It then emerged that Basillius still lived. The Master of the Vorpal Swords, Konvak Lann, testified that the saint was a false idol who had sent innocents into the jaws of the Dark Gods. The survivors, led by the Chapter, broke into his palace and found him unaccountably old. Within a year every shrine and sepulchre of the saint was destroyed. After his execution by the Inquisition an [[Soul Drinkers|Edict of Obliteration]] was carried out: his bones, his relics, his books of doctrine and thousands of living worshippers were put on a derelict bulk freighter and sent into a nearby star. The full reach of his corruption has never been found.

Gheistos Cataclysm

At an unrecorded date in the [[41st millennium]] a [[Daemon|daemon]] army invaded the world of Gheistos from the Warp. The Vorpal Swords made an orbital assault at several zones. One detachment tried to relieve the [[Planetary Governor]]'s fortress, which was assailed by the Bloodthirster [[Skarbrand]]. Another faced the infected population of the capital, where Plague Daemons and Nurglings roamed among the dead and the dying. A Drop Pod assault surrounded a daemon force at the Devlan Space Port, and the Chapter closed a Warp rift there. Neither side won, and the world was destroyed by [[Exterminatus]].

Organisation and beliefs

The Vorpal Swords wear light green armour with a black power pack and shoulder plates. The white squad role badge on the right shoulder carries a white numeral, and the colour of the chest [[Aquila (symbol)|Aquila]] gives the company in the manner of the Codex.

Notable members

  • Konvak Lann: [[Chapter Master]] at the time of the [[Abyssal Crusade]], who unmasked Basillius in 112.M38.
