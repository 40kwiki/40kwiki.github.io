Merelda Pereth was the last recorded [[Lord High Admiral]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] before the seat was lost in the [[Hexarchy]]'s coup in early M42. She was one of the High Lords of Terra, and she was killed in the fighting at the coup.

Her successor is not named in the public record, and the seat has been recorded as unfilled since the coup.

The Hexarchy's coup

The coup was led by the Hexarchy, a group of conspirators among the High Lords and their allies. Three of the conspirators who still held seats, the Grand Provost Marshal Aveliza Drachmar, the [[Lord Commander Militant]] [[Mar Av Ashariel]] and Pereth, were killed in the fighting.

Legacy

Pereth is commemorated in the Navy's records as a commander who held the fleets together in a time of war. The Navis Imperialis still uses her name in its lists of commanders, but no successor to her office has been named.
