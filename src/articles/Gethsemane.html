Gethsemane, earlier named Gethsamaine Colonus, is a [[hive world]] in the Cyclops Cluster of the [[Coronid Deeps]] in the [[Gothic Sector]]. It is remembered for the [[Gethsamaine Raid]] during the [[Horus Heresy]] and for the [[Battle of Gethsemane]] during the [[Gothic War]]. Its position in a zone of [[Warp]] turbulence means its contact with the wider Imperium is intermittent.

History

Gethsamaine was discovered in 942.M30, only sixty-four standard years before [[Horus]]'s rebellion. It was colonised but had not yet been given a task when the Heresy began. The world was the first Imperial world conquered by the Warmaster's forces on their march to [[Terra]], and the first human world subjected to the so-called [[Dark Compliance]].

In 006.M31 the [[Iron Hands]] [[Legion]], then loyal, arrived in the system aboard the frigate [[Malin Dawn]]. Its survivors from the [[Istvaan III]] atrocity were among the first to bring news of Horus's betrayal. The [[Gethsamaine Raid]] that followed was a bloody pogrom carried out by Loyalist survivors of the Legion against the world's population.

In 151.M41 the [[Battle of Gethsemane]] was fought during the Gothic War. The Imperial Navy, with aid from the [[Aeldari]], defeated the forces of Chaos and broke the back of the [[Archenemy]]'s naval forces. It marked the start of [[Lord Admiral Ravensburg]]'s counter-attack.
