Gethsemane, earlier named Gethsamaine Colonus, is a [[hive world]] in the Cyclops Cluster of the [[Coronid Deeps]] in the [[Gothic Sector]]. It is remembered for the [[Gethsamaine Raid]] during the [[Horus Heresy]] and for the [[Battle of Gethsemane]] during the [[Gothic War]]. Its position in a zone of [[Warp]] turbulence means its contact with the wider Imperium is intermittent.

History

Gethsamaine was discovered in 942.M30, only sixty-four standard years before [[Horus]]'s rebellion. A [[Chartist]] liner crossing the void between the forge world of [[M'Pandex]] and Zarnow went far off course and found a primordial world of steaming rainforests that held no fauna. Warp-arks were sent from the hives of the [[Segmentum Solar]], and the settlers built manufactoria and cities of plasteel and ferrocrete in the western polar regions. By the start of the Heresy the world was a sizeable civilised and industrial world with an astropathic choir, naval docks and two defence monitors, the Gaius Herab and the Brazen Bull, although it had not yet been given a task.

In 006.M31 the frigate Malin Dawn, a Gladiator-class ship of the [[Emperor's Children]] Legion, drifted into the system, badly damaged. Its dead crew and Legionaries lay in the corridors, and the logs showed that its loyal crew had been attacked by traitor kin at [[Istvaan III]]. Before the defenders could react, four Havoc-class destroyers of the Warmaster's fleet and the Sons of Horus battlecruiser Ikon arrived. They destroyed both monitors and boarded the Gaius Herab with Dreadclaw assault pods, and then the Ikon left a message demanding submission. Gethsamaine surrendered without being invaded and became the first world to fall to the so-called [[Dark Compliance]].

After the [[Drop Site Massacre]] a second lone ship arrived, the Grand Cruiser Red Talon of the [[Iron Hands]] under the Iron Father [[Autek Mor]] of Clan Morragul. The Iron Hands silenced the remaining gun platforms, bombarded the world and killed the traitor officials and those who had knelt to Horus. They left an atomantic device in each of the five major cities and detonated them as the ship departed, which killed millions. This was the Gethsamaine Raid, a bloody warning that those who bowed to the Warmaster were not safe from Loyalist retribution.

The raid left the world uninhabitable for several centuries. The Imperium resettled it after Horus's defeat, and its name slowly changed to Gethsemane.

In 151.M41 Lord Admiral [[Cornelius von Ravensburg]] fought the [[Battle of Gethsemane]] against a Chaos fleet during the [[Gothic War]]. An Imperial victory won with the help of an [[Aeldari]] fleet, it began the Imperial counter-attack, and Abaddon's forces could mount no major offensive afterwards.
