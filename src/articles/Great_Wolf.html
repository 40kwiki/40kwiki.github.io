The Great Wolf is the title of the [[Chapter Master]] of the [[Space Wolves]] [[Space Marine]] [[Chapter]]. Although he is one of the most powerful figures in the [[Imperium of Mankind]], the Great Wolf regards himself as a steward of the Chapter and not its lord. The Chapter holds that its [[primarch]], [[Leman Russ]], will return to lead it in its final battle.

Selection

The Great Wolf is chosen from among the Chapter's [[Wolf Lord]]s when his predecessor dies, and he holds the title until his own death. The Chapter votes by casting name-runes into the Dragon's Skull in the heart of [[The Aett]], the Chapter's [[fortress-monastery]]. The Wolf Lord with the most runes at midnight is named Great Wolf. A tie is settled by a duel on the feasting table of the Great Hall, and the victor is hailed as the new Great Wolf.

Role

The Great Wolf leads the Chapter in war and commands its eleven Great Companies, together with the Company of the Great Wolf. That company holds the Chapter's [[Wolf Priest]]s, [[Iron Priest]]s and [[Rune Priest]]s, and keeps its [[Dreadnought]]s in stasis. The Great Wolf's emblem is the Wolf That Stalks Between the Stars, the badge of Leman Russ.

The disappearance of Russ

The tradition of the Great Wolf began after the [[Horus Heresy]]. At a feast on Fenris to mark the Emperor's victory over [[Horus]], Leman Russ fell into a vision and left the hall with his closest retainers. He has not been seen since. Imperial accounts record this event, while Space Wolves tradition holds that the primarch remains in a place beyond the sight of the Chapter.The date of the feast is given as the end of the Horus Heresy, c. 014.M31, with the primarch's departure recorded as estimated. Accounts differ on the details.
