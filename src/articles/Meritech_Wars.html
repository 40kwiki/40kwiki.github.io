The Meritech Wars were a regional conflict between the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and a group of nomadic Secessionists based in the [[Merates Cluster]]. The war was fought from 211 to 226.M41, in the third century of the 41st millennium, in a cluster of worlds that lies between the [[Calixis Sector]] and the [[Ixaniad Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. The clans of the cluster seceded from the Imperium, drew many Renegade factions to their cause, and raided deep into the Calixis Sector. The result was widespread disruption and anarchy across much of the sector.

At its height the war posed the greatest threat to the stability of the Calixis Sector in generations. It also threatened to provoke conflict between the Calixis and Ixaniad sectors. The war was ended by the rise to power of [[Myram Harvala]] as Sector Governor, under whose command the Meritech clans were crushed and the cluster was scoured of life. The [[Inquisition]] later established that the [[Heretek]] conspiracy of the [[Logicians]] had caused the outbreak of the war.

Outbreak

The Merates Cluster is a grouping of about a dozen barely habitable worlds. It lies in the void between the two Imperial sectors, and neither sector claimed it fully, so it was considered more trouble than it was worth. The cluster was dominated by the scavenging Meritech Clans, [[Voidborn]] families who kept ramshackle caravan fleets that mined, traded, salvaged and fought off corsairs and Orks between the sectors. The clans grew strong enough to monopolise much of the border trade, and the size of their fleets became a concern to the local authorities, but other crises pressed and the matter was left to fester. The uprising began when a [[Battlefleet Calixis]] patrol squadron of frigates, pursuing suspected pirates in the cluster, was ambushed. All but one frigate was destroyed by clan warships, and the clans seceded.

The Meritech clans then launched many raids and strikes deep into both sectors. Their ships were small, but they carried advanced jamming systems and compact weapons that made them hard to hunt. Their boarding parties were heavily cyber-augmented shock troopers. For a time the whole Calixis Sector was shaken, and several worlds came close to civil war as rumours of the rebellion spread.

The Calixian response

While the powers of Ixaniad withdrew to defend their own borders, a newly enthroned Sector Governor took charge in Calixis. With the backing of the [[Calixian Conclave]] of the Inquisition, Harvala rallied the defence. She purged the [[Lucid Court]] on the sector capital, [[Scintilla]], of dissenters, and she purged the Court in a single night. In what is remembered as the Proclamation of Scant Mercy, she ordered the sector battlefleet to mass into an armoured fist and strike the heart of the Merates Cluster, and no quarter was given. The purge of the cluster went on for seventeen years. Imperial accounts say the war cost millions of lives.

In the later stages a task force of the [[Imperial Navy]] pursued the remnants of the Meritech clans and their Logician masters deep into the Ixaniad Sector. The pursuit caused a serious diplomatic incident and raised the spectre of conflict between sectors. The Calixian Conclave then called on the authority of the seat of the Inquisition in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] to intervene.

Aftermath

Inquisitorial investigation found that the [[Logicians]] had both instigated the crisis and supplied the clans' new technological strength. Imperial records say the Logicians saw the war as a series of field tests for their technology. They had also placed their conspirators in senior positions in the logistics of the Imperial response, so that they could profit from either side's victory. The Inquisition, with the Mechanicus faction known as the [[Divine Light of Sollex]], hunted down and scoured Logician influence from Hive Tarsus on Scintilla, [[Fenksworld]] and dozens of other commercial operations and military vessels. After the war the power of the Logicians in the Calixis Sector seemed broken, and it remained so for several centuries, though the conspiracy is said to be spreading its shadow again. The worlds of the cluster were scoured of life.
