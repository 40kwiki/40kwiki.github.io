The Meritech Wars were a regional conflict between the [[Imperium of Mankind]] and a group of nomadic Secessionists based in the [[Merates Cluster]]. The war was fought from 211 to 226.M41, in a cluster of worlds that lies between the [[Calixis Sector]] and the [[Ixaniad Sector]] in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. The clans of the cluster seceded from the Imperium, drew many Renegade factions to their cause, and raided deep into the Calixis Sector. The result was widespread disruption and anarchy across much of the sector.

At its height the war posed the greatest threat to the stability of the Calixis Sector in generations. It also threatened to provoke conflict between the Calixis and Ixaniad sectors. The war was ended by the rise to power of [[Myram Harvala]] as Sector Governor, under whose command the Meritech clans were crushed and the cluster was scoured of life. The [[Inquisition]] later established that the [[Heretek]] conspiracy of the [[Logicians]] had caused the outbreak of the war.

Outbreak

The Merates Cluster is a grouping of about a dozen barely habitable worlds. It lies in the void between the two Imperial sectors, and neither sector claimed it fully, so it was considered more trouble than it was worth. The cluster was dominated by its clans, which had long resisted Imperial rule. The uprising began when a [[Battlefleet Calixis]] patrol squadron of frigates, pursuing suspected pirates in the cluster, was ambushed. All but one frigate was destroyed by clan warships, and the clans seceded.

The Meritech clans then launched many raids and strikes deep into both sectors. Their ships were small, but they carried advanced jamming systems and compact weapons that made them hard to hunt. For a time the whole Calixis Sector was shaken, and several worlds came close to civil war as rumours of the rebellion spread.

The Calixian response

While the powers of Ixaniad withdrew to defend their own borders, a newly enthroned Sector Governor took charge in Calixis. With the backing of the [[Calixian Conclave]] of the Inquisition, Harvala rallied the defence. She purged the [[Lucid Court]] on the sector capital, [[Scintilla]], of dissenters, and she directed the campaign against the clans for seventeen years. Imperial accounts describe a purge that cost millions of lives.

In the later stages a task force of the [[Imperial Navy]] pursued the remnants of the Meritech clans and their Logician masters deep into the Ixaniad Sector. The pursuit caused a serious diplomatic incident and raised the spectre of conflict between sectors. The Calixian Conclave then called on the authority of the seat of the Inquisition in the [[Segmentum Obscurus]] to intervene.

Aftermath

Inquisitorial investigation found that the [[Logicians]] had both instigated the crisis and supplied the clans' new technological strength. Imperial records say the Logicians saw the war as a series of field tests for their technology. They had also placed their conspirators in senior positions in the logistics of the Imperial response, so that they could profit from either side's victory. After the war the power of the Logicians was broken, and the Merates Cluster remained largely uninhabited.
