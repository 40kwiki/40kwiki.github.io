Kolossi was a [[Knight world]] that lay beside the [[Charadon Sector]]. Its noble houses piloted [[Imperial Knight]]s, and it supported the [[Legio Metalica]] against the Orks of [[Ghazghkull Thraka]]. In the early 42nd millennium it was taken by the Chaos lord [[Be'lakor]] and renamed [[Korvosi]].

Imperial records count Kolossi among the knight worlds whose knights were most valued by the Imperium's neighbours. Its loss is recorded as a serious blow to the Charadon defence.

Ork wars

Kolossi's knights fought beside the Legio Metalica's forces in the Second War of 941.M41 and the Third War of 998.M41 against the Orks of Ghazghkull Thraka. The Legio's forces had also fought there against the Orks in the earlier conflicts that shaped the region.

Capture by Be'lakor

In early M42, Be'lakor took the world and renamed it Korvosi. The fall is recorded as one of the major losses of the Charadon campaign, which also included the attack on the [[Phalanx]] in 999.M41.
