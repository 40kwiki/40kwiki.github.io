Bohemond was the [[High Marshal]] of the [[Black Templars]] [[Chapter]] in the mid-32nd millennium. He fought in the desperate battles against the [[Ork]] [[WAAAGH!]] of the [[Warboss]] known as [[The Beast]]. That [[greenskin]] host threatened [[Terra]] itself during the [[War of the Beast]], which took place in 544–546.M32.

Background

Bohemond was known as a headstrong and forceful commander. The Black Templars stood apart from the other Successors of the [[Imperial Fists]], although they shared the lineage of [[Rogal Dorn]] and kept their own code and practices. When [[Captain]] [[Koorland]] of the Imperial Fists enacted the "Last Wall Protocol" after the disaster on [[Ardamantua]], Bohemond agreed to speak for his Chapter. A council of the Successor Chapters was to be held in the [[Phall System]].

As the longest-serving Chapter Master among the Scions of Dorn, he hosted the council aboard the [[Battle Barge]] ''Abhorrence''. He later led his Chapter in the assault by the Scions of Dorn on the greenskins' [[Attack Moon]], which orbited Terra. After the battle, a tense meeting was held between the Last Wall Chapter Masters and the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]], [[Udin Macht Udo]]. Bohemond openly voiced his scorn for the quarrelling [[High Lords of Terra]] and for the Lord Commander.

Ullanor

Once [[Ullanor Prime]] was identified as the origin of The Beast's host, Bohemond argued for an Imperial counter-strike to kill the greenskin warlord. He led his Chapter in the invasion of Ullanor and in the assault on the capital city of Gorkogrod. He fought beside the returned [[Salamanders]] [[Primarch]] [[Vulkan]].

The High Marshal repeatedly led from the front. He also repeatedly challenged the authority of Koorland, even when Vulkan was present. Vulkan set a test to decide the next Lord Commander. Whoever could lift the [[Thunder Hammer]] ''Doomtremor'' would take the title. Bohemond and the [[Crimson Fists]] Chapter Master Quesadra could not lift it. Koorland, together with the [[Fists Exemplar]] Chapter Master Maximus Thane and a squad of Astartes, lifted the weapon and hurled it like a javelin. Vulkan then named Koorland the best candidate for the office.

During the final push on The Beast's temple-[[Gargant]], Bohemond refused to follow Koorland and to withdraw as the Primarch had wished. Vulkan struck him to the ground for his disobedience. Bohemond stayed on the ground in shame until he withdrew with the rest of the Scions of Dorn.
