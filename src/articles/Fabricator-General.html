The Fabricator-General of Mars, also titled the Magos Mechanicus, is the ruler of [[Mars]], the secular head of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] and the high priest of the [[Omnissiah|Cult Mechanicus]]. The holder speaks for the Synod of Mars and holds a permanent seat among the [[High Lords of Terra]]. The title is also given generally to the archmagos who rules any other [[Forge world]].

The office is older than the Imperium. Mars was ruled for thousands of years by the tech-priests of the Cult Mechanicus as a technocratic parliament, and when the [[Treaty of Olympus]] was made in 739.M30 the Fabricator-General was the Mechanicum's ambassador to Terra, who spoke for the Martian Parliament and was given a seat on the War Council of the [[Great Crusade]]. The Fabricator-General [[Kelbor-Hal]] took the Mechanicum over to [[Horus]] in 005.M31 and began the [[Schism of Mars]]. His deputy, the [[Fabricator-Locum]] [[Zagreus Kane]], became Fabricator-General of the loyalist forces and oversaw the transition of the Mechanicum into the Adeptus Mechanicus.

Under the Treaty, Mars keeps its own laws and faith, and the High Lords cannot appoint or remove its ruler. In the [[Era Indomitus]] the office was held by [[Oud Oudia Raskian]] until his disappearance after the [[Fall of Cadia]], and then by [[Xasandera Valdet]]. The archmagos [[Belisarius Cawl]] asked the [[Lord Commander of the Imperium|Lord Commander]] several times to install him; [[Roboute Guilliman]] refused, explaining that he lacked the power to appoint him and that the attempt would unleash a second Martian civil war, since many orthodox believers of the Cult Mechanicus regard Cawl and his faction as hereteks.

Powers and duties

The Fabricator-General's authority rests on two foundations. As ruler of [[Mars]] and head of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]], the holder is its secular political leader and keeps a permanent seat among the High Lords of Terra. As Magos Mechanicus the holder is the high priest of the [[Omnissiah|Cult Mechanicus]].

"Fabricator-general" is also the general title of the archmagos who holds political and doctrinal sway as planetary governor over any other [[Forge world]]. Many forge worlds have a different title for their governing archmagos, according to their own histories and local forms of the Cult Mechanicus's belief.

On the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] the Fabricator-General speaks for the Mechanicus. [[Oud Oudia Raskian]], more machine than man, could be brought to the Great Chamber only after works of construction.

History

Before the Imperium (Age of Strife – 739.M30)

See also: [[Age of Strife]]

The tech-priests of the Machine Cult rose to dominance on Mars in the early centuries of the [[Age of Strife]]. They formed a technocratic parliament of theocratic, feudal overlords, originally known as the [[Mechanicum]], and built around the worship of the Machine God a strong culture and ordered civilisation.

The Treaty and the Great Crusade (739.M30 – 005.M31)

See also: [[Treaty of Olympus]]

In 739.M30 the Emperor came to Mars and concluded with its priesthood the [[Treaty of Olympus]], by which the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] supplied his armies and fleets in return for its autonomy, its faith and the Emperor's protection. The Fabricator-General became the Martian envoy to [[Terra]] and sat on the War Council of the Great Crusade (c. 798.M30 – 005.M31). Kelbor-Hal held the office in the last years of the Crusade.

The Schism of Mars (005–006.M31)

See also: [[Schism of Mars]]

Kelbor-Hal resented the Emperor's ban on forbidden technologies, which the Emperor had ordered sealed in the Vaults of Moravec, and he and his kind held the Emperor to be a false god. In 005.M31 Horus's envoy [[Regulus]] won him to the rebellion and gave him the means to open the Vaults of Moravec, whose contents spread corrupting scrapcode through the forges. The [[Schism of Mars]] that followed (005–006.M31) set Kelbor-Hal's faction, the later [[Dark Mechanicum]], against the forges that held for the Emperor.

The [[Fabricator-Locum]] Zagreus Kane, master of the Mondus Occulum forge, succeeded the traitor Kelbor-Hal as Fabricator-General of the loyalist Mechanicum. When [[Sigismund]] and four companies of the [[Imperial Fists]] landed on Mars to secure the forges, Kane had nearly all of his servitors load arms and armour onto transports for Terra. After the Heresy he oversaw the transition of the Mechanicum into the Adeptus Mechanicus, which was recorded as formally recognised in 012.M31, and he was first Fabricator-General of the new body and its first High Lord. He sat on the Grand Council of Reconstruction in 014.M31. Kelbor-Hal's fate after the Siege of Terra is unknown.

War of the Beast (544–546.M32)

See also: [[War of the Beast]]

During the [[War of the Beast]] the Fabricator-General was [[Kubik]]. Kubik concealed what Mars knew of the Orks and studied their teleportation technology in secret. After the war the Chapter Master [[Maximus Thane]] demanded that [[Ullanor]] be destroyed by Exterminatus. Kubik instead teleported the Ork capital, Gorkogrod, aboard the vessel Ark Majesty with technology taken from the Ork Attack Moons, intending to plunder it of every scrap of advanced technology. The discovery of this deceit was the immediate cause of the [[Beheading]] of 546.M32. Cornered by the Grand Master of Assassins [[Drakan Vangorich]], Kubik shut himself down rather than let a virus be loosed into the Mechanicus noosphere.

Age of Apostasy (M36)

In the [[Reign of Blood]] the Fabricator-General [[Gastaph Hediatrix]] demanded that the High Lords account for themselves and that [[Goge Vandire]] be brought to trial. Vandire dissolved the council and declared the Mechanicus traitor. Hediatrix sent regiments of the Martian Tech-Guard to Terra, where they joined several Space Marine Chapters in the assault on the Ecclesiarchal Palace in c. 378.M36, and afterwards helped restore the council and bring Vandire's servants to trial.

Later millennia (M37–M41)

During the [[Black Crusade|Eighth Black Crusade]] (999.M37) the Fabricator-General [[Uixot]], an insular ruler, at first refused the Adeptus Custodes' request for aid against traitors holding the Andromax System, and yielded only to a flattering embassy. He also oversaw the addition of great new power systems to the [[Golden Throne]].

Era Indomitus (999.M41 – present)

At the [[Thirteenth Black Crusade]] in 999.M41 the Fabricator-General was Oud Oudia Raskian, who had led investigations into the failing mechanisms of the Golden Throne for over five centuries without a remedy. He opposed the Chancellor [[Lev Tieron]]'s attempt to release the [[Adeptus Custodes]] from Terra. Raskian vanished soon after the Fall of Cadia and was presumed dead. A faction of the Synod then petitioned Cawl's representatives to make him Fabricator-General and to take Mars in a direction more independent of Terra; Cawl declined. [[Xasandera Valdet]] later became Fabricator-General and sits among the High Lords.The year of Valdet's accession is not recorded. She is first named as Fabricator-General among the High Lords restored by Guilliman during the Indomitus Crusade, and her accession is dated to the early 42nd millennium on that basis.
