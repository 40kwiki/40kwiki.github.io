Compliance, also called Imperial Compliance, was the process by which a human world was brought under the rule of the [[Imperium of Mankind]] during the [[Great Crusade]]. A world that accepted Imperial rule, by treaty or by force of arms, was said to have achieved Compliance, or to have been made "Compliant".

Compliance campaigns were carried out by every branch of the Imperial military. The [[Astra Militarum]] supplied most of the troops. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] supplied engineers and the [[Titan Legions]]. The [[Space Marine Legions]] led the hardest assaults. Once the fighting ended, a world passed to the civil arm of the Imperium and was governed under the [[Administratum]] according to the [[Imperial Truth]].

Process

Compliance began with a survey. An [[Expeditionary Fleet]] arrived in the system and its commander sent terms to the planet's rulers. The terms asked the world to accept the Imperial Truth, to pay the [[Imperial tithe]] and to place its armed forces under Imperial command. Imperial accounts say most human worlds accepted these terms after a short exchange of messages. Worlds that refused were attacked. Where a local dynasty refused to yield, its rulers were removed and its forces were folded into the Imperial Army.

Records of the early campaigns describe a common sequence. The fleet secured orbit and the main landing sites. Imperial forces then took the capital and any fortified cities. Last came the reorganisation of the planet's government. Imperial accounts give the sequence in this order, although the speed of each stage varied widely from world to world.

Administration after Compliance

A Compliant world kept its population and many of its local institutions, but it lost control of its armies and its trade. The Administratum appointed a [[Planetary Governor]] to run the world. A detachment of the Astra Militarum held the planet until its own forces could be raised for the Imperium. The [[Remembrancer Order]] sent [[Remembrancers]] to record the process, and their records were kept in the Imperial archives.

The Imperial tithe, set by the Administratum, was taken each year in supplies, minerals, weapons, ships or manpower. Some populations were moved to colonise distant worlds. The most dangerous peoples were pressed into the Imperial Army and sent to the front, which many warlike cultures, such as the [[Knight world|Knight Worlds]], regarded as an honour.

Dark Compliance

During the [[Horus Heresy]], [[Horus]] ran a new regime of conquest in the territories he held. Imperial scholars call this "Dark Compliance". Horus kept the forms of the old system but used them to build his own shadow empire. The Loyalist records of the Great Crusade describe the older process, which was not corrupted in this way.
