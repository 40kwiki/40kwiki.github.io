The Skitarii, collectively the Legiones Skitarii and sometimes called the Tech-Guard, are the soldiers of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]]. They are men and women whose bodies have been extensively replaced with machinery, armed with the most advanced weapons of the [[Forge world|forge worlds]] and directed in battle by the tech-priests of the Cult Mechanicus. Every forge world maintains its own Skitarii Legions, which guard it in peace and march with the Mechanicus's armies in war.

The Skitarii are not part of the [[Astra Militarum]] and do not answer to its commanders. They serve the Mechanicus alone, in accordance with the [[Treaty of Olympus]], and fight beside the [[Titan Legions]] and [[Imperial Knights|Knight houses]] of the forge worlds, or as part of the great expeditions of the [[Explorator|Explorator fleets]]. They are regarded in the Imperium with a mixture of respect and unease, because their discipline is absolute and their humanity uncertain.

Each Skitarius is connected to his commanders through the noosphere, the data-field of the Mechanicus. Through it the tech-priests can alter their soldiers' behaviour in the midst of battle by broadcasting doctrina imperatives, overriding fear, pain and even the will to survive.

History

Origins

The Skitarii existed on [[Mars]] before the Imperium. During the [[Age of Strife]], when Mars was cut off from [[Terra]] and torn by wars between rival forges, the tech-priests armed and augmented their followers to defend their domains. When the [[Treaty of Olympus]] joined Mars to the Imperium, these forces became the guardians of the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] and its [[Forge world|forge worlds]].

Great Crusade and Heresy

See also: [[Great Crusade]] and [[Horus Heresy]]

During the [[Great Crusade]] the armies of the forge worlds were organised into [[Mechanicum Taghmata|Taghmata]], the war hosts of the Mechanicum, in which the Skitarii fought beside [[Legio Cybernetica|battle-automata]], the Titans of the [[Titan Legions|Collegia Titanica]] and the [[Imperial Knight|Knight houses]]. They took part in many of the crusade's campaigns, and the Mechanicum's [[Explorator]] fleets travelled with every major expedition.

When the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) divided the Mechanicum, the Skitarii divided with it. On Mars, [[Fabricator-General|Fabricator General]] [[Kelbor-Hal]] sided with [[Horus]], and Skitarii fought Skitarii across the Red Planet in the [[Schism of Mars]]. [[Traitor]] forge worlds sent their Skitarii to the armies of the [[Warmaster]], and many of those who survived the Heresy fled with the traitors into the [[Eye of Terror]], where their descendants serve the [[Dark Mechanicum]].

Late 41st millennium

In the ten thousand years after the Heresy, the Skitarii fought wherever the interests of the Mechanicus were threatened: to defend forge worlds, to recover ancient technology, and to support Imperial campaigns. The Mechanicus records name the war against the [[T'au Empire]] on Vesh'yo in 747.M41, the defence of the research station of Tyran Primus against the [[Tyranids]] in 745.M41, and the fighting against the [[Necrons]] of Magogue in 991.M41, where Skitarii of [[Stygies VIII]] held an awakening tomb complex.

Era Indomitus

Further information: [[Era Indomitus]]

Since the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the Skitarii have been fully committed to the defence of the forge worlds, many of which lie in the [[Imperium Nihilus]] and have been cut off from Mars. [[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]] of [[Archmagos]] [[Belisarius Cawl]] accompanied the [[Indomitus Crusade]], and Skitarii forces have fought in the [[Pariah Nexus]] against the Necrons, and against [[Chaos]] forces seeking to capture the forges.

Creation

Skitarii are drawn from the populations of the [[Forge world|forge worlds]], from children raised in the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]]'s schools, and in some cases from bodies grown in vats. Each is altered by stages throughout his service. Limbs are replaced with mechanical prostheses, organs with augmetics, and senses with electronic equivalents. Ultimately even the mind is reshaped: memories are removed or rewritten, and implants allow the tech-priests to control the soldier through the noosphere. A veteran Skitarius may have little of his original body left, and his augmetics are a mark of honour, not of injury.

Despite this, Skitarii remain human in important respects. Unlike [[Servitor|servitors]], they keep their own minds and some of their own will, and they are capable of courage, initiative and loyalty. They worship the [[Omnissiah]] devoutly, and many hope to rise into the priesthood.

Organisation

The Skitarii are organised in Legions, each attached to a [[Forge world|forge world]] and named for it. A Legion is divided into macroclades, which are broken down into cohorts and maniples. The basic unit is the squad, led by an Alpha; the senior Alpha of a maniple is the Alpha Primus. A Skitarii Marshal commands a larger force, but above every Skitarius stands the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|tech-priest]] who directs his formation, usually a [[Tech-priest Dominus]], whose commands are law.

Each forge world arms and equips its Legions according to its own traditions. The Legion of [[Mars]] is the most glorious of all and its red, silver and black is seen in every Segmentum. That of [[Lucius]] wears plate scorched black by the artificial sun of its world. The Legion of [[Ryza]] has a reputation for unshakeable faith, and that of [[Stygies VIII]], which has been accused of heresy, seizes ancient knowledge wherever it can. Those of [[Graia]] have fought space battles across the hulls of the spaceborne cities of their masters, those of [[Metalica]] are fiercely independent of Mars, and those of [[Agripinaa]] fight across the war-blackened hellscapes of the [[Eye of Terror]].

Forces

Skitarii Vanguard

The Vanguard, known informally as rad-troopers, are infantry armed with radium carbines. Their weapons and their own bodies are so radioactive that their very presence weakens the enemy, and they are assigned to the most hazardous war zones.

Skitarii Rangers

The Rangers are infantry who hunt their quarry by stalking it over solar weeks or even months until it can run no more. They are armed with galvanic rifles, and the stink of electrocuted corpses is never far behind them.

Sicarian Ruststalkers and Infiltrators

The Sicarians are elite hunters, tall and slender, built for close combat and assassination. Ruststalkers were first devised as cybernetic assassins to hunt feral servitors on Mars, and fight with transonic blades. Infiltrators emit a neurostatic bombardment that robs their victims of their senses before the killing begins.

Ironstrider Ballistarii and Sydonian Dragoons

Both are mounted on long-legged Ironstrider combat walkers. The Dragoons charge into the foe with taser lances, while the Ballistarii act as marksmen, carrying heavy cognis weapons.

Onager Dunecrawlers

The Onager Dunecrawler is a combat walker used exclusively by the Skitarii, carrying heavy weapons and protected by an Emanatus force field. It descends from the Mars Universal Land Engine, designed by the techno-archaeologist Arkhan Land as a workhorse to escort the tech-priests across the red deserts of Mars, and later rebuilt as a weapon of war.

Doctrine

The Skitarii fight with precision and without fear. Their commanders can send doctrina imperatives through the noosphere, compelling their troops to adopt one form of behaviour at the expense of another. Protector imperatives make them steadfast and accurate at range; Conqueror imperatives drive them forward to close and destroy. The cost of an imperative is the Skitarius's freedom of action, and in extreme cases his life, because the commands override the body's natural instinct to survive.

The Skitarii are equipped with weapons that the rest of the Imperium rarely sees, including arc rifles, transuranic arquebuses, plasma calivers and phosphor weapons, and they are among the best-armed soldiers of the Imperium. Their weakness is their number: no [[Forge world|forge world]] can raise Skitarii in the quantities of the [[Astra Militarum]], and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicus]] guards its Legions jealously.

Heraldry and appearance

Skitarii wear hooded robes over their armour plates and augmetics, and the colours of both are fixed by the traditions of their [[Forge world|forge world]]. Most Legions wear some form of the red of Mars as a mark of loyalty to the Red Planet. Others depart from it: the Legions of [[Agripinaa]] reverse the Martian red and black, those of [[Lucius the Eternal|Lucius]] wear plate scorched almost black by the artificial sun at the heart of their world, and those of [[Ryza]] wear ochre. [[Stygies VIII]], a forge world long suspected of heresy, uses the colours of Mars redistributed to proclaim its orthodoxy. Each Skitarius also bears glyphs that identify his maniple and cohort, and the [[Purity seal|purity seals]] of blood-wax and parchment that mark him as free of scrapcode.
