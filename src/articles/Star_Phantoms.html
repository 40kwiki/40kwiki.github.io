The Star Phantoms are a Loyalist [[Space Marine]] Chapter of the [[23rd Founding]], created in the early [[38th millennium]] from an unknown genetic origin. They took part in the final days of the [[Badab War]] and in the final assault on the [[Astral Claws]]' homeworld of Badab Primaris, which helped bring the war to its end.

The Chapter was created during the so-called "Sentinel" Founding. Its mission was to take and hold regions on the borders of the Emperor's domains that were contested by [[Xenos|xenos]]. Many of its early campaigns were fought in such regions.
