Erekart Mesring was the [[Ecclesiarch]] at the outbreak of the [[War of the Beast]] in 544.M32. He was a scion of a merchant family who used his seat to protect his house's shipyards. He was an ally of the [[Lord High Admiral]] [[Lansung]], and he declared a [[War of Faith]] against the Beast.

Mesring's decision to declare the war was recorded by the Ecclesiarchy as a defence of the faith. Critics in the Administratum record that the war also protected the shipyards of his family, which lay in the path of the Beast's forces.

Death

Mesring was shot by [[Koorland]], the [[Imperial Fists]] Chapter Master, for heresy. The Chapter Master's action is recorded as part of the Imperial Fists' campaign against the High Lords who supported the Beast's cause. The date of the killing is estimated from the sequence of the war.The date of Mesring's death is estimated from the war's records; no record gives an exact day.
