The Militarum Tempestus, also known as the Ordo Tempestus, is one of the most rigidly codified organisations in the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. Its troops form the elite core of the [[Astra Militarum]] and serve as its special operations units. Its soldiers are chosen for skill, discipline and endurance, and are trained to act alone or in small groups where a full regiment cannot go.

Although the Militarum Tempestus is technically a sub-faction governed by the [[Administratum]], it enjoys far more autonomy than most Imperial regiments. Its officers answer to their own command structure, and their missions are set by the high command rather than by the [[Sector Governor|sector governor]] of the world where they serve.

Organisation

The branch is organised into a small number of elite formations, each drawn from particular Imperial worlds. Recruits are trained in the methods of the [[Imperial Guard]] and then in specialist fieldcraft. Their equipment is issued from the [[Departmento Munitorum]], which ensures that every unit is supplied to the same standard.

Deployment

Militarum Tempestus units are deployed to spearhead the most dangerous operations of a campaign, such as the seizure of key sites, the capture of enemy officers, and the defence of worlds that cannot wait for regular forces. They are often used in areas where the Imperial high command has little information.
