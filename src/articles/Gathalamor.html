Gathalamor is a [[Cardinal world]] of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is ruled directly by a [[Cardinal]] of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and pays its tithe to the church rather than to the [[Administratum]]. The world holds a reduced grade in the Imperial records, as its tithe is set by the Ecclesiarchy's own rules.

The world is best known for the [[Plague of Unbelief]], an uprising led by the apostate Cardinal Bucharis. He denied the teaching of the [[Ecclesiarchy]] and gathered a large following on the planet. The Administratum records the uprising under 300–310.M36.

Plague of Unbelief

Main article: [[Plague of Unbelief]]

Bucharis preached that the [[God-Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] was not to be worshipped in the manner of the church. His followers refused the tithe and drove out the clergy sent from [[Terra]]. The [[Imperial Cult|Ecclesiarchy]] sent [[Adepta Sororitas|Sisters of Battle]] and [[Astra Militarum|Imperial Guard]] regiments to suppress the rising. Imperial accounts describe the war as bloody, and Bucharis is said to have died in the final assault.The dates and the sequence of the uprising are estimated from Administratum records. Ecclesiarchy accounts are less precise.
