Wyrmwood is a rogue world in the [[Somnium Stars]], the region beyond the northern sectors of the [[Ultima Segmentum]]. It was made into a machine, and it served as the base of the renegade [[Vashtorr]]. The [[Dark Angels]] found it during their campaigns in the Somnium Stars.

Some Dark Angels sources claim that the world was built from the ruins of [[Caliban]], the Chapter's lost homeworld. Imperial scholars do not accept this claim, and the origin of the planet is not recorded.

Discovery

The Dark Angels found Vashtorr's base in the Somnium Stars and fought there against his forces. The world is considered a place of great danger, and the Chapter's records describe its machinery as hostile to life. Its location is marked as a warning in the Chapter's charts.The dates of the Dark Angels' expedition are not recorded. The account is taken from later Chapter histories.
