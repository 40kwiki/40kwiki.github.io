Juskina Tull was Speaker for the [[Chartist Captains]] and a member of the [[Senatorum Imperialis]] at the time of the [[War of the Beast]] (544–546.M32). She represented the merchant houses whose voidships make up most of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s hulls, and during the crisis she briefly became the most powerful voice in the Senatorum. Her Proletarian Crusade against the Ork [[Attack Moon]] over [[Terra]] ended in disaster, and she died in the Beheading.

Office

The Speaker commands the merchant fleets administratively and speaks for the chartered captains to the High Lords. From the Nexus Axiomatic on Terra she controls much of the Imperium's trade, so in a general war her influence can rival that of the [[Lord High Admiral]]. The record does not say when Tull took the office or who held it before her.

The War of the Beast

Main article: [[War of the Beast]]

As refugees fled the Beast's advance, Tull raised the price of passage and freight between the Segmentae. Her motives were commercial, and other High Lords noted it. The appearance of an Ork Attack Moon over Terra gave her an opening. With the Ecclesiarch [[Erekart Mesring]] she turned the Senatorum against Lord High Admiral [[Lansung]] and removed him from power.

She then denounced the Navy's defence of Terra and called for a mass assault on the Attack Moon by civilian volunteers and merchant ships, with Frateris militia and what Guard regiments could be spared. Vangorich, Veritus and Lansung called the plan madness but were overruled. Tull had no military training. The fleet launched, and the Ork defenders destroyed it. She fell into a stupor and said nothing at later meetings of the Senatorum.

She roused herself once, to support [[Koorland]] of the [[Imperial Fists]] and the [[Inquisition]] when they removed [[Udin Macht Udo]] as [[Lord Commander of the Imperium]].

Death

In 546.M32 [[Drakan Vangorich]], [[Grand Master of Assassins]], had the High Lords killed in a single day. Tull's handmaiden Anastay, in truth Vangorich's agent, worked on her guilt over the Crusade until Tull shot herself.
