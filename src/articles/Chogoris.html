Chogoris, recorded by Imperial astrocartographers as Mundus Planus, is a [[Feral world|feral world]] of the Yasan Sector in the [[Segmentum Ultima]] and the homeworld of the [[White Scars]]. It is the world on which the [[primarch]] [[Jaghatai Khan]] grew up among the horse tribes of its steppe, united them, and overthrew the empire of the Palatine, shortly before the [[Emperor of Mankind|Emperor]] and [[Horus]] found him in 865.M30.

The White Scars have recruited from its tribes for more than ten thousand years. Their fortress-monastery, Quan Zhou, stands on the highest summit of the Khum Karta mountains. Chogoris lies near the [[Maelstrom]], and in the [[Era Indomitus]] it was besieged by the [[Red Corsairs]] of [[Huron Blackheart]] in the [[War for Chogoris]].

Geography

Chogoris has wide temperate grasslands, mountain ranges and settled lowlands. The great steppe west of the old Palatine empire is called the Empty Quarter: a wind-swept plain where nomadic tribes follow their herds between summer pastures and winter valleys in the Khum Karta, "the mountains that scrape the stars". The east of the main continent held the cities of the Palatine, a feudal empire of armoured horsemen and walled cities.

The Chogoris System also holds a [[Forge world|forge world]], a [[Shrine world|shrine world]], a [[Planetary classification|dead world]] and other bodies listed in the [[White Scars]]' charts. The neighbouring Gartuli and Thaxis systems fell to the [[Red Corsairs]] in the [[War for Chogoris]].

History

The Palatine and the Empty Quarter

Chogoris was settled before the [[Age of Strife]] (c. M25–M30) and fell back to a feudal level. By the late [[30th millennium]] its east was ruled by the Palatine, whose nobles hunted the steppe tribes for sport and took them as slaves. [[White Scars]] scripture describes blood rites and sacrifices at the Palatine's court, and some Imperial scholars believe the empire worshipped the [[Chaos Gods|Dark Gods]].The settlement of Chogoris is undated; the Palatine's empire is recorded only in the generations before 865.M30.

Jaghatai Khan

Main article: [[Jaghatai Khan]]

Jaghatai's capsule came to rest near the Quonon River in about 792.M30. Ong Khan, chief of the small Talskar tribe, took the child as a gift of the gods and named him Jaghatai. When Ong Khan was killed by the rival Kurayed tribe, Jaghatai led the Talskars to destroy them and swore to end the tribes' wars with one another. He fought hundreds of battles, bound the tribes into a single confederacy and an army called the Mathuli, and mixed their warriors so that old loyalties gave way to loyalty to him.

When a hunting party led by the Palatine's son seized him, Jaghatai killed it and sent back the son's head with a message that the steppe people were no longer the Palatine's playthings. The Palatine marched west with a great army and was beaten. Jaghatai then conquered the Palatine's cities one by one, offering surrender or destruction, until the people of the capital killed the Palatine themselves and gave his head to the Khan. His conquest was complete less than six months before the Emperor arrived.The Khan's wars are dated only as ending shortly before 865.M30; c. 840–865.M30 is an estimate.

The Great Crusade and the Heresy

In 865.M30 a fleet of the [[Luna Wolves]] carrying the Emperor and [[Horus]] reached Chogoris. Jaghatai took command of the V Legion, which became known as the White Scars after the Blooding, the ritual scarring the Legion adopted on Chogoris in 875.M30. Chogorian tribesmen filled its ranks, and the Legion's culture, language and titles came from the steppe. During the [[Horus Heresy]] (005–014.M31) the main strength of the Legion was away at [[Prospero]] and [[Terra]], and Chogoris itself was held as a recruiting world. After the Heresy, Jaghatai fought the [[Drukhari]] around the Yasan Sector until he vanished into the [[Webway]] in 084.M31.

The 41st millennium

Further information: [[41st millennium]]

In 890.M41 a [[Necrons|Necron]] tomb ship entered orbit and bombarded an empty region of the planet for unknown reasons; the White Scars battle-barge Jaghatai's Pride broke its defences and the defence laser of Quan Zhou destroyed it with a single shot. In 943.M41 Great Khan Kyublai Khan rode out after a Drukhari raid on Chogoris and never returned, and Jubal Khan succeeded him. An Imperial transmission of 991.M41 lists Chogoris as infested by a burrowing xenos species called the Drugh.

The War for Chogoris

In 999.M41, as the [[Great Rift]] opened, the Maelstrom swelled and the Red Corsairs of Huron Blackheart besieged the Yasan Sector. The White Scars withdrew from the [[Wars for Armageddon|Third War for Armageddon]] and the Agrellan campaign to defend their world. Gartuli and Thaxis fell, and refugees streamed to Chogoris. The Chapter held the planet, and soon after the Red Corsairs withdrew, Battle Group Delphi II of Fleet Tertius of the [[Indomitus Crusade]] reached the system with [[Primaris Space Marines]] and the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] means to make more. The fighting that continues across the sector is called the Blackheart War.An earlier account credits the relief of Chogoris to [[Roboute Guilliman]] and Fleet Primus. The White Scars' own annals say Fleet Tertius arrived after the siege was broken; that version is followed here.

Since the Rift opened, the curse of the Warp has fallen on the tribes too: those showing [[Mutant|mutation]] are killed by their own people, and the Chapter's Stormseers watch for subtler corruption. Successor [[Chapters]] such as the [[Marauders (Chapter)|Marauders]], Rampagers and [[Destroyers (Chapter)|Destroyers]] have sent warriors to the Yasan Sector.

Society

Chogoris remains semi-feudal. It has cities and spaceports, but most of its people live as nomads in the Empty Quarter, armed with bows and swords and hunting with the great hawks called berkuts. The unity Jaghatai imposed did not outlast his departure, and the tribes have fought one another ever since. The pyre-tombs of fallen [[White Scars]] in the valleys of the Khum Karta are places of pilgrimage, and young warriors who survive the journey through them are taken as aspirants.

Quan Zhou is said to contain forests stocked with game and streams running through its galleries. It holds the Chapter's records, relics and the great defence laser that destroyed the [[Necrons|Necron]] ship in 890.M41.
