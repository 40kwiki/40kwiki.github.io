Terra, also called Holy Terra and in older records Earth, is the world on which humanity arose and the capital of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is the third planet of the [[Sol System]], in the heart of the [[Segmentum Solar]], and the seat of the [[Emperor of Mankind]] on the [[Golden Throne]]. Every Imperial world owes it tithe and allegiance, and every Imperial citizen is taught that it is the holiest of all places.

Terra was the site of the Emperor's rise, the [[Unification Wars]], the building of the [[Imperial Palace]] and the launch of the Great Crusade. During the [[Horus Heresy]] it was besieged by the traitor Legions in the [[Siege of Terra]], the war's final and greatest battle.

History

Before the Imperium

The earliest history of Terra is almost entirely lost. Imperial scholars hold that humanity arose on the planet in the deep past, and that it was home to a succession of civilisations, many of which were destroyed by their own weapons. Warp-faring humanity reached the stars in the [[Age of Technology|Dark Age of Technology]], and Terra became the capital of a great human civilisation. That civilisation collapsed in the [[Age of Strife]], when [[Warp storm|warp storms]] cut off the worlds from one another. Terra was torn by wars between techno-barbarian nations that began in the 27th millennium and used weapons that killed billions.

Unification

See also: [[Unification Wars]]

Towards the end of the Age of Strife the Emperor revealed himself and began the [[Unification Wars]], bringing the warring powers of Terra under his rule. The wars ended in 712.M30 with the Battle of Mount Ararat. The Emperor then concluded the [[Treaty of Olympus]], known on Terra as the Treaty of Mars, with the [[Adeptus Mechanicus|Mechanicum]] of [[Mars]], and from Terra launched the [[Great Crusade]].

Horus Heresy

Further information: [[Horus Heresy]]

The [[Traitor|traitor]] fleets of [[Horus]] came to the [[Sol System]] in 013.M31, and the [[Siege of Terra]] itself was fought in 014.M31. The [[Imperial Palace]] was nearly overrun. The [[Imperial Fists]], the [[Blood Angels]] and the [[White Scars]] stood among its defenders, much of the planet was levelled by the traitors' bombardment, and the suffering of the Terran civilians left a lasting fear of Space Marines among the people. The siege ended with the death of Horus aboard his flagship and the Emperor's interment on the Golden Throne.

The Imperial age

In the ten thousand years since, Terra has been repeatedly threatened and never lost. In 544–546.M32 the [[War of the Beast]] carried a vast Ork horde across the Imperium to the Throneworld. Soon afterwards, in the event called the Beheading, the Grand Master of Assassins [[Drakan Vangorich]] had the [[High Lords of Terra|High Lords]] killed and ruled the Imperium himself for decades. After the opening of the [[Great Rift]] the Throneworld itself was assailed by daemons in the [[Battle of Lion's Gate]]. [[Roboute Guilliman]] now holds the title of Lord Commander of the Imperium.

Geography

Terra has been reshaped by ten thousand years of Imperial rule. Almost the whole surface, apart from the Antarctic region, is covered by vast edifices of state and hive cities, and the soil is barren. The Himalazian range, with the [[Imperial Palace]] built upon it, is the one mountain chain left untouched. The old oceans boiled away in the years after the Heresy under the heat of the crowded population, and the planet's water is now brought in by freighters hauling icy comets from the outer [[Sol System]].

Countless orbital defence platforms circle the planet. The Moon, [[Luna]], is inhabited and holds immense defence lasers and, since the Great Crusade, the greatest shipyards and naval base of the Imperium.

Institutions and places

Terra is the headquarters of almost every institution of the Imperium. The [[Administratum]] has its heart in the Palace, and the [[Ecclesiarchy]] keeps its Ecclesiarchal Palace, a complex that covers almost all of the southern continent of Australia, with the Cathedral of the Emperor Deified nearby. The [[Inquisition]] keeps its headquarters in a heavily warded complex beneath the ice of the southern pole.

  • The [[Imperial Palace]], which uses the Himalazian range as its foundation and spreads across much of the northern hemisphere.
  • The Chamber of the Astronomican, carved from a single peak of the Himalazian range, where ten thousand psykers sustain the psychic beacon that guides the fleets.
  • The Scholastica Psykana, where [[Psyker|psykers]] are trained or put to the sacrifice.
  • The [[Ecclesiarchal Palace]] and the Cathedral of the Emperor Deified.
  • The Hall of Judgment, headquarters of the [[Adeptus Arbites]].

Population and society

The population of Terra has never been counted. It runs to hundreds of billions, crowded into the [[Hive world|hive cities]]. A small upper class of officials of the [[Adeptus Terra]], clergy, nobles of the Navis Nobilite and officers stands above the masses of labourers and serfs who toil in the manufactoria, and the greatest hope of many families is that a child will be accepted into the Adeptus Terra as a menial. Pilgrims arrive in their billions, though only millions are admitted each day, and many pilgrimages begin with ancestors and end centuries later.

Defence

Terra is among the most heavily defended places in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. Its defences include the Palace's walls, orbital batteries, the [[Adeptus Custodes]] and the [[Sisters of Silence]]. The [[Imperial Fists]] garrisoned the planet until the War of the Beast, when they became a fleet-based Chapter, and elements of the Chapter returned to guard it after the 13th [[Black Crusade]] in 999.M41.

Terra in the Imperial Cult

Terra is the holiest place in the Imperium. The [[Imperial Cult]] holds that even its barren soil is sacred, and the faithful of every world hope, once in their lives, to reach it. The long Eternity Gate passage into the Sanctum Imperialis ends the pilgrim trail, and the great cathedrals, among them the Cathedral of the Saviour Emperor, are said to hold relics of the Emperor's armour and of [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s cloak. Pilgrims who stray into restricted areas are put to death by the [[Adeptus Arbites]].

Terra and the Era Indomitus

See also: [[Indomitus Crusade]]

After the fall of [[Cadia]] the opening of the [[Great Rift]] caused the beacon of the [[Astronomican]] to falter, and Khorne launched an assault on the Imperial Palace, the [[Battle of Lion's Gate]]. A joint force of Primaris Space Marines, [[Adeptus Custodes]] and [[Sisters of Silence]] repelled it, though the battle showed how far the Chaos Gods can reach should the [[Golden Throne]] fail. Since his return as Lord Commander of the Imperium, [[Roboute Guilliman]] has sought to restore the Sisters of Silence to their old place as defenders of the Throneworld.
