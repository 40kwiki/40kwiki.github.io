Alaric Prime is an ancient [[Knight world]] in the [[Sanctus Reach]] on the edge of the [[Segmentum Obscurus]]. Its society is feudal, made up of toiling peasants and proud noble houses. Its nobles pilot [[Imperial Knight]]s and are paid in military service to the Imperium.

The planet is covered mostly by sulphurous seas and chemical tides. Its land forms islands strung into long chains, some of them thousands of miles from the nearest coast. Few lifeforms survive outside the hardiest species. Alaric Prime has more knightly houses than most Knight worlds, and the houses compete for the honour of supplying the greatest knights.

Houses and service

The Knight houses of Alaric Prime are bound to the Imperium by oath. A house sends its knights to war when the Imperium calls on it, and in return it keeps its lands and its feudal rights. [[House Terryn]] is the best recorded house of the world. In the late 41st millennium its Knights fought in the [[Eastern Fringe]], among other wars against the [[T'au Empire]].
