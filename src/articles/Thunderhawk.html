Thunderhawk
Gunship of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]
TypeHeavy assault gunship and transport
OperatorsMost [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] and [[Space Marine Legions|Legions]]
VariantsShadowhawk, Whispercutter

The Thunderhawk is the heavy assault gunship of the [[Adeptus Astartes]]. It carries Space Marines into battle from orbit, and it is armed for close support. Most Chapters operate Thunderhawks, and they are among the most recognisable craft of the Adeptus Astartes.

Some Chapters are notably lean in heavy weapons. Their battle companies rely on bikers, jetbikers, [[Land Speeder]]s, Thunderhawks, assault bikes and light armour.

Variants

The Legions' techmarines drew on the Kiavahran artificers' secret lore to make the Shadowhawk gunship, a Thunderhawk pattern nearly invisible to augurs. They also made the Whispercutter, a gravitic open-framed flyer that could land ten Legionaries in a war zone in silence. It has been speculated that the Kiavahran guild-artificers would otherwise have been declared outcast by the [[Mechanicus]] for their unorthodox practices.The sources describe these variants in general terms. The dates of their development are not recorded.
