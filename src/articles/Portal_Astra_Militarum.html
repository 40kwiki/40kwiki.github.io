The Astra Militarum is the vast mortal army of the Imperium of Mankind. Its regiments are raised from billions of worlds, from the fields of Cadia to the jungles of Catachan. This portal gathers the articles on its regiments, its commanders and its doctrine.

Selected article

[[Astra Militarum]] is the mortal army of the Imperium, raised from the populations of countless worlds and organised into regiments. Its doctrine is set down in the [[Tactica Imperium]], and its commissars enforce discipline in the name of the Emperor. Its regiments have held the line against every enemy of the Imperium for ten thousand years.
