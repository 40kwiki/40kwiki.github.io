The Terminus Est is a [[Daemonship]] of the [[Death Guard]] and the flagship of [[Typhus]], Herald of [[Nurgle]]. It was one of the first capital ships given to the Legion and has now sailed for more than ten thousand years. Its very name is a curse on the lips of [[Imperial Navy]] captains.

Wherever it has appeared, [[Plague Marines]] and the [[Destroyer Plague]] have followed. Its design, copied in M36 from stolen plans, became the [[Despoiler-class Battleship]], and the flaw it passed on has cost the Navy dozens of ships.

History

Construction and the Great Crusade

Imperial tradition holds that the ship was built before the [[Great Crusade]] in the yards of [[Luna]], to a pattern larger and more cumbersome than any earlier. Its forked prow served as both a launch bay and a gun deck, so it could land hundreds of [[Stormbird]] dropships while bombarding a planet. The [[Emperor of Mankind]] gave it to the Legion later named the Death Guard.The construction date, c. M30, is estimated from the narrative of the Great Crusade. No surviving record gives a year.

Its captain was Calas Typhon. He fell to Chaos before his Legion and before his primarch [[Mortarion]], and his corruption passed into the ship.

Horus Heresy

The ship took part in the [[Isstvan III]] atrocity and pursued the frigate [[Eisenstein]] as it fled to warn Terra, c. 005.M31. At the [[Drop Site Massacre]] on [[Isstvan V]] it destroyed the [[Raven Guard]] flagship Shadow of the Emperor, deprived the Loyalists of air support, and is recorded as an early battle between ships built to carry attack craft.Dates for the Isstvan battles are given as c. 005.M31 from the sequence of the Heresy.

Bound for [[Terra]], the ship was becalmed in the [[Warp]] and Typhon killed its [[Navigator]]. There Nurgle visited the Destroyer Plague on every soul aboard but Typhon, who became Typhus. When the ship came to Terra, its hull already showed the mark of Chaos, and legend says it was first to bombard the [[Imperial Palace]]. When [[Horus]] fell, it covered the Legion's retreat to the [[Eye of Terror]].

After the Heresy

It was thought destroyed until 545.M35, when it fell on the Imperium again, with plagues spreading wherever a battle was lost. In 216.M36 the [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] found its plans on [[Barbarus]], and the Despoiler class followed; its Gellar fields proved to fail after long exposure to the Warp.

In 497.M36 it was at Yermoth VIII, in 299.M37 it destroyed the Dominus Aquila star fort, and in 834.M37 it sealed the fate of the asteroid cities of the Emperor's Halo. In 620.M39 it fought the Orks of [[Octarius]] in the Dust War. In 139.M41 it was seen near Anvil 206 during the [[Gothic War]], and in the [[13th Black Crusade]] it destroyed the cruiser Depth of Fury above [[Kathur]], taking over thirty per cent structural damage that it regenerated.

Form and crew

Its bulkheads are held by rust, its decks are lined with living flesh, and its gun decks have been replaced by sphincter-like growths that spit caustic filth. Clouds of Warp-flies hide the hull from gunners and swarm boarding parties.

Only [[Plague Marines]] walk its corridors unharmed. The ship needs no crew, having a dark sentience of its own, and Typhus sits on a throne of bone at its helm. It often leads a [[Plague Fleet]] and is said to shadow plague-struck ships until the survivors pray to Nurgle.
