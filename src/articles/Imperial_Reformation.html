The Imperial Reformation was the reorganisation of the Imperium's armed forces in the early [[32nd millennium]], after the [[Horus Heresy]]. Its aim was to prevent a Legion from ever again becoming powerful enough to rebel. [[Space Marine Legions|The Legions]] were divided into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] under the [[Codex Astartes]], and the [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]] was separated from the Legions as an independent force.

The reform is attributed to the Loyalist leaders of the Heresy, among them Guilliman, who had seen the Legions' loyalty split under [[Traitor]] influence. The Reformation is recorded as one of the defining acts of the Imperium's early history.

Division of the Legions

The [[Codex Astartes]] divided each surviving Legion into [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] of about a thousand warriors. The Chapters were given their own homeworlds, fortresses and organisation, so that no single commander could command the whole force. Imperial records describe the change as a deliberate break with the Legion system that had allowed the [[Traitor|Traitors]] to act together.

The Army

The [[Imperialis Auxilia|Imperial Army]], which had been raised from the Legions' auxiliaries and the Legion forces themselves, was dissolved and rebuilt as a separate force. The change is recorded in the Imperial Army's own records as its formal separation from the [[Adeptus Astartes|Space Marines]]. The [[Tactica Imperium]] was written in the same years as a guide for the new army.
