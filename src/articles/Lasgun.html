The Lasgun, also sometimes called a Lasrifle, is a directed-energy antipersonnel weapon used by the military forces of the [[Imperium of Mankind]]. It is the most common and widely used type of laser weapon in [[Milky Way|the galaxy]]. Its beam is produced by a power cell, and its design has changed little over many centuries of Imperial service.

Lasguns are issued to the troops of the [[Astra Militarum]] in enormous numbers. The [[Thramas Crusade|Triplex]] [[Battle of Phall|Phall]] pattern is one of the most common variants. Imperial records describe the weapon as cheap, reliable and easy to maintain, but weak against armour and nearly useless against the [[Space Marine]]s' [[Power armour|power armour]].

Maintenance

Each Lasgun is serviced by its regiment's armourers and by the [[Departmento Munitorum]], which supplies the power cells and the replacement parts. The [[Adeptus Mechanicus]] produces many of the weapon's components in its forges.
