Gereon is an [[Agri-world|agri-world]] of the [[Sabbat Worlds]]. Its fields fed the armies of the region before the [[Sabbat Worlds Crusade|Sabbat Crusade]] reached it. It was taken by the forces of [[Anakwanar Sek]] during [[Long War|the long war]] in the Khan Group, and it became one of the worlds on which the Archenemy raised its own troops.

Gereon is known mainly for its place in the Sek campaigns. Its occupation brought the world into the war between the Archenemy's commanders for control of the crusade's flank. Its resistance was the first to be reached by a team under [[Ibram Gaunt]], and its liberation is recorded as one of the successes of the Sabbat crusade.

Occupation

After Sek's rival [[Urlock Gaur]] had been named Archon, Sek alone kept his forces together. He raised an elite army, the Sons of Sek, and trained it on Gereon. The world was ruled by [[Noches Sturm]], a former [[Imperial Army|Imperial]] [[Lord General Militant|Lord General]] who had defected to the Archenemy. Its fields and factories were turned to the needs of the army.

Liberation

Gereon's resistance was first reached by a team under [[Ibram Gaunt|Gaunt]]. The liberation that followed is not dated in the sources consulted. The Sons of Sek were removed from the world, and the [[Agri-world|agri-world]] was returned to the [[Governance of the Imperium|Imperial administration]].The date of the liberation is not recorded. Imperial accounts place it in the middle of the Sabbat campaign, but no record gives an exact year.
