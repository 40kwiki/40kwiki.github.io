The Crimson Fists are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created from the [[Imperial Fists]] Legion of [[Rogal Dorn]] in the [[Second Founding]]. They were a fleet-based Chapter for nine millennia before they were granted Rynn's World in the [[Loki Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]], and they are known across the Imperium for their wars against the [[Orks]]. In 989.M41 the Chapter was almost destroyed when its own fortress-monastery, the Arx Tyrannus, was struck by a misfired missile during the invasion of the Ork Warlord [[Snagrod]], and by some accounts fewer than a company of battle-brothers survived.

The survivors, under [[Chapter Master]] Pedro Kantor, held out until the Imperium relieved Rynn's World in 990.M41, and spent the following years rebuilding. In the [[Era Indomitus]], after [[Daemon|daemons]] struck Rynn's World in the storms that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the [[Indomitus Crusade]] under [[Roboute Guilliman]] relieved the planet and brought the Chapter companies of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. The restored Crimson Fists have since fought on [[Vigilus]] and across the Loki Sector.

History

Founding

The Crimson Fists were among the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] created from the [[Imperial Fists|Imperial Fists Legion]] when [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s [[Codex Astartes]] broke the Legions apart in 021.M31. Their first [[Chapter Master]] was [[Alexis Polux]], a warrior of the VII Legion who had won [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]'s respect during the [[Horus Heresy]] and who led the Chapter for its first eight hundred years. The Chapter's first recorded engagement was the defence of the colony of Uralek Prime against Aeldari Exodites. For the next nine millennia the Chapter was fleet-based, and its battle barge Rutilus Tyrannus served as its mobile fortress-monastery.

Crusades and Rynn's World

The Chapter recorded many campaigns over the following millennia, most against [[Orks]]. Its most famous was the Crusade of Righteous Liberation of the 36th millennium, which lasted three hundred standard years and left the Chapter with only 128 battle-brothers; the 1st Company has been kept at 128 in memory of it. In the 40th millennium the [[High Lords of Terra]] granted the Chapter Rynn's World, in the Loki Sector, as a reward for its success in the Voltigern Crusade against the Orks of the sector, and the Chapter built the Arx Tyrannus from the dismantled Rutilus Tyrannus in the Hellblade Mountains. Among its other campaigns were the Thule Intervention of 978.M39, in which it met an [[Aeldari]] army from [[Iyanden]] and inflicted heavy losses as the xenos withdrew; a feud with the [[Mortifactors]] in 819.M41; the Battle of the Steel Cross in 853.M41; the Hellabore Assault of 867.M41, led by Captain [[Alessio Cortez]] of the 4th Company; the planetstrike against the renegades called the [[Purge]] after the Vaxhallian Genocide of 926.M41; and the defeat of the warband of the [[Chaos]] Lord Sathash the Golden at Toran VI in 934.M41. The Chapter was one of the eight that attended the 104th [[Feast of Blades]] in 949.M41.

Battle of Rynn's World

In 989.M41 Snagrod, the Ork warlord called the Arch-Arsonist of Charadon, united the warring Ork factions around the Loki Sector and began a great WAAAGH!, boasting that his next conquest after the colony world of Badlanding would be Rynn's World. A 3rd Company strike force sent to kill him before he could attack failed when a scout's shot missed, and only twenty-eight of its eighty-four warriors returned. Snagrod then invaded Rynn's World.

As the Chapter prepared to meet him, a missile launched from the Arx Tyrannus's own defence batteries misfired and fell on the fortress-monastery's armoury. The explosion destroyed the Arx Tyrannus and killed many hundreds of the Chapter at a stroke, including six entire companies. Pedro Kantor was manning the outermost perimeter defences and was among only sixteen battle-brothers who survived the blast. They made their way through Ork-held country to New Rynn City, where they joined the detachment that had been guarding the capital.

For eighteen solar months the survivors and the planetary defence forces held New Rynn City against the Orks. They were relieved in 990.M41 by a large Imperial Navy fleet, with regiments of the Astra Militarum, contingents of several Space Marine Chapters and battlegroups of Titans, which drove the Orks off the world. Imperial accounts differ on the Chapter's losses: some give nearly sixty in a hundred of its battle-brothers, others leave fewer than a company alive. Snagrod escaped.

Rebuilding

The Chapter lost much of its gene-seed stock and ancient wargear with the Arx Tyrannus. Its survivors included a disproportionate number of veterans, Techmarines and Apothecaries, whose skills made the rebuilding possible. The Chapter recruited new aspirants from Rynn's World and across the Loki Sector, and re-formed its 10th Company from a few Scout squads.

The coming of the Avenging Son

After the [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, warp storms broke over Rynn's World and the Daemon Prince Rhaxor led a daemonic host against it. Kantor led three sorties to drive it back. The Indomitus Crusade arrived without warning; Guilliman himself led the relief, worked beside Kantor to destroy the daemons, and remained three days on Rynn's World while his fleet refitted. Archmagos [[Belisarius Cawl]]'s Ark Mechanicus Zar-Quaesitor delivered whole companies of Primaris Space Marines bearing the Chapter's heraldry, made from gene-seed close to Dorn's own, and the Chapter's Apothecarion was given the means to make more.

Before he left, Guilliman told Kantor to make the Loki Sector a fortress that could become the linchpin of the whole [[Segmentum]]. The Chapter integrated its new brothers through its rites, and began deploying measured strike forces and veteran kill-teams across the sector against Orks, rogue governors and daemon-cults. For the first time in decades it sent veterans to the Deathwatch.

Vigilus

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

During the [[War of Beasts]] on the Sentinel World of Vigilus, at the mouth of the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], Kantor led five companies, half his Chapter, beside the Imperial Fists. Placed under the Vigilus Senate of [[Marneus Calgar]], they were sent not against the Orks but into the Dirkden Hivesprawl, where they fought a web of delaying actions against the [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer cult]] called the Pauper Princes so that the civilian population could escape. Tens of thousands of lives were saved, although Dirkden itself was lost.

Organisation

The Chapter follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. Besides the Arx Tyrannus the Chapter kept the Cassar, a fortified keep in the Silver Citadel of New Rynn City, which survived the invasion of 989.M41 and became the Chapter's refuge. New recruits must pass the trial of the Bloodied Fist, returning with their bare hand stained with the blood of a barb-dragon, before they may wear crimson on the right gauntlet; the Chapter holds a Festival of the Bloodied Fist each year. Since the [[Era Indomitus]] its companies have combined [[Primaris Space Marines|Firstborn]] and Primaris warriors.

Beliefs and gene-seed

See also: [[Gene-seed]]

The Crimson Fists share the [[Imperial Fists]]' reverence for [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] and their stubbornness in defence, and their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] is highly stable and free of other major mutation, though like the Imperial Fists' it lacks the Betcher's gland and the Sus-an membrane. The Chapter's hatred of the [[Orks]] is a matter of honour as much as doctrine.

Heraldry

The Chapter's armour is dark blue. The right gauntlet is painted crimson, a right won in the trial of the Bloodied Fist. Its badge is derived from that of the Imperial Fists, painted red in honour of Alexis Polux, the Chapter's first Chapter Master.
