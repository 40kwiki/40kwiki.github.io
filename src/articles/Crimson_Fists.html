The Crimson Fists are a [[Space Marine Chapter]] of the [[Adeptus Astartes]], created from the [[Imperial Fists]] Legion of [[Rogal Dorn]] in the [[Second Founding]]. They held Rynn's World in the [[Loki Sector]] of the [[Segmentum Tempestus]] for ten thousand years and are known across the Imperium for their wars against the [[Orks]]. In 989.M41 the Chapter was almost destroyed when its own fortress-monastery, the Arx Tyrannus, was struck by a misfired missile during the invasion of the Ork Warlord [[Snagrod]], and only about a hundred battle-brothers survived.

The survivors, under [[Chapter Master]] Pedro Kantor, held out until the Imperium relieved Rynn's World in 990.M41, and spent the following decade rebuilding. In the [[Era Indomitus]], after [[Daemon|daemons]] struck Rynn's World in the storms that followed the opening of the [[Great Rift]], the [[Indomitus Crusade]] under [[Roboute Guilliman]] relieved the planet and brought the Chapter companies of [[Primaris Space Marines]]. The restored Crimson Fists have since fought on [[Vigilus]] and across the Loki Sector.

History

Founding

The Crimson Fists were among the [[Space Marine Chapter|Chapters]] created from the [[Imperial Fists|Imperial Fists Legion]] when [[Roboute Guilliman]]'s [[Codex Astartes]] broke the Legions apart in 021.M31. Their first [[Chapter Master]] was [[Alexis Polux]], a warrior of the VII Legion who had won [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]]'s respect during the [[Horus Heresy]] and who led the Chapter for its first eight hundred years. The Chapter's first recorded engagement was the defence of the colony of Uralek Prime against Aeldari Exodites. It took Rynn's World, a temperate [[Agri-world|agricultural world]] of the [[Loki Sector]], as its home, and built the Arx Tyrannus there.Some Chapter records place the formal founding a few years after 021.M31, on the arrival of the first companies at Rynn's World.

Ten thousand years

The Chapter recorded many campaigns over the following millennia, most against [[Orks]]. Among them were the Thule Intervention of 978.M39, in which it fought alongside [[Aeldari]] of [[Iyanden]]; a feud with the [[Mortifactors]] in 819.M41; the Battle of the Steel Cross in 853.M41; the Hellabore Assault of 867.M41, led by Captain [[Alessio Cortez]] of the 4th Company; the defeat of the renegades called the [[Purge]] after the Vaxhallian Genocide of 926.M41; and the destruction of the warband of the [[Chaos]] Lord Sathorael at Toran VI in 934.M41. The Chapter was one of the eight that attended the 104th [[Feast of Blades]] in 949.M41.

Battle of Rynn's World

In 989.M41 Snagrod, the Ork warlord called the Arch-Arsonist of Charadon, led his WAAAGH! out of the Charadon sector into the Loki Sector. A 3rd Company strike force sent to kill him before he could attack failed when a scout's shot missed; only twenty-eight of its eighty-four warriors returned. Snagrod then invaded Rynn's World.

As the Chapter prepared to meet him, a missile launched from the Arx Tyrannus's own defence batteries misfired and fell on the fortress-monastery's armoury. The explosion destroyed the Arx Tyrannus and killed most of the Chapter at a stroke. Pedro Kantor dug himself out of the ruins and gathered the survivors, who fought their way to New Rynn City. A few companies on campaign elsewhere, and isolated groups such as the warriors under Cortez at Traitor's Gorge, held on in the field.

For eighteen months, from mid-989 to 990.M41, the survivors and the planetary defence forces held New Rynn City against the Orks. They were relieved in 990.M41 by a large Imperial Navy fleet and six Space Marine Chapters, who drove the Orks off the world. Fewer than one in ten of the Chapter had survived. Snagrod escaped.

Rebuilding

In the years after the battle the Chapter rebuilt slowly. It recruited from a world devastated by the Orks, and it could not send its usual share of veterans to the [[Deathwatch]]. By the end of the 41st millennium it had restored perhaps three companies.

The coming of the Avenging Son

After the [[Fall of Cadia]] and the opening of the Great Rift in 999.M41, warp storms broke over Rynn's World and the Daemon Prince Rhaxor led a daemonic host against it. Kantor led three sorties to drive it back. The Indomitus Crusade arrived without warning; Guilliman himself led the relief, worked beside Kantor to destroy the daemons, and remained three days on Rynn's World while his fleet refitted. Archmagos [[Belisarius Cawl]]'s Ark Mechanicus Zar-Quaesitor delivered whole companies of Primaris Space Marines bearing the Chapter's heraldry, made from gene-seed close to Dorn's own, and the Chapter's Apothecarion was given the means to make more.The relief is dated from the early progress of the Indomitus Crusade; Chapter records give no exact year.

Before he left, Guilliman told Kantor to make the Loki Sector a fortress that could become the linchpin of the whole [[Segmentum]]. The Chapter integrated its new brothers through its rites, and began deploying measured strike forces and veteran kill-teams across the sector against Orks, rogue governors and daemon-cults. For the first time in decades it sent veterans to the Deathwatch.

Vigilus

Main article: [[War of Nightmares]]

During the [[War of Beasts]] on the Sentinel World of Vigilus, at the mouth of the [[Nachmund Gauntlet]], Kantor led five companies, half his Chapter, beside the Imperial Fists. Placed under the Vigilus Senate of [[Marneus Calgar]], they were sent not against the Orks but into the Dirkden Hivesprawl, where they fought a web of delaying actions against the [[Genestealer Cults|Genestealer cult]] called the Pauper Princes so that the civilian population could escape. Tens of thousands of lives were saved, although Dirkden itself was lost.

Organisation

The Chapter follows the [[Codex Astartes]]. Besides the Arx Tyrannus the Chapter kept the Cassar, a fortified keep in the Silver Citadel of New Rynn City, which survived the invasion of 989.M41 and became the Chapter's refuge. New recruits must pass the trial of the Bloodied Fist, returning with their bare hand stained with the blood of a barb-dragon, before they may wear crimson on the right gauntlet; the Chapter holds a Festival of the Bloodied Fist each year. Since the [[Era Indomitus]] its companies have combined [[Primaris Space Marines|Firstborn]] and Primaris warriors.

Beliefs and gene-seed

The Crimson Fists share the [[Imperial Fists]]' reverence for [[Rogal Dorn|Dorn]] and their stubbornness in defence, but they are known as a more humane Chapter than many, mindful of the people they protect. Their [[Gene-seed|gene-seed]] is regarded as pure, though it shares the Imperial Fists' known deficiencies. The Chapter's hatred of the [[Orks]] is a matter of honour as much as doctrine.

Heraldry

The Chapter's armour is dark blue. The right gauntlet is painted crimson, a right won in the trial of the Bloodied Fist. Its badge is a red clenched fist in a red ring on a blue field.
